12.29.2003 12:00

Keyboards, Quail Gardens

Arg. Got nailed by the cold that is going around everywhere. Almost over it. Bought a USB-TO-PS2 adaptor at Radio Shack after the great products one from Fry's. I can't believe that Fry's didn't have any USB split keyboards. Too many damn wireless keyboards. They are nice and all, but I got over the excitement when I had an IBM PCjr back in the days. The most important keyboard feature is not causing me pain followed by my typing rate. Apple has pretty pathetic keyboards. Too small for my hands and hard on the wrists.

The family all went to the Quail Gardens light show the night before Christmas. Now we can say that we did it. They need a better designer. Could have been awesome, but too many white lights just thrown on things. The waterfall was off. For $5 a head, it's not really worth it.

Now to get ready for the 3rd when we land on Mars. My sympathies for the Beagle 2 team. I hope it does wake up. I sat through the waiting with the Mars Polar Lander. I know what that is like.

12.16.2003 12:00

At JPL for MER - Mac OSX

I am now all setup for being on the MER team. Getting a tour of the facilities and talking to everybody has me totally fired up for this mission. It's going to be rockin! The ops center is enough to knock your socks off. Looks like many of the lessons learned from Pathfinder and Mars Polar Lander have been applied to setup. One major change from last time is a major shift in the OSes uses for the heavy lifting during operations. The dominant OSes in use are Linux and Mac OS X. I'll have my home base on a shiny new G5! There are a few Solaris file servers around plus the occasional MS Windows and SGI boxes around, but they don't get that much use. The ops center is much more prepared for laptops this time around, so using my G4 15 inch titanium laptop will be a breeze. Now I just have to learn me a couple hundred acronyms and I'll be ready to go. Woohoo!
