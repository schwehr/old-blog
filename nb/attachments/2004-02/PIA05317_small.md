# Map of Mars Exploration Rover Spirit's Path Toward Bonneville Crater (`PIA05317_small.jpg`)

## Overview
An annotated overhead orbital and surface mosaic map (`PIA05317`, "Spirit's Path to Bonneville," posted February 18, 2004) charting the Mars Exploration Rover *Spirit*'s traverse from its landing site at **Sleepy Hollow** northeastward through **Laguna Hollow** toward the rim of **Bonneville Crater**.

## Map & Traverse Details
- **Orbital Base Map**: A grayscale overhead orbital image of Gusev Crater on Mars, dominated in the upper-right quadrant by the large, dark circular shadow and bright rim of **Bonneville Crater**, with smaller secondary craters visible along the left side.
- **Surface Panorama Mosaic Inset**: In the lower-left and center-left portion of the image, a yellow-outlined polygon encloses a composite of ground-based radial projections taken by *Spirit*'s cameras along its route.
- **Rover Traverse Path**: A solid yellow line traces the rover's path starting near a hook-shaped turn at **Sleepy Hollow** (`sol 37`), continuing northeast to `sol 39`, `sol 41`, `sol 43`, and reaching **Laguna Hollow** at `sol 44`.
- **Projected Route**: From the `sol 44` position inside Laguna Hollow, a dashed red arrow extends straight toward the northeast, pointing directly at the southern rim of Bonneville Crater.
- **Circular Features**: Light cyan circles highlight small craters or hollows along the traverse, including two near Sleepy Hollow, two near Sol 41–43, and two at Laguna Hollow.
