# Carter Emmart Illustration of a Mars Canyon Rover (`canyonrover-carter-emmart.jpg`)

## Overview
A monochrome pencil/ink conceptual artwork by space artist **Carter Emmart** (dated 1996, posted February 1, 2004) illustrating astronauts using a heavy tracked crawler vehicle to lower or winch a six-wheeled pressurized rover down a steep, rocky Martian canyon slope.

## Scene & Vehicle Description
- **Tracked Crawler / Winch Vehicle (Center-Left)**: A wide, low-slung, heavy-duty vehicle resting on four large, tank-like caterpillar tracks cresting the brow of a steep ridge.
  - It features an angled, faceted truss-frame cab in the center and two vertical hydraulic stabilizers or gantry legs at its front (left) end, where a taut cable angles downward to the left to anchor the vehicle.
  - Mounted atop the vehicle is a circular parabolic dish antenna and tall whip antennas.
  - Extending from the right side of the tracked crawler is a heavy articulated boom holding a sleek, six-wheeled pressurized rover with large, balloon-style tires suspended in mid-air over the slope.
- **Astronauts (Bottom Right)**: Two astronauts in bulky pressurized spacesuits with life-support backpacks stand on the sunlit ridge to the right of the suspended rover. Both hold guide ropes or tether lines attached to the suspended wheeled rover to stabilize it as it is deployed onto the steep incline.
- **Martian Canyon Environment**: Behind the vehicles and astronauts, towering canyon walls rise steeply toward the upper right, with stratified cliff faces at the top and long talus slopes of loose boulders and scree sweeping diagonally downward. A dark, shadowed canyon wall forms the backdrop on the left.
