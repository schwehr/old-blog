# Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicle Operations Handbook Diagram (`lunar-rover-handbook.png`)

## Overview
A black-and-white technical line-art diagram from the NASA Apollo **Lunar Roving Vehicle Operations Handbook** (`LS006-002-2H`, posted February 3, 2004), showing a three-quarter perspective view of the Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV) chassis, wheels, seating, and control console with numbered/lettered circular callouts pointing to individual components.

## Diagram Details
- **Vehicle Structure**: The LRV is depicted stripped of its forward payload equipment (such as the high-gain and low-gain antennas and TV camera) to clearly illustrate the underlying frame and mobility/crew systems:
  - **Wheels & Fenders**: Four woven-wire mesh wheels with chevron tread strips and hubcaps, each covered by a curved fender. The front-left (left) and rear-left (center-bottom) wheels face the viewer, while the rear-right wheel is visible at the far right.
  - **Chassis & Forward Bay**: The tubular aluminum frame is divided into the forward chassis bay (left, holding battery and electronics boxes), the central floor pan with beaded/corrugated panels and foldable lawn-chair-style tubular seats with webbing straps for two astronauts, and the rear chassis frame (right).
  - **Control & Display Console**: Positioned between the two seats, featuring the T-handle hand controller, attitude indicator pitch/roll scale, and instrument display panel.
  - **Crew Restraints & Accessories**: Seat belts, inboard and outboard handholds, footrests, and under-seat stowage areas.

- **Header Text** (centered at top in uppercase monospaced font above a horizontal rule):
