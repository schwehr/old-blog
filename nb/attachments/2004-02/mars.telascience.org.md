# Maestro Headquarters Web Page Banner (`mars.telascience.org.png`)

## Overview
A promotional web graphic screenshot from **`mars.telascience.org`** ("Maestro Headquarters," posted February 29, 2004), advertising the public downloadable version of **Maestro**—the Java-based science activity planner and visualization software used by NASA scientists to operate the Mars Exploration Rovers *Spirit* and *Opportunity*.

## Graphic & Layout Description
- **Rover Rendering (Right)**: A high-resolution 3D computer rendering of a Mars Exploration Rover (*Spirit* / *Opportunity*) positioned on the right half of the banner, breaking out of the inner rectangular frame into the dark charcoal-gray top and right borders:
  - The white Pancam Mast Assembly (PMA) with stereo Pancam and Navcam camera bar rises above the top border.
  - The gold-colored Warm Electronics Box (WEB) body, spread solar array wings, UHF antenna, and circular high-gain antenna dish are clearly visible.
  - The robotic Instrument Deployment Device (IDD) arm is extended forward at left, and the six cleated aluminum wheels on the rocker-bogie suspension rest along the bottom edge.
- **Background**: Behind the rover and text is a grayscale photograph of a rocky Martian plain under a light gray sky, bordered at the top and right by a dark gray margin.

## Visible Text & Links
- **Upper-Left Promotional Copy** (in black sans-serif text over the light gray sky):
  > **Explore Mars** with the program that  
  > NASA scientists use to operate Spirit and  
  > Opportunity.
  >
  > **<u>Download Maestro</u>**, and look at Mars the way  
  > NASA does.
- **Call-to-Action Link**: Centered over the rocky grayscale terrain on the left in bold bright yellow text:
  > **`[ Explore ]`**
- **Bottom Banner Title**: Spanning the bottom left and center in large white uppercase sans-serif letters (with an italicized *`O`* in *MAESTRO*):
  > **`MAESTRO HEADQUARTERS`**
