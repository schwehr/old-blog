# "How to Stop Worrying and Love the Blob" Presentation Web Page (`viz04-blob.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Kurt Schwehr's **Viz04** web presentation page (posted October 29, 2004), titled *"How to stop worrying and love the blob"*, introducing research on applying 3D probability density estimations (`density` and `xcore`) of Anisotropy of Magnetic Susceptibility (AMS) to marine slope failures.

## Page Layout & Visual Elements
The web page has a dark charcoal-brown background (`#2b2626`) and is organized into three vertical sections:
1. **Top Composite Title Slide Banner**:
   - **Background Photo**: A washed-out overhead view of an oceanographic CTD Rosette water-sampling cage being lowered into the ocean, creating white circular ripples and splashes around the frame and cable.
   - **Center Title Text** (in bright orange/gold sans-serif text):
     - Top center: **`How to stop worrying`** / **`and love the blob`**
     - Center below the rosette ring: **`Kurt Schwehr`** / **`Oct 2004`**
   - **Four Corner Inset Images** (each framed with a thin light-gray border):
     - **Top Left**: A coastal photograph of the reddish-orange sea cliffs at Scripps/Black's Beach (the Ardath Shale slump sample locality) with a house perched on top of the cliff and birds on the wet beach below.
     - **Bottom Left**: A color-coded 3D shaded-relief bathymetric rendering (green to blue depth gradient with orange markers) of the **Gaviota Slide**, a modern submarine slope failure in the Santa Barbara Channel.
     - **Top Right**: A closer outcrop photograph of the contorted rocks of the **Ardath Shale slump** on the beach.
     - **Bottom Right**: A 3D volumetric visualization on a black background showing glowing blue, red, and yellow probability density "blobs" arranged in a ring/girdle pattern representing AMS fabric data.
2. **Middle Explanatory Text**: Two paragraphs of white sans-serif text describing the presentation and the five images in the title slide.
3. **Bottom Thumbnail Navigation Grid (`Next Image`)**: Under the gray heading **`Next Image`**, a two-row grid of 13 rectangular thumbnail images links to subsequent slides in the presentation:
   - **Row 1 (8 thumbnails)**:
     1. Close-up of the Ardath Shale slump rock outcrop.
     2. Dark 3D visualization scene with inset views.
     3. Aerial/elevated coastal view of a cliffside building above the beach.
     4. White 2D scatter plot with blue data points and a purple regression line.
     5. White 3D axis box with floating red, green, and blue data clusters.
     6. 3D geological block/slice diagram with a purple vertical plane.
     7. Black 3D plot showing three colored principal axes connected to glowing red/blue probability density blobs.
     8. Blue lower-hemisphere stereonet/bowl projection with red/yellow density contours.
   - **Row 2 (5 thumbnails)**:
     9. Dual ring-shaped blue/orange 3D density volume renderings on a black background.
     10. Single ring-shaped blue/cyan 3D density volume rendering on a black background.
     11. 3D bathymetric surface model with an inset map of the Gaviota Slide.
     12. Two parallel yellow sediment core sections on a black background.
     13. Red horseshoe-shaped U-channel / magnetometer loop diagram on a dark background.
