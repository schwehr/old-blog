# Virgin Atlantic GlobalFlyer 3D Flight Path Visualization (`globalFlyer.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a 3D interactive Open Inventor / Coin3D scene viewer window displaying a globe visualization of the Virgin Atlantic GlobalFlyer's solo non-stop circumnavigation flight path on March 1, 2005, generated by Alex (ACD) using Coin and a live GPS feed.

## Globe & Flight Path Visualization
- **3D Globe**: A stylized spherical Earth sits centered against a solid black space background. Oceans are rendered in deep royal blue with subtle specular lighting toward the upper-left Atlantic, while landmasses (North America, South America, Europe, Africa, and the Middle East) are rendered as flat, unshaded brown polygons.
- **Flight Track**: A thin white polyline traces the aircraft's trajectory starting from the central United States (Salina, Kansas), heading northeast toward the Great Lakes and eastern Canada, turning sharply south-southeast across the North Atlantic Ocean, then curving eastward across the Strait of Gibraltar, North Africa (Morocco, Algeria, Libya, Egypt), the northern Red Sea, and across the Arabian Peninsula toward the Persian Gulf.
- **3D Aircraft Model**: Positioned at the eastern end of the trajectory over the Persian Gulf / Arabian coast (on the right limb of the globe) is a white 3D model of the twin-boom Virgin Atlantic GlobalFlyer aircraft facing eastward.

## Viewer Interface & Controls
- **Left Border**: A vertical gray panel with a thumbwheel control labeled `RotX` at the bottom left.
- **Bottom Border**: A horizontal gray bar with a `RotY` thumbwheel on the left and the label `Dolly` on the far right.
- **Right Border**: A vertical toolbar at the top right featuring seven classic Open Inventor / Coin3D ExaminerViewer buttons:
  1. Red arrow cursor icon (Pick/Interact mode)
  2. White hand icon (Examine/View mode)
  3. House icon (Home view)
  4. Crosshair/target icon (Set home)
  5. Eye with globe icon (View all)
  6. Flashlight/magnifying glass icon (Seek)
  7. Red/yellow cube projection icon (Perspective/Orthographic toggle)
- Below the toolbar at the bottom right is a vertical thumbwheel control for `Dolly` (zoom).
