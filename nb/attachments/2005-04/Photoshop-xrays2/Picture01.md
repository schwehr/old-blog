# Aligning Core X-Ray Plates Using Rulers and Guides in Photoshop (`Picture01.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Adobe Photoshop CS** on Mac OS X illustrating the initial step of assembling digital X-ray plates of a sediment core (`bp04-5ga-s2`) by measuring the 10 cm distance between plastic alignment pegs with the Measure (Ruler) tool and placing a horizontal guide to align adjacent X-ray plate layers.

## Interface & Controls
- **Tool Palette (Left)**: The floating Photoshop toolbar is partially visible on the left edge, showing the **Measure Tool** (ruler icon, top right of the visible tool grid) active/selected, alongside the Notes tool, Hand tool, Zoom tool, Foreground/Background color swatches (black/black), Quick Mask mode buttons, screen mode buttons, and the ImageReady jump button.
- **Vertical Ruler**: Running along the left edge of the document canvas, calibrated in pixels with major tick labels visible from `2800` down to `3400` (`2800`, `2900`, `3000`, `3100`, `3200`, `3300`, `3400`).

## Canvas & X-Ray Alignment Details
- **Background & Labels**: Against a black canvas background to the left of the X-ray strip, bold white text labels mark the depth and core section identifier:
  - `030cm` near the top (`y ≈ 2840`)
  - `bp04-5ga-s2` in the middle (`y ≈ 3180`)
  - `40cm` near the bottom (`y ≈ 3365`)
- **X-Ray Strip & Measurement Line**:
  - On the right side of the canvas, two overlapping grayscale X-ray plate layers of the sediment core are visible.
  - Circular dark shadows of the plastic alignment pegs are visible inside the core X-ray at the `030cm` and `40cm` positions.
  - A vertical black measurement line with small crosshair endpoints (`+`) extends from the center of the upper peg (`030cm`) straight down to the center of the lower peg (`40cm`).
  - A horizontal white/cyan guide line spans across the canvas at the `40cm` mark (`y ≈ 3355`), passing directly through the lower crosshair and intersecting the `"40cm"` text label.
