# Result of Auto Levels on Core X-Ray Plate (`Picture06.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Adobe Photoshop CS** on Mac OS X showing the result of applying **Auto Levels** to the interior selection of the first X-ray plate (`bp04-5ga-s1`) in the 16-bit grayscale document `bp04-5ga-xray-auto.psd`. The contrast inside the selected rectangle is strongly enhanced, revealing internal sediment mottling, density variations, and structure between `0` and `25 cm`.

## Interface & Document Details
- **Window Title**: `bp04-5ga-xray-auto.psd @ 33.3% (Background copy, Gray/16)`
- **Rulers**: Horizontal ruler spans `0` to `1400` pixels in 100-pixel increments; vertical ruler spans `0` to `1400` pixels down the left side.
- **Tool Palette (Left)**: The two-column Photoshop toolbar is visible on the far left with the **Rectangular Marquee Tool** active (`Feather: 0 px`, `Style: Normal`).
- **Layers Palette (Right)**: Displays `Background copy` (selected in blue) above the locked `Background` layer.

## Core X-Ray Canvas
- **Visible Labels**: White text along the left of the X-ray strip reads `bp04-5ga-s1` (near `y = 130`), `10cm` (near `y = 530`), and `20cm` (near `y = 1040`).
- **Auto-Leveled Selection**:
  - The rectangular marquee (`x ≈ 950` to `1250`, `y ≈ 115` to `1385`) encloses the center of the core section while excluding the core liner walls and the circular alignment pegs along the left inner edge.
  - Within the marquee, the upper half (`0–13 cm`) shows horizontal banding and a circular ring-like structure near the top, while the lower half (`13–24 cm`) is much brighter (higher X-ray attenuation/density) with irregular clasts, mottling, and a dark low-density pocket along the right margin around `16–18 cm`.
