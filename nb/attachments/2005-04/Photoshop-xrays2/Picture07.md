# Opening Manual Levels Adjustment on a Copied Core Sub-Region (`Picture07.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Adobe Photoshop CS** on Mac OS X demonstrating how to isolate a specific sub-region of the raw core X-ray from the backup layer, paste it as a separate layer positioned in the black scratch space to the left of the core, and open `Image -> Adjustments -> Levels...` (`⌘L`) to manually tune the grayscale contrast.

## Interface & Menu Details
- **Menu Selection**: The `Image` menu and its `Adjustments` submenu are open, with `Levels... ⌘L` highlighted at the top of the submenu.
- **Background Window**: Behind the semi-transparent Mac OS X menu dropdown on the right, an Excel spreadsheet (`...-5gw-sec1.xls`) with column `E` headed `Description` is faintly visible through the menu translucency.

## Canvas Details (Left)
- **Copied Sub-Region**: On the far left of the black canvas (`x ≈ 20` to `320`), a rectangular slice of the unadjusted grayscale X-ray plate is positioned inside a marching-ants marquee selection.
- **Annotation & Labels**: Just to the right of the pasted sub-region is a small white text annotation reading `"Is this upside down?"` (partially covered by the menu). Below the menu, the `20cm` depth label and the bright auto-leveled lower portion of `bp04-5ga-s1` are visible.
