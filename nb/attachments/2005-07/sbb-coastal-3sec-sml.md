A downscaled and slightly cropped preview screenshot (`sbb-coastal-3sec-sml.png`, 600×400 pixels) of the IVS Fledermaus application displaying the 3-arc-second NOAA NGDC Coastal Relief Model (`sbb-ngdc-coastal-relief-3sec.sd`) of the Santa Barbara Basin, used as an inline clickable thumbnail in the July 31, 2005 blog post *"NGDC Coastal Relief Model"*.
