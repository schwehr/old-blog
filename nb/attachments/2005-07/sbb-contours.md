# GMT Bathymetric Contour Map of the Santa Barbara Basin (`sbb-contours.png`)

## Overview
A Generic Mapping Tools (GMT)Mercator-projection contour map (`sbb-contours.png`, 792×535 pixels) titled `Santa Barbara Basin Circulation`, showing the bathymetric contours of the Santa Barbara Basin bounded by the Southern California mainland coast to the north and the Northern Channel Islands to the south. Created using `mbm_grdplot` and `grdcontour` from `santabarbC_bath.grd` (documented in the July 28, 2005 blog post *"gmt contour map"*).

## Map & Contour Details
- **Geographic Frame & Coordinate Grid**:
  - **Longitude (Top and Bottom Axes)**: Labeled in degrees and minutes East (from `239°30'` to `240°40'` in `5'` increments, corresponding to `120°30'W` to `119°20'W`) with alternating black and white map border bars.
  - **Latitude (Left and Right Axes)**: Labeled from `33°55'` to `34°25'` North in `5'` increments (`33°55'`, `34°00'`, `34°05'`, `34°10'`, `34°15'`, `34°20'`, `34°25'`).
- **Coastlines & Landmasses (Reddish-Brown Fill)**:
  - **Northern Mainland**: The Santa Barbara / Ventura coastline runs across the top of the map from Point Conception on the left (`239°32'`, `34°27'`) eastward past Santa Barbara and Ventura, curving southward on the right (`240°40'`, `34°17'`).
  - **Northern Channel Islands**: Four islands span the southern portion of the map from west to east: San Miguel Island (`239°38'`, `34°02'`), Santa Rosa Island (`239°55'`, `33°58'`), Santa Cruz Island (`240°15'`, `34°01'`), and Anacapa Island (`240°36'`, `34°00'`).
- **Bathymetric Contours**: Concentric black contour lines map the seafloor of the Santa Barbara Channel between `34°05'N` and `34°26'N`, showing a closed central basin depression centered around `239°58'E, 34°15'N`, steep northern and southern basin walls, and a broad, gently sloping shelf/fan complex extending southeastward toward the eastern entrance of the channel.

## Visible Text & Labels
- **Map Title (Top Center)**: `Santa Barbara Basin Circulation`
- **Scale Bar (Bottom Left, below San Miguel Island)**: Black-and-white alternating bar labeled `km` above and `0`, `5`, `10` below.
