# iSight Frame of Moran Tugboats and Sarah Mildred Long Lift Bridge Raised (`2005aug17-mov4-sor3-6fps.png`)

## Overview
A color webcam still frame (`2005aug17-mov4-sor3-6fps.png`, 320×240 pixels) captured with an Apple iSight camera on August 17, 2005 (used as the preview image for the time-lapse video `2005aug17-mov4-sor3-6fps.mov` in the blog post *"isight movie"*), overlooking the Piscataqua River harbor in Portsmouth, New Hampshire, with the vertical lift span of the bridge raised.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Moran Tugboats & Waterfront)**:
  - Three classic red-and-black tugboats with white trim are moored side-by-side at a wooden pier in the lower left foreground. Each tugboat features a dark red wheelhouse/deckhouse and a black funnel emblazoned with a bold white `M` (Moran Towing Corporation).
  - In the lower right corner, the dark shingled pitched roof of a waterfront building angles into the frame.
- **Middle Ground (Piscataqua River)**:
  - Calm blue-gray river water stretches across the middle of the scene. A small boat leaves a long, curved white wake heading toward the open lift span of the bridge.
  - Dark brick waterfront buildings line the right (Portsmouth) shore.
- **Background (Bridge & Kittery Shoreline)**:
  - Spanning the upper right is a steel truss vertical lift bridge (the Memorial Bridge / Sarah Mildred Long Bridge area) with its central truss span hoisted high near the tops of the two tall steel towers.
  - Along the left background, small white boats are moored near a tree-lined shore dotted with buildings under a bright, slightly hazy sky.
