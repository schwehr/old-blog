# Corroded Saturn Muffler with Split Seam (`Image038.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up color photograph (`Image038.jpg`, 320×240 pixels) from the August 9, 2005 blog post *"Trouble with Saturn of San Diego"*, showing the heavily rusted and split outer casing of an automotive exhaust muffler removed from the author's Saturn after failing a New Hampshire vehicle inspection.

## Scene Description
- **Muffler Body & Tailpipe**:
  - The oval-shaped metal muffler body fills the upper two-thirds of the frame, resting on a white cardboard sheet over a gray concrete shop floor.
  - A blackened, curved exhaust pipe stub protrudes from the left end cap of the muffler.
  - The sheet-metal shell is mottled with extensive orange-brown rust, dark corrosion pitting, and flaking oxidized metal, with a vertical welded or crimped band visible right of center.
- **Seam Failure**: Along the lower horizontal rolled seam facing the camera, the corroded metal has split open, leaving a long dark gap running from the left end cap past the central band.
