# Close-Up of Rusted Hole Along Saturn Muffler Seam (`Image040.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up color photograph (`Image040.jpg`, 320×240 pixels) from the August 9, 2005 blog post *"Trouble with Saturn of San Diego"*, providing an angled macro view of a large, jagged rust hole rotted through the side of the Saturn's exhaust muffler next to its longitudinal crimp seam.

## Scene Description
- **Corroded Seam & Perforation**:
  - The cylindrical muffler body runs diagonally from the lower left to the upper right of the frame against a gray garage floor and a white mat in the lower right corner.
  - A raised longitudinal rolled seam bisects the muffler diagonally.
  - Immediately to the left of the raised seam near the center of the image, the rusted sheet metal has completely disintegrated into a ragged, flaking cavity exposing the dark interior of the muffler, with a crack also cutting across the lip of the seam itself.
