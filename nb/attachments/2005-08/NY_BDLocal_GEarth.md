# Blogdigger Local Search Results for New York in Google Earth (`NY_BDLocal_GEarth.jpg`)

## Overview
A screenshot from **Google Earth** (circa August 2005) showing spatially indexed weblogs ("geoblogs") across the New York City metropolitan area, generated via **Blogdigger Local**'s native Google Earth (KML) export feature and discussed in the August 23, 2005 blog post *"rest2web and geoblogs"*.

## Map & Scene Description
- **Geographic View**: An overhead satellite view centered on Manhattan, the Hudson River, Upper New York Bay, Western Queens/Brooklyn, and the New Jersey shoreline (including Jersey City, Union City, and West New York).
- **Placemark Icons**: Dozens of round yellow pushpin icons populate the map—concentrated heavily along the spine of Manhattan, with additional pins in Jersey City/Hoboken and Queens—each representing a geolocated blog entry or feed from Blogdigger Local. Many labels appear doubled or slightly offset due to overlapping KML placemarks.
- **City & Water Labels**:
  - Red circular city markers with red text outlined in black on the New Jersey side: `West New York`, `Union City` (cropped as `Inion City`), and `Jersey City` (cropped on the left edge).
  - Light cyan water body labels: `allets Cove` (Halletts Cove, Queens), `South Cove`, and `Upper Bay` (partially cropped as `Jpper Bay`).
