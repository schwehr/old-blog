# GVisit World Map Visitor Log for Kurt's Blog — Day 1 (`gvisit-day1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **GVisit.com** web visitor tracking dashboard (`Visitor log for Kurt's blog`) taken on August 16, 2005, showing the geographic locations of the first day's visitors to `http://schwehr.org/blog` plotted on an embedded Google Maps world view (from the August 16, 2005 blog post *"Taking advantage of wireless at Saturn"*).

## Interface & Map Details
- **Header**: Bold black sans-serif title at top left: `Visitor log for Kurt's blog` on a light gray page background.
- **Embedded Google Map (Left)**:
  - **Base Map**: A world map in `Map` mode showing pale off-white continents against a light blue ocean, with a dashed red line marking the Equator and prime/antimeridian boundaries.
  - **Continent & Ocean Labels**: `North America`, `South America`, `Europe`, `Africa`, `Asia`, `Australia`, `Pacific Ocean`, `Atlantic Ocean`, `Indian Ocean`.
  - **Controls**:
    - Top-left directional pan pad (four arrow buttons surrounding a center return button) and vertical zoom slider bar with `+` and `-` buttons (slider thumb positioned near the bottom, zoomed out to global view).
    - Top-right mode toggle buttons: `Map` (selected, bold with black border), `Satellite`, and `Hybrid`.
    - Bottom-left attribution: `POWERED BY Google` logo; bottom-right link: `Terms of Use`.
  - **Visitor Markers**: Five red teardrop-shaped Google Maps pushpins with black center dots clustered in two regions of North America:
    - Three overlapping pins along the California coast (Sunnyvale, La Cañada Flintridge, and San Diego).
    - Two pins on the U.S. East Coast in the Mid-Atlantic region (Reston, Virginia and Mt. Laurel, New Jersey).
