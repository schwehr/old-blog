# ANSS Earthquake Map of Colorado and New Mexico — August 11, 2005 (`quake-co-nm.gif`)

## Overview
A regional seismic activity map generated by the **ANSS (Advanced National Seismic System)** / USGS on Thursday, August 11, 2005, showing a magnitude 5.0 earthquake right on the Colorado–New Mexico border (36.960°N, 104.855°W, near Raton, NM / Trinidad, CO) along with a smaller earlier earthquake in western Colorado, featured in the August 11, 2005 blog post *"New Mexico Quake"*.

## Map Details
- **Geographic Extent**: Covers longitude `-110°` to `-100°` W and latitude `32°` to `42°` N (tick marks labeled at `35°` and `40°` N), encompassing all of **Colorado** and **New Mexico**, along with adjacent portions of Utah, Wyoming, Nebraska, Kansas, Oklahoma, and the Texas Panhandle.
- **Base Styling**: Tan/peach land area with thin blue state boundary lines and black outer border ticks, with small blue triangular navigation arrows at the corners and midpoints of the map frame.
- **Labeled Cities (marked with gray diamonds)**:
  - Colorado: `Denver`, `Grand Junction`, `Colorado Springs`
  - New Mexico: `Albuquerque`, `Las Cruces`
  - Texas: `Amarillo`, `Lubbock`, `Midland`
- **Plotted Earthquakes**:
  1. **Magnitude 5.0 (`last day` — large blue square)**: Centered at approximately `37° N, -104.9° W` straddling the central Colorado–New Mexico border directly south of Colorado Springs and Denver (occurred August 10, 2005 at 22:08:17 UTC).
  2. **Magnitude ~2 (`last week` — small yellow square)**: Located in western Colorado at approximately `38.3° N, -108.9° W`, southwest of Grand Junction near the Utah border.
