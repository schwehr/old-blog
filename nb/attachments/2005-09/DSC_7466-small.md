## Overview
A reduced-resolution preview image (366×558 pixels) of [`DSC_7466.jpg`](file:///usr/local/google/home/schwehr/backup/cowfish/schwehr/blog/nb/attachments/2005-09/DSC_7466.jpg), embedded inline in the September 2, 2005 blog post *"NOLA Pictures"* and credited to **DirectNIC.com** (`nola-intel.org`), showing National Guard / military relief trucks arriving along Poydras Street in downtown New Orleans following Hurricane Katrina.

## Scene Description
- **Street Activity**: Looking down onto Poydras Street at Camp Street, where a convoy of olive-drab military trucks moves toward the foreground past downed branches, windblown debris, and two white civilian sedans.
- **Cityscape & Landmarks**:
  - Framed on the right by a historic brown brick building in the foreground and a tall dark-glass office tower with a construction crane in the upper right.
  - Framed on the left by a surface parking lot, low-rise brick and stone buildings, a tan high-rise building, the reflective glass **W Hotel** (with its rooftop `W` sign), and the circular tower and spire of the **World Trade Center New Orleans** in the background.
