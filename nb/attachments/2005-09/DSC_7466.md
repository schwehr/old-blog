# Military Convoy on Poydras Street in Post-Katrina New Orleans (`DSC_7466.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle vertical color photograph (1312×2000 pixels) credited to **DirectNIC.com** (`nola-intel.org`), looking east/southeast down **Poydras Street** (at the intersection with Camp Street) in downtown New Orleans, Louisiana, on September 2, 2005, in the immediate aftermath of **Hurricane Katrina** (from the blog post *"NOLA Pictures"*).

## Scene Description
- **Street & Military Convoy (Center to Lower Left)**:
  - A column of at least seven olive-drab U.S. military / National Guard tactical trucks (2.5-ton and 5-ton cargo trucks, some with canvas troop covers and open beds) drives single-file down the right-hand (westbound/inbound) lanes of tree-lined Poydras Street toward the camera.
  - Two white civilian sedans are also visible on the roadway: one parked or stopped near the bottom foreground intersection and another driving amidst the military convoy near the center of the street.
  - Storm debris litters the asphalt, sidewalks, and neutral ground (median), including downed tree limbs, scattered trash, yellow caution tape/cones in the left parking lot, and overturned street fixtures at the bottom-left corner.
  - A blue street sign on the traffic signal mast arm near the lower center reads `Camp` (marking the Camp Street intersection).
- **Surrounding Buildings**:
  - **Right Foreground**: An ornate historic reddish-brown brick commercial building with white terra-cotta arched window hoods and decorative urn reliefs along its upper cornice.
  - **Upper Right**: A mid-rise white concrete office building with repetitive vertical window grids, backed by the dark bronze/black glass curtain wall of a tall skyscraper with an orange construction crane projecting from its left side.
  - **Left Midground**: A surface parking lot bordered by a four-story pale beige commercial building (`527` visible over the entrance awning), small brick buildings, a multi-story pinkish-tan residential/hotel tower on the far left, and the reflective dark-glass **W New Orleans** hotel tower (with a large white `W` logo at its roofline) in the upper left-center.
  - **Upper Left-Center Background**: The distinctive stepped circular crown and red-and-white communications mast of the **World Trade Center New Orleans** (2 Canal Street) rises behind the W Hotel.
- **Sky & Aircraft**:
  - Two helicopters are visible as small dark silhouettes in the hazy blue sky with scattered cumulus clouds: one just to the right of the World Trade Center antenna spire, and another further to the right below the construction crane.
