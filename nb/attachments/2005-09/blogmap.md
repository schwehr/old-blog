# GVisit Visitor Log Map for Kurt's Blog (`blogmap.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a Google Maps-based visitor log map generated by `gvisit.com` ("Visitor log for Kurt's blog"), published in a September 21, 2005 blog post titled *"blog map"*. The map displays the geographic locations of recent visitors to Kurt Schwehr's blog across North America using red teardrop map markers.

## Interface & Controls
- **Header Title**: At the top left above the map frame, bold black sans-serif text reads `Visitor log for Kurt's blog` against a light gray background.
- **Navigation & Zoom Controls**: Along the upper left inside the map frame are the classic 2005 Google Maps directional pan buttons (up, down, left, right arrows surrounding a central return/center button) and a vertical zoom slider bar with `+` and `-` buttons at the top and bottom. The slider thumb is positioned near the bottom (zoomed out to the continental scale).
- **Map Type Selector**: In the upper right corner are three rectangular mode buttons: `Map` (selected, with a bold label and thicker dark border), `Satellite`, and `Hybrid`.
- **Attribution & Footer**:
  - Bottom left: The `POWERED BY Google` logo in multicolor serif lettering.
  - Bottom right: Copyright and terms text reading `Map data ©2005 NAVTEQ™ - Terms of Use` (overlapping slightly with the `North` label of the North Atlantic Ocean).
