# CPSGeo Map Widget Demo in Mozilla Firefox (`cpsgeo.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a Mozilla Firefox browser window on Linux/X11 displaying the "CPSGeo Map Widget Demo", featured in a September 23, 2005 blog post discussing the CPSGeo web GIS product built on Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC) standards (GML and WMS) for geolocating CPS (Collaborative Portal Server) documents.

## Browser Window & Controls
- **Window Title Bar**: Gray X11 window title bar at the top with a Firefox icon on the left, centered title `CPSGeo Map Widget Demo - Mozilla Firefox`, and minimize, maximize, and close (`X`) buttons on the right.
- **Menu & Navigation Bars**:
  - Standard menu bar: `File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Go`, `Bookmarks`, `Tools`, `Help`.
  - Navigation toolbar: Green Back arrow, grayed-out Forward arrow, blue Reload arrow, grayed-out Stop (`X`) button, Home icon, address bar showing `http://lenny:8201/cps/portal_maps/geo_lo`, green `Go` button, and a Google search box on the right.

## Web GIS Application Layout
- **Top Selection Bar**:
  - Left: Label `Select base map` next to a dropdown menu currently showing `--- Choose Map ---`.
  - Right: A rectangular `print` button outlined in dark blue/black.
- **Left Sidebar**:
  - **Overview Mini-Map**: A small world map enclosed in a thin red border on the top and left, showing countries shaded in varying tones of pale and medium green over white oceans.
  - **Map Layers Panel**: Below the mini-map, a bold heading `Map Layers` followed by three checkboxes:
    - `[✓] borders` (checked)
    - `[ ] cities` (unchecked)
    - `[ ] cpsdocs` (unchecked)
- **Main Map Canvas**:
  - Displays a global cylindrical projection map with continents filled in solid medium gray and country borders outlined in dark gray over a white background.
  - Four small document page icons (white sheets with horizontal text lines and a folded corner) are plotted as geolocated markers:
    - One in central/southern North America (United States).
    - Three clustered in Europe and North Africa (around Germany/central Europe, northern Italy/France, and Libya/Mediterranean coast).
- **Bottom Map Controls & Search Form**:
  - **Navigation Icons**: Four small dark-blue square buttons below the lower-left corner of the main map: Zoom In (`+`), Zoom Out (`-`), Pan (four-way arrow cross), and Reset/Refresh (circular arrows).
  - **Scale & Coordinates**: Center text field labeled `scale 1:` containing `298,177,160`, and two empty coordinate boxes on the right labeled `lon:` and `lat:`.
  - **Document Search**: Instructional text reading `Search for documents. Results are displayed on the map above.` followed by `Searchable Text:`, a text input box containing `doc*`, and a `Search` button.
