# Google Areometer Area Measurement Tool (`google-areometer.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the "Google Areometer" web application (`acme.com/areometer/`) featured in a September 15, 2005 blog post titled *"Google Areometer"*. The tool overlays an interactive polygon with vertex markers on a Google Map of New Hampshire, southern Maine, and northeastern Massachusetts to calculate the enclosed surface area in multiple metric and imperial units.

## Page Header & Interface Controls
- **Background & Header**: Set against a pale sage-green webpage background (`#b8d8b0`), the top left displays a bold black serif heading `Google Areometer` followed by the subtitle `Measure areas using Google Maps!`. Partially visible text and buttons on the right edge (`Dra...`, `Cli...`, `Th...`, and two rectangular buttons) belong to the page's sidebar instructions and controls.
- **Map Controls**:
  - **Upper Left**: Standard 2005 Google Maps pan directional pad and vertical zoom slider bar with `+` and `-` buttons.
  - **Upper Right**: `Map` (active/bold), `Satellite`, and `Hybrid` mode buttons.
  - **Bottom Left**: `POWERED BY Google` logo alongside a dual-unit scale bar labeled `20 mi` and `50 km`.
  - **Bottom Right**: `Map data ©2005 NAVTEQ™ - Terms of Use`.

## Map & Polygon Details
- **Geographic Region**: Covers central and southern New Hampshire (including `Berlin`, `Bethlehem`, `Lincoln`, `Tamworth`, `Wolfeboro`, `Alton`, `Concord`, `Manchester`/`Nashua`), southwestern Maine (`Bethel`, `Bridgton`, `Sebago`, `Standish`, `Auburn`, `Portland`/`Scarborough`, `Biddeford`, `Kennebunk`, `Wells`, `Kittery`), and northeastern Massachusetts (`Lowell`, `Salem`, `Gloucester`, `Lynn`, `Boston`). Major highways (`I-93`, `I-95`, `I-295`, `I-190`, `I-495`, US Routes `1`, `2`, `3`, `3A`, and State Routes `10`, `16`, `26`) are marked with shield icons.
- **Measured Polygon**:
  - Eight map pins define a self-intersecting polygon across New Hampshire and southern Maine: seven red teardrop markers (placed near Whitefield/Jefferson NH, Meredith NH, Alton NH, Deerfield NH, Exeter NH, Hampton/Kingston NH, and York/Kittery ME) and one blue teardrop marker near Sebago Lake / Raymond, Maine.
  - Semi-transparent red and blue straight line segments connect the vertices, crossing near Ossipee, New Hampshire.
- **Calculated Area Readout**: Centered at the bottom below the map frame is a line of black serif text reporting the calculated area in six units:
  `1.829e+9 m² / 1.829e+5 hectares / 1829 km² / 1.969e+10 ft² / 4.519e+5 acres / 706.1 mile²`
