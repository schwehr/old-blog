# Hurricane Katrina Information Map on Scipionus.com (`googlemap-katrina.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a crowdsourced Google Maps mashup from `scipionus.com` ("Katrina Information Map") showing street-level flooding and status reports across New Orleans, Louisiana in the immediate aftermath of Hurricane Katrina, featured in a September 2, 2005 blog post.

## Interface & Map Controls
- **Upper Left**: Standard 2005 Google Maps directional pan buttons (up, down, left, right, and center) and a vertical zoom slider bar with `+` and `-` buttons, zoomed into the city street level.
- **Upper Right**: Three map mode toggle buttons: `Map` (selected in bold), `Satellite`, and `Hybrid`.

## Geographic Features & Crowd-Sourced Status Markers
- **Street Network & Landmarks**:
  - Shows central and northern New Orleans neighborhoods including Lakeview, Gentilly, Mid-City, and the Fair Grounds area.
  - Labeled highways and major thoroughfares include `I-10`, `I-610`, `Pontchartrain Expy`, `US-90`, State Routes `611`, `3021`, and `39`, `Robert E Lee Blvd`, `Paris Ave`, `Elysian Fields Ave`, `Franklin Ave`, `Almonaster Blvd`, `Alvar St`, `Florida Ave`, `France St`, `N Robertson St`, `Desaix Blvd`, `N Carrollton Ave`, and `Orleans Ave`.
  - Labeled institutions and landmarks include `Lake Lawn Park Cemetery`, `Delgado Community College`, `Fair Grounds-Racetrack`, `Dillard University`, `Hebrew Rest Cemetery`, and `New Orleans Baptist Theological SEM`.
- **Active Callout Bubble**:
  - A white rounded info window with a drop shadow and gray `X` close icon points to a pin near Delgado Community College / City Park (`Conti & N. Anthony`).
  - The message inside reads:
    ```

    Anthony
    ```

- **Pushpin Markers**:
  - Roughly three dozen small teardrop markers are scattered across the neighborhoods:
    - **Red markers** (majority): Clustered along Lakeview (west of City Park along Pontchartrain Expy), around Delgado Community College and the Fair Grounds-Racetrack, along Elysian Fields Ave, Franklin Ave, and I-10/I-610 corridors.
    - **Green markers**: Two near `Dillard University` and two along `N Carrollton Ave` / Mid-City.
    - **Purple/magenta markers**: Located near the bottom of `Pontchartrain Expy`, near `N Carrollton Ave`, and near the top of `Elysian Fields Ave`.
