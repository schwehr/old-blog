# Huntec Line 43 Channel 1 vs. Channel 2 Comparison (`huntec-line43-ch1-ch2.png`)

## Overview
A side-by-side comparison of two grayscale Seismic Unix (`supsimage`) sub-bottom seismic reflection profiles from Huntec deep-tow boomer Line 43, contrasting the internal hydrophone `CH1` (left) with the external towed streamer `CH2` (right) between shot/trace numbers `5950` and `6100`. Featured in a September 14, 2005 blog post explaining how Channel 2 (the lower-frequency receiver on a streamer behind the towfish) exhibits high-frequency wavy motion artifacts from flapping in the current.

## Panel Comparison & Visual Details
- **Shared Horizontal Scale**: Both panels have a black horizontal axis line at the top with upward-pointing tick marks labeled `5950`, `6000`, `6050`, and `6100` in black sans-serif numerals.
- **Left Panel (`Huntec Line 43 CH1`)**:
  - Bold white sans-serif overlay text in the upper left reads:
    ```
    Huntec Line 43
    CH1
    ```
  - **Water Column**: Uniform, fine-grained gray acoustic background noise above the seafloor, with two faint vertical dashed-looking single-trace glitches near traces `6065` and `6080`.
  - **Seafloor & Sub-bottom Stratigraphy**: A smooth, crisp, well-defined seafloor reflection dips into a trough on the left (near trace `5970`) and rises into a rounded bathymetric high/mound peaking between traces `6060` and `6090`. Below the seafloor, finely laminated, coherent parallel sub-bottom sediment layers follow the contour of the mound cleanly without vertical jitter.
- **Right Panel (`Huntec Line 43 CH2`)**:
  - Bold white sans-serif overlay text in the upper left reads:
    ```
    Huntec Line 43
    CH2
    ```
  - **Water Column**: Shows faint, repetitive zigzag/chevron noise bands across the middle of the water column.
  - **Seafloor & Sub-bottom Stratigraphy**: Depicts the same bathymetric trough and rounded mound as Channel 1, but both the seafloor reflection and all underlying sub-bottom sediment horizons exhibit a pronounced small-scale sawtooth/wavy vertical oscillation ("flapping in the breeze" streamer motion artifact that mimics high-frequency ship heave).
