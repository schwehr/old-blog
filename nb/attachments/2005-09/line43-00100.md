# Gnuplot Full-Trace Waveform for Water Bottom Pick (`line43-00100.gif`)

## Overview
A 2D `gnuplot` waveform plot displaying the full sample range of the 100th seismic trace (`'line43.sgy-00100.dat'`) from Huntec Line 43 (`line43.sgy`). Featured in a September 25, 2005 blog post titled *"sioseis water bottom picks"*, where Kurt Schwehr used this plot to visually choose an amplitude threshold of `10000` for automatic water-bottom picking (`wbt3`) in `sioseis`.

## Plot & Data Details
- **Axes & Ranges**:
  - **Horizontal Axis (Sample Number)**: Spans from `0` to `2500` with inward-pointing tick marks labeled at `0`, `500`, `1000`, `1500`, `2000`, and `2500`. The actual trace data extends from sample `0` to `2048` (2048 samples per trace).
  - **Vertical Axis (Amplitude)**: Spans from `-30000` to `30000` with inward-pointing tick marks labeled at `-30000`, `-20000`, `-10000`, `0`, `10000`, `20000`, and `30000`.
- **Legend**:
  - Upper right corner displays `'line43.sgy-00100.dat'` alongside a red line segment with a central `+` point marker.
- **Waveform Characteristics**:
  - Plotted in red using lines and `+` point markers (`with linespoints`).
  - **Water Column (`0` to ~`800`)**: Low-amplitude background noise centered around `0`, generally staying within `±5000` with occasional isolated negative spikes reaching `-8000` to `-11000`.
  - **Primary Seafloor Arrival (~`800` to `1250`)**: A sharp, high-amplitude burst beginning just after sample `800`, with positive peaks reaching `+22000` (near sample `815`) and `+30000` (at the top border near sample `900`), and negative peaks reaching `-22500`. A secondary packet of elevated reflections occurs around samples `1080`–`1200` (peaking at `+15000`).
  - **Intermediate Sub-bottom (`1250` to ~`1850`)**: Amplitude decays back toward background levels (`±5000` to `-13000`).
  - **Late Burst / End of Trace (~`1850` to `2048`)**: Another high-amplitude packet appears near the end of the trace between samples `1850` and `2048`, with peaks reaching `+24500` and `-25000`.
