# PlaceOpedia Google Maps Mashup Screenshot (`placeopedia.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of `PlaceOpedia.com` ("Connecting Wikipedia articles with their locations"), a Google Maps mashup linking Wikipedia articles to geographic pushpins, featured in a September 19, 2005 blog post titled *"placeopedia.com"*.

## Header & Navigation Banner
- **Title Banner**: At the top on a white background with a thin green top border, large light-blue small-caps serif lettering spells `PLACEOPEDIA`, underscored by the light-green sans-serif tagline `Connecting Wikipedia articles with their locations`.
- **Action Bar**: Below the title is a salmon-red horizontal toolbar containing two white-text action buttons with white icons:
  - Left: A plus sign icon (`+`) followed by `Add your place`.
  - Right: A concentric target/bullseye icon followed by `Random place`.

## Map Interface & Callout Details
- **Map Controls**:
  - Upper left: Standard 2005 Google Maps directional pan buttons and vertical zoom slider bar with `+` and `-` buttons (zoomed out to show North America, northern South America, the North Atlantic Ocean, and western Europe/Africa).
  - Upper right: `Map` (selected in bold), `Satellite`, and `Hybrid` buttons.
  - Bottom left: `POWERED BY Google` logo.
  - Bottom right: Partially visible `Terms of U...` link.
- **Geographic View & Markers**:
  - Shows a world map view centered on the western `North Atlantic Ocean`, surrounded by `Canada`, `United States`, `Mexico`, `Cuba`, `Venezuela`, `Colombia`, `Peru`, `Brazil`, `Bolivia`, `United Kingdom`, `France`, `Spain`, `Algeria`, `Mauritania`, and `Mali`. A faint pink dashed line marks the equator across northern South America.
  - **Red Teardrop Pushpins**:
    - One marker in western Canada (`BC`/`AB` border area).
    - Eight markers clustered across the eastern and central United States (including Texas, Missouri/Illinois, Indiana/Kentucky, Michigan, Georgia/South Carolina, Virginia/Maryland, and Boston, Massachusetts).
    - One partially visible marker at the right edge in Europe (Netherlands/Germany border).
- **Active Info Window**:
  - A white rounded speech-bubble callout with a drop shadow and gray `X` close button points to the easternmost US pin at Boston, Massachusetts:
    ```
    Northeastern
    University,
    Boston
    Wikipedia
    article
    ```
    (where `Wikipedia article` is formatted as a light-green hyperlink that wraps onto the transparent area just below the text box).
