# First Sioseis Wiggle/Variable-Area Plot of Huntec Data (`sioseis-1st.png`)

## Overview
A black-and-white seismic reflection section generated by `sioseis` from Huntec Channel 1 SEG-Y file `line43-ch1-5575-6175.sgy`, featured in a September 21, 2005 blog post titled *"My first sioseis plot"*. Although plotted without time-varying gains (`procs diskin prout plot end`), the image clearly resolves the undulating water-bottom topography (`wbt`) and shallow sub-bottom reflections.

## Plot & Data Details
- **Axes & Annotations**:
  - **Top Horizontal Axis**: A solid black horizontal line with three upward-pointing tick marks (corresponding to matching tick marks along the bottom edge of the data block). Above each top tick mark is a vertically oriented timestamp label printed in pixelated dot-matrix font showing Julian day `255` and time:
    - Left tick: `255 2100`
    - Middle tick: `255 2055`
    - Right tick: `255 2050`
  - **Vertical Time Reference**: At the right end of the top axis line is the bold label `0.600` (seconds of two-way travel time).
- **Seismic Section Appearance**:
  - **Water Column (Upper Region)**: Above the seafloor, background water-column noise appears as a uniform, medium-density salt-and-pepper stipple of small black positive-amplitude peaks on a white background.
  - **Seafloor (Water Bottom) & Sub-bottom (Lower Region)**:
    - A prominent, dark, undulating seafloor horizon descends from left to right in a series of gentle mounds/terraces—starting high on the left (near `255 2100`), stepping down across two intermediate lobes near the center (`255 2055`), and sloping down toward the right edge (`255 2050`).
    - Beneath the seafloor horizon, high-amplitude reflections saturate the un-gained variable-area wiggle plot into a very dark, nearly solid black zone with faint white sub-parallel internal bedding streaks and ragged horizontal trace excursions jutting out along the right margin.
