# Cutout Sub-Bottom Seismic Profile Before Tinting (`tint1.png`)

## Overview
A black-and-white seismic sub-bottom profiler cross-section with the water column masked out (cut out to pure white) using the Magnetic Lasso tool in Adobe Photoshop CS, featured as the first of three progressive images (`tint1.png`, `tint2.png`, `tint3.png`) in a September 12, 2005 blog post titled *"Highlighting an area of an image in Photoshop"*.

## Visual & Geological Details
- **Frame & Borders**: Bounded on the right and bottom by a thin black frame line with two small outward-pointing tick marks along the bottom axis (one near the far left and one near the far right) and one tick mark on the lower right border.
- **Cutout Water Column (Upper Left)**: The entire region above the seafloor reflection has been cleanly erased to solid white, isolating the sub-bottom sediment wedge.
- **Seafloor & Sub-bottom Stratigraphy**:
  - **Seafloor Horizon**: A bold, continuous black reflection line rises gently from the middle left edge toward the center, then curves more steeply upward to reach the top border near the upper right corner.
  - **Upper Stratified Unit**: Beneath the seafloor is a wedge of well-laminated, sub-parallel sedimentary reflectors that thickens toward the left and pinches/ramps upward onto the structural high on the right. Subtle small-scale undulations and internal truncations are visible within the horizontal-to-subhorizontal beds on the left.
  - **Deeper Unconformity / Basement Reflector**: A prominent, continuous dark reflector dips from the upper right edge (below the seafloor) down to the lower left corner, separating the smoother overlying laminated sequence from a steeper, more acoustically chaotic/speckled lower unit in the bottom-right quadrant (which also contains a parallel deeper reflection band).
