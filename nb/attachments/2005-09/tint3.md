# Seismic Reflection Profile with Tinted Green Sub-Bottom Layer (`tint3.png`)

## Overview
A processed black-and-white seismic reflection profile in which the lower-right structural wedge has been highlighted with a semi-transparent pale green color overlay in Adobe Photoshop (`40% opacity`, `H: 126`, `S: 16`, `B: 85`). This image represents the final composite result of a tutorial on tinting specific geological units within a seismic section using duplicated cutout layers.

## Visual & Geological Features
- **Upper Untinted Strata (Left/Center)**: Above the diagonal boundary, well-stratified, gently dipping to undulating black-and-white seismic reflectors extend from the left margin up toward the top-right crest, topped by a sharp, continuous black seafloor reflector below a white water column.
- **Tinted Structural Unit (Lower Right)**: The lower-right wedge—isolated in `tint2.png`—is shaded with a soft sage-green tint over the underlying black-and-white acoustic reflectors. This region exhibits steeper dipping layers along its upper flank and an internal dipping discontinuity, with stippled, chaotic reflections throughout its deeper interior.
- **Framing & Axes**: The section is bounded along the bottom and right edges by a thin black frame line with two vertical tick marks visible along the bottom axis (one near the left corner and one near the right corner) and one horizontal tick mark near the lower-right border.
