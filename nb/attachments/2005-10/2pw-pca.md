# Polar Equal-Area Stereoplot of Principal Component Directions (`2pw-pca.png`)

## Overview
A circular polar stereoplot titled `2pw-pca` generated in Python (`pmag-py` / `matplotlib`) showing paleomagnetic or anisotropy principal component analysis (PCA) directions across the lower and upper hemispheres. Blue circles represent lower-hemisphere inclinations, while two red circles highlight points projecting into the upper hemisphere.

## Plot Layout & Coordinate Grid
- **Title**: `2pw-pca` centered at the top above the north (`N`) label.
- **Azimuthal Perimeter Labels**: Eight cardinal and intercardinal compass directions arranged clockwise around the solid black outer boundary circle (`0°` inclination): `N` (top), `NE`, `E` (right), `SE`, `S` (bottom), `SW`, `W` (left), and `NW`. Radial dotted grid lines connect opposite compass points through the center origin (`90°` inclination).
- **Inclination Rings**: Eight concentric dotted circles spaced every `10°` of inclination from the outer perimeter (`0°`) inward to the center (`90°`), labeled along an east-northeast radial axis (`~22°` azimuth) from outer to inner: `10`, `20`, `30`, `40`, `50`, `60`, `70`, `80`.

## Plotted Data Points
- **Blue Markers (Lower Hemisphere)**: Approximately 55 dark blue circular markers with black outlines, concentrated primarily in two groups:
  - A dense cluster of ~35 points in the western/northwestern quadrant at moderate-to-steep inclinations (`40°` to `85°`), with a few shallower points out to `~28°` WNW and `~22°` SSW, plus one shallow outlier in the ENE sector near inclination `12°` (overlapping the `10` label).
  - A secondary arcuate scatter of ~15 points in the eastern and southeastern quadrants spanning inclinations from `35°` to `70°`.
- **Red Markers (Upper Hemisphere)**: Two brownish-red circular markers with black outlines:
  - One near the top perimeter just west of `N` (`~356°` azimuth, `~6°` inclination).
  - One in the western sector just south of the `W` axis (`~263°` azimuth, `~30°` inclination).
