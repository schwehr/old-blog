# Composite SEM Backscattered Electron Image with EDX Spot Comparison (`63-composition-sml.png`)

## Overview
A composite figure created in Adobe Illustrator combining a 2000x Backscattered Electron (BSE) scanning electron microscope (SEM) micrograph of dried sediment (sample `63`) with an inset Energy Dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX) spectrum and red callout circles indicating the two 5-micron measurement spots.

## Micrograph & Annotations
- **SEM Background**: Grayscale BSE micrograph showing a heterogeneous matrix of fine-grained clay/silt platelets, angular mineral grains, and a smooth, elongated platy mineral grain (~40 µm long) oriented diagonally below the center. A slightly higher-resolution rectangular inset patch with its own tiny scale bar is nested over the lower portion of the smooth diagonal grain.
- **EDX Inset & Callout Lines**:
  - In the upper-left quadrant, a rectangular blue-background EDX spectrum plot is framed in black, showing prominent yellow elemental peaks (dominated by silicon and oxygen, with smaller aluminum, potassium, calcium, and iron peaks) and grey circular element tags.
  - Two bright red lines extend downward from the bottom center of the EDX plot box to two red circles (~5 µm spot size): the left circle targets the fine-grained aggregate background matrix, while the right circle targets the smooth diagonal mineral grain.
