# Energy Dispersive X-Ray Spectroscopy (EDX) Spot Comparison Spectrum (`69_5um_edx_comparison.jpg`)

## Overview
An Energy Dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX) comparison plot acquired on the SIO Analytical Facility's FEI Quanta 600 E-SEM for mud sample `69`. The chart overlays two 5-micron spot spectra—a solid yellow filled histogram from the background sediment matrix (`69_5um_1on_background`) and a red outline trace from a bright pyrite framboid (`69_5um_1on_bright area`)—highlighting elevated sulfur (`S`) and iron (`Fe`) in the bright grain.

## Spectrum Peaks & Element Labels
- **Background & Legend**: Gradient slate-blue background with a vertical black cursor line at `3.017 keV`. In the top-right corner, two legend labels are stacked:
  - `69_5um_1on_background` (yellow text)
  - `69_5um_1on_bright area` (red text)
