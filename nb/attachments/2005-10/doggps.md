# Spaniel Wearing a Garmin Forerunner GPS Unit on Collar (`doggps.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up outdoor photograph (originally from Phillip Torrone's Flickr stream) of a Blenheim Cavalier King Charles Spaniel lying on an asphalt path with a Garmin Forerunner 201 personal GPS receiver attached to its collar.

## Scene Description
- **Subject (Dog)**:
  - A chestnut-and-white (Blenheim) Cavalier King Charles Spaniel lying down facing the camera with its mouth open, pink tongue lolling out, and large dark brown eyes looking directly at the lens.
  - Long, wavy chestnut-brown ears frame the dog's white muzzle and chest, with white feathered fur covering its front legs and paws.
- **GPS Device**:
  - Mounted horizontally under the dog's chin on its collar is a black rectangular wrist-style GPS unit—a **Garmin Forerunner 201**.
  - The monochrome LCD screen displays large seven-segment timer digits reading `00:00:00` (`00` small hundredths) at the top and smaller status/distance digits (`0.00`) along the bottom right.
  - To the left of the screen is white branding text reading `GARMIN` above smaller lime-green model text (`FORERUNNER 201`).
  - A thin blue cord or leash attaches near the collar beneath the GPS unit and trails toward the bottom right corner, where the edge of a person's blue denim jeans is visible.
- **Background**:
  - Coarse gray asphalt pavement covers most of the ground around the dog, with green grass blades growing along the upper left edge of the frame.
