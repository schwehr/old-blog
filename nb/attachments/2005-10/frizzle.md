# "iFizzle" Parody Apple Cinema Display Desktop (`frizzle.png`)

## Overview
A humorous mockup image (from `yanathin.com/ifizzle.html`) showing an aluminum Apple Cinema Display running a parody Mac OS X desktop interface titled **iFizzle**, with the menu bar items spelling out the sentence `"iFizzle My Latest Plot to Destroy MySpace"`.

## Visual Details
- **Hardware Frame**: Front-on product rendering of a brushed-aluminum widescreen Apple Cinema Display on its curved aluminum stand against a white background with a soft reflection underneath. A small silver Apple logo is centered on the bottom bezel, and a tiny power indicator LED is on the bottom right bezel.
- **Desktop Background**: The classic Mac OS X Tiger "Aqua Blue" default wallpaper featuring smooth, sweeping translucent blue arcs and gradients.
- **Menu Bar (Top)**:
  - Left side: Blue Apple logo, bold application title `iFizzle`, followed by menu items `My`, `Latest`, `Plot`, `to`, `Destroy`, `MySpace`.
  - Right side: Digital clock/numbers reading `18:6:8` and a blue Spotlight magnifying glass icon.
- **Dock (Bottom Center)**:
  - Translucent glass-like bar holding eight oversized Mac OS X icons:
    1. **Finder** (blue two-tone smiling face, with active black triangle indicator below)
    2. **Dashboard** (black round gauge widget icon, with active black triangle indicator below)
    3. **TextEdit** (notepad with mechanical pencil)
    4. **iPhoto** (camera in front of a sunset palm tree photograph)
    5. **iChat AV** (blue video camera speech bubble with yellow running figure)
    6. **iTunes** (CD with green eighth-note musical symbol)
    7. **iCal** (red-topped calendar page showing `10` at the top and `6` for the date)
    8. **Trash** (wire-mesh wastebasket filled with crumpled white paper, separated by a vertical divider line)
