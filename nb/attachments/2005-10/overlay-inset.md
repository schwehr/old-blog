# Adobe Illustrator Seismic Profile & Gnuplot Point Overlay Inset (`overlay-inset.png`)

## Overview
A composite graphic created in Adobe Illustrator showing a zoomed-in inset of a chirp sub-bottom seismic reflection profile overlaid with red gnuplot water bottom pick markers (`+`), demonstrating how point overlays preserve visibility of the underlying acoustic stratigraphy.

## Scene & Plot Details
- **Main Background Plot (partially visible at bottom and left):**
  - Displays a broader view of the seismic profile with a vertical axis on the far left labeled at `0.5`, `0.55`, `0.6`, `0.65`, and `0.7` (two-way travel time in seconds).
  - In the lower right corner, the grayscale acoustic profile is heavily covered by dense vertical red lines (from a line-mode gnuplot overlay with vertical drop-offs).
- **Zoomed Inset Box (center):**
  - Framed by a rectangular border with black perspective callout lines extending toward the main profile at the bottom right.
  - **Inset X-Axis (Horizontal):** Labeled at the bottom with shotpoint values `5700`, `5800`, `5900`, `6000`, and `6100` (spanning `5625` to `6175`).
  - **Inset Y-Axis (Vertical):** Inverted scale on the left side of the inset, labeled from top to bottom at `0.625`, `0.63`, `0.635`, `0.64`, `0.645`, `0.65`, `0.655`, and `0.66`.
  - **Seismic Data & Picks:** Shows three distinct, rounded seafloor mounds/ridges in dark-and-light speckled grayscale acoustic backscatter. Red plus (`+`) markers from gnuplot trace the upper boundary (water bottom reflection) of the three ridges cleanly without obscuring the underlying sediment data, except for a single vertical red spike near shotpoint `6140`.
