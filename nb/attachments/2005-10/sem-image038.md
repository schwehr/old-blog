# Dried Mud Slabs Mounted on SEM Stubs (`sem-image038.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph showing three sliced slabs of dried marine sediment (mud) mounted with conductive double-sided tape onto numbered aluminum pin stubs, seated inside a circular black multi-sample holder plate in preparation for Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) at the Scripps Institution of Oceanography (SIO) Analytical Facility.

## Scene Description
- **Sample Holder Base:** A circular, black anodized metal carousel/stage plate resting on a white laboratory countertop. The black plate has multiple machined holes, recesses, and white wear/reference markings near its center and perimeter.
- **Mounted Sediment Samples:** Three circular silver aluminum SEM specimen stubs are positioned symmetrically around the black holder plate (at roughly the 9 o'clock, 1 o'clock, and 6 o'clock positions):
  - **Left Stub (labeled `69`):** Holds a flat, roughly pentagonal/trapezoidal slab of dried olive-gray/tan mud adhered to black double-stick carbon tape. The number `69` is handwritten in black marker on the exposed right rim of the metal stub.
  - **Upper-Right Stub (labeled `54`):** Holds a thicker, blocky slab of tan dried mud. A handwritten number (`54`) is visible on the lower-right rim of the silver stub.
  - **Bottom Stub (labeled `63`):** Holds a rectangular slab of dried tan mud. The number `63` (oriented upside-down relative to the viewer) is handwritten in black ink on the front bottom edge of the silver stub.
- **Background:** White lab bench surface with a faint green line and the base of a small metallic pin stub visible in the upper right corner.

## Visible Text & Labels
- Handwritten numbers on the silver aluminum stubs: `69` (left), `54` (top right), and `63` (bottom).
