# Matplotlib X-Ray Diffraction (XRD) Spectrum Plot for Sample 4-63b (`xdr.py.png`)

## Overview
A 2D line plot generated by the Python script `xrd.py` (using `matplotlib`) displaying X-ray diffraction (XRD) intensity (`CPS and ESD`, counts per second and estimated standard deviation) versus diffraction angle (`Degrees`, 2-theta from `0` to `50`) for marine sediment sample `4-63b` measured at the SIO Analytical Facility.

- **Title:** Centered at the top: `4-63b`.

- **Y-Axis (`CPS and ESD`):** Ranges from `0` to `1000`, with major ticks labeled at `0`, `200`, `400`, `600`, `800`, and `1000`.
- **Data Series:**
  - **Upper Curve (Blue line, Counts Per Second / CPS):** Starts at a high low-angle scattering peak (~`910 CPS` at `2°`), drops steeply to ~`180 CPS` around `3°`, and decays gradually to a baseline of ~`20–30 CPS` by `15°`. Between `20°` and `50°`, several sharp crystalline diffraction peaks rise above the background:
