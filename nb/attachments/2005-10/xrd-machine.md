# Scintag XDS 2000 X-Ray Diffractometer at SIO Analytical Facility (`xrd-machine.png`)

## Overview
A vertical photograph of the Scintag XDS 2000 X-ray Diffraction (XRD) instrument housed in the Analytical Facility at the Scripps Institution of Oceanography (SIO), showing its upper radiation-shielded goniometer enclosure and lower electronic control/power cabinets.

## Scene Description
- **Upper Radiation Enclosure:**
  - A large, beige/cream sheet-metal hood with an angled upper front panel and a sliding vertical door equipped with a central metal handle at the bottom edge.
  - **Viewing Window:** A square lead-glass window in the center of the door reveals the internal motorized goniometer assembly: a circular silver sample stage and theta-theta goniometer rings in the center, flanked by the X-ray tube assembly on the left (with glowing red warning LEDs) and the detector arm on the right.
  - **Warning & Brand Labels:**
    - On the bottom-left corner of the window frame is a yellow radiation warning sticker reading `CAUTION X-RAYS` alongside a magenta/black radiation trefoil symbol.
    - On the bottom-right of the beige door is a black rectangular nameplate with a stylized logo reading `XDS 2000` and `SCINTAG INC., USA`.
- **Lower Instrument Console & Racks:**
  - Divided into two dark-gray/black 19-inch equipment bays beneath the enclosure.
  - **Left Bay:** Partially obscured by a black jacket or bag hanging over the back of a laboratory chair; a digital vacuum/temperature controller readout displaying red LED digits (`-99.8` / `18.2`) is visible inside the rack.
  - **Right Bay:** Features an angled black front control panel at the top with rotary dials (`mA` and `kV`), green digital LCD/LED readouts, illuminated red/orange square indicator buttons, and a red emergency stop push-button on the right.
- **Surrounding Lab Environment:** Perforated acoustic wall tiles on the upper left, a desk with a white plastic pitcher and papers on the right, and a cylindrical cryogenic dewar tank on the floor at the bottom right.
