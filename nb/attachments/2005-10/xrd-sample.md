# Powdered Mud Sample Mounted Diagonally on XRD Stage (`xrd-sample.png`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of a glass microscope slide holding a scraped patch of dried marine sediment (mud) powder, mounted diagonally inside the machined metal sample holder (`Stage 1.2"`) of the Scintag XDS 2000 X-ray diffractometer.

## Scene Description
- **Sample Holder (`Stage 1.2"`):**
  - A precision-machined, brushed stainless-steel/aluminum clamp mechanism protruding forward from the central circular goniometer hub.
  - A white rectangular label adhered to the front vertical face of the lower metal block reads `Stage 1.2"` in black text.
  - The upper and lower jaws of the metal stage have vertical center alignment lines scribed into the metal.
- **Glass Slide & Sediment Sample:**
  - Because the glass slide is slightly too narrow to span the metal holder jaws straight across, it is positioned diagonally across the stage, resting on a thin metal support plate underneath.
  - Near the center of the slide (aligned with the scribed crosshairs of the stage jaws) is an approximately 1 cm square flat patch of pale olive-tan powdered mud scraped from a dried sediment cube with a razor blade.
  - To the right on the same glass slide is a circular frosted/ground dimple containing a smaller residual pile of the same tan sediment powder, and two small circular holes/indentations near the right edge of the slide.
- **Background:** Concentric metallic rings and socket-head cap screws of the diffractometer's goniometer assembly.

## Visible Text & Labels
- **Front Stage Label:** `Stage 1.2"`
