# Scintag XDS 2000 X-Ray Generator Control Panel Settings (`xrd-settings.png`)

## Overview
A grayscale close-up photograph of the front power control panel on the Scintag XDS 2000 X-ray diffractometer, showing the operating tube current (`35.0 mA`), high voltage (`-35.0 kV`), filament current (`3.24 A`), and tube power (`1.23 kW`) settings used during measurement.

## Interface & Controls
- **Upper Row (Tube Current & High Voltage):**
  - **Left Dial (`mA`):** A black rotary knob with a brushed silver numbered skirt (`0` to `100` scale) labeled `mA` on the left.
  - **Left Digital Readout (Current in mA):** A 7-segment LCD/LED display showing `35.0`. Above the readout is a white placard reading `1.5 Shutdown`, and below it is a placard reading `40 MAX`.
  - **Right Digital Readout (Voltage in kV):** A 7-segment display showing `- 35.0`. Above the readout is a white placard reading `-11 Shutdown`, and below it is a placard reading `40 MAX`.
  - **Right Dial (`kV`):** A matching black rotary knob with a silver graduated dial skirt labeled `kV` on the right.
- **Lower Row (Filament Current & Max Tube Power):**
  - **Bottom-Left Control (`FILAMENT CURRENT (A)`):** Small black potentiometer knob with a silver dial lock labeled `FILAMENT CURRENT (A)` on the far left.
  - **Bottom-Left Digital Readout:** Displays `3.24` (amperes).
  - **Bottom-Right Digital Readout:** Displays `1.23` (kilowatts).
  - **Bottom-Right Control (`MAX TUBE POWER (kW)`):** Small black potentiometer knob with a silver dial lock labeled `MAX TUBE POWER (kW)` on the far right.
