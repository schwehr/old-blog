# Scintag DMSNT X-Ray Diffraction Software on CRT Monitor (`xrd-software1.png`)

## Overview
A photograph of a beige NEC MultiSync A700 CRT computer monitor displaying the Scintag DMSNT software interface on Windows during an active X-ray diffraction (XRD) scan at the Scripps Institution of Oceanography (SIO) Analytical Facility.

## Monitor & Hardware Details
- **Monitor**: A beige NEC MultiSync A700 cathode-ray tube (CRT) display resting on a desk, with papers partially visible on the right.
- **Front Bezel Labels & Controls**:
  - Top-left below screen: Black `NEC` logo.
  - Top-right below screen: `MultiSync A700` branding.
  - Bottom center-left: Six small oval adjustment buttons arranged in a control cluster (including directional up/down/left/right buttons, `EXIT`, `CONTROL`, `PROCEED`, and `RESET`).
  - Bottom right: A larger oval power button with a green/amber LED indicator light to its left.

## Software Interface & Plot Details
- **Window Title Bar**: Dark blue Windows title bar reading `DMSNT - [4-63b - 4-63b]`.
- **Menu Bar & Toolbars**:
  - Standard menu bar with items: `File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Zoom`, `Hardware`, `Analysis`, `Tools`, `Window`.
  - Two rows of classic gray Windows toolbar icons below the menu bar (file operations, print, zoom icons, peak tools, red triangle warning/alarm icon, `E` icon, magnifying glass, step arrows).
- **Plot Header Metadata**:
  - `File: 4-63b, ID: 4-63b`
  - `Date: 10/28/05 12:41  Step : 0.020°  Cnt Time: 0.300 Sec.`
  - `Range: 2.00 - 50.00 (Deg) Cont. Scan Rate : 4.00 Deg/min`
- **Diffractogram Plot**:
  - **Vertical Axis (`CPS`)**: Counts per second ranging from `0` at the bottom up to `4800` at the top in increments of `240` (`240`, `480`, `720`, `960`, `1200`, `1440`, `1680`, `1920`, `2160`, `2400`, `2640`, `2880`, `3120`, `3360`, `3600`, `3840`, `4080`, `4320`, `4560`, `4800`).
  - **Horizontal Axis (`Deg`)**: $2\theta$ diffraction angle in degrees ranging from `2.0` on the left to `47.0`+ on the right, labeled every `3.0` degrees (`2.0`, `5.0`, `8.0`, `11.0`, `14.0`, `17.0`, `20.0`, `23.0`, `26.0`, `29.0`, `32.0`, `35.0`, `38.0`, `41.0`, `44.0`, `47.0`).
  - **Curve**: A black trace starting high near `2.0°` (~960 CPS), dropping sharply into a low, flat background between `5.0°` and `20.0°`, and showing distinct diffraction peaks around `20.9°`, `26.6°` (the tallest sharp peak, reaching ~600 CPS), and smaller peaks between `27°` and `47°`. A crosshair cursor (`+`) floats in the upper right of the plot area.
- **Scan Status Dialog (Top Right)**:
  - Floating gray dialog box titled `Scan Status - 4-63b.raw`.
  - Displays `File Name: 4-63b.raw`.
  - A horizontal progress bar filled in blue/purple to `97%`.
  - `Scan Time Remaining` box showing `0:00:20` alongside an `Enable Grafics` button.
- **Bottom Status Bar**:
  - Divided into recessed gray panels reading `F.S. = 2990 CPS`, `User: Administrator`, `Time: 01:30:04`, `Int: 23 CPS`, and current goniometer angles (`2Th = 48.8450000`, `Th = 24.4...`).
