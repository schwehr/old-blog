# Close-Up of X-Ray Diffraction Curve and Status Bar in DMSNT (`xrd-software2.png`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of the lower portion of the CRT monitor screen running Scintag DMSNT software, showing a detailed view of the X-ray diffraction (XRD) intensity trace (diffractogram) and the bottom status bar for sample `4-63b`.

## Diffractogram Plot Details
- **Horizontal Axis ($2\theta$ Angle in Degrees)**:
  - Tick marks every `1.0` degree with numeric labels every `3.0` degrees: `2.0`, `5.0`, `8.0`, `11.0`, `14.0`, `17.0`, `20.0`, `23.0`, `26.0`, `29.0`, `32.0`, `35.0`, `38.0`.
- **Vertical Axis (Counts Per Second)**:
  - Partially visible labels along the left edge (`0` at the baseline, with higher ticks ending in `0` above).
- **Diffraction Pattern**:
  - High initial scattering intensity at low angles (`2.0°` to `3.0°`) that rapidly decays into a flat baseline curve between `4.0°` and `20.0°`.
  - A thin continuous baseline reference curve is drawn underneath the raw signal trace.
  - Several crystalline diffraction peaks rise above the background:
    - A sharp moderate peak just below `21.0°` (~`20.9°`).
    - Small minor peaks around `22.0°`, `23.5°`, and `24.2°`.
    - A prominent, sharp primary peak just past `26.6°` (characteristic of quartz/illite).
    - A broader secondary peak cluster around `27.8°`–`28.0°`.
    - Smaller peaks near `31.0°`, `35.0°`, and `36.5°`.

## Status Bar Details
Along the bottom of the window, five inset status panels display real-time instrument metrics:
- **Left panel**: `... 2990 CPS` (Full Scale intensity setting).
- **Second panel**: `User: Administrator`.
- **Third panel**: `Time: 01 : 30 : 04`.
- **Fourth panel**: `Int: 23 CPS` (current detector intensity in counts per second).
- **Fifth panel**: `2Th = 48.8450000` (current $2\theta$ angle).
- **Sixth panel (partially cut off on right)**: `Th = 24.4...` (current $\theta$ angle).
