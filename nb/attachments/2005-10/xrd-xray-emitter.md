# Scintag XDS 2000 X-Ray Diffraction Emitter Assembly (`xrd-xray-emitter.png`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph taken through the tinted lead-glass safety window of the Scintag XDS 2000 X-ray diffractometer at the Scripps Institution of Oceanography (SIO) Analytical Facility, showing the X-ray tube housing and emitter assembly during operation.

## Scene & Hardware Description
- **X-Ray Tube Housing**: A machined, rectangular silver/beige metal column tilted at an angle on the left side of the goniometer arm, secured with recessed hex bolts along its metallic side plate.
- **Radiation Warning Label**: On the top surface of the emitter block is a yellow and black caution sticker featuring the standard 3-blade radiation hazard trefoil symbol.
- **Illuminated Red Warning Indicator**: Attached to the side of the emitter column is a rectangular translucent red plastic light bar containing multiple bright red/orange LEDs illuminated to indicate that X-rays are actively being generated (`X-RAY ON`).
- **Collimator / Divergence Slit Assembly**: Protruding to the right from the circular mounting hub on the emitter housing is a smaller boxy silver/gray metal collimator block with a small dark aperture slit on its right-facing end, aimed toward the sample stage.
- **Goniometer Mount & Tinted Glass Reflections**: Behind the emitter is the circular beige goniometer track and mechanical mounting hardware. The warm amber/yellow color cast and faint horizontal reflections across the image come from shooting through the protective leaded glass enclosure.
