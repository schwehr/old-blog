# Matplotlib Plot of X-Ray Diffraction CPS and ESD (`xrd.py.png`)

## Overview
A 2D line plot generated by the Python script `xrd.py` (from `pmag-kds-py`) using Matplotlib, displaying X-ray diffraction (XRD) counts per second (CPS) and estimated standard deviation (ESD) for sample `4-63b` measured on the SIO Scintag XDS 2000 diffractometer.

## Plot Details
- **Axes & Labels**:
  - **Vertical Axis (`CPS and ESD`)**: Ranges from `0` to over `800`, with labeled tick marks at `0`, `200`, `400`, `600`, and `800`.
  - **Horizontal Axis (`Degrees`)**: Diffraction angle ($2\theta$) ranging from `0` to ~`35` degrees, with labeled tick marks at `0`, `10`, `20`, and `30`.
- **Data Curves**:
  - **Blue Curve (CPS - Counts Per Second)**: Starts above `800` at `2°` due to low-angle scattering, drops steeply to ~`180` around `2.7°`, and decays gradually to a noisy baseline of `20`–`40` CPS between `10°` and `20°`. Between `20°` and `35°`, several sharp diffraction peaks appear:
    - A distinct peak at ~`21.4°` reaching ~`200` CPS.
    - Smaller peaks at ~`22.6°` (~`90` CPS), ~`24.2°` (~`110` CPS), and ~`24.8°` (~`110` CPS).
    - A tall, sharp dominant peak at ~`27.2°` reaching ~`510` CPS.
    - A broader multiplet peak at ~`28.5°` reaching ~`160` CPS.
    - Three smaller peaks at ~`30.0°` (~`95` CPS), ~`31.0°` (~`75` CPS), and ~`32.3°` (~`115` CPS).
  - **Green Curve (ESD - Estimated Standard Deviation)**: A low-amplitude green trace hugging the bottom of the plot between `0` and `35` units, mirroring the shape of the blue CPS curve at a much smaller scale (peaking at ~`35` at `2°` and ~`25` beneath the main `27.2°` peak).
