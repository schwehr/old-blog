# Annotated XStar CHIRP Seismic Profile with Core Locations in Time Domain (`xstar-cores-timedomain.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a high-resolution grayscale XStar CHIRP sub-bottom seismic reflection profile being annotated in Adobe Illustrator with a yellow vector overlay showing the seafloor horizon and sediment core locations (`1`, `2`, and `7`) plotted in two-way travel time (TWTT) against shotpoint number.
