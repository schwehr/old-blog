# Schematic Diagram of Driscoll XStar CHIRP Towfish Underside (`chirp-bottom-schematic-small.png`)

## Overview
A labeled schematic diagram illustrating the layout of the acoustic transducers and hydrophone receiver arrays mounted on the underside of the Driscoll XStar CHIRP sub-bottom profiler towfish.

## Diagram Layout & Components
- **Towfish Body**: Shown in plan view from below on a light gray background as a black, flat-nosed, tapered trapezoidal hull (narrower at the nose at the top, wider at the stern at the bottom, with a small yellow semicircular tail protrusion at the bottom center).
- **Labeled Acoustic Components (Top to Bottom)**:
  1. **`0.5-6 kHz Transducer`**: A large dark gray circular transducer near the forward nose of the towfish, connected by a horizontal red callout line to the label on the right.
  2. **`2-16 kHz Transducer`**: A smaller dark gray circular transducer positioned directly aft of the large transducer along the centerline, connected by a horizontal red callout line to its label.
  3. **`High frequence receiver for sediment type (Ch 2)`**: A rectangular metallic/gray shaded module located centrally just aft of the `2-16 kHz` transducer between the inner receiver bars (with a short vertical stem extending aft), connected by a horizontal red callout line to the two-line text label (noting the spelling `"High frequence receiver"`).
  4. **`4 summed receivers (Ch 1)`**: Four parallel, elongated vertical gray hydrophone array bars running fore-and-aft across the rear half of the towfish (two on the port side, two on the starboard side), connected by a horizontal red callout line from the inner-right bar to the label on the right.
