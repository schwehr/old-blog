# Underside of the Driscoll XStar CHIRP Towfish (`chirp-underside-sml.png`)

## Overview
A high-contrast, level-adjusted color photograph showing the black underside of a suspended Driscoll XStar CHIRP sub-bottom profiler towfish, revealing its circular acoustic transducers and four linear hydrophone receiver arrays.

## Scene & Hardware Details
- **Towfish Hull**: The towfish is suspended above the deck (with a person's pink-sleeved arm partially visible at the upper left and metal lifting shackles/hardware attached to the yellow upper fairing). The image exposure and levels have been strongly boosted to reveal the dark underside in shadow, resulting in a blown-out white background and saturated yellow/orange tones along the starboard edge.
- **Transducers (Forward / Upper Section)**:
  - A large circular black rubber-faced `0.5–6 kHz` transducer mounted near the rounded forward nose.
  - A smaller circular black `2–16 kHz` transducer nestled immediately aft of the large transducer within a recessed teardrop-shaped cavity.
- **Receiver Arrays (Aft / Lower Section)**:
  - Four parallel longitudinal slots housing dark cylindrical hydrophone receiver arrays (`4 summed receivers, Ch 1`) running from the mid-body to the flat stern edge.
  - Between the two central receiver slots, directly behind the smaller circular transducer, is a flat rectangular black plate (`Ch 2` high-frequency sediment-type receiver) with a central recessed channel containing mounting bolts extending toward the stern.
- **Line / Cable**: A bright yellow-and-red line or strap hangs vertically across the right-center portion of the underside.
