# Yahoo! Maps Pirate Map Mashup (`piratemap.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Justin Everett-Church's "Yahoo Pirate Maps" web mashup, which styles a Yahoo! Maps view of the southeastern and south-central United States as an antique parchment scroll complete with a brass spyglass zoom control and an ornate compass rose.

## Visual & Interface Elements
- **Parchment Scroll Frame**:
  - The rectangular map viewport is framed on the left and right by rolled vertical parchment scroll edges capped at top and bottom with turned wooden finials, casting a soft drop shadow against a white background.
  - The bottom edge of the parchment has a rough, torn-paper appearance.
- **Brass Spyglass Zoom Control (Top)**:
  - A photorealistic, weathered brass and leather handheld telescope (spyglass) rests diagonally across the top border of the scroll.
  - Above the spyglass is a hand-drawn gray label reading `zoom` with a curved arrow pointing toward the extending brass drawtubes on the left.
- **Map Content & Geography**:
  - Sepia-toned road and state map covering the south-central and southeastern United States and northeastern Mexico.
  - **Visible State Names**: `Kansas`, `Missouri`, `Oklahoma`, `Arkansas`, `Texas` (partially cut off on left as `exas`), `Louisiana`, `Tennessee`, `Alabama`, `Georgia`, `North Carolina`, `South Carolina`, `Florida`, and partially visible `Kentucky`, `West Virginia`, and `Virginia` under the spyglass.
  - **Visible Cities (marked with small circular/diamond icons)**: `Wichita`, `Springfield`, `Saint Louis` (partially obscured), `Oklahoma City`, `Little Rock`, `Abilene` (`lene`), `Dallas`, `Shreveport`, `Waco`, `Austin`, `Houston`, `Beaumont`, `San Antonio`, `Nuevo Laredo` (`o Laredo`), `Corpus Christi`, `Reynosa`, `Mobile`, `Huntsville`, `Chattanooga`, `Knoxville`, `Atlanta`, `Montgomery`, `Charlotte`, `Savannah`, `Tallahassee`, `Jacksonville`, `Orlando`, `Saint Petersburg`, and `Everglades`.
- **Compass Rose & Attribution (Bottom)**:
  - Lower left (over the Gulf of Mexico near Corpus Christi/Houston): An ornate, semi-transparent 8-point mariner's compass rose with cardinal directions `N`, `S`, `E`, `W`.
  - Bottom left text: `© 2005 Yahoo! Inc.`
  - Bottom right: Semi-transparent `YAHOO!` watermark logo above `Data © 2005 Navteq and TeleAtlas`.
