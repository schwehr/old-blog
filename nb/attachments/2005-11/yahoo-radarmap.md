# Justin's Yahoo! Radar Map Mashup (`yahoo-radarmap.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of "Justin's Yahoo Radar Map," a stylized web map mashup rendering eastern North America and the western North Atlantic inside a circular, monochromatic green radar-scope display with horizontal scanlines and a sweeping radar beam.

## Visual Style & Radar Elements
- **Display Geometry**: A circular radar viewport set against a solid black square background, framed by a bright green outer perimeter ring.
- **Range Rings**: Four concentric bright green circles (three interior range rings plus the outer boundary ring) centered near the New York / Vermont / Massachusetts border region (around Albany/Hartford).
- **Sweep Beam**: A bright green radial sweep line extending from the center toward the southeast (approximately 130° azimuth, passing through the Connecticut coast and northeast of Bermuda), with a trailing phosphor-glow gradient fading counterclockwise toward the east.
- **Scanline Texture**: Fine horizontal dark/light green scanlines overlay the entire circular scope, mimicking a vintage CRT radar screen.
