# Mars Exploration Rover 3D Rendering on Martian Terrain (`zareh-rover1.jpg`)

## Overview
A grayscale computer-generated 3D rendering of a NASA Mars Exploration Rover (MER, such as *Spirit* or *Opportunity*) situated on a rocky Martian surface, created using the Dan Maas rover model by Zareh Gorjian and Eric De Jong for visualizing rover operations on Mars.

## Scene & Rover Description
- **Mars Exploration Rover (Left of Center)**:
  - Shown in a front-three-quarter view facing toward the viewer and slightly right.
  - **Pancam Mast Assembly (PMA)**: Rises vertically from the front of the rover deck, topped by the rectangular Pancam / Navcam camera bar angled toward the left.
  - **Solar Panels**: Deployed flat like wings to the left, right, and rear of the central warm electronics box (WEB), displaying individual photovoltaic cell grid patterns.
  - **Antennas & Deck Instruments**: The circular Dish High-Gain Antenna (HGA) is visible on the left side of the deck, and the vertical cylindrical Low-Gain Antenna (LGA) rises behind the mast.
  - **Mobility System (Rocker-Bogie & Wheels)**: Six cleated aluminum wheels are visible resting on the sandy surface, with the front-left and middle-left wheels prominent in the foreground and the right-side wheels partially in shadow.
  - **Shadow**: A sharp, dark shadow of the rover's solar arrays, mast, and body is cast across the Martian regolith to the right and slightly forward, indicating low-angle illumination from the upper left.
- **Martian Terrain & Tracks**:
  - **Foreground**: Two parallel sets of cleated wheel tracks curve from the bottom foreground directly up to the rover's left and right wheels, showing disturbed soil and imprints in the fine regolith.
  - **Midground**: Pebbly plains scattered with small angular rocks to the left and two prominent medium-sized boulders just to the right of the rover's shadow. Immediately behind the rover rises a low rocky outcrop or ridge with exposed layered bedrock and dark cobbles.
  - **Background**: Gently sloping hills and a broad crater rim or valley floor stretching toward a hazy gray horizon at the top of the frame.
