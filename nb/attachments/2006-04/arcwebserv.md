# ESRI ArcWeb Explorer Beta Screenshot (`arcwebserv.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **ArcWeb Explorer BETA** web mapping interface by ESRI, showing a hybrid satellite/street map view of the Piscataqua River waterfront in Portsmouth, New Hampshire (near Market Street and Harbour Place), with floating "Navigation" and "Map Types" widget windows open.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Header Bar**: A silver-gray horizontal bar featuring the title **"ArcWeb Explorer BETA"** on the left and three links on the right: **`Hide Widgets`**, **`Share`**, and **`Help`**.
- **Secondary Toolbar**: Directly below the header is a light gray bar with four tab buttons:
  - **`Navigation`** (depressed/active button state)
  - `Find` (inactive gray text)
  - `Directions` (inactive gray text)
  - **`Map Types`** (depressed/active button state)
- **Floating "Navigation" Widget** (top-left of map canvas): A rounded light-gray panel with a dark blue title **"Navigation"**, a close button (`x`) in the top-right corner, and a horizontal zoom slider bar with `-` on the left, `+` on the right, and a light-blue slider thumb positioned near the right (`+`) end (indicating a high zoom level).
- **Floating "Map Types" Widget** (top-center of map canvas): A rounded light-gray panel with a dark blue title **"Map Types"**, a close button (`x`) in the top-right corner, and three square thumbnail icons with labels underneath:
  - **`Street`**: Shows a vector street map icon.
  - **`Hybrid`** (selected/highlighted with a dark border): Shows a street network overlaid on satellite imagery.
  - **`Satellite`**: Shows raw aerial/satellite imagery.

## Map View & Visible Labels
- **Geographic Scene**: High-resolution aerial/satellite imagery of a coastal river/harbor channel (Piscataqua River / Portsmouth Harbor waterfront). Ripples and wave patterns are visible across the dark green-gray water in the center.
- **Shoreline & Vector Overlay**:
  - Along the bottom-left and bottom, urban waterfront buildings, parking lots, and streets are visible, bordered by a thin light-yellow shoreline polygon line.
  - **`Market St`** is labeled in black text along the road on the far left.
  - **`Harbour Pl`** is labeled in black text along an orange-red highlighted road segment in the bottom-right corner.
  - In the upper-right corner, a peninsula/island (Badger's Island in Kittery, Maine) with piers, docks, boats, and buildings is outlined by a thin light-yellow vector boundary line.
  - In the upper-left corner, a large white pile (salt pile along the Portsmouth waterfront) is partially visible.
- **Attribution & Logos**:
  - Bottom-left text in white: **`(c) 2006 ESRI, TANA Inc., AND, GlobeXplorer and Partners`**.
  - Bottom-right logo: A blue-and-green wireframe globe icon above a dark gray banner reading **`ESRI`**.
