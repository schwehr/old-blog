# AIS Ship Tracks Between Virginia and New Jersey in Google Earth (`ais-va-nj.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from **Google Earth (Beta 4)** showing Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel tracks plotted as white lines across the Mid-Atlantic coast of the United States—spanning from Hampton Roads / Chesapeake Bay (Virginia) to the Delaware Bay / Philadelphia and the New Jersey coast—overlaid with a `timeSpan` time slider window covering roughly 2.7 hours on October 26, 2006.

## Map & Geographic Features
- **Region Shown**: Oblique/overhead satellite and bathymetric view of the US Mid-Atlantic seaboard, including Virginia, Maryland, Delaware, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey, with the dark blue Atlantic Ocean and continental shelf break visible in the lower half.
- **Place Labels & Icons**:
  - **`Hampton Roads`**: Marked with a yellow circular pushpin icon at the mouth of the Chesapeake Bay (left).
  - **`Stratford Hall Plantation`**: Marked with a yellow circular historic/monument icon along the Potomac River in Virginia.
  - **`Washington D.C.`**: Marked with a black-and-white star-in-circle capital icon near the top center.
  - **`Baltimore`**: Marked with a red-and-white circular city dot northeast of Washington D.C.
  - **`Philadelphia`**: Marked with a red-and-white circular city dot along the Delaware River to the right.
  - **`New York`**: Marked with a red-and-white circular city dot in the upper right.
- **AIS Vessel Tracks**:
  - Numerous straight and piecewise-linear **white track lines** trace ship movements over the selected time window:
    - Several tracks converge toward and diverge from the mouth of Chesapeake Bay (`Hampton Roads`), including a long coastal track running northeast into the bay.
    - Dense shipping tracks run up the **Delaware Bay and Delaware River** channel all the way to **`Philadelphia`**, with multiple vessels fanning out into the Atlantic Ocean off Cape May / southern New Jersey.
    - Additional offshore vessel tracks crisscross the continental shelf and slope waters in the center and bottom of the view, including a long north–south track extending across the shelf break into deep water.

## Google Earth Interface Overlays
- **Time Slider Widget** (top-center):
  - Displays a clock icon on the left, a timeline bar with tick marks and a translucent draggable range window, a play button triangle on the right, and range-control/settings buttons.
  - Start timestamp label (left): **`26 OCT 2006` / `1:48am`**.
  - End timestamp label (right): **`26 OCT 2006` / `4:29am`**.
- **Navigation Controls** (top-right):
  - Semi-transparent circular compass ring (`N` rotated slightly clockwise), four directional panning arrows around a central hand/joystick ring, a horizontal tilt slider at the top, and a vertical zoom slider (`+` / `-`) along the right edge.
