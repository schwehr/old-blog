# Google Earth Resume – La Jolla Bathymetry & Pop-up Balloon (`ge-resume-lj.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from **Google Earth (Beta 4)** showcasing Kurt Schwehr's interactive "Google Earth Resume" (`schwehr-resume.kml`) zoomed into the coast of **La Jolla and San Diego, California**, displaying a multibeam bathymetry image overlay of the La Jolla Submarine Canyon alongside an open placemark info balloon titled **`La Jolla, CA - SIO - 2004`**.

## Map & Bathymetry Overlay (Left Side)
- **Satellite Base Map**: Shows the Southern California coastline from **`Encinitas`** (marked with a red dot and white label at the top-left) southward past **La Jolla** (`La Jol...` partially visible behind the balloon stem) and Mission Bay (`Ski...` visible near the bottom).
- **Bathymetry Image Overlay**: Off the coast of La Jolla, a false-color shaded-relief multibeam bathymetric grid (from Pat Iampietro / CSU Monterey Bay Seafloor Mapping Lab) is overlaid on the ocean surface. Depths are colored in shades of light cyan/turquoise on the shallow shelf, transitioning through royal blue to deep purple/magenta along the steep walls and tributary channels of the **La Jolla and Scripps Submarine Canyons**.
- **Placemark Icon**: A yellow circular pushpin icon sits at the head of La Jolla Canyon, anchoring the white speech-balloon popup to the right.
- **Top Controls**: At the top of the viewport, Google Earth's translucent time slider bar is visible (with clock icon, timeline ticks, and play button), along with the left edge of the navigation compass ring in the upper-right corner.
