# Google Earth Core 4-2004 Popup Overlay (`ge-sb-6.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from Google Earth showing a bathymetric image overlay of the Santa Barbara Basin off the California coast, with yellow pushpin placemarks for sediment core locations and an open information balloon displaying a multi-panel scientific plot for **Core 4-2004** from Kurt Schwehr's 2006 *Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems* ($G^3$) paper on detecting compaction disequilibrium using anisotropy of magnetic susceptibility (AMS).

## Map & Interface Details
- **Background Map**: Satellite imagery of the Santa Barbara coastline (top) transitioning into a color-shaded multibeam bathymetry overlay (bottom) colored in yellow-orange near the shelf and green-to-blue in deeper water.
- **Placemarks**: Three yellow circular pushpin icons are visible near the bottom right of the map:
  - `Core 4-2004` (active, with the popup balloon pointing to it)
  - `Core 2-2004`
  - `Core 1-2004`
- **Google Earth Controls (Top Right)**:
  - A time slider widget showing a selected time window from `7 DEC 2000` to `30 AUG 2004`.
  - Partially visible compass/navigation ring (`N`) and zoom/tilt sliders along the upper right edge.

## Popup Balloon & Scientific Plot Details
- **Header**: `Core 4-2004` in bold black text at the top left, with a blue close button (`x`) at the top right.
- **Left Margin**: Vertical axis labeled `Depth (cm)` ranging from `0` to `120` cm (with ticks every 20 cm), alongside a grayscale X-ray / core density radiograph strip and a `Features` column marking specific intervals (`F`, `B`, `L`, wavy lines at top).
- **Sub-panels (`a` through `e`)**:
  - **Panel `a` (`AMS` $\tau$)**: Horizontal axis from `0.31` to `0.35`. Shows three curves with symbols: blue ($\tau_3$), green ($\tau_2$), and red ($\tau_1$). Gray shaded horizontal bands are labeled `a`, `1`, `2`, `3`, and `4`, with a horizontal dashed line at ~40 cm depth.
  - **Panel `b` (`MDF (mT)`)**: Horizontal axis from `0` to `50`. Plots a red curve of median destructive field values, with a vertical bracket on the right labeled `Greigite signature` spanning roughly 65–115 cm.
  - **Panel `c` ($\chi_g (\mu\text{SI m}^3/\text{kg})$)**: Horizontal axis `0`, `2`, `4`. Plots mass-normalized magnetic susceptibility in red crosses.
  - **Panel `d` (`IRM 1T` $(\text{mA m}^2/\text{kg})$)**: Horizontal axis `0`, `2`, `4`. Plots isothermal remanent magnetization at 1 Tesla in red crosses.
  - **Panel `e` (`Weight percent water`)**: Horizontal axis from `35` to `60` (ticks at `35`, `40`, `45`, `50`, `55`, `60`). Shows a red data curve over a smooth black compaction trend curve.
- **Right Margin**: Vertical brackets dividing the core at the ~40 cm dashed line into `Grp 1` (0–40 cm) and `Grp 2` (40–120 cm).
- **Footer**: Standard Google Earth balloon links reading `Directions: To here - From here`.
