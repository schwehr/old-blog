# Google Earth Beta 4 Time Slider Widget (`google-timebar.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot of the on-screen time slider control from Google Earth Beta 4 (KML 2.1), used to animate time-stamped AIS vessel positions on October 25, 2006.

## Interface & Controls
- **Background**: Semi-transparent dark blue/slate overlay bar.
- **Left Icon**: A small white circular clock icon indicating time controls.
- **Timeline Ruler**: A horizontal graduated timeline bar with small and medium white tick marks, flanked by left and right white step arrows (`<` and `>`).
- **Range Selection Thumb**: A translucent white/gray rectangular slider bracket positioned near the center of the ruler, with draggable left and right edge markers and a textured grip in the bottom center.
- **Play Button**: A larger right-pointing play triangle button on the far right of the widget.

## Visible Text & Timestamps
- **Start Time (Left)**:
  - `25 OCT 2006`
  - `8:14:43pm`
- **End Time (Right)**:
  - `25 OCT 2006`
  - `8:25:08pm`
- Dotted white leader lines connect each timestamp to the corresponding left and right edges of the active time-range slider bracket.
