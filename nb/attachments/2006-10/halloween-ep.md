# Rhyme Torrents Halloween EP Cover Art (`halloween-ep.jpg`)

## Overview
Cover illustration for the *Rhyme Torrents Halloween EP* ("The Nerdcore Hip-Hop Compilation CD Project"), featuring a humorous cartoon mashup of Halloween, hip-hop, video game, and sci-fi pop-culture tropes.

## Scene & Character Description
- **Central Figure**: A muscular, shirtless character with a carved jack-o'-lantern pumpkin head wearing a backward red-and-white baseball cap, blue superhero gloves with forearm fins, green camouflage pants, and a heavy gold chain with a Pac-Man pendant and an NES controller strapped to his chest. He holds a glowing pink/red lightsaber raised in his right hand and a microphone in his left hand.
- **Left Figure**: A cartoon woman styled as Princess Leia in a gold bikini, wearing pink cat-eye glasses, white striped tube socks, and blue sneakers, holding onto the pumpkin character's right leg.
- **Right Foreground**: The pumpkin character's left sneaker rests triumphantly on the severed, X-eyed head of Bowser (King Koopa) from *Super Mario Bros.*, with blood pooling underneath and his tongue sticking out.
- **Retro Game Elements**: Pixelated 8-bit ghosts from *Pac-Man* (a blue vulnerable ghost on the upper left and an orange ghost on the right) and pixelated orange/red fireballs float around the stone dungeon archway in the background.

## Visible Text & Typography
- **Top Banner**: `RHYME TORRENTS` in jagged, dripping blood-red horror lettering outlined in black.
- **Bottom Banner**: `HALLOWEEN EP` in dripping slime-green horror lettering across a fiery orange floor.
