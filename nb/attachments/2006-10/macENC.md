# MacENC Electronic Navigational Chart Display (`macENC.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from **MacENC**, an Electronic Navigational Chart (ENC) software application for Mac OS X, displaying a vector nautical chart of the northern tip of **Santa Barbara Island** off southern California.

## Chart & Geographic Details
- **Landmass**: The northern peninsula of Santa Barbara Island is rendered in warm tan/buff, with a small green offshore islet on the left labeled `Shag Rock` and the northeastern headland labeled `Arch Point`.
- **Bathymetry & Depth Contours**:
  - Shallow nearshore waters inside the depth contour are shaded light blue.
  - Deeper offshore waters are shaded pale gray-green, divided by gray bathymetric contour lines.
  - Soundings (depths) are shown in gray numerals across the water: `27`, `28`, `31`, `33`, `42`, `45`, `48`, `54`, `60`, `66`, `72`, `78`, `84`, `108`.
- **Navigational Symbols & Hazards**:
  - **Lighthouse / Aid to Navigation**: A black circle inside a white ring near the northern tip of the island, with white sector lines and a magenta bearing/sector line extending NNE, accompanied by a white rectangular callout box reading `Santa Barbara Island Light`.
  - **Kelp Beds**: Stylized green branched kelp symbols scattered through the nearshore and intermediate waters.
  - **Rocks & Bottom Nature**: Black asterisk-like rock/awash symbols (`*`) along the shoreline; text abbreviations `rky` (rocky) and `sndy` (sandy) indicating seabed characteristics.
