# ArcticNet 2005 CCGS Amundsen Cruise Track Map (`Amundsen2005track.png`)

## Overview
A map from the 2005–2006 ArcticNet report showing the 2005 cruise track of the Canadian research icebreaker CCGS *Amundsen* across the Canadian Arctic Archipelago, Hudson Bay, Baffin Bay, and the Beaufort Sea, along with observatories, research locations, and Inuit communities.

## Map & Geographic Features
- **Regions & Water Bodies**:
  - Highlighted in medium blue: the Canadian Arctic Archipelago and northern Inuit settlement regions (Nunavut, Inuvialuit, Nunavik, Nunatsiavut).
  - Surrounding areas in light gray: mainland Canada, `Alaska` (far left), and `Greenland` (top center).
  - Labeled seas and bays: `Arctic Ocean`, `Beaufort Sea`, `Baffin Bay`, `Labrador Sea`, and `Hudson Bay`.
  - A dashed blue arc marks the `Arctic Circle`.
- **Labeled Cities, Islands & Communities**:
  - `Inuvik` (western Arctic near the Mackenzie Delta)
  - `Ellesmere I.` (far north)
  - `Baffin I.` and `Iqaluit`
  - `Churchill` (western shore of Hudson Bay)
  - `Kuujjuaq` (Ungava Bay)
  - `Nain` (Labrador coast)
  - `Québec City` (bottom right, marked with a star at the start/end of the St. Lawrence track)

## Legend & Symbology
A white boxed legend in the lower left defines five symbols:
- **Red circle**: `Marine Observatory` (located in the Beaufort Sea/Amundsen Gulf, northern Baffin Bay/Nares Strait, and Hudson Bay)
- **Green circle**: `Terrestrial Observatory` (near Churchill, southern Hudson Bay/James Bay, Iqaluit, Nunavik, and northern Baffin Island)
- **Yellow circle**: `Research Location` (numerous sites along the cruise track and coastline)
- **Small black dot**: `Inuit Community` (often co-located inside yellow circles along the coasts)
- **Solid red line**: `2005 Amundsen Cruise Track` tracing the ship's route from Québec City through the Labrador Sea, into Hudson Bay, north through Baffin Bay and Lancaster Sound, through the Northwest Passage into the Beaufort Sea, and returning.
