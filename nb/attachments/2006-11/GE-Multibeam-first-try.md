# First-Try Multibeam Bathymetry Overlay in Google Earth (`GE-Multibeam-first-try.png`)

## Overview
A tilted 3D perspective screenshot from Google Earth showing Kurt Schwehr's first automated conversion of raw EM120 multibeam sonar swath bathymetry data (processed with GMT and MB-System during cruise `CA0606RR` on the R/V *Roger Revelle*) overlaid onto the Southern California offshore region.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Background Scene**: Oblique Google Earth satellite view looking northeast across the dark blue Pacific Ocean toward the Southern California coastline (San Diego / La Jolla / Southern California Bight area, with yellow highway lines visible onshore).
- **Multibeam Swath Overlay**:
  - A long, narrow, rainbow-colored bathymetric swath extends from the lower right near the coast toward the upper left offshore.
  - Color shading reflects water depth: warm pink/orange/yellow tones in shallower nearshore loop tracks on the right, transitioning through bright green and cyan along the central transit/survey lines, to light blue and violet/magenta at the deeper northwestern and southeastern ends.
  - Because the data is raw (uncleaned), ragged outer-beam noise and overlapping track segments are visible along the edges of the swath.
