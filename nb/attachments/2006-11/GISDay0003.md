# UNH GIS Day 2006 — Whale Tagging Demonstration (`GISDay0003.png`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on the morning of November 1, 2006, during **GIS Day 2006** at Morse Hall, University of New Hampshire (UNH), showing CCOM/JHC presenter Briana explaining whale tagging and vocalizations to visiting school students.

## Scene Description
- **People (Right)**:
  - **Briana** stands leaning slightly forward behind a folding table, wearing glasses, long braided hair, a black long-sleeved shirt with a NOAA logo and a white name badge, gesturing or speaking toward the large display screen on the left.
  - Several students stand to the right and behind her, including a student in a red jersey and dark hoodie in the right foreground looking attentively toward the left display.
- **Displays & Booth Setup (Left to Center)**:
  - **Left Foreground**: A large tilted interactive/flat-panel monitor mounted in front of a black display board (with four scenic coastal/mountain photographs along its top edge). The screen displays a 3D GeoZui4D visualization of underwater whale dive tracks (green trajectories with dive depth axes and surface grids against a black background).
  - **Center Background**: Two silver Apple Cinema Display monitors sitting side-by-side on a white-draped table, showing bathymetric and geographic visualizations (including a circular polar/regional map on the left monitor and rainbow-colored multibeam bathymetry in Google Earth on the right monitor).
  - **Right Background**: Through an open hallway door past a maroon door with a directional sign, another display station is visible with a touchscreen kiosk and a monitor showing coastal bathymetry.
