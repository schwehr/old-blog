# UNH GIS Day 2006 — CCOM/JHC Ocean Mapping Exhibition Booth (`GISDay0007.png`)

## Overview
A photograph of the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC) exhibition booth at **GIS Day 2006** in Morse Hall at the University of New Hampshire (UNH) on Wednesday, November 1, 2006, showing Stephen and Briana talking in front of the display screens before the crowds arrived.

## Scene Description
- **People**: In the center-left, Stephen (seen from behind, wearing a light blue button-down shirt, blue jeans, and white sneakers, with his hair in a ponytail and hands on his hips) talks with Briana (standing in profile facing left, wearing glasses, long braids, and a black top with a NOAA logo).
- **Booth Displays**:
  - **Left**: A black exhibition backdrop with a banner reading `...astal & Ocean Mapping / ...graphic Center / ... Ocean Mapping and Hydrographic Sciences` above four scenic photographs and a large monitor displaying green 3D dive-track paths on a black background.
  - **Right**: Two wide Apple Cinema Display monitors on a white-draped table displaying ocean mapping and Google Earth bathymetric visualizations, with two scientific posters mounted on the wall behind them.

## Foreground Banner Text & Graphics
Hanging from the wooden balcony railing in the foreground is a large white and blue vinyl banner featuring a 6-panel collage of ocean mapping imagery (bathymetric charts, research vessels, buoys, 3D seafloor models, and exhibition visitors) on the left and the following text on the right:
- `Come see the NOAA Ocean Mapping Exhibition at` (with the circular blue-and-white NOAA emblem)
