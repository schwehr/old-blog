# UNH GIS Day 2006 — Opening Ceremony in the Morse Hall Atrium (`GISDay0009.png`)

## Overview
A high-angle vertical photograph looking down through the multi-story atrium of Morse Hall at the University of New Hampshire (UNH) during the opening ceremony of **GIS Day 2006** on November 1, 2006.

## Scene Description
- **Architecture**: Tall white cylindrical support columns rise through three visible levels of the atrium, bordered by glass-paneled wood railings along the upper walkways and glass-walled lab/office windows on the right.
- **Crowds & Visitors**:
  - Along the left foreground balcony, a line of students and visitors stands looking over the railing into the atrium below.
  - Along the second-floor and third-floor walkways on the right and center-back, dozens of attendees lean against the railings watching the ceremony.
- **Exhibits & Props**:
  - Suspended or mounted near the second-floor bridge in the upper-left center is a large yellow-and-blue oceanographic buoy accompanied by blue helium balloons and a colorful bathymetry display monitor.
  - On the ground floor of the atrium below, several wooden folding tables are set up with laptops, brochures, maps, and large vertical posters/banners along the walls.
