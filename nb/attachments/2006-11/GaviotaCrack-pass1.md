# Fledermaus Bathymetric Profile Across the Gaviota Crack (`GaviotaCrack-pass1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from the **Fledermaus** 3D visualization application on Mac OS X showing a color-shaded multibeam bathymetry surface of the Santa Barbara Basin (processed at sea on the R/V *Roger Revelle* during cruise `CA0606RR` using MB-System `mbgrid` at 30 m resolution) and an interactive cross-sectional depth `Profile` window across a seafloor feature referred to as the "Gaviota Crack."

## 3D Scene & Transect Line (Upper Half)
- **Window Title Bar**: Mac OS X title bar labeled `Fledermaus` with the yellow/blue Fledermaus icon.
- **Bathymetric Surface**: A 3D shaded-relief seafloor surface colored by depth in discrete contour bands—ranging from green/yellow on the upper slope (top left) through cyan and medium blue across the gently sloping ramp, to dark blue and purple in the deeper basin (bottom right), set against a black background.
- **Profile Transect**: A short red line segment on the cyan slope marks the selected cross-section; two bright yellow leader lines connect the ends of the red transect line to the left and right edges of the `Profile` window below.

## Profile Window & Controls (Lower Half)
- **Graph Area**:
  - **Vertical Axis (Depth in meters)**: Labeled from `-362` at the top to `-380` at the bottom in 2-meter increments (`-362`, `-364`, `-366`, `-368`, `-370`, `-372`, `-374`, `-376`, `-378`, `-380`), with horizontal gray grid lines.
  - **Horizontal Axis (Distance in meters)**: Labeled from `0` to `500` in 100-meter increments (`0`, `100`, `200`, `300`, `400`, `500`), extending to ~565 m.
  - **Bathymetric Curve**: Filled in teal/cyan below the seafloor profile. The slope descends from `-362 m` at `0 m` down to about `-377 m` around `300–330 m` (a broad trough/depression), rises slightly over a ridge at `-375 m` between `360 m` and `400 m`, and continues down to `-380.5 m` at the right end with a small bump at `520 m`.
