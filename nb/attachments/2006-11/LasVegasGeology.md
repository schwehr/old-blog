# Google Earth Polygon Highlighting Geologic Features Near Las Vegas (`LasVegasGeology.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from Google Earth showing satellite imagery of rugged desert mountains and alluvial fans west of Las Vegas, Nevada (near Red Rock Canyon / Blue Diamond Hill along Nevada State Route 159), with a semi-transparent purple KML polygon overlaid to highlight a specific bedrock geologic ridge.

## Map & Geologic Details
- **Terrain & Imagery**: High-relief desert mountain ranges with exposed carbonate/sedimentary strata in the upper left and center, flanked by braided alluvial fans and desert washes in tan, gray, and reddish-brown tones on the right.
- **Polygon Overlay**: A large, semi-transparent purple/magenta six-sided polygon extends from the lower left to the upper right, enclosing a prominent faulted/tilted ridge system in the left-center of the view while allowing the underlying satellite imagery and rock textures to remain visible.
