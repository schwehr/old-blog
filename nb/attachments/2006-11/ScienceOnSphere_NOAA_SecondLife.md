# NOAA "Science On a Sphere" Exhibit in Second Life (`ScienceOnSphere_NOAA_SecondLife.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from the 3D virtual world **Second Life** (captured by Roland Arsenault) showing a virtual recreation of NOAA's **Science On a Sphere** exhibition space.

## Scene & Exhibit Description
- **Central Globe**: Suspended in mid-air at the center of a gray circular pavilion is a large 3D spherical globe displaying global topography and bathymetry—showing the Indian Ocean in shades of purple/blue surrounded by Africa, Madagascar, Arabia, India, Southeast Asia, and Australia in green, yellow, and brown elevation shading.
- **Floor Platform**: Directly beneath the floating globe is a circular floor pad featuring an image of Earth from space and a white banner across the middle reading `SCIENCE ON A SPHERE™`.
- **Right Wall**: A curved gray architectural wall bearing the circular emblem of the **National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration** (`NOAA` / `NATIONAL OCEANIC AND ATMOSPHERIC ADMINISTRATION` / `U.S. DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE`).
- **Left Wall & Background Exhibits**:
  - The curved gray wall on the left displays two rectangular scientific map panels: a global rainbow-colored ocean/climate dataset on top and a false-color planetary/Martian topography map (showing Valles Marineris and Tharsis volcanoes) on the bottom.
  - Beyond the pavilion on the far left stands a large vertical rocket booster (`...TATES` visible on the white fuselage beside an orange external tank).
  - In the background between the walls sits a 3D model of the Apollo Lunar Module with gold foil descent stage and four landing legs.
