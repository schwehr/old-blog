# Piscataqua River Shallow Water Survey Bathymetry in Google Earth (`ShallowWaterSurvey-draft.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot showing a rainbow-colored multibeam bathymetry overlay of the lower **Piscataqua River** and Portsmouth Harbor (between Portsmouth/New Castle, New Hampshire, and Kittery, Maine) from the 2001 Shallow Water Survey (`ShallowWaterSurvey2001-draft.kmz`), converted from a UTM Fledermaus `.sd` file to geographic coordinates.

## Map & Bathymetric Overlay Details
- **Geographic Area**: The tidal channel of the Piscataqua River winding from the upper left (northwest, near the Interstate 95 bridge, Sarah Mildred Long Bridge, and Memorial Bridge) past Badgers Island and Portsmouth Naval Shipyard (center-left), bending east around Pierce Island and Four Tree Island, and then turning south past New Castle Island toward the river mouth at the lower right.
- **Bathymetric Color Scale**:
  - Deep channel thalweg areas are colored dark blue, purple, and cyan.
  - Intermediate depths are shaded bright green and yellow.
  - Shallow shoals and channel margins are colored orange, red, and magenta.
  - Faint diagonal striping/aliasing artifacts from the UTM-to-geographic grid reprojection are visible across the shaded surface.
- **Labeled Islands (Light Blue Text with Black Outlines)**:
  - Upper left / center-left: `Badgers Island`, `Wattlebury Island`, `Four Tree Island`, `Pierce Island`, `Little Island`, `Shapleigh Island`, `Goat Island`
  - Center / upper right: `Jamaica Island`, `Clarks Island`, `Hoyts Is`, `Phillips Island`, `Gooseberry Islan`, `Fishing Island`
  - Lower center / right: `Lady Isle`, `Pest Island`, `Leachs Island`, `Clampit Island`, `Goose Island`, `Blunts Island`, `New Castle Island`, `Wood Islan`
