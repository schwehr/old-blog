# Visitor Log Map Showing Fargo, North Dakota (`blogmap-fargo.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a web-based visitor log map widget (generated by `gvisit.com` powered by Google Maps) tracking recent visitors to "Kurt's blog" in late November 2006, with an open callout bubble highlighting a visitor from Fargo, North Dakota.

## Map & Interface Details
- **Header**: Top-right text above the map frame reads **"Visitor log for [Kurt's blog]"** in bold black sans-serif text, with "Kurt's blog" styled as a blue underlined hyperlink.
- **Base Map**: A standard 2006-era Google Maps Mercator world map view centered over the North Atlantic Ocean, showing North America on the left and Europe/North Africa on the right. Landmasses are light gray/off-white with gray country borders and green-tinted park/forest regions, while oceans are pale blue-gray.
- **Navigation Controls**: Located along the left side of the map:
  - Four directional pan arrow buttons arranged in a cross around a central return-to-center button.
  - A vertical zoom slider with `+` (top) and `-` (bottom) buttons and a horizontal slider thumb positioned near the bottom (zoomed out to a trans-Atlantic view).
  - The **"POWERED BY Google"** logo appears in the lower-left corner.
- **Visitor Markers**: Eight salmon-red teardrop pushpin markers with black center dots indicating visitor locations:
  - Five across North America: Southern California, Fargo (North Dakota), the U.S. Southeast (Georgia/South Carolina area), the Mid-Atlantic (Maryland/Virginia area), and New England (New Hampshire/Massachusetts area).
  - Three in Western Europe: England (United Kingdom), the Netherlands/Germany border area, and northeastern Spain / southern France.
