# Santa Barbara Basin Cruise Photo Collage (`ca0606rr-cover.png`)

## Overview
A composite photo collage from the November 2006 research cruise (`ca0606rr`) aboard the *R/V Roger Revelle* in the Santa Barbara Basin, California. A wide daytime marine photograph of an offshore oil platform against the coastal mountains serves as the full-bleed background, overlaid with four white-bordered inset photographs showing shipboard science operations and the vessel's superstructure.

## Background Scene
- **Offshore Oil Platform**: Centered in the deep blue waters of the Santa Barbara Channel is a large multi-deck steel offshore drilling/production platform with a tall white derrick tower, angled cranes, and yellow-painted structural elements.
- **Support Vessel**: A dark-hulled offshore supply or tug boat sits in the water just to the right (east) of the platform.
- **Coastline**: Behind the platform rises the steep, chaparral-covered Santa Ynez Mountains and coastal bluffs of the Gaviota coast under a hazy blue sky.

## Inset Photographs
- **Top-Left Inset (CTD Rosette Operations)**: A vertical photograph taken inside/beside the ship's staging bay showing four science party members wearing orange personal flotation work vests (PFDs) preparing or sampling a black Niskin bottle rosette frame. One person crouches in the foreground adjusting a red lanyard/line while another leans in above; two others stand in the sunlit doorway behind them.
- **Bottom-Left Inset (Deck/Lab Equipment Briefing)**: A horizontal photograph showing seven researchers and students gathered around a wooden deck box containing a yellow oceanographic instrument package. A man in a light blue t-shirt and white cap gestures while speaking to the group, while two people in dark blue shirts lean over the yellow instrument inside the crate.
- **Top-Right Inset (Nighttime A-Frame Deployment)**: A horizontal nighttime photograph illuminated by bright deck floodlights mounted on the ship's stern A-frame. Four crew/science members in orange life vests and hardhats work on the fantail deck around a tall cylindrical coring or lander frame suspended beneath the A-frame.
- **Bottom-Right Inset (*R/V Roger Revelle* Superstructure)**: A vertical daytime photograph looking forward/upward from the working deck toward the white aft control station and superstructure of the *R/V Roger Revelle*, showing the blue-and-gold Scripps stack stripe, spherical white radome, mast antennas, and a steep ship's ladder on the right.
