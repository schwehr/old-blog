# Loading a Google Earth KMZ File in Google Maps (`ccom-gm.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Google Maps web interface in November 2006 demonstrating how pasting the URL of a remote Google Earth `.kmz` file (`http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/GoogleEarth/CCOM.kmz`) into the Google Maps search bar displays the placemark on a hybrid satellite map of Durham, New Hampshire.

## Interface & Search Controls
- **Top Bar**:
  - Google Maps logo on the left, with navigation links above the search bar: `Web`, `Images`, `Video`, `News`, **`Maps`** (selected in bold black), and `more »`.
  - Search input box containing `http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/GoogleEarth/CCOM.kmz` next to a gray **`Search Maps`** button.
  - Sub-tabs below the input box: **`Search the map`** (highlighted in pale orange), `Find businesses`, and `Get directions`.
- **Sub-header Bar**: Light tan horizontal bar titled **`Maps`** on the left, with action links on the right: `Print`, `Email`, and `Link to this page` (each with a small blue icon).
- **Left Sidebar**:
  - Status text: `Displaying content from vislab-ccom.unh.edu` (green link) and `View on Google Earth` (blue link).
  - Disclaimer in gray text: *"The content overlaid onto this map is provided by a third party, and Google is not responsible for it."*
  - Heading **`CCOM.kmz`** with a salmon-red pushpin icon next to the blue link **`CCOM/JHC`** highlighted in a gray selection box.

## Map View & Callout Bubble
- **Map Mode & Controls**: Set to **`Hybrid`** mode (selected among `Map`, `Satellite`, and `Hybrid` buttons in the top-right corner of the map). Pan and zoom controls sit on the upper left; a collapsible overview inset map of the Durham/Route 4 region sits in the lower-right corner; a dual scale bar (`2000 ft` / `500 m`) and copyright notice (`©2006 Google - Imagery ©2006 Maine Office of GIS, Map data ©2006 NAVTEQ™ - Terms of Use`) appear along the bottom.
- **Geographic Features**: Satellite imagery overlaid with yellow road lines and highway shields around Durham, NH, including US Route `4`, State Routes `155A` and `108`, `Main St`, `Mast Rd`, `Madbury Rd`, and `Dover Rd`.
- **Placemark & Speech Bubble**: A salmon-red pushpin marks the CCOM/JHC building south of `Main St`, with an open white info bubble containing:
  - **`CCOM/JHC`**
  - `The Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping/Joint Hydrographic Center at the University of New Hampshire` followed by the purple visited link `ccom.unh.edu`.
  - Blue link at the bottom of the bubble: `Search nearby`.
