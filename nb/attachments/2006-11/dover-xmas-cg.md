# U.S. Coast Guard Response Boat in the Dover Christmas Parade (`dover-xmas-cg.png`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph from the November 26, 2006 Christmas parade in Dover, New Hampshire, showing a U.S. Coast Guard rigid-hull inflatable boat (RB-S / Defender-class) being towed on a trailer past a crowd of spectators in front of an Ocean National Bank branch.

## Scene Description
- **Coast Guard Vessel**:
  - A 25-foot Defender-class U.S. Coast Guard response boat (`RB-S`) with a bright orange-red inflatable collar bordered by a black rub strake, an unpainted aluminum cabin/pilothouse, and twin silver Honda outboard engines mounted on the stern.
  - White stenciled lettering along the port collar reads **`U.S. COAST GUARD`** in the center and hull number **`25...`** near the bow.
  - Above the cabin roof are a white Furuno radar dome, blue emergency strobe lights, loudspeakers, and a small U.S. flag and Coast Guard ensign flying from a mast.
  - Two people in civilian jackets stand on the forward deck near the bow, while uniformed personnel are visible inside the cabin.
- **Crowd & Parade Details**:
  - Spectators line the street in the foreground, seen mostly from behind, including children sitting on adults' shoulders (one in a pink jacket on the right) and someone holding up a compact digital camera in the center foreground.
  - A bright yellow SpongeBob SquarePants mylar helium balloon and a smaller yellow smiley/star balloon float on strings in front of the boat's bow.
- **Background**:
  - A single-story red brick commercial building with large storefront windows and signage reading **`Ocean`** (in white script) next to a yellow banner reading **`BreakFree! Checking`**.
  - Older multi-story red brick mill/commercial buildings and bare late-autumn trees rise in the background.
