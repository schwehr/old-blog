# Mounted Police Color Guard in the Dover Christmas Parade (`dover-xmas-horses.png`)

## Overview
A warm, late-afternoon photograph from the November 26, 2006 Christmas parade in Dover, New Hampshire, showing three mounted police officers riding abreast on dark black horses down a crowd-lined street.

## Scene Description
- **Mounted Officers & Horses**:
  - Three matching black horses march toward the right in formation, outfitted with black leather bridles with brass fittings, white-trimmed breast collars, and dark blue saddlecloths edged in gold and white with a gold emblem in the corner.
  - Three police officers in dark navy uniforms with gold shoulder patches and yellow-striped breeches ride the horses.
  - The officer closest to the camera wears a black-and-silver helmet and carries a tall wooden flagstaff in a stirrup socket flying a dark blue flag with gold lettering (`...PATROL...`) and yellow fringe.
  - The middle officer wears a light blue/white-topped helmet, and the far officer wears a white-and-black helmet.
- **Spectators & Background**:
  - In the foreground, parade-goers stand shoulder-to-shoulder with their backs to the camera watching the horses pass.
  - Across the street in the background, families and children (several in bright pink jackets) stand along the curb in front of parked cars, a white RV/camper on the left, and bare trees glowing in golden late-afternoon sunlight.
