# 3D Textured Pyramid Building in San Diego in Google Earth (`ge-building-sd.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from Google Earth showing a 3D photo-textured architectural model of the iconic pyramid-shaped Comerica Bank / Design Center building at the Miramar Road / I-15 area in San Diego, California (created by Sebastian Strzalkowski), viewed at a low oblique angle with terrain and road imagery.

## 3D Model & Scene Description
- **Pyramid Structure**:
  - A large, steeply pitched four-sided glass-and-frame pyramid rises in the center of the scene, textured with photographic imagery showing a white diagonal lattice framework over dark reflective glass panels.
  - Flanking the main pyramid on the left and right are darker, vertical glass-curtain-wall wing projections that intersect the sloped faces of the pyramid.
  - Near the base of the front pyramid face, palm trees and a building entrance sign are visible in the photographic texture mapped onto the facade.
- **Surrounding Terrain & Context**:
  - In the foreground, a multi-lane divided avenue (Miramar Road) curves past the circular driveway at the base of the building.
  - Smaller low-rise commercial/industrial buildings sit to the left and right of the pyramid, backed by arid, chaparral-covered hills in the distance under a black, star-flecked Google Earth sky.
