# Google Earth Multibeam Swath Pushpin Popup (`ge-mb-pushpin.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from Google Earth illustrating an automated proofing workflow for multibeam sonar lines collected aboard the *R/V Roger Revelle* near the Northern Channel Islands (San Miguel Island and Santa Rosa Island), California. Clicking a yellow pushpin placemark at the start of a survey line opens a callout balloon displaying an MB-System / GMT bathymetric swath plot for that file.

## 3D Map View (Left)
- **Geographic Setting**: An oblique 3D view looking southward across the Santa Barbara Channel toward **`San Miguel Island`** (middle left) and **`Santa Rosa Island`** (bottom left), rendered with higher-resolution brown/tan island satellite imagery surrounded by dark blue ocean.
- **Ship Track & Pushpins**:
  - Three bright yellow thumbtack pushpins mark the starting coordinates of consecutive multibeam survey lines: **`46_0`**, **`47_0`**, and **`48_0`** (with two dimmer olive-shaded pushpins just to the right of `48_0`).
  - White and red ship-track line segments connect the pushpins on the ocean surface.

## Callout Balloon & Swath Plot (Right)
- **Balloon Header**:
  - Title: **`47_0`**
  - Subtitle (bold): **`Start Of Multibeam Line: 0047_20061115_095753_raw.all.nav`**
- **Embedded MB-System Plot**:
  - A vertical rectangular map frame with alternating black-and-white degree/minute border ticks showing latitude (`34°16'00"` to `34°19'00"`) and longitude (`239°55'30"` to `239°58'30"` / ~120°W).
  - Left vertical label reads: `Data File: 0047_20061115_095753_raw.all.grd`.
  - Inside the map frame, a narrow diagonal multibeam swath runs from upper-left to lower-right, colored in shades of blue and cyan (with a small gap near the middle).
  - A vertical color bar on the right side of the plot is labeled `Depth (m)` and ranges from `625.0` (deep blue at the bottom) through cyan, yellow, and orange to `775.0` (top).
- **Balloon Footer**:
  - Standard Google Earth footer: `Directions: To here - From here`.
