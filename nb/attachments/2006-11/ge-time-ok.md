# Google Earth Single-Point Time View of 3D Ship Model (`ge-time-ok.png`)

## Overview
A full-window screenshot of Google Earth on Mac OS X showing the *Creole Queen* 3D paddlewheeler model (`creole_queen.kmz`) rendered properly as a single vessel when the time slider range is collapsed to a single point in time (`MAY 1083`).

## Left Sidebar Panels
- **Search Panel (Top Left)**:
  - Tabs for **`Fly To`** (selected in blue), `Find Businesses`, and `Directions`.
  - Search input box with gray example prompt `e.g., 1600 Pennsylvania Ave, 20006` and a magnifying glass search button.
  - Tour playback controls (`Play`, `Stop`, `Close`) below an empty search results pane.
- **Places Panel (Middle Left)**:
  - Shows a checked KML/KMZ container **`creole_queen.kmz`** expanded to reveal checked child placemarks/views: `view_1`, `view_2`, and `view_3` (partially visible), with a transparency slider and tour play/stop buttons underneath.
- **Layers Panel (Bottom Left)**:
  - `View:` dropdown set to `Core`.
  - Under `Primary Database`, **`Terrain`** is checked and `Featured Content` is unchecked.

## 3D Viewport (Right)
- **3D Riverboat Model**:
  - A single, cleanly rendered 3D model of the *Creole Queen* Mississippi River sternwheeler floating on brownish-gray water, featuring a red-and-black hull, two white passenger decks with blue windows, a single red-roofed pilothouse behind twin black smokestacks, a suspended black bow stage/gangplank on the left, and a red stern paddlewheel on the right.
- **Time Slider & Navigation Overlays**:
  - Along the top of the 3D view, the timeline slider has both range markers pinched together at a single time point labeled **`MAY 1083`**.
  - Top-right navigation controls overlap a white watermark box (`...martin.com / jpw`), showing the horizontal tilt slider, circular joystick/compass ring (`N` pointing right), and vertical zoom slider.
- **Scale Bar, Attribution & Status Bar**:
  - Bottom-left scale bar indicates **`7 m`**.
  - Center-bottom copyright text: `Image © 2006 DigitalGlobe`, `© 2006 Navteq`, `Image © 2006 Sanborn`, and the `Google` logo on the bottom right.
  - Bottom status bar reads: `Pointer 29°56'55.77" N 90°03'32.21" W elev 0 m Streaming |||||||||| 100%`.
