# Selecting "Add -> Network Link..." in Google Earth (`kmzimage-ge-netlink.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot of the top portion of Google Earth on Mac OS X showing the **`Add`** menu pulled down from the system menu bar, with **`Network Link...`** highlighted in blue to demonstrate how to load a remote `.kmz` file by URL.

## Menu & Interface Details
- **Mac OS X Menu Bar**:
  - Apple logo on the far left, followed by **`Google Earth`** (bold), `File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Tools`, **`Add`** (selected in blue), and `Help`.
- **Open `Add` Dropdown Menu**:
  - `Folder...` (`⇧⌘N`)
  - `Placemark...` (`⇧⌘P`)
  - *Divider line*
  - `Path...` (`⇧⌘T`)
  - `Polygon...` (`⇧⌘G`)
  - `Model...` (`⇧⌘M`)
  - *Divider line*
  - `Image Overlay...` (`⇧⌘O`)
  - **`Network Link...`** (highlighted in solid blue with white text)
- **Background Application Window**:
  - Left sidebar shows the `Search` panel (`Fly To`, `Find Businesses`, `Directions`) and the top header of the `Places` panel.
  - Main map viewport on the right shows high-resolution overhead satellite imagery of trees and campus buildings at the University of New Hampshire, with a purple star pushpin labeled **`CCOM/JHC`** in red text in the lower-right corner.
