# CCOM/JHC Georeferenced Logo Overlay in Google Earth (`kmzimage-ge.png`)

## Overview
A full-window screenshot of Google Earth on Mac OS X showing a georeferenced image overlay (`CCOM_JHC_Logo.kmz`) of the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center circular logo placed offshore in the Gulf of Maine / Atlantic Ocean east of the New Hampshire and Massachusetts coastline.

## Left Sidebar Panels
- **Window & Toolbar**: Standard Mac OS X window titled **`Google Earth`** with top toolbar buttons for toggling the sidebar, adding a placemark, polygon, path, or image overlay, measuring distance, emailing, and printing.
- **Search Panel**: `Fly To` tab active with prompt `e.g., San Francisco`.
- **Places Panel**: Shows `My Places` expanded with a checked folder **`UNH`**.
- **Layers Panel**: `View:` set to `Core`, with `Terrain` checked under `Primary Database`.

## Map Viewport (Right)
- **Coastline & Roads**:
  - Top-down satellite view of the New Hampshire, southern Maine, and northeastern Massachusetts coastline.
  - Major highways are marked with route shields and yellow lines: US Routes **`202`**, **`4`**, **`1A`**, and **`1`**, State Route **`101`**, and Interstates **`495`** and **`95`**.
- **Placemarks**:
  - **`CCOM/JHC`** (red text next to a purple star pushpin) inland at Durham, New Hampshire, along Route 4.
  - **`Base Station`** and **`Station 3`** (white text labels with a pale blue webcam/station icon) on the coast near Portsmouth/New Castle and the Isles of Shoals.
- **Georeferenced Logo Overlay**:
  - In the dark blue water of the Gulf of Maine to the right (east) of the coast, a large circular blue-and-gray emblem is overlaid directly onto the ocean surface.
  - The outer ring of the circular logo reads **`Center for Coastal & Ocean Mapping`** on the upper-left arc, **`Joint Hydrographic Center`** on the upper-right arc, and **`University of New Hampshire`** along the bottom arc.
  - Inside the circle is a stylized blue-and-white graphic of acoustic swath beams mapping a bathymetric grid, with **`CCOM`** and **`JHC`** stacked on the right.
