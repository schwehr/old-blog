# SDSU Google Earth Age of the Ocean Floor Thumbnail (`oceanfloor_tn.jpg`)

## Overview
A small thumbnail screenshot from Google Earth illustrating a global "Age of the Ocean Floor" KMZ overlay (`oceanfloor.kmz`) distributed by the San Diego State University (SDSU) Department of Geological Sciences.

## Globe & Map Details
- **Perspective**: A 3D globe view centered on North America, Central America, the Caribbean, and northern South America against a black space background with a thin purple-blue atmospheric halo around the Earth's limb.
- **Landmasses & Interior Waters**: Satellite imagery is visible over the continents, showing green vegetation across eastern North America and northern South America, tan/brown arid regions across western North America, white ice covering Greenland at the top, and dark blue water in Hudson Bay, the Great Lakes, the Gulf of Mexico, and the Caribbean Sea.
- **Seafloor Age Overlay**: Isochron bands color-coded by geological age cover the eastern Pacific Ocean (left), the western North Atlantic Ocean (right), and the Cayman Trough in the Caribbean:
  - **Youngest Crust (Red / Orange)**: Prominent along the East Pacific Rise, Juan de Fuca Ridge, Cocos-Nazca spreading system, and the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, as well as a narrow band in the central Caribbean (Cayman Trough).
  - **Intermediate Crust (Yellow / Green)**: Flanking the spreading ridges across the eastern Pacific and central Atlantic.
  - **Oldest Crust (Cyan / Light Blue / Medium Blue)**: Occupying the western North Atlantic basin along the eastern seaboard of North America and the far northwestern Pacific edge.
- **Interface Elements**:
  - Bottom left: A circular compass rose icon with `N`, `S`, `E`, `W` cardinal labels.
  - Bottom center: Small yellow copyright/attribution text (`Image NASA`, `TerraMetrics`, `Data SIO, NOAA, U.S. Navy, NGA, GEBCO`).
  - Bottom right: The white `©2006 Google` logo.
