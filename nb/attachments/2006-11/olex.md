# Olex Crowd-Sourced Bathymetric Coverage Map of the North Atlantic (`olex.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from the **Olex** marine navigation and seafloor mapping software showing crowd-sourced echo sounder bathymetric coverage across the North Atlantic Ocean, Nordic Seas, North Sea, and Barents Sea.

## Map Coverage & Geography
- **Landmasses**: Rendered in solid golden yellow with black coastline outlines. Visible regions include:
  - **Greenland** in the upper left.
  - **Iceland** near the center, surrounded by a well-mapped continental shelf.
  - **Jan Mayen** and **Svalbard** in the north/northeast.
  - **Fennoscandia (Norway, Sweden, Finland)** and northwestern Russia / Kola Peninsula in the upper right and center right.
  - **British Isles (Great Britain and Ireland)** and mainland Western/Central Europe (France, Denmark, Germany, Low Countries, Baltic coast) in the lower right.
  - **Newfoundland, Labrador, and Nova Scotia (Canada)** in the lower left.
- **Bathymetric Tracks & Depth Coloring**:
  - Unmapped ocean areas are left plain white.
  - Mapped seafloor areas and vessel transit tracks are shaded in shades of blue: light cyan/sky blue for shallow continental shelves (such as the North Sea, Irish Sea, Baltic approaches, Grand Banks, Barents Sea, and Icelandic shelf) and deep royal/navy blue for deeper continental slopes, ridges, and deep-ocean vessel tracks criss-crossing between Iceland, Greenland, the Faroe Islands, Norway, and Newfoundland.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Toolbar**: A beige menu bar along the top edge containing Norwegian interface buttons and indicators from left to right:
  - Buttons: `Diverse`, `Turer`, `Info`, `3D`, a circular target icon, `Slepestrek`, `Hardhet`, `Bunn`, `Snitt`.
  - Scale bar in the center: `500 nm` (500 nautical miles).
  - Color scale indicator: `0` to `600` with a cyan-to-dark-blue gradient box.
  - Navigation/view buttons: `<<<`, `>>>`, `Relieff`, `Bokser`, `Print` (in red text), current time `15:44:02`, and a small sun/brightness icon on the far right.
- **Compass / Scale Indicator**: A vertical purple arrow pointing north near the upper left over Greenland, flanked by two small purple horizontal tick marks.
- **Bottom Left Bar**: A beige bar labeled `Dybdekoter` (depth contours) with selectable interval buttons: `1`, `2`, `5`, `10`, **`20`** (depressed/active), `50`, `100`.
- **Bottom Right Logo & Status**: Displays `CPU 48°C` just above a rectangular `Olex` logo badge with a blue underline.
