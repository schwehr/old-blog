# Santa Barbara Basin Multibeam Coverage Map in Google Earth (`sbb-coveragemap.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot showing raw multibeam sonar swath coverage and numbered yellow pushpin placemarks from the November 2006 *R/V Roger Revelle* cruise (`CA0606RR`) in the Santa Barbara Basin off southern California, with an open placemark info balloon displaying a GMT/MB-System bathymetric plot for line `24_0`.

## 3D Map View & Multibeam Overlays
- **Geographic Setting**: Tilted 3D view looking northward across the Santa Barbara Channel toward the Santa Barbara County coastline (visible along the top edge).
- **Multibeam Swath Overlays**: Semi-transparent color-coded bathymetric swaths overlaid on the dark blue ocean surface, transitioning from pink/peach and yellow in the shallower northern slope areas to light green, cyan, and lavender-blue in the deeper central basin.
- **Ship Track Lines**: Thin white navigation tracklines trace the vessel's survey grid, including east-west lines on the left, north-south mower-pattern lines across the northern slope, and a dense cluster of maneuvers near the center-left.
- **Placemark Pins**: Yellow thumbtack/pushpin icons mark the start of each multibeam log file, labeled in white text with a file prefix and daily sequence suffix (`<line>_<count>`):
  - Northern/upper pins: `28_0`, `55_0`, `22_0`, `25_0`, `23...`
  - Central/western cluster: `7_1`, `7_0`, `3_4`, `51_0`, `40_0`, `8_0`, `41_0`, `8_1`, `36_0`, `26_0`, `27_0`, `1_3`, `29_0`, `30_0`, `6_0`, `2_3`, `9_1`, `5_2`, and `24_0`.
  - Some pins in the dense central cluster appear in a darker olive-yellow shade due to overlapping/selection state.
- **Map Attribution**: Bottom center reads `© 2006 Europa Technologies` and `© 2006 Navteq`.

## Open Info Balloon (`24_0`)
- **Balloon Header**: Pointing to pushpin `24_0` at the lower center-right is a large white rounded callout box with a blue close button (`x`) in the upper right corner.
- **Text Content**:
  - Title in bold: `24_0`
  - Subtitle in bold: `Start Of Multibeam Line:` followed on the next line by `0024_20061114_065127_raw.all.nav`.
- **Embedded GMT Plot**:
  - Displays a vertical GMT/MB-System grid plot titled `Data File 0024_20061114_065127_raw.all.grd`.
  - Shows a single north-south multibeam swath curving slightly southeastward at its southern end, colored from blue at the northern (shallower) end through cyan, yellow, and orange at the southern (deeper) end.
  - Includes a horizontal depth color bar at the bottom of the inset plot and `Directions: To here - From here` links at the bottom of the balloon.
