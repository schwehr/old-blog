# San Diego 30x60 Quadrangle Geologic Map in Google Earth (`sd-geo-kmz.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth on Mac OS X displaying an interactive geologic map overlay of the San Diego area (`san_diego_30_60.kmz`) created by the San Diego State University (SDSU) Department of Geological Sciences.

## Google Earth Interface (Left Sidebar & Toolbars)
- **Window Title Bar**: Standard Mac OS X window titled `Google Earth`.
- **Top Toolbar**: Contains a sidebar toggle button, `Add Placemark` (yellow pushpin), `Add Polygon`, `Add Path`, `Add Image Overlay`, `Measure` (ruler), `Email`, and `Print` icons.
- **Search Panel**: Top-left collapsible pane with tabs `Fly To` (selected), `Find Businesses`, and `Directions`, a text box with prompt `e.g., San Francisco`, a search button, and playback/clear controls (`►`, `■`, `x`).
- **Places Panel**: Middle-left collapsible pane showing the expanded KML folder hierarchy:
  - Unchecked folders: `Faults`, `Attitudes`, `Structure`.
  - Checked folder: `Slide Directions` -> expanded checked subfolder `Arrows` -> individual line items labeled `[no name]` with subtitle `Unknown Line Type` (illustrating the unlabelled arrow items mentioned in the blog post).
- **Layers Panel**: Bottom-left collapsible pane with `View: Core` dropdown and `Primary Database` tree showing `Terrain` (unchecked), `Featured Content` (unchecked), `roads` (unchecked), `borders` (partially checked `-`), and `Populated Places`.

## Map View & Geologic Overlay (Right Pane)
- **SDSU Legend Overlay**: A semi-transparent white box in the top-left corner of the 3D viewport headed with the red-and-black `SAN DIEGO STATE UNIVERSITY` logo and `Department of Geological Sciences`, listing six icon types:
  - Blue `i` icon: `Unit Descriptions`
  - Black hand lens / magnifying glass icon: `Samples`
  - Camera icon: `Field Photos`
  - Document/magnifying glass icon: `Publications`
  - Colored geological block icon: `Map Features`
  - Conical flask icon: `Geochemistry`
- **Geologic Map Coverage**: Covers the San Diego coastline (including La Jolla, Mission Bay, Point Loma, and northern San Diego Bay) and adjacent inland mesas and valleys:
  - **Offshore / Continental Shelf (left)**: Shaded in light blue and pale purple polygons with black dashed and solid fault traces (Rose Canyon fault zone) and circular/lobate offshore features.
  - **Onshore Geologic Units (right)**: Intricately mapped bedrock and quaternary surficial units in shades of tan, yellow, orange, brown, pink, purple, and blue, dissected by dendritic canyon drainage networks.
  - **Unit Labels & Placemarks**: White bold text labels with black halos accompanied by blue `i` (`Unit Descriptions`) icons mark specific formations, including `Qop2-4`, `Tf`, `Mzu`, `Kgd`, `Kt`, `Tt`, `Qpe`, `Tp`, `Tst`, `Tsc`, `Qvop7`, `Qvop11`, `Ta`, `Qvop10`, `Qmb`, `Qvop9`, `Kgu`, `Qop6`, `Qoa`, `Qaf`, `Qvop8`, `Kcs`, and `Tsd`.
- **Status Bar (Bottom)**: Displays `© 2006 Europa Technologies`, `© 2006 Google`, `Pointer 32°43'51.11" N 117°01'32.29" W`, `Streaming |||||||||| 100%`, and `Eye alt 35.20 km`.
