# Mac OS X Time Zone Selection Widget (`timezone.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot of the Mac OS X `Date & Time` System Preferences `Time Zone` tab, showing the world map time zone selector set to Eastern Standard Time (`EST`).

## Interface & Map Details
- **World Map Display**:
  - A rectangular satellite-style world map showing continents shaded in green (vegetated regions), tan/brown (arid regions), and white/gray (high-latitude snow and ice cover in northern Canada, Greenland, and Siberia) against a muted slate-blue ocean background.
  - A vertical, semi-transparent white band highlights the **UTC-5 / Eastern Time Zone** strip running from northern Canada through the eastern United States, Central America/Caribbean, and western South America.
  - A white circular target reticle icon marks the selected city location on the New England coast (`Boston`).
- **Text Labels & Controls**:
  - Below the map on the left: `Time Zone:` followed by `EST`.
  - Below that: `Closest City:` next to a Mac OS X Aqua dropdown menu displaying `Boston - U.S.A.` with a blue up/down arrow button on the right.
