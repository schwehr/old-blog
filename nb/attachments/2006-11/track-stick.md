# TrackStick USB GPS Data Logger (`track-stick.jpg`)

## Overview
A studio product photograph on a plain white background showing a **TrackStick** portable USB GPS data logger with its protective cap removed and a black wrist lanyard attached.

## Device Details
- **Main Body**: An elongated, rectangular black plastic enclosure (resembling a thick USB flash drive) oriented diagonally from lower-left to upper-right.
  - Near the rounded rear end on the top surface is a circular embossed globe/grid logo.
  - On the side edge is a small recessed slide switch (power on/off switch).
- **USB Connector & Cap**:
  - Extending from the front (lower-left) end is a standard gold/silver male USB Type-A plug with two square cutouts on its face.
  - Resting to the left of the connector is a smoky translucent dark-gray plastic protective cap.
- **Lanyard**: Attached to a loop at the rear corner of the device is a black woven wrist strap/lanyard with a black plastic slide clasp, curving across the upper portion of the frame.
