# SDSU Google Earth Conterminous US Geology Map Thumbnail (`us_geology_tn.jpg`)

## Overview
A small thumbnail screenshot from Google Earth displaying a colorful bedrock geologic map overlay of the conterminous United States (`us_geology.kmz`) hosted by the San Diego State University (SDSU) Department of Geological Sciences.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Geographic Extent**: Shows the lower 48 United States bounded by southern Canada to the north, northern Mexico to the south, the Pacific Ocean to the west, and the Atlantic Ocean and Gulf of Mexico to the east and southeast.
- **Geologic Map Colors**:
  - **Great Plains & Central Lowlands**: Broad bands of yellow, light green, blue, and olive representing Mesozoic and Paleozoic sedimentary basins and Cenozoic cover.
  - **Canadian Shield / Upper Midwest (Minnesota/Wisconsin/Adirondacks)**: Pink, red, and purple Precambrian crystalline basement rocks.
  - **Appalachian Mountains & Piedmont (East)**: Narrow, tightly folded northeast-trending bands of blue, purple, pink, black, and yellow.
  - **Atlantic & Gulf Coastal Plains (Southeast & South)**: Concentric bands of yellow, pale yellow, green, and gray Cenozoic coastal sediments wrapping from New Jersey through Florida to Texas and up the Mississippi Embayment.
  - **Western Cordillera, Basin and Range, & Pacific Coast (West)**: Complex mosaic of orange/brown (Columbia River basalts), yellow, green, cyan, pink, and dense black fault/contact networks across the Rocky Mountains, Colorado Plateau, Great Basin, and California.
- **Interface Elements**:
  - Top left: Miniature white legend box with the `SAN DIEGO STATE UNIVERSITY` header and six category icons (`Unit Descriptions`, `Samples`, `Field Photos`, `Publications`, `Map Features`, `Geochemistry`).
  - Bottom left: Circular Google Earth compass rose.
  - Bottom right: White `©2006 Google` logo.
