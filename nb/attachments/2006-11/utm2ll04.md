# DMagic Main Window Displaying UTM Bathymetry and Topography (`utm2ll04.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the full **DMagic** window (`/Users/schwehr/Desktop/Amy/`) on Mac OS X after loading the `gutmtest.dtm`, `gutmtest.geo`, and `gutmtest.shade` components into the main viewport, showing a color-shaded relief surface in UTM coordinates.

- **`Data Components` List**: Shows the three loaded files (`Hgt gutmtest.dtm` selected in blue, `Geo gutmtest.geo`, and `Col gutmtest.shade`) alongside transfer buttons `>`, `>>`, `<`, `<<`, and a help button `?`.
- **Action Buttons**: Four full-width rounded buttons stacked vertically:
  - `Create/Edit Geo-Referencing`
  - `Color Map Editor`
  - `Surface Shader`
  - `Assemble Fledermaus Objects`
- **`Fledermaus Objects` List**: A lower pane listing four `.sd` files in the project directory, with a `?` help button to its right:
  - `gutmtest.sd`
  - `stelwagenCRM3secUTM.sd`
  - `stelwagenCRMtry2.sd`
  - `WesternGoM.sd`

## Right Surface Viewport
- **Top Bar**: Contains a `Reset View` button, a `Position:` readout field displaying `Geo Coords (x,y,z) -> (1470901.62, 4461833.00, 5834.77)`, and a cursor-mode icon button on the far right.
- **Shaded Relief Surface**:
  - Covers the continental shelf, slope, and abyssal plain off the coast of New England / Gulf of Maine and Georges Bank.
  - **Upper Left to Center (Land & Continental Shelf)**: Colored in bright yellow and yellow-green with rougher, higher-relief topography in the upper left (orange-tinted hills) and smoother banks and channels across the continental shelf (including Georges Bank in the lower center-right).
  - **Lower Right (Continental Slope & Deep Basin)**: Incised submarine canyons cut into the shelf edge, transitioning sharply from green to cyan, deep blue, and magenta/purple in the deep Northwest Atlantic basin.
