# DMagic File Menu: Export Surface (`utm2ll05.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot of the **DMagic** (Fledermaus Data Magic) application menu bar and main window on Mac OS X, demonstrating how to export the loaded UTM surface by selecting `File -> Export Surface...`.

## Interface & Menu Details
- **Mac OS X Menu Bar**: At the top, featuring the blue Apple logo, bold `dmagic` application menu, highlighted **`File`** menu (in white text on a blue background), `View`, `Tools`, and `Help`.
- **Open `File` Dropdown Menu**:
  - `Open Project...` (shortcut `⌘O`)
  - `Import Gridded Data...`
  - `Import Ungridded Data...`
  - `Import From SD/Scene...`
  - `Import Imagery` (with a right-facing submenu arrow `►`)
  - **`Export Surface...`** (highlighted in blue with white text as the active selection)
  - `Screen Capture` (with a right-facing submenu arrow `►`)
  - `Recent Projects` (with a right-facing submenu arrow `►`)

## Background Application Window
- **Window Controls & Left Pane**: Shows the red, yellow, and green Mac window buttons and the `Data Components` table with columns `Type` and `File Name`, listing three loaded components partially obscured by the semi-transparent menu dropdown:
  - `Hgt` — `gutmtest.dtm` (selected in light blue)
  - `Geo` — `gutmtest.geo`
  - `Col` — `gutmtest.shade`
  - A `<<` transfer button is visible to the right of the list below the menu.
- **Right Viewport**: Partially visible on the right side, showing the `Reset View` button, the start of the `Position:` label (`Po`), and the upper-left corner of the yellow and orange color-shaded relief surface against a black background.
