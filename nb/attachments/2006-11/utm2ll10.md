# UTM Grid Zones of the World Map (`utm2ll10.png`)

## Overview
A web browser screenshot displaying the webpage `http://www.dmap.co.uk/utmworld.htm` titled "UTM Grid Zones of the World compiled by Alan Morton", showing the Universal Transverse Mercator (UTM) longitudinal zone numbers and latitudinal bands overlaid on a map of North and Central America.

## Interface & Map Details
- **Browser Toolbar**: At the top is a browser navigation bar featuring a green left back arrow, a disabled light-gray right forward arrow, a blue circular reload arrow, a disabled stop `X` button, a house (home) icon, and an address bar containing a purple globe/map favicon and the URL `http://www.dmap.co.uk/utmworld.htm`.
- **Page Header**: Below the toolbar on a pale mint-green background is the bold serif heading `UTM Grid Zones of the World` followed in slightly smaller bold serif text by `compiled by Alan Morto` (cropped at the right edge).
- **Map & Grid**:
  - A white background map with blue outlines depicting coastlines, islands, major lakes/rivers, US state boundaries, and Canadian provincial/territorial borders across North America, Central America, Greenland, and the northern tip of South America.
  - A black rectangular grid divides the map into vertical UTM longitudinal zones and horizontal latitude bands.
  - **Vertical Axis (Latitude Bands)**: Labeled along the left margin from top to bottom at 8-degree intervals (except band `X` which spans 12 degrees from `72N` to `84N`):
