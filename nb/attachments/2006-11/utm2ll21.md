# DMagic Geographic Coordinate Readout (`utm2ll21.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot of the coordinate status bar in IVS 3D DMagic confirming that the exported DTM/Geo surface is now in geographic coordinates (longitude, latitude, and Z value).

## Interface & Visible Text
- Set against a medium-gray status bar with white borders above and a black bar below.
- Displays white sans-serif text reading:
  - `-> (-59.87101364, 41.17904663, 9472.94)`
- Here `-59.87101364` represents longitude in decimal degrees West, `41.17904663` represents latitude in decimal degrees North, and `9472.94` represents the elevation/depth scalar value.
