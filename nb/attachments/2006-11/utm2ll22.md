# 3D Bathymetric Surface in Fledermaus Showing Aliasing Artifacts (`utm2ll22.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from IVS 3D Fledermaus showing a 3D perspective rendering of the reprojected bathymetric/topographic `.sd` surface off the New England / Nova Scotia continental margin, illustrating visible striping and aliasing artifacts across the slope.

## Scene & Plot Details
- **3D Terrain / Bathymetry Surface**:
  - Viewed looking roughly northward/northwestward from the deeper ocean basin up across the continental slope onto the flat continental shelf.
  - Color-coded by elevation/depth: cyan and teal in the foreground (deepest visible slope), transitioning upslope through bright green and chartreuse to a broad, flat yellow plateau (the continental shelf), with orange and reddish-pink higher-elevation terrain along the distant horizon against a black background.
  - Prominent diagonal linear striping and jagged grid-aliasing artifacts are visible across the green and cyan continental slope in the foreground.
