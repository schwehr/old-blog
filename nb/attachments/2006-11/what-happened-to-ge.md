# Google Earth Map of Boston with Alternate Japanese Placename (`what-happened-to-ge.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot of Google Earth centered on eastern Massachusetts and Boston, illustrating what happens when the alternate/localized placenames layer is inadvertently enabled, displaying Boston in both English and Japanese katakana (`(ボストン)`).

## Map Details
- **Geography & Imagery**: Satellite imagery of eastern Massachusetts in muted olive-brown tones, bounded by white state border lines to the north (New Hampshire) and south (Rhode Island/Connecticut) and bright yellow coastline outlines along Massachusetts Bay and Cape Cod Bay (with the hook of Provincetown / Cape Cod visible at bottom right) against dark blue ocean water.
- **Road Network**: Semi-transparent yellow-gold highway lines crisscross the region, including the I-95 and I-495 beltways and the Massachusetts Turnpike running east-west, marked on the left with a red, white, and blue Interstate shield for `90`.
- **Placemark & Labels**:
  - A dark red circular city marker with a white border is positioned at Boston.
  - To the right of the marker in bold white sans-serif text with a dark drop shadow is `Boston`.
  - To the left of the marker in matching bold white text is the parenthetical Japanese katakana label `(ボストン)`.
