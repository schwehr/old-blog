# Historical Encyclopedia Engraving of Geography, Hydrography, and Navigation (`wikipedia_hydrography.jpg`)

## Overview
A grayscale reproduction of a historical 18th-century copperplate engraving (from Ephraim Chambers' *Cyclopaedia, or an Universal Dictionary of Arts and Sciences*, 1728, retrieved via Wikipedia's "Hydrography" article) titled `TAB. GEOGRAPHY & HYDROGRAPHY.` and `NAVIGATION`, illustrating map projections, spherical geometry, and maritime navigational instruments and charts.

## Engraving Layout & Diagrams
- **Top Section (`TAB. GEOGRAPHY & HYDROGRAPHY.`)**:
  - **Map Projections (`Fig. 1. Map`, `Fig. 2. Map`, `Fig. 3. Map`)**: Three large circular globe projection diagrams across the top row showing polar concentric circles with radial meridians (left), an equatorial/stereographic hemisphere projection grid of parallels and meridians (center), and an oblique stereographic projection construction (right).
  - **Geometric & Horizon Diagrams (`Fig. 4` to `Fig. 9`)**: Smaller circular and geometric diagrams beneath the globes illustrating horizon, meridian, climate, and spherical projection geometry.
- **Middle & Lower Section (`NAVIGATION`)**:
  - Divided from the upper section by a horizontal rule labeled `NAVIGATION` in italicized capital letters.
  - **Compasses**: Shaded perspective engravings on the left showing a cylindrical mariner's compass (`Fig. 1. Compass`) and a gimbaled box azimuth compass (`Fig. 2. Azimuth Compass`).
  - **Sailing & Charting Diagrams**: Geometric triangles, rhumb-line courses, plane chart grids, and a small coastal map enclosed in a latitude/longitude border (`Mercator's Chart`).
  - **Navigational Instruments**:
    - On the middle left, a Davis quadrant / backstaff (`Backstaff`) and below it a circular `Steering Wheel` and `Logboard`.
    - In the lower center, a circular `Nocturnal` with a central sighting hole, toothed calendar/hour rings, and handle/index arm.
    - On the middle right, a `Forestaff` (cross-staff) with multiple transversal vanes.
    - In the bottom right corner, a gridded `Sinical Quadrant` and a circular `Astrolabe`.
    - In the bottom left and center, trigonometric course, latitude, departure, and `Rhumb Line` sailing triangles and sector diagrams.
