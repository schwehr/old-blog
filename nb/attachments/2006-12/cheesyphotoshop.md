# Photoshop Tutorial Lightning Bolts Over Dover Christmas Parade Float (`cheesyphotoshop.png`)

## Overview
A humorous composite photograph created while practicing a Photoshop tutorial from Bert Monroy's *PixelPerfect* series (Revision3), showing glowing blue-white lightning bolts radiating outward from a ball held by a performer dressed in a top hat and tails standing beside a decorative brick chimney on a Dover, New Hampshire Christmas parade float.

## Scene & Visual Effects
- **Foreground Subject (Parade Float)**:
  - A person dressed in black Victorian/Dickensian attire (a black top hat, black tailcoat, dark trousers, and black shoes, with a red scarf) stands in profile facing right atop an aluminum extension ladder rack mounted on the roof of a red vehicle/float.
  - Next to the person is a faux red-brick chimney prop decorated with white icicle trim along its top edge and a large green pine Christmas wreath with a bright red bow hanging on its side.
  - The performer holds a round sphere (basketball/globe) in front of their face.
- **Photoshop Lightning Effect**:
  - Seven jagged, glowing electric-blue and white lightning bolts radiate outward from the center of the sphere toward the top, left, right, and bottom corners of the frame.
- **Background**:
  - Behind the float is a large historic two-story red-brick building with a steep dark-green shingled gable roof, a small dormer window on the left, arched window tops, and bare late-autumn tree branches on the far right under a pale overcast sky.
  - Framed by a thin white inner border and black outer border.
