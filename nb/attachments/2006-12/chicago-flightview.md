# Boingo Wireless FlightView Flight Tracker for Chicago (`chicago-flightview.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the "Flight Finder" web widget provided on the Boingo Wireless captive portal page at Chicago O'Hare International Airport on December 31, 2006, displaying a FlightView radar and flight-tracking map for American Airlines flight `AA2276` from Phoenix to Chicago along with flight schedule details for a connecting flight from Chicago to Boston.

## Interface & Map Details
- **Header Banner**: White top box containing the `BOINGO WIRELESS` logo (red `BOINGO` text, blue `WIRELESS` text, and blue swooshing signal arcs) on the left and the red title `Flight Finder` in the center.
- **FlightView Map**:
  - A regional map of the United States and northern Mexico with tan/brown land, dark blue oceans and Great Lakes, and black state/international borders.
  - **Flight Path**: A white line connects circular bullseye markers at `PHOENIX` (southwest) and `CHICAGO` (northeast). A white airplane icon is positioned mid-route over the central Plains (near the Kansas/Oklahoma/Texas panhandle region) with a white callout tag reading `AA2276`.
  - **Weather Radar Overlay**: A large band of precipitation stretches from Lake Superior and Minnesota southward through Illinois, the Mississippi Valley, and the Gulf Coast to Texas/Louisiana, rendered in light blue (snow/winter precipitation on the western edge) and dark/light green with yellow embeds (rain/storms).Scattered light-blue patches also appear over the western states.
  - **Map Footer**: A white timestamp bar at bottom left reads `Sun Dec 31 10:42 EST`, and the `FlightView` logo (with an orange airplane/swirl emblem) is at bottom right.
- **Flight Status Table & Footer**:
  - Below the map is a tabular readout for the next flight leg:
    - `Airport`: `CHICAGO,IL` | `BOSTON,MA`
    - `Scheduled Time`: `11 :55 am` | `3 :10 pm`
    - `Actual` (blank)
    - `Status`: `Scheduled`
    - `Equipment`: `M80`
    - `Map`: `not available`
  - At the bottom is the `Powered by FlightView®` logo and copyright/attribution text linking to `RLM Software, Inc.` and `please contact us`.
