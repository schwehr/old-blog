# Early Morning View Over Dover, New Hampshire (`dover-early-morning.png`)

## Overview
A wide-angle morning photograph taken from the observation tower on Garrison Hill above Dover, New Hampshire on December 17, 2006, looking out over leafless winter treetops toward the town's residential neighborhoods and red-brick downtown mill/church buildings bathed in cool morning light.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**: A wooded hillside in the lower third of the frame filled with bare, gray-brown deciduous trees and brush, interspersed with several dark green pine and evergreen trees.
- **Midground (Town of Dover)**: Beyond the trees lies a dense New England townscape of white and light-colored multi-story wooden houses with pitched roofs, punctuated on the left and center-left by larger historic red-brick mill and civic buildings, church steeples, and towers.
- **Background & Horizon**: Forested rolling terrain stretches into the distance under a cool, hazy blue-tinted morning atmosphere and a bright pale horizon strip along the top edge.
- **Framing**: Enclosed in a thin gray inner border and black outer border.
