# Sunrise Sky From the Tower Above Dover, New Hampshire (`dover-sunrise.png`)

## Overview
A wide panoramic photograph taken at dawn on December 17, 2006 from the tower above Dover, New Hampshire, capturing a vibrant pink, lavender, orange, and gold sunrise sky above a dark silhouetted horizon.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Clouds**: Occupying the upper four-fifths of the frame, layered stratocumulus and cirrus clouds glow in a rich gradient of colors: deep violet-blue and soft lavender-pink across the upper sky, warm salmon and rose-pink through the middle, and brilliant golden-yellow and fiery orange-red along the horizon where the sun is just about to rise slightly left of center.
- **Horizon & Foreground Silhouette**: The lower fifth of the image is a nearly black silhouette of the distant undulating horizon line (with a low rounded hill peaking just left of the brightest solar glow), a few bare tree branches silhouetted on the far right edge, and faint glints of light or reflective roofs/water in the dark valley below.
- **Framing**: Surrounded by a thin gray inner border and black outer border.
