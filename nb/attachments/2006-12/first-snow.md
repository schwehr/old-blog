# First Sticking Snowfall on Detached Garage (`first-snow.png`)

## Overview
A vertical outdoor photograph taken on the morning of December 8, 2006 in New Hampshire, showing the season's first accumulating snowfall blanketing the hipped roof of a sage-green two-car garage and the surrounding yard and woods.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Garage**: Viewed from an elevated second-story window looking down at a detached two-car garage painted muted sage green with white trim. The garage has two side-by-side multi-panel doors, each featuring three vertical sections and three upper 4-pane windows (six windows total), flanked by black coach-style wall lanterns. Its pyramidal/hipped roof is covered in a smooth, fresh layer of white snow, with a vertical metal stove/vent pipe rising from the rear slope.
- **Yard & Driveway**: A light coating of white snow covers the driveway apron in front of the garage doors, the narrow walkway along the right side of the garage, and the grassy lawn to the left and right.
- **Trees & Background**: Bare deciduous tree branches frame the right foreground, while two tall dark-green conifer/spruce trees and a dense stand of leafless winter woods rise behind the garage against a pale, overcast gray-blue winter sky. Neighboring houses are faintly visible through the trees on the right.
- **Framing**: Enclosed in a thin white inner border and black outer border.
