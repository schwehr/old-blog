# Overhead View of LIDAR Road Points in Fledermaus (`fleder2ge-road-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from IVS 3D Fledermaus showing a top-down (plan-view) 3D point-cloud visualization of airborne LIDAR altimetry points along a road segment, after surrounding non-road points have been flagged/hidden during a workflow to extract a 3D road polygon for Google Earth.

## Plot & Interface Details
- **Background Grid**: A fine, bright green Cartesian grid over a gray background fills the viewport.
- **LIDAR Point Cloud (Road Swath)**:
  - A wide diagonal band of colored square points runs from the upper left to the lower right, representing the main road surface. Points are colored by elevation from deep blue and cyan on the left to blue-green and bright green toward the lower right, with a few isolated red/orange high-elevation outlier points along its southern edge.
  - To the right of the main swath, a secondary shorter segment of road/ramp branches off toward the right edge, colored in green, yellow, and orange.
- **3D Navigation Controls & Scale Axis**:
  - On the left is a 3D metallic gray vertical pitch/tilt wheel control with white up/down arrows and a central slider.
  - Near the center is a circular 3D trackball rotation ring surrounding a shaded metallic sphere at the origin.
  - A horizontal white scale bar with tick marks extends left and right from the center sphere, labeled in white serif text: `-40.0m`, `-20.0m`, `20.0m`, and `40.0m`.
