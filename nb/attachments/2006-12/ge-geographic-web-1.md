# Google Earth Geographic Web Layers Panel (`ge-geographic-web-1.png`)

## Overview
A close-up screenshot of the **Layers** tree panel in **Google Earth** (Mac OS X interface), highlighting the newly introduced **Geographic Web** folder and its sub-layers.

## Interface & Layer Hierarchy
The tree view shows the expanded hierarchy under `Primary Database`:
- **`Primary Database`** (expanded with a minus `-` checkbox and stacked-layers icon):
  - **`Terrain`** (unchecked checkbox, grey folder icon)
  - **`Geographic Web`** (checked blue checkbox, green five-pointed star icon, followed by a red superscript **`New!`** badge, highlighted with a light beige selection bar):
    - **`Best of Google Earth Commu...`** (checked blue checkbox, yellow-and-white information `i` icon)
    - **`Panoramio`** (checked blue checkbox, blue circle icon containing a white four-pointed star)
    - **`Wikipedia`** (checked blue checkbox, grey-and-white Wikipedia puzzle globe icon)
