# Hiram M. Chittenden Locks (Seattle Locks) and Bascule Bridge (`ge-geographic-web-4.png`)

## Overview
A daytime color photograph of the **Hiram M. Chittenden Locks** (Ballard Locks) in Seattle, Washington, showing the canal lock walls and the raised Salmon Bay steel bascule railroad bridge in the background. This is the test photograph uploaded by Kurt Schwehr to Panoramio.

## Scene Description
- **Waterway & Lock Walls:**
  - Calm blue water of the Lake Washington Ship Canal fills the lower left and center of the frame.
  - A concrete lock wall runs along the bottom left corner with yellow-painted mooring bollard bases.
  - A massive stepped concrete lock wall with metal safety railings and light poles occupies the right foreground, curving away toward the middle distance.
  - A central concrete pier extends diagonally into the water in the middle ground, topped with grass, light poles, and a yellow sign.
- **Railroad Bridge:**
  - Spanning horizontally across the canal in the background is a dark green/black steel through-truss railroad bridge (the Salmon Bay Bridge).
  - On the right side of the span, the heavy steel bascule leaf and its large overhead counterweight truss are raised at an angle, with a small white operator's house mounted on the pier below.
- **Background & Sky:**
  - Beyond the bridge across the water, residential houses and green trees line the hillside of Magnolia/Ballard.
  - Bright autumn foliage in orange and green appears on the far right edge behind a tall metal double-headed streetlamp pole against a clear blue sky.
