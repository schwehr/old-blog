# Panoramio Photo Upload and Geotagging Web Interface (`ge-geographic-web-5.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Panoramio** web interface (`panoramio.com`) for uploading and geotagging a photograph using an embedded Google Maps Hybrid view of the Hiram M. Chittenden Locks in Seattle, Washington.

## Left Column: Upload Controls & Thumbnail
- **Header:** A tilted stack of photo thumbnails icon next to large orange bold text: `Upload your photos`.
- **File Selection Form:**
  - Label: `Select photo to upload:`
  - A blank text input box followed by `Browse...` and `Finish` buttons.
- **Uploaded Photo Status Box:**
  - A light blue-grey panel containing a square thumbnail preview of the Seattle Locks photograph on the left.
  - `Title:` text input box containing `Seattle Locks`.
  - Status text in grey: `Uploaded successfully!`
  - A beige rounded button with the blue Panoramio star icon reading: **Map this photo** / *(It doesn't stop uploads)*.

## Right Column: Interactive Map Geotagger
- **Header Text:**
  - `Location: Seattle` [`[change it]`](#)
  - `Click on the map or drag the marker`
- **Google Maps Viewport:**
  - Displays a high-resolution satellite view (`Hybrid` mode selected among `Map`, `Satellite`, `Hybrid` buttons at top right) of the Ballard Locks in Seattle, showing the lock chambers, spillway dam with white foamy water below, and a white boat inside the upper lock chamber.
  - Standard pan directional arrows (`↑`, `↓`, `←`, `→`) and zoom (`+`, `-`) buttons appear in the upper left corner.
  - An orange-pink Google Maps marker pin with a black center dot is positioned on the north lock wall, just above a small grey `X` crosshair on the concrete wall.
  - Bottom attribution: `POWERED BY Google` logo on the left and `Map data ©2006 TeleAtlas - Terms of Use` on the right.
- **Footer Controls:**
  - A `Save position` button in the center and a `Cancel mapping (it can be done later)` link on the right.
