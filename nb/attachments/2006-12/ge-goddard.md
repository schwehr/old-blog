# NASA Goddard SVS Surface Pressure Animation in Google Earth (`ge-goddard.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** displaying a global atmospheric **Surface Pressure** time-series overlay from the **NASA Goddard Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS)** Web Map Service (WMS), centered over North America.

## Globe & Data Visualization
- **Globe Orientation:** Full-globe view centered on North America, bounded by yellow coastline and international border outlines against a black space background with a glowing blue atmospheric halo.
- **Surface Pressure Color Overlay:**
  - A false-color raster layer covers both continents and oceans, illustrating atmospheric surface pressure variations due to elevation and weather systems.
  - High-elevation regions—including the Rocky Mountains, Sierra Madre, and Greenland ice sheet—stand out in bright green, yellow, and orange tones (lower surface pressure, roughly 650–850 hPa).
  - Lower-elevation plains, basins, and ocean surfaces are shaded in deep red, magenta, and purple tones (higher surface pressure, roughly 950–1050 hPa).
  - Subtle concentric purple/magenta low-pressure depressions are visible over the North Atlantic and Caribbean/Atlantic Hurricane basin.

## Legend, Timeline & UI Elements
- **Time Slider (Top Center-Right):**
  - A Google Earth timeline control bar with a clock icon, play button, and range markers displaying the timestamp:
    - `31 AUG 2004`
    - `10:00pm`
- **Color Scale Legend (Bottom Right):**
  - Title: `Surface Pressure`
  - Horizontal color bar spanning from white/violet on the left (`450`) through blue (`550`), cyan (`650`), green (`750`), yellow (`850`), orange/red (`950`), to magenta/pink on the right (`1050`).
  - Units label below: `hectopascals (millibars)`.
