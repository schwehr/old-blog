# Reading e-Science Centre GODIVA2 Ocean Data Portal (`ge-godiva2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **GODIVA2** web portal developed by the **Reading e-Science Centre**, demonstrating interactive visualization of global ocean general circulation model data (`sea_water_potential_temperature`) overlaid on a Google Map with export links to Google Earth.
