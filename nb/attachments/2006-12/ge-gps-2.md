# Global View of GPS Satellite Orbits and Coverage Rays in Google Earth (`ge-gps-2.png`)

## Overview
A zoomed-out **Google Earth** globe view centered on North America, showing a dense web of grey coverage rays and thick red vertical/radial lines extending into space toward medium-Earth-orbit (MEO) GPS satellites.

## Visualization Details
- **Earth Globe:** Centered on the United States, Canada, Mexico, and northern South America, with yellow continental coastlines, national borders, and white-capped Greenland in the upper right.
- **Coverage Cones & Grid Lines:**
  - Numerous fine semi-transparent grey lines fan out from high-altitude GPS satellites off-screen, forming intersecting geometric cones that drape across the globe's surface and extend into space on the left side of the Earth.
- **Satellite Position Vectors:**
  - Thick bright-red radial lines project outward from points on the Earth's surface (in Alaska, the central US, the eastern Pacific, the North Atlantic, South America, and the Southern Ocean) straight out into space toward the orbital positions of the GPS constellation.
