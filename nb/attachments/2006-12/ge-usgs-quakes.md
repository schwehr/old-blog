# USGS Earthquakes in Google Earth (`ge-usgs-quakes.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth displaying recent earthquake data and tectonic plate boundaries over California and western Nevada, provided via a USGS (United States Geological Survey) KML/KMZ network link feed.

## Legend & Overlay Controls
On the left side of the viewport, a semi-transparent gray overlay panel with rounded corners displays the legend and attribution:
- **Header Logo**: "DATA BY" in white uppercase text above a light blue and white "USGS" logo banner with the agency's wave emblem.
- **Magnitude**: Depicts 8 white-outlined concentric/stacked circles graduated by size and labeled vertically from `8` (largest, at the top) down to `3` inside the circles, with `2` and `1` labeled to the right of the two smallest circles at the bottom.
- **Age**: Color-coded circular markers indicating how recently each earthquake occurred:
  - Red circle: `Past hour`
  - Orange circle: `Past day`
  - Yellow circle: `Past week`
- **Plates**: Tectonic plate boundary symbology:
  - Red horizontal line segment: `Boundaries`
  - White-outlined right-pointing arrow: `Convergence`

## Map & Earthquake Details
- **Geographic Extent**: Satellite imagery of California (labeled `California` in white/light-blue text with a drop shadow near the center-right) and western Nevada, bordered by the dark blue Pacific Ocean on the left and light gray/lavender state boundary lines for California, Nevada, and Oregon. The coastline and San Francisco Bay are outlined in yellow.
- **Fault / Plate Boundaries**: A reddish-brown tectonic plate boundary line (the San Andreas Fault system) runs northwest–southeast along the California coast and inland through the Coast Ranges.
- **Seismic Activity**: Numerous yellow and orange circular markers are plotted across the region, representing earthquakes from the past week and past day:
  - A dense linear cluster of yellow and orange markers traces the San Andreas and Calaveras/Hayward fault zones from the San Francisco Bay Area southeast toward central California.
  - Around the San Francisco Bay Area, a larger orange circle (with a smaller yellow dot inside) sits near the East Bay/Peninsula, accompanied by another orange circle further east.
  - A prominent large yellow circle (indicating a higher-magnitude quake from the past week) is located in the eastern California / western Nevada border region (near Mammoth Lakes / Walker Lane), surrounded by smaller yellow and orange circles.
  - Additional scattered clusters of yellow and orange circles appear in Northern California (mendocino/Shasta region) and down through the Owens Valley / Mojave Desert region in thesoutheast corner.
