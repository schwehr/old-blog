# GeoMapApp LDEO Sediment Cores in Google Earth (`geomapapp-cores.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth displaying the Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory (LDEO) sediment core database (`ldeo_sediment_cores.kmz` from GeoMapApp) plotted over the eastern United States and western North Atlantic Ocean, with an expanded placemark cluster and an open information balloon for core `KZ81-11`.

## Map & Placemark Details
- **Globe View**: Shows the curvature of the Earth at the upper left against black space, covering eastern North America (including the Great Lakes outlined in yellow, the U.S. East Coast, Florida, and the Gulf of Mexico) and the western North Atlantic Ocean with shaded bathymetry.
- **Core Locations**: Hundreds of bright yellow square icons cover the Atlantic Ocean and Gulf of Mexico, representing sediment core sampling stations.
- **Expanded Cluster**: Off the Mid-Atlantic coast (delmarva / Chesapeake Bay margin), clicking a dense stack of overlapping placemarks has triggered Google Earth's spiderfy/fan-out view, radiating dozens of yellow square icons outward on white leader lines over a darkened circular shadow footprint.
