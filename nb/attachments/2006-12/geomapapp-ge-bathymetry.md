# GeoMapApp Global Bathymetry Overlay in Google Earth (`geomapapp-ge-bathymetry.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the full Earth globe in Google Earth overlaid with the GeoMapApp global bathymetry and elevation layer (`GMA4GE.kml`), centered on Australia, Southeast Asia, and the southwestern Pacific and eastern Indian Oceans.

## Globe & Bathymetry Details
- **View Orientation**: A full-disk perspective of the globe set against a black background with faint white stars, centered on the Australian continent.
- **Color Palette & Relief**:
  - **Landmasses**: Covered in a muted, semi-opaque green-tan elevation tint from the GeoMapApp raster overlay, with yellow vector coastline and international border outlines (visible around Australia, Tasmania, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Southeast Asia).
  - **Continental Shelves & Shallow plateaus**: Rendered in light gray and pale off-white tones (prominent across the Sunda Shelf, Sahul Shelf/Arafura Sea between Australia and New Guinea, and the Campbell Plateau/Lord Howe Rise around New Zealand).
  - **Ocean Basins & Ridges**: Deep ocean basins appear in shades of medium to dark blue-violet, while mid-ocean ridges (such as the Southeast Indian Ridge south of Australia) and submarine plateaus are highlighted in warm pinkish-tan, cyan, and light blue shaded relief.
  - **Tectonic Trenches & Island Arcs**: Sharp bathymetric relief defines the Java (Sunda) Trench, the Philippine Trench, the Mariana Trench, and the Tonga-Kermadec Trench system east of New Zealand.
- **South Pole**: At the bottom edge of the globe, Antarctica is outlined in yellow with a circular gray and white polar cap hole where the cylindrical map projection stops near the South Pole.
