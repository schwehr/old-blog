# GeoMapApp PetDB Petrology Data in Google Earth (`geomapapp-petrology.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth showing georeferenced marine petrology rock sample stations (`Endeavor_Petrology.kml` from GeoMapApp / PetDB) overlaid on shaded-relief seafloor bathymetry along the Endeavour segment of the Juan de Fuca Ridge in the northeast Pacific Ocean.

## Seafloor Map & Sample Markers
- **Bathymetric Base**: High-resolution shaded-relief bathymetry in tan and gray-brown tones showing linear, northeast–southwest-trending abyssal hills, fault scarps, and axial ridge topography.
- **Sample Icons**: Circular target/donut markers (black outer rings with either bright red, gray, or white interiors and a central black dot) mark individual rock sampling stations.
- **Expanded Cluster**: Near the center, a tightly grouped cluster of stations has been clicked to fan out on thin white (and one red) leader lines, displaying multiple red circular markers and labeled white text strings with dark drop shadows:
