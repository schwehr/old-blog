# Iron-Stained Orange Creek in Woods (`geotag-sml-DSCN4932.JPG`)

## Overview
A vertical outdoor photograph of a narrow woodland stream with vivid rust-orange water and streambed sediment, used by Kurt Schwehr to test geocoding digital photos with the Mac utility `Geotagger` and exporting them to Google Earth via `iPhotoToGoogleEarth`.

## Scene Description
- **The Stream**: A small creek winds diagonally from the upper center toward the bottom left corner. The water and underlying streambed are brightly colored in opaque, rusty orange-ochre tones—characteristic of iron-oxidizing bacteria or iron-rich groundwater seepage.
- **Streambanks & Rocks**:
  - Along the right bank, several angular rocks covered in bright green moss protrude from the muddy edge and shallow water, accompanied by a couple of pale fallen leaves resting on the wet rocks.
  - The left bank is slightly undercut and lined with brown fallen autumn leaves, small tufts of green grass/ferns near the water's edge, and a decaying branch extending over the water.
- **Surrounding Woods**: Both banks are blanketed in thick layers of dry brown fallen leaves and bare, leafless twigs and saplings, indicating late autumn or early winter in a deciduous forest.
