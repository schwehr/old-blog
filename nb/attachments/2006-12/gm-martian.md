# MARs Tools for Interactive ANalysis (MARTIAN) Web Interface (`gm-martian.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **MARs Tools for Interactive ANalysis (MARTIAN)** web application, displaying mapped geological normal faults by Martian epoch and modeled gravitational potential energy (GPE) stress vectors overlaid on a shaded-relief topographic map of the Tharsis volcanic province on Mars.

## Left Sidebar & Controls
Set against a pale cream background below the maroon serif title header `MARs Tools for` (partially cropped at right):
- **Navigation Links** (maroon underlined text):
  - `Table of Contents`
  - `References and Credits`
  - `+Background Map`
- **`-Normal Faults` Section**: Five checkboxes with color-coded square swatches indicating fault age categories (all five checked):
  - Green swatch: `Middle/Late Amazonian`
  - Teal/blue-gray swatch: `Late Hesperian/Early Amazonian`
  - Dark blue swatch: `Early Hesperian`
  - Purple swatch: `Late Noachian/Early Hesperian`
  - Brownish-red swatch: `Noachian`
- **`-Stress Solutions` Section**: Radio buttons for toggling modeled stress fields:
  - Selected radio button: `GPE Stress Solution`
  - Sub-option radio buttons (unselected): `Misfit: Full` and `Misfit: Pre-Existing`
- **`+Locations`**: Expandable section link at the bottom.

## Interactive Mars Map
- **Map Controls**: Google Maps-style pan arrows (up, down, left, right, and center return button) and a vertical zoom slider (`+` to `-`) in the upper left corner of the map canvas.
- **Topographic Base**: A MOLA (Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter) shaded-relief elevation map centered on the Tharsis bulge, showing Alba Patera to the north, Olympus Mons to the west, the three Tharsis Montes volcanoes aligned diagonally near the center, and Valles Marineris extending to the southeast.
- **Fault Traces**: Dense networks of colored line segments (teal, dark blue, brown, purple, and green) trace grabens and normal faults radiating from the Tharsis rise.
- **Stress Tensor Grid**: A regular grid of crossed principal stress axes covers the map: black crosses dominate the northern plains and flanks, while red crosses (representing strong extensional/compressional deviatoric stresses) concentrate over the high-elevation Tharsis dome and Olympus Mons.
