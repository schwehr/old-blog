# Google Mars Spacecraft Search Results (`google-mars.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Google Mars** (`www.google.com/mars/`) web interface showing search results for `spacecraft` plotted on a color-coded MOLA shaded-relief elevation map of Mars, with an information balloon open for the **MER Opportunity Rover (USA)**.

## Header & Search Bar
- **Logo**: The `Google Mars LABS` logo at the top left, featuring a green Erlenmeyer flask replacing the letter `l`.
- **Category Links**: Three columns of blue underlined quick-search links next to the logo:
  - Column 1 (highlighted in a light lavender-gray rounded box): `Regions`, `Spacecraft`, `Stories`
  - Column 2: `Mountains`, `Canyons`, `Dunes`
  - Column 3: `Plains`, `Ridges`, `Craters`
- **Search Input**: A text box containing `spacecraft` next to a `Search` button.
- **Sub-header Bar**: A pale yellow horizontal strip with a chain-link icon and `Link to this page`.

## Left Sidebar (`Search results for spacecraft`)
Displays `Found 11 matches` with links to `Limit results to current region` and `glossary`, followed by a vertical list of yellow map pin icons and mission summaries:
1. **Mars 2 Lander (USSR)**: `Failed during descent. First man-made object on Mars.`
2. **Mars 3 Lander (USSR)**: `First successful landing on Mars, sent signal for 20 seconds after landing.`
3. **Mars 6 Lander (USSR)**: `Failed, contact lost just before touchdown.`
4. **Viking 1 Lander (USA)**: `Landed successfully, operated for more than 6 years. Returned extensive science results.`
5. **Viking 2 Lander (USA)**: `Landed successfully, operated for more than 3.5 years. Returned extensive science results.`
6. **Mars Pathfinder Rover (USA)**: `Landed successfully, operated for just under 3 months.`

## Map View & Opportunity Rover Balloon
- **Map Canvas**: Displays the Chryse Planitia, Ares Vallis, and Meridiani Planum region of Mars in false-color elevation (blue/cyan lowlands in the north transitioning to green, yellow, and orange cratered highlands to the south). Standard Google Maps pan arrows and a vertical zoom slider appear at the upper left.
- **Markers**: Three yellow pin markers are visible on the map: one on the left (Viking 1 in Chryse Planitia), one near the center (Mars Pathfinder in Ares Vallis), and one at the lower right in Meridiani Planum.
- **Information Balloon**: Anchored to the lower-right pin (with a dark drop shadow on the map) is a white pop-up card displaying:
  - **Spacecraft:** `MER Opportunity Rover (USA)`
  - **Location:** `1.95S, 5.53W`
  - **Launched:** `2003`
  - **Results:** `Landed successfully in January of 2004, and still running.`
  - **Thumbnail Image**: A color artist's rendering on the right showing the Mars Exploration Rover on the reddish Martian surface, framed by a blue border.
