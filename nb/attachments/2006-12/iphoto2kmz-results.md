# iPhotoToGoogleEarth Geotagged Photo Balloon in Google Earth (`iphoto2kmz-results.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth displaying the result of exporting a geocoded photo from iPhoto to a KMZ file (`SmallCreek.kmz`) using the Mac utilities `Geotagger` and `iPhotoToGoogleEarth`, showing a yellow pushpin and an open pop-up balloon containing the creek photograph `geotag-sml-DSCN4932`.

## Map & Placemark Details
- **Background Satellite Imagery**: Low-resolution satellite imagery of a wooded and built-up area (visible along the left and right edges behind the pop-up balloon) with dark green trees and white/gray building roofs and roads.
- **Placemark**: On the left side of the image, a yellow pushpin icon is anchored to the satellite map next to the white label `geotag...` (partially obscured by the balloon tail).

## Information Balloon (`geotag-sml-DSCN4932`)
Occupying most of the frame is a white Google Earth pop-up balloon with a close (`x`) button in the upper right corner:
- **Title**: `geotag-sml-DSCN4932` in bold black sans-serif text.
- **Embedded Image**: A pixelated/low-resolution vertical preview of `geotag-sml-DSCN4932.JPG`, showing the bright rust-orange iron-stained stream winding through brown autumn leaves and mossy green rocks on the right bank.
- **Footer Links**: `Directions: To here - From here` at the bottom left.
