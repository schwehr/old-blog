# iSight Camera Capture Through Office Window (`isightcapture.png`)

## Overview
A 640×480 webcam photograph captured from an Apple iSight FireWire camera using the Mac OS X command-line tool `isightcapture` (`isightcapture -t png image.png`), looking out through Kurt Schwehr's office window into a wooded area.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Midground**: A sloping bank of tall, dry, brownish-green late-autumn/winter weeds, brush, and tall grasses fills the lower half of the frame, with a patch of greener grass visible on the right slope.
- **Trees & Canopy**: Behind the brush stands a dense line of tall pine and bare deciduous trees. Dark green pine needles and vertical tree trunks dominate the upper left and center-right, while bare, curving branches frame the upper right corner.
- **Sky & Lighting**: The overcast sky behind the tree canopy is blown out to bright white. Because the photograph was taken through an indoor office window without a lens hood, there is a slight soft haze and faint glass reflection across the upper center of the image.
