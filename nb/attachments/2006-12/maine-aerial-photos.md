# Maine Office of GIS Aerial Photography Viewer (`maine-aerial-photos.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Maine Aerial Photography** web GIS viewer (`http://megisims.state.me.us/website/orthomap/viewer.htm`) running in the Mac OS X Safari browser, displaying high-resolution color orthoimagery of Kittery and Eliot, Maine, alongside grayscale aerial imagery covering Portsmouth and Newington, New Hampshire, across the Piscataqua River.

## Browser Interface
- **Window Title & Address Bar**: Safari window titled `Maine Aerial Photography` with standard back/forward/reload/stop/home buttons, the URL `http://megisims.state.me.us/website/orthomap/viewer.htm`, and a Google search box containing `maine gis geotiff`.
- **Bookmarks Bar**: Links for `C++Anno`, `CS 757/867`, `nsf.gov - Office of C...`, `U.S. Hydro Interim W...`, `Safari`, `UNH/USNH Controlle...`, and `Pandora`.
- **Browser Tabs**: Five open tabs:
  1. `maine gis geotiff...` (Google search)
  2. `SMMS METADAT...`
  3. `Maine GIS Data ...`
  4. `Maine Aerial Pho...`
  5. `Maine Aerial Pho...` (active tab)

## Web GIS Controls & Layout
- **Top Banner**: Black banner with `Maine Aerial Photography` in white text on the left, and two dropdown menus on the right: `Display Metadata` and `HTML`.
- **Left Vertical Toolbar**: A column of 14 small square GIS tool icons along the left edge (toggle overview map, toggle legend/layers, zoom in, zoom out, zoom to full extent, zoom to previous extent, pan, identify `(i)`, query/find, measure, select by rectangle, select by polygon, clear selection, print, and help `?`).
- **Overview Inset Map**: In the upper left corner of the map canvas, a gray silhouette of the state of Maine shows a tiny yellow dot at the very southern tip (Kittery/York County).
- **Right Sidebar (`Layers`)**: A table of map layers with `Visible` checkboxes, `Active` radio buttons, color/thumbnail swatches, and `Layer Name`:
  - `Town Lines` (Visible checked)
  - `1 Foot Color Index` (unchecked)
  - `2 Foot Color Index` (unchecked)
  - `Fort Fairfield Index` (unchecked)
  - `.5 Foot Color Index` (unchecked)
  - `1 Meter Greyscale Index` (unchecked)
  - `Parcels` (unchecked)
  - `Land Cover` (no checkbox, color swatch)
  - `1 Foot Color Photography` (Visible checked)
  - `2 Foot Color Photography` (unchecked)
  - `Fort Fairfield Photography` (unchecked)
  - `.5 Foot Color Photography` (unchecked)
  - `Town Names` (Visible checked, Active selected)
  - `County Names` (Visible checked)
  - `Road Names` (Visible checked)
  - Below the layer list are `Refresh Map` and `Help ?` buttons.
- **Bottom Bar**: `Zoom to a town` with a `Town:` dropdown set to `Select a Town`, and `Done` in the browser status bar at the bottom left.

## Aerial Map Details
- **Geographic Area**: Centered on the Piscataqua River estuary separating southern Maine (`Eliot`, `Kittery`, and `York` labeled in light blue text with light blue town boundary lines) from New Hampshire (Portsmouth and Pease International Tradeport).
- **Imagery Contrast**: The Maine side (and parts of the immediate riverfront in NH, including the Interstate 95 bridge crossing) is covered by sharp, natural-color orthoimagery (`1 Foot Color Photography`), whereas the southwestern quadrant in New Hampshire (showing the long white runway of Pease International Tradeport at middle-left and Great Bay tributaries) is rendered in older grayscale orthophotography.
- **Footer Overlays**: `Maine Office of GIS` watermark at the bottom left of the map, and a scale bar from `0` to `1.4mi` at the bottom right.
