# Mars: 2020: Springtime Animated Short Still (`mars_2020_spring.png`)

## Overview
A still frame from the humorous 3D computer-animated short video **"Mars: 2020: Springtime"** (shared via YouTube), depicting a small wheeled rover emerging down the deployment ramp of a spacecraft lander on the surface of Mars.

## Scene Description
- **The Lander**: Occupying the left and center of the frame is a gray, pyramidal/A-frame metallic Mars lander resting on circular footpads on the rusty orange Martian desert floor.
  - A tall vertical communications antenna rises from the apex of the A-frame structure.
  - Two large, diamond-treaded gray petal ramps extend outward and downward—one toward the bottom left foreground and the other toward the bottom right.
  - Beneath the structural corners of the lander frame are conical gray retro-rocket thruster nozzles casting dark shadows onto the ground below.
- **The Rover**: Rolling down the right-hand deployment ramp is a small, four-wheeled gray robotic rover. It features:
  - A dark rectangular solar panel or dish mounted on an angled mast at the rear.
  - A camera/sensor head tilted upward on a short neck near the front.
  - An articulated robotic arm extending forward from the front of the chassis, ending in a two-pronged gripper claw dangling over the ramp.
- **Background**: Rolling, sandy peach-and-terracotta Martian dunes stretch across the horizon beneath a hazy, pale salmon-pink Martian sky.
