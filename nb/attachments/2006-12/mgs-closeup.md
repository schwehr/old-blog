# Close-Up of New Deposit on Martian Crater Wall (`mgs-closeup.png`)

## Overview
A magnified grayscale close-up view from NASA's **Mars Global Surveyor (MGS)** Mars Orbiter Camera (detail from `PIA09027`, September 2005), highlighting the lower terminus of the newly formed light-toned gully deposit on a Martian crater wall.

## Visual Details
- **Background Terrain**: A textured, medium-gray Martian crater wall sloping from the upper left toward the lower right, marked by faint parallel downslope striations, shallow rills, and a few tiny dark specks (boulders or image artifacts).
- **The Deposit**: Running diagonally from the upper left to the lower right is a bright, light-gray to off-white flow-like feature.
  - In the upper-left and central sections, the deposit fills and follows a narrow, slightly sinuous channel.
  - Toward the lower right (where a bold white arrow points from the bottom center), the deposit fans out and splits into multiple narrow, finger-like (digitate) lobes and branches that terminate on the textured slope.
