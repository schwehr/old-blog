# NOAA Great Lakes Coastal Forecasting System in Google Earth (`noaa-great-lakes-ge.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth viewport screenshot showing data overlays from the NOAA Great Lakes Environmental Research Laboratory (GLERL) Great Lakes Coastal Forecasting System (GLCFS) across Lake Michigan, Lake Huron, Lake Erie, and the eastern end of Lake Superior.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Geographic Region**: Centered on the state of **Michigan** (Lower and Upper Peninsulas) and the surrounding Great Lakes, including:
  - **Lake Michigan** (left)
  - **Lake Huron** and Georgian Bay (center-top and upper right), with **Manitoulin Island** labeled in white text
  - **Lake Erie** and Lake St. Clair (bottom right)
  - A small portion of eastern **Lake Superior** (top left) and western **Lake Ontario** (far right)
- **Bathymetry / Forecast Overlays**: Each of the Great Lakes is filled with a smooth blue-to-cyan color gradient overlay representing GLCFS model output (such as water temperature, depth, or ice/surface conditions), ranging from deep royal blue across the main basins of Lake Michigan and northern Lake Huron to brighter light cyan in shallow or eastern/southern areas (eastern Lake Huron, Georgian Bay, and central/eastern Lake Erie).
- **Shorelines & Borders**:
  - Bright yellow vector outlines trace the shorelines of the Great Lakes and the international US–Canada boundary running down the middle of Lake Huron, through the St. Clair and Detroit rivers, and across Lake Erie.
  - Thin light-gray/white state and county/jurisdictional boundary lines cross the land and water (including state borders for Michigan, Wisconsin, Illinois, Indiana, and Ohio).
- **Placemarks & Icons**:
  - A white-and-gray circular globe/sphere icon near the southwestern shore of Lake Michigan (Chicago area).
  - A small white pawn-like placemark icon in southern central Michigan (near Lansing/Ann Arbor).
  - A white pawn-like placemark icon inside a blue circle at the eastern end of Lake Erie / Niagara River (Buffalo area).

## Visible Text & Labels
- **`Manitoulin Island`** (upper center-right, over Manitoulin Island in Lake Huron)
- **`Michigan`** (center-left, over the Lower Peninsula of Michigan)
