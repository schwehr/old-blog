# OSSIM 1.6.6 rc8 Mac OS X Installer (`ossim.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Mac OS X installer window for **OSSIM_1.6.6 rc8** (Open Source Software Image Map), displaying the welcome screen and an embedded graphic for the "LARGE DATA Joint Capabilities Technology Demonstration."

## Interface & Controls
- **Window Title Bar**: Standard Mac OS X Aqua title bar with red, yellow, and green window buttons on the left, a small yellow/orange package box icon, and the title **`Install OSSIM_1.6.6 rc8`**.
- **Main Heading**: Bold black text at the top of the right pane reads **`Welcome to the OSSIM_1.6.6 rc8 Installer`**.
- **Left Step Sidebar**: Lists the seven installation stages vertically over a semi-transparent background showing a USGS-style topographic map of the California coast:
  - **`Introduction`** (currently active, marked with a blue radio-style bullet)
  - **`Read Me`** (gray bullet)
  - **`License`** (gray bullet)
  - **`Select Destination`** (gray bullet)
  - **`Installation Type`** (gray bullet)
  - **`Install`** (gray bullet)
  - **`Finish Up`** (gray bullet)
- **Bottom Navigation Buttons**:
  - **`Go Back`** (disabled/grayed-out pill button)
  - **`Continue`** (active pulsing blue-outlined Aqua pill button)

## Graphics & Visible Text
- **Right Scrollable Content Pane**:
  - **Header Logo**: Large dark blue text **`LARGE DATA`** flanking a gray wireframe globe icon, with gray subtitle text underneath:
    - **`Joint Capabilities Technology Demonstration`**
    - **`USSTRATCOM - NGA - NRL - DISA - INSCOM`**
  - **3D Globe Graphic**: Below the logo is a 3D perspective rendering of the southeastern United States and Florida seen from space, overlaid with a green latitude/longitude grid and dense orange/red rectangular tile grids covering parts of the Gulf Coast, Georgia, the Carolinas, and Florida.
- **Background Map (Bottom Left)**: A classic topographic quadrangle map of Marin County, California, showing the coastline and labels including **`STATE`**, **`Stinson Beach`**, and **`GOLDEN GATE NATIONAL`** (Golden Gate National Recreation Area).
