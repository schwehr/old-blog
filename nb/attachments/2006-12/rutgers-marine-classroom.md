# Rutgers Marine & Coastal Sciences C.O.O.L. Classroom Website (`rutgers-marine-classroom.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the homepage for the **Rutgers Marine & Coastal Sciences C.O.O.L. Classroom** (`coolclassroom.org`), an educational marine science website created by the Coastal Ocean Observation Laboratory (COOL) at Rutgers University.

## Layout & Visual Elements
- **Color Palette & Background**: Deep ocean-blue background with soft lighter-blue circular highlights, accented by a bright lime-green horizontal navigation bar at the top and a matching lime-green banner across the upper middle.
- **Top Navigation Bar**: A rounded lime-green bar across the top contains five white text links:
  - **`what's c.o.o.l.?`** | **`c.o.o.l. facts`** | **`c.o.o.l. projects`** | **`teachers' guide`** | **`sitemap`**
- **Header Banner**:
  - Large title reading **`c.o.o.l.`** in bold yellow lowercase letters and **`classroom`** in white letters (with a stylized double-s).
  - Below the title is a horizontal banner featuring a photograph of colorful chalk and an eraser on a wooden chalkboard ledge on the left, and white silhouettes of a satellite, an underwater sensor/radar array, and a seahorse over a lime-green background on the right.
- **Left Column**:
  - White wave/crest logo above **`RUTGERS` / `MARINE & COASTAL` / `SCIENCES`** and **`c.o.o.l. classroom`**.
  - A circular underwater photograph bordered in green showing a school of fish swimming near a reef.
