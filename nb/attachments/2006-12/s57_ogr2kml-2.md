# S-57 Caution Area (`OBJL: 27`) Placemark Popup in Google Earth (`s57_ogr2kml-2.png`)

## Overview
A close-up screenshot of Google Earth showing an open KML info balloon for a selected yellow pushpin placemark from the `ogr2ogr` conversion of NOAA S-57 chart `US5VA17M.000` (Norfolk Harbor and Elizabeth River). The selected feature has an S-57 Object Label (`OBJL`) of `27` (Caution Area / `CTNARE`).

## Map & Placemark Details
- **Map Background**: The left half of the image shows the white polygon background of the converted S-57 chart crossed by red and light-green vector lines along the Elizabeth River channel.
- **Clustered Pushpins**: Dense rows of bright yellow pushpins follow the channel. Around the selected pin in the middle-left, overlapping pushpins are darkened/dimmed to an olive-brown shade by Google Earth's overlapping-placemark selection behavior.
- **Info Balloon**: A white rounded rectangular popup box on the right points to a bright yellow pushpin near the center-left and has a small blue close button (`x`) in its upper-right corner.
