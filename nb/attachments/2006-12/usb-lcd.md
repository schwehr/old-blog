# Crystalfontz USB LCD Display Modules (`usb-lcd.png`)

## Overview
A composite product photograph showing two compact **Crystalfontz** USB LCD character display modules (models **631** and **633**) with integrated keypad buttons, stacked vertically.

## Module Descriptions
- **Top Module (`Crystalfontz 631`)**:
  - Mounted in a sleek black anodized bezel/faceplate with a small red Crystalfontz logo in the bottom-left corner.
  - **Display**: A 2-line LCD screen with glowing red text and graphics on a dark black/maroon background. On the left side of the screen is a custom pixel-art Crystalfontz icon, followed by two lines of text:
    - **`Crystalfontz 631`**
    - **`HW v1.0  FW v1.0`**
  - **Keypad**: Four square red-backlit buttons arranged in a 2x2 grid on the right side of the bezel.
- **Bottom Module (`Crystalfontz 633`)**:
  - Exposed green printed circuit board (PCB) with four corner mounting holes.
  - **Display**: A black-bezel 2-line LCD screen on the left with bright white/light-blue text on a vivid blue backlit background:
    - **`Crystalfontz 633`**
    - **`HW:v1.5a FW:k1.9`**
  - **Keypad**: On the right side is a translucent blue-backlit 6-button directional keypad cluster featuring four directional arrow keys (up, down, left, right) surrounding a center check-mark (`✓`) confirm button, plus a circular cancel button marked with a red `X` at the lower left of the keypad.
