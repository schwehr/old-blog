# Yahoo! Animated Loading Progress Bar (`yahoo-slow.png`)

## Overview
A small, tightly cropped screenshot of a web interface loading progress indicator (barber-pole style animated progress bar) from Yahoo!'s 2006 web interface redesign.

## Visual Details
- **Progress Bar**: A horizontal, pill-shaped rounded rectangle outlined in medium-dark blue on a white background, surrounded by a thin gray outer border.
- **Fill Pattern**: Inside the bar are alternating diagonal stripes of medium periwinkle blue and pale ice-blue/lavender slanting from bottom-left to top-right, indicating an indeterminate "loading" or "busy" state.
