# Flash Earth Satellite Imagery Viewer (`flashearth.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X Firefox browser screenshot of **Flash Earth** (`http://www.flashearth.com`), a Flash-based web application for browsing and comparing satellite and aerial imagery of the Earth from multiple providers, centered over the Chesapeake Bay and Virginia/Maryland coastline.

## Browser Window & Interface Controls
- **Browser Chrome**:
  - **Window Title**: `Flash Earth ...satellite and aerial imagery of the Earth in Flash`
  - **Toolbar**: Standard Mac OS X Firefox navigation buttons (Back, Forward, Reload, Stop, Home), the URL bar showing `http://www.flashearth.co`, a green Go arrow, and the Google search box.
  - **Status Bar (Bottom)**: Displays `Transferring data from wms.jpl.nasa.gov...`.
- **Left Provider Selection Panel**: A translucent dark overlay panel in the top-left corner titled **FLASH EARTH** in white stenciled serif lettering, followed by a vertical list of radio buttons:
  - `Google Maps`
  - `Microsoft VE (aerial)`
  - `Microsoft VE (labels)`
  - `Yahoo! Maps`
  - `Ask.com (aerial)`
  - `Ask.com (physical)`
  - `OpenLayers`
  - `NASA Terra (daily)` (selected radio button)
- **Right Navigation & Zoom Controls**:
  - **Compass Rose**: A circular translucent compass dial in the upper right with a four-pointed star and cardinal direction labels (`N`, `E`, `S`, `W`).
  - **Zoom Slider**: A vertical zoom track beneath the compass with a `+` button at the top, a horizontal slider bar in the middle, and a `-` button at the bottom.
- **Bottom-Right Search & Location Panel**: A translucent dark overlay panel containing:
  - `Search for a location:` with a text input field and magnifying glass icon.
  - `Link to this location:` with a chain-link icon.
  - `Latitude: 37° 49' 33" N`
  - `Longitude: 76° 32' 7" W`

## Satellite Imagery & Attribution
- **Scene**: True-color daily satellite imagery from NASA Terra MODIS showing the Chesapeake Bay, its tributaries (including the Potomac, Rappahannock, York, and James Rivers), the Delmarva Peninsula to the east, and bands of white clouds across the southern portion of the frame. A white crosshair (`+`) marks the center of the viewport.
