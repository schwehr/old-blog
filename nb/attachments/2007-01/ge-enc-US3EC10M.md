# NOAA S-57 ENC Chart US3EC10M in Google Earth (`ge-enc-US3EC10M.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** on Mac OS X displaying vector layers converted from NOAA S-57 Electronic Navigational Chart (ENC) **US3EC10M** ("Bay of Fundy to Cape Cod") using `ogr2ogr -f KML US3EC10M.kml US3EC10M.000` (from GDAL 1.4.0).

## Google Earth Interface & Sidebar
- **Left Sidebar (`Search`, `Places`, `Layers`)**:
  - **Search Panel**: Shows the `Fly To` tab active with placeholder text `e.g., Hotels near JFK`.
  - **Places Panel**: Lists the S-57 object class folders generated by `ogr2ogr` from the ENC file, with checkboxes indicating which layers are currently enabled:
    - `PILPNT` (unchecked)
    - `PIPSOL` (unchecked)
    - `PRCARE` (checked — Precautionary Area)
    - `RTPBCN` (unchecked)
    - `RDOSTA` (unchecked)
    - `RESARE` (checked — Restricted Area)
    - `RIVERS` (unchecked)
    - `SEAARE` (unchecked)
    - `SBDARE` (unchecked)
    - `SLCONS` (unchecked)
    - `SILTNK` (unchecked)
    - `SOUNDG` (unchecked)
    - `TOPMAR` (unchecked)
    - `TSSBND` (checked — Traffic Separation Scheme Boundary)

## Map & Chart Overlay Details
- **Geographic Area**: An oblique 3D satellite view looking northeast across eastern Massachusetts, **Cape Cod** (`Barnstable`), `Bristol`, **Boston**, **New Hampshire** (`Home` placemark near the coast), and southern Maine.
- **ENC Vector Overlays**:
  - **Coastline & Intertidal Features**: Bright red and yellow/orange vector outlines tracing the complex coastline, harbors, and islands from Cape Cod and Boston Harbor northward along the New Hampshire and Maine coasts.
  - **Traffic Separation Schemes & Precautionary Areas**: Red wireframe polygons extending offshore into Massachusetts Bay and the Gulf of Maine marking shipping lanes (`TSSBND`, `TSSLPT`) and restricted/precautionary zones (`RESARE`, `PRCARE`), along with three prominent solid white circular/oval precautionary areas near Boston Harbor, Cape Cod Bay, and off the New Hampshire/Maine coast.
- **Status Bar & Attribution**:
  - Scale bar: `28 km`
