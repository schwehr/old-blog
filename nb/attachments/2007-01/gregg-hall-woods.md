# Sunrise Through Snow-Dusted Woods Toward Gregg Hall (`gregg-hall-woods.jpg`)

## Overview
A landscape photograph taken on the morning of January 28, 2007, showing the sun rising behind **Gregg Hall** at the University of New Hampshire (UNH), viewed through a stand of evergreen and deciduous trees after a light overnight snowfall.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Forest Floor**:
  - The ground is covered in a light blanket of fresh white snow, speckled with fallen brown leaves, pine needles, and dark tree shadows stretching toward the viewer.
  - Young hemlock/pine saplings and dark tree trunks frame the left, center, and right sides of the composition.
- **Lighting & Background**:
  - The low morning sun shines brightly just to the right of the silhouette of **Gregg Hall**, creating a warm golden glow that backlights the pine needles and branches in the center of the frame.
  - Long, crisp shadows of the tree trunks cut across the sunlit patches of snow in the middle ground.
  - The brick structure and roofline of Gregg Hall are partially visible through the dense evergreens on the left-center side of the image.
