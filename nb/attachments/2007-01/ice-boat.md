# Husky Airboat Ice Rescue Craft (`ice-boat.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a red-and-gray **Husky airboat** ice rescue vessel resting on a frozen lake or river surface, referenced in a January 12, 2007 blog post about a KARE 11 (NBC Saint Paul) news story (*"Ice no obstacle for new rescue boat"*).

## Vessel & Scene Description
- **Vessel Exterior**:
  - **Hull**: A wide, flat-bottomed hull painted bright red along the upper topsides and light gray along the lower ice-riding bottom section, angled slightly to the left (port bow view).
  - **Cabin**: An enclosed forward cabin with a black roof, angled front windshields, side windows, and two blue emergency strobe beacon lights mounted on the roof.
  - **Propulsion**: A large black wire mesh propeller cage at the stern housing the aircraft-style pusher propeller and dual vertical directional rudders.
- **Environment**:
  - **Foreground**: A flat expanse of white and light-gray lake/river ice with a rough ridge of broken ice chunks across the lower foreground.
  - **Background**: A steep, wooded shoreline with bare winter deciduous trees and brown brush rising behind the boat.
