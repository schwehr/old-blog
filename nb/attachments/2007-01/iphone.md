# Original Apple iPhone Promotional Image (`iphone.png`)

## Overview
An official Apple promotional image from the January 9, 2007 Macworld announcement of the original **Apple iPhone**, showing a left hand holding the device against a solid black background with the home screen displayed.

## Device & Screen Details
- **Header Branding**: In the top-left corner, a white Apple logo is followed by the word **iPhone** in clean white sans-serif typography.
- **Hardware Design**:
  - The device features a glossy black front face framed by a curved, polished chrome/stainless-steel bezel, held upright in a person's left hand.
  - Above the touchscreen is the horizontal earpiece speaker slot; below the screen is the circular concave physical **Home** button with a rounded-square icon.
- **Status Bar (Top of Screen)**:
  - Signal strength bars, carrier name **`cingular`**, Wi-Fi indicator icon, time **`12:34 PM`** centered, and battery level icon on the right.
- **Home Screen Application Icons (Grid of 11 Icons)**:
  - **Row 1**:
    - `Text` (green icon with white `SMS` speech bubble)
    - `Calendar` (white calendar page showing `Tuesday` in a red header and `9`)
    - `Photos` (yellow sunflower against a blue sky)
    - `Camera` (metallic camera lens shutter icon)
  - **Row 2**:
    - `Calculator` (brown/orange circular arithmetic buttons `+`, `-`, `x`, `÷`)
    - `Stocks` (blue chart with a white line graph)
    - `Maps` (road map showing Interstate `280` shield and a red pin)
    - `Weather` (bright sun over a blue sky showing `73°`)
  - **Row 3**:
    - `Notes` (yellow legal pad with a brown header bar)
    - `Clock` (white analog clock face with black numerals and red second hand)
    - `Settings` (interlocking metallic gears)
- **Bottom Dock (4 Icons on a Brushed/Reflective Silver Tray)**:
  - `Phone` (green icon with white telephone handset)
  - `Mail` (blue sky with white envelope)
  - `Web` (blue Safari compass icon)
  - `iPod` (orange/yellow icon with white iPod silhouette)
