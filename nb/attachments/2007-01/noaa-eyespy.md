# NOAA EyeSpy Online Chart Viewer: Approaches to Chesapeake Bay (`noaa-eyespy.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of NOAA's online raster nautical chart viewer (**Powered by eyespy**) displaying a portion of the nautical chart **Approaches to Chesapeake Bay** (Chart 12221 / 13205 viewer) around **Cape Henry, Virginia**.

## Chart Content & Geographic Features
- **Chart Title Block (Lower Left on Land Mass)**:
  - Circular **NOAA** / U.S. Department of Commerce / Coast Survey emblem.
  - `UNITED STATES - EAST COAST`
  - `VIRGINIA`
  - **`APPROACHES TO CHESAPEAKE BAY`**
  - `Mercator Projection`
  - `Scale 1:50,000 at Lat. 36° 54'`
- **Land & Coastal Features**:
  - Yellow-tinted landmass of **Cape Henry** and **North Virginia Beach**, showing Route `60`, **Crystal Lake** in the lower center, `LOOKOUT TOWER`, and the **Cape Henry Light** (`Gp Fl (2) 20s 164ft 17M`).
  - Blue-tinted shallow water zone along the shoreline with depth contours and soundings in feet.
- **Navigational Channels & Regulated Areas**:
  - **Thimble Shoal Channel**: Dashed parallel channel lines extending northwest from the Chesapeake Bay entrance.
  - **Traffic Separation Lanes & Precautionary Area**: Magenta-shaded traffic separation lanes converging from the northeast and southeast into a large dashed magenta circular **PRECAUTIONARY AREA (`see note G`)** and **PILOTAGE AREA** centered on the `RW "CB" Mo (A) WHIS RACON (- • -)` sea buoy.
  - **Naval Anchorages & Restricted Areas**: Magenta-outlined zones labeled `NAVAL ANCHORAGE E`, `NAVAL ANCHORAGE A`, `RESTRICTED AREA`, and `LOCAL MAGNETIC DISTURBANCE (see note)` in the lower right.
  - Numerous blue-tinted wreck/obstruction symbols (`Obstn`, `Wk`) with cleared depth circles scattered across the entrance and offshore approaches.

## Viewer Controls (Bottom Bar)
- Left side: **`POWERED BY eyespy`** logo (`eye` in red, `spy` in teal).
- Right side: Six circular gray control buttons:
  - Zoom In (`+`)
  - Zoom Out (`-`)
  - Pan Left (`◀`)
  - Pan Right (`▶`)
  - Pan Up (`▲`)
  - Pan Down (`▼`)
  - Reset/Home (`🏠` house icon)
