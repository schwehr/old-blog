# NOAA Estuarine Bathymetry in Google Earth: Chesapeake Bay & Norfolk (`noaa-ge-estuarinebathy.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** displaying a KML overlay from the **NOAA Estuarine Bathymetry** dataset (`estuarinebathymetry.noaa.gov`) covering the southern **Chesapeake Bay** and **Hampton Roads** entrance, looking north from over **Portsmouth**, **Norfolk**, and **Chesapeake**, Virginia.

## Legend & Screen Overlay (Top Left)
A white rectangular legend box in the upper-left corner displays:
- **Title**: `Chesapeake Bay`
- **Info Icon**: Red circle with a white `i` next to `Click for Information`
- **Polygon Key**: Light green rectangle with an olive border labeled `Spatial Extent`
- **Shaded Relief**:
  - `Value` with a vertical grayscale gradient bar (white at top to black at bottom)
- **Bathymetry**:
  - `Depth (meters)` with a rainbow color bar ranging from red/orange/yellow at the top (`High : 1.58`) through cyan to deep blue at the bottom (`Low : -52.93`)

## 3D Scene & Geographic Labels
- **Bathymetric Surface**:
  - The southern Chesapeake Bay and Hampton Roads harbor entrance are rendered with color-coded shaded-relief bathymetry. Deep shipping channels (such as Thimble Shoal Channel and the main harbor entrance north of Norfolk) appear in dark blue and cyan, while shallower shoals and banks appear in yellow, orange, and light green.
  - As noted in the accompanying blog post, the bathymetric coverage stops at the mouth of the Elizabeth River just north of Craney Island and Norfolk.
- **Visible Place Labels**:
  - `Craney Island` (light blue text on the left)
  - `Charters Island` (light blue text near the center)
  - `Portsmouth` (green text on the left and white bold text with a red circle marker in the center)
  - `Norfolk` (white bold text with a red circle marker)
  - `Chesapeake` (white bold text with a red circle marker on the right)
- **Controls & Attribution**:
  - Standard Google Earth tilt slider and circular navigation ring in the upper right.
