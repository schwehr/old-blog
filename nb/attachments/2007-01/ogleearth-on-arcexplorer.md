# ArcGIS Explorer Beta 3D View of Ushuaia (`ogleearth-on-arcexplorer.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from an Ogle Earth review showing **ESRI ArcGIS Explorer** (powered by ArcGIS Online Beta), a 3D virtual globe application similar to Google Earth, displaying terrain around Ushuaia, Argentina, with test KML/vector overlays and pop-up info windows.

## Scene & 3D Globe Details
- **Terrain & Imagery**: An oblique 3D perspective looking across mountainous coastal terrain with green valleys, snow-capped peaks in the background, and a dark blue bay/channel in the lower left under a pale blue sky.
- **Vector Overlays**:
  - A red pushpin marker planted near the coastline on the left at the port of Ushuaia.
  - A wide, solid orange polygon (`A region`) draped across the central mountains and hills.
  - Red polylines (`A line`) winding across the terrain and overlapping the orange polygon.

## Interface, Overlays & Visible Text
- **Branding & Compass**:
  - Top-right badge: `Powered by ArcGIS® Online Beta` in white text on a translucent blue-gray rounded rectangle.
  - Bottom-left: A 3D metallic 8-point compass rose with the east/north pointer highlighted in red.
  - Bottom-right badge: `ESRI® Copyright © 2006` logo box.
- **Pop-up Info Windows**: Three light-blue floating callout boxes with pushpin icons, dropdown arrows, close (`X`) buttons, and scrollbars:
  1. **`Ushuaia`** (top left, pointing to the red pushpin on the coast):
     - `Argentine port.`
     - `Get Driving Directions, From Here, To Here` (with underlined blue links).
  2. **`A region`** (top right, pointing to the orange polygon):
     - `Just testing.`
  3. **`A line`** (bottom center, pointing to the red line):
     - `Just testing.`
     - `Fly Along` (underlined blue link).
