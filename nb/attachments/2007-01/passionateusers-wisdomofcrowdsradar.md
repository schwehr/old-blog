# "What Gets Created?" Wisdom of Crowds vs. Individuals Radar Charts (`passionateusers-wisdomofcrowdsradar.png`)

## Overview
An infographic diagram from the *Creating Passionate Users* blog (`http://headrush.typepad.com/creating_passionate_users`) titled **"What gets created?"**, contrasting four 5-axis radar (spider) plots arranged in two columns: `"Wisdom of Crowds"` on the left versus `"Individuals"` on the right.

## Layout & Radar Plot Details
Each of the four circular radar charts consists of concentric green rings (darkest in the middle rings, lighter toward the center and outer edge) with five radial axes labeled around the perimeter:
- **Top-left**: `Power`
- **Top-right**: `Sexiness`
- **Bottom-right**: `Edgy/Hipness`
- **Bottom**: `Fun`
- **Left**: `Simplicity`

### Left Column: `"Wisdom of Crowds"`
- **Header**: `"Wisdom of Crowds"` in tan/brown sans-serif font.
- **Top-left Radar Plot**: Shows a small, relatively compact, beige/peach polygon centered near the inner rings, extending slightly toward `Simplicity` but remaining modest across `Power`, `Sexiness`, `Edgy/Hipness`, and `Fun`.
- **Bottom-left Radar Plot**: Shows an even smaller, nearly symmetrical beige pentagon clustered tightly around the bullseye center across all five dimensions.
- **Bottom Caption**:
  `Safe, well-balanced, non-offensive products` (in bold dark teal text).

### Right Column: `Individuals`
- **Header**: `Individuals` in an orange handwritten/script font.
- **Top-right Radar Plot**: Shows a sharp, elongated bright-orange polygon spiking dramatically outward to the outer ring along the `Fun` axis while staying close to the center on the other axes.
- **Bottom-right Radar Plot**: Shows a dramatic, asymmetric bright-orange polygon with two long spikes reaching the outer rings on `Sexiness` and `Simplicity`, while staying low on `Power`, `Edgy/Hipness`, and `Fun`.
- **Bottom Caption**:
  `Risky, unbalanced, astonishing ideas` (in bold dark teal text).
