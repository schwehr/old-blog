# A Periodic Table of Visualization Methods (`periodic.png`)

## Overview
A graphic chart titled **"A PERIODIC TABLE OF VISUALIZATION METHODS"** (from `visual-literacy.org`), organizing data, information, concept, strategy, metaphor, and compound visualization techniques into a color-coded layout modeled after the periodic table of chemical elements, with an interactive pop-up preview showing a **Gantt Chart** (`Gc`).

## Color-Coded Categories (Legend)
At the top center of the table, five visible category boxes explain the background colors of the "element" tiles:
- **Pale Yellow (`Data Visualization`)**: *Visual representations of quantitative data in schematic form (either with or without axes)*
- **Light Green (`Information Visualization`)**: *The use of interactive visual representations of data to amplify cognition. This means that the data is transformed into an image, it is mapped to screen space. The image can be changed by users as they proceed working with it*
- **Mint/Cyan-Green (`Concept Visualization`)**: *Methods to elaborate (mostly) qualitative... ideas, plans, and analyses.*
- **Light Sky Blue (`Strategy Visualization`)**: *The systematic use of complementary visual representations in the analysis, development, formulation, communication, and implementation of strategies in organizations.*
- **Light Salmon/Pink (`Metaphor Visualization`)**: *Visual Metaphors position information graphically to organize and structure information. They also convey an insight about the represented information through the key characteristics of the metaphor that is employed*
- **Periwinkle/Lavender (Rightmost column)**: Compound/graphic visualization methods (legend box partially covered by the Gantt Chart pop-up).
