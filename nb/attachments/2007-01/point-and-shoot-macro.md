# Macro Photograph of Moss and Lichen on Rock (`point-and-shoot-macro.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up macro photograph taken with a Nikon Coolpix 4600 point-and-shoot digital camera showing clumps of vibrant green moss and pale crustose/foliose lichen growing on a weathered gray rock surface.

## Scene & Visual Details
- **Foreground & Focus**: Sharp focus rests on the lower-center clusters of dark-green and bright-green moss tufts, where individual tiny pointed leaves and reddish-brown spore capsules/tips are clearly visible.
- **Lichen & Rock Texture**: Between the green cushions of moss, patches of chalky white and pale seafoam-green lichen encrust the dark gray, rough-textured stone surface on the left and center.
- **Depth of Field & Background**: Due to the shallow depth of field typical of macro mode, the moss-covered rock slopes upward and falls progressively out of focus toward the upper half of the frame, with a softly blurred pale blue-white background at the top corners.
- **Framing**: The image is bordered by a thin gray inner line and a black outer frame.
