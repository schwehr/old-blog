# Sunrise Light on Forest Trees (`sunrise-forestfire.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical outdoor photograph of a stand of forest trees in New Hampshire illuminated by intense, warm orange-red early morning sunrise light against a clear blue sky.

## Scene Description
- **Central Subject**: A tall eastern white pine tree rises near the center of the frame, its straight trunk and upper canopy of needles glowing bright copper-orange in the low-angle morning sunlight.
- **Surrounding Woods**: Bare winter deciduous branches arch in from the right foreground and flank the pine on the left, catching the same fiery orange and golden sunlight. Darker shaded undergrowth and young saplings line the bottom of the frame.
- **Sky & Lighting**: The background sky transitions from a soft pale blue near the horizon behind the branches to a deeper, clear cerulean blue at the top left, creating a strong warm-versus-cool color contrast that makes the sunlit trees look as though they are glowing like fire.
- **Framing**: Surrounded by a thin white inner border and a black outer border.
