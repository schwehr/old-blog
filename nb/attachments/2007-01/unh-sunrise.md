# Sunrise Behind the UNH Heating Plant Smokestack (`unh-sunrise.png`)

## Overview
A landscape photograph of a vibrant winter sunrise at the University of New Hampshire (UNH) campus, showing dark silhouettes of bare trees and the tall heating plant smokestack releasing a plume of steam against a colorful sky.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Silhouettes**: Across the bottom of the frame, bare deciduous trees, evergreens on the far left, campus buildings/rooftops on the right, a shorter central stack/chimney, and a tall industrial smokestack on the left are rendered in deep black silhouette against the bright horizon.
- **Steam Plume**: A billowy cloud of blue-gray steam rises from the top of the tall smokestack on the left, drifting to the right and dissipating into a fainter, translucent gray mist across the upper center of the sky.
- **Sunrise Sky**: The sky displays a dramatic gradient of colors—brilliant glowing yellow and gold right at the lower-right horizon behind the tree trunks, blending upward into warm peach, salmon pink, androse-tinted stratus clouds, and finally transitioning into deep twilight purple-blue at the top edge.
- **Framing**: Framed by a thin white inner border and a black outer border.
