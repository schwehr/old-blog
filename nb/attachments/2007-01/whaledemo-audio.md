# Whale Localization Demo in Google Earth – Acoustic Detection (`whaledemo-audio.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** displaying a synthetic whale-tracking visualization demo (*"Whale Localization Demo by Schwehr and Weber"*) in the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary off Massachusetts, highlighting an acoustic detection balloon (`B3`) with an embedded waveform plot and bearing cone.

## Interface & Sidebar (Left)
- **Search Panel (Top Left)**: Tabs for `[Fl]y To` (active), `Find Businesses`, and `Directions`, with `, San Francisco` in gray above a text box and magnifying glass button.
- **Places Panel (Middle Left)**: Tree view with checked boxes:
  - `Positions` (folder)
  - `B1 Sighting`, `B2 Sighting`, `B3 Sighting` (purple polygon icons)
  - `sbnms_py` (red polygon path icon for the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary boundary)
  - `Whale 0035 path` (path icon)
  - `B1`, `B2`, `B3` (orange buoy/hydrophone icons; `B3` is highlighted/selected, showing `[IMG]files/b3-acoustics.png...`)
- **Layers Panel (Bottom Left)**: `View:` dropdown set to `Core`, showing `Primary Database` and `Terrain` (checked).
