# Whale Localization Demo in Google Earth – Aerial Overflight Sighting (`whaledemo-overflight.png`)

## Overview
A **Google Earth** screenshot from the Schwehr and Weber whale-tracking demonstration over Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary, showing the pop-up balloon for an aerial overflight observation (`Sighting 19:21`) with an aerial photograph of a humpback whale bubble-net feeding.

## 3D Map View (Left)
- **Bathymetry & Sanctuary Boundary**: False-color bathymetry (green and yellow over Stellwagen Bank, blue in deeper water, tan/orange over Cape Cod in the lower left with a U.S. Route `6` shield) enclosed by the red polygon outline of Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (`Stellwagen Bank N...` pink pushpin at top).
- **Buoys, Bearings & Whale Path**:
  - Orange buoy icons `B3` (left), `B1` (upper center), and `B2` (right, unlabeled behind balloon tail) project narrow purple/gray directional bearing wedges that intersect at the whale-tail icon (`Sighting...`).
  - A black polyline traces the whale's movement between white teardrop markers labeled with timestamps: `13:09` (bottom), `01:43`, `05:52`, `07:40`, `10:02`, `12:54`, and `14:16`.
