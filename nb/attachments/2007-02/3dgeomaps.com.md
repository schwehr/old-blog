# 3D Geology Maps Web Application on Microsoft Virtual Earth (`3dgeomaps.com.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **3D Geology Maps** website (`3dgeomaps.com`), a web mapping application built on Microsoft Virtual Earth showing a hybrid satellite and road map view centered over the state of Utah alongside a collapsible region tree and submission info panel.

## Map View & Controls (Left Panel)
- **Title Banner**: A light green rectangular banner centered at the top of the map reads **`Welcome to 3D Geology Maps`** in bold black serif text.
- **Navigation Control Widget (Top Left)**: A vertical white control bar containing:
  - Mode toggle buttons at the top: **`2D`** (selected) and `3D`.
  - A vertical zoom slider with `+` at the top and `-` at the bottom.
  - Two view-style icons next to the slider: a curved map sheet icon (selected with a blue border) and a 3D city buildings icon below it.
  - Three map layer buttons at the bottom: `Road`, `Aerial`, and **`Hybrid`** (highlighted in light blue).
- **Geographic Features & Labels**:
  - Displays satellite imagery of **`UTAH`** (labeled in red-outlined uppercase letters in the center) with state borders outlined in thin yellow lines.
  - Cities and towns marked with circular icons and white labels: `Salt Lake City` (red bullseye target icon), `Roy`, `Clearfield`, `Layton`, `Cottonwood Heights`, `Bennion`, `Sandy`, `Pleasant Grove`, `Provo`, `Spanish Fork`, `Cedar City`, `St. George`, and `Grand Junction` (on the right edge in Colorado).
  - Physiographic and park labels: `Great Salt Lake Desert`, `Ashley National Forest`, `Uinta Mountains`, `Wasatch Range`, `Castle Valley`, `Uintah and Ouray I.R.`, `East Tavaputs Plateau`, `Roan Plateau`, `Wasatch Plateau`, `Pavant Range`, `Snake Range`, `Schell Creek Range`, `Egan Range`, `Tushar Mountains`, `Black Mountains`, `Dixie National Forest`, `Henry Mountains`, `Island in the Sky`, `Uncompahgre Plateau`, `Grand Staircase-Escalante N.M.`, `Glen Canyon N.R.A.`, `Glen Canyon`, `Kaibab I.R.`, `Vermilion Cliffs`, `Monument Valley`, and `Ute Mountain I.R.`.
  - Rivers labeled: `Bear`, `Green`, `Colorado`, `San Miguel`.
- **Branding & Scale Bar (Bottom)**:
  - Bottom left: **`Microsoft Virtual Earth™`** logo in white text.
  - Bottom right: A white distance scale bar labeled **`80 miles`**, above copyright text reading `© 2006 Microsoft Corporation © 2006 NAVTEQ` and `Image courtesy of NASA`.

  - `▼ United States` (expanded)

    - `▼ Utah` (expanded)
