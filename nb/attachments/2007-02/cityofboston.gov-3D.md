# Boston Redevelopment Authority 3D City Model (`cityofboston.gov-3D.jpg`)

## Overview
A 3D shaded digital rendering of downtown Boston, Massachusetts, and surrounding neighborhoods from the Boston Redevelopment Authority (BRA) 3D Models collection (`cityofboston.gov`). The perspective looks southwest/west from above Boston Harbor toward the downtown skyline, Back Bay, and South Boston waterfront.

## Scene & Geographic Details
- **Water Bodies**: Rendered as flat medium-gray surfaces, including Boston Harbor in the foreground/right, the Fort Point Channel separating South Boston (left) from Downtown (center), and the Charles River Basin along the upper-right edge.
- **Downtown & Financial District (Center)**: A dense cluster of extruded light-gray 3D building massings representing high-rise skyscrapers, commercial blocks, and wharves protruding into the harbor.
- **North End & Charlestown Bridges (Center-Right)**:
  - Dense low-rise residential block massings of the North End on the peninsula to the right of downtown.
  - Detailed 3D representations of the Leonard P. Zakim Bunker Hill Memorial Bridge and highway Interchange loops (I-93) crossing toward Charlestown on the far right.
- **Boston Common, Public Garden & Back Bay (Upper Center)**:
  - Boston Common and the Public Garden are clearly visible as bright green polygons in the middle ground, complete with the light-blue lagoon pond in the Public Garden.
  - Beyond the Public Garden, the regular grid of Back Bay avenue rowhouses leads toward the tall towers of the Prudential Center and John Hancock Tower in the upper center, and the greenEmerald Necklace / Fenway area in the upper right.
- **South Boston Waterfront (Left)**: Large tan/beige parcel footprints with fewer buildings, showing the long rectangular roof of the Boston Convention and Exhibition Center (BCEC), piers, and industrial/port structures along the Reserve Channel and Fort Point Channel.
