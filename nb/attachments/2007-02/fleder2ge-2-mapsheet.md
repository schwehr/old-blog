# Fledermaus 6.4: Map Sheet Preview Window (`fleder2ge-2-mapsheet.png`)

## Overview
Second in an eight-part screenshot series demonstrating how to export a bathymetric/topographic scene from **IVS Fledermaus 6.4** on Mac OS X to Google Earth. This screenshot shows the **`Map Sheet`** dialog window layered over the main Fledermaus window (`Fledermaus -- ...Scenes/santabarbara-fullbasin-land.scene`), previewing the full Santa Barbara Basin and surrounding Southern California coastal land topography.

## Map Sheet Dialog Controls (Left Sidebar)
- **Window Title**: `Map Sheet`
- **`Map Scale:`**:
  - `1 :` **`24000.0`** (active text input field highlighted with a blue focus ring)
- **`Map Size (inches):`**:
  - `Width`: `418.413` (read-only gray field)
  - `Height`: `266.104` (read-only gray field)
- **`Annotated Map Size (inches)`**:
  - `Width`: `420.913` (read-only gray field)
  - `Height`: `268.604` (read-only gray field)
- **Buttons**:
  - **`Convert to Metric`** (blue Aqua button below the size fields)
  - **`Export Map Sheet`** (white pill-shaped button near the bottom left)
  - **`Close`** (white pill-shaped button at the bottom left)

## Map Preview Canvas (Right Area)
- Centered inside a dark gray frame is a white rectangular canvas bordered in black, displaying a top-down shaded-relief composite of the Santa Barbara Channel region:
  - **Land Topography (`land_topo_geo.sd`)**: Rendered in purple for low coastal elevations/valleys, transitioning to deep blue, green, yellow, orange, red, and white on the high peaks of the Transverse Ranges (Santa Ynez, San Rafael, and Topatopa Mountains) to the north and east, plus the Northern Channel Islands (San Miguel, Santa Rosa, Santa Cruz, and Anacapa) in purple at the bottom.
  - **Basin Bathymetry (`santabarbC_bath.sd`)**: Nestled in the Santa Barbara Channel between the mainland coast and the Channel Islands, shaded from brown/olive along the shelf edge through green and cyan to deep blue in the central Santa Barbara Basin.
