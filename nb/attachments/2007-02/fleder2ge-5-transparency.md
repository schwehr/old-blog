# Fledermaus ImageViewer: Setting a Transparent Color (`fleder2ge-5-transparency.png`)

## Overview
Fifth in an eight-part screenshot series demonstrating how to export a bathymetric/topographic scene from **IVS Fledermaus 6.4** on Mac OS X to Google Earth. This cropped screenshot shows navigating the **`Image` -> `Transparency` -> `Set Transparent Color`** menu in **`imageviewer`** so that the white background surrounding the irregular map area becomes transparent.

## Menu Bar & Cascading Menus
- **Top Menu Bar**: Shows the Apple menu, **`imageviewer`**, `File`, `Edit`, **`Image`** (highlighted in blue), `Tools`, `Georeferencing`, `Select`, and `Help`.
- **`Image` Menu**:
  - `Mode` (`▶`)
  - `Colours` (`▶`)
  - **`Transparency`** (`▶`, highlighted in blue)
- **`Transparency` Submenu**:
  - `Add Alpha Channel` (disabled/grayed out)
  - `Remove Alpha Channel`
  - `Clear Alpha Channel`
  - **`Set Transparent Color`** (highlighted in blue)
