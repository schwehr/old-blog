# Google Earth Displaying Exported Fledermaus Santa Barbara Basin Overlay (`fleder2ge-8-ge.png`)

## Overview
Eighth and final screenshot in a series demonstrating how to export a bathymetric/topographic scene from **IVS Fledermaus 6.4** on Mac OS X to Google Earth. It shows **Google Earth** displaying the exported GeoTIFF (`capture.tif`) as a transparent KML ground overlay seamlessly aligned over the Santa Barbara Channel, Northern Channel Islands, and adjacent Southern California coastline.

## Map Viewport & Overlay Details (Right Pane)
- **Fledermaus Ground Overlay**:
  - Covers the Santa Barbara Channel and coastal mountain ranges with transparent surrounding areas so the underlying satellite imagery and ocean floor remain visible.
  - **Onshore Topography**: Colored purple along coastal plains/valleys and blue, green, yellow, and orange/red across the Transverse Ranges. Yellow highway lines (including **`101`** with a route shield near Ventura/Oxnard) drape directly over the overlay.
  - **Offshore Bathymetry**: Colored brown/olive along the shelf and green-to-cyan/blue across the deep Santa Barbara Basin between the mainland and the Northern Channel Islands.
  - **Channel Islands**: `San Miguel Island`, `Santa Rosa Island`, and `Anacapa Island` are labeled in cyan text over the purple island topography of the overlay; `Santa Catalina Island` is visible in the lower right.
- **Surrounding Geography & Labels**:
  - `Bakersfield` visible at the upper right beneath the navigation controls.
  - Greater Los Angeles highways and labels on the right edge: Interstate shields **`210`**, **`10`**, **`105`**, and **`110`**, alongside truncated labels `Los A...`, `anah...`, `Lon...`, and `Sant...`.
- **Viewport Overlays & Status Bar**:
  - Top-right navigation controls: Tilt slider at top, circular compass ring with `N` at top, directional pan arrows in the center, and vertical zoom slider (`+` / `-`) on the right.
  - Scale bar at bottom left: **`73 km`**.
  - Attribution text at bottom center/right: `© 2007 Europa Technologies`, `Image © 2007 TerraMetrics`, `© 2007 Navteq`, and the **`Google™`** logo.
  - Bottom status bar: `Pointer 33°23'12.72" N 118°44'01.78" W elev 37 m`, `Streaming |||||||||| 100%`.

## Left Sidebar Panels
- **Top Toolbar**: Icons for toggle sidebar, add placemark, add polygon, add path, add image overlay, measure ruler, email, and print.
- **`Search` Panel**: Tabs for **`Fly To`** (active), `Find Businesses`, and `Directions`, with an empty search input below `e.g., New York, NY`.
- **`Places` Panel**:
  - Checked items: `Home`, `Hampton Roads`, and `C2CEN` (`USCG Command and Control...`), above an opacity slider and play/stop tour buttons.
- **`Layers` Panel**:
  - `View:` dropdown set to `Core`.
  - Expanded `Primary Database` tree showing `[x] Terrain` and `[ ] Geographic Web`.
