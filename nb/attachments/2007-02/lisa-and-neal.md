# Lisa Tauxe and Neal Driscoll on a Rocky Beach (`lisa-and-neal.JPG`)

## Overview
A candid outdoor photograph showing geologists/geophysicists Lisa Tauxe and Neal Driscoll (the blog author's PhD co-chairs at Scripps Institution of Oceanography) along with a third person examining rocks and pebbles on a cobble beach beside a large weathered sandstone boulder.

## Scene Description
- **Top Center (Lisa Tauxe)**: Standing and leaning forward over the cobble beach, wearing glasses, a dark burgundy/maroon long-sleeved sweater with subtle dark green/teal pattern accents, a teal turtleneck or collar underneath, and dark pants. Her left hand rests on a pitted, light gray rock ledge to steady herself while she smiles and looks down toward the rock specimen being held in the foreground.
- **Middle Left (Neal Driscoll)**: Crouching down on the pebbles in profile facing right. He has brown hair and a mustache, and wears a loose white long-sleeved polo pullover, dark shorts, white socks, and brown shoes/boots. His left arm rests across his knee as he looks intently toward the foreground.
- **Bottom Right (Foreground)**: A woman with reddish-brown hair tied back in a low ponytail with a black hair tie, wearing a blue denim jacket. She is crouched in the foreground facing right, holding a small, dark, rounded cobble/pebble between the fingers of both hands as she inspects it closely.
- **Geological Setting**: The ground is covered with a dense assortment of smooth, rounded beach pebbles and cobbles in varied hues of gray, brown, reddish-pink, and tan. On the right side of the frame is a large, smooth, sculpted coastal boulder or outcrop showing distinct tan/orange-brown iron-stained sedimentary layering on its right face and a paler gray weathered surface with small honeycombed/pitted erosion cavities where Lisa's hand rests. Additional smooth gray boulders are scattered across the pebble beach in the upper left background.
