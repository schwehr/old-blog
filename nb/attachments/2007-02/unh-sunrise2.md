# Winter Sunrise over the UNH Campus (`unh-sunrise2.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format photograph of a vibrant winter sunrise at the University of New Hampshire (UNH) campus in Durham, New Hampshire, taken on the morning of February 28, 2007, featuring backlit steam plumes rising from building chimneys against a glowing orange sky.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Lighting**: The sky transitions from a brilliant pale yellow glow along the low horizon on the right through intense fiery orange and amber in the middle band to a deeper reddish-purple and russet near the top edge.
- **Steam Plumes**: Near the center of the composition, two bright, backlit plumes of exhaust steam rise vertically from rooftop chimney stacks. Illuminated from behind by the rising sun, the billowing steam glows a luminous yellow-white like flames against the orange sky.
- **Silhouettes**:
  - **Foreground Horizon**: The rooflines, gables, and multiple rectangular chimney/vent stacks of campus buildings form a stark, pitch-black silhouette across the bottom third of the frame.
  - **Trees**: Bare, leafless winter deciduous trees frame both the left and right sides of the image, their intricate network of dark branches silhouetted sharply against the warm dawn sky.
