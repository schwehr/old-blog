# 3D Sonar Visualization of Offshore Fish Cages near the Isles of Shoals (`ccom-annual-report.png`)

## Overview
A 3D hydrographic visualization from the 2006 UNH Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) Annual Report, depicting a survey vessel conducting a multibeam sonar survey over two submerged aquaculture fish cages and their mooring lines near the Isles of Shoals, New Hampshire.

## Visualization Details
- **Background & Seafloor**:
  - Set against a solid black background representing the water column and surrounding space.
  - The seafloor is rendered as a shaded, textured bathymetric surface in muted sage-green and pale gray tones, showing subtle seabed roughness and dark acoustic shadows cast behind the vertical structures.
- **Submerged Fish Cages & Moorings (Left & Center)**:
  - Two prominent vertical, columnar/conical green-and-white shaded pillars rise from the seafloor toward mid-water, representing the acoustic surfaces/pillars of the submerged fish cages.
  - Atop each pillar is a dense, diamond-shaped cluster of red and white 3D acoustic point/vector returns outlining the top structure of the cages.
  - Radiating outward from both cages in four directions are catenary mooring lines rendered as chains of small red-and-white vertical acoustic return ticks suspended in the water column and sloping down toward anchor points on the seabed.
- **Survey Vessel & Multibeam Swath (Right)**:
  - A 3D model of a white-and-blue hydrographic survey vessel (resembling the *R/V Coastal Surveyor*) is positioned at the water surface in the upper right.
  - Extending downward and outward from a bow-mounted transducer pole is a wide, vibrant royal-blue triangular fan representing the multibeam echosounder swath intersecting the seafloor along the right side of the scene.
