# Evening Reflections on the Cochecho River in Downtown Dover, NH (`dover-evening.jpg`)

## Overview
An evening photograph taken in downtown Dover, New Hampshire, showing a white commercial building supported on concrete piers directly over the calm waters of the Cochecho River, illuminated by warm late-afternoon sunlight.

## Scene Description
- **Over-Water Building (Center)**:
  - A flat-roofed, single-story white commercial structure spans across the river channel, resting on a row of seven vertical concrete and stone piers/pilings standing in the water.
  - Three square multi-pane windows are spaced evenly along the right half of the white facade, which is brightly lit by golden-hour sun and shows weathered patches and rust/water staining along the lower trim and left corner.
  - A smaller white siding-clad section extends further to the left over the shadowed riverbank.
- **Background Architecture & Trees**:
  - Rising above the flat roof of the white building in the center background is the ornate red-brick upper story, arched windows, and pitched roof with a tall brick chimney of a historic downtown Dover mill/civic building.
  - Bare winter trees and smaller town buildings are visible on the left and right horizons against a pale, clear evening sky.
- **Bridge & Reflections (Foreground)**:
  - Along the right edge of the frame, a concrete bridge sidewalk and black metal railing recede from the foreground toward the street intersection on the right bank.
  - The glassy surface of the river in the foreground creates a sharp, mirror-like inverted reflection of the sunlit white building, its three windows, the dark shaded space between the supporting piers, and the deep blue sky.
