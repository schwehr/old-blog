# Eight-Outboard 2,000 HP Drug-Running Rigid Hull Inflatable Boat (`drug_runner_boat.jpg`)

## Overview
A stern-quarter photograph of a heavily modified, high-speed rigid-hull inflatable boat (RHIB) used for smuggling drugs in Europe, outfitted with eight 250-horsepower Yamaha outboard engines totaling 2,000 horsepower across its transom.

## Vessel & Scene Description
- **Eight Outboard Engines (Foreground Stern)**:
  - Mounted side-by-side across an extra-wide custom transom are eight dark gray **Yamaha VX 250** (VMAX/OX66 250 HP) outboard motors, each marked with `VX 250` in white lettering above a red horizontal stripe.
  - The engines are arranged with two outer pairs mounted slightly higher on the port and starboard wings of the transom and four central engines mounted slightly lower in the middle section.
  - Heavy black hydraulic steering lines, fuel hoses, and electrical looms run along a reinforced metal transom bar above the motors.
  - The dark water below the stern reflects the row of eight engine cowlings and lower units.
- **Hull & Cabin**:
  - The vessel features large-diameter dark gray/black inflatable sponsons (tubes) along both gunwales enclosing a wide gray deck.
  - Forward of the open aft deck is an enclosed, boxy light-gray aluminum wheelhouse/cabin offset slightly to port, featuring an open rear door on the right, a small dark hatch on the left rear bulkhead, interior helm seating, and a white circular marine radar radome mounted on the cabin roof.
- **Dockside Surroundings**:
  - The boat is moored in calm harbor water alongside a dark vertical timber/sheet-pile seawall in the background and the white hull of a larger vessel with two bright orange spherical fenders hanging over its side on the right.
  - In the bottom-right corner is a white watermark reading `www.orsm.`.
