# Pico Computing E-12 CompactFlash FPGA Card (`e-12card_sm.jpg`)

## Overview
A product photograph of the **Pico E-12** miniature FPGA computing module in a Type II CompactFlash form factor, shown both in its brushed-metal enclosure and with its exposed printed circuit board alongside a U.S. quarter dollar coin for scale.

## Hardware & Visual Details
- **Enclosed Module (Bottom Left)**:
  - A square CompactFlash card with a black plastic frame and a brushed silver metal faceplate.
  - Printed on the faceplate is a stylized white tornado/vortex logo next to `PICO` (`the mighty smallest`) and the model designation `E-12` in the lower right corner.
- **Exposed Circuit Board (Top Right)**:
  - Displays the internal green PCB of the E-12 card with its black 50-pin CompactFlash connector along the bottom edge and a metal top edge connector/receptacle.
  - Dominated in the center by a square white/light-gray **Xilinx Virtex-4** FPGA chip package (`VIRTEX-4` printed on the heat spreader/lid) surrounded by surface-mount memory chips and passive components.
- **Scale Reference (Bottom Right)**:
  - A U.S. 25-cent quarter coin (showing George Washington's profile facing left and `LIBERTY`) is placed on the white background next to the card, illustrating that the entire FPGA computer is only slightly larger than a coin.
