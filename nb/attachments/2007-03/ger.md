The large white pop-up window is titled **Core 4–2004** at the top left and includes a blue horizontal scrollbar at the bottom and `Directions: To here - From here` links. Inside the balloon is a 5-panel scientific figure (`a` through `e`) plotted against **Depth (cm)** from `0` to `120` cm:

- **Panel [c] – `χlf (μSI m³/kg)`**: Plots low-frequency magnetic susceptibility from `0` to `4`+ with a red curve decreasing steadily downcore.
- **Panel [d] – `IRM 1T (mA m²/kg)`**: Plots Isothermal Remanent Magnetization at 1 Tesla from `0` to `4`+ with a red curve that drops sharply in the upper 45 cm and remains low below.
- **Panel [e] – `Weight percent water`**: Plots water content from `35` to `55`% with a red data curve overlaid on a smooth dark gray compaction trend curve.
