# Boston Waterfront View from Inside the Institute of Contemporary Art (`ica-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph (`ica-2.jpg`) taken from inside the cantilevered upper level of the Institute of Contemporary Art (ICA) in Boston, looking out through angled floor-to-ceiling windows across the harbor water toward neighboring waterfront buildings and piers.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Framing**: The view is framed by the dark, shadowed interior of the museum on the bottom right and the underside of the building's dramatic overhanging soffit/ceiling along the top. Five diagonal window mullions/cables angle from upper left to lower right across the glass.
- **Waterfront & Pier**: Below the window, greenish-blue harbor water fills the lower left and center foreground, bordered by a timber-piling pier supporting a paved waterfront parking lot. Several cars and SUVs are parked along the lot.
- **Buildings & Flag**:
  - On the left and center sits a historic-style two-story red-brick and red-clapboard waterfront building (the jimmy's Harborside / waterfront restaurant complex) with white window trim, multiple gabled roofs, blue awnings, and a tall square brick chimney.
  - A large American flag flies from a flagpole near the right end of the red building.
  - In the right background across an inlet of water stands a long, industrial gray warehouse/pier building (Boston Marine Industrial Park / Black Falcon area) with repetitive bays of rectangular windows and white structural trusses along its dock.
