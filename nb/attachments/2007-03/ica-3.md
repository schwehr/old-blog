# Samuel Adams Grave Marker at the Granary Burying Ground, Boston (`ica-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up outdoor photograph (`ica-3.jpg`) of the boulder monument and oxidized green bronze memorial plaque marking the grave of Founding Father Samuel Adams in Boston's historic Granary Burying Ground, taken during a March 2007 visit to Boston.

## Monument & Plaque Details
- **Boulder**: A large, rough-hewn dark brown and gray conglomerate ("puddingstone") boulder sits on bare earth, with small pebbles placed along its flat top ridge by visitors.
- **Bronze Plaque**: Mounted on the left-center face of the boulder is a rectangular bronze tablet with a bright verdigris (green patina) finish and raised relief lettering.
- **Sons of the Revolution Marker**: Planted in the ground immediately to the right of the boulder is a matching green-patinated bronze star/cross medallion on a stake, framed by a laurel wreath.
- **Background**: Older dark slate headstones, bare winter tree trunks, and the brick and tan stone facades of multi-story buildings along Tremont Street are visible behind the monument.

## Visible Text & Labels
The inscription on the bronze plaque reads:
- `HERE LIES BURIED`
- `SAMUEL ADAMS`
- `SIGNER OF THE DECLARATION OF INDEPENDENCE`
- `GOVERNOR OF THIS COMMONWEALTH`
- `A LEADER OF MEN AND AN ARDENT PATRIOT`
- `BORN 1722   DIED 1803`
- Bottom seal flanked by text: `MASSACHUSETTS SOCIETY` (left), circular emblem (center), `SONS OF THE REVOLUTION` (right), and `1898` along the lower border.
