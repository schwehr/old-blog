# Cutaway Diagram of an LNG Tanker Hull and Containment System (`lng-tanker.gif`)

## Overview
An educational cutaway diagram (`lng-tanker.gif`) from the Bradwood Landing LNG project website illustrating the double-hull construction and multi-layer membrane cargo containment system of a Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) carrier.

## Diagram Layout & Details
- **Upper Illustration – Cutaway LNG Carrier**:
  - Shows a 3D rendering of a large membrane-type LNG tanker with a red lower hull, black upper hull with a white `G` logo near the bow, green main deck with piping and manifolds, and a white aft superstructure and bridge.
  - A large section of the midship hull and cargo tank is cut away to reveal the interior cargo hold and the thick double-hull wall structure (highlighted in blue).
  - A red circle around the lower port hull cutaway connects via two diverging red callout lines to the detailed cross-section box below.
- **Lower Illustration – Cross-Section Callout Box**:
  - Enclosed in a rounded red border with the blue heading:
    `A Cross-Section of the LNG Ship's Hull and Containment System - In Total, More Than Six Feet in Width`
  - Shows a 3D block cross-section moving from the outer seawater side (left) to the interior LNG cargo tank side (right), with red pointer lines and black text labels:
    - `Ship's Hull` (outer steel plate)
    - `Water Ballast` (wide blue compartment filled with water between the outer and inner hulls)
    - `Ship's Inner Hull` (inner steel bulkhead enclosing the ballast tank)
    - `Secondary Insulation` (bracketed layer of tan/wood-colored insulation panels)
    - `Secondary Membrane` (thin metallic barrier layer between the insulation layers)
    - `Primary Insulation` (bracketed inner insulation layer)
    - `Primary Membrane` (corrugated stainless-steel inner containment surface that directly contacts the LNG cargo)
