# Underwater Mud Volcano Erupting Gas and Mud in the Gulf of Mexico (`mudvolcano1.png`)

## Overview
An underwater video frame capture (`mudvolcano1.png`) taken by an ROV camera (from Immersion Presents / NOAA Flower Garden Banks 2007 expedition in the Gulf of Mexico) showing an active submarine mud volcano venting a plume of gas bubbles and a sinuous mud flow down its flank.

## Scene Description
- **Lighting & Color Palette**: The entire underwater scene is illuminated by artificial ROV lights, casting the seafloor and water column in deep cyan and cobalt blue tones.
- **Mud Volcano Cone & Gas Plume**: In the upper-center background, the rounded summit of the small mud volcano rises against dark blue water. A vertical stream of bright gas bubbles and suspended sediment erupts upward from the vent into the water column.
- **Mud Flow ("River of Mud")**: Originating from the vent at the top of the mound, a well-defined, lighter-toned channel of viscous liquid mud winds in an S-curve near the summit before flowing straight down the slope toward the lower-right foreground, bordered by slightly raised lateral levees and pockmarked seafloor sediment on either side.
