# R/V Coastal Surveyor Out of the Water on Boat Stands (`rvcs-by-michelle-w.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph (`rvcs-by-michelle-w.jpg`, taken by Michelle W.) showing the UNH Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) hydrographic research vessel **R/V Coastal Surveyor** hauled out of the water on blocks and boat stands in a snowy boatyard.

## Scene & Vessel Details
- **R/V Coastal Surveyor (Center)**:
  - The vessel has a bright blue hull (darker blue above the waterline with a white rub rail, lighter blue anti-fouling paint below the waterline) and a white cabin superstructure.
  - Markings visible on the vessel: `COASTAL SURVEYOR` on the starboard cabin side, registration number `NH 1452 B` near the starboard bow, an orange life ring on the aft cabin wall, and a row of white cylindrical fenders hanging along the starboard gunwale.
  - **Bow Strut**: Mounted vertically on the bow is a heavy-duty metallic deployment strut/ram (used for mounting multibeam sonar transducers) with a black upper section and a pulley sheave at the top.
  - **Mast & Electronics**: Above the pilothouse, a white mast assembly supports a Furuno open-array radar scanner (`FURUNO` in blue letters), GPS/navigation domes, and whip antennas.
- **People & Foreground**:
  - Dirty, melting winter snowbanks cover the boatyard ground in the foreground.
  - Two people stand near the starboard side of the hull: one person in the center foreground wearing a bright orange jacket and blue jeans inspecting the boat stands/hull, and a second person further aft wearing a black jacket and dark pants walking away.
  - The hull rests on wooden keel blocks and blue/orange steel tripod boat stands.
- **Surrounding Boats & Background**:
  - On the far left, the blue bow of another hauled-out boat is visible on tripod stands.
  - To the right behind the *Coastal Surveyor*, another workboat with a dark hull and white/red wheelhouse sits on blocks against a backdrop of bare deciduous trees and evergreens under an overcast gray sky.
