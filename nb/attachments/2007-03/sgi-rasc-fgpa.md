# SGI RASC FPGA Blade and Rackmount Enclosure (`sgi-rasc-fgpa.jpg`)

## Overview
A promotional product graphic (`sgi-rasc-fgpa.jpg`) from Silicon Graphics, Inc. (SGI) showcasing **SGI RASC (Reconfigurable Application-Specific Computing) Technology**, featuring a rackmount chassis on the left and an exposed internal FPGA/memory blade on the right.

## Visual Elements & Hardware Details
- **Background & Typography**: Set against a dark-to-light gray horizontal gradient background with a glossy reflective floor, the top-left text in white reads:
  - **SGI® RASC™ Technology**
  - `Application Acceleration with FPGA-based`
  - `Reconfigurable Computing`
- **Rackmount Chassis (Lower Left)**: A 2U dark gray rackmount enclosure with honeycomb front ventilation grilles on the left and right, a central control bezel with a small LCD status screen and control buttons, the white `sgi` logo to the left of the bezel, and `RASC Technology` printed near the bottom right of the front panel.
- **Internal FPGA Blade (Right)**: Standing vertically at an angle is an open server blade exposing its green printed circuit board (PCB):
  - **FPGAs / Heatsinks**: Four large square silver finned heatsinks arranged in a 2×2 grid on the left half of the board, cooling the FPGA chips and interface ASICs.
  - **Memory Slots**: Two vertical banks of eight DIMM slots each (16 slots total) occupying the right half of the board, populated with memory modules.
  - **Connectors**: Dual high-density NUMAlink / backplane connectors on the rear (left) edge and a perforated metallic faceplate on the front (right) edge.
