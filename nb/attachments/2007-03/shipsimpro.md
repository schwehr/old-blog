# Ship Simulator Professional Dual-Monitor Navigation Banner (`shipsimpro.jpg`)

## Overview
A promotional banner graphic (`shipsimpro.jpg`) for **Ship Simulator Professional** by VSTEP (with `WinGPS` branding in the bottom-right corner), illustrating how the 3D bridge simulator pairs with an external electronic navigation chart (ECDIS / chartplotter) across dual monitors.

## Banner Layout & Visual Details
- **Center Title & Logo**:
  - Centered against a black background with a faint dark-gray compass rose emblem behind it is the title **Ship Simulator** in bold white italic sans-serif lettering above **Professional** in bold golden-yellow italic lettering.
  - In the lower-right corner, `WinGPS` is written in white script font.
- **Left Setup (Monitor + Hardware Chartplotter)**:
  - Shows a desktop LCD monitor on the far left displaying a 3D first-person view inside a modern ship's bridge looking out over water, with two captain's chairs, radar/conning displays, engine telegraph controls, and a central helm console.
  - Next to it sits a dedicated marine chartplotter hardware unit with physical control buttons on its right bezel, displaying a nautical chart with shipping lanes and vessel tracks.
- **Right Setup (Dual Desktop Monitors)**:
  - Shows two side-by-side desktop LCD monitors angled slightly toward each other:
    - The **left monitor** displays a full-screen electronic nautical chart (`WinGPS`) with a data sidebar on the left and plotted waterways, depth contours, and AIS/vessel position markers.
    - The **right monitor** displays the 3D ship's bridge interior view matching the simulator screen on the far left.
  - All monitors cast soft reflections onto the glossy black surface below them.
