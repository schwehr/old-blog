# Fledermaus Forum Simple Machines Forum Banner (`smf.png`)

## Overview
A cropped web banner graphic from the IVS Forum (`ivsforum.com`), an online user discussion board for Interactive Visualization Systems (IVS) Fledermaus software powered by the Simple Machines Forum (SMF) platform, posted on March 13, 2007.

## Banner Design & Layout
- **Background**: A horizontal rectangular banner with a smooth vertical gradient transitioning from a medium-dark slate blue at the top to a lighter steel blue-gray at the bottom.
- **Left Title**: Set in bold, white sans-serif text on the left side is the title **"Fledermaus Forum"** with a subtle dark drop shadow.
- **Center Icon**: In the middle of the banner is a stylized light-gray icon of a simple machine—specifically a lever/seesaw consisting of a triangular fulcrum with a small circular pivot point supporting a tilted plank angled upward to the right.
- **Right Subtitle**: To the right of the lever icon, set in thin, light-blue/white sans-serif lowercase lettering, is the platform name **"simple machines forum"**.
