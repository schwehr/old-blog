# Bare Tree Branches Against Blue Sky (`tree1.JPG`)

## Overview
An upward-angled outdoor photograph looking up into the bare, leafless canopy of a deciduous tree in Kurt Schwehr's backyard against a clear blue late-winter sky, taken on March 9, 2007 as part of a self-assigned photography exercise.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Trunk**: At the bottom center, the thick, dark silhouette of the upper tree trunk splits into several primary boughs. Subtle ridges of rough, furrowed bark catch faint sidelight on the right fork, while the main mass of the trunk is in deep shadow.
- **Branch Structure**: Gnarled, twisting branches extend outward and upward in all directions, dividing into a dense, intricate network of smaller limbs and fine twigs that fill the frame from edge to edge. Several limbs on the left and right exhibit sharp, sinuous bends where side branches were previously pruned or broken.
- **Lighting & Sky**: Bright directional sunlight from the right illuminates the undersides and edges of several upper and right-hand branches, giving them a pale gray-brown highlight that contrasts sharply with the dark silhouettes against the vibrant, cloudless azure sky.
- **Framing**: The square-ish composition is bordered by a thin black-and-white inner frame around the edges of the image.
