# Close-Up of Furrowed Tree Bark (`tree2.JPG`)

## Overview
A macro-style close-up photograph looking steeply upward along the rough, deeply furrowed bark of a backyard tree trunk, taken on March 9, 2007 as the second part of a backyard tree photo assignment.

## Scene & Texture Details
- **Bark Ridges & Furrows**: Deep vertical and slightly diagonal fissures divide the bark into thick, rugged, scaly ridges that converge toward the upper right as the trunk tapers upward.
- **Color & Lichen/Moss**: The bark displays warm golden-brown, tan, and reddish-brown tones warmed by direct sunlight, interspersed with dusty greenish-yellow patches of moss and lichen nestled within the crevices, particularly along the left side.
- **Depth of Field & Lighting**: Shallow depth of field keeps the central bark ridges in crisp focus while the immediate bottom foreground and the upper-right receding trunk soften into a gentle blur. A small wedge of bright white sky is visible at the upper right corner.
- **Framing**: The image is enclosed by a thin black-and-white border.
