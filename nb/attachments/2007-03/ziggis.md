# zigGIS Open Source PostGIS Plugin Logo (`ziggis.jpg`)

## Overview
The circular project logo for **zigGIS**, an open-source plugin enabling ESRI ArcGIS to load and view PostGIS spatial database layers, featured in a March 6, 2007 blog post.

## Graphic Elements & Typography
- **Circular Emblem**: A white circular badge framed by a thick lime-green outer ring and a thinner yellow-green inner ring.
- **Rocket Illustration**: Rising diagonally from the lower left toward the upper right is a stylized, faceted gray-and-silver cartoon rocket ship or spaceplane with swept delta wings, lower tail fins, and a black-outlined light-gray cockpit canopy near its pointed nose cone. Twin plumes of golden-yellow exhaust flames billow from dual thrusters at the base of the rocket, overlapping the lower-left portion of the green ring.
- **Text Overlay**: Across the center of the circle, superimposed over the midsection of the rocket, is the stylized title **"zigGiS"** rendered in a blocky, futuristic, double-lined light-blue font with a white outline and a subtle gray drop shadow (the "z" and "g" resemble digital segments "2" and "9").
