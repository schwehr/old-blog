# NOAA and Coast Survey 200th Anniversary Wreath-Laying Ceremony at the Jefferson Memorial (`brennan.jpg`)

## Overview
A formal group photograph taken inside the Thomas Jefferson Memorial in Washington, D.C., on April 13, 2007, celebrating the 200th anniversary of the Survey of the Coast (NOAA's Office of Coast Survey) with a wreath-laying ceremony. CCOM/JHC graduate Lt. Rick Brennan (NOAA Corps) stands second from the left.

## Scene & People
- **Group Lineup**: Fourteen individuals—twelve uniformed NOAA Corps officers in dark navy service dress uniforms with gold rank stripes on their sleeves and white peaked combination caps, and two men in dark business suits—stand in a horizontal row across the marble floor in front of the bronze statue of Thomas Jefferson.
  - **Center**: U.S. Secretary of Commerce Carlos Gutierrez stands in the center in a dark suit, white shirt, and gray tie, flanked by NOAA Corps officers of various ranks (from ensign/lieutenant up to rear admiral).
  - **Second from Left**: CCOM/JHC alumnus Rick Brennan stands second from the left in his NOAA Corps uniform with gold sleeve stripes.
  - **Far Right**: A civilian official in a dark suit and yellow tie stands at the far right end of the lineup.
- **Memorial Setting & Wreath**:
  - Directly on the marble floor in front of Secretary Gutierrez lies a circular floral memorial wreath composed of greenery, red and white carnations/roses, and red, white, and blue ribbons. A second wreath is partially visible behind the group against the black pedestal base of the statue.
  - Above the group, the lower bronze cloak, breeches, boots, and pedestal of the monumental Thomas Jefferson statue rise between massive fluted stone columns backed by bright daylight.
  - On the curved interior stone wall at the upper left, a portion of the engraved Jefferson quotation is visible, ending with the lines: *"...THE TIMES, WE MIGHT AS WELL / REQUIRE A MAN TO WEAR STILL THE / COAT WHICH FITTED HIM WHEN A BOY / AS CIVILIZED SOCIETY TO REMAIN / EVER UNDER THE REGIMEN OF THEIR / BARBAROUS ANCESTORS."*
