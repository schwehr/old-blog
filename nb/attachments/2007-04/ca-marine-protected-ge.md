# California Marine Protected Areas in Google Earth (`ca-marine-protected-ge.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth displaying a 3D oblique coastal view of the Monterey Bay and Central California coastline overlaid with the Ocean Conservancy's `MPAsSouthCentralCoast.kmz` layer, featuring an open information pop-up balloon for the **Soquel Canyon State Marine Conservation Area**, posted on April 20, 2007.

## Map & 3D Coastal View (Right)
- **Perspective & Coastline**: Looking southward across Monterey Bay toward the Monterey Peninsula, Carmel Bay, and Point Lobos along the California coast. Bathymetric contour lines (in light blue) trace the deep submarine canyon cutting into Monterey Bay.
- **Marine Protected Area Polygons & Labels**: Outlined polygons in light blue and pink mark designated Marine Protected Areas along the coast and offshore, including:
  - **Soquel Canyon** (marked with a yellow/blue/orange "F" icon and yellow label in the foreground)
  - **Portuguese Ledge SMCA**
  - **Lovers Point SMR**
  - **Asilomar SMR**
  - **Carmel Bay SMCA**
  - **Carmel Pinnacles SMR**
  - **Pt. Lobos SMCA**
- **Google Earth Attribution**: At the bottom center and bottom right are the standard credits: `© 2007 Europa Technologies`, `Image © 2007 TerraMetrics`, `Image AMBAG`, and the `© 2007 Google™` logo.

## Fact Sheet Pop-Up Balloon (Left)
Occupying the left half of the screen is a large tan-bordered Google Earth pop-up balloon displaying **Factsheet 2: Soquel Canyon State Marine Conservation Area**:
- **Header Banner**: Light blue and cream banner titled **"Soquel Canyon State Marine Conservation Area"** with **"Factsheet 2"** in a circular badge at the top right.
- **Key Statistics (Upper Left)**:
  - **Size of MPA:** `23.41 mi²`
  - **Depth range of MPA:** `247–2113 feet`
  - **Key habitats protected:** `Submarine canyon, deepwater rock, cold water corals.`
- **Photographs**:
  - A large underwater photograph showing a dense school of silver fish swimming in deep blue canyon waters.
  - A smaller inset close-up photo on the left showing the head and open mouth of a bottom-dwelling rockfish resting among white invertebrates.
- **Inset Map (Lower Left)**: A 2D nautical map of Monterey Bay (showing Santa Cruz, Watsonville, Moss Landing, Marina, and State Waters boundary) highlighting the dark-blue rectangular polygons for **Soquel Canyon SMCA** and **Portuguese Ledge SMCA**, complete with a `0 1 2 3 Nautical Miles` scale bar and photo credit (`Photos © Marc Shargel / LivingSeaImages.com`).
- **Descriptive Bullet Points (Lower Right)**: Under the heading **"The Monterey Submarine Canyon is a unique and biologically productive habitat."**, five bullet points describe the canyon's depth, capture of an entire side-branch of the Monterey Submarine Canyon, rockfish/coral/sponge habitats, Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute (MBARI) submarine surveys and monitoring station, and allowance for commercial/recreational pelagic finfish fishing (salmon, mackerel, sardines) in surface waters.
- **Footer Links**: A hyperlink reading `Download All Seven April 2007 Fact Sheets in PDF format` above `Copyright © 2007 GreenInfo Network`.
