# Roland and Robert Posing with the Fetch 3.5 AUV at UNH (`fetch-unh2.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor full-length portrait of researchers Roland Arsenault and Robert standing beside the **Fetch 3.5** Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) in the high-bay acoustic/engineering test tank facility at UNH's Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory (CCOM/JHC) on April 13, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **People**:
  - **Left**: Roland stands to the left of the AUV wearing glasses, a red zip-up hoodie, khaki cargo pants, and black shoes. He holds a small clamshell handheld computer/PDA in his right hand and rests his left hand on the top lifting bar of the AUV's aluminum frame, where a green nylon lifting strap is attached.
  - **Right**: Robert stands to the right of the AUV wearing glasses, a dark brown t-shirt, dark blue jeans, and black shoes, smiling at the camera while holding a tablet laptop computer in his left hand.
- **Fetch 3.5 AUV (Center Foreground)**:
  - Positioned diagonally in its rectangular aluminum transport/launch frame on the wet concrete floor.
  - The vehicle features a white-and-yellow streamlined hull, black dive planes/fins near the bow, a glossy black hemispherical nose cap, bright green cylindrical tubes along the top, and two large white cylindrical fenders mounted to the sides of the aluminum cage.
- **Background**: Behind the men is the concrete wall of the large indoor test tank, with metal railings, industrial lighting, and high-bay laboratory equipment visible in the background.
