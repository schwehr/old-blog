# OpenInventor / Coin3D Material Properties Experiment in `simpleview` (`materials.png`)

## Overview
A 3D computer graphics rendering produced with `simpleview` (an OpenInventor / Coin3D viewer modified to use a single light source) demonstrating the visual effects of different `Material` node properties (`ambientColor`, `diffuseColor`, `specularColor`, and `shininess`) on spheres and cubes for use in Pydro, posted on April 11, 2007.

## Scene & 3D Objects
Against a solid black background framed by a thin gray border, four green 3D geometric primitives are arranged in perspective:
- **Large Wireframe-Overlay Sphere (Lower Left)**: A large green shaded sphere overlaid with a bright red latitude-longitude wireframe mesh (`12` longitudinal segments visible across the front hemisphere), indicating a selected or highlighted object in OpenInventor.
- **Foreground Cube (Bottom Center-Right)**: A bright lime-green cube tilted at an angle, outlined along its visible outer silhouette and right-hand face edges in thin red wireframe lines.
- **Midground Cube (Center)**: A smaller, un-outlined bright green cube floating between the large wireframe sphere and the upper-right spheres.
- **Concentric / Overlapping Spheres (Upper Right)**: A bright, smoothly shaded green inner sphere with a soft specular highlight near its center sits inside or in front of a larger, dark forest-green circle/sphere rendered with flat ambient-only or low-diffuse shading.
