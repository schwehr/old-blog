# NOAA Virtual Island in Second Life (`noaa-second-life.jpg`)

## Overview
This image is an annotated overhead aerial view (orthographic screenshot) of **NOAA's virtual Earth-science island** in the 3D online virtual world **Second Life**, developed by the NOAA Earth System Research Laboratory (ESRL) and announced on April 30, 2007. The island features interactive educational exhibits simulating atmospheric, oceanic, and geophysical phenomena.

## Scene & Island Layout
The virtual island is surrounded by textured blue-gray ocean water and divided into distinct themed terrain zones connected by brick plazas, roads, and wooden piers:

- **Northwest — Airport & Hurricane / Weather Exhibits**:
  - A dark gray asphalt runway (`6` at the north end, `27` at the south taxiway) and tarmac apron sit on the western side of the island.
  - A white four-engine turboprop **NOAA Hurricane Hunter research aircraft** (WP-3D Orion style) is parked diagonally on the ramp, with two smaller white twin-engine aircraft parked below it.
  - A large silver-gray arched hangar stands at the northeast corner of the tarmac.
  - White text labels on the left and top-left read **`Hurricane Ride`** (with a white pointer line to the runway/apron) and **`Weather Balloon`** near the southern end of the runway.

- **North-Central — Real Time Weather Map & Plaza**:
  - South of the hangar is a reddish-brown brick courtyard featuring a circular ground map display (**`Real Time Weather Map`**) and a small turquoise kiosk.
  - To the east of the courtyard is a multi-tiered tan building complex labeled **`Meeting Hall`** at the top center, bordered by sandy beaches with palm trees.

- **Northeast & East — Tsunami Exhibit**:
  - A rocky green hill rises in the northeast corner, topped by a small white-and-black lighthouse on the northern point and several cross-shaped markers or wind turbines along the ridge.
  - Directly east is a recessed bay and a flat coastal residential strip with a beach, a two-lane road, a green-roofed house, and a brown-roofed building, labeled **`Tsunami`** in the open water to the north (where users can trigger a simulated tsunami wave).

- **Center — Science On a Sphere**:
  - Dominating the center of the island is a large, gleaming white geodesic/spherical dome building housing NOAA's **`Science on a Sphere`** exhibit, pointed to by a white line and label at the lower right.

- **Southwest — Glacier**:
  - The southwestern corner of the island is covered in jagged, fractured white and pale-blue ice sheets and calving icebergs floating in the water, with a small wooden dock extending between two ice lobes. It is labeled **`Glacier`** in the lower left.

- **South — Sea Life & Pier**:
  - Extending southwest from a sandy beach and a red-roofed pavilion at the southern shore is a long white pier with a yellow submarine/submersible moored at its tip. This underwater exploration area is labeled **`Sea Life`** at the bottom center.
