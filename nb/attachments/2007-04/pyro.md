# Pyro (Python Remote Objects) Flaming Logo (`pyro.jpg`)

## Overview
This image is the logo graphic for **Pyro** (**Py**thon **R**emote **O**bjects), a distributed object middleware system written in Python. The graphic features the word **`PYRO`** rendered in bold, three-dimensional serif capital letters engulfed in stylized flames.

## Visual Details
- **Typography**: The four capital letters **`P Y R O`** are spaced evenly across the horizontal banner in a wide, heavy serif typeface. The letters are shaded in dark crimson, burnt orange, and deep brown with bright orange-yellow highlights along their upper edges, giving them a glowing, metallic appearance.
- **Flame Effect**: Soft, semi-transparent tongues of fire and smoke rise from the top edges of all four letters against a plain white background. The flames transition from bright yellow and orange at the base (especially above the `Y`, `R`, and `O`) to smoky reddish-brown and pale gray wisps as they fade into the white background at the top.
