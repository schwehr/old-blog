# 3D Coastal Relief Model of Stellwagen Bank and the Gulf of Maine (`sbnms-coastalrelief-3s.png`)

## Overview
This image is a 3D perspective shaded-relief rendering of the 3-arc-second **NOAA NGDC Coastal Relief Model** covering the **Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (SBNMS)**, Massachusetts Bay, Cape Cod, and the western Gulf of Maine, visualized in IVS Fledermaus against a black background with a thin gray border.

## Geographic & Bathymetric Features
The digital elevation model (DEM) is viewed from the south-southwest looking north-northeast along the New England coast:

- **Landmasses (Gray/Silver Shaded Relief)**:
  - Terrestrial topography is rendered in monochromatic gray/silver shaded relief on the left (west) side of the model, with higher elevations and mountainous terrain (southern New Hampshire and Maine hills) rising with vertical exaggeration in the upper-left corner.
  - **Cape Cod**: The distinctive curved hook of Cape Cod extends prominently into the lower-right foreground, enclosing **Cape Cod Bay** (shaded yellow-green).
  - **Islands & Bays (Bottom Left to Center)**:
    - **Martha's Vineyard** (center-bottom gray island) and part of **Nantucket** (bottom-right gray island) border **Nantucket Sound** and **Vineyard Sound** (warm orange-yellow).
    - **Buzzards Bay** and **Narragansett Bay** cut deeply into the southern Massachusetts and Rhode Island coastline in the lower-left corner, with **Block Island** and the eastern tip of Long Island visible at the far bottom-left edge.
  - **Massachusetts Coastline (Center)**: **Boston Harbor** sits at the indentation north of Cape Cod Bay, with **Cape Ann** jutting out northeast of Boston, and the New Hampshire and southern Maine coast (including Casco Bay) extending toward the top center/right.

- **Submarine Bathymetry (Rainbow Color Ramp)**:
  - **Shallow Nearshore & Shoals (Orange to Yellow)**: Very shallow estuaries, harbors, Nantucket Shoals (southeast of Cape Cod with complex sand-wave ridges), and Georges Bank approaches in the lower right.
  - **Continental Shelf & Banks (Yellow-Green to Green)**:
    - **Stellwagen Bank** is clearly visible as a crescent-shaped green/yellow-green submarine plateau stretching north-northwest between the tip of Cape Cod (Race Point) and Cape Ann, separating Massachusetts Bay on the west from the deeper Wilkinson Basin on the east.
    - **Jeffreys Ledge** forms a narrow, sinuous green ridge extending northeast from Cape Ann toward the Maine coast.
  - **Deep Basins (Cyan, Dark Blue, and Purple)**:
    - East of Stellwagen Bank and Jeffreys Ledge, the seafloor drops into the deeper basins of the **Gulf of Maine** (including Wilkinson Basin and Scantum Basin), colored in deep blue and violet/purple at the deepest levels along the right side of the dataset.
