# First Batch of Garden Seedlings in Window Bins (`seeds-first-batch.jpg`)

## Overview
This image is a photograph of two clear plastic storage tubs placed side by side on an indoor windowsill, holding 22 small paper cups filled with dark potting soil and Newly sprouted **snow pea** and **sunflower** seedlings (one week after planting).
