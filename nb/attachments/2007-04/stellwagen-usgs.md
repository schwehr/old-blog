# 3D USGS Multibeam Bathymetry of Stellwagen Bank (`stellwagen-usgs.png`)

## Overview
This image is a 3D perspective shaded-relief bathymetric visualization of the **U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) multibeam sonar dataset** covering the **Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (SBNMS)** and eastern Massachusetts Bay, rendered in IVS Fledermaus against a black background with a thin gray border.

## Bathymetric & Geomorphological Details
Viewed from the south looking north across Massachusetts Bay toward Cape Ann:

- **Stellwagen Bank (Foreground Center-Right)**:
  - The prominent, smooth-topped sandy shoal of **Stellwagen Bank** curves across the right-center foreground in bright yellow and warm orange-yellow tones (indicating shallow depths of ~20–35 meters).
  - Its steep western flank drops off into the deeper green and cyan waters of **Massachusetts Bay** on the left, while its eastern edge slopes down into dark blue and purple deeper waters along the right margin.

- **Western Massachusetts Bay & Coastal Approaches (Left)**:
  - Along the western (left) boundary of the survey polygon, shallower nearshore ledges and glacial terrain appear in yellow and orange-brown, with two narrow survey trackline extensions protruding off the western edge (one forming a small `A`-like triangle on the mid-left and another forming an `L`-shaped trackline near the lower-left cutout).
  - Between the western coast and Stellwagen Bank lies the broad, smooth depression of **Stellwagen Basin**, shaded in cyan and light blue with scattered small drumlins or rocky knolls rising in green.

- **Northern Complex & Jeffreys Ledge (Upper Center & Right)**:
  - North of Stellwagen Bank, the seafloor transitions into a rugged, glacially sculpted landscape of ridges, channels, and deep scoured depressions colored in dark blue and magenta/purple (representing the deepest basins in the survey).
  - In the upper center, the southern portion of **Jeffreys Ledge** extends northeastward as an elongated green/yellow-green ridge.
