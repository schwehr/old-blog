# QuickTime Player Showing Google Earth AIS Animation (`ais-1-avi-without-perian.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X QuickTime Player window screenshot playing an AVI video file (`ais-1.avi`) recorded with CamStudio, showing a 3D Google Earth view of AIS vessel traffic and EarthNC nautical chart overlays along the Delaware Bay entrance and Mid-Atlantic coast. Taken on May 9, 2007, to demonstrate successful playback of a Microsoft Video 1 (CRAM) AVI file after removing the conflicting Perian 0.5 QuickTime component.

## QuickTime Player Interface & Controls
- **Title Bar**: Brushed-metal Mac OS X window header with red, yellow, and green window control buttons on the left and a document icon titled `ais-1.avi` centered at the top.
- **Playback Timeline**: Positioned below the video frame, displaying elapsed time `00:00:05` at the left and a horizontal scrub bar with a dark gray triangular playhead near the beginning of the track.
- **Transport & Audio Controls**: Along the bottom brushed-metal panel:
  - A speaker icon and horizontal volume slider on the far left.
  - Five circular transport buttons in the center: Jump to Beginning (`|<<`), Rewind (`<<`), Play (`>`), Fast Forward (`>>`), and Jump to End (`>>|`).
  - Diagonal resize grip lines in the bottom-right corner.

## Google Earth Video Content
- **Geographic View**: An oblique 3D perspective looking northwest across the Atlantic Ocean toward the mouth of Delaware Bay, Cape May (New Jersey), Cape Henlopen (Delaware), the Delmarva Peninsula, and Chesapeake Bay, with `Washington D.C.`, `Maryland`, `Baltimore`, `Delaware`, `Philadelphia`, and `New Jersey` labeled on the terrain.
- **Time Slider**: At the top center of the Google Earth viewport, a temporal range slider shows the window `25 OCT 2006 10:44pm` to `25 OCT 2006 11:50pm`.
- **Navigation Controls**: In the upper right of the Google Earth viewport, the circular compass/joystick ring (with `N` indicator and directional arrows), zoom slider (`+` to `-`), and tilt slider are overlaid.
- **Nautical Chart & AIS Overlays**:
  - **Traffic Separation Lanes**: Bright green outlines marking the incoming and outgoing shipping lanes converging at the entrance to Delaware Bay, with a green directional arrow pointing seaward.
  - **Buoys & Navigational Aids**: Numerous red and green buoy icons, lighthouse icons, orange `CG` (Coast Guard) square markers, and white/orange diamond markers along the coast and bay entrance.
  - **Visible Labels**:
    - `Chincoteague Inlet Lighted Whistle Buoy CI` (left)
    - `Lighted Whistle` (center)
    - `Hereford Inlet Lighted Whistle Buoy H` (right)
    - Buoy/station identifiers including `Q`, `10`, `8`, `2`, `5CG`, `6`, `LG`, `4`, `1FS`, `CG`, `1HC`, `2MS`, `3FB`, `4FB`, `2FB`, and `DG` (inside rectangular dumping ground / danger zone boxes offshore).
- **Status Bar & Attribution**:
  - Center bottom attribution: `Image © 2007 NASA`, `© 2007 Europa Technologies`, `Image © 2007 TerraMetrics`, and the `©2006 Google` logo in the lower right.
  - Bottom status bar: `Pointer 38°44'19.67" N 74°47'41.72" W elev 0 ft Streaming |||||||||| 100%` on the left, and `Eye alt 78.34 mi` on the right.
