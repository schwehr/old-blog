# AIS Base Station Setup in Communications Closet at NOAA Atlantic Marine Center (`comm-closet.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph of a telecommunications and electrical equipment closet at the NOAA Atlantic Marine Center in Norfolk, Virginia, taken during the U.S. Hydro 2007 conference in May 2007. A bright green rectangle highlights the temporary AIS Base Station hardware and Mac laptop set up by Kurt Schwehr, with GPS and VHF antenna cables routed up to the building's roof.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **Highlighted AIS Setup (Green Box)**:
  - Inside the annotated bright green rectangle in the lower-left foreground sits a black wheeled equipment cart.
  - On top of the cart is a black rack-mount chassis (the AIS Base Station / transponder unit) with a silver Apple PowerBook / MacBook Pro laptop resting on top of it and coiled dark coaxial cables on the right side of the unit.
  - Directly behind the laptop is a white oscillating pedestal fan aimed at the equipment for cooling.
- **Surrounding Closet Infrastructure**:
  - **Back Wall**: Thick black coaxial cables and metal conduits run along the upper wall and down into cylindrical metallic surge arrestors/filters mounted next to the cart. To the right, rows of industrial telecommunications punch-down blocks, circuit modules, and orange warning labels are mounted on plywood backboards beneath a white wall-mounted unit.
  - **Right Side**: A wooden desk holds a beige tower server / UPS unit with a bright green Ethernet/serial cable plugged into its front panel, next to a vertical metal relay rack packed with rows of 66/110 telephone punch-down blocks. A tan folding chair sits tucked between the black cart and the desk.
  - **Left Foreground**: Red and black cables hang inside the open doorway frame under warm yellow/amber tungsten/fluorescent room lighting.
