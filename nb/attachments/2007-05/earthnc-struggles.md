# EarthNC Charts of Norfolk Harbor and Hampton Roads in Google Earth (`earthnc-struggles.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth overhead view of Hampton Roads, the Elizabeth River, Norfolk, and Portsmouth, Virginia, displaying EarthNC KMZ nautical chart layers after manually extracting the Virginia ENC charts and disabling the cluttered depth sounding layers. Created on May 8, 2007, in preparation for the U.S. Hydro 2007 conference.

## Geographic Features & Landmarks
- **Waterways & Coastline**: Shows the confluence of the James River / Hampton Roads in the upper left and top center flowing south into the Elizabeth River shipping channel between `Portsmouth` (west/southwest bank) and `Norfolk` (east bank).
- **Craney Island**: The large rectangular dredged-material containment facility (`Craney Island`) protrudes northward into Hampton Roads to the left of center, Divided into three horizontal basins and outlined in yellow chart boundaries.
- **Naval Station Norfolk**: Along the eastern side of the main north-south channel in the upper half of the image, a row of large gray naval piers extends into the channel, highlighted with green pier outlines.

## Chart Overlays, Navigational Aids & Placemarks
- **Channel & Depth Contours**:
  - Dark green shaded dredged channel bands mark the main shipping lanes turning from Hampton Roads southward down the Elizabeth River.
  - Colored contour lines (blue, cyan, yellow, green, red) trace depth curves and shoreline boundaries along the riverbanks and tributaries.
- **Buoy Light Characteristics & Fog Signals**:
  - Dozens of red, green, yellow, and white teardrop light flares mark buoys and channel markers along the shipping channel, labeled with their flash periods in color-coded text: `1s`, `2.5s`, `4s`, `6s`, `10s`, and `0s`.
  - Multiple white concentric-arc `FOG` horn signal icons appear at key channel junctions and bridge/tunnel crossings.
  - Two white rectangular `DG` (degaussing range / dumping ground) markers sit near the southeast corner of Craney Island.
- **Custom Red Pin Placemarks**:
  - `C2CEN`: Red pin with a black dot just south of Craney Island (`Coast Guard Command and Control Engineering Center`).
  - `NOAA Atlantic Marine Center`: Red pin on the eastern bank of the Elizabeth River near downtown Norfolk.
  - `US Hydro 2007`: Red pin slightly further south along the downtown Norfolk / Portsmouth waterfront marking the conference venue.
