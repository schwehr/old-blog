# EarthNC Nautical Chart with AIS Vessel Tracks at Delaware Bay Entrance (`earthnc_and_ais.png`)

## Overview
An oblique 3D Google Earth screenshot combining EarthNC Electronic Navigational Chart (ENC) layers with white AIS vessel tracklines and white square vessel markers along the Traffic Separation Scheme (TSS) at the mouth of Delaware Bay (Cape Henlopen / Cape May), captured on May 8, 2007.

## Map & Chart Features
- **Traffic Separation Lanes (Green)**:
  - Two prominent bright-green-outlined shipping approaches converge toward the entrance of Delaware Bay near the top right:
    - A long southwest-to-northeast two-way traffic lane extending diagonally across the center-left, divided by a central green separation zone and tipped with a small green directional arrow at its northeastern end.
    - A north-south two-way traffic lane on the right side of the frame, with a green southbound arrow at the bottom left lane and a northbound green arrow at the top right lane.
- **AIS Vessel Tracks & Positions**:
  - Thin white lines trace the historical paths of vessels moving through both traffic lanes and curving northward into the bay entrance.
  - White square icons mark individual ship positions along the lanes, with two ships accompanied by small blue `i` information icons (one at the southwest entrance of the left lane and one at the northern top of the right lane).
- **Buoys, Coast Guard Stations & Contours**:
  - Green can/pillar buoy icons with white labels: `1FS` (upper left), `1HC` (upper right-center), `3HC`, and `5` (upper right).
  - Red nun/pillar buoy icons with white labels: `6`, `4`, `2` (along the northern entrance), `2MS` (middle right), and an unlabeled red buoy in the lower-right corner.
  - Along the coastline at the top: orange `CG` (Coast Guard) station rectangles, white/orange diamond markers, yellow/orange flag icons, and a white square caution icon (`!`) near the upper left labeled `LG`.
  - Red and blue bathymetric contour lines wind across the dark blue ocean floor.
