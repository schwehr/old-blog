# Rototilled and Planted Backyard Garden with Wire Cages (`garden-after.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph of Kurt Schwehr's fenced backyard vegetable garden after removing the black plastic sheeting, double-digging the back row, working in 360 pounds of compost with a borrowed rototiller, and planting seedlings in four mounded soil rows. Taken in late May 2007 (`05.31.2007`).

## Scene Description
- **Fencing & Background**:
  - The square garden plot is enclosed by a white wooden picket fence along the left, back, and right sides, with the tips of the front white picket fence visible along the bottom right edge.
  - Behind the back fence stands a sage-green clapboard garage wall with two dark rectangular windows framed in white trim.
  - Lush green bushes and tree foliage grow along the outside of the right picket fence.
- **Soil Rows & Plantings**:
  - Four horizontal raised/mounded rows of freshly tilled, light-brown soil run across the plot, separated by shallow walking paths.
  - **Foreground Row**: Seven small green vegetable starts are planted in darker, moist compost pockets spaced evenly along the front ridge.
  - **Middle & Back Rows**: Numerous circular wire tomato cages and metal plant supports are arranged over young green seedlings, accompanied by small white plastic plant identification tags stuck into the dirt.
  - **Back Row (Against the Fence)**: A line of clear glass jars and green plastic bottles/cloches sits along the rear double-dug row near the base of the white fence.
- **Left Edge**:
  - A short section of tan brick edging/path is visible between the front and second rows on the left.
  - A bright red plastic tub/bucket holding a hand trowel sits just inside the left fence.
