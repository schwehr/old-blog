# Backyard Garden Covered in Black Plastic Sheeting (`garden-before.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph of Kurt Schwehr's fenced backyard garden plot covered in black plastic sheeting weighed down with bricks to suppress weeds over the winter and spring prior to tilling. Taken in spring 2007 and posted on May 31, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Fencing & Background**:
  - The rectangular garden bed is surrounded on all four sides by a white wooden picket fence (the tops of four white pickets of the front gate/fence appear in the immediate foreground).
  - Behind the rear fence is the light sage-green horizontal siding of a garage with two white-trimmed windows.
  - Bare early-spring branches and dry brownish grass are visible outside the fence on the left and right edges.
- **Black Plastic Cover**:
  - Sheets of shiny black polyethylene plastic cover the entire ground surface inside the fence to prevent weeds from taking over and attempt soil solarization.
  - Orange-red and tan bricks and flat stones are arranged in rows along the seams and edges of the plastic sheets to hold them down against the wind.
  - Shallow puddles of rainwater and fallen leaves have collected in depressions across the crinkled surface of the plastic, and a short vertical metal pipe/post sticks up through the plastic near the bottom center.
  - A glimpse of blue tarp material peeks out along the back fence line in the upper left.
