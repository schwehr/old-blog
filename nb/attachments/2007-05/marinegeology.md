# Marine Geology Journal Article Header (`marinegeology.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the title banner and author block from the first page of Kurt Schwehr's peer-reviewed journal article published in **Marine Geology** (Volume 240, 2007, pages 19–41), posted on May 5, 2007, to announce that the paper was about to appear in print.

## Visible Text & Layout
- **Top Journal Banner**:
  - **Left Logo**: The classic `ELSEVIER` woodcut emblem depicting a scholar standing beneath a vine-entwined elm tree with the motto ribbon (`Non Solus`), above the serif wordmark `ELSEVIER`.
  - **Center Citation**: `Marine Geology 240 (2007) 19–41`.
  - **Right Logo**: Bold italic sans-serif title `MARINE GEOLOGY` between horizontal rules, with the subtitle `INTERNATIONAL JOURNAL OF MARINE GEOLOGY, GEOCHEMISTRY AND GEOPHYSICS` and URL `www.elsevier.com/locate/margeo` below.
- **Article Title & Authors**:
  - **Title**: `Origin of continental margin morphology: Submarine-slide or downslope current-controlled bedforms, a rock magnetic approach`
  - **Authors**: `K. Schwehr *, N. Driscoll, L. Tauxe`
  - **Affiliation**: *`Scripps Institution of Oceanography, University of California San Diego, San Diego, CA 92093-2002, USA`*
  - **Publication History**: `Received 24 March 2006; received in revised form 24 January 2007; accepted 31 January 2007`
- **Watermark**: Light gray diagonal watermark text reading `copy` (from *"Author's personal copy"*) behind the right side of the title and author block.
