# Overnight AIS Vessel Traffic in Norfolk Harbor and Hampton Roads (`norfolk-ais-traffic.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot displaying a dense web of white AIS vessel tracklines recorded overnight between May 16 and May 17, 2007, in Hampton Roads and the Elizabeth River around Norfolk and Portsmouth, Virginia, during the U.S. Hydro 2007 conference. The data was captured by an AIS Base Station installed in a communications closet at the NOAA Atlantic Marine Center.

## Map & AIS Track Details
- **Time Slider (Top Center)**:
  - The Google Earth temporal slider at the top of the window shows the recording interval from `16 MAY 2007 5:53pm` to `17 MAY 2007 8:07am`.
- **Navigation Compass (Top Right)**:
  - Circular Google Earth directional ring with `N` at the top.
- **Geographic Features & Labels**:
  - `Craney Island` is labeled in light cyan text along the southern edge of the large rectangular dredged-material facility on the left side of the main channel.
  - `Charters Island` is labeled in light cyan text on the right (east) side of the image in Norfolk.
  - `US Hydro 2007` is marked with a red Google Earth pin with a black center dot near the bottom right along the downtown Norfolk / Portsmouth waterfront on the Elizabeth River.
- **AIS Vessel Traffic Patterns (White Lines)**:
  - A thick, bright white highway of overlapping ship tracks runs north-south down the main Elizabeth River / Hampton Roads channel between Craney Island and Naval Station Norfolk.
  - Multiple branching white tracks peel off into the commercial and naval piers along the eastern waterfront, into the Newport News channel to the northwest, and into terminals and shipyards along the Southern and Western Branches of the Elizabeth River at the bottom.
  - A single small loop track appears isolated in the open water northwest of Craney Island, and a straight white line extends eastward across Norfolk from the lower Elizabeth River.
