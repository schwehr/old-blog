# Downtown Norfolk Skyline at Night Across the Elizabeth River (`norfolk-evening.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide nighttime photograph taken from Portsmouth, Virginia, looking east across the dark waters of the Elizabeth River toward the illuminated skyline of downtown Norfolk during the U.S. Hydro 2007 conference in May 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Skyline & Architecture**:
  - From left to right across the horizon:
    - A mid-rise rectangular building on the far left next to a brightly lit white tower with a glowing peaked/domed roof.
    - In the center, the Waterside festival marketplace / waterfront complex glows brightly along the river's edge, backed by two prominent illuminated high-rise hotel/office towers (including the Sheraton Norfolk Waterside and adjacent downtown office buildings with red/blue rooftop signage).
    - Slightly further back in the center-right sit two darker mid-rise buildings with illuminated upper floors, behind a long horizontal waterfront hotel building.
    - On the right stands a tall, dark skyscraper with lit bands of windows near its top and two bright white crown lights on its roof.
    - On the far right, bright street and bridge lights mark the Berkley Bridge crossing the river.
- **Water Reflections & Sky**:
  - A continuous ribbon of golden-orange and white lights lines the Norfolk waterfront pier and shoreline.
  - Vertical streaks of warm gold, orange, white, and vivid magenta/purple light reflect across the rippled surface of the Elizabeth River in the foreground under a dark amber-brown night sky.
