# Garden Soil pH Test Kit Comparison Chamber (`soiltest.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up photograph of a plastic colorimetric soil pH test comparator container (such as a Rapitest soil tester) sitting on a wooden surface, used to test garden soil before adding compost in a May 31, 2007 blog post about gardening.

## Scene & Test Details

- **Container Design**: A clear rectangular plastic comparator box with a bright green snap-on cap at the top and a lime-green printed front backing card.
- **Test Chamber (Left)**: A vertical transparent column holding a settled layer of brown garden soil at the bottom and cloudy amber/orange-tinted water above it.
  - A dashed horizontal line near the bottom is labeled with an arrow: **`<— FILL WITH SOIL`**.
  - A dashed horizontal line near the top is labeled with an arrow: **`<— FILL WITH WATER`**.
  - The water column's color is an orange-amber hue corresponding roughly to pH 6.0 (Acid) to pH 6.5 (Slightly Acid).
- **Color Chart & Labels (Right)**:
  - Header at top right in bold black serif text: **`pH TEST`**.
  - Seven rounded rectangular color swatches stacked vertically from top to bottom with corresponding pH values and acidity classifications:
    1. Dark green swatch: **`pH 7.5`** / **`ALKALINE`**
    2. Medium green swatch: **`pH 7.0`** / **`NEUTRAL`**
    3. Olive green swatch: **`pH 6.5`** / **`SLIGHTLY ACID`**
    4. Golden amber swatch: **`pH 6.0`** / **`ACID`**
    5. Bright orange swatch: **`pH 5.5`** / **`ACID`**
    6. Red-orange swatch: **`pH 5.0`** / **`VERY ACID`**
    7. Bright red swatch: **`pH 4.5`** / **`VERY ACID`**
