# Garden Soil Potash, Phosphorus, and Nitrogen Test Comparators (`soiltests-3.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up photograph of three side-by-side plastic colorimetric soil nutrient test comparators for Potash (K), Phosphorus (P), and Nitrogen (N) sitting on a wooden surface, from a May 31, 2007 blog post on testing garden soil prior to amending with compost.

## Scene & Test Details

Each clear plastic box has a color-coded cap, a narrow vertical liquid test chamber on the left with a dashed fill line near the top, and five reference color blocks arranged vertically on the right from level 4 (`SURPLUS`) down to level 0 (`DEPLETED`).

### Left Container: Potash (`K TEST`)
- **Cap & Card Color**: Bright orange cap and orange front label card.
- **Header**: **`K TEST`** / **`Potash`**.
- **Test Chamber**: Filled with an orange-amber solution with fine suspended sediment, matching around the `K3 SUFFICIENT` or `K2 ADEQUATE` range.
- **Reference Scale**:
  - **`K4`** / **`SURPLUS`** (deep rust-orange)
  - **`K3`** / **`SUFFICIENT`** (medium orange)
  - **`K2`** / **`ADEQUATE`** (light orange)
  - **`K1`** / **`DEFICIENT`** (pale yellow-orange)
  - **`K0`** / **`DEPLETED`** (clear/uncolored window)

### Center Container: Phosphorus (`P TEST`)
- **Cap & Card Color**: Dark navy/black cap and slate-gray front label card.
- **Header**: **`P TEST`** / **`Phosphorus`**.
- **Test Chamber**: Filled with a deep blue liquid that matches the upper end of the chart (`P3 SUFFICIENT` to `P4 SURPLUS`).
- **Reference Scale**:
  - **`P4`** / **`SURPLUS`** (deep cyan-blue)
  - **`P3`** / **`SUFFICIENT`** (medium sky-blue)
  - **`P2`** / **`ADEQUATE`** (light blue)
  - **`P1`** / **`DEFICIENT`** (very pale blue)
  - **`P0`** / **`DEPLETED`** (clear/uncolored window)

### Right Container: Nitrogen (`N TEST`)
- **Cap & Card Color**: Purple cap and rose/salmon-pink front label card.
- **Header**: **`N TEST`** / **`Nitrogen`**.
- **Test Chamber**: Filled with a nearly clear, milky-white liquid showing virtually no pink coloration, indicating **`N0 DEPLETED`** (confirming the blog note that the garden soil badly needed nitrogen).
- **Reference Scale**:
  - **`N4`** / **`SURPLUS`** (vivid magenta-pink)
  - **`N3`** / **`SUFFICIENT`** (bright pink)
  - **`N2`** / **`ADEQUATE`** (medium rose-pink)
  - **`N1`** / **`DEFICIENT`** (pale lilac-pink)
  - **`N0`** / **`DEPLETED`** (clear/uncolored window)
