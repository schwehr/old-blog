# Tiled Display Wall Showing a 4-Screen Video at CCOM (`tiled-display2.jpg`)

## Overview

A photograph of consultant Matt and Kurt Schwehr standing beside a multi-monitor tiled display wall at the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM), University of New Hampshire, demonstrating a video playing across a 2x2 array of LCD panels. Featured in a May 8, 2007 blog post titled "Tiled display progress continues."

## Scene & Hardware Details

- **Tiled Display Wall (Left & Center)**:
  - Constructed on an extruded aluminum framing rack (80/20 style) with silver-bezeled widescreen LCD monitors arranged in a 3x3 grid configuration (with the top-center panel absent, exposing the black computer cases and mounting brackets behind the rack).
  - **Bottom-Left 2x2 Monitors**: Four displays actively show a single synchronized wide-angle 3D visualization/movie of a coastal river and harbor scene (the Piscataqua River / Portsmouth Harbor area), featuring a green steel truss bridge in the upper-left monitor, tree-lined riverbanks in the upper-right monitor, and shaded bathymetric/topographic terrain models across the lower two monitors.
  - **Other Monitors**: The top-left monitor is powered off (black), the middle-right and bottom-right monitors behind the standing men are dark, and the top-right monitor displays a light blue desktop background with a white terminal or text window on the left.
  - Power strips and tangled black/white power and video cables rest on the floor beneath the aluminum frame.
- **People (Right)**:
  - **Left Person (Matt)**: Standing in front of the right column of monitors, smiling at the camera while holding a black computer keyboard in his hands and resting his left hand on the back of a dark blue rolling office chair. He wears a black polo shirt and light khaki cargo pants.
  - **Right Person (Kurt Schwehr)**: Standing next to Matt in front of a whiteboard covered with handwritten notes and diagrams, smiling and wearing glasses, a dark olive fleece vest over a long-sleeve button-down shirt, and blue jeans.
