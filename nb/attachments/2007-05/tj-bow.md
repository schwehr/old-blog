# Bow View of NOAA Ship Thomas Jefferson and Paddlewheeler in Norfolk (`tj-bow.jpg`)

## Overview

A waterfront photograph taken in Norfolk, Virginia, during the U.S. Hydro 2007 conference (May 18, 2007), showing the bow of the NOAA survey ship *Thomas Jefferson* (S 222) moored at a pier beside the Elizabeth River while a sternwheel riverboat passes by.

## Scene Description

- **NOAA Ship *Thomas Jefferson* (Center-Right)**:
  - Viewed nearly head-on from the port bow as she rests moored along a concrete pier with black cylindrical floating fenders and heavy mooring bollards in the foreground.
  - Both sides of the white flared bow display **`NOAA`** and **`S 222`** alongside the circular blue NOAA logo and recessed anchors.
  - Above the bow rise the white forward superstructure, wide bridge wings and pilothouse windows, radar scanners, and the tall white mainmast.
  - To the right of the ship on the pier is an elevated white steel gangway/passenger boarding structure with green-tinted windows and a small utility tractor parked beneath it.
- **Passing Vessel (Left)**:
  - On the choppy gray water of the Elizabeth River to the left is a white multi-deck tourist paddlewheel boat (the *Carrie B* in Norfolk) with a red stern paddlewheel, two tall dark smokestacks, and an American flag flying from its upper deck.
- **Background Skyline**:
  - Across the water behind the vessels stand two prominent waterfront buildings: a tall dark brick/brown high-rise building on the left and a mid-rise gray gabled condominium building in the center, set under a hazy, pale peach-colored sky.
