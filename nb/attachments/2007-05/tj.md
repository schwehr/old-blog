# NOAA Survey Vessel Thomas Jefferson at Dusk (`tj.jpg`)

## Overview

A port-side profile photograph of the NOAA hydrographic survey ship *Thomas Jefferson* (S 222) moored along a pier in Norfolk, Virginia, during the U.S. Hydro 2007 conference in May 2007.

## Scene & Vessel Details

- **Vessel (`NOAA S 222`)**:
  - The white-hulled survey ship *Thomas Jefferson* faces left (bow at left, stern cut off at far right).
  - On the port bow in large bold black characters is painted **`NOAA`** above the hull number **`S 222`**, flanked to the right by the circular blue-and-white NOAA emblem and to the left by the dark anchor hawsepipe.
  - Forward on the bow deck stands a white mast and a black articulated crane/boom rested horizontally.
  - The white multi-level superstructure features bridge windows, radar domes, a tall central mainmast carrying navigation antennas and sensors, and a tall white exhaust stack with a dark blue-and-black top band toward the stern.
  - Mounted on davits along the aft port quarter is a gray hydrographic survey launch boat.
- **Pier & Background**:
  - Orange-and-white striped traffic barrels line the concrete pier in the foreground alongside the hull.
  - Directly behind the ship's superstructure rises a multi-story gray waterfront condominium/apartment building with peaked gable roofs.
  - To the left, calm river water (the Elizabeth River) stretches toward a distant tree-lined shore with a water tower; to the right, industrial port cranes and another vessel are silhouetted against the bright golden-white evening sky.
