# NASA JPL Robotics Section 347 Web Banner (`CLARAty.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the top navigation header and graphical banner from the NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) Robotics website (`www-robotics.jpl.nasa.gov`), accompanying a June 20, 2007 blog post titled "First Public Release of CLARAty Software."

## Banner & Interface Details

- **Top Institutional Header**:
  - Left side on a light gray background: The blue circular **`NASA`** "meatball" logo next to dark gray text reading **`Jet Propulsion Laboratory`** above **`California Institute of Technology`**.
  - Center: A vertical divider line followed by a link reading **`+ View the NASA Portal`**.
  - Right side on a medium blue background: **`SEARCH JPL`** in white uppercase letters above a white text input box with a small diagonal arrow icon next to it.
- **Navigation Bar**:
  - Five dark gray horizontal menu tabs separated by thin white vertical lines: **`JPL HOME`**, **`EARTH`**, **`SOLAR SYSTEM`**, **`STARS & GALAXIES`**, and **`TECHNOLOGY`**.
- **Graphical Collage Banner**:
  - **Left**: An artistic rendering of a blimp/aerostat with a false-color thermal/elevation overlay and hull registration **`N4 466R`**, positioned above bold white italicized text reading **`Robotics`**.
  - **Center-Left**: A small four-wheeled solar-powered planetary rover with a camera mast driving over a green, yellow, and red gridded terrain mesh with white path vectors.
  - **Center-Right**: A three-legged Mars lander (such as Mars Polar Lander / Phoenix) with two circular solar array wings and a robotic surface scoop arm digging into a false-color blue cratered surface.
  - **Right**: A close-up yellow wireframe-highlighted rendering of a Mars Exploration Rover (Spirit/Opportunity) showing its Pancam mast, instrument deployment arm, and front hazard cameras.
  - **Bottom Right Corner**: A dark blue tab badge labeled **`SEC 347`** (JPL's Autonomous Systems / Robotics section) in bold white italic text.
