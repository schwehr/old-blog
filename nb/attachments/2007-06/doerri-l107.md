# Testing 802.11 Wireless Connection to Doerri AUV at Mendum's Pond (`doerri-l107.jpg`)

## Overview

A photograph of researcher Roland Arsenault sitting in a folding camp chair on the wooden dock at Mendum's Pond in New Hampshire, operating a ruggedized laptop while communicating over 802.11 wireless with "Doerri" (a Fetch autonomous underwater vehicle, or AUV) floating in the water nearby. Taken during a June 5, 2007 field test documented in the June 6, 2007 blog post "AUV trip to Mendum's Pond."

## Scene Description

- **Operator on Dock (Left)**:
  - Roland sits in a black folding canvas chair near the edge of a weathered wooden plank dock, with one leg crossed over the other.
  - He wears glasses, a bright blue short-sleeve t-shirt with a graphic on the back, dark shorts, white socks, and dark brown hiking boots.
  - On his lap rests an open silver-and-black ruggedized laptop (a Panasonic Toughbook) displaying the graphical LabVIEW control interface for the AUV.
- **Fetch AUV "Doerri" (Right)**:
  - Floating at the surface of the calm, rippled gray water a short distance off the dock is the yellow-and-white torpedo-shaped hull of the Fetch AUV.
  - A tall, thin dark communications mast/antenna extends vertically upward from the top of the AUV, casting a wavy reflection on the water's surface, while a small dark fin tip protrudes near the tail.
