# Wider View of Roland Operating the Doerri AUV from the Mendum's Pond Dock (`doerri-l108.jpg`)

## Overview

A wider-angle photograph of Roland Arsenault seated on the UNH Sailing Club dock at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, controlling the Fetch AUV "Doerri" via 802.11 wireless on a ruggedized laptop, with the forested shoreline of the pond visible in the background. From the June 6, 2007 blog post "AUV trip to Mendum's Pond."

## Scene Description

- **Foreground (Dock & Operator)**:
  - On the lower left, Roland sits in a black folding chair on the wooden dock with his legs crossed, typing on a Panasonic Toughbook laptop resting on his lap.
  - He is wearing a blue t-shirt with yellow-and-white lettering on the back, dark shorts, and brown boots.
  - The laptop screen clearly shows the LabVIEW-based Fetch AUV mission supervisor graphical interface.
- **Middle Ground (Water & AUV)**:
  - To the right of Roland, floating just below the water surface, is the yellow-and-white Fetch AUV ("Doerri") with its tall vertical black whip antenna mast and small black tail fin sticking out of the water.
- **Background (Shoreline)**:
  - Across the calm, rippled expanse of Mendum's Pond rises a dense green mixed-forest shoreline with a small blue-tarp-covered boat or dock structure visible along the far right bank.
