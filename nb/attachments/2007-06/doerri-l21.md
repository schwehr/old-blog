# Fetch² Mission Supervisor 1.0 LabVIEW User Interface (`doerri-l21.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up outdoor photograph of a ruggedized laptop screen displaying the LabVIEW-based user interface (`Fetch² Mission Supervisor 1.0`) used to control and monitor the Fetch autonomous underwater vehicle ("Doerri") at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire. Featured in the June 6, 2007 blog post "AUV trip to Mendum's Pond."

## Interface & Scene Details

- **Setting**: The black-bezeled ruggedized laptop sits on a light-colored portable table at the edge of Mendum's Pond, with green lakeside foliage on the left and right and bright sunlight reflecting off the water in the background.
- **Software Interface (`Fetch² Mission Supervisor 1.0`)**:
  - **Title Banner**: Green text at top center reading **`Fetch² Mission Supervisor 1.0`** on a dark gray panel background.
  - **Top Row Panels**:
    - Left: A small dark inset image showing an underwater vehicle graphic.
    - Center: A white multi-line mission sequence box listing numbered mission steps (e.g., diving, heading, speed, rudder/elevator settings).
    - Right: Status indicators including a bright green circular light and a vertical red/white bar gauge.
  - **Middle Row Instruments**:
    - A semi-circular pitch/roll or battery gauge with a green-yellow-red arc and needle.
    - A circular white compass rose / heading indicator dial.
    - Numeric coordinate readouts showing latitude **`43° 09.3526' N`** and longitude **`71° 03.6554' W`** along with depth, speed, and heading fields, flanked by red and green status buttons.
    - A wide horizontal yellow bar scale with a blue triangular pointer and a bright green status banner below reading **`No Problem Detected`**.
  - **Bottom Log Panel**:
    - A wide white scrolling text log labeled **`Messages from Fetch`** showing timestamped operational telemetry messages (`16:51:57`, `16:52:45`, etc., including `Deploying Boat`, `Initializing Hardware`, `Primary Mission`, and GPS status warnings).
  - **OS Taskbar**: Standard blue Windows XP taskbar at the very bottom with the green **`start`** button on the left.
