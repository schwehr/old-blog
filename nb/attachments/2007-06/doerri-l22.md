# Doerri AUV Heading Out into Mendum's Pond After a Thunderstorm (`doerri-l22.jpg`)

## Overview

A scenic photograph of the Fetch autonomous underwater vehicle ("Doerri") cruising away from the shore across the calm waters of Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, just after a thunderstorm passed through on June 5, 2007. Featured in the June 6, 2007 blog post "AUV trip to Mendum's Pond."

## Scene Description

- **AUV & Water (Center)**:
  - Near the center of the frame, the Fetch AUV is almost completely submerged as it moves out into the lake, leaving a small V-shaped surface wake and concentric ripples on the silver-gray water.
  - Its thin vertical antenna mast sticks straight up above the water surface.
- **Shoreline & Sky**:
  - **Foreground Left**: Dark silhouetted leaves and branches of a shoreline tree frame the left edge of the image.
  - **Background**: Two densely wooded dark-green peninsulas or islands jut out from the left and right, framing a distant forested shoreline across the pond.
  - **Sky**: Above the trees, post-storm cumulus and stratocumulus clouds in shades of slate gray and soft lavender-gray are backlit by a warm, bright pale-gold evening sky.
