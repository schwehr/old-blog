# Roland Running AUV Missions from a Lakeside Table at Mendum's Pond (`doerri-l29.jpg`)

## Overview

A photograph of Roland Arsenault seated at a folding table among lakeside bushes at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, operating the Fetch AUV ("Doerri") through a series of missions using a FreeWave Ethernet bridge. Featured in the June 6, 2007 blog post "AUV trip to Mendum's Pond."

## Scene Description

- **Operator & Workstation (Center-Right)**:
  - Roland sits in profile facing left in a black folding camp chair set up at the edge of the dock amidst lush green shoreline shrubs and saplings.
  - He rests his chin thoughtfully on his right hand while looking intently at a ruggedized laptop sitting on a white folding table with crossed metal legs.
  - He wears glasses, dark shorts, and a blue t-shirt with white and yellow lettering across the back.
- **Lake & Background (Left)**:
  - To the left of the bushes, the calm dark-green water of Mendum's Pond stretches toward a wooded hillside shore in the distance, where a small blue-covered boat is moored near a sandy patch of bank.
