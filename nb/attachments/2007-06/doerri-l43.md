# Kurt Schwehr Driving the Doerri AUV at Mendum's Pond (`doerri-l43.jpg`)

## Overview

An elevated photograph looking down at Kurt Schwehr sitting at a portable folding table on the wooden dock at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, operating the Fetch AUV ("Doerri") via its LabVIEW interface, with an equipment trailer parked behind him. Featured in the June 6, 2007 blog post "AUV trip to Mendum's Pond."
