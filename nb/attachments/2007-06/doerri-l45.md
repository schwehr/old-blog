# Water-Level View of the Doerri AUV Approaching the Dock (`doerri-l45.jpg`)

## Overview

A wide, low-angle, water-level photograph taken by Roland Arsenault showing the Fetch autonomous underwater vehicle ("Doerri") approaching the dock at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire—humorously captioned "Attack of the AUV" in the June 6, 2007 blog post "AUV trip to Mendum's Pond."

## Scene Description

- **AUV & Wake (Center)**:
  - The yellow nose cone of the Fetch AUV breaks the dark, glassy surface of the pond near the bottom center, moving toward the camera.
  - A tall, slender antenna mast banded in black and yellow rises vertically from the hull, with a small rear fin tip protruding just behind it.
  - Long, bright white-lit surface ripples and wake lines extend horizontally across the dark green water on both sides of the vehicle.
- **Background**:
  - Directly behind the AUV across the water is a dense, sunlit green forest of deciduous and coniferous trees lining the shore, with a couple of gray boulders resting at the water's edge on the right.
