# Viewing Sidescan Sonar via VNC on a Panasonic Toughbook at Mendum's Pond (`doerri-l57.jpg`)

## Overview
An over-the-shoulder outdoor photograph taken during an Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) field trial with the Fetch AUV *Doerri* at Mendum's Pond (near the UNH Sailing team dock) on June 5, 2007. The photo shows Kurt Schwehr looking at a ruggedized Panasonic Toughbook laptop displaying a VNC relay of sidescan sonar software running aboard the AUV.

## Scene & Equipment Description
- **Foreground Subject**: Viewed from behind and slightly to the left over the right shoulder of a person (Kurt Schwehr) with short brown hair wearing a dark grey t-shirt, leaning toward the laptop screen with a hand resting near his chin.
- **Laptop**: A silver-and-black ruggedized **Panasonic Toughbook** (`Panasonic` logo and model badge `CF-29` centered below the screen bezel) resting on a white portable table surface.
- **Screen Display**: The laptop screen shows a Windows desktop environment with a green `Start` button and blue taskbar at the bottom, running a VNC window displaying sidescan sonar waterfall imagery:
  - The central waterfall plot features warm bronze, orange, and dark brown acoustic backscatter swaths on either side of a dark vertical nadir gap, traced by bright yellow-orange bottom-track lines and a bright yellow acoustic target reflection on the right edge.
  - Along the left side of the sonar application is a vertical column of grey control buttons and status readouts. Across the top of the waterfall display is a coordinate and telemetry readout bar (partially legible: `43° ... 71° ...`).
- **Background**: Out-of-focus wooden planks of the dock angle away to the left toward dark floating dock pontoons and calm grey-blue water, with a sprig of green leaves on the far right.
