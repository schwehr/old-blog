# Forested Shoreline Reflections on Mendum's Pond (`doerri-l63.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical scenic nature photograph of Mendum's Pond in New Hampshire, taken on June 5, 2007, during a field test of the Fetch AUV *Doerri* after a passing thunderstorm.

## Scene Description
- **Forest & Shoreline**: Dense New England mixed woodland rises directly from the water's edge, dominated by tall eastern white pines (*Pinus strobus*) with layered horizontal needle canopies in the upper left and center, dark-trunked conifers on the upper right, and bright green deciduous shrubs overhanging the right bank.
- **Rocks & Waterline**: Along the middle-right shoreline, a prominent angular light-grey granite boulder and smaller rocks emerge just above the calm water surface, with a smooth dark grey rock visible farther back near the center shoreline.
- **Water & Reflections**: The lower third of the frame is filled with the dark, glassy surface of Mendum's Pond, creating a rippled mirror reflection of the deep green forest canopy and the pale granite rock in the foreground. A small patch of overcast white sky is visible at the top left above the treetops.
