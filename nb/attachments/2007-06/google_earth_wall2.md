# Multi-Monitor Mac OS X Display Wall Running Google Earth (`google_earth_wall2.jpg`)

## Overview
An angled photograph of a large-scale tiled LCD display wall running **Google Earth** with 3D buildings across a grid of Apple Cinema Displays powered by a cluster of Mac Pros using Chromium OpenGL distributed rendering.

## Scene Description
- **Tiled Display Grid**: The photo shows a 6-column by 3-row matrix of flat-panel monitors (18 visible screens) framed in brown/wood-toned bezels mounted on a dark exhibition wall.
- **Overhead Banner Text**: Along the top left black header above the screen grid, orange/gold text reads: **`Large Scale Visualization with Mac ...`** (part of an Apple demonstration of Mac OS X visualization clusters).
- **Displayed Imagery**: Across the seamless virtual canvas, Google Earth renders a high-resolution oblique aerial view of the **Las Vegas Strip** (Nevada):
  - Grey 3D extruded building models of major resorts, casinos, convention halls, and parking structures span the center and left monitors.
  - Yellow highway lines with road labels—including **`Stardust Rd`** and **`Las Vegas Blvd ...`**—run vertically through the rightmost column of monitors alongside desert lots and hotel complexes.
  - A cylindrical dark brown high-rise tower (Wynn/Encore style) is prominent in the bottom-center tile, next to a curved resort building with a bright turquoise pool area.
- **Foreground**: The shoulder of a viewer wearing a bright blue shirt appears in the lower-left corner.
