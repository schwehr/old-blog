# Wood and Metal Stakes Anchoring the Hog Wire Garden Arch (`hogwire-arch-stakes.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up view inside the fenced backyard garden bed in June 2007, showing how the base of the bent hog wire arch is held in place using a combination of cut tree-branch wooden stakes, green plastic-coated metal garden stakes, and climbing twine for bean and sweet pea plants.

## Scene Description
- **Left Base of the Arch**: The lower left section of the galvanized welded-wire mesh panel presses outward against two thick, freshly sawed dark-barked wooden branch stakes driven into the dirt, supplemented by tall green plastic-coated metal stakes angled upward toward the top of the arch. Black plastic weed-barrier sheeting runs along the lower-left edge of the frame.
- **Right Base of the Arch**: Across the bare soil walkway on the right side, the opposite end of the hog wire panel rests near the white wooden picket fence and is similarly pinned in place by rustic wooden branch stakes and vertical green metal stakes.
- **Plants & Climbing Lines**: Young green bean and sweet pea seedlings sprout from the dark tilled garden soil around the base of the arch and along the fence line, with thin dark green lines/strings stretched from the soil stakes up to the arch to guide the climbing vines. A terracotta-red rectangular planter box sits in the back left corner of the fenced bed.
