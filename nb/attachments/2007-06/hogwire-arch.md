# Full-Length Hog Wire Garden Arch Behind White Picket Fence (`hogwire-arch.jpg`)

## Overview
A backyard photograph taken in late June 2007 showing a tall, full-length semicircular garden trellis arch bent from a single heavy-gauge galvanized hog wire panel, installed inside a fenced garden enclosure to support climbing beans and sweet peas.

## Scene Description
- **White Picket Fence (Foreground & Midground)**: A white wooden picket fence with pointed Gothic-style picket tops encloses the rectangular garden bed in the foreground, with a second fenced section visible behind it to the right. Young leafy green plants grow along the outside base of the front fence, and a short section of grey PVC/drain pipe rests on the grass at the bottom right corner.
- **Hog Wire Arch (Center)**: Rising high above the white picket fence is a wide, smooth arch formed from rigid welded wire mesh ("hog wire" purchased from Agway).
  - Tall green plastic-coated metal garden stakes and vertical green climbing strings are tied near the top of the arch and anchored into the soil below, with small pink ribbon/flagging tape markers tied to the stakes on the left and right sides of the arch.
- **Garden Interior & Background**: Inside the front left fence, black landscape fabric/plastic mulch lines a garden row. Mature green deciduous shade trees fill the background across the yard.
