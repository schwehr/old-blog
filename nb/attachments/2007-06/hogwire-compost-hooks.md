# Brass Double Hook Fastening the Hog Wire Compost Bin (`hogwire-compost-hooks.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up detail photograph taken in June 2007 showing one of the brass double hooks used to join the overlapping ends of the heavy-gauge hog wire panel into a circular backyard compost bin without cutting the wire.

## Scene Description
- **Wire Grid & Brass Fastener**: Viewed at an oblique angle in bright golden sunlight, two overlapping layers of thick galvanized welded steel wire mesh meet along a vertical seam.
- **Brass Double Hook**: Near the center of the frame, a horizontal cast-brass double hook (snap/link hardware) clasps two adjacent vertical wire rods together, holding the tension of the bent wire cylinder.
- **Background**: Behind the sunlit metallic grid, dark shaded compost material consisting of green moss/grass clippings and scattered brown dry leaves fills the interior of the bin.
