# Circular Hog Wire Compost Bin in the Backyard (`hogwire-compost.jpg`)

## Overview
A warm, late-afternoon sunlit photograph taken in June 2007 showing a large circular backyard compost bin constructed by bending a rigid galvanized hog wire panel into a cylinder at the wooded edge of Kurt Schwehr's yard.

## Scene Description
- **Hog Wire Cylinder**: A heavy-gauge galvanized welded-wire fence panel with rectangular grid openings is bent into a wide, freestanding circular enclosure.
- **Compost Pile Contents**: The lower portion of the wire cylinder is packed with dry brown autumn leaves, while the middle and upper layers are topped with fresh green grass clippings, weeds, and leafy yard trimmings mounded in the center.
- **Wooded Surroundings**: The compost bin is tucked into a shaded corner at the edge of the yard, directly in front of a thick tree trunk and several smaller saplings, with green maple leaves and woodland undergrowth illuminated by golden low-angle sunlight.
