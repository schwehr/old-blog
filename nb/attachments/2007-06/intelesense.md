# Intelesense Technologies Website Homepage (`intelesense.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the top portion of the **Intelesense Technologies** website (`http://www.intelesense.net/about`) captured in June 2007, highlighting their "Global Integrated Monitoring" platform combining sensor networks with NASA WorldWind 3D geospatial visualization.

## Interface & Visible Text
- **Top Navigation Bar**: A black horizontal menu bar across the top with white text links separated by vertical dividers:
  - `About` | `Technology` | `Products` | `Deployments` | `View Data` | `Support` | `Contact Us`
- **Banner Header**:
  - **Left Logo**: On a slate-blue square background, a stylized black stick-figure emblem (resembling an ancient petroglyph or warrior) holds a jagged lightning-bolt spear overhead.
  - **Center/Right Banner**: Over a dark blue shaded-relief satellite map of the Earth (showing North/South America, Africa, Europe, and Asia), white sans-serif text reads **`Intelesense Technologies`** on the left and bold white text reads **`Global Integrated Monitoring`** on the lower right.
- **Sign-In Bar**: A light grey horizontal strip below the banner with right-aligned text: `You are not signed in.` **`Sign in now.`** (in blue bold text).
- **Main Content Area**:
  - **Left QuickTime Video Player**: Embedded media player showing a black title card with the stick-figure lightning-spear logo superimposed over a blue globe on the left, white bold text **`Intelesense Technologies`**, and yellow bold text **`Global Integrated Monitoring`**. Below the video frame are standard media transport controls (skip back, rewind, play/stop toggle button, fast-forward, skip forward, a green progress/volume bar, and a checked `Auto hide` checkbox).
  - **Right Call-to-Action Box**: A rounded light-grey box containing:
    - Heading: **`Get an account today!`**
    - Body text: *"Sign up now to download our advanced GIS and world visualizer software, and get access to public data sources all over the world! It's easy, and free!"*
    - Link: **`Sign up now!`** (blue bold underlined link).
