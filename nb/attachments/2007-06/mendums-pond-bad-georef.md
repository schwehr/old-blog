# Misaligned Mendum's Pond Polygon in Google Earth (`mendums-pond-bad-georef.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from **Google Earth** taken on June 8, 2007, illustrating a severe georeferencing/geocoding offset (over 600 meters to the northwest) for the hydrological lake polygon of **Mendum's Pond** in New Hampshire relative to the underlying satellite imagery and road network.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Satellite Imagery**: Vertical aerial view of **Mendum's Pond** (the dark, elongated, multi-cove body of water in the center of the image with several small forested islands) surrounded by dense green New England forest.
- **Misaligned Blue Polygon**: To the left (west-northwest) of the actual dark water body, a light-blue-outlined semi-transparent polygon representing Google's vector water layer for Mendum's Pond is superimposed directly over the green forested land and local roads, shifted roughly 600+ meters west-northwest of the true shoreline.
- **Roads & Labels**:
  - **`Wood Rd`** (top left)
  - **`Gerrish Dr`** (middle left, running directly underneath the misplaced blue lake polygon)
  - **`Holiday Lake Shore Rd`** (curving along the eastern shore of the pond)
  - **`Hall Rd`** (bottom right)
  - **`Mendums Pond`** (bold cyan label at the southern tip of the lake)
- **HUD Elements & Attributions**:
  - **Top Left**: World overview map inset with a red crosshair over New England.
  - **Top Right**: Circular compass ring indicating North (`N`).
