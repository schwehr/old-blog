# NOAA Chart 13003 Overlay in Zoomatron on Microsoft Virtual Earth (`noaa-zoomatron.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X web browser screenshot taken on June 1, 2007, showing **Zoomatron** (`http://www.zoomatron.com/Charts/Chart.aspx?chart...`), a web mashup that overlays raster NOAA Nautical Charts (specifically **Chart 13003: Cape Sable to Cape Hatteras**) with adjustable transparency on top of **Microsoft Virtual Earth** road maps, accompanied by a zoomed-in inset of the New Hampshire and northern Massachusetts coast.

## Browser & Interface Controls
- **Browser Frame**: Mac OS X browser window (Camino/Firefox style) titled **`Zoomatron :: Cape Sable To Cape Hatteras (13003)`**, with URL bar showing `http://www.zoomatron.com/Charts/Chart.aspx?chart` and a Google search box containing `noaa zoomatron`.
- **Map Navigation Widget (Top Left)**: Microsoft Virtual Earth control box with **`Road`** (selected) and `Aerial` view toggles, a 4-way directional pan ring, and a horizontal zoom slider (`-` to `+`).
- **Bottom Control Bar**:
  - Links and buttons: `| Zoomatron Home |`, `Style` button, and **`Chart Transparency:`** selector buttons for `Hidden`, `80%`, `60%`, `40%`, `20%`, and `Opaque`.
  - Chart caption below: **`Cape Sable To Cape Hatteras, Chart 13003`**.
  - Google AdSense bar at the bottom: **`Ads by Google`** followed by links for `Cape Hatteras Art`, `Nautical Map`, `Nima Nautical Charts`, `Marine Chart`, and `Sea Maps`.
  - Browser status bar at bottom left reads `Done`.

## Map & Inset Details
- **Main Map View**: Covers the eastern United States from Lake Ontario, Vermont, and Maine in the north down to North Carolina in the south.
  - The base layer is Microsoft Virtual Earth's road map showing state names (`VERMONT`, `NEW HAMPSHIRE`, `MASSACHUSETTS`, `NEW YORK`, `PENNSYLVANIA`, `WEST VIRGINIA`, `VIRGINIA`, `NORTH CAROLINA`), cities, and orange highway networks, with the `Microsoft Virtual Earth` logo at bottom left and a `150 miles` scale bar and `© 2006 Microsoft Corporation © 2006 NAVTEQ` copyright at bottom right.
  - Semi-transparently overlaid across the Atlantic coast and New England states is NOAA Nautical Chart 13003, showing depth soundings, Loran/compass lines, and printed chart title/notes blocks over New York and Vermont.
- **Zoomed Inset (Lower Right)**: A black-bordered square inset showing a higher-zoom view of the Gulf of Maine / New Hampshire / Massachusetts coastline where the semi-transparent nautical chart aligns with the underlying road map:
  - Visible coastal towns and features include **`Mt. Agamenticus`**, **`Dover`**, **`Portsmouth`**, **`Exeter`**, **`Isles of Shoals`**, **`Newburyport`**, **`Merrimack R`**, **`Gloucester`**, and **`CAPE ANN`**, along with navigational aids (`HORN`, `WHISTLE`, `BELL`, light characteristics, depth soundings in fathoms, and an `"Unexploded mine (rep Aug. 1981)"` caution note).
