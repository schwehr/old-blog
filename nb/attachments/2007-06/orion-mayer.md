# ORION Ocean Research Interactive Observatory Network Diagram (`orion-mayer.png`)

## Overview
A presentation slide diagram from Larry Mayer's plenary talk (`DI_Mayer_plenary.pdf`) illustrating the multi-scale architecture of the **ORION (Ocean Research Interactive Observatory Network)** program, funded by the National Science Foundation (NSF). Three large curved light-blue arrows link three main conceptual graphics arranged on a black background: a global/pelagic satellite-linked buoy observatory, a regional seafloor fiber-optic cable network off the Pacific Northwest, and a coastal/shelf mooring array.

## Header Banner
- **Left Logo**: A blue globe icon containing white wave lines and the text **"ORION"**.
- **Title Text**: **"Ocean Research Interactive Observatory Network"** in white serif lettering over a dark teal banner patterned with a glowing green network grid.
- **Right Logo**: The gold gear-shaped **NSF (National Science Foundation)** logo in the top right corner.

## Three Main Observatory Components
1. **Global / Pelagic Observatory (Upper Left)**:
   - Shows a 3D Earth globe centered on North and Central America with a satellite dish on land communicating via orange arched trajectories with four orbiting satellites.
   - A grey projection cone extends from the eastern Pacific Ocean to a two-tiered cylindrical cutaway of the water column and seafloor.
   - The water column cutaway shows a surface buoy with a satellite transmitter, profiling moorings, acoustic transducers emitting sonar cones, an autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV), and benthic instrument nodes linked on the seafloor.
2. **Regional Cabled Seafloor Observatory Map (Middle Right)**:
   - Titled **"Cable System for Interactive Seafloor Observatory"** at the top.
   - A shaded bathymetric and topographic map of the Pacific Northwest coast showing **Victoria**, **Seattle**, and **Portland**, along with tectonic labels for the **Explorer Plate**, **Pacific Plate**, **Juan de Fuca Plate**, **Gorda Plate**, and **North American Plate**.
   - Depicts a loop of **Fiber Optic Cable** (light green/yellow lines) spanning the Juan de Fuca Plate with red dots marking **Junction Boxes** and yellow dots marking **Moorings**.
   - **Key (Bottom Left of Map)**:
     - Red dot: `Junction Boxes`
     - Light green line: `Fiber Optic Cable`
     - Yellow dot: `Moorings`
     - Green double line: `Plate Boundaries`
     - Grey dashed line: `Borders`
     - Blue line: `Rivers`
3. **Coastal & Shelf Pioneer Array Cutaway (Bottom Left)**:
   - A 3D block diagram of a coastal shelf environment stretching from a green shoreline (with meteorological towers, coastal radar, and a river plume) across the continental shelf to the shelf break.
   - Shows dense lines of instrumented surface buoys, vertical profiler moorings, gliders/AUVs, and seafloor nodes, with blue arrows indicating shelf currents and upwelling/downwelling circulation.
