# OpenSceneGraph 2.0 Logo (`osglogo2.0.png`)

## Overview
The official logo graphic for **OpenSceneGraph (OSG) 2.0**, an open-source C++ and OpenGL 3D graphics application programming interface and scene graph toolkit.

## Visual Elements & Design
- **Central Globe**: At the center is a glossy, three-dimensional shaded globe showing white continents (North America, South America, Europe, and Africa) against a blue ocean with a dark shadow crescent along the right/bottom-right limb, outlined by a dark navy ring.
- **Three Surrounding Chevrons (Cube / Bounding Box Motif)**: Surrounding the globe in a hexagonal arrangement are three stylized, curved boomerang- or chevron-shaped brackets outlined in dark gray, representing the three axes/planes of 3D space (red, green, and blue):
  - **Top Chevron**: Bright red, forming an upward-pointing peak above the globe.
  - **Bottom-Left Chevron**: Bright lime green, forming the lower-left corner.
  - **Bottom-Right Chevron**: Steel blue, forming the lower-right corner.
- **Background**: Solid white background.
