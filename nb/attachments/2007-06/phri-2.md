# Portsmouth Harbor Response Initiative Web GIS – Marine Debris Survey Popup & Shoreline Legend (`phri-2.png`)

## Overview
A second screenshot of the **Portsmouth Harbor Response Initiative (PHRI)** web GIS interface, centered on Little Bay / Great Bay and Pease International Tradeport. The map displays an open photo popup from a Marine Debris Survey alongside color-coded Environmental Sensitivity Index (ESI) shoreline classifications listed in the active **`Legend`** tab.

## Map View & Popup Details
- **Geography & Base Map**:
  - Shows the peninsula of **Pease International Tradeport** / **Pease AFB** bounded by Little Bay / Great Bay on the west and the **New Hampshire Tpke** (`Greenland Rd`, Route `33`, Route `151`) on the southeast.
  - Shoreline segments around the bay are highlighted in red, orange, purple, and blue lines corresponding to ESI shoreline sensitivity classifications.
  - Clusters of small grey camera icons and yellow circular flag icons mark survey stations along the eastern shoreline of the peninsula.
- **Open Popup Window**:
  - Positioned on the left side of the map, titled **`Marine Debris Survey`** / **`2007/06/11`**.
  - Contains an embedded color photograph showing two volunteers in white t-shirts and jeans collecting trash with a white plastic bag among large coastal rocks and cobbles under a light blue sky.
- **Scale & Coordinates (Bottom Right of Map)**:
  - `Scale = 1 : 54K`
  - `Permalink` link
  - Coordinates: `-70.83263, 43.05472`
