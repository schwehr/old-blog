# Portsmouth Harbor Response Initiative Web GIS – Regional Layers View (`phri-3.png`)

## Overview
A third screenshot of the **Portsmouth Harbor Response Initiative (PHRI)** web GIS application, zoomed out (`Scale = 1 : 108K`) to show the broader New Hampshire and southern Maine seacoast (Portsmouth, Kittery, and Rye) with bathymetric/geologic polygons, coastal radar/coverage wedges, and the **`Layer`** tree tab open on the right.

## Map View Details
- **Geographic Extent**:
  - Covers the Piscataqua River mouth and Atlantic coastline from **Kittery** and **Kittery Point** (Route `103`, `US 1`, `Maine Tpke`) in the north, past **Portsmouth** (`I-95`, `New Hampshire Tpke`, `US 1 Bypass`, `Lafayette Rd`, Route `1A`), down to **Rye Harbor State Park** in the south.
- **Overlaid Spatial Layers**:
  - **Estuarine & Offshore Polygons**: Color-coded depth/substrate or sensitivity polygons in dark blue, medium blue, cyan, green, yellow, and dark gray filling Portsmouth Harbor, Great Bay approaches, and the offshore Gulf of Maine waters.
  - **Directional Wedges / Corridors**: Three semi-transparent gray-green fan-shaped polygons extending southeastward into the offshore waters from the mouth of Portsmouth Harbor and the Rye coastline.
- **Scale & Coordinates (Bottom Right of Map)**:
  - `Scale = 1 : 108K`
  - `Permalink` link
  - Coordinates: `-70.64072, 43.05911`

## Layer Sidebar Details
The active **`Layer`** tab on the right displays six collapsible top-level layer groups, each with a `+` expand box and `(Clear | All)` quick-toggle links:
- **`+ CCOM Layers`** `(Clear | All)`
- **`+ ESI Layers`** `(Clear | All)` (followed by a green circular down-arrow icon)
- **`+ NOAA Layers`** `(Clear | All)`
- **`+ NEXRAD`** `(Clear | All)`
