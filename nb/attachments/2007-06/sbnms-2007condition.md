# Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary Condition Report 2007 Cover (`sbnms-2007condition.png`)

## Overview
The front cover page of the **Gerry E. Studds Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary Condition Report 2007** (published in April 2007 by NOAA's National Marine Sanctuaries program). The cover combines a shaded bathymetric boundary map of the sanctuary in Massachusetts Bay with a collage of six historical and wildlife photographs.

## Title & Header Text
- **Top Left**:
  - `Gerry E. Studds` (medium teal sans-serif)
  - `Stellwagen Bank` (large light-blue serif)
  - `National Marine Sanctuary` (smaller light-blue serif)
- **Top Right (separated by a vertical blue rule)**:
  - **`CONDITION REPORT 2007`** in bold dark-blue uppercase sans-serif lettering.

## Central Collage & Sanctuary Map
- **Bathymetric Map (Right Side)**:
  - A shaded-relief bathymetric chart of Massachusetts Bay between Cape Ann (north) and the hook of **Cape Cod** / **Cape Cod Bay** (south), bordering the **Atlantic Ocean** to the east.
  - The polygonal boundary of the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary is outlined in red/orange, encompassing **Stellwagen Bank**, **Stellwagen Basin**, and southern Jeffreys Ledge. Red circular markers on the coast indicate sanctuary offices/headquarters (such as Scituate, MA).
- **Six Inset Photographs (Left Side)**:
  1. **Top Left**: A vintage black-and-white full-length portrait of 19th-century hydrographer Lieutenant Commander Henry S. Stellwagen in naval dress uniform holding a sword, with his right hand resting on a draped table.
  2. **Top Middle**: Two seabirds (shearwaters) on the water surface, one with wings raised.
  3. **Upper Middle**: A close-up underwater photograph of a sea scallop (*Placopecten magellanicus*) with its orange mantle and rows of small blue eyes visible between its ribbed shells.
  4. **Middle Left**: The NOAA sanctuary research vessel R/V *Auk* (a gray twin-hulled aluminum catamaran patrol/research boat flying the U.S. flag) underway near a stone breakwater.
  5. **Bottom Left**: A dense school of silver sand lance (*Ammodytes*) swimming diagonally through dark green water.
  6. **Bottom Middle**: A humpback whale lunge-feeding at the surface with its pleated ventral throat grooves and open jaws emerging amidst white spray.

## Footer Elements
- **Bottom Left**: **`April 2007`** in bold teal sans-serif text.
- **Bottom Right Logos**:
  - The stylized blue whale-tail and waves logo for **`NATIONAL MARINE SANCTUARIES`**.
  - The circular blue-and-white **NOAA** (`National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration / U.S. Department of Commerce`) emblem.
