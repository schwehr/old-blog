# Lakefront House at Naticook Lake (`3rd-july-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A late-afternoon outdoor photograph taken from the water on Naticook Lake, New Hampshire, on July 3, 2007, showing a two-story light-colored lakefront house perched on a landscaped hill among tall pine trees and reflected in the calm lake surface.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Water):** Dark green, gently rippling lake water spans the lower third of the frame. The ripples catch warm golden evening light and form a bright, wavy reflection of the white house and surrounding trees.
- **Shoreline & Hillside:** A strip of tall green reeds and marsh grass lines the water's edge. Above the shoreline, a sloped lawn and pine-needle-mulched hillside rise toward the house, dotted with small rounded evergreen shrubs. A central concrete or stone stairway with a boat ramp/walkway leads straight down from the front of the house to the water.
- **House & Vehicles:** A two-story light yellow/cream house with a flat or low-pitched roof sits at the top of the slope, featuring a full-width second-story white balcony and a ground-level porch with white railings and columns. A white staircase descends from the right end of the upper balcony toward a driveway where a blue sedan and a dark vehicle are parked.
- **Trees & Sky:** Tall, mature white pines and deciduous trees frame the house on both the left and right sides, set against a clear blue late-afternoon sky. Warm golden sunlight illuminates the front facade of the house and the hillside.
