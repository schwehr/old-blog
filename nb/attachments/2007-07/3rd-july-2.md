# Dusk Fireworks Burst Over Naticook Lake (`3rd-july-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A twilight photograph taken during an Independence Day celebration at Naticook Lake on July 3, 2007, capturing a bright pink and white aerial firework bursting next to silhouetted pine trees against a deep blue evening sky.

## Scene Description
- **Firework Burst:** Positioned slightly left of center, a mortar shell explodes mid-air into long, sweeping arcs of bright white and pinkish-magenta light trails. A secondary cluster of finer pink and white sparks radiates upward above the main burst, while glowing orange embers and illuminated pinkish smoke drift downward.
- **Launch Trail:** A thin, curving orange-gold launch trail rises from a bright neon-green and white flash at the bottom edge of the frame (near the shoreline launch area) up to the center of the explosion.
- **Trees & Horizon:** Dark silhouettes of tall pine trees frame the left side of the image, with smaller tree silhouettes extending across the lower center and lower right shoreline. A couple of small warm lights are visible near the ground among the trees.
- **Sky:** The background sky transitions from a rich cobalt blue at the top to a lighter twilight blue with dark grey-blue horizontal clouds near the right horizon.
