# Multi-Colored Fireworks Trails Against Deep Blue Sky (`3rd-july-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A long-exposure twilight photograph from the July 3, 2007 fireworks display at Naticook Lake, showing intersecting green, red, and glittering gold firework trails against a dark blue dusk sky.

## Scene Description
- **Firework Trails:** Multiple firework shells burst from the bottom center and lower right of the frame:
  - **Left/Center Burst:** Sharp, wavy neon-green and bright red light streaks shoot upward and outward from the bottom left-center, with one lone green streak zigzagging all the way to the top center edge of the frame.
  - **Right/Center Burst:** A bright orange-gold core in the lower right-center radiates long, delicate glittering gold and orange-red trails that arc across the center and right side of the image, shedding fine golden sparks.
- **Background & Foreground:** A dark, out-of-focus silhouette of tree foliage occupies the lower left corner. The background is a uniform deep twilight navy blue.
