# High-Arcing Red and Gold Firework Over Lake Shoreline (`3rd-july-4.jpg`)

## Overview
A twilight photograph taken on July 3, 2007 at Naticook Lake, capturing the steep orange launch trail and directional burst of an aerial firework above silhouetted pine trees and calm lake water.

## Scene Description
- **Launch & Burst:** A bright orange-red vertical light trail rises sharply from the shoreline in the lower left-center, surrounded by a soft reddish smoke cloud. High in the upper left quadrant, the shell bursts into a tight fan of brilliant gold, white, and red-pink streaks shooting diagonally upward to the top center and downward to the left.
- **Shoreline & Reflections:** Dark silhouettes of tall pine trees dominate the left and center of the frame, with a lower treeline extending to the right. At the bottom left, a bright greenish-yellow outdoor light illuminates a small structure or dock area, while tiny orange-red reflections from the firework launch and shore lights glint on the dark water along the bottom edge.
- **Sky:** Deep blue twilight sky with dark, moody cloud bands stretching across the upper right and lower right.
