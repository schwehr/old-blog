# Shoreline Firework Launch and Dock Rack (`3rd-july-5.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up night photograph from the July 3, 2007 Naticook Lake celebration showing a brilliant, fiery upward blast of a firework launching from the shoreline in front of dense pine trees.

## Scene Description
- **Firework Launch Plume:** On the left side of the frame, an intense, overexposed column of white, yellow, and orange fire and sparks erupts upward from the ground, with a streak of bright green and red trails near the top of the plume and a single trail of sparks continuing to the upper center edge. Thick golden-lit smoke billows around the base and drifts right across the trees.
- **Foreground & Launch Setup:** Along the bottom edge, dark silhouettes of shoreline grass and reeds stand in front of the blast. To the right along the water's edge is a low horizontal wooden structure or dock holding a row of ~16 vertical cylindrical mortar tubes lined up side-by-side, with a small glowing red point at the far right end of the rack.
- **Background:** Tall, dark evergreen trees form a dense wall behind the launch site, with glimpses of dark blue twilight sky filtering through the branches at the top.
