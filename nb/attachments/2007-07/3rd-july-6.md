# Bright Firework Fountain Behind Shoreline Lamp Post (`3rd-july-6.jpg`)

## Overview
A night photograph taken on July 3, 2007 at Naticook Lake, capturing a blazing ground/low-level firework eruption illuminating surrounding pine trees and smoke behind a silhouetted decorative lamp post.

## Scene Description
- **Firework Eruption:** Left of center, a tall, blindingly bright pillar of white-hot and yellow-orange light shoots straight up from behind the shoreline grass, splitting at the top into distinct red, green, and white vertical streaks. Dense clouds of smoke around the blast are lit in dramatic shades of fiery orange and amber, illuminating the branches of the tall pine trees immediately behind it.
- **Foreground Elements:**
  - **Lamp Post:** Right of center in the foreground, a dark, unlit colonial-style post lantern sits atop a low stone or concrete pillar, its glass panes catching warm orange reflections from the firework.
  - **Shoreline:** Silhouetted reeds and tall grass line the lower left and center, with a second smaller post or figure silhouetted directly in front of the bright white core of the blast.
- **Background:** Pitch-black night sky on the right half of the frame, with a small warm horizontal reflection or distant dock light visible near the far right middle edge.
