# Lighting Sparklers at Night (`3rd-july-7.jpg`)

## Overview
A candid, close-up night photograph taken on July 3, 2007 at Naticook Lake showing two people—a woman on the left and a man on the right—illuminated by the intense golden-white glow of handheld sparklers in the dark.

## Scene Description
- **Sparkler Burst:** Near the lower center of the frame, one or more lit sparklers produce a brilliant, starburst-like sphere of white-hot light with dozens of sharp golden-orange needle-like sparks shooting outward in all directions.
- **People:**
  - **Left:** A woman with shoulder-length hair wearing a hooded sweatshirt (with partial lettering `"PERT"` or similar across the chest) looks toward the sparkler, her face and clothing bathed in warm orange light.
  - **Right:** A man with short hair wearing a dark t-shirt leans in from the upper right, looking down at the sparkler as he holds it in his extended left hand.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere:** The background is completely black, creating high-contrast, warm red-orange chiaroscuro lighting across both subjects' faces and hands with slight motion blur typical of a handheld low-light exposure.
