# NASA Ames Viz/Revizit 3D GPR Viewport Thumbnail (`Viz-interface-sml.jpg`)

## Overview
A cropped, smaller thumbnail version of `Viz-interface.jpg` showing only the central 3D visualization viewport from NASA Ames Research Center's `RevizitTestApplication` (without the surrounding Eclipse window title bar, menus, or bottom status tabs).

## 3D Scene & GPR Visualization
- **Rover Model & Orientation Rings:** On the right side of the dark blue 3D viewport, a four-wheeled robotic survey rover with a blue box chassis, vertical GPS dome mast, and twin whip antennas sits at the leading edge of a survey track. Two concentric 3D orientation rings—an inner green ring and an outer yellow ring, each with large forward-pointing triangular arrowheads—encircle the rover.
- **Traverse Arrows:** Along the top edge of the curved track behind the rover, two parallel trails of arrows (small yellow inner arrows and larger light-blue/lavender outer arrows) trace the path taken by the rover.
- **GPR Subsurface Curtain:** Hanging vertically below the rover's path is a curved 3D ribbon displaying Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) cross-sectional data. The curtain is predominantly bright green with distinct horizontal and dipping bands of yellow, orange, red, and dark blue representing subsurface radar reflections at depth.
- **Grids:** A dark blue ground grid extends across the scene, with a second angled grid visible along the lower left side of the GPR curtain.
