# Bailey the Cat After His Summer Haircut (`bailey-after.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor overhead photograph taken in July 2007 showing Bailey, a seal-point long-haired cat (Ragdoll/Himalayan/Birman type), lying on his side on a patterned rug shortly after receiving a "lion cut" summer haircut.

## Scene Description
- **Cat (Bailey):** Bailey is stretched out on his right side facing left, looking downward to the left. His torso and belly fur have been clipped very short into a smooth, suede-like beige/fawn coat, leaving longer fur on his head, neck ruff, lower legs ("boots"), and a full, fluffy dark brown plume tail. His pointed coloration features a dark seal-brown mask on his face, dark ears, and dark brown paws, with a red collar and tag partially visible at his neck.
- **Setting & Flooring:** He lies on a faded rose-pink and mauve floral area rug with an ornate cream border, placed over warm honey-colored hardwood floorboards visible along the top edge of the frame.
