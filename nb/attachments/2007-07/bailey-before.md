# Bailey the Cat Before His Summer Haircut (`bailey-before.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken in July 2007 showing Bailey, a fluffy long-haired seal-point cat, lounging in a bright patch of sunlight on a beige carpet before getting his summer haircut.

## Scene Description
- **Cat (Bailey):** Bailey lies sprawled on his side/back across the floor with his head at the lower left and his body extending toward the upper right. His coat is long, thick, and shaggy—creamy white across his chest and belly (brightly lit by direct sun) and dark seal-brown on his face mask, ears, extended front legs, and hind paws. His pale blue-green eyes look directly toward the camera.
- **Setting & Lighting:** Strong, low-angle sunlight streams from the right, casting a long shadow across the light beige wall-to-wall carpet toward the upper left. The base of a dark red/brown cat scratching post or toy sits immediately behind his head on the far left.
