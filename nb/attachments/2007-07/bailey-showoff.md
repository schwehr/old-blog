# Bailey Showing Off His Summer Lion Cut on the Couch (`bailey-showoff.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical indoor portrait of Bailey the seal-point cat lounging atop the back cushion of a tan sofa in front of a sunny window, showing off his freshly clipped summer "lion cut" and fluffy tail.

## Scene Description
- **Cat (Bailey):** Bailey reclines across the rounded top of a textured tan/olive-brown couch cushion with his head raised on the right, looking directly at the camera. His body fur is clipped short to a velvety cream/fawn length, while his dark brown face mask, ears, front "mitted" paws, lower back leg, and long, bushy dark-brown tail remain full-length. A round metal ID tag and a red plastic tag hang from his collar below his chin.
- **Background & Setting:** A light blue-grey fleece blanket is draped over the left side of the sofa beneath his dangling hind leg and tail. Behind him is a white-framed window looking out onto green grass, with a section of red wall visible at the upper right. Bright natural light from the window highlights the left side of his back and bushy tail.
