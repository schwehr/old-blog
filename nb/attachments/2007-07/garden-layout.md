# Annotated Vegetable Garden Layout Diagram and Inset Photos (`garden-layout.png`)

## Overview
A top-down schematic diagram created in Adobe Illustrator showing the planting layout of Kurt's fenced backyard vegetable garden as of July 4, 2007, accompanied by five small color photographs stacked vertically along the right margin showing each section of the garden.

## Diagram Structure & Perimeter Labels
- **Enclosure & Fence Posts:** A large rectangular outer boundary with a gate gap on the middle-left side and black circular dots marking fence posts along the perimeter. A `1ft` scale bar is located in the upper right corner inside the enclosure.
- **Perimeter Plantings:**
  - **Top Edge (left to right):** `SF`, `SF`, `SF` (Sunflowers).
  - **Left Edge (top to bottom below gate):** Ten `SF` labels along the left fence line.
  - **Bottom Edge (left to right):** `Pumpkin`, `Butternut Squash`, `Squash`, `Buttercup Squash`, `Buttercup Squash`, `Buttercup Squash`.
