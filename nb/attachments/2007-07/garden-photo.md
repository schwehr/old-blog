# Fenced Backyard Vegetable Garden (`garden-photo.png`)

## Overview
A sunny outdoor photograph taken on July 4, 2007 showing Kurt's fenced backyard vegetable garden with parallel planting beds, wire tomato cages, and vertical bean/pea trellises enclosed by a white wooden picket fence.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Trellises:** In the center and lower right foreground, tall green garden stakes (topped with pink flagging tape) and wire mesh panels form a vertical climbing trellis and arch over a dirt bed planted with climbing bean and pea vines. Along the lower left corner, square concrete stepping stones sit just inside the white picket fence.
- **Middle Beds & Walkways:** Parallel soil planting beds run diagonally from the lower left toward the upper right, separated by narrow pathways covered in light-colored plastic or landscape fabric weighted down at the corners with red bricks. Young tomato plants, melons, Swiss chard, and peppers grow in the dark brown soil.
- **Back Corner & Perimeter Fence:** In the upper left corner, tall broad-leaved sunflower plants and wire tomato cages stand against the white picket fence. Beyond the upper left fence, orange daylilies bloom in front of a light green clapboard house wall. Along the right fence line sit a dark green rectangular planter trough and a terracotta-colored planter box.
