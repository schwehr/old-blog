# Phoenix Mars Lander Testbed in the PIT (`ort4-1-pit.JPG`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken during Phoenix Mars Lander Operational Readiness Test 4 (ORT 4) at the Science Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona, showing the spacecraft engineering testbed inside the Payload Interoperability Testbed (PIT) under warm, orange-tinted simulated Martian lighting.

## Scene Description
- **Spacecraft Testbed**:
  - Positioned in the center of the frame on three silver-gray tripod landing legs with circular footpads resting on the simulated terrain surface.
  - The octagonal spacecraft deck supports several science instrument mockups and engineering units:
    - A tall vertical mast on the left-center topped by the rectangular white housing of the Surface Stereo Imager (SSI).
    - White cylindrical and prismatic enclosures on the deck, along with a dark rectangular box containing four cylindrical canisters (part of the TEGA / MECA instrument suite).
    - Underneath the main deck, a large spherical tank wrapped in shiny gold multi-layer insulation (MLI) foil is mounted between the landing struts.
  - Flanking the lander body on both the left and right sides are large, flat, octagonal decagonal solar array wing tables extending horizontally.
- **Simulated Martian Terrain & Background**:
  - The floor around the lander is covered in warm orange-lit fabric, sand, and sculpted dark brown/orange foam blocks simulating Martian boulders and rocks—including a prominent vertical rock block directly in front of the center-right landing leg and an angled dark rock mass on the left.
  - Heavy tan/brown curtains hang along the back-left wall, while a black curtain forms the backdrop on the right, with red and black support cables and tripods visible around the test area.
