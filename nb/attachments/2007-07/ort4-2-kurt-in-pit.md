# Kurt Schwehr Standing Outside the Phoenix Testbed PIT (`ort4-2-kurt-in-pit.JPG`)

## Overview
A slightly motion-blurred indoor portrait of Kurt Schwehr standing just outside the static-electricity safety boundary of the Phoenix Mars Lander testbed inside the Payload Interoperability Testbed (PIT) during Operational Readiness Test 4 (ORT 4) in Tucson, Arizona.

## Scene Description
- **Subject (Foreground Left)**:
  - Kurt Schwehr stands in the foreground from the waist up, wearing a brown baseball cap, glasses, and a light white/cream V-neck T-shirt.
  - Clipped to the front of his shirt is a rectangular mission visitor/staff badge featuring a red top banner, a star logo on the right, and a lanyard around his neck.
- **Background (Phoenix Testbed)**:
  - Behind Kurt to the right, illuminated by warm amber/orange overhead lights reflecting off a foil-covered ceiling, sits the Phoenix Mars Lander testbed.
  - Visible components of the lander include its white-wrapped landing leg strut, the shiny gold foil-wrapped spherical tank beneath the deck, and the upper deck instruments including the tall lattice mast of the Surface Stereo Imager (SSI).
  - A green doorway frame is partially visible along the right edge of the photo.
