# Kurt Schwehr with the Phoenix Mars Lander Mock-Up (`ort4-3-kurt-with-mockup.JPG`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken during Operational Readiness Test 4 (ORT 4) at the Phoenix Science Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona, showing Kurt Schwehr standing next to a full-scale display mock-up of the Phoenix Mars Lander.

## Scene Description
- **Kurt Schwehr (Left)**:
  - Standing on the left side of the frame facing the camera, wearing glasses, a backwards cap, a black short-sleeved graphic T-shirt with a colorful emblem on the chest, a white mission ID badge on a lanyard around his neck, and olive-green cargo pants.
- **Phoenix Mars Lander Mock-Up (Center & Right)**:
  - A full-scale structural model of the Phoenix lander stands on a smooth concrete floor, surrounded by white stanchion posts and a string of small colored lights running along the floor in front of the landing legs.
  - The lander features three metallic tripod landing legs with circular footpads, a gold foil-covered body housing a spherical tank underneath, and two wide octagonal solar panel wings extending horizontally to the left and right with white informational placards resting on them.
  - Rising from the left side of the deck are the black-segmented Robotic Arm (RA) poised in a raised position and the tall open-lattice mast of the Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) topped by its white stereo camera head.
- **Background**:
  - A high beige wall behind the mock-up displays a two-row grid of eight dark-framed informational posters (four on the top row, four on the bottom row) featuring Mars surface imagery and mission graphics.
