# Nick and Neil Configuring a MERboard Display at the Phoenix SOC (`ort4-4-nick-and-neil.JPG`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken during Operational Readiness Test 4 (ORT 4) at the Phoenix Science Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona, on July 18, 2007, showing team members Nick and Neil configuring a Mac mini connected to a large "MERboard" plasma display.

## Scene Description
- **People (Right)**:
  - Two young men stand on the right side of the frame in profile, looking intently toward the large display screen on the left.
  - In the foreground right is a man wearing glasses, a black short-sleeved T-shirt, and a wristwatch, with his left hand resting on a white computer keyboard in front of the screen.
  - Behind him to the left is a second man wearing a red collared polo shirt, also focused on the display.
- **MERboard Display (Left)**:
  - A large black-bezeled plasma screen occupies the left half of the image, with a stylized yellow-and-red `MERboard` sticker affixed to the top-right edge of the bezel.
  - The screen displays a Mac OS X desktop background showing a black-and-white Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) mosaic of the Phoenix lander's gridded solar panel and deck.
  - Open in the center of the screen is the Mac OS X `System Preferences` -> `Displays` dialog window, showing a list of screen resolutions on the left (with one resolution highlighted in blue) and a dual-display arrangement diagram on the right.
- **Mission Clock (Top Left)**:
  - Mounted high on the wall above the display is a black digital mission clock with red LED characters displaying three rows of time:
    - Top row: `2007/200 01:53:20 UTC`
    - Middle row: `07/18/07 18:53:20 LOCAL`
    - Bottom row (partially obscured by the monitor bezel): `... 00:40:59 MARS`
