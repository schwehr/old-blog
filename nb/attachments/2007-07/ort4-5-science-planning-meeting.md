# Sol 3 Science Planning Meeting at the Phoenix SOC (`ort4-5-science-planning-meeting.JPG`)

## Overview
A wide-angle indoor photograph of a crowded Sol 3 midday science team planning meeting inside the Phoenix Science Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona, during Operational Readiness Test 4 (ORT 4) in July 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Meeting Participants & Layout**:
  - Around two dozen scientists and engineers are gathered in a large room furnished with conference tables, maroon upholstered office chairs, and beige fabric cubicle partitions.
  - Near the center-right, a man wearing a green cap and a light green patterned short-sleeved shirt stands at the table addressing a man seated at the far left in front of a bright white projection screen.
  - Seated participants around the central tables have laptops (including Apple PowerBooks/MacBooks and black PC laptops), LCD monitors, water bottles, and notebooks open in front of them.
  - In the foreground right, a man in a green-and-white plaid collared shirt stands behind a cubicle partition looking toward the speaker on the left.
- **Room Details & Signage**:
  - Two white ceiling-mounted digital projectors hang from the drop ceiling near the top center.
  - Cubicle partitions and walls carry printed station signs, including a sign reading `Geology` near the center-left, a sign on the right cubicle partition reading `Chemistry & Mineralogy` above a seating/layout chart, and a white flip-chart easel on the left.
  - In the background, two large black-bezeled plasma displays (MERboards) are mounted in cubicle bays on the left and right, displaying grayscale Mars lander imagery.
  - Along the left wall hang large vertical color posters showing rusty-orange Martian surface landscapes.
