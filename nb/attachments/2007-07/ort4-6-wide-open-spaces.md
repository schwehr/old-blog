# Wide Open Spaces Inside the Phoenix SOC (`ort4-6-wide-open-spaces.JPG`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken during Operational Readiness Test 4 (ORT 4) in July 2007 inside a large, mostly empty hall at the Phoenix Science Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona, slated to be set up as a press or operations area.

## Scene Description
- **People**:
  - **Left**: A young man (Nick) stands facing the camera wearing an olive-green polo shirt with horizontal chest stripes, a white mission badge clipped to his shirt, blue jeans, and flip-flops/sandals, holding a rolled-up white object or bottle in his right hand.
  - **Right**: A man wearing glasses, a light blue short-sleeved button-down shirt with a badge on a lanyard around his neck, dark jeans, and white sneakers stands facing the camera with both arms stretched wide apart to emphasize the vast, empty room.
- **Room & Architecture**:
  - The room features a wide, bare concrete floor, high off-white walls with vertical conduit pipes, and a suspended acoustic tile ceiling.
  - Two large cylindrical dark-red fabric HVAC air ducts run along the ceiling near the upper-left and upper-right edges.
  - In the far left background stand a couple of brown movable partition boards and some white tubing/materials stacked against the base of the back wall.
