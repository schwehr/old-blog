# Phoenix Mars Lander 2007 Mission Logo (`phoenix_mars_lander.png`)

## Overview
The official circular mission patch/emblem graphic for the NASA Phoenix Mars Lander 2007 mission.

## Graphic & Design Details
- **Central Illustration**:
  - Set against a black starfield inside a thin red inner ring, a stylized mythical Phoenix bird rises in a swirling spiral of fiery red, orange, and yellow feathers mixed with cool icy blue and white plumes—symbolizing the mission's exploration of water ice in the Martian northern polar region.
  - Behind the swirling Phoenix sits the globe of Mars rendered in rusty red-orange terrain with a prominent bright white polar ice cap on its upper-right limb.
- **Typography & Visible Text**:
  - **Top Title**: Large stylized red hand-lettered text with a thin white outline arches across the upper half of the globe: `Phoenix`.
  - **Bottom Subtitle**: Matching stylized cyan/light-blue hand-lettered text curves along the lower portion of the emblem: `Mars Lander 2007`.
  - **Outer Border Ring**: White serif text runs clockwise around the wide black outer perimeter band, separated by small white circular bullet dots:
    - `NASA • The University of Arizona/Lunar & Planetary Laboratory • Jet Propulsion Laboratory • Lockheed Martin Space Systems • Canadian Space Agency •`
