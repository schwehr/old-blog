# Neon Green Illuminated Saguaro Cactus Sculpture at Night (`tucson-nights.JPG`)

## Overview
A nighttime photograph taken in Tucson, Arizona, during Kurt Schwehr's July 2007 visit for Phoenix Mars Lander Operational Readiness Test 4 (ORT 4), showing a saguaro cactus-shaped outdoor light sculpture glowing in bright neon green against the dark night sky.

## Scene Description
- **Illuminated Cactus Sculpture**:
  - Centered vertically in the frame is a tall artificial saguaro cactus outlined with vertical ribs of bright green rope lighting or neon tubing.
  - The cactus features a tall central trunk and three upward-curving side arms—one curving out from the left side, a smaller high arm near the upper right of the trunk, and a third arm curving out from the right side.
  - Slight camera motion blur during the nighttime exposure creates a double-line halo effect along the glowing bright green and white-hot light tubes.
- **Background**:
  - Pitch-black night sky in the upper half, with dark green desert bushes and foliage faintly illuminated by the green glow of the cactus around its base.
