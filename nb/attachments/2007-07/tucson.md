# Sunset and Monsoon Clouds Over Palm Trees in Tucson (`tucson.jpg`)

## Overview
An evening twilight photograph taken in Tucson, Arizona, in mid-July 2007 during monsoon season, showing silhouetted palm trees and buildings against a dramatic pink, orange, and purple sunset sky beneath dark storm clouds.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Silhouettes**:
  - The lower third of the frame is in deep shadow, showing the dark silhouettes of low-rise buildings, leafy trees, and several small pinpoint street or building lights glowing in white and warm amber.
  - Rising above the roofline into the sky are three distinct pairs of tall, slender fan palm trees—one pair on the left, a shorter pair in the center with windswept fronds, and a tall pair on the right—along with a tiny distant palm silhouette near the lower-right horizon.
- **Sky & Clouds**:
  - Heavy, dark charcoal-black monsoon clouds fill the upper-left and upper-center portions of the sky.
  - Beneath and between the dark clouds, the twilight sky glows with vibrant shades of coral pink, salmon orange, pale yellow near the left horizon, and dusty violet-blue toward the right.
