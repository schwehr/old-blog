# R/V Alliance Viewed from the Flamingo in the Black Sea (`alliance_from_flamingo_2007aug13.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime maritime photograph taken on August 13, 2007 by Roland Arsenault from the deck of the vessel *Flamingo* during the Black Sea expedition with the DOERRI AUV, showing the NATO research vessel *NRV Alliance* on the horizon.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Deck of the *Flamingo*)**:
  - A white inflatable rigid-hulled/tender boat (RIB) sits diagonally across the deck on bright green artificial turf ("astro turf").
  - The port bow of the inflatable boat bears large black Cyrillic registration lettering: `СЕВ 0404К` (Sevastopol registration), with faint stencil text (`ПР 9...`) near the stern on the right.
  - Inside the tender is a central console with a clear windshield framed in stainless steel tubing, white bench seating, a small yellow-and-purple/red flag mounted behind the console, and a black outboard motor at the stern.
  - Behind the tender, a wooden-topped white metal deck railing runs horizontally across the mid-ground, with a tall vertical white pole/antenna rising on the right.
- **Background (Sea & *NRV Alliance*)**:
  - A calm, deep blue Black Sea stretches to a flat horizon under a clear blue sky with a soft bank of pale pinkish-white clouds on the upper right.
  - In the upper-left distance, the white-hulled research vessel *Alliance* is seen in port-bow profile on the water, featuring its distinctive buff/yellow and black stack and tall masts/superstructure.
