# Cropped GeoZui4D View of Black Sea Cruise Location (`blacksea-geozui-sml.jpg`)

## Overview
A modified, smaller (`604 x 328`) version of [`blacksea-geozui.jpg`](file:///usr/local/google/home/schwehr/backup/cowfish/schwehr/blog/nb/attachments/2007-08/blacksea-geozui.jpg) edited by Kurt Schwehr to fit inline in the August 13, 2007 blog post *"Black Sea cruise - Images by Roland"*. The image retains the top `GeoZui4D` window header/menu bar and the bottom timeline bar while vertically cropping the central 3D viewport to focus closely on Europe and the Black Sea.

## Interface & Cropped Viewport Details
- **Top Window Frame & Menu Bar**: Displays the `X GeoZui4D` title bar and the menu items `File`, `View`, `Options`, `Tools`, `Commands`, `GZConsole`, `Mission Planner`, and `Help`.
- **Cropped 3D Map Area**:
  - Shows the northern half of the 3D terrain model, spanning from the Aegean Sea, Anatolia, and the Caucasus Mountains at the bottom up to Scandinavia and the North Atlantic at the top.
  - A 3D white-and-grey ship model is prominently centered in the Black Sea just off the Crimean Peninsula, with the Sea of Azov to its northeast and the light-blue Caspian Sea further east.
  - High mountain ranges (the Alps on the left and the Greater Caucasus to the east of the Black Sea) are rendered with white/grey peaks against tan and brown terrain.
- **Bottom Timeline Bar**: Preserves the dual-scale green-and-white timeline strip (`57m`, `15s`, `45m`, `10h`), green playback transport buttons (`<`, `||`, `>`, `1:1`) on the left, and the timestamp `Mon Aug 13 09:57:12 2007` on the right.
