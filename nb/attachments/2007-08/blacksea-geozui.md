# GeoZui4D 3D Visualization of Black Sea Cruise Location (`blacksea-geozui.jpg`)

## Overview
A full-sized (`1280 x 979`) screenshot of the `GeoZui4D` software application running under an X11 window manager, displaying a 3D perspective digital elevation and bathymetry model of Europe, North Africa, the Mediterranean, the Red Sea, and the Black Sea, with a 3D ship icon marking the Black Sea expedition location on August 13, 2007.

## Interface & Window Controls
- **Title Bar**: An X11 window title bar at the top left shows an `X` icon and the window title `GeoZui4D`, with minimize, maximize, and close (`X`) buttons at the top right.
- **Menu Bar**: Directly below the title bar, a horizontal menu bar on a white background contains:
  `File` | `View` | `Options` | `Tools` | `Commands` | `GZConsole` | `Mission Planner` (left-aligned) and `Help` (far right).
- **Bottom Timeline & Playback Bar**:
  - **Upper Timeline Strip**: A black bar with green tick marks showing fine time increments, labeled `57m` on the far left and `15s` near the center, with a blue vertical cursor line at the center.
  - **Lower Timeline Strip**:
    - On the far left, green playback control icons: play-reverse (`<`), pause (`||`), play-forward (`>`), and a `1:1` speed indicator button.
    - A white/light-green horizontal timeline bar with green tick marks labeled `30m`, `45m`, and `10h`.
    - On the far right, a black timestamp box displaying `Mon Aug 13 09:57:12 2007` in white text.

## 3D Map & Bathymetry View
- **Geographic Coverage**: The 3D terrain surface is tilted in perspective looking northward from North Africa/Arabia in the foreground toward Scandinavia and the North Atlantic at the top.
- **Color Coding**:
  - **Land**: Shaded in yellows, tans, and dark browns by elevation, with grey/white high-altitude mountain peaks along the Alps, Caucasus Mountains, and East African highlands.
  - **Water**: Shaded in bright cyan for shallow shelves/seas (such as the North Sea, Baltic Sea, Sea of Azov, Caspian Sea, and Red Sea margins) transitioning to deep royal blue in deep basins (the Mediterranean Sea, Black Sea, and Atlantic Ocean).
- **Vessel Marker**: A 3D grey-and-white research ship model is positioned in the northern Black Sea just south of the Crimean Peninsula, indicating the operating area of the *Flamingo* and *Alliance* cruise.
