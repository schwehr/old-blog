# Climbing Bean Vines on a Wire Garden Arch (`garden-arch-beans.JPG`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph of Kurt Schwehr's backyard vegetable garden from the August 12, 2007 blog post *"Garden update"*, showing green bean vines climbing up a curved wire-mesh trellis arch next to a white picket fence.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Garden Arch**:
  - A tall, curved arch constructed from heavy-gauge galvanized wire fencing panels spans over the entrance path of the garden, supported by green garden stakes (some tipped with pink flagging tape).
  - Leafy green pole bean vines climb up the left side of the wire arch, with several long green bean pods hanging down near the top left of the trellis.
  - At the base of the arch on the left are low, broad-leafed squash or cucumber plants and an empty terracotta-colored plastic planter trough resting against the fence.
  - A row of square concrete stepping stones runs along the inside of the white picket fence.
- **Right Garden Bed**:
  - Dark soil beds lined with strips of black landscape fabric / plastic mulch.
  - Feathery green carrot tops grow in the lower right foreground, with leafy green and red-veined beet greens beside them.
  - Further back on the right, tomato plants bear clusters of green and ripening orange-red tomatoes, and a small yellow sunflower blooms near the right side of the wire arch.
- **Background**:
  - A white wooden picket fence encloses the garden plot, with a yellow plastic bag (`SADDLERY...`) hanging on a fence picket on the far right.
  - Beyond the fence is a green lawn, dense dark green trees, and a two-story light-colored neighboring house with dark shutters on the upper left.
