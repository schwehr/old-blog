# Tall Row of Sunflowers Next to Garage (`garden-row-of-sunflowers.JPG`)

## Overview
A daytime outdoor photograph from the August 12, 2007 *"Garden update"* blog post showing a row of towering sunflower plants growing along the back edge of a fenced garden plot right in front of a green-shingled garage.

## Scene Description
- **Sunflowers (Mid-ground)**:
  - A line of tall, thick-stemmed sunflower plants rises well above the white picket fence.
  - The tallest "mammoth" sunflower on the left-center reaches all the way up to the roofline/gutter of the garage, crowned with a large bright yellow flower head facing to the right.
  - Adjacent sunflower stalks with broad, sunlit green leaves and smaller yellow buds or blooms stand across the center and right, supported by tall green stakes (some marked with pink ribbon).
- **Foreground Garden & Fence**:
  - In the foreground inside the fenced plot are several wire tomato cages holding up tomato vines (with a couple of round green and orange tomatoes visible at the lower left) and leafy garden plants at the bottom right.
  - A white wooden picket fence surrounds the garden on the left, back, and right edges.
- **Background (Garage & Trees)**:
  - Directly behind the back picket fence is a single-story garage with sage-green clapboard/shingle siding, two white-trimmed windows, a white gutter and downspout on the left corner, and a grey asphalt-shingle hip roof.
  - Dense green forest foliage borders the left side of the garage, and a tall leafy shrub stands on the right edge of the frame.
