# Unopening Sunflower Bud Next to Fence Post (`garden-sunflower-newbloom.JPG`)

## Overview
A close-up vertical photograph from the August 12, 2007 *"Garden update"* blog post showing a spiky green sunflower bud just before blooming (which the author describes as looking *"like a alien spoor thing"*).

  - The fuzzy green stem rises vertically on the right, with large sunlit green leaves extending to the lower-left and upper-right.
  - A weathered green-painted wooden/bamboo stake runs diagonally upward to the left of the bud, tied or positioned alongside the plant for support.
- **Fence & Background**:
  - Immediately to the left of the bud is the square-cut top of a white-painted wooden fence picket/post showing visible wood grain.
  - Further back on the upper left is the white picket fence enclosing the garden and green lawn in the background.
