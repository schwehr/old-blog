# Yellow Sunflower Bloom Next to the Garden Gate (`garden-sunflower.JPG`)

## Overview
A close-up vertical photograph from the August 12, 2007 *"Garden update"* post showing a bright yellow sunflower blossom in warm sunlight right beside a white wooden picket fence and garden gate.

## Scene Description
- **Sunflower Blossom**:
  - Centered in the frame is a vibrant, fully opened yellow sunflower head tilted slightly upward toward the viewer.
  - Long, pointed, golden-yellow ray petals radiate evenly around a compact, dark brown central disc floret head.
  - A tiny insect sits near the tip of the upper-leftmost yellow petal.
  - Below and to the left of the flower head are large, textured, heart-shaped green sunflower leaves catching the bright sunlight.
- **Background & Surroundings**:
  - Directly to the right of the bloom are the pointed white wooden pickets of the garden gate/fence, dappled with shadows.
  - Behind the plant in the upper left, another section of white picket fence runs horizontally, with an orange flower pot visible on the ground near the fence corner.
  - Below the flower on the lower left, square concrete stepping stones are set into the grass along the garden path.
