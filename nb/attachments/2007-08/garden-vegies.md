# Harvested Garden Vegetables and Sunflowers on Kitchen Counter (`garden-vegies.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph from the August 28, 2007 blog post *"Garden update"* showing a colorful assortment of freshly harvested garden vegetables, herbs, and cut sunflower heads spread out on a wooden kitchen countertop/island before making homemade pasta sauce.

## Harvest Items & Arrangement
- **Tomatoes (Center & Left)**:
  - A large pile of ripe red-orange medium globe and plum/paste tomatoes occupies the center left of the table.
  - A cluster of small, bright red cherry/grape tomatoes lies at the bottom center.
- **Root Vegetables (Bottom Foreground)**:
  - **Carrot**: At the bottom left-center, an orange carrot with a funny forked/twisted double root and long feathery green tops extending diagonally up to the right.
  - **Beets**: Next to the carrot and on the bottom right are two dark purplish-red, elongated/cylindrical beets with long red-stemmed, green-and-burgundy leaves extending upward.
- **Cucumbers & Green Beans (Far Left)**:
  - Three dark green cucumbers lie parallel to each other on the left edge, topped by a handful of long, slender green string beans.
- **Squash & Corn (Right Center)**:
  - Three pale yellow/cream-colored summer squash (donated by neighbors) rest to the right of the tomatoes, alongside husks of fresh sweet corn in the upper center-right.
- **Herbs (Top Center)**:
  - A large, lush bunch of fresh green sweet basil stems and leaves is piled at the top center.
- **Cut Sunflower Heads (Far Left & Far Right)**:
  - On the far right is a large, round, maturing sunflower seed head ringed by pointed green bracts with dried orange petals.
  - On the upper left behind the cucumbers and tomatoes are two more cut sunflower heads with dark brown centers and curling dried golden-orange petals.
