# Gnuplot 3D Surface Grid Plot (`gnuplot-grid.png`)

## Overview
A 3D wireframe surface plot generated by `gnuplot` (`splot`) showing a test dataset (`4.dat`) on a white background, posted on August 5, 2007, to demonstrate 3D gridding (`set dgrid3d`).

## Plot & Data Details
- **Surface Mesh**: A red wireframe grid representing a 4×4 or 5×5 surface over an $(x, y)$ domain from `0.5` to `3.5`. Most of the interior grid points dip down toward `0.0`–`1.0`, forming a shallow basin, while the back corner at $(0.5, 0.5)$ rises sharply to a peak value of `2` on the vertical $z$-axis.
- **Axes & Bounding Box**:
  - Drawn in solid black lines with inward/outward tick marks.
  - **Left Vertical Axis ($z$-axis)**: Labeled from bottom to top with `0.5` (at the base corner), `0.6`, `0.8`, `1`, `1.2`, `1.4`, `1.6`, `1.8`, and `2`.
  - **Front-Left Horizontal Axis ($x$-axis)**: Runs from the left corner (`0.5`) down and right to the front corner (`3.5`), with intermediate tick labels at `1`, `1.5`, `2`, `2.5`, and `3`.
  - **Front-Right Horizontal Axis ($y$-axis)**: Runs from the front corner (`3.5`) up and right to the right corner (`0.5`), with intermediate tick labels at `3`, `2.5`, `2`, `1.5`, and `1`.
  - Vertical black posts connect the four corners of the horizontal base plane up to the edges of the red wireframe surface.
- **Legend**: In the top right corner, the dataset label `'4.dat'` is printed in black monospace font beside a short horizontal red line segment.
