# ROV Hercules Recovering the DOERRI AUV on the Black Sea Floor (`hercules_argus_doerri_1.jpg`)

## Overview
A deep-sea underwater photograph taken from the towed camera sled *Argus* looking down at the remotely operated vehicle (ROV) *Hercules* as it positions the flooded autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) *DOERRI* into a recovery elevator frame on the floor of the Black Sea in August 2007.

## Scene Description
- **ROV *Hercules***: Occupying the center-right of the frame is the large, boxy yellow syntactic-foam flotation body of ROV *Hercules*, viewed from above and behind. Its dark tether/umbilical cable trails off to the right, and two white diagonal alignment stripes are visible on top of its yellow hull. Bright underwater lights mounted on the front of *Hercules* illuminate the seafloor and the recovery apparatus ahead of it.
- **AUV *DOERRI* & Recovery Frame**: Directly in front of *Hercules* (to the left) is a rectangular tubular frame resting on the pale, fine-grained sediment of the seafloor. Lying inside the horizontal tray of the frame is the slender, white, torpedo-shaped hull of the *DOERRI* AUV, brightly lit by *Hercules*'s floodlights.
- **Flotation Column**: Rising vertically from the left side of the recovery frame is a tall tower structure topped with a vertical stack of four round yellow flotation spheres enclosed in yellow netting/harnesses, with a dark acoustic release cylinder mounted alongside the top spheres.
- **Surrounding Environment**: The surrounding water of the Black Sea is pitch dark at the top and right edges, transitioning to a murky blue-green halo around the illuminated sediment patch on the bottom.
