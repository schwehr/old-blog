# Close-Up of ROV Hercules Manipulator Holding AUV DOERRI (`hercules_argus_doerri_2.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up underwater photograph captured from ROV *Hercules*'s onboard camera showing its robotic manipulator arms placing the flooded *DOERRI* AUV onto a netted recovery sled on the seafloor of the Black Sea.

## Scene Description
- **AUV *DOERRI***: The white cylindrical, torpedo-shaped body of the *DOERRI* AUV stretches horizontally across the middle of the frame from left to right.
  - **Nose & Forward Section (Left)**: The rounded white nose cone has a black transducer/sensor window on its lower side and blue tape wrapped around a seam, followed by a gray collar and a dark blue band.
  - **Midbody & Top**: A bright yellow upper hull section features black stenciled text (partially visible at an angle) and a long, angled metal whip antenna with yellow-and-black striped tape near its tip. Below the yellow section, `PR M` with a circular logo is printed on the white hull. Mounted atop the aft-midsection is a dark gray cylindrical instrument (such as a pinger or sensor) secured to a black bracket.
  - **Tail Section (Right)**: The tapered white tail section features a blue-and-yellow cartoon mascot decal (the University of Delaware Fightin' Blue Hen, "YoUDee") next to a blue `F3` label. A cloud of fine sediment rises around the tail fins at the far right.
- **Manipulator Arms**: Two heavy dark metal robotic manipulator arms from ROV *Hercules* frame the top left and top right corners. The left claw is actively grasping a T-shaped lifting handle/bail recessed into the gray collar of the AUV.
- **Recovery Sled & Background**: Beneath the AUV is a black mesh net stretched across a white tubular frame. In the background behind the AUV are a black plastic milk crate holding yellow ropes, a red strap, and a white perforated metal vertical housing. Along the bottom edge is part of *Hercules*'s front bumper and equipment tray, including a round red-capped canister.
