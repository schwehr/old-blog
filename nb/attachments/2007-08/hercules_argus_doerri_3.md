# Hoisting AUV DOERRI and Recovery Sled Aboard Ship (`hercules_argus_doerri_3.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph showing deck crew in white coveralls and hardhats along the port rail of a research vessel (NRV *Alliance*) guiding the recovery elevator frame holding the *DOERRI* AUV as it is hoisted out of the Black Sea.

## Scene Description
- **Recovery Rig & AUV *DOERRI***: Suspended over the deep blue water by orange lifting straps on the right side of the image is the tall recovery elevator apparatus:
  - **Top Section**: A vertical column of three large, ribbed yellow spherical/hemispherical syntactic flotation buoys strapped into a yellow metal frame, with a dark gray cylindrical acoustic release mounted vertically on its left side.
  - **Bottom Tray**: A rectangular white pipe frame lined with black netting hangs below the buoys, tilted at an angle. Resting inside the right side of the netted tray is the white-and-yellow torpedo-shaped hull of the *DOERRI* AUV, nose pointing down toward the water with water dripping from it.
- **Ship Deck & Crew (Left)**:
  - Along the left side of the frame is the white superstructure and railing of the ship.
  - In the foreground, a crew member in white coveralls and a white hardhat leans out over the railing, reaching with his right hand to steady the red-and-white vertical post of the recovery frame.
  - Just behind him, a second crew member in white coveralls and an orange-red hardhat holds a long wooden pole/boat hook extended toward the rigging.
  - Further back along the rail stand two more crew members (one in an orange-red hardhat, one in a blue hardhat holding coiled line), with two additional observers visible on an upper deck level at the top left.
