# Tagged Humpback Whale Surfacing Near NOAA Ship Nancy Foster (`humpback2.jpg`)

## Overview
A NOAA photograph showing a humpback whale (*Megaptera novaeangliae*) with a suction-cup acoustic tag (DTAG) attached to its back surfacing in the foreground, while the white NOAA research vessel *Nancy Foster* (`R 352`) steams across the background during a whale-tagging study in Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary.

## Scene Description
- **Tagged Humpback Whale (Foreground)**:
  - Low in the center-right foreground, the dark black back and stubby dorsal fin (with white barnacle/scarring marks along its ridge) of a humpback whale break the calm, deep blue ocean surface.
  - Forward of the dorsal fin (toward the right), the small, pale suction-cup DTAG package is visibly attached to the whale's upper back. A faint mist from the whale's blow rises above the center of its back.
- **NOAA Ship *Nancy Foster* (Background)**:
  - Viewed in port-side profile across the upper two-thirds of the image against a clear blue sky is the white-hulled oceanographic research ship *Nancy Foster*.
  - **Bow Markings**: On the port bow, large dark blue block letters read `NOAA` above hull number `R 352`, flanked by the circular blue-and-white NOAA emblem and a small bow thruster symbol below. Rust streaks trail down from the dark anchor hawsepipe and deck scuppers.
  - **Superstructure & Aft Deck**: Forward of amidships rises the multi-level white bridge and deckhouse topped with radar domes, antennas, and masts flying the U.S. flag. Aft of the twin blue-trimmed exhaust stacks is the open working deck equipped with a heavy black A-frame and a black articulated knuckle-boom crane.
