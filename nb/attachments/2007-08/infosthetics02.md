# Circular Network Graph Data Visualization (`infosthetics02.jpg`)

## Overview
An abstract radial/concentric network visualization (referenced from a *Smashing Magazine* article on modern data visualization approaches, originally via *Information Aesthetics*) displaying interconnected nodes and curved edges arranged in a spiral/concentric pattern on a white background.

## Visual Details
- **Nodes (Red Circles & Dots)**: Hundreds of bright red circular nodes of varying sizes are arranged along sweeping concentric arcs that spiral outward from a tight central ring toward the right side of the image. Most nodes are small solid red dots, while larger nodes appear as thick red open rings (especially prominent along the outer right arcs).
- **Edges (Black Curved Lines)**: Dense bundles of thin, semi-transparent black curved lines (splines) connect nodes both within the same concentric layer and across different layers. The line density is heaviest on the right half of the spiral and around the tightly wound inner core, creating dark, heavily shaded bands of overlapping curves, while the left side shows sparser, looser sweeping arcs reaching outlying red dots.
