# Deer and Fawn on the Lawn at NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (`jpl-deer.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph taken on the campus of NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) in Pasadena, California, showing a young buck grazing on a green lawn beside a resting spotted fawn in front of a campus building and parked cars.

## Scene Description
- **Deer on the Lawn (Foreground)**:
  - Across the bottom half of the frame is a manicured, sunlit green grass lawn dappled with shade.
  - On the lower left, a small brown fawn with white spots along its back lies curled on the grass, facing right with its large ears perked up.
  - On the right, an adult/young male mule deer with a tan coat and small velvet antlers stands in left profile with its head lowered, grazing on the grass.
- **Trees, Cars & Building (Background)**:
  - Behind the lawn on the left, a light blue sedan is parked next to a bright red fire hydrant and a multi-trunked crape myrtle tree in full bloom with vibrant magenta-pink flowers.
  - Behind the trees and parked cars stands a light beige JPL building (Building 168 / Image Processing Laboratory area) with dark-framed windows and a red double-door entrance.
  - To the right, a black metal fence runs along a raised patio/walkway beside a red octagonal `STOP` sign and lush green shade trees.
