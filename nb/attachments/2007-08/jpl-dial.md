# Digital Image Animation Laboratory (DIAL) at JPL (`jpl-dial.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph of the Digital Image Animation Laboratory (DIAL) at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), showing a control-room-style workspace filled with Sun Microsystems and Apple Mac workstations, equipment racks, CRT/LCD video monitors, and a digital timecode clock.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Workstations**:
  - In the right foreground, the back of a large silver-and-white **Sun Microsystems** flat-panel monitor (displaying the diamond logo and `Sun microsystems` text) sits on a desk with a curved monitor arm, mouse, and keyboard.
  - Arranged in a curved row from left to right across the main desk are four flat-panel displays: a large silver Apple Cinema Display on the far left, a black-bezel LCD monitor, a white-bezel Apple Studio/Cinema Display on an articulated arm, a beige CRT monitor in the center, and another white-bezel LCD monitor on the right displaying a colorful pink/purple abstract desktop wallpaper.
- **Equipment Racks & Video Wall (Midground & Background)**:
  - Behind the desks, floor-to-ceiling wooden and metal 19-inch equipment racks house banks of video decks, patch panels, waveform monitors/vectorscopes, and small preview CRTs.
  - Above the left rack bay, a red LED digital clock displays `8:23:39` beneath a white informational sign reading `DIAL — Digital Image Animation Laboratory`.
  - Along the top of the racks sit five large television/video monitors: the upper-left screen shows a green-tinted graphic interface, the next displays white title text (`HiRISE view of...`), the center-left screen shows a planetary rover/lander animation frame, the center-right screen is dark green, and the far-right screen shows a brightly lit broadcast feed of a person speaking.
