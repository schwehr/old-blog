# Illuminated Planetary Image Lightboards in JPL Hallway (`jpl-lightboards.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph looking down a dimly lit carpeted hallway outside the Image Processing Laboratory at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), where large backlit lightboards display planetary and space mission imagery along both walls.

## Scene Description
- **Hallway Layout**: The view looks straight down a narrow corridor with dark gray walls, a tan/beige carpeted floor illuminated by pools of warm light from the displays, and a suspended acoustic-tile ceiling. At the far end of the corridor, an open doorway leads into an equipment/office room with a red fire extinguisher mounted on the left doorframe and small flyers/posters taped to the brown wall on the right.
- **Left Wall Lightboards**:
  - In the left foreground is a large, silver-framed vertical backlit display showing a high-contrast planetary crater or icy surface feature with a dark irregular center surrounded by a bright glowing rim.
  - Beyond it hangs a second smaller horizontal backlit frame glowing brightly.
- **Right Wall Lightboards**:
  - In the right foreground is a silver-framed backlit display featuring an orange-tinted Martian surface landscape above a dark caption bar with orange title text and a white descriptive text block. Behind and below it on the wall is a clear plastic brochure holder.
  - Further down the right wall are two more framed displays (one glowing greenish-white and another showing a purple/blue image).
