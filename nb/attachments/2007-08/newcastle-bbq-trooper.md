# Attendees and Trooper the Dog at the New Castle Coast Guard Station BBQ (`newcastle-bbq-trooper.jpg`)

## Overview
A candid outdoor photograph taken on August 29, 2007, at the New Castle, New Hampshire Coast Guard station barbecue, showing four people standing on a grassy lawn watching an energetic black dog named Trooper straining on his leash.
