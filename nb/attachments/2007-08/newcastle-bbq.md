# Picnic Pavilion and Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse at USCG Station Portsmouth Harbor (`newcastle-bbq.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide twilight photograph taken at dusk on August 29, 2007, during a barbecue at the U.S. Coast Guard Station Portsmouth Harbor (Fort Constitution) in New Castle, New Hampshire, showing the waterfront picnic pavilion in the foreground and Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse in the background.

## Scene Description
- **Picnic Pavilion (Foreground)**: Occupying the lower right two-thirds of the frame is a low, brown wood-sided open-air picnic shelter with a gently sloping gray shingled roof and a short brick chimney on the right. Underneath the shelter roof, wooden picnic tables are visible on the grass overlooking the calm water on the far right. A grassy slope rises to the left of the shelter.
- **Coast Guard Station & Fort Constitution (Left Background)**: Beyond the grassy slope on the left is an asphalt parking area where two pickup trucks (one black, one silver) are parked near the historic stone and brick entrance archway of Fort Constitution. To the right of the fort wall stands a flagpole flying the U.S. flag and a tan Coast Guard station building topped with a glass-enclosed watchtower and radio antennas.
- **Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse & Water (Center-Right Background)**: Rising above the roofline of the picnic shelter in the center of the frame is the white cylindrical tower and black lantern room of Portsmouth Harbor Light, set against the dark tree line of the opposite shore and a soft pink-and-lavender sunset sky.
