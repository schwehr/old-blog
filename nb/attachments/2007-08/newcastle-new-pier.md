# Construction of the New UNH Pier at New Castle (`newcastle-new-pier.jpg`)

## Overview
A dusk photograph taken on August 29, 2007, at New Castle, New Hampshire, showing two large lattice-boom construction cranes working on the new concrete University of New Hampshire (UNH) pier, with small research/work boats moored in the calm harbor foreground.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Boats & Float**: Moored in the glassy water in the lower-center foreground is a square floating dock structure enclosing an open-water pen/frame with netting, flanked by two small workboats:
  - **Left Boat**: A red-hulled open utility boat with a small white canopy/pilothouse roof over the helm and an outboard motor on the stern.
  - **Right Boat**: A white-hulled open utility boat with a forward cabin/windshield and orange fenders along its side.
  - Slightly further back to the right, a small white sailboat and a white skiff with a blue cover are moored near red mooring balls.
- **New UNH Pier & Cranes (Midground)**:
  - Stretching horizontally from the left edge across most of the frame is the newly constructed concrete deck of the UNH pier, supported by rows of dark vertical pilings.
  - Positioned behind/alongside the pier on barges are two tall lattice-boom cranes angled upward to the right against the pale pink and blue twilight sky: a taller crane on the left with a jib extension and hanging hook block, and a second crane further out near the right end of the pier. Tall vertical spud pilings rise from the construction barges.
- **Background**: Across the harbor behind the pier is a low, dark green tree-covered shoreline dotted with houses and white boats moored in the distance.
