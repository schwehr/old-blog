# Night Launch of the Phoenix Mars Lander (`phx-launch.png`)

## Overview
A night video frame capture showing the August 4, 2007 liftoff of NASA's *Phoenix* Mars Lander atop a Delta II rocket from Cape Canaveral Air Force Station, Florida, as the rocket clears the launch tower amidst a billowing exhaust plume.

## Scene Description
- **Rocket & Exhaust Plume (Center)**:
  - High in the center-left of the dark frame, a brilliant, star-like white-and-orange fireball from the Delta II rocket's main engine and solid rocket boosters blazes intensely, casting long diagonal lens-flare rays across the smoky night sky.
  - Below the ascending rocket, thick clouds of illuminated gray-white and orange-tinted exhaust smoke billow outward across the launch pad.
- **Launch Pad Structures & Foreground**:
  - Silhoutted in dark gray against the glowing smoke clouds at the base of the plume are the vertical launch tower/mast on the left-center and a boxy rectangular building/structure on the right.
  - The lower third of the image is pitch black except for two small, bright reddish-white points of light near the bottom-left foreground.
