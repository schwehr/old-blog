# Red and Yellow Garden Sunflower (`sunflower.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up outdoor photograph of a vibrant bi-colored sunflower in full bloom in Kurt Schwehr's home garden in late August 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Central Florets (Disc)**: At the center of the composition is a large, circular dark brown and burgundy disc composed of tightly packed florets. An outer ring of open florets forms a fluffy, textured dark reddish-brown wreath around a slightly recessed, greenish-brown inner disk of unopened buds arranged in spiral patterns, with flecks of golden-yellow pollen visible.
- **Ray Petals**: Radiating outward from the central disc is a dense crown of pointed petals displaying a striking color gradient: bright golden-yellow at the base next to the disc, transitioning through rich fiery orange-red across the middle of each petal, and tapering to bright yellow-orange tips. Strong sunlight from the left casts a dark shadow from the raised central disc across the base of the petals on the right side.
- **Foliage & Background**: Broad, serrated green sunflower leaves frame the flower head in the lower-left and lower-right corners, while darker shaded leaves and stems appear in the upper-left. Behind the plant, vertical white wooden slats of a garden fence are partially visible against dark background shadows, and the orange-yellow petals of a second sunflower peek into the frame at the top-right edge. A thin grey border surrounds the photograph.
