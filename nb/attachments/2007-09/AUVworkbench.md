# AUV Workbench Mission Planner Map View (`AUVworkbench.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the 2D mission planning map view from the Naval Postgraduate School's **AUV Workbench** software, displaying an autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) waypoint track and search pattern overlaid on a coastal chart and aerial orthophoto.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Toolbar**: A light grey toolbar at the top left includes:
  - A two-button tool selector containing a black **Selection Arrow** icon (depressed/active) and a **Pan/Move** four-way arrow icon.
  - A blue 8-directional navigation compass rose button array for panning the map view.
  - Two small blue magnifying glass icons with `+` and `-` symbols for zooming in and out.
  - A white text box showing the current map scale: `1:103,137`.
  - A stacked blue-squares **Layers** icon to the right of the scale box.
- **Scale Bar**: In the upper-left corner of the map canvas is a white rectangular box bordered in black containing two horizontal black scale bars labeled `1 Mile` (top, longer) and `1 Km` (bottom, shorter).
