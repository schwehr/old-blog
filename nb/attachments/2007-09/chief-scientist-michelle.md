# Chief Scientist Michelle Checking an Oscilloscope on R/V Cocheco (`chief-scientist-michelle.jpg`)

## Overview
An onboard photograph taken during an acoustic equipment test cruise aboard the University of New Hampshire research vessel *R/V Cocheco* on September 13, 2007, showing Chief Scientist Michelle crouching on deck to adjust an oscilloscope.

## Scene Description
- **Subject**: In the center of the frame, Michelle crouches in profile facing left. She wears a bright lime-green long-sleeved shirt, olive-khaki cropped pants, olive strappy sandals, and a bright orange Type I offshore personal flotation device (life vest) with black webbing straps and a white reflective patch on the shoulder. Her brown hair is tied back, and she reaches with her right hand to adjust a top dial on the oscilloscope while steadying herself with her left hand on the black equipment case.
- **Equipment**: Resting on top of a large black plastic ruggedized transport case on the left are two pieces of electronic test gear:
  - In the center-left foreground, a portable teal-blue and white analog/digital **oscilloscope** propped up at an angle on its white fold-out handle/stand, with Michelle's hand adjusting its upper controls.
  - To the far left, a smaller beige/grey rectangular electronic instrument box with BNC coaxial cables (black and orange) connected to its front panel.
- **Surroundings**: On the right side of the frame, another crew member stands in grey trousers, a bright blue shirt, and black-and-white sneakers. Bright sunlight illuminates the deck and equipment, while red and black cables and yellow webbing straps lie coiled on the deck floor in the lower right.
