# UNH Coastal Marine Laboratory Building in New Castle (`cml.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor daytime photograph of the historic brick Harbor Defense Mine Building that houses the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Coastal Marine Laboratory (and the `tide3` water-level/AIS logging computer) in New Castle, New Hampshire, taken on September 28, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Building Exterior**: A single-story, gable-roofed red brick building occupies the center of the frame under a deep blue sky with scattered white clouds.
  - **Doors & Windows**: At the center of the facade is a pair of large white wooden double barn-style doors with black strap hinges. Flanking the doors on the ground level are two multi-pane sash windows protected by black iron security bars and topped with pale stone lintels. Centered high in the gable peak is a third barred window, with a vertical metal conduit pipe running down the brick wall to its left and black overhead utility wires leading to its top frame.
  - **Roof & Chimney**: A brick chimney rises from the right slope of the roof near the peak, with a vertical white VHF/AIS whip antenna mast mounted beside it.
- **Signage**:
  - In front of the left ground-floor window stands a wooden post frame holding a white rectangular sign with faded lettering reading `UNH COASTAL MARINE LABORATORY`, mounted above a wooden planter box holding old glass bottles and buoys.
  - Mounted on the brick wall to the right of the right-hand window is a small brown plaque reading `HARBOR DEFENSE MINE BUILDING`.
- **Foreground & Surroundings**:
  - On the far left, stepped rough-cut granite blocks of the adjacent historic fort wall (Fort Constitution) jut into the foreground.
  - Along the bottom edge are marine gear and equipment, including a black four-bladed rotor/impeller, a thick coil of heavy white braided hawser rope, a metal boat trailer with fenders, and a white PVC pipe frame draped with coiled green garden/washdown hose on the right. The hood of a white UNH pickup truck is partially visible at the far right edge in front of a dark stone wall.
