# EPDIS Ship Bridge Simulator Console (`epdis-osg.png`)

## Overview
A promotional photograph of a full-mission ship's bridge simulator from the **EPDIS** (Electronic Pilot Display and Information System) project, featuring a five-bay integrated bridge console in front of a three-panel panoramic 3D projection screen rendered with OpenSceneGraph (OSG).

## Scene Description
- **Panoramic 3D Projection Screens (Background)**:
  - Three large rear-projection or flat wall screens are arranged in a wide triptych behind the bridge console, framed by grey riveted structural beams.
  - **Center Screen**: Displays the view directly ahead over the red-and-grey bow and foredeck of the own-ship heading into a harbor channel, with distant city skyline buildings, cranes, and a conical hill on the right headland.
  - **Left Screen**: Shows a large container ship loaded with multicolored shipping containers passing across the port bow, with white hull lettering reading `DSR-SENATOR` and a coastal city skyline behind it.
  - **Right Screen**: Shows open blue water extending toward a low coastline and a smaller vessel in the distance off the starboard bow.
- **Integrated Bridge Console (Foreground)**:
  - A curved, modular dark-blue and white ergonomic bridge console with wooden grab rails along the top of each monitor bay and slotted ventilation grilles near the floor.
  - **Five Display Bays (Left to Right)**:
    1. **Far Left Bay**: Displays system monitoring/conning status panels with green bar graphs and instrumentation blocks, accompanied by a trackball on the console shelf.
    2. **Center-Left Bay**: Displays a circular blue marine radar / ARPA PPI scope showing coastline returns in yellow/green, with physical rotary knobs and buttons below and a gooseneck console lamp mounted to its left.
    3. **Center Helm Station**: Features a physical grey-and-black ship's steering wheel (helm) in the center foreground, flanked by a keypad on the left, a circular heading/compass repeater above left, a central conning/instrument display screen behind the wheel, and dual engine telegraph / thruster levers (with a bright red cover/handle) on the right.
    4. **Center-Right Bay**: Displays a second circular blue marine radar / ARPA PPI scope matching the left radar unit, with a gooseneck lamp and alarm/indicator button panels to its right.
    5. **Far Right Bay**: Displays a 2D Electronic Chart Display and Information System (ECDIS) map showing yellow land masses and light-blue/green depth contours along a coastal channel, with an integrated trackball below.
