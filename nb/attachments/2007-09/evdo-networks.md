# Verizon VZAccess Manager Networks Tab (`evdo-networks.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of the **VZAccess Manager** application window displaying the **Networks** tab while connected to Verizon Wireless EVDO (`NationalAccess - BroadbandAccess`).

- **Window Frame & Top Toolbar**:
  - A brushed-metal Mac OS X window titled `VZAccess Manager` with standard close, minimize, and zoom buttons on the top left and a toolbar toggle pill button on the top right.
  - Four icon tabs along the top toolbar:
    - **`Networks`** (selected/highlighted inside a rounded rectangular bevel): Purple/white radio tower icon emitting concentric signal rings.
    - **`Statistics`**: Four colored vertical bar chart columns (red, orange, green, purple).
    - **`Usage`**: Circular analog gauge/speedometer icon with a red needle.
    - **`Support`**: Red question mark (`?`) icon.
- **Network List Area**:
  - The main central pane features alternating white and pale blue horizontal rows.
  - The top row is active and highlighted in light grey, displaying:
    - Left icon: A black square wireless connection symbol with a red right-pointing arrow.
    - Network name in bold black text: **`NationalAccess - BroadbandAccess`**.
    - Right icon: A green antenna mast with four vertical signal strength bars (three solid green bars lit, fourth tallest bar greyed out).
- **Bottom Banner & Status Bar**:
  - Above the status bar is a black-to-red horizontal gradient banner featuring the white-and-red `verizonwireless` logo on the left and two rounded Aqua push buttons on the right: **`Get Access`** and **`Disconnect`**.

    - Upper row: `Verizon Wireless` on the left, `BroadbandAccess (Rev-A)` (with `Broadband` in red and `Access` in bold black) in the center, and a 3-bar battery level icon on the right.
