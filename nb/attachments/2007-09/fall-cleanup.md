# Overflowing Wire Compost Bin in Fall (`fall-cleanup.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken in Kurt Schwehr's yard in Dover, New Hampshire, on September 29, 2007, showing a cylindrical wire-mesh compost bin heaped high above the rim with fallen autumn leaves and green garden trimmings.

## Scene Description
- **Compost Bin**: Occupying the center of the frame is a circular enclosure made of galvanized welded wire fencing with rectangular grid openings.
  - The lower and middle sections inside the wire cage are packed with layers of green plant stalks/leaves at the bottom and a thick middle band of dry, brown fallen autumn leaves.
  - Piled high above the top edge of the wire mesh is a mound of fresh green garden weeds, leafy stems, and small pale lavender/whitelate-season aster or herb blossoms on the upper left.
- **Lighting & Surroundings**:
  - The bin sits in a wooded corner of the yard on ground covered with brown fallen leaves.
  - Three tree trunks rise vertically behind and beside the compost pile (one on the far left edge, one dark-barked trunk in the upper center, and one smoother trunk on the upper right), surrounded by green deciduous foliage.
  - Dappled morning sunlight filters through the trees from the upper right, creating bright, slightly overexposed highlights on the top-right mound of vegetation and slight motion softness across the frame.
