# Final Fall Garden Harvest: Halved Melon, Tomato, and Jalapeño (`fall-harvest.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle indoor still-life photograph showing a halved homegrown mini-melon, a red plum/roma tomato, and a green jalapeño pepper arranged on a light wooden butcher-block cutting board from Kurt Schwehr's final garden harvest of the season on September 29, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Halved Mini-Melon (Center-Left & Upper-Right)**:
  - Two halves of a small round melon lie cut-side up across the middle and upper-right of the frame.
  - Each half shows a pale, greenish-white to light-green outer rind (indicating it was harvested before fully ripening), transitioning inward to a pale salmon-orange center cavity filled with fibrous netting and oval cream-colored seeds.
  - On the right half, the striped light-and-dark green exterior skin and blossom-end scar of the melon are visible along its curved lower side.
- **Red Tomato (Top Center)**:
  - Above the gap between the two melon halves rests a small, glossy, bright red oblong/plum tomato with its dark green stem stub attached on the right.
- **Jalapeño Pepper (Bottom Right)**:
  - In the lower-right quadrant lies a single smooth, medium-green jalapeño pepper with a short curved green stem pointing upward and slightly left, showing faint longitudinal corking lines near its tapered tip.
- **Surface**: All four items rest on a warm, light-colored hardwood butcher-block countertop with visible horizontal wood grain.
