# Fort Foster Pier (`fort-foster-pier.jpg`)

## Overview

A daytime waterfront photograph showing the end of a weathered wooden pier at Fort Foster in Kittery, Maine, taken from a boat (`R/V Cocheco`) on September 13, 2007, while scoping out future acoustic instrument deployment sites.

## Scene Description

- **Pier Structure**: The end section of a heavy timber pier stands above calm, emerald-green water.
  - **Pilings and Bracing**: Clusters of thick vertical wooden pilings (weathered gray and white on the outer faces, darker and damp near the waterline) support the deck. Underneath the deck, a dense network of diagonal wooden cross-braces crisscrosses between the pilings in shadow.
  - **Decking and Stringers**: Heavy horizontal timber beams run beneath the deck, fastened with large, rusted iron bolts and square washers. A white vertical board (possibly a tide staff or mounting plank) is bolted to the center piling cluster.
  - **Railings**: A weathered gray wooden safety railing with horizontal rails and vertical posts encloses the top of the pier. A rusted metal bollard or post is visible near the left corner of the deck.
- **Water & Background**:
  - **Foreground Water**: Calm, dark teal-green water in the foreground reflects the light gray pilings in gentle ripples.
  - **Background**: Beyond the pier deck, green coastal trees with a small patch of early autumn orange/red foliage are visible under a bright, overexposed white sky, with a glimpse of blue water and rocky shoreline to the far left.
