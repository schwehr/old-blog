# Garden Pole Beans on Trellis (`garden-beans.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up photograph of green pole beans growing on a wire trellis in front of a white picket fence in Kurt Schwehr's backyard garden in Dover, New Hampshire, taken on September 15, 2007.

## Scene Description

- **Bean Vines & Pods**: Lush green bean vines with broad, textured, three-lobed leaves climb up a metal wire grid and a dark green support pole on the right. Several long, slender green bean pods hang vertically near the center-right of the frame. A few leaves show minor late-summer browning or spotting along their edges.
- **Fence & Trellis**: Behind the foliage sits a white wooden picket fence with pointed tops, along with a rectangular wire mesh trellis supporting the climbing stems.
- **Background**: Dense, shaded green trees and a glimpse of sunlit grass at the bottom right appear behind the picket fence.
