# Jalapeño Pepper on the Plant (`garden-jalapeno.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up vertical photograph of a green jalapeño pepper growing among green leaves in Kurt Schwehr's home garden in Dover, New Hampshire, featured in a September 7, 2007 blog post on fall gardening.

## Scene Description

- **Jalapeño Pepper**: Centered in the frame is a smooth, glossy, dark green jalapeño pepper hanging vertically from a stem node, partially tucked behind bright green pepper leaves. A smaller developing pepper bud or stem is visible slightly above and to the right.
- **Foliage**: Vibrant green, lance-shaped pepper plant leaves with prominent lighter green central veins surround the pepper, largely concealing it from above.
- **Soil & Ground**: In the background and lower left, dry brown garden soil is visible along with small low-growing green weeds at the base of the plant stems.
