# Mini Melon on a Garden Brick (`garden-mini-mellon.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up photograph of a small, round green melon resting on a weathered red clay brick in Kurt Schwehr's garden in Dover, New Hampshire, taken in early September 2007.

## Scene Description

- **Melon**: A spherical mini melon with pale sage-green skin and darker green longitudinal ribs sits near the center of the frame, attached by its stem at the lower left to the surrounding vine.
- **Brick Support**: The melon rests on the corner of a weathered, pinkish-red brick placed on the dark soil to keep the fruit elevated off the damp ground.
- **Foliage & Flowers**: Large, ruffled green melon leaves with spots of whitish powdery mildew and dark speckling surround the fruit. Small yellow melon blossoms and wood sorrel (clover-like leaves) are visible in the lower left and among the vines against the dark brown soil.
