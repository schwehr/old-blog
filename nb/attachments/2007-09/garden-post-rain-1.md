# Backlit Sunflower After Rain (`garden-post-rain-1.jpg`)

## Overview

A low-angle photograph looking up at a backlit orange-and-yellow sunflower against a blue sky and dark green trees after a morning rain on September 15, 2007.

## Scene Description

- **Primary Sunflower**: A large sunflower head dominates the center of the frame, viewed from below against bright sunlight.
  - **Petals**: The ray petals glow with translucent amber, deep orange, reddish-brown, and golden-yellow hues as sunlight shines through them.
  - **Center Disk**: The circular central disk is in deep shadow, appearing nearly black against the bright sky.
  - **Stem & Leaves**: A hairy green stem extends down to the bottom edge, flanked by broad green leaves on the lower left (some with small insect holes) illuminated by the sun.
- **Secondary Flower & Background**:
  - A smaller yellow sunflower blooms in the lower right corner against the dark foliage.
  - Tall green trees form a dark silhouette across the lower half of the background under a bright blue sky scattered with soft white clouds.
