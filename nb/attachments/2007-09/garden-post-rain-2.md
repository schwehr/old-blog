# Deep Red Sunflower After Rain (`garden-post-rain-2.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up photograph of a deep burgundy-red sunflower glistening with water droplets next to a white picket fence after a morning rain on September 15, 2007.

## Scene Description

- **Sunflower**: Positioned slightly right of center, a striking dark red/burgundy sunflower faces toward the viewer.
  - **Petals**: Multiple layers of rich crimson and maroon petals radiate outward, still wet and shiny from recent rain.
  - **Center Disk**: The dark brown and black central disk floret head shows a tight spiral pattern in the very center surrounded by a ring of open florets with tiny flecks of yellow-brown pollen.
- **Foliage & Stems**: Broad, bright green sunflower leaves with prominent veins frame the flower on the right, left, and bottom, while a fuzzy, purple-tinged stem angles diagonally across the left side.
- **Background**: White wooden fence pickets stand in the lower left and lower right foreground, with a soft-focus green lawn and shaded trees visible in the upper background.
