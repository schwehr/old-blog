# Bee on a Bicolored Sunflower (`garden-sunflower-bee.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up photograph of a bee foraging on a vibrant yellow-and-orange bicolored sunflower in front of a white picket fence and light green shingled house siding in Dover, New Hampshire, posted on September 7, 2007.

## Scene Description

- **Sunflower & Bee**:
  - **Petals**: Bright golden-yellow ray petals with a warm reddish-orange ring around the base radiate symmetrically from the center.
  - **Central Disk**: The brown circular disk consists of concentric rings of open tubular florets dusted with yellow-orange pollen surrounding a darker, unopened center.
  - **Bee**: A fuzzy bee with yellow pollen on its legs and body perches near the lower-right edge of the central disk (around the 4 to 5 o'clock position), feeding on the florets.
- **Stems, Buds & Background**:
  - Thick, hairy green sunflower stems and large green leaves frame the right and upper portions of the shot, along with a green unopened flower bud near the top left.
  - Behind the plant sits a white wooden picket fence on the lower left and light sage-green cedar shingle siding on the upper left.
