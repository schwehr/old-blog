# Red and Orange Sunflowers (`garden-sunflower.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up photograph of two deep rust-red and orange sunflowers blooming alongside yellow sunflowers and unopened buds near the side of a house in Dover, New Hampshire, posted on September 7, 2007.

## Scene Description

- **Foreground Sunflowers**:
  - **Upper-Left Bloom**: A fully open sunflower with rich burnt-orange and mahogany-red petals and a detailed circular center disk displaying a tight geometric spiral of florets rimmed with golden-orange pollen.
  - **Lower-Right Bloom**: A second deep red-orange sunflower sits partially tucked behind the first bloom and a large green leaf, with a darker brown central disk.
  - **Unopened Buds**: A spiky bud with reddish-purple bracts points upward at the upper right, while two smaller green spiky buds emerge on stems in the lower left.
- **Background**: Several bright yellow sunflowers and lush green foliage are visible in soft focus on the left, while pale green/white horizontal house siding and a soffit appear along the right edge.
