# Autumn Sedum Turning Pink (`garden-turning-red.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up overhead photograph of dense clusters of perennial stonecrop (*Sedum*, "Autumn Joy" or similar cultivar) transitioning from white to dusty rose-pink in Kurt Schwehr's garden at the start of autumn on September 7, 2007.

## Scene Description

- **Flower Clusters**: Large, broccoli-like corymbs (flower heads) composed of hundreds of tiny five-pointed star-shaped blossoms fill the frame.
  - The blossoms display a soft gradient of pale blush pink, coral pink, and creamy white unopened buds.
  - Smaller, slightly out-of-focus flower clusters appear in the lower left and upper left background.
- **Foliage**: Thick, succulent dark green leaves are partially visible around the edges of the frame (left edge, upper right corner, and bottom center) beneath the heavy flower heads.
