# Dehydrated Tomatoes and Green Beans on Food Dryer Tray (`garden-vegi-drying.jpg`)

## Overview

An angled overhead photograph of a circular plastic food dehydrator tray resting on a wooden tabletop, filled with dried tomato slices and green beans from Kurt Schwehr's garden on September 7, 2007.

## Scene Description

- **Dehydrator Tray**: A round, translucent off-white plastic dehydrator tray with a circular central cutout rests on a light-colored butcher-block or oak wood surface. The plastic surface has faint orange staining from tomato juice.
- **Dried Tomatoes**: Across the upper half and upper-right quadrant of the tray are roughly 45–50 shriveled, deep red slices of cherry/plum tomatoes and several larger halved tomato pieces on the right, showing concentrated red flesh and yellow seeds.
- **Dried Green Beans**: Across the lower half and left side of the tray are dozens of dehydrated whole and halved green bean pods, shrunken and wrinkled into slender, pale olive-green and bright green curls.
