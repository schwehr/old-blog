# Adding a Photo Object in Google Earth (`ge-photo-1.png`)

## Overview

A Mac OS X screenshot of Google Earth taken on September 17, 2007, showing the `Add > Photo...` menu selection while viewing coastal waters near New Castle, New Hampshire / Kittery, Maine.

## Interface & Controls

- **Top Menu Bar**: The Mac OS X application menu bar at the top reads `Google Earth  File  Edit  View  Tools  Add  Help`, with the **Add** menu open and **Photo...** highlighted in blue. The dropdown items are:
  - `Folder...` (`⇧⌘N`)
  - `Placemark...` (`⇧⌘P`)
  - `Path...` (`⇧⌘T`)
  - `Polygon...` (`⇧⌘G`)
  - `Model...` (`⇧⌘M`)
  - `Photo...` (selected)
  - `Image Overlay...` (`⇧⌘O`)
  - `Network Link...`
- **Left Sidebar**:
  - **Search Panel**: Active tab `Fly To` (alongside `Find Businesses` and `Directions`) with prompt `Fly to e.g., Hotels near JFK`.
  - **Places Panel**: Under `My Places`, checked item `SBNMS Boundary` (`OBJECTID: 1`, `AREA: 0.23956600000`) and unchecked folder `USCG-MISLE-2005`. Under `Temporary Places`, two unchecked camera-icon items: `New Castle Coast Guard Statio` and `Untitled Photo Overlay`.
  - **Layers Panel**: `View: Core` dropdown, showing `Primary Database` with `Terrain` checked, and unchecked layers `Geographic Web`, `Featured Content`, `Global Awareness`, and `roads`.
- **3D Viewport**:
  - Displays high-resolution aerial imagery of dark teal-green rippled water (`Image © 2007 Maine Office of GIS`), a `5 m` scale bar at lower left, navigation compass/zoom controls at upper right, and status bar at bottom reading `Pointer 43°04'14.58" N  70°42'24.92" W`, `elev 0 m`, `Streaming |||||||||| 100%`, `Eye alt 20 m`.
