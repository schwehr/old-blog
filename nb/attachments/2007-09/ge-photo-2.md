# Google Earth Photo Overlay Mode (`ge-photo-2.png`)

## Overview

A Mac OS X screenshot of Google Earth taken on September 17, 2007, demonstrating the interactive "Photo" viewing mode after double-clicking the `New Castle Coast Guard Station` photo object.

## Interface & Viewport Details

- **3D Viewport in Photo Mode**:
  - The camera is positioned low over the water (`Eye alt 16 m`) looking toward the horizon under a light blue-violet sky.
  - Floating in the center of the 3D scene is a framed color photograph showing the white lighthouse tower and buildings of the Portsmouth Harbor Light / New Castle Coast Guard Station across blue water, with a small yellow buoy and a white float in the foreground water.
  - **Photo Mode HUD**: Near the top right of the 3D view, a camera icon and label read `New Castle Coast Guard Station` next to a dark gray `Exit Photo` button and a semi-transparent square directional panning pad with four white arrows.
- **Left Sidebar**:
  - In the **Places** panel under `Temporary Places`, both `New Castle Coast Guard Statio` and `Untitled Photo Overlay` now have their checkboxes checked.
  - The **Search** and **Layers** panels remain visible on the left, and the bottom status bar reads `Streaming |||||||||| 100%` and `Eye alt 16 m`.
