# Zooming and Panning Inside a Google Earth Photo Object (`ge-photo-3.png`)

## Overview

A Mac OS X screenshot of Google Earth taken on September 17, 2007, showing the user zoomed in and panning inside the `New Castle Coast Guard Station` photo overlay using the scroll wheel and on-screen navigator.

## Scene & Interface Details

- **Zoomed Photo View**: The photograph now fills the entire main viewport:
  - **Foreground**: Rippled blue harbor water with a bright yellow mooring/monitoring buoy topped by white cylindrical instrumentation on the lower left, and a small white lobster/mooring float to the right.
  - **Background Shoreline**: Across the water, a stone seawall and rocky riprap line the shore of New Castle, New Hampshire. Behind the wall stand the Coast Guard station buildings (including a light-colored building with a rooftop observation cupola/tower on the left, a white tent, a brick fort structure in the center, and the white tower of Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse with its green lantern room on the upper right).
- **Photo Navigator HUD**:
  - In the top right corner, the header displays a camera icon, `New Castle Coast Guard Station`, and an `Exit Photo` button.
  - Directly below the header is a semi-transparent pan/zoom control widget showing a thumbnail preview of the full photograph with a white rectangle indicating the currently zoomed viewport, surrounded by four directional pan arrows and a vertical zoom slider (`+` / `-`) on the right.
- **Left Sidebar**: Displays the `Search`, `Places` (with `New Castle Coast Guard Statio` selected), and `Layers` panels.
