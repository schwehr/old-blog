# Placing a Ship Noise Data Plot in Google Earth (`geclass-graph.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot from Lecture 04 of Kurt Schwehr's CS 767 Interactive Data Visualization course (posted September 18, 2007), showing the Google Earth "Edit Image Overlay" dialog alongside a 3D view of Massachusetts Bay where a ship noise graph has been placed inside the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary boundary.

## Interface & Map Details

- **Image Overlay Dialog (Left)**:
  - **Name Field**: `[ Ship Noise ]`
  - **Link Field**: `.../schwehr/Desktop/Class4/sbnms-graph.png` with a `Browse...` button.
  - **Transparency Slider**: Set all the way to the right at `Opaque`.
  - **Tabs**: `Description` (active), `View`, `Altitude`, `Refresh`, `Location`.
  - **Description Box**: Contains the text `Graph of ship noise for one week`.
- **3D Map Viewport (Right)**:
  - Shows an oblique aerial view looking north-northwest across Cape Cod (bottom right), Massachusetts Bay, Boston Harbor (center left), and Cape Ann (upper center).
  - **Sanctuary Boundary**: A red polygon outline marks the boundary of the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (`SBNMS`) in Massachusetts Bay.
  - **Graph Overlay**: Inside the southern portion of the red sanctuary polygon sits a small rectangular white image overlay (`sbnms-graph.png`) framed by bright green editing handles (corner brackets, edge midpoints, and a center crosshair) for positioning and scaling the plot on the globe.
