# Jackson Estuarine Laboratory Building and AIS Receiver Location (`jel-ais.png`)

## Overview
An annotated daytime exterior photograph of the University of New Hampshire's Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL) at Adams Point in Durham, New Hampshire, with an orange arrow pointing to the upper-story dormer window housing the Automatic Identification System (AIS) antenna and SR162 receiver.

- **Main Laboratory Building**:

  - Along the left end of the brick building rises a tall square red-brick chimney topped with a tall mast and a horizontal multi-element VHF/TV-style directional antenna array.

  - Above the central roofline sits a raised clerestory or dormer projection with a pair of windows; a bright **orange arrow** points diagonally down and left directly at the left window of this rooftop dormer, indicating where the AIS receiver and antenna sit on the windowsill.

  - Outside the greenhouse along a paved driveway/parking area sit several rectangular fiberglass or wooden seawater tanks/tables on concrete blocks, a white cylindrical barrel, and the front end of a white pickup truck on the far left.
- **Foreground & Landscaping**:
  - In the foreground, a low post-and-chain fence follows the edge of the paved drive next to rocky banking, shrubs, small trees, and grassy marsh vegetation near the water's edge on the lower right.
