# Water Level Data Logger and Yagi Antenna at JEL Pier (`jel-waterlevel.JPG`)

## Overview
A close-up daytime photograph taken at the end of the Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL) pier on Great Bay, New Hampshire, showing the water-level tide gauge housing, vertical staff gauge, weatherproof data logger enclosure, and directional Yagi radio antenna overlooking the bay.

## Equipment & Pier Details
- **Data Logger Enclosure (Foreground)**:
  - Mounted to a vertical wooden/metal post at the corner of the pier is a white square weatherproof fiberglass/plastic junction box with a clear acrylic front window.
  - Inside the window, black coaxial/serial cables and bulkhead connectors are coiled and attached to internal electronics.
  - Black cables and a gray flexible conduit exit the bottom of the box, neatly coiled and secured with white zip ties.
- **Antenna & Tide Gauge Pipe**:
  - Extending horizontally to the left from the mounting post is a silver aluminum 6-element directional Yagi-Uda radio antenna used for wireless serial telemetry.
  - Behind the enclosure rises a vertical rusted steel stilling well pipe topped with a flat square rusted steel plate and a small rectangular metal box on top.
  - Adjacent to the steel pipe is a vertical post bearing a white tide staff ruler marked with black graduation ticks and the numeral `4`.
  - Metal safety chains run between the posts along the edge of the pier.

## Background Harbor Scene
- Beyond the pier, calm greenish-blue waters of Great Bay stretch toward a wooded shoreline along the horizon.
- Two boats are moored on the water in the upper half of the frame: a small tan/white cabin boat toward the upper left and a larger blue-and-white research or work vessel equipped with a stern A-frame (likely the R/V *Gulf Challenger* or a similar UNH vessel) in the upper center-right.
