# Kurt Schwehr on the R/V Cocheco Near Whaleback Light (`kurt-atsea-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime portrait photograph taken by Michelle aboard the R/V *Cocheco* during an acoustic test deployment on September 13, 2007, showing Kurt Schwehr smiling at the camera on the aft deck with a stone lighthouse building on a rocky island in the background.

## Scene Description
- **Subject (Kurt Schwehr)**:
  - Kurt stands facing the camera in the center of the frame, smiling.
  - He is wearing a bright orange life vest with silver reflective shoulder patches and black buckles over a white T-shirt, olive-green cargo shorts, glasses, and a brown baseball cap worn forward.
  - His right hand rests on a black deck railing post with chain lifelines extending to the left, while his left arm rests behind him near an orange ring buoy mounted on the black railing.
- **Vessel Details (Foreground)**:
  - In the lower left sits a red portable marine fuel tank on the white deck.
  - On the lower right is a white deck box/engine cover with a small red warning placard.
  - On the upper right, a thick black angled frame (the vessel's A-frame) rises out of the frame.
- **Background**:
  - Across the calm blue water on the left sits a small rocky islet with a weathered stone tower/building structure (the old fog signal/fort structure near Whaleback Light / Fort Constitution at the mouth of the Piscataqua River) and two birds flying high in the sky above it.
  - A low wooded coastline stretches across the distant horizon under a clear pale blue sky.
