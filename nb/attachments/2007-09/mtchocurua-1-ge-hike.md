# Google Earth 3D Perspective View of the Mount Chocorua Hike (`mtchocurua-1-ge-hike.png`)

## Overview
A 3D oblique terrain visualization from Google Earth looking east-southeast across the southern White Mountains of New Hampshire, illustrating the route of a September 2, 2007 hike up Mount Chocorua from the Kancamagus Highway (NH Route 112) via the Champney Falls Trail.

## Map & Terrain Details
- **3D Terrain & Trail Overlay**:
  - The foreground shows the rugged, forested ridgelines and valleys of Mount Chocorua rendered in 3D relief with satellite imagery draped over the topography.
  - In the lower left corner, a yellow highway line with a circular white route shield labeled `112` marks the Kancamagus Highway (NH Route 112), with a bright red circular dot immediately adjacent to it indicating the trailhead parking lot at 370 meters elevation.
  - A thin white/gray trail line traces the hiking route winding up the valley and ridge toward the bare, light-colored rocky summit of Mount Chocorua on the lower right (1,065 meters elevation), which is marked by a second bright red circular dot.
  - Beyond Mount Chocorua, flatter terrain with lakes, yellow highway lines, and pale cyan county/township boundary lines stretches toward the horizon under a pale blue-lavender sky.
- **World Overview Inset (Top Left)**:
  - A small rectangular world map inset in the upper left corner shows the globe with a red crosshair over New England indicating the camera location.
