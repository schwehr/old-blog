# Champney Falls Waterfall on Mount Chocorua (`mtchocurua-2-waterfall.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical outdoor photograph taken during the September 2, 2007 hike up Mount Chocorua in New Hampshire's White Mountains, showing thin curtains of water cascading over stepped, moss-covered granite ledges into a shallow pool along the Champney Falls trail.

## Scene Description
- **Rock Ledges & Waterfall**:
  - Stepped horizontal strata of angular, blocky granite cross the center and upper right of the frame, coated in green moss, lichen, and dark wet streaks.
  - Multiple delicate, sheer streams of white water spill vertically over the overhanging rock lip in the center, falling several feet with a slight motion blur.
- **Pool & Foreground**:
  - At the base of the rock face, the falling streams splash into a clear, shallow sandy-bottomed pool illuminated by warm, dappled sunlight, creating soft white foam rings where the water hits the surface.
  - Dark, wet, moss-covered rocks and scattered fallen autumn leaves border the pool in the lower foreground.
- **Forest Background**:
  - Above the granite ledge in the upper left, bright green deciduous foliage and slender tree trunks are backlit by strong late-summer sunlight filtering through the forest canopy.
