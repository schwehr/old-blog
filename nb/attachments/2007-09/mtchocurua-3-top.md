# View from the Rocky Summit of Mount Chocorua (`mtchocurua-3-top.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic landscape photograph looking down from the bare granite summit cone of Mount Chocorua (1,065 meters / 3,490 feet) in the White Mountains of New Hampshire on September 2, 2007, showing hikers scrambling across the rocks in the foreground and an expansive panorama of forests and lakes stretching to the horizon.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Granite Summit Ledges & Hikers)**:
  - Weathered, light-gray granite boulders and ledges mottled with dark patches of lichen cover the lower right and bottom edge of the frame, with a prominent angular boulder perched on the left edge.
  - Three hikers are visible along the rocky path: one hiker on the right walking upward in a blue-green T-shirt, khaki pants, and a white sweater tied around his waist; a second hiker in the lower center-left wearing a dark shirt, white cap, and backpack facing left; and a third hiker sitting or crouching near low alpine scrub between them.
- **Midground & Background (Forests & Lakes)**:
  - Directly beyond the rocky outcrop, a steep, densely forested green ridge slopes down into the surrounding valleys.
  - Across the wide, tree-covered plain below sit several blue lakes—including Chocorua Lake on the middle right and Silver Lake / Ossipee Lake further in the distance toward the upper center—under a bright, slightly hazy blue sky.
