# Group Portrait at the Summit of Mount Chocorua (`mtchocurua-4-group.JPG`)

## Overview
A bright, sunlit group photograph of seven hikers—including Kurt Schwehr—posed together on the rocky summit of Mount Chocorua in New Hampshire on September 2, 2007, with the distant White Mountains and lakes visible behind them.

## People & Arrangement
- **Front / Lower Row**:
  - **Bottom Left**: A bearded man with short brown hair wearing a white collared shirt, leaning in and smiling broadly.
  - **Bottom Center**: A slender young man wearing wire-rimmed glasses, a tan baseball cap, and a gray-and-brown raglan T-shirt.
  - **Center Right**: A man with a closely shaved head wearing a white-and-maroon raglan baseball T-shirt and khaki shorts, smiling in the bright sun.
- **Middle / Back Row**:
  - **Upper Left**: A man wearing a white Nike baseball cap, dark oval sunglasses, a dark navy T-shirt, and backpack straps, resting his right hand on the shoulder of the bearded man in front.
  - **Center**: A woman with curly brown hair wearing dark sunglasses, a light gray/tan T-shirt, and a black backpack, smiling at the camera.
  - **Top Center**: Kurt Schwehr, wearing glasses and a dark blue T-shirt, leaning forward behind the group and smiling.
  - **Far Right**: An Asian man wearing wire-rimmed glasses, a backward gray baseball cap, a white T-shirt, and a gray-and-black backpack, smiling at the camera.

## Background
- Bare granite summit rocks appear along the lower right edge behind the hikers.
- In the background, a vast expanse of green forest, blue lakes, and rolling blue mountain ranges stretches across the horizon under a clear, bright sky.
