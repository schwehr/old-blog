# Portsmouth Naval Prison Seen Across a Misty Salt Marsh (`naval-prison.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format morning landscape photograph taken on Friday, September 28, 2007 near New Castle / Rye, New Hampshire, showing low autumn fog hanging over a tidal salt marsh at low tide with the historic Portsmouth Naval Prison ("The Castle") visible in the distance.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Tidal Creek & Mudflats)**:
  - Calm, glassy water fills the bottom of the frame, reflecting the pale gray sky on the left and dark green trees on the right.
  - Above the water line, exposed gray-brown mudflats and a sandy bank on the left mark a very low tide.
- **Midground (Salt Marsh & Tree Line)**:
  - A flat expanse of golden-green and russet-brown autumn salt marsh grass stretches horizontally across the middle of the composition.
  - Beyond the marsh, a line of dark green conifers and deciduous trees borders the water, with a thick band of white morning mist and fog hovering just above the water surface between the trees.
- **Background (Portsmouth Naval Prison)**:
  - Rising above the mist and tree line slightly right of center is the large, castle-like concrete structure of the decommissioned Portsmouth Naval Prison on Seavey's Island (Kittery, Maine), featuring its distinctive twin central towers and rows of tall arched windows under an overcast gray sky.
