# Freshly Harvested Sweet Basil on Kitchen Island (`operation-pesto-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken on September 15, 2007 showing a large pile of freshly harvested sweet basil stems lying on a kitchen counter in preparation for making homemade pesto ("Operation Pesto"), alongside an Apple PowerBook/MacBook Pro laptop connected to a Verizon EVDO USB modem.

- **Foreground (Sweet Basil)**:
  - A lush, sprawling heap of freshly cut sweet basil stalks with large, glossy green leaves spans the foreground of the wooden kitchen counter, resting on a lime-green plastic cutting board.
- **Kitchen Counter Items (Midground)**:
  - To the right of the basil sits a large, shiny stainless-steel mixing bowl.
  - Behind the basil in the center is a round dark green/black granite trivet.
  - To the left of the trivet lies a small red-and-silver flip phone, and beside it a black Verizon Wireless USB720 EVDO cellular modem with a glowing green status LED, attached via a black dual-USB Y-cable to the laptop.
  - On the far left of the counter stands a green-and-silver aluminum can of Diet Coke with Lime (`Coke`).
  - Near the back edge of the counter sits an open silver Apple laptop (PowerBook G4 or MacBook Pro) angled away from the camera, with its white power cord trailing to the right.
- **Background (Kitchen / Entryway)**:
  - Beyond the counter, an open dark red wooden door with a brass knob stands next to a white cast-iron radiator and a black folding stool on a white-and-black tiled floor, where a pair of gray socks lies near an interior doorway.
