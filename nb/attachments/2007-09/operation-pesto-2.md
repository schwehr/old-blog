# Stripped Basil Stems and Bowl of Basil Leaves (`operation-pesto-2.jpg`)

## Overview
The second photograph in the September 15, 2007 "Operation Pesto" series, showing the kitchen counter after the sweet basil leaves have been stripped from their stems and collected into a stainless-steel mixing bowl.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Stripped Stems & Cutting Board)**:
  - On the lime-green plastic cutting board in the lower left/center lies a bundle of bare, pale-green basil stalks with only tiny terminal shoots remaining after all the large leaves were plucked off.
  - A few stray green basil leaves rest on the wooden counter to the left of the cutting board.
- **Midground (Bowl of Basil Leaves & Electronics)**:
  - On the right, the stainless-steel mixing bowl is now filled to the brim with fresh green sweet basil leaves.
  - In the center sits the round dark granite trivet with a few water droplets on its surface.
  - To the left are the red-and-silver flip phone (with its external screen lit up orange) and the black Verizon USB EVDO modem with its illuminated green light, connected via a black cable to the open silver Apple laptop in the upper center.
- **Background**:
  - A glimpse of the black-and-white patterned tile floor and white radiator appears in the upper left corner.
