# Toasting Pine Nuts in a Skillet for Pesto (`operation-pesto-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up overhead photograph from the September 15, 2007 "Operation Pesto" series, showing a single layer of pine nuts toasting in a dark skillet under warm kitchen lighting.

## Scene Description
- **Pine Nuts & Skillet**:
  - Hundreds of small, elongated, teardrop-shaped pine nuts (pignoli) are spread across the center of a dark non-stick or cast-iron frying pan.
  - Illuminated by warm golden-orange kitchen light, the pine nuts glisten with a light sheen of oil or natural oils as they toast.
  - The curved dark rim of the frying pan frames the upper right and lower left corners, with two metal rivet heads visible along the lower left interior wall of the pan where the handle attaches.
