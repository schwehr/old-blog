# Tugboat "Pit Bull" on the Water (`pit-bull.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph of the compact, high-pilothouse workboat/tugboat *Pit Bull* operating on the water near the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL) pier construction site on September 13, 2007.

## Vessel & Scene Description
- **Foreground**: A dark metal railing from the dock or vessel where the photographer is standing angles across the bottom-left corner of the frame.
- **Main Subject (*Pit Bull*)**: Centered in the water is a small, boxy, industrial push-tug or workboat with a rusted dark-brown and black steel hull fitted with black rubber tire fenders along its side and bowpush knees at the right (bow) end. Rising from the middle of the deck is a tall, narrow, two-story white superstructure/pilothouse that gives the operator an elevated view over barges. The pilothouse has a bright red cabin door with a small window on its side, wraparound forward windows, and tall vertical rusted exhaust stacks rising along the sides of the cabin up to the roofline.
- **Markings**: Painted in white block letters on a dark plate mounted on the stern/port quarter is the vessel name **`PIT BULL`** with a smaller hailing port line underneath.
- **Background**: Just behind and to the left of the *Pit Bull* is a white single-masted sailboat moored on the blue water with its green sail cover wrapped around the boom. Across the calm blue harbor water in the distance is a tree-lined shoreline dotted with coastal buildings, docks, and cranes on the left horizon under a clear, bright blue sky.
