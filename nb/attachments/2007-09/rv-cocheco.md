# R/V Cocheco at the JEL Pier at Sunrise (`rv-cocheco.jpg`)

## Overview
A morning photograph taken around 7:00 AM on September 13, 2007, showing the research vessel *R/V Cocheco* alongside the floating dock at the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL) prior to departing for an acoustics deployment test.

## Scene & Vessel Description
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**: Shot looking toward the rising sun on the right edge of the frame, casting a bright golden glare across the calm, glassy water of Great Bay / Little Bay and backlighting the boats and dock structures. The sky transitions from warm pale yellow near the horizon to clear blue overhead.
- **Foreground & Dock**: In the foreground, a metal gangway with silver pipe handrails and safety chains leads down from the left onto a wooden floating dock extending away from the viewer. A dark vertical piling occupies the far right edge of the frame. Several small white skiffs and outboard motorboats are tied up on both sides of the floating dock, catching bright reflections of sunlight on their hulls.
- **Main Subject (*R/V Cocheco*)**: Moored on the left side of the floating dock behind a small white outboard skiff is the *R/V Cocheco*, a medium-sized work and research vessel with a dark hull and a white cabin/pilothouse. A round NOAA/UNH emblem is visible on the side of the cabin beneath the window, and the sunlight shines directly through the pilothouse windows. At the stern (right end of the boat), a dark A-frame crane angles out over the aft deck, which is equipped with a metal safety railing and an orange life ring. A crew member in dark silhouette stands on the wooden dock just aft of the vessel's stern.
- **Background**: Across the calm water, a dark, continuous silhouette of forested shoreline spans the horizon.
