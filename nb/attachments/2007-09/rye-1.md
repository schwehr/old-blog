# Salt Marsh Pier and Moored Boat in Rye, NH (`rye-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect autumn morning photograph of a coastal salt marsh and tidal inlet in Rye, New Hampshire, taken on September 28, 2007, showing a wooden pier, a metal gangway, and a small white lobster/work boat moored at a float.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**: Golden-brown, rust-colored, and olive-green salt marsh grasses cover the foreground, with a line of small gray rocks and a small yellow posted sign mounted on a thin stake near the center-bottom.
- **Midground (Pier & Boat)**: On the left, a wooden walkway/pier supported by dark pilings extends out from a tree-covered point of land to the edge of the water. Attached to the end of the wooden pier is a white truss-style metal gangway sloping down to a small floating dock. Moored neatly against the right side of the float is a white cabin motorboat (downeast/lobster boat style) reflected in the still, misty tidal water. Dark exposed mudflats and low rocks surround the channel at low tide.
- **Background**: Across the calm, reflective gray-blue water of the marsh inlet, large multi-story coastal houses (white and cream-colored with pitched roofs and chimneys) sit on green lawns backed by a dense forest softened by pale morning mist and fog under an overcast sky.
