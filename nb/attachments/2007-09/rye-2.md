# Misty Morning Low Tide in Rye Salt Marsh (`rye-2.jpg`)

## Overview
An atmospheric photograph of a winding tidal channel and exposed mudflats at very low tide in a salt marsh near Rye / New Castle, New Hampshire, taken on a misty morning on September 28, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Lighting & Sky**: Warm, diffused golden-pink morning light filters through a misty, overcast sky, creating a bright, shimmering reflection across a distant bend of the water channel near the center of the frame.
- **Foreground & Marsh**: Dark silhouettes of tall weeds and marsh plants rise up in the bottom-left foreground. The right foreground and midground consist of dark, shadowed banks of salt marsh grass curving along the tidal creek.
- **Tidal Channel & Mudflats**: Between the marsh banks, glistening wet mudflats and shallow pools reflect the pale peach and gold tones of the sky, winding from the lower-left foreground back toward the bright sunlit channel in the middle distance.
- **Background**: A soft, dark silhouette of a continuous tree line spans the horizon through the morning haze.
