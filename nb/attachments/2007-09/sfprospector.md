# SFProspector Web GIS Interface Screenshot (`sfprospector.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **SFProspector** web-based geographic information system (GIS) application (`http://www.sfprospector.com/`), developed by GIS Planning, Inc. for the City & County of San Francisco Mayor's Office of Business & Economic Development, captured on September 20, 2007.

## Interface & Layout
- **Top Header Bar**:
  - Top-left logo: **`SFGOV`** (`san francisco`) inside a circular emblem.
  - Blue banner title: **`SFProspector`** on the left, and **`City & County of San Francisco`** / **`Mayor's Office of Business & Economic Development`** on the right.
  - Black navigation bar beneath with links: **`Property Search`** | **`Analysis by Geographic Area`**.
- **Left / Center Map Area**:
  - **Main Map Panel**: Shows an outline map of the San Francisco peninsula surrounded by light blue water. Parks are shaded in green, major streets and highways are drawn in gray and red, and yellow text labels highlight commercial corridors (`Polk St`, `6th St`, `Divisadero`, `3rd St`, `Ocean Ave`, `San Bruno Ave`, `Visitacion Valley`).
  - **Vertical Map Control Strip** (to the right of the map): A dark blue rounded control bar containing:
    - **`MOVE MAP`** compass rose with 8 orange directional arrows (`N`, `S`, `E`, `W`, and diagonals).
    - **`ZOOM OUT -`** / **`+ ZOOM IN`** vertical zoom ladder with orange/red horizontal bars.
    - A small inset overview map of the San Francisco Bay Area with a red bounding box over San Francisco and an airplane icon marking SFO airport.
    - A link below: `Instructions for using the map tools`.
  - **Horizontal Tool Bar** (below the main map): A dark blue bar with four circular orange icons labeled **`ZOOM IN`**, **`ZOOM OUT`**, **`MOVE`**, and **`IDENTIFY`**.
- **Bottom-Left Layer Selection Box**:
  - Two tabs at top-left: **`Show Map`** (active, dark blue) and **`Legend`** (orange).
  - Two columns of checkboxes for map layers:
    - Left column: `[ ] Neighborhood Marketplace Initiative (NMI) Corridor`, `[ ] Enterprise Zone`, `[ ] Redevelopment Areas`, `[x] Parks` (checked), `[ ] Public Schools (requires zoom-in)`, `[ ] Traffic Counts`, `[ ] Planning Zones (requires zoom-in)`, `[ ] Port Jurisdiction`, `[ ] Census Tracts (requires zoom-in)`.
    - Right column: `[ ] Aerial Photos (requires zoom-in)`, `[ ] Federal Renewal Community`, `[ ] Neighborhoods`, `[ ] Public Parking (requires zoom-in)`, `[ ] Supervisorial Districts`, `[ ] ZIP Codes`, `[ ] Public Transit(MUNI)`, `[ ] MONS Neighborhoods`.
  - A **`Display`** button at bottom-left and note: `Note: Certain layers available only when zoomed in to map.`
- **Right Search Panel (`Find`)**:
  - Table titled **`Find`** / **`Search for available retail space`**:
    - `Minimum Size: (square feet)` text input field.
    - `Maximum Size: (square feet)` text input field.
    - `[ ] Check to show all`
    - `[ ] properties for lease`
    - `[ ] properties for sale`
    - `[ ] in Enterprise Zone`
    - `[ ] in Federal Renewal Comm.`
    - `Lease Rate:` dropdown (`--select lease rate--`).
    - `NMI Corridor:` dropdown (`--select--`).
    - **`Search`** button at bottom-right of the form.
  - **Disclaimer & Footer**: Legal disclaimer paragraph below the search table, followed by the **`APPLICATION DEVELOPED BY GISPlanning (WWW.GISPLANNING.COM)`** logo and copyright notice `(c) 2002 Software code: GIS Planning, Inc. All rights reserved.`
