# Gnuplot Map of United States State Boundaries (`states.gif`)

## Overview
A 2D line plot generated by `gnuplot` showing the polygon boundaries of the 50 United States (the contiguous 48 states, Alaska, and Hawaii) extracted from U.S. Census Bureau ASCII boundary files (`st99_d00_ascii.zip`).

## Plot Details
- **Axes & Coordinate System**:
  - **Horizontal Axis (Longitude)**: Spans from **`-180`** to **`-60`** degrees in increments of 20 (`-180`, `-160`, `-140`, `-120`, `-100`, `-80`, `-60`), with inward-facing tick marks along both the bottom and top borders.
  - **Vertical Axis (Latitude)**: Spans from **`10`** to **`80`** degrees in increments of 10 (`10`, `20`, `30`, `40`, `50`, `60`, `70`, `80`), with inward-facing tick marks along both the left and right borders.
- **Plotted Data**:
  - Drawn in bright red continuous lines (`with l`) on a white background inside a black rectangular border.
  - **Alaska**: Positioned in the upper-left quadrant between roughly `-170` to `-130` longitude and `54` to `71` latitude, showing mainland Alaska and the southeastern panhandle.
  - **Hawaii**: Positioned in the lower-left quadrant around `-160` to `-155` longitude and `19` to `22` latitude, showing the main islands as small red polygons.
  - **Contiguous United States**: Positioned in the center-right region between `-125` to `-67` longitude and `25` to `49` latitude, showing all 48 state outlines in unprojected geographic (latitude/longitude) coordinates.
- **Legend**: In the top-right corner inside the plot frame, the data series label **`'states.gp'`** is printed in black monospace text next to a short horizontal red line segment.
