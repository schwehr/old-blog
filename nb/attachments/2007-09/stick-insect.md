# Walking Stick Insect on a Door (`stick-insect.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up camera-phone photograph taken on September 3, 2007, showing a walking stick insect (order *Phasmatodea*) resting on the light-colored exterior surface of a door.

## Scene & Subject Description
- **Main Subject**: A slender, elongated walking stick insect angled diagonally from the lower-left toward the upper-right center of the frame. Its stick-like body is segmented and colored in shades of golden brown and olive green, mimicking a twig.
- **Limbs & Posture**:
  - The insect's six long, spindly legs extend outward from its thorax: the middle and hind pairs splay out to the left and right sides with banded brown-and-tan femurs and pale greenish-tan tibiae, while the front pair of legs and antennae extend forward/downward toward the bottom-left corner and upward/right from the thorax.
  - The tip of its abdomen points toward the upper-right center.
- **Background**: A smooth, slightly out-of-focus pale gray-green door panel with a subtle horizontal seam near the bottom and a brighter edge along the top border. A tiny dark speck is visible on the surface near the top center, just right of the upper leg tip.
