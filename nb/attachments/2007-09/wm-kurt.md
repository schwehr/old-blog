# Kurt Sitting on a River Boulder in the White Mountains (`wm-kurt.JPG`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken in late September 2007 along the Kancamagus Highway (NH Route 112) in the White Mountains of New Hampshire, showing Kurt Schwehr sitting on a large granite boulder in the middle of a rocky mountain river surrounded by early autumn foliage.

## Scene Description
- **Main Subject**: Slightly left of center, Kurt is seated atop a large, sunlit gray granite boulder in the riverbed. He is wearing glasses, a light gray short-sleeved t-shirt with a circular star logo on the left chest, and dark green shorts, resting his forearms on his knees and looking toward the camera.
- **Foreground & River**: Smooth, water-worn granite boulders occupy the lower-left and center foreground, catching bright sunlight. To the right and behind the boulders, clear shallow river water ripples over cobbles and rocks, sparkling in the bright sun.
- **Background**: Lining the riverbank behind Kurt is a dense mixed forest of dark green conifers (spruce/fir and pine) and deciduous trees turning vibrant shades of orange, red, and yellow against a bright pale sky.
