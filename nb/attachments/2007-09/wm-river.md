# Rocky Mountain River and Early Autumn Foliage in the White Mountains (`wm-river.JPG`)

## Overview
A low-angle landscape photograph of a rocky river (the Swift River along NH Route 112 / Kancamagus Highway) in the White Mountains of New Hampshire, taken in late September 2007 as autumn colors were emerging.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Riverbed**: Looking upstream along a wide, shallow mountain river filled with rounded tan and gray granite cobbles and boulders. Small whitewater riffles flow toward the lower-left foreground, while a dry or shallowly exposed gravel and boulder bar lines the right bank, anchored by a large rounded tan boulder on the far right.
- **Forest & Foliage**:
  - **Left Bank**: A dense stand of mixed evergreens (tall white pines and conifers) and deciduous trees with crowns beginning to turn orange, rust, and yellow.
  - **Right Bank**: Bright crimson and scarlet red leaves on understory bushes and maple branches contrast sharply against green foliage and pale birch trunks.
- **Background**: Through the gap where the river bends in the center distance, a hazy blue-green mountain ridge rises beneath a bright, pale overcast sky.
