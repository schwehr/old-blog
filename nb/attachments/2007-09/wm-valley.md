# White Mountains Valley View with Red Autumn Scrub (`wm-valley.JPG`)

## Overview
A scenic landscape photograph looking across a bright red autumn clearing toward layered mountain ridges in the White Mountains of New Hampshire (along NH Route 112 near Conway / Kancamagus Pass), taken in late September 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**: A wide, dense field of low bushes and scrub (such as blueberry/huckleberry or sumac/maple brush) ablaze in vivid crimson, scarlet, and orange autumn foliage, interspersed with patches of green leaves and golden grass at the bottom-left edge.
- **Midground**: Framing the back of the red clearing is a mixed forest line of dark green conifers, white-barked birch trees, and deciduous trees in shades of green, yellow, orange, and red. To the right, a steep forested mountain slope rises toward the upper-right edge of the frame.
- **Background**: Beyond the treeline, multiple receding mountain peaks and ridges appear in soft, hazy blue-gray silhouettes under a pale blue sky streaked with light white clouds.
