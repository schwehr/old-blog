# Aerial Sunset Over the Chicago Shoreline (`chicago-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken from the cabin window of a Southwest Airlines passenger jet on the evening of October 27, 2007, looking back toward a brilliant orange and yellow sunset over the Lake Michigan shoreline near Chicago.

## Scene Description
- **Aircraft Wing**: Extending diagonally from the bottom center-left up to the top left corner is the swept wing of a commercial airliner. The wing surface is bathed in warm golden-orange light from the setting sun, showing panel seams, flap track fairings protruding from the trailing edge, and an upturned blended winglet at the tip.
- **Visible Text**: Along the inner surface of the upturned winglet in the upper left, large grey block letters read **`SOUTHWEST.COM`**.
- **Sun & Sky**: Near the upper right corner, the blazing orb of the sun sits just above a hazy horizon, radiating intense white and yellow light that transitions into deep fiery orange and red across the atmospheric haze below.
- **Coastline & Landscape**: Beneath the wing, the dark, flat expanse of land meets the smooth, reflective surface of Lake Michigan. Harbor breakwaters, piers, river mouths, and inland waterways catch the bright golden reflection of the sunset along the curved shoreline.
