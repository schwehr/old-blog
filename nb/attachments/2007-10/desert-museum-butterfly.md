# Queen Butterfly on Gregg's Mistflower at the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum (`desert-museum-butterfly.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up macro nature photograph taken in October 2007 at the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum near Tucson, Arizona, showing a Queen butterfly (*Danaus gilippus*) perched on pale lavender-white mistflower blossoms.

## Scene Description
- **Butterfly**: Positioned on the right side of the frame in profile with its wings folded upright, the butterfly displays rich burnt-orange and mahogany-brown wings bordered by black margins speckled with two rows of small white dots. The hindwing shows dark black veins outlining the orange cells, while the forewing features several larger white spots near the apex. Its black head and thorax are also dotted with white markings, and its long, slender black antennae curve upward toward the flower petals.
- **Flowers**: On the left and top center are fluffy, filamentous flower heads of Gregg's mistflower (*Conoclinium greggii*), composed of dozens of delicate, thread-like pale pinkish-lavender florets radiating from compact green receptacles on slender bright green stems.
- **Background**: Sunlit green leaves occupy the lower left foreground, contrasting against a deeply shaded, out-of-focus dark green foliage background on the right.
