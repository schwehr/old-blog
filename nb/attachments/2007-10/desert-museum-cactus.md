# Totem Pole Cactus at the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum (`desert-museum-cactus.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical photograph taken in October 2007 at the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum near Tucson, Arizona, showcasing several knobby, spineless columns of a Totem Pole Cactus (*Pachycereus schottii* f. *monstrosus*, also known as *Lophocereus schottii* f. *monstrosus*).

## Scene Description
- **Totem Pole Cacti**:
  - In the right foreground stands a cluster of thick, upright, olive-green cactus stems covered in irregular, smooth, spineless tubercles and folds that give the plant a sculpted, melting, or totem-like appearance.
  - On the middle-left stand two additional tall, slender columnar stems with the same bumpy, spineless texture.
- **Desert Ground & Surrounding Flora**:
  - The cacti grow out of a rocky desert bed strewn with angular reddish-tan sandstone rocks and small clumps of silvery-white dried desert grasses.
  - Immediately behind the right-hand cactus is a reddish-spined barrel cactus, and in the background is a dense thicket of branching cholla cacti, prickly pear pads, and green desert shrubs under bright sunlight.
