# Resting Mountain Lion at the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum (`desert-museum-cat.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken in October 2007 at the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum near Tucson, Arizona, depicting a large mountain lion (cougar / puma, *Puma concolor*) resting on a sunlit rock ledge beneath a dark overhanging cave roof.

## Scene Description
- **Mountain Lion**: The tawny-tan big cat lies stretched out on its side along the flat top of a rough sandstone-like ledge in the middle of the frame. Its head is raised on the left, facing right in profile with its eyes closed or half-closed, rounded ear visible, and dark markings around its muzzle. Its large front paws rest tucked in front of its chest while its long body and hind legs extend to the right into the shadow.
- **Rock Ledge & Overhang**:
  - The lower half of the image is filled by the warm, textured, reddish-tan rock outcrop, brightly illuminated by direct sunlight on the front face.
  - The upper half of the frame is dominated by a heavy, shadowed rock overhang forming a cave-like shelter.
  - Directly behind the mountain lion's head is a bright rectangular gap of overexposed sky and green foliage visible through the opening in the rock grotto.
