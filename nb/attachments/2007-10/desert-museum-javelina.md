# Collared Peccary (Javelina) at the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum (`desert-museum-javelina.jpg`)

## Overview
A wildlife photograph taken in October 2007 at the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum near Tucson, Arizona, showing a collared peccary—commonly known as a javelina (*Pecari tajacu*)—standing in a sunlit desert scrub habitat.

## Scene Description
- **Javelina**: Positioned centrally in left-facing profile, the pig-like mammal has a stocky body covered in coarse, grizzled dark brown and grey-tan bristles, with a faint lighter band (collar) curving across its shoulder. It has a long, tapered snout, small upright ears, dark eyes, and slender dark legs standing firmly on the rocky dirt, casting a dark shadow beneath its belly.
- **Desert Habitat**:
  - The ground is covered in warm orange-tan desert soil scattered with small angular stones and pebbles.
  - Low, dry desert bushes and brittlebrush surround the animal, while a sprawling buckhorn or staghorn cholla cactus and a thicker tree trunk rise in the upper background.
