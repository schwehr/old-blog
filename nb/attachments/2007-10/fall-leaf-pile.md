# Large Autumn Leaf Pile in the Backyard (`fall-leaf-pile.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on October 28, 2007, in the author's backyard in New Hampshire, showing a large mound of wet fallen autumn leaves raked together in front of a wooded tree line.

## Scene Description
- **Leaf Pile**: Occupying the entire foreground and middle ground is a wide, heaped mound of fallen maple and deciduous leaves in shades of russet brown, copper, golden yellow, and pale tan. Many of the leaves glisten slightly from moisture, and small twigs are mixed into the pile.
- **Compost Bin & Brush Pile**:
  - Directly behind the crest of the leaf mound in the center stands a cylindrical wire-mesh compost bin filled to the top with brown leaves.
  - To the left, at the edge of the woods, is a stacked pile of fallen tree branches and sticks.
- **Wooded Background**: A row of slender deciduous tree trunks rises behind the yard, still holding bright yellowish-green autumn foliage illuminated by low sunlight filtering through from the left.
