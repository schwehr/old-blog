# Southern California Wildfire Smoke Plumes Viewed from JPL (`fires-viewed-from-jpl-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide, panoramic-cropped photograph taken on October 23, 2007, from the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) in Pasadena, California, showing dark tree silhouettes and office buildings against a sky streaked with orange-pink wildfire smoke during the October 2007 Southern California wildfires.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Trees**: Dark, nearly black silhouettes of tall pine and deciduous trees frame the left, center-right, and far right edges of the composition. Along the bottom, a line of curved metal streetlamp poles and overhead utility wires crosses the scene.
- **Buildings & Foothills**:
  - Partially visible behind the trees on the left is the rectangular upper facade of a multi-story JPL building, with the sloped silhouette of a distant foothill ridgeline just to its right.
  - Near the center stands another light-colored multi-story JPL building with a utility pole in front of it.
- **Smoke-Filled Sky**: The upper sky is blown out to bright white, while the lower sky above the horizon is veiled by a horizontal layer of grey-brown and salmon-pink smoke drifting across the basin, glowing reddish-orange near the trees.
