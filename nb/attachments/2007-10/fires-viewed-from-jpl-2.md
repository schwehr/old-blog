# Sun Glaring Through Wildfire Smoke Over Mountains Near JPL (`fires-viewed-from-jpl-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide, panoramic-cropped photograph taken on October 23, 2007, from the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) area in Pasadena, California, capturing the sun blazing through heavy layers of orange-brown wildfire smoke along the San Gabriel Mountain foothills.

## Scene Description
- **Sun & Smoke Plumes**: Near the center of the frame, the blinding white disk of the sun sits right at the base of the mountain slope, casting a vertical golden-orange lens flare downward and radiating red-orange glare across the foreground. Extending to the right of the sun are thick, horizontal bands of brownish-grey and amber smoke hanging in the air.
- **Mountain Ridgeline & Silhouette**:
  - Rising to the left of the sun is the dark, hazy brown silhouette of the San Gabriel Mountain ridgeline, softened by atmospheric smoke.
  - Conifer branches frame the far left foreground in dark silhouette, while pointed pine tree tops rise against the smoky orange sky on the right.
  - Along the bottom center, the pale rooftops of low industrial or laboratory buildings catch the orange sunlight.
