# Stone Patio, Garden Wall, and Pond at the Gamble House (`gamblehouse-back-pond.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on the morning of October 27, 2007, in the rear garden of the Gamble House in Pasadena, California, showing its signature Craftsman clinker-brick and Arroyo boulder wall, terracotta-tiled patio, and lush pond planting.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Shrub & Wall**: In the lower center foreground, a sculpted, spreading green juniper or bonsai-like evergreen shrub grows out of a curved planter bed built into the low garden wall. The wall itself curves away toward the upper right, constructed from dark, irregularly shaped clinker bricks mixed with rounded pale grey Arroyo cobbles and boulders, with creeping green vines trailing down its right side.
- **Lantern**: Perched atop a raised brick pillar on the wall in the upper right is a Craftsman-style outdoor lantern with a pyramidal green copper roof and leaded glass panels.
- **Patio & Pond Area**:
  - To the left of the wall is a flat patio paved with square reddish-brown quarry tiles bordered by herringbone brick patterns, with two pale ceramic pots visible at the far left edge.
  - Beyond the foreground shrub, dense clumps of green papyrus and tall golden reeds surround a small garden pond enclosed by a low stone and brick border wall.
  - A dense canopy of dark green trees fills the background, with a small patch of bright green lawn visible on the far right.
