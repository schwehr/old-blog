# Front Exterior of the Gamble House in Early Morning (`gamblehouse.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle photograph taken around 7:00 AM on October 27, 2007, showing the front facade of the historic Gamble House—the iconic 1908 American Craftsman residence designed by Greene and Greene—in Pasadena, California.

## Scene Description
- **Architecture**: Seen from across the front lawn looking slightly up at the two-and-a-half-story residence, the house features dark-stained wood shingle siding, broad overhanging gabled roofs with exposed rafters and timber brackets, a rectangular masonry chimney rising above the roof peak, and wide sleeping porches on the upper right. Warm, reddish-brown wood-framed casement and picture windows punctuate both stories.
- **Lighting & Entryway**: In the dim early-morning light under an overcast pale sky, several warm yellow-orange exterior lanterns are illuminated—two under the left side porte-cochère/porch, two flanking the front entry terrace, and one on the right porch. Low stone terrace walls and wide steps lead up to the front door, flanked by two tall, slender Italian cypress trees and rounded ornamental shrubs.
- **Grounds**: A lush, vibrant green manicured lawn stretches across the foreground, divided on the lower right by a curving paved driveway or walkway. Large leafy trees frame the left and far right sides, with the crown of a palm tree visible behind the roofline.
