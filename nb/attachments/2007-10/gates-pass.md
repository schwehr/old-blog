# Historic Stone Cabin and Corrugated Roof at Gates Pass (`gates-pass.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical landscape photograph taken in October 2007 at Gates Pass in the Tucson Mountains west of Tucson, Arizona, looking over the rusted corrugated metal roof and stone chimney of a historic hillside structure toward rugged desert peaks.

## Scene Description
- **Stone Structure (Foreground)**: Occupying the lower left half of the image is a rustic one-story building constructed from mortared, irregular desert stones in shades of tan, brown, and rust. Round wooden vigas (roof beams) protrude just beneath the eaves. The gabled roof is covered in corrugated metal panels weathered to a rich rusty orange-brown, with a square mortared-stone chimney rising from the roof ridge, surrounded by a thick cement flashing collar at its base.
- **Mountains & Desert Terrain (Background)**:
  - Rising directly behind the cabin in the center of the frame is a steep, conical rocky desert peak dotted with green desert scrub, creosote, and small saguaro cacti.
  - Flanking peaks and ridges of the Tucson Mountains rise on the upper left and upper right, with a narrow dirt hiking trail visible winding along the sunny slope to the right of the roof against a clear pale blue sky.
