# Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) Campus Viewed from the Arroyo Seco (`jpl-from-the-arroyo.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide, panoramic-cropped photograph taken on October 18, 2007, looking north-northeast from the brushy floor of the Arroyo Seco (near the Oak Grove disc golf course) toward the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) campus nestled against the San Gabriel Mountains in Pasadena, California.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**: Dry, scrubby olive-green and brown chaparral bushes and wild grasses cover the flat alluvial wash of the Arroyo Seco across the bottom of the frame.
- **JPL Campus (Middle Ground)**:
  - A dense cluster of white and cream-colored modernist multi-story laboratory, office, and administration buildings rises above the brush in bright afternoon sunlight.
  - Prominent taller structures on the left and center-left (including Building 180 and Building 167/169) feature grid-like window facades, while lower horizontal buildings with vertical window slits and utility poles spread across the center and right.
- **Foothills & Mountains (Background)**:
  - Directly behind the buildings rise the steep, dry, tan-and-green chaparral-covered foothills of the San Gabriel Mountains.
  - Higher mountain peaks rise on the far left and far right beneath a clear, cloudless blue sky.
