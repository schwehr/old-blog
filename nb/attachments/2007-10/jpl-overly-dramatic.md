# Evening View of JPL Campus (`jpl-overly-dramatic.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide panoramic photograph of NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) campus in Pasadena, California, captured in the evening on October 16, 2007, with heavily saturated magenta, orange, and purple color tones giving it a vintage 1970s-style aesthetic.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Trees**: Dark silhouettes of dense trees and tall conifers span across the lower portion of the frame, with a tall pine tree rising prominently on the right side and another near the left-center.
- **JPL Buildings**:
  - **Center-Right**: The tall, rectangular high-rise administration building (Building 180) stands out with its light-colored facade catching the warm evening light. An American flag flies from a flagpole mounted atop the building's roof.
  - **Left**: A multi-story building with a grid of horizontal balconies/windows and a small white radome/dish on its roof is partially visible behind the trees.
  - **Right**: A mid-rise rectangular building with vertical dark window columns and a light grid structure sits to the right of the main tower, with smaller white structures dimly visible near the far right edge.
- **Background & Sky**: The San Gabriel Mountains rise faintly in the background behind the campus, shrouded in purple-magenta haze and low clouds illuminated in shades of violet, rose, and dusky pink.
