# Sunrise Over the JPL Campus (`jpl-sunrise.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format photograph capturing a bright, golden sunrise over the mountains and buildings at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) in Pasadena, California, taken on October 17, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Sun & Sky**: The rising sun forms a brilliant, glowing white-yellow orb near the center of the frame just above the horizon, casting a vertical lens flare streak straight down toward the bottom edge. Broken stratocumulus clouds across the sky are lit in warm shades of gold, amber, orange, and soft gray.
- **Mountains**: To the left of the sun, the ridgeline of the San Gabriel Mountains recedes into a golden atmospheric haze.
- **Foreground & Campus**:
  - The lower third of the image is largely in deep shadow, silhouetted against the intense backlight.
  - Framed by dark pine branches on the far left and evergreen trees on the far right.
  - Below the sun, flat-roofed JPL campus buildings, rooftop mechanical equipment, streetlights, and parked cars in a lot on the lower left catch faint glints of morning light.
