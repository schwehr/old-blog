# Sunset Reflected in a House Window (`la-window-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview
An evening photograph taken in the South Pasadena / Los Angeles area on October 18, 2007, showing a single-story white stucco house whose large front picture window reflects a vivid orange and gold sunset.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Tree**: A massive, mature tree with thick, textured dark trunks and sprawling, twisting branches dominates the left side and upper canopy of the frame. Below the tree is a green front lawn bordered by low leafy groundcover in the lower left corner and a curved concrete driveway on the right.
- **House**:
  - A single-story white stucco bungalow with a dark shingled hipped roof and a tall white chimney on the right side.
  - To the left of the front entrance is a small hexagonal window.
  - To the right of the front door is a large three-part picture window (a wide central pane flanked by narrower multi-pane casement sections). The glass acts like a mirror, reflecting a brilliant gradient of deep orange, amber, and yellow sunset sky along with the dark silhouettes of distant hills and tree branches.
- **Surroundings**: A tall, slender palm tree rises in the background behind the house against a pale, washed-out evening sky. Part of a neighboring two-story house and a trimmed hedge line the driveway on the far right.
