# Adobe Photoshop CS3 Licensing Error Dialog (`norton-takesout-cs3.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of an error dialog box from Adobe Photoshop CS3 indicating that product licensing has stopped working (caused by a conflict with Norton software when attempting to use a JPEG 2000 plugin).

## Interface & Controls
- **Dialog Window**: A standard Mac OS X Aqua-style modal alert window with a gray top title bar and a subtle horizontal pinstripe background.
- **Application Icon**: On the left side is the square blue Adobe Photoshop CS3 icon featuring the white letters **`Ps`** on a gradient blue background.
- **Action Button**: In the bottom right corner is a glossy blue pill-shaped default button labeled **`OK`**.

## Visible Text & Labels
- **Heading (bold black text)**:
  `Licensing for this product has stopped working.`
- **Body Text**:
  `You cannot use this product at this time. You must repair the problem by uninstalling and then reinstalling this product or contacting your IT administrator or Adobe customer support for help.`
