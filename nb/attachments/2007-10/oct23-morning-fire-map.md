# San Diego County Wildfire Perimeter and Evacuation Map (`oct23-morning-fire-map.jpg`)

## Overview
A Google Maps screenshot from the morning of October 23, 2007, illustrating the active wildfire perimeters, fire origin/hotspot icons, and evacuation/shelter locations across San Diego County, California, during the October 2007 Southern California wildfires.

## Map / Plot Details
- **Fire Perimeters (Red Shaded Polygons)**:
  - **Witch / Guejito Fire (Central/Northern)**: A large, sprawling semi-transparent red polygon extending westward from east of **Ramona** through the **Escondido** / **Poway** corridor almost to the coast near **Encinitas** and **Solana Beach**.
  - **Harris Fire (Southern)**: A large semi-transparent red polygon in southeastern San Diego County stretching northwest from **Tecate** toward **Spring Valley** and **El Cajon**.
  - **Northern Perimeter**: A smaller shaded area near State Route 78 between **Vista** and **Valley Center**.
- **Map Markers**:
  - **Flame Icons (Red/Orange Fire Symbols)**: Four flame icons mark major fire incidents:
    1. Along Highway 78 between **Vista** and **Valley Center** (McCoy / Rice Fire area).
    2. Northeast of **Ramona** near **San Felipe** / Santa Ysabel (Witch Fire origin).
    3. East of **Ramona** near **Julian** and Cuyamaca Rancho State Park.
    4. Near the U.S.–Mexico border at **Tecate** (Harris Fire origin).
  - **Red Cross Icons (Evacuation Centers / Shelters)**: Seven bold red cross symbols mark shelter and medical/evacuation sites at:
    1. Near **Valley Center** / north of **Escondido**.
    2. Along the coast at **Encinitas** / **Solana Beach** / **Del Mar**.
    3. Near **Poway** (central corridor).
    4. Along State Route 56 between I-5 and I-15.
    5. Near **Santee**.
    6. Near **La Mesa** / State Route 163 & I-8.
    7. Between **El Cajon** and **Spring Valley**.
    8. In **Chula Vista** along I-805.
