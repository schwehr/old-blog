# Team Members Using IPRW During Phoenix Mars Lander ORT-6 (`ort6-iprw.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken at the Spacecraft Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona, during Operational Readiness Test #6 (ORT-6) for the Phoenix Mars Lander on October 4, 2007, showing mission team members seated at a table working on Apple laptops.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Left to Center)**:
  - Two men wearing glasses and red mission lanyards are seated at a wooden folding table with two 15-inch silver Apple PowerBook G4 / MacBook Pro laptops open in front of them.
  - The man on the far left (wearing a black polo shirt and goatee) leans forward and smiles toward a large black display monitor positioned on the right side of the frame.
  - Beside him, a man in a light tan/plaid short-sleeved button-down shirt smiles at the monitor; his laptop screen displays a web or software interface with thumbnail images and panels.
- **Middle Ground (Seated Colleagues)**:
  - Behind and to the right of the two men, several other team members sit in a row of office chairs facing the right: two men in light-colored shirts, a woman in a light pink long-sleeved top and brown pants, a woman in a black top with glasses, and a person seen from behind wearing a black-and-white horizontally striped shirt.
- **Background (High-Bay / Display Area)**:
  - Directly behind the group is a full-scale model of the **Phoenix Mars Lander** surrounded by white stanchions and chains, with its gold metallic thermal blanketing, deck, and black robotic arm extending upward.
  - Along the far back wall, large framed posters and corkboard display panels stand on easels on the concrete floor.
