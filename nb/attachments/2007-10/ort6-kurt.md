# Kurt Schwehr with the Phoenix Mars Lander Model at ORT-6 (`ort6-kurt.jpg`)

## Overview
A self-portrait photograph of Kurt Schwehr standing in front of a full-scale mockup of the Phoenix Mars Lander inside the Tucson Spacecraft Operations Center (SOC) during Operational Readiness Test #6 (ORT-6) in October 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Subject)**:
  - Kurt Schwehr is positioned in the left foreground from the shoulders up, looking directly at the camera with a serious expression.
  - He is wearing rectangular wire-rimmed glasses, a backwards dark gray/olive baseball cap, and a burgundy/maroon hooded sweatshirt.
- **Background (Phoenix Mars Lander Model)**:
  - Behind him to the right stands a full-scale display model of the **Phoenix Mars Lander** enclosed by white stanchions and a decorative chain/garland barrier.
  - Key visible components of the lander include:
    - The flat gray octagonal science deck topped with gray instrument boxes and enclosures.
    - The black articulated **Robotic Arm (RA)** rising vertically from the deck on the left, with its scoop/camera assembly hanging down.
    - The white **Surface Stereo Imager (SSI)** camera head mounted atop a slender lattice mast behind the arm.
    - Gold multi-layer insulation (MLI) thermal blanketing and spherical propellant tanks beneath the deck, supported by silver tripodal landing legs.
  - In the far left background of the high-bay room, wooden crates and display boards sit against the light-colored walls.
