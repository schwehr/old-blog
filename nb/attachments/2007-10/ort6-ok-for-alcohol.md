# Bowling Night Toast with Tiny Cups and "Ok For Alcohol" Wristbands (`ort6-ok-for-alcohol.png`)

## Overview
A candid flash photograph taken during an evening bowling outing in Tucson, Arizona, during Phoenix Mars Lander Operational Readiness Test #6 (ORT-6) in October 2007, showing Kurt Schwehr and a colleague toasting with tiny plastic cups of 50-cent beer while wearing age-verification wristbands.
