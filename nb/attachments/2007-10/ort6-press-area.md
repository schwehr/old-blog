# Press and Display Area at the Tucson SOC During ORT-6 (`ort6-press-area.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle indoor photograph of the press and public exhibit hall inside the Tucson Spacecraft Operations Center (SOC) during Phoenix Mars Lander Operational Readiness Test #6 (ORT-6) on October 4, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Ceiling & HVAC**: The high white drop ceiling is lit by rectangular fluorescent light fixtures. Three long, bright red fabric air-distribution ducts (inflatable HVAC socks with rows of small ventilation holes) stretch horizontally across the upper portion of the hall from metallic ductwork on the right.
- **Left Foreground (Lander Model & Posters)**:
  - A full-scale mockup of the **Phoenix Mars Lander** sits on the polished gray concrete floor surrounded by white stanchions and chains, showing its silver landing legs, gold thermal blanketing, flat circular solar arrays, and tall black robotic arm.
  - On the white wall behind the lander, two rows of four dark-background educational/mission posters (eight total) are mounted next to a larger vertical poster.
- **Center (Meeting / Briefing Group)**:
  - A group of roughly a dozen team members sit in a circle of chairs around a projection screen and a standing presenter in a dark shirt.
- **Right (Exhibit Displays)**:
  - Freestanding black metal grid display racks hold informational panels and images.
  - Along the tan/gold back wall, blue-bannered vertical exhibit displays, display pedestals, and a draped equipment rack on the far right complete the room setup.
