# San Gabriel Mountains and Pasadena Skyline at Sunset (`pasadena-skyline.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format landscape photograph taken on October 21, 2007, showing the rugged peaks of the San Gabriel Mountains bathed in warm golden-orange late-afternoon sunlight above the dark silhouetted skyline of trees in the Pasadena area.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Tree Line Silhouette)**:
  - Across the bottom third of the image is a continuous, deep black silhouette of diverse urban forest trees, including tall, slender California fan palms sticking up on the left and center, rounded broadleaf canopies, and dense conifers/eucalypts rising higher on the right side.
- **Middle Ground (San Gabriel Mountains)**:
  - Rising steeply behind the silhouetted trees is the rocky, dissected front range of the San Gabriel Mountains.
  - The western-facing slopes and ridges glow in warm amber, copper, and golden-pink sunset light, while the steep canyons and east-facing facets fall into cool purple-blue and slate shadows.
- **Sky**: Above the mountain crest is a clear, pale cream-to-light-blue early evening sky completely free of clouds.
