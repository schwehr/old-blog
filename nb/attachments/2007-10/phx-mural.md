# Phoenix Mars Mission Mural at the Tucson SOC (`phx-mural.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle outdoor photograph taken at sunrise on October 5, 2007, showing the colorful **Phoenix Mars Mission Mural** painted across four concrete wall panels on the exterior of the Spacecraft Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona. Noticeable barrel distortion from a wide-angle lens curves the top edge of the wall.

## Scene Description
- **Main Central Artwork**:
  - **Left (Earth & Launch)**: On the left, a glowing blue-and-orange globe of Earth erupts with a stream of golden-orange fire that curves upward across a starry black space background.
  - **Center-Left (Mythological Phoenix & Chariot)**: Within the rising flames, a fiery orange figure rides a horse-drawn chariot that transforms into a giant mythical Phoenix bird with outstretched flaming wings.
  - **Center-Right (Entry & Descent)**: Emerging from the beak/wings of the fiery Phoenix is the yellow-and-orange heatshield/aeroshell and the **Phoenix Mars Lander** firing its descent thrusters as it approaches a large, rust-orange globe of Mars with a white polar ice cap on the right.
  - **Bottom Center (Martian Surface)**: Below a bright blue Martian sky and white sun, the landed Phoenix spacecraft sits on reddish-brown polygonal patterned ground with its octagonal decagonal fan-like solar arrays deployed, robotic arm digging a trench (with an inset showing white subsurface ice), and mast raised.
  - **Bottom Insets**: To the left of the lander is a square inset of microscopic particles/soil; to the right is a circular false-color elevation map of Mars's northern polar region.
- **Left Border Column (8 Square Icons, Top to Bottom)**:
  1. NASA "Meatball" logo.
  2. Stylized blue/red symbol.
  3. Portrait of a historical astronomer/scientist.
  4. Silhouetted figures looking up at the sky.
  5. Spacecraft/satellite icon on yellow background.
  6. Delta II rocket launching on green background.
  7. White mission/observatory building dome.
  8. University of Arizona block **`A`** logo at the bottom left.
- **Right Border Column (8 Square Icons, Top to Bottom)**:
  1. Red-and-blue block **`T`** logo at the top right.
  2. Mars orbiter spacecraft.
  3. Red planet/mission emblem.
  4. Swirling vortex/galaxy artwork.
  5. Portrait of an older man in glasses and suit.
  6. **Marvin the Martian** cartoon character against an orange background.
  7. Purple/blue crystalline texture square.
  8. Circular mission patch logo at the bottom right.

## Visible Text & Labels
- Along the bottom edge of the mural in small white capital letters are artist credits and the title on the bottom right: **`PHOENIX MARS MISSION MURAL 2006`**.
