# Photoshop Magic Wand Selection with Stroke and Semi-Transparent Fill (`ps-ellipse-10.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from an Adobe Photoshop tutorial ("Making ellipses in Photoshop", October 5, 2007) showing an irregular region on the front wall of a single-story house in Tucson after applying a red stroke outline and a semi-transparent green fill, with the "marching ants" selection marquee still active.

- **Highlighted Selection Region**:
