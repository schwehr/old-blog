# Final Result of Photoshop Irregular Highlight with Red Outline and Green Fill (`ps-ellipse-11.png`)

## Overview
The final screenshot in a series demonstrating how to highlight regions in Adobe Photoshop (October 5, 2007), showing the completed irregular highlight on a Tucson house after deselecting the Magic Wand marquee.

## Scene Description & Visual Details
- **Base Photograph**: A street-level photograph of a single-story, flat-roofed brick house in Tucson with a rooftop evaporative cooler (swamp cooler), a chain-link fence around a gravel yard, a paved street in the foreground, and a blue curbside bin on the right.
- **Completed Highlight Overlay**:
  - The irregular region covering the brick front wall of the house features a clean, solid bright red border (stroke) and a semi-transparent green interior fill that allows the underlying brick texture to show through.
  - Rectangular and vertical cutouts around the dark front doorway and windows are cleanly bordered in red, with the dashed selection marquee ("marching ants") now removed.
