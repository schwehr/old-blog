# Using the Magic Wand Tool for Irregular Selections in Photoshop (`ps-ellipse-9.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Adobe Photoshop on Mac OS X demonstrating the use of the **Magic Wand Tool** to select an irregular region of brickwork on the facade of a Tucson house, part of a tutorial on highlighting image features (October 5, 2007).

## Interface & Controls
- **Tools Palette (Left)**: The vertical two-column Photoshop toolbar is visible on the left side, with the **Magic Wand Tool** (second row, right column) actively selected and shaded dark gray.
- **Document Window (`DSCN8041.JPG @ 66.7% (RGB`)**:
  - Displays the red, yellow, and green Mac OS X window buttons in the title bar alongside a small image thumbnail icon.
  - The Magic Wand cursor icon (a small wand with a starburst tip) hovers just above the roofline to the left of center.

## Selection Details
- An irregular, contiguous "marching ants" selection outline covers a large portion of the brown brick front wall of the single-story house, wrapping around the windows, dark doorway, and bushes where the brick color and brightness matched the Magic Wand tolerance.
