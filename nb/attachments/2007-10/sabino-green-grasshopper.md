# Green and Yellow Grasshopper in Sabino Canyon (`sabino-green-grasshopper.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up nature photograph taken during a late-afternoon hike with Jeff in Sabino Canyon near Tucson, Arizona, on October 6, 2007 (posted October 7), showing a large green, yellow, and reddish-brown grasshopper perched on a plant stem.

## Subject & Scene Description
- **Grasshopper**:
  - Positioned in profile facing left along a horizontal green plant stem with a reddish-pinkish upper tinge.
  - Features an olive-green and bright yellow patterned head and thorax, long yellowish-orange antennae extending to the upper left, and a dark compound eye.
  - Its folded forewings (tegmina) are reddish-brown, while its long, powerful hind femur displays bold longitudinal stripes of bright yellow, dark green/black, and a black band near the joint.
  - Its yellow-green forelegs and middle legs grip the junction where a smaller vertical stem branches downward.
- **Foliage & Background**:
  - Bright green lance-shaped leaves and thin stems frame the insect in the foreground.
  - Behind the plant, out-of-focus tan and pale gray desert canyon rocks provide a textured natural backdrop.
