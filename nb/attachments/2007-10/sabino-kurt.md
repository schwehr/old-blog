# Kurt Schwehr Hiking in Sabino Canyon (`sabino-kurt.jpg`)

## Overview
A portrait photograph of Kurt Schwehr standing on a trail in Sabino Canyon in the Santa Catalina Mountains near Tucson, Arizona, taken during a late-afternoon hike on October 6, 2007 (posted October 7).

## Subject & Scene Description
- **Foreground Subject (Kurt Schwehr)**:
  - Positioned slightly right of center in the lower third of the frame, smiling toward the camera with one knee raised as if stepping onto a rock or ledge.
  - Wearing a dark brown/gray baseball cap with an emblem on the front, glasses, a plain white short-sleeved t-shirt with a small dark chest logo on the left, and olive-green cargo pants/shorts.
  - His hands are clasped near his waist holding a small pink/purple item.
- **Canyon Landscape & Background**:
  - Steep, rugged canyon walls rise dramatically on both sides, forming a deep V-shaped gorge in the center.
  - The lower slopes, particularly on the right side, are covered in lush green desert scrub and numerous tall, columnar saguaro cacti standing upright against the hillside.
  - Above the shaded lower canyon, jagged reddish-brown and tan layered rock cliffs ascend toward high mountain peaks, where the uppermost crags on the right catch golden late-afternoon sunlight beneath a bright sky with soft white clouds and blue patches.
