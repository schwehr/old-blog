# Sunset Over Sabino Creek in Sabino Canyon (`sabino-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic landscape photograph taken at sunset in Sabino Canyon near Tucson, Arizona, on October 6, 2007 (posted October 7), looking along a rocky desert stream bed toward the glowing horizon between steep canyon walls.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Stream & Rocks**:
  - A calm pool of water in Sabino Creek occupies the lower-left and center foreground, reflecting the bright pale-gold sunset sky and the dark silhouette of a tree.
  - A small gravel bar curves into the water on the left, and a tiny mossy/rocky islet sits in the middle of the foreground pool.
  - Surrounding the water and extending upstream through the middle ground is a wide bed of rounded gray and tan boulders, cobbles, and bare white branches of riparian saplings/shrubs.
- **Canyon Walls & Silhouette**:
  - Steep canyon slopes frame the scene on the left and right, cast in deep shadow as the sun dips behind the right-hand ridge.
  - Along the V-shaped notch where the canyon walls meet in the center distance, a tall broadleaf riparian tree (such as a sycamore or cottonwood) stands silhouetted against the brilliant white-and-gold backlit sky.
