# San Diego County Wildfire Perimeter and Evacuation Map (`sd-fire-map-oct24-morning.png`)

## Overview
An official emergency GIS map from San Diego County (`sdcountyemergency.com` / SignOnSanDiego, `10242007_0500hrs_Evac_FirePerem.pdf`) posted on the morning of October 24, 2007, showing active wildfire perimeters, satellite thermal hotspots, mandatory evacuation zones, and road corridors across San Diego County during the devastating October 2007 Southern California wildfires.

## Map & Plot Details
- **Geographic Extent**: Covers coastal and inland San Diego County from the Orange/Riverside County border (`S.D. COUNTY`) and Oceanside in the north down to San Diego Bay, Coronado, Chula Vista, and Imperial Beach at the U.S.–Mexico border in the south.
- **Fire Perimeters & Hotspots**:
  - Large salmon/orange shaded polygons represent the burned wildfire perimeters:
    - **Central / North-Central (Witch - Guajito-Poomacha Fire complex)**: A massive contiguous burn area stretching from the northeast near Lake Henshaw (`1051`, `1052`, `1071`, `1072`) southwest through Ramona (`1152`–`1154`), Escondido (`1129`, `1130`), Rancho Bernardo, Poway (`1170`, `1171`), and westward across Interstate 15 toward Rancho Santa Fe and Encinitas (`1148`, `1149`), with a southern arm reaching toward Santee/Lakeside (`1193`, `1213`).
    - **Northern Border (Fallbrook / McCoy / Ammo Fires)**: Burn polygons in the upper left (`1024`, `1025`) on Camp Pendleton and north of Fallbrook (`998`, `1028`) along I-15.
    - **Southern Border (Harris Fire)**: A large burn polygon in the lower right covering southeast San Diego County from Potrero and Barrett Junction (`1314`, `1315`, `1335`) northwest toward Jamul and Chula Vista/Spring Valley (`1291`, `1293`, `1294`, `1312`).
  - Clusters of bright red dots mark active satellite thermal hotspots concentrated along the expanding edges of the fires (around Lake Hodges/Escondido, Poway, Julian/Santa Ysabel on the east edge, Fallbrook, and the Harris Fire perimeter).
- **Evacuation & Emergency Overlays**:
  - **Purple Diagonal Hatching**: Large purple cross-hatched polygons indicate evacuation zones encompassing north-county mountain/backcountry areas, Ramona, Escondido, Poway, Rancho Santa Fe, Del Mar heights, Alpine/El Cajon fringes, and the Dulzura/Jamul/Chula Vista east county region.
  - **Yellow Highlighted Roads**: Major evacuation and highway corridors (such as SR-78, SR-67, SR-52, SR-94, SR-79, and sections of I-5) are highlighted in bright yellow.
  - **Green Triangles**: Small dark green triangle icons mark emergency shelters/evacuation centers around Oceanside, MiraCosta, Escondido, Del Mar, Mira Mesa, San Diego, El Cajon, and Chula Vista.
  - **Blue Rectangles**: Two bold blue rectangular callout boxes are drawn on the map—one near Ramona (`1132`/`1152`) and one in the far southeast corner (`1316`/`1336`).
- **Grid & Labels**:
  - Overlaid with a numbered Thomas Bros. page grid (green 4-digit numbers from `995` to `1336`).
  - Callout label on the right edge: `Witch - Guajito-Poomac[ha]`.
  - Labeled cities include `OCEANSIDE`, `VISTA`, `CARLSBAD`, `SAN MARCOS`, `ESCONDIDO`, `ENCINITAS`, `SOLANA BEACH`, `DEL MAR`, `POWAY`, `SANTEE`, `SAN DIEGO`, `EL CAJON`, `LA MESA`, `LEMON GROVE`, `CORONADO`, `NATIONAL CITY`, `CHULA VISTA`, and `IMPERIAL BEACH`, with `Pacific Ocean` labeled on the left.
