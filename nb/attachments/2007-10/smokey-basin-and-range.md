# Aerial View of Smoke-Filled Basin and Range Province (`smokey-basin-and-range.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken from a commercial passenger airplane window on October 27, 2007, looking out over the Basin and Range province veiled in thick, brownish-gray wildfire smoke after winds shifted inland from the Southern California wildfires.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Aircraft)**:
  - The curved white cowling of the jet engine nacelle occupies the lower-left foreground, showing rows of flush rivets, panel seams, and service latches, with the leading edge of the aircraft wing extending toward the lower right.
- **Landscape & Smoke**:
  - Below the aircraft, rugged desert mountain ranges with light-colored rocky crests and dry alluvial fans are partially visible in the lower left and center.
  - A dense, soupy blanket of tan and gray smoke fills the wide valleys and basins between the mountain ranges, obscuring the ground toward the right and middle distance and creating a hazy, diffused horizon line where only the highest distant snow-dusted peaks poke above the murk.
- **Sky**:
  - Above the thick brownish-orange smoke layer, the upper sky transitions to a clear blue at the top left, with one small, bright white cumulus cloud puff floating just above the lower-left mountain ridge.
