# Sunset Over the Arroyo Seco Parkway (CA-110) in South Pasadena (`sopas-110.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-contrast, golden-hour photograph taken from an overpass in South Pasadena on Thursday, October 18, 2007, looking down along the curving Arroyo Seco Parkway (State Route 110 / Pasadena Freeway) as the setting sun blazes through the trees.

## Scene Description
- **Freeway & Traffic**:
  - The multi-lane divided highway curves gracefully in an S-bend from the distant overpass in the center toward the bottom foreground.
  - On the left roadway (approaching traffic), roughly a dozen cars travel toward the viewer with their headlights illuminated and roofs glinting in the warm orange sunlight.
  - On the right roadway (receding traffic), a single dark sedan is visible in the foreground with its taillights glowing red.
  - Long, dramatic dark shadows from the roadside trees stretch diagonally across the sunlit asphalt lanes and central jersey barrier.
- **Lighting & Surroundings**:
  - Dense trees and foliage lining both sides of the sunken parkway are cast into deep black silhouette.
  - Just left of center, the low sun bursts through the tree canopy in a brilliant flare of yellow, gold, and orange, washing the sky and the highway surface in warm amber light.
