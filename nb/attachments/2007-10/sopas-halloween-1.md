# Craftsman House Decorated for Halloween in South Pasadena (`sopas-halloween-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A street-level photograph taken during an evening walk in South Pasadena on October 18, 2007, showing a large two-story Craftsman-style home decorated with Halloween cobwebs, a scarecrow, a ghost, and a front-yard graveyard.

## Architecture & Scene Description
- **House**:
  - A handsome two-story Craftsman house painted slate blue-gray with crisp white bargeboards, exposed rafters, and dark red-framed multi-pane windows.
  - Prominent river-rock (arroyo stone) piers support the front porch pergola and flank the concrete front steps at the sidewalk.
  - Warm interior lights glow through the lower-left casement windows beneath a shingled window hood.
- **Halloween Decorations**:
  - **Front Porch**: A tall scarecrow figure wearing a pointed hat, white shirt, and dark pants stands with arms outstretched in front of the wooden front door, accompanied by a smaller white sheet ghost figure sitting on the left side of the porch steps and dark garlands draped below the left window.
  - **Front Lawn & Entry**: Stretchy white fake cobwebs are draped over the river-rock lamp posts at the sidewalk steps and across the manicured bushes on the left and center-right lawn.
  - **Graveyard**: On the right side of the green lawn, four gray foam tombstones (one clearly inscribed `RIP`) are arranged around a scatter of white skeletal bones on the grass.
