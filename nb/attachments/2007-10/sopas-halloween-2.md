# Yellow House with Pumpkin-Headed Scarecrows in South Pasadena (`sopas-halloween-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken during an early evening walk in South Pasadena on October 18, 2007, showing a charming yellow cottage-style house behind a white picket fence with three life-sized pumpkin-headed scarecrow figures displayed in the front yard for Halloween.

  - A classic white picket fence runs across the entire width of the foreground along the concrete sidewalk, bordered by low flowering plants with orange blossoms.
  - Leafy green and autumn-tinted trees frame the house on the left and right.
