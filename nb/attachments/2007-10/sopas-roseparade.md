# Tournament House / Rose Parade Headquarters in Pasadena (`sopas-roseparade.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime exterior photograph of the Tournament House (Wrigley Mansion), the headquarters of the Pasadena Tournament of Roses Association, taken during an afternoon walk through Pasadena and South Pasadena on October 18, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Main Building**: A large, stately two-story white Italian Renaissance Revival mansion topped with a distinctive green-tiled hipped roof and a central rooftop balustrade.
- **Architectural Details**:
  - A prominent central covered portico with an arched entrance and a balustraded balcony above it.
  - Symmetrical rows of tall rectangular windows on both the first and second floors; the second-floor windows feature dark green wrought-iron or wooden window boxes/balconets.
  - Extended open-air loggias/pergolas flanking both the left and right wings of the house, with arched colonnades on the right and a white pergola beam structure on the left.
  - A tall white chimney rising on the right side of the roof.
- **Grounds & Landscaping**:
  - A wide, manicured expanse of bright green lawn stretches across the entire foreground, striped with long afternoon shadows and golden sunlight filtering from the left.
  - Several tall, slender palm trees and mature leafy shade trees stand across the lawn in the foreground and middle ground, framing the mansion.
  - Manicured hedges and flower beds line the base of the mansion's front terrace.
- **Lighting & Sky**: Bright, high-contrast late-afternoon sunlight coming from the left creates a warm glow on the grass and trees against a bright, washed-out sky.
