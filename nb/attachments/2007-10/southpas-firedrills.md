# Fire Department Ladder Practice in the Arroyo (`southpas-firedrills.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format daytime photograph showing multiple fire engines, an ambulance/rescue squad vehicle, and firefighters conducting ladder practice near a park and bridge in the Arroyo Seco area of South Pasadena on October 13, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Emergency Vehicles**:
  - **Left Foreground**: A red paramedic/rescue ambulance parked along the right side of a two-lane paved road, showing its rear doors with bright yellow-and-red high-visibility chevron stripes.
  - **Center-Left to Center**: Two large red fire engines (including a hook-and-ladder truck) parked along a side street bordering a grassy park. An aerial ladder is extended vertically into the tree canopy above the central truck.
  - **Right Background**: A fourth red fire engine parked further down the road, partially visible behind a large tree trunk.
- **Firefighters**: A group of roughly six to eight firefighters wearing yellow/tan turnout gear and helmets are gathered beside the central ladder truck.
- **Setting & Environment**:
  - A paved road with a double yellow centerline curves from the bottom left toward a concrete bridge railing on the far left, marked by yellow diamond traffic warning signs (intersection and curve ahead).
  - A green lawn bordered by short wooden/metal bollards and a large, dark-trunked shade tree occupies the right foreground.
  - Dense trees, including sycamores and tall palm trees, fill the background under an overcast sky.
