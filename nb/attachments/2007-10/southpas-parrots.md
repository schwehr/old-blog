# Wild Parrots Perched in Treetops in South Pasadena (`southpas-parrots.jpg`)

## Overview
A telephoto photograph showing three wild green parrots perched high on the bare upper branches of deciduous trees in South Pasadena, California, taken on October 13, 2007.

- **Parrots**:

  - **Center Parrot**: Perched in profile facing right on a thin vertical branch near the center of the frame, showing a bright green body and tapered tail.
  - **Right Parrot**: Perched near the far-right edge facing left, silhouetted against the pale sky.
