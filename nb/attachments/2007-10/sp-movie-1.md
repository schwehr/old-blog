# Spanish-Style House Used as a Film Location in South Pasadena (`sp-movie-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An early-evening exterior photograph of a single-story Spanish Colonial Revival residence in South Pasadena, California, which was being used as a movie filming location on October 16, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **House**:
  - A low-slung, white-stucco Spanish-style house featuring a red clay barrel-tile roof and a warm yellow/ochre painted trim band (frieze) running beneath the eaves.
  - Several tall, multi-pane casement and double-hung windows are protected by decorative black wrought-iron grilles (rejas) with scrollwork at the top.
  - On the left side of the facade, a dark wooden front door is recessed within a molded stone or concrete surround.
- **Landscaping & Fence**:
  - A dense bed of dark green English ivy covers the sloped front bank in the immediate foreground.
  - A black wrought-iron picket fence with pointed finials runs diagonally across the foreground, anchored on the left by a white-painted brick pillar topped with a pyramidal concrete cap.
  - Tall bird-of-paradise (*Strelitzia nicolai*) plants with broad, paddle-shaped green leaves rise in the center in front of the house, with tall palm trees and leafy shade trees visible in the background against a pale dusk sky.
