# Corner View of Film Location House in South Pasadena (`sp-movie-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A second early-evening photograph of the Spanish Colonial Revival house in South Pasadena used for movie filming, taken from the street corner showing its wrap-around corner window and illuminated tropical landscaping on October 16, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Architectural Features**:
  - The corner of the white-stucco single-story residence is shown at close range, highlighting the red clay tile roof and yellow/ochre band beneath the eaves.
  - On the far left, a square white-stucco chimney rises above the roofline, pierced near the top by small rectangular vent openings.
  - The corner of the building features a large wrap-around window enclosed by an ornate black wrought-iron grille.
  - To the right along the front wall is the carved stone-framed dark wood entrance door with decorative iron hardware, flanked on the right by a glowing warm-orange exterior lantern tucked into a trimmed shrub.
- **Landscaping & Lighting**:
  - A tall cluster of giant bird-of-paradise (*Strelitzia*) stalks rises directly in front of the corner window, dramatically lit from below at the base by a warm golden-orange landscape spotlight.
  - A manicured green lawn and a border of dark green ivy leaves occupy the foreground under a pale twilight sky.
