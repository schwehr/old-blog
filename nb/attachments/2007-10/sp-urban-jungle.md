# Electrical Transmission Tower Silhouetted at Sunset (`sp-urban-jungle.jpg`)

## Overview
A dusk photograph taken in South Pasadena on October 16, 2007, showing a tall steel lattice electrical utility tower and overhead power lines silhouetted against a warm golden-orange sunset sky ("The urban jungle").

## Scene Description
- **Transmission Tower & Power Lines**:
  - Positioned on the left side of the frame, a slender vertical steel lattice utility tower rises from a solid cylindrical base above the tree line.
  - Multiple crossarms and angled insulator assemblies project from the upper and middle sections of the tower.
  - Numerous high-voltage power cables fan out diagonally downward to the bottom left, while four upper lines stretch horizontally across the frame to the right, and a guy wire angles down toward the trees on the right.
- **Horizon & Sky**:
  - Along the bottom of the frame, a dense row of leafy trees and a pair of small palm trees on the far left are completely silhouetted in black.
  - The clear twilight sky transitions smoothly from a bright pale yellow glow near the lower-left horizon through warm amber-orange in the center to a muted slate-grey at the top.
