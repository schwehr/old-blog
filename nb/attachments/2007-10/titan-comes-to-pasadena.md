# Smoke-Filled Sky Over Pasadena During the Southern California Wildfires (`titan-comes-to-pasadena.jpg`)

## Overview
A dramatic photograph taken from the roof of Building 168 at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) in Pasadena, California, on October 23, 2007, showing the sun reduced to a small glowing red-orange dot through thick wildfire smoke—a scene the author compared to the hazy orange atmosphere of Saturn's moon Titan.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Smoke**:
  - Dense layers of wildfire smoke fill the entire sky, creating a surreal gradient that transitions from a bright golden-yellow and fiery orange near the lower horizon to a dark, brooding brownish-black across the upper half of the frame.
  - Billowing, streaky smoke clouds catch the diffused orange light across the middle of the image.
- **The Sun**:
  - Slightly above and to the right of center, the sun appears as a small, sharply defined bright white-and-red disc piercing through the dark upper smoke layer.
- **Foreground Silhouette**:
  - Along the bottom edge and rising on the right side, the foliage of trees and a low hillside ridge are silhouetted in solid black against the bright orange lower sky.
