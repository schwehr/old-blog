# Sonoran Desert Landscaping and Santa Catalina Mountains in Tucson (`tucson-morning.jpg`)

## Overview
A bright morning landscape photograph taken in Tucson, Arizona, on October 6, 2007, showing a desert garden path lined with cacti and succulents in the foreground and the rugged Santa Catalina Mountains rising under a clear blue sky in the background.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Desert Flora & Path**:
  - A curved, compacted-gravel walking path winds from the bottom right toward the left-center, bordered by a neat row of rounded light-tan desert boulders and stones.
  - In the left-center foreground stands a large, dense cluster of prickly pear cactus (*Opuntia*) with flat, oval green pads, flanked at the base by a bluish-green agave with sharp, pointed leaves and a pinkish-tan boulder in the bottom center.
  - On the far left, branching cholla cacti and a ribbed barrel cactus are visible.
  - Behind the prickly pear rises a tall saguaro cactus (*Carnegiea gigantea*) with several upward-curving arms, surrounded by feathery green palo verde and mesquite trees. Two additional slender saguaro cacti are visible in the right middle ground.
- **Background Mountains & Sky**:
  - The steep, rocky ridges and canyons of the Santa Catalina Mountains stretch across the horizon, bathed in morning sunlight.
  - Above the central mountain peak floats a small cluster of bright white cumulus clouds in an otherwise deep azure-blue sky.
