# Sunrise View of the Mountains Behind a Tucson Neighborhood (`tucson-mtns.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format early-morning photograph taken near the Phoenix Mars Lander Spacecraft Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona, on October 5, 2007, showing the rugged silhouette of the Santa Catalina Mountains rising behind a quiet residential neighborhood at dawn.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Neighborhood**:
  - A paved residential street curves across the lower left corner, bordered by chain-link and masonry fences, dry grassy verges, and desert trees.
  - A red-and-white triangular `YIELD` sign topped with a green street-name blade stands near the left intersection, with a small `FOR SALE` yard sign visible beside the fence to its right.
  - Low-roofed, single-story tan stucco houses and utility poles with overhead wires occupy the shaded middle ground.
- **Background Mountains & Sky**:
  - The jagged, multi-peaked profile of the Santa Catalina Mountains spans the entire width of the horizon, rendered in soft, hazy blue-grey morning tones.
  - A long, flat band of grey-white stratus clouds hovers just above the central and right mountain peaks against a bright, pale dawn sky.
