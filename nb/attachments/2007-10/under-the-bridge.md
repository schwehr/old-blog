# Colorado Street Bridge Arch Framing Vista del Arroyo in Pasadena (`under-the-bridge.jpg`)

## Overview
A sunny late-afternoon photograph taken from below in the Arroyo Seco in Pasadena, California, on October 18, 2007, looking up through the concrete arch of the historic Colorado Street Bridge toward the Spanish Colonial Revival tower of the Vista del Arroyo building (U.S. Court of Appeals).

## Scene Description
- **Colorado Street Bridge**:
  - The massive, curving reinforced-concrete arch and open spandrel columns of the historic 1913 bridge dominate the foreground and upper frame, spanning from the bottom left to the bottom right.
  - Along the top of the bridge deck, the ornate concrete balustrade, projecting viewing alcove (refuge bay) on the upper right, and black metal safety fencing are silhouetted against the clear blue sky.
- **Framed Building & Trees**:
  - Directly framed inside the curve of the main concrete arch stands the multi-story, cream-and-terracotta Vista del Arroyo building, crowned by its signature octagonal domed bell tower and red-tiled roofs, glowing warmly in the late-afternoon sun.
  - Lush green sycamore and shade trees in the arroyo rise along the lower left and center below the building.
