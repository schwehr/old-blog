# NOAA Trajectory Model Map for M/V Cosco Busan Oil Spill (`CoscoBusanSpillFlowMovie.png`)

## Overview
This image is a still frame from a NOAA trajectory model animation (`noaa-sf-spill.mov`) illustrating the modeled distribution of spilled fuel oil in San Francisco Bay following the *M/V Cosco Busan* allision with the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge on November 7, 2007.

## Map & Model Details
- **Geographic Coverage**: The map depicts San Francisco Bay and the Golden Gate strait, with landmasses rendered in light tan/peach and water in white.
- **Labeled Locations**:
  - **San Francisco**: Marked with a small dot on the northern tip of the San Francisco peninsula.
  - **Treasure Island**: Marked with a dot in the central bay, connected to Yerba Buena Island just south of it.
  - **Oakland**: Marked with a dot on the East Bay shoreline.
- **Release Track Line**: A grey line segment with arrows at both ends lies in the channel between San Francisco and Yerba Buena / Treasure Island, labeled with a pointer line reading `"Release Track Line"`.
- **Modeled Oil Distribution**: Clusters of black stippled dots represent the modeled oil slick at 16:00 on November 7, 2007:
  - A dense plume of black dots extends westward through the Golden Gate strait and fans out into the Pacific Ocean just west-northwest of San Francisco.
  - Smaller clusters of black dots trace along the northeastern shoreline of the San Francisco waterfront (Embarcadero) and around the western perimeter of Yerba Buena and Treasure Island.
- **Coordinate Grid**: Faint latitude and longitude grid lines cross the map with small coordinate labels along the left margin (`37°45'N`, `37°50'N`, `37°55'N`, `38°00'N`) and bottom margin (`122°35'W`, `122°30'W`, `122°25'W`, `122°20'W`).

## Visible Text & Labels
- **Top Left Header**:
  - `*M/V Cosco Busan* Nov. 7, 2007 08:30`
  - `Spill Hour 0 - 32`
  - Timestamp box (white background): `11/07/2007 16:00`
- **Top Right Explanatory Box** (bordered white rectangle with black serif text):
  - **Title**: `NOAA Trajectory Model Run for Cosco Busan Incident`
  - **Paragraph 1**: `This map shows modeled oil distributions based on a 58,000 gallon spill beginning 0830 on Nov 7.`
  - **Paragraph 2**: `The model is based on over-flight observations, but does not represent actual observed oil. Strong convergence lines and complex wind patterns are not modeled. The model will tend to overestimate oil coverage because convergence zones collect oil.`
- **Bottom Right Logo**: The circular blue-and-white NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) emblem.
