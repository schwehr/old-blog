# "Stranger on the Bridge" Maritime Training DVD Cover (`StrangeronBridgeDVD.jpg`)

## Overview
This image is the promotional cover artwork for the maritime safety training DVD titled *"Stranger on the Bridge"*, produced by The American Club (American Steamship Owners Mutual Protection and Indemnity Association) to educate ship deck officers and crew on bridge resource management and working effectively with harbor pilots.

## Scene & Artwork Description
- **Atmosphere & Palette**: The illustration features a dark, moody, monochromatic blue-green night or heavy fog scene over calm water.
- **Vessel**: In the lower-middle ground, a dark silhouette of a commercial cargo ship or bulk carrier is seen in port-bow profile moving across calm,rippled water.
  - A bright white masthead light glows near the bow mast.
  - A second bright white light shines atop the aft superstructure/bridge mast, accompanied by a glowing red port navigation light on the side of the bridge wing.
- **Foreground**: Dark, wet coastal rocks or a breakwater jetty line the bottom edge of the frame, emphasizing the close proximity to navigational hazards.
- **Background**: Behind the ship, a large, eerie, pale greenish-white cloud of fog or mist billows upward into the dark sky, serving as a backdrop for the title text. Faint dark land silhouettes appear along the horizon behind the vessel.

## Visible Text & Logos
- **Title Text** (top half, rendered in large, slightly irregular/hand-styled white capital letters):
  - `STRANGER`
  - `ON THE`
  - `BRIDGE`
- **Bottom Center Logo**:
  - Red stacked capital text on the left: `THE AMERICAN CLUB`
  - Beside it on the right is a blue pennant/burgee emblem featuring a white five-pointed star in the center and a blue-and-white ribbon below bearing the founding year `1917`.
