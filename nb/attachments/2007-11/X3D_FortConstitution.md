# Panoramic Waterfront Photograph of Fort Constitution and Portsmouth Harbor Light (`X3D_FortConstitution.jpg`)

## Overview
This wide, horizontal panoramic photograph shows the shoreline of Fort Constitution and the Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse (New Castle, New Hampshire) viewed from the water. As described in the accompanying blog post, this image (`FortConstitution_429.JPG`, taken by Matt Plumlee) was used as an `ImageTexture` mapped onto a 3D billboard polygon in X3D for digital charting experiments at UNH CCOM/JHC.

## Scene Description
- **Right Side (Lighthouse & Fort Walls)**:
  - A tall, cylindrical white lighthouse tower (**Portsmouth Harbor Light**) with a black lantern room and gallery deck stands prominently on the right.
  - Immediately to the left of the lighthouse is a white building with a pitched brown/red roof and a small dormer window.
  - Surrounding the base of the lighthouse and extending to the right and left are the historic grey granite walls of **Fort Constitution**, featuring regular vertical rectangular embrasures (openings) along the stone wall.
- **Center**:
  - A long, low brick/red-roofed structure and granite fort wall sit behind a rocky dark Riprap shoreline.
  - Deciduous green trees rise behind the fort walls against an overcast, pale grey-blue sky.
- **Left Side (USCG Station Portsmouth Harbor)**:
  - On the left stands a light-colored, two-story rectangular building (the U.S. Coast Guard station building) with rows of rectangular windows, a flat roof, an elevated lookout tower on its far-left corner, and a tall lattice radio/radar tower rising behind it.
  - A cluster of dark green trees separates the Coast Guard building on the left from the historic fort walls in the center.
- **Foreground**: A narrow band of calm grey harbor water runs along the bottom edge of the image in front of the dark, rocky shoreline.
