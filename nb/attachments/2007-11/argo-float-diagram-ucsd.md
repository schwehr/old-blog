# Cutaway Technical Diagram of an Argo Profiling Float (`argo-float-diagram-ucsd.gif`)

## Overview
This image is an annotated cutaway diagram (from UCSD) showing the internal components and external sensors of an autonomous oceanographic **Argo float**, used globally to monitor ocean temperature and salinity profiles.

## Diagram & Component Details
The float is depicted vertically as a yellow cylindrical pressure hull with a rounded black top cap, a horizontal circular damping ring, and an external bladder assembly at the bottom.

- **Left Side Dimension Marker**:
  - A vertical black dimension line with arrows at both ends spans the main body of the cylinder (from the top cap shoulder to the bottom shroud), labeled **`~110cm`**.
- **Right Side Component Labels** (from top to bottom, with black leader lines pointing to each part):
  1. **`Satellite Antenna`**: A thin, vertical black whip antenna extending straight up from the left side of the top cap.
  2. **`Profiling module`**: A perforated silver/grey cylindrical sensor cage mounted vertically on the right side of the top cap.
  3. **`Temperature Probe`**: A small sensor element at the base of the profiling module on the top cap.
  4. **`Circuit boards & satellite transmitter`**: Vertical green printed circuit boards housed just inside the upper section of the cylinder beneath the black top dome.
  5. **`Stability Disk`**: A flat, horizontal grey circular disk/collar mounted around the exterior of the upper hull.
  6. **`Foam support`**: A white/grey structural block supporting the electronics at the upper-left interior of the hull.
  7. **`Gear motor`**: A brass/gold-colored cylindrical motor assembly mounted in the upper-middle interior of the hull.
  8. **`Single stroke pump`**: A vertical grey shaft extending downward from the gear motor along the central axis of the cylinder.
  9. **`Battery`**: Two dark brown/maroon rectangular battery packs flanking the central pump shaft in the lower-middle section of the hull.
  10. **`Hydraulic pump (piston)`**: A dark grey piston head inside the central cylinder just above the red hydraulic fluid chamber.
  11. **`Hydraulic fluid`**: A bright red column of fluid at the bottom of the internal central cylinder, connected via narrow red passages at the base.
  12. **`Hydraulic bladder`**: A dark red/brown flexible external bladder located inside the protective yellow shroud at the very bottom of the float, which inflates or deflates to change the float's volume and buoyancy.
