# 24-Hour AIS Vessel Tracks at the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge (`cosco-allision-1.png`)

## Overview
This image is a screenshot from Google Earth displaying 24 hours of Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel track lines passing under the western span of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge on November 7, 2007—the day the container ship *M/V Cosco Busan* (MMSI `477968900`) allided with the Delta tower fender of the bridge.

## Map & Track Details
- **Base Imagery**: Satellite/aerial view of San Francisco Bay water (dark teal blue) crossed diagonally from bottom-left to top-right by the western suspension span of the **San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge**. A central bridge tower pier (the Delta tower) is visible just below the center of the image.
- **AIS Vessel Tracks**:
  - Dozens of thin white polylines crisscross the frame, representing 24 hours of commercial vessel traffic transiting beneath the Bay Bridge spans.
  - Heavy concentrations of tracks pass through the main navigation channels on the north-east (upper-right) and south-west (lower-left) sides of the bridge towers.
  - Near the center, just above and touching the Delta tower pier, one erratic white track hooks sharply across the bridge pier structure and loops back down to the right, marking the path of the *Cosco Busan* allision.

## Visible Text & Labels
- **Center Banner**: A prominent white rectangular box with bold black sans-serif text reading **`Not for official use`** is positioned just above the center of the bridge span.
