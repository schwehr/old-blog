# Isolated AIS Track of M/V Cosco Busan Bay Bridge Allision (`cosco-allision-2.png`)

## Overview
This image is an oblique 3D Google Earth screenshot showing the isolated Automatic Identification System (AIS) track of the container ship *M/V Cosco Busan* (MMSI `477968900`) as it struck the Delta tower of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge on November 7, 2007.

## Map & Track Details
- **Geographic Features**:
  - **Bottom Left**: The piers and waterfront of **San Francisco** (Embarcadero) where the western suspension span of the Bay Bridge begins.
  - **Center to Upper Center**: **Yerba Buena Island** (labeled in light blue text: `Yerba Buena Island`) and the artificial polygon of **Treasure Island** jutting north into the bay, with the eastern span of the Bay Bridge extending off to the right.
  - **Bay Bridge Western Span**: Runs diagonally from the San Francisco waterfront (bottom-left) to Yerba Buena Island (center).
- **Vessel Track & Impact Highlight**:
  - A single thin white polyline traces the *Cosco Busan*'s path coming from the right (East Bay / Oakland), curving southwestward toward the Bay Bridge, crossing directly over one of the bridge towers (the Delta tower), and continuing northwestward into the central bay before ending.
  - A bright **yellow circle/oval** highlights the exact point where the white AIS track intersects the Delta tower pier of the western Bay Bridge span.
  - Two small white elongated shapes (large anchored ships captured in the underlying satellite imagery) are visible in the lower-right waters south of the bridge.

## Visible Text & Annotations
- **Island Label**: `Yerba Buena Island` (light cyan text over the island).
- **Disclaimer Text**: Bold black sans-serif text to the right of the yellow circle reads **`Not for official use`**.
