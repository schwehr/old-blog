# Full Port-Side View of Damaged Container Ship M/V Cosco Busan (`cosco-busan-uscg-full.jpg`)

## Overview
This U.S. Coast Guard (USCG) photograph shows a full port-bow three-quarter view of the container ship *M/V Cosco Busan* anchored in foggy San Francisco Bay after alliding with the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge on November 7, 2007.

## Vessel & Scene Details
- **Vessel Exterior & Markings**:
  - The large container ship has a dark navy-blue/black hull with a red lower waterline boot-top and an anchor chain dropping straight down into the water from the port hawsepipe.
  - On the flared port bow, a white stylized circular emblem (`H` logo inside three horizontal white stripes) is painted forward of the ship's name **`COSCO BUSAN`** in white capital letters.
  - Along the midships port hull, large white block letters spell **`HANJIN`**.
  - White draft marks and bulbous bow / thruster symbols are painted near the waterline at the bow.
- **Hull Damage**:
  - Along the port side, just below and slightly forward of the `"HAN"` in `"HANJIN"`, a long, dark, ragged horizontal gash and crumpled indentation is clearly visible in the hull plating just above the red waterline.
  - An accommodation ladder / gangway slopes down along the hull over the second `"N"` in `"HANJIN"`.
- **Cargo & Superstructure**:
  - The deck is stacked five to six tiers high with colorful intermodal shipping containers (predominantly light blue Hanjin containers, along with red/brown, white, and grey containers).
  - A white foremast stands on the forecastle, and the white bridge/accommodation superstructure rises aft behind the container stacks.
- **Environment**: The ship floats on calm grey-green water under a hazy, overcast sky.
