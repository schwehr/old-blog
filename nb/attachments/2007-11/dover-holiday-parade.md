# Bicyclists in Santa Hats at the Dover Holiday Parade (`dover-holiday-parade.jpg`)

## Overview
This photograph captures a group of riders on customized lowrider, chopper, and vintage stingray-style bicycles wearing red-and-white Santa Claus hats during the annual Dover Holiday Parade in Dover, New Hampshire (November 25, 2007).

## Scene Description
- **Parade Riders (Foreground & Midground)**:
  - Six riders (the "biker gang" joked about in the blog post) are pedaling left-to-right across a paved two-lane street marked with a double yellow center line and a white shoulder line.
  - Every rider wears a festive red Santa hat with white trim and winter jackets/gloves:
    - **Far Left**: A bearded man in a black jacket, blue jeans, and white gloves riding a dark blue lowrider bicycle with high "ape-hanger" handlebars and a tall sissy bar backrest.
    - **Front Left-Center**: A rider in a grey hoodie, brown vest, tan pants, and black gloves riding a red three-wheeled lowrider tricycle with whitewall tires and chrome fenders.
    - **Center**: A woman in a white-and-olive jacket and blue jeans riding a metallic green stingray-style bicycle with a white banana seat and high handlebars.
    - **Behind Left/Center**: Two additional riders in dark jackets and Santa hats, one with a tall white padded sissy bar backrest.
    - **Center-Right**: A man in a tan/blue jacket riding a dark lowrider bicycle.
    - **Far Right**: A rider in a red-and-black buffalo plaid flannel jacket and blue jeans riding an orange stingray bicycle with high handlebars and a banana seat.
- **Background**:
  - A crowd of spectators—adults and children bundled in colorful winter coats (orange, bright pink, blue, black) and hats—lines the far curb on the right.
  - Parked cars and brick commercial/town buildings of downtown Dover are visible in the sunlit late-afternoon background.
