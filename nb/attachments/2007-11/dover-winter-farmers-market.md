# Chainsaw Ice Carving at the Dover Winter Farmers Market (`dover-winter-farmers-market.jpg`)

## Overview
This photograph shows two men carving an intricate ice sculpture outdoors with a chainsaw during the Dover Winter Farmers Market held at the Atlantic Culinary Academy / McIntosh College cooking school in Dover, New Hampshire (November 17, 2007).

## Scene Description
- **Ice Sculpture (Center)**:
  - A large, translucent block of ice sits atop a wooden pallet raised on black plastic crates.
  - The ice is being sculpted into a rounded arch or medallion containing a curved bird-like figure (resembling a swan or turkey/cornucopia motif) with feather-like textures along the upper arch and a carved spiral wave scroll at the lower right base.
  - Fine white ice spray/dust rises into the air from the top of the sculpture where the chainsaw blade is cutting.
- **Carvers**:
  - **Left Foreground**: A man seen from behind wearing a crisp white long-sleeved chef's coat, a white chef's toque (hat), safety glasses, and work gloves, holding a blue-handled tool at his right hip while observing the cut.
  - **Right Background**: A second man wearing a black baseball cap, safety glasses, and a heavy black-and-grey winter jacket leans in from the right, operating a chainsaw whose guide bar (` Oregon` / red-and-silver bar tip) is visible through the translucent upper-left arch of the ice block.
- **Setting**:
  - In the background is the grey stucco exterior wall of a building with two large arched multi-pane windows and a strip of green grass along the foundation.
  - An orange traffic cone stands at the far bottom-left edge, and bare autumn tree branches with buds frame the right side of the image.
