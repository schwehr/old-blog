# First Snowfall of the Season Through an Office Window (`first-snow.jpg`)

## Overview
This vertical photograph, taken on November 20, 2007, looks out through an office window at the first winter snowfall of the season blanketing brush, grass, and woods in New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**:
  - A sloped, unmowed field or clearing of tall grasses, weeds, and low brush is dusted with a fresh, patchy layer of white wet snow. Greenish blades of grass and brown dried stems poke through the snow cover.
- **Background Woods**:
  - Behind the brushy clearing stands a dense grove of tall eastern white pines (with dark green needles) mixed with bare deciduous trees, some still holding scattered brown and golden autumn leaves.
  - The sky above the treetops on the upper right is a bright, solid overcast white.
- **Window Reflection**:
  - Near the top center/left of the frame, faint horizontal yellowish-white bands of indoor fluorescent ceiling lights are reflected in the glass of the office window through which the photo was taken.
