# Furuno NavNet 3D Marine Chartplotter Display Comparison (`furuno-navnet3d.png`)

## Overview
This composite image consists of two vertically stacked screenshots captured from a *Power Cruising* video demonstration (referenced via Panbo) of the **Furuno NavNet 3D** commercial marine navigation and 3D chartplotting system. In the blog post, Kurt compares its 3D bathy/chart visualization capabilities to research systems developed at UNH CCOM/JHC (such as GeoNav, GeoZui3D, and GeoZui4D).

## Interface & Display Details

### Top Panel: 3D Bathymetric & Hazard View
- **3D Perspective Chart**: Looking forward along a straight navigation channel (Government Cut, Miami, Florida) rendered in 3D perspective.
- **Color Coding**:
  - The dredged central shipping channel is shown in light grey/white with black range/channel lines and depth soundings.
  - Deeper surrounding waters are shaded in gradients of royal blue and dark blue.
  - Shallow shoals, jetties, and land structures flanking the channel entrance are highlighted in bright orange-red overlays over grey satellite imagery.
- **Vessel & Aids to Navigation**:
  - A 3D red boat icon with a heading vector sits in the middle of the channel.
  - Green and red channel buoys/markers line the edges of the cut.
- **Overlays**:
  - **Bottom Left**: A translucent grey data box displaying navigation readouts: `0.6 COG`, `5.8 SOG`, and depth `41.3 DFT ft`.
  - **Bottom Right**: White serif watermark logo reading **`POWER CRUISING`**.

### Bottom Panel: 3D Aerial Photo-Textured Bathymetry View
- **3D Perspective Chart**: Shows the same Miami harbor channel entrance from a slightly higher/wider 3D perspective with high-resolution aerial/satellite photography draped over the 3D terrain and underwater channel walls.
- **Channel & Vessel**:
  - The steep, dredged walls of the shipping channel ("GOVERNMENT CUT") are clearly visible as 3D troughs cutting between the coastal islands (Fisher Island on the left, Miami Beach on the right).
  - The orange/red 3D vessel icon travels along the center of the channel with a forward course line extending ahead toward the inner harbor junction.
- **Overlays**:
  - **Top Yellow Status Bar**: A yellow banner across the top of the screen reading `Internal DEMO mode` on the left, with small status icons on the right.
  - **Top Right Coordinate Box**: A translucent grey box displaying latitude and longitude in white digits:
    - `25°45.1689' N`
    - `80°08.8810' W`
