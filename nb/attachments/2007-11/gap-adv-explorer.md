# G.A.P. Adventures Promotional Photo of Expedition Cruise Ship M/S Explorer (`gap-adv-explorer.jpg`)

## Overview
This promotional banner image from G.A.P. Adventures shows the polar expedition cruise ship **M/S *Explorer*** moored alongside sea ice in Antarctica under a clear blue sky. The blog post includes this image while reporting on the sinking of the *M/S Explorer* in the Bransfield Strait, Antarctica, on November 23, 2007, after striking submerged ice (with all 154 passengers and crew rescued by the Norwegian vessel *M/S Nordnorge*).

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Subject**:
  - Standing on the flat snow-covered sea ice in the left-foreground is an expedition guide or passenger facing the camera, wearing a black knit beanie, dark sunglasses, a bright red-and-black polar parka, black snow pants, and dark gloves, with a walking pole or tripod leg visible at the bottom.
- **Vessel (*M/S Explorer*)**:
  - Behind the person, stretching from center-left to the right side of the frame, is the ice-strengthened expedition cruise ship **M/S *Explorer*** seen in starboard-bow profile against the ice edge.
  - The ship features a bright red hull with a thick white stripe below the main deck line and the ship's name **`EXPLORER`** painted in dark letters on the white bow band, above a white bow thruster symbol.
  - Above the hull is a multi-deck white superstructure with rows of rectangular cabin windows, enclosed white lifeboats hanging from davits along the boat deck, a white radar/communications mast atop the bridge, and a red-and-blue funnel with a tall black exhaust pipe aft.
  - A gangway ladder is deployed from a midships side door down onto the flat sea ice, where three small figures in red and yellow parkas stand near the hull.
- **Environment**: Flat white and pale blue sea ice stretches across the foreground, meeting a cloudless, deep azure blue sky.
