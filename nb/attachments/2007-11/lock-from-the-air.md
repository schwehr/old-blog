# Aerial View of the Lockport Lock and Dam Near Chicago (`lock-from-the-air.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken through an airplane window just west of Chicago Midway Airport, showing a navigation lock and dam structure along a canalized waterway (the Chicago Sanitary and Ship Canal / Des Plaines River corridor) with the blue cowling of the aircraft's jet engine in the left foreground.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Aircraft Engine)**: The left third of the frame is occupied by the curved blue-and-silver nacelle intake cowling of a commercial airliner's wing-mounted turbofan engine (with a hint of orange/red Southwest Airlines livery at the bottom left).
- **Waterway & Lock Complex**:
  - Running diagonally from the bottom center toward the upper right is a straight, engineered canal channel bordered by narrow levees.
  - In the lower-center portion of the canal sits a lock and dam complex: a narrow concrete lock chamber on the right side of the channel, a wider dam/powerhouse structure with a reddish-brown roof spanning the left side of the channel, and a line of white mooring cells/piers extending upstream into the wider forebay.
  - Parallel to the left (west) of the canal is a natural, tree-lined river channel (the Des Plaines River), with brown late-autumn fields and dark wooded or marshy patches between the waterways.
- **Surrounding Landscape & Infrastructure**:
  - To the right (east) of the canal, railroad tracks and highways curve parallel to the waterway alongside open brown fields, industrial lots, and scattered buildings.
  - In the upper right distance, two highway/rail bridges cross the canal and river near industrial storage yards.
