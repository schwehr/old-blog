# Aerial View of Stricken Cruise Ship M/S Explorer Surrounded by Antarctic Sea Ice (`msexplorer-antartica-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A wider aerial photograph taken by the Chilean Navy from an aircraft (with the dark window frame visible in the bottom-right corner) showing the expedition cruise ship *M/S Explorer* capsizing on its starboard side amidst an expansive field of sea ice in Antarctica on November 23, 2007.

## Scene Description
- **Stricken Vessel**:
  - Positioned in the lower-center foreground, the *M/S Explorer* lies heeled sharply onto its starboard side, exposing the length of its port red hull, a dark waterline smudge along the midships bottom, and the white stripe bearing **"EXPLORER"** near the bow.
  - The white superstructure, bridge windows, empty lifeboat davits, and red-and-black funnel tilt away from the camera into a dark pool of open water on the ship's starboard side.
- **Ice Field & Horizon**:
  - A vast sheet of fractured white pack ice and pancake floes surrounds the ship in the foreground and middle distance.
  - Beyond the edge of the pack ice in the upper third of the image lies dark gray-blue open ocean under an overcast gray polar sky, with a prominent white iceberg visible in the upper left distance and a smaller flat-topped ice floe near the right horizon.
