# Google Earth Visualization of the San Francisco Bay Oil Spill (`oilspill-googleearthblog.jpg`)

## Overview
A screenshot from Google Earth (originally published on the Google Earth Blog) displaying KML overlays of the November 2007 *M/V Cosco Busan* oil spill in San Francisco Bay, featuring shaded red oil-slick polygons, placemark icons, and an open information balloon with an aerial photograph of an oil slick off Angel Island.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Geographic View**: A top-down satellite view of the San Francisco Peninsula, Golden Gate, Marin Headlands, Angel Island, Treasure Island, and parts of the East Bay.
- **Oil Spill Polygons & Shorelines**:
  - Semi-transparent dark red polygons mark areas of floating oil slicks outside the Golden Gate along the Marin coast, around Angel Island, in the central bay north of Treasure Island, and along the San Francisco waterfront.
  - Bright red lines trace impacted shoreline segments along Crissy Field, Fort Mason, and the eastern San Francisco piers.
- **Placemarks & Labels**:
  - `Angel Island Closed` (blue teardrop pin and red warning triangle icon on Angel Island)
  - `Video: Cleanup at Kirby Cove` (yellow video/TV screen icon and red warning triangle on the Marin Headlands)
  - `Cleanup: Oil skimmer at work` (blue water-waves icon near the Golden Gate Bridge)
  - `Command Center` (green hiker/worker icons and blue pin at Fort Mason / Crissy Field)
  - `Ship hits Bay Bridge tower` / `Ship strikes br...` (blue pin at the western span of the San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge)
  - `Swim portion...` (orange pin near Alcatraz / central bay)
  - `Oi...` (red warning triangle with exclamation mark in the East Bay)
  - `Ship at anchor` (blue teardrop pin in the southern anchorage off San Francisco)
