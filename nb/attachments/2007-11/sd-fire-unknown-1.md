# Elk Standing in a River Before a Wall of Forest Fire (`sd-fire-unknown-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A widely circulated wildfire photograph (forwarded via email during the 2007 San Diego fires, originally depicting the famous 2000 Bitterroot Valley wildfire in Montana by John McColgan) showing two elk standing in a shallow river while a towering crown fire engulfs the conifer forest on the hillside behind them.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (River & Wildlife)**:
  - A calm, shallow river or wide stream curves from the center background toward the bottom center/left, bordered by dark silhouetted riparian bushes and trees on both banks.
  - Two dark silhouetted elk stand in the shallow water near the middle of the river, facing toward the left/camera as bright yellow-orange firelight reflects off the rippled water surface around them.
- **Background (Burning Forest)**:
  - Across the entire upper two-thirds of the frame, a steep forested hillside is consumed by intense orange and bright yellow flames.
  - Tall, dark silhouettes of pine/fir trees stand out against the glowing wall of fire and smoke, with the brightest yellow-white blaze concentrated just beyond the bend in the river.
