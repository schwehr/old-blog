# Nighttime View of Wildfires Burning in the Mountains Above San Diego (`sd-fire-unknown.jpg`)

## Overview
A dramatic nighttime photograph shared during the October/November 2007 Southern California / San Diego wildfires, showing flames and glowing smoke plumes engulfing mountain ridges immediately behind a densely lit urban valley.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (City Lights)**: Across the lower third of the image, thousands of bright white, yellow, and amber street and building lights form a dense grid across a valley floor and lower foothills, with a dark tree silhouette visible at the far right edge.
- **Midground & Background (Burning Mountains)**:
  - Dark silhouetted mountain peaks and ridgelines rise directly behind the city lights.
  - Intense orange-yellow flames erupt along multiple fronts: a bright, concentrated blaze on the right-hand slope, a glowing fire line along the base of the central valley where the mountains meet the city, a tall plume of fire and illuminated orange smoke billowing from the crest of the central mountain peak, and another patch of fire high on the far left ridge.
- **Sky**: The night sky above the mountains is filled with thick, dark brown and rust-orange smoke lit from below by the wildfires.
