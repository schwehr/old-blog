# Damaged Fender at the Base of the San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge Tower (`sf-bay-bridge-fender.png`)

## Overview
A still frame captured from a U.S. Coast Guard assessment video following the November 7, 2007 *M/V Cosco Busan* allision, showing close-up structural damage to the protective fender system at the base of the Delta tower pier of the San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge.

## Scene Description
- **Damaged Fender Structure**:
  - The left and center portions of the image show the corner of the bridge pier's protective fender structure, where concrete and timber/steel fendering have been crushed, sheared, and torn open by the impact of the container ship's hull.
  - Broken, splintered vertical and diagonal pilings and twisted structural beams hang down into the shadowed cavity beneath the upper concrete cap.
- **Intact Fender Section**:
  - To the right of the impact zone, two tiered rows of vertical timber/composite fender pilings with rectangular slots between them remain upright along the side of the pier.
- **Water Foreground**:
  - In the bottom quarter of the frame, choppy greenish-blue bay water with whitecaps and foam flows past the base of the damaged bridge fender.
