# USCG Operational Overview Map of the Cosco Busan Oil Spill Response (`sf-spill-ops-maps.png`)

## Overview
A GIS operational overview map produced by the U.S. Coast Guard / Unified Command (dated November 18, 2007) for the *M/V Cosco Busan* oil spill in San Francisco Bay, showing operational branches, lettered shoreline divisions, shoreline oiling severity, protection strategies, and response staging locations.

## Map Layout & Geographic Coverage
- **Extent**: Covers the central and northern San Francisco Bay Area, including the Marin County coast (Point Reyes/Bolinas to Golden Gate and San Pablo Bay), Contra Costa County (Pinole Point to Richmond), Alameda County (Berkeley, Oakland, Alameda), San Francisco County, and northern San Mateo County.
- **Operational Branches (Large Bold Black Labels)**:
  - `MARIN COUNTY BRANCH`
  - `CONTRA COSTA COUNTY BRANCH`
  - `ALAMEDA COUNTY BRANCH`
  - `SAN FRANCISCO COUNTY BRANCH`
  - `SAN MATEO COUNTY BRANCH`
- **Shoreline Divisions**: Black boundary lines perpendicular to the coast divide the shoreline into lettered operational divisions (`Division A` through `Division Z`, including `Division L`, `Division M`, `Division N`, `Division O`, `Division P`, `Division Q`, `Division R`, `Division S`, `Division T`, `Division U`, `Division X`, `Division Y`, `Division Z`, and `Division A`–`K`).

## Legend & Symbology (Bottom Left Box)
- **Incident Markers**:
  - **Red ship outline (`M/V Cosco Busan`)**: Located at anchorage in the East Bay (`Division C` south of the Bay Bridge) and near the bridge.
  - **Red circled X (`Spill Incident`)**: Marked at the San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge (`Division E`).
- **Points of Interest**:
  - `S` in beige circle: `Central Supply and Decon` / `Alameda Staging` / `MSRC Richmond Staging`
  - `D` in white square: `Alameda Decom`
  - Blue-and-white diagonal square: `ICP` (Incident Command Post at Fort Mason/San Francisco waterfront)
  - Green walking person icon: `work_crews` (distributed along Marin, San Francisco, Angel Island, and East Bay beaches)
  - Black circle: `Beaches_of_concern`
  - Three red diamonds (`♦♦♦`): `Protection Strategies` (boom deployments at sensitive marshes)
  - Pink dot: `County Seats`
  - Solid black line: `Operational Divisions`
- **Shoreline Oiling (Color-Coded Coastal Lines)**:
  - **Blue**: `No Oil Observed`
  - **Green**: `Very Light`
  - **Yellow**: `Light`
  - **Orange**: `Moderate`
  - **Red**: `Heavy`
