# Saint Lawrence Seaway Vessel Traffic Services (VTS) Control Center (`sls-vts-kam-chin.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph (credited to Kam Chin, via the U.S. DOT Volpe Center and gCaptain) showing a watchstander working at a console inside a Saint Lawrence Seaway Vessel Traffic Services (VTS) operations center (near Eisenhower Lock).

## Scene Description
- **Operator & Console (Foreground)**:
  - A male VTS operator wearing a dark burgundy/maroon long-sleeved shirt sits in a high-backed dark office chair at a curved, wood-trimmed control desk, writing notes with a pen on paper forms illuminated by a black desk lamp.
  - Built into the angled console in front of him are a control panel with rows of illuminated pushbuttons and indicator lights, a gooseneck microphone, three recessed flat-screen monitors (one showing tabular vessel traffic schedules/data and two displaying electronic nautical charts of the seaway channel), a white keyboard, a white mouse on a mousepad, and a black multi-line push-button telephone console in the bottom right corner.
- **Wall Displays & CCTV Monitors (Background)**:
  - **Left Wall Rack**: A floor-to-ceiling equipment rack houses a grid of monochrome/color CCTV monitors showing live camera feeds of lock chambers and approach walls, topped by schematic lock status indicator boards (with yellow lettering partially reading `...ENHOWER LOCK...` for Eisenhower Lock). Two round analog wall clocks hang above the rack, and three monthly calendar pages hang vertically to its right.
  - **Right Wall**: Three large black-bezel wall-mounted display screens show wide-area electronic chart displays (ECDIS/VTS map views) tracking vessel movements along consecutive reaches of the Saint Lawrence River channel.
