# Optical Glory and Brocken Spectre Around an Airplane Shadow on Clouds (`sundog-maybe.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken from a passenger airplane window looking down at a smooth deck of clouds, capturing a bright circular rainbow-colored optical **glory** (and **Brocken spectre**) centered around the dark silhouette shadow of the aircraft.

## Scene Description
- **Aircraft Shadow (Brocken Spectre)**: At the center of the image, the soft-edged dark gray shadow of a commercial jetliner (showing its fuselage, swept wings, and tail fin oriented horizontally toward the right) is cast directly onto the cloud tops below.
- **Concentric Rainbow Rings (Optical Glory)**:
  - Surrounding the airplane's shadow is a bright, glowing whitish-yellow inner disk that transitions outward into concentric circular bands of orange, red, violet, blue, green, and a fainter secondary outer orange-red ring produced by backscattered sunlight in cloud water droplets.
- **Cloud Deck & Horizon**:
  - A uniform, softly textured beige-gray cloud layer fills nearly the entire frame, topped by a narrow band of pale blue sky along the upper edge of the image.
