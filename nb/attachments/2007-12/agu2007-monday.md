# Researchers at the AGU 2007 Fall Meeting Poster Session (`agu2007-monday.jpg`)

## Overview
A candid photograph taken on Monday evening at the 2007 American Geophysical Union (AGU) Fall Meeting in San Francisco, showing three smiling earth/marine scientists posing in front of a Scripps Institution of Oceanography (SIO) scientific poster.

## People & Foreground
- **Left**: A bald man wearing a dark gray long-sleeved crewneck shirt, khaki pants, and a conference lanyard leans forward with a big smile, making "rock on" / devil-horns hand gestures with both hands.
- **Center**: A man with short gray-brown hair wearing a dark gray patterned polo shirt with a black collar, blue jeans with a brown braided leather belt, and holding a small glass tumbler in his right hand smiles warmly at the camera.
- **Right**: A tall younger man with blondish-brown hair (**Danny Brothers**, as seen on his conference badge and first-author credit on the poster behind him) wearing a dark green-and-black plaid button-down long-sleeved shirt over a white undershirt, pleated khaki pants with a brown belt, and an AGU 2007 Fall Meeting name badge on a white lanyard, holding a drink glass in his right hand and smiling broadly.

## Background Poster Details
- Behind the three men is a large academic poster mounted on a dark green display board, titled:
  - **`New Seismic CHIRP Evidence for Transpression and Transtension Beneath the Salton Sea, California`**
  - **Authors**: `Brothers, D.*, N. Driscoll, G. Kent, J. Dingler, A. Harding, J. Babcock` (Scripps Institution of Oceanography, University of California, San Diego).
  - The top-right corner features the yellow-and-blue **`SIO SUBSCAN LAB`** logo.
  - Visible sections on the poster include a bathymetric/tectonic map of the Salton Sea on the left, a center panel titled **`Seismic Stratigraphy`** with high-resolution sub-bottom CHIRP seismic reflection profiles, and a right panel titled **`Transpression and Transtension`** showing faulted and folded sedimentary strata in CHIRP profiles.
- To the left, mounted on an adjacent black divider panel, is another poster titled *"Present-day Kinematics of the East African Rift..."* with an NSF logo and maps of East Africa.
