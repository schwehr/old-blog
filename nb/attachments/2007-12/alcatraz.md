# Distant View of Alcatraz Island in San Francisco Bay (`alcatraz.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide, cropped telephoto photograph of Alcatraz Island in San Francisco Bay, bathed in warm golden late-afternoon or early-morning sunlight against a hazy backdrop.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**:
  - Dark green leafy bushes and tree branches frame the bottom-left and bottom-right corners, with rounded tree canopies covering a hillside in the lower center.
- **Midground (Alcatraz Island & Bay)**:
  - Across a strip of calm blue-gray bay water sits **Alcatraz Island**, illuminated by warm golden light on its western/southwestern cliffs and historic structures.
  - Prominent landmarks on the island include the main cellhouse block along the crest, the tall white **Alcatraz Lighthouse** rising to the right of the cellhouse, the water tower and smaller industrial/guard buildings toward the left (north/west end), and the steep rocky shoreline.
- **Background**:
  - Behind the island to the left, the sloped silhouette of East Bay / Marin hills is faintly visible through a thick, warm peach-and-amber atmospheric haze that fills the sky.
