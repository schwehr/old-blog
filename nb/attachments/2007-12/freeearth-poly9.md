# Poly9 FreeEarth 3D Browser Globe (`freeearth-poly9.png`)

## Overview
A browser screenshot of **Poly9 FreeEarth** (`http://freeearth.poly9.com/`), a Flash/browser-based cross-platform 3D virtual globe demonstrated during the GeoBrowsers / OceanDIVA session at the 2007 AGU Fall Meeting.

## Interface & Visual Details
- **Browser Bar (Top)**:
  - Shows a web browser toolbar with Reload, Stop (disabled), and Home buttons, the URL `http://freeearth.poly9.com/` in the address bar with a small orange-and-white globe favicon, and a green Go arrow on the right.
- **Header & Logos**:
  - Centered at the top against the black space background is a circular white globe icon with orange continents next to bold white text reading **`Poly9 FreeEarth`**.
  - In the bottom-right corner is the stylized **`Poly9`** logo in white and orange above a bright green horizontal bar.
  - Along the bottom edge, white caption text reads: `"Poly9 FreeEarth is a cross-browser, cross-platform 3D globe which does not require any download¹. Give it a spin!"`
- **3D Globe & Flight Path**:
  - Centered in the viewport is a 3D satellite-textured globe focused on North America, Central America, the Caribbean, northern South America, Greenland (snow-covered white), and the surrounding Pacific and Atlantic Oceans.
  - A curved red great-circle flight path arcs across the North Atlantic from **`MIA`** (Miami, Florida, labeled in yellow text) northeastward to **`CDG`** (Paris Charles de Gaulle, labeled in yellow text on the horizon of Western Europe), where a white airplane icon is positioned.
- **Readouts & Navigation Controls**:
  - In the upper-left corner, white sans-serif telemetry text displays:
    - `Altitude: 19134.40 km`
    - `Target: ( 46.5886, -112.4897 )`
    - `Cursor: ( 34.6185, -92.6466 )`
  - In the upper-right corner are a horizontal slider bar with left/right arrows, a full-screen toggle button, a north-pointing compass arrow labeled `N`, and a vertical zoom slider bar with up/down arrows.
