# Google Maps Street View in Boston (`google-maps-streetview-boston.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Maps showing the newly launched **Street View** feature in Boston, Massachusetts (December 2007), displaying a street-level panorama bubble at the intersection of **Henchman St / Commercial St** in the North End adjacent to the **US Coast Guard Station**.

## Interface & Map Details
- **Top Map Controls**:
  - Across the top right of the satellite map view are five white map-type/overlay buttons: **`Street View`** (selected with a blue border), **`Traffic`**, **`Map`**, **`Satellite`** (selected in bold), and **`Terrain`**.
- **Bottom Satellite Base Map**:
  - Shows high-resolution overhead aerial/satellite imagery of Boston's North End waterfront.
  - Roads with Street View coverage are outlined in blue with yellow centerlines (`Commercial St`, `Charter St`, `Foster St`, `Henchman St`) and small red one-way arrows.
  - An orange-yellow pegman icon stands on a green circular base at the intersection of **Commercial St** and **Henchman St**, just west of the labeled **`US Coast Guard Station`** complex and its piers/slips along the harbor.
- **Street View Pop-Up Bubble**:
  - **Header**: Bold black title **`Henchman St / Commercial St`** on the left; blue links **`Street View Help`**, **`Full-screen`** (with an expand icon), and a blue close button (`x`) on the right.
  - **Street-Level Panorama**:
    - Shows a multi-story red-brick building (part of the USCG Base Boston / North End streetscape) with a modern glass-walled skybridge/atrium connecting to a stone-and-brick building on the right, a gated driveway entrance in the center, and a red fire hydrant on the right sidewalk.
    - Four sedans and a station wagon/SUV are parallel-parked along the curb on the left beneath green street trees, with a bicycle locked to a sign pole.
  - **Navigation Overlays**:
    - In the upper-left of the photo are left/right rotation arrow buttons and a vertical zoom slider (`+` / `-`).
    - Semi-transparent yellow and white road lines overlay the pavement with directional arrows and street labels: **`Commercial St`** (`NW` pointing left/away and `SE` pointing right/foreground) and **`Henchman St`** (`SW` pointing toward the lower-left foreground).
    - A watermark in the bottom right of the panorama reads `© 2007 Google`.
