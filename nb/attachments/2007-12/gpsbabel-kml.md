# GPSBabel NMEA-to-KML Track in Google Earth (`gpsbabel-kml.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot of the San Francisco Bay Area displaying a KML placemark/track (`-0`) on the San Francisco Peninsula, generated by converting NMEA sentences logged from an i.Trek Z1 Bluetooth GPS receiver aboard a Caltrain commuter train using `gpsbabel`.

## Map & Geographic Details
- **Geographic Coverage**:
  - Overhead satellite imagery showing the **San Francisco Peninsula**, **San Francisco Bay**, the **Golden Gate** at the top left, the **East Bay** (Oakland, Alameda, Hayward, Fremont) and Diablo Range to the right, and the Pacific Ocean in deep blue on the left.
  - Bridges visible crossing the bay include the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge, the San Mateo–Hayward Bridge (just north of the marker), and the Dumbarton Bridge further south, along with colorful reddish-orange and green salt evaporation ponds at the southern end of the bay.
- **GPS Placemark Overlay**:
  - Positioned along the eastern side of the San Francisco Peninsula (near San Mateo / Redwood City, around `37.527°N, 122.284°W`) are two overlapping green-and-white triangular mountain/peak icons labeled **`-0`** in white text with a black drop shadow.
