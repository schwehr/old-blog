# NASA Kids' Club Promo Graphic (`nasa-new-2.png`)

## Overview
A promotional web banner graphic from the December 2007 NASA website redesign advertising the "NASA Kids' Club" section (`Play Fun Games`), featuring a 3D cartoon astronaut character against a starry orange space background with a ringed planet.

## Scene & Character Description
- **Cartoon Character (Left)**: A close-up, angled view of a stylized 3D-rendered cartoon character (Nebula / NASA Kids' Club mascot) with a round tan face, two very large white oval eyes with black pupils, and no visible nose or mouth. The character wears a shiny royal-blue space helmet and suit accented with vertical white stripes, and raises a white four-fingered gloved hand in a wave toward the viewer at the bottom center.
- **Background & Space Elements (Right)**: A warm reddish-orange and brown starfield speckled with tiny white stars. In the lower right corner is a 3D-rendered brown-and-orange striped gas giant planet encircled by tilted, semi-transparent rings.
