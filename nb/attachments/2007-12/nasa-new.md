# NASA Homepage Redesign Screenshot (`nasa-new.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the newly redesigned NASA homepage (`www.nasa.gov`) captured in December 2007, featuring a dark space-themed interface, top navigation bar, user login controls, a featured news carousel highlighting Space Shuttle *Atlantis* (mission STS-122), and a grid of graphical topic buttons on the right.

## Header & Navigation Bar
- **Top Left**: The circular blue NASA "meatball" insignia logo on a dark gray panel.
- **Primary Navigation Tabs**: Five uppercase white text links in dark gray rectangular tabs across the top:
  - `HOME`
  - `NEWS`
  - `MISSIONS`
  - `MULTIMEDIA`
  - `ABOUT NASA`
- **Language & Preferences (Top Right)**: Two small gray links reading `› en Español` and `› More Preferences`.
- **Login & Search Row**:
  - Left side: Two dark gray text input fields (`Username` and password) followed by `› Log In` and `› Sign Up` links.
  - Right side: A white text input box next to a blue `Search` button.
- **Audience Sub-Navigation Bar**:
  - Left side: `For Public` (bold white), `For Educators`, `For Students`, `For Media`, `For Policymakers`, `For Employees`, and `MyNASA` (in light blue text separated by vertical bars).
  - Right side: `Bookmark` (with a plus/tag icon) and `Send` (with a right-arrow icon).

## Featured Story & Accordion Panel (Left Column)
- **Active Story Header**: A dark header bar with a pause icon (`||`), a red downward-pointing triangle, and bold white title text: `Atlantis to Launch Thursday, Deliver Columbus to ISS`.
- **Main Graphic**: A computer-rendered illustration of the Space Shuttle *Atlantis* in orbit over Earth with its payload bay doors open, showing the cylindrical European Space Agency *Columbus* laboratory module in the cargo bay and the International Space Station (ISS) robotic arm (Canadarm2) above.
- **Mission Patch Inset (Top Left of Graphic)**: The diamond-shaped STS-122 / 1E mission patch showing a yellow-and-white shuttle ascending over Earth with a stylized blue/green wave and crew surnames around the border: `LEOPOLD • FRICK • POINDEXTER • LOVE • SCHLEGEL • MELVIN • WALHEIM`, along with `122` and `1E`.
- **Caption**: Below the illustration, white text reads:
  > Commander Steve Frick and his Atlantis crewmates are set to launch at 4:31 p.m. EST Dec. 6, carrying the European Space Agency's Columbus module to the International Space Station. `› Space Shuttle Section`
- **Collapsed Accordion Headlines**: Below the main story are two collapsed dark gray bars with pause icons (`||`) and red right-pointing triangles:
  - `From the Gridiron to the Shuttle`
  - `NASA Scientist Confirms Light Show on Venus`
