# Aerial View of Snow-Dusted Nevada Mountains from Southwest Airlines Flight (`nevada-airphoto-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from the passenger window of a Southwest Airlines Boeing 737 flying over the Basin and Range terrain of Nevada on the morning of December 17, 2007, showing the aircraft's engine cowling and winglet in the foreground and snow-dusted desert mountain ranges below.

## Aircraft Foreground (Left)
- **Engine Nacelle**: A large, dark, shadowed jet engine cowling occupies the lower-left quadrant of the frame, with bright morning sunlight glinting off the curved top of the intake lip and pylon.
- **Wing & Winglet**: Extending diagonally upward from the left edge toward the upper-left center is the swept wing of the aircraft, tipped with a tall blended winglet painted in Southwest Airlines' canyon blue, red, and orange livery. White-and-yellow uppercase lettering along the winglet reads **`SOUTHWEST.COM`**.

## Landscape & Sky (Right & Background)
- **Terrain**: Below the wing stretches an expansive high-desert Basin and Range landscape across Nevada. Rugged, dendritic mountain ridges dusted with white snow catch the warm, low-angle morning sunlight, alternating with broad, flat tan-and-gray desert valleys and playa basins.
- **Horizon & Sky**: A soft layer of pale atmospheric haze and thin clouds sits along the distant horizon, transitioning upward into a clear, deep gradient of cerulean and cobalt blue sky.
