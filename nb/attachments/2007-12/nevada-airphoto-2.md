# Oblique Aerial Photograph of Nevada Mountain Range and Lake (`nevada-airphoto-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical oblique aerial photograph taken from a commercial airliner flying across Nevada on the morning of December 17, 2007, looking along the spine of a rugged Basin and Range mountain chain bordered by a large desert lake on the right.

## Geographic & Scene Description
- **Mountain Range (Center)**: A prominent north-south trending fault-block mountain range runs vertically through the center of the frame.
  - **Foreground Peaks**: The higher peaks in the lower half of the image are blanketed in bright white snow on their sunlit eastern/southeastern slopes, while deeply incised canyons and western slopes lie in cool blue-gray shadow. A small wisp of white cloud or blowing snow hovers over the central snow-covered crest.
  - **Midground Ridges**: Farther north along the range, the elevation drops slightly into bare, arid, reddish-tan rocky ridges and alluvial fans illuminated by warm golden morning light.
- **Lake & Shorelines (Right)**: Along the right edge of the midground lies a broad expanse of dark teal-blue water (such as Walker Lake or Pyramid Lake) abutting the steep eastern base of the mountain range, with a flat desert shoreline curving around its northern end.
- **Valleys & Horizon (Left & Top)**: To the left (west) of the mountain crest lies a wide, shadowed basin with smaller snow-dusted hills. In the distance toward the top of the frame, additional parallel desert ranges fade into a pale, hazy horizon beneath a clear blue sky band.
