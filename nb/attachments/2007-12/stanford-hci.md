# Stanford University Human-Computer Interaction Seminar Icon (`stanford-hci.png`)

## Overview
A square album-art style badge graphic from Stanford on iTunes U for the "Human-Computer Interaction Seminar" course, referenced in a December 30, 2007 blog post about OpenCourseWare and university lecture series.

## Visual Design & Typography
- **Background**: A deep cardinal red square with a subtle watermark of the circular Stanford University seal faintly visible in the lower two-thirds, framed by a thin gray outer border.
- **Top Header**: Centered white serif uppercase text reading:
  - `STANFORD`
  - `UNIVERSITY`
  - Underlined by a thin, tapered horizontal white divider rule.
- **Course Title**: Stacked, centered white serif text filling the main body of the square:
  - `Human-`
  - `Computer`
  - `Interaction`
  - `Seminar`
