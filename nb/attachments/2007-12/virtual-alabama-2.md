# Virtual Alabama County Data Availability Map (`virtual-alabama-2.png`)

## Overview
A status map and data layer summary from the "Virtual Alabama" Google Earth Enterprise portal (dated `02Nov2007`), showing county-by-county data ingestion status across the State of Alabama on the left and a 3D Google Earth building preview with a list of statewide/regional GIS layers on the right.
