# Virtual Alabama Splash Screen Interface (`virtual-alabama.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the "Virtual Alabama" web portal splash page (`www.virtu-al.org`) unveiled by the State of Alabama Department of Homeland Security in December 2007, featuring the agency's circular seal in the center surrounded by five floating graphical navigation icons on a bright royal-blue background.

## Central Seal & Title
- **Circular Seal**: The official seal of the **State of Alabama Homeland Security**:
  - Outer white ring with dark blue serif text: `STATE OF ALABAMA` across the top and `HOMELAND SECURITY` along the bottom, separated by pairs of five-pointed blue stars on the left and right.
  - Inner circle: The crimson St. Andrew's cross (saltire) of the Alabama state flag over a faint map of Alabama and neighboring states, surmounted by a central shield showing a blue star-spangled chief above a landscape/river/coastline emblem.
- **Overlay Text**: Directly across the middle of the seal in semi-transparent gray sans-serif lettering is the title **`Virtual Alabama`**.

## Orbiting Navigation Icons
Five graphical icons are arranged in a circle around the central seal against the solid blue background:
1. **Top (`11–12 o'clock`)**: A cut-out photograph of a red emergency response SUV angled upward to the right, bearing a circular state seal decal on its front passenger door.
2. **Upper Right (`2 o'clock`)**: Two stylized 3D human bust icons—one with a yellow head and blue shoulders in front, and one with a gold head and bright green shoulders behind—representing users, personnel, or collaboration.
3. **Lower Right (`4–5 o'clock`)**: A glowing cyan-and-blue concentric target or bullseye radar ring icon.
4. **Bottom (`6–7 o'clock`)**: A tilted gray newspaper icon with the bold black masthead **`NEWS`**, simulated column lines, and a small globe thumbnail.
5. **Middle Left (`9 o'clock`)**: A photorealistic 3D globe of Earth showing North America, clouds, and oceans (representing the Google Earth Enterprise globe interface).
