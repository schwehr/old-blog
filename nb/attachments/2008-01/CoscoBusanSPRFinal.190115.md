# Cover Page of the M/V Cosco Busan Oil Spill ISPR Report (`CoscoBusanSPRFinal.190115.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the cover page of the official U.S. Coast Guard Incident Specific Preparedness Review (ISPR) Phase I report titled *"Incident Specific Preparedness Review (ISPR) M/V Cosco Busan Oil Spill in San Francisco Bay: REPORT ON INITIAL RESPONSE PHASE"*, dated `11 January 2008`.

## Title Block (Top)
Centered bold black serif text at the top of the white page:
- `Incident Specific Preparedness Review (ISPR)`
- `M/V Cosco Busan Oil Spill in San Francisco Bay`
- `REPORT ON INITIAL RESPONSE PHASE`
- `11 January 2008`

## Overlapping Incident Photographs (Center)
Three rectangular color photographs arranged in a stepped, overlapping diagonal cascade down the center of the page:
1. **Top Right Photograph**: An aerial view of two small response boats towing a curved yellow containment boom across dark blue water, with a third skimmer/work boat stationed at the apex of the boom.
2. **Middle Left Photograph**: An oblique view of roughly two dozen cleanup workers wearing white hazardous-materials protective suits (Tyvek suits) walking and bending over along a sandy beach at the water's edge to collect oiled debris.
3. **Bottom Right Photograph**: A water-level view of the dark hull of the container ship *Cosco Busan* (displaying large white hull lettering `H A N J I N` below stacked cargo containers), showing a massive horizontal gash torn into the port bow/side plating from its allision with the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge tower, while a U.S. Coast Guard response boat with an orange hull stripe passes in the foreground.

Eleven organizational logos and seals arranged in two horizontal rows across the bottom of the cover page:
- **First Row (Left to Right)**:
  1. `BAYKEEPER` (blue fish-outline logo)

  4. `NOAA` (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration circular emblem)
  5. `PMSA` (Pacific Merchant Shipping Association square logo showing four ship silhouettes)
  6. Sea turtle black-and-white square emblem
- **Second Row (Left to Right)**:
  7. Seal of the City and County of San Francisco
  8. `SFDEM` (`SAN FRANCISCO DEPARTMENT OF EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT` red logo with Golden Gate Bridge tower graphic)
  9. U.S. Coast Guard emblem on a dark blue square
  10. U.S. Coast Guard Marine Safety / Prevention circular seal
  11. Pacific States / British Columbia Oil Spill Task Force map logo (showing Alaska, BC, Washington, Oregon, California, and Hawaii)
