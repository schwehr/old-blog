# Winter View of the Boston Waterfront and Charlestown Navy Yard Area (`boston-pog.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime winter photograph taken on January 16, 2008 during a visit to the Boston Port Operations Group (POG) meeting, looking across a snow-covered park and parking lot toward the industrial waterfront and harbor under a clear blue sky.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Left & Bottom)**:
  - On the far left, a curved red-painted tubular metal railing and the corner of a multi-story brick-and-stone building frame the shot.
  - Across the lower portion of the image, bare deciduous trees and low hedges sit in a small landscaped park blanketed in white snow. To the right, a plowed asphalt roadway and parking lot with several parked dark SUVs and sedans are bordered by snowbanks.
  - Four overhead black utility/power cables stretch horizontally across the upper third of the frame.
- **Midground & Background (Waterfront Buildings)**:
  - **Left Building**: A light-colored two-story industrial/commercial building with large dark windows and wall lettering on its facade, topped by an tall, angled metal lattice structure or ship-like mast/gantry framework on the roof.
  - **Center**: A low, wide, cylindrical green storage tank sits behind the waterfront fence near a small harbor inlet/basin where an American flag flies from a flagpole.
  - **Right Building (Power Plant)**: A massive, blocky industrial power plant structure with two tall vertical smokestacks rising side by side against the clear blue sky, flanked by a lower brick building on the far right.
