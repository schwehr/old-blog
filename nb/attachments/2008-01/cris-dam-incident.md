# Aerial View of Towboat and Barge Incident at a River Dam (`cris-dam-incident.png`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph from a U.S. Army Corps of Engineers presentation on the Coastal River Information Service (CRIS), showing a white inland river towboat and multiple barges—including a sunken/submerged barge pinned against the dam gates—at a multi-bay gated river navigation dam.

## Dam Structure (Upper Half)
- A concrete gated dam spans horizontally across a muddy brown river from the left bank to the right bank.
- Five tall tan concrete piers with rectangular machinery houses on top support four main spillway/roller gate bays connected by a white steel service bridge.
- Turbulent, frothy white water churns downstream (above the dam in the photo's perspective), while the upstream pool in the foreground is calmer dark brown water.
- On the right end of the dam, a tall lattice-boom crane sits on a barge/work platform next to sheet-pile cofferdams and construction materials, with a second crane visible on the far-right embankment.

## Vessels & Barges (Foreground & Center-Right)
- **Pinned/Submerged Barge**: Directly against the upstream face of the third and fourth dam piers (center-right), the rust-brown deck of a partially sunken barge is pinned broadside against the structure with white water washing over its hull.
- **Red-Brown Covered Hopper Barge**: Extending diagonally from the foreground toward the fourth dam pier is a long, intact reddish-brown covered hopper barge.
- **White Towboat**: Positioned alongside the lower-right side of the hopper barge is a multi-deck white inland river pushboat/towboat with a high pilothouse and twin black stacks.
- **Crane Barge & Workboat**: Sandwiched between the towboat/hopper barge and the right-hand dam pier is a flat deck barge carrying yellow heavy equipment/excavators, with a smaller white workboat moored along its outer (right) side.
