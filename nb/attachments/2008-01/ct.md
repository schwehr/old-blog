# Smokestack Plume at Sunset Along I-395 in Connecticut (`ct.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect landscape photograph taken at dusk/sunset around January 22, 2008 along Interstate 395 in Connecticut (while traveling to/from an RTCM AIS Working Group meeting at the U.S. Coast Guard Research and Development Center in Groton, CT), showing a tall industrial smokestack venting a sunlit steam plume between dark silhouetted treelines.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Framing (Left & Right)**: Dense stands of bare winter deciduous trees and dark evergreens form deep black silhouettes sloping inward from both the left and right edges of the frame.
- **Industrial Plant & Smokestack (Center)**: In the gap between the treelines stands a tall, slender cylindrical smokestack rising from a white-and-tan industrial/power plant building at the base. The low-angle setting sun illuminates the western face of the building and the side of the stack in warm golden light. A thin red-and-white communications tower is faintly visible just to the right of the building.
- **Steam Plume & Sky**: A thick, billowing plume of white and peach-tinted steam rises from the base/stack area and trails diagonally upward to the right from the top of the smokestack, catching the warm orange and pink glow of sunset against a muted slate-blue and gray-violet evening sky.
