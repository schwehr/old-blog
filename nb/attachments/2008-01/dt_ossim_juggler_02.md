# ASU Decision Theater Multi-Screen 3D Globe Visualization (`dt_ossim_juggler_02.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle photograph of the Decision Theater at Arizona State University (ASU), showcasing a panoramic multi-screen rear-projection display running a 3D virtual globe visualization (Minerva / OSSIMPlanet / VR Juggler) centered over the Florida peninsula and the Bahamas.

## Scene Description
- **Panoramic Projection Wall**:
  - Seven faceted, seamless large-format projection screens form a curved semi-hexagonal/octagonal immersive display wall spanning the room.
  - Projected across the center five screens is a 3D view of Earth from space against a black background:
    - **Geographic Features**: A bright green and tan satellite/topographic rendering of the southeastern United States (prominently featuring the Florida peninsula, Lake Okeechobee, and the Gulf Coast) sits at the center. To the right (east) in the deep blue Atlantic Ocean are the bright turquoise shallow carbonate banks of the Bahamas (Great Bahama Bank and Little Bahama Bank) and Cuba to the south.
    - **On-Screen Overlays**: Small white multi-line diagnostic text blocks (displaying frame rates/coordinates) appear at the bottom-left corner of each individual screen panel. Small white crosshairs (`+`) are visible on the outer left, right-center, and far-right panels.
- **Conference Room & Furniture**:
  - In the foreground, dark gray modular conference tables are arranged in a horseshoe/U-shape facing the screens, accompanied by bright orange upholstered office chairs.
  - A higher podium/workstation desk with a chair stands inside the right side of the U-shaped table arrangement.
  - Above the screens, off-white wall panels house mounted rectangular speakers, a dark wall clock, and ceiling-mounted tracking/projection hardware.
