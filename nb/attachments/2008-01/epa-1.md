- **Left Column Explanatory Text**:
  - Paragraph 1: *"There are no absolute chemical concentrations that correspond to sediment toxicity, but effects range low (ERL) and effects range medium (ERM) values are used as guidelines. The ERM is the concentration of a contaminant that resulted in adverse biological effects in 50% of literature studies evaluated. A more protective indicator of contaminant concentrations is the ERL criterion, which is the concentration of a contaminant above which ecological effects are observed about 10% of the time."*

## Map & Chart Details
- **Map Panel**:
  - Titled **`Delaware Estuary`** / **`Sediment Contamination`** at the top center over a light gray land background showing state borders (New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Delaware, Maryland) including the curved Twelve-Mile Circle boundary of northern Delaware.
  - Sampling stations along the tidal Delaware River and upper Delaware Bay are marked with color-coded icons:
    - **Good** (green circles): Distributed primarily in the lower river/upper bay and several mid-river sites.
    - **Fair** (yellow triangle): Located in the upper-middle estuary south of Wilmington.
    - **Poor** (red `X` markers): Clustered near the urban/industrial Philadelphia–Camden and Wilmington reaches.
    - **Missing** (blue inverted triangles): Scattered from the head of tide near Trenton down through the estuary.
