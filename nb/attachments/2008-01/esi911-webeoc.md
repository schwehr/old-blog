# ESi WebEOC Mapper Emergency Operations GIS Interface (`esi911-webeoc.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **WebEOC Mapper** running inside Microsoft Internet Explorer on Windows XP, showing an ArcGIS Server-based emergency management map of the central United States overlaid with live weather radar reflectivity and red incident markers.

- **Browser & Application Header**:
  - Windows XP title bar: `WebEOC Mapper - Microsoft Internet Explorer`.

  - Secondary gray toolbar with GIS navigation and measurement icons (Zoom In, Zoom Out, Pan, Full Extent, Identify, Measure, Redline/Draw, Clear) and `Help` on the far right.

## Map Details
- **Geographic Extent**: Covers the Great Plains and Midwest from Wyoming, Colorado, and New Mexico in the west to Illinois, Missouri, Arkansas, and Louisiana in the east, showing interstate highways (`I-25`, `I-35`, `I-40`, `I-70`, `I-80`, `I-90`) and major cities (`Denver`, `Cheyenne`, `Rapid City`, `Kansas City`, `Saint Louis`, `Chicago`, `Dallas`, `Little Rock`, `Memphis`, `Shreveport`).
- **Weather Radar & Incident Overlays**:
  - A large curved frontal band of NEXRAD weather radar precipitation reflectivity (in blue, green, yellow, orange, and red) stretches from northern Texas/Oklahoma up through Kansas, Nebraska, Iowa, and Minnesota.
  - Red circular point markers with bold red/orange text labels indicate emergency operations centers and simulated incidents, including `User Conference Test board` (near Cheyenne), `Tim's Office` and `ESF6 Denver` (near Denver), `NCRO` (Kansas City), `mymap` (Lawton, OK), `cog` (Dallas), `Spill` (northeast Texas), and `CAPCOG` (Arkansas).
