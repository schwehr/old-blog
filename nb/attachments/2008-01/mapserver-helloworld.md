# MapServer "Hello World" Coastline Map of the New Hampshire Seacoast (`mapserver-helloworld.png`)

## Overview
A 512×512 pixel minimalist vector outline map generated by UMN **MapServer** (`shp2img`) from the USGS National Atlas state boundaries shapefile (`statesp020.shp`), showing the coastlines and state borders of southern Maine, the New Hampshire Seacoast, and northeastern Massachusetts.

## Map Details
- **Geographic Extent & Projection**:
  - Unprojected geographic coordinates (`EXTENT -71.25 42.5 -70.25 43.5`), rendered as a dark gray single-pixel line drawing (`OUTLINECOLOR 100 100 100`) on a plain white background inside a thin gray frame.
- **Visible Geographic Features (North to South)**:
  - **Southern Maine & Piscataqua River**: The southern coast of Maine curves down from the top right to Portsmouth Harbor, where the Maine–New Hampshire border follows the Piscataqua River inland to Great Bay (the prominent multi-lobed estuary near the center) and turns north-northwest along the Salmon Falls River.
  - **New Hampshire Seacoast**: A short stretch of Atlantic coastline extends southward from Portsmouth past Rye and Hampton Harbor.
  - **Northeastern Massachusetts**: The New Hampshire–Massachusetts state border runs west-to-east across the lower left before meeting the coast near Salisbury/Newburyport and the Merrimack River mouth, followed southward by Plum Island, Cape Ann (Gloucester/Rockport peninsula jutting eastward in the lower right), and the northern edge of Massachusetts Bay / Salem Sound at the bottom.
