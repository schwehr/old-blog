# MapServer New Hampshire Seacoast Map with PostGIS AIS Ship Tracks in EPSG:900913 (`mapserver-ships-900913.png`)

## Overview
A 512×512 pixel map generated by **MapServer** combining state boundary polygons from a USGS National Atlas shapefile (`statesp020.shp`) and vessel track lines from a PostGIS database (`ais` `track_lines`), reprojected from WGS84 geographic coordinates (`EPSG:4326`) into the Google Maps Spherical Mercator projection (`EPSG:900913`).

## Map Details
- **Projection & Extent**:
  - Projected in `EPSG:900913` with meter coordinates `EXTENT -7925000 5240000 -7825000 5400000`. Compared to the unprojected geographic view (`mapserver-helloworld.png`), the map is vertically stretched as characteristic of the Mercator projection at ~43°N latitude.
- **Base Layer (`states`)**:
  - Thin gray outlines (`RGB 100, 100, 100`) on a white background depict the southern Maine coast, the Maine–New Hampshire border along the Piscataqua River into Great Bay, the New Hampshire Seacoast, the New Hampshire–Massachusetts border, Plum Island, and the Cape Ann peninsula.
- **Overlay Layer (`shiptrack`)**:
  - Bold red vector lines (`COLOR 255 0 0`, `WIDTH 2`) trace Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel tracks navigating through the mouth of the Piscataqua River (Portsmouth Harbor) between New Castle/Rye, NH and Kittery, ME, extending southeastward into the Gulf of Maine approaches.
