# United States National Atlas State Boundaries in Google Earth (`nationalatlas-states.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Google Earth 3D virtual globe displaying a KML overlay of United States state and territory boundaries (`statesp020.kml`), converted from the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) National Atlas shapefile (`statesp020.shp`) using `ogr2ogr`.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **3D Globe View**:
  - The globe is centered over North America against a black space background dotted with small white stars.
  - The Earth's surface shows satellite and aerial imagery with blue oceans, green and tan continental landmasses across North America, Central America, and northern South America, and white polar/subpolar cloud or ice cover near the top of the globe over northern Canada and the Arctic.
- **Red Vector Boundary Overlay**:
  - Thick bright red polygon outlines trace the borders of the 48 contiguous United States across the center of the continent, clearly delineating individual state boundaries from the Pacific coast to the Atlantic coast and New England.
  - **Hawaii** is outlined in red in the central Pacific Ocean toward the lower left of the globe.
  - **Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands** are outlined in red in the Caribbean Sea toward the lower right.
  - **Alaska** in the upper left exhibits a rendering artifact where only the southern coastal boundary, the Aleutian Islands chain, the Alexander Archipelago (Southeast Alaska), and parts of the northern Arctic coast are drawn in red, while the interior land border with Canada and mainland polygon fill/outline are missing or clipped.
