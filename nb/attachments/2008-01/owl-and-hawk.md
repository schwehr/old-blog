# Hawk and Owl Perched in a Bare Winter Tree (`owl-and-hawk.jpg`)

## Overview
A wildlife photograph taken on the morning of January 15, 2008, capturing both a hawk perched high on an upper branch and the barred owl perched lower among dense twigs in the same deciduous tree against a clear blue winter sky.

## Scene Description
- **Upper Left (Hawk)**:
  - High in the tree at the upper left, a medium-to-large hawk (likely a Cooper's hawk or red-tailed hawk) perches upright on a fork of a dark branch.
  - Illuminated from the left by low-angle morning sunlight, its breast glows warm buff/rufous-orange while its back and folded wings are in dark shadow, and its long, narrow barred tail hangs straight down below the branch.
- **Lower Right (Owl)**:
  - Lower in the frame toward the bottom right, the barred owl sits tucked beneath a thick, snow-topped main limb amidst a dense tangle of fine twigs.
  - The owl's brown-and-white vertically streaked breast and barred tail feathers are partially obscured by the intersecting branches, while warm sunlight catches the right side of its plumage.
- **Tree & Sky**:
  - A network of bare, dark grey-brown branches and fine twigs with scattered dried red berries or buds crisply silhouettes against an unclouded, bright blue sky.
  - A strip of white snow rests along the top surface of the heavy horizontal bough above the owl.
