# Raccoon Climbing the Base of a Maple Tree in the Snow (`raccoon-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime wildlife photograph taken on January 19, 2008, showing a raccoon clinging vertically to the side of a large maple tree trunk in a snow-covered yard while attempting to chase a squirrel.

## Scene Description
- **Subject (Raccoon)**:
  - Positioned just right of center, the raccoon is pressed upright against the right side of a thick, rough-barked tree trunk, looking upward into the canopy.
  - Its left forepaw reaches up onto the furrowed grey-brown bark, its thick grizzled brown-and-grey winter coat is illuminated by morning light, and its striped, bushy ringed tail hangs straight down to touch the snow at the base of the tree.
- **Surrounding Yard & Vegetation**:
  - The large maple tree trunk dominates the center of the image.
  - The ground is blanketed in white snow with footprints, depressions, and scattered fallen leaves and twigs.
  - A small bare deciduous shrub with thin reddish-brown branches grows at the left base of the trunk, with a clump of dried weeds/wildflowers further back in the snow on the left.
  - Bright green evergreen branches (white pine needles) frame the right side of the image, sunlit against brown underbrush in the background.
