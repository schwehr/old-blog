# Raccoon Walking Through Snow Toward Pine Trees (`raccoon-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime wildlife photograph taken on the morning of January 19, 2008, showing a raccoon walking through a snow-covered yard away from a large maple tree trunk toward low-hanging evergreen pine branches.

## Scene Description
- **Subject (Raccoon)**:
  - Positioned in the lower right quadrant, the raccoon walks on all fours from left to right across the uneven, footprints-marked snow toward the cover of pine boughs.
  - Its arched back, black-and-white ringed bushy tail extending to the left, white-rimmed ear, pointed snout, and black eye mask are clearly visible. The fur along its lower belly and legs appears slightly damp and clumped from moving through the snow.
- **Surrounding Environment**:
  - To the left stands the wide, deeply furrowed dark trunk of a mature maple tree, with bare twigs and dried golden-brown seed heads protruding from the snow at its base.
  - To the right, dense green white-pine branches hang low over the snow, illuminated by bright morning sunlight that casts dappled shadows across the snowpack.
  - Brown leaf litter and brush are visible in the sunlit background beyond the tree trunk.
