# GigaPan Panorama Popup for Boston Waterfront in Google Earth (`sargent-gigapan.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth 4.2 showing an open information balloon for the "GigaPan Panoramas" layer over Boston Harbor, featuring a panoramic photograph of the Boston Waterfront taken by Randy Sargent (`rsargent`) from near Logan International Airport.
