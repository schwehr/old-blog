# SimpleDEMViewer Digital Elevation Model Application on Mac OS X (`simpledemviewer.jpg`)

## Overview
A promotional screenshot from the Apple Mac OS X Downloads page showing **SimpleDEMViewer**, a Mac OS X geographic and terrain visualization application displaying a color-coded relief map of Japan and East Asia alongside a 3D bird's-eye view and an elevation cross-section profile of Mount Fuji (`Mt. Fuji`).

## Interface & Window Details
- **Mac OS X Menu Bar (Top)**:
  - Shows the Apple logo, `SimpleDemViewer`, `File`, `Edit`, `Map_handling`, `Tools`, `Window`, and `Help` on the left, and system status icons, clock (`20:59`), and user name (`Henry`) on the right.
- **Main Map Window (`Picture-9`, Background)**:
  - Displays a hypsometric and bathymetric shaded-relief map of Japan, the Sea of Japan, the Korean Peninsula, and eastern China with white latitude/longitude graticule lines.
  - Ocean depths are colored in shades of blue, lowlands in green, and mountain ranges in yellow, orange, and brown.
  - Red marker dots and labels identify `Beijing`, `Seoul`, and `Tokyo` (with bilingual English and Japanese/Chinese text).
- **Preferences Dialog (`Preferences`, Top Left)**:
  - The `Colors` tab is active (alongside `Shading`, `Lat/Long`, and `Misc` tabs).
  - On the left, a vertical `Elevation colors` table maps elevation thresholds (in meters, from `5000`, `3000`, `1500`, `500`, `0`, `-200`, `-1000`, `-3000`, `-5000`, `-8000`, `Sea`, `Lakes`) to brown, orange, yellow, green, and blue color swatches.
  - On the right, a `Color set` box shows `etopo` selected in a dropdown with `Save as` and `Delete` buttons, above four checked checkboxes: `White Antarctica`, `Use gradation`, `Draw lakes`, and `Treat sea as no data, when etopo2 exist`.
  - Bottom action buttons: `Defaults`, `Save`, and `Apply` (highlighted in blue).
- **3D Bird's-Eye View Window (`Mt.Fuji`, Top Center-Right)**:
  - A floating window rendering a 3D perspective terrain view of snow-capped **Mount Fuji** rising in the center with orange/yellow mid-slopes, green lower valleys and ridges, and blue water in the foreground.
- **Elevation Profile Window (`DemProfile-1`, Bottom Left)**:
  - Titled `Mt. Fuji`, showing a 2D cross-sectional elevation profile of the volcano's symmetrical cone colored from green at the base through yellow and orange to brown at the summit peak (reaching just under `4000 m` on the right vertical axis).
