# UNH CCOM/JHC U.S. Law of the Sea Atlantic Margin Bathymetry in Google Earth (`unclos-ge-2007.png`)

## Overview
A composite screenshot created by Kurt Schwehr, Jim Gardner, and Rich Sullivan (May 2007) illustrating how multibeam bathymetry data from the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC) U.S. Law of the Sea Mapping Program can be explored and downloaded through Google Earth.

## Scene & Interface Details
- **3D Oblique Globe View**:
  - Looking northwestward from the deep Atlantic Ocean toward the U.S. East Coast (with New England, Long Island, New Jersey, Delaware Bay, and Chesapeake Bay outlined in yellow along the coastline and white state borders inland).
  - Along the continental slope and rise off the U.S. Atlantic margin, high-resolution multibeam bathymetry is rendered as a color-coded shaded-relief overlay (transitioning from yellow/green at the upper continental slope through turquoise and light blue to deep indigo in the abyssal plain), crisscrossed by submarine canyons and channels.
  - A translucent red 3D bounding box highlights the central Atlantic margin survey block, with a white `i` information icon at its center and green numbered pin placemarks to the right.
- **Google Earth Information Balloon (Upper Left-Center)**:
  - Anchored to the `i` icon in the red-outlined survey block, the white popup displays:
    - `Central Area of the Atlantic Margin Mapping showing data colored by multibeam shaded relief`
    - Blue link: `UNH - CCOM/JHC USGS Law of the Sea Mapping Program`
    - Bold heading: `Cruise Report USNS Henson - HEN0401`
    - Blue link: `U.S. Law of the Sea cruise to map the foot of the slope and 2500-m isobath of the Northeast US Atlantic continental margin`
    - Bold heading: `Download Data`
    - Three horizontal blue links: `ASCII Data`, `ESRI Data`, and `IVS3D SD Data`
    - Footer: `Directions: To here - From here`.
- **Inset Web Page Preview (Right)**:
  - Connected by an orange callout line from the `IVS3D SD Data` link is an inset screenshot of the UNH/NOAA `Data for the U.N.C. Law of the Sea` web page (`IVS3D SD Data` tab, `Atlantic` sub-tab), displaying download links and metadata tables (`Depth min`, `Depth max`, `Resolution`, `Size`, `FGDC Compliant Metadata`) for bathymetry and backscatter files (`Far North Bathy`, `North Bathy`, `Central Bathy`, `South Bathy`, `Far South Bathy`, etc.).
