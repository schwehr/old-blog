# UNH CCOM/JHC U.S. Law of the Sea Mapping Program Global Survey Map (`unclos_map.gif`)

## Overview
A dual-globe map graphic from the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) illustrating the geographic locations and survey years (2003–2007) of U.S. Law of the Sea (UNCLOS Article 76) multibeam bathymetric mapping cruises across the Atlantic, Arctic, Gulf of Mexico, and Pacific Oceans.

## Map & Label Details
- **Left Globe (Centered on North America)**:
  - Shows shaded-relief topography and bathymetry of North America and surrounding ocean basins, with yellow polygons marking surveyed continental margin regions and black arrows pointing to each site:
    - `Arctic Ocean 2003 2004` (top left): Points to yellow survey areas over the Chukchi Cap / Northwind Ridge in the Arctic Ocean north of Alaska.
    - `Gulf of Alaska 2005` (middle left): Points to a yellow survey strip along the continental margin off southern and southeastern Alaska.
    - `Atlantic 2004, 2005` (lower right): Points to a large, elongated yellow survey swath along the U.S. East Coast continental slope and rise.
    - `Gulf of Mexico 2007` (bottom center-right): Points to two yellow survey areas in the central and eastern Gulf of Mexico.
- **Right Globe (Centered on the Pacific Ocean)**:
  - Shows shaded-relief bathymetry of the Pacific Ocean basin (including the Mariana Trench, Hawaiian-Emperor seamount chain, and Bering Sea), with yellow survey polygons and black callout arrows:
    - `Bering Sea 2003` (top right): Points to yellow survey areas in the Bering Sea along the Aleutian Basin / Bowers Ridge, with the Gulf of Alaska swath also visible on the upper right limb.
    - `Marianas 2006` (upper center-left): Points to a yellow survey block along the Mariana Trench / island arc system in the western Pacific.
    - `Kingman Reef Palmyra Atoll` (lower right): Points to two small yellow survey patches in the central equatorial Pacific (Line Islands).
