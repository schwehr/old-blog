# Rose Point Coastal Explorer Cruise Mode Interface (`CoastalExplorer-cruisemode.jpg`)

## Overview

A screenshot of Rose Point Navigation Systems' **Coastal Explorer** electronic charting software in Cruise Mode, displaying a NOAA raster nautical chart of Portsmouth Harbor (between Kittery, Maine, and New Castle, New Hampshire) alongside a Guide Book pane featuring NOAA Coast Pilot text, a navigation compass, a data console, and a list of nearby places.

## Chart View (Main Pane)

- **Geographic Coverage:** Shows the mouth of the Piscataqua River and Portsmouth Harbor at a `1:20,000` chart scale (indicated by the `1:20,000` / `NORTH UP` orientation indicator with a blue upward arrow in the upper-left corner).
- **Landmasses & Features:**
  - **North / West (Maine):** `KITTERY` at upper left, `SEAVEY ISLAND` (with `Portsmouth Naval Shipyard` area, `Pier No 2`, ` Henderson Pt`, `Sullivan Pt`, ` Jamaica Island`, `Back Channel`), `Clark I`, `Kittery Point`, `Barters Creek`, and `CROCKETTS NECK` at upper right.
  - **South / West (New Hampshire):** `Pierces I` on the left, `Goat I`, ` Shapleigh I`, `Frame Pt`, `Lady Isle`, `Pest I`, and `NEW CASTLE ISLAND` (`New Castle`, `Boatswain Hill`, `Salamander Pt`, `Fort Pt.`, and `PORTSMOUTH HARBOR (NEW CASTLE)` light).
- **Hydrography & Aids to Navigation:** Soundings in feet, depth contours (white for deep channel, pale blue for shoaler water), magenta regulated/restricted area notes (`REGULATED NAVIGATION AREA 165.101`, `RESTRICTED AREA 334.50`, `SPECIAL ANCHORAGE 110.1 & 110.10`, `Cable Area`), red and green buoys/beacons, light sectors, and pink diamond tide/current station markers (e.g., `7.35 ft` near Fort Point, `7.24 ft` near Pepperrell Cove).

- **Top-Right Header & Navigation Compass:**
  - Displays the date and time: `Monday, January 28  14:10:56`.
  - **Navigation Compass:** Shows `000°T` (True) and `006°M` (Magnetic) above a circular compass rose marked every 30 degrees (`030`, `060`, `E`, `120`, `150`, `S`, `210`, `240`, `W`, `300`, `330`, `N`) with a red north pointer.

  - `SOG`: `0.00 kn`
  - `Water Speed`: `- - -`
  - `Depth`: `- - -`
  - `Water Temp`: `- - -`
- **Nearby Places (Lower-Right):**
  - Scrollable list of Coast Pilot entries (blue `i` icons) and tide/current predictions (magenta diamond icons):
    - `Clarks Island` (`Coast Pilot`)
    - `Clark Island, southwest of` (`current prediction`)
    - `Spruce Creek` (`Coast Pilot`)
    - `Clark Island, south of` (`current prediction`)
    - `Salamander Point, north of` (`current prediction`)
    - `Kittery Point Bridge` (`current prediction`)
    - `Jamaica Island, northeast of` (`current prediction`)
    - `Hick Rocks and Clark Island, between` (`current prediction`)
    - `Salamander Point` (`current prediction`)
    - `New Castle` (`Coast Pilot`)
    - `Portsmouth Harbor` (`Coast Pilot` - highlighted)
    - `Henderson Point, west of` (`current prediction`)
    - `Seavey Island, north of` (`current prediction`)
    - `Fort Point` (`tide prediction`)
- **Guide Book Pane (Bottom):**
  - Blue title bar labeled `Guide Book` on the left and `Nearby Places` on the right.
  - Displays NOAA Coast Pilot text under the heading **Portsmouth Harbor**:
    - `(180) Portsmouth Harbor, 37 miles southwestward of Cape Elizabeth and about 25 miles northward of Cape Ann Light, is the only harbor of refuge for deep-draft vessels between Portland and Gloucester. No large vessel should proceed northward of Kitts Rocks Lighted Whistle Buoy 2KR (43°03.0'N., 70°41.5'W.) without a pilot, as the anchorage area is limited.`

    - `(182) Several U.S. Navy activities, including the Portsmouth Naval Shipyard and a regional medical clinic, are on Seavey Island at Kittery, on the north side of the harbor opposite Portsmouth.`
- **Bottom Toolbar & Status Bar:**
  - Nine large rectangular action buttons: `MOB`, `Instant Route`, `RBL`, `Event`, `Next WP`, `Reset XTE`, `1:10,000` (highlighted white), `Heading Up`, `Go To Boat`, and `More ►`.
  - Status bar at the very bottom reads `Coastal Explorer` on the left, `Off Chart` in the center, and `NO GPS` on the right.
