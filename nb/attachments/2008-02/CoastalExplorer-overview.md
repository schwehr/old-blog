# Rose Point Coastal Explorer North America AIS Overview (`CoastalExplorer-overview.jpg`)

## Overview

A screenshot from Rose Point's **Coastal Explorer** zoomed out to show North America, Central America, and the eastern Pacific Ocean, illustrating real-time AIS vessel clusters received simultaneously from multiple coastal regions across the United States and Canada.

## Map & AIS Target Details

- **Base Map:** A simplified two-tone world basemap showing North America, Central America, the Caribbean, and northern South America in medium gray against light gray oceans.
- **Warning Text:** In the lower-left corner, partially cropped red bold text reads: `...available for this area or scale` (part of `No vector charts are available for this area or scale`).
- **AIS Vessel Clusters:** Dense star-like clusters of dark blue triangular vessel icons mark active AIS feeds in major maritime regions:
  - **Pacific Northwest:** A dense cluster around Puget Sound / Strait of Georgia (Seattle and Vancouver).
  - **California:** A large cluster around San Francisco Bay and central California.
  - **Hawaii:** A cluster in the lower-left corner enclosed within a dark blue rectangular chart-boundary box.
  - **Gulf of Mexico:** A cluster along the Texas/Louisiana Gulf Coast (Houston/Galveston/New Orleans).
  - **Southeast U.S. & Florida:** Three smaller clusters along the southern Atlantic coast (South Florida/Miami, Georgia/South Carolina, and North Carolina/Virginia).
  - **Northeast U.S.:** A dense cluster spanning New York Harbor, Long Island Sound, and New England (Massachusetts/New Hampshire).
- **Connecting Line:** A thin pink/magenta diagonal line runs from the upper-left edge through the Pacific Northwest across the continent toward the mid-Atlantic coast and out into the Atlantic Ocean.
