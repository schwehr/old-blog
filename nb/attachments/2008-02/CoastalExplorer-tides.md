# Rose Point Coastal Explorer ENC Vector Chart and Tide Station View (`CoastalExplorer-tides.jpg`)

## Overview

A screenshot of Rose Point's **Coastal Explorer** displaying an Electronic Navigational Chart (ENC) vector view of Portsmouth Harbor alongside the **Kittery Point** Tide Station properties pane and an interactive 24-hour tidal height curve at the bottom.

## ENC Vector Chart Pane (Upper Left)

- **Landmasses & Buildings:** Shows `New Castle` on the left and `Gerrish Island` on the right in tan/ochre, with individual small brown square and rectangular building footprints along the shoreline.
- **Hydrography & Aids to Navigation:**
  - Depth areas shaded in blue (deeper water), pale blue, and greenish-blue (intertidal/shoal areas), with soundings in fathoms and fractions (e.g., `2¼`, `3½`, `4¼`, `12¼`, `10¾`, `1¼`).
  - Navigation aids and labels: `"Cod Rock Lighted Buoy 5"` (`Fl G 4s 4NM`), `"Fishing Island Ledge Buoy 4"`, `"Portsmouth Harbor (New Castle) Light"` (`F G 52ft 12NM`), `"Stielman Rocks Buoy 3"`, and dashed magenta light sector and channel lines (`266.00° deg`).
  - Seabed nature abbreviations (`S G`, `S`, `P`, `Sh G`, `M`, `R`) and rock/kelp symbols.
  - Tide station diamond icons in pink/white showing real-time predicted heights: `7.35 ft` at Fort Point (New Castle) and `7.53 ft` at Kittery Point (selected, on the eastern shore).
