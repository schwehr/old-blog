# Acoustic Backscatter Mosaic Over Portsmouth Harbor in Google Earth (`backscatter-newcastle.png`)

## Overview

A 3D oblique perspective screenshot from Google Earth showing a grayscale multibeam acoustic backscatter mosaic draped over the Piscataqua River mouth and Portsmouth Harbor between New Castle, New Hampshire, and Kittery/Gerrish Island, Maine.

- **Backscatter Overlay:** A gray-toned hydrographic survey mosaic covers the main navigation channel curving around the eastern tip of New Castle Island (`Fort Point`) toward `Fishing Island` and `Wood Island`.

  - The outer boundaries of the survey area exhibit stepped/jagged edges corresponding to individual sonar survey lines, with transparent `no-data` regions allowing the underlying satellite imagery of the water and shoreline to show through.
