# CCOM/JHC 2007 Performance and Progress Report Cover (`ccom2007-report.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the cover page of the 2007 Annual Performance and Progress Report for the NOAA-UNH Joint Hydrographic Center / Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM/JHC) at the University of New Hampshire, featuring a 3D bathymetric visualization of high-Arctic multibeam mapping over the Chukchi Plateau.

## Header & Title Block (Top)

Set against a pale cyan background box:
- **Logo (Upper Left):** Circular emblem reading `Center for Coastal & Ocean Mapping` • `Joint Hydrographic Center` • `University of New Hampshire` around a stylized blue coastal/sonar swath graphic labeled `CCOM JHC` in white letters.
- **Report Text (Centered Right):**
  - **Title:** `Performance and Progress Report` (large bold black serif font)
  - **Reference Number:** `NOAA Ref No: NA0NOS4001153`
  - **Project Title:** `Project Title: Joint Hydrographic Center`
  - **Report Period:** `Report Period: 01/01/2007 – 12/31/2007`
  - **Principal Investigator:** `Principal Investigator: Larry A. Mayer` (bold italic font)

## Arctic Bathymetry Graphic & Caption (Center & Bottom)

- **3D Seafloor Visualization:**
  - Displays an oblique 3D shaded-relief view of the Arctic Ocean basin and the **Chukchi Plateau / Chukchi Cap** north of Alaska.
  - Colorful multibeam sonar ship tracks and bathymetric swaths (colored in rainbow hues from purple/blue in deep water through cyan, green, yellow, and orange on shallower ridges) trace the survey lines collected during the summer 2007 expedition over the darker blue-gray regional bathymetric base map.
- **Caption (Bottom):**
  - Centered italic serif text on a white background reads:
    `CCOM/UNH mapping this past summer of the Chukchi Plateau in the high Arctic (to 82.17 deg N) has redefined the location of the foot of the continental slope and in doing so may greatly impact the potential extension of U.S. sovereign rights over resources in the Arctic.`
