# CCOM/JHC Digital Coast Pilot 3D Prototype Interface (`ccom_Digital_Coast_Pilot.jpg`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the UNH CCOM/JHC "Chart of the Future" research prototype for a **Digital Coast Pilot**, combining an interactive 3D terrain/bathymetry view of Portsmouth Harbor with georeferenced vertical "billboard" photographs of coastal landmarks and a synchronized, hyperlinked NOAA Coast Pilot text pane on the right.

## 3D Visualization Pane (Left)

- **Bathymetry & Topography:**
  - An oblique 3D perspective looking northward into the mouth of the Piscataqua River / Portsmouth Harbor.
  - High-resolution multibeam bathymetry in the main channel is rendered in shaded grayscale relief, flanked by blue shoal/nearshore water and green terrestrial elevation models of the islands and mainland (including New Castle Island on the left and Gerrish Island / Wood Island on the right).
  - Red and green 3D buoy/beacon markers sit along the navigation channel.
- **Georeferenced Photo Billboards & Leader Lines:**
  - Five upright rectangular photographs stand vertically in the 3D scene, connected by thin magenta leader lines to the specific coastal landmarks they depict:
    - **Upper Left:** A large white historic building complex (`Wentworth by the Sea` hotel on New Castle Island).
    - **Center-Left (Highlighted with a Red Border):** A panoramic shoreline view showing `Portsmouth Harbor (New Castle) Light` (white conical lighthouse tower) and adjacent Fort Point / Coast Guard station buildings.
    - **Upper Center:** A coastal fort/headland structure (`Fort McClary` on Kittery Point).
    - **Upper Right:** Red-and-white channel range markers/lights on a wooded hillside.
    - **Middle Right:** The weathered wooden `abandoned Coast Guard station on Wood Island`.
    - **Lower Center (Foreground):** The gray granite conical tower of `Whaleback Light`.
- **Logo:** In the lower-left corner sits the circular `CCOM / JHC` (`Center for Coastal & Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center / University of New Hampshire`) logo.
