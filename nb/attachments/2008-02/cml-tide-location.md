# UNH Pier and Tide Gauge Location in Dense Fog at New Castle (`cml-tide-location.jpg`)

## Overview

A color photograph taken on a foggy February day at the UNH Judd Gregg Marine Research Complex / Coastal Marine Lab pier in New Castle, New Hampshire, with a yellow circle highlighting the location of the tide gauge and AIS station serial radio link near the floating dock.

## Scene Description

- **Boathouse & Pier:** Looming through thick gray fog in the center background is a large, two-story white clapboard boathouse/support building with a pitched dark gray gable roof and a horizontal band of windows across its upper facade. The building sits atop a dark timber-pile pier with a wooden railing.
- **Floating Dock & Vessels:**
  - A metal gangway ramp angles down from the left side of the pier to a floating dock.
  - Moored along the floating dock in the center is a small white UNH research boat with an enclosed cabin/pilothouse and an outboard motor on the stern.
  - Further to the right, a dark-red/maroon open workboat with a center console or small shelter is tied up alongside the dock, with a smaller boat between them.
- **Highlighted Tide/AIS Sensor Location:** A bright **yellow circle** is drawn around the stern of the white UNH boat, the lower end of the gangway ramp, and the adjacent pier pilings/stilling tubes where the water-level tide sensors and radio modem antenna serial line are installed.
- **Foreground:** In the lower-left foreground, the angled top rail and barbed-wire/chain-link mesh of a dark security fence frame the view overlooking the calm, misty gray harbor water.
