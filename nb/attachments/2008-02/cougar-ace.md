# Car Carrier MV Cougar Ace Listing Severely to Port (`cougar-ace.jpg`)

## Overview
A water-level photograph from the United States Coast Guard (USCG) showing the stern and starboard quarter of the car carrier vessel *Cougar Ace* listing dramatically at roughly 60 degrees to port in calm coastal waters near the Aleutian Islands during salvage operations.
