# 3D Multibeam Bathymetry of Healy Seamount in the Arctic Ocean (`healy-seamount.jpg`)

## Overview
A 3D perspective color-coded multibeam bathymetric rendering against a solid black background depicting **Mount Healy** (Healy Seamount), an undersea mountain in the Arctic Ocean discovered and mapped by the USCGC *Healy* and UNH Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC) rising more than 3,000 meters from the seafloor.

## Bathymetric Visualization Details
- **Color Scale & Elevation**: The surface is shaded using a rainbow bathymetric color ramp coupled with artificial hillshading to emphasize steep gullies, ridges, and scarps:
  - **Deepest Seafloor (Purple / Magenta / Dark Blue)**: Surrounds the base of the seamount on the right and lower foreground, showing individual multibeam swath edges and outer-beam texture.
  - **Lower to Mid Flanks (Cyan / Turquoise / Green)**: Steep, heavily incised flanks rising sharply from the abyssal plain, as well as a broader flat-topped terrace or ridge extending off to the left edge of the image.
  - **Upper Ridge & Summit (Yellow / Orange / Red)**: The highest crest of the seamount sits left of center, rendered in bright orange and red at the peak, with a rugged yellow-green ridge extending toward the upper right where it curves around a semi-circular embayment or crater-like depression before rising to a secondary green peak in the background.
- **Morphology & Swath Coverage**: Radial and linear multibeam survey swath boundaries are visible along the outer margins of the dataset (especially the finger-like swath extensions at the lower left and rippled outer-beam edges along the purple abyssal base on the right).
