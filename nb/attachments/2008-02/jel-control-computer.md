# Shuttle Data Logging Computer Mounted in the Rafters at Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (`jel-control-computer.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up low-angle photograph showing a small-form-factor **Shuttle** desktop computer mounted on an improvised wooden shelf high against the pine wood rafters and ceiling boards at the UNH **Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL)**, serving as the station's AIS and tide gauge data logger.

## Hardware & Mounting Details
- **Wooden Shelf & Bungee Restraint**:
  - An unpainted plywood and dimensional lumber shelf is screwed into the horizontal wood beam below the tongue-and-groove pine ceiling planks.
  - The black-cased Shuttle PC sits atop the wooden platform and is held in place by a red-and-black patterned bungee cord hooked into a metal eye bolt screwed into the front-left corner of the wooden ledge.
- **Rear Panel Connections**:
  - The rear I/O panel of the Shuttle box faces toward the right, revealing a circular exhaust fan grille near the top.
  - Connected to the serial port near the top of the backplate is a white rectangular serial adapter/inline device (with a black `CE` label) wired to a black cable and a small green circuit board edge.
  - Below that are a blue VGA connector, a purple PS/2 keyboard connector, a green PS/2 mouse connector, an Ethernet network cable (black `CAT 5e` jacket visible on the lower right), and white power and serial cables looping upward and coiled against the wood beam to the right.
