# Aanderaa Instruments Datalogger 3634 Front Panel at Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (`jel-tide-control.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of the brushed-aluminum front control panel of an **Aanderaa Instruments Datalogger 3634** inside the tide station enclosure at the UNH **Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL)**, displaying an anomalous water level reading of `0.00 m`.

## Front Panel Layout & Visible Text
- **Header & Identification (Top Left & Center)**:
  - Large black serif lettering at top left: `DATALOGGER 3634`
  - Below the model name: `SERIAL NO: 224` (with `224` handwritten/engraved) and `REFERENCE NO: 736` (with `736` handwritten/engraved).
  - Center-top boxed manufacturer logo: `AANDERAA INSTRUMENTS` | `BERGEN NORWAY` with a stylized wave/instrument emblem on the left.
- **LCD Display (Upper Left)**:
  - A two-line dark-gray liquid crystal display in a recessed rectangular cutout reads:
    - Top line: `T2 Water level          0.00 m`
    - Bottom line: `Interval: Nonstop    No.of channels:03`
- **Rotary Switches & Instructions (Upper Right)**:
  - Top instruction block:
    - `PROGRAMMING:`
    - `- Turn the MODE Switch to the MENU Position`
    - `- Press the MODE Switch towards the SET Position to select one of the choices of the FUNCTION Switch`
  - Two silver knurled rotary selector knobs:
    - Left knob labeled **MODE** below, with positions `OFF`, `ON` (currently selected), `MENU`, and spring-return `SET`.
    - Right knob labeled **FUNCTION** below, with positions `POS`, `CHAR` (currently selected), and `SHIFT`.
  - Legend below the knobs:
    - `• POS  – Moves the cursor`
    - `• CHAR – Enters menus and selects letters and figures`
    - `• SHIFT – Alternates between small and capital letters`
- **Bottom Connectors & Ports**:
  - Bottom left: `CE` compliance mark.
  - A outlined bottom-center group labeled `SENSOR INPUTS (CHANNEL NO.) FOR VR22 OR SR10 SENSORS` contains four black capped circular receptacles numbered `2`, `3`, `4`, and `5`, followed by a fifth multi-pin metallic circular connector labeled `2-5` with a black cable attached.
  - Along the right edge are a black capped circular port labeled `10-BIT PDC-4` and a lower metallic circular power/modem connector labeled `7-14VDC` (with `+` and ground symbols), `RS-232C`, and `MODEM`, with a bright blue cable curving into the bottom foreground.
