# Tide Station and Pier at Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (`jel-tide-station.jpg`)

## Overview
A winter photograph taken on the snow- and slush-covered wooden pier at the UNH **Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL)** on Little Bay / Great Bay in Durham, New Hampshire, showing the tide gauge stilling well, staff gauge, and white weatherproof instrument enclosure at the edge of the dock.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Pier Deck)**:
  - The wooden planks and raised curbing on the left edge of the pier are covered in wet white snow and slush.
- **Tide Station Equipment (Center)**:
  - Mounted vertically off the left side of the pier is a metal tide gauge support frame and cylindrical stilling pipe topped with a flat metal plate.
  - Attached to the vertical support post is a white tide staff gauge marked with black graduations and large numerals `3` (lower) and `4` (upper).
  - Mounted on wooden posts along the chain-railed edge of the pier to the right of the stilling well is a white weatherproof fiberglass or metal instrument box (housing the Aanderaa datalogger) with a clear front window and gray/blue electrical conduits and cables running from its bottom to the sensor assembly.
  - A diagonal guy wire or cable angles across the frame from the lower-left dock cleat toward the upper right.
  - A tall dark post behind the white enclosure holds a dark blue rectangular sign with white lettering reading: `JACKSON ESTUARINE LABORATORY EMPLOYEES ONLY Beyond this Point`.
- **Background (Estuary & Opposite Shore)**:
  - Calm, dark gray-green water with fine ripples fills the middle ground beyond the pier.
  - Across the bay, a tree-lined shoreline with scattered houses and snow patches along the bank stretches across the horizon under a uniform gray overcast sky.
