# Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) Rover Mockup at JPL (`msl-by-judy.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken by Judy at NASA's **Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)** during the 50th anniversary celebration of Explorer 1, showing a full-scale engineering mockup of the **Mars Science Laboratory (MSL)** rover (*Curiosity*) on outdoor display beneath a white canopy tent.

## Scene Description
- **MSL Rover Mockup (Center)**:
  - Viewed from its rear-left (port-quarter) angle, the large six-wheeled rover mockup dominates the space inside a white plastic chain barrier supported by white stanchion posts.
  - **Wheels & Suspension**: Three large black cleated aluminum wheels are visible along the left side (and two on the right side), attached via silver-gray tubular titanium/aluminum **rocker-bogie** suspension struts and vertical steering actuators to the white rectangular rover chassis.
  - **Rear RTG Housing**: Projecting upward at an angle from the aft end of the rover body (right of center) is the white multi-mission radioisotope thermoelectric generator (MMRTG) heat-exchanger housing with dark gray U-shaped cooling tube/fin patterns along its sides.
  - **Mast & Deck**: Rising from the forward deck (left of center) is the vertical Remote Sensing Mast topped by a boxy white camera head (ChemCam / Mastcam housing) with yellow/orange trim, along with black cylindrical and structural components mounted on the rover deck and a black-and-yellow striped cylindrical hazard/antenna fixture on the right.
- **Surrounding Crowd & Canopy**:
  - Above the rover is the metal framework and white fabric roof of a large outdoor event tent, with sunlight filtering through green trees in the background.
  - Visitors and JPL staff stand around the perimeter of the stanchions inspecting and photographing the rover, including a man in a black shirt leaning in on the right and several people in casual jackets, a green long-sleeved shirt, and an orange shirt standing along the left and rear.
