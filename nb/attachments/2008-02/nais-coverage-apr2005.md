# USCG Nationwide AIS (NAIS) Capability Coverage Map, April 2005 (`nais-coverage-apr2005.png`)

## Overview
A map of North America (including Alaska and an inset of Hawaii) from a April 19, 2005 United States Coast Guard (USCG) Maritime Domain Awareness Program Integration Office presentation (`MDA_USCG.ppt`), illustrating the operational status and Common Operational Picture (COP) integration of Coast Guard **Automatic Identification System (AIS)** and Vessel Traffic Service (VTS) sites.

## Legend & Color Coding
In the lower-left corner is a three-item legend titled **AIS Capability**:
- **Solid Bright Green (`In COP`)**: Operational AIS sites currently incorporated into the Common Operational Picture.
- **Cross-Hatched Dark Green (`Not yet in COP`)**: Operational AIS sites that do not yet feed into the COP.
- **Cross-Hatched Light Blue / Cyan (`Projects underway`)**: Planned or under-construction AIS projects not yet operational.

## Geographic Coverage & Labels
- **Alaska (Top Left)**:
  - `VTSPWS` (green text, solid green circle): Vessel Traffic Service Prince William Sound.
  - `Alaska SPAIS` (blue text, three arrows pointing to cyan hatched circles in the Aleutians/Unimak Pass, Kodiak/Cook Inlet approaches, and Southeast Alaska Inside Passage).
- **Hawaii Inset (Far Left)**:
  - `R&DCen` (green text, solid green circle over Oahu/western islands) and `R&DCen` (blue text, cyan hatched circle over neighboring islands).
- **U.S. West Coast**:
  - `VTSPS` (blue text) and `R&DCen` (green text): Pointing to cross-hatched dark green and solid green circles around Puget Sound and the Washington coast.
  - `VTSSF` (blue text) and `R&DCen` (green text): Pointing to a solid green circle at San Francisco Bay.
  - `VTSLA-LB` (green text): Pointing to a dark-green cross-hatched circle at Los Angeles / Long Beach.
  - `SCC-J San Diego` (green text): Pointing to the Joint Navy/Coast Guard Sector Command Center at San Diego.
- **Great Lakes & St. Lawrence Seaway**:
  - `VTSSMR` (green text): Pointing to dark-green cross-hatched coverage around St. Marys River / northern Great Lakes.
  - `St. Lawrence Seaway` (green text): Pointing to dark-green cross-hatched coverage along Lake Erie, Lake Ontario, and the St. Lawrence River.
- **U.S. East Coast**:
  - `VTSNY` (green text): Pointing to New York Harbor.
  - `R&DCen` (green text): Two labels with arrows pointing to wide solid green coastal coverage zones stretching from New England (Massachusetts/Long Island Sound) down through New Jersey, Delaware Bay, and the Chesapeake Bay entrance.
  - `SCC-J Norfolk` (green text): Pointing to the Joint Navy/Coast Guard Sector Command Center at Norfolk / Hampton Roads.
  - `SCC Charleston` (green text): Pointing to a dark-green cross-hatched circle at Sector Command Center Charleston, South Carolina.
  - `R&DCen` (green text): Pointing to solid green circles covering southern Florida, Miami, and the Florida Keys / Straits of Florida.
- **Gulf of Mexico**:
  - `VTSLMR` (green text): Pointing to a solid green circle at the Lower Mississippi River / New Orleans.
  - `VTSHG` (green text): Pointing to the Houston/Galveston area.
  - `D8 Offshore Platforms` (blue text): Pointing to a broad cyan cross-hatched swath covering the Texas and Louisiana offshore oil and gas platform corridor in the northern Gulf of Mexico.
