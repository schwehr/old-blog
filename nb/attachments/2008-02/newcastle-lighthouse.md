# Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse at New Castle, New Hampshire (`newcastle-lighthouse.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical morning photograph of **Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse** (Fort Constitution Light) in New Castle, New Hampshire, showing the white cylindrical cast-iron tower and its wooden access footbridge against a calm harbor and overcast dawn sky.

## Scene Description
- **Lighthouse Tower (Center-Left)**:
  - The tall, slightly tapering white cylindrical tower rises from a circular concrete and stone base platform to a black iron parapet gallery and lantern room at the top.
  - The lantern room features dark glass panes, a black circular gallery railing supported by curved black brackets underneath, and a pointed black dome roof topped with a lightning rod and finial ball.
  - Two small rectangular multi-pane sash windows with decorative pedimented lintels are staggered vertically on the white exterior wall, along with a small dark circular vent hole just below the gallery deck.
  - At the base of the tower facing the walkway is a white wooden entryway door with a curved top arch and black strap hinges and latch hardware.
- **Walkway & Foreground**:
  - A gray-painted wooden walkway/footbridge with a vertical-baluster railing on the right leads from the lower-center foreground straight to the lighthouse door.
  - To the right of the walkway, patches of white snow rest on dark coastal rocks beside the stone foundation wall.
- **Background**:
  - Beyond the lighthouse base, calm gray-silver water stretches across the harbor to a low, dark tree-covered shoreline (Kittery / Gerrish Island, Maine) on the horizon.
  - The sky is filled with soft, textured gray morning clouds with a warm peach/pinkish glow near the horizon to the right of the tower.
