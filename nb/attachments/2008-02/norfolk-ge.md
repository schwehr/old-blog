# Google Earth Satellite View of Town Point Park, Norfolk, Virginia (`norfolk-ge.png`)

## Overview
A top-down satellite/aerial imagery screenshot from **Google Earth** showing the waterfront of **Town Point Park** and the adjacent marina along the Elizabeth River in downtown **Norfolk, Virginia**, marking the site of a shipboard laser scanning (LiDAR) trial conducted aboard the NOAA vessel *Bay Hydrographer*.

## Map & Geographic Features
- **Labels & Markers**:
  - Center: A bold downward-pointing orange arrow icon placed on the lawn of the park, labeled **Town Point Park, Norfolk** in white text with a dark drop shadow.
  - Upper Center-Left: A circular white highway shield reading **337** along the curved multi-lane roadway, with the white road label **Waterfront Drive Acc Road** curving along the northern edge of the park.
  - Upper Right: Vertical white street label reading **Martins Lane**.
- **Waterfront & Structures**:
  - **Town Point Park (Center & Left)**: Green grass lawns with scattered trees, curved pedestrian walkways, a circular amphitheater/plaza near the western (left) seawall, and a broad brick/concrete waterfront promenade bordering the dark waters of the Elizabeth River along the bottom left.
  - **Boat Basin & Vessels (Lower Right)**: Immediately east (right) of the park label is a rectangular recessed boat slip where a large white multi-deck excursion ship (such as the *Spirit of Norfolk*) is moored along the western quay. Further right in the lower-right corner is a marina basin with several smaller white yachts and boats docked at finger piers.
  - **Urban Buildings (Top)**: North of the curving highway are a large white semi-circular commercial building complex (center-top), a construction/cleared lot (top left), and buildings with parking lots (top right).
