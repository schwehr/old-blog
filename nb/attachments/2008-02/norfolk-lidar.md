# Shipboard LiDAR 3D Point Cloud of the Norfolk, Virginia Waterfront (`norfolk-lidar.png`)

## Overview
A 3D perspective grayscale point-cloud rendering against a black background showing the downtown **Norfolk, Virginia** waterfront around **Town Point Park**, created by merging vessel-based Riegl laser scanner (LiDAR) data collected from the moving NOAA survey launch *Bay Hydrographer* with airborne LiDAR data.

## Point Cloud Scene Details
- **Waterfront Promenade & Park (Foreground)**:
  - Across the lower left and center runs the brightly illuminated shoreline bulkhead and fenced promenade of Town Point Park, backed by dense clusters of deciduous trees and open lawn surfaces rendered in bright white and light gray laser returns.
  - To the right of center, the seawall indents into a rectangular boat basin/harbor slip, with another pier and low-roofed pavilion buildings (Waterside area) extending to the lower-right corner.
- **Label Marker**:
  - Near the center of the image, just above the park trees in front of the mid-rise building, is a small red dot accompanied by the white text label **Thursday**.
- **Downtown Norfolk Skyline (Background)**:
  - Rising behind the waterfront park are detailed 3D point-cloud facades of downtown Norfolk high-rise and mid-rise buildings:
    - Left of center: A tall vertical office tower behind a wide, multi-story horizontal building with rows of rectangular windows.
    - Center: A slender spire or tower visible in the distance and smaller commercial buildings.
    - Upper Right: Two prominent tall rectangular high-rise towers (hotel/office buildings) with grid-like window facades standing side by side above lower waterfront structures.
