# Paleomagnetic Principles and Practice Kindle Edition Cover (`tauxe-pmag-kindle.jpg`)

## Overview
A promotional book cover graphic from Amazon showing the Kindle Edition of the geophysics textbook *Paleomagnetic Principles and Practice* by Lisa Tauxe, published by Kluwer Academic Publishers as part of the *Modern Approaches in Geophysics* series.

## Cover Design & Visual Elements
- **Background & Layout**: The main cover features a deep navy-blue background bordered by thin horizontal white lines near the top and bottom.
- **Diagrams**: In the center of the cover are paleomagnetic data plots rendered in white and light blue lines:
  - **Top Row**: Two circular equal-area stereonet projections side by side. The left circle displays scattered directional data points in the upper-right quadrant and fainter points in the lower-left quadrant, with tick marks along the horizontal and vertical axes. The right circle shows two tight, elongated confidence ellipses/clusters in the upper-right and lower-left quadrants.
  - **Bottom Row**: Three square plots arranged horizontally beneath the stereonets, showing frequency distribution histograms (bootstrap/parametric distribution curves) with single and double peaks.
- **Kindle Edition Overlay**: Along the bottom edge is a white banner with gray text reading **"Kindle Edition"**, accompanied in the lower-right corner by a tilted product rendering of a first-generation white Amazon Kindle e-reader displaying a page of text, partially overlapping a diagonal white corner ribbon on the book cover.
