# Wireless Projector Mounted Above the CCOM Wave Tank (`wave-tank-projector.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph of the high-bay engineering laboratory at the University of New Hampshire's Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory (CCOM/JHC), showing a newly mounted wireless projector beaming a real-time wave-staff measurement plot onto the wall above the wave/tow tank.

## Scene Description
- **Projector & High-Bay Structure**:
  - On the right side of the frame, a white vertical steel I-beam column rises from the concrete floor to the exposed white metal roof trusses.
  - Mounted near the top of the I-beam on a black bracket is a silver/gray digital projector, aimed toward the far wall.
  - A bright overhead high-bay light fixture glows intensely near the upper center of the ceiling trusses.
- **Projected Display**:
  - On the upper-left brown and tan paneled wall, a large rectangular projection shows a Windows desktop interface running a data acquisition and plotting program (such as LabVIEW).
  - The projected window features control panels on the left (including a red indicator box) and a dark graph on the right displaying a bright yellow/orange sinusoidal waveform representing water surface elevation over time measured by a wave staff.
- **Wave Tank & Laboratory Floor**:
  - Running horizontally across the middle ground is the concrete wall of the wave tank, topped with a wooden cap rail and fitted with a white circular life ring on its left end.
  - Two long wooden tables sit in front of the concrete tank wall, with several small red objects lined up on the left table.
  - To the right, an A-frame wooden/fiberglass stepladder stands beside the tank wall in front of a large green chalkboard filled with a grid table.
  - In the left background, storage racks hold spools of cable and laboratory gear, and a double door with windows is visible beneath the projected screen.
