# Whale Conservation Research Collage for David Wiley Seminar (`wiley-talk.png`)

## Overview
A three-photo composite collage illustrating whale tagging and conservation fieldwork in the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary, used to announce a CCOM/JHC seminar by Dr. David Wiley (*"Endangered Whale Conservation Research in the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary"*).

## Panel Descriptions
- **Top-Left Panel (Suction-Cup Tagging)**:
  - A researcher wearing a blue life vest, khaki trousers, and a sun visor leans over the bow of a gray rigid-hull inflatable boat (RHIB) on calm gray-blue water.
  - The researcher holds a long, flexible carbon-fiber pole extending to the right, pressing a white suction-cup acoustic/movement tag onto the dark gray back of a surfacing humpback whale just behind its dorsal fin.
- **Bottom-Left Panel (NOAA Research Vessel & Whale)**:
  - In the foreground, the dark back of a whale surfaces in blue ocean water.
  - In the background, the white NOAA research vessel *Nancy Foster* (`NOAA R 352` painted on the bow alongside the circular blue-and-white NOAA emblem) is seen in port-side profile with its superstructure forward and black A-frame/cranes on the aft working deck.
- **Right Panel (Surfacing Right Whales)**:
  - Positioned on the right side overlapping the inner edges of the two left panels, this close-up photograph shows two North Atlantic right whales surfacing side by side in choppy gray water.
  - The whales' arched rostrums and heads display characteristic rough, pale yellowish-white callosities and mottled light-gray skin patches.
