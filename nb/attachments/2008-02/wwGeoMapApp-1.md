# GeoMapApp 1.6.2 Initial 3D WorldWind Globe View (`wwGeoMapApp-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of `GeoMapApp 1.6.2` running the NASA WorldWind wrapper (`haxby.worldwind.WWWrapper`) on Mac OS X, displaying the initial full-Earth 3D globe view with global shaded-relief topography and bathymetry.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Menu & Toolbar**:
  - Mac OS X menu bar showing the application name `haxby.worldwind.WWWrapper` and menus: `File`, `Available Data`, `View`, `Places`, `Help`, plus system status icons on the right.
  - Window title bar: `GeoMapApp 1.6.2`.
  - Upper toolbar beneath the title bar:
    - A floppy-disk save icon button on the far left.
    - A horizontal slider labeled `VE:` (Vertical Exaggeration), with the slider thumb positioned near the far left (minimum exaggeration).
    - Cursor/camera coordinate readouts on the right: `Lat: 45.906°`, `Lon: -75.832°`, `Elev: 330m`, `Alt: 19134km`.

## Globe & Map Overlays
- **3D Globe**: Centered in the black viewport, showing North America, Central America, northern South America, Greenland, the Caribbean, and the North Atlantic Ocean (including the Mid-Atlantic Ridge and broad light-gray continental shelves).
- **Elevation Color Bar (Top Left)**: A vertical color scale bar labeled `km` at the top, ranging from `-6` km (deep blue/purple) through `-4`, `-2`, `0` (gray/green sea level), `2`, `4`, to `6` km (tan/white).
- **Compass Rose (Top Right)**: A circular gray ring with a red north pointer and a small yellow indicator arrow on the upper-left rim.
- **Scale Bar (Bottom Right)**: A white horizontal distance scale bar labeled `2000 Km`.
