# GeoMapApp 3D Perspective of the U.S. East Coast Continental Slope (`wwGeoMapApp-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of `GeoMapApp 1.6.2` (`haxby.worldwind.WWWrapper`) showing a low-angle, obliquely tilted 3D perspective view of the submarine canyons and continental slope off the U.S. East Coast (Mid-Atlantic Bight / New Jersey and Hudson Canyon region) with vertical exaggeration enabled.

## Interface & Readouts
- **Menu & Title Bar**: Mac OS X menu bar (`haxby.worldwind.WWWrapper`, `File`, `Available Data`, `View`, `Places`, `Help`) and window title `GeoMapApp 1.6.2`.
- **Toolbar Controls**:
  - Save icon and `VE:` (Vertical Exaggeration) slider with the knob moved roughly halfway across the track to accentuate submarine relief.
  - Coordinate readouts: `Lat: 39.415°`, `Lon: -73.468°`, `Elev: -39m`, `Alt: 38km`.
- **Overlays**:
  - Vertical elevation/depth scale bar (`km`, from `-6` to `6`) in the upper left.
  - Foreshortened elliptical compass ring in the upper right indicating the tilted camera pitch looking northwest toward the coast.

## 3D Bathymetry & Terrain Details
- **Foreground (Abyssal Rise & Continental Slope)**: Detailed shaded-relief multibeam bathymetry colored in pinkish-tan and gray tones, revealing deeply incised submarine canyons, gullies, and slump scars cutting into the steep continental slope.
- **Middle Ground (Continental Shelf)**: The flat, smooth light-gray continental shelf stretching landward behind the shelf break.
- **Background (Coastline & Land)**: Green and tan coastal plains and highlands along the U.S. East Coast beneath a black sky horizon.
