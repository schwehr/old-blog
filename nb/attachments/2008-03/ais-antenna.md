# Temporary AIS Whip Antenna on R/V Auk (`ais-antenna.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical color photograph taken aboard the NOAA vessel *R/V Auk* during a cruise in the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary, showing a small temporary AIS whip antenna rigged to the vessel's aluminum mast structure.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **Aluminum Mast & Rigging:** On the right side of the frame is the tubular aluminum mast and radar arch structure of the *R/V Auk*. A horizontal yardarm extends to the left near the top of the image, from which a taut white line (halyard) runs vertically down toward the deck.
- **AIS Antenna:** Attached halfway up the white line is a small black vertical whip antenna with radial ground-plane elements sticking out at angles, fed by a black coaxial cable that loops downward and wraps around the aluminum mast tubing.
- **Other Marine Sensors:**
  - Mounted on the horizontal aluminum bar is a white disk-shaped GPS antenna (`GARMIN` visible in small lettering along its top rim) on a short white pole.
  - Below the bar is a white multi-plate radiation shield (gill shield) for an air temperature/humidity weather sensor mounted to the vertical support post.
  - Dual chrome horn speakers are mounted underneath the horizontal platform, and a thicker white fiberglass marine VHF antenna rises on the far right.
- **Foreground & Background:** In the lower left foreground, the head and shoulders of a crew member or researcher ("Mike") wearing an orange life vest/float coat are visible against the white foamy wake of the vessel and a deep blue ocean horizon under a clear blue sky.
