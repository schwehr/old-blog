# Temporary AIS Logging Setup on R/V Auk (`ais-logging.jpg`)

## Overview
A color photograph showing a temporary outdoor AIS data-logging setup tucked into a sheltered corner of the upper deck aboard the NOAA vessel *R/V Auk*.

## Equipment & Scene Details
- **AIS Logging Hardware (Lower Center/Right):**
  - Sitting in the shaded corner on the right is an open silver Apple MacBook Pro / PowerBook laptop with a dark screen.
  - Next to the laptop in the center foreground, behind the tubular aluminum safety railing, is a clear plastic storage bin filled with coiled cables, adapters, and electronics (housing the SR162-G AIS receiver), resting in front of a dark gray plastic crate lid.
  - To the left of the bin on the flat deck surface is a green clipboard holding white paper and a black device with a cable leading toward the bin.
- **Helm & Bulkhead (Left/Center):**
  - On the left is the auxiliary upper-deck aluminum helm station featuring a stainless steel destroyer-style steering wheel, black twin-lever engine throttle/shift controls, a black spherical compass, and a small white magnetic-mount GPS puck antenna resting on top of the console box.
  - A black cable runs from the GPS puck across to the equipment bin, while another black antenna wire runs vertically up the aluminum bulkhead, secured at the top lip with two strips of white tape.
  - A circular white-rimmed speaker is mounted low on the aluminum bulkhead behind the plastic bin.
- **Right Side & Ocean:** On the far right, an orange life ring with silver reflective tape hangs on the railing, with a white-and-black whip antenna mounted to the gunwale and deep blue ocean water visible beyond the vessel.
