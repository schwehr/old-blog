# Gulf of Alaska Coastal Imagery Site – Map Layers (`alaska-coastline-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Gulf of Alaska Coastal Imagery Site** web GIS interface (powered by Geocortex / ArcIMS), showing coastal photopoints and environmental data layers around northern Kodiak Island while the map is loading.

## Interface & Banner
- **Top Banner:** A dark blue header with a white RCAC logo on the left and satellite imagery of Alaska on the right reads:
  - `Gulf of Alaska Coastal Imagery Site`
  - `Sponsored by the Cook Inlet Regional Citizens Advisory Council (CIRCAC) and Exxon Valdez Oil Spill Trustee Council (EVOS)`
- **Navigation Tabs:** Blue tabs across the top read `About`, `Layers`, `Legend`, `Key Map`, `Bookmarks`, `Print PDF`, `Help`, and `Exit`.
- **Toolbar Icons:** Below the tabs is a row of map tool icons: Zoom In, Zoom Out (outlined in red as active), Zoom to Full Extent, Zoom to Previous Extent, Pan (hand), Identify (`i`), Measure/Clear, Image Animation (film camera), and Toolbox (red).
- **Bottom Status Bar:** Displays `Scale: 1: 653,371` with a `go` button, `Map Tool: Zoom Out`, `Active Layer: Kodiak Island - Photopoints 2005`, and a `Powered by geocortex` logo on the right.

## Map & Layer Panel
- **Left Map Panel:** Shows the coastline of northern Kodiak Island and Afognak Island (tan land against light blue water) outlined by thick orange and tan lines representing coastal photo points. A gray progress dialog box in the center reads `Loading map ...` with a red-and-gray striped progress bar.
- **Right Panel (`Map Layers`):** Features a `Feedback` link at the top right and a hierarchical tree of folders and checkboxes:
  - Collapsed folders: `Outer Kenai Photo Points`, `Outer Kenai Environmental Data`, `Upper Cook Inlet Photo Points`, `Upper Cook Inlet Environmental Data`, `Eastern Lower Cook Inlet Photo Points`, `Eastern Lower Cook Inlet Environmental Data`, `Western Lower Cook Inlet Photo Points`, `Western Lower Cook Inlet Environmental Data`.
  - Expanded folder `Kodiak Island Photo Points`:
    - Checked: `Kodiak` and `Kodiak Island - Photopoints 2005` (highlighted with a black `i` active-layer icon).
  - Expanded folder `Kodiak Island Environmental Data`:
    - Unchecked: `Points`, `Shoreline`, `Polygon Boundaries`, `Unit Breaks`, `Dominant Morphology`, `Splash Zone`, `Marsh grasses, Herbs, and Sedges`, `Dune Grasses`, `Sedges`, `Barnacles`.
    - Checked: `Sediment Type`, `Biological Wave Exposure`, `Oil Residence Index`, `Habitat Class`.
