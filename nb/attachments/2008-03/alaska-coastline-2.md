# Gulf of Alaska Coastal Imagery Site – Map Legend (`alaska-coastline-2.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot of the **Gulf of Alaska Coastal Imagery Site** web GIS application, displaying the **Map Legend** panel on the right alongside a zoomed-in view of the Kodiak Island coastline on the left.

## Map View (Left)
- Shows a closer view of bays, inlets, and islands along Kodiak Island with light tan land and pale blue water.
- The shorelines are heavily outlined by thick, overlapping peach/tan and bright orange lines from the `Kodiak` and `Kodiak Island - Photopoints 2005` layers, which partially obscure thinner underlying colored shoreline segments (green, purple, dark red, and cyan) representing environmental attributes.

## Map Legend Panel (Right)
- **Header:** Bold black title `Map Legend` on the left and a blue `Settings` link on the right, separated from the content by a horizontal gray line.
- **Legend Categories & Symbols:**
  - **Places:** Dark red dot for `Land`.
  - **Kodiak:** Light peach dot.
  - **Kodiak Island - Photopoints 2005:** Bright orange dot.
  - **Kodiak Island - Sediment Type** (zigzag line symbols):
    - Dark brown/maroon: `Gravel` (listed twice)
    - Light purple/lavender: `Sand + Gravel` (listed twice)
    - Yellow: `Sand` (listed three times)
    - Cyan/light blue: `Mud`
    - Olive green: `Wetland`
  - **Kodiak Island - Biological Wave Exposure** (zigzag line symbols):
    - Dark maroon: `Very Exposed`
    - Lavender: `Exposed`
    - Light pink: `Semi-Exposed`
    - Cyan: `Semi-Protected`
    - Bright green: `Protected`
    - Yellow: `Very Protected`
  - **Kodiak Island - Oil Residence Index** (zigzag line symbols at the bottom):
    - Pale pink: `days to weeks`
    - Light salmon pink: `weeks to mths`
    - Medium coral red: `wks to months`
