# Gulf of Alaska Coastal Imagery Site – Image Animation (`alaska-coastline-3.png`)

## Overview
A browser window screenshot of the **Gulf of Alaska Coastal Imagery Site** (`http://maps.geocortex.net - Geocortex Internet Mapping Framework (IMF)`), demonstrating the **Image Animation** tool displaying an oblique aerial photograph of the Kodiak Island coastline alongside the interactive map.

## Interface & Map Panel (Left)
- **Window & Banner:** Captured in a Mac OS X browser window with the blue `Gulf of Alaska Coastal Imagery Site` banner at the top, navigation tabs (`About`, `Layers`, `Legend`, `Key Map`, `Bookmarks`, `Print PDF`, `Help`, `Exit`), and map toolbar icons below.
- **Map View:** Shows the Kodiak Island archipelago with tan land and light blue water, bordered by thick orange and peach coastal photopoint tracks.
  - A north arrow icon is in the upper right corner of the map.
  - A black crosshair cursor (`+`) marks the current camera position along an inlet in the center of the map.
  - A red dot labeled `Kodiak` appears near the lower right coast.
  - A black scale bar at the bottom left spans `0` to `17.6mi`, and the lower right corner displays `Map center: 58°1' N, 152°57' W`.
- **Bottom Status Bar:** Shows `Scale: 1: 742,528`, `Map Tool: Zoom Out`, `Active Layer: Kodiak Island - Photopoints 2005`, `Powered by geocortex`, and browser status `Done`.

## Image Animation Panel (Right)
- **Header:** Dark blue title bar reading `Image Animation` with a `? HELP` button on the right.
- **Aerial Photograph:** Displays an oblique low-altitude aerial photo of a shallow braided estuary/tidal flat bordered by dense dark green coniferous forest. White stamped video/photo overlay text across the top of the image reads:
  - `5801N4596 15257W1258`
  - `172308 2005-05-25`
- **Playback Controls:** Below the photograph are a horizontal progress slider bar, media transport buttons (step backward, play backward, pause, play forward, step forward), a `Speed:` slider with `-` and `+` buttons, and three view/zoom icons (including a magnifying glass over an image).
