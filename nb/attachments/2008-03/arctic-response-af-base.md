# Abandoned White Alice Air Force Station at Northeast Cape, St. Lawrence Island (`arctic-response-af-base.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph showing the abandoned, decaying buildings and tropospheric scatter antennas of the former U.S. Air Force Station and White Alice Communications System (WACS) site at Northeast Cape, St. Lawrence Island, Alaska.

## Scene & Structural Details
- **Foreground Operations & Barracks Complex:** Spread across the flat, brownish-green tundra in the lower half of the image is a sprawling cluster of weathered white and gray one- and two-story rectangular military buildings with flat or low-pitched roofs. Several structures show signs of roof collapse, peeling paint, and missing wall panels. To the right stands a separate brown-roofed rectangular warehouse/hangar structure, and dirt roads wind around the perimeter.
- **Middle Ground (White Alice Antennas):** Further upslope in the center middle distance, at the base of the mountain valley, stand four dark, billboard-like parabolic tropospheric scatter ("tropo") antennas angled skyward, flanked by small support buildings.
- **Background Mountains & Weather:** Rising steeply behind the site are dark, rocky, treeless mountains (the Kinipaghulghat Pluton / Kangukhsam Mountain) dotted with lingering patches of white snow in gullies and hollows. A low, thick ceiling of gray fog and cloud shrouds the mountain summits across the top of the frame.
