# Google Earth Location Map of St. Lawrence Island Incident Site (`arctic-response-ge-location.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot showing the regional geography of the northern Bering Sea and Bering Strait between Alaska and Chukotka (Russia), with yellow pushpins marking communities and a simulated Arctic spill/incident workshop location on St. Lawrence Island.

## Geographic Features & Markers
- **Water Bodies:** Deep blue ocean areas are labeled `Chukchi Sea` at the top and `Bering Sea` at the bottom left.
- **Landmasses & Borders:** Yellow coastline outlines trace the Chukotka Peninsula (Russia) on the upper left (partially labeled `tka`) and the Seward Peninsula / western mainland of Alaska on the right.
- **Labeled Placemarks:**
  - `Nome, AK` is marked with a gray square target reticle on the southern coast of the Seward Peninsula.
  - On **St. Lawrence Island** in the center of the northern Bering Sea, three yellow pushpins mark key sites from west to east:
    1. `Gambell` at the northwestern cape.
    2. `Savoonga` along the north-central coast.
    3. `Incident Location` at Northeast Cape on the eastern end of the island.
