# USCG Cutter Healy Position Track on Sailwx (`arctic-response-healy.png`)

## Overview
A map graphic from `www.sailwx.info` showing reported positions of the U.S. Coast Guard icebreaker **USCGC Healy (callsign NEPP)** in the northern Bering Sea southwest of St. Lawrence Island in March 2008.

## Map & Geographic Features
- **Landmasses & Water Bodies:** Tan landmasses with dark brown coastlines against a pale blue ocean background:
  - On the left and top is the Russian Far East (Chukotka), with blue italic labels for `Zaliv Kresta`, `Zaliv Kanchalan`, `Proliv Senyavina` (labeled twice), and the large bay `Anadyrskiy Zaliv` (Gulf of Anadyr).
  - On the right, between latitudes `63 00` and `64 00`, is the distinctive elongated shape of **St. Lawrence Island**.
  - Near the bottom right (`60 00` latitude) is **St. Matthew Island**.
- **Latitude Grid Lines:** Curved gray latitude parallels are labeled along both the left and right margins from `60 00` (`60°N`) up to `65 00` (`65°N`).

## Vessel Track & Labels
- **Header & Title:** `www.sailwx.info` in the top left corner; `USCG Healy (callsign NEPP)` in large black text at the bottom right.
- **Scale Bar (Bottom Left):** A black-and-white segmented bar labeled `0`, `37`, `74`, `111`, `148 mi`.
- **Reported Positions (Red Dots):**
  - A cluster of three red dots southwest of St. Lawrence Island along the `63 00` parallel labeled `1800`, `2100`, and `0000`, with another dot slightly southwest labeled `1500`.
  - Further south between `61 00` and `62 00` latitude: a dot labeled `16-Mar 1200`, and a tight cluster of three dots just north of it labeled `1500`, `1800`, and `2100`.
  - The most recent position is marked by a larger red circle further west near the Russian coast (around `62°15'N`), labeled `18-Mar 1500`.
