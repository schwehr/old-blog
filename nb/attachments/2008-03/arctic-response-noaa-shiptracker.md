# NOAA Ship Tracker Showing Vessels in the Gulf of Alaska (`arctic-response-noaa-shiptracker.png`)

## Overview
A web browser screenshot of the **OMAO NOAA Ship Tracker** (`http://shiptracker.noaa.gov/default.aspx`), displaying the real-time positions and cruise tracks of NOAA vessels operating in Alaskan waters (`DY` – *Oscar Dyson* and `MF` – *Miller Freeman*).

## Browser & Page Interface
- **Browser Bar:** Shows the URL `http://shiptracker.noaa.gov/default.aspx` and a Google search box containing `noaa ship location`.
- **Top Banner:** Features the NOAA emblem and `OMAO NOAA SHIP TRACKER` over a blue graphical banner with `Show All Ships` and `Google Maps Version` links on the right.
- **Left Map Controls:** A vertical zoom slider (with `+` and `-` magnifying glasses) and tool buttons for pan/zoom/identify, a globe reset icon, and a `?` help icon.
- **Right Control Panel:**
  - `Pick a Ship`: Dropdown set to `All Ships`
  - `Pick a Timeframe`: Dropdown set to `Current Cruise`
  - `Pick a Background`: Dropdown set to `Default Image`
  - Link below: `Current Ship Data`
- **Footer:** Displays coordinates `Longitude: -142.3619  Latitude: 63.9713` on the left, `Map Information` on the right, and standard NOAA / U.S. Department of Commerce footer links (`Revised August 17, 2006`).

## Map & Vessel Tracks
- **Base Map:** A shaded relief topographic and bathymetric map of Alaska, the Bering Sea, the Aleutian Islands, and the Gulf of Alaska.
- **Tracked Vessels:**
  - **`DY` (NOAA Ship *Oscar Dyson*):** Marked by a red triangle near Kodiak Island / Shelikof Strait, following a dense zigzag acoustic survey track through Shelikof Strait between Kodiak Island and the Alaska Peninsula.
  - **`MF` (NOAA Ship *Miller Freeman*):** Marked by a red triangle in the eastern Gulf of Alaska off Southeast Alaska (near Sitka/Cross Sound), with a white track line extending westward across the Gulf of Alaska toward Kodiak and Prince William Sound.
  - No NOAA vessels are present in the northern Bering Sea or Arctic region near St. Lawrence Island.
