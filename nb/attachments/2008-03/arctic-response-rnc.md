# Coastal Explorer Raster Nautical Chart of Northeast Cape, St. Lawrence Island (`arctic-response-rnc.png`)

## Overview
A Windows application screenshot of **Coastal Explorer** (`foo - Coastal Explorer`) displaying the NOAA Raster Nautical Chart (RNC) for **Northeast Cape, St. Lawrence Island, Alaska**, along with a planned 115.6-nautical-mile route from Nome, Alaska to Kitnagak Bay (Seevoo Point).

## Chart View (Main Panel)
- **Chart Scale & Orientation:** An inset box at the upper left of the chart shows `1:400,000` with a yellow warning triangle and a blue upward arrow labeled `NORTH UP`.
- **Geographic Features on St. Lawrence Island:**

  - **Named Features:** `ST LAWRENCE ISLAND`, `Seepanpak Lagoon` (large blue coastal lagoon on the left), `Seevoo Pt.` (promontory in the center), and `Northeast Cape` on the right.
  - **Bathymetry & Aids/Lines:** Soundings in fathoms (`1_5`, `3_4`, `6_2`, `7`, `8_4`, `11`, `12`, `13`, `14`, `15`, `16`, `17`, `20`, `21`) and bottom character abbreviations (`G` for gravel, `S` for sand) are plotted offshore, along with a gray `TERRITORIAL SEA (see note X)` boundary line and green/magenta Loran-C lines (`9990-Z-48000`).
- **Plotted Route:** A straight dark blue route line enters from the upper right (from Nome) and terminates in the shallow bay (`Kitnagak Bay`) just east of `Seevoo Pt.`.
