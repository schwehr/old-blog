# St. Lawrence Island Topographic Map Excerpt (`arctic-response-topo.jpg`)

## Overview
An excerpt from a 1970 U.S. Geological Survey 1:250,000-scale topographic map of eastern St. Lawrence Island, Alaska, highlighted with a yellow oval around the Lietnik Landing Area and Kitnagak Bay on the northern coast near Northeast Cape. This map was used during an Arctic spill response workshop ("Opening the Arctic Seas: Envisioning Disaster & Framing Solutions") to examine the location and surroundings of the former Northeast Cape Air Force Station / White Alice communications site.

## Map & Topographic Details
- **Highlighted Area (Yellow Oval)**: A bright yellow oval is drawn along the north-central shoreline encompassing **Kangighsak Pt**, **Lietnik** and **Lietnik Landing Area** (marked with a black aircraft silhouette symbol), **Kitnagak Pt**, **Kitnagak Bay**, **Seevoo Pt**, and benchmark **VABM 26 Stoke** (marked with a triangle).
- **Mountainous Interior**:
  - Directly south of the yellow oval rises **Kangukhsam Mtn** with benchmark **VABM 1820 Heli** and brown topographic contour lines (labeled `1000`).
  - Extending southward through the center of the peninsula are the **Kinipaghulghat Mts** (with a `1210` elevation label, `1000` contour, and southern peak **VABM 1140 Kinnip**).
  - East of the **Tapisaghak River** valley (which flows north toward Kitnagak Bay) rises **Seevookhan Mtn** (peak labeled `1448`) and southern ridges reaching `1025`.
- **Western Tundra & Lagoons**:
  - The low-lying tundra to the west is dotted with dozens of small blue lakes and ponds (one labeled `65`).
  - Along the northwest coast are **Sevak Camp**, **Salghat Beach**, **VABM 30 Panpak**, and the large **Seepanpak Lagoon** with **VABM 16 Seep** on its southern shore.
  - Along the southwest coast is **Maknik Lagoon**, separated from the sea by a narrow barrier spit.
- **Eastern Coast & Northeast Cape**:
  - At the northeastern tip are **Camp Kulowiye**, **VABM 102 Pinnacle**, **Northeast Cape**, and **Eayagit Pt**.
  - Just inland from the eastern shore is **Sivneghak Lagoon**.
  - Further south along the eastern coast are **Niyghapak Pt** (**VABM 20 Hapak**), the long barrier-enclosed **Kiloknak Lagoon**, **VABM 9 Kilok**, and **Apavawook Cape** at the southern tip.
  - Offshore bathymetric contours in light blue mark the `30` and `60` foot depth curves off Northeast Cape.

## Visible Text & Grid Labels
- **Township & Range Grid**: Black grid lines divide the map with vertical range labels `R 56 W`, `R 55 W`, `R 54 W`, `R 53 W` and horizontal township labels `T 25 S`, `T 26 S`.
- **Directional Distance Notes**:
  - Upper right (angled in the Bering Sea): `TO NOME 133 MI.`
  - Middle right (off Niyghapak Pt): `TO NORTH MOUTH OF YUKON RIVER 130 MI.`
