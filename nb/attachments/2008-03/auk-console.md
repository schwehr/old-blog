# Bridge Helm Console of NOAA R/V Auk (`auk-console.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph of the starboard bridge helm station and electronics console aboard the NOAA research vessel *R/V Auk*, used in the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (SBNMS). The vessel is docked alongside a wooden pier visible through the forward and side pilothouse windows.

## Helm & Console Equipment
- **Steering & Propulsion Controls**:
  - A large, polished stainless-steel six-spoke destroyer-style ship's wheel is mounted on the angled wooden-trimmed helm console in the lower foreground.
  - To the right of the wheel are twin black dual-lever engine throttle/shift controls with round black knobs, and further right is a black rotary Simrad jog/steering dial.
  - Beneath the left side of the wheel is a white cylindrical fire extinguisher mounted to the console base, along with red push-button switches and toggle switches.
- **Navigation & Marine Electronics**:
  - **Upper Left Monitor**: A flat-panel LCD monitor mounted on the raised wooden counter to the left displays a large digital depth readout of `2.0 m` above a sonar/fathometer echogram trace window. Next to its base are a white coffee mug with red lettering and a small grey case.
  - **Flush-Mounted Console Displays**: Set into the black angled instrument panel above the wheel are several marine electronics units:
    - A small rectangular digital display at top left and another small display below it.
    - A large central color chartplotter/radar unit (Furuno NavNet) displaying a yellow/green land mass (Cape Cod / Massachusetts coast) against a dark background with an orange target overlay on the left.
    - Beneath the central chartplotter is a square Simrad autopilot control unit (`AP50` style) with a monochrome LCD screen and keypad buttons.

  - **Compass & Side Workstation**:
    - Mounted on top of the dash below the forward window is a black Ritchie magnetic binnacle compass.
    - On the far right edge, a second computer keyboard and LCD monitor sit on a raised wooden shelf with a laminated reference sheet tucked behind them.

Behind it, dark grey cabinet doors and drawers are framed with warm honey-toned wood trim, with a flush-mounted car/marine stereo receiver set into the panel below the left monitor.
