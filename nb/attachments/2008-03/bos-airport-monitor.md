# Boston Logan International Airport PASSUR AirportMonitor Screenshot (`bos-airport-monitor.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the web-based **PASSUR AirportMonitor** flight-tracking interface for **Boston Logan International Airport (BOS)**, showing near-real-time and replay radar tracks of aircraft operating around Boston Harbor and the Greater Boston metropolitan area on March 11, 2008.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Header Bar**:
  - Left: `AirportMonitor™` logo over a faint blue watermark.
  - Center: `Boston Logan International Airport` with a subtitle link `(back to massport.com/logan)`.
  - Right: `POWERED BY PASSUR.com` logo with stylized radar arcs.
- **Mode & Replay Toolbar (Blue Panel)**:
  - **Current Mode**: Contains a green oval `CURRENT` button and a yellow oval `PAUSE` button.
  - **Replay Mode**: Contains dropdown menus under `Enter date & time(eastern)`: `March`, `11`, `2008`, `Hour` `9`, `Minute` `03`.
  - **Playback Controls**: `Select speed & click start:` followed by a green `START` button, a yellow `PAUSE` button, `Help ?`, and four dark blue speed buttons with green play arrows: `NORMAL`, `2x`, `5x`, and `10x`.
- **Right Sidebar Panels**:
  - **MAP RANGE (from center)**: A magnifying glass icon next to five radio buttons: `4 MILES`, `10 MILES` (selected), `20 MILES`, `40 MILES`, and `90 MILES`.
  - **Flight Information**: Prompts `Click on any airplane at left for details`, followed by text fields:
    - `Date/Time`: `3/11/2008 08:56:02`
    - `Flight ID`, `Aircraft Type`, `Altitude`, `Origin`, `Destination` (all blank).
  - **Legend**:
    - Circular purple airplane icon: `General E.L. Logan Intl Airport (BOS)`
    - Green square: `Departures`
    - Blue square: `Arrivals`
    - Grey helicopter icon: `Helicopter`
    - Red square: `Selected`
    - Dark grey square: `In Transit`
