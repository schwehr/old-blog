# Coastal Explorer Cruise Plan with Lighthouse Range Rings (`ce-cruiseplan-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Coastal Explorer** (`cruise-plan-ais-test1 - Coastal Explorer`) running on Windows XP inside a virtual machine, displaying a NOAA vector electronic navigational chart (ENC) of Cape Cod and Stellwagen Bank / Massachusetts Bay. The view highlights a planned vessel route and a custom mark at Highland Light surrounded by three concentric lime-green range circles.
