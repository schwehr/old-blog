# EarthNC NOAA AWOIS Wreck Popup for F/V Marianna in Google Earth (`earthnc-noaa-awois-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** displaying the **EarthNC** KML layer for the **NOAA Automated Wreck and Obstruction Information System (AWOIS)** over Massachusetts Bay and the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (SBNMS). An open information balloon provides details on the sunken commercial fishing vessel **MARIANNA**.

## Map & Layer Details
- **3D Perspective View**: Looking west-northwest across the dark blue waters of Massachusetts Bay toward the coastline of Boston Harbor and the North Shore in the upper background, where yellow highway lines and a dense cluster of pink wreck icons (`WK`) and yellow markers are visible.
- **Sanctuary Boundary & Wreck Markers**:
  - White polygon lines trace the boundary of the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary and offshore zones in the right half of the image.
  - Small pink oval wreck icons labeled `WK` are scattered across the bay; in the lower right foreground, two adjacent pink `WK` icons are labeled `MARIANNA` and `VILLANOVA` in white text, with a red line connecting them.
