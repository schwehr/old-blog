# EarthNC NOAA AWOIS Wreck Popup for F/V Anne El in Portsmouth Harbor (`earthnc-noaa-awois-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** showing a low-angle 3D oblique view of **Portsmouth, New Hampshire**, and the **Piscataqua River** with the **EarthNC NOAA Wrecks** KML layer loaded. An open pop-up balloon displays the NOAA Automated Wreck and Obstruction Information System (AWOIS) record for the sunken fishing vessel **ANNE EL**.

## Map & Scene Details
- **Geographic View**: Tilted toward the horizon under a light blue/violet sky strip at the top. The city of `Portsmouth` (marked with a red-centered dot and white text label on the left) and its road network (including yellow highway lines and the vertical label `US Route 1 Bypass` crossing the river in the foreground) are draped over satellite imagery.
- **River & Wreck Icon**: In the lower foreground along the Piscataqua River channel sits a pink oval wreck symbol (`WK`) labeled `ANNE EL` in large grey-outlined white capital letters, alongside a purple circle icon and a small yellow ring. Two additional purple circle icons appear on land to the left.
