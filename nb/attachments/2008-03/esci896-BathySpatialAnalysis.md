# Four-Panel Google Earth Collage for Bathymetric Spatial Analysis Class (`esci896-BathySpatialAnalysis.png`)

## Overview
A 2x2 composite image of four **Google Earth** screenshots prepared for the UNH CCOM course **ESCI 896: Bathymetric Spatial Analysis** (`200802-ESCI896`), illustrating different techniques for visualizing multibeam bathymetry, 3D Collada models, georeferenced bibliographic citations, and survey trackline metadata in KML.

## Panel Descriptions
- **Top-Left Panel (Portsmouth Harbor / Piscataqua River Multibeam Bathymetry)**:
  - Shows the full Mac OS X Google Earth application window (`Search`, `Places`, and `Layers` sidebars on the left).
  - Under `Temporary Places`, an item titled `Shallow Water Survey` is checked with descriptive notes below it (`This needs to be redone! There are... aliasing issues in going from UTM...`).
  - The 3D view displays a rainbow-colored multibeam bathymetric surface (red/yellow for shallow channel margins, green/blue/purple for deeper channel depths) draped along the winding Piscataqua River between Portsmouth, NH, and Kittery, ME.
- **Top-Right Panel (3D Collada Model of a Ferris Wheel)**:
  - A close-up oblique 3D view in Google Earth (`Image © 2007 DigitalGlobe`) showing a detailed white-truss 3D Ferris wheel model with red passenger gondolas superimposed over satellite imagery of an urban fairground or park (`Eye alt 236 m`).
- **Bottom-Left Panel (Georeferenced Literature Citation in Santa Barbara Basin, CA)**:
  - Shows a rainbow-shaded bathymetric Digital Elevation Model (DEM) of the **Santa Barbara Basin** offshore Southern California, north of the Channel Islands.
  - A yellow pushpin labeled `Santa Barbara Basin, CA - ODP - 2006` has an open information balloon containing:
    - Title: `Santa Barbara Basin, CA - ODP - 2006`
    - Links (`PDF`, `Data page (html)`, `Google Earth KML from will.com`) and an embedded 3D perspective image of the basin bathymetry.
    - Bibliographic citation: `PDF Schwehr, K., L. Tauxe, N. Driscoll, and H. Lee (2006), Detecting compaction disequilibrium with anisotropy of magnetic susceptibility, Geochem. Geophys. Geosyst., 7, Q11002, doi:10.1029/2006GC001378.`
- **Bottom-Right Panel (Multibeam Survey Tracklines & Swath Coverage Popup)**:
  - Displays a 3D view of overlapping multibeam sonar swaths and white survey tracklines marked with numerous numbered yellow pushpins (`28_0`, `25_0`, `15_0`, `22_0`, `9_1`, `40_0`, `41_2`, `7_1`, `7_0`, `53_0`, `3_4`, `29_0`, `30_0`, `2_3`, `24_0`, etc.).
  - Pin `24_0` is selected, opening a white balloon on the right titled `24_0 / Start Of Multibeam Line / 0024_20061114_005127_raw.all.nav`, which embeds a plan-view plot of the individual multibeam swath colored by depth (blue to orange) with a horizontal colorbar at the bottom.
