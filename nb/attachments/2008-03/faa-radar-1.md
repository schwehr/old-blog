# North Truro FAA Radar Dome Viewed from Entrance Road (`faa-radar-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor landscape photograph of the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) long-range radar dome (radome) at the former North Truro Air Force Station in Truro on Cape Cod, Massachusetts, taken during a site visit to nearby Highland Lighthouse on March 17, 2008.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**:
  - An asphalt road with a double yellow centerline runs across the bottom right corner, meeting a paved driveway that curves away toward the center of the frame.
  - On the left side of the driveway stands a weathered split-rail wooden fence over dry brown winter grass, with one angled wooden rail extending right like a gate bar.
  - Flanking the driveway entrance on the left and right are two wooden posts bearing small rectangular red, white, and blue warning/no-trespassing signs.
- **Middle Ground**:
  - On the far left edge rises a tall, slender triangular metal lattice communications/meteorological tower equipped with a small solar panel and white instrument enclosure near its base, alongside a smaller grey post structure in the grassy field.
  - Beyond the open brown field, a dense line of dark green coastal scrub pines and bare deciduous trees spans the horizon.
- **Background (FAA Radome & Sky)**:
  - Rising above the tree line in the center is a large white spherical geodesic radar dome mounted atop a steel-truss tower structure, with a smaller white dome visible just below it at the end of the access road.
  - Above is a pale blue late-afternoon sky filled with soft white and pinkish-grey stratocumulus clouds.
