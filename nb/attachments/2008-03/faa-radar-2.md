# Former North Truro Air Force Station Buildings and FAA Radar Dome (`faa-radar-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph looking across the grounds and decommissioned military buildings of the former **North Truro Air Force Station** (being repurposed as an art center, the Truro Center for the Arts at Castle Hill / Highlands Center) on Cape Cod, Massachusetts, with the white FAA radar dome visible in the background.

## Scene Description
- **Left Foreground (Heating Plant & Smokestack)**:
  - On the far left stands a weathered, peeling cream/yellow-painted concrete utility building connected by metal ductwork to a tall, cylindrical red-and-dark-brown brick smokestack that extends above the top edge of the frame. A vertical metal rung ladder runs up the right side of the brick chimney.
- **Middle Ground (Grounds & Barracks)**:
  - An open area of dry, golden-brown winter grass and low coastal scrub pine bushes surrounds a curving asphalt roadway in the center and right foreground, with a yellow fire hydrant visible near the center-left.
  - On the right stands a two-story, wood-shingled L-shaped barracks building with a low-pitched hipped roof and rows of double-hung windows.
  - In the center distance, between the brick smokestack and the shingled barracks, are several flat-roofed, light yellow and tan concrete/stucco military buildings and a single-story wood-shingled building.
- **Background (FAA Radome)**:
  - Rising behind the central buildings against a clear blue sky with low pinkish-white clouds on the left horizon is the large white spherical FAA radar dome mounted on its steel tower platform, with a wooden utility pole standing directly in front of it.
