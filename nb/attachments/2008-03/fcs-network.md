# U.S. Army Future Combat Systems (FCS) Network Architecture Diagram (`fcs-network.jpg`)

## Overview
An official U.S. Army architectural diagram titled **GLOBAL INFORMATION GRID / LANDWARNET**, illustrating how the **Future Combat Systems (FCS)** five-layer network architecture (`Sensors`, `APPLICATIONS`, `SERVICES`, `TRANSPORT (Telecommunications)`, and `STANDARDS`) integrates into the broader military Global Information Grid across air, land, and naval forces.

## Diagram Structure & Layers
- **Top Headers & Brackets**:
  - A wide black curly bracket at the top spans the entire diagram under the title `GLOBAL INFORMATION GRID`.
  - Inside it, a green curly bracket and vertical dashed green lines bound the central cylinder under the heading `LANDWARNET`.
  - Vertical labels run along the far left (`INFORMATION MANAGEMENT`) and far right (`NETWORK OPERATIONS`) edges of the outer cylinder.
- **Layer 1 — Sensors & Platforms (Top Red/Green Dome & Disc)**:
  - **Central Green Dome (`Sensors`)**: Depicts Army and FCS manned and unmanned ground/air platforms with text labels: `APACHE`, `CL I`, `CL IV`, `SOLDIER`, `ARV-A(L)`, `MULE-C`, `UGV`, `MULE-T`, `NLOS-LS`, `UGS`, `NLOS-C`, `NLOS-M`, `MV`, `ICV`, `FRMV`, `C2V`, `RSV`, `MCS`. Flanking the dome on the green band are `ABRAMS`, `CMD TOC`, `DCGS-A(V3)` on the left and `DCGS-A`, `BRADLEY`, `PATRIOT` on the right.
  - **Left Red Wing (`C2 CONSTELLATION` - Air Force)**: Shows aircraft and satellites labeled `MC2A`, `TSAT`, `AWACS`, `CV22`, `RQ-4A`, `JSTARS`, and `F22`.
  - **Right Red Wing (`FORCENET` - Navy / Marines)**: Shows aircraft, troops, and warships labeled `E2C`, `F35`, `MARINES`, `EFV`, `CVN` (aircraft carrier), and `DDX` (destroyer).
- **Layer 2 — `APPLICATIONS` (Light Green Disc)**:
  - Central cylinder shows a screenshot of a tactical map/matrix interface above the label `FCS BATTLE COMMAND`.
  - Inner green ring labels: `ASAS`, `FBCB2`, `AMDWS`, `BCS3`, `MCS` on the left; `JC2`, `DTSS`, `IMETS`, `TAIS`, `AFATDS`, `GCCS-A` on the right.
  - Outer light-green ring labels: `MEDICAL`, `GCCS` on the left; `BUSINESS APPLICATIONS` on the right.
- **Layer 3 — `SERVICES` (Yellow Disc)**:
  - Central cylinder displays three software architecture block diagrams titled `Standard Edition`, `Real Time Edition`, and `Micro Edition` above the label `FCS SOSCOE` (System of Systems Common Operating Environment).
  - Inner green ring labels: `PUB/SUB`, `DNS`, `LDAP`, `LINK 16` on the left; `USMTF`, `WEB SERVICES`, `JVMF` on the right.
  - Outer yellow ring labels: `SW DIST FROM CENTRAL LOC`, `NCES`, `WEB SERVICES` on the left; `ASSURED COMPUTING SERVICES`, `EMAIL DELIVERY` on the right.
- **Layer 4 — `TRANSPORT (Telecommunications)` (Blue Disc)**:
  - Central cylinder and inner green ring show tactical radio and satellite hardware icons labeled `JTRS`, `SINCGARS`, `EPLRS`, `WIN-T PoP`, `SCAMP`, `HMS`, `WIN-T`, `GMR`, `TCDL`, `CDL`, `SMART-T`, `WIN-T PoP`, `TROJAN SPIRIT`, `LINK 16`.
  - Outer blue ring labels: `SATCOM`, `TELEPORT`, `DISN`, `MSS` on the left; `RF NETS`, `COMMERCIAL FIBER`, `WIRELESS COMMS` on the right.
- **Layer 5 — `STANDARDS` (Bottom Purple/Green Disc)**:
  - Outer purple ring labels: `DOCTRINE`, `SPECTRUM`, `GOVERNANCE` on the left; `ARCHITECTURE`, `ENGINEERING`, `POLICY` on the right.
