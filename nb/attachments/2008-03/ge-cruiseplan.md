# Google Earth View of Exported Coastal Explorer Cruise Plan (`ge-cruiseplan.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** displaying a 10-waypoint vessel cruise plan exported from **Coastal Explorer**, overlaid on satellite imagery of Massachusetts Bay, Cape Cod, and the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (SBNMS).

## Map & Route Details
- **Geographic Coverage**:
  - On the left is the eastern Massachusetts coastline from Boston Harbor (`Suffolk`, with highway shields for `1` and `93`) south along the South Shore past Scituate and `Plymouth` (with highway shields for `495` and `44`, and `Bristol` at the bottom left).
  - At the bottom right is the curved hook of outer **Cape Cod** (Provincetown and Truro).
  - Filling the center and right is the dark blue water of **Massachusetts Bay** and the Atlantic Ocean.
- **Exported Cruise Plan & Marks**:
  - **Loop Route**: A thick white polygon line with circular white-and-black waypoint nodes traces a four-sided clockwise loop starting at Scituate Harbor (where several closely spaced waypoints overlap at the harbor entrance), heading north-northeast to a turn point off Cohasset/Minot's Light, running east across Massachusetts Bay and Stellwagen Bank to a northeastern waypoint, angling south-southeast parallel to the outer sanctuary boundary to a southeastern waypoint east of Cape Cod, and returning west-northwest across the tip of Cape Cod back to Scituate.
  - **Additional Marks**: Two standalone circular waypoint icons mark **Highland Light** on the outer forearm of Cape Cod and an inland/harbor reference point near Boston/Quincy on the left.
- **Sanctuary & Shipping Lane Overlays**:
  - Thin white lines trace the trapezoidal boundary of the **Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary** and the shipping lanes leading north-northwest toward Boston.
