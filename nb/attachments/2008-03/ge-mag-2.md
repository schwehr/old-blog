# Geomagnetic Field Lines and Magnetic Equator in Google Earth (`ge-mag-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Google Earth** globe displaying a 3D visualization of Earth's main geomagnetic field lines and magnetic inclination/dip equator (created by Stefan Maus of NOAA using Andy Jackson's historical main field model and the International Geomagnetic Reference Field, IGRF).

## Globe & Magnetic Field Visualization
- **Perspective & Base Globe**:
  - The Earth is shown in space against a black starfield, surrounded by a soft purple/blue atmospheric glow halo.
  - Yellow coastline and border outlines show North America, Central America, the Caribbean, and northern South America beneath the semi-transparent magnetic field overlay.
- **3D Magnetic Field Lines & Dip Equator**:
  - Dozens of meridional ribbons of 3D magnetic field lines arch above the Earth's surface from the Southern Hemisphere to the Northern Hemisphere, converging tightly into a bright blue focal point at the **North Magnetic Pole** in the Canadian Arctic (near the top center of the globe, slightly offset from the geographic North Pole located further upper-right).
  - **Color Coding by Inclination/Hemisphere**:
    - North of the magnetic equator, the field lines and vertical curtain fans are shaded in **blue** and **white** as they curve downward into the North Magnetic Pole.
    - Along the **magnetic equator** (dip equator, where magnetic inclination is zero), a prominent **yellow/cream** band wraps diagonally across the lower portion of the globe over the eastern equatorial Pacific and northern South America.
    - South of the yellow magnetic equator band, the field lines and curtain fans transition to **red/pink** and **white**.
