# Google Earth Magnetic Field Lines Model (`ge-mag-3.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from Google Earth showing a 3D globe visualization of Earth's magnetic field lines (meridional compass-pointing lines) and magnetic equator, generated from Stefan Maus's (NOAA) historical and IGRF geomagnetic field model animations (covering 1590 to 2010).

## Globe & Geographic Features
- **Perspective**: The 3D Earth globe is centered over the Western Hemisphere, showing North America, Central America, the Caribbean, the northern and central portions of South America, Greenland (covered in white ice at the top right), the Atlantic Ocean, and the western edge of Europe and Northwest Africa along the right limb.
- **Boundaries & Coastlines**: Political borders and coastlines are traced in bright yellow lines across the continents and islands.
- **Atmosphere & Background**: The globe is surrounded by a soft blue-violet atmospheric glow set against a black space background with scattered tiny white stars.

## Magnetic Field Overlay Details
- **Field Lines (Blue and Red)**: Numerous closely spaced meridional lines curve northward and southward across the globe, converging toward the North Magnetic Pole in the Canadian Arctic Archipelago (near Ellesmere Island / northern Canada, where the blue lines form a dense blue cluster).
  - North of the magnetic equator, the field lines are rendered in **blue**.
  - South of the magnetic equator, the field lines transition to **red**.
- **Magnetic Equator (White/Light Yellow Line)**: A continuous, smooth white/pale-yellow curve runs horizontally/diagonally across the southern part of the globe, passing through the eastern Pacific Ocean, across Peru and Brazil in South America, and northeastward across the tropical Atlantic Ocean toward West Africa, marking the boundary where magnetic inclination is zero and the field lines change color from red to blue.
