# Distant View of Highland Light and FAA Radar Dome from Sea (`highland-light.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide telephoto photograph taken from the NOAA research vessel *R/V Auk* at sea during a Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (SBNMS) cruise, showing the distant coastal bluffs of Cape Cod (North Truro, Massachusetts) with the FAA radar dome and Highland Light silhouetted on the horizon.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Water)**: Choppy blue ocean water spans the lower third of the frame, with small whitecaps glinting in the sunlight.
- **Middle Ground (Coastal Bluffs)**: A long, flat-topped dark brown and gray-blue bluff line stretches horizontally across the entire width of the image, bordered at its base by a thin pale strip of sandy beach.
- **Horizon Landmarks**:
  - On the left side of the bluff, a prominent spherical **FAA radar dome** (radome) on a tower rises above the ridge line.
  - Just to the right of the radar dome stands the vertical cylindrical tower of **Highland Light** (Cape Cod Light).
  - Near the far right edge of the horizon, another small vertical tower silhouette is visible against the sky.
- **Sky**: A clear, pale peach-tinted/hazy sky occupies the upper half of the frame.
