# Highland Lighthouse and Keeper's House on Cape Cod (`highland-lighthouse-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A sunny outdoor photograph of Highland Light (also known as Cape Cod Light) and its attached keeper's house in North Truro, Massachusetts, viewed along the sandy approach path.

## Scene Description
- **Lighthouse Tower (Right of Center)**: A tall, cylindrical white brick tower topped by a black cast-iron lantern room with glass panes and two circular black gallery walkways (catwalks) with metal railings. A small window is visible on the upper shaft just below the lower gallery.
- **Keeper's House & Connecting Buildings (Center)**: Attached to the base of the lighthouse via low white connecting wings with red gabled roofs is a two-story dark gray/weathered-shingle keeper's dwelling. The house features a steep reddish-brown gable roof with dormers, two white chimneys with black caps, white window trim, and a small white entry staircase.
- **Foreground & Landscape**:
  - A wide, sandy/dirt driveway curves from the lower right toward the lighthouse complex in the center.
  - Along the left side of the path runs a rustic split-rail wooden fence bordered by dry winter/early-spring brush, grasses, and a wind-sculpted green pine tree.
  - Leafless shrubs and evergreen bushes flank the right side of the path.
- **Sky**: A clear, bright blue sky spans the background.
