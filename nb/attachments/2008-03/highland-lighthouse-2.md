# Close-Up of Highland Lighthouse Lantern Room and Catwalks (`highland-lighthouse-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A low-angle close-up photograph looking up at the upper tower, lower gallery catwalk, and glass-enclosed lantern room of Highland Lighthouse on Cape Cod, Massachusetts, against a clear blue sky.

## Architectural Details
- **Tower Shaft**: The upper section of the cylindrical white-painted masonry tower fills the lower half of the frame, illuminated from the right by bright sunlight. A recessed rectangular multi-pane window (4x4 grid) with stone lintel and sill is centered just below the lower gallery platform.
- **Lower Catwalk (Gallery)**: A black-painted projecting circular deck with a thin black metal handrail and vertical balusters wraps around the tower above the window. On the white wall inside the lower catwalk (left of center) is a small, arched-top white metal access door with a latch—the door Kurt mentions stepping out onto—and a vertical metal ladder mounted on the right side leading up to the upper gallery.
- **Upper Catwalk & Lantern Room**: Above the white watchroom level sits a second black circular catwalk with a metal railing surrounding the cylindrical glass-paned lantern room. Inside the lantern room, the optical beacon apparatus and interior structural framing are faintly visible through the curved glass panes.
- **Dome & Finial**: The lantern room is capped by a black conical dome roof with a central ventilator ball and lightning rod finial extending to the top edge of the frame.
