# 3D Perspective Shaded-Relief Map of the Arctic Ocean Bathymetry (`ibcao.png`)

## Overview
A 3D perspective shaded-relief visualization from the International Bathymetric Chart of the Arctic Ocean (IBCAO), illustrating the submarine basins, ridges, continental shelves, and surrounding land topography of the Arctic region.

## Geographic & Bathymetric Features
- **Foreground (Bottom)**: Rugged brown and tan mountain ranges of Alaska (Brooks Range and Alaska Range) and the Yukon/Mackenzie region of northwestern Canada, with green river valleys (such as the Yukon and Mackenzie deltas) and the deep blue Gulf of Alaska / Aleutian trench cut off at the bottom center-left edge.
- **Central Ocean Basin (Arctic Ocean)**:
  - A broad, pale-white and light-blue shallow continental shelf (the Chukchi and East Siberian shelves on the left) drops off steeply into the deep blue **Canada Basin** in the center.
  - Across the middle of the deep blue Arctic basin run prominent submarine ridges—including the **Alpha-Mendeleev Ridge**, **Lomonosov Ridge**, and **Gakkel Ridge**—separating the Amerasian Basin (foreground/center) from the Eurasian Basin (upper center/right), which connects through the Fram Strait toward the North Atlantic at the top right.
- **Left Side (Siberia / Russia)**: Green coastal lowlands and tan uplands of Chukotka and eastern Siberia bordering the wide white continental shelf.
- **Right Side (Canadian Arctic Archipelago & Greenland)**: The complex network of islands and channels of the Canadian Arctic Archipelago in green and tan leads toward **Greenland** on the upper right, which is crowned by a smooth, elevated yellow-tan ice sheet plateau.
