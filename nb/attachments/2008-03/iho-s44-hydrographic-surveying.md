# Cover of IHO Standards for Hydrographic Surveys, Special Publication No. 44 (`iho-s44-hydrographic-surveying.png`)

## Overview
An image of the front cover of the International Hydrographic Organization (IHO) publication *IHO Standards for Hydrographic Surveys, 5th Edition, February 2008 (Special Publication N° 44)*.

## Visual Elements & Layout
- **Top Section**:
  - Header text at top center: `INTERNATIONAL HYDROGRAPHIC ORGANIZATION`.
  - Top left corner: The official IHO emblem/crest in gold, blue, and red, featuring a fouled anchor, globe, and monogram above a ribbon banner reading `IHB MONACO BHI`.
  - Below the header is a cutout photograph of a large white hydrographic survey ship with a yellow funnel, mast with signal flags, and stern A-frame, angled from bow (left) to stern (right).
- **Middle Section (Title & Swath Graphic)**:
  - Centered black title text over a gray-and-rainbow shaded-relief multibeam bathymetry background:
    - `IHO Standards for Hydrographic Surveys`
    - `5th Edition, February 2008`
    - `Special Publication N° 44`
  - A semi-transparent blue-white triangular sonar swath fan projects downward from the hull of the survey ship onto a color-coded 3D multibeam bathymetric surface (shaded in brown, tan, green, and blue).
- **Bottom Inset Strip & Footer**:
  - Four small rectangular inset illustrations run horizontally near the bottom:
    1. A 3D schematic cross-section diagram of a navigation channel with depth and clearance annotations.
    2. A 3D visualization of multiple survey vessels mapping a seafloor with sonar swaths and offshore platforms.
    3. A nautical chart excerpt showing depth contours, fairways, and coastlines.
    4. A photograph of harbor dredging/construction equipment and pilings along a waterfront.
  - Bottom right footer text: `Published by the International Hydrographic Bureau`.
