# Eastern Point Lighthouse on Cape Ann, Massachusetts (`ma-epl-1-the-lighthouse.jpg`)

## Overview
An overcast outdoor photograph of Eastern Point Light at the tip of Cape Ann in Gloucester, Massachusetts, taken by Mike Thompson during a site visit to test AIS antenna reception and data logging.

## Architectural & Scene Details
- **Lighthouse Tower (Center)**: A cylindrical white-painted brick tower rising from a low grassy mound. The tower is topped by a black gallery platform with curved metal support brackets and a black iron railing, enclosing a glass-paned lantern room with a red conical roof and ventilator ball. A vertical pole mast mounted on the left side of the gallery carries a white sensor/beacon housing and a thin whip antenna above it.
- **Attached Structures**:
  - **Left**: A covered white wooden walkway and vestibule with dark reddish-brown shingled gable roofs and small square windows steps down to the left toward the edge of the white clapboard keeper's house (partially visible at the far left margin). Underneath the raised walkway is dark latticework.
  - **Right**: A small, flat-roofed white fog-signal / equipment building abuts the right base of the tower, with a brown fuel storage tank bearing a red hazard diamond placard positioned in front of it.
- **Grounds & Weather**: The lighthouse sits on a small brownish-green winter grass hillock with a metal chain-link fence sloping down toward the ocean on the lower right. Heavy, uniform gray overcast clouds fill the sky.
