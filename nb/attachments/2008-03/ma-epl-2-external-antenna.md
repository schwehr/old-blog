# Kurt Schwehr with External Marine AIS Antenna on Eastern Point Light Catwalk (`ma-epl-2-external-antenna.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken by Mike Thompson on the exterior gallery catwalk of Eastern Point Light in Gloucester, Massachusetts, showing Kurt Schwehr standing next to a temporarily mounted marine AIS whip antenna overlooking the Atlantic Ocean.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **Person (Kurt Schwehr)**: Standing on the left side of the frame beside the lantern room window, wearing glasses, a black zip-up jacket over a dark shirt, and blue jeans, with his hands in his jacket pockets and looking at the camera.
- **AIS Antenna Setup (Center)**: A tall, white fiberglass standard marine VHF/AIS whip antenna with a black tip is temporarily clamped to a vertical post of the black iron catwalk railing using a silver metal bracket/duct-tape mount just below the spherical post finial.
- **Lantern Room & Catwalk**:
  - Along the left edge is the black metal framing and rain-speckled glass panes of the lighthouse lantern room.
  - A black-painted iron railing with vertical balusters runs across the lower right half of the frame.
- **Background**: Far below the railing in the lower right corner, brownish-red coastal granite rocks and white surf are visible at the shoreline. Beyond stretches a wide expanse of dark gray-blue ocean meeting an overcast gray sky at a flat horizon.
