# SR162G AIS Receiver Setup Inside the Eastern Point Light Lantern Room (`ma-epl-3-sr162g-recv.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph taken by Mike Thompson inside the lantern room of Eastern Point Light, showing an SR162G AIS receiver with a black whip antenna resting on the interior window ledge against rain-streaked glass.

## Equipment & Scene Details
- **AIS Receiver Box**: A compact, rectangular black metal housing sits on the angled black metal window frame at the lower right. White silk-screened text on the top of the unit reads `SR162G` and `AIS Receiver` (oriented upside-down relative to the viewer).
- **Connections & Antenna**:
  - **Left side of unit**: A BNC/TNC coaxial connector attaches to a tall, black rubber-duck style vertical whip antenna rising up the left side of the frame. Above it are a smaller gold SMA connector (for GPS) and a power/control jack with thin black and red wires trailing downward.
  - **Right side of unit**: A beige DB-9 serial cable is plugged into the right side of the receiver, curving away to the right toward the logging laptop.
- **Window & Exterior View**: Through the raindrop-covered glass pane of the lantern room, the black iron railing of the exterior gallery catwalk is visible, looking steeply down onto brownish granite shore rocks and white ocean surf breaking against the coast.
