# Kurt Schwehr Logging AIS Data Inside Eastern Point Light (`ma-epl-4-datalogging.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle indoor photograph taken by Mike Thompson inside the lantern room of Eastern Point Light, showing Kurt Schwehr crouching on the floor and typing on an Apple MacBook Pro laptop while logging AIS data.

## Scene Description
- **Person (Kurt Schwehr)**: Crouched in the confined walkway space of the lantern room, wearing a black baseball cap backwards (with white embroidered text reading `Mars Lander...` across the back arch), glasses, a black jacket over a dark shirt with white cuffs, and blue jeans. He is looking down at the screen and typing with both hands on a silver 15-inch Apple MacBook Pro resting on his knees.
- **Cables & Equipment**: White power/adapter cords and a serial-to-USB cable loop across the foreground from the upper window ledge down to the laptop. A black coaxial cable runs along the floor base to the left.
