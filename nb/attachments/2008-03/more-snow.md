# Late-March Snowfall Over Garage and Wooded Yard (`more-snow.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical outdoor photograph looking out an upper window at a fresh late-March snowfall covering a detached garage, driveway/yard, and surrounding trees in New Hampshire on March 28, 2008.

## Scene Description
- **Garage (Lower Left Foreground)**: The hipped roof of a light sage-green clapboard garage is blanketed in a smooth, fresh layer of white snow. Below the roof eave, two green garage doors with upper rows of square 4-pane windows are visible, along with a black exterior coach lantern mounted on the right wall corner.
- **Yard & Ground (Bottom Center)**: Smooth, undisturbed white snow covers the ground and driveway area to the right of the garage, extending back toward a line of bare trees.
- **Trees & Woods (Right and Background)**:
  - On the right side, the overhanging branches of a large deciduous tree and dark green hemlock/evergreen boughs are heavily frosted with clinging wet snow.
  - In the background, tall bare deciduous trees stand in a misty, snow-filled haze, with a neighbor's light-colored house/building faintly visible through the trunks in the middle distance.
- **Sky**: A pale, uniform gray-white snowy sky fills the upper left.
