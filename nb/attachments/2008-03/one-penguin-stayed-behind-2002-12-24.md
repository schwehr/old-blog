# Lone Penguin Facing Ship While Seven Others Flee Across Ice Floe (`one-penguin-stayed-behind-2002-12-24.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle photograph taken on December 24, 2002, from the research vessel icebreaker *RVIB Nathaniel B. Palmer* in Antarctica, showing a single Adélie penguin standing in the foreground looking toward the approaching ship while seven other penguins hurry away toward the edge of the ice floe.

## Scene Description
- **Ice Surface**: The entire frame is filled with a bright, sunlit, lumpy snow-and-ice floe covered in footprints, hummocks, and blue-tinted shadows cast by the low-angle polar sun. In the upper right corner, dark blue open ocean water is visible at the edge of the floe.
- **Lone Foreground Penguin (Bottom Left)**: A single penguin stands upright in the lower-left quadrant, facing the camera (and the oncoming icebreaker) with its white belly exposed, wings held slightly out to its sides, and a long dark shadow stretching behind it across the snow.
- **Retreating Group of Penguins (Upper Right)**: Near the upper right, a group of seven penguins is seen from behind (showing their black backs and outstretched flippers for balance) waddling and scurrying away across the bumpy snow toward the dark water at the top right corner.
