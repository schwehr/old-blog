# Kurt Schwehr with Boy Scouts Order of the Arrow Lodge Members (`order-of-the-arrow.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor group photograph showing eight Boy Scouts in uniform wearing Order of the Arrow sashes standing in a classroom alongside guest speaker Kurt Schwehr (on the far right) after his presentation on careers in ocean mapping.

## People & Attire
- **Eight Boy Scouts (Left to Center-Right)**: Eight young men stand in a staggered row facing the camera and smiling.
  - All eight wear standard tan short-sleeved Boy Scout uniform shirts with council/troop shoulder patches, rank badges, and dark green Scout trousers or blue jeans.
  - Seven of the Scouts clearly display white **Order of the Arrow (OA)** sashes draped diagonally over their right shoulders, decorated with the signature red arrow emblem.
  - Two Scouts in the front center-right also wear red neckerchiefs around their collars and long-sleeved undershirts (one dark gray/black, one green) beneath their short-sleeved uniform shirts.
- **Kurt Schwehr (Far Right)**: Standing to the right of the Scouts with his hands in the front pockets of his blue jeans, wearing glasses and a dark navy/black long-sleeved pullover shirt with a small white logo (`CCOM`) on the left chest.
