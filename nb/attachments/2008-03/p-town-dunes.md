# Provincetown Dunes and Salt Marsh Inlet (`p-town-dunes.jpg`)

## Overview
- **Image Type:** Landscape photograph
- **Subject / Context:** A wide-angle coastal view of a salt marsh inlet and sand dunes near Provincetown ("P-Town"), Cape Cod, Massachusetts, taken on March 17, 2008, during a trip to visit Highland Lighthouse and surrounding sites.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Water:** A wide, shallow tidal inlet or salt marsh channel dominates the left and center foreground. The clear water displays a striking, vibrant turquoise and emerald-green color rippled by the wind. Along the right edge, a sloped shoreline revetment of light gray granite blocks or concrete slabs is bordered by dry, golden-tan winter marsh grass.
- **Middle Ground:** Low islands and banks of golden-brown salt marsh grass extend into the channel from both the left and right sides. On the right bank, a row of weathered wooden pilings (remnants of an old pier or bulkhead) juts out into the water along a sandy beach.
- **Background & Sky:** Across the flat expanse of golden marshland rises a line of coastal sand dunes covered in bare, brownish-gray winter scrub and trees, with bright patches of exposed white-tan sand visible on the dune faces. Above, the sky is completely clear and pale blue, fading toward white at the horizon.
