# Annotated RM808 Class A AIS Setup on R/V Coastal Surveyor (`rm808-old-setup.png`)

## Overview
- **Image Type:** Annotated photograph
- **Subject / Context:** An interior photograph of the bridge/electronics bench aboard the UNH research vessel *R/V Coastal Surveyor* (taken in 2006 and posted March 3, 2008), showing how an L-3 Klein / Raytheon / Nauticast RM808 Class A Automatic Identification System (AIS) transponder was wired and set up for research.

## Scene Description & Equipment
- **Left Side (Navigation Display):** A dark gray Dell flat-panel LCD monitor sits on the white counter, with a white label strip across the top bezel reading `R/V Coastal Surveyor` and a red Dymo tape label on the upper left corner.
  - The screen displays hydrographic/navigation chart software showing a nautical chart of `Furber Strait` and `Adams Pt` (Great Bay / Piscataqua River region, New Hampshire) with depth contours and a red/green cross-track error bar at the bottom.
  - A black wired optical mouse and a blue `CARIS` mousepad sit in front of the monitor, with handwritten pencil notes (`~50 cm...`) on the white surface.
- **Center & Right (AIS Hardware & Annotations):** Three orange ovals and white sans-serif text labels highlight the key components of the AIS installation:
  - **`AIS Cable`:** White text with a white pointer line at top center indicates a coiled loop of thick black coaxial/data cable hanging from the wooden trim strip along the bulkhead.
  - **`Power Supply`:** Circled in orange behind the AIS unit is a beige/tan benchtop DC power supply (with black side heat-sink fins and a front screw-terminal strip with red and black leads attached).
  - **`AIS Unit`:** Circled in orange in the lower right foreground is the black **Raytheon Marine** (Nauticast RM808) Class A AIS Minimum Keyboard and Display (MKD) unit. It features a green backlit LCD screen, soft-key buttons along the left and bottom of the screen, a four-way directional arrow pad on the right, and a full alphanumeric keypad across the bottom half.
  - **`Junction Box`:** Circled in orange to the right of the AIS unit is a dark gray rectangular junction box resting on the wooden shelf lip.
- **Bulkhead Wiring:** Above the power supply and junction box, the carpeted cabin wall holds a metal deck/bulkhead gland fitting, a gray metal distribution box with red and black binding posts/switches, a vertical black fuse/terminal block at upper right, and bundles of yellow, green, red, and gray marine wiring.
