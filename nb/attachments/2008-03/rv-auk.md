# NOAA Research Vessel R/V Auk in Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (`rv-auk.jpg`)

## Overview
- **Image Type:** Photograph
- **Subject / Context:** The NOAA Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (SBNMS) research catamaran *R/V Auk* heading back toward its berth in Scituate Harbor, Massachusetts, at the end of a field day on March 24, 2008.

## Scene Description
- **The Vessel (*R/V Auk*):** Viewed from astern and slightly to the starboard quarter, the unpainted aluminum twin-hull (catamaran) research vessel moves away from the camera across calm harbor water, leaving a gentle white wake behind its twin transoms:
  - **Stern & Aft Deck:** The name `Auk` is painted in dark blue on the center transom bridge between the twin hulls, with a circular blue-and-white NOAA emblem on the port transom corner. A silver aluminum A-frame gantry rises over the stern deck, fitted with blocks and cables. A crew member wearing a bright red float coat/jacket, dark pants, and a cap stands on the aft deck near the cabin door and ladder.
  - **Hull & Fenders:** Bright orange spherical buoys/fenders and cylindrical white/gray fenders hang along the starboard gunwale and hull side. Faded dark lettering along the starboard hull side reads `Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary`.
  - **Superstructure & Mast:** The stepped aluminum cabin has dark-tinted windows along the side, an open aft door, a flybridge/upper deck with a dark canvas cover and white equipment boxes, and a central aluminum mast tower supporting a white radar dome, GPS/VHF whip antennas, and navigation lights.
- **Background:** Calm greenish-blue harbor water dotted with small white mooring balls and a green channel buoy (`green can`) on the right. Along the distant shoreline, coastal houses, piers, a seawall, bare late-winter trees, and a building with large letters `S H Y C` (Scituate Harbor Yacht Club) are illuminated by warm, low late-afternoon sunlight.
