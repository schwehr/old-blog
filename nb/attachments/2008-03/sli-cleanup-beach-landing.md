# Beach Landing of Cleanup Barge at St. Lawrence Island (`sli-cleanup-beach-landing.png`)

## Overview
- **Image Type:** Photograph (extracted from a U.S. Army Corps of Engineers report)
- **Subject / Context:** A shallow-water landing craft / push-boat and flat-deck cargo barge grounded on Cargo Beach (Kitnagak Bay) at Northeast Cape, St. Lawrence Island, Alaska, during the 2005 Formerly Used Defense Sites (FUDS) White Alice Tram and Debris Removal cleanup operation.

## Scene Description
- **Landing Craft / Tug (Left):** On the left, floating in calm, dark blue coastal water just off the beach, is a dark-hulled, twin-bow-post workboat/landing craft with a yellow sheer stripe and an elevated white pilothouse mounted on black steel support legs above the deck:
  - A plaque on the front of the white pilothouse reads `BRISTOL BAY` / `CHIGNIK` (or similar vessel name).
  - The open deck beneath the pilothouse carries red and orange gear/containers, and the squared bow pushes directly against the stern of the barge.
  - Above the pilothouse roof is a mast fitted with radar scanners, floodlights, and VHF/GPS whip antennas.
- **Flat-Deck Barge & Ramp (Center to Right):**
  - A rusted, dark gray steel flat-deck barge extends from the bow of the workboat right onto the gravel/sand beach, with a black rubber tire fender hanging along its port side.
  - At the bow of the barge (right), two tall black steel A-frame derrick/ramp towers rise vertically, supporting cables for a heavy steel loading ramp lowered onto the dark sandy/gravelly beach.
  - Four workers are visible near the bow and ramp: one wearing light blue coveralls walking up the beach at the base of the ramp, one standing on the ramp, and two standing on the forward deck between the black A-frame towers. A fifth worker in a white hard hat or shirt is visible near the center of the barge deck.
- **Environment:** Small white shorebreak ripples lap against the dark brown/gray coarse sand beach in the foreground under a clear, cloudless blue sky.
