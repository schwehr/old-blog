# Northeast Cape FUDS Cleanup Site Location Map, St. Lawrence Island (`sli-cleanup-site-map.png`)

## Overview
- **Image Type:** Engineering / environmental remediation site map (CAD drawing with red markup overlays)
- **Subject / Context:** Figure 3-1 (`NE Cape Work Areas, White Alice Tram and Debris Removal, Northeast Cape, St. Lawrence Island, Alaska`) from the September 2006 U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (Alaska District) FUDS Removal Action Report, prepared by Bristol Environmental & Engineering Services Corporation.

## Map & Callout Details
- **Geographic Layout:** The map covers the coastal plain and mountain drainage moving inland (south) from the northern coastline of Northeast Cape on St. Lawrence Island:
  - Along the top shoreline, `BERING SEA` is labeled on the left and `KITNAGAK BAY` on the right.
  - A rectangular runway outline in the upper-left quadrant is labeled `AIRSTRIP (5000' X 150') ELEV. 21' MSL`.
  - A network of site roads (`CARGO BEACH ROAD`, `AIRSTRIP ROAD`, tramway/trail routes) and stream crossings (`STREAM CROSSING #1` through `#10`) extends southward from the coast past the central `FORMER AFS OPERATIONS AREA` and `BORROW AREA` up into the southern mountain work areas.
- **Red-Outlined Cleanup Work Areas:** Twelve distinct red polygons and rectangles highlight specific cleanup and debris removal zones across the map:
  - **Coastline & Kitnagak Bay (Top):**
    - Red box near the northern point north of the airstrip (`POLE GROUP A` / `MARSTON MATTING`).
    - Red box on the Kitnagak Bay shoreline labeled `BARGE LANDING AREA (CARGO BEACH)` and `CARGO BEACH DEBRIS`.
    - Adjacent red box inland from Cargo Beach labeled `SITE 3` / `CARGO BEACH ROAD DEBRIS`.
  - **Mid-Section / Airstrip & Operations Area:**
    - Two elongated red ovals along the road and pole lines (`SITE 2`, `POLE LINE #1`, `SEASONAL SUBSISTENCE CAMP`).
    - Rounded red enclosure west of the main road (`POLE GROUP`, `METALLIC DEBRIS`, `TRAM ANCHORS`).
    - Tilted red rectangle on the far left enclosing `SITE 25` and `SITE 24` (`POLE GROUP D`, `STEEL ANTENNAS`).
    - Red rectangle east of the road labeled `SITE 7`.
    - Small red square near the road junction and a larger red square outlining the `FORMER AFS OPERATIONS AREA`.
  - **Southern Mountain / Tramway Area (Bottom):**
    - Red rectangle south of the Ops Area labeled `SITE 31`.
    - Long diagonal red polygon along the upper tramway/mountain road enclosing `SITE 32` at the top and `SITE 33` / `SITE 34` (White Alice tropospheric scatter site on Kangukhsam Mountain) at the southern terminus.
- **Grid, Legend, Notes & Title Block:**
  - The background has a fine coordinate grid labeled `EASTING (FEET)` along the top and `NORTHING (FEET)` along the interior right axis, referenced to `SURVEY CONTROL POINT BSM-D (ORIGIN OF COORDINATES) N 100,000 E 100,000 ELEV. 75.87 FT. MSL`.
  - In the lower right of the map frame are sections for `NOTES` (3 numbered notes), `LEGEND` (`MSL`, `'`, `ELEV.`, `FT.`), a north arrow, and a bar scale (`0`, `1000`, `2000`, `4000` `APPROXIMATE SCALE IN FEET`).
  - Along the right border is the standard engineering title block showing the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Alaska District logo, contractor logo (`Bristol ENVIRONMENTAL & ENGINEERING SERVICES CORPORATION`), `Contract No: DACA85-02-C-0011` (and `W911KB-04-C-0019`), `Date: September 2005`, `Drawn By: MTG`, `Checked By: SAJ`, `Reference Number: D-1`, and `Figure 3-1` / `Project No: 25037`.
