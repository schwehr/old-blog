# Google Earth St. Lawrence Island Incident & Response Route Map (`sli-incident.png`)

## Overview
- **Image Type:** Annotated Google Earth screenshot
- **Subject / Context:** A regional satellite map created for Group E at the March 2008 *Opening the Arctic Seas: Envisioning Disaster & Framing Solutions* workshop at UNH, depicting a simulated maritime incident at Northeast Cape, St. Lawrence Island, Alaska, and the 115-nautical-mile response route from Nome.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Geographic Features & Placemarks:**
  - **St. Lawrence Island (Lower Left):** Outlined in yellow in the Bering Sea, with three placemarks along its northern and eastern shores:
    - `Gambell` (yellow thumbtack pin at the northwest cape).
    - `Savoonga` (light green paddle pin on the north-central coast).
    - `Incident Location` (red/coral paddle pin with a black star on Northeast Cape at the eastern end of the island).
  - **Seward Peninsula / Alaska Mainland (Upper Right):** `Nome` is marked with a red paddle pin (`A`) on the southern coast of the Seward Peninsula, just east of `Sledge Island` (labeled in cyan text). The Yukon Delta coastline appears in the lower right corner, and a bit of the Russian Chukotka peninsula is visible at the upper left corner.
- **Route Overlays & Labels:**
  - **`Equipment Transport Route` (Green Line):** A straight bright-green line connects `Nome` directly across the Bering Sea (approx. 115 NM southwest) to the `Incident Location` at Northeast Cape, labeled in bold green sans-serif text `Equipment Transport Route`.
  - **`Tug & Barge` (Red Track Line):** A multi-segment red line traces the path of the simulated incident vessel (`Tug & Barge`, labeled in dark red bold text) coming up from the south, rounding the east side of St. Lawrence Island, and turning southwest into Northeast Cape to terminate at the `Incident Location` pin.
- **Google Earth Interface Elements:**
  - Upper right: Standard Google Earth navigation compass ring (`N`), directional arrows, zoom slider (`+` / `-`), and tilt slider.
  - Lower left: Scale bar labeled `122 km`.
  - Bottom center: Copyright text `© 2008 Europa Technologies`, `Image © 2008 TerraMetrics`, `Image NASA`.
  - Bottom status bar: `Pointer 64°24'40.68" N 166°53'06.54" W elev 0 m Streaming |||||||||| 100%` and `Eye alt 413.84 km` below the `©2007 Google` logo.
