# Nautical Chart Comparison at Northeast Cape, St. Lawrence Island (`sli-rnc.png`)

## Overview
- **Image Type:** Nautical chart software screenshot (Coastal Explorer)
- **Subject / Context:** A quilted view in Rose Point Coastal Explorer at `1:800,000` scale showing the dramatic boundary between the detailed NOAA Raster Nautical Chart (RNC) covering the northern/eastern edge of Northeast Cape, St. Lawrence Island, and the sparse Electronic Navigational Chart (ENC) vector coverage to the south and west.

## Chart & Interface Details
- **Upper-Left Scale Widget:** A rectangular inset box in the top-left corner displays `1:800,000` next to a yellow warning triangle (`!`), a blue upward-pointing compass arrow, and the label `NORTH UP`.
- **Lower-Left & Bottom Half (Vector ENC View):**
  - Displays a highly generalized tan landmass labeled `St Lawerence Island` [sic] and `Apavawook Cape`, bordered by flat blue shallow-water tint and light gray-white deeper water with only a single sparse sounding (`24`) and three small islet dots off the southeastern cape.
- **Upper-Right Quadrant (Raster Nautical Chart Inset):**
  - Shows a scanned paper NOAA chart covering the waters north and east of `ST LAWRENCE ISLAND` (`Northeast Cape`, `Seepanpak Lagoon`, `Seevoo Pt`, and a circular landmark symbol labeled `PARABOLIC ANTENNA` representing the former White Alice site).
  - The offshore area is densely populated with sounding numbers (in fathoms, ranging from `6`–`12` nearshore to `14`–`23` offshore), bottom type abbreviations (`G`, `S`, `rky`), a curved gray boundary line labeled `CONTIGUOUS ZONE (see note X)`, and overlapping green (`9990-Z-47800`, `47850`, `47900`) and magenta (`9990-X-17775`, `17800`, `17820`, `17840`, `17860`, `17880`, `17900`, `17920`) Loran-C hyperbola grid lines, along with gray dashed lines (`9990-Y-30250` through `30550`).
