# Truro Public Safety Facility and Communications Tower (`truro-cell-tower.jpg`)

## Overview
- **Image Type:** Photograph
- **Subject / Context:** Exterior view of the Truro Public Safety Facility (344 Route 6, Truro, Cape Cod, Massachusetts) and the tall lattice cellular/communications tower rising directly behind it, photographed on March 17, 2008, during a site survey at nearby Highland Lighthouse (which has direct line-of-sight to this tower for strong EVDO cellular reception).

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Building:**
  - Across a dormant, yellowish-brown winter lawn stands a traditional Cape Cod-style single-story municipal building clad in gray clapboard/shingle siding with crisp white trim and a weathered gray shingled pitched roof.
  - Four gabled dormers (two on the left, two on the right) flank a large central triangular pediment with a four-pane sunburst/fanlight window over the recessed front entrance porch.
  - White columns support the entrance portico, where a dark sign board hangs above a glass front door bearing the street number `344`. An orange traffic cone sits beside the right column.
  - A white flagpole stands on the lawn just right of center with a U.S. flag flying briskly to the left in the wind. A bare deciduous tree stands at the far left, an evergreen tree grows near the right front windows, and yellow security bollards line the bottom-right drive.
- **Communications Tower (Upper Right):**
  - Rising high into the clear, bright blue sky behind the right side of the roof is a tall, three-legged steel lattice communications tower equipped with multiple horizontal triangular antenna arrays holding cellular/EVDO panel antennas and vertical whip antennas.
