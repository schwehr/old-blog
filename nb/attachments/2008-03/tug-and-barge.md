# Tugboat Towing a Container Barge at Sea (`tug-and-barge.jpg`)

## Overview
- **Image Type:** Marine photograph
- **Subject / Context:** A wide horizontal telephoto shot of an articulated/oceangoing tugboat towing a loaded container barge on a long hawser across Massachusetts Bay / Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary, photographed from the NOAA research vessel *R/V Auk* on March 24, 2008.

## Scene Description
- **Tugboat (Right):** On the right side of the frame, a red-and-white tugboat steams toward the right (east/southeast) with a white bow wave and foaming wake trailing astern. It has a dark lower hull, a red main deckhouse, a white upper pilothouse, and a tall mast rigged with radar and navigation antennas.
- **Towline & Wake (Center):** A long, thin white wake trail and submerged/catenary tow hawser stretch across the wide gap of open, choppy dark-blue sea from the stern of the tug on the right to the bow of the barge on the left.
- **Container Barge (Left):** Following on the left is a dark-hulled ocean barge stacked three to four tiers high with white (and a few red and green) intermodal shipping containers. White water breaks beneath the raked bow of the barge as it is pulled through the swells.
- **Sky & Sea:** The horizon line is crisp and level across the lower third of the frame, separating the deep blue, wind-rippled ocean from a clear, pale blue-gray afternoon sky.
