# Book Cover: Arc Marine – GIS for a Blue Planet (`ArcMarine_cvr_lg.jpg`)

## Overview
The front cover image of the ESRI Press book *Arc Marine: GIS for a Blue Planet* by Dawn J. Wright, Michael J. Blongewicz, Patrick N. Halpin, and Joe Breman.

## Visual Design & Composition
- **Upper Background (Nautical Map):**
  - A pale blue and teal cartographic globe/map view sits across the upper third of the cover, showing latitude and longitude graticule lines, coastal outlines, and spaced serif letters `C E A N` from the word "OCEAN".
- **Lower Background (Underwater Scene):**
  - The map blends smoothly into a deep cobalt and navy blue underwater photograph showing five silver-and-blue fish with bright yellow longitudinal stripes and yellow forked tails swimming toward the right.
- **Typography & Credits:**
  - **Main Title:** `Arc Marine` in large, bold white sans-serif lettering with a subtle drop shadow, centered across the middle of the cover.
  - **Subtitle:** `GIS for a Blue Planet` in white italic sans-serif text directly below the main title.
  - **Authors (Bottom Right):** Stacked in clean white sans-serif text:
    - `Dawn J. Wright`
    - `Michael J. Blongewicz`
    - `Patrick N. Halpin`
    - `Joe Breman`
  - **Bottom Watermark:** `Arc Marine` repeated in oversized, semi-transparent dark blue letters spanning the bottom edge of the cover.
