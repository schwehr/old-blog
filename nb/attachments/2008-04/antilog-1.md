# AntiLog RS232 Serial Data Logger OEM Board – Component Side (`antilog-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of an OEM AntiLog RS232 serial data logger circuit board resting on a light oak wood desk, connected to a gray serial splitter/pass-through cable and a black DC barrel power cord.

## Hardware Details & Layout
- **AntiLog Circuit Board (Right):**
  - A compact square green PCB with four corner mounting holes.
  - **Top Left of PCB:** Black cylindrical DC barrel power jack with a black power cable plugged into it, next to a surface-mount electrolytic capacitor and inductors/regulators.
  - **Center of PCB:** A large square surface-mount microcontroller chip bearing a circular white inspection sticker printed with red text reading `PASSED` / `PASS` and `T3`.Above the chip is a silver crystal oscillator can.
  - **Right Edge of PCB:** Two black dual-row male pin headers running vertically along the edge.
  - **Bottom Edge of PCB:** White silkscreen text reading `REV F6` and `AntiLog SN ASL/16/1457` (partially visible), along with a smaller 14-pin surface-mount IC (`MAX...` RS232 transceiver).
- **Serial Cable & Splitter (Center to Bottom Left):**
  - A gray DB9 connector with long thumbscrews is plugged into the left side of the AntiLog PCB; its top shell carries a white label printed with `ALOG`.
  - A short gray round cable curves to the lower left, terminating in the center of a gray T-shaped dual-DB9 serial pass-through adapter (allowing the logger to tap into an RS232 connection between two devices).
