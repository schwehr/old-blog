# AntiLog RS232 Serial Data Logger OEM Board – SD Card Slot Side (`antilog-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of the underside of the OEM AntiLog RS232 serial data logger circuit board on a wooden surface, showing the metal SD card socket and a blue SD memory card alongside the connected serial tap cable and power wire.

## Hardware Details & Layout
- **AntiLog PCB Underside (Right):**
  - Green square circuit board flipped over to reveal a metallic surface-mount Secure Digital (SD) card holder occupying the center of the board.
  - White silkscreen text along the left edge of the board (oriented vertically) reads `AntiLog Rev F6` and `(c) Anticyclone Systems Ltd, 2006`.
  - White silkscreen markings near the bottom read `SCL1` `94V0` `0629` next to a triangle logo.
  - Through-hole solder joints for the pin headers and DB9 connector are visible around the perimeter.
- **SD Memory Card (Top Right):**
  - A blue SD card lies face-down above the circuit board, exposing its gold contact pins at the bottom edge.
- **Serial Pass-Through Cable & Power Cord (Left to Center):**
  - The gray DB9 plug connects to the left side of the PCB.
  - The gray cable extends left to the T-shaped dual-DB9 serial pass-through adapter, which now shows its top white sticker labeled `P1` and `P2` with directional arrows pointing toward the two DB9 ports.
  - A black-and-white striped two-conductor DC power wire runs along the bottom from the barrel plug on the PCB toward the left edge of the frame.
