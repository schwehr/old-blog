# Comparison of OEM and Enclosed AntiLog RS232 Serial Data Loggers (`antilog-3.jpg`)

## Overview
An overhead photograph on a wooden desk comparing two AntiLog RS232 serial data logger units from Anticyclone Systems Limited: the bare OEM circuit board on the left and the opened plastic enclosure of the boxed retail unit on the right, showing its main board, daughterboard with coin-cell battery, and four enclosure screws.

## Hardware Components & Visible Labels
- **Bare OEM AntiLog Board (Center Left):**
  - Green square PCB (`REV F6`) with serial number label reading `AntiLog SN ASL/16/1457` along the bottom edge and `(c) Anticyclone Systems Ltd 2006` along the left.
  - Features a DB9 serial connector on the left, a black DC power jack at top left next to a silver `220 16V` electrolytic capacitor, a silver crystal oscillator, and a central square microcontroller with a round white/red/blue `PASSED T3` sticker.
- **Mounting Screws (Top Left):**
  - Four silver Phillips-head self-tapping screws resting on the wood surface above the OEM board.
- **Upper Enclosure Half with Main Board (Top Right):**
  - Light gray plastic shell with a rectangular cutout on the right side for battery compartment access.
  - Houses an identical `REV F6` AntiLog main PCB with serial number label `AntiLog SN ASL/16/1587` (without the circular sticker on the main QFP microcontroller).
- **Lower Enclosure Half with Daughterboard (Bottom Right):**
  - Light gray plastic shell containing a green daughterboard (`AntiLog DCARD`, `REV D1`, `SCL1 94V0`, `(c) Anticyclone Systems Limited, 2007`) with serial label `ASL/18/861` and a `RoHS 2002/95/EC` checkmark emblem.
  - Holds a silver `Panasonic CR2032 3V` lithium coin-cell battery for real-time clock backup and a 14-pin dual-row female socket header on the right that mates with the main board's pin header when the case is closed.
  - To the right of the daughterboard is a black foam block inside the 9V battery compartment (`9V` molded into the plastic floor) and a white sticker on the inner right wall reading `S/No. ASL/16/1587`.
