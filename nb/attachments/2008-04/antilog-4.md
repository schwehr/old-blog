# Disassembled Retail AntiLog Serial Data Logger Enclosure and PCB (`antilog-4.jpg`)

## Overview
An overhead photograph of the disassembled retail AntiLog RS232 serial data logger on a wooden surface, showing the front control panel with `Off` and `On` pushbuttons, the underside of the main PCB with an inserted SD card, the back plastic shell with its regulatory label, the sliding battery compartment door, and four case screws.

- **Enclosure Screws (Top Right):**
  - Four silver self-tapping Phillips screws lying on the wood grain to the right of the PCB.
- **Front Enclosure Half (Bottom Left):**
  - Light gray plastic housing featuring a white rectangular front label bordered in black.
  - Top controls: A large red circular pushbutton labeled `Off` on the left, a small recessed indicator LED in the center, and a large green circular pushbutton labeled `On` on the right.
  - Center text: `AntiLog` in large black sans-serif letters above `www.anticyclone.co.uk`.
  - Bottom text: `Anticyclone Systems` in italic text with an upward-pointing arrow along the bottom right border.
- **Rear Enclosure Half & Battery Door (Bottom Center & Right):**
  - Rear gray plastic shell showing four corner screw holes, a cutout at the bottom for the battery compartment cover, and the DB9 connector protruding from the top edge.

  - To the far right lies the detached gray plastic sliding battery compartment lid.
