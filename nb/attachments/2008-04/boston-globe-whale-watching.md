# The Boston Globe Front Page: Whale-Watching Boats Study (`boston-globe-whale-watching.jpg`)

## Overview
A scan of the upper half of the front page of *The Boston Globe* from Monday, April 28, 2008 (`VOLUME 273 NUMBER 119`), featuring a lead science story by Beth Daley titled **"Whale-watching boats are putting whales in jeopardy, study says"** accompanied by an infographic illustrating voluntary speed restriction zones around whales in the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary.

## Main Article & Infographic
- **Headline & Byline (Right Column):**
  - Headline: **Whale-watching boats are putting whales in jeopardy, study says**
  - Subheadline: *Skippers ignoring speed limits in effort to wow passengers*
  - Byline: **By Beth Daley** (`GLOBE STAFF`)
  - Article text discusses a study published in *Conservation Biology* led by Dave Wiley (research coordinator for the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary) and funded by the International Fund for Animal Welfare, which used undercover GPS tracking on 46 trips in 2003–2004 and found commercial whale-watching vessels frequently exceeded voluntary speed limits near whales. Also quotes Steve Milliken, owner of the Dolphin Fleet Whale Watch in Provincetown.
- **Infographic (Center):**
  - Caption at top: **Commercial whale watching vessels** *rarely complied with voluntary speed restrictions near whales, a recent study documented.*
  - Shows a surface illustration of a white multi-deck whale-watching boat above an underwater view of two humpback/right whales and a white signpost reading: **WHALE HABITAT – Speed restrictions are designed to prevent collisions with whales and to minimize chances of disturbing them.**
  - Overlaid in the lower right is a circular bullseye diagram showing a boat trajectory (dotted black line with black vessel silhouettes) cutting across three concentric speed-limit zones around a whale icon at the center (`Violations to voluntary speed limits occurred in all zones.` / `Speeding occurred most often in Zone 3.`):
    - **ZONE 1:** `100 feet to about 1/4 mile – No more than 8 m.p.h.`
    - **ZONE 2:** `About 1/4 to 1/2 miles – No more than 11.5 m.p.h.`
    - **ZONE 3:** `1/2 to 2.2 miles – No more than 15 m.p.h.`
  - Credit line at bottom of graphic: `SOURCE: Dave Wiley, Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary` | `JAVIER ZARRACINA / GLOBE STAFF`.
