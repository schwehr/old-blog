# Many Eyes Bubble Chart of Maritime Casualties (`casualty-gcaptain-manyeyes.jpg`)

## Overview
A packed-circle bubble chart created on IBM's *Many Eyes* visualization platform and featured on *gCaptain.com*, illustrating the relative volume or cost of different categories of maritime casualties using proportionally sized colored circles.

## Chart Details & Data Labels
The visualization consists of 14 non-overlapping colored circles arranged on a white square canvas bordered in gray. Nine of the circles contain white centered text labels with the casualty type and numerical value (in thousands, `K`), while five smaller circles are unlabeled due to size:

- **Labeled Circles (from largest to smallest):**
  - **Grounded (`137K`):** Very large tan/beige circle dominating the upper-left quadrant.
  - **Engine Failure (`65.7K`):** Large olive-green circle in the lower-left quadrant.
  - **Sank (`41.6K`):** Medium-large dusty rose/pink circle near the lower center.
  - **Shift of Cargo (`33.9K`):** Medium-large terracotta/brick-red circle in the upper-right center.
  - **Machinery Failure (`29.8K`):** Medium brown/bronze circle at the bottom center.
  - **Collision (`22K`):** Medium slate-blue/indigo circle on the middle-right edge.
  - **Fire (`21.9K`):** Medium dark olive-green circle at the top right.
  - **Adrift (`21.9K`):** Medium lavender/periwinkle circle in the lower-right corner.
  - **Heavy... (truncated, likely Heavy Weather):** Small-medium golden-yellow circle on the far right edge.
  - **Miss... (truncated, likely Missing):** Small dusty-pink circle on the middle-left edge.
- **Unlabeled Smaller Circles:**
  - Five small circles tucked between the larger bubbles in shades of gold/ochre (between *Grounded*, *Fire*, and *Shift of Cargo*), pale green (above *Sank*), light yellow-green and dark reddish-brown (between *Engine Failure* and *Sank*), and medium blue-purple (between *Machinery Failure* and *Adrift*).
