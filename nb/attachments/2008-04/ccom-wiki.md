## Overview
A screenshot of a Mac OS X web browser displaying the Main Page of **CCOMwiki** (`http://localhost:8080/index.php/Main_Page`), the internal MediaWiki knowledge base for the Center for Coastal & Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC) at the University of New Hampshire.
