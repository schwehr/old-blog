# Cellular Wireless Technology Comparison Table (`digi-remote-wireless-table.png`)

## Overview
A tabular chart from Digi International's *Remote Wireless Networking 101* guide comparing cellular wireless data technologies across generations (1G through 3G), listing their connection types, theoretical maximum speeds, carrier maximum speeds, and typical real-world throughput in kilobits per second (Kbps).

## Table Design & Structure
The table consists of 6 columns and 12 data rows (plus a header row), bordered by thin black grid lines:
- **Header Row:** Styled with a medium-dark blue background and bold white centered sans-serif text.
- **Data Rows:** Alternate between white and light steel-blue background fills with black centered text.
