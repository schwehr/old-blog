# Foggy Highway While Driving West to Cornell (`driving-west-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect photograph taken through the windshield of a car while driving west toward Cornell University in early April 2008, capturing dense fog rising over a two-lane rural highway as sudden warm weather melted the roadside snow.

## Scene Description
- **Roadway:** A wet, two-lane asphalt highway stretches straight ahead into the center distance. A double yellow centerline divides the lanes, and a solid white fog line marks the right edge of the pavement.
- **Roadside & Snow:** Along the right side of the road, a grassy brown embankment rises toward a dark stand of evergreen and deciduous trees. Small patches of unmelted white snow remain visible at the base of the ditch along the right shoulder, along with a thin vertical roadside reflector post.
- **Fog & Trees:** Thick, ghostly white and cool blue-gray fog blankets the highway ahead, completely obscuring the distant horizon and enveloping tall pine trees along the left side of the road.
- **Lighting & Color Palette:** The scene is suffused with cool, muted cyan, slate-gray, and dark forest-green tones under an overcast sky.
