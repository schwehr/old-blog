# Eastern Point Light Schematic and Reference Photos (`eastern-point-light-schematic.png`)

## Overview
A composite architectural elevation schematic and photographic reference sheet of **Eastern Point Light** in Gloucester, Massachusetts, drawn over a light gray square grid background with three inset photographs showing the lighthouse structure and its existing VHF antenna installation.

## Schematic Drawing
- **Background Grid:** The entire canvas is covered by a uniform light-gray engineering graph-paper grid.
- **Lighthouse Tower (Center):** A black-and-white line drawing depicts the slightly tapered cylindrical tower of Eastern Point Light rising from the center of the complex. It features a single rectangular window midway up the shaft, a railed gallery deck at the top, a glass lantern room housing a vertical orange light element, and a conical roof topped by a spherical ventilator ball, lightning rod, and a twin-pronged antenna mast on the left side of the dome.
- **Attached Buildings (Left & Right):**
  - **Left Wing:** A stepped two-story entryway/connector adjoins the left side of the tower, extending further left into a long, low single-story covered walkway or boathouse/storage wing with two square windows and a far-left doorway with three small steps.
  - **Right Wing:** A low connector with a single small square window links the right side of the tower to a rectangular single-story fog-signal/equipment building with a peaked/parapet roof, two small roof vents, and a tall central window.
- **Caption:** Centered beneath the base of the tower in bold black sans-serif text:
  - `Eastern Point Light`
  - `Gloucester, MA`

## Inset Photographs
Three framed color photographs taken on an overcast, foggy day are positioned around the upper portion of the schematic:
1. **Far Left Inset (`Existing VHF Antenna`):** Shows a close-up view from the lighthouse gallery looking past a tall vertical white fiberglass whip antenna mounted to the black iron railing, with the long stone Dogbar Breakwater extending into the gray ocean waters in the background.
2. **Middle-Left Inset (`Existing Antenna Viewed from Ground`):** A ground-level telephoto view looking up at the curved black iron gallery railing of the lighthouse, showing the vertical whip antenna mounted to the outer rail against a gray sky.
3. **Upper-Right Inset (Lighthouse Overview):** A full view of the white brick Eastern Point Light tower with its red lantern roof, black iron gallery railing, attached white wood-frame and brick outbuildings, and grassy mound in the foreground.
