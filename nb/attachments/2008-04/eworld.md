# eWorld Banner Logo (`eworld.png`)

## Overview
A wide promotional banner graphic for **eWorld**, a Java-based traffic simulation and OpenStreetMap (OSM) environment developed at the Hasso Plattner Institute (HPI).

## Graphic & Typography Details
- **Background:** A glossy horizontal banner with a dark charcoal-gray to black gradient that transitions from lighter gray on the left to pitch black on the right, featuring a reflective floor effect along the bottom half.
- **Globe Icon (Left):** A 3D-rendered terrestrial globe centered on South America and the Atlantic Ocean, showing physical relief shading in green, tan, and blue with white cloud wisps. A soft, semi-transparent mirror reflection of the globe appears directly beneath it.
- **Main Title (`eWorld`):** Large, bold sans-serif lettering spelling **eWorld** in metallic silver-blue with a curved horizontal specular highlight across the middle of the letters. Beneath the baseline, a faded upside-down reflection (`eWorld` mirrored vertically) reinforces the glossy surface effect.
- **HPI Logo (Top Right):** Two overlapping squares in crimson red and golden orange-yellow with the white sans-serif initials **HPI** inside the upper-right square.
