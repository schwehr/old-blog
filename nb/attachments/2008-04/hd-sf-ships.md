# Hi-Def San Francisco — The Shipping News Webpage (`hd-sf-ships.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the "Hi-Def | San Francisco" (`Hd-SF.com`) webpage titled **"The Shipping News (Beta)"** (`http://hd-sf.com/shippingreport.html`), captured on April 4, 2008. The site automatically photographs vessels crossing an imaginary line between a high-definition webcam and the Transamerica Pyramid in San Francisco Bay using AIS (Automatic Identification System) transmissions, displaying vessel metadata alongside inbound and outbound camera snapshots.

Only ships over 50M (164 feet) are shown unless they are close to the camera. Click the pictures for a larger view or take a look at the **map of all the ships we're currently tracking**."*
