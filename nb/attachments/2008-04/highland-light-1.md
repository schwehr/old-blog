# Highland Light Vector Tracing Over Photograph (`highland-light-1.png`)

## Overview
A work-in-progress illustration created in Adobe Illustrator showing black vector line art traced directly over a color reference photograph of **Highland Light** (also known as Cape Cod Light) in North Truro, Massachusetts. This is the first of four images illustrating Kurt Schwehr's process of creating an architectural schematic of the lighthouse complex.

## Scene & Architectural Details
- **Reference Photograph**:
  - Set against a clear, pale blue sky with bare, leafless winter/early-spring trees and brush visible in the lower right foreground around the base of the lighthouse and in the far left foreground behind a chain-link fence.
  - A narrow vertical strip of light gray graph-paper grid is visible along the far left margin where the photo layer ends short of the canvas edge.
- **Keeper's House (Left)**:
  - A two-story, gray-shingled Cape-style keeper's dwelling with white trim, a pinkish-brown gabled roof, and two tall white chimneys with dark caps rising from the roof ridges.
  - The main front section features two pitched-roof dormer windows on the upper story (one on the main section, one on the recessed left wing), two first-floor sash windows on the left, and a white front entry door on the right approached by a short flight of dark steps with white side railings.
- **Connecting Passageways (Center)**:
  - Two lower, single-story white-walled connecting structures with reddish-brown pitched roofs link the keeper's house on the left to the lighthouse tower on the right.
  - The left connector has a small square window and a solid door; the right connector has a small window and a roof that curves upward slightly where it joins the cylindrical tower.
- **Highland Light Tower (Right)**:
  - A tall, slightly tapering white cylindrical tower rising on the right side of the composition.
  - Features a single rectangular window near the top of the white masonry shaft, just below the lower gallery deck.
  - Topped by two circular watch/gallery decks with black metal railings and an external vertical ladder connecting the lower and upper balconies on the right side.
  - Surmounted by a glass-paned cylindrical lantern room with a black conical roof, a small round ventilator ball, and a lightning rod spire at the peak.

## Vector Overlay
- Crisp black outlines trace the major architectural edges of the lighthouse tower, lantern room panes, curved balcony railings, ladder rungs, rooflines, dormers, chimneys, windows, doors, and front steps over the semi-transparent/faded photograph beneath.
