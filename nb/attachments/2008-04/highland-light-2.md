# Highland Light Perspective Line Drawing on Grid (`highland-light-2.png`)

## Overview
The second stage in a four-part series demonstrating the creation of a schematic diagram of **Highland Light** (Cape Cod Light) in Adobe Illustrator. This image displays the black-and-white vector line drawing traced from the reference photograph, superimposed over a uniform light-gray square background grid with the underlying photograph hidden.

## Drawing & Grid Details
- **Background Grid**:
  - A fine, regular Cartesian grid of light gray horizontal and vertical lines covers the entire white canvas, enclosed by a medium-gray outer border.
- **Keeper's House & Connectors (Left to Center)**:
  - Clean black line art outlines the two-story keeper's house on the left, including the two chimneys, pitched rooflines, two gabled dormers, front door with five horizontal step lines at the bottom right of the main house, and the recessed left wing.
  -Note that the two first-floor left windows on the main house facade (present in the photograph) are omitted in this traced stage, while the door and steps are drawn.
  - To the right of the main house, the two single-story connecting buildings are drawn with double horizontal lines along the eaves, a square window and door in the middle section, a small capped window in the right section, and a curved roofline meeting the lighthouse shaft.
- **Highland Light Tower (Right)**:
  - The tapering cylindrical shaft rises on the right with a single rectangular window below the lower gallery.
  - Because the drawing was traced directly from a ground-level photograph, the two circular gallery decks and the lantern room roof exhibit upward perspective curvature (drawn with splines/elliptical arcs), along with vertical mullions for the glass lantern panes, the exterior ladder between galleries, and the finial/lightning rod at the top.
