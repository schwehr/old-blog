# Highland Light Perspective Line Drawing Without Grid (`highland-light-3.png`)

## Overview
The third stage in a four-part series illustrating the creation of a schematic drawing of **Highland Light** (Cape Cod Light) in Adobe Illustrator. This image shows the isolated black-on-white vector line art traced from the photograph, with both the reference photograph and the background coordinate grid turned off.

## Visual Details
- **Style**: Minimalist black line art on a plain white background framed by a thin gray border.
- **Left Wing & Keeper's House**:
  - Far left wing with a sloped porch roof, a single dormer window, and a tall rectangular chimney.
  - Main keeper's house volume with a central chimney, pitched roof, single upper-story dormer, and a front doorway with five horizontal steps at the lower right.
- **Connecting Structures**:
  - Two lower one-story linking structures between the house and tower, featuring a small square window and doorway on the left connector and a small window topped by a narrow lintel bar on the right connector, whose roof curves upward into the side of the lighthouse tower.
- **Lighthouse Tower**:
  - Tapering cylindrical tower on the right with one upper-shaft rectangular window, two curved perspective gallery decks with thin railing posts and top rails, a vertical ladder between the lower and upper decks, a multi-paned cylindrical lantern room, and a pointed dome roof with a finial and lightning rod.
