# Highland Light Orthographic Schematic Drawing on Grid (`highland-light-4-ortho-schematic.png`)

## Overview
The fourth and final image in Kurt Schwehr's series documenting the creation of a schematic diagram of **Highland Light** (Cape Cod Light). Rather than tracing perspective curves with splines over a photograph, this version redraws the entire lighthouse complex as a clean **orthographic elevation schematic** aligned directly to the square background grid.

## Architectural & Orthographic Details
- **Background Grid**:
  - A uniform light-gray square grid fills the white canvas inside a gray border, serving as the snap/alignment guide for all horizontal and vertical architectural elements.
- **Keeper's House (Left)**:
  - Redrawn with strictly horizontal roof ridges and eaves and vertical walls.
  - Includes the left wing with its chimney and rectangular upper window, and the main house block with its central chimney, upper rectangular dormer window, two first-floor rectangular windows on the left (restored compared to the earlier traced version), and the right-hand entrance door above four horizontal steps.
- **Connecting Buildings (Center)**:
  - Two single-story rectangular structures connect the house to the tower, featuring flat horizontal roof ridges and eaves, a small window and door in the left connector, and a small window in the right connector.
  - An angled straight line (instead of a curved spline) marks the sloped roof intersection with the lighthouse tower, with internal structural lines visible where the connector overlaps the base of the tower.
- **Lighthouse Tower (Right)**:
  - The tall tapering shaft has straight, symmetrical outer walls and internal vertical guide lines extending from the lantern room down to the base.
  - Features the rectangular window below the lower deck, two flat horizontal gallery decks with rectangular railing outlines, a vertical door and five-rung ladder on the watch room level, a flat-topped lantern room with vertical pane divisions, a triangular pitched roof cap, a diamond-shaped ventilator ball, and a vertical lightning rod at the apex.
