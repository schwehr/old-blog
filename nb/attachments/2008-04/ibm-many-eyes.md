# IBM Many Eyes Visualization Webpage — Strictness Index (`ibm-many-eyes.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **IBM Many Eyes** (`http://services.alphaworks.ibm.com/manyeyes/home`), an experimental collaborative data visualization website created by IBM Research / alphaWorks, captured in April 2008. The page displays an interactive choropleth map of the United States titled **"Visualizations : Strictness index"**, shaded in gradations of brown/tan by state.

## Page Layout & Navigation
- **Top Header Banner**:
  - Top left: Stylized abstract line-art logo of two overlapping faces/profiles in blue, tan, and white beside the lowercase title **"many eyes"** on a warm tan/terracotta gradient banner.
  - Top right: `Sign in` link above a search bar with a text input box, a dropdown menu set to `data sets`, and a rounded brown `search` button.
- **Left Sidebar Menu**:
  - Divided into vijf sections with bold brown headings and orange-red links:
    - **explore**: `visualizations`, `data sets`, `comments`, `topic hubs`
    - **participate**: `register`, `create visualization`, `upload data set`, `create topic hub`
    - **learn more**: `quick start`, `visualization types`, `data format & style`, `about Many Eyes`, `FAQ`, `blog`
    - **contact**: `contact us`, `report a bug`
    - **legal**: `terms of use`
