# Don Gorvett Piscataqua Fine Arts Studio — Woodcut by Linda Schwehr (`linda-woodcut.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the "Workshops and lessons" webpage for **Don Gorvett — Piscataqua Fine Arts Studio** (`http://www.dongorvettgallery.com/workshops.html`), captured on April 30, 2008. The page announces spring and summer printmaking workshops and features a reduction woodcut print titled *"Pocket Garden"* created by Kurt Schwehr's mother, **Linda Schwehr**, in 2007.

## Page Layout & Left Sidebar
- **Color Scheme**: Seafoam/sage green outer border and left navigation column, paired with a warm cream content box on the right.
- **Studio Logo & Header (Top Left)**:
  - A black-and-white woodcut illustration of a printmaker turning the large spoked wheel of an etching/relief press.
  - Framed text box reading:
    - **DON GORVETT**
    - **PISCATAQUA FINE ARTS**
  - Spaced serif capitals below the box: **S T U D I O**.
- **Navigation Menu (Left Column)**:
  - Five right-aligned menu links separated by thin horizontal black lines:
    - `About our studio`
    - **`Workshops and lessons`** (highlighted in coral/salmon pink to indicate the active page)
    - `Press time rental`
    - `Blog`
    - `Gallery`

## Main Content & Featured Woodcut
- **Workshop Announcement Text**:
  - Paragraph 1: *"This spring and summer Piscataqua Fine Arts will be offering a comprehensive schedule of workshops in Reduction Woodcut, Etching, and Plate Lithography. Classes will be taught by gallery artists including Don Gorvett, Sean Hurley, Shane Chick, and other invited guest instructors (to be announced). The workshops will be conducted in our gallery/studio on our two large presses. Final schedules and costs are still being determined, but workshops will be conducted in once-weekly, twice-weekly, and week-long intensive formats. We expect to begin in May - a schedule of prices and times will be posted as soon as possible."*
  - Paragraph 2: *"Space is very limited and will be allotted on a first-come first-serve basis, so interested parties are encouraged to contact us as soon as possible. Those of you who have already expressed interest over the winter and signed our guestbook will be receiving a call or mailing shortly, and will be given priority."*
  - Paragraph 3: *"Last summer we ran just a few classes and they were a great success - come join us this year!"*
