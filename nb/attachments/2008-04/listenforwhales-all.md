# Listen for Whales — All 13 Buoys with Active Right Whale Detections (`listenforwhales-all.png`)

## Overview
A status map from the **Right Whale Listening Network** (`http://listenforwhales.org/`) captured on April 15, 2008, when every single acoustic monitoring buoy in Massachusetts Bay and Cape Cod Bay simultaneously reported active North Atlantic right whale detections within a 24-hour window.

## Header & Map Features
- **Top Banner**:
  - A dark brown header bar across the top with bold white text:
    **"351 Right Whale Calls Detected Within 24 Hours."**
- **Geographic Coverage & Labels**:
  - Covers the eastern Massachusetts coastline from **Cape Ann** and **Gloucester** in the north, past **Boston** on the western shore, across **Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary** (outlined by a thin dark-blue polygon), and down into **Cape Cod Bay**, **Provincetown**, and the hook of **Cape Cod** in the south.
  - Bathymetry is shaded in blues, with lighter blue indicating shallower banks (such as Stellwagen Bank and coastal shoals) and deeper blue indicating deeper waters.
- **Scale Bar & Compass Rose**:
  - Top right: Alternating black-and-white scale bar for `0`, `2.5`, `5`, `10`, `15`, `20` **Nautical Miles**.
  - Bottom right: White compass rose icon topped with a bold **`N`** pointing north.

## Buoy Detections & Legend
- **Active Whale Detections**:
  - Ten acoustic listening buoys spaced along the **Boston Shipping Lane** (Traffic Separation Scheme, drawn as parallel dark-blue lines curving east from Boston Harbor across Stellwagen Bank and turning southeast past Cape Cod) and three buoys inside **Cape Cod Bay** southwest of Provincetown (13 buoys total).
  - Every single one of the 13 buoys displays a **red right-whale silhouette icon encircled by a red ring** inside its translucent light-blue listening radius, signifying that right whale calls were detected at all 13 stations within the past 24 hours.
- **Map Legends Box (Lower Left)**:
  - A rounded semi-transparent white overlay box titled **`Map Legends`** in blue text, defining five symbols:
    - Four parallel blue lines: `Shipping Lane`
    - Pale blue circle with central dot: `Buoy Listening Radius`
    - Gray sphere icon: `Offline Buoy`
    - Bright green sphere icon: `Online Buoy`
    - Red whale icon: `Right Whale Detected within Listening Radius`
