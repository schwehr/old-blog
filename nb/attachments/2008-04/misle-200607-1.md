# USCG MISLE Marine Casualty Dataset in Google Earth — Global / North America View (`misle-200607-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** displaying a global/hemispheric view centered on North America, overlaid with thousands of custom incident icons generated from the 2006 US Coast Guard **MISLE** (Marine Information for Safety and Law Enforcement) marine casualty dataset.

## Google Earth Interface & Controls
- **Overview Map (Top Left)**:
  - A rectangular world map inset showing a red bounding box spanning the northern hemisphere.
- **Navigation Controls (Top Right)**:
  - Circular look/tilt joystick with a central eye icon and `N` (North) indicator at the top.
  - Circular pan joystick below with a central hand icon and four directional arrows.
  - Vertical zoom slider bar with `+` and `-` buttons.
- **Attributions (Bottom)**:
  - Centered at bottom: `Image NASA`, `Image © 2008 TerraMetrics`, `Image © 2008 DigitalGlobe`.
  - Bottom right: `©2008 Google` logo.

## Plotted MISLE Incident Icons
- **Geographic Distribution**:
  - Dense clusters of marine casualty icons trace the US East Coast, Gulf of Mexico, Mississippi/Ohio/Great Lakes inland river systems, US West Coast, Alaska (including the Aleutian Islands and Southeast Alaska), Hawaii, Puerto Rico/Caribbean, and Panama, with scattered incidents out in the Pacific and Atlantic oceans and near high-latitude Arctic waters.
- **Icon Symbology**:
  - As noted by Kurt Schwehr in the accompanying blog post, the visualization uses a mix of JWZ-style OSHA hazard symbols (bright green, red, yellow, cyan, and magenta square badges showing industrial/safety pictograms, plus orange/yellow radiation trefoil symbols used as placeholders for vessel abandonment events) alongside standard Google Earth icons (flames for fires/explosions, "For Sale" signs, cars, mountains, and maritime markers).
