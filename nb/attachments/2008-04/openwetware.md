## Overview
A screenshot of the front page of **OpenWetWare** (`http://openwetware.org`), an MIT-originated MediaWiki-based open-science collaboration portal for biology and biological engineering researchers, captured in April 2008.

  - Standard wiki page tabs along the top left: **`article`** (active), `talk`, `view source`, and `history`.
- **OpenWetWare Banner Logo**:
  - On the left, a stylized purple-and-blue double-helix illustration in which the base pairs are formed by raised human arms/hands clasping or holding tools in blue and lime green.
  - Next to the helix is the bold uppercase title **"OPENWETWARE"**, with **"OPEN"** in medium blue and **"WETWARE"** in olive/lime green.
- **Mission Box**:
  - A pale blue-lavender banner box beneath the logo containing blue text:
