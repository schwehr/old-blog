# Dusk View of Salem Harbor, Massachusetts (`salem-harbor.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect twilight photograph of **Salem Harbor** in **Salem, Massachusetts**, taken on April 5, 2008 (posted April 6, 2008) during a visit to the historic coastal city.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Lighting**:
  - Captured at dusk under an overcast sky suffused with deep violet, lavender, and purple-blue twilight hues, with a soft pinkish-orange glow near the horizon line in the center distance.
- **Foreground Shoreline (Bottom Left)**:
  - A dark, exposed low-tide mudflat and rocky/pebbly beach curves from the lower left across the bottom margin, reflecting the dim purple light on wet patches between small dark stones.
- **Left Midground — Pier & Marina**:
  - On the left shore, dark silhouettes of evergreens and leafless deciduous trees frame waterfront buildings and several sailboats stored on land under white winter shrink-wrap covers, with their tall white masts rising vertically against the purple sky.
  - A long wooden pile pier/dock extends from the left shoreline far out into the calm harbor toward the center of the frame.
- **Right Distance — Opposite Headland**:
  - Across the calm, slate-blue waters of the harbor on the right, a low wooded peninsula/shoreline stretches to the right edge, dotted with a row of warmly lit waterfront houses glowing orange-gold in the gathering darkness, with small mooring buoys dotting the water in front of them.
