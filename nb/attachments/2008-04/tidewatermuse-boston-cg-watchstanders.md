# U.S. Coast Guard First District Command Center Watchstanders in Boston (`tidewatermuse-boston-cg-watchstanders.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle photograph showing U.S. Coast Guard watchstanders working at multi-monitor computer consoles inside the newly renovated First District Command Center in Boston, Massachusetts, overlooking Boston Harbor on March 28, 2008 (U.S. Coast Guard photo by Petty Officer 2nd Class Luke Pinneo).

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Workstation**: In the lower center, a uniformed Coast Guard watchstander wearing dark blue working uniforms and glasses sits in an ergonomic office chair inside a U-shaped cubicle pod. The desk wraps around him and is equipped with four flat-panel LCD monitors (two on the left, one center, one angled right, plus another monitor on the far right wing of the desk), multiple keyboards and mice, a multi-line desk telephone, speakers, binders, and reference sheets.
- **Background Workstations**: Directly behind the foreground pod is a second large curved row of workstations facing the harbor windows. On the left side of this row, a watchstander in a light-colored shirt and tie sits in a chair speaking on a handset in front of dual LCD monitors. Additional multi-monitor stations, file organizers, binders, and an office copier/printer (far right) fill the room.
- **Windows and Harbor View**: Large panoramic windows span the back wall, looking out over the water of Boston Harbor, piers, and bridge/industrial structures. Mounted on the left window frame is a heavy-duty pair of mounted observation binoculars ("big eyes"), and two wall-mounted status screens hang above the center divider between the windows.
