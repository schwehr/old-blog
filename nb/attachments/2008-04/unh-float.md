# Recovered UNH Oceanographic Mooring Float and Instrument (`unh-float.jpg`)

## Overview
An overhead photograph of a bright orange spherical University of New Hampshire (UNH) mooring float, tangled mooring line, and a cylindrical black oceanographic sensor/logger lying on asphalt pavement after washing ashore in New Castle, New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Orange Float**: A large, glossy, bright fluorescent-orange spherical buoy dominates the upper-left center of the frame. Bold black stenciled/taped letters spelling **`UNH`** curve vertically along its right side.
- **Mooring Line**: Coils of thick, weathered white three-strand twisted rope surround and connect the float and instrument, with some frayed dark green/brown marine growth attached near the upper-right knots.
- **Subsurface Instrument & Tags**: Below the float lies a black cylindrical underwater housing (such as an acoustic receiver or data logger) tied to the rope. Attached to the cylinder are a rectangular orange plastic identification tag and a small clear/green cylindrical vial or desiccant/marker capsule.
- **Handwritten Note**: Taped to the rope just to the right of the black cylinder is a white slip of paper with cursive handwriting explaining where the washed-up gear was found.
