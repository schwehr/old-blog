# Visitors Browsing Plants Inside the UNH Greenhouse (`unh-greenhouse-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior photograph of the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Greenhouse during a public open house, showing rows of potted seedlings and visitors inspecting plants under a glass roof hung with spider plants.

## Scene Description
- **Greenhouse Tables & Plants**: Three long, tiered white planting benches angle across the foreground and midground from left to right. They are tightly packed with small black square nursery pots containing leafy green seedlings and starter plants. Almost every pot has a small white arched plastic or paper identification label sticking up from the soil, including a visible `PW` (Proven Winners) tag near the bottom center-right.
- **Hanging Baskets**: Four green plastic hanging pots filled with variegated spider plants (*Chlorophytum comosum*) hang from the metal roof trusses along the upper left and center.
- **Visitors**: Five people stand along the back aisle beyond the plant tables, dressed in warm jackets (a pink/white jacket on the far left, a red jacket, a dark vest over grey hood, a bright red hoodie, and a black jacket on the right), examining the potted plants.
- **Structure**: The greenhouse features a pitched glass-and-metal-frame roof and side walls, through which bright daylight enters and snow/white outdoor surfaces are faintly visible outside on the right.
