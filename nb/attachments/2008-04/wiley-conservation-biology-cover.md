# Conservation Biology Journal Cover — April 2008 (`wiley-conservation-biology-cover.png`)

## Overview
The front cover of the scientific journal *Conservation Biology* (Volume 22, No. 2, April 2008), published by Blackwell Publishing for the Society for Conservation Biology, featuring a three-frame vertical photographic sequence of a humpback whale breaching in front of whale-watching observers.

## Layout & Visual Details
- **Background Color**: Olive-green / mossy-green cover background surrounded by a thin black border.
- **Header Typography**:
  - Top title in large black italic serif font: `Conservation Biology` above a horizontal black rule.
  - Issue line centered between two horizontal rules: `Volume 22 • No. 2 • April 2008`.
- **Central Triptych Photograph**: A vertically stacked three-panel sequence framed in black showing a humpback whale (*Megaptera novaeangliae*) leaping out of the ocean in close proximity to the bow/flybridge of a vessel where four people in red and dark jackets stand watching and photographing, with a small boat visible on the distant left horizon:
  1. **Top Frame**: The whale launches high into the air at a steep upward angle, its white-underside pectoral fin extended and water cascading off its tail flukes.
  2. **Middle Frame**: The whale arches horizontally in mid-air above a white splash plume as its body rotates toward the water.
  3. **Bottom Frame**: The whale's massive body slams back onto the sea surface, sending up a broad curtain of white spray on the right.
- **Footer Typography**:
  - `The Journal of the Society for Conservation Biology`
  - `Blackwell Publishing, Inc.`
  - `ISSN 0888-8892`
