# Newly Hatched Praying Mantis Nymphs on Foliage (`wilwheaton-mantids.jpg`)

## Overview
A macro photograph (taken by Wil Wheaton) showing several tiny, newly hatched praying mantis nymphs (*Mantodea*) clambering over fuzzy green plant leaves with soft-focus pink flowers in the background.

  - Two nymphs stand side-by-side near the upper-left leaf tips, facing upward with their prominent triangular heads, dark compound eyes, and slender antennae clearly visible.
  - Two more nymphs climb along the upper-right leaf tip—one perched near the top edge facing left, and another clinging to the underside/inner margin just below it.
  - Another nymph crawls along a lower-right green leaf stem, while faint silhouettes of additional nymphs appear near the far right edge and on the blurred pink petal at the bottom center.
- **Foliage & Background**: The central plant stem and curling young leaves are covered in fine, silvery-white pubescent hairs (trichomes). Smooth, darker green leaves frame the lower left and right, while out-of-focus coral-pink blossoms (such as alstroemeria) create a vibrant bokeh background at the top left and bottom center.
