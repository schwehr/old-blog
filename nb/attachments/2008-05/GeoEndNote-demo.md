# GeoEndNote GEBCO Visual Library Proof of Concept (`GeoEndNote-demo.png`)

## Overview
A composite illustration demonstrating the "GeoEndNote" proof-of-concept tool (developed by Monahan, Schwehr, Wigley, Tinmouth, Goncalves, Jinidasa, Uddin, and Ito for the May 2008 GEBCO meeting in Japan), which converts EndNote bibliographic records containing Well-Known Text (WKT) figure geometries into interactive Google Earth KML placemarks.

## Central Globe & Placemarks
- **3D Globe**: Centered against a black background is a Google Earth globe view centered over the Pacific Ocean, showing bathymetrical shading, white cloud patterns, and surrounding landmasses (North America on the northeast limb, Arctic/Alaska at the top, New Zealand and Fiji to the southwest, and Hawaii near the center).
- **Red Pushpins**: Numerous red-and-black Google Earth pin markers are distributed across the Pacific, Arctic, and coastal margins, marking the geographic locations of scientific figures from marine geology and bathymetry publications.

## Callout Popups & Visible Citations
Five Google Earth info-balloon popups are arranged around the perimeter of the globe, each connected by a white pointer triangle to its corresponding red placemark on the globe. Every popup displays a close button (`x`) in the upper-left corner, a bold citation header, a blue hyperlinked full citation, an embedded thumbnail of a bathymetric or geophysical figure from the paper, and `Directions: To here - From here` links at the bottom:

1. **Top Center (Chukchi Borderland, Arctic Ocean)**:
   - **Header**: `Jakobsson, Martin et al. 2005`
   - **Citation**: *Jakobsson, Martin, Gardner, James V., Vogt, Peter R., Mayer, Larry A., Armstrong, Andrew, Backman, Jan, Brennan, Rikk, Calder, Brian, Hall, John K., Kraft, Barbara*, "Multibeam bathymetric and sediment profiler evidence for ice grounding on the Chukchi Borderland, Arctic Ocean," *Quaternary Research*, 63:2, 150-160, 2005.
   - **Figure**: Grayscale shaded-relief multibeam bathymetry showing iceberg ploughmarks/grounding scours paired with four inset sub-bottom acoustic profiles.

2. **Upper Right (Santa Monica Bay, California)**:
   - **Header**: `Gardner, J.V. et al. 2002`
   - **Citation**: *Gardner, J.V.*, "Multibeam Backscatter of Santa Monica Bay, California," *Geospatial_Data_Presentation_Form*, 2002.
   - **Figure**: Color-coded 3D perspective/plan-view multibeam bathymetry of the Santa Monica Bay continental shelf and slope over a grayscale land DEM.

3. **Lower Right (Galapagos Triple Junction, Eastern Pacific)**:
   - **Header**: `Klein, E.M. et al. 2005`
   - **Citation**: *Klein, E.M., Smith, D.K., Williams, C.M., Schouten, H.S.*, "Counter-rotating microplates at the Galapagos triple junction," *letters to nature*, 433:BRZ NUMBER, 855-858, 2005.
   - **Figure**: Color-shaded bathymetric and tectonic lineament map with latitude/longitude axes covering the Galapagos microplate boundary.

4. **Bottom Right (South Pacific Triple Junction)**:
   - **Header**: `Viso, Richard F. et al. 2005`
   - **Citation**: *Viso, Richard F., Larson, Roger L., Pockalny, Robert A.*, "Tectonic evolution of the Pacific-Phoenix-Farallon triple junction in the South Pacific Ocean," *Earth and Planetary Science Letters*, 233:1-2, 179-194, 2005.
   - **Figure**: Grayscale swath sidescan/bathymetry track map showing seafloor spreading fabric labeled `relict transform / spreading centers`.

5. **Bottom Left (Viti Levu Seismic Zone, Fiji)**:
   - **Header**: `Rahiman, T.I.H. and Pettinga, J.R. 2006`
   - **Citation**: *Rahiman, T.I.H., Pettinga, J.R.*, "The offshore morpho-structure and tsunami sources of the Viti Levu Seismic Zone, southeast Viti Levu, Fiji," *Marine Geology*, 232:3-4, 203-225, 2006.
   - **Figure**: Grayscale shaded-relief morpho-structural map of southeastern Viti Levu and its offshore submarine canyons and fault scarps.

6. **Middle Left (Ogasawara Fracture Zone, Western Pacific)**:
   - **Header**: `Lee, Tae Gook et al. 2005`
   - **Citation**: *Lee, Tae Gook, Hein, James R., Lee, Kiseong, Moon, Jae Woon, Ko, Young Tak*, "Sub-seafloor acoustic characterization of seamounts near the Ogasawara Fracture Zone in the western Pacific using chirp (3-7 kHz) subbottom profiler," *Deep Sea Research Part I: Oceanographic Research Papers*, 52:10, 1932-1956, 2005.
   - **Figure**: Grayscale map showing four detailed contour insets of seamounts (`OSM2`, `OSM4`, `OSM5`, `OSM7`) plotted by latitude and longitude.
