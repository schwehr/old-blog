# Aerial Photograph of the McAlpine Locks and Dam in Louisville, Kentucky (`Louisville-lock.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial color photograph taken from the left window of a Southwest Airlines passenger jet looking down at the Ohio River, the McAlpine Locks and Dam ("Louisville lock"), and the surrounding metropolitan area of Louisville, Kentucky, and southern Indiana.

## Scene Description
- **Aircraft Wingtip (Left Foreground)**: A red-and-blue blended winglet of a Boeing 737 projects into the upper-left corner of the frame, bearing the blue-on-red text `SOUTHWEST.COM` along its leading upward angle, with a small portion of the white wing and red flap fairing visible below.
- **Ohio River & McAlpine Locks and Dam**: The wide, muddy-tan Ohio River winds from the upper-left horizon toward the right, curving around a sharp bend in the lower center-right. Inside the bend, the river splits around Shippingport Island and the Louisville and Portland Canal, showing the long parallel concrete lock chambers of the McAlpine Locks and Dam on the southern (left/lower) bank and the dam structure spanning the northern channel.
- **Bridges & Urban Infrastructure**: Multiple bridges cross the river and canal in the foreground and midground, including the Fourteenth Street railroad bridge (cutting diagonally across the canal and island), the Clark Memorial Bridge, the Kennedy Bridge, and the Sherman Minton Bridge downstream along the bend.
- **Surrounding Landscape**: Dense urban grids, highway interchanges (including I-64 running along the riverfront), industrial warehouses, and green tree canopy cover Louisville on the left/bottom and Jeffersonville/New Albany, Indiana, on the right/upper bank, fading into pale atmospheric haze toward the upper horizon.
