# Phoenix Mars Lander Robotic Arm Workspace Terrain Model — Thumbnail (`Viz-RA-workspace-sml.png`)

## Overview
A smaller, scaled-down thumbnail version of `Viz-RA-workspace.png` (framed by a thin gray border), showing a top-down 3D visualization and false-color depth-coded terrain model of the NASA Phoenix Mars Lander's Robotic Arm (RA) workspace generated by the NASA Ames Viz system and Stereo Pipeline from Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) stereo imagery.

## Lander Model & Polar Grid
- **Background & Border**: Set on a black background enclosed by a 1-pixel gray border, overlaid with a white polar coordinate grid centered on the Robotic Arm's shoulder pivot.
- **Concentric Rings & Axes**: Twelve concentric white range rings with faint numeric distance labels (`0.6` to `2.6` in `0.2` increments) along the vertical axis, intersected by perpendicular horizontal and vertical white axis lines.
- **Spacecraft Geometry**:
  - Gray 3D model of the Phoenix Mars Lander positioned in the lower-left portion of the grid, showing the octagonal science deck, surface instruments, and three landing leg struts with circular footpads.
  - Two fan-like solar panel arrays extending to the lower-left and lower-right, with dark blue panel blades radiating from central hubs.
  - The Robotic Arm extending straight up along the vertical axis from the lander deck toward the top of the terrain arc.

## False-Color Terrain Mesh
- **Workspace Arc**: A textured, shaded 3D terrain mesh spans from the upper-left (around 10 o'clock) across the top to the right side (around 3 o'clock) of the grid.
- **Elevation Color Scale**:
  - **Red**: Low-lying trough areas across the inner arc and far-right margin.
  - **Green & Yellow**: Mid-level terrain along the upper-left lobe and outer upper-right ridge, showing small rocks and clods.
  - **Blue & Cyan**: Higher-elevation polygon interior on the right side of the workspace (`1.0`–`1.6` m radial distance).
