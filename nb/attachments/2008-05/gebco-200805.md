# GEBCO Visual Library Proof of Concept Poster (`gebco-200805.png`)

## Overview
A scientific poster layout titled **GEBCO VISUAL LIBRARY: Proof of Concept**, illustrating a conceptual model for organizing, geo-referencing, and disseminating oceanographic and multibeam bathymetric research using Google Earth.

## Header & Logos
- Set against a pale yellow banner at the top:
  - **Left Logos**: Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC) circular emblem and the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Thompson Hall tower logo.
  - **Center Title**: `GEBCO VISUAL LIBRARY` in large bold black sans-serif text, with `Proof of Concept` centered beneath it.
  - **Right Logos**: The Nippon Foundation logo (black stylized hands icon with Japanese and English text) and the circular blue GEBCO (General Bathymetric Chart of the Oceans) globe logo.

## Poster Sections & Layout
- **Top Introductory Banner**: A dark blue box with a light blue border spanning the width of the poster, containing three paragraphs of white text explaining that the GEBCO Visual Library uses Google Earth as a user-friendly virtual globe platform to display multibeam bathymetry figures, captions, publication references, and abstracts via interactive "thumbtack" placemarks over the GEBCO world chart.
- **Left Column (`CONCEPT:`)**: A pale yellow box on the left with blue header text and five bullet points in black text detailing the goals of providing an accessible Google Earth interface, linking bathymetric survey data with gazetteered undersea feature names, identifying data density and scarcity, encouraging interdisciplinary collaboration, and promoting capacity building.
- **Center Graphic**: A 3D Google Earth globe centered on the Pacific Ocean draped with the blue-shaded GEBCO global bathymetric chart and dotted with small orange/red thumbtack icons. Six white pop-up callout windows surround the globe, connected to specific oceanic locations by white leader lines, each displaying a 3D bathymetric map or acoustic backscatter figure along with bibliographic citation text and links.
- **Right Column (`POTENTIAL:`)**: A pale yellow box on the right with blue header text and seven bullet points describing future expansion to all oceanographic research, bounding-box spatial searches, direct overlays of scaled bathymetry images in Google Earth, publisher integration, archiving with URLs, Google Geo search/Wikipedia/EndNote integration, and ocean mapping education.
- **Bottom Row**: Seven small circular Google Earth globe thumbnails arranged horizontally across the bottom, each showing a different regional view of Earth with a small white pop-up window containing a portrait photo and bio of a project team member, above a tiny white caption at the bottom center reading `GEBCO world sheet draped onto Google earth`.
