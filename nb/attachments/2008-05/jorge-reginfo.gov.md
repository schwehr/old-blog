# RegInfo.gov Federal Regulation Information Homepage (`jorge-reginfo.gov.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X web browser screenshot of the `RegInfo.gov` homepage (`http://www.reginfo.gov/public/`), a U.S. government portal produced by the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) and the General Services Administration (GSA) providing federal regulatory information.

## Browser & Header Elements
- **Window Frame**: Mac OS X browser window titled `RegInfo.gov - Where to Find Federal Regulation Information`, displaying back/forward/reload/stop/home toolbar buttons, the URL `http://www.reginfo.gov/public/`, and a Google search bar on the right.
- **Top Government Banner**: A gray bar with the blue `GSA` square logo and white text `General Services Administration` on the left, and `Office of Management and Budget` next to the circular Executive Office of the President seal on the right.
- **Site Banner**: Large blue serif text `RegInfo.gov` followed by smaller blue text `Where to find Federal Regulatory Information`, and a close-up photograph of a waving U.S. flag on the right.

## Page Content & Columns
- **Left Sidebar (Dark Blue Background, White Text)**:
  - Explains that `RegInfo.gov` is produced by OMB and GSA.
  - Describes GSA's `Regulatory Information Service Center` (RISC) and its role in publishing the semiannual Unified Agenda and annual Regulatory Plan under Executive Order 12866.
  - Describes OMB's `Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs` (OIRA) under the Paperwork Reduction Act and Executive Order 12866.
- **Center Column (Main Sections with Blue-Gray Headers)**:
  - **Unified Agenda and Regulatory Plan**: Describes uniform reporting across over 60 federal departments, agencies, and commissions, featuring bold black text `SPRING 2008 NOW AVAILABLE` and a link to `Current and Past Agendas and Plans and How to Use Them`.
  - **EO 12866 Regulatory Review**: Summarizes `Executive Order 12866` principles and links to `Current and Past Regulatory Reviews` and `Other Information Relating to OIRA Regulatory Matters`.
  - **Information Collection Review**: Summarizes the `Paperwork Reduction Act` and links to `Inventory of Approved Information Collections, Collections Under Review, Recently Approved/Expired` and `Other OIRA Information About Paperwork Requirements`.
  - **Public Comment on Federal Rulemaking**: Bullet link to `Regulations.gov (Find, review, and submit comments on proposed regulations)`.
  - **E-Forms for Doing Business with the Government**: Bullet link to `Forms.gov (Links to Federal agency forms)`.
- **Right Sidebar (`Related Resources`, Light Blue Background)**:
  - Bulleted links with italic descriptions: `The Reg Map`, `Federal Register`, `Code of Federal Regulations`, `Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (e-CFR)`, `Business.gov`, `Small Business Administration Regulatory Alerts`, `ADDITIONAL RESOURCES`, and `Download Adobe Acrobat Reader`.
  - Bottom right features the blue and red `USA.gov Government Made Easy` logo.
- **Footer**: Links for `Disclosure | Accessibility | Privacy Policy | Contact Us`.
