# Regulations.gov Federal Rulemaking Portal Homepage (`jorge-regulations.gov.png`)

## Overview
A web browser screenshot of the `Regulations.gov` homepage (`http://www.regulations.gov/search/index.jsp`) captured on May 5, 2008, showcasing search and public comment tools for U.S. federal regulations.

## Browser & Header Elements
- **Browser Toolbar**: Displays navigation buttons, the URL `http://www.regulations.gov/search/index.jsp`, and a Google search box.
- **Header**: Displays the date `May 05, 2008` at top left above the `Regulations.gov` logo (dark blue serif text over a light blue stylized eagle wing and stars emblem) with the red tagline `Your voice in Federal decision-making`.

## Page Layout & Features
- **Left Navigation Menu**: Four stacked light-blue rectangular buttons with black text:
  - `What Is on This Site`
  - `How to Use This Site`
  - `Site Features`
  - `Links of Interest`
- **Main Action Columns (Top Row)**:
  1. **SEARCH** (mauve/brown rounded header): "Search all documents by entering a keyword, phrase, identification number, or other term." Includes a text input box and a `Go >>` link.
  2. **COMMENT OR SUBMISSION** (medium blue/purple rounded header): "Enter a keyword, phrase, or other term to find documents accepting comments or to file a submission." Includes a text input box and a `Go >>` link.
  3. **MORE SEARCH OPTIONS** (gray-green rounded header): "Need more help finding what you're looking for? Click 'Go' for more search options. `Go >>`" followed by "Looking for our old searches? Advanced Docket Search `click here`. Advanced Document Search `click here`."
- **Informational Cards (Middle Row)**:
  1. **About Regulations.gov**: Thumbnail image of dictionary text highlighting the word `goal`, with text explaining that the site is a one-stop website to find, view, and comment on federal regulations (`Learn more >>`).
  2. **What's New?**: Thumbnail image of stacked newspapers, highlighting full text search, narrowed search results, and an RSS feed (`Learn more >>`).
  3. **Feature Highlight**: Thumbnail photo of three office workers around a laptop, promoting a daily RSS feed for regulations and other actions posted daily (`Learn more >>`).
- **Footer**:
  - A blue bar with `Change Text Size A A a` on the left, links for `Privacy and Use Notice | Contact Us | Glossary | FAQ` in the center, and an orange `RSS` button on the right.
  - Partner logos at the very bottom: `Business.GOV`, `E-GOV` (with a red star), and `USA.gov`.
