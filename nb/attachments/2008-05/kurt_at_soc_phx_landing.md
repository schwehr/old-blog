# Kurt Schwehr at the Phoenix Mars Lander Science Operations Center (`kurt_at_soc_phx_landing.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph of Kurt Schwehr seated at a multi-monitor computer workstation in the studio control area of the Phoenix Mars Lander Science Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona, taken roughly an hour before the Phoenix spacecraft landed on Mars.

## Scene Description
- **Subject**: Kurt Schwehr is seated in an office chair on the right side of the frame, facing left toward a row of computer monitors while turning his head slightly toward the camera. He wears rectangular wire-rimmed glasses, a black t-shirt featuring the colorful Phoenix Mars Lander mission patch on the chest, blue jeans, a black `JPL` / `Jet Propulsion Laboratory` lanyard around his neck, and a pink wristband on his left wrist. His right hand rests on a compact white Apple keyboard on the desk.
- **Workstation & Equipment**:
  - A long black table holds multiple flat-panel displays: a silver Apple Cinema Display in the center showing a Mac OS X desktop with the Phoenix mission logo wallpaper and a list window open; a partially visible monitor on the far left; a black flat-panel monitor to Kurt's right with a black keyboard in front of it; and two additional silver monitors on the desk tier behind them.
  - On the desk surface near Kurt's right hand are a gray two-button mouse, a small silver flip cell phone, and a white power/data adapter dongle. A second white Apple keyboard and a silver shuttle/pc tower unit sit further left on the desk.
  - Bundles of power and display cables and a black UPS battery backup unit are visible on the floor beneath the table.
- **Background**: High on the dark wall in the background are three large wall-mounted flat-screen displays showing live video feeds of crowds and mission personnel gathered for the landing event.
