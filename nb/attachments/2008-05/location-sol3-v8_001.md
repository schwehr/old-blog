# Annotated Mars Phoenix Lander Surface Mosaic Showing Named Features (`location-sol3-v8_001.png`)

## Overview
An annotated grayscale panoramic mosaic captured by the Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) on NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander on Sol 3, showing the polygonal Arctic plains around the lander labeled with informal storybook feature names (`Humpty Dumpty`, `Sleepy Hollow`, `King's Men`, `King's Horses`, `Wall`, `Headless`, `Alice`, `Ichabod`, and `National Park`).

## Scene & Annotations
- **Martian Terrain & Spacecraft Shadow**:
  - The grayscale surface shows gently troughed polygonal ground littered with small pebbles and rocks.
  - Across the lower half of the image, the dark silhouette shadow of the Phoenix lander body and its tall lattice mast topped by the Surface Stereo Imager camera head stretches toward the right across the soil.
  - Parts of the lander deck sit in the lower left foreground, and a circular black workspace/footpad area is visible at the bottom center-right.
- **Green Dashed Ellipses (Regions / Troughs / Polygons)**:
  - `Humpty Dumpty`: Large horizontal bright-green dashed oval near the upper left-center enclosing a rounded polygon interior.
  - `Wall`: Smaller green dashed circle below `Humpty Dumpty` along the left edge of the shadow.
  - `Sleepy Hollow`: Large tilted green dashed ellipse in the center enclosing a shallow trough/depression.
- **Yellow Dashed Circles (Individual Rocks)**:
  - `King's Men`: Yellow dashed circle with a yellow leader line pointing to a rock between `Humpty Dumpty` and `Sleepy Hollow`.
  - `King's Horses`: Yellow dashed circle around a dark rock in the shadow edge between `Wall` and `Sleepy Hollow`.
  - `Headless`: Yellow dashed oval around a flat rock just to the right of `Sleepy Hollow`.
  - `Alice`: Yellow dashed circle around a rock obscured by the shadow of the camera head near the upper right.
  - `Ichabod`: Yellow dashed circle with a leader line pointing to a rock near the base of the mast shadow in the lower right.
- **Boundary Line & Azimuth Scale**:
  - A vertical bright-magenta line runs from the top to the bottom at roughly 355 degrees azimuth, with a right-pointing magenta arrow and label reading `National Park` indicating the protected surface zone to the right of the line.
  - Along the bottom edge is a white numerical azimuth scale in degrees: `330`, `340`, `350`, `0`, `10`, `20`, `30`, `40`, `50`, `60`, `70`.
