# OpenStreetMap Detailed Street View of Portsmouth, New Hampshire (`maps-osm-portsmouth-nh.png`)

## Overview
A 2008 screenshot of the OpenStreetMap (OSM) web interface centered on downtown Portsmouth, New Hampshire, and the Piscataqua River waterfront, demonstrating rich street-level mapping and the expanded layer switcher menu.

## Interface & Sidebar
- **Top Navigation Tabs**: `View` (active), `Edit`, `Export`, `GPS Traces`, and `User Diaries`, with `log in | sign up` at the top right.
- **Left Sidebar**:
  - **Logo**: `OpenStreetMap` title, magnifying glass over a map icon, and `The Free Wiki World Map`.
  - **About Box**: Description of OpenStreetMap and hosting credit to `UCL VR Centre` and `bytemark`.
  - **Links Box**: `Help & Wiki`, `News blog`, `Shop`, `Map key`.
  - **Conference Promo Box**: Light blue/lavender box reading: `Come to the second OpenStreetMap Conference, The State of the Map: 12th-13th July 2008, Limerick, Ireland.`
  - **Search Box**: `Search` header with a `Where am I?` link, text input box, `Go` button, and examples (`'Alkmaar'`, `'Regent Street, Cambridge'`, `'CB2 5AQ'`, etc.).

## Map Details & Controls
- **Geographic Features & Roads**:
  - Shows the Piscataqua River and North Mill Pond in muted blue-gray and downtown Portsmouth in light gray/off-white.
  - Major red/pink primary highways include the US Route 1 bypass / bridge to the northwest (`Northwest St` nearby) and `United States Highway 1` crossing the Memorial Bridge into downtown via `Daniel St`, `Congress St`, and `Middle St`.
  - Secondary yellow/orange roads include `Islington Street`, `Marcy St`, `Newcastle Ave`, and `Market St`.
  - Numerous white residential and downtown streets are labeled, including `Maplewood Ave`, `Deer St`, `High St`, `Bow St`, `State St`, `Court St`, `Pleasant St`, `Junkins Ave`, `Pierce Island Rd`, and the dashed `Boston and Maine Railroad` line.
  - Across the bridge in Kittery, Maine (top right), streets such as `Government St`, `Water St`, `Walker St`, and `Wentworth St` are visible.
- **Map Overlays & Controls**:
  - **Layer Switcher (Top Right)**: Expanded semi-transparent dark-blue panel showing `Base Layer` radio buttons (`Mapnik` selected, `Osmarender` unselected) and `Overlays` checkbox (`Maplint` unselected).
  - **Zoom/Pan (Top Left)**: Blue directional pad and zoom slider set near the top (high zoom).
  - **Bottom Bar**: Scale bar (`200 m` / `1000 ft`) at bottom left and `Permalink` at bottom right.
