# OpenStreetMap View of St. Lawrence Island Showing Coastline Rendering Artifact (`maps-osm.png`)

## Overview
A 2008 screenshot of the OpenStreetMap (OSM) web interface centered on St. Lawrence Island, Alaska, showing an unpopulated map of the island with sharp rectangular tile-boundary coastline rendering artifacts.

## Interface & Sidebar
- **Top Navigation Tabs**: `View` (active), `Edit` (italicized), `Export`, `GPS Traces`, and `User Diaries`, with `log in | sign up` links at the top right.
- **Left Sidebar**:
  - **Logo**: `OpenStreetMap` title above a graphic of a magnifying glass over a hand-drawn street map and the subtitle `The Free Wiki World Map`.
  - **Description Box**: Three short paragraphs explaining that OpenStreetMap is a free editable map of the whole world made by people like you, with hosting kindly supported by the `UCL VR Centre` and `bytemark`.
  - **Bottom Links**: Gray box with bold links for `Help & Wiki`, `News blog`, `Shop`, and `Map key` (partially cut off).

## Map Area & Rendering Bug
- **Map Canvas**: Displays St. Lawrence Island in off-white against a muted blue-gray sea background, with no roads, place names, or trails present.
- **Rendering Artifact**: Instead of a natural island outline, only the northwestern peninsula and northern/eastern coastline segments are recognizable; the central and southern portions of the island are blocky, rectangular white tiles and geometric triangles caused by incomplete shoreline polygon rendering across map tiles.
- **Map Controls**:
  - Blue circular pan arrows and vertical zoom slider bar (`+` to `-`, set near a low-to-medium zoom level) in the upper left.
  - Blue `+` layer switcher tab on the upper right edge.
  - Scale bar at bottom left showing `50 km` and `20 mi`.
  - Blue `Permalink` link at bottom right.
