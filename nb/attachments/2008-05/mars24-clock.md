# NASA GISS Mars24 Sunclock Application Windows (`mars24-clock.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot showing three windows of the NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies (GISS) **Mars24** sunclock application (`Mars24 - Sunclock`, `Mars24 - Time`, and `Mars24 Settings`), tracking Martian solar time and spacecraft landing sites including the Mars Phoenix Lander (`PHX`).

## Window 1: `Mars24 - Sunclock` (Background Left)
- Displays an orthographic globe projection of Mars centered on its northern hemisphere (`Sunclock (Orthographic) Centered on 0.00°W, 30.00°N` in green text at the very bottom).
- The bright white North Polar residual ice cap is prominent near the top, surrounded by a dashed tan longitude-latitude grid and a curved day/night terminator shading the left side of the planet.
- White text and a small crosshair mark the `PHX` landing site in the high northern latitudes just west of the polar cap.

## Window 2: `Mars24 - Time` (Foreground Lower Left)
- Set against a black background with digital-style green, red, and white monospace text:
  - **`• MARS •` Section**:
    - Left column: `MER-A 15:02:09` (green), `Sol 1558` (red), `MSD 47773.168` (red), `04:02:05 MTC` (red).
    - Right column: `MER-B 03:01:00` (green), `Sol 1538` (red), `Ls 75.00°` (red), `Late SH Autumn` (red).
  - **`• EARTH •` Section**:
    - Left column: `00:20:47 UTC` (green), `2008-05-22` (red), `MJD 54608.014` (red).
    - Right column: `17:20:47 MST` (green), `2008-05-21` (red), `OWLT 15:03 min` (One-Way Light Time, red).

## Window 3: `Mars24 Settings` (Right)
- **Top Controls**: `Time` and `Plot` tabs (with `Plot` active), and a `Show:` dropdown set to `Sunclock Map`.
- **`Sunclock Options` Panel**:
  - `Map Projection:` dropdown set to `Orthographic`.
  - `Center of Map:` radio buttons with `Lon. 0.00 °W, Lat. 90.00 °N` selected, and `Landmark: Airy Crater` unselected below.
  - `Nightside Darkness:` text field set to `70.0 %`.
  - Checked box for `Show Lon-Lat grid, Opacity: 50.0 %`.
  - **`Points of Interest:` Table** (with columns `Mark`, `ID`, `Name`, `Lon W`, `Lat N`):
    - Checked (`☑`): `Subsolar Point` (`242.8`, `24.5`), `Sub-Earth Point` (`277.7`, `16.8`), `MERA` (`MER-A Spirit site`, `184.5`, `-14.7`), `MERB` (`MER-B Opportunity site`, `5.5`, `-2.0`), and `PHX` (`Mars Phoenix site`, `120.0`, `70.0`).
    - Unchecked (`☐`): `MPF` (`Mars Pathfinder site`, `33.3`, `19.5`), `VIK1` (`Viking 1 site`, `48.0`, `22.7`), `VIK2` (`Viking 2 site`, `225.7`, `48.3`), `BEA` (`Beagle 2 site`, `269.5`, `11.6`), `M2` (`Mars 2 site`, `302.0`, `-45.0`), `M3` (`Mars 3 site`, `158.0`, `-45.0`), and `M6` (`Mars 6 site`, `19.4`, `-23.9`).
