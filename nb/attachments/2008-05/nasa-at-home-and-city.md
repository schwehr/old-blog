# NASA @ Home and City Interactive Flash Website (`nasa-at-home-and-city.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X browser window screenshot of the interactive Adobe Flash website **NASA @ Home and City** (`http://www.nasa.gov - NASA @ Home and City`), featuring a 3D cartoon-style floating island city that highlights everyday commercial technologies and spinoffs derived from NASA research.

## Scene & 3D Graphics
- **Floating Island City**: A green grass-covered circular island floats against a dark blue starry sky background.
  - **Left Side (`HOUSEHOLD`)**: A yellow two-story suburban house with blue shutters, a red roof, a white picket fence, stepping stones, and green trees, accompanied by a white callout bubble reading `▶ HOUSEHOLD / Discover NASA in your household`.
  - **Center Background**: Three tall modern office skyscrapers (the tallest topped with a white satellite dish) and a blank white highway billboard on posts in front of them.
  - **Right Side (`AIR TRAVEL`)**: A modern glass-walled airport terminal with white boarding bridges, a small commercial airliner on a runway, a yellow sports stadium in the background, and a factory with three orange-tipped smokestacks, accompanied by a white callout bubble reading `▶ AIR TRAVEL / Discover NASA all around you`.
  - **Rotation Arrows**: Two large glossy 3D blue curved arrows sit on the left and right edges of the island to rotate the 3D scene.

## Navigation Bars
- **Top Bar**:
  - Left title: `▾ CURRENTLY EXPLORING: NASA CITY`.
  - Center icon dock: Eight rounded rectangular buttons with 3D icons representing categories: an airplane, a delivery van, a magnifying glass, a classical column, a medical cross, a factory with smokestacks, a shopping cart, and a sports stadium.
  - Right button: Blue pill button reading `▶ Visit NASA @ HOME`.
- **Bottom Bar**: Three light-blue/white folder-style tabs on the bottom right reading:
  - `NASA SPINOFF-RELATED ARCHIVE`
  - `NASA TECHNOLOGY IMPACT NEWS`
  - `NASA SPINOFF CHALLENGE`
