# JMARS Screenshot of HiRISE Image Mosaicking Over the Phoenix Landing Ellipse (`phoenix_ellipse_mosaicking.jpg`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of the **JMARS** (Java Mission-planning and Analysis for Remote Sensing) GIS application centered at `234.009E 68.117N`, displaying the green landing ellipse for the Mars Phoenix Lander overlaid with yellow rectangular footprints of High Resolution Imaging Science Experiment (HiRISE) orbital images across the "Green Valley" region of Mars.

## Map View & Overlays
- **Background Imagery**: A composite orbital view of the northern plains of Mars showing blueish-gray and reddish-brown polygonal terrain, with a prominent circular impact crater (`Heimdall` vicinity / smaller crater with ejecta) to the upper right of the ellipse and dark unmosaicked background gaps on the left and lower right.
- **Phoenix Landing Ellipse**: A bright green elongated oval (with small green vertex dots along its perimeter) oriented northwest-to-southeast across the center of the view.
- **HiRISE Image Footprints & Labels**: Numerous overlapping, slightly tilted yellow rectangular bounding boxes spanning the landing ellipse, each labeled in white text with its HiRISE observation ID (`PSP_xxxxxx_xxxx`), including:
  - `PSP_007629_2485`, `PSP_002025_2485`, `PSP_002012_2485`, `PSP_007774_2485`, `PSP_001972_2485`, `PSP_007563_2485`, `PSP_001880_2485`, `PSP_001946_2485`, `PSP_002099_2485`, `PSP_002170_2485`, `PSP_001893_2485`, `PSP_001906_2485`, `PSP_002104_2485`, `PSP_007497_2480`, `PSP_007642_2480`, `PSP_007708_2485`, `PSP_007853_2485`, `PSP_007787_2480`, and `PSP_007932_2480`.

## Interface & Controls
- **Title Bar & Menu**: Window titled `JMARS 234.009E 68.117N` with `File`, `View`, and `Help` menu items.
- **Top Toolbar**: `Lon, Lat:` coordinate input box containing `234.009E, 68.117` on the left, and a `Zoom:` dropdown set to `512` on the right.
- **Bottom Overview Map & Status Bar**:
  - A wide horizontal shaded-relief polar stereographic grid strip showing the high northern latitudes of Mars with a small red rectangle and green marker indicating the main viewport location.
  - Bottom status bar displaying cursor/view telemetry: `236.71E   68.29N`, `360.63x   -1.70y`, and `1120r   308d`.
