# Global Topographic Map of Mars Landing Sites Including Phoenix (`phx-landing.jpg`)

## Overview
A global Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter (MOLA) color-coded shaded relief map showing the locations of six successful NASA Mars landers and rovers: **Phoenix**, **Viking 1**, **Viking 2**, **Mars Pathfinder**, **Opportunity**, and **Spirit**.

## Map & Geographic Features
- **Axes & Framing**: Framed by a black-and-white dashed border with white latitude labels along the left margin at `60`, `30`, `0`, `-30`, and `-60` degrees.
- **Topographic Color Scale**:
  - Deep blue and purple tones represent the lowest elevations, including the vast northern lowlands (Vastitas Borealis) across the top of the map and the deep circular Hellas Planitia impact basin in the southern hemisphere (lower right-center).
  - Green and yellow tones represent intermediate elevations and the dichotomy boundary.
  - Orange, red, pink, and white tones represent the heavily cratered southern highlands, the Tharsis volcanic plateau on the left (with white peaks for Olympus Mons and the three Tharsis Montes volcanoes, plus the blue-green canyon system of Valles Marineris extending eastward), and Elysium Mons on the right.

## Labeled Spacecraft Landing Sites
Each landing site is marked with a black circular target dot with a white center and bold black text outlined in white:
1. **`Phoenix`**: Located near the top left in the high northern arctic plains above `60°N` (Vastitas Borealis, ~68°N).
2. **`Viking 1`**: Located in Chryse Planitia north of Valles Marineris near `22°N`.
3. **`Mars Pathfinder`**: Located just east-southeast of `Viking 1` at the mouth of Ares Vallis near `19°N`.
4. **`Opportunity`**: Located near the equator (`0°`) in Meridiani Planum, southeast of `Mars Pathfinder`.
5. **`Viking 2`**: Located in the northern plains of Utopia Planitia in the upper right quadrant near `48°N`.
6. **`Spirit`**: Located near the far right edge south of the equator (`~14°S`) in Gusev Crater.
