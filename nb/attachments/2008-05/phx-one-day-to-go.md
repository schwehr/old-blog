# Phoenix Mars Lander Mission Phases & Landing Countdown Widget (`phx-one-day-to-go.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a web widget from the NASA/University of Arizona Phoenix Mars Mission website, captured approximately one day and five hours before the spacecraft's landing on Mars in May 2008.

## Layout & Visual Elements
The widget has a black background framed by thin gray borders and is divided vertically into a mission phase description panel and a digital countdown clock:

- **Header & Phase Selector**:
  - Top label in light gray uppercase text: `MISSION PHASES`.
  - Below it, a dark brown/gray horizontal navigation bar with left and right double-triangle arrow buttons (`<<` and `>>`) in gold/tan flanking the bold white title: **`4: LANDING`**.
- **Phase Description & Graphic**:
  - An orange-red uppercase subheading reads: **`ENTER, DESCEND, & LAND`**.
  - Left-aligned light gray body text explains:
    > First friction, then a parachute, and finally thrusters will set Phoenix down on the Martian surface.
  - Below the paragraph is an underlined gold link: **`LEARN MORE`**.
  - On the right side of the panel, a grayscale rendering of the Phoenix lander is shown with its three landing legs deployed and three vertical plumes of thruster exhaust glowing beneath its baseplate.
- **Landing Countdown Panel**:
  - Separated by a horizontal gray divider line, a centered label reads `LANDING COUNTDOWN` in light gray uppercase letters.
  - Inside a dark taupe rectangular box, large cream/white serif numerals display the remaining time:
    **`001 : 05 : 36 : 52`**
  - Directly beneath each number group are gray unit labels: `DAYS`, `HRS`, `MIN`, and `SEC`.
