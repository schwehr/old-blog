# Artist's Rendering of Phoenix Mars Lander Firing Descent Thrusters (`phx-rockets.png`)

## Overview
A computer-generated artist's rendering (frame from a NASA/JPL mission animation, cataloged as PIA10666) depicting the **Phoenix Mars Lander** firing its pulsed descent rocket thrusters moments before touching down on the northern polar plains of Mars.

## Scene Description
- **Spacecraft (Upper Left)**: The Phoenix lander is shown descending toward the lower right in a slightly banked attitude. Its three extendable tripod landing legs with circular footpads are fully deployed. Beneath the spacecraft chassis, two clusters of bright white-and-yellow rocket exhaust plumes shoot downward toward the ground. The spacecraft's folded circular solar arrays, propellant tanks, bio-barrier/robotic arm enclosure, and deck instruments are visible on the upper structure.
- **Sky & Sun (Center Right)**: The Martian sky is a dusty butterscotch/ochre-orange gradient. Low on the right horizon, the small, hazy white disc of the Sun glows through thin, wispy horizontal clouds and atmospheric dust.
- **Martian Surface (Bottom)**: Across the foreground and horizon lies a dark brown, rocky, hummocky polar plain with subtle polygonal trough textures and small patches of pale frost or bright dust along the distant ridges.
