# Posamentier & Vail (1988) Sequence Stratigraphy Diagram: Type 1 Unconformity (`posementier-slug-model-2.png`)

## Overview
A geological cross-section diagram ("slug model") labeled **`FIG. 23.—Type 1 unconformity.`** from Posamentier and Vail (1988), *Eustatic Controls on Clastic Deposition II — Sequence and Systems Tract Models* (SEPM Special Publication No. 42). The diagram illustrates the stratigraphic geometry, facies distribution, and bounding surfaces associated with a Type 1 sequence boundary formed during a rapid eustatic sea-level fall.

## Diagram Components

### 1. Stratigraphic Cross-Section (Center)
A sigmoidal sedimentary wedge pinching out landward to the upper left and prograding basinward to the lower right, color-coded by depositional environment:
- **Facies Labels & Colors**:
  - `ALLUVIAL PLAIN`: Orange/tan wedge at the far upper-left landward margin.
  - `COASTAL PLAIN`: Reddish-pink strata interfingering between the alluvial plain and beach deposits, appearing both in the main highstand wedge on the left and in a smaller perched wedge (`ALLUVIAL PLAIN/COASTAL PLAIN`) near the shelf break.
  - `BEACH`: Bright yellow stair-stepping nearshore sand bodies at the topset-foreset rollover of both the main wedge and the lower shelf-margin wedge.
  - `OFFSHORE MARINE`: Large green clinoform body forming the bulk of the prograding slope and basin-floor deposits.
  - `DEEP-SEA FAN`: Bright yellow mounded deposit with a wavy upper contact resting at the toe-of-slope on the far right.
- **Bounding Surfaces**:
  - `TYPE 1 UNCONFORMITY`: Indicated by a wavy/crenulated erosional line across the top of the upper alluvial/coastal plain and shelf (`TYPE 1 UNCONFORMITY` arrow pointing down on the left) and continuing beneath the `DEEP-SEA FAN` at the base of the slope (`TYPE 1 UNCONFORMITY` arrow pointing up on the bottom right).
  - `DISCONFORMITY (DOWNLAP SURFACE)`: Indicated by a downward arrow on the far right pointing to the upper surface of the `DEEP-SEA FAN` where overlying clinoforms downlap.

### 2. Eustacy Curve (Upper Right)
A sine-wave plot titled `EUSTACY` showing sea level (`HIGH` with upward arrow vs. `LOW` with downward arrow) over `TIME` (rightward arrow):
- Two vertical dashed lines intersect the falling limb of the sea-level curve:
  - The first dashed line (just before the steepest inflection point, marked by a black dot) is labeled `TYPE 1 UNCONFORMITY`.
  - The second dashed line (approaching the lowstand trough) is labeled `DISCONFORMITY`.

### 3. Caption
Centered at the bottom in serif font:
`FIG. 23.—Type 1 unconformity.`
