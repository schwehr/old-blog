# Posamentier & Vail (1988) Sequence Stratigraphy Diagram: Highstand Systems Tract (`posementier-slug-model.png`)

## Overview
A color-coded geological block diagram and cross-section (informally known in sequence stratigraphy as a "slug model") from Posamentier and Vail (1988), *Eustatic Controls on Clastic Deposition II — Sequence and Systems Tract Models* (SEPM Special Publication No. 42). The figure illustrates the depositional architecture and facies distribution of a **Highstand Systems Tract** during the late highstand stage of a eustatic sea-level cycle.

## Diagram Components

### 1. Enlarged Cross-Section Callout (Top Left)
An oval inset magnifies the clinoform geometries of the `HIGHSTAND SYSTEMS TRACT` at the shelf margin, showing how coastal and fluvial sediments interfinger with offshore marine shales:
- **Upper Package**: Bracketed on the right as `LATE HIGHSTAND (PREDOMINANTLY PROGRADATIONAL)`, showing flatter, strongly prograding red (fluvial) and yellow (coastal plain/nearshore) strata stepping basinward over green (offshore marine) clinoforms.
- **Lower Package**: Bracketed below as `EARLY HIGHSTAND (PREDOMINANTLY AGGRADATIONAL)`, showing more steeply climbing, aggradational yellow and red topsets transitioning into green sigmoidal foresets.

### 2. 3D Block Diagram (Center)
Shows a passive continental margin stretching from the upland source area on the right to the deep marine basin on the left:
- **Surface Features & Bathymetry**:
  - `ALLUVIAL PLAIN` (colored magenta/red with meandering river channels flowing from gray bedrock uplands on the far right).
  - `SHELF` and shoreline (`0` contour line) bordered by a yellow band representing coastal plain and nearshore environments.
  - `SEA LEVEL` indicated by the blue water body on the left.
  - Green continental slope and basin floor marked with depth contour lines: `0`, `200m`, `400m`, `600m`, and `800m`, flattening out at the bottom left labeled `BASIN`.
- **Front Cross-Sectional Profile**: Displays the lens-shaped green, yellow, and red stratigraphic wedge of the `HIGHSTAND SYSTEMS TRACT` (circled and connected to the upper inset), resting atop older sequence units on the gray basement wedge.

### 3. Lithology Legend (Bottom Left)
Five rectangular swatch boxes under the heading `LITHOLOGY`:
- **Stippled light tan/white**: `SUBMARINE FAN FACIES`
- **Stippled yellow**: `COASTAL PLAIN AND NEAR SHORE FACIES`
- **Red with circles/dots**: `FLUVIAL FACIES`
- **Green with dashed lines**: `OFFSHORE MARINE FACIES`
- **White box with channel-and-levee symbol**: `LEVEED CHANNEL FACIES`

### 4. Eustacy Curve (Bottom Right)
A sinusoidal curve titled `EUSTACY` plotting sea level (`HIGH` vs. `LOW`) against `TIME` (with a rightward arrow). A highlighted blue arc over the first peak/early falling limb marks the timing of the Highstand Systems Tract within the eustatic cycle, with small dots marking inflection points along the curve.
