# Annotated Rock Size Measurements Near the Phoenix Mars Lander (`sizes-sol3-v8.png`)

## Overview
An annotated grayscale Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) mosaic from Sol 3 of the NASA Phoenix Mars Lander mission, labeled by Kurt Schwehr using stereo measurements computed with NASA Ames Research Center's Viz software to show the dimensions of four rocks in the Robotic Arm workspace.

## Scene Description & Visual Elements
- **Martian Surface & Shadows**: Looking steeply downward at the fine-grained, pebble-strewn regolith immediately adjacent to the lander. A dark, jagged shadow of the lander body fills the lower half of the image, while the long, lattice-like shadow of the SSI camera mast and camera head projects outward onto the sunlit soil on the right. Because the mosaic combines frames taken at different times of day, the shadow of the mast and lander deck exhibits sharp vertical discontinuities.
- **Lander Hardware**: Portions of the metallic lander deck and circular calibration targets appear on the lower left, a dark circular rim sits at the bottom center-right, and the edge of a solar panel extends into the far right margin.

## Visible Text & Labels
- **Yellow Rock Dimension Callouts**: Four rocks are measured with bright yellow horizontal dimension bars (`|---|`) positioned directly above each rock and labeled in yellow sans-serif text:
  - `4.8 in` (upper left, measuring a rounded cobble near the edge of the deck)
  - `8.4 in` (upper center-left, measuring a flat, angular rock embedded in the soil)
  - `5.4 in` (center-left, measuring a rock resting right along the edge of the lander's shadow)
  - `5.2 in` (upper center-right, measuring a light-toned flat rock to the left of the camera mast shadow)
- **Azimuth Scale (Bottom Edge)**: White numbers inside black rectangular boxes along the bottom border mark the compass/azimuth angle in 10-degree increments from left to right:
  - `330`, `340`, `350`, `0`, `10`, `20`, `30`, `40`, `50`, `60`, `70`
