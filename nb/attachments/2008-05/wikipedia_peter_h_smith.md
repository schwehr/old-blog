# Screenshot of the Peter H. Smith Wikipedia Article (`wikipedia_peter_h_smith.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X Safari web browser screenshot captured on May 10, 2008, showing the newly created English Wikipedia biographical article for **Peter H. Smith**, Principal Investigator for NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander mission at the University of Arizona's Lunar and Planetary Laboratory (LPL).

## Browser Interface & Controls
- **Window Title Bar**: Standard Mac OS X Aqua brushed-metal window header with red, yellow, and green window buttons on the left and the title `Peter H Smith - Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia`.
- **Browser Toolbar**: Back/Forward green arrow buttons, blue Reload button, Stop (`X`) button, brown Home icon, address bar displaying `http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_H_Smith` with a Wikipedia `W` favicon and orange RSS icon, green Go arrow, and a Google search field on the right.
- **Wikipedia User Bar (Top Right)**: Logged in as user `Goatbar`, followed by links for `my talk`, `my preferences`, `my watchlist`, `my contributions`, and `log out`.
- **Article Tabs**: `article` (selected), `discussion`, `edit this page`, `history`, `move`, and `watch`.
- **Left Sidebar**: Classic Wikipedia jigsaw globe logo (`WIKIPEDIA The Free Encyclopedia`), followed by `navigation` (`Main Page`, `Contents`, `Featured content`, `Current events`, `Random article`), `interaction` (`About Wikipedia`, `Community portal`, `Recent changes`, `Contact Wikipedia`, `Donate to Wikipedia`, `Help`), a `search` input box with `Go` and `Search` buttons, and `toolbox` (`What links here`).

## Article Content & Portrait
- **Heading**: `Peter H Smith` (`From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia`).
- **Infobox Thumbnail (Right)**: A color photograph captioned `Peter Smith` showing him smiling, wearing wire-rimmed glasses and a blue collared shirt with a gray mustache and white hair, seated in front of a colorful planetary poster partially reading `...s Lander` and a bookshelf.
- **Visible Body Text**:
  - Paragraph 1: Details his 1969 graduation in physics from the University of California Berkeley and his 1977 master's degree from the University of Arizona Optical Sciences Center.
  - Paragraph 2: Notes his employment at the UA's Lunar and Planetary Laboratory since 1978, advancing from Research Assistant to Senior Research Scientist while participating in seminal solar system space missions.
  - Paragraph 3: Covers his work on the 1978 Pioneer Venus mission, 1979 Pioneer Saturn mission, 1994 Hubble Space Telescope mapping of Titan, and serving as Project Manager for the descent camera on the Huygens probe that landed on Titan in early 2005.
  - Paragraph 4: Describes his Mars camera work starting in 1993 with the Imager for Mars Pathfinder (1997), followed by building cameras for the 1999 Mars Polar Lander and the cancelled 2001 Surveyor mission.
