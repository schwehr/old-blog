# Shadow-Removed Dodo-Goldilocks Trench on Sol 24 (`SS024EFF898338344_12DEER1M1_002-sml.jpg`)

## Overview
This grayscale photograph captured by the Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) on NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander on Sol 24 (June 19, 2008) shows the widened "Dodo-Goldilocks" trench after digital shadow-removal processing by Joel Hagen. Featured in the June 19, 2008 blog post (*"Location and shadow removed trench"*), this image was part of the historic sequence demonstrating that bright crumbs in the lower-left portion of the trench had sublimated (vaporized) between Sol 20 and Sol 24, proving the bright material was water ice.

## Scene & Trench Features
- **Double-Width Trench Structure**: The trench extends diagonally from the bottom-center toward the upper-right and consists of two parallel excavated tracks:
  - **Left Track (Dodo side)**: A slightly shallower shelf along the left side where a small cluster of dark-shadowed pebbles and soil clods sits near the upper-middle step, where bright ice crumbs had previously been observed on Sol 20 before sublimating away.
  - **Right Track (Goldilocks side)**: A deeper, flat-bottomed trough running the full length of the trench, exposing a smooth, bright white/light-gray subsurface ice table scored with longitudinal scrape marks from the Robotic Arm scoop. Near the lower-right floor of the trough is a bright, irregular white patch of exposed ice alongside a darker scuffed depression.
- **Spoil Mounds & Rims**: Two distinct rounded mounds of excavated soil sit side-by-side at the far upper-right headwall of the trench, while coarse, crumbly soil clods are piled around the foreground rim at the bottom-left and bottom-center.
- **Lander Hardware & Shadows**:
  - In the lower-right corner, a brightly sunlit section of the Phoenix lander's deck or solar panel structure—complete with white wiring bundles tied along a strut—protrudes into the frame.
  - In the upper-left corner, a dark, unadjusted shadow cast by lander hardware falls across the pebbly Martian soil.
