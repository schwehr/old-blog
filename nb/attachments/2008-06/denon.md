# Denon AK-DL1 Ultra Premium Link Ethernet Cable (`denon.gif`)

## Overview
A studio product photograph on a plain white background showing the two terminated ends of a **Denon AK-DL1 "Ultra Premium Denon Link Cable"**—a 1.5-meter Cat-6/RJ-45 Ethernet patch cable marketed to audiophiles for $500, discussed in a June 15, 2008 blog post critiquing extreme audiophile cable marketing.

## Visual Details
- **Connectors (Plugs)**: Two oversized, custom-molded RJ-45 modular plugs angled toward the bottom-left corner:
  - Each plug features a clear polycarbonate front housing with visible gold-plated contact pins, a metallic silver internal shield, and a clear plastic strain-relief latch tab.
  - The main connector bodies are encased in a sculpted, metallic dark-blue anodized or painted housing with black rubberized rear strain-relief boots and side grip indentations.
- **Markings & Signal Direction Arrows**:
  - The upper-left connector housing has **`DENON`** printed in silver sans-serif capital letters along its top spine.
  - The lower-right connector housing has **`DENON`** above **`LINK`** separated by two opposing silver directional arrows (one pointing forward toward the plug tip, the other pointing backward toward the cable), illustrating the "signal directional markings" noted in the product description.
- **Cable Jacket**: Two segments of thick, round cable extend toward the upper right, wrapped in a black woven mesh sleeving accented with a repeating spiral pattern of light-blue/cyan chevrons.
