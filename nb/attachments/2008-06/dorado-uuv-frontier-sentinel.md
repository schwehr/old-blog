# Dorado UUV Semi-Submersible Vehicle at Frontier Sentinel (`dorado-uuv-frontier-sentinel.png`)

## Overview
A still frame captured from a *SeacoastOnline* YouTube video covering the **Frontier Sentinel** joint U.S.–Canadian maritime homeland security and mine-countermeasures exercise in Portsmouth, New Hampshire (June 2008). The image shows a close-up low-angle view of the stern propulsor and control planes of the Canadian Navy's **Dorado** unmanned underwater vehicle (UUV) / semi-submersible mine-hunting drone being hoisted by a crane.
