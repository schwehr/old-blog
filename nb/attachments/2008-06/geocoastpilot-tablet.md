# GeoCoastPilot Application on a Windows Tablet (`geocoastpilot-tablet.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical (portrait-mode) screenshot of the **GeoCoastPilot** 3D coastal navigation and electronic chart software (developed at the UNH Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center) running on a 12-inch Lenovo Windows XP Tablet PC. The view shows the entrance to Portsmouth Harbor, New Hampshire, integrating 3D bathymetry, navigational aids, geo-referenced coastal photographs, and interactive text from the NOAA *United States Coast Pilot*.

## 3D Perspective Viewport (Upper Two-Thirds)
- **Window Title Bar**: Standard blue Windows XP title bar at the top labeled **`GeoCoastPilot`** with minimize, maximize, and close buttons, above a menu bar (`File`, `Edit`, `Help`).
- **Terrain & Bathymetry**: An oblique 3D view looking northwest across the Piscataqua River entrance and coastal islands:
  - Landmasses are rendered in pale yellow, bordered by blue depth-contour bands along the shoreline and grey-shaded 3D bathymetric relief on the seafloor.
  - Magenta lines trace navigational channels and chart boundaries.
- **Navigational Aids & Embedded Photos**:
  - Green cylindrical/can buoys, red conical/nun buoys (`Cod_Rock_Lighted_Buoy_5` in the foreground), and yellow markers are rendered as 3D symbols on the water.
  - Multiple geo-referenced inset photographs of coastal landmarks (including the Wentworth by the Sea hotel, lighthouses, towers, and the Memorial/Sarah Long bridges) float above their respective geographic locations with `100 m` scale bars and labels such as `tower`, `church spire/minaret`, and `spire/minaret`.
- **HUD Overlays**:
  - Bottom left: A vertical color scale bar labeled **`Depth (feet)`** ranging from `0.0 ft` (blue) through `10.0 ft` to `20.0 ft` (black), next to a circular **CCOM / JHC** watermark logo.
  - Bottom right: A 3D compass rose floating over the water showing `N`, `W`, `S`, `E`.

## Text & Control Panels (Lower Third)
- **Left Panel (Coast Pilot Text)**: Displays hyperlinked paragraphs from the NOAA Coast Pilot alongside chart links:
  - Paragraph `(174)` discussing Cape Neddick, Portsmouth Harbor, York Harbor, Godfreys Cove, and Brave Boat Harbor.
  - Links in blue text: **`Chart 13283`**, **`Chart 13274`**, **`Chart 13285`**.
  - Paragraph `(175)` on **Brave Boat Harbor (43°06.0'N., 70°39.6'W.)** and **Raynes Neck**.
  - Paragraph `(176)` on **Cutts Island** and Gerrish Island.
- **Right Panel (Controls & Coordinates)**:
  - Search box with `<` and `Find >` buttons.
  - **Base Display** radio buttons: `Nautical Chart` (unchecked) and `Bathymetry` (selected).
  - **Display key features** radio buttons: `Actual size` (unchecked) and `Exaggerated` (selected).
  - Position & camera readout: **`43°04'36.78"N  070°43'33.67"W`**, `Lat` `43.076885`, `Lon` `-70.726021`, `Heading` `285.22`, `Pitch` `8.00`, with a `Goto` button.
  - Navigation buttons: `Home`, `Aerial`, `Overview`, `NorthUp`, and a pink-highlighted button `Show Rotation & Measurement Tools`.
  - A blank text area at the bottom labeled **`Feature:`**.
