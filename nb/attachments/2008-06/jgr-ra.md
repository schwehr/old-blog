# Phoenix Mars Lander Robotic Arm Workspaces Diagram (`jgr-ra.png`)

## Overview
A 3D CAD schematic illustration from a 2008 *Journal of Geophysical Research* paper by Bonitz et al. (*"NASA Mars 2007 Phoenix Lander Robotic Arm and Icy Soil Acquisition Device"*). The diagram depicts the **Phoenix Mars Lander** and uses color-coded, semi-transparent 3D geometric volumes to show the operational reach and designated workspaces of its Robotic Arm (RA).

## Diagram & Visual Details
- **Spacecraft Model**:
  - The three-legged Phoenix lander is shown in an isometric view against a light blue-to-grey vertical gradient background.
  - Two circular decagonal solar panel arrays in deep purple extend to the upper left and lower left.
  - The white octagonal science deck supports the stowed/extended Robotic Arm along the center-right, along with science instruments (TEGA and MECA) on the upper deck.
  - Green-and-yellow strut legs terminate in circular yellow footpads.
- **Translucent Workspace Volumes**:
  - **Green (`Surface Workspace 1`)**: A curved arc volume at the lowest level on the ground in front of the lander (bottom right), representing the primary trenching and soil-acquisition zone.
  - **Yellow (`Surface Workspace 2`)**: A wider curved arc volume directly overlapping and surrounding the green workspace on the surface.
  - **Orange (`Surface Workspace 3`)**: A tall vertical curved column extending from the surface up to the height of the robotic arm boom on the right.
  - **Blue (`Dump Workspace`)**: Two rectangular/wedge-shaped blue volumes located to the left and rear of the orange column, designated for dumping excess soil samples.
  - **Red (`Deck Workspace`)**: A large semi-cylindrical red volume above the lander's science deck, encompassing the instrument delivery ports (such as TEGA and MECA).
- **Legend (Bottom Left)**:
  ```
  Surface Workspace 1  green
  Surface Workspace 2  yellow
  Surface Workspace 3  orange
  Dump Workspace       blue
  Deck Workspace       red
  ```
