# Kurt Schwehr Running Graphics at Phoenix Mars Press Conference (`kurt-phx-production-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A horizontal color photograph taken inside the video production and control room at the University of Arizona Science Operations Center (SOC) in June 2008, showing **Kurt Schwehr** operating the still-image and animation video feed during a NASA Phoenix Mars Lander press conference.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Left (Kurt Schwehr)**: Kurt sits in profile facing right at a multi-monitor computer workstation. He wears a backwards black baseball cap, glasses, a communications headset with a boom microphone, and a maroon hooded sweatshirt (UNH). His hands rest on a white Apple keyboard and mouse, with a second slim aluminum Apple keyboard, mouse, and color reference sheets on the desk in front of him.
- **Monitors & Equipment**:
  - The left LCD monitor (with yellow sticky notes on its bezel) displays an overhead visualization of the **Phoenix Mars Lander** with its two circular solar arrays deployed over polygonal patterned ground on Mars.
  - The right LCD monitor shows video editing / playback software (Final Cut Pro) with a timeline and video preview window.
  - Behind the monitors sit a white office telephone and stacked rack-mount audio/video decks with blue digital readouts.
- **Background Right**: A colleague in a tan button-down shirt, dark tie, red lanyard, glasses, and a headset sits at an adjacent workstation behind another flat-panel monitor, holding his left hand to his headset microphone while watching the production feed.
