# Wide View of the Phoenix Mars Press Conference Control Room (`kurt-phx-production-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle color photograph taken from behind **Kurt Schwehr's** workstation inside the broadcast production control room during a June 2008 NASA Phoenix Mars Lander press conference, showing the full team, equipment racks, and live broadcast monitors.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Workstations)**:
  - **Center**: Kurt Schwehr is seen from behind, wearing a maroon hooded sweatshirt, a black baseball cap worn backwards (with a small red emblem), and a headset, looking toward his dual Apple Cinema Display monitors and a MacBook Pro laptop on the right. The monitors show Final Cut Pro timelines and Mars surface imagery.
  - **Left**: Another operator sits in front of a large flat-panel monitor showing a Surface Stereo Imager view of the Martian surface and the lander's deck/solar panel.
- **Middle Ground & Background (Broadcast Control Area)**:
  - **Left**: Tall black 19-inch equipment racks filled with video switchers, audio patch bays, and signal processors with glowing blue and green LEDs. A technician in a grey t-shirt stands beside the racks.
  - **Center & Right**: Several team members—including a seated person in a dark shirt, a standing woman with long brown hair wearing a dark t-shirt (`PHOENIX 2007` on the back), and a tall standing man in a white short-sleeved shirt and cap—monitor three large wall-mounted flat-screen displays.
  - **Wall Displays & Clock**:
    - Above the glass partition hangs a red digital LED wall clock reading **`10:57`**.
    - The left large display shows a quad-split video feed of speakers at the press briefing podium with an American flag in the background.
    - The center display shows a title graphic with a glowing crescent of Mars.
    - The right display shows the live camera view of the press conference panel.
