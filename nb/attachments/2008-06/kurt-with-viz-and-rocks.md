# Kurt Schwehr with Phoenix Lander Mockup and Viz Terrain Printout (`kurt-with-viz-and-rocks.jpg`)

## Overview
A horizontal color photograph taken on June 5, 2008, inside the exhibit hall at the Phoenix Mars Lander Science Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona. **Kurt Schwehr** stands beside a full-scale engineering mockup of the Phoenix lander and a floor printout of the 3D `Viz` terrain model populated with physical rocks matching the rock distribution at the Martian landing site.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Left (Kurt Schwehr)**: Kurt stands facing the camera, wearing glasses, a maroon **UNH** (University of New Hampshire) hooded sweatshirt with green-and-white collegiate lettering, khaki pants, and a mission credential badge on a black lanyard.
- **Phoenix Lander Mockup & Rock Testbed**:
  - Behind Kurt sits a full-scale model of the **Phoenix Mars Lander**, featuring its metallic gold-and-silver octagonal body, three silver landing legs with circular footpads, a raised Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) mast, and its black articulated **Robotic Arm (RA)** extended forward and downward toward the floor.
  - On the floor beneath the robotic arm lies a large glossy color printout of the Martian surface generated from `Viz` terrain data, marked with concentric white radial distance arcs and grid lines.
  - Real rocks and pebbles of varying sizes are arranged across the floor printout to physically replicate the rock distribution observed by the lander on Mars.
  - White stanchion posts connected by white plastic chains enclose the display area.
- **Background**: Warm orange-painted museum/exhibit walls lined with tall blue-and-white educational display panels titled:
  - **`NEPTUNE`**
  - **`PLUTO`**
  - **`EXTREMOPHILE LIFE`**
  - **`THE SEARCH FOR WATER / THE SEARCH FOR LIFE`**
  - **`THE KUIPER BELT`**
