# Phoenix Mars Lander Viz Overhead Map of Diggable Area and Scoops (`main_viz-4-scoops-v4-sml.jpg`)

## Overview
An overhead annotated 3D visualization graphic created in **NASA Ames Viz** and Adobe Illustrator (released June 4, 2008, as NASA image `238784main_viz-4-scoops-v4.jpg`) showing the **Phoenix Mars Lander**, its Robotic Arm, and a false-color elevation map of the Martian soil workspace in front of the lander.

## Graphic & Annotation Details
- **Spacecraft Model (Lower Half)**:
  - A top-down 3D CAD rendering of the Phoenix lander against a dark charcoal-grey background.
  - The two decagonal dark-blue solar panels extend to the lower left and lower right.
  - The grey octagonal deck displays the lander's instruments (TEGA, MECA, SSI mast) and the silver Robotic Arm extending straight up into the terrain fan.
- **Color-Coded Terrain Fan (Upper Half)**:
  - A fan-shaped digital elevation model (DEM) generated from Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) stereo pairs extends across the upper portion of the image.
  - Elevation is false-colored across a rainbow spectrum: low depressions are shaded purple/magenta and dark blue, intermediate flat areas are green and yellow, and higher mounds or rocks are orange.
- **Labels & Overlays**:
  - **`Diggable Area`**: Outlined by a thick white dashed arc-shaped boundary enclosing the central portion of the terrain mosaic within reach of the Robotic Arm.
  - **`First Scoop`**: In white serif text on the left, with a white arrow pointing to a **magenta rectangle** outlining the initial test trench ("Baby Bear" / Dodo region) at the tip of the robotic arm.
  - **`Next Scoop`**: In white serif text at the upper left, with a white arrow pointing to an adjacent **yellow rectangle** just to the right of the magenta box, followed by a third **cyan/light-blue rectangle** to its right.
