# Phoenix Mars Lander Footpad and "Snow Queen" Mosaic for Aviation Week (`phx-aviation-week-cover.jpg`)

## Overview
A composite false-color image mosaic acquired by the **Phoenix Mars Lander's Robotic Arm Camera (RAC)** on May 31, 2008, looking underneath the lander at one of its three circular landing footpads and an adjacent exposed slab of hard subsurface water ice informally named **"Snow Queen"**. The image includes an inset of the **June 9, 2008 cover of *Aviation Week & Space Technology*** magazine where the mosaic was featured.

## Mosaic & Scene Details
- **Lander Leg & Footpad (Left)**:
  - Extending diagonally down from the upper left is one of Phoenix's metallic landing leg struts, wrapped in a coiled spring-like cable/harness, alongside a secondary support strut.
  - The struts meet at a circular grey footpad resting securely in the reddish-brown Martian regolith.
- **"Snow Queen" Ice Feature (Upper Right)**:
  - Just to the upper right of the footpad, where the lander's pulsed descent thrusters blew away the loose overlying topsoil during landing, a smooth, pale tan/buff-colored slab of hard subsurface ice ("Snow Queen") is exposed, surrounded by darker pebbly dirt and a small rectangular white target/plate near its lower right edge.
- **Inset Magazine Cover (Bottom Left)**:
  - A thumbnail reproduction of the June 9, 2008 issue of **`AVIATION WEEK & SPACE TECHNOLOGY`** featuring the headline **`PAY DIRT! Phoenix Hits Its Icy Target`** over the same footpad photograph. Next to the cover is yellow text reading `9 June 2008`.
