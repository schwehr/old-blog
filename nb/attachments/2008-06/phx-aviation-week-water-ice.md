# Aviation Week Article "Phoenix Lander Confirms Martian Water Ice" (`phx-aviation-week-water-ice.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a web article from **Aviation Week** (`aviationweek.com`) dated June 20, 2008, written by **Craig Covault** and titled **"Phoenix Lander Confirms Martian Water Ice"**, featuring a shadow-enhanced Surface Stereo Imager photograph (processed by Joel Hagen) of the "Dodo-Goldilocks" trench.

## Layout & Visible Text
- **Header Banner**: A blue horizontal banner at the top displaying the white **`AVIATION WEEK`** logo with its stylized winged-globe emblem.
- **Article Header**:
  - Headline: **`Phoenix Lander Confirms Martian Water Ice`**
  - Date: `Jun 20, 2008`
  - Byline: `By Craig Covault`
- **Article Photograph (Left)**:
  - A grayscale/monochrome close-up image of the rectangular trench excavated by Phoenix's robotic arm in the Martian soil. The interior shadows of the trench have been lightened to clearly reveal the bright white patch of water ice along the upper floor/wall of the trench and small white crumbs in the lower pit, with part of the lander structure visible at the bottom right corner.
- **Article Body Text (Right & Bottom)**:
  > The Phoenix Mars lander has confirmed that water ice critical to the mission's life habitat studies lies under only 4 centimeters of soil all around the spacecraft.
  >
  > The finding has been confirmed using the stereoscopic imager. The initial adjoining "DoDo" and "Goldilocks" trenches dug with the lander's robotic arm have a distinctive white band that lies just 2-4 centimeters (0.8-1.6 inches) under the soil. When the dig was completed, chunks of the white material also fell into the trench.
  >
  > Between June 17-20 the stereo imager has documented the site using the same angular settings. Out of this was created a short movie, in which as many as 8 features of the band and chunks of material from the band disappear in the latter frames of the movie.
