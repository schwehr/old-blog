# Phoenix Mission Press Release: "Bright Chunks At Phoenix Lander's Mars Site Must Have Been Ice" (`phx-ice-vanish.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a June 19, 2008 press release and accompanying photograph from the NASA/University of Arizona Phoenix Mars Mission website announcing that dice-sized crumbs of bright material at the bottom of the "Dodo-Goldilocks" trench vanished between Sol 20 and Sol 24, proving the material was water ice that sublimated after exposure.

## Right Column: Headline & Article Text
Set against a dark charcoal-brown background, the article text is formatted with a bold gold/amber headline and light gray body paragraphs:
- **Headline**:
  **`Bright Chunks At Phoenix Lander's Mars Site Must Have Been Ice`**
- **Paragraph 1**:
  **`June 19, 2008`** `-- Dice-size crumbs of bright material have vanished from inside a trench where they were photographed by NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander four days ago, convincing scientists that the material was frozen water that vaporized after digging exposed it.`
- **Paragraph 2**:
  `"It must be ice," said Phoenix Principal Investigator Peter Smith of the University of Arizona, Tucson. "These little clumps completely disappearing over the course of a few days, that is perfect evidence that it's ice. There had been some question whether the bright material was salt. Salt can't do that."`
- **Paragraph 3**:
  `The chunks were left at the bottom of a trench informally called "Dodo-Goldilocks" when Phoenix's Robotic Arm enlarged that trench on June 15, during the 20th Martian day, or sol, since landing. Several were gone when Phoenix looked at the trench early today, on Sol 24.`
