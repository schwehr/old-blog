# Phoenix MECA Optical Microscope 3D CAD Schematic (`phx-om-schematic.png`)

## Overview
A semi-transparent, color-coded 3D CAD rendering on a black background illustrating the internal layout of the **Optical Microscope (OM)** and sample wheel assembly inside the **Microscopy, Electrochemistry, and Conductivity Analyzer (MECA)** instrument on NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander.

## Visual & Mechanical Details
- **Outer Enclosure (Translucent Dark Blue)**: The rectangular box housing of the MECA enclosure is rendered in semi-transparent deep blue with glowing wireframe edges, mounting tabs along the bottom perimeter, fastener bolts across the top lid, and an upper-right sample delivery chute opening where the Robotic Arm sprinkles Martian soil.
- **Optical Microscope Tube Assembly (Red/Orange & Blue-Gray)**:
  - On the left side of the interior, a cylindrical **Optical Microscope** barrel rendered in translucent copper-red/orange extends horizontally toward the right.
  - The rear of the microscope is mounted in a square orange block supported by a blue-gray structural frame with diagonal struts bolted to a baseplate.
  - The front objective nozzle of the microscope points directly at a pink/magenta illumination and lens bezel block facing the sample wheel.
- **Sample Wheel & Translation Stage (Yellow & Cyan/Light Blue)**:
  - In the center-right, a tilted circular **sample wheel** rendered in bright translucent yellow shows a ring of small circular sample substrates (coupons/holders) around its bevelled rim. Soil particles dropped through the upper chute land on these substrates and are rotated in front of the microscope objective.
  - Supporting the yellow sample wheel is a tilted mechanical actuator and translation stage rendered in translucent cyan/light blue, featuring two circular motor/bearing hubs and a base bracket mounted to the floor of the enclosure.
