# Sam Kounaves on the TODAY Show / MSNBC Video Player (`phx-sam-today-show.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the MSNBC.com web video player (`TODAY on msnbc.com`) from June 27, 2008, showing a split-screen television interview between TODAY Show anchor Ann Curry in New York and Phoenix Wet Chemistry Lab lead scientist **Samuel Kounaves** (Tufts University) in Tucson, Arizona, discussing the discovery that Martian soil is alkaline and contains mineral nutrients capable of supporting life.

- **Top Header**: Above the video player against a dark reddish-brown gradient background is the white **`TODAY ON msnbc.com`** logo with the NBC peacock and **`msn Home`** butterfly logo in the top-right corner.
- **Split-Screen Interview**:
  - **Left Box (`NEW YORK`)**: Anchor **Ann Curry** in the New York studio against a blue `TODAY` backdrop, wearing a white top under a dark cardigan.
  - **Right Box (`TUCSON, AZ`)**: **Sam Kounaves** seated in the Phoenix Science Operations Center in Tucson (with the spacecraft testbed/mockup and Martian surface backdrop behind him), wearing a dark navy blue Phoenix Mars Mission polo shirt with a mission patch on the left chest.
- **Lower-Third Banner**: Centered below the two video boxes in bold white uppercase text on black and red bars:
  - **`PHOENIX MISSION`**
  - **`IS THERE LIFE ON MARS?`**

  `Related link: TODAYshow.com home page`
