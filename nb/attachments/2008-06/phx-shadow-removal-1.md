# Selecting Shadow/Highlight in Adobe Photoshop CS3 (`phx-shadow-removal-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Adobe Photoshop CS3 on Mac OS X demonstrating how to navigate the top menu bar (`Image -> Adjustments -> Shadow/Highlight...`) to brighten dark shadows cast across a Martian trench image taken by NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander.

## Interface & Menu Navigation
- **Top Menu Bar**: The Mac OS X application menu bar shows the Apple logo, **`Photoshop`**, `File`, `Edit`, **`Image`** (highlighted in blue), `Layer`, `Select`, `Filter`, `View`, `Window`, and `Help`.
- **Cascading Menus**:
  - Under **`Image`**, the dropdown menu lists `Mode`, **`Adjustments`** (highlighted in blue), `Duplicate...`, `Apply Image...`, `Calculations...`, `Image Size...`, `Canvas Size...`, `Pixel Aspect Ratio`, `Rotate Canvas`, `Crop`, `Trim...`, `Reveal All`, `Variables`, `Apply Data Set...`, and `Trap...`.
  - Under **`Adjustments`**, the submenu lists `Levels...`, `Auto Levels`, `Auto Contrast`, `Auto Color`, `Curves...`, `Color Balance...`, `Brightness/Contrast...`, `Black & White...`, `Hue/Saturation...`, `Desaturate`, `Match Color...`, `Replace Color...`, `Selective Color...`, `Channel Mixer...`, `Gradient Map...`, `Photo Filter...`, **`Shadow/Highlight...`** (selected in blue), `Exposure...`, `Invert`, `Equalize`, `Threshold...`, `Posterize...`, and `Variations...`.
- **Left Tool Palette**: The vertical single-column Photoshop CS3 toolbar (`Ps`) is visible along the left edge with the **Magic Wand Tool** active in the options bar (`Tolerance: 15`, `All Layers` checked).
- **Bottom Status Bar**: The document window footer shows zoom level **`66.67%`** and **`Doc: 1.00M/1.00M`**.

## Martian Trench Image Content
Behind the open menus is a grayscale Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) photograph of a trench dug into the Martian soil by Phoenix's Robotic Arm:
- The upper-left third of the frame shows brightly sunlit, pebbled Martian regolith and a bright rock on the far left.
- A sharp diagonal shadow cast by the spacecraft covers the lower-right two-thirds of the image, obscuring the excavated trench and the pile of rocky soil clods at the bottom of the trench, where a small white patch of ice or bright material glints near the lower right.
