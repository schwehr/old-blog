# Adjusting Shadows/Highlights Dialog on a Phoenix Trench Image (`phx-shadow-removal-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Adobe Photoshop CS3 showing the result of applying the **Shadows/Highlights** adjustment (`Shadows Amount: 88%`, `Highlights Amount: 0%`) to a grayscale Phoenix Mars Lander trench photograph, revealing fine details inside the previously dark shadowed trench.

## Dialog Box (`Shadows/Highlights`)
Positioned in the upper-left corner of the image is the Mac OS X **Shadows/Highlights** modal dialog window:
- **Shadows Group**:
  - `Amount:` text input box highlighted with **`88`** `%`, with the horizontal slider thumb dragged near the right end.
- **Highlights Group**:
  - `Amount:` text input box set to **`0`** `%`, with the horizontal slider thumb at the far left.
- **Checkboxes & Action Buttons**:
  - Unchecked `Show More Options` box at the bottom left.
  - On the right column: **`OK`** (default pulsing blue button), **`Cancel`**, **`Load...`**, **`Save...`**, and a checked **`Preview`** checkbox.

## Brightened Martian Trench Image
With the shadow reduction set to 88%, the diagonal spacecraft shadow across the lower-right two-thirds of the photograph is dramatically lightened:
- A subtly darker halo band marks the diagonal boundary between the sunlit upper-left soil and the shadow-corrected lower-right region.
- The elongated rectangular trench dug by the Robotic Arm is now clearly visible running from the upper-right mound of excavated soil down toward the lower-center foreground.
- Parallel scrape marks from the scoop blade, exposed bright patches along the upper trench floor, and a cluster of angular pebbles and clods piled at the bottom end of the trench are all clearly discernible.
