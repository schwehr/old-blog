# SpaceWeather.com Front Page Featuring Martian Ice (`phx-spaceweather.com.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **spaceweather.com** home page from **June 22, 2008**, displaying current solar wind and X-ray flare conditions on the left alongside a featured news story on the right titled **`MARTIAN ICE:`** with a close-up photograph of the sublimating water ice in the trench dug by NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander.
