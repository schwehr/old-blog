# Phoenix Mars Mission Gallery: "Soil Sample Poised at TEGA Door" (`phx-tega-scoop-material.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the University of Arizona / NASA **Phoenix Mars Mission** web gallery page displaying a grayscale Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) photograph taken on Sol 11 (June 5, 2008), in which Kurt Schwehr selectively brightened the interior of the Robotic Arm scoop in Photoshop to reveal the clumpy Martian soil sample poised above the Thermal and Evolved-Gas Analyzer (TEGA) oven door.

## Website Banner & Navigation
- **Top Banner**: Features the circular blue-and-orange Phoenix Mars Mission emblem on the left, the title **`PHOENIX MARS MISSION`** in gold and orange uppercase lettering across the Martian horizon, a 3D rendering of the Phoenix lander with deployed octagonal solar arrays on the right, and the NASA meatball logo on the far right.
- **Navigation Bar**: Dark glossy tabs for `MISSION`, `EDUCATION`, `BLOGS`, `NEWS`, and **`GALLERY`** (highlighted with a glowing gold bar), followed on the right by **`Keyword Search:`** with a white text input box (`Search`) and a `GO` link.

## Image & Caption Overlay (Left Pane)
- **Photograph**: A grayscale Surface Stereo Imager image looking down across the lander deck toward the Martian surface:
  - In the center, the metal **Robotic Arm scoop** hangs tilted downward over the foreground lander deck. Inside the shadowed mouth of the scoop, the selectively brightened soil sample is clearly visible, showing fine-grained dirt mixed with brighter, light-colored clods along the lower lip of the scoop.
  - Behind the scoop lies the pebble-strewn Martian regolith, with the edge of one of the lander's solar panels visible on the middle-left.
  - In the bottom foreground, the partially open door and slotted screen of TEGA's Oven 4 are visible beneath a semi-transparent dark caption overlay.
- **Caption Overlay Text**:
  - Paragraph 1: *"This image was taken by NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander's Surface Stereo Imager on Sol 11 (June 5, 2008), the eleventh day after landing. It shows the Robotic Arm scoop containing a soil sample poised over the partially open door of the Thermal and Evolved-Gas Analyzer's number four cell, or oven."*
  - Paragraph 2: *"Light-colored clods of material visible toward the scoop's lower edge may be part of the crusted surface material seen previously near the foot of the lander. The material inside the scoop has been slightly brightened in this image."*
