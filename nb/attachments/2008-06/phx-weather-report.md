# Canadian Space Agency Martian Weather Report Table (`phx-weather-report.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a tabular Martian weather report from the Canadian Space Agency (CSA) Meteorological Station (MET) aboard NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander, covering Sols 12 through 22 with daily sky conditions, minimum and maximum temperatures, atmospheric pressure, wind speed and direction, and atmospheric transparency/visibility ratings.

## Table Layout & Visual Icons
The table is arranged in 11 rows (Sols **12** through **22**) and 8 columns separated by light gray gridlines on a white background. The second column displays rounded square golden-yellow weather icons:
- **Sols 12–15**: A golden icon showing a stylized sun half-obscured behind wavy horizontal dust/haze lines (`Clear with dust haze`).
- **Sols 16–17 & 21–22**: A golden icon showing a full, unobstructed radiant sun (`Sunny`).
- **Sols 18–20**: Dash placeholders (`-`) across all columns indicating days when weather report data was unavailable.
