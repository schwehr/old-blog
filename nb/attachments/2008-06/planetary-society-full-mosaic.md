# The Planetary Society Weblog: Phoenix Mission Success Panorama (`planetary-society-full-mosaic.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a blog entry by **Emily Lakdawalla** on **The Planetary Society Weblog** dated June 24, 2008, titled *"A milestone for the Phoenix mission success panorama"*, showcasing an early 360-degree color mosaic ("Peter Pan") of the Martian arctic plains surrounding the Phoenix Mars Lander assembled by James Canvin.

- **Body Text**:
  *"I mentioned at the end of my earlier blog entry that on sol 28 Phoenix downlinked the last of the images necessary to make the first complete 360-degree color view around the lander. That's true, sort of. Here's the first attempt I've seen to assemble those images in color. It's quite a cool view."*
- **Embedded Panorama Image**:
  - Framed inside a light blue border is a wide, horizontal 360-degree cylindrical projection color mosaic showing the flat, polygonal-patterned rusty-brown Martian arctic plain stretching to a dusty tan-orange horizon under a dark sky band at the top.
  - The lower portion of the panorama contains blacked-out rectangular gaps (unimaged foreground areas of the lander deck), while the circular decagonal solar panels appear on the left and right sides and the Robotic Arm extends into the upper-left middle ground.
