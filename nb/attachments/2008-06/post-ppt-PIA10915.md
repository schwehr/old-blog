# Phoenix Wet Chemistry Lab Crucible Cutaway Diagram (`post-ppt-PIA10915.jpg`)

## Overview
A cutaway 3D CAD diagram (released as NASA/JPL Planetary Photojournal figure **PIA10915: Phoenix's Wet Chemistry Lab**) illustrating the row of tiny cylindrical reagent crucibles inside the actuator assembly at the top of a **Wet Chemistry Lab (WCL)** cell on NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander, annotated with magenta pointer lines and black labels. Kurt Schwehr featured this image in a blog post explaining the visual artifacts caused when scientists transport images via Microsoft PowerPoint.

## Diagram & Mechanical Components
- **Background & Framing**: The upper portion of the image is a rectangular cutaway CAD rendering set against a light sky-blue gradient background enclosed by a black border, with text labels placed in the white margin below.
- **Translucent Blue Housing & Helical Spring**:
  - A semi-transparent blue-violet block houses a vertical actuator mechanism on the left (containing a coiled silver helical spring and a rectangular wire bail) and a horizontal cylindrical bore extending to the right.
  - A dark red baseplate with a central circular golden-olive port and a yellow threaded lower collar sits at the bottom left, secured by silver socket-head cap screws and a small green spring beneath the right screw.
- **Row of Five Cylindrical Crucibles**:
  - Aligned horizontally inside the blue bore are five small gray cylindrical **crucibles** pushed from the right by a long threaded lead screw connected to a purple gear/plate at the far right end.
  - The leftmost two crucibles sit near the drop point where they are punctured/dispensed into the wet chemistry beaker below; the rightmost three crucibles have bright white circular tops.

## Labels & Pointer Lines
Three magenta leader lines extend downward from the crucibles in the CAD drawing to black sans-serif labels in the white lower margin:
1. **`Crucible With 2nd Calibrant`**: Points to the first (leftmost) gray cylinder positioned at the drop chute.
2. **`Acid Test`**: Points to the second gray cylinder from the left.
3. **`Sulfate Test`**: Three converging magenta lines point to the third, fourth, and fifth white-topped cylinders on the right (the barium chloride crucibles used for sequential sulfate titration tests).
- **PowerPoint Export Artifacts**: Tiny stray white pixel dots are faintly visible along the bottom edge of the yellow threaded collar and green spring area—remnants of the PowerPoint clipping/transparency bug discussed in Kurt Schwehr's blog post.
