# Residual White Pixel Artifacts at Base of Wet Chemistry Lab CAD Diagram (`post-ppt-missed-dots.png`)

## Overview
A tightly cropped, zoomed-in screenshot from the bottom portion of the NASA Phoenix Mars Lander Wet Chemistry Lab (WCL) CAD diagram (released as JPL Photojournal figure PIA10915), illustrating residual white pixel artifacts ("white dots") along the lower edges that remained after manual cleanup of a figure exported from Microsoft PowerPoint.

## Visual Details
- **Red Mounting Flange**: A dark red, three-dimensional CAD-rendered flange curves across the upper and middle portions of the frame, featuring a bluish-gray o-ring or groove along its upper surface and a light lavender/silver bolt or pin inserted on the right side.
- **Lower Protrusions**:
  - Beneath the left side of the red flange is a cylindrical gold/olive-yellow threaded or ribbed fitting.
  - Beneath the right side is a bright green double-ringed fitting next to the rounded silver bottom tip of the bolt.
- **Callout Line**: A vertical bright magenta/pink callout line cuts nearly straight down through the right-center of the image, passing between the gold cylinder and the green fitting.
- **White Dot Artifacts**: Against the pale ice-blue background, jagged stair-stepped white pixels cling to the anti-aliased bottom-left edge of the red flange, the left edge of the gold cylinder, and the left edge of the green fitting—artifacts caused by PowerPoint transparency/clipping export issues.
