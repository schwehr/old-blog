# White Pixel Artifacts in PowerPoint Export of Wet Chemistry Lab CAD Model (`ppt-white-dots.png`)

## Overview
A zoomed-in screenshot of a 3D CAD rendering of the Phoenix Mars Lander's Wet Chemistry Lab (WCL) beaker assembly, demonstrating severe white pixel ("white dot") artifacts scattered across the model edges and surfaces due to exporting or copying the graphic through Microsoft PowerPoint.

## Visual Details
- **CAD Assembly Elements**:
  - **Base**: A dark red lower mounting flange runs along the bottom left, with a dark gray/blue o-ring channel resting in a groove.
  - **Beaker / Housing**: A semi-transparent lavender-blue rectangular block forms the main body of the WCL cell in the center, with a vertical cylindrical post on the left topped by a white collar.
  - **Upper Internal Components**: At the top center, a silver cylindrical sensor or actuator protrudes downward into the translucent housing; to the right, a gray U-shaped bracket and a ribbed/coiled grey cylindrical element are visible, with a yellowish-green vertical component along the far right edge.
- **White Dot Artifacts**: Numerous isolated, single-pixel bright white dots are peppered along the vertical edges of the left cylindrical post, down the right boundary of the lavender housing against the olive-green side piece, along the bottom-left red flange border, and sparsely within the interior of the rendering.
