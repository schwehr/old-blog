# 3D Color-Coded Elevation Model of the Dodo-Goldilocks Trench in Viz (`sol019-viz-stp-trench.png`)

## Overview
A 3D terrain visualization rendered in NASA Ames's "Viz" software from a Stereo Pipeline (STP) digital elevation model acquired on Sol 19 of the Phoenix Mars Lander mission (released as JPL Photojournal image PIA10904), showing the excavated "Dodo-Goldilocks" trench false-colored by elevation.

## Scene & Elevation Details
- **Background**: The 3D surface mesh is floating at an oblique perspective angle against a solid dark gray background.
- **Color-Coded Elevation Ramp**: Elevation is mapped across a rainbow spectrum from highest (magenta/red at the top rear) through orange, yellow, green, cyan, and dark blue (lowest depth at the bottom of the excavated trench).
- **Dodo-Goldilocks Trench**:
  - A rectangular, scoop-excavated trench runs diagonally from the upper-left center toward the lower-right foreground.
  - The steep back wall and upper floor of the trench (the "Goldilocks" side) transition from yellow-orange at the rim to bright lime green on a flat, hard shelf where white ice material was exposed.
  - The deeper central and foreground section of the trench floor plunges into cyan and deep royal blue, with a mound of excavated soil Tailings piled up at the lower-right rim (colored green and yellow-brown) casting a dark shadow/occluded data gap (seen as dark gray horizontal cutouts on the right wall of the trench).
- **Surrounding Martian Surface**:
  - The undisturbed polygonal ground around the trench is textured with small pebbles and clods.
  - Several larger rocks sit to the left of the trench (rendered in yellow-orange and green) and along the upper red/magenta edge of the mesh.
