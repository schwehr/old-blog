# Annotated Sol 23 Location Map of the Snow White Trench and Wonderland Workspace (`sol23-location-figure-sml.png`)

## Overview
An annotated grayscale Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) context figure from Sol 23 of the NASA Phoenix Mars Lander mission, mapping the locations of key informal surface features—`Cheshire Cat`, `Wonderland`, `Snow White Trench`, and `Croquet Ground`—just off the edge of the lander's solar array, paired with a shadow-removed inset view of the newly dug Snow White Trench.

## Scene & Annotations
- **Background Context Image**:
  - A grayscale oblique view looking across the pebble-strewn Martian regolith, with the dark gridded panels of Phoenix's decagonal solar wing occupying the bottom-right corner and casting a sharp diagonal shadow onto the soil to its left.
- **Labeled Features & Outlines**:
  - **`Cheshire Cat` (Cyan)**: Large cyan text on the middle-left with a solid cyan leader line pointing to a large, wide cyan dotted ellipse encompassing a broad polygonal trough/depression area in the lower half of the scene.
  - **`Wonderland` (Green)**: Bright green text inside the left portion of the cyan ellipse, with a green leader line pointing to a smaller green dotted ellipse nested within the lower center of the `Cheshire Cat` region, straddling the solar panel shadow boundary.
  - **`Snow White Trench` (Yellow)**: Within the `Wonderland` ellipse, inside the dark shadow cast by the lander, a tilted rectangular yellow dotted box marks the footprint of the `Snow White Trench`. A solid yellow line connects this rectangle to the label `Snow White Trench` (in yellow text over the solar panel at bottom right), and another yellow line extends upward from the label to the bottom border of the inset image.
  - **`Croquet Ground` (Magenta)**: Just above the `Snow White Trench` rectangle, at the top edge of the `Wonderland` ellipse, a small magenta dotted triangle marks the dump pile (`Croquet Ground`). At the top center-left of the main image, large pink/magenta text reads `Croquet Ground` with a solid magenta leader line pointing directly to the mound of dumped soil at the top of the inset photograph.
- **Inset Photograph (Top Right)**:
  - Framed by a solid yellow border in the upper-right quadrant is a vertical close-up grayscale image of the `Snow White Trench` where the lander's shadow has been digitally lightened/removed (by Joel Hagen) to reveal interior detail.
  - The rectangular trench shows parallel vertical scrape marks along its floor, loose excavated soil clods scattered around the bottom and sides, and a conical pile of dumped dirt (`Croquet Ground`) at the top end of the trench.
