# CAD Model of Phoenix TEGA Instrument Highlighting Oven Cell 4 (`tega-cad-cell4-sml.png`)

## Overview
This color 3D CAD rendering illustrates the Thermal and Evolved-Gas Analyzer (TEGA) instrument aboard NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander, annotated to point out "Cell 4" (Oven 4). Featured in the June 11, 2008 blog post (*"TEGA oven 4 full of dirt!"*), the diagram shows the location of the first TEGA oven to be successfully filled with Martian soil from the "Baby Bear" trench.

## Diagram & Component Details
- **Annotation**: In the upper-left corner, large yellow sans-serif text reads **`Cell 4`** with a bold yellow arrow pointing down and to the right at the leftmost rectangular oven door on the front bank of four cells.
- **TEGA Oven Assembly (Left / Foreground)**:
  - Mounted on a bright red octagonal baseplate with perimeter bolt holes, the dual-sloped, tent-like oven housing is rendered in metallic silver, gray, and tan.
  - Four long, narrow rectangular oven doors/shutters are arranged side-by-side along the angled front-left face (with a matching bank of four doors on the opposite sloped face). Each cell features blue latch mechanisms at the bottom and a perforated cylindrical rod/hinge assembly running along the lower edge.
  - Motorized actuator cylinders in silver are attached at the lower-left and middle-right corners of the sloped housing, and a thin gray tubing/capillary line connects the oven assembly to the analyzer box on the right.
- **Mass Spectrometer / Electronics Enclosure (Right / Background)**:
  - Positioned to the upper right of the oven unit is a large, boxy enclosure with a dark navy-blue rounded top cover resting on a translucent amber/brown base frame supported by bright green corner brackets.
  - Along the lower-right side of the enclosure, four bright yellow rectangular electrical connectors (`D-sub` style) are mounted in pairs.
