# Silhoutted Palm Trees Against a Fiery Tucson Sunset (`tucson-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview
This wide color photograph captures a vibrant evening sunset in Tucson, Arizona, posted on June 1, 2008 (*"Sunset"*) while the author was working at the Phoenix Mars Lander Science Operations Center (SOC) at the University of Arizona.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Clouds**: Textured altocumulus and cirrus clouds fill the sky, illuminated from below by the setting sun in a brilliant palette of glowing golden-yellow, fiery orange, coral pink, and soft magenta-violet toward the right side of the frame.
- **Silhouetted Vegetation & Horizon**:
  - In the center of the frame, two tall fan palm trees stand side-by-side in stark black silhouette, their slender trunks rising above a dark cluster of bushy trees along the lower roofline.
  - To the lower right, a second pair of shorter palm trees is silhouetted against a paler pinkish-white patch of sky, with a single tiny distant palm crown visible near the far bottom-right corner.
  - On the left side, leafy tree branches frame the upper-left corner and a translucent tree crown glows golden-orange above the dark silhouette of low buildings along the bottom-left horizon.
