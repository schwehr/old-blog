# Fourth of July Nighttime Pool and Tucson City Lights (`4th-o-july.jpg`)

## Overview
A nighttime photograph taken on the Fourth of July 2008 from an elevated foothills residence in Tucson, Arizona, showing an illuminated green swimming pool in the foreground and the glowing city lights of Tucson across the valley in the distance.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**:
  - Along the bottom center and right, a rectangular swimming pool glows bright emerald/turquoise green from underwater lighting.
  - A dark wrought-iron fence with a row of decorative circular ring motifs runs along the near side of the pool, silhouetted against the glowing green water.
  - On the left, dense leafy tree branches and shrubs fill the left third of the frame, warmly lit in dim amber tones.
  - On the right, fan-shaped fronds of a palm tree are highlighted in warm orange/amber light against the dark night sky, with a dark potted plant silhouette near the pool's right edge.
- **Midground & Distance**:
  - Beyond a dark band of silhouetted foothills and trees, a dense horizontal ribbon of bright yellow, orange, and red-gold city lights stretches from left of center across the horizon under a pitch-black night sky.
