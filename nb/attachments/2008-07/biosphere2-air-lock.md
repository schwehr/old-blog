# Biosphere 2 Terai Airlock Chamber (`biosphere2-air-lock.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a heavy-duty yellow steel airlock chamber labeled **"TERAI AIRLOCK"** inside the Biosphere 2 facility in Oracle, Arizona.

## Airlock Structure & Details
- **Enclosure**: A freestanding rectangular yellow-painted steel chamber with lifting lugs on the top corners and a large rectangular bolted glass window on its left side panel.
- **Bulkhead Door**:
  - A rounded-corner submarine-style steel door is centered on the front face, embossed with three rows of vertical rectangular stiffening panels in its lower half.
  - A circular porthole window with a bolted metal rim is centered in the upper half of the door.
  - Three heavy latching mechanisms with red-painted semi-circular backing plates are mounted along the left edge of the door, including a long vertical red lever handle in the middle, while two heavy hinges are attached on the right edge.
- **Surrounding Environment**: A dark cylindrical metal trash can/drum wrapped in clear plastic liner stands in the lower-right foreground.

## Visible Text & Labels
Centered above the bulkhead door in bold red uppercase stencil lettering (across three lines):
- `TERAI AIRLOCK`
- `CAUTION` (partially behind a small round protruding fixture)
- `LOW HEAD CLEARANCE`
