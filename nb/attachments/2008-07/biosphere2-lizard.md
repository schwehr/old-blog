# Whiptail Lizard Outside Biosphere 2 (`biosphere2-lizard.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up horizontal photograph of a slender desert whiptail lizard (*Aspidoscelis* sp.) resting in profile on a sunlit tan concrete walkway outside the Biosphere 2 facility in Oracle, Arizona.

## Scene Description
- **Lizard**:
  - Oriented horizontally facing to the right, with its extraordinarily long, tapering tail extending all the way to the left edge of the frame (accounting for roughly two-thirds of its total length).
  - Its head is smooth and reddish-tan with a distinct dark eye and ear opening.
  - Its back and sides feature an intricate pattern of pale cream/yellowish vertical bars and spots over a dark brown and rust-orange background, with greenish-yellow mottling over the hind leg and hip.
  - Both right legs (foreleg and long-toed hind leg) and the toes of the left hind foot are visible resting on the ground, casting a sharp, dark shadow directly beneath the body and chin.
- **Background**: A uniform, slightly textured peach/tan concrete or stone surface in bright sunlight, framed by a thin gray border.
