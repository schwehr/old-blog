# Biosphere 2 Spaceframe Steel Support Structure (`biosphere2-structure.jpg`)

## Overview
An upward-angled close-up photograph of the white-painted tubular steel spaceframe truss structure that supports the glass enclosure of Biosphere 2 in Oracle, Arizona.

## Structural & Scene Details
- **Spaceframe Geometry**:
  - Taken from the base of a V-shaped structural valley where two angled roof/wall planes of the spaceframe meet along a central line of nodal hubs receding into the upper-center distance.
  - Heavy white cylindrical steel struts radiate upward and outward to the left and right from bolted steel node plates in the bottom foreground, forming a repeating three-dimensional grid of triangles and pyramids.
  - A single darker gray conduit or pipe runs diagonally alongside one of the right-hand struts.
- **Background**:
  - Through the glass panels on the right side of the truss valley, the artificial rock cliffs, green coastal/savanna vegetation, and water tank edge of the Biosphere 2 interior biome are visible.
