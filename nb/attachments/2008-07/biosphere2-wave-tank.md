# Biosphere 2 Ocean Biome and Wave Tank (`biosphere2-wave-tank.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide interior photograph of the main ocean and coastal biome inside Biosphere 2 in Oracle, Arizona, showing the large enclosed water tank, artificial rock cliffs, tropical/savanna vegetation, and the soaring glass-and-steel spaceframe roof overhead.

## Scene Description
- **Glass & Spaceframe Enclosure**:
  - The left wall and vaulted ceiling consist of a vast geometric lattice of white steel spaceframe trusses glazed with triangular glass panels, letting in bright Arizona daylight.
- **Ocean / Wave Tank**:
  - In the lower center and right, dark green-tinted water fills a rectangular basin bordered along its far end by a low stone-faced wall (housing the wave-generation machinery) with white PVC pipes along the water line, and along its right side by a concrete ledge with small pumps, ladders, and instrumentation.
- **Cliffs & Vegetation**:
  - Rising steeply on the right side above the water basin is a sculpted artificial rock cliff face with horizontal strata, topped by green trees, shrubs, and desert/savanna plants along the upper ridge.
  - Leafy green shrubs and palm fronds frame the lower-left foreground, while dense green bushes grow atop the far wall of the wave tank (with a small white cylindrical blower or sensor housing visible among the foliage).
