# Television Camera Crane Visiting the Phoenix Mars Lander Operations Center (`camera-crane-visiting-phx.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken at the Phoenix Mars Lander Science Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona on July 6, 2008, showing a visiting television production crew operating a large wheeled camera crane/jib in front of the illuminated Mars lander testbed and backdrop.

## Scene Description
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**: The entire large room is bathed in warm amber and orange-red studio lighting, with bright golden light reflecting off the polished floor in the center foreground and two intense white floodlights shining over the Mars testbed in the background.
- **Camera Crane & Crew**:
  - In the center, a long black horizontal camera jib/boom arm is mounted on a heavy four-wheeled dolly tripod base. The boom extends toward the right over a railing toward the Mars testbed, with heavy counterweights and a small glowing monitor screen at the left (operator) end.
  - Five silhouetted crew members and observers surround the rig: two people standing near the left/rear counterweight end operating the crane (with another person seated to their left and one bending near the tripod base), and two people seated on chairs to the right of the crane base watching the stage.
  - An equipment cart on caster wheels sits in the dark left foreground.
- **Background Stage (Mars Testbed)**:
  - Behind a metal crowd-control barrier fence is a simulated Martian surface deck with an engineering model of the Phoenix Mars Lander illuminated by bright white studio lights against a wide panoramic Mars horizon backdrop banner.
