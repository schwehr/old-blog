# ERMA Web GIS Map Tracking R/V Auk and Nancy Foster Whale Tagging (`erma-whale-tagging.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **ERMA (Environmental Response Management Application)** web GIS interface developed at the University of New Hampshire, displaying AIS vessel positions and tracks in Massachusetts Bay / Stellwagen Bank during a July 1, 2008 whale-tagging cruise involving the research vessels *R/V Auk* and *Nancy Foster*.

## Header & Navigation Bar
- **Top Banner**: Dark blue-green ocean wave photographic banner reading **`ERMA`** in large white text followed by **`Portsmouth, NH`** on the left, and **`Admin`** on the far right.
- **Menu Bar**: A slate-gray toolbar below the banner with links on the left (`Map`, `Print`, `Info`, `Help`), a collapse arrow `>` in the center, and right-hand tabs (`Legend`, `Layer`, `Data` [highlighted/active], `Tools`).

## Map Canvas (Left Panel)
- **Geographic Area**: Massachusetts Bay stretching from **Cape Ann** (`Gloucester`) and `Marblehead` in the upper left down to the South Shore (`Scituate`, `Norwell`) in the lower left.
- **Map Controls**:
  - Upper left: Blue circular directional pan arrows (`N`, `S`, `E`, `W`) above a vertical zoom slider bar (`+` to `-`), and two tool icons at lower left (a white hand pan icon and a blue magnifying glass zoom-box icon).
  - Upper right of map canvas: A blue `+` layer/overview button.
  - Lower right of map canvas: `Scale = 1 : 433K`, `Zoom = 10/19`, and coordinates `-70.43483, 42.53704`.
- **Tracked Vessels (Orange Triangles & Labels)**:
  - `WESTERN VENTURE` (pointed northeast near Gloucester)
  - `NORTHERN CANYON` (pointed south in northern Massachusetts Bay)
  - `MEAND...` (near the eastern edge of the map canvas)
  - `R/V AUK` (near the center of the bay, marked with a black `X` selection symbol and short red track line)
  - `NANCY FOSTER` (just southeast of *R/V Auk*, pointed south with a red track line)
  - `GATEWAY ENDEAVOR` (southwest of *R/V Auk*, heading northwest with a red track segment)
  - Overlapping labels on the middle-left edge (`JASMINE EXPR` / `CLARIS...`) and `GALE WINDS` in the lower left.
