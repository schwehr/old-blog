# Wooden Boat Docks and Seagulls on Lake Winnipesaukee (`lake-winnipesaukee.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic daytime photograph of Lake Winnipesaukee in New Hampshire, showing a series of wooden boat dock piers with perched seagulls in the left foreground and a floating gazebo/bandstand against tree-covered hills across the water.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Docks & Birds (Left)**:
  - Three parallel wooden finger piers supported by vertical timber pilings extend from the left edge into the rippled blue-gray lake water.
  - On the nearest dock, two white-and-gray gulls rest atop the twin wooden pilings at the outer end—one standing upright in profile facing right at the top of the right piling, and another crouched on the left piling.
  - Another gull is perched atop a piling on the second dock further back.
- **Lake & Background Shoreline (Right)**:
  - Gently rippled slate-blue water stretches across the middle of the frame.
  - In the right-center mid-distance, a hexagonal or octagonal covered pavilion/bandstand floats on the water, topped by a pitched roof and a small American flag.
  - Beyond the lake rises a continuous rolling shoreline ridge densely covered in dark green summer forest, with several lakeside houses, boathouses, and a white motorboat visible along the far right shore under a pale overcast sky.
