# Google Doodle for NASA's 50th Anniversary (`nasa50th.gif`)

## Overview
The commemorative Google Doodle logo graphic celebrating NASA's 50th anniversary (July 29, 2008), integrating iconic spacecraft, rockets, and celestial bodies into the letters of the word **"Google"** against a dark starry space background.

## Graphic & Letter Details
Set inside a dark rectangular space backdrop with soft airbrushed edges and colorful twinkling stars:
- **`G`**: Rendered in blue serif lettering on the left, with a glowing spiral galaxy angled behind its upper curve.
- **First `o` (Mars)**: Replaced by the orange-red globe of **Mars**, with the **Viking lander** perched on its upper surface and a six-wheeled **Mars Exploration Rover** (Spirit/Opportunity) driving along its lower-right rim.
- **Space Probe**: Above the gap between Mars and the Moon floats the **Pioneer / Voyager** deep-space probe with its white high-gain dish antenna and boom.
- **Second `o` (The Moon)**: Replaced by the gray cratered full **Moon**, with the gold-and-silver **Apollo Lunar Module** resting on its upper-right limb.
- **`g`**: Rendered in blue serif lettering.
- **`l` (Saturn V Rocket)**: Replaced by a tall, black-and-white multi-stage **Saturn V** launch vehicle angled slightly to the left, blasting bright orange and yellow exhaust plumes from its base.
- **`e`**: Rendered in red serif lettering on the far right.
