# NASA Images (nasaimages.org) Homepage Screenshot (`nasaimages-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the homepage of **NASA Images** (`http://nasaimages.org`), a digital archive collaboration between NASA and the Internet Archive launched in July 2008.

## Header & Branding
- **Logo (Upper Left)**: The classic blue circular NASA "meatball" insignia paired with bold white text reading **`NASA Images`**, and a subtitle below reading:
  - `a service of the`
  - `Internet Archive`
- **Search Bar (Upper Right)**: A white rectangular text input field next to a rounded gray **`Search`** button, with the label **`Search by Keyword`** directly underneath.
- **Background**: A large curved limb of Earth seen from space sweeps diagonally across the upper-left background against a black page background.

## Main Category Thumbnails
Four square photographic category tiles are arranged horizontally across the middle of the page, each with a white label underneath:
1. **`Universe`**: A blue and violet star-forming nebula with bright central stars.
2. **`Solar System`**: A composite illustration of Saturn with its rings alongside a large cratered moon (Dione/Tethys) and smaller moons against black space.
3. **`Earth`**: A full-disk view of Earth showing North/South America, the Atlantic Ocean, and Africa, framed by a light blue border with a stack of receding blue folder/card tabs above it indicating an active hover/stack animation.
4. **`Astronauts`**: An astronaut in a white extravehicular mobility unit (EMU) spacesuit floating untethered in space above Earth's blue horizon.

## Historical Mission Timeline & Footer
- **Timeline Banner**: Below the category tiles is a horizontal stepped collage of panoramic mission banners labeled in white text:
  - **`Pioneer`** (bottom row, stretching across the foreground with spacecraft imagery and the year **`1992`** printed below)
  - **`Galileo`** (second row right)
  - **`Shuttle`** (third row center-right)
  - **`Voyager`** (fourth row center)
  - **`Hubble`** (top row right)
- **Footer Links**: Along the bottom edge are four light-blue links:
  - `About NASA Images.org`
  - `Powered by Luna Imaging`
  - `Terms and Conditions of Use`
  - `Visit NASA.gov`
