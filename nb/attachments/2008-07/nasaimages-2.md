# NASA Images Search Results Interface (`nasaimages-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a web search results page on the **NASA Images** website (`nasaimages.org`), a collaboration between NASA and the Internet Archive, posted on July 31, 2008. The interface displays faceted search filtering and a result for a Mars rover photograph.

## Interface & Layout
- **Header Banner**:
  - Top left shows the blue circular NASA "meatball" logo alongside white text: **"NASA Images"** and smaller subtitle **"a service of the Internet Archive"**.
  - Background of the header features a curved view of Earth from space.
  - Top center-right navigation links: **"Welcome | Login | Register"**.
  - Top right search bar labeled **"Search NASA Images"** containing the query text `Sun OR Mercury OR Venus O`, with a gray **"Search"** button and an **"Advanced Search"** link underneath.
  - Horizontal metallic-blue navigation bar below the banner with links: **Home**, **Explore...**, **Create...**, **Share This**, **Embed This**, and **Help**.
- **Left Sidebar (`Narrow your search:`)**:
  - **Remove** section: Shows an active filter bullet `- Mars`.
  - **What** section: Includes a `Show More` dropdown button and bulleted facet counts:
    - `Sun (7747)`
    - `Crater (3127)`
    - `Mars Global ... (2936)`
    - `2001 Mars Od ... (2841)`
  - **Where** section: Includes a `Show More` dropdown button and bulleted facet counts:
    - `Jet Propulsi ... (8742)`
    - `Jet Propulsi ... (4160)`
    - `California (3960)`
    - `Arizona (3849)`
    - `Washington (3570)`
- **Main Results Area**:
  - **Query Heading**: Bold white text on a black background reads: **"Search Results: All Fields SimilarTo 'Sun or Mercury or Venus or Mars or Jupiter or Saturn or Uranus or Neptune or Pluto' and What Equals 'Mars'"**.
  - **Toolbar**: A **"Printer Friendly"** link with an icon on the right, above a pagination and view control bar showing pages `1 2 3 [4] 5 6 7 ... 223 224` (with page 4 highlighted), item range **`151-200 of 11,186`**, three grid density icons (2x2 selected, 3x3, 4x4), and three thumbnail size squares (medium selected).
  - **Search Result Item**: Displays a square grayscale fisheye photograph from a Mars Exploration Rover looking down at the Martian surface, showing the deflated airbags and lander petal with rover tracks in the soil, captioned **"Six Wheels on Mars"** below the image.
