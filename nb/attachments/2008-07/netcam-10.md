# StarDot NetCam SC Sample View of Wooded Lawn (`netcam-10.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle sample photograph captured by a 5-megapixel **StarDot NetCam SC** network camera looking out an office window at the University of New Hampshire (UNH) on July 29, 2008, demonstrating the camera's wide-angle field of view and slight softness through the uncleaned glass.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Lawn**: A grassy clearing slopes gently upward toward the surrounding woods. Bright patches of dappled midday sunlight illuminate the center of the grass, surrounded by cool tree shadows.
- **Left Tree**: A tall, straight deciduous tree trunk stands prominently on the far left edge, with a bright blue ribbon or string tied around its lower trunk near the bottom left corner. Noticeable barrel (spherical) distortion from the wide-angle lens curves the vertical trunk slightly inward near the top.
- **Forest Background**: Dense summer woods of mixed deciduous and coniferous trees fill the background and upper canopy, with bright white sky peeking through the leaves along the top edge.
- **Timestamp Banner**: A tiny, low-resolution dark overlay bar in the top-left corner contains faint white status text from the StarDot NetCam SC system.
