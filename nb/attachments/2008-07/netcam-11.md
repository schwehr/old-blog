# StarDot NetCam SC Upper Canopy Sample View (`netcam-11.jpg`)

## Overview
A second wide-angle sample photograph captured by a **StarDot NetCam SC** network camera from an office window on July 29, 2008, tilted higher than `netcam-10.jpg` to frame the full height of the surrounding forest canopy.

## Scene Description
- **Forest & Canopy**: Tall deciduous trees fill almost the entire frame from the shaded forest floor at the bottom edge up to the bright sunlit treetops at the top.
- **Left & Center Trees**: The same prominent tree trunk Seen in `netcam-10.jpg` rises along the left margin, exhibiting noticeable wide-angle barrel curvature, while a second slender trunk angles upward just left of center and a cluster of multi-stemmed trees stands on the right.
- **Window Frame & Overlay**:
  - In the upper-right corner, a small diagonal section of the light-colored interior window frame or soffit is visible.
  - In the top-left corner, a tiny dark status bar displays faint white camera telemetry text.
  - Only the very back edge of the leaf-covered forest floor is visible along the bottom margin, dappled with small patches of sunlight.
