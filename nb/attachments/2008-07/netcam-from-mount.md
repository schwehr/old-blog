# Mounted StarDot NetCam SC View with Window Reflections (`netcam-from-mount.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle image captured by the newly mounted **StarDot NetCam SC** network camera at 15:39:10 on Thursday, June 26, 2008 (per the camera clock, posted July 29, 2008), looking down onto a grassy clearing and wooded edge through an office window.

## Scene Description
- **Lawn & Woods**: The camera is angled downward more steeply than in earlier unmounted tests, showing a broad expanse of shaded green grass occupying the lower half of the frame, bordered by a leaf-strewn bank and dense summer forest across the upper half.
- **Left Tree**: The large tree trunk on the left edge has a blue ribbon tied around its base; its bark is brightly lit by afternoon sunlight coming from the left.
- **Window Reflections & Artifacts**: Because the camera is mounted inside an uncleaned office window without a dark non-reflective backing, faint ghost reflections of interior office objects (including a pale vertical rectangular reflection left of center and small circular dust spots/glare specks across the middle right) are superimposed over the trees and grass.
