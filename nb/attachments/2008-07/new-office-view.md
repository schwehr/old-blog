# Lush Green Forest View from New Office Window (`new-office-view.jpg`)

## Overview
A horizontal photograph taken looking out Kurt Schwehr's new office window on July 25, 2008, showing a vibrant green summer forest edge and a sunlit grassy clearing.

## Scene Description
- **Trees & Foliage**: Rich, saturated green foliage fills the upper three-quarters of the frame.
  - On the far left, a thick rough-barked tree trunk (with a secondary trunk immediately behind it) stands in the foreground.
  - Near the center-left, a tall, slightly leaning deciduous tree trunk rises from the edge of the clearing into the canopy.
  - Behind and to the right of the main trunks, dense understory saplings, ferns, and leafy branches create a deep green backdrop.
- **Clearing & Ground**: Along the bottom edge, bright summer sunlight filters onto a grassy lawn in the foreground and a brown leaf-covered slope at the base of the trees on the right.
