# Kurt Posing Beside Blooming Night-Blooming Cereus Flowers (`night-flowers-kurt.jpg`)

## Overview
A flash-lit nighttime photograph taken at **Tohono Chul Park** in Tucson, Arizona on July 2, 2008, showing Kurt Schwehr crouching on the desert ground next to a cluster of blooming **Queen of the Night** (*Peniocereus greggii*, night-blooming cereus) cactus flowers.

## Scene Description
- **Person**: On the right side of the frame, Kurt Schwehr is crouching low to the ground, facing the camera. He is wearing wire-rimmed glasses, a charcoal-gray short-sleeved t-shirt with a small colorful logo on the left chest, and green cargo shorts, resting his left forearm across his knee.
- **Night-Blooming Cereus Flowers**: To the left of Kurt in the foreground, thin, woody, stick-like stems of a night-blooming cereus cactus hold eight large, bright white, multi-petaled blossoms open in the night air, each showing a pale yellow-cream center:
  - One blossom at the top of a tall vertical stem near the center.
  - A cluster of four blossoms in the middle (two overlapping on the left, two to the right).
  - One blossom low near the bottom center.
  - Two blossoms side-by-side near the lower left.
- **Desert Background**: Behind the cereus stems on the far left is a green prickly pear (*Opuntia*) cactus pad cluster, and behind Kurt and the flowers is dark desert soil and the shadowed base of a desert tree or shrub against the pitch-black night background.
