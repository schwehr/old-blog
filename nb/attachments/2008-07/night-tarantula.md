# Desert Tarantula by Prickly Pear Cactus at Night (`night-tarantula.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up, flash-illuminated nighttime photograph taken at **Tohono Chul Park** in Tucson, Arizona on July 2, 2008, showing a desert tarantula (*Aphonopelma chalcodes*, Arizona blond / desert tarantula) walking across sandy desert soil at the base of a prickly pear cactus.

## Scene Description
- **Tarantula**: Positioned in the lower-center foreground, facing away and slightly up toward the cactus base:
  - Its eight thick, hairy legs are dark charcoal-black.
  - Its cephalothorax (carapace) is covered in pale tan/golden-brown fuzz.
  - Its rounded abdomen in the foreground is covered in dark brown hair with bright reddish-orange urticating hairs across the rear.
- **Cactus & Ground**:
  - Rising diagonally across the left side of the image is a pale green prickly pear (*Opuntia*) cactus stem armed with long, sharp white spines and clusters of golden-brown glochids at the areoles.
  - Along the bottom edge lies a weathered, pale gray piece of dead wood or cactus rib.
  - The surrounding ground consists of coarse tan desert sand, pebbles, dried twigs, and curled brown plant debris on the upper right.
