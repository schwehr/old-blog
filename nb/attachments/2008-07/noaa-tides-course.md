# NOAA Tide Measurement Station Schematic Diagram (`noaa-tides-course.jpg`)

## Overview
An educational schematic diagram from the **NOAA National Ocean Service** online course (*Tides and Water Levels*, posted July 16, 2008), illustrating the components of a modern acoustic and backup pressure tide gauge station mounted on a pier.

## Diagram Details & Components
- **Structure & Environment**:
  - A yellow-gold **Tide House** with a peaked roof sits atop a wooden pier deck supported by four vertical pilings extending down into blue water and a sloping tan/gold seabed on the left.
  - A solid horizontal blue water fill represents the **Mean Water Level**, while a dashed black sinusoidal curve passing through the pilings and protective well indicates **Waves**.
- **Tide House Equipment**:
  - **GOES Antenna**: A small white satellite dish mounted on the right side of the Tide House roof.
  - **Primary Data Collection Platform**: A rectangular unit labeled `DATA LOGGER` mounted on the right interior wall of the Tide House, connected upwards to the GOES antenna and downwards to the acoustic sensor.
  - **Backup Data Collection Platform**: A white rectangular box mounted on the left interior wall of the Tide House, connected via a line running down the second piling to a submerged pressure sensor.
- **Water Level Sensors**:
  - **Acoustic Sensor & Well (Right)**: Mounted on the rightmost piling is a vertical white cylinder (**Protective Well**) extending from above the pier deck down into the water, with flared horizontal intake plates at its submerged base. Inside the well is a narrower vertical tube (**Calibration/Sounding Tube**) topped by an **Acoustic Sensor** dome above the deck, measuring the **Instantaneous Water Level Inside Well** (shown as a horizontal line inside the tube).
  - **Pressure Sensor (Left-Center)**: Attached to the second piling from the left is a vertical graduated staff containing a small submerged **Pressure Sensor** near the bottom.

## Visible Text & Labels
- Top center-left: `TIDE HOUSE`
- Top right: `GOES ANTENNA`
- Upper right: `PRIMARY DATA COLLECTION PLATFORM` (pointing to `DATA LOGGER`)
- Upper left: `BACKUP DATA COLLECTION PLATFORM`
- Middle right: `ACOUSTIC SENSOR`
- Center right: `PROTECTIVE WELL` and `CALIBRATION/SOUNDING TUBE`
- Lower right: `INSTANTANEOUS WATER LEVEL INSIDE WELL`
- Lower left: `WAVES` and `MEAN WATER LEVEL`
- Bottom center-left: `PRESSURE SENSOR`
