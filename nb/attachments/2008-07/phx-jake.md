# Audience and Operators Viewing the Phoenix Mars Lander 5-Projector Display (`phx-jake.jpg`)

## Overview
A composite photograph from the **Phoenix Mars Lander Science Operations Center (SOC)** in Tucson, Arizona (posted July 25, 2008), showing team members wearing 3D anaglyph glasses while viewing and controlling the large 5-projector curved stereo display wall, with a picture-in-picture inset of the screen itself in the upper-left corner.

## Scene Description
- **Main Photograph**:
  - **Right Foreground (Jake)**: A young man (Jake) sits on the right wearing glasses, a dark flat cap, a light blue collared button-down shirt, and a red lanyard, looking intently toward the projection screen to the left.
  - **Center Foreground**: An older man with white hair and white-framed 3D glasses sits beside Jake in a light plaid short-sleeved shirt and khaki pants, balancing an open silver Apple MacBook Pro laptop (with a glowing white Apple logo) on his lap while extending his right arm forward to point at a feature on the screen.
  - **Middle Ground & Background**: To their left, a woman in a dark jacket with red trim adjusts her white paper 3D glasses, and another bearded man in 3D glasses is partially visible behind Jake. Further back in the darkened room are rows of empty gray metal folding chairs, two standing men conversing near the back wall, and a cameraman standing on the far left operating a video camera on a tripod.
  - **Ceiling**: In the upper-right corner, one of the ceiling-mounted digital projectors is visible below a red-painted structural beam.
- **Upper-Left Inset**:
  - A small rectangular inset photo bordered in gray shows the wide, curved 5-projector screen glowing in the darkened auditorium, displaying a bright panoramic view of the Martian surface and part of the lander while four silhouetted viewers watch from the front row.
