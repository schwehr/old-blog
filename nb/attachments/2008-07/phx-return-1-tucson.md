# Aerial View of Cumulus Clouds and Mountains Over Tucson from Southwest Flight (`phx-return-1-tucson.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken through a passenger window aboard a **Southwest Airlines** flight departing Tucson, Arizona (posted July 25, 2008), showing towering white cumulus clouds building over desert mountains and suburban valleys below.

## Scene Description
- **Aircraft Wing & Winglet**:
  - Extending from the upper-left edge is the silver-white wing tip and upturned blended winglet of a Boeing 737 painted in Southwest Airlines' canyon blue, red, orange, and gold livery, with yellow text reading **`SOUTHWEST.COM`** along the blue leading edge.
  - Below the wing on the mid-left edge, a bright orange-red flap track fairing tip protrudes into the frame.
- **Clouds & Sky**:
  - A tall, billowing white cumulus cloud tower rises diagonally across the center and right side of the frame, illuminated by bright sunlight with soft gray shaded undersides.
  - Higher layer clouds and patches of deep blue sky are visible at the top.
- **Terrain Below**:
  - Beneath the clouds lies rugged, arid brown mountain terrain (the Santa Catalina / Tucson area foothills) with residential subdivisions, roads, and a golf course or racetrack oval visible in the lower-left corner.
