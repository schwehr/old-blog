# Aerial View of Lake Mead Shoreline and Islands in Nevada (`phx-return-2-nevada.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken from an airplane window approaching Las Vegas, Nevada on a flight from Tucson (posted July 25, 2008), looking down at the intricate desert shoreline, peninsulas, and turquoise-green waters of **Lake Mead**.

## Scene Description
- **Water & Shoreline**:
  - Deep emerald and turquoise-green reservoir water fills the lower and right portions of the frame.
  - Extending from the upper left toward the center is an arid, pale tan and pinkish-brown desert landmass with deeply indented coves, dendritic dry washes, and a bright white "bathtub ring" mineral deposit outlining the low-water shoreline.
  - A large, jagged island/peninsula sits near the center with darker gray-brown hills in its interior, trailed by a chain of tiny sandy islets and submerged green shoals stretching toward the bottom center.
- **Boats & Wakes**:
  - Several tiny white specks with thin white V-shaped wakes are scattered across the open green water on the right side of the image.
- **Aircraft Feature**:
  - In the top-left corner, the bright red-orange tip of the airplane's wing flap fairing projects into the frame.
