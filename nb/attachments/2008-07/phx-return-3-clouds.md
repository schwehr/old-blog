# Sun Flare and Cloud Deck Past Airplane Wingtip at Sunset (`phx-return-3-clouds.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken from a passenger window during Kurt Schwehr's return flight from Tucson on July 25, 2008, looking out past the silhouetted aircraft wing and winglet into a blazing low sun over a sea of clouds.

## Scene Description
- **Sun & Lens Flare**:
  - On the far left edge, the low sun creates an intense, blinding white orb surrounded by warm golden-orange glare.
  - Several long, horizontal and slightly angled light streaks and internal window reflections shoot across the sky from the sun toward the right edge, passing across the winglet.
- **Cloud Deck & Sky**:
  - Below the horizon line, a continuous deck of billowy stratocumulus and cumulus cloud tops stretches into the distance, bathed in warm golden and amber light along their upper edges with dusky gray-violet shadows beneath.
  - The sky above transitions from pale golden-white near the sun to deep twilight blue at the top right.
- **Aircraft Wing**:
  - On the right side of the frame, the outer wing, upturned blended winglet, and lower flap fairing are seen in dark silhouette against the bright sky and cloud tops.
