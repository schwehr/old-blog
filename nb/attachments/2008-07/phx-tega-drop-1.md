# 3D Animation Frame of Phoenix Robotic Arm Positioned Over TEGA (`phx-tega-drop-1.png`)

## Overview
A rendered 3D computer animation frame produced in LightWave 3D by the **Phoenix Mars Lander** Science and Sequence Visualization (SSV) team (posted July 31, 2008), depicting the Phoenix lander deck on the Martian surface with its Robotic Arm (RA) poised to deliver an icy soil sample to the **TEGA** (Thermal and Evolved Gas Analyzer) instrument.

## Scene Description
- **Robotic Arm & Scoop**:
  - Two long metallic-gray segments of the Robotic Arm form an inverted V above the deck: the upper boom rises out of the top-left of the frame from its shoulder mount on the lower-left deck, while the forearm angles downward from top-center toward the right, holding the scoop over the TEGA instrument.
- **Lander Deck & Instruments**:
  - **Surface Stereo Imager (SSI)**: On the far left, the cylindrical SSI camera head sits atop a tall black open-lattice mast rising from a cylindrical mast canister.
  - **TEGA (Center)**: In the middle of the silver-gray deck sits the angled, wedge-shaped TEGA instrument housing with slotted doors on its slanted front face, flanked by a square gray box in front and a black rectangular housing behind.
  - **MECA & Deck Features**: Four black square-framed openings run diagonally along the left-center of the deck alongside metallic cable raceways and brown wiring harnesses. On the right edge of the deck are a small American flag decal, a black circular calibration target / sundial area, and a round color calibration target.
  - **Solar Array**: Extending to the right background is one of Phoenix's decagonal (10-sided) fan-out solar panels covered in dark blue-gray photovoltaic cells.
- **Martian Terrain**: Surrounding the lander is a realistic brown, polygonal-patterned Martian arctic plain dotted with small dark pebbles and shallow troughs.
