# Cutaway 3D Render Blending Phoenix Deck with Internal TEGA Cell (`phx-tega-drop-2.png`)

## Overview
A close-up 3D animation frame created by the **Phoenix Mars Lander** SSV team (posted July 31, 2008), blending the exterior model of the Phoenix lander deck and Robotic Arm scoop with a semi-transparent cutaway view revealing the newly modeled internal **TEGA** (Thermal and Evolved Gas Analyzer) cell assembly.

## Scene Description
- **Semi-Transparent TEGA Housing & Internal Cell**:
  - In the center of the frame, the wedge-shaped outer housing of the TEGA Hopper/Doors assembly is rendered in semi-transparent green, allowing a clear view inside the rightmost bay.
  - Inside the bay sits a detailed, glowing cyan-and-green wireframe/translucent CAD model of a single TEGA oven cell and funnel assembly, tilted at the same angle as the outer housing.
- **Robotic Arm Scoop**:
  - Directly above the highlighted TEGA cell, the metallic scoop at the end of the Robotic Arm is positioned to drop Martian soil onto the open screen of the cell.
- **Surrounding Deck Hardware**:
  - To the left, four black square frames with mounting holes are arranged in a diagonal row beside a raised metallic cable trough and a gray shroud at the top left.
  - In the lower-left foreground is a boxy gray electronics/instrument enclosure with orange-brown wiring harnesses routed around its base.
  - To the right of TEGA, a small rectangular U.S. flag decal is mounted on the white deck next to a recessed gold-lined cutout and the edge of the solar panel in the upper-right corner.
