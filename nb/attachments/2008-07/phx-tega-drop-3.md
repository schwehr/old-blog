# Isolated X-Ray Style 3D Model of a Single Phoenix TEGA Cell (`phx-tega-drop-3.png`)

## Overview
A close-up, isolated 3D computer graphics rendering on a solid black background (posted July 31, 2008), showing the internal mechanical structure of a single **TEGA** (Thermal and Evolved Gas Analyzer) cell from the **Phoenix Mars Lander** in an X-ray / translucent wireframe style.

## Model & Color Details
- **Upper Hopper & Screen Assembly (Green)**:
  - Tilted diagonally across the upper-left portion of the mechanism is a rectangular green-tinted translucent cover and tapered funnel/screen channel where Martian soil samples are received and sieved. Small circular fastener holes are visible at the top and bottom corners of the green cover.
- **Structural Frame & Guides (Cyan / Blue)**:
  - The main supporting chassis, angled guide rails, internal gears/pivots, and base plate are rendered in semi-transparent cyan and light blue with crisp glowing edges.
- **Internal Spring & Actuator Mechanism (Purple / Magenta)**:
  - Running diagonally through the right-center of the blue frame is a bright purple/magenta rod topped with a coiled compression spring, along with a horizontal purple cylindrical bar near the bottom.
- **Sample Tube & Lower Linkage (Orange / Peach)**:
  - Extending downward from the lower-left end of the green funnel into the bottom of the blue housing is a narrow vertical channel and latch linkage highlighted in warm orange/peach tones, leading down to the miniature oven receptacle at the base.
