# Phoenix Mars Lander TEGA Sample Delivery CAD Model (`phx-tega-drop-4.png`)

## Overview
A 3D translucent wireframe and shaded CAD rendering illustrating the internal sample delivery mechanism of a Thermal and Evolved Gas Analyzer (TEGA) cell aboard the NASA Phoenix Mars Lander. The image shows a white spherical particle representing a Martian soil sample being vibrated down a vertical delivery tube toward the sample holder at the base of the instrument.
