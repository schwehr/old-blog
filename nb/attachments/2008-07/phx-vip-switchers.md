# Extron Video Switchers and Cabling in the Phoenix SOC (`phx-vip-switchers.png`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of the rear panel of an equipment rack inside the NASA Phoenix Mars Lander Science Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona. The image shows three stacked Extron video matrix switchers and network hardware densely wired with colorful coaxial and control cables.

## Hardware & Cabling Details
- **Top Rack Units**:
  - At the very top is a narrow 1U dark gray device with a power cord receptacle on the left, a glowing circular fan or indicator, and two horizontal rows of silver BNC coaxial connectors.
  - Directly below it is a silver/gray 1U network switch featuring two banks of RJ-45 Ethernet ports across its face.
- **Extron Matrix Switchers**:
  - Below the network switch are large, multi-unit black rackmount Extron wideband matrix switchers fitted with rows of silver BNC connectors for RGBHV/component video routing.
  - White silk-screened labels on the black chassis include `RED`, `GREEN`, `INPUTS`, `OUTPUTS`, and `SYNC`, along with yellow caution/information stickers on the right side.
  - Green Phoenix-style captive screw terminal blocks are visible along the lower edges of the switcher panels.
- **Cables & Bundles**:
  - A thick bundle of multi-colored coaxial cables—colored bright red, green, blue, yellow, gray, and black—loops across the center and bottom of the frame, connecting to the BNC jacks.
  - Two thick black insulated cable conduits and a braided silver-gray flexible conduit sweep diagonally across the upper right foreground.
