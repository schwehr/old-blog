# Bird Perched on a Saguaro Cactus in Pima Canyon (`pima-canyon-bird.jpg`)

## Overview
An early morning telephoto photograph taken during a hike in Pima Canyon near Tucson, Arizona, showing a silhouetted bird perched atop a tall saguaro cactus against a hazy desert mountain backdrop.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**:
  - A tall, single-trunk saguaro cactus (*Carnegiea gigantea*) rises vertically through the center of the frame in dark silhouette, showing vertical ribs, rows of spines, and a cluster of dried fruit/flowers near its crown on the right.
  - Perched at the very top right edge of the saguaro crown is a medium-sized bird in profile facing left, with its tail angled downward to the right.
  - A long, spiny ocotillo branch angles diagonally upward from the bottom left toward the middle of the saguaro trunk.
  - Dark silhouettes of desert shrubs and smaller cacti line the bottom edge of the frame.
- **Background**:
  - Beyond the desert foothills lies the sunlit floor of the Tucson valley, with faint horizontal bands of buildings and roads catching the early morning light.
  - In the distance, a rugged mountain range rises in soft, hazy pinkish-brown tones beneath a pale, cloudless morning sky.
