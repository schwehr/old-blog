# Desert Home at the Base of Pima Canyon (`pima-canyon-house.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide landscape photograph taken during an early morning hike in Pima Canyon, Tucson, Arizona, featuring a large contemporary desert house nestled in the foothills at the base of the Santa Catalina Mountains.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**:
  - Lush green desert scrub, mesquite bushes, and tall multi-armed saguaro cacti occupy the foreground, illuminated by warm early morning sunlight.
- **Middle Ground (House)**:
  - Centered at the base of the mountain slope is a sprawling, single-story contemporary southwestern-style residence finished in earth-toned tan stucco with flat parapets and a small tiled hip roof section.
  - Broad picture windows and a covered patio face left and toward the viewer, fronted by a blue rectangular swimming pool with a stone or concrete retaining wall.
  - A smaller structure is partially visible in the distance at the far lower left.
- **Background (Mountains & Sky)**:
  - Dramatic, craggy rock peaks and steep canyon walls of the Santa Catalina Mountains rise directly behind the house.
  - Warm golden morning light illuminates the left-facing rocky crags and ridges, while the steeper right-hand slope rests in deep shadow under a clear, pale blue sky.
