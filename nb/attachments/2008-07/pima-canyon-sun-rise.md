# Sunrise Over Pima Canyon Trail (`pima-canyon-sun-rise.jpg`)

## Overview
A landscape photograph looking up into Pima Canyon in the Santa Catalina Mountains near Tucson, Arizona, as the bright morning sun rises behind the steep canyon ridges.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Trail**:
  - In the lower left corner, a narrow, sandy, rock-lined hiking trail winds into the shadowed desert scrub.
  - Across the dark foreground and middle ground, numerous saguaro cacti, prickly pear pads, and desert bushes stand in deep shadow across the canyon floor.
- **Canyon Walls & Lighting**:
  - Steep, rugged mountain slopes frame the canyon on both the left and right sides, converging toward a V-shaped saddle in the center distance.
  - The right-hand cliff face is dark and heavily shadowed in silhouette against the blinding glare of the rising sun just behind its upper ridge.
  - The upper slopes of the left-hand ridge catch warm, golden-green early morning sunlight.
- **Sky**:
  - Above the canyon, the sky transitions from a deep clear blue at the top left to a brilliant, overexposed white solar glare above the right-hand peak.
