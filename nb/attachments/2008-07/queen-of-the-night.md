# Night-Blooming Cereus ("Queen of the Night") Flowers (`queen-of-the-night.jpg`)

## Overview
A nighttime flash photograph taken at Tohono Chul Park in Tucson, Arizona, showing six large white blossoms of the night-blooming cereus cactus (*Peniocereus greggii*, commonly known as "Queen of the Night") blooming in the dark.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Blossoms**:
  - In the lower right foreground, two fully open, brightly lit flowers sit side by side along a slender, dark, woody-looking cactus stem that angles from the middle left toward the bottom right corner.
  - Each flower has multiple layers of pointed,pure white inner petals surrounded by pale greenish-yellow outer tepals, with a dense central cluster of golden-yellow stamens and a pale style.
- **Background Blossoms & Stems**:
  - Four additional white blossoms appear slightly dimmer and farther back against the pitch-black background:
    - One flower on the far left in three-quarter profile attached to the main stem.
    - A pair of overlapping flowers near the upper center-left.
    - A single flower near the upper center-right, just above the left foreground blossom.
  - A second thin, dark, ribbed cactus stem angles downward toward the bottom center in deep shadow.
