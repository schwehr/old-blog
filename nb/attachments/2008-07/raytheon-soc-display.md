# Presentation on the Multi-Projector Wall Display at the Phoenix SOC (`raytheon-soc-display.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken inside a darkened auditorium/presentation room at the NASA Phoenix Mars Lander Science Operations Center (SOC) in Tucson, Arizona, showing an audience watching a wide multi-panel panoramic projection of the Martian surface.

## Scene Description
- **Projection Screen (Upper Left to Center)**:
  - A large, wide-aspect wall screen spans the left and central background, brightly illuminated by a multi-projector display driven by an 8-core Intel Mac with three ATI Radeon HD 2600 XT graphics cards.
  - The screen displays a high-resolution panoramic mosaic of the rocky, sunlit Martian plains around the Phoenix lander, with part of the lander's robotic arm or scoop visible at the far upper left.
  - Vertical seam lines between the tiled projector panels are faintly visible across the bright terrain image.
- **Speaker & Audience**:
  - Standing in silhouette directly in front of the right half of the projection screen is a presenter facing the audience.
  - Rows of seated attendees fill the darkened room in the foreground and middle ground, viewed from behind and illuminated in warm amber/orange indoor lighting.
  - Several audience members near the left are holding 3D glasses up to their eyes to view stereoscopic imagery, while a person seated on the right works at an open laptop computer.
