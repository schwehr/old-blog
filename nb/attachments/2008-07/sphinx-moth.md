# Male Sphinx Moth Resting on Wood (`sphinx-moth.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up macro flash photograph of a large male sphinx moth (hawk moth, family Sphingidae) resting horizontally on a rough, painted tan wooden surface at night during a visit to Tohono Chul Park in Tucson, Arizona.

## Subject & Scene Description
- **Moth Appearance**:
  - The moth is oriented horizontally with its head facing left and its long, swept-back forewings tapering toward the right in a streamlined triangular profile.
  - Its wings and thorax feature intricate, camouflaged mottling in shades of dark charcoal gray, olive-brown, black, and silvery-white, resembling tree bark.
  - Small white dash-like markings line the outer margins (fringes) of the forewings along the right edge.
  - Along the dorsal centerline of the thorax and abdomen, between the inner edges of the folded wings, faint dark and yellowish-tan abdominal bands are visible.
  - Two segmented legs extend forward and downward on the left side, gripping the textured wood, while a hind leg tips out near the upper middle edge of the left wing.
- **Background Surface**:
  - The moth rests just above a rough, weathered horizontal crack or grain ridge along the bottom of a tan-painted wooden beam or post.
