# Rear Connector Panel of StarDot NetCam SC Camera (`stardot-connectors.jpg`)

## Overview
A studio/desktop close-up photograph showing the rear connector panel of a StarDot Technologies NetCam SC 5-megapixel network camera next to a black Sharpie marker placed for scale.

## Hardware & Connector Details
- **Camera Housing**:
  - The camera has a rectangular, off-white/beige extruded metal enclosure with beveled vertical side edges and four corner Phillips screws securing the rear faceplate.
- **Rear Panel Ports & Labels** (clockwise from top left):
  - **`24VAC`**: A two-position green Phoenix-style screw terminal block in a rectangular cutout at the upper left.
  - **`S1 (AUX)`**: A 9-pin male DB-9 (DE-9) serial connector with hex jackscrews at the upper right.
  - **`12VDC`**: A circular barrel power jack on the lower right, printed with a polarity diagram directly above it indicating a center-positive pin (`- (o) +`).
  - **`VIDEO`**: A protruding silver BNC female coaxial connector in the lower center-right for composite analog video output.
  - **`I/O` (`1 2 3 4`)**: A four-position green and orange spring-clamp terminal block in the lower center-left with numbered pins `1`, `2`, `3`, and `4`.
  - **`NET (PoE)`**: An RJ-45 Ethernet jack with integrated link/activity LEDs at the lower left supporting Power over Ethernet.
- **Scale Reference**:
  - To the right of the camera lies a standard black fine-point Sharpie permanent marker angled diagonally from upper center to lower right on the white tabletop.
