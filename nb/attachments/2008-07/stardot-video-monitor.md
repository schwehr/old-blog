# Sony CRT Video Monitor Displaying StarDot NetCam Live Feed (`stardot-video-monitor.jpg`)

## Overview
A handheld photograph of a small Sony Trinitron standard-definition CRT video monitor used to check the analog composite video output and rough optical focus of a StarDot NetCam SC camera pointed out an office window.

## Monitor & Image Details
- **CRT Monitor**:
  - A compact beige/gray Sony CRT monitor (`SONY` logo centered below the dark gray screen bezel) sits in a dimly lit room.
  - A bright green power LED glows on the lower control panel next to a flip-down compartment door on the bottom left and a power button on the bottom right.
- **Displayed Camera Feed**:
  - The glowing CRT screen shows a bluish-tinted, wide-angle outdoor scene looking through an office window into a wooded area with tall tree trunks, branches, and bright patches of sunlight on the ground.
  - Along the bottom of the screen, the window sill or frame curves noticeably upward toward the corners, illustrating strong barrel/spherical distortion from the wide-angle camera optics.
