# NASA Ames "Viz" 3D Stereo Reconstruction of Polar Star Propeller Shaft (`trov-acd-underwater-stereo-1999.gif`)

## Overview
A grayscale screenshot of the "Viz" 3D visualization software developed by the Intelligent Mechanisms Group at NASA Ames Research Center (circa 1999), displaying a 3D surface model reconstructed from underwater stereo camera imagery captured by the TROV (Telepresence Remotely Operated Vehicle) of a propeller shaft housing on the USCG icebreaker *Polar Star*.

## Interface & Controls
- **Window Frame & Title Bar**:
  - Classic Motif/X11-style beveled gray window frame with the title bar text: `Viz - Intelligent Mechanisms Group (NASA Ames)`.
- **Right Toolbar Icons** (top to bottom):
  - Black arrow cursor selection tool.
  - Open hand manipulation/panning icon.
  - Question mark (`?`) help icon.
  - Home (`🏠`) reset view icon.
  - Home with arrow (`🏠↙`) set/go-to home viewpoint icon.
  - Eye icon with rays (view/lighting/visibility toggle).
  - Crosshair target icon (pick/seek focal point).
  - 3D faceted crystal/polyhedron icon (rendering style toggle between shaded and wireframe).
- **Thumbwheel Sliders**:
  - **Left Vertical Border**: Thumbwheel slider labeled `Rotx` at the bottom left for X-axis rotation.
  - **Bottom Horizontal Border**: Thumbwheel slider labeled `Roty` to its left for Y-axis rotation.
  - **Right Vertical Border**: Thumbwheel slider labeled `Dolly` at the bottom right for camera zoom/translation.

## 3D Viewport Contents
- Set against a solid black viewport background are two representations of the same underwater structural section (a propeller shaft strut/housing on the icebreaker *Polar Star*):
  - **Upper Left**: A grayscale photo-textured 3D surface mesh showing a curved cylindrical shaft housing with three circular recessed ports or sacrificial anodes/bolts in a row.
  - **Lower Right**: A white triangulated 3D wireframe mesh of the same reconstructed surface geometry, illustrating the underlying polygon triangulation produced from the stereo camera disparity map.
