# University of Arizona Mars Web Portal Screenshot (`ua-mars.png`)

## Overview
A webpage screenshot of the University of Arizona's public Mars portal (`The University of Arizona Goes to Mars`), highlighting UA's leadership in the NASA Phoenix Mars Mission, HiRISE, and the Mars Odyssey Gamma Ray Spectrometer (GRS).

## Page Layout & Visible Content
- **Top Navigation & Banner**:
  - **Red Header Bar**: Bears the official `A THE UNIVERSITY OF ARIZONA®` logo on the left and links on the right: `UA Map | Phonebook | Calendars | A-Z Index`.
  - **Blue Panoramic Banner**: Shows a tinted blue Martian horizon with a small illustration of the Phoenix lander on the left and the `UA MARS` globe logo on the right.
  - **Main Heading**: Centered in large dark blue sans-serif font: `The University of Arizona Goes to Mars`.
- **Left Column (Slideshow & News)**:
  - **Interactive Image Gallery**: Shows a grayscale close-up image of Martian soil dumped onto an instrument doorway on the Phoenix lander deck. Beneath the photo is a playback/pagination bar with numbers `01` through `12` (`06` highlighted in bold) and transport controls.
  - **`Top Mars Stories from UA News`**:
    - *Phoenix Scrapes 'Almost Perfect' Icy Soil for Analysis* — "Samples hold clues to history of Martian water, climate and possible habitability."
    - *Phoenix Scrapes to Icy Soil in Wonderland* — "The 'Snow White' trench is ideal for sampling top to bottom."
    - *Phoenix Lander Puts Soil in Chemistry Lab, Team Discusses Next Steps* — "Phoenix placed a sample of Martian soil in the spacecraft's wet chemistry laboratory today for the first time."
    - *Phoenix Poised to Deliver Sample for Wet Chemistry* — "Phoenix is set to do the first wet chemistry test of polar Martian soil."
    - *Phoenix Lander Prepares for Microscopy, Wet Chemistry on Mars* — "The lander performed a diagnostic test that melted Earth ice to water."
    - *Frozen Water Confirmed on Mars* — "The Phoenix Mars Lander has confirmed frozen water on Mars. The search continues for organic materials and other related substances."
    - Bottom right link: `More Mars Stories »`.
- **Right Column (Mission Links & Weather Widget)**:
  - **`Phoenix Mars Mission`**: "The first in NASA's 'Scout Program' - designed as a highly innovative and relatively low-cost complement to the agency's Mars Exploration Program." Sub-link: `Phoenix Education`.

  - **`Things To Do on Campus`**: "Visit various spots across campus for the best view before, during and after landing." Sub-link: `Campus Map`.
  - **`HiRISE`**: "The highest resolution images ever captured of the surface of Mars from the UA's telescopic camera aboard the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter." Sub-link: `HiRISE Education`.
  - **`Gamma Ray Spectrometer`**: "Launched aboard the 2001 Mars Odyssey to study, among other things, the distribution of water on the Martian surface." Sub-link: `GRS Education`.

    - `MIN`: `-108.4° F / -78° C` (with thermometer icon)
