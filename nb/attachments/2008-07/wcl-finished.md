# Rebuilt Phoenix Wet Chemistry Lab Diagram (`wcl-finished.png`)

## Overview
A clean, rebuilt diagram of a NASA Phoenix Mars Lander Wet Chemistry Laboratory (WCL) single-cell instrument unit, created by Kurt Schwehr to fix transparency and legibility issues in an earlier release figure (archived by NASA JPL Photojournal as `PIA10916`). As described in the July 1, 2008 blog post *"Figure troubles"*, the background on the left was extended in Adobe Photoshop by cloning and smudging the studio backdrop, and crisp red Helvetica labels and pointer lines were added in Adobe Illustrator before flattening the final image without transparency.

## Diagram Layout & Callouts
- **Background:** A uniform, seamless off-white/light-cream studio background extends across the entire frame, eliminating the black alpha-channel block seen in earlier versions.
- **Labels & Pointer Lines:** Six large, crisp, bright red (`#FF0000`) sans-serif (Helvetica) labels are arranged along a gentle curve on the left side, each connected by a solid, heavy red line (without arrowheads) to its corresponding component:
  1. **`Water Tank`** (top left) — line angles down and right to the rectangular stainless-steel reservoir tank at the top rear of the unit.
  2. **`Stir Motor`** — line extends nearly horizontally to the small cylindrical gray motor cap atop the upper assembly.
  3. **`Crucible Dispenser`** (two lines, right-aligned) — line angles up and right to the black cylindrical dispenser tube mounted horizontally across the upper section.
  4. **`Funnel`** — line angles up and right to the lower lip of the angled black rectangular input funnel.
  5. **`Drawer`** — line angles up and right to the center of the circular black rotating/sliding drawer face beneath the funnel.
  6. **`Beaker`** (bottom left) — line angles up and right to the lower matte-black rectangular beaker enclosure.

## Hardware Details
- **Lower Beaker Housing:** A matte-black anodized or painted rectangular box with slotted mounting flanges protruding from the bottom corners and socket-head cap screws securing the top plate.
- **Upper Sample Handling & Actuator Assembly:**
  - **Funnel & Sieve:** An angled black chute with a grate of parallel silver wires across the top opening to screen incoming Martian soil samples delivered by the robotic arm.
  - **Drawer & Crucible Dispenser:** Below the funnel is the circular drawer assembly that receives soil and reagents; above it is the black cylindrical crucible dispenser with a clear/amber cylindrical extension on the right held by a blue zip tie.
  - **Water Tank & Wiring:** A metallic box on the upper left with faint stenciled lettering visible under the white and red wires (`...EY CO...`) and amber Kapton tape.
  - **Identification Stencils:**
    - Top-right black rectangular block has white stenciled text reading **`SPC`**, **`004144-004`**, **`ME-0023`**, **`J01-04`**.
    - Right-side vertical black plate has white stenciled text reading **`24225 D`** (top) and **`SN 004`** (bottom) on either side of two silver screw heads.
    - Translucent gray RTV silicone potting/strain relief secures a bundle of red, white, and blue wires at the lower right corner of the upper deck.
