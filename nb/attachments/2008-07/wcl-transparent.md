# Apple Finder Cover Flow Preview Showing WCL TIFF Transparency (`wcl-transparent.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the macOS Leopard (10.5) Finder Cover Flow view displaying the file `WCL cell.tif` (a labeled diagram of the NASA Phoenix Mars Lander's Wet Chemistry Laboratory). Posted on July 1, 2008 in *"Figure troubles"*, Kurt Schwehr used this screenshot to prove that the left side of the TIFF image actually contained a transparent alpha channel: in Finder's Cover Flow, the black background of the Cover Flow stage shows directly through the transparent left portion of the image while the red text and arrows float in space without a bounding box.

## Interface & Visual Details
- **Finder Cover Flow Stage:**
  - The background is the glossy black 3D stage of macOS Finder's Cover Flow mode, complete with mirror reflections along the bottom.
  - On the far left edge, a light-blue textured book/document cover is angled inward in 3D perspective, casting a dark blue reflection onto the glossy floor below.
  - On the far right edge, a sliver of another dark red/black graphic is angled away in the Cover Flow stack.
- **Centered Preview (`WCL cell.tif`):**
  - The right portion of the selected item is a sharp rectangular studio photograph of the WCL instrument on an off-white background.
  - The left portion of the item has **no background fill at all**—the red labels (**`Water Tank`**, **`Stir Motor`**, **`Crucible Dispenser`**, **`Funnel`**, **`Drawer`**, **`Beaker`**) and their thin red arrow lines float directly over the black Cover Flow backdrop, slightly overlapping the angled blue icon to the left.
  - Below the image, the Cover Flow floor displays a vertically flipped, faded reflection of the white-background hardware photo (and no reflection on the left where the image is transparent).
- **Caption Text:**
  - Centered near the bottom over the reflection are two lines of clean sans-serif text:
    - **`WCL cell.tif`** (bold white)
    - **`TIFF image`** (light gray)
