# Waterfall and Plunge Pool in Franconia Notch, White Mountains (`white-mts-falls.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical nature photograph of a small waterfall cascading over smooth, sculpted granite bedrock into a clear emerald-green plunge pool in the White Mountains / Franconia Notch region of New Hampshire. The photo was published in a July 29, 2008 blog post titled *"Franconia and White Mts"* recounting a weekend trip visiting the Flume Gorge and Lost River Gorge & Boulder Caves.

## Scene Description
- **Waterfall & Log:**
  - In the center of the frame, white water spills over a curved, water-polished granite ledge into a deep basin.
  - A long, stripped wooden log or tree trunk is wedged diagonally across the face of the falls from upper-left to lower-right, with foaming white water cascading both over and behind it.
- **Granite Bedrock & Boulders:**
  - To the right of the falls, sunlit orange-tan granite bedrock slopes smoothly down toward the water, topped by a horizontal row of squared granite boulders along the upper bank.
  - On the upper left, a dark, shadowed, overhanging rock face juts out above the pool, deeply undercut at the water level.
  - In the lower-right foreground, a smooth, rounded brown boulder protrudes from the swirling whitewater foam.
- **Plunge Pool & Foliage:**
  - The lower half of the image is filled with the turbulent plunge pool, transitioning from bright white aerated foam on the right to deep, translucent emerald and forest green water in the shadowed left foreground, with frothy white surface currents swirling toward the bottom left corner.
  - The top third of the frame is framed by overhanging dark-green hemlock/conifer branches in silhouette in the foreground and brightly lit, lush green deciduous and evergreen forest foliage in the background.
