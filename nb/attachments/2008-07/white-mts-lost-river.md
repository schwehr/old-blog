# Covered Wooden Footbridge in the White Mountains (`white-mts-lost-river.jpg`)

## Overview
A horizontal photograph of a rustic covered wooden pedestrian bridge spanning a rocky mountain stream amidst a dense green forest in the White Mountains / Franconia region of New Hampshire (near Lost River Gorge / Flume Gorge). The image was included in the July 29, 2008 blog post *"Franconia and White Mts"*.

## Scene Description
- **Covered Bridge & Walkway:**
  - In the foreground, a narrow wooden boardwalk with unpeeled round log handrails leads straight toward a short flight of five wooden steps.
  - At the top of the steps is a small open wooden deck/landing on the left, which turns 90 degrees to the right into a covered wooden footbridge.
  - The bridge features weathered gray vertical board siding reach-high along its sides, heavy timber post-and-beam framing with diagonal knee braces inside, and a gabled shingle roof dusted with fallen pine needles and moss.
  - Above the entrance gable of the bridge is a small rectangular wooden plaque with light-colored lettering (three lines of text, too small to read clearly).
  - A small dark-green directional sign with a white right-pointing arrow is mounted on the left railing post by the landing.
- **Stream & Forest Surroundings:**
  - Beneath the right span of the bridge, a shallow, clear mountain brook flows over a bed of rounded cobbles, gravel, and boulders, with a pale fallen branch resting on the stones at the bottom right.
  - The bridge is completely surrounded by lush summer woodland: a rough-barked tree trunk stands in the immediate left foreground, tall conifers and birches rise above the roof in the background, and vibrant green leafy branches frame the right side of the composition.
