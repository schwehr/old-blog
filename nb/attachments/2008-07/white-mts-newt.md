# Eastern Newt in Shallow Pond Water, White Mountains (`white-mts-newt.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up nature photograph looking down through shallow, clear water at an aquatic adult Eastern Newt (*Notophthalmus viridescens*) resting on a sandy, silt-covered pond or stream bottom among emergent marsh grasses. The photograph was published in the July 29, 2008 blog post *"Franconia and White Mts"* from a weekend trip to Franconia and the White Mountains of New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Eastern Newt:**
  - Positioned in the lower-left-of-center portion of the frame, the newt is oriented diagonally with its head pointing toward the upper right and its long, laterally compressed tail extending toward the lower left.
  - Its dorsal coloration is olive-green to dark yellowish-brown, with a brighter yellowish underbelly visible along the lower flank and jaw, and its short front and hind legs splayed out against the sediment.
- **Substrate & Aquatic Vegetation:**
  - The background consists of a uniform, fine-grained golden-brown and tan silty/sandy bottom speckled with tiny pale mineral grains and organic detritus.
  - Tall, slender, bright-green blades and stems of emergent sedge or grass rise vertically and diagonally across the left and right sides of the frame, out of focus in the foreground above the water surface.
  - Delicate, drooping seed heads with small pale-green/white spikelets arch across the upper left and upper right corners of the image.
