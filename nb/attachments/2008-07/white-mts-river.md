# Cascading Mountain Stream in the White Mountains (`white-mts-river.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical landscape photograph looking upstream along a rocky mountain river cascading through a lush green forest gorge in the White Mountains / Franconia Notch area of New Hampshire. The image leads off the July 29, 2008 blog post *"Franconia and White Mts"*.

## Scene Description
- **River & Rapids:**
  - White, frothy rapids tumble down the center of the frame from a calm, darker pool in the upper background toward the bottom foreground, splitting around large granite boulders.
  - In the lower foreground, the stream divides into two main chutes around a prominent, elongated pinkish-tan granite boulder in the bottom center: a narrow ribbon of water spills down the left side of the boulder while a wider, churning torrent of white foam rushes down the right channel.
- **Granite Boulders:**
  - The streambed is choked with angular and rounded granite boulders in shades of warm tan, pink, gray, and dark wet brown.
  - Larger boulders line the lower left and right banks, while smaller stepping-stone boulders form natural ledges across the middle and upper reaches of the stream.
- **Forest Canopy:**
  - Dense, vibrant summer foliage of overhanging deciduous and coniferous trees frames both the left and right edges of the river corridor, creating deep shadows along the left bank and a natural green tunnel effect at the top of the frame.
