# USCGC Healy Aloftcon Webcam View of Arctic Pack Ice and Melt Ponds (`20080825-2001-sml.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle photograph captured by the aloftcon (aloft conn tower) webcam aboard the U.S. Coast Guard icebreaker *USCGC Healy* (WAGB-20) on August 25, 2008 at 20:01 UTC (`20080825-2001`), looking forward over the ship's bow across Arctic sea ice and turquoise melt ponds. The image was featured in the August 25, 2008 blog post *"Healy science team spots a polar bear"*, where Kurt Schwehr remarked on the spectacular color of the ice ponds visible in the hourly aloftcon camera feed from the Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory (LDEO) Marine Geoscience Data System.

## Scene Description
- **Ship's Bow & Foreground:**
  - At the bottom center of the wide-angle frame is the curved foredeck and bow of *USCGC Healy*, painted dark red/brown with white lifelines/railings along the deck edge and a central jackstaff mast rising from the stem with a small red flag flying near the top.
  - To the left and right of the bow along the bottom edge, white safety railings and twin white mast/antenna stanchions with crossbar sensors frame the foreground view.
- **Arctic Sea Ice & Melt Ponds:**
  - Stretching from the bow to the horizon is a vast expanse of fractured late-summer Arctic pack ice under an overcast gray-blue polar sky.
  - On the right side and directly ahead of the bow, large flat white ice floes are dotted with vivid, jewel-toned **turquoise and cerulean-blue melt ponds**—most notably a large, rounded turquoise pond on the middle-right and two smaller bright blue ponds just off the starboard and port sides of the bow.
  - On the left side and extending diagonally into the middle distance is a dark gray-black open-water lead filled with smaller broken ice floes, brash ice, and slush.
