# Portrait of NASA Scientist William "Bill" Borucki (`BillBorucki-kepler-1.png`)

## Overview
A close-up video still of NASA Ames Research Center space scientist and Kepler Mission Principal Investigator William "Bill" Borucki, captured from a YouTube video about the NASA Kepler Mission (`http://www.youtube.com/v/ba4LnxwBgjw`). Featured in Kurt Schwehr's August 24, 2008 blog post *"Bill Borucki - Finding planets"*, the post reminisces about Borucki being Schwehr's first boss at NASA in 1989 when working on robotic telescope photometry (`Acquire`) and extrasolar planet transit searches.
