# NASA Kepler Principal Investigator Bill Borucki in a Cleanroom (`BillBorucki-kepler-2.png`)

## Overview
A video still showing NASA Kepler Mission Principal Investigator William "Bill" Borucki wearing cleanroom attire inside an aerospace laboratory/cleanroom facility (Ball Aerospace), captured from a video profiling the Kepler space telescope mission. The image appears as the second screenshot in Kurt Schwehr's August 24, 2008 blog post *"Bill Borucki - Finding planets"*.

## Scene Description
- **Subject:**
  - Bill Borucki is positioned just right of center, shown from the chest up and facing slightly to the left while speaking.
  - He is wearing thin-framed eyeglasses, a white disposable bouffant cleanroom hairnet cap pulled down over his ears, and a white cleanroom smock/lab coat over a collared shirt.
  - On the left chest of his white smock is a dark embroidered logo reading **"Ball"** (for Ball Aerospace & Technologies Corp., the prime contractor for the Kepler spacecraft and photometer).
- **Cleanroom Environment:**
  - In the background is a brightly lit, high-bay industrial cleanroom or laboratory with pale greenish-white walls.
  - To the left stands a large, boxy off-white/gray test fixture or electronics enclosure with a dark monitor on top, an office chair behind it, and a tall vertical metal ladder rising against a gray structure in the center background.
  - In the lower-right corner, a faint translucent watermark (`DC` or channel bug) is superimposed over the sleeve of his smock.
