# Polar Bear on Arctic Pack Ice Spotted from USCGC Healy (`HealyPolarBear.jpg`)

## Overview
A distant horizontal photograph of a lone polar bear standing on broken Arctic sea ice at the edge of an open-water melt pond or lead, illuminated by low, warm golden polar sunlight. The photograph was featured in Kurt Schwehr's August 25, 2008 blog post *"Healy science team spots a polar bear"*, documenting a sighting by the science party aboard the U.S. Coast Guard icebreaker *USCGC Healy* during its 2008 Arctic mapping cruise (`HLY0805`).

## Scene Description
- **Polar Bear:**
  - Located slightly below and to the right of the center of the frame, a cream-white polar bear stands in left profile near the water's edge on a flat ice floe.
  - Its head is lowered slightly toward the left as it looks out across the narrow channel of calm water, with a faint dark reflection of its body visible in the still water directly beneath the ice lip.
- **Sea Ice & Leads:**
  - The surrounding seascape consists of a mosaic of flat, snow-covered white and pale-blue ice floes separated by calm, dark steel-gray channels and pools of water in the foreground and middle ground.
  - Directly behind the bear rises a higher, hummocky pressure ridge of sculpted ice catching softpeach and golden highlights on its right-facing slopes and cool blue shadows on its left.
- **Horizon & Sky:**
  - Beyond the pressure ridge, packed white sea ice extends flat to a sharp horizontal horizon.
  - The sky above is a soft gradient of warm peach, amber, and muted lavender-gray clouds, characteristic of low-angle Arctic sun conditions.
