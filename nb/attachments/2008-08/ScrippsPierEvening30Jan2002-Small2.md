# Sunset View of Scripps Pier and Tide Pools in La Jolla, California (`ScrippsPierEvening30Jan2002-Small2.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format evening photograph of the Ellen Browning Scripps Memorial Pier at the Scripps Institution of Oceanography in La Jolla, California, silhouetted against a vivid orange sunset over the Pacific Ocean on January 30, 2002. Kurt Schwehr shared this archival photo in an August 10, 2008 blog post titled *"Scripps Pier"*, noting that he took it on January 30, 2002 while working on a paper about deformation in the Ardath Shale exposed along the nearby sea cliffs.

## Scene Description
- **Scripps Pier & Sunset Horizon:**
  - Extending from the left edge to just past the center of the frame is the dark silhouette of Scripps Pier, supported by a long, regular colonnade of twin vertical concrete pilings marching out into the surf.
  - At the seaward (right) end of the pier, the deck widens around the silhouette of the pier building, crane/hoist structures, and instrument masts.
  - Just to the right of the pier's end, the setting sun forms a bright white-yellow dome right on the ocean horizon, surrounded by a glowing band of fiery orange and peach light that fades upward into a clear, pale amber-gray evening sky.
- **Beach, Reflections & Tide Pools:**
  - Below the pier, low white surf breaks gently along the dark ocean surface.
  - Across the middle ground, wet, flat low-tide sand acts like a mirror, reflecting the vertical black pier pilings and the warm golden-orange glow of the sunset, with several tiny dark silhouettes of shorebirds standing near the water's edge on the right.
  - In the dark, shadowed foreground of the beach, several shallow, irregular tide pools and wet sand depressions reflect the pale sky like silver-blue mirrors, punctuated by small dark rounded cobbles and rocks protruding above the water surface.
