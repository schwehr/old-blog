# CAD Rendering of the WHOI Replacement HOV Submersible (`alvin-replacement-whoi-rhov_en_76988.jpg`)

## Overview

A three-dimensional computer-aided design (CAD) rendering of the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution's (WHOI) Replacement Human Occupied Vehicle (RHOV), designed to succeed the deep-sea research submersible *Alvin* and reach depths of over four miles (6,500 meters). Featured in Kurt Schwehr's August 26, 2008 blog post "Alvin's replacement in the works."

## Vehicle Design & Components

- **Orientation & Background**:
  - The submersible is shown in a three-quarter front-left view against a smooth dark blue-gray gradient background.
- **Hull & Fairing**:
  - The main body is enclosed in a streamlined, white fiberglass or composite outer hull fairing that tapers toward the stern on the right.
  - The top deck features a raised, flat-topped gray section behind the sail.
- **Conning Tower / Sail (Top Left)**:
  - A bright red, slightly tapered conning tower (sail) protrudes from the forward upper deck, topped with a gray hatch cover and small sensor fittings.
  - White rectangular nameplates or decals are visible on the side of the red sail, though the text is too small to read at this resolution.
  - A dark gray tubular guardrail surrounds the base of the red sail, with forward-facing lights and cameras mounted on the rail.
- **Personnel Sphere & Viewports (Front Left)**:
  - Beneath the white brow of the forward fairing, the dark metallic gray spherical personnel cabin is partially visible.
  - Three circular viewports with thick gray bezels are visible in this view: one facing forward-left, one angled downward toward the front work area, and a third side viewport recessed into a contoured cutout in the white hull side panel.
- **Manipulator Arms & Sample Basket (Lower Left)**:
  - Two multi-jointed gray mechanical manipulator arms with claw grippers hang vertically on either side of the forward personnel sphere (port and starboard).
  - Projecting forward from the lower frame beneath the sphere is a rectangular gray metal sample basket with a perforated grid floor, supported by tubular metal struts.
- **Thrusters**:
  - A black-and-gray vertical/lateral thruster pod is mounted mid-ship along the upper port side of the white hull.
  - At the tapered stern (far right), two black ducted propellers (thrusters) are mounted on the port side—one near the upper stern and one lower down protected by a tubular metal guard frame.
