# Forging the Titanium Personnel Sphere for the Replacement Alvin Submersible (`alvin-replacement-whoi.jpg`)

## Overview

A photograph inside a heavy industrial foundry or metal-forging facility showing the glowing, red-hot titanium hemisphere being formed for the personnel sphere of the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution's (WHOI) replacement Human Occupied Vehicle (HOV) for *Alvin*. The image was included in an August 26, 2008 blog post titled "Alvin's replacement in the works," referencing a *New York Times* article ("New Sphere in Exploring the Abyss") and WHOI's Replacement HOV project page.

## Scene Description

- **Glowing Titanium Hemisphere (Foreground Center)**:
  - A massive, bowl-shaped titanium hemisphere sits upright in a circular metal die ring set into a recessed floor pit in the foreground.
  - The thick metal walls of the hemisphere glow a vivid, incandescent magenta-pink and orange-red from extreme forging heat, with darker, scaly oxidation patches scattered across its upper exterior rim.
  - Small bright yellow-orange flames flicker around the right base of the hemisphere inside the circular mounting ring.
- **Upper Press / Die (Top Center)**:
  - Suspended directly above the glowing bowl is a large, dark gray hemispherical dome—either the upper forging die or a mating sphere component—with a small circular opening visible at its bottom apex.
- **Foundry Workers & Observers (Midground)**:
  - Roughly a dozen workers and observers wearing hard hats (white, yellow, green, and blue), safety glasses, and work jackets or shirts stand around the perimeter of the recessed pit watching the operation.
  - In the center, directly behind the glowing hemisphere, a worker in a teal-green jacket and white hard hat stands with hands on his hips looking down into the bowl.
  - On the left side, a group of workers in dark coveralls and hard hats clusters near the edge of the pit, illuminated by a bright work light on a stand at the far left.
  - In the upper left background, a worker stands inside the yellow cage of an elevated boom lift (cherry picker).
  - To the right of the pit, several more workers in white and blue hard hats stand near a large, hollow cylindrical metal forging ring lying on its side and a yellow industrial vehicle.
- **Industrial Interior (Background)**:
  - The background shows a cavernous, dimly lit industrial bay with dark steel support columns, heavy machinery, overhead cranes, and a row of high clerestory windows along the upper right wall letting in daylight.
