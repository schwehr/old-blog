# The Anchor Newsletter Excerpt: "Boy Scouts Learn about Mapping" (`anchor-newsletter-kurt-scouts.png`)

## Overview

A cropped screenshot of an article from *The Anchor*, the newsletter of the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) / Joint Hydrographic Center (JHC) at the University of New Hampshire. Shared in an August 1, 2008 blog post titled "Me in the Anchor newsletter," the excerpt highlights Kurt Schwehr's presentation on multibeam sonar mapping to a local Boy Scouts Order of the Arrow chapter in Epping, New Hampshire.
