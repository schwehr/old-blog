# Kurt Schwehr Photographing Boston Harbor (`boston-kurt.jpg`)

## Overview

A photograph of Kurt Schwehr standing on a survey boat taking pictures of the Boston waterfront during an August 20, 2008 photographic survey of Boston Harbor navigation features with colleague Matthew Plumlee (posted August 21, 2008 in "Boston photo survey").

  - Kurt Schwehr is shown from the waist up in left profile in the lower right corner of the frame, facing left toward the water and piers.
  - He has short brown hair and wears dark sunglasses, a bright red short-sleeved t-shirt, and a blue-and-yellow inflatable life vest (PFD) with black and yellow webbing straps across his back.
  - He holds a silver-and-black DSLR camera with a wide-angle zoom lens up to his eye with both hands, capturing photos of the harbor.
