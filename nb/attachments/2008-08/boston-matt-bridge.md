# Matthew Plumlee Under the Tobin Bridge in Boston Harbor (`boston-matt-bridge.jpg`)

## Overview

A vertical, low-angle photograph of researcher Matthew Plumlee standing aboard a small survey boat directly beneath the towering green steel truss structure of the Maurice J. Tobin Memorial Bridge (U.S. Route 1) spanning the Mystic River in Boston Harbor. Taken by Kurt Schwehr on August 20, 2008 during their Boston Harbor navigation photo survey and posted on August 21, 2008.

## Scene Description

- **Subject (Lower Center Foreground)**:
  - Matthew Plumlee stands in the cockpit of an aluminum survey boat, smiling toward the camera against bright sunlight.
  - He wears dark sunglasses, a black t-shirt under an olive-gray zip-up jacket, khaki pants, and a blue inflatable life vest (PFD).
  - Around his neck hangs a black Canon DSLR camera fitted with a large telephoto lens, which he cradles with both hands at chest level.
  - In the immediate bottom foreground are the metallic gray windshield frame and a white cushioned bolster/seat of the boat, with white antenna masts rising at the left and right edges.
- **Bridge Structure (Center & Upper Frame)**:
  - Directly behind Matt rises a massive, twin-legged concrete bridge pier stained with vertical rust streaks near the top and resting on a stone masonry base at the waterline.
  - Extending diagonally from the lower left to the upper right is the underside of the high-level green steel truss bridge deck, showing an intricate lattice of green steel cross-beams, stringers, and diagonal braces against a vivid, cloudless blue sky.
