# Matthew Plumlee Photographing the Boston Waterfront (`boston-matt-photo.jpg`)

## Overview

A horizontal photograph showing Matthew Plumlee in action aboard a survey boat in Boston Harbor, aiming a camera toward the downtown Boston skyline and waterfront piers near the Charlestown Navy Yard (described in the August 21, 2008 blog post "Boston photo survey" as *"Matt in action just around the corner from the Constitution..."*).

## Scene Description

- **Subject (Left Foreground)**:
  - Matthew Plumlee stands on the left side of the boat's cabin/deck in right profile, facing the Boston skyline.
  - He wears an olive-green jacket over a black shirt, light khaki trousers, and a blue inflatable PFD around his neck.
  - He holds a black DSLR camera with a zoom lens up to his eye, sighting toward the right.
  - Behind him on the flat gray cabin roof at the far lower left are small pieces of boat hardware and another black camera resting on the surface.
- **Harbor & Boat Elements (Midground)**:
  - The sloped gray metal cabin roof and white railing of the survey boat occupy the lower left and bottom center, with a vertical white antenna whip and a thinner black rod rising in the center of the frame.
  - Dark blue harbor water stretches between the boat and the waterfront piers.
- **Boston Waterfront & Skyline (Right Background)**:
  - On the right, a wooden pile-supported pier extends into the harbor in front of multi-story white waterfront residential/commercial buildings (such as Rowes Wharf / Lewis Wharf / Sargent's Wharf condos) and docked white motor yachts.
  - Rising behind the white waterfront buildings is the downtown Boston skyline—including the Custom House Tower near the center and numerous brown, gray, and glass high-rise office buildings—under a clear, pale blue sky.
