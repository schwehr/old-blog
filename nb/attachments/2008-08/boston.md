# Red Navigation Buoy #8 and Distant Boston Skyline (`boston.jpg`)

## Overview

A scenic daytime photograph of Boston Harbor taken on August 20, 2008, during a 100-mile, 3,000-photo navigation feature survey conducted by Kurt Schwehr and Matthew Plumlee for the GeoCoastPilot project (posted August 21, 2008 under "Boston photo survey"). The image shows a red lighted buoy in the foreground with harbor islands and the downtown Boston skyline in the distance.

## Scene Description

- **Foreground (Water & Buoy)**:
  - Calm, greenish-teal harbor water with light ripples fills the lower two-thirds of the frame.
  - In the lower left foreground floats a bright red navigation buoy (nun/lighted buoy) marked with a white number `8` on a triangular red dayboard atop a short central post, capped with a small white lantern/light at the top and a yellowish-white weathered ring along the waterline of its cylindrical base.
- **Midground (Harbor Islands)**:
  - Stretching across the left and center horizon is a low-lying Boston Harbor island with a narrow sandy/gravelly beach spit leading to a cluster of green trees on its right point.
  - Further left, a sloped green hill dotted with buildings rises above the shoreline, and to the right across an open channel lies another tree-covered island or peninsula.
- **Background (Boston Skyline & Sky)**:
  - Between the low hill on the left and the clump of trees in the center, the distant skyscrapers of downtown Boston (including the Prudential Tower and John Hancock Tower on the left and the Financial District towers toward the center) rise faintly against the horizon.
  - Above, a bright blue summer sky is partially covered by a broad band of soft white and pale pinkish-gray cumulus and stratocumulus clouds.
