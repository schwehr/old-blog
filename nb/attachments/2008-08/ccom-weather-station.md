# Rooftop Weather Station on the CCOM Building (`ccom-weather-station.jpg`)

## Overview

A low-angle exterior photograph looking up at a meteorological weather station mounted on the roof parapet of the newly expanded Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory / Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) building at the University of New Hampshire. Posted on August 28, 2008 under the title "CCOM Weather Station," noting that colleague Andy M. installed the station on the roof.

## Scene Description

- **Building Facade (Lower Left to Bottom Right)**:
  - The lower half of the frame shows the upper story of a modern institutional building clad in rectangular tan/brown masonry or architectural block tiles arranged in a regular grid pattern.
  - A bright white metal coping caps the stepped flat roofline, which angles diagonally from the middle-left down toward the lower right.
  - Four rectangular dark-tinted windows with light-colored aluminum frames are set into the wall along the bottom of the frame (one larger window on the far left and three partially visible windows toward the center and right).
