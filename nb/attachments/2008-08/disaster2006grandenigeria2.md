# Collision Between Roll-On/Roll-Off Vessels Nada V and Grande Nigeria (`disaster2006grandenigeria2.png`)

## Overview

A maritime incident photograph showing the aftermath of a daytime collision between two large roll-on/roll-off (Ro-Ro) vehicle/container carriers—the *Nada V* and the *Grande Nigeria*—with two harbor tugboats assisting alongside. Featured in Kurt Schwehr's August 13, 2008 blog post "Passing arrangements?" referencing a *gCaptain* / *CargoLaw* "Incident Photo of The Week - Head On Collision" article.

## Scene Description

- **Collided Vessels**:
  - **Left Vessel (*Nada V*)**: A massive Ro-Ro car carrier with a dark navy/black lower hull and a tall light gray box-like upper superstructure, angled away from the camera from left to center. Its flat transom stern is visible on the left, featuring a large folded stern quarter ramp on the starboard side and white lettering on the dark stern reading `NADA V` above `PANAMA`. A black-and-red funnel with a white horizontal stripe sits atop the upper deck.
  - **Right Vessel (*Grande Nigeria*)**: A second large Ro-Ro/container carrier belonging to Grimaldi Lines, stretching from the center to the right distance. It has a white hull and superstructure with a bright yellow bow/lower hull section and large dark lettering along its port side reading `GRIMALDI LINES`. Red and blue shipping containers are stacked on its upper weather deck forward of the bridge.
  - **Point of Impact (Center)**: The bow of the *Nada V* and the forward port quarter of the *Grande Nigeria* are wedged together near the center of the image.
- **Assisting Tugboats**:
  - **Foreground Tug (Left-Center)**: A harbor tugboat with a dark hull and a bright yellow wheelhouse/superstructure moves through the choppy greenish-gray water in the left foreground, kicking up a white bow wave as it approaches the stern of *Nada V*.
  - **Midground Tug (Center)**: A second tugboat with a black hull and white superstructure sits directly alongside the point of collision between the two large ships.
  - **Distant Boat (Far Right)**: A small white-and-blue pilot or patrol boat is visible near the stern of the *Grande Nigeria* at the far right edge.
- **Environment**:
  - The scene takes place on choppy, olive-gray open water or an estuary under a hazy, overcast pale gray sky.
