# TrackPlot 3D Ribbon Visualization of Whale DTAG Data (`dtag-trackplot-colin-ware.jpg`)

## Overview

A 3D scientific visualization created by Professor Colin Ware (University of New Hampshire, Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping) using the *TrackPlot* software to display underwater movement and foraging behavior of tagged whales recorded by suction-cup digital acoustic recording tags (DTAGs). Included in Kurt Schwehr's August 23, 2008 blog post "Seismic air guns and whales in the Gulf of Mexico."

## Visualization Details

- **Background & Layout**:
  - Set against a pure white background framed by a thin gray border, showing a complex, looping 3D trajectory viewed from a perspective angle.
- **Ribbon Trajectory & Color Coding**:
  - The whale's path through the water column is represented as a continuous 3D ribbon that dives steeply downward, levels off in convoluted horizontal loops at depth, and ascends steeply back toward the surface.
  - The main body of the ribbon is light cyan/blue, banded with fine purple/magenta cross-stripes along the steep vertical descent/ascent segments (indicating time ticks or fluke strokes).
  - Along the edges of the ribbon are prominent sawtooth-like triangular fins or spikes colored bright red/orange on one side and cyan/turquoise on the other, representing roll, pitch, or side-to-side fluke/body orientation maneuvers during foraging.
  - Several tightly curved segments along the bottom loops of the trajectory are highlighted in bright yellow-green with red edge spikes.
  - Smooth dark gray ribbon segments connect or shadow portions of the track, particularly along the upper surface intervals and steep vertical transitions.
- **Visible Text / Credit**:
  - In the lower right quadrant, bold dark gray sans-serif text with a soft drop shadow reads: `Credit: Colin Ware, UNH`.
