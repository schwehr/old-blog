# Onions and Newly Sprouted Bean Plants in the Garden (`garden-beans-onios.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up, high-angle photograph looking down through a wire mesh trellis at maturing white onions and newly sprouted bean seedlings in Kurt Schwehr's backyard garden. Posted on August 9, 2008 in "Rain and gardening" with the caption: *"Here are the first onions finishing up and two beans just planted."* (Note: the filename spells "onions" as `onios`.)

## Scene Description

- **Wire Grid & Stake (Foreground)**:
  - The foreground is crossed by the rectangular grid of a galvanized metal wire fence/trellis and a vertical green-coated metal garden stake running down the left-center of the frame.
- **Soil & Plants (Midground)**:
  - The bed consists of damp, clumpy brown soil bordered on the left and upper right by edges of black plastic sheeting.
  - **Onions (Center)**: Three round, bright white onion bulbs sit partially exposed at the soil surface near the center of the frame, with their long, tubular green stalks bent over and yellowing as they finish maturing. A fourth smaller white bulb is visible further back toward the upper left.
  - **Bean Seedlings (Lower Left & Lower Right)**: Two small, freshly sprouted bean plants with pairs of bright green primary leaves stand upright in the foreground soil—one just to the left of the green stake at the bottom left, and the other near the bottom right corner.
  - **Background Foliage (Top)**: In the upper left and top center behind the onions, dense green leafy herbs/carrots and thin, yellowing stems sprawl across the soil alongside a white plastic plant tag.
