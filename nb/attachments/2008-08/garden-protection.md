# Backyard Garden with Plastic Bottle Cloches Protecting Seedlings (`garden-protection.jpg`)

## Overview

A low-angle photograph of Kurt Schwehr's backyard vegetable garden showing cut-off clear plastic bottles placed over young pea and bean seedlings to protect them from pests, along with newly planted cabbage starts in the foreground. Posted on August 12, 2008 under the title "Gardening - protection for seedlings."

## Scene Description

- **Foreground Row (Cabbages)**:
  - Along the bottom edge of the frame, five small, newly transplanted leafy seedlings—three light green cabbages and two dark purple/red cabbages—are planted in a row in dark, wet soil just in front of a strip of black plastic mulch sheeting.
- **Midground Rows (Onions & Plastic Bottle Protectors)**:
  - In the middle row behind the black plastic strip, green onion stalks and a bushy clump of feathery green foliage grow in the dark soil.
  - Scattered along the middle and back rows are at least six improvised mini-cloches made from cut-off clear/translucent plastic jugs and one green plastic 2-liter soda bottle placed upside-down over emerging seedlings to keep animals from eating them.
- **Trellis, Fence, & House (Background)**:
  - Rising above the middle of the garden is a tall, curved wire mesh arch trellis and several vertical green stakes with green bean vines climbing toward the top. Two pink ribbon flags are tied to the upper left side of the wire arch.
  - Behind the garden is a white wooden picket fence, and directly behind the fence stands a single-story sage-green clapboard house with two white-trimmed windows and a shingled hip roof against an overcast white sky.
