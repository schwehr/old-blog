# Backyard Vegetable Garden After Heavy Rain (`garden.jpg`)

## Overview

A photograph of Kurt Schwehr's fenced backyard vegetable garden following two inches of rain over two days, posted on August 9, 2008 in the blog entry "Rain and gardening."

## Scene Description

- **Garden Bed & Plants (Foreground & Midground)**:
  - The rectangular garden plot contains dark brown, wet soil divided into rows by strips of black plastic landscape sheeting weighted down at the corners with red bricks.
  - Standing muddy rainwater pools in shallow puddles along the black plastic troughs, especially across the lower-middle strip.
  - In the foreground row, small green onion shoots stand in a line across the damp soil.
  - In the middle rows, several circular wire tomato cages support young vegetable plants, basil, squash/cucumber vines with broad green leaves and a yellow blossom on the right, and wispy green herbs/foliage on the left. White plastic plant labels stick out of the soil near several plants.
  - Spanning the back half of the garden bed is a tall, arched metal wire trellis supported by vertical green garden stakes; climbing bean or pea vines wind partway up the green stakes along the back row.
- **Fence & House (Background)**:
  - A white wooden picket fence encloses the left and back sides of the garden plot.
  - Beyond the picket fence stands the exterior wall of a house painted sage/olive green with horizontal clapboard siding and two white-trimmed sash windows. Dark green trees and bushes are visible to the left of the house.
