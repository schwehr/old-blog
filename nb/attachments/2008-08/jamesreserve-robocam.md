# James Reserve Robocam Web Interface (`jamesreserve-robocam.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the interactive "Robocam" pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) webcam control interface at the James San Jacinto Mountains Reserve (`jamesreserve.edu`), displaying a live camera feed of tree branches alongside a full-width panoramic overview image at the bottom.

## Interface & Controls
- **Camera Selector**: At the top left, a label `Camera :` precedes a disabled/grayed-out dropdown menu displaying `Camera` with up/down selector arrows.
- **Main Live Viewport**: A square video feed on the upper left shows a close-up view looking up into dense, sunlit conifer branches and needles against a bright sky.
  - **Tilt Scrollbar**: Along the right edge of the main video feed is a vertical slider bar with a blue thumb positioned at the very top and up/down arrow buttons at the bottom.
  - **Pan Scrollbar**: Along the bottom edge of the main video feed is a horizontal slider bar with a blue thumb near the center and left/right arrow buttons on the right.
- **Zoom & Control Buttons (Right Panel)**:
  - A far-right vertical slider bar (with a blue thumb at the top) controls camera zoom, flanked by a single-tree icon (`[tree]`, zoom in / telephoto) at the top and a three-trees icon (`[three trees]`, zoom out / wide angle) at the bottom.
  - Below the tree icons is a square button showing a sun/brightness control icon.
  - Below a horizontal divider line is a large button with an illustration of a motorized webcam emitting red signal waves and the label `Start Control`.
- **Snapshot Button**: Below the bottom-left corner of the main video feed is a small square button with a blue camera icon.
- **Panoramic Reference View (Bottom)**: A wide rectangular panoramic photograph shows a sunlit forest clearing and dirt slope surrounded by pine/conifer trees, framed by white curved camera-housing structures on the far left and right edges. A black rectangular bounding box with a small blue crosshair indicator at its top edge outlines the field of view currently available to the camera.

## Visible Text & Labels
- **Top Dropdown**: `Camera :` | `Camera`
- **Control Button**: `Start Control`
