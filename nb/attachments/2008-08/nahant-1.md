# Nahant Rocky Shoreline and Beachfront Houses (`nahant-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A coastal photograph taken near Tides restaurant in Nahant, Massachusetts, showing a row of tightly packed New England beachfront houses lining a concrete seawall above a rocky, boulder-strewn tidal shoreline.

## Scene Description
- **Shoreline & Water**: On the left, calm greenish-gray ocean water laps gently against a wet sandy and rocky beach at low tide. Numerous rounded tan and gray boulders and patches of green seaweed are scattered across the foreground and middle ground.
- **People on the Beach**: Near the center foreground, a shirtless man in dark swim trunks stands on the wet sand near the water's edge, while a couple of small children in bright pink/red clothing play among the rocks slightly to the right.
- **Seawall & Waterfront Homes**: A weathered concrete and stone retaining wall rises behind the rocks, supporting a dense row of multi-story coastal houses painted in white, cream, pale gray, and reddish-brown. Several homes feature wooden decks, porches, and chain-link or wooden fences overlooking the water, with an American flag flying from a pole near the right-center house.
- **Sky & Background**: Dense green summer trees rise behind the houses on the right, under an overcast sky filled with soft white and gray cloud cover with a hint of blue sky on the left and a single seagull flying high in the upper right corner.
