# Historic White Mansion and Hillside Gardens in Nahant (`nahant-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a grand, historic white clapboard coastal estate with a red roof in Nahant, Massachusetts, viewed from below across a lush, flower- and fern-covered hillside.

## Scene Description
- **Hillside Flora (Foreground)**: The foreground is filled with vibrant green summer vegetation rising above a low stone retaining wall of large granite blocks at the bottom right. Masses of bright green ferns and bushes with small yellow wildflowers cover the slope, accented by a bright purple/blue hydrangea bush on the left lawn.
- **Main Residence**: Set atop the hill is a large two-and-a-half-story white clapboard mansion featuring dark green window shutters, a red shingled roof with dormer windows and a red brick chimney, a projecting bay window with a red roof overhang, and a sun porch on the left wing.
- **Carriage House / Cupola Structure**: To the left of the main house stands a smaller matching white building with dark shutters and a red hipped roof topped by a square white cupola with a steep pointed red roof.
- **Trees & Sky**: Tall, mature pine and deciduous trees frame the estate and partially shade the front facade against a bright, overcast white sky.
