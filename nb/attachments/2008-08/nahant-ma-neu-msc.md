# Northeastern University Marine Science Center Entrance, Nahant (`nahant-ma-neu-msc.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of the entrance driveway, lawn, flagpole, and earth-covered WWII coastal defense bunker at Northeastern University's Marine Science Center (MSC) at East Point in Nahant, Massachusetts.

## Scene Description
- **Driveway & Grounds**: A paved asphalt driveway curves gently from the bottom left into the middle distance, bordered by neatly mowed bright green lawns on both sides and a small shrub bed in the center.
- **Sign & Flagpole**: On the right lawn stands a rectangular red sign mounted on two dark brown posts, displaying white text and the Northeastern University logo. To the right of the sign stands a tall white flagpole flying an American flag that hangs limply in calm air.
- **Bunker & Forested Hill**: Ahead at the end of the driveway, nestled into a dense hillside of dark green deciduous trees, is the gray concrete facade and large entrance doors of a historic WWII artillery bunker (Battery Murphy), with a pair of blue sawhorses on the grass to the left and a small yellow/tan enclosure right outside the bunker door.
