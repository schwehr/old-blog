# Lamprey River Dam and Waterfall in Newmarket, NH (`newmarket-waterfall.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical photograph of tannin-stained brown water rushing and foaming over the historic concrete spillway and stone dam on the Lamprey River in Newmarket, New Hampshire, following heavy summer rains.

## Scene Description
- **Waterfall & Rapids**: In the foreground and left-center, a heavy volume of amber/golden-brown river water plunges vertically over the left spillway lip and shoots out in two turbulent chutes from the left concrete bay, churning into a thick, frothy white foam pool at the bottom of the frame.
- **Concrete Dam Structure**: Behind the falls is a weathered gray concrete dam structure with three rectangular bays/openings (one discharging water on the left, two dark shadowed openings on the right) and stone block masonry on the left pier and right retaining wall. The year `1925` is cast into the concrete lintel above the right bays.
- **Sluice Gate Platform & Mill Building**: Atop the concrete deck, enclosed by a black metal picket fence, sit mechanical sluice gate hoists with tall vertical threaded steel stems and a small white shed box. In the background rises a multi-story historic red-brick New England mill building with arched window tops, flanked by green trees on the left.

## Visible Text & Labels
- **Concrete Inscription (upper right bay lintel)**: `1925`
