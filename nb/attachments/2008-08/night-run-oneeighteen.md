# Night Run on the Houston Ship Channel (`night-run-oneeighteen.png`)

## Overview
A still frame from a nighttime time-lapse video (by Flickr user `oneeighteen`) taken from the bridge wing or bow of a commercial vessel transiting the brightly lit industrial corridor of the Houston Ship Channel at night.

## Scene Description
- **Vessel Bow (Foreground)**: The dark, silhouetted bow and forward mast/kingpost of the ship occupy the bottom center of the frame, pointing straight ahead down the center of the waterway.
- **Industrial Waterfront & Lights**: Both banks of the channel are lined with heavily illuminated refineries, chemical plants, storage tanks, docks, cranes, and moored cargo ships/barges.
  - On the left bank, intense golden-yellow and white lights illuminate large industrial structures and docked bulk carriers with deck cranes, while tall smokestacks billow thick white plumes of steam and smoke into the night sky.
  - On the right bank, bright sodium-vapor orange and crisp white floodlights illuminate a long waterfront warehouse, tall shipyard/port cranes, and a brightly lit tugboat or workboat near the lower right dock.
- **Water Reflections**: The dark surface of the ship channel reflects vivid vertical streaks of gold, orange, white, and pale green light from the surrounding shore facilities.
