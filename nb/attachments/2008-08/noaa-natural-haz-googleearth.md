# NOAA NGDC Natural Hazards Database in Google Earth (`noaa-natural-haz-googleearth.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot displaying the NOAA National Geophysical Data Center (NGDC) Historical Natural Hazards Database KML layer along the northern California and southern Oregon coast, with legends on the left and an open "Tsunami Runup" info balloon for Humboldt Bay, California.

## Map & Legend Details
- **Base Map & Markers**: Satellite imagery shows the Pacific Ocean on the left (dark blue) and the forested coastline of northern California and southern Oregon on the right, including U.S. Highway 101 (`101` shield at the bottom, `199` shield partially visible above the popup) and the California-Oregon state border line at the top. Pink cross icons (`+`) mark tsunami runup locations along the coast, green squares mark tsunami events, and green circles mark earthquake epicenters.
- **Left Legend Overlay**: Four semi-transparent dark gray rounded boxes explain the map symbology:
  1. **Significant Volcanic Eruptions** (*Number of Fatalities Caused by Eruption:*):
     - Green triangle: `None or unknown`
     - Blue triangle: `Few (~1 to 50)`
     - Yellow triangle: `Some (~50 to 100)`
     - Orange triangle: `Many (~100 to 1000)`
     - Red triangle: `Very many (>1000)`
  2. **Significant Earthquakes** (*Number of Fatalities Caused by Earthquake:*):
     - Green circle: `None or unknown`
     - Blue circle: `Few (~1 to 50)`
     - Yellow circle: `Some (~50 to 100)`
     - Orange circle: `Many (~100 to 1000)`
     - Red cross/circle: `Very many (>1000)`
  3. **Tsunami Events** (*Number of Fatalities Caused by Tsunami:*):
     - Green square: `None or unknown`
     - Blue square: `Few (~1 to 50)`
     - Yellow square: `Some (~50 to 100)`
     - Orange square: `Many (~100 to 1000)`
     - Red square: `Very many (>1000)`
  4. **Tsunami Runups** (*Type of Measurement:*):
     - Bright pink cross (`+`): `Water height observation`
     - Dark purple cross (`+`): `Tide gauge`
     - Light purple cross (`+`): `Deep-ocean pressure gauge`
