# Mars Phoenix Lander Wet Chemistry Lab (WCL) 3D CAD Model (`phx-wcl-wet-chem-lab.jpg`)

## Overview
A translucent 3D computer-aided design (CAD) rendering (by Mike Stetson) of one of the four Wet Chemistry Lab (WCL) cells on NASA's Mars Phoenix Lander, set against a solid black background and color-coded to highlight internal mechanical, thermal, and electroanalytical components.

## Model & Component Details
- **Upper Assembly & Soil Funnel (Blue & Green)**:
  - On the upper left, a wide, angled semi-transparent blue soil-receiving funnel/chute with a slotted dark grate across its top opening leads into a bright green cylindrical sliding drawer/actuator assembly on the left side.
  - Above the cylinder is a semi-transparent blue rectangular housing containing a reagent/leaching reservoir and a long horizontal cylindrical actuator tube extending to the upper right with visible internal orange helical springs.
- **Central Heater & Actuator Chamber (Orange/Yellow & Green)**:
  - The central cylindrical section glows bright green and yellow-orange, showing serpentine orange etched-foil heater traces wrapping around the side and bottom of the actuator block, secured with hex-socket bolts and a black bellows seal on the right.
- **Lower Beaker Assembly (Purple/Violet)**:
  - Beneath the upper mechanism sits a rectangular lower containment box rendered in semi-transparent blue and outlined in bright purple/magenta.
  - On the front-left face of the purple beaker cell, four small circular sensor/electrode ports with yellow-green rims protrude outward in a 2x2 grid, with additional faint circular sensor ports visible inside the right wall.
