# Sunrise Over Scituate Harbor and Lighthouse (`scituate-sunrise.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect coastal photograph capturing a vibrant orange and golden-yellow sunrise over Scituate Harbor, Massachusetts, taken from near the NOAA Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary dock.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Sunrise Glow**: The sky transitions from a bright, pale yellow-white glow on the right (where the sun is about to rise behind the buildings) through rich golden-amber and deep fiery orange toward the left horizon. A lone bird is visible as a tiny silhouette high in the right-center sky.
- **Harbor Entrance & Lighthouse**: Along the distant horizon on the far left stands the distinct vertical silhouette of Old Scituate Light atop Cedar Point, with a long dark breakwater jetty extending across the harbor mouth toward the center.
- **Water & Shoreline**: Calm harbor waters on the left and center reflect the warm orange and gold hues of the dawn sky, lapping gently against a dark, silhouetted rocky and pebble-strewn shoreline curving through the foreground.
- **Buildings (Right)**: On the right side of the frame, waterfront houses, a utility pole, and a peaked-roof building with a square rooftop cupola or tower are cast in solid black silhouette against the bright morning sky.
