# Virginia Tech AUV Underwater at San Diego AUVSI Competition (`sd-auv-competition-1.png`)

## Overview
An underwater video still from the 2007 AUVSI/ONR Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) Competition at the SPAWAR TRANSDEC pool facility on Point Loma in San Diego, showing the Virginia Tech AUV hovering in green water.

## Scene Description
- **Autonomous Underwater Vehicle**: Viewed from the front/quarter angle in bright green pool water, the compact AUV consists of an aluminum frame supporting multiple clear acrylic cylindrical pressure housings and shrouded thrusters:
  - **Vertical Acrylic Cylinders (Foreground)**: Three clear vertical cylinders sit at the front of the vehicle, connected at the top by black waterproof cables and hoses. The right cylinder features an orange-and-maroon turkey mascot decal (the Virginia Tech HokieBird), and the left cylinder has a small white-and-red label. Below the central cylinder sits a wider circular clear acrylic dome/housing with a light-blue band reading `VIRGINIA TECH`.
  - **Main Hull & Thrusters**: Behind the vertical cylinders is a larger horizontal cylindrical hull capped with a circular metallic plate bearing a white `VirginiaTech` logo sticker. Two large, shrouded horizontal thrusters with black propeller blades extend outward on arms to the left and right.
- **Pool Environment**: The surrounding water of the test tank is a vivid emerald green, illuminated from above by sunlight creating a bright light patch on the pool floor in the lower right and a faint orange buoy blurred in the background on the right.
