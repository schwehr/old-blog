# Diver Tending Yellow Pelican-Case AUV Underwater (`sd-auv-competition-2.png`)

## Overview
An underwater video still from the 2007 AUVSI Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) Competition in San Diego, showing a scuba diver in a black wetsuit tending a compact AUV built around a bright yellow waterproof Pelican case.

## Scene Description
- **Autonomous Underwater Vehicle**: Centered in the murky greenish-gray water is a small custom AUV:
  - **Main Housing**: A bright yellow rectangular waterproof hard case (Pelican case) serves as the primary electronics and buoyancy enclosure at the top center of the frame.
  - **Frame & Thrusters**: Beneath the yellow case is a horizontal black and silver T-slot aluminum frame bar. Mounted at the left and right ends of the crossbar are two forward-facing thrusters with red motor housings and light-blue circular nozzle shrouds. A third red thruster is mounted vertically beneath the center of the frame, below a clear cylindrical dome/sensor housing.
  - **Surface Tether / Recovery Float**: A white vertical mast or line rises from the top of the yellow case toward the shimmering water surface at the top edge of the image.
- **Divers**:
  - On the left, a diver wearing a black wetsuit, hood, clear diving mask, and snorkel/regulator in their mouth reaches their bare left hand just below the AUV's left blue thruster shroud.
  - On the right, a second diver in a dark wetsuit is partially visible in the background behind the right thruster.
