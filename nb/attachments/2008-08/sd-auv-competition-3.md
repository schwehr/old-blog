# Upward Underwater View of AUV Against Sunlit Surface (`sd-auv-competition-3.png`)

## Overview
An upward-looking underwater video still from the 2007 AUVSI Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) Competition at the SPAWAR pool in San Diego, showing the silhouette of a compact AUV hovering near the water surface against a dazzling burst of sunlight.

## Scene Description
- **Upward Angle & Sunburst**: Shot from near the bottom of the greenish-blue test pool looking straight up toward the surface, a blindingly bright circular white flare of sunlight refracts through the water to the left of the vehicle, surrounded by small sparkling air bubbles and surface ripples.
- **AUV Silhouette**: Adjacent to the sunburst at upper center is the underside and dark silhouette of a boxy, open-frame AUV equipped with black cables, cylindrical housings, side-mounted thrusters, and a circular thruster or sensor ring at its upper right corner, creating a splash of white bubbles at the surface.
- **Foreground Detail**: A curved gray cable or pool structure line crosses the upper-right corner of the frame.
