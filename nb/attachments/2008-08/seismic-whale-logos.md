# Sperm Whale Seismic Study Banner (`seismic-whale-logos.jpg`)

## Overview
A promotional graphic and title banner for the Sperm Whale Seismic Study (SWSS) in the Gulf of Mexico, combining a marine photograph by Jonathan Gordon with project title typography and the logos of participating government, academic, and industry funding organizations.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Whales)**: Two sperm whales are diving in choppy blue ocean water, with their dark gray tail flukes raised high above the surface. White water cascades down from the trailing edges of both flukes as they submerge.
- **Midground (Research Vessel)**: In the background to the left, a large dark-hulled oceanographic research vessel with a white superstructure, dual masts, and an aft A-frame crane is seen in profile on the horizon under a pale overcast sky.
- **Border**: The entire image is framed by a thin gray border.

## Visible Text & Logos
- **Main Title (Top)**:
  - **`SWSS`** on the left in large, bold, dark slate-blue serif letters, with stylized whale-fluke serifs on the ends of the "S" characters.
  - **`SPERM WHALE SEISMIC STUDY`** stacked on two lines to the right in dark gray serif capital letters.
- **Sponsor Logos (Middle Right)**:
  - **Industry Research Funding Coalition**: A rectangular blue box containing bold white sans-serif text reading `INDUSTRY RESEARCH FUNDING COALITION`.
  - **Office of Naval Research (ONR)**: Below left of the blue banner, a white rectangular logo featuring the Department of the Navy emblem, a gold `ONR` banner, the Marine Corps emblem, and text reading `Science & Technology`.
  - **National Science Foundation (NSF)**: To the right of the ONR logo, the circular blue-and-gold sunburst logo with `NSF` in the center.
  - **Minerals Management Service (MMS)**: To the far right, a large circular seal reading `U.S. DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR` along the top arc and `MINERALS MANAGEMENT SERVICE` along the bottom arc, surrounding an eagle crest with a shield and a banner reading `MINERAL REVENUES • OFFSHORE MINERALS • STEWARDSHIP`.
- **Footer Text & Credits (Bottom)**:
  - Bottom left: A circular white-and-maroon seal for the Texas A&M Research Foundation next to white sans-serif text reading `Managed by Texas A&M Research Foundation`.
  - Bottom right: White sans-serif credit text stacked on two lines reading `Jonathan Gordon` and `Photo`.
