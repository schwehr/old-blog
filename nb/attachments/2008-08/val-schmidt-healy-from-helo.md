# Aerial View of USCGC Healy from a Helicopter (`val-schmidt-healy-from-helo.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken by Val Schmidt from a helicopter during transit to the U.S. Coast Guard Cutter *Healy* (WAGB-20), showing the polar icebreaker underway in open Arctic water.

## Scene Description
- **Vessel (*USCGC Healy*)**: The icebreaker is viewed in starboard profile, positioned slightly above the center of the frame and heading to the right (eastward in the frame).
  - The hull is painted Coast Guard red with the signature diagonal white racing stripe near the bow and white `U.S. COAST GUARD` lettering along the midships hull.
  - Above the red hull is a large white forward superstructure and bridge, topped by a dark mast/aloft conning station.
  - Aft of the main superstructure is an orange-tan exhaust stack with a black top, crane structures, and a flat flight deck at the stern.
- **Water & Lighting**: The ship is surrounded by a vast expanse of dark slate-blue ocean water with light surface ripples and no visible sea ice. The lighting is diffuse and slightly hazy, typical of overcast Arctic conditions.
