# Bumblebee Inside a Squash Blossom (`bee.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up macro photograph looking directly into the bright yellow-orange interior of a zucchini or squash blossom in a garden, where a pollen-dusted bee is foraging at the base of the flower's central stamen.

## Scene Description
- **Flower Interior**: The frame is filled almost entirely by the vibrant golden-yellow petal wall of the squash flower, showing delicate radiating veins, ridges, and fine translucent plant hairs (trichomes) leading toward the slightly greenish-yellow throat of the blossom at the upper right.
- **Stamen & Pollen**: Protruding from the circular center of the flower at upper right is a knobby, bright yellow-orange anther/stamen covered in coarse grains of yellow pollen, with loose clusters of pollen grains scattered along the petal surface just below it.
- **Bee**: Curled around the right and bottom side of the central stamen is a fuzzy bumblebee with a black-and-yellow striped abdomen, dark wings, and pollen-coated legs and thorax as it gathers pollen deep inside the blossom.
