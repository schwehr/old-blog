# Cape Cod AIS Base Station Equipment Rack (`cape-cod-ais-station.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph of a newly installed Automatic Identification System (AIS) base station and computer rack inside a utility room at Cape Cod National Seashore, set up by a joint team from the National Park Service, U.S. Coast Guard, NOAA, and the University of New Hampshire (CCOM).

## Equipment & Rack Layout
- **Rack Frame**: A black open-frame 19-inch equipment rack sit on a wooden workbench against a painted cinder-block wall, with white bold lettering across the top crossbar reading `BLACK BOX`.
- **Top Shelf (Monitor & Network/Controller Unit)**:
  - Mounted on the left is a small black LCD flat-panel monitor displaying a blue desktop screen with two desktop icons.
  - On the right side of the top shelf is a 1U black electronic unit with a glowing blue LCD status screen and a circular blue five-button navigation pad on its right faceplate.
- **Second Shelf (AIS Transceiver & Minimum Keyboard Display)**:
  - On the left sits a compact gray metal box (AIS transceiver/modem unit) with a glowing green status LED and a front serial port.
  - On the right is a gimbal-mounted black C.N.S. Systems Minimum Keyboard Display (MKD) unit featuring a green monochrome LCD screen above a 4x4 white membrane keypad.
- **Third Shelf (Transponder / Server Unit)**:
  - A silver/gray rack-mounted chassis with two thick gray serial/data cables connected to its front ports and a spiral-bound manual resting on top.
- **Lower Rack Units (Power Management, DC Power Supply, Input Devices & UPS)**:
  - Below the third shelf is a 1U black rackmount power bar labeled `BLACK BOX` on the left and `Rackmount Remote Power Manager` on the right.
  - Below that, mounted to a silver cross-rail, is a silver industrial DC power supply unit with a red `MW` (Mean Well) logo and model label `DRP-240-24`, with green terminal blocks wired at the bottom.
  - Resting on the shelf above the base is a compact black keyboard on the left and a black optical mouse on the right.
  - At the very bottom of the rack is a black APC uninterruptible power supply (UPS) with illuminated green status LEDs and a red `APC` logo at bottom center.
- **Surrounding Environment**: Behind the rack on the left wall is a white metal electrical enclosure with indicator lights; to the right is a tall white cylindrical antenna/filter tube and the edge of a second black equipment rack.
