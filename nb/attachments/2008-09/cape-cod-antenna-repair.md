# Antenna Repair on the Roof at Cape Cod (`cape-cod-antenna-repair.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor telephoto photograph looking up at the multi-tiered shingled roof and octagonal cupola of a Cape Cod National Seashore building (Province Lands Visitor Center) against a clear blue sky, where a technician is perched at the peak working on the station's vertical VHF/AIS antenna mast.

A person wearing a light-colored shirt, dark pants, and a climbing harness is kneeling/crouching atop the narrow flat cap of the cupola at the base of a tall, thin vertical whip antenna mast with horizontal radials. A smaller weather sensor or secondary antenna is mounted on the right corner of the cupola, and a green safety rope trails down the shingled slope.

- **Foreground Foliage**: Lush green pine and coastal shrub branches frame the lower left and bottom right corners of the image.
