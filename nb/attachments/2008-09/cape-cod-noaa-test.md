# ERMA Map Screenshot of Cape Cod NOAA Test AIS Transponder (`cape-cod-noaa-test.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from the ERMA (Environmental Response Management Application) web mapping interface showing Massachusetts Bay and Cape Cod Bay, displaying real-time AIS vessel targets and the newly activated `NOAA TEST` transponder at the tip of Cape Cod.
