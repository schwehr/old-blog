# Tall Trees in College Woods at the University of New Hampshire (`college-woods.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical low-angle outdoor photograph looking upward through a dense stand of tall deciduous and coniferous trees in College Woods at the University of New Hampshire (UNH) in Durham, New Hampshire, at the beginning of autumn.

## Scene Description
- **Tree Trunks & Canopy**: Slender, dark vertical tree trunks rise straight from the shadowed forest floor at the bottom of the frame toward the high canopy. Both broadleaf deciduous trees with vibrant green foliage and tall straight pine trunks in the background fill the mid-level and upper canopy.
- **Lighting & Sky**: Bright, diffused white sunlight shines through gaps in the upper tree crowns near the top left and top center, illuminating the bright green leaves from behind and creating a lush, layered woodland scene before full autumn foliage colors set in.
