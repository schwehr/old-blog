# Cosco Busan Marine Radar Display Moments Before Allision (`cosco-busan-radar.png`)

## Overview

This image is a screenshot from the marine radar display aboard the container ship *Cosco Busan* captured moments before its November 7, 2007 allision with the Delta-Echo tower of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge. Featured in a September 19, 2008 blog post referencing NTSB docket transcripts and a gCaptain analysis (*"Pilotage Paradox"* by Paul Drouin), the display shows the vessel approaching the bridge span in San Francisco Bay at 16:29:44 UTC (08:29:44 PST).

## Radar Plan Position Indicator (PPI) Display

- **Center & Compass Rose**: A large circular radar sweep occupies the left and center of the dark screen, bordered by a green degree scale marked every 10° from `000` (North at top, `N UP` orientation) clockwise through `350`.
- **Own Ship & Vectors**:
  - Near the center of the radar circle, the ship's position is marked alongside a white dashed Variable Range Marker (`VRM 1`) circle with a radius of `0.33 NM`.
  - A solid green heading line extends from the vessel's position toward `334.8°`.
  - A white dashed Electronic Bearing Line (`EBL 1`) extends northwest toward `310.2° T`.
  - A green course-over-ground (`COG`) vector with a double-arrow tip points west-northwest (`294°`), heading directly toward a yellow radar return representing a bridge pier along the Bay Bridge line.
- **Radar Returns (Yellow)**:
  - Prominent bright yellow radar echoes represent surrounding land, piers, and bridge structures:
    - To the southwest and west (bearings `190°` to `290°`), dense yellow returns show the San Francisco waterfront and piers.
    - Extending from the southwest shore toward Treasure Island / Yerba Buena Island (located north-northwest around `345°`–`005°`), a line of discrete yellow dots marks the towers and spans of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge, including the bright RACON sweep extending northeast from the center of the Delta-Echo span right at the vessel's bow.
    - Additional yellow islands/structures appear northeast of the ship (`000°`–`040°`) and along the eastern edge (`060°`–`085°`, Oakland waterfront).
