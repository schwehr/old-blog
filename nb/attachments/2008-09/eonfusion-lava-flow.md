# Eonfusion 3D Lava Flow Modelling Visualization (`eonfusion-lava-flow.png`)

## Overview

This promotional graphic from Myriax's Eonfusion software illustrates a 3D geospatial and temporal simulation of a volcanic lava flow over a triangulated terrain mesh. It was featured in a September 9, 2008 blog post introducing Eonfusion (`http://eonfusion.myriax.com/`).

## Visualization & Graphic Details

- **3D Volcanic Crater & Terrain Mesh**:
  - Set against a pure black background, a steep volcanic cone and breached crater are rendered with photorealistic dark gray rocky texture overlaid by a semi-transparent white wireframe triangulated irregular network (TIN) mesh with small circular vertex nodes.
  - Inside the crater rim, a central raised mound (dome) is also outlined by the white triangular wireframe mesh.
- **Simulated Lava Flow**:
  - A semi-transparent deep red polygon fills the interior of the crater around the central dome and pours outward and downward toward the lower-left foreground through a breach in the crater wall.
  - A brighter red flow path with a large directional arrowhead at its leading edge traces the main channel of lava descending the flank of the volcano.
- **Visible Callout Labels**:
  - Upper left: White sans-serif text reading `lava flow modelling` above a horizontal white leader line that terminates in a small open circle inside the red lava pool within the crater.
  - Lower left: White sans-serif text reading `effusion rate:0.50m³/sec` above a horizontal white leader line that terminates in a small open circle right at the tip of the red flow arrow.
