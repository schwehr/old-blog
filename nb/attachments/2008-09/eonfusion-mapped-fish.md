# Eonfusion 3D Bathymetry and Acoustic Fish Survey Visualization (`eonfusion-mapped-fish.png`)

## Overview

This image is a 3D marine geospatial visualization created with Myriax's Eonfusion software, showing acoustic fish biomass or abundance data along survey transects and cruise tracks overlaid on coastal bathymetry and topography. It was included in the September 9, 2008 blog post on Eonfusion.

## Scene & Data Layers

- **Coastal Topography & Bathymetry**:
  - In the upper-left and left foreground, an indented, fjord-like coastline and islands are rendered in dark olive green.
  - Offshore, a 3D shaded-relief bathymetric surface transitions from light cyan in shallow coastal waters to deeper royal blue over a submarine shelf and slope, dropping off into black background space on the far right.
  - A faint gray coordinate grid with numeric coordinate labels (such as `6100000` and `500000`) is draped across the land and seafloor.
- **Survey Transects & Fish Abundance Bars**:
  - Two sets of parallel yellow straight line transects cross the continental shelf—one series of eight parallel lines in the lower-right foreground oriented northeast–southwest, and another series of short parallel lines in the upper center.
  - Winding across the central nearshore area is a thick, glowing bright-green vessel trackline forming a connected loop with several straight cross-segments.
  - Extending vertically upward in 3D from both the green trackline and several of the yellow transect lines are dark red/maroon 3D bar histograms (curtain/column plots), including four tall vertical spikes in the upper-center region that represent localized high concentrations of mapped fish or acoustic backscatter.
