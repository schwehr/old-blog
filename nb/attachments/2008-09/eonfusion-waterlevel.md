# Eonfusion Coastal Sea Level Rise Projection Overlay (`eonfusion-waterlevel.png`)

## Overview

This promotional image from Myriax's Eonfusion website blends an oblique aerial coastal photograph of steep, rugged mountain cliffs with a 3D gridded sea-level rise projection polygon. In his September 9, 2008 blog post, Kurt Schwehr commented on the visual blending between the photograph and the modeled sea-level change.

## Scene & Graphic Details

- **Coastal Landscape & Ocean**:
  - On the right side of the frame, dramatic, serrated coastal mountain ridges (resembling the Na Pali Coast of Kauai) recede into atmospheric haze from dark silhouetted foreground slopes to sunlit pale blue-gray peaks in the distance.
  - On the left, the blue ocean surface stretches to the horizon, with white surf breaking along the rocky shoreline and a dark vignette shading the upper-left corner.
  - A perspective white wireframe grid is superimposed across the water surface, with a diagonal white line angling toward the shoreline.
- **Projected Sea Level Rise Overlay**:
  - A semi-transparent dark red/maroon polygonal band with white vertex dots along its perimeter hugs the coastline, extending inland over the low-lying coastal strip and beaches to represent the inundation zone.
- **Visible Callout Labels**:
  - Upper label: White sans-serif text reading `10 year projected sea level rise` above a horizontal white leader line ending in a small open circle on the inner (landward) boundary of the red inundation zone.
  - Lower label: White sans-serif text reading `current sea level` above a horizontal white leader line ending in a small open circle on the outer (seaward) shoreline edge.
