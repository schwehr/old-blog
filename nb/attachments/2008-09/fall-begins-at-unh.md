# Early Autumn Foliage at the UNH Reservoir Dam (`fall-begins-at-unh.jpg`)

## Overview

This outdoor photograph captures the first bright orange autumn leaves appearing among green trees beside the reservoir dam and brick gatehouse near College Woods at the University of New Hampshire (UNH). It was posted on September 29, 2008 in the blog entry *"Fall begins at UNH"* with the caption `"The fall colors are just about here"`.

## Scene Description

- **Trees & Autumn Foliage**:
  - Dominating the center and right of the frame is a tall cluster of deciduous trees set against an overcast white sky.
  - While the surrounding taller canopy remains lush summer green, a prominent maple tree in the center-left foreground displays vibrant golden-orange and amber foliage.
- **Reservoir Dam & Gatehouse (Lower Left)**:
  - At the base of the trees on the left stands a small, square red-brick utility building (gatehouse) topped with a hipped roof and a white-framed window facing left.
  - Below the brick building, a concrete spillway wall slopes down alongside a cascading waterfall where white, frothy water spills over the dam crest into the stream bed below.
  - A chain-link fence runs along the grassy bank behind the concrete wing wall.
- **Bridge & Foreground (Lower Right)**:
  - In the lower-right corner, a wooden pedestrian footbridge with vertical balusters and a flat top rail crosses over the rocky stream channel toward a grassy path on the right.
