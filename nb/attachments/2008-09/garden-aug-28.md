# Backyard Vegetable Garden with Arched Wire Trellis on August 28 (`garden-aug-28.jpg`)

## Overview

This outdoor photograph shows Kurt Schwehr's backyard vegetable garden enclosed by a white picket fence behind a green clapboard house, taken on August 28, 2008. It was published in a September 1, 2008 blog post titled *"Garden update"* as a baseline comparison alongside a photograph taken four days later (`garden-sep-01.jpg`).

## Scene Description

- **Foreground & Middle Ground Beds**:
  - In the immediate foreground, a row of six small, young leafy starts (some with dark reddish-purple leaves and others bright green) grows in brown soil in front of a strip of black landscape fabric (weed barrier).
  - Behind the first strip of landscape fabric is a middle bed of exposed brown soil with a row of slender, upright green onion or allium stalks and scattered small seedlings.
  - A second strip of black landscape fabric separates the middle bed from the rear trellis area, with a red brick resting on the fabric at the left edge next to a dark green metal fence post.
- **Arched Trellis & Climbing Plants**:
  - Spanning the back half of the garden is an arched wire mesh trellis supported by tall vertical dark green and light green stakes (some topped with wooden blocks or pink flagging tape).
  - Pole bean vines climb up the vertical stakes and wire arch on the left and center, while bushier green squash/zucchini and tomato plants supported by wire cages fill the right side of the bed.
- **Background**:
  - A white wooden picket fence encloses the left, back, and right sides of the garden plot.
  - Directly behind the back fence stands a single-story sage-green clapboard building (house or garage) with a hipped shingle roof, white trim, white downspouts, and two double-hung windows.
