# Close-Up of a Bright Yellow Squash or Zucchini Blossom in the Garden (`garden-flower.jpg`)

## Overview

This close-up overhead photograph shows a vibrant yellow squash or zucchini blossom blooming amidst broad green leaves and dark garden soil. It was posted on September 3, 2008 in the blog entry *"A flower in the garden"*, where the author noted how vigorously the plant was spreading across the garden bed.

## Scene Description

- **Blossom**:
  - Positioned in the lower-center foreground, a large, five-pointed golden-yellow flower is fully open, facing upward toward the camera.
  - Pale greenish-yellow veins radiate outward from the center of the corolla into each pointed petal lobe, surrounding a round, bright yellow central stamen/pistil cluster.
- **Leaves, Stems & Soil**:
  - Directly above the blossom is a large, palmate, ruffled dark-green squash leaf supported by thick, fuzzy light-green stems and petioles branching across the right side of the frame.
  - Coiled, wiry green tendrils curl out from the stem nodes to the left and right of the flower.
  - Just to the left of the flower, a white plastic plant identification tag with a barcode sticks out of the dark, moist, crumbly brown soil and small pebbles.
