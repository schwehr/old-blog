# Backyard Vegetable Garden Four Days Later on September 1 (`garden-sep-01.jpg`)

## Overview

This outdoor photograph shows Kurt Schwehr's backyard vegetable garden on September 1, 2008—four days after `garden-aug-28.jpg`. Featured in the September 1, 2008 blog post *"Garden update"*, it illustrates four days of rapid late-summer plant growth and the addition of rectangular plastic planter boxes along the front edge of the main bed.

## Scene Description

- **Foreground & Planter Boxes**:
  - In the immediate shadowed foreground, a row of slender green onion/allium stalks rises from dark brown soil.
  - Just behind the onions, three rectangular planter containers have been placed along the landscape fabric strip: a small black pot on the far left, a long brown plastic window-box planter in the left-center, and an orange-terracotta plastic planter box on the far right.
- **Arched Trellis & Plant Growth**:
  - Compared to four days earlier, the pole bean vines climbing the vertical stakes and the arched wire mesh trellis in the center and left have grown noticeably denser and taller, reaching above the top of the arch along the central green stakes.
  - On the right side of the garden bed, the squash/zucchini and caged plants have filled out substantially, illuminated by bright late-afternoon sunlight slanting from the right.
- **Background**:
  - The garden is bordered on three sides by the white wooden picket fence, with the sage-green clapboard house/garage and its two windows and hipped roof directly behind the back fence, and sunlit green trees on the right.
