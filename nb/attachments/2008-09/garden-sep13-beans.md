# Tall Pole Bean Vines Climbing Garden Stakes in Mid-September (`garden-sep13-beans.jpg`)

## Overview

This vertical outdoor photograph shows tall, lush green pole bean vines climbing up vertical garden stakes in Kurt Schwehr's backyard garden on September 13, 2008. It was published in the September 14, 2008 blog post *"Mid-September Garden"*, where the author noted that the two varieties of beans were producing far more beans than expected.

## Scene Description

- **Pole Bean Vines & Stakes**:
  - Rising from the garden soil to the very top of the frame are several tall, slender green garden poles completely wrapped in dense, broad-leaved green bean vines.
  - Clusters of long, slender green bean pods hang among the trifoliate leaves on the left and center vines, illuminated by bright daylight.
  - At the base of the stakes in the lower center, a patch of feathery light-green foliage (such as chamomile or carrot tops) and smaller leafy plants grow along the edge of the brown soil bed.
- **Surrounding Garden & Background**:
  - In the lower-right corner, a strip of dark gray/black landscape fabric borders the bare brown dirt path alongside the bean row.
  - Behind the bean vines on the left is the sage-green clapboard wall, white window frame, and overhanging roof eaves of the house/garage, while the white picket fence and a neighboring tan-roofed structure and trees are visible on the right.
