# GeoCoastPilot Boston Harbor Compass and Planning Chart (`geocoastpilot-boston-direction.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph taken from the helm/dashboard of an aluminum workboat in Boston Harbor during a GeoCoastPilot field survey (posted September 1, 2008). The image shows the vessel's analog magnetic compass and an annotated harbor chart resting on the console, used to record viewing direction alongside GPS-logged photographs, with an industrial waterfront facility across the water in the background.

## Foreground & Vessel Console
- **Boat Console**: Brushed aluminum dashboard and windscreen coaming running horizontally across the lower third of the frame.
- **Magnetic Compass (Left)**: A black-housed Ritchie-style marine binnacle compass mounted on the left side of the dash. The spherical dome shows white degree markings around the dial reading roughly `120` to `150` degrees (southeast).
- **Annotated Planning Chart (Center-Left)**: A printed black-and-white nautical chart of Boston Harbor clipped to a red clipboard or folder. Bright green highlighter markings trace planned vessel tracklines, critical waypoints, and Coast Pilot features across the harbor channels and islands, with `38` written in green near the upper right corner of the page.
- **Helm Hardware (Right)**: A circular white deck plate/cover and black dual lever engine throttle/shift controls mounted on the right side of the console.

## Background Harbor Scene
- **Water**: Rippled blue-gray harbor water under a clear sky. A small navigation buoy or daymark is faintly visible in the water near the right edge.
- **Industrial Waterfront**: Across the channel stands a large dark brick industrial complex (such as the Domino Sugar refinery or a power/manufacturing plant along the Boston waterfront) featuring several light-colored cylindrical silos, a tall vertical smokestack rising into the sky at center, a long pier, and a blue steel gantry crane structure on the far right.
- **Shoreline**: A low tree-lined shore extends to the left in the distance.
