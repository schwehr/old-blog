# Furuno RD-30 Marine GPS Display Check (`geocoastpilot-gps-check.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of a Furuno RD-30 navigational data organizer/repeater display mounted on a vessel's console during a Boston Harbor GeoCoastPilot survey. The photograph was taken to verify the GPS coordinates and synchronize camera clock timestamps with the ship's navigation system.

## Device Hardware & Controls
- **Enclosure**: A matte dark-gray rectangular marine instrument housing recessed into a speckled gray metal console panel.
- **Branding**: White `FURUNO` logo printed in bold uppercase letters at the top left of the bezel, and `RD-30` printed in white italics near the bottom right.
- **Buttons (Right Side)**:
  - A circular 8-way directional cursor pad at the top right with white triangles indicating cardinal directions.
  - Four small oval function buttons arranged in a 2x2 grid labeled `MENU`, `ENT`, `DISP`, and `DIM`.
  - A single oval button below them marked with an asterisk (`*`).
  - A round power button labeled `PWR` next to the `RD-30` model label at the bottom right.

## Screen & Visible Data
The monochrome LCD screen is divided into three sections by thin dark horizontal and vertical lines:
- **Top Panel (Position)**:
  - Label: `POSN` on the left.
  - Latitude: `42° 19.567' N`
  - Longitude: `70° 58.264' W` (located in Boston Harbor).
- **Bottom-Left Panel (Date & Time)**:
  - Header: `TIME`
  - Date: `20-AUG-08` (August 20, 2008)
  - Time: `10:54:48`
- **Bottom-Right Panel (Speed)**:
  - Header: `SOG (KT)` (Speed Over Ground in knots)
  - Value: `10.4` displayed in large bold digits.
