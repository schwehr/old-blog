# Hamster Sleeping Inside Green Plastic Hideout (`hamster-during-the-day.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up indoor photograph looking inside a translucent green plastic pet hideout/igloo where a golden-brown and white Syrian hamster is nestled in soft gray paper bedding during the daytime.

## Scene & Subject Description
- **Hamster**: Positioned in the lower-right center of the frame, buried up to its shoulders in fluffy shredded paper bedding. The hamster has golden-tan fur on its face and cheeks, white fur under its chin and chest, a bright pink nose, long thin white whiskers, a partially open mouth showing its lower incisors, and one small dark eye peeking open.
- **Hideout Enclosure**: The upper two-thirds and left side of the frame are dominated by the curved, ribbed interior wall of a bright emerald-green translucent plastic hamster house. A molded circular vent or knob is visible on the upper right interior roof of the dome.
- **Bedding**: The floor of the enclosure in the foreground is piled high with crumpled gray/off-white recycled paper bedding (CareFRESH style) mixed with a few bright blue pieces of paper bedding.
