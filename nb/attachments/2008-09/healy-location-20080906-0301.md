# Google Earth Map of USCGC Healy Position off Barrow, Alaska (`healy-location-20080906-0301.jpg`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot from Google Earth showing the geographic coordinates (`71 18.2'N, 156 54.0W`) of the U.S. Coast Guard Cutter *Healy* just offshore of Barrow (Utqiagvik), Alaska, on September 6, 2008, during a helicopter offload of the science party.

## Map & Geographic Features
- **Ocean (Left)**: The Chukchi Sea / Arctic Ocean occupies the left half of the view, rendered in mottled dark blue-gray bathymetric/satellite shading.
- **Coastline & Barrow (Right)**:
  - The northeast-trending Arctic coastline of Alaska cuts diagonally from the bottom center to the top right.
  - **Town of Barrow**: Street grids, buildings, and roads are visible along the coast, with a red-centered circular placemark icon labeled `Barrow` near the bottom center.
  - **Wiley Post–Will Rogers Memorial Airport**: A prominent horizontal gray runway strip extends east-west just north of the `Barrow` label, with taxiways and apron buildings on its north side.
  - **Lagoons & Tundra**: North of the town and airport is a large olive-green coastal lagoon (Imikpuk Lake / Isatkoak Lagoon) and a dark reddish-brown wetland/pond to the east, drained by a dark winding stream channel extending toward the bottom right across the brown tundra.
