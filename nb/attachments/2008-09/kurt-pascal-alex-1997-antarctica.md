# Kurt Schwehr, Pascal Lee, and Alex Foessel at Patriot Hills, Antarctica (`kurt-pascal-alex-1997-antarctica.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor field photograph taken during the 1997–1998 austral summer expedition at the Adventure Network International (ANI) basecamp near Patriot Hills, Antarctica. Three researchers—Kurt Schwehr, Pascal Lee, and Alex Foessel—pose together in heavy polar gear on the snow-covered ice sheet in front of rugged Antarctic mountains, holding expedition banners.

## People & Foreground Details
From left to right:
- **Kurt Schwehr (Left)**: Wearing dark wrap-around glacier sunglasses and a dark gray/black and slate-blue hooded polar parka. He holds the left edge of an orange cloth flag with his gloved hand.
- **Pascal Lee (Center)**: Wearing a thick dark purple/navy down parka with the insulated hood pulled up over his head, smiling with a dark beard, and resting his right arm over Kurt's shoulder while holding a wooden pole with an orange banner attached.
  - **Orange Flag**: Reads `ASTROSCAM` in hand-drawn arched lettering across the top, with smaller handwritten text below (`"We make stars the old fashioned way..."`).
- **Alex Foessel (Right)**: Smiling in a bright red fleece/jacket under black bibs/overalls, holding up a white rectangular sign with the red-and-black logo and text of `THE ROBOTICS INSTITUTE` (Carnegie Mellon University).
- **Camp Equipment**:
  - To the far left is a bright yellow rectangular storage box/chest sitting on the snow.
  - Directly behind the three men is the yellow dome of a polar tent.
  - To the right, resting upright in the snow beside the tent, is a dark-framed mountain/road bicycle with two wheels clearly visible.

## Background Landscape
- A flat expanse of white snow and ice stretches across the middle ground behind the camp.
- Rising prominently across the background under a clear blue sky is the dark, windswept rocky ridge and snow-draped slopes of the Patriot Hills (Heritage Range, Ellsworth Mountains).
