# MaxSea TimeZero 3D Nautical Chart Navigation Interface (`maxsea-timezero.jpg`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **MaxSea TimeZero** marine navigation software displaying a perspective 3D view of a raster nautical chart blended with satellite/aerial imagery around **Bahia Honda Key** in the Florida Keys, complete with a 3D sailboat icon and surrounding compass rose.

## User Interface & Toolbars
- **Top-Left Branding**: A glossy metallic bezel housing a blue oval `MAXSEA` logo in yellow-and-white capital letters, with small blue undo/redo and page icons to its right.
- **Workspace Tabs (Top Center)**: Four folder-style tabs along the top bar:
  - `Navigation` (active, highlighted in blue)
  - `Double Navigation` (grayed/inactive)
  - `Animation` (grayed/inactive)
  - `Update` (grayed/inactive)
- **Horizontal Top Toolbar**: Ten square graphical icon buttons directly below the tabs:
  1. `3D` boat perspective view icon (selected)
  2. Vessel centering/heading icon
  3. `V` icon
  4. `R` icon
  5. Satellite/chart photo-fusion layer toggle icon
  6. Single buoy/mark icon with magnifying glass
  7. Multiple buoys/marks icon with magnifying glass
  8. Depth/contour shading icon
  9. Split vertical bar indicator icon
  10. Tidal currents (yellow/blue arrows) and wind sock (red/white) icons
- **Left Vertical Tool Palette**: Eight circular buttons stacked along the middle-left edge:
  - Red boat icon, red-and-white life ring (MOB) icon, hand pan tool (highlighted in yellow), magnifying glass zoom tool, dividers/calipers measurement tool, blue waypoint pin tool, route line tool, and camera/photo tool.
- **Bottom-Left Tooltip**: A small yellow tooltip box reading `Cliquez ici pour commencer` ("Click here to start" in French).

## Chart & 3D Map View
- **Geographic Area**: Shows **Bahia Honda Key**, **Ohio Key**, **Missouri Key**, and **Little Duck Key** linked by the Overseas Highway (US 1) bridges in the Florida Keys, with `NATIONAL KEY DEER WILDLIFE REFUGE (protected area)` printed to the north (upper left).
- **Photo-Chart Fusion**: High-resolution aerial/satellite imagery of the islands, beaches, bridges, and shallow turquoise reefs is seamlessly blended into the NOAA raster nautical chart showing depth soundings (in feet, e.g., `2`, `6`, `11`, `14`, `20`, `24`, `26`, `27`, `28`), bottom characteristics (`S`, `Grs`, `rky`, `Co`), and magenta Aids to Navigation (`BAHIA HONDA Fl 2.5s 16ft 5M`, cable area lines, and compass notes).
- **Vessel & Compass Rose**:
  - A large red 3D circular compass ring with degree ticks surrounds the center of the chart.
  - Inside the lower-right quadrant of the red circle is a 3D model of a red-hulled sailboat under sail heading north-northeast, with black heading/course vector lines extending forward across the chart.
