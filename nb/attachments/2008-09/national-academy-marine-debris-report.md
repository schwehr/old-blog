# National Academy Marine Debris Report Figure 2.1 (`national-academy-marine-debris-report.png`)

## Overview
An excerpt from the prepublication copy of the National Research Council / National Academies report *"Tackling Marine Debris in the 21st Century"* (2008), showing **Figure 2.1**, a two-part composite figure illustrating shoreline and deep-sea marine debris.

## Photographs
- **Panel `(a)` (Left):** A vertical color photograph looking along a wet, sandy shoreline littered with washed-up plastic and marine trash.
  - **Foreground:** A crushed pale-yellow plastic jug with a handle, a bright blue plastic cup or container fragment, broken white seashells, and pieces of wood.
  - **Midground & Background:** A dense line of wrack and debris stretching up the beach, including a blue plastic bucket lying on its side, a black swim fin/flipper, a yellow bottle cap, red and white plastic bags and cups, and assorted plastic bottles next to gentle surf on the upper left.
- **Panel `(b)` (Right):** A horizontal underwater photograph taken at 250 meters depth on a dark, silty seafloor off the central California coast.
  - **Subject:** A derelict commercial fishing trap (pot) covered in dark mesh netting and fouled with fine sediment and marine growth. A thick, sediment-covered rope extends from the trap toward the upper right.
  - **Marine Life:** Near the center-right of the mesh frame, pinkish-red spot prawn and a blotchy red-and-white rockfish are visible swimming beside the abandoned gear.

## Visible Text & Captions
- **Header:** Centered at the top in serif font: `Prepublication Copy`.
- **Figure Caption:** Below the two panels:
  > `Figure 2.1 (a) Image of a typical trash-covered beach (used with permission from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration). (b) Spot prawn and rockfish swimming among a derelict commercial trap at 250 m depth off the central California coast (used with permission from Diana Watters, National Marine Fisheries Service, Southwest Fisheries Science Center, Fisheries Ecology Division).`
