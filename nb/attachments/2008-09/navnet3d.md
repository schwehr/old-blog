# Furuno NavNet 3D Multifunction Marine Display (`navnet3d.jpg`)

## Overview
A promotional product photograph of a **Furuno NavNet 3D** marine navigation multifunction display (MFD) unit shown at a slight three-quarter angle against a blue-and-black graphic background featuring inset chart snippets in the upper left corner.

## Screen Display (Split-Screen Interface)
The display screen is divided into three active panes with a status/header bar at the top (`742`, `12.6 NM RNG`, `295.3 °M BRG`, `1.5 kt`, `HDG 293.7 °T`):
- **Left Pane (3D Chart & Satellite Photo Overlay):** A perspective 3D view looking along a harbor entrance channel lined with breakwaters/jetties. A raster nautical chart with depth soundings (`15`, `20`, `18`, `19`, `16`) and red/green channel buoy symbols is seamlessly blended with high-resolution aerial photography of the surrounding coastline and harbor basins. A bottom data strip reads `293.3 °M COG`, `10.0 kt SOG`, `24.84 m DPT`, and `1.129 NM WPT / TTG`.
- **Top-Right Pane (Radar Display):** A green-and-yellow concentric range ring radar sweep on a black background showing strong red and orange radar returns matching the coastline and harbor structures. Blue semi-transparent slide-out menu tabs appear along the right edge (`Co...`, `Auto`, `3D`, `Track`, `Ov...`).
- **Bottom-Right Pane (Fishfinder / Echo Sounder):** A vertical sonar echogram with a dark blue water column, a suspended fish school target in yellow/orange near the center, and a undulating seafloor profile in red, orange, yellow, and cyan. A large white depth readout `25m` is at the bottom left of the pane, with depth scale ticks (`10`, `20`, `30`) and an A-scope bar on the right.

- **Left Bezel:** `FURUNO` logo printed in white at the upper left of the dark gray housing.
- **Right Control Panel:**
  - **Top Directional Pad:** Circular `CURSOR` pad with a center button and an adjacent contextual key below right.
  - **Function Keys:** Oval black buttons labeled `GO TO / LIST`, `POINTS / ROUTE`, `CANCEL`, `DATA / VOL`, `DISP`, `MENU`, `GAIN / TX`, `CTRL`, and `SAVE / MOB`.
  - **Rotary Knob & Range Rocker:** A prominent knurled rotary encoder knob labeled `PUSH TO ENTER` next to a vertical rocker switch labeled `OUT / RANGE / IN`.
  - **Bottom Directional Pad:** Circular `SCROLLING` pad with `SHIP / 3D` in the center, and a blue-labeled `BRILL` (power/brilliance) button below right.
  - **Card Slot Cover:** Lower right hinged door bearing the metallic **`NAVnet 3D`** logo badge.
