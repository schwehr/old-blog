# AIS Vessel Tracking Map of Neptune LNG Pipeline Construction (`neptune-construction.png`)

## Overview
A digital map screenshot showing Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel positions off the coast of Salem and Marblehead, Massachusetts, during the September 2008 construction and trenching of the Neptune LNG (liquefied natural gas) subsea pipeline connecting to the Algonquin HubLine.

## Map Geography & Road Network
- **Coastline & Islands (Left & Upper Center):** The irregular shoreline of Massachusetts Bay around Salem Sound and Marblehead Neck is rendered in off-white (`#F2EFE9`) against a pale gray-blue ocean background (`#C6D4D6`). Small offshore islands (such as Children's Island / Bakers Island) appear north-northeast of Marblehead.
- **Cities & Roads:**
  - **`Salem`** is labeled in black text on a white rectangular background near the upper left.
  - **`Marblehead`** is labeled in the center-left peninsula.
  - Major state routes are drawn in pink/red and yellow/orange casings, with rectangular red-bordered highway shields for **`SR 107`** (above Salem) and **`SR 1A`** (south of Salem). Local streets appear as a fine light-gray grid.

## AIS Vessel Targets (Bottom Right)
Roughly 3 miles east-southeast of Marblehead, a tight cluster of four orange triangular/wedge-shaped vessel icons (indicating heading/orientation, each with a small red dot inside) marks the offshore construction fleet working on the subsea pipeline:
- **`SMITH PREDATOR`**
- **`NORTHERN CANYON`**
- **`ATLANTIC HORIZON`** (partially overlapping with `NORTHERN CANYON`)
- **`MARTHA EUGENIA`**
