# NOAA NHC Five-Day Forecast Track for Hurricane Gustav (`noaa-gustav-track-mon-morning.gif`)

## Overview
An official National Hurricane Center (NHC) / National Weather Service (NWS) 5-day forecast cone and coastal warning map for **Hurricane Gustav** issued at **4 AM CDT Monday, September 1, 2008** (Advisory 31), just prior to Gustav's landfall along the southeastern Louisiana coast.

## Map & Forecast Track Details
- **Base Map:** Bright green landmasses with black state/international borders and state abbreviations (`UT`, `CO`, `KS`, `MO`, `IA`, `AZ`, `NM`, `OK`, `AR`, `TX`, `LA`, `MS`, `AL`, `GA`, `FL`, and `Mexico`), set against a medium-blue Gulf of Mexico and gridded with black latitude/longitude lines every 5 degrees (`25N` to `40N`, `80W` to `110W`).
- **Current Position & Track Line:**
  - The current center at **`4 AM Mon`** (`28.4 N 89.5 W`, just off the Mouth of the Mississippi River) is marked by an orange circle with a black hurricane symbol inside.
  - A black forecast track line extends northwestward across Louisiana into eastern Texas, curving slightly eastward by Days 4–5:
    - **`1 AM Tue`:** Black circle labeled **`H`** (Hurricane, sustained wind > 73 mph) over central/western Louisiana.
    - **`1 AM Wed`:** Black circle labeled **`S`** (Tropical Storm, sustained wind 39–73 mph) near the Louisiana–Texas border.
    - **`1 AM Thu`:** Black circle labeled **`D`** (Tropical Depression, sustained wind < 39 mph) over northeast Texas.
    - **`1 AM Fri` & `1 AM Sat`:** Three closely spaced **`D`** markers stalling/drifting over northeastern Texas.
- **Cone of Uncertainty & Coastal Warnings:**
  - **Days 1–3 Cone:** Solid white region enclosed by a black outline.
  - **Days 4–5 Cone:** White diagonal cross-hatched circle extending across Texas, Oklahoma, and Arkansas.
  - **Hurricane Warning (Red):** Highlighted in red along the northern Gulf Coast from the Upper Texas coast across all of coastal Louisiana (including Lake Pontchartrain) to the Mississippi–Alabama border.
  - **Tropical Storm Warning (Blue):** Highlighted in blue from the Alabama coast eastward across the Florida Panhandle.

## Legends, Scale Bar & Logos
- **Scale Bar (Top Left):** A pink-tinted box reading `Approx. Distance Scale ( Statute Miles )` with alternating black/white segments from `SM` (`0`), `125`, `250`, `375`, to `500`, and `True at 30.00N`.
- **Information & Legend Box (Top Right):** White box with black border containing:
  - `Hurricane Gustav`
  - `September 1, 2008`
  - `4 AM CDT Monday`
  - `NWS TPC/National Hurricane Center`
  - `Advisory 31`
  - `Current Center Location 28.4 N 89.5 W`
  - `Max Sustained Wind 115 mph`
  - `Current Movement NW at 16 mph`
  - Legend symbols for `Current Center Location`, `Forecast Center Positions` (`H`, `S`, `D`), `Potential Day 1-3 Track Area`, `Potential Day 4-5 Track Area`, `Hurricane Warning`, and `Tropical Storm Warning`.
- **Agency Logos (Bottom Corners):** National Weather Service (NWS) circular logo at bottom left; NOAA emblem at bottom right.
