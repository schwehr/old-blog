# NSF Visualization Challenge Poster: Stream Micro-Ecology (`nsf-visualization-contest1768-10-med.gif`)

## Overview
A medium-resolution thumbnail of an informational scientific poster titled **"Stream micro-ecology: life in a biofilm"**, created by Andrew Dopheide and Gillian Lewis (University of Auckland, New Zealand) and recognized as an Honorable Mention in Informational Graphics in the **2008 NSF / *Science* International Science & Engineering Visualization Challenge**.

## Visual Composition & Layout
- **Title & Introduction (Top Left):**
  - Large pale-green sans-serif title at the top left: **`Stream micro-ecology: life in a biofilm`**.
  - Beneath the title are two paragraphs of white introductory text explaining how the green or brown "slime" growing on boulders and driftwood in streams is a complex biofilm community of microscopic algae, bacteria, fungi, protozoa, ciliates, and rotifers that forms the base of the aquatic food web.
- **Central 3D Illustration:**
  - The background is a richly detailed 3D/composited microscopic landscape dominated by tangled, translucent green filamentous algae and cyanobacteria across the lower half, set against a dark brownish-green organic matrix above.
  - Scattered throughout the filaments and water column are diversos microorganisms rendered with internal cellular detail and numbered with small orange circle markers: elongated blue-tinted rotifers and ciliates swimming near the upper right, oval diatoms, amoeboid protozoa, and spherical colonial green algae.
- **Micrograph Insets (Left & Bottom Columns):**
  - Along the left margin are three square light-microscope photographs with accompanying explanatory text blocks:
    1. **Top Left Inset:** Golden-green boat-shaped **diatoms** / unicellular algae.
    2. **Middle Left Inset:** Tangled bright-green filaments of **cyanobacteria / filamentous green algae**.
    3. **Bottom Left Inset:** Clusters of **bacteria and fungi** / organic detritus.
  - Two additional small square micrographs appear along the bottom edge showing tiny coccoid bacteria/spores (bottom left-center) and motile **protozoa / ciliates** (bottom right-center).
- **Ecosystem Nutrient & Food Web Diagram (Bottom Right):**
  - A rectangular inset diagram with a blue-to-green vertical gradient background depicting a circular network of curved white, yellow, and light-blue arrows connecting functional groups (`Sunlight and dissolved nutrients`, `Algae and cyanobacteria`, `Bacteria and fungi`, `Protozoa`, `Rotifers and micro-invertebrates`, and `Higher trophic levels / grazing insects and fish`), with a summary caption block below and credit URL (`www.streambiofilm.org.nz`) at the bottom right corner.
