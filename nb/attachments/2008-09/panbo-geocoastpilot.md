# Panbo Blog Post: GeoCoastPilot Early Adopters Needed (`panbo-geocoastpilot.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a September 8, 2008 blog post by Ben Ellison on **Panbo: The Marine Electronics Weblog** titled **"GeoCoastPilot, early adopters needed!"**, featuring a screenshot of the UNH Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM/JHC) **GeoCoastPilot** interactive 3D Coast Pilot visualization software.

## Website Header & Article Title
- **Background Image:** An aerial photograph of a calm coastal harbor/anchorage with small sailboats moored near a wooded island.
- **Site Branding:**
  - **`Panbo`** in bold white slab-serif text at the top left.
  - **`The Marine Electronics Weblog`** in dark blue sans-serif text.
  - Subtitle in white text: `Sponsored by Power & Motoryacht and Sail Magazines, and now by MadMariner.com.`
- **Post Headline & Date:**
  - **`GeoCoastPilot, early adopters needed!`** in large black sans-serif font.
  - **`Sep 8, 2008`** in blue text directly above the embedded screenshot.

## Embedded GeoCoastPilot Application Screenshot
The embedded Windows application window (`GeoCoastPilot`, with menu bar `File  Edit  Help`) is divided into three main sections:
- **Top 3D Perspective Viewport:**
  - Displays a tilted 3D nautical chart of **Portsmouth Harbor, New Hampshire** (showing `NEW CASTLE ISLAND` on the left, `GERRISH ISLAND` on the right, and the Piscataqua River entrance channel).
  - Vertical 3D billboarded photographs of coastal landmarks, lighthouses, and harbor range lights stand upright at their geographic locations on the chart, with dark green sight-line wedges/cones extending from the viewpoints across the channel.
  - A circular **CCOM / JHC** logo emblem is overlaid in the lower-left corner of the 3D view, and a 3D compass/orientation ring floats in the gray sky area near the top center.
- **Bottom-Left Text Pane (`Coast Pilot` Text):**
  - Displays hyperlinked paragraphs from the NOAA *United States Coast Pilot* under the heading **`Channels`** (e.g., paragraphs `(216)` through `(222)` describing depths in Portsmouth Harbor, `Portsmouth Harbor (New Castle) Light`, `Portsmouth Harbor Channel Range Lights`, `Fort Point`, `Kittery`, and `Piscataqua River`). Geographic feature names are highlighted in green/blue links or gray selection boxes that cross-link to the 3D map.
- **Bottom-Right Navigation & Control Panel:**
  - Displays current cursor/view coordinates (`43°03'33.29"N  070°42'20.03"W`), `Lat` / `Lon` / `Heading` / `Pitch` numeric fields, checkboxes for `Base Display` (`Nautical Chart`, `Bathymetry`) and `Display 3D Objects` (`Actual size`, `Exaggerated`), camera preset buttons, and the selected feature label: `Feature: Portsmouth Harbor Channel Range Rear Light`.
