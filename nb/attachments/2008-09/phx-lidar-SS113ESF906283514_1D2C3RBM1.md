# Phoenix Mars Lander Upward-Looking Lidar Beam (`phx-lidar-SS113ESF906283514_1D2C3RBM1.jpg`)

## Overview
A tall, narrow grayscale Experimental Data Record (EDR) image captured by the Surface Stereo Imager (SSI) aboard NASA's Phoenix Mars Lander on Sol 113 (September 2008), showing the lander's upward-looking lidar laser beam firing into the Martian atmosphere.

## Scene & Visual Details
- **Lidar Beam**: A bright, thin, nearly vertical white line extends from the bottom edge of the frame (just right of center) upward to approximately 90% of the frame's height, tilting very slightly to the left as it ascends.
- **Beam Structure & Scattering**: Near the bottom of the frame, the beam appears as a series of discrete bright speckles and dots before coalescing into a continuous bright streak through the middle and upper sections, where atmospheric dust and ice crystals scatter the laser light, tapering to a sharp point near the top.
- **Background**: The surrounding background is a uniform, grainy medium-to-dark gray field typical of a raw CCD subframe exposure, with a slightly darker horizontal band along the top edge and lower third of the image.
