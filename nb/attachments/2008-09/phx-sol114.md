# Phoenix Mars Lander Sol 114 Image Release Products (`phx-sol114.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from the Phoenix Mars Lander Image Product Release Website (IPRW) interface displaying three approved press release products—a cloud animation from Sol 113 and two color versions of a trench image showing frost—prepared for public release in September 2008.

## Thumbnail Previews
Three thumbnails are arranged horizontally across a white background:

1. **Left Thumbnail (`ID: 16145`)**: A square grayscale frame from an animated GIF (`cloud movie sol 113`) showing a flat, rocky Martian plain stretching to a level horizon beneath a lighter gray sky with faint clouds. A small black box in the bottom-left corner displays the frame counter `0000` in white digits.
2. **Middle Thumbnail (`ID: 16156`)**: A vertical false-color/enhanced-color view of a dug Martian soil trench surrounded by disturbed mounds of soil, rendered in yellowish-tan tones with bright white-and-blue frost patches visible inside the recessed rectangular floor of the trench.
3. **Right Thumbnail (`ID: 16151`)**: The same vertical trench scene rendered in natural/true-color reddish-brown Martian soil tones, with subtle pale whitish frost visible in the lower trench depression.
