# Chirp Sub-Bottom Profiler System Diagram and 3D Seismic Fence Visualization (`pythonpapers-3-2-fig1-chirp.png`)

## Overview
A three-panel composite figure (`a`, `b`, `c`) from Kurt Schwehr's 2008 paper *"seismic-py: Reading seismic data with Python"* (*The Python Papers*, Vol. 3, No. 2), illustrating how a marine Chirp sub-bottom seismic profiling system operates, the internal transducer/receiver layout of the towfish, and 3D fence-diagram visualizations of the resulting seismic profiles.

## Panel Descriptions

### Panel `a)` — Survey Vessel and Towed Fish Geometry
- **Diagram**: A schematic side-profile illustration showing a black silhouette of a research vessel at the sea surface towing a yellow submersible Chirp towfish (`Fish`) through a deep blue water column above a layered yellow and brown seafloor.
- **Components & Annotations**:
  - `GPS`: Black text at top with a red pointer line indicating the GPS antenna mast on the vessel's superstructure.
  - `Wire out`: White text below the black tow cable running from the ship's stern A-frame winch down to the nose of the yellow towfish.
  - `Fish`: White text above the yellow torpedo-shaped towfish.
  - `Layback`: White text above a horizontal red dimension bar spanning the horizontal distance between the ship's stern and the submerged towfish.
  - **Acoustic Pulses**: Two sets of magenta curved wavefront arcs underneath the towfish, showing a downward-propagating pulse and a reflected pulse returning from the yellow/brown sub-bottom sediment layers.

### Panel `b)` — Towfish Bottom-View Schematic
- **Diagram**: A plan-view diagram looking at the underside of the black Chirp towfish body against a light gray background, with red leader lines pointing to internal acoustic components labeled in black sans-serif text on the right:
  - `0.5-6 kHz Transducer`: Large circular gray transducer near the nose of the vehicle.
  - `2-16 kHz Transducer`: Smaller circular gray transducer positioned directly behind the low-frequency transducer.
  - `High frequency receiver`: Central bright silver rectangular element located between the longitudinal receiver arrays.
  - `4 low frequency receivers`: Four long vertical gray bar receivers arranged parallel to the length of the towfish body, with a yellow nose cone tip visible at the bottom edge.

### Panel `c)` — 3D Seismic Fence Diagram
- **Main View**: A grayscale 3D perspective rendering of two intersecting vertical Chirp seismic reflection profiles ("fence diagram") crossing at roughly right angles against a gray background, revealing finely stratified subsurface sediment layers, fault offsets, and acoustic basement topography.
- **Inset Image (bottom right)**: A color 3D visualization from Fledermaus showing semi-transparent blue bathymetric surfaces and subsurface seismic curtains draped within a coastal embayment bordered by rugged mountains in the background.
