# Kurt Schwehr CCOM Research Summary Slide (`schwehr-20080926-ccom.png`)

## Overview
A multi-panel collage presentation slide created by Kurt Schwehr for the opening Fall 2008 seminar at the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM), University of New Hampshire (September 26, 2008), summarizing his diverse research projects across marine conservation, AIS vessel tracking, planetary exploration, 3D visualization, polar fieldwork, and open-source scientific software.

## Slide Layout & Visual Panels

### Top Row
- **Top-Left Title & Waterfront Panel**:
  - Upper dark gray box with centered text:
    - `Kurt Schwehr` (large black sans-serif font)
    - `Come back Oct 3 for more` (light gray text advertising his upcoming CCOM seminar talk)
    - `http://schwehr.org/blog` (light gray URL)
  - Below the title box is a wide-angle photograph of the Boston Harbor waterfront skyline taken from the water, showing industrial piers in the foreground and downtown high-rise buildings under a blue sky with clouds.
- **Top-Center Polar Cruise Photograph**: A photograph of Kurt Schwehr wearing a gray hooded sweatshirt, dark sunglasses, and a black knit beanie, standing on the bridge wing of a yellow-painted research icebreaker (RVIB *Nathaniel B. Palmer*) with a towering tabular Antarctic iceberg and sea ice in the background.
- **Top-Right Right Whale Acoustic Buoys Map (`351 Right Whale Calls Detected Within 24 Hours.`)**:
  - A map of Massachusetts Bay and Cape Cod Bay showing the **Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary** boundary and the Boston shipping lanes.
  - Along the shipping lane east of **Boston** and north of **Cape Cod** / **Provincetown**, ten circular listening zones are plotted, each containing a red whale icon indicating right whale acoustic detections within the buoy listening radius.
  - Additional geographic labels include `Cape Ann`, `Gloucester`, `Boston`, `Provincetown`, `Cape Cod Bay`, `Cape Cod`, a scale bar (`0 2.5 5 10 15 20 Nautical Miles`), a compass rose (`N`), and a **Map Legends** inset box (`Shipping Lane`, `Buoy Listening Radius`, `Offline Buoy`, `Online Buoy`, `Right Whale Detected within Listening Radius`).

### Middle Banner (`PHOENIX MARS MISSION`)
- A wide horizontal banner spanning the left two-thirds of the slide featuring the circular **Phoenix Mars Mission** emblem on the left, the title `PHOENIX MARS MISSION` in gold and orange capital letters, a 3D rendering of the Phoenix Mars Lander deployed on the Martian arctic plains in the center, and the blue circular **NASA** "meatball" logo on the right.

### Bottom Row
- **Bottom-Left Column (e-Navigation Diagram & CCOM Wiki)**:
  - **Upper Diagram**: A conceptual graphic of coastal e-Navigation and Automatic Identification System (AIS) communications showing `TCARI Finite Element Water Level` and `Tide Aware ENC` insets at the top, a `GOES` satellite in orbit, shore stations (`USCG`, `NOAA`), and vessels navigating a `Tide Based Path` through a buoyed channel.
  - **Lower Screenshot**: A webpage screenshot of the **CCOM Wiki** (`Welcome to the CCOM Wiki`) built on MediaWiki with the CCOM/JHC logo in the top-left corner, displaying sections for `Making a Start with the Wiki`, `In-Brief Information`, `Service Announcements`, `IT Systems`, and `Administration`.
- **Bottom-Center Column (3D Field Scanning & Spacecraft Visualization)**:
  - **Top Photo**: A researcher standing in front of the white sinter cone of White Dome Geyser in Yellowstone National Park while conducting terrestrial laser/stereo scanning.
  - **Middle Photo**: Two researchers kneeling on barren Arctic terrain on Devon Island (Haughton Crater) beside a tripod-mounted panoramic stereo camera pair connected to a ruggedized laptop inside a Pelican case.
  - **Bottom Rendering**: A 3D computer-graphics overhead view of the Mars Pathfinder lander petals and ramps with the Sojourner rover beside a semi-transparent green wireframe bounding box on the Martian surface.
- **Bottom-Right Panel (Python, Fink, and GeoEndNote / Google Earth)**:
  - Against a black background, the **Python** logo (`python`) and **Fink** logo (`FINK`) appear in the upper left.
  - Centered on the right is a **Google Earth** globe centered on the Pacific Ocean with red pin placemarks and five white callout popups displaying scientific figures and bibliographic citations from marine geology papers (the *GeoEndNote* / *GeoCite* project).
