# College Woods Trail Closed Sign for UNH Wind Tunnel Construction (`trail-closed.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a posted University of New Hampshire (UNH) notice sign and a striped wooden sawhorse barricade closing a forest trail near College Woods due to the construction of the new Wind Tunnel Research Facility. Featured in the September 29, 2008 blog post *"Fall begins at UNH"*.

## Scene Description
- **Signboard**: On the left side of the frame, a rectangular navy-blue metal or composite sign with a white double-line border and gold/white serif lettering is mounted on a vertical pressure-treated wooden post.
- **Barricade**: To the right of the sign post, an A-frame wooden sawhorse barricade blocks the trail. The horizontal crossbeam is painted white with diagonal bright orange reflective stripes and black stenciled letters reading `UNH G+R` (UNH Grounds and Roads).
- **Forest Background**: Behind the sign and barricade, a dirt footpath covered in fallen brown autumn leaves winds into a dense stand of deciduous trees with bright green late-summer/early-fall foliage.

## Visible Text & Labels
The text on the blue notice sign is divided into an upper header box and a lower informational body:

- **Upper Section**:
  - `NOTICE` (underlined)
  - `THIS TRAIL IS CLOSED` (`CLOSED` underlined)
  - `TRAIL IS BLOCKED 500 FEET AHEAD`
  - `BY A CONSTRUCTION ZONE`
- **Lower Section**:
  - `WIND TUNNEL`
  - `RESEARCH FACILITY`
  - First paragraph: `Beyond, on Water Works Road, the University is constructing a wind tunnel research facility that will be approximately 300 feet long, 70 feet wide and 30 feet tall. This facility will serve an array of important new research endeavors within the College of Engineering and Physical Sciences.`
  - Second paragraph: `The Water Works Road site was chosen as the most appropriate for the wind tunnel facility after careful evaluation of numerous sites by the University's Land Advisory Committee, which is a standing committee made up of faculty and staff most familiar with the habitats, ecology, and open land activities on campus. While the site has been a forested area for the last 40 years, it is NOT part of the College Woods Natural Area. In fact, the 2004 Campus Master Plan designates the area for research development.`
  - Footer contact line: `Questions about this project should be directed to the office of Campus Planning at 862-2217.`
  - Bottom-right date stamp: `POSTED: August 7, 2008`
- **Barricade Stencil**:
  - `UNH G+R`
