# Elevated View of Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory from UNH Facilities Camera (`unhconstruction.jpg`)

## Overview
A webcam snapshot captured by a high-elevation University of New Hampshire (UNH) facilities camera looking down at the Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory (home of CCOM/JHC) and the surrounding forest in Durham, New Hampshire. Featured in the September 23, 2008 blog post *"UNH Construction camera - CCOM from up on high"*.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Structure**: In the lower-left foreground, the steep, dark-gray shingled pitched roof of a tall campus building (or tower) juts into the frame, forming a sharp triangle pointing upward and to the left.
- **Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory (Lower Right)**: Below and to the right of the foreground roof sits the multi-wing Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory complex:
  - A two-story light-beige and brick wing on the left with a bright white flat roof, rectangular windows, and a small curved retaining wall and parked car near a lower garage entrance.
  - A central connecting bridge/corridor leading to a larger beige-and-red-brick main building with dark flat roofs, rooftop HVAC units, and a single-story brick wing extending toward the lower right corner.
  - Grassy lawns and small ornamental trees border the building courtyard.
- **Background & Sky**: Beyond the laboratory building, a dense canopy of green pine and deciduous forest (College Woods area) stretches across the middle distance under a hazy light-blue and white sky.
