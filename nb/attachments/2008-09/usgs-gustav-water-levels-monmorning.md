# USGS Hurricane Gustav Water Levels Map for Southeastern Louisiana (`usgs-gustav-water-levels-monmorning.png`)

## Overview
A Google Maps screenshot from the USGS WaterWatch Hurricane Gustav Hydrologic Impacts web page showing real-time and non-real-time streamgage/water-level monitoring stations across southeastern Louisiana and coastal Mississippi on the morning of Monday, September 1, 2008, as Hurricane Gustav approached the Gulf Coast. Featured in the September 1, 2008 blog post *"USGS Gustav Water Levels"*.

## Map & Sensor Details
- **Geographic Coverage**: The terrain-shaded map centers on Lake Pontchartrain, Lake Borgne, and the Greater New Orleans metropolitan area (`New Orleans`, `Metairie`, `Kenner`, `Westwego`, `Marrero`), extending north to `Hammond`, `Amite City`, `Bogalusa`, and `Poplarville`, west to `Lake Maurepas`, `Gramercy`, and `Reserve`, south to `Lake Salvador` and `Eloi Bay`, and east across `Slidell`, `Waveland`, `Pass Christian`, `Gulfport`, `Biloxi`, `Ocean Springs`, `Gautier`, `Moss Point`, `Mississippi Sound`, and `Chandeleur Sound`. Major interstate highways (`I-10`, `I-12`, `I-55`, `I-59`, `I-110`, `I-310`) and US routes (`US 51`, `US 90`) are marked with standard highway shields.
- **Sensor Markers**:
  - **White Circles with Black Outlines**: Represent active real-time USGS water-level/streamflow gauging stations scattered along rivers, bayous, Lake Pontchartrain, and coastal passes.
  - **Red Squares**: Heavily clustered around New Orleans, Metairie, Marrero, and the southern shore of Lake Pontchartrain, representing non-real-time sensors or storm-surge/flood-monitoring sites.
- **Active Info-Window Popup**: Pointing to a circular station marker in the Rigolets pass between Lake Pontchartrain and Lake Borgne (southeast of Slidell) is a white rounded speech bubble with a drop shadow and a close button (`x`) in its upper right corner:
  - Underlined blue hyperlink: `USGS 301001089442600 Rigolets at Hwy 90 near Slidell, LA`
  - Readout text: `Stage: 2.56 ft, 2008-09-01 03:30:00`
