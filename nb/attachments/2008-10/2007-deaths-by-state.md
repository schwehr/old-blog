# Distribution of 2007 U.S. Recreational Boating Deaths by State (`2007-deaths-by-state.png`)

## Overview
A black-and-white outline map of the United States from the U.S. Coast Guard's *2007 Recreational Boating Statistics* report (`Figure 9`), showing the percentage distribution of 2007 recreational boating fatalities across each U.S. state, territory, and adjacent ocean region.

## Map Layout & Visual Elements
- **Title**: Centered at the top in bold black sans-serif capital letters:
  - `Figure 9 DISTRIBUTION OF 2007 DEATHS BY STATE`
  - `EXPRESSED AS A PERCENTAGE`
- **Geographic Features**: An unshaded white outline map of the contiguous 48 United States with black state borders and light gray shading filling the Great Lakes (Lake Superior, Lake Michigan, Lake Huron, Lake Erie, and Lake Ontario) and Great Salt Lake. Inset outlines of **Alaska** (top left) and **Hawaii** (bottom left) are included, along with tabular text lists for U.S. territories and offshore waters along the bottom.
