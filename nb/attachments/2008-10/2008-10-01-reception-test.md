# AIS Zone Message Reception Test on R/V Auk in Massachusetts Bay (`2008-10-01-reception-test.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth / GIS map visualization overlaid on a NOAA raster nautical chart of Massachusetts Bay and the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary, showing the results of an October 1, 2008 Automatic Identification System (AIS) reception test conducted aboard the research vessel R/V *Auk* receiving binary zone messages transmitted from the Cape Cod National Seashore visitor center Class A AIS transponder.

## Map Overlays & Visual Elements
- **Base Nautical Chart**: A NOAA nautical chart showing depth soundings (in fathoms/feet), bathymetric contour lines, Loran-C grid lines, magenta dashed precautionary/sanctuary boundaries, and the purple-shaded Boston Traffic Separation Scheme (shipping lane) running east–west across the upper-middle portion of the chart.
- **Acoustic Buoy Listening Zones (Green Circles)**: Seven large, overlapping, semi-transparent green circles with solid green perimeters follow the shipping lane from west to east across the chart, representing the 5-nautical-mile (9,260 m) detection zones around the right whale acoustic monitoring buoys.
- **LNG Terminal Markers (Top Left)**: Two cyan circular target icons with black rings and centers are plotted in the upper-left area inside the westernmost green circles, marking the offshore liquefied natural gas (LNG) deepwater port locations.
- **Vessel Track (`AIVDO` Position Messages — Red Line)**:
  - A thick translucent red trackline begins at **Scituate** harbor in the lower-left corner (`Scituate`, `North River`, `New Inlet`, `Fl R 4s 23ft 6M "2A"`), heads northeast across the bay into the shipping lane, zigzags through the middle green circles, and another segment runs along the southern and eastern portion of Stellwagen Bank.
- **Received AIS Zone Messages (Yellow & Orange Pushpins)**:
  - Numerous yellow and orange thumbtack/pushpin icons are plotted along the red trackline wherever the Furuno FA-150 AIS unit aboard the R/V *Auk* successfully received binary zone broadcast messages from the Cape Cod transponder.
  - Dense clusters of orange and yellow pushpins appear along the north–south transects crossing the shipping lane and along the eastern flank of Stellwagen Bank, as well as a line of yellow pushpins running east–west across the southern bank.
