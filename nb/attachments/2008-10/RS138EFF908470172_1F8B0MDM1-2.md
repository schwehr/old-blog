# Phoenix Mars Lander Robotic Arm Camera View of Soil on TEGA Cells with Partial Shadow Brightening (`RS138EFF908470172_1F8B0MDM1-2.png`)

## Overview
A grayscale photograph (`RS138EFF908470172_1F8B0MDM1`, released as NASA/JPL Photojournal image `PIA11236`) captured by the Robotic Arm Camera (RAC) on NASA's *Phoenix* Mars Lander at 3:03 p.m. local solar time on Sol 138 (October 14, 2008). The image shows Martian soil piled over four of the eight oven cells (cells 7, 6, 5, and 4 from left to right) of the Thermal and Evolved-Gas Analyzer (TEGA) after the robotic arm delivered a soil sample to cell 6 on Sol 137. In this version (`-2.png`), Kurt Schwehr tested selectively brightening the dark shadow region in the upper-left corner and blurring the stitch boundary.

## Scene & Hardware Details
- **TEGA Oven Cells & Doors**: Four rectangular sample receiving bays (numbered 7, 6, 5, and 4 from left to right) are arranged in a row across the lower-center and right portions of the frame, angled away from the camera:
  - **Perforated Mesh Screens**: Fine perforated metal mesh screens are visible at the bottom of cell 7 (far left) and cell 4 (far right), designed to sieve particles smaller than 1 millimeter into the underlying ovens.
  - **Angled Metal Chutes / Doors**: Pairings of bright, slanted metallic doors/flaps with bolted lower flanges flank the central cells, with narrow vertical slots between adjacent doors.
- **Martian Soil Deposit**: A thick, dark-gray mound of clumpy, fine-grained Martian regolith covers the central TEGA doors and fills the troughs over cells 6 and 5, spilling across the upper cylinder and down toward the lower edge of the assembly.
- **Background & Upper-Left Brightening**: Behind the TEGA doors runs a shiny, horizontal cylindrical tube. In the upper-left corner, a small rectangular metal tray or scoop component holding a clump of soil is visible against the dark background. Along the left and upper-left shadow zone, blocky JPEG Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) compression artifacts and a faint tonal transition line show where the localized shadow-brightening adjustment meets the unedited portion of the image.
