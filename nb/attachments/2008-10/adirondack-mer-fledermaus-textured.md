# Fledermaus 3D Textured DEM of "Adirondack" Rock on Mars (`adirondack-mer-fledermaus-textured.png`)

## Overview
A 3D perspective rendering in IVS Fledermaus showing a microscopic Digital Elevation Model (DEM) of the rock "Adirondack" on Mars, with a grayscale Microscopic Imager (MI) photograph draped as a texture over the surface. The model was created in early 2004 by Mark Lemmon and Kurt Schwehr using a series of Mars Exploration Rover (MER) Spirit Microscopic Imager frames acquired at different focus standoff distances over a Rock Abrasion Tool (RAT) hole (associated with NASA/JPL Planetary Photojournal release PIA05175).

## 3D Surface & Texture Details
- **Background**: Solid black (`#000000`) surrounding the tilted 3D surface mesh.
- **Circular RAT Hole & Rim**:
  - The upper and central portion of the surface forms a broad, shallow circular depression created by the rover's Rock Abrasion Tool (RAT).
  - Along the top and upper-right perimeter, a steep, jagged, elevated rock rim rises sharply above the abraded floor, showing vertical striated cliff-like facets where the elevation drops into the circular cut.
  - The abraded interior floor is relatively smooth and medium gray, with subtle concentric arc grooves near the upper edge, scattered light-gray mineral grains, small dark pits, and a faint sinuous fracture line running horizontally across the middle of the depression.
- **Unabraded Rock Face (Right Foreground)**:
  - Along the lower-right side, outside the circular RAT depression, the natural rock surface rises into a rougher, brighter, mottled ridge with high-contrast white and light-gray speckles and irregular small cavities.
- **Foreground Edge**: The bottom-left and bottom edges of the 3D surface block show a thin vertical skirt/profile along the cut boundary of the DEM grid.

## Axes & Scale Labels
- White 3D coordinate axes with tick marks are rendered on the left side of the model:
  - A vertical axis rises near the upper-left quadrant with tick marks and a white numeric label reading `20.0`.
  - A horizontal axis extends to the left from the origin with tick marks and a white numeric label reading `-200.0`.
  - Another white numeric label reading `200.0` floats near the center-left of the abraded surface along the inward axis direction.
