# Fledermaus 3D Color-Coded Topographic DEM of "Adirondack" Rock on Mars (`adirondack-mer-fledermaus-topo.png`)

## Overview
A 3D perspective rendering in IVS Fledermaus showing a shaded, color-coded Digital Elevation Model (DEM) of the Rock Abrasion Tool (RAT) hole on the Martian rock "Adirondack" without photographic texture draped over it. The DEM was generated by Mark Lemmon and Kurt Schwehr in early 2004 from focus-stacking multiple Microscopic Imager (MI) images taken by the Mars Exploration Rover (MER) Spirit at varying standoff distances.

## 3D Surface & Hypsometric Coloring
- **Background**: Solid black (`#000000`) framing the tilted rectangular surface grid.
- **Color Ramp (Elevation Coding)**:
  - **Highest Elevations (Red, Orange, Yellow, and Green)**: Along the top and upper-right curved margin of the abraded hole, the uncut rock rim rises sharply in bands of green, yellow, orange, and red/magenta peaks.
  - **Intermediate Floor Elevations (Teal and Medium Blue)**: The broad, circular interior floor ground down by the RAT dominates the center and foreground in shades of cyan, teal, and medium blue. The surface exhibits a fine, bumpy, granular micro-topography with small mounds and shallow depressions cast in relief by directional shading.
  - **Lowest Elevations (Dark Blue and Purple/Magenta)**: On the far left edge of the surface, the elevation drops into a deeper trough colored in dark blue, violet, and deep purple.
- **Flat Rectangular Patch (Right-Center)**: In the lower-right quadrant of the blue floor is a smooth, flat, unshaded rectangular/trapezoidal patch of uniform slate-blue color (a void/interpolated patch where focus data was missing or masked), bordered on its right edge by a sharp shadow and a raised green/teal ridge extending toward the bottom-right corner.

## Axes & Scale Labels
- White 3D reference axes with tick marks are overlaid on the left side of the scene:
  - A vertical axis rises near the upper left with tick marks and the white label `50.0` near the green-to-orange transition on the back rim (and a short vertical tick segment visible below the front edge).
  - A horizontal axis extends leftward with tick marks and the white label `-200.0`.
  - A white label reading `200.0` appears to the right of the vertical axis over the blue central floor.
