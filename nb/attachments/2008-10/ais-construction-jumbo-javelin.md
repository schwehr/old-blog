# AIS Vessel Track of Heavy Lift Construction Vessel Jumbo Javelin (`ais-construction-jumbo-javelin.png`)

## Overview
A trackline plot rendered in Google Earth showing the Automatic Identification System (AIS) position history for the heavy-lift construction vessel *Jumbo Javelin* (MMSI `246481000`) during part of 2007 while working on the construction of a deepwater Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) terminal offshore Massachusetts. The plot illustrates the distinctive star-like anchor-handling / mooring-installation movement pattern of an offshore construction vessel compared to normal transit shipping.
