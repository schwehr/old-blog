# MacBook Pro and Milltech SR162G AIS Receiver Setup Aboard R/V Auk (`ais-sr162g.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on October 1, 2008, inside the cabin of the research vessel *R/V Auk* (operated by the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary) showing Kurt Schwehr's data-logging workstation during an AIS reception test cruise. An Apple MacBook Pro laptop is secured to a wooden counter below a cabin window, connected to a Milltech Marine SR162G AIS/GPS receiver with portable antennas taped directly to the window glass.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **Laptop Workstation (Center)**:
  - A silver 15-inch Apple MacBook Pro sits open on a light-brown plywood counter with a raised wooden lip along the front edge. The laptop screen is dark/asleep.
  - Strips of black gaffer/electrical tape on the left and right front corners of the palm rest hold the laptop in place against vessel motion on the counter.
- **Window & Taped Antennas (Upper Center & Left)**:
  - Directly behind the laptop is a large rectangular marine cabin window with rounded corners and a metal frame, looking out over gray, lightly rippled ocean water under an overcast sky.
  - **VHF Antenna (Center)**: A black rubber-duck VHF whip antenna is taped vertically to the center of the window glass using two horizontal strips of translucent beige masking/packing tape. Its black coaxial cable leads down behind the laptop screen.
  - **GPS Patch Antenna (Left)**: A small square dark-gray magnetic/patch GPS puck antenna is taped to the lower-left portion of the window glass with a horizontal strip of beige tape, with its thin black wire trailing down to the left of the laptop.
- **Peripherals, Cables, & Logbook**:
  - **Left of Laptop**: A tangle of black power and serial/USB cables, a DB-9 serial-to-USB adapter, and the compact black enclosure of the SR162G AIS receiver tucked near the corner.
  - **Right of Laptop**: A black USB hub or adapter plugged into the right side of the laptop, white Apple MagSafe powerbrick and power cords plugged into a dual wall outlet box mounted on the right bulkhead, and a spiral-bound white notebook resting on the front-right counter with handwritten notes and a blue-capped ballpoint pen lying diagonally across the page.
