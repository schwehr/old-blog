# AIS Transponder Rack in the Dark ("Zombie Room") at Cape Cod (`ais-transponder-dark.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken in the dark on October 5, 2008, showing the glowing status LEDs and LCD screens of the Automatic Identification System (AIS) base station / transponder equipment racks at the Cape Cod National Seashore visitor center (nicknamed the "Zombie Room" by the park ranger). This is the dark-room counterpart to `ais-transponder-testing.jpg`.

## Scene & Lighting Details
- **Background**: Pitch-black room interior where only the self-illuminated displays, status LEDs, and their colorful reflections on the rack frames and cables are visible.
- **Left / Center Equipment Rack**:
  - **Top Blue Glow**: A bright rectangular blue-white glow near the top right of the main rack from the front LCD display of an AIS unit, casting blue light onto the adjacent rack post.
  - **Yellow-Green Display & Keypad (Upper Center-Right)**: Below the blue glow is the illuminated yellow-green rectangular LCD screen and faintly backlit 4×4 keypad grid of a transponder control/display unit.
  - **Intense Green LED Halo (Middle Left)**: A brilliant green status LED on one of the stacked VDL6000 transponder units creates a large, saturated circular green lens flare/halo illuminating the surrounding cables and shelf.
  - **Warm Orange/Amber Glow (Middle & Frame)**: An orange-red internal or rear indicator light casts a warm amber/orange glow across the horizontal silver rack rails, white coaxial cables, and vertical rack uprights in the center of the image.
  - **Bottom Green UPS LEDs**: Near the bottom right of the left rack, bright green status indicator lights glow on the front panel of a rack-mounted uninterruptible power supply (UPS).
- **Right Equipment Rack**:
  - **Upper Right Blue Display**: A bright blue-white rectangular LCD readout with a blue arrow/button glow sits on the middle shelf of the secondary rack, below a dimly lit beige box warmed by orange light.
  - **Middle Shelf Indicators**: Small red/orange and green status lights glow on networking/interface boxes (including a router/modem and serial device) beneath the shelf.
  - **Bottom Right UPS LEDs**: Bright green status LEDs glow on the front panel of a second rack-mounted UPS at the base of the right rack.
- **Far Left & Lower Left**: A single isolated green indicator light glows in the upper-left background (on the wall-mounted Veeder-Root panel), and a small red/orange light (optical mouse or power indicator) glows near the workbench surface at lower left.
