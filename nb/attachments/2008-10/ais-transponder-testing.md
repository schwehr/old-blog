# AIS Transponder Station Equipment Racks at Cape Cod (`ais-transponder-testing.jpg`)

## Overview
A well-lit indoor photograph taken on October 5, 2008, showing the Automatic Identification System (AIS) transponder and receiver test setup at the Cape Cod National Seashore visitor center. The equipment is mounted in two black open-frame desktop racks sitting on a wooden-topped metal workbench against a painted cinder-block wall, with a MacBook Pro laptop on a cardboard box to the left.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **Left / Center Open-Frame Rack (`BLACK BOX`)**:
  - A tall black metal two-post desktop relay rack with white lettering at the top reading `ACK BOX` (partially covered on the left by a mounted monitor).
  - **Top Left**: A small black LCD monitor mounted to the upper-left rack post, displaying a bright white screen.
  - **Top Shelf**: A dark rack unit with a glowing blue rectangular LCD display on the right.
  - **Upper-Middle Shelf**: On the left, two small gray metal boxes stacked on top of each other (including a Milltech SR162G AIS receiver with a bright green LED on the upper unit). On the right, a black handheld/bracket-mounted keypad and yellow-green LCD display unit.
  - **Middle Shelf**: Two silver/gray VDL6000 AIS transponder chassis stacked together with white coaxial and serial cables connected to their front panels, mounted above a black 1U rack panel and a silver brushed-metal rack bar holding a small gray box in the center.
  - **Bottom Unit**: A black 2U APC/rackmount Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) with illuminated green status LEDs on its right front panel and a black computer mouse resting on top of its right corner.
- **Right Open-Frame Rack**:
  - A slightly shorter black two-post open-frame rack sitting on the right side of the workbench.
  - **Middle Cantilever Shelf**: A silver metal shelf supporting a black unit with a glowing blue LCD display on the right, with a smaller white/gray box (`AisRadioInterface` / converter hardware) resting on top of it. Behind the rack against the wall are two tall vertical white cylindrical VHF cavity filters / duplexers with coaxial jumpers looped at the top.
  - **Lower Shelf**: Small networking and interface boxes, including a blue-and-black Linksys router/switch with green LEDs and a small dark box with red/orange LEDs.
  - **Bottom Unit**: A second black rackmount UPS with green status LEDs, and an open white binder/notebook lying flat on the workbench in front of it.
- **Wall & Left Foreground**:
  - **Wall (Upper Left)**: A beige wall-mounted industrial monitoring box labeled `VEEDER-ROOT` and `TLS-300C` (a tank gauge/monitoring system) with a black thermal receipt printer strip hanging out on the left and a numeric keypad and status LEDs on the right, connected via metal electrical conduits.
  - **Workbench Left**: A blue-and-white spiral-bound manual (`VDL 6000 MANUAL`) propped against the wall next to an optical mouse with a red tail light.
  - **Cardboard Box & Laptop (Bottom Left)**: An open silver Apple MacBook Pro laptop displaying terminal text windows sits on a brown cardboard shipping box in the lower-left foreground, next to a small black wireless router/modem with antennas and connected cables. Under the workbench are two metal drawers and a brown cardboard box.
