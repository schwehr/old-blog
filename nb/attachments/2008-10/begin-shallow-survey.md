# Sunset Over Portsmouth Harbor at the Start of Shallow Survey 2008 (`begin-shallow-survey.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic landscape photograph taken on the evening of October 20, 2008, marking the opening night of the Shallow Survey 2008 conference in Portsmouth, New Hampshire. The image captures calm coastal waters at dusk reflecting warm sunset colors, with waterfront homes and tree-lined shores in the middle distance.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Clouds (Upper Half)**:
  - The evening sky transitions from a bright, pale warm yellow-white glow near the left horizon (toward the setting sun) to soft blue-gray on the right.
  - Wispy, horizontal bands of stratocumulus clouds stretch across the upper sky, tinged with pastel pink, peach, and lavender-gray on their undersides.
- **Shoreline & Buildings (Middle Distance)**:
  - A dark, tree-covered shoreline extends across the horizon on both the left and right sides, separated by a narrow water channel near the center-left where a small wooded island or point stands out in silhouette.
  - On the right side, a low grassy spit/point extends leftward into the harbor. Above it on the wooded slope sit several large New England coastal houses with pitched roofs, dormers, and white trim; the leftmost house's white gable catches the bright golden-orange reflection of the setting sun, and a small American flag is visible on a flagpole near the rightmost house.
- **Water & Reflections (Foreground)**:
  - The harbor water fills the lower half of the frame with a fine, Rippled texture.
  - The water surface mirrors the sky's gradient, shifting from shimmering pale gold and peach on the left to deep slate-blue and navy in the lower-right foreground, with dark silhouettes of a couple of small mooring buoys or channel markers dotting the water in the left-center distance.
