# Frost-Killed Backyard Garden at the End of Fall (`end-of-garden.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a small fenced backyard vegetable garden at the end of the growing season in late October, after autumn frosts have wilted the plants and covered the ground with fallen leaves.

## Scene Description
- **Garden Enclosure & Trellis**:
  - The garden plot is bordered on the left, back, and right by a low white wooden picket fence.
  - Spanning across the center of the garden bed is a high arched metal wire mesh trellis supported by tall green metal T-posts and bamboo stakes.
  - Tall green climbing vines (such as beans or peas) hang from the arched wire trellis, their leaves wilted and drooping from frost.
  - Wire tomato cages stand in the right and front sections of the bed among browned, withered stems.
- **Foreground & Tools**:
  - In the foreground, black landscaping fabric covers parts of the soil, surrounded by grass and scattered orange and brown autumn leaves.
  - Two rectangular terracotta-colored plastic planter boxes sit at the front edge of the garden (one on the left beside a small dark green round pot, and one on the far right).
  - Leaning against the white picket fence post on the far left is a long-handled garden shovel.
- **Background**:
  - Directly behind the back picket fence is the exterior wall of a house painted light sage green with white window trim and two double-hung windows.
