# Furuno FA-150 AIS Transponder Box and Pilot Port Connection (`fa150-pilot-port.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of the dark gray control/transponder unit for a Furuno FA-150 Universal AIS (U-AIS) system mounted on a wooden bulkhead aboard the research vessel *R/V Auk*, with a red circle highlighting the serial pilot port connection at the bottom.

## Equipment & Wiring Details
- **Transponder Unit**:
  - A square, dark slate-gray metal enclosure mounted vertically against a light wood-grain panel.
  - On the left side of the front panel is a black rocker switch labeled `POWER` with `I` (on) above and `O` (off) below.
  - Centered near the top of the front panel are three stacked manufacturer and regulatory labels:
    1. A black-and-silver nameplate at the top reading `U-AIS TRANSPONDER`, `MODEL FA-150`, `TRANSPONDER UNIT`, `TYPE FA-1501`, `FCC ID: ADB9ZWRFA150`, `COMPASS SAFE DISTANCE`, and `FURUNO ELECTRIC CO., LTD. MADE IN JAPAN`, with a small wheelmark certification sticker (`0735 / 05`) at its upper right corner.
    2. An orange-and-white bilingual safety label reading `WARNING: To avoid electrical shock, do not remove cover. No user-serviceable parts inside.` in English above Japanese warning text.
    3. A small silver sticker at the bottom reading `USCG APPROVAL NO. 165.155/EC0735/0060`.
- **Highlighted Pilot Port & Cables**:
  - Along the bottom edge of the unit, several gray, white, and braided-shield coaxial and data cables connect to ports.
  - A bright red circle highlights a light gray DB9 serial connector plugged into the bottom-left port of the unit, used as a pilot port for logging AIS traffic.
  - To the right of the Furuno unit is a black electrical terminal block with blue and red crimp connectors attached to red and black wires.
  - The letter `A` is written in black marker on the wooden panel near the bottom right cables.
