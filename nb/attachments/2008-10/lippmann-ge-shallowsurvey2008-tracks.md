# Single-Beam WaveRunner Survey Tracklines in Google Earth (`lippmann-ge-shallowsurvey2008-tracks.png`)

## Overview
A top-down (nadir) screenshot of Google Earth showing dense white vessel tracklines from Tom Lippmann's single-beam WaveRunner survey for the Shallow Survey 2008 Common Dataset around the mouth of the Piscataqua River and Portsmouth Harbor, New Hampshire and Maine.

## Map & Trackline Details
- **Survey Tracklines**:
  - Drawn as thin white lines over satellite imagery of the harbor entrance and adjacent coast.
  - The tracks form three main survey zones:
    1. **Northern Harbor / Inner Channel**: Dense diagonal (southwest-to-northeast) parallel survey lines between `New Castle Island`, `Fishing Island`, `Gooseberry Island`, `Phillips Island`, and `Hoyts Island`.
    2. **Eastern Offshore Grid**: Dense north-south parallel lines south of `Gerrish Island`, wrapping around `Wood Island`, `White Island`, and `Horn Island`.
    3. **Western Nearshore Strip**: Closely spaced east-west comb-like survey lines running perpendicular to the shoreline south of `New Castle Island` along the Rye coast.
  - Long straight white lines connect distant points across the survey zones where the line generator connected non-contiguous segments without splitting on distance thresholds.
- **Visible Labels & Metadata**:
  - **Island Labels** (cyan-tinted white text): `Jamaica Island`, `Clarks Island`, `Goat Island` (partially cut off on left as `oat Island`), `Pest Island`, `New Castle Island`, `Hoyts Island`, `Phillips Island`, `Gooseberry Island`, `Fishing Island`, `Gerrish Island`, `Wood Island`, `White Island`, `Horn Island`.
  - **Scale Bar** (bottom left): `2046 m`.
  - **Coordinates & Elevation** (bottom status bar): `lat 43.054986° lon -70.679788°` and `elev 0 m`.
