# Gridded Single-Beam WaveRunner Bathymetry in Google Earth (`lippmann-ge-shallowsurvey2008.png`)

## Overview
A tilted 3D perspective screenshot in Google Earth showing a color-coded Digital Elevation Model (DEM) of single-beam bathymetry from Tom Lippmann's WaveRunner survey (part of the Shallow Survey 2008 Common Dataset) covering the Piscataqua River mouth, New Castle, and outer Portsmouth Harbor, New Hampshire.

## Map & Bathymetry Details
- **Geographic Area**:
  - The view looks roughly north-northwest at an oblique angle across the coast of New Hampshire and southern Maine (Kittery/Gerrish Island on the right, New Castle and Rye on the left).
  - Two small islands inside the eastern survey block are labeled in white text: `White Island` and `Horn Island`.
- **Gridded Bathymetry Overlay**:
  - The bathymetric grid was generated in Fledermaus and exported to Google Earth, colored by depth from red and orange (shallowest areas near the river entrance and around the islands) through yellow, green, and cyan to dark blue and purple (deepest channels and offshore edges).
  - Because the grid cell size is smaller than the track spacing, individual survey track lines and narrow ungridded gaps are visible across the surface—especially along the long nearshore strip extending down the Rye coastline to the lower left and in the dense east-west/north-south survey grid around `White Island` and `Horn Island`.
- **Interface Controls**:
  - In the upper right corner are Google Earth's translucent white-and-blue navigation controls: a circular pan/look ring with a hand icon in the center and a vertical zoom slider (`+` to `-`) below it.
