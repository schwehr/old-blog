# Listen for Whales Right Whale Detection Status Map (`listenforwhales-20081010.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the interactive Flash status map from the `listenforwhales.org` website on October 10, 2008, displaying real-time North Atlantic right whale acoustic detections across ten Cornell auto-buoys along the Boston Traffic Separation Scheme (shipping lane) and in Cape Cod Bay.

## Map & Buoy Status Details
- **Header Banner**:
  - Dark brown top banner with bold white text: `92 Right Whale Calls Detected Within 24 Hours.`
- **Geographic Features & Labels**:
  - Tan landmass showing eastern Massachusetts from `Cape Ann` and `Gloucester` in the north, past `Boston` on the left, down to `Cape Cod`, `Cape Cod Bay`, and `Provincetown` in the south.
  - Blue ocean bathymetry shading with the polygon outline of the `Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary` labeled in dark blue text.
  - A black-and-white scale bar at the top right spans `0`, `2.5`, `5`, `10`, `15`, `20` `Nautical Miles`, and a white compass rose with `N` is in the bottom right corner.
