# Mars Exploration Rover Opportunity "Robert E." Microscopic DEM in Fledermaus (`mer-opp-fleder-robertE-DEM.png`)

## Overview
A 3D perspective rendering in IVS Fledermaus of a micro-topographic Digital Elevation Model (DEM) of the Martian rock outcrop target "Robert E.", derived from focus stacks of Microscopic Imager (MI) frames taken by the Mars Exploration Rover *Opportunity* (NASA/JPL Photojournal PIA05276, created by Mark Lemmon and Kurt Schwehr).

## Visualization & Topographic Details
- **Surface Morphology**:
  - The 3D mesh displays the fine-scale relief of a layered rock outcrop and small spherical hematite concretions ("blueberries") against a solid black background.
  - Elevated ridges and rounded spherules appear in warm colors (red, orange, and yellow), mid-level slopes appear in green and cyan, and lower depressions/troughs on the left side are colored deep blue and purple.
- **Coordinate Axes**:
  - White 3D coordinate axes with tick marks are superimposed on the left side of the model:
    - The horizontal axis extending left-to-right shows tick marks and labels `-200.0` and `200.0`.
    - The vertical axis extending upward on the left shows tick marks and the label `500.0`.
