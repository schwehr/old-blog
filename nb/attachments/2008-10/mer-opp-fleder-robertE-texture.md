# Textured Microscopic DEM of Mars Target "Robert E." in Fledermaus (`mer-opp-fleder-robertE-texture.png`)

## Overview
A 3D perspective rendering in IVS Fledermaus showing the grayscale Microscopic Imager (MI) photograph draped as a texture over the focus-derived Digital Elevation Model (DEM) of the "Robert E." rock target and hematitic spherules ("blueberries") imaged by the Mars Exploration Rover *Opportunity* (PIA05276, created by Mark Lemmon and Kurt Schwehr).

## Visualization & Surface Details
- **Textured 3D Model**:
  - Rendered against a black background from a slightly steeper, rotated oblique angle compared to the color-coded DEM view.
  - The high-resolution grayscale MI image is mapped directly onto the 3D surface relief, highlighting fine sand grains, pitted rock layers, and prominent rounded spherules ("blueberries"), including a well-defined spherical concretion perched near the upper center ridge.
  - Steep vertical sides along the ridge edges show stretched dark and light pixels where the vertical relief drops off sharply.
- **Coordinate Axes**:
  - White coordinate axes with tick marks are overlaid on the upper left of the scene:
    - Horizontal axis labeled `-200.0`, `200.0`, and `400.0`.
    - Vertical axis labeled `500.0` near the top.
