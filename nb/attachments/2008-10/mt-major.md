# Autumn View of Lake Winnipesaukee from Mount Major (`mt-major.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle outdoor landscape photograph taken from the rocky summit ledges of Mount Major in Alton, New Hampshire, on October 12, 2008, during peak autumn foliage season ("leaf peeping"), looking out across hillside forests toward Lake Winnipesaukee and distant mountain ranges.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Left & Bottom)**: Bare, weathered gray granite ledges slope down from the left edge toward the bottom center. Scattered low shrubs, rust-red ground cover, yellow-leafed saplings, and several small, vibrant green coniferous (spruce/pine) trees grow out of crevices in the rock face. A lone hiker in dark clothing stands on the upper left rocky ridge looking out over the view.
- **Midground**: A vast, rolling expanse of dense mixed forest blankets the descending slopes and hills, displaying brilliant autumn foliage in fiery shades of orange, rust-red, golden yellow, and patches of dark evergreen.
- **Background (Right & Horizon)**: On the right side of the frame, the calm blue waters of Lake Winnipesaukee stretch across the valley, dotted with several dark, tree-covered islands and indented peninsulas. Across the top horizon, layered blue-gray mountain ridges (including the Belknap and Ossipee ranges and distant White Mountains) rise beneath a pale, hazy overcast sky.
