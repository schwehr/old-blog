# Autumn Sunset Over the Lamprey River in Newmarket, NH (`newmarket-fall.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken at sunset on October 12, 2008, in Newmarket, New Hampshire, overlooking the Lamprey River, historic brick and stone textile mill buildings, and vibrant autumn foliage.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**: Lush ornamental bushes and shrubs with reddish-bronze and deep green leaves fill the lower third of the frame, bordered by a black wrought-iron fence with a small metal gate and walkway on the left and a tall, dark-black lamppost near left-center. Two conical evergreen trees (blue spruce and juniper/cedar) rise from the riverbank shrubbery in the center and right foreground.
- **Midground (Waterway)**: The calm surface of the Lamprey River curves through the middle of the scene, brightly reflecting the warm, pale golden light of the evening sky in the center while remaining dark and shadowed along the rocky left shoreline. Several small white mooring buoys and rocks dot the water, and a few small boats are tied to a dock along the right bank.
- **Background**:
  - **Left Bank**: Dense deciduous trees line the rocky shoreline, displaying bright autumn colors in warm orange, yellow, and green.
  - **Right Bank**: Historic multi-story Newmarket Manufacturing Company mill buildings—constructed of gray stone (far right foreground) and red brick (extending further upstream)—stand along the water's edge, with a white church-like steeple rising behind the brick mill roof and a row of parked cars along the riverfront lot.
  - **Sky**: A bright, blown-out warm white and pale yellow sunset sky illuminates the scene from above the distant tree line.
