# NGA Pub. 140 Limits of Sailing Directions Index Map (`nga-pub140-map.png`)

## Overview
A black-and-white index map from NGA *Pub. 140: Sailing Directions (Planning Guide)* illustrating the geographic coverage boundaries and publication numbers for the *Sailing Directions (Enroute)* volumes covering the North Atlantic Ocean, Baltic Sea, North Sea, and Mediterranean Sea.

## Map & Geographic Details
- **Map Extent & Graticule**:
  - Covers latitudes from `0°` (Equator) to `70°N` and longitudes from `100°W` (North/Central America) to `40°E` (Eastern Europe/Middle East/Africa), with degree ticks marked along the outer border and a dotted horizontal line marking the `TROPIC OF CANCER` (~23.5°N).
  - Landmasses (`CANADA`, `UNITED STATES`, `GREENLAND`, `ICELAND`, `EUROPE`, `AFRICA`, Central/South America, and Caribbean islands) are shaded in medium gray with black coastlines; oceans and seas are white.
- **Title Block (Lower Right, over Africa)**:
  - Reads:
    ```
    PUB. 140
    LIMITS OF SAILING DIRECTIONS
    (ENROUTE)
    FOR THE
    NORTH ATLANTIC OCEAN,
    BALTIC SEA, NORTH SEA,
    AND THE
    MEDITERRANEAN SEA
    ```
- **Numbered Enroute Publication Regions**:
  - **`131`**: Western Mediterranean Sea (Strait of Gibraltar, Spain, France, Italy, Corsica, Sardinia, Morocco/Algeria/Tunisia).
  - **`132`**: Eastern Mediterranean Sea (Adriatic/Aegean Seas, Greece, Turkey, Crete, Cyprus, Suez).
  - **`141`**: Scotland and Northern UK waters.
  - **`142`**: Ireland and the Irish Sea.
  - **`143`**: Eastern North Atlantic and West Coast of Europe/Africa (Bay of Biscay, Portugal, Azores, Madeira I., Canary I., Las Palmas, Cape Verde I., Mauritania, Senegal, Guinea, Liberia).
  - **`145`**: Nova Scotia and St. Lawrence River/Gulf of St. Lawrence (`CANADA`).
  - **`146`**: Newfoundland, Labrador Sea, and Hudson Bay (`Ottawa I.`, `Belcher I.`).
  - **`147`**: Caribbean Sea Volume 1 (`Bermuda`, `BAHAMA ISLANDS`, `CUBA`, `JAMAICA`, `DOMINICAN REPUBLIC`, `PUERTO RICO`, `VIRGIN ISLANDS`, `GRENADA`, `Port of Spain`).
  - **`148`**: Caribbean Sea Volume 2 and Gulf of Mexico (Central/South American coast from `MEXICO` and `HONDURAS` to `PANAMA`, `COLOMBIA`, and `VENEZUELA`).
  - **`191`**: English Channel (`ENGLAND` / `FRANCE`).
  - **`192`**: North Sea (`ENGLAND`, `NETHERLANDS`, `GERMANY`).
  - **`193`**: Skagerrak and Kattegat between `NORWAY`, `SWEDEN`, and Denmark.
  - **`194`**: Southern and Central Baltic Sea.
  - **`195`**: Gulf of Finland and Gulf of Bothnia (`SWEDEN`, `FINLAND`, `RUSSIA`).
