# NGA Pub. 140 United Kingdom Pilotage and Regulations Page (`nga-pub140-uk.png`)

## Overview
A sample two-column page (page `583`) from the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency's *Pub. 140: Sailing Directions (Planning Guide)* detailing territorial seas, maritime boundary disputes, pilotage rules, port entry control signals, and examination services for the **United Kingdom**.

## Page Layout & Visible Text
- **Header & Footer**:
  - Top center header in bold serif font: `United Kingdom`, with page number `583` at top right above a horizontal rule.
  - Bottom right footer: `Pub. 140`.
- **Left Column**:
  - **Territorial Seas**: Notes claims for the Isle of Man (12-mile territorial sea and 12-mile fisheries zone), the Bailiwick of Guernsey (3-mile territorial sea and 12-mile fisheries zone), and the Bailiwick of Jersey (12-mile territorial sea and 3-mile fisheries zone, with a special regime in the Bay of Granville).
  - **`Maritime Boundary Disputes`**: Notes the dispute with Denmark over the Faroe Islands' continental shelf boundary outside 200 miles.
  - **`Pilotage` / `United Kingdom Pilotage`**: Explains port-specific pilotage, VHF shore pilot stations, 12-hour advance ETA notification requirements (with 2-hour adjustment notice), thick-weather Morse code sound signals (`G` for vessels requiring a pilot, `H` for pilot vessels on station), and pilot boat hull markings (orange or black hull with `P` or `PILOT`).
  - **`Deep Sea Pilotage`**: References IMO Resolution A.486(xii) adopted 19 November 1981 regarding Deep Sea Pilots in the North Sea, English Channel, and Skagerrak, followed by numbered lists of pilot stations (`1. Brixham Deep Sea Pilots`, `2. Brixham (Deep Sea and Coastal Pilots Ltd, Gravesend)`, `3. Brixham (Torbay)`, `4. Dirkzwagers Coastal Pilotage, Maassluis`, `5. Hammond Deep Sea Pilots`) and continental authorities (`1. Dunkerque Deep Sea Pilots`, `2. German Deep Sea Pilots`, `3. Cherbourg Deep Sea Pilots`).
- **Right Column**:
  - **`Regulations`**: Describes Ministry of Defence port entry and movement controls, Examination or Traffic Control Vessels, and "Dangerous Area" restrictions.
  - **`Port Entry Control Signals` Table**: A bordered 3-column table (`Day signal`, `Night signal`, `Meaning`):
    1. `Three red balls, vertically disposed` | `Three flashing red lights, vertically disposed` | `Entry to port prohibited`
    2. `No signal` | `Three green lights, vertically disposed` | `Entry to port permitted`
    3. `One square blue flag` | `One green light between two red lights, vertically disposed` | `Movement within the port or anchorage prohibited`
  - **`Examination Service`**: Details the distinguishing flags of Examination Service vessels (a special flag with a blue border and red-and-white squared center, plus the Blue Ensign or White Ensign) and anchoring restrictions.
