# NGA Pub. 142 South Coast of Ireland & Fastnet Light Page (`nga-pub142-page.png`)

## Overview
A sample page (page `4`) from NGA *Pub. 142: Sailing Directions (Enroute) — Ireland and the Irish Sea*, covering **Sector 1. South Coast of Ireland—Mizen Head to Carnsore Point**, featuring coastal navigation descriptions and two color photographs of **Fastnet Light**.

## Page Layout & Content
- **Header & Footer**:
  - Top left page number `4` and centered bold title `Sector 1. South Coast of Ireland—Mizen Head to Carnsore Point` above a horizontal line.
  - Bottom left footer: `Pub. 142`.
- **Left Column**:
  - Describes coastal landmarks and hazards east of Mizen Head, including `Carrigower Rock` (0.5 mile E of Mizen Head), `Mount Gabriel` (404m elevation, 12 miles NE of Mizen Head with radar domes), and `Barley Cove` (`51°27'N., 9°46'W.`) with `Barley Rock` (`Devil's Rock`).
  - **`Tides—Currents`**: Details spring tidal currents up to 4 knots close off Mizen Head and current directions between Mizen Head and Crookhaven.
  - **`Fastnet Rock` (`51°23'N., 9°36'W.`)**: Described as lying about 9 miles ESE of Mizen Head, with a prominent 54m-high gray granite lighthouse tower (`Fastnet Light`) and racon, plus a `Caution` note regarding the IMO-approved Traffic Separation Scheme 5 miles SE of Fastnet Rock.
  - **Lower Left Photograph (`Fastnet Light`)**: Shows a wide view of the jagged, dark rock island of Fastnet Rock under a cloudy gray sky, with the stone lighthouse rising behind the keeper's complex and two small red-and-white motorboats in the choppy dark water in the foreground.
  - **`1.3 Crookhaven` (`51°28'N., 9°42'W.`)**: Begins at the bottom of the left column and continues into the right column, describing the harbor entrance between Streek Head and Rock Island, `Alderman Rocks`, and `Black Horse Rocks`.
- **Right Column**:
  - **Upper Right Photograph (`Fastnet Light`)**: A tall vertical close-up photograph of the cylindrical stone tower and white lantern dome of Fastnet Light perched atop the craggy sea stack, with white surf breaking at the base against a pale blue-gray sky.
  - **Lower Right Text**: Continues the description of `Crookhaven`, noting the 4.5m draft anchorage, lack of licensed pilots (fishermen board vessels displaying the pilot signal), and a `Caution` regarding a submarine pipeline running WSW across the harbor entrance close W of Crookhaven Light (`51°28.6'N., 9°42.2'W.`).
