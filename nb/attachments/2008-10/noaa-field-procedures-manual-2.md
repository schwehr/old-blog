# NOAA Field Procedures Manual Figure 4.10: Acoustic Refraction Errors in HIPS (`noaa-field-procedures-manual-2.png`)

## Overview
A figure (`Figure 4.10`) from the NOAA Office of Coast Survey *Field Procedures Manual (FPM)* showing a CARIS HIPS Subset Editor visualization of acoustic refraction-induced bathymetry errors ("smile/frown" artifacts across multibeam swaths) paired with a BASE surface standard deviation reference image.

## Visual Details & Caption
- **Upper Panel (BASE Surface Standard Deviation Reference Image)**:
  - Displays a plan-view raster surface colored in shades of dark red and orange-red.
  - Three sub-vertical green survey tracklines cross the area from top to bottom.
  - Parallel to the tracklines, brighter orange-red striping/ribbing bands mark higher standard deviation values along the outer swath edges where adjacent multibeam swaths overlap.
  - Near the top, a narrow, horizontal yellow bounding box outlines the cross-line subset slice being inspected, with a small upward-pointing yellow arrow at its top center indicating the viewing direction.
- **Lower Panel (HIPS Subset Editor 2D Profile View)**:
  - Displays a cross-section of 3D multibeam soundings against a black background with a faint gray grid.
  - Soundings from five adjacent overlapping survey lines are color-coded by line from left to right: **red**, **cyan**, **magenta**, **pale yellow/tan**, and **bright green**.
  - Each swath exhibits a pronounced upward-curving arc ("smile" or "wings-up" refraction artifact) caused by sound velocity errors, causing the outer beams of adjacent swaths to diverge vertically where they overlap.
- **Caption (Bottom)**:
  - `Figure 4.10: HIPS Subset Editor view (bottom) of acoustic refraction-induced bathymetry errors, using BASE surface standard deviation image for reference.`
