# Ray-Traced 3D Visualization of Phoenix Mars Lander and Stereo Pipeline Terrain (`phx-pia10768-stp.png`)

## Overview
A 3D computer graphics rendering (NASA/JPL Photojournal `PIA10768`, created in LightWave 3D by Kurt Schwehr and Zareh Gorjian using NASA Ames Stereo Pipeline terrain meshes) showing the **Phoenix Mars Lander** spacecraft model alongside a 3D textured Triangulated Irregular Network (TIN) of the Martian surface around its workspace.

## Visual Details
- **Background**: Solid dark brown/charcoal void surrounding the reconstructed terrain swath.
- **Spacecraft Model (Center & Right)**:
  - Viewed from an elevated oblique angle looking down at the lander deck.
  - Two large octagonal UltraFlex solar array wings fan out to the lower-left and upper-right, rendered with dark blue photovoltaic cells and silverradial ribs.
  - The circular spacecraft deck carries detailed 3D models of the scientific payload and instruments:
    - The **Robotic Arm (RA)** extending from the deck toward the upper-center digging workspace on the terrain mesh, casting a distinct shadow on the ground.
    - The white-shrouded **Surface Stereo Imager (SSI)** camera mast rising near the upper right of the deck, and the **Thermal and Evolved-Gas Analyzer (TEGA)**, **MECA** box, and calibration targets mounted on the deck surface.
    - One of the three lander legs and circular footpads is clearly visible extending down to the lower right.
- **Reconstructed Martian Terrain (Left Arc)**:
  - A crescent-shaped gray-and-tan 3D terrain mesh curves around the left and top sides of the lander, generated from stereo image pairs captured by the Surface Stereo Imager (SSI).
  - The surface displays realistic relief of small rocks, pebbles, shallow troughs, and the darker excavated soil trench ("Dodo-Goldilocks" / "Snow White" workspace area) directly beneath the extended robotic arm tip at the top.
