# ICAN Regulus II Closeup of Selected Right Whale Detection Zone (`pog-ican-regulus-zone-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A zoomed-in screenshot (`1:150,000` scale) of ICAN's **Regulus II** electronic charting software during the October 15, 2008 Boston Port Operations Group (POG) demonstration, showing the detailed status readout when a specific Right Whale acoustic detection zone (Zone 2) is selected on the chart.

## Interface & Chart Details
- **Window Title Bar**:
  - Reads `Regulus II - [Info (1:150,000) 13272 S57 1:10,000 DU=FEET Base Display North Up]`.
- **Left Information Panel**:
  - Under the `Right Whale` tab, the upper inspection pane displays bold black key-value details for the selected circular notice:
    - `Time to Expire`: `03h 37m 04s`
    - `Latitude`: `42° 20' 25.15" N`
    - `Longitude`: `070° 33' 58.73" W`
    - `Radius`: `9260 m`
    - `Start Time`: `2008.10.15 05:43:00`
    - `Type`: `Right whale detection`
    - `MMSI`: `3669734`
  - **Lower Left Table (`Timed Circular Notices`)**:
    - Lists the four visible notices in the current view (`20:51:40` without detection, and three `3669734: Right whale detection` notices at `20:49:45`, `20:47:42`, and `20:45:39`).
- **Main Chart Viewport (Right)**:
  - Shows a closeup of the western end of the Boston Traffic Separation Scheme (magenta dashed lines and directional arrows).
  - On the left is the westernmost **green horizontally-hatched circle** (Zone 1, no whale detection).
  - Overlapping it to the east are three **yellow cross-hatched circles** representing active **Right whale detection** zones.
  - At the center of the first yellow circle (Zone 2, located at `42° 20' 25.15" N, 070° 33' 58.73" W`), a black four-cornered selection box (`[ ]`) highlights the selected buoy location.
- **Bottom Status Bar**:
  - Scale dropdown displays `1:150,000` alongside navigation tools, alarm `Silence`/`Ack` buttons, and the UTC event log (`UTC 15:21:14 ... BLog289a.08E logfile opened`).
