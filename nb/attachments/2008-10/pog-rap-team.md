# Boston Port Operations Group Right Whale AIS Project Demonstration Team (`pog-rap-team.jpg`)

## Overview
A color indoor photograph taken during the October 15, 2008 Boston Port Operations Group (POG) meeting, showing Kurt Schwehr alongside Joel and Dudley from ICAN standing behind a row of folding tables covered with AIS hardware and laptops used to demonstrate the Right Whale AIS Project (RAP) zone message integration with ICAN's Regulus II electronic charting software.

## People & Setting
- **Left**: Kurt Schwehr stands with his hands in the pockets of his blue jeans, wearing a black hooded sweatshirt with a small white logo on the left chest.
- **Center-Right**: Joel (from ICAN) stands behind the middle table, wearing a light blue long-sleeved dress shirt, a patterned gold/yellow necktie, and khaki trousers, with his left hand resting on a black notebook on the table.
- **Far Right**: Dudley (from ICAN) stands to Joel's left, wearing a light blue dress shirt, a dark patterned necktie, and dark trousers, leaning slightly forward with his left hand near the end of the table.
- **Room Environment**: The background features a light gray/off-white wall with horizontal venetian blinds covering recessed clerestory windows along the top edge. Dark blue upholstered metal-frame conference chairs are arranged around and under the gray folding tables. Orange power extension cords run across the dark carpet under the tables.

## Hardware & Equipment on the Tables
From left to right across the gray folding tables:
- **Silver Apple MacBook Pro**: Open on the far left of the table with the glowing white Apple logo visible on the lid, white power adapter brick and cables beside it, and a bound document/booklet with circular cover graphics lying in the lower-left foreground.
- **AIS Transponder & Interface Units**:
  - A gray metal box (power supply or radio unit) next to a roll of white tape and a black mouse.
  - A black rectangular unit with a white specification label on the front, encircled by a neat coil of gray coaxial/data cable.
  - A silver multi-port junction/transponder assembly with six white cylindrical cable glands/connectors protruding from the side and an exposed circuit board in front of it.
  - A bright yellow-and-black ruggedized rectangular chassis (L3 / VDL6000 AIS transponder hardware) with a white label on the yellow side panel.
- **Right End of Table**: A black PC laptop (running ICAN Regulus II) open on the far right, accompanied by a green plastic soda bottle, power strips, black power bricks, and interconnecting serial and network cables.
