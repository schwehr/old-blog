# Echosounder Display of a Pop-Up Buoy Sinking to the Seafloor (`popup-sinking-to-the-bottom.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of an LCD echosounder display screen aboard the R/V *Auk* on October 1, 2008, capturing the acoustic trace of a Cornell bioacoustic "pop-up" bottom-mounted hydrophone buoy free-falling through the water column toward the seafloor.

## Echosounder Plot & Acoustic Features
- **Upper Pane (Water Column Zoom View)**:
  - Displays a depth window from **`19 m`** (labeled at the top right of the grid) to **`44 m`** (labeled at the bottom right of the grid), divided by horizontal dotted grid lines.
  - **Descending Pop-Up Trace**: A distinct, thin diagonal blue dashed line cuts across the white background from the upper-center-left down toward the lower-right, tracking the pop-up unit's steady descent over time as the vessel remains roughly stationary above it.
  - **Right-Edge Returns**: Along the far-right vertical axis (representing the most recent pings or a secondary profile strip), dense vertical clusters of blue speckles appear between roughly 22 m and 36 m depth, alongside a vertical A-scope / amplitude indicator strip on the far right showing blue diamond-shaped signal envelopes along a vertical center line.
  - A white mouse cursor arrow is visible in the lower-left quadrant of the upper pane.
- **Lower Pane (Bottom Lock / Seafloor Expansion View)**:
  - Displays a vertical range from **`-6 m`** to **`4 m`** relative to the seafloor.
  - A thick, brightly colored horizontal band represents the strong acoustic return of the seabed, shaded according to echo intensity using the color bar at the top right (transitioning from dark red/magenta at the strongest surface reflection through yellow, green, cyan, and blue at the trailing edge).
  - A matching red-and-green amplitude envelope appears in the narrow vertical A-scope column on the lower right.

## Interface & Visible Text
- **Top Left Readout**: Large bold black text reads **`82.3 m`** (indicating total water depth or range reading).
- **Top Right Color Scale**: A horizontal segmented color bar ranging from black and dark blue on the left through cyan, green, yellow, orange, and dark red on the right.
- **Bottom Status Bar**:
  - Left side panels: **`E0003`** and **`L0028`**.
  - Right side timestamp: **`11:51:56 AM`** above a blue Windows taskbar edge.
