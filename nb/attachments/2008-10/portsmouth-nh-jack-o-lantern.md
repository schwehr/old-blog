# Scuba Divers with an Underwater Jack-o'-Lantern in Portsmouth Harbor (`portsmouth-nh-jack-o-lantern.jpg`)

## Overview
An underwater color photograph taken in July 2008 in Portsmouth Harbor, New Hampshire, showing two scuba divers—Glenn McGillicuddy and Liz Kintzing—posing near the seabed with a carved yellow-orange jack-o'-lantern during a current-meter deployment dive.

## Scene Description
- **Left Diver**: Positioned in the left foreground, wearing a black drysuit or heavy wetsuit, black hood, clear-framed dual-lens dive mask, black regulator mouthpiece with a silver circular face ring, a buoyancy compensator (BC) with a bright yellow low-pressure inflator hose looped on the left side, and dark dive gloves.
- **Right Diver**: Positioned to the right and slightly further back, wearing a dark suit with gray sleeves featuring orange logo markings on the shoulders, a black hood, a blue-framed dual-lens dive mask, and a black regulator with a pink/red accent and a circular emblem on the front cover. A stream of small air bubbles rises toward the surface above their head.
- **The Jack-o'-Lantern**: Held in the gloved hand between the two divers is a round, bright yellow-orange gourd/pumpkin with a tall fibrous stem on top. It is carved with a classic frowning face consisting of two triangular eyes, a triangular nose, and a downturned mouth with small vertical tooth notches visible inside.
- **Underwater Environment**: The surrounding water has a turquoise-green coastal tint with suspended particulate matter (marine snow/sediment) illuminated in the foreground and a rocky/cobbly harbor bottom dimly visible below.
