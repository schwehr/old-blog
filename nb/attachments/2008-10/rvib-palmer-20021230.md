# Kurt Schwehr on the Sea Ice in Front of the RVIB Nathaniel B. Palmer (`rvib-palmer-20021230.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor color photograph taken on December 30, 2002, showing Kurt Schwehr standing on Antarctic sea ice directly in front of the towering bright-orange bow of the United States Antarctic Program research vessel and icebreaker RVIB *Nathaniel B. Palmer* during a transit from Christchurch, New Zealand, to McMurdo Station.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Person)**: Kurt Schwehr stands in the lower-left foreground facing the camera, wearing dark oval wrap-around sunglasses, a bright red heavy polar parka with a black inner hood/collar and a black velcro name-patch strip on the left chest, over a dark gray crew-neck undershirt.
- **Background (Vessel)**:
  - **Hull**: The massive, steeply raked icebreaking bow of the RVIB *Nathaniel B. Palmer* dominates the frame behind him. Painted high-visibility international orange, the hull shows dark scuffs and scraped paint near the waterline and lower bow where it has broken through pack ice, along with circular hawseholes/scuppers, a recessed anchor pocket near the bow tip, and a row of rectangular freeing ports along the upper bulwark.
  - **Vessel Name**: Painted in light blue/gray block capital letters along the starboard bow flare: **`NATHANIEL B. PALMER`**.
  - **Superstructure & Foredeck**: Above the orange bulwark rises the cream/buff-colored forward superstructure with small rectangular windows, exterior walkways with railings, an orange cylindrical life-raft canister on the far upper left, a buff-painted foredeck crane boom with blue-and-white striped markings, and a black forward mast topped with a small flag at the bow peak.
- **Environment**: Snow-covered sea ice with broken ice rubble along the ship's dark waterline stretches across the bottom of the frame under a flat, overcast pale-gray polar sky.
