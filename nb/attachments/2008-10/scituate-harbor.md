# Entering Scituate Harbor Aboard the R/V Auk (`scituate-harbor.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle color photograph taken on October 1, 2008, from the upper deck of the research vessel R/V *Auk* looking forward over the bow as crew member Mike Thompson stands ready with fenders while entering Scituate Harbor, Massachusetts, after an AIS reception and pop-up buoy test cruise.

## Scene Description
- **Foredeck & Crew**:
  - Viewed from the upper helm/flybridge deck (whose gray non-skid edge and silver metal trim cut diagonally across the lower right corner).
  - On the white foredeck below, Mike Thompson stands facing forward toward the harbor entrance, wearing a dark long-sleeved jacket, black pants, and a bright orange life vest (PFD) with a white reflective strip across the upper back and black stenciled lettering (`STELLWAGEN BANK ...`) on the lower back.
  - Two large, round, fluorescent orange-pink inflatable buoy fenders rest on the deck beside him—one tied to the port bow railing and the other positioned between his legs.
  - A white metal bow railing encloses the foredeck, with a white bench seat on the port side and an anchor chain running through a deck hawsepipe in the lower left corner.
- **Water & Harbor Entrance**:
  - Choppy olive-green harbor water fills the middle ground with small whitecaps and wave swells moving past the breakwater.
  - **Breakwater & Lighthouse**: Extending from the right side into the center distance is a rough stone rubblemound jetty/breakwater with white surf breaking along its edge. At the end of the spit stands the white cylindrical tower and dark lantern room of **Old Scituate Light**, accompanied by coastal houses and buildings along the shoreline and a distant tree-lined coast with moored sailboats on the far left under an overcast gray sky.
