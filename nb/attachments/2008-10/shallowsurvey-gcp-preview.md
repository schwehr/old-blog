# GeoCoastPilot Presentation Slide Preview for Shallow Survey 2008 (`shallowsurvey-gcp-preview.png`)

## Overview
A presentation slide preview image for the Shallow Survey 2008 talk *"Linking the Coast Pilot with Geo-referenced Imagery & Chart Information"* by Matthew Plumlee, Kurt Schwehr, Briana Sullivan, and Colin Ware, illustrating how the **GeoCoastPilot** software transforms printed NOAA *Coast Pilot* text into an interactive 3D coastal visualization environment.

## Slide Layout & Visual Elements
- **Background & Header**: Set against a dark charcoal-gray textured slide background with a centered serif title in pale cream/gold text reading **`GeoCoastPilot`**, followed by a bullet point in white serif text:
  - `• Transform printed text to an interactive 3D environment`
- **Left Side (Printed Coast Pilot Page Excerpt)**:
  - A white vertical column showing a scanned two-column-style excerpt from the printed NOAA *United States Coast Pilot* under the bold heading **`Prominent features`**, covering paragraphs `(192)` through `(196)` describing **Gerrish Island**, **Portsmouth**, **New Castle Island**, **Seavey Island**, **Whaleback Light**, **Wood Island**, **Fort McClary**, **Kittery Point**, **Portsmouth Harbor (New Castle) Light**, **Fort Point**, and **Portsmouth Harbor Coast Guard Station**.
  - Two red rectangular highlight boxes are drawn around mentions of **`Whaleback Light`** in paragraph `(193)` and **`Whaleback Light (43°03.5'N., 70°41.8'W.)`** at the start of paragraph `(194)`.
  - Partially visible yellow slide footer text `08` appears just to the right of the bottom corner of the page excerpt.
- **Right Side (GeoCoastPilot Application Window)**:
  - A screenshot of the **GeoCoastPilot** desktop application (`File  Edit  Help` menu bar).
  - **Upper 3D Perspective Viewport**: Shows a 3D oblique view of the Portsmouth Harbor entrance with blue-shaded bathymetry, yellow/tan land contours, purple navigation channel lines, 3D red and green navigation buoys/beacons, and floating billboard photographs of coastal landmarks (including lighthouses and towers). A red box highlights the 3D model/photo of **Whaleback Light** in the center of the scene. In the lower-left of the 3D view are a vertical **`Depth (feet)`** color scale (`0.0 ft`, `10.0 ft`, `20.0 ft`) and a circular **UNH CCOM / JHC** logo emblem; in the lower-right is a 3D compass rose (`N`, `W`, `E`).
  - **Lower Left Text Pane**: Displays the hyperlinked digital text of **`Prominent features`** (`[192]` through `[196]`) with green paragraph numbers, purple/blue underlined clickable feature links, and a pink highlight over `[194] Whaleback Light (43°03.5'N., 70°41.8'W.)`.
  - **Lower Right Controls & Feature Details Pane**:
    - Search bar with `Find >` button.
    - `Base Display:` radio buttons (`Nautical Chart`, `Bathymetry` selected) and `Display key features:` (`Actual size`, `Exaggerated` selected).
    - Navigation & camera panel: **`43°03'34.34"N  070°41'47.13"W`**, `Lat 43.059540`, `Lon -70.696425`, `Heading 330.00`, `Pitch 8.00`, `Goto`, `Home`, `Aerial`, `Overview`, `NorthUp`, and a pink `Show Rotation & Measurement Tools` button.
    - `Feature: Whaleback Light` metadata box listing:
      - `Lat: 43° 03' 31.77" N   Lon: 70° 41' 46.67" W`
      - `Purpose: General warning mark`
      - `Shape: Beacon tower`
      - `Color: Grey`
      - `Status: Permanent`
      - `Info: Conical`
      - `--Light characteristics (exhibited at night)--`
      - `Category: emergency`
      - `Color: White, fixed, faint`
- **Red Connecting Arrows**: Three thick red arrows illustrate the interactive linkage:
  1. From the red box around `Whaleback Light (43°03.5'N., 70°41.8'W.)` in the printed text on the left to the highlighted line in the digital text pane of GeoCoastPilot.
  2. From the highlighted digital text line up to the 3D lighthouse model of Whaleback Light in the 3D viewport.
  3. From the highlighted digital text line across to the `Feature: Whaleback Light` Light List attribute table in the lower-right pane.
