# Washed Homegrown Sweet Potatoes on a Wooden Table (`sweet-potato-clean.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up indoor color photograph taken in late October 2008 showing a small harvest of freshly washed, irregularly shaped homegrown sweet potato tubers arranged on a light-colored wooden tabletop after having the garden soil rinsed off.

## Scene Description
- **Sweet Potato Tubers**:
  - Seven main bright reddish-orange sweet potato tubers of varying sizes and elongated, curving shapes are spread out on a warm-toned wood grain surface—described humorously in the blog post as looking like *"strange alien fish."*
  - **Left Group**: A plump spindle-shaped tuber on the far left with a thin root tail at the bottom; above it, a smaller curved finger-like tuber with a stubby side knob; next to it, another medium oval tuber with a long, thin red root runner draping downward off the bottom edge of the frame.
  - **Center Group**: Three slender, elongated carrot-like tubers nestled side by side, with long trailing root tendrils extending both upward and downward.
  - **Right Group**: A bulbous, rounded tuber with a pointed tip at the bottom, a smaller tapered lobe at the top, and long, spindly root runners extending out to the right and curving around the lower right corner, plus fine pale root hairs projecting from its sides.
