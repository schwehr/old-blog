# Freshly Dug Dirty Sweet Potato Cluster Straight from the Garden (`sweet-potato-dirty.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor high-angle close-up photograph taken in late October 2008 showing a tangled cluster of freshly unearthed sweet potato tubers, fibrous roots, and green vine stems covered in garden soil and dried autumn leaves, resting on a concrete slab immediately after being dug out of the backyard garden.

## Scene Description
- **Sweet Potato Cluster**:
  - Two interconnected root crowns sit side by side on a rough gray-brown concrete surface scattered with loose dirt crumbs and fallen winged maple seeds (samaras).
  - **Left Crown**: A green vine stem angles out to the left from a dense knot of soil-caked fibrous roots. Beneath the crown hang several reddish-orange tubers—a thick spindle-shaped tuber on the left, a plump central tuber near the top, a shorter middle tuber, and a curved horizontal tuber near the bottom—intertwined with long, trailing red root runners extending toward the bottom of the frame.
  - **Right Crown**: Connected by a horizontal green stem segment, the right crown features a dark soil-covered root mass at the top with a slender vertical orange tuber hanging down in the center and several long, thin reddish-orange root tendrils splaying out toward the right edge alongside withered brown leaves.
