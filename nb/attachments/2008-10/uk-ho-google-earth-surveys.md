# UK Maritime and Coastguard Agency Multibeam Surveys in Google Earth (`uk-ho-google-earth-surveys.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of Google Earth displaying Civil Hydrography Programme multibeam bathymetric survey coverage polygons and metadata placemarks in the Thames Estuary / Margate Road area off southeastern England, released in KML format by the UK Maritime and Coastguard Agency (MCA) / UK Hydrographic Office (UKHO).

## 3D Viewport & Survey Overlay Details
- **Perspective & Geography**: An oblique 3D view looking northward across the North Kent coast (Margate / Ramsgate in the bottom foreground) into the outer Thames Estuary (`51°34'35.34" N`, `1°18'14.59" E`, eye altitude `6.18 km`).
- **Bathymetric Survey Polygons**: Red-and-orange shaded relief multibeam bathymetry surfaces are draped on the water surface, showing detailed seafloor sandwaves, channels, and shoals:
