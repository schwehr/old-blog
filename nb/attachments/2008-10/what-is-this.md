# Green Pumpkin in the Garden (`what-is-this.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up outdoor photograph of a large, unripe dark green pumpkin growing on the ground amidst dense garden foliage and vines. The photo was featured in a blog post on October 11, 2008, titled "The mystery vegitable solved," where Kurt Schwehr noted that after harvesting what people initially thought was a squash, he discovered it was a 10-inch green pumpkin with a small patch of orange underneath.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Subject:** Centered in the lower half of the frame is a rounded, ribbed dark green pumpkin resting on brown soil. Bright sunlight reflects off the glossy top-center surface of the rind, while a dark vertical shadow falls across its right side. A pale green leaf on a fuzzy stem extends in front of the left side of the pumpkin, and small green three-leaved clover/oxalis plants grow in the soil at the lower right.
- **Foliage & Stems:** Thick, upright green squash/pumpkin stalks rise on both the left and right sides of the pumpkin. In the upper left, large broad squash leaves display mottled white powdery mildew patches across their green surfaces. Bright green bean or garden leaves fill the right side of the frame.
- **Background:** Behind the plants, a white wooden picket fence is partially visible in bright sunlight. A bright yellow squash blossom blooms near the top center-right, and a rusted green metal garden stake (`U`-post) and a translucent plastic cloche or container are visible at the far left.
