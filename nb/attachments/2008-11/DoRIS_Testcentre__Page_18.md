# Austrian DoRIS Inland ENC Chart of a Danube Lock and Weir (`DoRIS_Testcentre__Page_18.png`)

## Overview
An Inland Electronic Navigational Chart (Inland ENC) excerpt from a presentation on DoRIS (Donau River Information Services / Austrian River Information System), depicting a lock, weir/dam, and river kilometer markers along the Danube River in Austria (near river kilometer 1949–1950, Greifenstein power plant/lock area).

## Chart Details & Symbology
- **Geographic Coordinates & Grid:**
  - Dashed green latitude/longitude graticule lines cross the chart vertically and horizontally.
  - Green coordinate labels on the left read `48°21.50'N` (top left) and `48°21.00'N` (bottom left).
- **Waterway & Land Features:**
  - The main channel of the **Danube** (labeled `Danube` in pale gray text in three places) flows left to right in light blue, with brown/tan land on both banks and a narrower secondary stream/oxbow channel in the upper right.
  - Dashed brown lines mark the navigable fairway/channel boundaries along the river.
- **Dam / Weir & Lock Complex (Center):**
  - At river kilometer `1950.0` (left of center), a row of circular gray weir/barrage symbols crosses the northern half of the river, each accompanied by red-white-red Austrian regulatory sign flags.
  - A central dividing pier/island separates the weir on the north from the double lock chambers on the south.
  - Magenta circular prohibition symbols (a horizontal bar inside a circle) appear upstream and downstream of the weir bay.
  - Black-and-white two-aspect lock traffic signal symbols (`[•|o]`) mark the approaches to the lock chambers.
- **River Kilometer & Hectometer Markers:**
  - Small magenta diamonds along the north and south banks mark hectometer intervals (`1` through `9`), with larger numbers and black-circled dots in the channel marking full river kilometers: `1950.0` (`1950`), `1949.0` (`1949`), and `1948.0` (`1948`).
- **Berths, Mooring & Navigation Symbols:**
  - A magenta anchor symbol is located in the left section of the river between hectometers `6` and `7`.
  - Blue squares and red-white-red squares along the riverbanks and inside the southern harbor basin (near km `1948.5`–`1949.0`) indicate mooring/berthing areas and signage.
