# Common Source Logistics Database Slide from Austrian DoRIS Presentation (`DoRIS_Testcentre__Page_20.png`)

## Overview
A presentation slide titled **"Common Source Logistics Database"** from the Austrian DoRIS (Donau River Information Services) test centre overview, produced by the *also danube* project. Featured in a blog post on November 19, 2008, discussing Mario Sattler's presentation on European River Information Services (RIS).

## Header & Branding
- **Top Left Logos:** The `also danube` logo (gray text beneath a green curved wave line) alongside an orange circular geometric icon with a white upward-right arrow.
- **Title:** `Common Source Logistics Database` in bold blue sans-serif text across the top, underlined by a blue-to-white horizontal gradient bar.
- **Bottom Right Footer:** `© also danube` in blue text.

## Architecture Diagram (Right Side)
- **Central Hub (`CSL. DB`):** Three overlapping royal-blue circles arranged in a triangle over a dark navy circle labeled `CSL. DB` in the center, connected by a red ring with six glowing orange-yellow spheres at the intersections:
  - Top circle: `Operational Data for Logistic & Supply Operations`
  - Bottom-left circle: `Operational Data for Transshipment & Transport`
  - Bottom-right circle: `Planning Data for Logistic & Transport Operations`
- **External Databases:** Two clusters of four 3D gray cylinders (representing databases) at the top right and bottom right feed into the central `CSL. DB` via curved dark blue arrows.
- **Output Arrows:** Two curved dark blue arrows point outward from the central `CSL. DB` toward the two application screenshots on the left.
