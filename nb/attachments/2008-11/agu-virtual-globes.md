# AGU 2008 Virtual Globes Session Presentation Cards (`agu-virtual-globes.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from the AGU Fall Meeting 2008 "Virtual Globes" session webpage (`conferences.images.alaska.edu/agu/2008/presentations.html`) showing a two-column grid of six rounded gray presentation cards (`IN41A-1124` through `IN41A-1126` on the left, and `IN41A-1135` through `IN41A-1137` on the right), each containing a thumbnail image, presentation title, authors, and institutional affiliations. Featured in a blog post on November 11, 2008.

## Presentation Cards (Left Column)
1. **IN41A-1124**
   - **Thumbnail:** 3D perspective shaded-relief bathymetric/topographic digital elevation model.
   - **Title:** **Virtual Ocean, an Example of Augmenting KML to Move Beyond Visualization to Analysis and Synthesis**
   - **Authors:** WIlliam Ryan¹, Justin Coplan²
   - **Affiliations:** (1) Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory, Palisades, NY 10964; (2) Rochester Institute of Technology, Rochester, NY 14623
2. **IN41A-1125**
   - **Thumbnail:** NASA World Wind 3D globe view centered on Africa and Europe against space.
   - **Title:** **World Wind Java Enabling Technology**
   - **Authors:** Patrick Hogan¹, Tom Gaskins¹, John Bailey²
   - **Affiliations:** (1) NASA, Ames Research Center, Moffett Field, CA 94035; (2) Arctic Region Supercomputing Center, Fairbanks, AK 99775
3. **IN41A-1126**
   - **Thumbnail:** 3D meteorological visualization over Baja California and Mexico in World Wind.
   - **Title:** **Using the WorldWind at the MM5 Grid Portal**
   - **Authors:** Oh-Kyoung Kwon¹, Minsu Joh¹, Jaegyoon Hahm¹, Pill Woo Lee¹
   - **Affiliations:** (1) KISTI, Daejeon, Republic of Korea
