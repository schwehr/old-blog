# TrueHeading Blue-Pilot Bluetooth AIS Pilot Port Adapter (`blue-pilot.png`)

## Overview
A studio product photograph of a wireless Bluetooth AIS Pilot Port interface kit (the TrueHeading "Blue-Pilot" / OLTP serial-to-Bluetooth adapter and external USB battery pack) resting on a warm orange/tan surface. Featured in a blog post on November 18, 2008, titled "Bluetooth Pilot Interface," discussing wireless adapters that allow maritime ship pilots to connect a laptop to a vessel's AIS Pilot Plug.

## Hardware Components
- **AIS Pilot Plug & Cable (Bottom Left):** A round black multi-pin military/marine circular connector (the standard AMP/concentric AIS Pilot Plug) attached to a light gray cable terminating in a beige DB-9 serial connector.
- **Bluetooth Serial Adapter (Center):** A compact black rectangular RS-232 serial-to-Bluetooth module screwed onto the DB-9 connector. It features:
  - A gray-and-blue oval label reading `OLTP` (upside-down relative to the camera).
  - A black articulated whip antenna standing vertically on the right end of the adapter.
  - A black power cord plugged into the side of the adapter, leading via a white USB cable to the battery pack.
- **External Battery Pack (Upper Right):** An elongated black rectangular USB battery pack with embossed lettering along its top casing and a lanyard loop on its right end, supplying power to the Bluetooth adapter via the white USB-A cable.
