# Sunset View from Airplane Window Leaving Manchester, NH (`delaware-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken through a commercial airliner window at dusk while departing Manchester, New Hampshire, en route to Delaware. Featured in a blog post on November 6, 2008, titled "Arriving in Delaware" (`title="View of the window of the plane when leaving Manchester, NH"`).

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Aircraft Wing & Engine):** The lower portion of the frame shows the leading edge of the aircraft's wing and a white pylon supporting a deep blue jet engine nacelle on the right. A bright orange-red rim of sunset light reflects along the curved forward lip of the blue engine cowling.
- **Horizon & Sky:** Across the middle of the frame, a vivid horizontal band of brilliant yellow, orange, and crimson-pink sunset glow separates the dark, hazy purple-blue landscape below (where a few faint lights glimmer on the ground) from an overcast slate-blue cloud deck above.
