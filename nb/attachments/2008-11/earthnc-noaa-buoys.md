# EarthNC NOAA Weather Buoys KML Overlay in Google Earth (`earthnc-noaa-buoys.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth displaying the EarthNC NOAA Weather Buoys KML network link (`earthncwxbuoysnl.kmz`) over the Pacific Northwest coast (Washington State and Vancouver Island, British Columbia), with an open information balloon for NDBC Station 46087 at Neah Bay, WA, and a "Buoy Legend" screen overlay on the left. Featured in a blog post on November 19, 2008, titled "EarthNC NOAA Buoys."

- **Geographic View (Right):** Shows the Pacific Ocean on the left and the coastline of Washington State and Vancouver Island on the right, outlined in bright yellow chart coverage polygons.
