# EPA "Cleanups in My Community" Map Using Microsoft Virtual Earth (`epa-ms-virtual-earth-cleanups.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) **"Cleanups in My Community"** web mapping application displaying Superfund and RCRA cleanup sites in the Seacoast New Hampshire and southern Maine area (searched for `Dover, NH`) on a Microsoft Virtual Earth map.

## Header & Search Controls
- **Banner:** Displays the circular blue-and-white U.S. Environmental Protection Agency seal on the left, `U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY` across the top right, a dark blue title bar reading **Cleanups in My Community** with a `Bookmark` link on the right, and breadcrumb navigation: `You are here: EPA Home » Cleanups » Cleanups in My Community » Mapping Result`.
- **Search Controls:** Links for `Recent Additions | Contact Us`, radio buttons for `Search: ( ) All EPA (*) This Area` with a text box and `Go` button.
- **Subheader:** `Initial Searched Geographic Area: Dover, NH` and a dropdown menu `Area of Interest: [ Choose One ]`.
- **Bottom Action Buttons:** Three dark blue pill buttons below the map: `New Search`, `Go to WME`, and `Online Help`.

## Map View (Left & Center)
- **Map Interface:** A Microsoft Virtual Earth road map covering southeastern New Hampshire (`Dover`, `Durham`, `Portsmouth`, `Great Bay`, `Exeter`, `Hampton`, `Rye`) and southern Maine (`South Berwick`, `Eliot`, `Kittery`, `York`, `York Beach`). The top-left toolbar shows a circular directional pan/zoom control and view mode buttons: `2D`, `3D`, `Road` (selected), `Aerial`, `Bird's eye`, and `Labels`. A `5 miles` scale bar is at the bottom right.
- **Markers & Info Popup:** Three red circular markers (at Dover, Pease Air Force Base, and Coakley Landfill near Breakfast Hill) and one dark blue/black circular marker at Kittery/Portsmouth are plotted on the map.
- **Popup Box:** An open rectangular callout box points to the dark marker on Seavey Island:
  - **Title:** `PORTSMOUTH NAVAL SHIPYARD` (blue underlined link)
  - **Address:** `SEAVEY ISLAND, KITTERY, ME 03904`
  - **Table (`Cleanup programs involved:`):** Three columns (`Brownfields`, `RCRA`, `Superfund`), with blue checkmarks (`✓`) under `RCRA` and `Superfund`.
