# GeoNav Title Frame: "Coastal Images for Preparation and Situational Awareness" (`geonav-panoramas.png`)

## Overview
A title-screen screenshot from the video **"Coastal Images for Preparation and Situational Awareness,"** produced by Matt Plumlee at the CCOM Visualization Lab (University of New Hampshire) to demonstrate panoramic coastal imagery integrated with electronic charting in GeoNav. Featured in a blog post on November 28, 2008, titled "Chart of the Future Videos on YouTube."

## Visual Layout & Scene Description
The screen is split horizontally into two synchronized views of the Piscataqua River / Portsmouth Harbor area, with bold white title text overlaid across the center:

- **Upper Pane (Shipboard Panoramic View):**
  - Shows a 3D vessel bow with railings and a jackstaff in the center foreground, looking upriver across the water.
  - Along the horizon, a stitched photographic panorama shows the Portsmouth/Kittery shoreline, the twin towers of the Sarah Mildred Long Bridge in the left-center distance, and the green steel truss span of the Memorial Bridge on the right.
- **Lower Pane (2D Overhead Chart & Aerial Orthoimage):**
  - Displays a top-down hybrid map combining high-resolution aerial orthophotography of Portsmouth, NH, and Kittery, ME (showing highways, bridges, buildings, and docks) with shaded-relief multibeam bathymetry in the river channel and purple/yellow navigation chart overlays and buoys.
  - The circular **CCOM / JHC** (Center for Coastal & Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center, University of New Hampshire) logo is in the bottom-right corner.
