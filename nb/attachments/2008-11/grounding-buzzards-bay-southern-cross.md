# Grounded Tug Southern Cross in Buzzards Bay (`grounding-buzzards-bay-southern-cross.png`)

## Overview
An aerial video frame capture showing the Boston-based tugboat *Southern Cross* listing heavily and partially submerged after grounding near Buzzards Bay, Massachusetts, on November 9, 2008, while a U.S. Coast Guard rescue boat stands by nearby.

## Scene Description
- **Water**: Dark, deep greenish-blue ocean water with small surface ripples covering the entire frame.
- **Tugboat (*Southern Cross*)**: Positioned in the lower-right quadrant, viewed from an elevated aerial angle.
  - The vessel is listing severely to its port side (left in the image), with its port gunwale and stern deck partially awash in the dark water.
  - The wheelhouse/superstructure is painted white, with green and yellow/white trim visible along the starboard side of the hull and cabin.
  - Mast antennas angle to the left from the top of the wheelhouse.
  - The dark brown/black lower hull on the starboard side rolls upward out of the water due to the heavy port list.
- **Rescue Vessel**: In the upper-right area, a small bright orange U.S. Coast Guard rigid-hull inflatable boat (RHIB) or response boat sits on the water facing toward the upper right, with a white outboard motor cover or stern wake mark at its rear.
