# 3D MATLAB Surface Plot of Laser-Scanned Face (`laser-scan-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A 3D surface plot generated in MATLAB showing the reconstructed topography of a human face and neck produced by the home-brew 3D laser scanner.

## Plot & Visualization Details
- **3D Reconstructed Surface**:
  - The front of a human face—clearly showing the forehead, eye sockets, protruding nose, lips, chin, and cylindrical neck—is rendered in smooth shades of blue and cyan.
  - Behind and to the right of the facial surface, radiating horizontal spikes and planes of noisy background/edge measurements fan outward in false colors ranging from deep blue through cyan, green, yellow, orange, and red.
- **Coordinate Axes & Grid**:
  - Enclosed in a 3D white bounding box with dotted black grid lines.
  - **Vertical Axis (Left)**: Labeled from `-400` at the bottom to `-50` near the top in increments of 50 (`-400`, `-350`, `-300`, `-250`, `-200`, `-150`, `-100`, `-50`).
  - **Depth Axis (Bottom-Left)**: Labeled `200`, `100`, `0`, `-100`.
  - **Horizontal Axis (Bottom-Right)**: Labeled `-50`, `0`, `50`, `100`, `150`, `200`.
