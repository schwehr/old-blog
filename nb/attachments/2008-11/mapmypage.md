# MapMyPage Google Maps Popup on Weblog Entry (`mapmypage.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Kurt's Weblog demonstrating the **MapMyPage** browser widget/script, which automatically detects geographic terms in webpage text, inserts small globe icons next to them, and opens an embedded Google Maps popup window when hovered or clicked.

## Webpage & Popup Details
- **Background Blog Post Text**:
  - Heading in bold dark blue: `ORIGINS OF AIS`
  - Body text: `Jorge is talking at eNav2008. I didn't know about ADSSE on Alaskan tankers from the Oil Pollution Act of 1990.` (with "Oil Pollution Act of 1990" underlined as a link).
  - Link below: `The Prince William Sound automated...`
  - Monospace citation block:
    - `Radice, J.T.; Cairns, W.R.`
    - `Position Location and Navigation Sympos...`
    - `Volume , Issue , 11-15 Apr 1994 Page(s)...`
    - `Digital Object Identifier 10.1109/PLA...`
    - `Summary:The US Coast Guard [globe icon] is present... to implement an automated dependent sur... Prince William Sound (PWS), Alaska [globe icon].`
  - Both `US Coast Guard` and `Alaska` have small blue-and-green globe icons appended, and `Alaska` is highlighted with an orange rectangular border.
- **MapMyPage Floating Map Window (Right)**:
  - A gray-bordered popup overlay with an `X` close button in the top-right corner.
  - **Map Controls**: Top-left buttons for `Street View` and `More...`, with pan/zoom (`↓`, `+`, `-`) controls below; top-right map type buttons for `Map` (selected in bold), `Satellite`, and `Terrain`.
  - **Map View**: Shows a standard Google Map of Alaska and northwestern Canada with green park/reserve shading and a green downward-pointing arrow marker placed over southern Alaska.
  - **Info Bubble**: Centered over the map with a close box (`x`) and the title **Alaska**, followed by links:
    - `Get directions: To here - From here`
    - `Check weather - Send to email`
    - `Export to Google Earth - Save to GPS`
    - `Change location - Send feedback`
