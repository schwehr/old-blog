# Aldebaran II Marine Protected Area MIO Display (`mio-aldebaran-marine-protected-area.png`)

## Overview
A full-screen Windows screenshot of the **Aldebaran II** electronic charting system (developed with ICAN and CARIS for NOAA) displaying Marine Information Objects/Overlays (MIO) for Marine Protected Areas (MPAs) around southern Florida and the Florida Keys.

## Interface & Window Elements
- **Window Title Bar**: `Aldebaran - [Info (1:1,000,000) USMM0FL1 S57 1:10,000 DU-FEET User Display North Up]`.
- **Menu Bar**: `Main`, `Chart`, `3D`, `Survey`, `Route`, `Nav Aids`, `SAR`, `Nav Elements`, `Tow Boat`, `AIS`, `Light Level`, `Voyage`, `Event!`, `DR!`, `Man Overboard!`, `View`, `Window`, `Help`.
- **Left Scale Bar**: Vertical orange-and-blue distance scale bar along the far left edge of the chart area, marked from `64 Nm` at the top (`DR` and orange north arrow `N` beside it) through `32 Nm`, `0`, `16 Nm`, `32 Nm`, `48 Nm`, to `64 Nm` at the bottom.
- **Bottom Control Bar**:
  - Dropdowns for `CP: MRE` and `R: Low`.
  - Zoom controls (`Out`, `In`, scale box `1:1,000,000`), ship centering button, cursor/pan mode icons, `Silence` and `Ack` alarm buttons.
  - Log box showing `• UTC 05:22:30 Chart List Created`.
- **Windows Taskbar**: Shows the classic `Start` button, open windows (`NOAA MEP Meeting`, `ICAN DataStore`, `Presentation`, `Windows Task Manager`, two `MIO for MPA on ICAN Ald...` windows, `Google Chrome`, and the active `Aldebaran - [Info (1...` window), and system tray clock at `1:25 AM Friday`.

## Chart & Overlay Details
- **Chart Area**: Set against a black background showing the outline of the southern Florida peninsula, Florida Bay, the Florida Keys, and the Dry Tortugas at a `1:1,000,000` scale.
- **Boundaries & Overlays**:
  - Dashed and solid blue lines outline coastal jurisdiction, sanctuary, and marine protected area boundaries around the southern tip of Florida and along the Florida Keys reef tract.
  - The landmasses of the Florida Keys and Dry Tortugas are rendered in gray.
  - Three overlapping semi-transparent pink/magenta rectangular highlight zones cover sections of the middle and lower Florida Keys, with a circular target crosshair positioned in the westernmost pink box and a vertical blue cursor line extending to the top of the screen.
