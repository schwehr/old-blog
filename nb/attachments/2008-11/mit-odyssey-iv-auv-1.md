# MIT Odyssey IV AUV at UNH Chase Ocean Engineering Tank - Side View (`mit-odyssey-iv-auv-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken at the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory showing the **MIT Odyssey IV** autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) resting on a wooden cradle while being prepared for tank testing on November 5, 2008.

## Scene Description
- **AUV (*Odyssey IV*)**:
  - The vehicle features a streamlined, flat-sided hull painted bright yellow on the upper and lower fairings with a wide matte-black central side panel fastened by rows of silver screws.
  - Stenciled on the upper-right yellow hull section in red and black block letters is:
    - `MIT` (in red) `AUTONOMOUS UNDERWATER VEHICLE` (in black)
    - `MAX DEPTH 6,000 M`
  - Two circular cross-body thrusters with black multi-blade propellers are recessed into the black side panel (one toward the left/bow and one toward the right/stern).
  - Mounted externally near the middle-right of the black panel is a large ducted thruster supported by a stainless-steel tubular frame and X-brace that attaches to brackets on the upper and lower yellow hull sections.
  - On top of the hull are a black lifting bail/fin and a clear dome strobe/recovery beacon in a metal guard cage.
- **Technician & Equipment**:
  - On the left, a man wearing a gray hooded sweatshirt leans forward, working with his hands on a white circular fitting on the forward port side of the AUV.
  - Beneath him is a blue mesh bag filled with small blue spherical floats/balls.
  - Two vertical ropes hang in the foreground in front of the vehicle, which sits on a wooden support cradle.
