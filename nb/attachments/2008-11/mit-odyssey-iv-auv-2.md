# MIT Odyssey IV AUV on Cradle with Forklift (`mit-odyssey-iv-auv-2.jpg`)

## Overview
An angled three-quarter view photograph of the **MIT Odyssey IV** autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) on its wheeled wooden cradle inside the UNH Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory during tank testing preparations.

## Scene Description
- **AUV (*Odyssey IV*)**:
  - Viewed from the front/starboard quarter, showing the yellow upper and lower hull sections and black side panels.
  - Red and black lettering on the curved upper yellow hull reads `MIT SEA GRANT AUV LAB` and `AUVLAB.MIT.EDU`, with white lettering `ODYSSEY IV` partially visible on the black panel near the rear.
  - The externally mounted starboard ducted thruster is clearly visible on the left, held out from the hull by angled stainless-steel struts and a vertical frame.
  - A circular cross-body thruster tunnel is visible in the center of the black side panel.
  - At the flat vertical nose/endplate on the right, a stainless-steel D-ring / tow loop protrudes above a blue mesh bag hanging down in the foreground.
- **Foreground & Background Details**:
  - The blue mesh bag in the lower-right foreground is filled with spherical floats and printed with white text: `UNITED DIVERS INC.`, `SOMERVILLE, MA`, `617-666-0410`.
  - A man in a gray sweatshirt bends over on the far right, reaching toward the bottom of the mesh bag.
  - The AUV rests on a custom wooden cradle mounted on a flatbed cart with caster wheels.
  - Behind the AUV on the left stands a dark blue and black industrial forklift with white vertical numbers (`23629`) chalked on its mast.
