# OBIS-SEAMAP Dataset Page: Duke Harbor Porpoise Tracking (`obis-seamap-by-dataset.png`)

## Overview
A webpage screenshot from the **OBIS-SEAMAP** (Ocean Biogeographic Information System - Spatial Ecological Analysis of Megavertebrate Populations) portal displaying the dataset detail view for **Duke Harbor Porpoise Tracking** (`SEAMAP ID 83`) from the Duke University Marine Laboratory.

- **Top Navigation Bar**: Orange tabs on a teal banner: `Home`, `Datasets` (active), `Species`, `Providers`, `About`.
- **Header**: `Duke Harbor Porpoise Tracking` and subheading `Duke University Marine Laboratory`.
- **Left Species Filter**: Lists `All species` (selected), `Marine mammals`, and `Phocoena phocoena`.
- **Interactive Map Panel (Center)**:
  - Top toolbar above map: Zoom in/out icons, box selection tools, opacity slider (`0` to `1.0`), `Tracks` toggle button, `Environ.` button, `Google Earth` button, and playback control buttons (`||`, `|`, `▶`).

  - Colored grid cells (1-degree / 0.1-degree blocks) overlay the Gulf of Maine and Bay of Fundy in red, orange, yellow, green, and blue, showing harbor porpoise observation density.
