# OBIS-SEAMAP Species Page: Fin Whale (`obis-seamap-by-species.png`)

## Overview
A webpage screenshot from the **OBIS-SEAMAP** portal showing the species profile and global observation map for ***Balaenoptera physalus*** (**fin whale**).

## Page Layout & Map Interface
- **Header**: Main title `Balaenoptera physalus` and subtitle `fin whale`.
- **Left Dataset List**: Scrollable list with `All datasets` selected at the top, followed by individual contributing datasets:
  - `Alnitak Cetaceans and sea turtles surveys off Southern Spain`
  - `Bahamas Marine Mammal Research Organisation Strandings`
  - `PIROP Northwest Atlantic 1965-1992`
  - `Cetaceans in the Southern Indian Ocean 2004`
  - `Allied Finback Whale Catalogue`
  - `European Seabirds at Sea - JNCC All Trips`
  - `MMS Aerial Survey, PNW 1989-1990`
  - `MMS High Altitude Survey 1980-1983`
  - `MMS High Altitude Survey, SCB 1975-1978`
  - `MMS Low Altitude Survey 1980-1983`
  - `MMS Low Altitude Survey, SCB...`
- **Interactive Global Map (Center)**:
  - Top toolbar with zoom/selection tools, opacity slider (`0` to `1.0`), `Environ.`, `Google Earth`, and `Online Mapper` buttons.
  - Google Map (`Satellite` view) showing North and South America, the Atlantic Ocean, Europe, and Africa.
  - Fin whale sighting density cells are plotted across the North Atlantic (dense red/orange/yellow cluster off New England and Nova Scotia, blue cells across the mid-Atlantic, off Spain/UK, in the Mediterranean), along the U.S. West Coast and Alaska, and near the Antarctic Peninsula.
- **Right Map Legend & Summary**:
  - `#obs: 6,389`, `#datasets: 65`, small illustration of a fin whale, `Size: S | L`, `points` vs. `0.01 0.1 1 degree`, and the `Observations` color scale (`1-5` blue, `5-10` green, `10-50` yellow, `50-100` orange, `> 100` red).
