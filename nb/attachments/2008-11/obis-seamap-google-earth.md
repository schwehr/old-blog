# OBIS-SEAMAP Fin Whale Observations in Google Earth (`obis-seamap-google-earth.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** on Mac OS X displaying an **OBIS-SEAMAP** KML overlay of *Balaenoptera physalus* (fin whale) observation distributions and a temporal histogram bar chart off the coast of New England (Massachusetts Bay, Cape Cod, and the Gulf of Maine).

## Interface & Sidebar (Left)
- **Menu & Toolbar**: Standard Mac OS X Google Earth menu bar (`Google Earth`, `File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Tools`, `Add`, `Help`) and top icon toolbar (sidebar toggle, placemark, polygon, path, image overlay, ruler, sunlight, sky, email, print, Google Maps).
- **Search Pane**: `Fly To` tab active with example prompt `Fly to e.g., 1600 Pennsylvania Ave, 200...`.
- **Places Tree**:
  - `My Places` -> `SBNMS facilities` (unchecked)
  - `Temporary Places` -> `OBIS-SEAMAP Maps` (`OBIS-SEAMAP, Ocean Biogeographic Information`)
    - `Balaenoptera physalus` (`fin whale`)
      - Checked: `Observation Distribution` -> `Distribution Map` and `Legend` (`#observations per cell. Cell size is 1/0.1/0.01`)
      - Unchecked: `Point Observations` -> `By dataset` (`Observation points are grouped by contributing`) and `Environment` (`Environmental layers`).

## 3D Map & Overlays (Right)
- **Geographic View**: Oblique/top-down satellite view of eastern Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Cape Cod, Massachusetts Bay, Stellwagen Bank, and the Wilkinson Basin / Great South Channel region, with an inset world overview map in the upper-left corner of the viewport.
- **Highway Shields**: Interstate `295`, `95`, `195`, and U.S./State routes `44` and `6` (on Cape Cod) are labeled.
- **Fin Whale Observation Grid**: Hundreds of small colored squares (blue, green, yellow, orange, and red) are plotted across Massachusetts Bay, Stellwagen Bank, east of Cape Cod, and extending southeastward along the continental shelf.
- **Legend & Yearly Bar Chart Overlay (Bottom)**:
  - **Color Legend (Bottom-Left)**:
    - Red: `> 20`
    - Orange: `10-20`
    - Yellow: `5-10`
    - Green: `2-5`
    - Blue: `1`
  - **Temporal Bar Chart (Bottom-Center/Right)**: A white rectangular chart showing observation counts on the vertical axis (`0`, `200`, `400`, `600`, `800`) versus years from `1966` to `07` (`2007`) along the horizontal axis. Yellow vertical bars show a major peak in `79` (~`900`) and `80` (~`800`), with smaller peaks around `77`–`81`, `86`–`93`, and `95`–`04`.
