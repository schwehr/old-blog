# NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer Bridge Communications Console (`oe-bridge-2.jpg`)

## Overview

A photograph of the Furuno Global Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS) A3 Area communications console on the bridge of the NOAA ship *Okeanos Explorer*, taken during a tour of the vessel in November 2008 while it was docked in Seattle, Washington.

## Equipment & Console Layout

The olive-gray metal console houses dual redundant maritime radio, satellite, and teletype communication stations arranged side by side:

- **Top Shelf (Printers):** Two beige Furuno dot-matrix printers sit side by side on top of the console, loaded with tractor-feed paper under tinted plastic acoustic covers.
- **Middle Rack (Radio & Control Units):**
  - **Left Unit:** A dark gray Furuno control unit with a rectangular amber/orange monochrome screen on the left, a red distress button under a protective flip cover, a grid of function keys, and a rotary selector knob on the right.
  - **Center-Right Unit:** A dark gray Furuno MF/HF or VHF radiotelephone transceiver with a green-tinted LCD screen, a numeric keypad (`0`–`9`, `*`, `#`), and four small rotary knobs along the bottom edge.
  - **Far Right Panel:** A circular analog meter or gauge and a small indicator light with a white label underneath.
- **Lower Bay (Terminals & Keyboards):** Two matching dark gray CRT/LCD display terminals with integrated 3.5-inch floppy disk drives sit recessed in the lower compartments, each paired with a compact beige QWERTY keyboard resting on the angled desk surface in front.
- **Handsets:** Two black coiled-cord telephone/radio handsets are mounted in cradles—one on the left vertical face of the console below the main placard, and another mounted on an angled bracket between the two top printers.
