# NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer at Sunset (`okeanos-explorer-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview

An evening photograph of the NOAA ship *Okeanos Explorer* moored at Pier 66 in Seattle, Washington at dusk, with the Olympic Mountains silhouetted against a vibrant orange-and-blue sunset sky across Elliott Bay.

## Scene Description

- **Sky & Horizon:** The sky transitions from deep twilight blue at the top to a bright golden-orange band along the western horizon, silhouetting the jagged peaks of the Olympic Mountains across Puget Sound / Elliott Bay. The calm water on the left reflects the warm orange and cool blue hues of the sky.
- **The Vessel (*Okeanos Explorer*):**
  - Framed from the same elevated vantage point astern of the starboard quarter as `okeanos-explorer.jpg`, the ship's deck floodlights are illuminated, casting a warm yellow glow across the white superstructure, yellow side davit, and working decks.
  - The tall black lattice tower and large white spherical satellite radome stand out sharply against the twilight sky.
  - At the stern, the yellow A-frame gantry and two dark knuckle-boom cranes are silhouetted against the water.
- **Pier & Background Lights:**
  - Several parked cars—including a dark sedan, a white Ford Mustang coupe, and a white pickup truck—remain parked along the illuminated concrete pier apron beside the ship.
  - Ahead of the ship on the far right, the neighboring moored vessel and pier structures are lit up, topped by a glowing red neon `E` sign (the Edgewater Hotel sign on Pier 67).
