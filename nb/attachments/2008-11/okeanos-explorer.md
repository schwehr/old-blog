# NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer Docked at Pier 66 (`okeanos-explorer.jpg`)

## Overview

An elevated daytime photograph of the NOAA ocean exploration vessel *Okeanos Explorer* (R 337) moored starboard-side to the pier at Pier 66 in Seattle, Washington, seen from the conference center window during the eNavigation 2008 conference on November 18, 2008.

## Vessel & Scene Description

- **The Vessel (*Okeanos Explorer*):**
  - Viewed from the stern quarter looking forward along the starboard side, the ship has a white hull and white superstructure with twin blue-and-black exhaust stacks midships.
  - **Radome & Mast:** A prominent black lattice tower rises amidships, topped by a large spherical white satellite communications radome (for high-bandwidth telepresence). Forward of the radome tower is a white mast structure with radar scanners and antennas.
  - **Stern Working Deck:** The aft deck features a heavy yellow A-frame gantry at the stern with an American flag flying from the stern staff, flanked by two tall black articulated knuckle-boom cranes (`HYDRA PRO` stenciled in white on the booms, along with safe working load markings `SWL 6600 LBS @ 40 FT`). A secondary yellow J-frame/davit sits on the starboard side just forward of the aft crane, and an orange rigid-hull inflatable rescue boat (RHIB) is stowed on chocks on the upper boat deck.
  - **Hull Markings:** `OKEANOS EXPLORER` is painted in large black capital letters along the starboard quarter, with `SEATTLE, WA` visible in smaller black lettering on the white transom at the far left.
  - **Gangway:** A long aluminum brow/gangway extends from the forward starboard deck down to the concrete pier.
- **Pier & Foreground Vehicles:**
  - Along the concrete apron of Pier 66, a line of parked cars, pickup trucks, and work vans sits beside the ship, including two white service vans with red oval `MM` logos and roof-mounted ladders in the foreground, a dark cargo van, a white Ford Mustang coupe, a white pickup truck, and several dark sedans and an SUV farther forward.
- **Background:**
  - Beyond the bow of the *Okeanos Explorer*, another vessel is moored ahead along the pier. Across the calm gray waters of Elliott Bay, low gray hills and shoreline sit beneath an overcast, cloud-layered sky.
