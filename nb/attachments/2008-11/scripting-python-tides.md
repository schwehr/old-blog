# Coastal Water Level Telemetry and Modeling Diagram (`scripting-python-tides.png`)

## Overview

An illustrated conceptual diagram created for a CCOM student seminar on Python scripting (November 2008), depicting how coastal tide/water-level gauge stations transmit real-time telemetry via the GOES satellite to NOAA and the U.S. Coast Guard (USCG) to support hydrodynamic modeling, electronic charting, and maritime navigation.

## Diagram Elements & Layout

- **Main Coastal & Ocean Scene:**
  - A 3D-style perspective view shows a deep blue ocean on the left meeting a green coastline with bays, estuaries, islands, a city skyline, an airport runway, and scattered buildings on the right.
  - **Tide / Water-Level Stations:** Four circular icons containing a red thermometer/staff gauge and a blue sine wave represent coastal water-level sensor stations distributed along the shoreline and inlets.
  - **Satellite Telemetry (`GOES`):** Blue arrows point upward from each of the four water-level stations to a geostationary satellite labeled `GOES` in the upper right sky. A long orange arrow points downward from the `GOES` satellite to a building onshore labeled `NOAA`, located near a coastal facility labeled `USCG` with two radio/radar communication towers.
  - **Vessels & Aircraft:** Several ships—including container ships, a naval warship, a Coast Guard cutter, a tug/workboat, and sailboats—navigate the offshore and nearshore waters. A dashed bright-green navigation track leads into a river/estuary channel. An unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV/drone) flies over the ocean on the left, a fighter jet flies near the lower coast, and a small aircraft approaches the coastal bay.
- **Three Inset Panels:**
  - **Top Left Inset (Hydrodynamic Mesh Model):** A finite-element triangular mesh model of an estuary and river system (resembling the Piscataqua River / Great Bay estuary in New Hampshire/Maine), colored from dark blue in the main channel to cyan and green in the upper tributaries.
  - **Top Center Inset (Electronic Navigational Chart):** A zoomed-in ENC view of a harbor channel showing tan land with building footprints, light blue dredged channel areas, yellow buoy symbols, a green navigation aid triangle, and a pink vessel icon.
  - **Bottom Left Inset (Water-Level Time Series Plot):** A line graph of tidal water levels over two tidal cycles, with an orange oval highlighting a sharp anomaly/spike at the peak of the second high tide.
