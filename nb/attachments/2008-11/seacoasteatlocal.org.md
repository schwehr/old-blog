# Seacoast Eat Local Promotional Graphic (`seacoasteatlocal.org.png`)

## Overview

A promotional web banner graphic from `seacoasteatlocal.org` highlighting local food guides, food pantry donation programs, and Holiday & Winter Farmers' Markets in the Seacoast region of New Hampshire and southern Maine.

## Layout & Visual Elements

Set against a light powder-blue background, the graphic consists of a header logo, two rounded promotional cards in the middle, and a dark slate-blue banner across the bottom:

- **Top Header:**
  - **Left Logo:** A rounded blue square with a dark brown border showing a green map outline of New Hampshire and eastern Massachusetts overlaid with a rust-red circle centered on the New Hampshire Seacoast region.
  - **Right Title:** `SEACOAST EAT LOCAL` in tall, stylized black sans-serif capital letters.
- **Middle Row (Two Rounded Cards):**
  - **Left Card (`SEACOAST HARVEST`):** A white square card with a thin tan border showing an illustrated red barn with white cross-braced doors and a weathervane set amid rolling green fields, a stone wall, and an apple tree under a blue sky, titled `SEACOAST HARVEST` at the top and subtitled `A LOCAL FOOD GUIDE` at the bottom.
  - **Right Card (`Share`):** A horizontal card with a photograph of fresh root vegetables (red beets, carrots, yellow squash, and purple cabbage) on the left and black text on a white background on the right reading:
    - `Food pantries & soup kitchens that accept donations of fresh food >`
    - `Share` (in light gray cursive script at the bottom right).
- **Bottom Banner:**
  - A dark slate-blue rectangle with a black border containing light blue text:
    - **Left:** `Holiday & Winter Farmers' Markets` in large bold serif text.
    - **Right:** Three stacked navigation links:
      - `participating farms >`
      - `locations, dates, + directions >`
      - `locally grown foods for the winter >`
