# University of Alaska Autonomous Aircraft Launch from NOAA Ship Oscar Dyson (`univ-of-alaska-uas-1.jpg`)

## Overview

A composite side-by-side photograph showing a University of Alaska unmanned aircraft system (UAS)—an Insitu ScanEagle autonomous fixed-wing drone—being launched from the deck of the NOAA fisheries research vessel *Oscar Dyson* in October 2008 (left) and an aerial photograph of the *Oscar Dyson* taken from the aircraft in flight (right).

## Scene Description

- **Left Panel (Catapult Launch from Deck):**
  - Viewed from the aft deck of the *Oscar Dyson* under an overcast gray sky, a swept-wing, tail-less autonomous aircraft with twin vertical wingtip stabilizers is captured mid-air immediately after launching from a pneumatic wedge catapult launcher mounted on the ship's fantail.
  - A crew member wearing a white hard hat and a yellow-and-black jacket stands in the right foreground with their back to the camera, watching the aircraft climb away from the ship.
  - On the left side of the deck, a white spherical satellite communications radome labeled `NERA` is mounted atop a brown mast pedestal. On the right edge, a brown metal mast ladder rises vertically.
- **Right Panel (Aerial View from the UAS):**
  - A grainy, oblique aerial camera frame looking down at the stern quarter of the white-hulled NOAA ship *Oscar Dyson* underway in dark green-gray ocean swells.
  - The vessel's name `OSCAR DYSON` is painted in large black letters along the port quarter above the stern ramp and twin white stern gantry towers.
