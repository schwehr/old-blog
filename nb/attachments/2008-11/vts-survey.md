# USCG Vessel Traffic Service (VTS) Customer Satisfaction Survey (`vts-survey.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the online U.S. Coast Guard (USCG) Customer Satisfaction Survey of Vessel Traffic Services (`http://www.navcen.uscg.gov/vts2008/survey.aspx`), showing questions 1 through 7 organized under themed assessment categories.

- **Section 1: `Acquiring information`** (red underlined subheading on a gray bar)
