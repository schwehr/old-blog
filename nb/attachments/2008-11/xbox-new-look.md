# Xbox.com Promotional Banner for "The New Xbox Experience" (`xbox-new-look.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the promotional hero banner on `xbox.com` in November 2008 advertising the launch of "The New Xbox Experience" (NXE) dashboard update for the Xbox 360, featuring 3D customizable Avatars and integrated Netflix streaming.

## Visual Elements & Text

- **Top Navigation Bar:**
  - A light gray horizontal menu bar with dark gray links: `Xbox 360`, `Games`, `Accessories`, `Xbox LIVE`, `Support`, and `Community`.
- **Main Banner Background & Headline:**
  - Set against a curved sage-green backdrop decorated with faint concentric circles and a reflective light-gray floor, large white sans-serif text reads:
    - `The New Xbox Experience`
    - `Available Now!`
- **3D Xbox Avatars:**
  - **Left:** Two cartoon-style 3D avatars—one in the foreground wearing glasses, a light blue t-shirt, khaki cargo pants, and white sneakers pointing his left index finger upward; and another standing behind him in a red graphic t-shirt and dark jeans making a rock-on hand gesture.
  - **Right:** A bald, bearded 3D avatar wearing a white open collared shirt over a gray undershirt, blue jeans, and gray shoes, raising both arms in celebration.
- **Carousel of Featured Content Tiles (Center):**
  - Five rounded rectangular tiles recede diagonally from left to right with glossy floor reflections:
    1. **`Lips`:** Yellow-gold tile showing a young man and woman singing into wireless microphones.
    2. **`NETFLIX`:** Signature dark red tile displaying the white `NETFLIX` logo in bold capital letters.
    3. **Xbox Avatars Tile:** Shows three 3D avatars posing together.
    4. **`ROCK` (`Scene It?` / Movie Trivia Tile):** Shows three people laughing together on a couch.
    5. **`Guitar Hero World Tour`:** Yellow-and-red game art tile at the far right.
