# Zort Labs netblox Internet Filtering System (`zort-labs-netblox.png`)

## Overview
A promotional product graphic from the Zort Labs website illustrating the **netblox™** hardware internet filtering appliance and its key features. The blog post notes that several colleagues from the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM)—Brian L., Jim C., and Nathan P.—left to start Zort Labs and develop this inline network security device.

## Layout & Visual Elements
- **Left Column (Feature Highlights)**: Three vertically stacked feature blurbs on a light off-white background, each accompanied on the left by a rounded-square glossy orange icon with white line art:
  1. **Clock Icon**: A circular clock face showing hands at roughly 12:00 and 3:00.
  2. **Wrench Icon**: A diagonal open-end wrench silhouette.
  3. **Shield with Hand Icon**: A security shield containing an upright open palm ("stop" gesture).
- **Right Side (Product Hardware & Traffic Diagram)**:
  - In the upper right sits a three-quarter perspective photograph of the **netblox** hardware unit: a compact, matte-black square plastic enclosure with a recessed checkmark/logo on the top lid, a circular DC power jack on the left of the rear/front face, two side-by-side RJ-45 Ethernet ports with metallic shielding, and small ventilation slots along the bottom edge.
  - Entering the left Ethernet port is a wide diagonal band split into two color-coded streams:
    - **Upper Stream (Red/Orange Gradient)**: Labeled **"Blocks"** on the left and **"Malware / Pornography / Viruses / and much more..."** near the port, stopping at the edge of the device.
    - **Lower Stream (Green Gradient)**: Labeled **"Lets safe content through"**, entering the left Ethernet port, making a right-angle turn inside the box, and exiting as a solid green band from the right Ethernet port.
