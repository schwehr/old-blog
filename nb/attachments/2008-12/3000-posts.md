# Monthly Blog Post Frequency Chart for 3,000th Post (`3000-posts.png`)

## Overview
A vertical bar chart illustrating the monthly posting frequency on Kurt Schwehr's NanoBlogger weblog from July 2003 through December 2008, created to commemorate reaching his 3,000th blog post on December 1, 2008.

## Plot Details
- **Chart Type**: Vertical bar graph on a white background with thin gray horizontal grid lines and black/gray axes.
- **Horizontal Axis (Time)**: Spans from **Jul 2003** on the far left to late **2008** on the far right, with major tick labels oriented vertically at one-year intervals:
  - `Jul 2003`
  - `Jul 2004`
  - `Jul 2005`
  - `Jul 2006`
  - `Jul 2007`
  - `Jul 2008`
  - Smaller tick marks appear every two months along the baseline.
- **Vertical Axis (Post Count)**: Runs from `0` at the bottom to `140` at the top, with horizontal grid lines every 20 units and numeric labels at `0` and `100`.
- **Data Trends (Bright Blue Bars)**:
  - **Jul 2003 – Late 2004**: Very low monthly activity (typically 0 to 15 posts per month, with a few months in mid-2004 having zero or near-zero posts).
  - **Late 2004 – Early 2006**: A sharp surge in blogging frequency, with several months reaching between 50 and 100 posts per month (peaking right at 100 around September 2005 and February 2006).
  - **Mid-2006 Dip**: A brief drop in mid-2006 (down to ~3 posts in July 2006) followed by a steady climb.
  - **Late 2006 – Late 2008**: Sustained high output averaging roughly 50 to 90 posts per month, culminating in an all-time peak of nearly 120 posts in October/November 2008.
