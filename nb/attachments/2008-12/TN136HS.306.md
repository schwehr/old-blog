# R/V Thomas G. Thompson Hydrosweep DS Multibeam Swath Plot (`TN136HS.306.png`)

## Overview
A color-shaded multibeam bathymetry swath map generated using MB-System (`mbm_plot`) and GMT from an old Atlas Hydrosweep DS dataset (`TN136HS.306`, MBIO format 21) collected aboard the *R/V Thomas G. Thompson* (cruise `TTN136B`) off the Eel River / Humboldt area of northern California in November 2001.

## Map & Plot Details
- **Title**: Centered at the top in sans-serif black text: `Data File TN136HS.306`.
- **Geographic Extent & Map Frame**:
  - **Longitude (Horizontal Axis)**: Spans from `-125°00'` on the west (left) to `-124°22'` on the east (right), labeled every 5 minutes (`-125°00'`, `-124°55'`, `-124°50'`, `-124°45'`, `-124°40'`, `-124°35'`, `-124°30'`, `-124°25'`) along both top and bottom alternating black-and-white GMT map borders.
  - **Latitude (Vertical Axis)**: Spans from roughly `40°35'`N at the bottom to `40°51'`N at the top, labeled every 5 minutes (`40°40'`, `40°45'`, `40°50'`) along the left and right borders.
- **Swath Bathymetry & Ship Track**:
  - The survey swath runs WNW–ESE across the continental slope and shelf off Humboldt County.
  - On the western (left) end (`-124°58'` to `-124°48'`), the swath is wide and colored in deep blues and cyan, representing water depths of 2,100 to 2,800 meters.
  - Moving eastward (`-124°48'` to `-124°30'`), the seafloor shoals up the continental slope, transitioning through green and yellow (`1,200–1,800 m`) into orange and light tan (`300–900 m`), while the swath width narrows correspondingly with shallower depth.
  - Around `-124°28'`, `40°40'`N, the vessel executes a tight, overlapping series of loops/figure-eight turns in shallow water (`< 300 m`) before heading northeast toward `-124°23'`, `40°44'`N.
  - A thin black trackline runs down the center of the swath, marked with `x` symbols and time/Julian-day annotations including `01:00`, `02:00`, `00:00/306`, `06:00`, `09:00/306`, and `10:00`.
- **Color Scale Bar**:
  - Positioned horizontally below the map frame, labeled `Depth (meters)` at the bottom.
  - Ranges from `0` (white/light peach) on the left to `3000` (dark blue) on the right, with tick marks every 300 meters (`0`, `300`, `600`, `900`, `1200`, `1500`, `1800`, `2100`, `2400`, `2700`, `3000`).
