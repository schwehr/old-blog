# Dan Goldman's Interactive Video Object Manipulation at Adobe Research (`adobe-dan-goldman-video-manip.png`)

## Overview
A three-panel composite screenshot illustrating research by Dan Goldman at Adobe Research on **Video Visualization and Interaction / Interactive Video Object Manipulation**, where users can track, annotate, and deform moving objects directly within video sequences.

## Panel Descriptions
- **Left Panel (Particle/Feature Point Tracking)**:
  - Shows two people seen from behind walking away from the camera, overlaid with a dense cloud of bright yellow feature-tracking dots across the background wall and floor, and color-coded red (left person) and magenta/purple (right person in a hoodie) point clouds segmenting the moving foreground figures.
- **Center Panel (Overhead Trajectory Annotation)**:
  - A high-angle view looking down at an indoor atrium or café floor with round white tables and metal chairs.
  - A person in a yellow jacket and dark pants walks toward the lower left; a wide, semi-transparent yellow-green curved arrow is composited onto the floor surface, tracing their walking path from the upper left table area down toward the bottom right corner.
  - A partial video timestamp box (`01:23`) is visible at the bottom edge.
- **Right Panel (Facial Feature Deformation)**:
  - A close-up portrait of a young man with dark hair facing the camera against a brick wall.
  - Interactive control handles are overlaid on his face: a yellow dot with a short white vector line on the right side of his nose, a yellow dot on his chin, and a ring of eight outward-pointing yellow arrows (a radial expansion/scaling widget) centered on the left corner of his mouth, with a white mouse cursor arrow hovering over the center dot.
