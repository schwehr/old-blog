# AGU 2008 Fall Meeting Poster Hall (`agu-posters.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle photograph looking out over half of the massive poster hall at the December 2008 American Geophysical Union (AGU) Fall Meeting in Moscone Center, San Francisco.

## Scene Description
- **Poster Rows & Aisles**:
  - Dozens of long, parallel rows of gray fabric-covered poster boards stretch from the foreground deep into the background of the cavernous exhibition hall.
  - Each board is covered with multi-panel scientific posters featuring maps, plots, satellite imagery, and text columns.
  - Red carpet covers the main cross-aisle in the foreground and the narrow aisles between the poster rows.
- **Attendees**:
  - Hundreds of scientists and conference attendees are gathered in the aisles, standing in small groups discussing posters, walking with shoulder bags and lanyards, or seated near the end-caps of the rows on the far left.
- **Hall Architecture & Numbered Banners**:
  - Large square off-white structural columns rise to a high ceiling illuminated by warm overhead lighting fixtures.
  - Hanging from the ceiling along the right and rear aisles are large orange-and-white vertical section banners displaying row numbers in bold stencil digits: `1000`, `1100`, `1200`, `1400`, `1500`, `1600`, `1700`, and `1800`.
