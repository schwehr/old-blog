# Scratched and Crazed Commercial Airplane Window (`airplane-window.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph taken from a passenger seat looking out through a heavily crazed, scratched, and residue-streaked acrylic airplane window while parked at an airport gate. In the accompanying blog post, Kurt contrasts this "pretty miserable view" with a scenic aerial photograph of mist in the Cascade Mountains shot through the exact same window during flight.

## Scene Description
- **Window Surface**:
  - The entire frame is dominated by dense, concentric and vertical patterns of fine crazing, scratches, and dried water/de-icing fluid streaks on the outer plastic pane, catching the light in pale whitish-gray arcs that curve around the lower center of the window.
- **Background Gate Equipment**:
  - Seen dimly and out of focus through the semi-opaque window surface is the concrete airport tarmac marked with red and yellow ground-guidance lines.
  - A bright yellow tubular ground-support duct or towbar/hose assembly curves across the lower half of the frame above a green hose.
  - In the upper right, the gray underside and yellow safety railing of a passenger jetway (jet bridge) are visible, along with a small white-and-red warning placard and blue rectangular markings.
