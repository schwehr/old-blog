# Ben Smith's Dinghy-Based Single-Beam Hydrographic Survey (`ben-smith-hydrography.png`)

## Overview
A composite graphic showing survey tracklines and the resulting contoured bathymetric chart produced by Ben Smith's home-brew dinghy-based single-beam sonar system (a Humminbird WAAS-GPS/fishfinder and Persistor ASCII datalogger mounted on a 12-foot RIB from the sailing vessel *S/V Mother of Perl* in the Caribbean).

## Plot & Map Details
- **Left Half (Raw & Filtered Survey Tracklines)**:
  - **Top-Left Plot**: Dense, crisscrossing red survey lines plotted on a white background, showing high-frequency horizontal jitter/noise along the tracks.
  - **Bottom-Left Plot**: The same sinusoidal/curving survey pattern after smoothing or filtering, showing clean, continuous red tracklines running back and forth across the survey area above a black horizontal axis with tick marks.
- **Right Half (Gridded & Contoured Bathymetric Map)**:
  - Displays a triangular/fan-shaped bathymetric grid set against a solid medium-gray background within a black-and-white tick-marked border.
  - **Color Ramp & Contours**:
    - **Shallow Areas (East, South, and Northwest Margins)**: Colored in warm tans, browns, and light yellows/greens, representing shallow reef or shoal depths (with red contour labels reading `-4.000000` and `-6.000000`).
    - **Intermediate Slopes**: Transition through cyan and light blue with closely spaced black contour lines.
    - **Deep Basin / Channel (Center and Northeast)**: Colored in rich dark blues androyal blue, representing the deepest parts of the anchorage/channel, with red contour labels indicating depths down to `-16.000000`, `-18.000000`, and `-22.000000`.
  - A regular grid of tiny white dots (grid reference marks) is faintly visible across the map area.
