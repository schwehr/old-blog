# Portrait of Colin Ware in Front of Global Ocean Flow Visualization (`colin-globalflow.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up portrait photograph taken by Kurt Schwehr of University of New Hampshire (UNH) professor and CCOM Visualization Lab director **Colin Ware** standing in front of a large display showing his animated global ocean currents visualization, which was featured in a December 14, 2008 *Foster's Daily Democrat* article on mapping ocean currents for the Smithsonian Institution.

## Scene Description
- **Subject (Colin Ware)**:
  - Centered in the foreground, facing the camera directly from the chest up.
  - He has a bald top of the head with short gray hair on the sides, a trimmed gray-and-white beard and mustache, oval wire-rimmed glasses, and wears a dark collared button-down shirt.
  - Warm side lighting illuminates his forehead, nose, and glasses frames from the right.
- **Background Visualization**:
  - Behind him is a vibrant, high-resolution visualization of global ocean circulation over dark landmasses.
  - Continental coastlines are outlined in glowing white and electric blue, while ocean currents—including a prominent equatorial flow streak running horizontally behind his glasses—are rendered as dense, swirling particle streamlines in shades of bright blue, cyan, yellow, and green.
