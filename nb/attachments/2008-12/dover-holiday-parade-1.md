# Vintage Farmall 1206 Turbo Tractor in the Dover Holiday Parade (`dover-holiday-parade-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A street-level photograph taken during the **Dover Holiday Parade** in Dover, New Hampshire, in late November 2008 during the first snowfall of the season, showing a restored red and cream vintage **Farmall 1206 Turbo** tractor driving down the parade route.

## Scene Description
- **Main Subject (Tractor & Driver)**:
  - A classic red **International Harvester / Farmall 1206 Turbo** open-cab farm tractor moves from right to left along a wet asphalt street marked with double yellow center lines.
  - The tractor features a bright red engine hood and chassis with a cream-white side panel bearing the black-and-silver `FARMALL` badge, white cursive `Turbo` script near the front, a `1206 DIESEL` emblem near the bottom of the cowling, a tall vertical black exhaust stack, and a small red rectangular plaque mounted below the frame reading `TOM & LISA WATT`.
  - The front grille is decorated with a festive Christmas evergreen wreath with a red bow.
  - A male driver wearing a dark cap with a red brim, light tan jacket, dark pants, and gloves sits high on the operator's seat steering the tractor.
- **Spectators & Background**:
  - In the immediate foreground, the backs of two spectators' heads (one wearing a light pink winter jacket) frame the bottom of the shot.
  - Along the far sidewalk on both the left and right, bundled-up parade spectators—including children in pink and striped winter coats—watch the procession in front of local commercial and residential buildings under an overcast sky with faint falling snow streaks.
