# Tangled Thicket of Ice-Coated Branches in NH Ice Storm (`freezing-rain-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A detailed close-up photograph from the December 12, 2008 New Hampshire ice storm, capturing a dense, crystalline tangle of fine shrub twigs heavily glazed in clear ice.

## Scene Description
- **Visual Details**:
  - Filling the entire frame is a complex, overlapping lattice of thin reddish-brown and orange woody twigs, each encased in a thick cylinder of clear, refractive glaze ice.
  - Small frozen icicle drips hang from the undersides of the horizontal branch segments, creating a glass-like texture across the bush.
  - Near the lower right corner, a pale tan dried leaf is trapped within the icy branches.
