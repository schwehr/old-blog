# Red Berries Encased in Ice After Freezing Rain (`freezing-rain-3.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up outdoor photograph taken on December 12, 2008, during a major freezing rain and ice storm in New Hampshire (likely Dover or Durham). The image captures bright red berries and bare woody shrub branches (such as Japanese barberry) completely encased in thick, translucent shells of clear glaze ice.

- **Foreground Branches & Berries**: A dense network of reddish-brown and gray woody twigs stretches diagonally and horizontally across the frame. Hanging from the branches are numerous small, oval, vibrant red berries.

Many of the ice shells around the red berries taper downward into small icicle points or frozen water droplets at their tips, magnifying and refracting the bright red color inside.
