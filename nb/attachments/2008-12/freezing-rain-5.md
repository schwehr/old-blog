# Ice-Encased Fern-Like Branch in Front of a House (`freezing-rain-5.jpg`)

## Overview

A vertical close-up photograph taken on December 12, 2008, during a New Hampshire ice storm. It highlights a heavily iced, arching shrub branch with parallel side shoots resembling a ribcage or fern frond, with a two-story green house visible in the background.

## Scene Description

- **Central Ice Formation**: In the center of the frame, a main woody stem curves toward the viewer with symmetrical lateral twigs branching off to the left and right. Thick, clear glaze ice encases every twig, fusing several adjacent shoots together and forming short, blunt icicles hanging from their tips. The dark reddish-brown bark and small red buds or berries remain visible inside the transparent ice tubes.
- **Foreground Detail**: At the very bottom of the frame, an out-of-focus branch extends horizontally right in front of the lens, showing bulbous, rounded ice nodules encasing brownish-red leaves or berries.
- **Background House**: Above the tangle of icy bushes, the upper story and roofline of a house with sage-green clapboard siding are visible against an overcast white sky. A double window with white trim and brick-red/orange shutters sits beneath the eaves, alongside a projecting porch or entryway roof overhang on the left.
