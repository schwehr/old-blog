# GeoDjango Website Banner and Logo (`geodjango-fink.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the header and hero graphic from the official GeoDjango website (`geodjango.org`), posted on December 7, 2008, to accompany a blog entry announcing the packaging of GeoDjango (`django-gis-py25`) for the Fink project on Mac OS X.

## Graphic & Layout Details

- **Top Navigation Bar**: A dark forest-green horizontal navigation bar runs across the top edge, displaying light yellow-green text links:
  - `django`
  - `wiki`
  - `mercurial`
  - `docs`
  - `presentations`
  - `vmware (user: geo, pass: where2.0)`
- **Background Bands**: Below the navigation bar, the background is divided into three horizontal green bands: a dark forest green at the top, a bright olive/chartreuse green in the middle, and a muted sage green at the bottom, with subtle concentric circular rings radiating behind the central globe.
- **Speech Bubble & Title**: A large, rounded white speech bubble with a thick dark-green border sits in the upper center, pointing downward toward the North American continent on the globe below. Inside the bubble:
  - **`geodjango`** in large, bold, dark-green lowercase sans-serif lettering.
  - **`A world-class geographic web framework`** in smaller light-green serif text underneath.
- **Pixelated Globe**: Centered in the lower half is a glossy, circular Earth globe rendered with a fine grid/mosaic pixel texture, showing North and South America, the Pacific and Atlantic Oceans, and white cloud swirls, encircled by a dark green ring.
