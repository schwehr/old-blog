# Google Reader Settings After Importing Subscriptions (`google-reader-imported.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the **Settings** page in Google Reader, posted on December 22, 2008, showing the list of 87 RSS/Atom feeds imported via an OPML file exported from the Vienna newsreader, with a hand-drawn purple oval highlighting the `Import/Export` tab.

## Interface & Controls

- **Header & Settings Navigation**:
  - Top-left displays the `Google Reader` logo.
  - Inside a warm yellow-orange banner header, the title **Settings** appears next to a `« Back to Google Reader` link.
  - Five navigation tabs sit along the bottom of the yellow header: `Preferences`, **Subscriptions** (active tab with a white background), `Folders and Tags`, `Goodies`, and `Import/Export` (circled with a thick magenta/purple annotation).
- **Bulk Action & Filter Bar**:
  - Left side: `Select: All 87 subscriptions, None, Unassigned`, a `More actions...` dropdown menu, and a rounded `Unsubscribe` button.
  - Right side: A text input field labeled underneath with `Filter by name, tag, or URL`.
