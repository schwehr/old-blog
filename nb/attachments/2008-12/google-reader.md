# Reading RSS Feeds in Google Reader (`google-reader.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the Google Reader web application running in Mozilla Firefox on Mac OS X, posted on December 22, 2008, as part of a blog entry discussing migrating RSS/Atom subscriptions from the Vienna desktop newsreader to Google Reader.

## Interface & Controls

- **Browser Window & Header**:
  - **Window Title**: `Google Reader (321)`
  - **Address Bar**: `http://www.google.com/reader/view/#stream/user%2F0726749611439173395` with RSS and bookmark star icons, plus a Google search bar on the right.
  - **Top Google Bar**: Links on the left for `Gmail`, `Calendar`, `Documents`, `Photos`, **Reader** (selected in bold black), `Web`, and `more ▼`; links on the right for `Settings`, `My Account`, `Help`, and `Sign out`.
  - **Search Bar**: Beneath the Google Reader logo sits a text input box, a dropdown menu set to `All items`, and a `Search` button.
- **Left Sidebar**:
  - **Top Section**: An `+ Add a subscription` button followed by navigation links: `Home`, **All items (321)** (highlighted in light blue), `Starred items`, and an expanded `Your stuff` section (`Shared items`, `Notes`, `Trends`, `Browse for stuff`), plus a `Start sharing` button beneath a note reading *"Your shared items will be visible to your Google Friends. Learn More"*.
  - **Subscriptions List**: A scrollable pane listing RSS feeds with unread counts in bold:
    - **Personal aggregator (1)**
    - `Phoenix Mars Mission ...` (two entries)
    - `pogma`
    - `Portsmouth Harbour Pr...`
    - **PyPI recent updates (80)**
    - `RealityPrime`
    - `Roberto De Almeida - ...`
    - `rob's weblog`
    - **Schneier on Security (3)**
    - A `Manage subscriptions »` link at the bottom left.

- **Stream Header & Toolbar**: Titled **All items** with view toggles `Show: Expanded - List` on the right. Below is a control bar reading `Show: 321 new items - all items` along with `Mark all as read`, `Refresh`, and `View settings...` buttons.
- **Active Post (Top)**: The bottom portion of an article discussing Python/Django form widgets (`captcha = forms.CharField(widget=NHTextInput())` and `captcha = forms.CharField(widget=NHTextInput)`), a bold subheading **Worth It?**, and two concluding paragraphs.
