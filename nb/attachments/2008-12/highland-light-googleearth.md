# Highland Light 3D Model Displayed in Google Earth (`highland-light-googleearth.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of Google Earth on Mac OS X taken on December 9, 2008, showing Christiana Arsenault's 3D model of Highland Light (Cape Cod Light in North Truro, Massachusetts) placed onto coastal terrain after being exported from Google SketchUp 7.

## Interface & Controls

- **Mac OS X Menu Bar & Window Header**: Shows the `Google Earth` application menu (`File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Tools`, `Add`, `Help`) at the top left and system status icons (MenuMeters network `Tx: 47B/s Rx: 2.7KB/s`, CPU `12%`) at the top right.
- **Top Toolbar**: Contains standard Google Earth icons for toggling the sidebar, adding a placemark, polygon, path, or image overlay, measuring distances (ruler), toggling sunlight and Sky mode, and emailing/printing/viewing in Google Maps.
- **Left Sidebar (`Places` Panel)**:
  - Under `My Places`: `SBNMS boundaries`, `71 18.2'N, 156 54.0W`, `Transponder`, and `Untitled Placemark`.
  - Under `Temporary Places`: **`SUPreview1`** (highlighted in gray), with description *"Created with Google SketchUp 7.0.8656"*, containing child items `Tour` and `Model` (both checked).
  - At the bottom of the `Places` pane is a playback slider with play and stop buttons; `Search` (collapsed at top) and `Layers` (collapsed at bottom) sit above and below.

## 3D Viewport Details

- **Highland Light Model**: Viewed from an oblique low-altitude angle looking toward the ocean cliff, the 3D model features the tall, cylindrical white lighthouse tower topped by a black lantern room and gallery deck on the right. Attached to the base of the tower via a covered walkway is the keeper's house with gray-shingled walls, white trim, dormer windows, chimneys, and brown pitched roofs. As noted in the blog post, the cylindrical tower shows vertical facet shading artifacts where surface normals were degraded during import/export.
- **Terrain & Status Bar**: The model sits on pale sandy paths and green coastal bluff imagery overlooking the dark blue Atlantic Ocean and a starry black sky at the horizon.
