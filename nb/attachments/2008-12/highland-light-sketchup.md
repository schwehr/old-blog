# Scaling and Placing Highland Light in Google SketchUp 7 (`highland-light-sketchup.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of **Google SketchUp 7** on Mac OS X (`highland.skp - SketchUp`), posted on December 9, 2008, showing Christiana Arsenault's 3D model of Highland Light being scaled and positioned atop a grayscale Google Earth terrain snapshot prior to exporting to Google Earth.

## Interface & Controls

- **Menu Bar & Toolbars**:
  - Mac OS X menu bar at top: `SketchUp`, `File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Camera`, `Draw`, `Tools`, `Window`, `Help`.
  - **Horizontal Window Toolbar**: Contains standard tools including Select, Line, Rectangle, Circle, Arc, Make Component, Eraser, Tape Measure, Paint Bucket, Push/Pull, Move (highlighted), Rotate, Follow Me, Orbit, Pan, Zoom, and Zoom Extents.
  - **Left Vertical Tool Palette**: Two-column tool palette with drawing, modification (Scale tool selected/depressed), measurement, walk/camera, and Google Earth integration icons.
- **Bottom Status Bar**:
  - Left: SketchUp/Google Earth icons and help prompt reading `Select a grip and move it to scale the objects. Option=Abou...`
  - Right: `Scale` input box displaying `1.04`.

## 3D Viewport Details

- **Highland Light Model & Scale Bounding Box**: The 3D model of the lighthouse tower (on the left) and attached keeper's house (on the right) is selected, displaying blue-violet wireframe edges over its textured surfaces. Surrounding the entire structure is a yellow 3D bounding box with bright green cubic scaling grips at the corners, edge midpoints, and face centers.
- **Google Earth Terrain Snapshot**: The model rests on a flat, grayscale satellite image of the paths and grounds around Highland Light, aligned with red, green, and blue 3D coordinate axes against a pale beige sky background.
