# Aerial View of Ammunition Bunkers at Hawthorne Army Depot, Nevada (`is-this-hawthorne.jpg`)

## Overview

An oblique aerial photograph taken through an commercial airplane window and posted on December 28, 2008. It shows the sprawling desert valley immediately south of Walker Lake, Nevada, covered with geometric grids of ammunition storage bunkers belonging to the Hawthorne Army Depot.

## Scene Description

- **Walker Lake Shoreline**: In the upper-left corner, the deep blue water and pale sandy shoreline of the southern end of Walker Lake are visible.
- **Hawthorne Army Depot Bunkers**: Across the center and right side of the frame, hundreds of small, earth-covered storage bunkers (igloos) are arranged in vast, orderly geometric arrays across the reddish-brown and tan desert floor. One group of bunkers in the lower-left center catches the bright sunlight, appearing as rows of pale tan dots, while extensive neighboring grids stretch across the darker, shadowed alluvial plain to the north and east.
- **Roads, Mountains & Lighting**: Straight roads, railway lines, and drainage channels crisscross the desert floor around the bunker fields. In the bottom-left corner, rugged, sunlit desert mountains rise sharply with eroded gullies. A dark vignette shadow obscures the right edge and bottom-right corner of the frame from the airplane window angle.
