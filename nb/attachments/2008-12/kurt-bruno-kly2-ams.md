# Composite of Kurt Schwehr Demonstrating the KLY-2 Kappabridge for AMS (`kurt-bruno-kly2-ams.png`)

## Overview

A four-panel composite image assembled from video stills and Flash animations created by Genevieve Tauxe for the Scripps Institution of Oceanography (SIO) Paleomagnetic Lab tour, posted on December 14, 2008. The image illustrates Kurt Schwehr demonstrating how to measure Anisotropy of Magnetic Susceptibility (AMS) using "Bruno," a KLY-2 Kappabridge instrument.

## Panel Descriptions

- **Top-Left Panel (Kurt Holding Sample)**: A video frame showing a young Kurt Schwehr in the SIO paleomagnetics laboratory wearing a gray hooded sweatshirt and a backward yellow baseball cap. He holds up a small cylindrical paleomagnetic rock core specimen in his left hand while explaining the measurement procedure, with scientific posters mounted on the wall behind him.
- **Top-Right Panel (Kappabridge Coil Schematic)**: A Flash diagram on a dark red background illustrating the internal induction coil mechanism of the KLY-2 Kappabridge:
  - On the left, a brown rock cube sample inside a white holder is lowered into a vertical black cylinder wrapped in gold wire coils, with a green upward arrow outside and a blue upward arrow inside.
  - In the center, a blue reference coil is wrapped in gold wire and connected to two analog dial gauges on the right labeled **`Current`** (top) and **`Offset`** (bottom).
- **Bottom-Left Panel (Magnetic Grain Alignment Diagram)**: An illustration of elongated, oval mineral grains in shades of yellow, tan, orange, and olive green packed together, with four horizontal blue arrows pointing to the right to represent the applied magnetic field during susceptibility measurement.
- **Bottom-Right Panel (Inserting Sample into the KLY-2)**: A video frame showing Kurt's hand lowering a rock core specimen into the top measurement cavity of the brown box-shaped KLY-2 Kappabridge unit sitting on a wooden laboratory bench.
