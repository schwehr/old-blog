# Lighting the Bonfire at Light Up Durham (`light-up-durham-1.jpg`)

## Overview

A nighttime camera-phone photograph taken on December 17, 2008, during the community "Light Up Durham" holiday celebration in the Durham Market Place ("DUMP") parking lot in Durham, New Hampshire. The image shows a tall, conical wooden bonfire structure just as the fire is catching inside its base.

## Scene Description

- **Bonfire Structure**: Dominating the center of the frame is a tall, teepee-like pyramid built from long wooden planks, boards, and logs leaning together at a steep angle. Inside the lower-left and central core of the wooden cone, bright yellow-orange flames are beginning to burn and illuminate the inner surfaces of the upright boards.
- **Spectators & Background**: To the right of the bonfire pyramid, several bundled-up onlookers stand in the dark watching the fire, with the warm orange glow reflecting off a person's face and a bright rectangular light or sign near their waist. Above the crowd on the right, parking lot streetlights shine brightly against the pitch-black night sky.
