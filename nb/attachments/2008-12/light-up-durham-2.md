# Crowd Gathered Around the Bonfire at Light Up Durham (`light-up-durham-2.jpg`)

## Overview

A wide nighttime camera-phone photograph posted on December 17, 2008, showing a crowd of spectators silhouetted around the blazing bonfire in the Durham Market Place parking lot during the "Light Up Durham" event in Durham, New Hampshire.

## Scene Description

- **Central Bonfire**: Slightly left of center, the conical pile of wooden planks is fully engulfed in bright white, yellow, and orange flames, sending a faint reddish-purple plume of smoke and sparks upward into the dark sky.
- **Silhouetted Crowd**: A dark ring of people stands in the foreground and midground encircling the fire to stay warm and watch the blaze.
- **Parking Lot Background**: Tall parking lot light poles glow on the far left and upper right, with a tall evergreen tree dimly illuminated near the right-center streetlight and strings of distant vehicle headlights or storefronts visible in the background.
