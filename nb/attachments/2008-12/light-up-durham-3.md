# Light Up Durham Bonfire (`light-up-durham-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A nighttime camera-phone photograph of the dying embers and remaining flames of a community bonfire in the Durham Market Place (DUMP) parking lot during the December 2008 "Light Up Durham" holiday event in Durham, New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Fire**: In the center-foreground, a stack of wooden planks and pallets burns brightly. Intense white and yellow-orange flames rise from the center of the pile, while purple-white glowing coals and embers illuminate the charred wooden slats underneath and to the right.
- **Spectators & Perimeter**: A horizontal caution tape or rope barrier stretches across the middle ground behind the fire. Several spectators stand behind the barrier in the dim light, dressed in winter jackets and hats, watching the flames. Motion blur from the slow nighttime shutter speed creates small white light trails near two of the figures on the right.
- **Background**: To the left behind the crowd, a parked SUV or car is visible near a brightly lit green-tinted storefront canopy (the Durham Market Place shopping center) and a streetlamp against the dark night sky.
