# Sunrise and Setting Moon over Manchester, NH (`manchester-nh.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect panoramic landscape photograph looking out over Manchester, New Hampshire, at sunrise on the morning after the December 2008 New England ice storm, with the full moon setting in a warm orange-pink sky.

- **Horizon & Hills**: Rolling silhouette hills and distant mountains (including prominent rounded peaks like Uncanoonuc Mountains) stretch across the horizon line in soft purple-brown tones.
- **Valley & Cityscape**: The lower portion of the image shows the wooded and urbanized valley around Manchester bathed in low-angle reddish-gold sunlight.
