# MarView Authenticated Home Page (`marview-home.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the authenticated home dashboard of the U.S. Department of Transportation Maritime Administration's **MarView** web portal (`marview.gov`), displaying navigation menus and six core Marine Transportation System (MTS) information categories.

## Navigation & Sidebar
- **Top Navigation Bar**: A dark slate-blue horizontal bar with links in gold text (with `Home` highlighted in white): `Home`, `MY MarView`, `Ports`, `Vessels`, `Waterways`, `Intermodal`, `Trade`, `Mariners`, and `Shipyards`.
- **Left Sidebar**:
  - **Workshop Panel**: A rounded slate-gray box titled `Workshop` containing four icon links:
    - `Data Sources` (database cylinder icon)
    - `Collaboration` (globe icon)
    - `Library` (file cabinet icon)
    - `Links` (chain-link icon)
  - **Crisis Tracking Button**: A prominent yellow-gold rounded button below the Workshop box with a traffic light icon and white text reading `Crisis Tracking`.

## Main Content & Category Grid
- **Header & Welcome Text**: Displays the `MarView` logo with a four-pointed blue compass star over the "V", followed by an introductory paragraph:
  > "Welcome to MarView. This site is designed to provide timely and accurate information related to the Marine Transportation System (MTS). This project, led by the U.S. Department of Transportation Maritime Administration, collects, stores and displays data on the MTS. Provided is the ability to fuse data together to create models and simulations for scenario planning in the event of a crisis. The ability to run economic impact scenarios, demand forecasting, capacity planning and plans for mitigating and reacting to emergence situations."
- **Six-Section Grid** (each with a square thumbnail photo and summary description):
  - **Ports**: Thumbnail of green port cranes. *"Physical attributes information on national and international ports and related facilities; Ports Authorities; Ownership; Cargo types and Ship types supported."*
  - **Trade**: Thumbnail of a cargo ship at a container terminal. *"Marine transportation information about waterborne domestic and foreign trade cargo, passenger and vessel movements."*
  - **Vessels**: Thumbnail looking down at a tanker ship's deck. *"Vessel characteristics including physical attributes and historical information. World fleet; U.S. Flag vessels; Jones Act vessels; Military Useful vessels; Vessel Status Card files."*
  - **Mariners**: Thumbnail of uniformed merchant mariners/officers in white dress uniforms saluting. *"Statistics on trained and licensed Mariners; qualifications requirements; Crewing information; USCG related information & regulations; TWIC compliance by region."*
  - **Waterways**: Aerial thumbnail of a river lock and dam bend. *"Physical attributes and real-time status information on Locks; Physical attributes of Waterways and Waterways facilities; Information on Dams and Bridges; Waterway navigation aids."*
  - **Shipyards**: Thumbnail of vessels in drydock at a shipyard. *"Information about domestic and foreign shipbuilding and repair yards. Shipbuilding facility attributes; Survey of U.S. Shipbuilding and Repair Facilities report."*
