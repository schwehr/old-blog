# MarView Waterways Lock Status and Virtual Earth Map (`marview-locks.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the `Waterways` section of the MarView web portal showing lock status information for the Harvey Lock in Louisiana (`LA` / `HARVEY`), where a browser layout bug in Firefox causes the embedded Microsoft Virtual Earth aerial map to render over the left side of the page.

## Map & Interface Details
- **Embedded Aerial Map (Left Half)**:
  - A vertical rectangular map widget positioned along the left side of the screen displays high-resolution aerial imagery of an urban and industrial corridor in Harvey, Louisiana.
  - Top map toolbar controls include `2D`, `3D`, `Road`, `Aerial` (selected), and `Labels`, along with a circular directional pan pad and `+` / `-` zoom magnifying glass icons in the upper left corner.
  - A red pushpin placemark sits in the upper-left-center of the aerial view along a street flanked by industrial warehouses, rows of circular storage tanks on the right, and dense residential blocks on the left (illustrating the blog post's observation that the pin marks the street address rather than the physical canal lock itself).
- **Page Content & Controls (Right Half)**:
  - **Top Banner & Navigation**: A space-themed globe banner with orbital track lines sits above the navigation bar (`Intermodal`, `Trade`, `Mariners`, `Shipyards`).
  - **Waterways Header**: `Waterways` heading with overlapping subtitle text (`...hysical attributes and real-time status information on Locks; Physical attri...` overlapping `Waterways Information`).
  - **Selection Dropdowns**: Two Mac OS X Aqua-styled dropdown menus selected to state `LA` and lock `HARVEY`.
  - **Status Section & Pie Chart**: Below the `...tatus` header, a large 3D-shaded circular pie chart is displayed in the bottom right. The chart is predominantly dark reddish-brown (approx. 88%), with two small slices at the top-left in burnt orange (approx. 6%) and teal-green (approx. 6%).
