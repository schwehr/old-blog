# MarView Vessels Section and Top 10 Vessel Companies Chart (`marview-vessels.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the `Vessels` tab on the U.S. Maritime Administration (MARAD) MarView portal (`marview.gov`), featuring links to the MARAD Vessel Status Cards database, downloadable world containership fleet PDF reports, and a 3D bar chart of the top 10 U.S. barge and vessel companies.

## Layout & Left Column
- **Navigation & Sidebar**: Top navigation bar with `Vessels` highlighted in white, plus the left-hand `Workshop` panel (`Data Sources`, `Collaboration`, `Library`, `Links`) and yellow `Crisis Tracking` button.
- **Vessel Header & Overlapping Text**: At the top of the main content area, the heading `Vessel` and subheading `Vessel Data` overlap slightly with a tanker thumbnail and introductory text (`S... Vessel characteristics including physical attributes and historical information. World fleet; U.S. Flag vessels; Jones Act vessels; Military Useful vessels; Vessel Status Card files.`).
- **Search Vessel Status Card**: A rounded card with a photo of the bow of a loaded container ship and descriptive text:
  > "The MARAD Vessel Status Cards System is a product of the Maritime Administration's Office of Statistical and Economic Analysis. It contains the complete Maritime Administration file of U.S. Flag Vessel Status Cards since 1920 (over 10,000), which has been transferred to both database (since 1995) and image format (before 1995)."
