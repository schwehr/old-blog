# U.S. Maritime Administration MarView Public Login Webpage (`marview.gov.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the public landing and login page of the U.S. Department of Transportation Maritime Administration (MARAD) **MarView** web portal (`http://marview.gov`), captured in Mozilla Firefox 3.0.4 on December 5, 2008. Because the site's CSS layout was designed specifically for Microsoft Internet Explorer, the dark blue-gray page footer bar renders prematurely across the middle of the page in Firefox, overlapping the bulleted text in the main content column.

## Interface & Layout Details
- **Top Banner**:
  - **Logo**: On the left against a white background, serif gold-and-black text reads **`MarView`** with a four-pointed blue compass rose star behind the letter "V".
  - **Banner Graphic**: Fading in toward the right is an oblique aerial photograph of a green coastal waterway or canal with white boat docks and waterfront buildings, bordered below by a slate-blue horizontal bar.
- **Left Column (`Login to MarView`)**:
  - Under the blue heading **`Login to MarView`**, two rectangular buttons are displayed side by side: a gray button labeled **`Login to MarView`** and a white-outlined button labeled **`Forgot my password`**.
  - Below the buttons is a dark blue promotional banner card showing an aerial photograph of a gray naval/MARAD roll-on/roll-off cargo vessel underway, accompanied by white text:
    > **WELCOME TO MARVIEW**
    > *MarView is an integrated, data-driven environment providing essential information to support the strategic requirements of the U.S. Marine Transportation System and its contribution to the economic viability of the Nation.*
- **Right Column (`Welcome to MarView`)**:
  - Under the heading **`Welcome to MarView`**, introductory paragraphs and two bulleted lists describe the portal's mission, data sources, and capabilities:
    - *"The U.S. Department of Transportation Maritime Administration is the proud owner of MarView, an integrated data-driven environment providing essential information to support the strategic requirement of the U.S. Marine Transportation System (MTS) and its contribution to the economic viability of the Nation."*
    - *"MarView provides the ability to fuse data together to create models and simulations for capacity planning, economic impact analysis, on-demand forecasting, plans for mitigating and reacting to emergency situations."*
    - **`Available information includes:`**
      - `• Over 2500 links of transportation data sources`
      - `• Most comprehensive collection of vessel tracking information through Automated Identification System (AIS) and Voluntary Observation Ships (VOS) program`
      - *(Partially obscured by overlapping footer)* Information on U.S. ports and related facilities, U.S. Waterways and facilities, Waterborne commerce/foreign trade, Intermodal freight transportation, and domestic and foreign shipbuilding and repair yards.
    - **`Capabilities include:`**
      - `• Crisis Tracking and Emergency Notification (CTEN)`
      - `• Geospatial information on the MTS and Intermodal Transportation`
      - `• Distance calculator`
      - `• Innovative electronic collaboration room`
      - `• Business intelligence tools for data analysis and data manipulation.`
    - Bottom call-to-action: *"This rich and varied array of data can be rapidly combined and recombined to assist meaningful analysis throughout the MTS, from end to end. **Registration is required.**"* followed by a rounded gray button labeled **`Register for MarView`**.
- **Overlapping Footer Bar**: A wide, semi-opaque slate-blue horizontal bar cuts across the center of the page (a Firefox CSS rendering bug), containing centered white text:
  - `MARAD.DOT.gov | DOT.gov | Contact Us | Privacy Policy | FOIA`
  - `USA.gov | WhiteHouse.gov | Regulations.gov | USAJobs.gov`
  - `For MarView Help Desk Support Email ServiceDesk@dot.gov or Call DOT Helpdesk 1-866-466-5221`
  - `Click Here for the User Feedback Form.`
  - `U.S. Maritime Administration • 1200 New Jersey Avenue, SE • Washington, DC 20590`
