# Aerial View of Mist and Snow in the Cascade Mountains, Washington (`mist-in-the-cascade-mountains.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph of the snow-capped Cascade Mountains in Washington State, taken through a heavily scratched and smudged commercial airliner window in early December 2008 and color-adjusted to emphasize the atmospheric haze, mist, and rugged alpine relief.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Midground Ridges**: Jagged, dark-shadowed mountain ridges and steep glacial valleys cross the lower and middle portions of the frame. Fresh white snow clings to the upper crags, chutes, and sun-facing slopes (particularly on the left and right flanks), while deep purple-blue shadows fill the lower valleys.
- **Valley Mist & Low Clouds**: Wisps and banks of soft, low-lying mist and stratus clouds weave between the dark mountain spurs in the central valley and along the lower-right ridgeline.
- **Background Peaks & Horizon**: In the upper-center distance, a prominent, heavily glaciated volcanic peak (such as Mount Baker or Glacier Peak) rises above the surrounding range, bathed in warm pinkish-gold sunlight against a hazy, rose-tinted horizon and sky.
- **Window Artifacts**: Subtle, soft vertical smudges and dark spots near the top center of the sky reflect the scratched, dirty aircraft window through which the photograph was captured.
