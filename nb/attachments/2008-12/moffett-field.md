# Black-and-White Aerial Photograph of Moffett Federal Airfield and the Santa Cruz Mountains (`moffett-field.jpg`)

## Overview
A black-and-white oblique aerial photograph taken from a commercial airplane flying near San Jose International Airport, looking west-southwest across **Moffett Federal Airfield (NASA Ames Research Center)**, Mountain View, and Sunnyvale toward the Santa Cruz Mountains on the San Francisco Peninsula. Kurt Schwehr converted the photograph to grayscale to overcome poor color casts from the aircraft window.

## Geographic & Structural Features
- **Moffett Federal Airfield & Hangars (Center)**:
  - **Parallel Runways**: The twin parallel concrete runways (14L/32R and 14R/32L) and taxiways of Moffett Field stretch horizontally across the center of the frame.
  - **Hangar One**: On the far (west/southwest) side of the runways near the center sits the iconic, massive, light-toned curved structure of **Hangar One**, a historic naval airship hangar.
  - **Hangars Two and Three**: Closer to the foreground on the east side of the runways (slightly right of center) stand the two large, rectangular, arched-roof blimp hangars (**Hangar 2** and **Hangar 3**), with their dark open doorway bays facing left (south-southeast).
  - **NASA Ames Campus**: Clusterings of large research buildings, wind tunnels, and office complexes occupy the northwestern end of the airfield to the right of Hangar One.
- **South San Francisco Bay Salt Ponds (Right)**: Along the middle-right edge of the frame, flat, calm expanses of salt evaporation ponds and marshland sloughs border the northern edge of Moffett Field along the shoreline of South San Francisco Bay.
- **Urban Grid & Highways (Foreground)**: The lower third of the image shows the dense urban and industrial grid of Sunnyvale and Mountain View, including U.S. Route 101 curving diagonally from the lower-left corner past the southern threshold of the Moffett runways, surrounded by office parks, warehouses, and residential neighborhoods.
- **Santa Cruz Mountains (Background)**: Beyond the wooded suburban foothills of Los Altos, Palo Alto, and Cupertino in the upper midground, the dark, continuous ridgeline of the Santa Cruz Mountains spans the entire horizon under a bright, hazy sky.
