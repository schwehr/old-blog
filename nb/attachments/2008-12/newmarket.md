# Winter Sunrise Over the Lamprey River and Newmarket Mills (`newmarket.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic winter sunrise photograph taken from 6 Bay Road in Newmarket, New Hampshire, looking across the partially frozen Lamprey River toward the historic brick and stone Newmarket Manufacturing Company mill buildings bathed in warm early-morning sunlight.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Riverbank & Fence):**
  - A black metal picket fence runs horizontally across the lower foreground, bordered by snow-dusted evergreen shrubs and bushes at the bottom left and right corners.
  - A small, symmetrical conical evergreen tree stands near the center-right foreground just behind the fence, and a tall, thin dark lamppost rises vertically to the left of center.
- **Middle Ground (Lamprey River):**
  - The dark, rippling water of the Lamprey River flows across the middle of the scene, interspersed with large flat sheets of snow-covered white ice, particularly upstream in the center distance where the river bends.
- **Background (Historic Mill Buildings & Treeline):**
  - On the right bank, the massive multi-story historic mill complex catches the bright golden-orange light of the rising sun.
  - Closest on the right is a tall four-story stone mill building with a snow-covered pitched roof, a square projecting stair tower, and a small rooftop cupola/weathervane at the far upper right edge.
  - Extending further upstream toward the center are red-brick mill wings with rows of tall multi-pane windows, a bridge or walkway crossing the water, and a white church steeple rising behind the brick roofs.
  - Further in the distance at the bend of the river, smaller white and tan town buildings sit along the snowy shoreline.
  - On the left bank, a dense stand of tall pine and bare deciduous trees is cast in cool morning shadow with snow covering the ground along the water's edge.
- **Sky:**
  - The morning sky transitions from soft pinkish-gray and warm apricot clouds on the left to clear blue sky at the upper right.
