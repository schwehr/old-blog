# Adobe Photoshop CS3 Layer Effects and Clipping Experiment (`photoshop-cs3.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from **Adobe Photoshop CS3** on Mac OS X demonstrating an experiment with text layers, clipping masks, drop shadows, glow effects, and brush strokes over a transparent checkerboard canvas, accompanied by the floating **Layers** palette on the right. Created following a New Hampshire Adobe User Group presentation by Penny Savage on December 9, 2008.

## Canvas & Visual Elements
- **Background**: Standard gray-and-white square transparency checkerboard filling the entire workspace.
- **Text & Graphic Elements**:
  - Top right: Large sans-serif text reading `How Do I Do This?` with a clipping mask applied (`Layer 4 copy`), coloring the black letters with blue and yellow brush strokes.
  - Upper left: The word `Photoshop` in solid blue-to-teal gradient-like coloring.
  - Upper middle-left: A second `Photoshop` text graphic rendered in a pale gray/beige fill with a dark drop shadow and beveled/outlined appearance, slightly overlapped on its right by blue text reading `Drop2` with a drop shadow.
  - Center left: Bright blue sans-serif text reading `Drop Shadow` with a soft dark gray drop shadow behind it.
  - Bottom left: A large, thick, airbrushed blue squiggle painted across the lower-left canvas, with yellow text reading `Backwards!` across it and dark black fuzzy airbrushed spots/shadows behind and to the right of the text.

## Layers Palette Details
The floating **Layers** panel on the right displays the layer stack from top to bottom (with `Normal` blend mode, `Opacity: 100%`, and `Fill: 100%`):
1. `Layer 4 copy` (clipped to the layer below, showing blue paint in its thumbnail)
2. `How Do I Do This? copy` (text layer `T`, underlined to indicate clipping base)
3. `Backwards!` (text layer `T`, clipped to `Layer 4` below)
4. `Layer 4` (pixel layer with blue paint, underlined as a clipping base)
5. `Drop2` (text layer `T` with `fx` icon, expanded to show `Effects` -> `Drop Shadow`)
6. `Drop Shadow` (currently selected/highlighted in light blue; text layer `T` with `fx` icon, expanded to show `Effects` -> `Drop Shadow`)
7. `Layer 3` (pixel layer with `fx` icon, expanded to show `Effects` -> `Drop Shadow`, `Inner Shadow`, `Outer Glow`, and `Inner Glow`)
- Along the bottom of the palette are the standard Photoshop layer icons: Link Layers, Add a layer style (`fx`), Add layer mask, Create new fill or adjustment layer, Create a new group, Create a new layer, and Delete layer (trash can).
