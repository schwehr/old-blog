# Ice-Encrusted Brush and Trees Outside CCOM (`post-storm-ice-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A closer-angle outdoor photograph taken outside the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) in Durham, New Hampshire, following the December 2008 ice storm. Intense sunlight from the left illuminates a thicket of ice-sheathed trees and bowed undergrowth on a hillside.

## Scene Description
- **Backlit Ice Canopy**: On the left edge, intense white sunlight floods through the trees, turning the ice-covered twigs and branches into a glowing, glittering white web. Towards the center and upper right, bare deciduous branches and evergreen boughs are thickly encased in translucent ice against patches of pale blue sky.
- **Bowed Undergrowth & Hillside**: Across the lower half of the frame, low bushes, brambles, and tall grasses on the bank are bent over into rounded,overlapping mounds under the heavy weight of the glaze ice. The low-angle sun catches the arched, icy stems on the left and center, while the lower-right corner transitions into darker greenish-brown shadow.
