# Aerial View Looking South Over La Jolla, Mission Bay, and San Diego Bay (`san-diego-la-jolla.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from an airplane window looking south over coastal San Diego, California, edited with contrast and color adjustments in Photoshop. The view encompasses La Jolla in the foreground, Mission Bay in the middle distance, and Point Loma, San Diego Bay, and Coronado in the sunlit background.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (La Jolla & Pacific Beach)**: In the lower left, a dark airplane window frame edge is briefly visible next to a curving freeway (Interstate 5) winding through the hills and canyons of La Jolla. Closer to the center and right, the rectangular street grid of Pacific Beach and coastal La Jolla meets the white surf line of the Pacific Ocean.
- **Middle Distance (Mission Bay & Point Loma)**: Just beyond the Pacific Beach grid lies Mission Bay, with its intricate network of dredged channels, islands, peninsulas, and the Mission Beach sandbar separating the bay from the ocean, flanked by the entrance channel jetties. Beyond Mission Bay rises the dark, elongated peninsula of Point Loma extending southward into the Pacific.
- **Background (San Diego Bay & Coronado)**: Left of Point Loma, the waters of San Diego Bay glow in warm golden-orange reflection under low sunlight. The curved Silver Strand and Coronado peninsula wrap around the bay, with the Coronado Bridge faintly visible on the far left and the hazy silhouettes of the Coronado Islands offshore in the upper right distance.
