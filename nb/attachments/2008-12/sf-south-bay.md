# Aerial View Looking Northeast Over the South San Francisco Bay (`sf-south-bay.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle morning aerial photograph taken from an airplane looking northeast across the southern tip of San Francisco Bay, California. The scene features the geometric salt evaporation ponds and wetlands along the bay shoreline in the foreground and middle ground, backed by the East Bay cities and the Diablo Range mountains in the distance.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Shoreline Industrial & Office Parks)**: Across the bottom edge of the frame, low-rise white commercial and tech office buildings, parking lots, and residential neighborhoods border the open brown fields and marshlands along the southern edge of the bay (near Sunnyvale/Mountain View/Alviso). One building facade near the bottom center catches a bright specular reflection of the morning sun.
- **Middle Ground (Salt Ponds & Sloughs)**: Extending from the left side across the center are large, flat, polygonal salt evaporation ponds divided by narrow earthen levees and winding tidal sloughs. The calm waters of the ponds reflect the pale blue and golden tones of the morning sky, bordered by reddish-brown marshland.
- **Background (East Bay & Mountains)**: Beyond the salt ponds, urbanized development spreads across the flat plain of Fremont, Milpitas, and North San Jose toward the base of the steep, ridged foothills of the Diablo Range (including Mission Peak) rising prominently on the right. Soft morning haze hangs in the valley, topped by wispy white clouds against a blue-and-peach sky.
