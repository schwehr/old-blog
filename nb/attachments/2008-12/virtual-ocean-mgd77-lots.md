# Dense MGD77 Cruise Track Lines in Virtual Ocean (`virtual-ocean-mgd77-lots.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Virtual Ocean 1.7.8** on Mac OS X displaying a high-altitude 3D perspective of the western United States and the Northeast Pacific Ocean, illustrating the massive density of MGD77 marine geophysical track lines (depth, gravity, and magnetics records) off the coast of California, Oregon, and Washington.

- **Landmass**: Looking eastward from the Pacific Ocean across the curved horizon of western North America against a black background. Prominent geographic features include the green-floored Central Valley of California on the right, the Sierra Nevada and Cascade mountain ranges (including a distinct snow-capped peak resembling Mount Shasta near the center), and the arid tan-colored Great Basin / Columbia Plateau further inland.

In several nearshore and ridge areas, the tracks are so concentrated that they form solid black patches.
