# Multibeam Coverage Mask in Virtual Ocean (`virtual-ocean-multibeam-mask.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Virtual Ocean 1.7.8** on Mac OS X demonstrating the multibeam coverage mask feature over the western United States and Northeast Pacific Ocean. An orange circle highlights the mask toggle button in the top toolbar, which dims areas lacking high-resolution multibeam sonar data to reveal where Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory (LDEO) has integrated submitted multibeam surveys into the global elevation model.

## 3D Scene & Coverage Mask
- **Perspective View**: Looking eastward from an altitude of `388km` over the Northeast Pacific (off Oregon and Northern California) toward the North American continent and the curved limb of the Earth against a black sky.
- **High-Resolution vs. Masked Areas**:
  - Bright, unmasked regions show where high-resolution multibeam bathymetry (and coastal/land elevation data) has been incorporated, clearly illuminating the continental shelf/slope, the Mendocino Fracture Zone, the Gorda and Juan de Fuca ridge systems, and individual transit swaths cutting across the dark grey-blue masked abyssal plains.
  - On land, a dark grey boundary line traces the edge of higher-resolution regional elevation coverage across California, Oregon, and Nevada.
- **Viewport Overlays**:
  - Top-right: 3D compass orientation ring.
  - Bottom-right: Scale bar labeled `222.2km`.
