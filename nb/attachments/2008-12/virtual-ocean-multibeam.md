# Multibeam Bathymetry Track Lines and Data Download in Virtual Ocean (`virtual-ocean-multibeam.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Virtual Ocean 1.7.8** on Mac OS X showing 3D shaded-relief bathymetry and multibeam sonar track lines off the Northern California coast (near Cape Mendocino, Eureka, and the Mendocino Fracture Zone). A specific ping file from the `MBARI98_eureka` expedition is highlighted in red, and the `Download selected ping file` button in the lower-right corner is outlined with a brown rectangle to show how users can download raw multibeam source files.

## 3D Scene & Bathymetry Details
- **Terrain & Seafloor**: Looking eastward across the Gorda Basin and Mendocino Fracture Zone toward the Northern California Coast Ranges and Central Valley. Submarine canyons, steep fault scarps, and continental slope gullies are clearly resolved in high-resolution shaded relief, accentuated by a vertical exaggeration of `7.25`.
- **Multibeam Track Lines**: Thin black and white ship track lines trace multibeam survey swaths across the abyssal plain and continental margin.
- **Selected Swath Segment**: On the continental slope left of center, a survey line segment is highlighted in bright red amidst white neighboring track lines.
- **Viewport Overlays**:
  - Top-right: 3D compass indicator ring.
  - Bottom-right of 3D view: Scale bar labeled `22.22km`.
