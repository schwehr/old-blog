# Selecting Single-Channel Reflection Profiles in Virtual Ocean (`virtual-ocean-single-chan-sel.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Virtual Ocean 1.7.8** on Mac OS X showing the application's cascading menu bar (`Available Data` -> `Select Data From Menu` -> `Custom Data Viewers` -> `Single Channel Reflection Profiles`) over a 3D globe view centered on North America and the North Atlantic Ocean.

## Menu Bar & Cascading Menus
- **Mac OS X Top Menu Bar**:
  - Left side: Apple logo, `Virtual Ocean` (bold), `File`, `Available Data` (highlighted in blue), `View`, `Places`, `Help`.
  - Right side system tray: Bluetooth/status icons, Wi-Fi indicator, MenuMeters network throughput readout (`Tx: 13B/s`, `Rx: 50B/s` in green and red), and dual CPU usage bar graphs (`18%`, `21%`).
- **`Available Data` Dropdown**:
  - `Open Searchable List...`
  - `Select Data From Menu` (highlighted in blue, opening a submenu).
- **`Select Data From Menu` Submenu**:
  - `Custom Data Viewers` (highlighted in blue, opening a third-level menu).
  - `General Data Viewers`
- **`Custom Data Viewers` Submenu**:
  - `Bathymetry, Gravity and Magnetic Profiles`
  - `Multibeam Bathymetry Swaths`
  - `Single Channel Reflection Profiles` (highlighted in blue)
  - `Multi-Channel Reflection Profiles`
  - `PetDB Bedrock Chemistry`
  - `Earthquake Locations and Epicenters`
  - `Earthquake Focal Mechanism Solutions`
  - `Drilling-DSDP/ODP/BRG`

## Globe View & Toolbar
- **Application Toolbar**: Shows the window title `Virtual Ocean 1.7.8`, the four standard left icon buttons (Save, List, Info `i`, and Mask), and the `VE:` slider.
- **3D Globe**: Displays the northern hemisphere of the Earth against a black background, featuring shaded-relief land topography and ocean bathymetry across North America, the Canadian Arctic Archipelago, Greenland, the Gulf of Mexico, the Caribbean, and the North Atlantic Ocean (including the Mid-Atlantic Ridge).
- **Color Scale Bar**: Along the upper-left edge of the black viewport is a vertical elevation/depth color bar segmented into purple-blue, light blue, tan, green-grey, and grey-white bands with tick marks on the right.
