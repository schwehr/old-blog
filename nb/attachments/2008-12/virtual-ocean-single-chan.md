# Single-Channel Seismic Reflection Profiles in Virtual Ocean (`virtual-ocean-single-chan.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Virtual Ocean 1.7.8** on Mac OS X displaying single-channel seismic (SCS) reflection survey tracks off the U.S. East Coast (Mid-Atlantic Bight, from Chesapeake and Delaware Bays north to New York/Long Island and Georges Bank) alongside a 2D seismic reflection profile image from the Lamont single-channel seismics data rescue project.

## Map & Track Line Details
- **Geographic Region**: Oblique view looking northward across the U.S. Atlantic continental margin. The dendritic estuaries of Chesapeake Bay and Delaware Bay are prominent on the left, Long Island and the Hudson Canyon sit near the top center, and the steep continental slope drops off into the blue-tinted western North Atlantic abyssal plain on the right.
- **Seismic Track Lines**:
  - Numerous black ship track lines radiate out from the New York / New Jersey harbor area across the continental shelf, slope, and rise.
  - A selected cruise track (`C0806` / Robert D. Conrad leg) is highlighted in blue extending southward across the shelf and back northward along the slope.
  - A specific segment along the southern outer shelf and continental slope is highlighted in thick red, corresponding to the seismic profile displayed in the bottom pane.
- **Viewport Overlays**:
  - Top-right: 3D compass ring with yellow and red markers.
  - Bottom-right: Scale bar labeled `222.2km`.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Toolbar**:
  - Window title: `Virtual Ocean 1.7.8`.
  - Left icon buttons: Save, List, Info (`i`), and Mask.
  - Vertical exaggeration slider: `VE:` set to `1`.
  - Coordinate readout: `Lat: 34.983°  Lon: -73.497°  Elev: -3932m  Alt: 631km`.
- **Right Control Panel (`Single Channel Reflection Profiles`)**:
  - Top buttons: `Close` and `Detach Table`.
  - Scrollable list under `All SCS Legs`: `C0707`, `C0708`, `C0801`, `C0802`, `C0803`, `C0804`, `C0805`, `C0806`, `C0807`, `C0808`.
  - Bottom action buttons: `Load Previously Digitized Products` and `View Profile`.
- **Bottom Seismic Profile Viewer**:
  - **Left Digitizing Toolbar**: Seven square icon buttons (Save disk, Horizon/layer icon, Selection arrow cursor, Polygon/line tool, T-bar tool, Zoom In magnifier `+`, Zoom Out magnifier `-`, and a second Save disk icon) above a text box reading `no objects`.
  - **Seismic Section**: A grayscale scanned single-channel seismic reflection profile showing horizontal and gently dipping sedimentary strata beneath the continental shelf on the left, transitioning sharply on the right into the steep continental slope drop-off, which is traced by a thin red digitized horizon line.
  - **Footer Attribution**: Logos for `Columbia LDEO` and `NOAA NGDC` next to the label `Lamont single-channel seismics data rescue project`.
