# Aerial Photograph of Walker Lake, Nevada (`walker-lake-nv.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from an airplane window looking across the snow-covered Wassuk Range toward **Walker Lake**, an endorheic desert lake in the Great Basin of western Nevada (Mineral County), bathed in warm low-angle sunlight.

## Scene & Geographic Details
- **Walker Lake**: Situated in the middle ground, the elongated, deep blue body of Walker Lake stretches diagonally from upper-left to lower-right within its arid basin floor.
- **Wassuk Range (Foreground)**: In the immediate foreground and along the left side of the frame, the rugged, steep-sided peaks and canyons of the Wassuk Range (including Mount Grant) are blanketed in bright snow. Warm golden-orange sunlight illuminates the upper ridges and eastern/southern slopes, while deep blue shadows fill the incised valleys on the shaded faces.
- **Surrounding Desert Basin**: Beyond the northern and eastern shores of the lake, the Walker River valley, Gillis Range, and surrounding Great Basin desert mountain ranges extend into a hazy, pale pink and tan distance. To the lower right (south of the lake), the flat desert valley floor where the Hawthorne Army Depot is located lies partially in dark shadow.
- **Airplane Window Framing**: A dark curved silhouette of the aircraft window frame or engine cowling is visible in the bottom-left corner, and a dark shadow/vignette obscures the lower-right corner.
