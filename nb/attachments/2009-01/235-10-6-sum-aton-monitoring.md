# AIS Binary Application Message Summary Form (`235-10-6-sum-aton-monitoring.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a Microsoft Word summary table (`235-10-6-sum-aton-monitoring.doc`) documenting an **AIS Binary Message Type 6 (DAC 235, FI 10)** used for **Aids to Navigation (AtoN) monitoring** by UK Trinity House, the Commissioners of Irish Lights, and the Northern Lighthouse Board. The form was created as a group project by the IALA AIS working group at their January 2009 meeting in Key West, Florida.
