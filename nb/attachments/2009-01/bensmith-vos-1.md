# VOS Vessel Tracks Across the North Pacific and Arctic Ocean in Google Earth (`bensmith-vos-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** on Mac OS X displaying Ben Smith's time-enabled KML visualization of **Voluntary Observing Ship (VOS)** tracks across the North Pacific and Arctic Oceans (`VOS tracks by callsign and date`), colored by vessel/callsign and spanning December 2000 to December 2008.

## 3D Globe & Vessel Track Details
- **Globe Orientation**: Centered over the North Pacific Ocean (`45°40'50.41" N`, `161°45'07.47" W`, `Eye alt 10082.86 km`), showing Japan and East Asia on the lower left, Kamchatka, the Bering Sea, and Alaska in the upper center, the Canadian Arctic Archipelago and Arctic ice cap at the top, the U.S./Canadian West Coast on the right, and the Hawaiian Islands in the lower right.
- **Trans-Pacific & Arctic Shipping Tracks**:
  - A dense bundle of pink, magenta, cyan, yellow, and green great-circle track lines spans the North Pacific between East Asian ports (Japan/China/Korea) and North American West Coast ports (Puget Sound/Vancouver, San Francisco/Oakland, Los Angeles/Long Beach), with many routes passing near the Aleutian Islands.
  - Several cyan and pink tracks branch south toward the Hawaiian Islands.
  - A prominent cluster of salmon-pink/coral track lines operates in the Beaufort Sea, Chukchi Sea, and Canadian Arctic Archipelago north of Alaska and western Canada.
- **Viewport Overlays**:
  - Top-left of viewport: World overview inset map with a red bounding rectangle over the Pacific and North America.
  - Top-right time slider: Ranges from `Dec 27, 2000` to `Dec 30, 2008`.
  - Far top-right: Standard Google Earth compass, pan, and zoom controls.
  - Bottom credits: `Image NASA`, `Image © 2009 TerraMetrics`, and `©2008 Google`.
