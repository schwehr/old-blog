# Canadian Vessel VOS Tracks in the Arctic Ocean in Google Earth (`bensmith-vos-arctic-canadians.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** on Mac OS X showing Voluntary Observing Ship (VOS) tracks compiled by Ben Smith across the **Beaufort Sea** and **Canadian Arctic Ocean**, featuring extensive high-latitude voyages by Canadian vessel callsign `CGBN` north of Alaska, Yukon, and the Northwest Territories.

## 3D Map & Vessel Track Details
- **Geographic Region**: Looking northward across the northern coast of Alaska and Yukon (divided by the vertical yellow U.S.–Canada border line at the 141st meridian west) and the Mackenzie River Delta (`70°11'27.52" N`, `136°28'59.50" W`, `Eye alt 415.17 km`) out over the deep blue waters of the Beaufort Sea and Canada Basin, with Banks Island (`Northwest Territories`) visible on the upper right.
- **Arctic Vessel Tracks (`CGBN`)**:
  - An intricate network of coral-pink track lines spans the Beaufort Sea from the coastal shelf north to the high Arctic pack-ice region.
  - In the lower right, near the Mackenzie Bay / Tuktoyaktuk coast, two pink square timestamped placemarks are labeled `CGBN@2008-08-19 00:00:00` and `CGBN@2008-08-17 00:00:00`.
- **Viewport Overlays**:
  - Top-left inset map: World map with a red box over the North American Arctic.
  - Top-right time slider: Spans `Dec 27, 2000` to `Dec 30, 2008`.
  - Right navigation controls: Compass ring, directional pad, and zoom slider.
  - Bottom scale bar & credits: Scale bar reading `117 km` on the lower left; `Image NASA`, `Image © 2009 TerraMetrics`, `© 2008 Europa Technologies`, `© 2008 Tele Atlas`, and `©2008 Google` on the lower right.
