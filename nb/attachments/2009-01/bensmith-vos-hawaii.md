# VOS Vessel Tracks Around the Hawaiian Islands in Google Earth (`bensmith-vos-hawaii.png`)

## Overview
A close-up screenshot from **Google Earth** showing Voluntary Observing Ship (VOS) tracks compiled by Ben Smith passing through the **Hawaiian Islands**, featuring a cyan inter-island cruise track (`8PSH`) visiting the islands in October 2008 and a magenta trans-Pacific track (`A8CN8`) from January 2007.

## Map & Vessel Track Details
- **Geographic Features**:
  - The main Hawaiian island chain stretches diagonally from **Niihau** and **Kauai** in the upper left, past **Oahu**, **Molokai**, **Lanai**, **Kahoolawe**, and **Maui** in the center, to the Big Island of **Hawaii** (labeled `Hawaii` in white text) in the lower right.
  - Yellow coastline outlines border each island, and shaded-relief submarine bathymetry (showing the Hawaiian Ridge, deep troughs, and surrounding seamounts) is visible in dark blue across the Pacific seafloor.
  - A green-and-blue globe/map icon sits over Kauai, and small grey rectangular high-resolution imagery patches appear offshore near Niihau, Molokai, and the southern tip of Hawaii.
- **Vessel Tracks & Timestamped Placemarks**:
  - **Callsign `8PSH` (Cyan Track)**: Traces a multi-island route approaching from the northeast, looping around Oahu (Honolulu) and Kauai, and sailing southeastward through the channels between Maui, Lanai, Kahoolawe, and the west coast of the Big Island (Kailua-Kona / Kawaihae), before heading back northwest and northeast. Four cyan square placemarks along the central islands are labeled:
    - `8PSH@2008-10-13 00:00:00` (north of Maui)
    - `8PSH@2008-10-14 00:00:00` (near Maui/Lanai)
    - `8PSH@2008-10-15 00:00:00` (off the Kohala/Kona coast of the Big Island of Hawaii)
    - `8PSH@2008-10-16 00:00:00` (southwest of Lanai/Kahoolawe)
  - **Callsign `A8CN8` (Pink/Magenta Track)**: Runs roughly east-west across the lower half of the view, passing through the Alenuihaha Channel between Maui and the Big Island of Hawaii, with a pink square placemark labeled `A8CN8@2007-01-13 00:00:00` southwest of the islands.
