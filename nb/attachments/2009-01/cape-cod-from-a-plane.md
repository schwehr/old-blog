# Aerial View of Cape Cod and Barnstable Harbor from an Airplane (`cape-cod-from-a-plane.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from a commercial airplane window looking eastward along the curved peninsula of **Cape Cod, Massachusetts**, illuminated by golden morning sunlight reflecting off the Atlantic Ocean and coastal salt marshes.

## Scene & Geographic Details
- **Foreground (Barnstable Harbor & Sandy Neck)**: Directly below the aircraft's wing and engine cowling in the lower-left and center foreground lies **Barnstable Harbor** and the **Great Marshes**, where intricate, winding tidal creeks and channels glow bright gold with reflected sunlight, sheltered on the north (left) by the barrier beach spit of **Sandy Neck** protruding into **Cape Cod Bay**.
- **Midground (Mid-Cape)**: Dark silhouetted land stretches across the middle of the frame, dotted with numerous inland kettle ponds, lakes, and bays (such as Wequaquet Lake and Pleasant Bay further east), with the **Cape Cod Canal** and Buzzards Bay area near the far left edge and **Nantucket Sound** to the south (right).
- **Background (Outer Cape & Provincetown Hook)**: Extending toward the upper-left horizon, the narrow arm of Outer Cape Cod curves northward and curls back to the west at the **Provincetown Hook** (Race Point and Long Point), clearly outlined against the wide, shimmering golden sun glint on the Atlantic Ocean and Cape Cod Bay.
- **Aircraft Elements**: In the bottom foreground, the leading edge of the airplane's wing (left) and the curved top of the jet engine nacelle (center-right) are visible, with a bright white specular reflection of the sun glinting off the metallic engine cowling.
