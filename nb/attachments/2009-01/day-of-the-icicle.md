# Icicles on Tuttle's Red Barn (`day-of-the-icicle.jpg`)

## Overview
A winter photograph taken in January 2009 showing a dense curtain of large, jagged icicles hanging from the snow-covered roof eaves of Tuttle's Red Barn in Dover, New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Building & Windows**: The background structure is a traditional red-painted wooden clapboard building ("Tuttle's Red Barn") featuring a row of four large, multi-pane glass windows framed in muted grey-blue trim. Through the dark window panes, indoor plants, greenery, and hanging displays are faintly visible inside the store.
- **Snow & Icicles**: A thick blanket of white snow rests atop the slanted roof. Extending downward from the roof edge across the entire width of the building is a continuous row of long, translucent icicles. Several of the largest icicles hang down past the bottom sills of the multi-pane windows, reaching several feet in length.
- **Foreground**:
  - On the left, the forked trunk and rough bark of a deciduous tree stand close to the building, with fresh white snow packed into the crook of its branches.
  - Along the bottom edge, a deep drift of smooth white snow covers the ground at the base of the red wall.
  - On the right, bare, twiggy brown branches of a shrub or small tree are lightly dusted with small clumps of snow.
