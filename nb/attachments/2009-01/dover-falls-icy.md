# Frozen Cocheco River Falls in Downtown Dover (`dover-falls-icy.jpg`)

## Overview
A winter camera-phone photograph looking upstream toward the frozen Cocheco River dam and waterfall in downtown Dover, New Hampshire, taken on the morning of January 20, 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Frozen Falls & Riverbed)**: The lower half of the image shows the rocky riverbed below the dam, heavily encrusted with thick, bulbous mounds of white snow and pale, yellowish-grey ice formations. Dark, unfrozen water flows through a narrow channel between the snow-covered boulders in the middle ground.
- **Middle Ground (Dam & Bridge)**:
  - Spanning horizontally across the center of the frame is the vertical drop of the dam, coated in a solid wall of textured, discolored vertical ice columns and topped by a flat, undisturbed layer of white snow over the impoundment above.
  - Directly behind the dam is a concrete and steel road bridge (Central Avenue / Washington Street area) with a dark metal pedestrian railing running along its top edge.
- **Background (Downtown Buildings & Sky)**:
  - On the far left stands a multi-story red-brick commercial/mill building with illuminated yellow windows on its upper floors.
  - In the center-right background sits a single-story, light-colored commercial storefront building with dark blue awning trim and a tall streetlamp rising above the bridge.
  - On the far right stands a three-story brown brick building bordering the river.
  - Bare winter trees line the background beneath an overcast sky streaked with grey and pale yellow morning clouds.
