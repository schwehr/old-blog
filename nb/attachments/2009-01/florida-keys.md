# Pigeon Key and the Old Seven Mile Bridge, Florida Keys (`florida-keys.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect photograph taken in January 2009 while driving along U.S. Route 1 (the Overseas Highway) through the Florida Keys, showing Pigeon Key and a section of the historic Old Seven Mile Bridge.

## Scene Description
- **Island & Grounds**: A small, low-lying tropical island (Pigeon Key) sits in shallow turquoise-green water. The shoreline is bordered by exposed pale limestone/coral rocks and a rectangular, concrete-walled tidal boat basin/pool at the bottom center-right, where two people are standing on the narrow concrete seawall.
- **Structures & Vegetation**:
  - Tall coconut palm trees and lush green coastal trees dot the grassy interior of the island.
  - Two small white open-air picnic pavilions with peaked roofs sit near the rocky shore framing the concrete basin.
  - A tall white flagpole stands near the center of the island flying an American flag.
  - Partially visible behind the palms are historic painted wooden buildings, including a yellow structure near the center and a white two-story house with a grey roof on the right.
- **Old Seven Mile Bridge**:
  - Running horizontally across the background is the elevated steel-and-concrete deck of the historic Old Seven Mile Bridge supported by concrete piers.
  - On the left side of the island, a curved concrete vehicular/pedestrian ramp sweeps down from the bridge deck onto the island next to a small green shed.
  - Further to the left over the open water, a gap in the bridge span is visible where a section was removed for boat passage.
- **Sky & Water**: The calm shallow water in the foreground transitions to pale blue-green open water in the background beneath a hazy, overcast light-grey sky.
