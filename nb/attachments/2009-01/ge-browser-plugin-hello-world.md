# Google Earth Browser Plugin "Hello, Earth!" with Highland Light (`ge-browser-plugin-hello-world.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a web page demonstrating a "Hello World" implementation of the Google Earth Browser Plugin (`Hello, Earth!`), displaying a 3D SketchUp model of Highland Light (Cape Cod Light) in North Truro, Massachusetts.

## Scene & 3D Model Description
- **Header**: Centered above the 3D viewport in black serif text on a white background is the heading `Hello, Earth!`.
- **3D Lighthouse Complex**: Prominently positioned on the left-center of the terrain is a 3D model of Highland Light:
  - A tall, cylindrical white lighthouse tower with two small ground-floor windows, an upper gallery deck with a black railing, and a black lantern room with a pointed roof and lightning rod.
  - Attached to the base of the tower by two small white connecting sheds with reddish-brown roofs is a two-story dark grey-blue keeper's house with white window trim, dormer windows, two chimneys, and a pitched dark grey roof.
  - Dark shadows are cast onto the ground to the right of the structures.
- **Terrain & Coastline**: The model rests on low-resolution satellite imagery draped over 3D coastal terrain showing sandy white pathways, mottled green and brown coastal scrubland, and the dark blue water of the Atlantic Ocean / Cape Cod Bay along the horizon under a pale blue sky.

## Interface & Overlays
- **Navigation Controls (Top Right)**: Standard Google Earth plugin semi-transparent white circular controls:
  - Upper compass/look wheel with directional arrows, a central eye icon, and an `N` (North) indicator tilted slightly to the upper right.
  - Middle pan wheel with four directional arrows and a central hand icon.
  - Lower vertical zoom slider with `+` and `-` buttons.
