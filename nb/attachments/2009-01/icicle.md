# Giant Icicle Hanging from a Neighbor's House (`icicle.png`)

## Overview
A winter photograph taken in late January 2009 showing an extraordinarily long, thick icicle hanging from the roof corner of a neighbor's white clapboard house, reaching nearly to the snow-covered driveway below.

## Scene Description
- **The Icicle**: Positioned just left of center, a massive, single vertical icicle extends from the upper roof eave all the way down past the height of the first-floor window and a parked car in the background, tapering to a sharp point just above the snowbank at the bottom.
- **House & Foreground**:
  - On the right half of the frame is the corner of a light-colored horizontal clapboard house with a dark-framed window reflecting warm indoor or sunset light.
  - A dark vertical tree trunk stands in the immediate foreground just to the right of the icicle, with thin bare branches crossing in front of the house wall.
- **Background**:
  - To the left of the house, a silver/grey sedan is parked in a driveway between high banks of plowed white snow.
  - Above the car, dark silhouettes of bare tree trunks and branches—one heavily coated with snow along its upper side—stand against a snowy hillside lit by warm golden late-afternoon sunlight at the top left.
