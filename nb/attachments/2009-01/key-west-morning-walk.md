# Early Morning View Along U.S. Route 1 in Key West (`key-west-morning-walk.jpg`)

## Overview
A dawn photograph taken around 7:00 AM in January 2009 from a bridge along U.S. Route 1 in Key West, Florida, looking down a calm mangrove-lined water channel toward the open bay under a pink and lavender morning sky.

## Scene Description
- **Waterway**: A wide, tranquil tidal channel or inlet stretches from the bottom-left foreground toward the center horizon, where the surface of the water reflects the soft purple, lavender, and rose hues of the early morning sky. Distant boats and a low shoreline are faintly visible across the open water at the horizon.
- **Mangrove Shorelines**:
  - Along the left bank, dense green mangroves and coastal palms form a continuous, rounded canopy overhanging the water's edge.
  - In the right foreground and middle ground, lush green mangrove branches and foliage frame the view.
- **Sign & Dock (Right)**: Partially nestled among the green foliage on the right bank is a brightly lit white rectangular business sign topped by a small red indicator, with red and blue lettering advertising rentals (including `JET SKIS`), and a warm yellow dock or building light glowing just below it near a small moored boat.
- **Sky**: The upper half of the frame is filled with soft horizontal bands of pastel lavender, violet, and pale pink dawn clouds.
