# Sunset Over Key West Rooftops and Palm Trees (`key-west-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview
A warm, golden-hour photograph taken in January 2009 looking out over the palm trees, rooftops, and streets of Key West, Florida, as the sun sets near the horizon.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Setting Sun**: The upper third of the frame features a vibrant orange and golden-yellow sunset sky streaked with wispy horizontal clouds. Just right of center, the brilliant white-yellow disk of the setting sun glows intensely along the flat horizon line.
- **Trees & Silhouette**:
  - On the left side of the frame, several tall coconut palm trees rise into the sky in dark silhouette against the bright orange sunset.
  - Additional palm fronds and tropical trees are scattered across the middle ground.
- **Buildings & Parking Lot (Foreground & Middle Ground)**:
  - At the bottom center-left, the pale, angled metal roof of a low building catches the warm orange glow of the sun.
  - In the bottom-right corner, three white commercial work vans (two with roof racks) are parked side-by-side in an asphalt lot next to a small shed and utility pole.
  - Across the center distance, low-rise coastal buildings, warehouses, and parked vehicles are bathed in the reddish-gold light of the setting sun.
