# Port Metro Vancouver AIS Map on Microsoft Virtual Earth (`mac-virtual-earth-ais.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Port Metro Vancouver interactive map running inside a web browser on Mac OS X using Microsoft Virtual Earth and Silverlight, showing the navigation menu expanded over satellite imagery of Howe Sound and West Vancouver, British Columbia.

## Interface & Controls
- **Navigation Control (Top Left)**: A circular dark gray pan ring with four directional arrows (up, down, left, right) sits on the upper left, with two square zoom buttons beneath it showing a magnifying glass with a `+` sign (zoom in) and a `-` sign (zoom out).
- **Top Toolbar**: Extending horizontally from the circular pan ring is a translucent dark gray bar containing four main menu items with icons:
  - **Map** (selected, highlighted in lighter gray with a blue globe icon)
  - **Weather** (with a white cloud/sun icon)
  - **Webcams Wall** (with a silver digital camera icon)
  - A square road/map view toggle icon on the right end.
- **Dropdown & Submenu**:
  - Dropping down from **Map** is a dark gray menu listing four options:
    1. **Marine** (highlighted, featuring a small red-and-white maritime crest/pilothouse icon and a right-pointing arrow)
    2. **Terminals and Facilities**
    3. **Find Place**
    4. **Settings**
  - Expanding to the right of **Marine** is a two-item submenu:
    1. **Region** (with a right-pointing arrow)
    2. **Search for a ship**

## Map Details & Geographic Labels
- **Base Map**: Satellite imagery with hybrid road and place-name labels covering the entrance to Howe Sound, Burrard Inlet, and Greater Vancouver.
- **Visible Labels**:
  - Islands & Inlets: `Gambier Island`, `Howe Sound` (partially behind toolbar), `Bowen Island`, `Burrard Inlet`.
  - Municipalities & Reserves: `Sechelt` (behind pan ring), `Lions Bay`, `Whytecliff`, `Horseshoe Bay`, `West Vancouver`, `North Vancouver` (partially clipped on right), `Seymour Creek Indian Reserve`, `Ubc`, `Musqueam Indian`, `Vancouver`.
  - Roads, Peaks & Ferry Routes: Dashed white ferry lines across the water including `Horseshoe Bay-Departure Bay Ferry`, green highway shield `1` (`Upper Levels Hwy`), `Crown Mtn`, and `Grouse Mtn 1211 m`.
