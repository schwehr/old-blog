# Minerva Canyon Geologic Map Coverage in Google Earth (`minerva-canyon-gbnp-ge.png`)

## Overview
A 3D perspective screenshot from Google Earth showing a converted 1995 Arc/Info V7 geologic map coverage (`minerva-canyon.kml`) draped over rugged mountain terrain in Great Basin National Park (GBNP), Nevada, with two open KML placemark attribute balloons.

## Map & Terrain Visualization
- **3D Terrain**: Shows a steep mountain range and canyon system (Minerva Canyon area in the Snake Range of Great Basin National Park) viewed from an oblique angle, with flat desert valley floor and agricultural fields visible in the upper-left background.
- **Geologic Polygon Boundaries**: White vector lines outline intricate geological units, fault lines, alluvial fans, and canyon-bottom deposits draped directly over the satellite imagery and elevation mesh, enclosed within a rectangular quadrangle boundary.
- **Placemarks**:
  - A yellow pushpin on the middle-left flank of the range near the western boundary of the coverage.
  - A blue paddle pin near the high ridge crest at the upper right.
