# Microsoft Virtual Earth Silverlight Vessel Traffic Interface (`ms-virtual-earth-silverlight-vessel-traffic.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Port Metro Vancouver **Interactive Map** web application built with Microsoft Virtual Earth and Silverlight, showing the **Marine Current** vessel-tracking dialog box overlaid on an aerial hybrid map of Greater Vancouver, the Fraser River Delta, and Boundary Bay.

## Interface & Dialog Controls
- **Top Page Tabs**: Two folder-style tabs at the top left of the frame:
  - **Interactive Map** (active, dark gray background with white text)
  - **Photo Gallery** (inactive, light gray background with reddish-brown text)
- **Virtual Earth Map Controls (Upper Left)**:
  - Circular dark pan control ring with four directional arrows and `+`/`-` zoom magnifying glass buttons below it.
  - Horizontal translucent toolbar containing **Map** (globe icon), **Weather** (cloud/sun icon), **Webcams Wall** (camera icon), and a map-style toggle icon.
- **Marine Current Floating Panel (Right)**:
  - A semi-transparent dark gray dialog window with a rounded double white border and a collapse tab on its left edge.
  - **Title Bar**: Features a white `x` close button on the left and the title `Marine Current`.
  - **Select Vessel**: A label next to a single-line white text input box, with a large empty white list box directly below it.
  - **Vessel Count**: Reads `0`.
  - **Action Buttons**: Three gray rectangular buttons along the bottom of the panel labeled **Track**, **Clear**, and **Replay**.

## Map Details & Geographic Features
- **Base Map**: Satellite imagery with road shields, municipal names, and the yellow dashed US–Canada international border line cutting horizontally across the lower third (`Point Roberts` and `Blaine`, Washington below; British Columbia above).
- **Visible Place Labels**:
  - `Vancouver`, `Burnaby`, `Belcarra`, `Anmore`, `Ioco`, `Port Moody`, `Coquitlam`, `Port Coquitlam`, `Coast Mountains`.
  - `Sea Island`, `Richmond`, `Lulu Island`, `New Westminster`, `Ladner`, `Delta`.
  - `Tsawwassen Indian Reserve`, `Tsawwassen`, `Semiahmoo Bay`, `Point Roberts`, `Maple Beach`, `South Beach`, `Blaine`, `Drayton Harbor`, `Cottonwood Beach`, `Birch Bay`, `Custer`, `Lynden`, `Kickerville`, `Greenwood`.
  - Highway shields: `99`, `99A`, `1A`, `91`, `10`, `17`, `13`, `539`, and Interstate `5`.
