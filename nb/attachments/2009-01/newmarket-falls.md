# Frozen Log Jam at Lamprey River Falls in Newmarket (`newmarket-falls.jpg`)

## Overview
A winter twilight photograph of a snow- and ice-encrusted tree trunk and branch jam perched on the lip of the dam waterfall on the Lamprey River in Newmarket, New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Waterfall & Ice Formations**:
  - In the lower third of the frame, dark river water spills smoothly over the horizontal crest of a dam, creating vertical streaking curtains of white and olive-tinted falling water.
  - Perched right on the dam's edge in the center-left foreground is a fallen evergreen tree and tangle of branches heavily coated in thick, bulbous white and cream-colored icicles and snow mounds that hang down into the rushing waterfall.
- **Middle Ground (Frozen River & Bridge)**:
  - Upstream of the dam lip, a pool of dark open water reflects the branches on the right, while a flat sheet of lavender-white snow-covered river ice extends across the left side.
  - Behind the river pool, a historic stone masonry retaining wall runs along the left bank, leading to a low dark bridge spanning the river channel in the center.
- **Background**:
  - Bare deciduous trees and evergreens line both banks of the river against a soft pink, lavender, and pale blue dusk sky.
  - A white historic building with tall windows and a cupola/tower is partially visible on the far left above the stone wall and street railing, with utility wires crossing overhead.
