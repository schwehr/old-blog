# Winter Sunset over Newmarket, New Hampshire (`newmarket-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview
A dusk photograph capturing vibrant orange and pink sunset clouds over historic brick and clapboard buildings and snow-covered grounds in Newmarket, New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Clouds**:
  - The upper two-thirds of the frame is dominated by a dramatic winter sunset sky. Billowing clouds across the left and center glow in vivid shades of fiery orange, salmon pink, and coral where they catch the low sun, contrasting with darker charcoal-gray cloud undersides and a cool slate-blue sky at the upper right.
  - Near the horizon in the center, the sky glows bright golden-yellow just behind the roofline and bare tree branches.
- **Buildings & Foreground**:
  - In the left foreground stands a dark, three-story pitched-roof New England building with a single central chimney, two gable windows, three second-story windows, a second-story doorway on the left corner, and four dormer windows protruding from the snow-covered right slope of its roof.
  - To the left is part of a flat-roofed industrial mill building.
  - To the right, bare deciduous trees and utility poles with power lines rise from a snow-covered embankment and parking/roadway area, with smaller brick buildings visible at the far right.
