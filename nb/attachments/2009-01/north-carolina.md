# Aerial View of Cape Hatteras and the Outer Banks at Sunset (`north-carolina.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from an airplane window looking over the Outer Banks barrier islands and Pamlico Sound at Cape Hatteras, North Carolina, bathed in golden sunlight.

## Scene & Geographic Description
- **Aircraft Engine Cowling (Bottom)**: The curved metallic top of the airplane's jet engine nacelle is visible along the bottom right edge of the frame.
- **Barrier Islands & Coastline**:
  - A thin, dark ribbon of barrier island (Hatteras Island) curves gracefully from the bottom-left corner up toward the center-left, forming the sharp seaward point of **Cape Hatteras** before bending back toward the upper center.
  - The seaward shoreline on the left forms a smooth, sweeping arc separating the open Atlantic Ocean from the intricate marshlands, inlets, and shallow shoals on the sound side to the right.
  - Three small bright pinkish-white lens reflections/glints appear near the bottom-left tip of the island.
- **Water, Clouds & Lighting**:
  - Both the Atlantic Ocean (left) and Pamlico Sound (center/right) reflect a brilliant, warm amber-gold sheen from the low sun.
  - Scattered, low-altitude cumulus clouds cast dark gray-blue shadows across the golden water surface, particularly over the sound on the right half of the image, while a distant aircraft contrail is faintly visible near the top horizon.
