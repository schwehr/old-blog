# NOVA "Is There Life on Mars?" SSV Visualization Room (`nova-visualization-room.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the PBS NOVA online video player in Safari on Mac OS X showing a scene from **"Is There Life on Mars?"** (Chapter 5: *The Rise of Phoenix*), depicting scientists and engineers gathered inside the darkened Solar System Visualization (SSV) VIP room viewing stereo 3D imagery from the Mars Phoenix Lander.

## Browser & Video Player Interface
- **Browser Window**: Mac OS X Safari window titled `NOVA | Is There Life on Mars? | Watch the Program | PBS` at URL `http://www.pbs.org/wgbh/nova/programs/ht/tm/3518-l.html?site=45&pl=qt&rate=hi&ch=5`.
- **Top Navigation Bar**: Black PBS header with the circular PBS logo, links for `Feedback | Help | Support PBS programming | Buy the DVD`, the stylized blue/teal title `NOVA IS THERE LIFE ON MARS?`, and right-aligned `REDUCE` and `E-MAIL` buttons.
- **Chapter Bar**: Dark gray bar with `NEXT` and `PLAY ALL` links in cyan text.
- **Bottom QuickTime Controls**: A silver QuickTime transport bar at the bottom of the video frame with volume, pause (`||`), and timeline scrubber controls.

## Video Scene Description
- **Visualization Room**: A darkened conference and visualization room with a long table in the foreground where several silhouetted team members are seated wearing 3D glasses, with water bottles and papers on the table.
- **Display Screens**:
  - **Main Projection Screen (Center)**: A large square rear-projection wall screen displaying a wide panoramic view of the Martian horizon and lander workspace at the top, above grayscale robotic arm camera views.
  - **Side Monitors (Left Wall)**: Three wall-mounted flat-panel monitors on the left; the leftmost displays terminal/status text and circular gauges, the middle is dark, and the rightmost shows a Martian surface image.
