# OpenStreetMap 2008: A Year of Edits Visualization (`osm-a-year-of-edits.png`)

## Overview
A still frame from ITO World's animation **"OSM 2008: A Year of Edits"**, showing a 3D globe centered on North America, the Caribbean, and the North Atlantic, where OpenStreetMap contributor activity throughout 2008 is illuminated in glowing purple, yellow, and white against a dark Earth.

## Visualization Details
- **Globe & Atmosphere**: The Earth is rendered as a dark navy/black sphere with a soft pale blue atmospheric glow along the western limb on the left.
- **Edit Intensity Highlighting**:
  - **United States**: Almost the entire contiguous United States is densely covered in a bright purple/magenta glow (reflecting the TIGER road data import and subsequent community edits), punctuated by intense yellow and bright white hotspots around major metropolitan areas, coastlines, and interstate corridors.
  - **Canada, Mexico & Central America**: Southern Canadian provinces along the US border, major Mexican highway corridors, and parts of Central America and the Caribbean (including Hispaniola and Puerto Rico) show distinct yellow and white road networks and city clusters.
  - **Europe & West Africa (Upper Right)**: Across the Atlantic on the upper-right limb, Western Europe (including the UK, Ireland, France, Germany, and the Iberian Peninsula) blazes with brilliant white and golden-yellow light indicating heavy mapping activity.
- **Labels & Timeline**:
  - **Top Right Logo**: `ito!` in white with a magenta exclamation mark.
  - **Bottom Left Timeline**: A horizontal 12-month timeline bar labeled `J | F | M | A | M | J | J | A | S | O | N | D`, with a vertical progress marker positioned in early December (`D`).
  - **Bottom Right Credits**: Reads `OpenStreetMap`, `CC-by-SA www.itoworld.com`, and `Map data www.openstreetmap.org 20 Dec 2008`.
