# Rivet Media Sharing Preferences and Menu on Mac OS X (`rivet-xbox360-mac.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Rivet** media-streaming application on Mac OS X, showing both its top menu bar status menu and its **Movies** preferences window used for sharing Mac media folders with an Xbox 360 or PlayStation 3.

## Menu Bar & Dropdown Menu (Top Right)
- **Mac OS X Menu Bar**: Shows `Go`, `Window`, and `Help` on the left, and status icons on the right including the highlighted blue **Rivet** power/broadcast icon, a growl/status icon, Quicksilver (`Q`), a coffee cup icon, AirPort Wi-Fi signal icon, and a network throughput meter (`Tx: 19.8KB/s`, `Rx: 522B/s`).
- **Rivet Dropdown Menu**:
  - `About Rivet`
  - `✓ Share Movies`
  - `✓ Share Music`
  - `✓ Share Photos`
  - `Preferences...  ⌘,`
  - `Quit Rivet      ⌘Q`

## Movies Preferences Window
- **Toolbar Tabs**: Six icon tabs across the top of the window:
  - `General` (light switch/gear icon)
  - `Movies` (selected, film reel icon)
  - `Music` (blue eighth-note icon)
  - `Photos` (35mm film canister icon)
  - `Access Control` (up/down arrows on yellow sign)
  - `Update` (blue globe with arrow)
- **Sharing Controls (Left Side)**:
  - Checked checkbox: `Enable Movie Sharing`
  - Under `Sources:`, three checked checkboxes:
    - `Share iTunes movies`
    - `Share iPhoto movies`
    - `Share movies located in folders:`
  - Below is an empty two-column table with headers `Share As...` and `Local Folder Name`, accompanied by `+` and `-` buttons at the bottom left.
- **Format Information (Right Side)**:
  - Bulleted list of supported video formats partially visible beneath the dropdown menu: `• H.264`, `• DivX`, `• WMV`, `• AVI`.
  - Explanatory paragraph noting that the Xbox 360 and PS3 support specific versions and profiles of each format.
  - Two wide rounded buttons at the bottom right: **`Xbox 360 Supported Formats...`** and **`PS3 Supported Formats...`**.
