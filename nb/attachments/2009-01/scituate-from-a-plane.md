# Aerial View of Scituate and the Massachusetts Coastline (`scituate-from-a-plane.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from the left window of a passenger airplane during a flight south from New England to Florida on January 15, 2009. The image captures the Massachusetts coastline around Scituate and the southern approach toward Cape Cod Bay bathed in warm, golden sunlight.

## Scene Description
- **Aircraft Wing (Foreground Left)**: A silver airplane wing extends diagonally from the middle-left edge down toward the bottom center-right, where the curved upper cowling of a wing-mounted jet engine is visible. Bright sunlight reflects off the leading edge of the wing and engine nacelle, while the wing surface shows rectangular flap panels and small vortex generators.
- **Coastline & Water (Center & Right)**: The Atlantic Ocean and Massachusetts Bay occupy the left and upper-center portions of the frame, glowing with a wide, golden-orange band of reflected low-angle sunlight. Along the irregular shoreline of Scituate and the South Shore, coastal spits, barrier beaches, harbor inlets, and tidal marshes cut into the darkened landmass on the right.
- **Inland Features & Horizon**: The land on the right is cast in deep shadow with scattered small ponds and clearings catching faint reflections. Near the top center, the curved hook and southern landmass of Cape Cod extend horizontally into the hazy orange distance along the horizon.
