# "DTS/SIO" Bathymetry Signature in Google Earth (`sio-revision-in-ge.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from Google Earth showing an 80-mile-wide artificial bathymetric "watermark" reading `DTS/SIO` (for David T. Sandwell / Scripps Institution of Oceanography) embedded in the seafloor topography of the eastern Indian Ocean (`0°N, 94.78°E`), west of Sumatra and Pulau Nias, Indonesia. The signature was placed in the `SRTM30_PLUS` global bathymetry grid to track how the dataset was being used.

## Map & Bathymetry Details
- **Seafloor Signature (Left)**: Inside a bright magenta/pink oval annotation added to the screenshot, the capital letters `DTS/SIO` appear etched into the deep blue shaded-relief seafloor on the abyssal plain west of the Sunda Trench.
- **Trench & Islands (Right)**: Running diagonally from top-center toward the bottom-right is the steep bathymetric trough of the Sunda Trench and the forearc ridge containing Indonesian islands overlaid with satellite imagery and yellow coastline vectors:
  - `Pulau Nias` (upper right)
  - `Pulau Bawa` (just south of Nias)
  - `Pulau Simuk`, `Pulau Telo`, `Pulau Tanahmasa` / `Pulau Tanahbala` (Batu Islands, lower right)
