# 3D GRASS GIS Geologic Visualization of Wheeler Peak, Great Basin National Park (`wheeler-peak-gbnp-grass.png`)

## Overview
A 3D perspective shaded-relief geologic map visualization of the Wheeler Peak area in Great Basin National Park (GBNP), Nevada, rendered by Kurt Schwehr in April 1995 using GRASS GIS on an SGI workstation from digitized Arc/Info V7 geologic coverages.

## Visualization & Map Details
- **3D Terrain Mesh**: The rectangular digital elevation model (DEM) is viewed from an oblique angle against a solid black background, framed by a dark gray border strip and white tick marks along the left and bottom axes.
- **Geologic Units & Color Palette**: Distinct black contact lines and fault boundaries divide the mountainous terrain into brightly colored geologic map polygons draped over the shaded relief:
  - **Orange**: A broad band along the western (left) flank and valley floor.
  - **Pale Olive / Tan-Green**: Extensive upland surfaces across the central and upper mountain slopes.
  - **Bright Magenta / Pink & Yellow**: Interfingered bands and structural units cutting across the middle and upper-right slopes.
  - **Cyan / Light Blue**: Outcrops in the upper-left corner.
  - **Vibrant Greens (Light, Medium, and Dark Forest Green)**: Complex folded and faulted units across the lower-center and lower-right ridges.
  - **Lavender & Deep Purple**: Units exposed in the lower-right foreground glacial cirque/valley area.
- **Orientation & Labels**:
  - In the lower-left corner, a white-outlined square box contains a white north arrow pointing upward.
  - Centered along the bottom margin in bright magenta serif text is the credit label: `Kurt Schwehr - SSR - 4/95`.
