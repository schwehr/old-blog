# Minoru 3D Stereoscopic Webcam (`0309-WEBCAM_x600.jpg`)

## Overview
A studio product photograph of the **Minoru 3D** stereoscopic webcam, featured in *MIT Technology Review* and blogged on February 24, 2009. The camera is designed in a playful, anthropomorphic robot-like form with two side-by-side lenses for capturing 3D stereo video and images.

## Product Description
- **Camera Head (Top)**: An elongated, horizontally oriented bright red plastic oval housing that resembles a robot's head. Set into the left and right sides are two circular camera lenses surrounded by textured white/silver focus rings, spaced apart at human interpupillary distance to capture stereoscopic 3D imagery. A wide, polished chrome band wraps vertically around the center of the head, flanked by two small dark indicator/microphone slots.
- **Neck & Base (Bottom)**: A cylindrical chrome neck connects the head to a hinged, three-legged bright red plastic tripod stand. Two angled front legs and one rear leg (with ribbed grip texture visible on its underside) rest on a glossy lime-green surface, casting soft reflections against a gradient light-green studio backdrop.
