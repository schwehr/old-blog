# 1999 LExEn Proposal 3D Model of a Deep-Penetration Hot Spring Robot (`1999-lexan-deep-pen-robot.png`)

## Overview
A 3D computer-graphics rendering (from a VRML 1.0 model) of a conceptual cylindrical underwater robot designed in 1999 by Kurt Schwehr, Peter Coppin, and Mike Wagner at Carnegie Mellon University's Field Robotics Center (FRC) for an NSF Life in Extreme Environments (LExEn) proposal to explore geothermal hot springs.

## Model Description
- **Upper Body & Thrusters/Floats**: Rendered against a solid black background, the vertically oriented vehicle has a white cylindrical central hull topped with a conical white nose cone and a thin blue accent ring. Four elongated, capsule-shaped bright blue pods (ballast/flotation tanks or thrusters) with twin white horizontal bands are mounted symmetrically around the upper hull.
- **Mid-Section Payload Bay**: Below the white upper cylinder is a semi-transparent brownish wireframe/mesh cage enclosing a central vertical shaft. Inside the cage are two blue rectangular sensor housings with protruding cylindrical lenses (cameras or lights) facing left, surrounded by a yellow ring-shaped guardrail.
- **Lower Hull & Manipulator Arm**: At the bottom of the cylindrical body, a white collar with blue and grey trim tapers to a rounded pivot joint. Extending horizontally to the right from this joint is a white telescoping robotic manipulator arm ending in a two-fingered pincer/claw with blue-shaded inner surfaces.
