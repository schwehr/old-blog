# Sailboat on San Diego Bay with Downtown San Diego Skyline (`2003-sep-downtown-San-Diego.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic waterfront photograph taken in September 2003 from the Scripps Institution of Oceanography (SIO) Nimitz Marine Facility (MarFac) on Point Loma, looking east-southeast across San Diego Bay toward the downtown San Diego skyline at sunset.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Sailboat**: Calm, rippled gray-blue waters of San Diego Bay fill the foreground. In the right foreground, a white sloop-rigged cruising sailboat glides toward the left under full main and headsail (jib), with a small dark emblem near the top of the mainsail and several passengers visible in the cockpit near the stern.
- **Midground & Harbor**: To the far left, the dark rocky tip of a jetty or riprap shoreline juts into the frame. In the middle distance toward the left-center, a smaller sailboat and a tiny boat are visible on the bay.
- **Background Skyline & Sky**: Across the water, the buildings and high-rises of downtown San Diego and the surrounding waterfront (including arched aircraft hangars on North Island / Coronado and four white cylindrical storage tanks along the shore) glow warm gold and orange in the late afternoon sun. Distant hazy mountains rise on the far right beneath a soft lavender-gray and peach sunset sky with scattered horizontal cloud bands.
