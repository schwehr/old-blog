# Voluntary Observing Ship (VOS) Tracks in Google Earth by Ben Smith (`ben-smith-vos.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of **Google Earth** displaying Voluntary Observing Ship (VOS) vessel tracks and weather/position reports off the coast of Nova Scotia and the Gulf of Maine, generated using KML network links created by Ben Smith at UNH CCOM/JHC.

## Map & Vessel Track Details
- **Geographic View**: The 3D globe view is centered over Nova Scotia, the Bay of Fundy, and the western North Atlantic (`43°17'34.31" N`, `65°33'59.60" W`, eye altitude `368.33 km`), with a time slider in the upper left of the map view set to `Dec 30, 2007`.
- **Ship Tracks & Waypoints**: Green and purple line segments trace ship routes across the Atlantic south and east of Nova Scotia, marked by square waypoint icons labeled with vessel callsigns including `CGDG`, `8PSH`, and `8PNK`.
- **Open Information Balloon (`CGDG`)**: Clicking a waypoint at the southern tip of Nova Scotia displays a light-green callout balloon with the following vessel details for the Canadian Coast Guard ship *Hudson*:
  - `CGDG`
  - `HUDSON`
  - `Ice breaker`
  - `Canada`
  - `LOA 85 m`
  - `Beam 15 m`
  - `Freeboard 3 m`
  - `2007-10-10`
  - `43N0.000 065W30.000`
