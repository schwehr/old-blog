# CommandBridge Port Security Interface on Microsoft Virtual Earth (`commandbridge-1.png`)

## Overview
A video frame captured from a MSN Soapbox promotional video demonstrating **CommandBridge**, a maritime and port security situational awareness system built on **Microsoft Virtual Earth** (showing the Hampton Roads / Norfolk, Virginia harbor area).

## Map & Interface Details
- **Geographic View**: High-resolution aerial/satellite imagery of the Norfolk, Virginia waterfront, Elizabeth River entrance, Naval Station Norfolk piers (upper center), and Craney Island Dredged Material Management Area (the large rectangular peninsula to the left of center).
- **Sensor & Vessel Overlays**:
  - A semi-transparent cyan/teal wedge-shaped camera or radar field-of-view cone fans out from a point in the harbor channel toward the east across the Norfolk container terminal and pier complex.
  - Green, red, and blue triangular/square vessel and Aid-to-Navigation (AtoN) icons with small white callout labels are distributed along the shipping channels and river mouths.
- **UI Controls & Overlays**:
  - **Top Menu Bar**: Dark gray bar with buttons on the left for `Data`, `Tools`, `Map Layers`, and `Map Views`, and `CommandBridge™` / `MAP` branding on the right.
  - **Top Left Info Panel**: A dark translucent floating window displaying a thumbnail and status text (`RADAR_HD_VIDEO`, `LogRef: ...`, `AIS Class: ...`, `ALRM`) for a selected target or sensor.
  - **Left Map Navigation**: Vertical Virtual Earth pan/zoom control pad on the middle-left edge.
  - **Watermarks**: `soapbox on msn video` watermark in the upper-right corner and a USCG/homeland security badge graphic in the lower-right corner.
