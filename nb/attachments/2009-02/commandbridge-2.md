# Dual-Monitor Operator Workstation Running CommandBridge (`commandbridge-2.png`)

## Overview
A video still from a MSN Soapbox video on **CommandBridge**, showing an over-the-shoulder view of an operator in a darkened command center looking at a dual-monitor workstation running the CommandBridge port security software.

## Scene Description
- **Dual LCD Displays**: Two side-by-side flat-panel monitors illuminate the dark room:
  - **Left Monitor**: Displays the CommandBridge geospatial map view (aerial imagery of a harbor coastline with green sensor/zone overlays) with an open white rectangular dialog window and a red alert box in the upper-left corner.
  - **Right Monitor**: Displays a structured management dashboard or matrix interface containing rows of dark square status/video icons and gray control panels.
- **Operator & Watermark**: In the lower-right foreground, the back and right side of a male operator's head is dimly visible facing the screens. In the upper-right corner is the white `soapbox on msn video` watermark with the four-color MSN butterfly logo.
