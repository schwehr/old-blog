# CommandBridge Port Security Interface (`commandbridge-3.png`)

## Overview
A grayscale screenshot taken from a Microsoft MSN Video ("Soapbox on MSN Video") presentation showing the "CommandBridge" collaborative situational awareness and port security interface built on Microsoft Virtual Earth.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Navigation Bar**: Features four tabs on the upper left labeled `Dock`, `Tools`, `Map Layers`, and `Map Views`. On the far right is the title `CommandBridge™ MAP`.
- **Watermark**: A prominent semi-transparent overlay in the upper right reads `soapbox on msn video` with the four-square MSN butterfly logo.
- **Sensor / Alert Panels (Top Left)**: Two floating rectangular status widgets sit side by side:
  - Left box: Labeled `Sensor 1` (or `Device 1`) with status fields (`ID:`, `Lat/Lon:`, `AIS Class:`), and a bottom bar reading `ALERT` with a circular icon and small control buttons.
  - Right box: Labeled `Sensor 2` with status fields (`NAME: NA`, `Lat/Lon:`, `AIS Class: Base...`) and a bottom `ALERT` dropdown bar.
- **Map Navigation (Middle Left)**: A vertical Virtual Earth navigation control panel on the left edge includes directional panning arrows in a circle, `+` and `-` zoom buttons, and `2D`/`3D` or `Road`/`Aerial` mode buttons.
- **Center-Right Video/Camera Popup**: An inset window titled `Sensor 1` (or camera feed) overlays the lower-right portion of the map. It has a row of action tabs along its top bar (with the third tab highlighted in dark gray) and displays a high-angle surveillance view of a port building, pier, and parallel railroad tracks or roadways, flanked by black vertical pillarbox bars.

## Map & Scene Details
- The background displays an oblique 3D aerial view of a harbor, coastline, and port industrial area in grayscale.
- Several small directional vessel/sensor markers (triangles with labels and small icons for `zoom`, `info`, and `check/status`) are plotted in the harbor waters near the center of the view.
