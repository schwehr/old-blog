# Digipilot Region Selection Interface (`digipilot-region-selection.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the main "Area selection" screen from **Digipilot** (`HP 1D`), an official digital nautical publication (sailing directions) produced by the Netherlands Hydrographic Service covering North Sea approaches to the Dutch and Belgian coasts.

## Layout & Left Sidebar
- **Left Vertical Banner**: Divided into an orange-red upper block containing bold dark-blue vertical text `HP 1D`, and a dark-blue lower block with light-blue vertical text `PILOT`.
- **Upper-Left Instruction Box**: A dark blue rounded rectangle with a white border containing white text:
  > "Area selection" (main page)
  >
  > After selecting an area all kind of information will become available. This information has been classified in Themes, Subjects, Chapters and paragraphs.
- **Lower-Left Title**: Stacked large bold text reading:
  - `North Sea` (light blue)
  - `Approaches` (bright yellow)
  - `Destination` (light blue)

## Map & Interactive Elements
- **Map View**: A stylized dark-blue map of the Netherlands coastline and the `NORTH SEA` (spaced white letters curving along the coast). The inland water body `IJsselmeer` is shaded in light blue-gray, and major ports are marked with red dots and labels for `Amsterdam` (with the North Sea Canal shown in yellow) and `ROTTERDAM`.
- **Approach Corridors**: Colored gradient bands extend from the coast into the North Sea, labeled in white angled text from north to south:
  - `Ems estuary`
  - `Friesche Zeegat`
  - `Zeegat van Terschelling`
  - `Zeegat van Texel`
  - `IJmuiden`
  - `Scheveningen`
  - `Zeegat van Hoek van Holland`
  - `Zeegat van Goeree`
  - `Oosterschelde` (partially covered by menu)
- **Pull-Down Menu & Callout**: At the bottom center, a vertical list menu displays selectable approach regions (`Westerschelde / Belgian Coast` highlighted in light cyan with a hand cursor hovering over it, followed by `Oosterschelde`, `Zeegat van Goeree`, `Zeegat van Hoek van Holland`, `Scheveningen`, `IJmuiden`, `Zeegat van Texel`, `Zeegat van Terschelling`, and `Friesche Zeegat`).
- To the right of the menu is a callout box reading `"Select an Approach area by using the pull down menu, or....."` above a small thumbnail preview of the `Westerschelde / Belgian Coast` approaches screen.
