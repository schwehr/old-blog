# Digipilot Sailing Directions Popup Text (`digipilot-sailing-dir-popup.png`)

## Overview
A close-up screenshot of the **Digipilot** interactive chart for **Zeegat van Texel**, showing the semi-transparent text overlay that appears when clicking or hovering over numbered waypoint `1` on the southern approach to Den Helder.

- A hand cursor hovers over the orange circular marker `1` at the lower left of the fairway.

  - **Body Text**:
