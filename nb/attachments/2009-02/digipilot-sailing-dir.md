# Digipilot Sailing Directions for Zeegat van Texel (`digipilot-sailing-dir.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Digipilot** (`HP 1D`) digital sailing directions interface displaying the interactive chart and approach routes/fairways for **Zeegat van Texel** and **Den Helder** in the Netherlands.

## Header & Left Sidebar
- **Left Sidebar**: Red top block labeled `HP 1D` above a dark blue vertical bar reading `APPROACHES` in light blue letters.
- **Top Banner**: Dark blue header featuring a small maritime illustration of a ship's bow cutting through waves on the left, followed by the title `Sailing directions` (light blue) and subtitle `Routes / Fairways Zeegat van Texel` (white).
- **Overview Inset Map (Top Right)**: A coastline outline of the northern Netherlands with a red-bordered semi-transparent rectangle indicating the current chart extent around Texel and Den Helder.
- **Interactive Chart Button**: A bright yellow rounded rectangular button with blue text reading `Interactive Chart` sits at the upper left of the chart area.

## Chart & Information Layers
- **Chart Features**: Shows the southern tip of Texel (`Oudeschild` in white text) at the top and `Den Helder` / `Kijkduin` at the bottom, separated by the `Marsdiep` and `Texelstroom` channels, with the `Noorderhaaks` sandbank in the center-left and `Balgzand` / `Malzwin` to the east.
- **Fairways & Sector Lights**: Red/orange route lines mark the channels (`Molengat`, `Texelstroom`, `Malzwin`, and the southern approach). Colored yellow, red, and green light sectors radiate from lighthouses (`Kijkduin` and Texel lights) along yellow leading lines. Navigation buoys (`TX 3`, `NH`, `MR`, `ZH`, `S 2`, `S 1`, `MG`, `MG 1`, `MG 2`, `T 11`, `T 13`, `T 17`, `GvS 2`, `GvS 6`, `M 10`, `T 5`, `MH 4`) are plotted across the water.
- **Numbered Waypoints**: Circular red markers with white numbers (`2`, `3`, `3`, `4`) and an orange circle (`1`) at the bottom-left approach (with a white hand cursor hovering over it) indicate clickable sailing direction notes.
- **Right Layer Buttons**: A vertical stack of six pill-shaped buttons on the right edge controls chart layers:
  - `Sailing dir.` (dark blue, pointed to by a dark red callout box reading `"Activate the "Information layers" of the chart by pressing the buttons."`)
  - `Areas` (cyan blue)
  - `Images` (orange-gold)
  - `Com.` (olive yellow-green)
  - `Heavy Seas` (light blue-white)
  - `Locks` (tan-gold)
- **Bottom-Right Navigation**: A four-way red diamond pan control and a dark blue `Overview` button in the bottom right corner.
