# ERMA Web GIS Showing Portsmouth Harbor Multibeam Bathymetry Over Google Ocean (`erma-google-oceans-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **ERMA** (`Portsmouth, NH`) web GIS interface showing high-resolution color-coded multibeam bathymetry of the Piscataqua River, Great Bay entrance, and Portsmouth Harbor approach overlaid on the Google Maps/Ocean satellite and regional bathymetric basemap.

## Interface & Layer Tree
- **Header & Toolbar**: Ocean-wave banner reading `ERMA Portsmouth, NH` with `Admin` on the right; toolbar below with `Map`, `Print`, `Info`, `Help`, `>`, `Legend`, `Layer` (active/highlighted tab), `Data`, and `Tools`.
- **Right Sidebar (`Layer` Tab)**: Expandable layer tree with `(Clear | All)` links and green download icons next to several top-level folders:
  - Collapsed categories (`+`): `Response Planning`, `Public Safety`, `Incident Information`, `Field Photographs`, `Resources at Risk NH`, `Resources at Risk ME`, `Restoration`, `Marine Debris`, and `Environmental Quality`.
  - Expanded category (`-`): `Charts, Surveys, Ships (Clear | All)`:
    - Checked (`☑`): `Shiptrack`, `Ships`, `Bathymetry`
    - Unchecked (`☐`): `Backscatter (Multibeam)`, `Backscatter (Sidescan)`, `Navigation Chart`, `Depth Contour`, `Depth Area`, `Buried Cable`, `Fairway`, `Navline`, `Depth Area GB`
- **Bottom Status Bar**: Reads `Scale: 1 : 217K`, `Zoom Level: 11`, and `Location: -70.62836°, 43.03326°`.

## Map View & Bathymetric Overlay
- **Basemap**: Google satellite imagery (`Imagery ©2009 TerraMetrics`) of the New Hampshire and southern Maine coastline, including Pease International Tradeport runway (marked with a small red star) near Great Bay/Little Bay on the upper left, and the Isles of Shoals offshore to the southeast surrounded by shaded blue regional seafloor topography.
- **Bathymetry Overlay**: Rainbow-colored high-resolution bathymetry (reds and oranges in shallow river channels and shoals transitioning through yellow and green to cyan and blue in deeper offshore waters) traces Little Bay, the Piscataqua River past Portsmouth and New Castle, and fans out into the Gulf of Maine offshore of Odiorne Point (marked with a black `X`).
- **Map Controls**: Standard pan/zoom controls on the left edge, plus polygon-select and ruler measurement icons in the upper right of the map canvas.
