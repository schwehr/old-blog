# Ice Crystals on the Old Mill Building in Newmarket, NH (`feb-newmarket.png`)

## Overview
A winter photograph taken in February 2009 in Newmarket, New Hampshire, showing thick white frost and ice crystals coating vegetation growing out of the stone wall of an old historic mill building alongside the steaming Lamprey River.

## Scene Description
- **Right Foreground (Mill Building & Ice)**: The multi-story stone masonry facade of the historic Newmarket mill recedes at a steep angle from the right foreground toward the upper center-right, lined with rows of rectangular windows. Growing from the masonry and window ledges on the right is a dense, bushy mass of plants and branches completely encased in bright white rime ice and frost crystals, illuminated by sunlight.
- **Center & Left Foreground (River & Mist)**: Below the mill wall lies dark, open water of the river with wisps of white steam/mist rising off the surface and reflecting the blue sky and clouds. A small wooden piling or post capped with a mound of white snow sticks out of the water near the center-left, and a curved branch emerges from the water near the lower right wall.
- **Background (Snow & Forest)**: Beyond the open water, a flat expanse of snow-covered river ice stretches across the middle distance to a dense treeline of dark evergreen and bare deciduous trees on the far bank under a bright pale sky.
