# GeoNetwork Dataset Distribution Info Panel (`geonetwork-distribution-types.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot of the **Distribution info** metadata section inside **GeoNetwork opensource** for the sample dataset *"Hydrological Basins in Africa"*, illustrating the available download and web-mapping formats.

## Visible Text & Fields
Enclosed in a light gray rectangular fieldset box with a thin gray border and a bold blue legend header reading **`Distribution info`**, the panel lists six key-value rows:
- **`Name`**: `ShapeFile`
- **`Version`**: `Grass Version 6.1`
- **`OnLine Resource`**: Underlined link reading `Online link to the 'Water Resources and Irrigation in Africa'- website`
- **`Data For Download`**: Underlined link reading `Hydrological basins in Africa (Shapefile Format)`
- **`Interactive Map`**: Underlined link reading `Hydrological basins in Africa` followed on the next line by `(OGC-WMS Server: http://geonetwork3.fao.org/ows/296 )`
- **`View In Google Earth`**: Underlined link reading `Hydrological basins in Africa ` next to a square blue Google Earth globe icon.
