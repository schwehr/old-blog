# Google Earth 5 Seafloor Bathymetry with DTS/SIO Tag (`google-earth-5-bathy.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from **Google Earth 5** showing shaded blue seafloor bathymetry in the eastern Indian Ocean (near Sumatra) where the letters **`DTS/SIO`** (Digital Tectonic Activity Map / Scripps Institution of Oceanography) are embossed directly into the bathymetric terrain grid, demonstrating that Google Earth 5 reports negative seafloor elevations below sea level.

## Map & Bathymetric Features
- **Seafloor Terrain**: Deep blue shaded-relief bathymetry covers the entire viewport. In the center-left, a flat abyssal plain features the large, block-lettered watermark **`DTS/SIO`** etched as shallow topographic depressions/ridges in the seafloor model. To the right, rougher submarine ridges, seamounts, and continental slope topography rise toward the upper right.
- **Navigation Controls (Right)**: Semi-transparent Google Earth 3D navigation controls on the right side include the look/compass ring with an `N` (North) indicator at the top, the pan disc below it, and the vertical zoom slider bar with `+` and `-` ends.
