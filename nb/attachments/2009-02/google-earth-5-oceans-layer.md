# Google Earth 5 Ocean Layer and Stellwagen Bank Popup (`google-earth-5-oceans-layer.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of **Google Earth 5.0** showcasing the newly introduced **Ocean** layer hierarchy in the left sidebar, the `View` pull-down menu highlighting the `Water Surface` toggle, and an open feature balloon for the **Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary**.

## Interface, Menus & Layers
- **Top Menu Bar & Toolbar**: Mac OS X menu bar with `Google Earth` and the `View` menu open, displaying:
  - `✓ Toolbar (⌥⌘T)`, `✓ Sidebar (⌥⌘B)`, `View Size ▶`, `Show Navigation ▶`, `✓ Status Bar`, `Grid (⌘L)`, `Overview Map (⌥⇧⌘M)`, `Scale Legend`, `Atmosphere`, `Sun`, `Historical Imagery`, `✓ Water Surface` (highlighted in blue), `Switch to Sky`, and `Make this my start location`.
  - Beneath the window title bar, the Google Earth toolbar shows icons for historical imagery (clock), sunlight, planet switcher (Saturn icon), ruler, email, print, and view in Google Maps.
- **Left Sidebar (`Layers` Pane)**: Shows the expanded `Primary Database` tree, specifically the expanded **`Ocean`** folder (checked) containing:
  - `☑ Explore the Ocean`
  - `☐ National Geographic`
  - `☐ BBC Earth`
  - `☐ Cousteau Ocean World`
  - `☐ Ocean Sports`
  - `☑ Shipwrecks`
  - `☑ Ocean Expeditions`
  - `☑ Marine Protected Areas` (selected in gray)
  - `☑ ARKive: Endangered Ocean ...`
  - `☐ State of the Ocean`
  - `☑ Animal Tracking`
  - `☑ Census of Marine Life`
  - `☑ Marie Tharp Historical Map`
  - `☐ Underwater Features`

## Map & Info Balloon
- **Map Background**: Shows Massachusetts Bay and Cape Cod (Provincetown hook visible at bottom center with a shipwreck icon labeled `HENRY ENDIC...`) and the white boundary outline of the Stellwagen Bank sanctuary.
- **Popup Balloon**: Pointing to a circular blue/green globe icon in the sanctuary is a large white info window:
  - **Logos (Top)**: `protect planet ocean` logo on the left; `IUCN` and `WCPA (World Commission on Protected Areas)` logos on the right.
  - **Title**: Blue underlined link `Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary` with subtitle `Ice Age glaciers formed this massive underwater bank.`
  - **Tabs**: `Photos` (active), `Stories`, `Facts`, and `Add`.
  - **Photograph**: Shows a humpback whale's open mouth lunging out of the water, captioned `Credit: (Photo: NOAA Office of National Marine Sanctuaries)` and `Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary`, with `<` and `>` navigation arrows and links for `See all photos (1) - Add Photos` and `Why Marine Protected Areas? - Protect Your Ocean`.
