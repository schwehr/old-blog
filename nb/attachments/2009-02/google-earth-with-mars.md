# Google Earth 5 Mars Mode Showing Phoenix Lander Panorama (`google-earth-with-mars.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of **Google Earth 5.0** running in **Mars** mode (`Google Mars 3D`), displaying the interactive 360-degree surface photo panorama (`PIA11734: Full-Circle Color Panorama of Phoenix Landing Site`) taken by the **NASA Phoenix Mars Lander**.

## Interface, Search & Layers
- **Top Toolbar & Planet Switcher**: In the top toolbar, the Saturn-shaped planet selector icon is clicked, opening a dropdown menu showing `Earth`, `Sky`, and `✓ Mars` (checked).
- **Left Sidebar (`Search` Pane)**:
  - Under the `Fly To` tab (`Fly To e.g., Olympus Mons`), the search box contains `phoenix`, returning the checked result `NASA Phoenix Lander`.
- **Left Sidebar (`Layers` Pane)**:
  - **`Global Maps`** (radio button group): `◉ Visible Imagery` (selected), `○ Colorized Terrain`, `○ Daytime Infrared`, `○ Nighttime Infrared`, `○ MDIM Image Map`.
  - **`Spacecraft Imagery`**: Unchecked boxes for `HiRISE Image Browser`, `CTX Image Browser`, `MOC Image Browser`, `HRSC Image Browser`, and `CRISM Image Browser`.
  - **`Mars Gallery`** (checked) -> **`Rovers and Landers`** (checked):
    - `☑ Phoenix Lander (USA)`
    - `☑ Beagle 2 Lander (UK)`
    - `☑ MER Opportunity Rover (...`
    - `☑ MER Spirit Rover (USA)`

## 3D Viewport & Phoenix Lander Panorama
- **Panorama Controls (Upper Right)**: Title overlay reads `Full-Circle Color Panorama of Phoenix Landing Site` next to an `Exit Photo` button. Below it is a rectangular thumbnail overview of the full 360-degree panorama with a white bounding rectangle showing the current field of view and four directional arrow buttons (`▲`, `▼`, `◀`, `▶`).
- **Scene Content**:
  - In the foreground, the metallic deck of the Phoenix lander is visible along with one of its circular decagonal solar array wings extending to the left.
  - In the lower-left black un-imaged cutout area of the lander deck mosaic, the **Phoenix Mars Mission logo** (orange stylized phoenix bird rising over Mars with red text reading `Phoenix` incorporating the Mars symbol `♂` on the `o`) is displayed.
  - Beyond the lander deck, rusty-brown, pebble-strewn Martian arctic plains with polygonal patterned ground stretch to the horizon.
  - Bottom right status bar shows the `©2009 Google` logo and `Eye alt -13536 ft`.
