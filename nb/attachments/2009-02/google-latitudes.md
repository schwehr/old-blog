# Google Latitude iGoogle Widget (`google-latitudes.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the compact **Google Latitude** gadget embedded on an iGoogle page, displaying the user's current city-level location on a Google Map of **Newmarket, New Hampshire**.

## Widget Layout & Map Details
- **Header Bar**: Light blue title bar with dark blue bold text reading `Google Latitude` on the left and two small window control icons (minimize/dropdown arrow and maximize/expand square) on the right.
- **Map View**:
  - Shows a road map of **`Newmarket`**, NH, including State Route **`152`** (`S Main St` / `High Falls Rd`), `Exeter Rd`, `Packers Falls Rd`, `Bay Rd`, `Old Rd`, `New Rd`, `Ross Rd`, railroad tracks, and the Lamprey River flowing into Great Bay.
  - `+` and `-` zoom buttons sit in the upper-left corner of the map.
  - Centered over downtown Newmarket is a square gray-bordered user avatar pin containing a generic blue-gray person silhouette icon, resting over a faint blue accuracy circle.
  - Map footer reads `©2009 Google - Map data ©2009 Tele Atlas - Terms of Use`.
- **Notification Banner**: Below the map, a pale yellow banner reads **`Get Google Latitude on your phone.`** accompanied by a small flip-phone icon and a gray close (`×`) button on the right.
- **User Status Card (Bottom)**: Flanked by left (`◀`) and right (`▶`) blue carousel arrows, the status area shows the generic silhouette avatar thumbnail alongside:
  - **`Kurt Schwehr`** (bold black text)
  - `Newmarket, NH, USA` (black text) followed by `2 hrs ago` (gray text)
  - `view profile »` (blue underlined link)
