# Kurt Schwehr Presenting GeoCoastPilot at a Google Tech Talk (`google-tech-talk-1.png`)

## Overview
A video frame from Kurt Schwehr's December 2008 **Google Tech Talk** (recorded around the AGU Fall Meeting), showing Kurt standing at a wooden podium on the right while projecting a demonstration of the **GeoCoastPilot** software application on a large screen to the left.

## Scene & Speaker
- **Speaker (Right)**: Kurt Schwehr stands behind a light-wood lectern against a light beige wall, wearing a dark blue polo shirt with a small white emblem on the left chest and a lapel microphone clipped to his collar.
- **Projection Screen (Left)**: A large pull-down projection screen fills the left two-thirds of the frame, displaying the **GeoCoastPilot** desktop window (`GeoCoastPilot` in the window title bar with `File  Edit  Help` menu items).

## Projected GeoCoastPilot Interface
- **Upper 3D Perspective View**:
  - Displays a 3D oblique visualization of **Portsmouth Harbor / Piscataqua River entrance** (New Castle Island and Gerry Island / Whaleback Light area), combining a 3D terrain/bathymetry model, nautical chart textures, and vertical 3D inset photographs standing upright at specific landmark locations on the water and shoreline.
  - In the lower-left corner of the 3D viewport is a vertical blue **Depth (feet)** color bar (`0.0 ft`, `10.0 ft`, `20.0 ft`) next to a circular **UNH CCOM / JHC** (Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center) logo and a 3D compass rose in the lower right.
- **Lower Text & Control Panels**:
  - **Bottom-Left Pane (`Prominent features`)**: Shows hyperlinked text from the NOAA *United States Coast Pilot* (paragraphs `[192]` through `[196]`), with blue underlined links for geographic features (`Gerrish Island`, `Wood Island`, `Whaleback Light`, `Portsmouth Harbor (New Castle) Light`, `Fort Point`, `Portsmouth Harbor Coast Guard Station`). Paragraph `[194]` (`Whaleback Light (43°03.5'N., 70°41.8'W.)...`) is highlighted as the active selection.
  - **Bottom-Center Control Panel**: Search box with a `Find` button and radio-button display options under `Chart Display` (`Nautical Chart`, `Bathymetry`) and `Display Sea Surface` (`Actual tide`, `Exaggerated`).
  - **Bottom-Right Feature Details Panel**: Shows coordinates (`43°03'31.77"N  070°41'46.67"W`), `Heading`, `Pitch`, `FOV`, navigation buttons, and a structured attribute table for **`Feature: Whaleback Light`** listing its latitude/longitude, purpose, structure (`Granite tower`), color (`Gray`), status (`Permanent`), and light characteristics.
