# Google Tech Talk Presentation: Google Earth Resume (`google-tech-talk-2.png`)

## Overview

A video still from Kurt Schwehr's December 2008 AGU Google Tech Talk presentation, showing Kurt standing at a podium in front of a projected slide titled "Google Earth Resume (GER)" that displays a Google Earth placemark pop-up window over Southern California.

## Scene & Speaker Description

- **Speaker**: Kurt Schwehr stands at a light-wood lectern in the lower right portion of the frame, wearing a short-sleeved slate-blue polo shirt with a small white emblem on the left chest and a lapel microphone clipped near his collar.
- **Setting**: A lecture room with a beige wall behind the speaker and a large projection screen framed by a black border occupying the upper left and center of the image.

## Projected Slide & Google Earth Interface

- **Slide Title**: On the tan right-hand background of the projected slide, grey text reads:
  - `Google`
  - `Earth`
  - `Resume`
  - `(GER)`
- **Satellite Map View**: The center of the projection shows a Google Earth satellite view of the Southern California coastline around San Diego and La Jolla, with terrain relief visible inland. White map labels include `La Jolla, CA - SIO - 2004`, `San Diego`, and partial text higher up reading `SIO - 2004` and `... Pathfinder / NASA Ames ...`.
- **Placemark Balloon**: A large white Google Earth information balloon is open on the left side, anchored by a pointer tail to a circular placemark at La Jolla (`La Jolla, CA - SIO - 2004`). Inside the balloon from top to bottom:
  - **Header**: Blue bold text reading `La Jolla, CA - SIO - 2004` with a small square close button (`x`) in the upper right corner.
  - **Description Text**: A short paragraph in small blue/black text describing the creation of a Fledermaus scene file combining multibeam bathymetry, chirp seismic lines, geologic faults, photos, and a figure from Driscoll and Tauxe (2004), followed by two underlined blue links (`LaJolla-mbari.scene.gz` and `LaJolla-2004-demo.mov`).
  - **Embedded Composite Figure**: A multi-panel 3D visualization screenshot showing perspective views of the La Jolla submarine canyon and seafloor bathymetry rendered in Fledermaus, inset with a small seismic profile, field photographs, and a rose/compass diagram.
  - **Institutional Logos**: Below the 3D visualization sit two circular blue-and-white logos side by side: the Scripps Institution of Oceanography (SIO) seal on the left and a second oceanographic program logo on the right.
  - **Footer**: Standard Google Earth balloon links at the bottom left reading `Directions: To here - From here`.
