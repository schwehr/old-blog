# Google Tech Talk Presentation: 3D Lighthouse Model in Google Earth (`google-tech-talk-3.png`)

## Overview

A video still from Kurt Schwehr's December 2008 AGU Google Tech Talk presentation showing Kurt speaking at a lectern while demonstrating a 3D Google SketchUp model of Highland Light (Cape Cod Light) rendered inside Google Earth on Mac OS X.

## Scene & Speaker Description

- **Speaker & Audience**: Kurt Schwehr stands behind a light-wood podium on the right, wearing a blue polo shirt with a lapel microphone. In the foreground at the bottom center and bottom left, the tops of two audience members' heads are visible facing the projection screen.
- **Projection Screen**: Occupies the upper left and center of the frame, displaying a full-screen Mac OS X desktop running Google Earth alongside an inset preview image of the 3D lighthouse model.

## Projected Google Earth Interface & 3D Model

- **Mac OS X Menu Bar**: At the very top of the projected screen, the Apple menu bar shows `Google Earth`, `File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Tools`, `Add`, `Help`, and system status icons on the right.
- **Left Sidebar (`Places` Panel)**:
  - Shows a collapsed `Search` bar at the top and an expanded `Places` tree below with an `Add Content` button.
  - Under `My Places`: items include `SBNMS boundaries`, `71 18.2'N, 156 54.0W`, `Transponder`, and `Untitled Placemark`.
  - Under `Temporary Places`: a highlighted blue selection reads `SUPreview1` (`Created with Google SketchUp 7.0.8656`), with sub-items `Tour` and `Model`.
- **Main 3D Viewport**:
  - Displays a tilted 3D perspective view of coastal terrain with roads/paths traced in white over satellite imagery.
  - Standing on the coastal bluff is a 3D architectural model of **Highland Light** (created by Christiana Arsenault), consisting of a tall white cylindrical lighthouse tower with a black lantern room connected by a covered walkway to a brown-roofed keeper's house with dormers.
  - Standard Google Earth navigation controls (compass ring, directional pad, and zoom slider) appear in the upper right corner of the 3D view, and copyright text (`Image USGS`, `© 2008 Tele Atlas`, `Image © 2008 DigitalGlobe`, `Google`) is visible along the bottom.
- **Inset Model Render**: Overlapping the lower-left portion of the screen is a clean, standalone 3D rendering of the same lighthouse and keeper's house against a blue gradient sky, labeled in the upper right corner `Model by C. Arsenault`.
