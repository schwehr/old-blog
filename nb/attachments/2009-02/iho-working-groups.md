# International Hydrographic Organization (IHO) Committees and Working Groups Chart (`iho-working-groups.png`)

## Overview

An organizational chart of the International Hydrographic Organization (IHO) illustrating its top-level bodies, advisory working groups, three main committees, and subordinate working groups and sub-committees, with an orange highlight box drawn around the Standardization of Nautical Publications Working Group (`SNPWG`).

## Top-Level Structure & Advisory Groups

- **Root & Core Bodies**:
  - At the top center is a light-blue rounded rectangle with a drop shadow labeled `IHO`.
  - Connected below `IHO` by two-way black arrows forming a triangle of mutual relationships are two large boxes: `INTERNATIONAL HYDROGRAPHIC CONFERENCE` on the left and `INTERNATIONAL HYDROGRAPHIC BUREAU` on the right.
- **Direct Working Groups**: Branching immediately below the main horizontal connector line are three rounded boxes:
  - `Legal Advisory WG (To be established only when required)` on the left, enclosed in a dashed border.
  - `Staff Regulations WG (SRWG)` in the center-right, with a solid border.
  - `IHO Strategic Plan WG (ISPWG)` on the far right, with a solid border.

## Main Committees & Subordinate Bodies

Three primary committee boxes branch off the main horizontal line:

### 1. Hydrographic Services and Standards Committee (HSSC) — Left Column
A vertical stem descends from the left side of `Hydrographic Services and Standards Committee (HSSC)` connecting to 10 subordinate boxes:
- `Transfer Standard Maintenance and Application Development WG (TSMAD)`
- `Data Protection Scheme WG (DPSWG)`
- `Digital Information Portrayal WG (ex CSMWG) (DIPWG)`
- `Standardization of Nautical Publications WG (SNPWG)` — **highlighted with a bright orange rectangular outline**.
- `Chart Standardization and Paper Chart WG (CSPCWG)`
- `Data Quality WG (DQWG)`
- `Marine Spatial Data Infrastructure WG (MSDIWG)`
- `Tidal and Water Level WG (ex Tidal Committee) (TWLWG)`
- `Hydrographic Dictionary WG (ex Committee on the Hydrographic Dictionary) (HDWG)`
- `ENC Updating WG (EUWG)`
- Connected via a dashed vertical line at the bottom of the column: `Advisory Board on the Law of the Sea (ABLOS) – (Liaison)`

### 2. Inter-Regional Coordination Committee (IRCC) — Center Column
A vertical stem descends from `Inter-Regional Coordination Committee (IRCC)` connecting to:
- Solid-line branches:
  - `Regional Hydrographic Commissions (RHC)`
  - `Hydrographic Commission on Antarctica (HCA)`
  - `Promulgation of Radio Navigational Warning Sub-Committee (PRNW)`
  - `Capacity Building Sub-Committee (CBSC)`
- Dashed-line branches (`Inter-organization liaisons`):
  - `IAB on Standards of Competence for Hydrographic Surveyors and Nautical Cartographers (IAB)`
  - `General Bathymetric Chart of the Oceans (GEBCO) Guiding Committee`, which itself has a solid sub-tree containing two sub-committees:
    - `Technical Sub-Committee on Ocean Mapping (TSCOM)`
    - `Sub-Committee on Undersea Feature Names (SCUFN)`

### 3. Finance Committee (FC) — Right Column
- A single committee box on the right labeled `Finance Committee (FC)` with no subordinate working groups.

## Legend

- At the bottom right, a horizontal dashed line (`------`) is labeled `Inter-organization liaisons`.
