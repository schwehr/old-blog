# Korean Research Icebreaker Araon Hull Thickness Profile and 3D Model (`korean-icebeaker-araon.png`)

## Overview

A two-part composite illustration of the South Korean icebreaking research vessel *Araon* (`IBRV`), pairing a port-side schematic profile diagram showing hull steel plate thicknesses (in millimeters) on the left with an isometric 3D computer-rendered model of the vessel on the right.

## Left Panel: Port-Side Profile & Hull Plate Thickness Diagram

- **Superstructure & Deck Equipment**:
  - Drawn facing right (bow on the right, stern on the left) against a white background.
  - At the stern (left) is an A-frame crane, deck crane, and elevated helipad with a grey helicopter parked on top.
  - Amidships rises a tall lattice mast flying the South Korean flag, a tan funnel bearing a circular blue KOPRI logo, a lifeboat and orange enclosed rescue boat stowed in davits, and a multi-tiered white bridge/superstructure topped with radar masts.
  - On the foredeck (right) are brown cargo containers and a forward mast/crane structure.
- **Color-Coded Hull Thickness Zones**:

  - **Upper Ice Belt (Waterline Zone)**:

    - Midbody section (yellow-green): `22.0 mm`
    - Forward shoulder section (orange): `28.0 mm`
    - Bow icebreaking stem (red): `30.0 mm`
  - **Lower Hull / Bottom Strakes**:
    - Stern skeg/run above the propeller and rudder (tan-olive): `25.0 mm`
