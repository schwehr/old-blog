# Korea Polar Research Institute (KOPRI) Website Banner Featuring Icebreaker Araon (`korean-polar-research-institute.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the interactive flash/web banner from the Korea Polar Research Institute (`KOPRI`) English homepage showcasing their new research icebreaker *Araon* against a composite backdrop of snow-covered polar mountains, sea ice, seals, and penguins.

## Scene & Composite Visuals

- **Background**: High snow-capped polar mountain peaks under a clear light-blue sky, with broken sea ice floes across the middle ground.
- **Vessel (*Araon*)**: In the lower left foreground is a photograph of the Korean research icebreaker *Araon* under construction/outfitting at a shipyard, showing its bright red-orange hull with white Korean Hangul (`아라온`) and English (`ARAON`) hull lettering, a tall white superstructure, bridge windows, and a white funnel stamped with `KOPRI`.
- **Polar Wildlife**:
  - In the bottom center foreground, two light-grey seals rest on snow and ice.
  - In the bottom right foreground, two chinstrap penguins stand on a rocky outcrop.

- **Info Callout Box**: Floating above the ship in the upper center-left is a semi-transparent dark grey rounded rectangle containing:
  - **Thumbnail Photo**: A framed photo on the left showing the red-and-white *Araon* sitting on blocks in a drydock/shipyard with cranes in the background.
  - **Title**: White bold text reading `Korean Research IceBreaker` above a horizontal divider line.
