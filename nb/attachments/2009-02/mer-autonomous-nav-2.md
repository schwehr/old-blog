# Mars Exploration Rover Autonomous Navigation: 3D Point Cloud on Terrain Grid (`mer-autonomous-nav-2.png`)

## Overview

A 3D visualization frame from the JPL Mars Exploration Rover (MER) autonomous navigation animation showing the rover positioned on a wireframe elevation grid while projecting a dense white 3D stereo point cloud onto the terrain ahead of it.

## Scene & Visual Elements

- **Mars Rover Model**:
  - Positioned near the upper center against a solid black sky background, viewed from an elevated front-right three-quarter angle.
  - Features dark blue solar panels, a gold-colored warm electronics box (body), six silver rocker-bogie wheels, a high-gain antenna dish, and the vertical Pancam/Navcam mast looking forward.
- **Wireframe Terrain Mesh**:
  - The surrounding surface is rendered as a dark purple/magenta and light-blue rectilinear grid that conforms to the 3D topography of hills and depressions.
  - A bright **green line** traces the rover's path across the grid beneath its wheels, and a single grid cell to the left of the rover is filled in bright **green**.
- **Stereo Point Cloud**:
  - Directly in front of the rover's wheels, thousands of bright **white dots** form a wedge-shaped 3D point cloud generated from stereo camera range data.
  - The point cloud densely covers the ground in front of the rover and wraps up over a rounded mound/boulder obstacle just ahead of the front-left wheel, with a white contour loop outlining the base of the obstacle on the grid.
