# NOAA Atlantic Hydrographic Branch Waterfront at Dusk (`noaa-ahb.jpg`)

## Overview
A dusk photograph looking across the calm waterfront of the NOAA Atlantic Hydrographic Branch (AHB) facility in Norfolk, Virginia, taken during Kurt Schwehr's visit on February 25, 2009. The scene features a two-masted sailing schooner moored in the foreground against a twilight sky and harbor lights.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Vessel & Pier**: In the left foreground, a traditional two-masted wooden schooner is moored alongside a wooden pier supported by dark vertical pilings. Its tall foremast and mainmast rise high into the frame, complete with shrouds, stays, ratlines, a long bowsprit extending to the right, and furled sails wrapped in dark covers along the booms. A warm yellow light glows near the cabin structure, and a circular lifebuoy and small triangular pennant flags hang along the dock railing to the left.
- **Middle Ground & Harbor Structures**: Beyond the schooner, a long pier extends from right to left across the water. A two-story pitched-roof building with warm interior and exterior lights sits on the pier near the center. To its left stands a blocky, multi-story concrete building with a distinctive row of gabled/peaked roofline bays. To the right of the pier building, ships and harbor buildings are brightly lit with golden-orange floodlights.
- **Right Shoreline**: On the right side of the frame, dark brick or stone seawalls and a multi-story building with a few illuminated windows sit beneath the bare, branching canopy of large deciduous winter trees silhouetted against the evening sky.
- **Sky & Water Reflections**: The sky transitions smoothly from a warm golden-orange and pale peach glow along the left horizon to a deep twilight cobalt blue overhead. The calm harbor water in the foreground reflects the dark pilings, the blue evening sky, and vertical streaks of orange and gold light from the distant waterfront.
