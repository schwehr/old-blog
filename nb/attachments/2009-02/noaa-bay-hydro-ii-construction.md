# Interior Construction in Progress on R/V Bay Hydro II (`noaa-bay-hydro-ii-construction.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior photograph of the pilothouse ceiling and helm area aboard the *NOAA Bay Hydro II* during outfitting on February 26, 2009, illustrating unfinished electrical wiring and overhead insulation prior to final commissioning.

## Scene Description
- **Overhead Ceiling & Insulation**: The upper half of the frame shows the exposed aluminum roof structure of the cabin before ceiling panels have been installed. Thick sheets of shiny, silver foil-backed thermal/acoustic insulation are fitted between the longitudinal and transverse aluminum framing beams.
- **Exposed Wiring & Access Hatches**: Above the forward windshield windows, a cream-colored molded overhead console features circular cutout holes and two rectangular access hatches with their hinged white plastic doors hanging open. Bundles of colorful electrical wires (red, black, yellow, green, blue, and white with crimped spade/bullet connectors) hang down from the circular routing holes and open hatches, awaiting connection to overhead instruments or switches.
- **Helm & Windshield**: Below the overhead console, four forward-facing slanted pilothouse windows (with windshield wiper arms visible) let in bright daylight. Along the bottom of the frame is the helm console, showing the top of the stainless-steel destroyer-style steering wheel, a mounted display unit on the far left, twin engine monitor panels, circular gauges, red and green push buttons, and chrome twin-lever engine throttle/shift controls on the right.
