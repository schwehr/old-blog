# Aft Deck, Moon Pool, and Sonar Mount on R/V Bay Hydro II (`noaa-bay-hydro-ii-sonar-mount.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of the aft deck and rear cabin bulkhead of the *NOAA Bay Hydro II* survey catamaran, highlighting its flush deck moon pool cover and vertical aluminum sonar deployment strut designed for swapping hydrographic sonars.

## Scene Description
- **Aft Deck & Moon Pool**: In the lower foreground, the wide flat aluminum aft working deck features flush-mounted rectangular access hatches. Near the bottom center-left is a textured dark gray rectangular cover over the vessel's moon pool, sitting directly in front of the vertical sonar mount assembly.
- **Sonar Mount & Deployment Strut**: Centered against the aft cabin bulkhead is a heavy-duty vertical aluminum deployment channel/strut that extends from the deck (resting on a wooden block) up through the upper deck overhang. A vertical ladder with black non-slip rungs is mounted immediately to the right of the sonar strut, leading up to the flying bridge/upper deck.
- **Aft Cabin Bulkhead**: The brushed aluminum rear wall of the cabin has a black-framed entry door on the left, a dark-tinted window in the center, and two square louvered ventilation grilles near the deck on either side. Two bright orange ring lifebuoys with silver reflective tape are mounted in brackets on the left and right sides of the bulkhead. A KvH or builder's logo decal is affixed to the starboard side of the upper deck fascia, along with a circular NOAA logo further forward.
- **Upper Deck**: Enclosed by aluminum pipe railings, the upper deck holds white cylindrical life raft canisters mounted in cradles on the port and starboard sides, as well as an auxiliary helm wheel near the center.
- **Background**: Beyond the wooden pier pilings on the right, modern multi-story buildings and a curved cylindrical parking or office structure are visible along the Norfolk waterfront.
