# Helm and Navigation Console of R/V Bay Hydro II (`noaa-bay-hydro-ii-wheel.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up interior photograph of the helm station and navigation instrumentation aboard the *NOAA Bay Hydro II* taken on February 26, 2009, showing the stainless-steel steering wheel, Furuno NavNet chartplotter, engine displays, and controls.

## Helm & Instrumentation Details
- **Steering Wheel**: In the center foreground is a polished five-spoke stainless-steel ship's wheel with a black center hub cap, mounted on a brushed aluminum helm console trimmed with warm wooden edging.
- **Chartplotter / Radar Display (Left)**: Mounted on a bracket to the left of the helm is a gray **Furuno NavNet** (`NAVnet vx2`) multifunction display unit. Its color LCD screen is powered on and shows an electronic nautical chart of the Norfolk harbor pier area in blue and tan with a vessel track/vector line. To the right of the screen are vertical rows of soft keys, a directional trackball/cursor pad, rotary knob, and numeric/function buttons.
- **Main Instrument Dash (Center & Right)**: The angled black dashboard panel houses symmetric port and starboard engine monitoring and control instruments:
  - **Engine LCD Displays**: Two rectangular digital engine diagnostic screens (powered off) with six vertical soft keys on their right sides, flanking the center gauges.
  - **Center Gauges & Compass**: Between the digital screens is a large circular rudder angle indicator in the center flanked by two smaller white-faced analog gauges. Directly on top of the dash sits a black-domed magnetic binnacle compass.
  - **Start/Stop Button Panels**: Below both the port and starboard digital displays are rectangular black panels labeled `PORT MAIN ENGINE` and `STBD MAIN ENGINE`, each with three large illuminated-style push buttons: a green `START` button, a small red `STOP` button, and a larger red `E-STOP` (emergency stop) mushroom button.
  - **Binoculars & Switches**: Resting on the ledge behind the wheel is a pair of olive-drab armored marine binoculars. To the left of the binoculars is a small toggle switch panel with a red safety guard.
  - **Engine Throttles (Far Right)**: On the right side of the wooden ledge is a polished chrome twin-lever electronic engine throttle and shift control head with keypad buttons at its base.
