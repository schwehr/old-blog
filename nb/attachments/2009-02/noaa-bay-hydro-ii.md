# Bow View of NOAA R/V Bay Hydro II (`noaa-bay-hydro-ii.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up daytime photograph showing the bow and pilothouse of the NOAA hydrographic survey catamaran *R/V Bay Hydro II* (`S5401`) moored alongside a wooden pier in Norfolk, Virginia, taken during a vessel tour on February 26, 2009.

## Vessel Details
- **Hull & Bow Design**: The vessel features an unpainted, brushed aluminum twin-hull (catamaran) design with sharp symmetrical bows and a central tunnel. An anchor is recessed into the center bow slot between the two hulls, with a black mooring line draped across the front.
- **Hull Markings**: Black block lettering along the starboard bow reads:
  - `R/V BAY HYDRO II`
  - `S5401`
- **Foredeck & Railings**: An aluminum safety railing encloses the foredeck. Mounted on the front face of the cabin trunk is a circular NOAA emblem decal featuring a dark blue upper half, light blue lower half, white stylized seabird/wave, and `"NOAA"` text encircled by `"NATIONAL OCEANIC AND ATMOSPHERIC ADMINISTRATION • U.S. DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE"`. A bright orange ring lifebuoy with reflective silver tape hangs on the starboard railing, and white round fenders with blue tops hang over the starboard side against the wooden pier pilings.
- **Pilothouse**: Above the foredeck, the angled aluminum pilothouse has five forward-facing dark-framed windows, each equipped with a top-mounted pantograph windshield wiper. Inside the center window, a circular NOAA emblem is visible. A person wearing sunglasses and a brown long-sleeved shirt stands on the starboard side deck beside the cabin.

## Background
- To the right of the *Bay Hydro II*, the gray bow of a naval vessel (USS *Wisconsin*, BB-64) is visible with large white hull numbers `64` and white draft marks near the waterline.
- Behind the vessels are brick/tan multi-story residential or commercial buildings with white balconies along the Norfolk waterfront.
