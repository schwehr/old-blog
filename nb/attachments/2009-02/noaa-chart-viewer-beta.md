# NOAA Office of Coast Survey Paper/RNC Interactive Catalog Beta (`noaa-chart-viewer-beta.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the NOAA Office of Coast Survey's public beta web application for the **Paper/RNC Catalog** (Interactive Nautical Chart Catalog), captured on February 9, 2009. The interface uses an embedded Google Maps view overlaid with colored nautical chart bounding boxes covering the Chesapeake Bay, Virginia, and North Carolina coast.

## Header & Navigation Bars
- **Top Banner**: Features the circular NOAA logo on the left, large white text reading `Office of Coast Survey` over faded `OCS` lettering, a prominent red `BETA` label in the center, and a graphic of a hydrographic survey ship mapping a rainbow-colored multibeam swath on the right. Small top-right links read `HOME | ABOUT US | CONTACT | REGIONAL MANAGERS`.
- **Primary Blue Navigation Bar**: Links for `Nautical Charts & Pubs`, `Surveys & Wrecks`, `GIS & Other Products`, `Research & Development`, `Customer Service`, `Business Opportunities`, and `Education`.
- **Catalog Tab Bar**: Tabs for `Paper/RNC Catalog` (selected, black text on white/gray), `ENC Catalog`, `Download Files by State`, `Download Files by CGD`, `Text Paper/RNC Catalog`, and `Text ENC Catalog`.

## Map View & Chart Overlays
- **Base Map**: A standard Google Maps street/regional map (`Map data ©2009 Tele Atlas`) showing eastern Virginia (including Richmond, Williamsburg, Newport News, Hampton, Norfolk, Portsmouth, Virginia Beach, Suffolk, and Chesapeake) and northeastern North Carolina (Elizabeth City, Albemarle Sound, Currituck Sound, Kitty Hawk, Nags Head, Roanoke Island, and Greenville).
- **Map Controls**: Standard Google Maps pan/zoom slider on the upper left, a `search the map` input box with a `Search` button at the top left, `Map`, `Satellite`, and `Hybrid` toggle buttons at the top right, and a `20 mi / 50 km` scale bar next to the Google logo at the bottom left.
- **Chart Footprints**: Numerous overlapping rectangular and polygonal outlines in magenta/purple, red, green, and yellow indicate the coverage areas of different nautical charts at varying scales. A red map pin labeled `A` is dropped directly on Norfolk/Portsmouth, with Chart `12253` highlighted in bright yellow around Norfolk Harbor.
