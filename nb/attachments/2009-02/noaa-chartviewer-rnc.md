# NOAA Online Raster Nautical Chart Viewer for Chart 12253 (`noaa-chartviewer-rnc.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the NOAA Office of Coast Survey's online Raster Nautical Chart (RNC) viewer displaying **Chart 12253** (*Norfolk Harbor and Elizabeth River*), zoomed in on the Elizabeth River, Lambert Point, Pinner Point, West Norfolk, and downtown Norfolk, Virginia.

## Header Information & Links
- **Top Metadata Bar**: Bold black text across the top center reads:
  - `Chart: 12253`
  - `Edition: 46`
  - `Edition Date: 8/1/2008`
  - `Clear Dates: NM - 2/7/2009   LNM - 2/3/2009`
- **Top-Right Links**: Partially visible blue hyperlinks on the far right read `Download...`, `Notice Li...`, and `Find A Cha...`.

## Chart Display & Interface Controls
- **Overview Inset Map (Upper Left)**: A small boxed thumbnail of the full vertical extent of Chart 12253, with a red rectangle near the top indicating the currently zoomed viewport around Norfolk and the Elizabeth River entrance.
- **Main Raster Chart View**:
  - **Waterways**: Shows the dredged shipping channel running diagonally from `CRANEY ISLAND REACH` at the top left through `LAMBERT BEND`, `LAMBERT BEND TO PINNER POINT`, and `PINNER POINT TO TOWN POINT REACH` toward the bottom right. Also shows the `Western Branch` (`Lilly Cr`) entering from the bottom left. Water depths are marked in feet with blue tinting in shoal areas and white in deeper channels, alongside magenta restricted area boundaries, buoys, beacons, and anchorages.
  - **Land & Port Facilities**: Buff/tan land areas depict extensive rail yards and coal/cargo piers at Lambert Point (center), Pinner Point and `PORT NORFOLK` (bottom center), `WEST NORFOLK` (lower left), and the dense street grid of `NORFOLK` (right), including the star-shaped waterfront fortification of Fort Norfolk and surrounding docks.
- **Bottom Navigation Toolbar**: Floating over the lower center of the chart are translucent dark-gray icon buttons for zooming in (`+`), zooming out (`-`), panning left, up, down, and right (arrow icons), and resetting the view (circular arrow icon).
