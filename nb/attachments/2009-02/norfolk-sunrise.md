# Sunrise Reflected in a Building Facade in Norfolk, Virginia (`norfolk-sunrise.jpg`)

## Overview
An architectural photograph taken in Norfolk, Virginia, on February 26, 2009, showing a vivid orange and gold sunrise reflected across the gridded glass curtain wall of a modern building.

## Scene Description
- **Building Facade & Window Grid**: The entire frame is spanned by the exterior wall of a building divided vertically by five wide, light-tan/pinkish stone or precast concrete pilasters (columns). Between these vertical piers are tall multi-pane window bays framed by dark gray/bronze mullions arranged in a regular rectangular grid.
- **Reflected Sunrise & Roofline**: Reflected in the glass panes is a glowing orange, amber, and golden-yellow morning sky. Extending across the left and center window bays is the dark silhouette of a neighbouring building's pitched, standing-seam metal roof, which slopes down toward the center-right.
- **Reflected Cityscape & Lights**: Right of center, where the pitched roofline drops away, the brightest white-yellow glare of the rising sun appears just above the horizon line. In the right-hand window bays, the silhouette of a distant multi-story downtown building and several warm glowing streetlamps or building lights are visible against the deep orange sky. Inside the building behind the glass, faint vertical blinds and interior ceiling fixtures can be seen in the upper left panes.
