# ODEC Bathy 2000 Subbottom Survey Tracklines in Google Earth (`odec-bathy2000-ge.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from Google Earth showing two overlapping ship tracklines generated from an ODEC Bathy 2000 subbottom seismic data file (`y0902-01.dat`) and its SIOSEIS-converted SEG-Y counterpart (`y0902-01.segy`), plotted over deep ocean bathymetry in the North Atlantic (~39.46° N, 65.52° W).

## Map & Trackline Details
- **Background Bathymetry**: The entire view consists of shaded blue seafloor bathymetry in Google Earth, showing a submarine slope or seamount flank transitioning from medium cobalt blue in the upper left to darker navy/indigo depths with lighter blue ridges in the lower center and right.
- **Plotted Tracklines**: Two nearly identical straight diagonal line segments run from lower-left (southwest) to upper-right (northeast) in the upper half of the image:
  - A **magenta/pink line** representing one of the KML exports.
  - A **white line** running immediately parallel to and slightly below the magenta line, showing the close agreement between the `simplesegy` (`segy-info`) direct KML output and the `sioseis`-converted SEG-Y track.
- **Placemark & Labels**:
  - A yellow Google Earth pushpin icon is anchored near the middle-right portion of the trackline.
  - To the left of the pushpin, white text with a dark drop shadow reads `Bathy2000/y0902-01.dat`.
  - To the right of the pushpin, matching white text reads `y0902-01.segy`.
