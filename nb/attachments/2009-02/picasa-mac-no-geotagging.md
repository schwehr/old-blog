# Picasa for Mac Disabled Geotag Menu (`picasa-mac-no-geotagging.png`)

## Overview
A close-up screenshot of the Mac OS X menu bar in **Picasa 3**, demonstrating that all options under the `Tools > Geotag` submenu are grayed out and unavailable on the Mac version of the software.

## Menu & Interface Details
- **Mac Menu Bar**: Along the top edge, the menu items `...ture` (`Picture`), `Create`, `Tools` (highlighted in blue), and `Help` are visible on the left, while status bar icons (including a Growl/utility icon, paw print icon, and AirPort Wi-Fi signal icon) appear on the right.
- **`Tools` Dropdown Menu**:
  - `Folder Manager...` (grayed out)
  - `Upload Manager...` (grayed out)
  - `Back Up Pictures...` (grayed out)
  - `Upload ►` (enabled, black text)
  - `Geotag ►` (highlighted in blue, expanding the submenu)
  - `Experimental ►` (enabled, black text)
  - `Configure Buttons...` (enabled, black text)
- **`Geotag` Submenu**: All four geospatial integration items are completely disabled (grayed out):
  - `Geotag With Google Earth...`
  - `View in Google Earth...`
  - `Clear Geotag`
  - `Export to Google Earth File`
- **Background**: Behind the open menus is the top filmstrip bar of Picasa 3 showing six small thumbnails of Boston Harbor photos and a right-pointing navigation arrow button.
