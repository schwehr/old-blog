# Ship Tracks on the Madeira Abyssal Plain (`shiptracks-that-show-in-google-oceans.png`)

## Overview
A black-and-white scientific figure from a 1987 report by R. C. Searle et al. (*The Geology of the Madeira Abyssal Plain: Further studies relevant to its suitability for radioactive waste disposal*), showing the dense network of oceanographic research vessel tracks that created a rectangular grid artifact in Google Ocean's bathymetric data off the coast of Northwest Africa.

## Map & Plot Details
- **Coordinate System & Extent**:
  - **Latitude (Vertical Axis, Right)**: Labeled from `30°` N at the bottom to `33°` N at the top, with tick marks and labels every 30 arcminutes (`30°`, `30° 30'`, `31°`, `31° 30'`, `32°`, `32° 30'`, `33°`).
  - **Longitude (Horizontal Axis, Bottom)**: Labeled from `26°` W on the left to `23°` W on the right, with tick marks and labels every 30 arcminutes (`26°`, `25° 30'`, `25°`, `24° 30'`, `24°`, `23° 30'`, `23°`).
- **Vessel Track Lines**:
  - Fine black lines trace overlapping, crisscrossing ship survey paths across the map area.
  - Many tracks follow systematic, parallel, back-and-forth "mowing the lawn" survey patterns oriented predominantly roughly north-northeast to south-southwest and west-northwest to east-southeast.
  - A particularly dense concentration of intersecting tracks and tight loops appears between `31°` N and `31° 30'` N, and `24° 30'` W and `25° 30'` W.
- **Highlighted Study Boundaries & Labels**:
  - A thick, irregular black polygon outlines the broader survey boundary across the central portion of the figure, extending from roughly `30° 15'` N to `32° 35'` N.
  - Inside this polygon, a bold rectangular box spans approximately `31° 05'` N to `31° 35'` N and `24° 30'` W to `25° 40'` W, enclosing the highest-density survey zone.
  - Within the bold rectangle, a thick diagonal line segment runs from northwest to southeast with the letter **`A`** near its upper-left end (around `31° 27'` N, `24° 55'` W) and the letter **`J`** near its lower-right end (around `31° 10'` N, `24° 32'` W).
