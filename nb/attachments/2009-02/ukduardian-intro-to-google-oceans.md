# Sylvia Earle and John Hanke Introducing Google Ocean (`ukduardian-intro-to-google-oceans.png`)

## Overview
A video still captured from a promotional interview featured on *The Guardian* (`guardian.co.uk`) in early February 2009, showing oceanographer Sylvia Earle and Google Earth Director John Hanke standing at a marina to introduce the launch of Google Ocean in Google Earth 5.0.

## Scene & People
- **Left — Sylvia Earle**:
  - Oceanographer and explorer Sylvia Earle stands on the left, wearing a red zip-up vest/jacket over a black turtleneck sweater.
  - She has short brown hair, is looking toward the camera/interviewer, and holds her hands together at chest height while speaking.
- **Right — John Hanke**:
  - John Hanke stands to the right, wearing a dark black/charcoal zip-up hoodie over a dark collared shirt with his hands clasped in front of him, looking slightly toward Sylvia Earle with a mild smile.
- **Background (Marina)**:
  - Behind them is a harbor/marina filled with moored white sailboats with tall masts and blue sail covers.
  - Prominent in the center background is a large white vessel (a Coast Guard cutter or research ship) with black mast structures and radar domes, set against low coastal hills under an overcast sky.
