# Aerial View of Virginia Estuarine Sandbars and Tidal Shoals (`virginia-coastal-processes-2.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from an aircraft at approximately 14,000 feet during Kurt Schwehr's flight to Norfolk, Virginia on February 25, 2009, highlighting intricate shallow-water sandbars, subaqueous dunes, and tidal channels along the Chesapeake Bay / Virginia coastline.

## Geographic & Coastal Features
- **Submerged Shoals & Sand Waves**:
  - A broad, shallow subaqueous sandbank rims the irregular shoreline on the right and sweeps outward toward the lower left and upper left.
  - Bright pale-green and tan underwater sandbars contrast sharply against the deeper dark-teal water of the bay on the left and upper center.
  - Across the lower-left and central shoals, series of parallel, rhythmic sand waves and megaripples are clearly visible beneath the water surface, sculpted by tidal currents and wave action.
  - Narrow, darker-green incised tidal channels cut through the bright sand flats from small marsh creeks on the right shoreline out into the deeper basin.
- **Shoreline & Landforms**:
  - Along the right side of the image, the coast is indented by small tidal creeks, pocket beaches, and marshy peninsulas.
  - Inland areas show rectangular agricultural fields, dark green pine forest stands, rural roads, and scattered waterfront houses with private docks extending into the creeks (such as at the bottom center).
  - At the top of the frame, another wooded peninsula or island is bordered by wide, bright underwater sand spits extending into the bay.
