# Aerial View of Barrier Islands, Salt Marshes, and Tidal Shoals (`virginia-coastal-processes-3.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from an airplane window at roughly 14,000 feet en route to Norfolk, Virginia on February 25, 2009, showing a complex coastal barrier island and salt marsh system surrounded by extensive underwater tidal shoals and sand waves.

## Scene & Geographic Details
- **Aircraft Wingtip**:
  - A bright red-orange aircraft wingtip or horizontal stabilizer projects into the upper-left corner of the frame.
- **Barrier Islands & Salt Marshes**:
  - In the upper center and upper right, a vast network of brownish-tan coastal salt marshes is intersected by winding, dendritic tidal channels and creeks.
  - Extending southward into the center of the frame are several low-lying marsh islands and sandy barrier islets rimmed by bright white/tan beach ridges and recurved spits.
- **Subaqueous Sand Shoals & Bedforms**:
  - Surrounding the islands across the lower and middle portions of the frame are expansive, shallow underwater sand shoals rendered in vivid shades of yellow-tan, chartreuse, and emerald green.
  - Prominent sets of curved, parallel subaqueous sand ridges and wave-refraction patterns wrap around the outer margins of the shoals in the lower left, center, and lower right.
  - Faint vertical light streaks in the lower right are window reflections from inside the aircraft cabin.
