# John Hunter Lecture on Matplotlib and VTK 3D Brain Visualization (`vtk-video-1.png`)

## Overview
A side-by-side split-screen screenshot from a video lecture by Matplotlib creator John D. Hunter (*Matplotlib*, presented at the NIPS '08 Workshop on Machine Learning Open Source Software in Whistler and hosted on `videolectures.net`), showing a 3D Visualization Toolkit (VTK) medical rendering of intracranial electrode grids overlaid on a semi-transparent human skull.

## Right Panel: Slide / Screen Capture
- **3D Skull & Electrode Visualization**:
  - Set against a black background, a translucent 3D CT volume rendering of a human skull is shown in right-lateral/oblique profile facing left.
  - Overlaid on the right hemisphere of the cranium is a dense array of spherical subdural/intracranial electrocorticography (ECoG) electrodes:
    - A rectangular grid of **red** spheres across the lateral frontal/parietal/temporal cortex, each labeled with small yellow numbers.

  - Two curved, semi-transparent gray cutting/projection planes frame the back and top of the 3D volume.
