# John Hunter Lecture Showing VTK 3D Cortical Electrode Connectivity (`vtk-video-2.png`)

## Overview
A second side-by-side split-screen screenshot from John D. Hunter's NIPS '08 MLOSS workshop lecture on `videolectures.net`, demonstrating an interactive 3D VTK window (`View3 Window`) that visualizes cortical surface potential maps, electrode grids, and 3D network connectivity arcs intersecting orthogonal MRI/CT slice planes.

## Left Panel: Lecture Video Feed
- **Speaker & Projection Screen**:
  - John D. Hunter stands on the left in a light blue shirt, raising his left arm in motion to point toward the projection screen.
  - The projection screen displays the `View3 Window` application along with a bottom caption reading `MLOSS: December 2008`.
- **Video Player Overlay & Controls**:
  - The `http://videolectures.net` watermark appears in the lower right of the video frame.
  - The bottom playback bar shows pause/stop buttons, current timestamp **`07:24`**, total duration **`47:09`**, fullscreen button, and volume control.

## Right Panel: `View3 Window` Application Capture
- **Window Chrome & Toolbars**:
  - A desktop application window titled **`View3 Window`** at the top left.
  - A vertical toolbar along the left edge contains seven small 3D manipulation and view icons.
  - A bottom control bar includes two dropdown menus set to **`delta`** and **`abs.`**, followed by three checkboxes: **`[x] GM`**, **`[ ] Auto`**, and **`[ ] Selected`**, plus a small green circular icon.
- **3D Visualization Contents**:
  - **Orthogonal Slice Planes**: Two intersecting grayscale anatomical MRI/CT cross-sectional slices (axial and coronal/sagittal) are rendered in 3D space inside colored wireframe bounding boxes.
  - **Interpolated Cortical Grid Surfaces**: Two curved rectangular surface patches covered with red and green spherical electrode nodes are color-mapped with continuous scalar fields (blue, green, yellow, orange, and red gradients representing brain activity in the `delta` band).
  - **3D Connectivity Arcs**: Numerous white and green 3D tubular arcs arch between pairs of electrodes across and within the cortical grids, along with colored vertical/diagonal tracts connecting down to yellow and light-blue nodes in deeper basal/temporal structures.
