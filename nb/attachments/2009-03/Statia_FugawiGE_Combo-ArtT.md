# Fugawi Marine ENC with Google Earth Integration (`Statia_FugawiGE_Combo-ArtT.png`)

## Overview
A desktop screenshot of **Fugawi Marine ENC Ver. 4.5** GPS navigation software running on Microsoft Windows, provided by Art Trembanis. The window displays a split-screen view of the western coastline of Sint Eustatius ("Statia") in the Caribbean Netherlands, pairing an electronic navigational chart (`DOM REPUBLIC-GRENADA - Marine e-chart (3G111XL.NV2)`) on the left with a synchronized Google Earth satellite view on the right.

## Interface & Controls
- **Title Bar**: Displays the Fugawi globe icon and text `Fugawi Marine ENC Ver. 4.5 - DOM REPUBLIC-GRENADA - Marine e-chart (3G111XL.NV2)`, with classic Windows minimize, restore, and close buttons on the right.
- **Menu Bar**: Contains items `File`, `Maps`, `Settings`, `View`, `GPS`, `3D`, `PDA`, `Places`, `ENC`, `Tools`, `Google`, and `Help`.
- **Top Toolbar**: Features icons for opening/closing charts, map layer management, zoom in/out, pan arrows (left, right, up, down), a `3D` view toggle, an `i` information button, a `MOB` (Man Overboard) emergency button, an anchor alarm icon, and the Google Earth globe icon (highlighted/depressed to show the active Google Earth split pane).
- **Left Vertical Toolbar**: Includes tools for zoom, pan (hand cursor), selection box, help (`?`), waypoints (`WP`), routes (`RTE`), tracks (`TRK`), camera/photo, dividers/measurement (`A` compass), drawing/annotation pencil, and specialized ENC display toggles.
- **Bottom Panels**:
  - **AIS Vessels**: A collapsed dockable bar with up-arrow expand buttons.
  - **Navigation**: An expanded instrument panel showing:
    - A 4-point compass rose (`N`, `S`, `E`, `W`) next to a scale bar (`0` to `700 m`, `1 : 27 000`).
    - A black circular compass dial with white cardinal/intercardinal markings and a vertical white needle pointing North.
    - Readouts for `Speed: ***`, `Course: ***`, and `Altitude: ***`.
    - `GPS:` status indicator with a bright red rectangle and `INVALID FIX`.
    - Waypoint navigation readouts: `WP: ***`, `CTS: ***`, and `XTE: ***`.
  - **Navionics Bar**: A collapsed panel labeled `Navionics® - DOM REPUBLIC-GRENADA - Marine e-chart`.
  - **Status Bar**: At the very bottom, displays `GPS : None`, `CUR: 17° 29.7452' N 62° 59.2800' W 120.4 m`, `WGS84`, `Soundings in Feet`, `GMT-00:00`, and `2:24:26`.

  - **Landmarks & Labels**: From north to south along the coast: `JENKIN'S BAY`, `BERGJE 277FT`, `PISGA 188FT`, `TANKS`, `TUMBLE DOWN DICK BAY`, `SIGNAL`, `INTERLOPERS POINT`, `FORT ROYAL`, and `WHITE HOUSE`.
