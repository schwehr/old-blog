# Google Earth Buoy and Ship Observations KML Layer (`google-earth-gallery-ships-and-buoys.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** displaying the **Buoy and Ship Observations** KML layer (created by Weather Decision Technologies Inc. / iMapWeather and ClearPoint) over the Northeast United States coast (showing Long Island, New York, New England, and Nova Scotia/Bay of Fundy). Multiple 3D-style buoy and sailboat icons are plotted offshore with two open information balloons showing real-time meteorological and oceanographic observations.

## Map & Legend Details
- **Ship and Buoy Legend (Top Left Overlay)**:
  - A rounded rectangular blue gradient box titled `Ship and Buoy Legend` with three columns representing wind severity:
    1. Standard (unlabeled column, white icons): white `Buoy Observation` tower icon and white `Ship Observation` sailboat icon.
    2. `Tropical Storm Force Winds`: yellow buoy icon and yellow sailboat icon.
    3. `Hurricane Force Winds`: red buoy icon and red sailboat icon.
- **Map Markers**:
  - Numerous white buoy icons on circular gray rings and white sailboat icons with orange hulls are distributed across the Atlantic waters south of Long Island, off Cape Cod, in the Gulf of Maine, and in the Bay of Fundy.
