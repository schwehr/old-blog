# NOAA Science On a Sphere at Nauticus in Norfolk, VA (`norfolk-science-on-a-sphere.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph of the **NOAA Science On a Sphere** exhibit inside the **Nauticus** maritime science center in Norfolk, Virginia, showing a suspended illuminated globe displaying the logos of NOAA and Nauticus over a global map projection.

## Scene Description
- **Science On a Sphere Globe**: Suspended near the top-center of the darkened exhibit hall is a large spherical projection screen illuminated in vibrant blue and cyan.
  - Continents (including Australia and parts of Southeast Asia on the left) are visible in dark green and brown against deep blue oceans with swirl patterns.
  - Projected onto the front of the sphere are two large circular emblems side by side:
    - **Left**: The circular **NOAA** (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) emblem featuring a stylized white seabird/wave and `NOAA` text.
    - **Right**: The **NAUTICUS** logo in bright green/cyan featuring a stylized wave crest encircling the word `NAUTICUS`.
- **Foreground Enclosure**: Directly beneath the suspended sphere is a circular barrier made of curved dark metal stanchions supporting a horizontal handrail and angled information plaques.
- **Background Exhibit Hall**:
  - To the left is a brightly lit wooden display structure resembling a coastal shack or boathouse with horizontal siding, a white door, framed notices, and maritime gear (buoys/floats) hanging on its corner, alongside a low wooden-post fence and a large warm-toned coastal marsh mural.
  - To the right, a hallway wall features two rows of framed pictures, an overhead directional sign, and distant lit display cases.
