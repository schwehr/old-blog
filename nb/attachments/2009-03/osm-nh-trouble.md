# Missing Great Bay and Little Bay in OpenStreetMap (`osm-nh-trouble.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **OpenStreetMap (OSM)** centered on the New Hampshire and southern Maine seacoast (`Durham`, `Portsmouth`, `Kittery`, `Exeter`, `Hampton`), illustrating a rendering/coastline polygon bug where **Great Bay** and **Little Bay** are completely missing as water bodies, leaving their islands (`Goat Island`, `Moat Island`, `Footman Islands`, `Vols Island`, `Nannie Island`, `Swan Island`) stranded on dry land.

## Map Details & Geographic Features
- **Missing Estuary Water Bodies**:
  - While the Atlantic Ocean (right/southeast) and the Piscataqua River down to Portsmouth/Kittery are rendered in light blue, the entire inland tidal estuary of **Little Bay** and **Great Bay** between Durham, Newington, Stratham, and Greenland is filled with beige land background instead of water.
  - Only the bridges/causeways (such as the Spaulding Turnpke / US-4 bridge at `Goat Island` / Dover Point) and island labels remain visible where the bays should be.
- **Labeled Islands on "Dry Land" (Great Bay & Little Bay)**:
  - `Goat Island`
  - `Moat Island`
  - `Footman Islands`
  - `Vols Island`
  - `Nannie Island`
  - `Swan Island`
- **Labeled Islands in the Piscataqua River, Portsmouth Harbor & Isles of Shoals**:
  - River/Harbor: `Frankfort Island`, `Badgers Island`, `Four Tree Island`, `Phillips Island`, `Fishing Island`, `Cutts Island`, `Raynes Island`, `Lady Isle`, `Goose Island`, `Wood Island`, `White Island`.
  - Coast & Isles of Shoals (offshore to the southeast): `Cape Neddick Nubble`, `Boon Island`, `Rocks Nose`, `Duck Island`, `Appledore Island`, `Malaga Island`, `Lunging Island`, `White Island`.
- **Municipalities & Highways**:
  - Towns: `Durham`, `Portsmouth`, `Kittery`, `Exeter`, `Hampton`.
  - Highways: Interstate 95 (blue motorway), Spaulding Turnpike / Route 16 (blue), US-4 (pink/red primary road), Route 101 (`9`, `10` exits in blue), Route `3A`, and Pease International Tradeport runway (light pink polygon west of Portsmouth).
  - Numerous brown triangle peak/hill summit symbols are scattered across the region.
