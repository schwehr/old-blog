# Waterfront in Portland, Maine (`portland-me-waterfront.jpg`)

## Overview
A horizontal outdoor photograph of a working commercial fishing pier and waterfront staging area in Portland, Maine, published in a March 23, 2009 blog post titled *"The beginning of spring"*. The scene features stacks of brightly colored wire lobster traps, piles of colorful lobster buoys, plastic storage barrels, an American flag, and weathered industrial waterfront buildings under a clear blue sky.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Fishing Gear**:
  - **Left**: A wooden pier structure with a vertical timber post on the far left. An American flag hangs from a pole next to blue plastic 55-gallon drums, a tan cylindrical bin, wire traps, and dark green cylindrical fenders/rollers lying horizontally on wooden planks.
  - **Center**: Large, dense piles of colorful lobster trap buoys (primarily bright green, orange, white, and yellow-green spindles with small square blue and orange flags attached to their sticks) heaped behind and inside blue plastic barrels and a white rectangular dock box.
  - **Right**: Neat stacks of rectangular yellow wire-mesh lobster traps (with orange rectangular tags/vents visible on the sides) stacked three high, resting next to gray wire traps and dark green/black traps piled in the middle.
- **Background Buildings**:
  - Behind the fishing gear stand several utilitarian waterfront warehouse and processing buildings clad in light tan/beige and white corrugated metal siding, showing streaks of rust along the rooflines and seams.
  - A taller beige multi-story building with a green roof rises behind the central warehouse, against a cloudless, deep blue sky.
