# XML Coast Pilot Language Presentation Slide (`snpwg-schwehr.png`)

## Overview
A presentation slide titled **"XML Coast Pilot Language"** from Kurt Schwehr's presentation (*"GeoCoastPilot: Linking the Coast Pilot with Geo-referenced Imagery & Chart Information"*) given at the IHO Standardization of Nautical Publications Working Group (SNPWG) meeting in Norfolk, Virginia on February 26, 2009 (posted March 7, 2009). The slide illustrates a proposed workflow for collecting, validating, and publishing electronic updates to the *United States Coast Pilot* and 3D geospatial visualization tools.

## Slide Layout & Visual Elements
- **Background & Typography**: Black background with white sans-serif text and yellow directional arrows connecting the workflow stages.
- **Title & Subtitle**:
  - Main heading at top center: `XML Coast Pilot Language`
  - Subtitle below: `Create tools for NOAA and USCG to contribute electronic updates to the Coast Pilot during the course of normal duties`
- **Workflow Stages (Left to Right / Bottom)**:
  1. **Field Data Entry (Left)**:
     - A photograph bordered in white showing a US Coast Guard crew member inside a small boat cabin wearing a dark blue USCG baseball cap, dark blue uniform shirt, and an orange life vest/PFD. He is looking at a small handheld electronic device/PDA resting on a wooden chart table while holding a paper printout from a small thermal printer next to marine radar/navigation displays.
     - Label below photo: `Field Data Entry`
     - A yellow right-pointing arrow leads from this photo to the middle photo.
  2. **Validation (Center)**:
     - A photograph bordered in white showing an operator seated at a vessel traffic or command center console with multiple wall-mounted status monitors on the left, three large wide-screen displays ahead, a desk lamp, telephone, and dual computer monitors on the desk.
     - Label below photo: `Validation`
     - Two yellow arrows originate from the operator's computer screen: one pointing down and slightly left to the 3D visualization screenshot, and another pointing down and right to the *Coast Pilot* book cover.
  3. **3D GeoCoastPilot Visualization (Bottom Center)**:
     - A screenshot bordered in red showing the UNH CCOM/JHC **GeoZui** 3D marine visualization environment (with the circular CCOM/JHC logo in the bottom-left corner).
     - The left side of the window displays a 3D perspective view of a coastal harbor/channel (rendered in blue and gray bathymetry/topography with green land areas) containing embedded vertical 3D billboard photographs of lighthouses and coastal landmarks linked to their geographic positions.
     - The right side of the window shows a scrollable pane of *Coast Pilot* text with highlighted purple/pink hyperlinks corresponding to the geographic features and imagery.
  4. **Publication (Right)**:
     - Label above cover: `Publication`
     - An image bordered in red showing the front cover of **United States Coast Pilot 5: Gulf of Mexico, Puerto Rico, and Virgin Islands (34th Edition, 2006)** published by the U.S. Department of Commerce / National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) / National Ocean Service. The blue-toned cover features a photograph of a white lighthouse with a red roof overlooking a rocky tropical coastline.
