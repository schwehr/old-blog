# Squirrel on a Sapling in the Snow (`squirrel.jpg`)

## Overview
A telephoto outdoor photograph of an eastern gray squirrel clinging to a thin vertical sapling trunk against a snow-covered background outside the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) at the University of New Hampshire, from a March 10, 2009 blog post titled *"Squirrel"*.

## Scene Description
- **Subject**: An eastern gray squirrel (*Sciurus carolinensis*) positioned vertically about halfway up a slender, dark-barked sapling just left of center. The squirrel's body and long, bushy tail are pressed along the left side of the thin trunk, while its head and front paws reach around the trunk, looking in profile toward the right.
- **Foreground & Trees**:
  - On the right side of the frame is a wide, deeply furrowed trunk of a mature hardwood tree (such as an oak).
  - Between the thin sapling and the large tree trunk, a delicate, bare twig rises from the lower right and branches out in the center of the frame.
- **Background**: Sunlit white snow covers the ground behind the trees, crossed by soft, diagonal gray-blue tree shadows and faint tracks/indentations in the snow surface.
