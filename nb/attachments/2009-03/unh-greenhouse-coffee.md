# Coffee Cherries Ripening on a Branch at the UNH Greenhouse (`unh-greenhouse-coffee.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of coffee cherries (*Coffea*) in various stages of ripening along the branches of a coffee plant inside the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Greenhouse during its annual open house, published in a March 29, 2009 blog post titled *"UNH Greenhouse"*.

## Scene Description
- **Coffee Berries (Cherries)**:
  - Tight clusters of smooth, glossy oval coffee berries grow along woody, light-brown twigs.
  - The berries display a full spectrum of ripening colors simultaneously: bright lime green (unripe), yellow-orange and pale red (turning), and deep crimson/burgundy red (ripe) prominently in the lower-right foreground.
- **Foliage & Lighting**:
  - Broad, glossy dark-green coffee leaves with prominent veins surround the branches.
  - Bright, diffused natural sunlight filters down from the greenhouse roof in the upper-left background, creating soft highlights on the waxy skin of the berries and illuminating the translucent green leaves.
