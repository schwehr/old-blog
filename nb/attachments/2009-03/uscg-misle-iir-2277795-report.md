# USCG CGMIX Investigation Activity Report for Activity 2277795 (`uscg-misle-iir-2277795-report.png`)

## Overview
A web browser screenshot displaying the top section of a U.S. Coast Guard CGMIX **Investigation Activity Report** (`http://cgmix.uscg.mil/IIR/IIRSearchResults.aspx?ActID=2277795`), published in a March 11, 2009 blog post titled *"Cosco Busan USCG Marine Causualty Report"*. The report documents the January 14, 2005 allision of the heavy-lift freight ship *M/V Zhen Hua 4* with Pier No. 1 at Norfolk International Terminal after a towline parted on the assisting tug *Cape Henry*.

    > This incident was downgraded to the data collection level of investigation as authorized by Subparagraph (M) of COMDT MSG P 042025Z OCT 01. 14JAN05: The M/V ZHEN HUA 4, a 701-foot heavy lift freight ship, allided with the north side of Pier No. 1 at Norfolk International Terminal as it was docking. The ship was under the conn of a docking master with three tugs assisting. Weather was poor, with winds gusting to 25 knots, setting the ship on the pier. The tugs were deployed along the same side of the ship with a line up on the ship to slow the set toward the pier. A line on the UTV CAPE HENRY parted while the tug was backing down. The line parted because the ship's crew led the line to a bitt forward of the steel fixed containment around the ship's fuel vents. This caused the line to chafe on the containment. The ship's master acknowledged the docking master's request that he send crew to make fast a new line, but did not act to secure the line before the allision. [The master had limited command of English and likely did not fully comprehend the docking master's order.] The vessel sustained an indent in the shell plating. Damage to the pier is estimated at over $25000.
