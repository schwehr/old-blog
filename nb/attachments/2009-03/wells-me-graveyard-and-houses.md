# Ocean View Cemetery and Coastal Houses in Wells, Maine (`wells-me-graveyard-and-houses.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide horizontal landscape photograph of a historic New England cemetery (Ocean View Cemetery in Wells, Maine) in early spring, looking east across dormant golden-brown grass and rows of granite gravestones toward coastal beach houses and the blue band of the Atlantic Ocean on the horizon, published in a March 23, 2009 blog post titled *"The beginning of spring"*.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Cemetery Grounds**:
  - Rows of diverse granite and marble headstones, family monuments, and small plot markers stand across flat, sunlit golden-brown winter turf.
  - Identifiable family names carved on the foreground monuments include `LAWRENCESON` (or similar on the gray stone at bottom left), `NORTON / ...MWOOD` (on the polished reddish-brown granite stone behind it), `COLE` (lower center-right), and `FENDERSON` (on a larger gray stone to the right).
  - Several small American flags on short staffs are planted beside graves, and a couple of dark green trimmed evergreen shrubs grow beside the larger central monuments.
  - A tall, slender white obelisk monument rises in the mid-ground left of center.
- **Trees, Coastline, and Horizon**:
  - A large, bare deciduous tree with an expansive, finely branched crown stands slightly left of center in the middle distance, flanked by smaller bare trees on the left and right.
  - Beyond the cemetery and a narrow tidal marsh/estuary, a dense line of gabled coastal cottages and beach houses (along Wells Beach / Drakes Island) stretches across the horizon, backed by a flat, deep blue strip of the Atlantic Ocean under a clear blue sky.
