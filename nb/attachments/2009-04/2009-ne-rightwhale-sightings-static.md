# 2009 Northeast Right Whale Sightings in Google Earth (`2009-ne-rightwhale-sightings-static.png`)

## Overview
A tilted 3D Google Earth screenshot showing North Atlantic right whale (*Eubalaena glacialis*) sightings from early 2009 in Cape Cod Bay and adjacent Massachusetts waters, developed in collaboration with Tim Cole at NOAA's Northeast Fisheries Science Center (NEFSC).

## Map & Placemark Details
- **Geographic View**: An oblique perspective looking roughly northeast across Cape Cod Bay, with the curved hook of Provincetown and Race Point on the left, the outer Cape Cod peninsula curving across the upper right, and Plymouth/Barnstable along the lower left shoreline.
- **Whale Sighting Icons**: Custom black whale-fluke icons diving into white water splashes mark individual right whale sighting locations. Most icons in Cape Cod Bay are labeled in orange-red text with their sighting date:
  - **February sightings**: `Feb 11`, `Feb 22`, `Feb 25` (two icons).
  - **March sightings**: `Mar 10` (two icons), `Mar 15` (five icons), `Mar 17` (eight icons), `Mar 20`, `Mar 26`, `Mar 29`, `Mar 30`.
  - **April sightings**: `Apr 01` (two icons).
  - Additional unlabeled smaller whale-fluke icons appear further offshore in the upper left (Massachusetts Bay / Stellwagen Bank) and along the northern edge of Cape Cod Bay.

## KML Info Balloon & Interface Controls
- **Selected Placemark Balloon**: Pointing to the `Feb 25` whale icon in the left-center of Cape Cod Bay, a white Google Earth pop-up balloon titled **Right Whale Sighting** contains a bordered table with an orange-gold header row:
  - **Field** | **Value**
  - `RID` | `46`
  - `Date` | `2009-Feb-25`
  - `Group Size` | `2`
  - `Region` | `Northeast`
  - `Species` | `Definite`
- **Banner Graphic**: Below the table inside the balloon is a small banner graphic showing a surfacing whale, the NOAA logo on the right, and white text reading: `Northeast US Right Whale Sighting Advisory System (SAS)`.
- **Navigation Controls**: In the upper right corner are the standard Google Earth 3D navigation controls: a rotated compass/look ring (`N` oriented toward the lower left, indicating a northeast-facing camera heading), a pan ring with a hand icon, and a vertical zoom slider (`+` / `-`).
