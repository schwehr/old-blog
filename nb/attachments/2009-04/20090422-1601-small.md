# USCGC Healy Aloftcon Sunrise Over Arctic Sea Ice (`20090422-1601-small.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle webcam photograph taken from the Aloftcon station aboard the icebreaker USCGC *Healy* (WAGB-20) on April 22, 2009, showing a bright orange sunrise over broken pack ice in the Bering Sea.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Sunrise**: The sun sits right on the horizon to the right of center, surrounded by a brilliant white-yellow glare that transitions into warm golden-orange along the horizon and pale blue-green at the top of the sky. Small water droplets and ice/salt specks on the camera dome catch the sunlight across the upper half of the frame.
- **Sea Ice**: From the foreground out to the horizon, the ocean is covered in a mosaic of flat, snow-covered white and gray-blue pancake and pack ice floes separated by narrow leads of dark water.
- **Ship's Bow**: At the bottom center, *Healy*'s rounded bow and foredeck are visible from above, with an orange flag flying from the forward jackstaff mast. White safety railings and twin A-frame meteorological/antenna towers flank the lower left and lower right foreground.
