# AIS Binary Message Area Notice Diagram: Storm Front (`ais-bin-msg-area-stormfront.png`)

## Overview
A clean line diagram illustrating how an AIS Area Notice binary message represents a storm front (`Area Type: 23`) outside a harbor using a five-vertex polyline.

## Diagram Details
- **Harbor Shoreline**: On the left side, a smooth, curving black line outlines a coastal indentation labeled `Harbor` in black sans-serif text.
- **Storm Front Polyline (`Area Type: 23`)**:
  - To the right (seaward) of the harbor entrance, a solid black polyline connects five dark-gray circular vertices with black outlines labeled `0` (top), `1`, `2`, `3` (bottom), and `4` (far right), forming a curved arc facing the harbor.
  - Along the upper/right (windward) side of the polyline between vertices `0` and `4`, a continuous hand-drawn black scalloped/looped cloud line indicates the advancing storm front.
  - Above and to the right of the scalloped line is the two-line black text label:
    - `Storm Front`
    - `Area Type: 23`
