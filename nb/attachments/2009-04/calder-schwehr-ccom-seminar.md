# CCOM/JHC Seminar Announcement Flyer: Brian Calder and Kurt Schwehr (`calder-schwehr-ccom-seminar.png`)

## Overview
A seminar announcement flyer for the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC) Seminar Series featuring a joint presentation by Dr. Brian Calder and Dr. Kurt Schwehr on April 24, 2009.

Centered black serif text in the upper half of the flyer reads:
- **Header**:
  - `Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping/Joint`
  - `Hydrographic Center Seminar Series`
- **Date, Location & Time**:
  - `Friday, April 24th, 2009`
  - `Chase Ocean Engineering Lab, Room 130`
  - `3:00 PM`
- **Speakers**:
  - `Presents`
  - `Brian Calder, Ph.D. and Kurt Schwehr, Ph.D.`
  - `Research Associate Professors, CCOM`
- **Talk Title**:
  - `“Traffic Analysis for the Calibration of Risk Assessment Methods”`

## Embedded Figures & Captions
The lower half of the flyer displays two side-by-side illustrative figures:
- **Left (`Figure 1`)**: Two side-by-side color-mapped vertical plots with colorbars:
  - Left subplot: `Large Ship Profile` / `Bathymetry (m), with Log Risk Overlay` (ranging from `-5` in dark red to `-20` in dark blue), showing a curved white ship trajectory ascending from bottom (`0`) to top (`2500`) with five semi-transparent fan-shaped potential motion zones and yellow risk overlays.
  - Right subplot: `Large Ship Profile` / `Log Probability of Grounding, with Log Risk Overlay` (colorbar from `0` to `-12`), showing the corresponding grounding probability field with the same fan-shaped motion zones.
  - **Caption**: `Figure 1: Example of risk assessment for a large (>100m) ship following a trajectory (in white) through shallow bathymetry (left) and associated local risk (right). The overlaid semi-transparent regions are potential motion zones within the next two minutes from each center location; the overlaid yellow figures are additional risk that would be incurred by heading in the indicated directions.`
- **Right (`Figure 2`)**: A 3D perspective visualization over Massachusetts Bay and Cape Cod labeled `Tanker` in the upper right, showing 3D extruded red, orange, yellow, and green histogram bars representing gridded vessel traffic density along the Boston Harbor approaches and Traffic Separation Scheme (outlined in white).
  - **Caption**: `Figure 2: Gridded ship traffic density in Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary`
