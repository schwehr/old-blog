# UNH/NOAA Joint Hydrographic Center 2008 Annual Report Cover (`ccom-annual-report.png`)

## Overview
The front cover graphic of the 2008 UNH/NOAA Joint Hydrographic Center (JHC) / Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) Performance and Progress Report.

## Layout & Visual Elements
- **Background**: A deep navy-blue shaded-relief bathymetric map covers the background, bordered near the top by a horizontal light-green accent stripe.
- **Horizontal Photo Strip**: Five square inset photographs bordered in light green run horizontally across the upper-middle section of the cover (from left to right):
  1. A close-up of a bright orange shipboard life ring with black stencil lettering reading `HEALY` (from the icebreaker USCGC *Healy*).
  2. A view from inside a vessel's bridge looking past a helm compass and barometer through forward windows toward a bow mast and open ocean.
  3. An aerial photograph of UNH's Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory surrounded by autumn foliage in Durham, New Hampshire.
  4. Three researchers inside a shipboard lab or control room examining multi-monitor sonar/mapping displays.
  5. An overhead/oblique visualization or aerial view of a shallow submerged reef/coastal marsh structure in green tones.
- **Logo & Title**:
  - Below the photo strip on the left is the circular emblem of the `Center for Coastal & Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center / University of New Hampshire` (`CCOM / JHC`), featuring a 3D multibeam sonar swath over a seafloor surface.
  - To the right of the emblem, clean white sans-serif text reads:
    - `UNH/NOAA Joint Hydrographic Center`
    - `Performance and Progress Report`
