# Sailing Vessel Mother of Perl Moored with R/V Cocheco in Background (`cocheco-ma-perl.jpg`)

## Overview
A sunny harbor photograph showing the two-masted sailing vessel *S/V Mother of Perl* moored in the foreground on the Piscataqua River near New Castle, New Hampshire, with the UNH vessel *R/V Cocheco* and Fort Constitution visible in the background.

- **Foreground Vessel (*S/V Mother of Perl*)**:
  - A dark blue-hulled, two-masted ketch/schooner with a white deckhouse and white trim sits moored to a small round white buoy in the center foreground, facing right.
  - Its tall mainmast (forward) and shorter mizzenmast (aft) rise high against the blue sky with sails furled under white covers along the booms. Orange life rings are mounted near the stern cockpit.
- **Background**:
  - On the far left edge, the blue-and-white *R/V Cocheco* is visible in the distance on the sparkling water.
  - Behind *Mother of Perl* on the left sits the dark granite wall of **Fort Constitution**, with the top of **Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse** peeking above the left end of the fort wall.
  - To the right of the fort are waterfront buildings, piers, and a yellow boat hoist/crane at the UNH Pier / USCG Station Portsmouth Harbor facility in New Castle.
