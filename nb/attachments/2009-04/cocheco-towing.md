# R/V Cocheco Towing R/V Coastal Surveyor Near Whaleback Light (`cocheco-towing.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime marine photograph taken from the bow of the *R/V Coastal Surveyor* during spring towing drills near Portsmouth Harbor, New Hampshire, showing the UNH research vessel *R/V Cocheco* ahead under tow line with Whaleback Lighthouse and Fort Constitution visible in the background.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Vessel (*R/V Cocheco*):**
  - Viewed from astern (port-quarter angle), the blue-and-white motor vessel *R/V Cocheco* moves forward through dark blue, choppy harbor water, kicking up a white frothy wake behind its transom.
  - Bold white block letters across the dark blue stern transom read `"COCHECO"`.
  - A taut white tow line extends from the stern of the *Cocheco* diagonally down and to the bottom-right corner of the frame toward the camera vessel (*R/V Coastal Surveyor*).
  - The vessel features an enclosed white cabin with a blue circular emblem on the port side, a heavy black A-frame mounted over the aft deck, white cylindrical fenders/buoys stowed on the port aft deck, an orange life ring on the starboard aft frame, a white helm chair with two crew members in dark clothing and red PFDs visible inside the cabin, and a white radar dome, life raft canister, and tall whip antennas mounted on the cabin roof.
- **Background (Landmarks & Sky):**
  - On the right half of the horizon, a low rocky island/ledge supports a historic stone fort wall (Fort Constitution / Whaleback area), a white conical lighthouse tower with a black lantern room (Whaleback Light), and a white-walled house with a pitched dark red roof.
  - On the far left horizon, a small distant navigation buoy or daymark stands in the open water.
