# R/V Cocheco Passing Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse (`cocheco.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime maritime photograph showing the University of New Hampshire research vessel *R/V Cocheco* underway on the Piscataqua River in front of Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse and Fort Constitution in New Castle, New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Vessel (*R/V Cocheco*)**:
  - Positioned in the left-center foreground, the small survey/training launch *R/V Cocheco* moves toward the left (bow angled toward the viewer's lower-left) across dark blue, lightly rippled water, creating a small white bow wave.
  - The boat has a royal-blue hull, a white cabin superstructure with three forward windshields, an orange life ring resting on the cabin hood in front of the center window, white radio antennas, a radar dome on the cabin roof, and a black A-frame at the stern.
- **Background Landmarks (New Castle, NH)**:
  - Directly behind the vessel stands the tall, cylindrical white tower of **Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse** with its black lantern room and gallery deck, set on a rocky dark ledge connected by a wooden walkway.
  - To the right of the lighthouse is the historic granite fortification wall of **Fort Constitution** (with five vertical window/embrasure openings showing rust-colored staining) and a white two-story building with a red roof rising behind the fort wall.
  - A line of green pine trees and leafless early-spring deciduous trees stretches along the shoreline under a clear pale-blue sky.
