# CCOM Seamanship Fire Extinguisher Training (`extinguish.png`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken on April 28, 2009, outside the Jere A. Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory (CCOM/JHC) at the University of New Hampshire, showing graduate student Monica Wolfson practicing extinguishing a controlled pan fire with a portable fire extinguisher under the supervision of two instructors.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Action:**
  - On the right side of the frame, Monica stands in a wide stance on a grassy lawn, seen from a rear three-quarter angle, wearing a sleeveless blue-and-brown paisley/patterned top and light blue jeans.
  - She holds a red-and-yellow portable fire extinguisher in both hands, discharging a dense, forceful cone of white dry-chemical or CO2 extinguishing agent leftward into a rectangular metal burn pan on the grass.
  - Bright orange flames leap upward from the left side of the metal pan while thick white smoke and extinguishing cloud billow out from the pan. A black supply hose runs along the grass from the bottom-left corner to a fitting on the front of the metal burn pan.
- **Instructors & Equipment:**
  - Standing behind the burn pan in the center background is an instructor wearing a dark baseball cap, white polo shirt, and black pants, holding a long-handled ignition torch/wand connected to a white propane tank on the grass.
  - Standing on the far right is a second instructor wearing a dark baseball cap, black polo shirt, and khaki cargo pants, watching Monica's technique.
  - Clustered on the grass around the instructors are roughly a dozen red fire extinguishers, two tall silver pressurized water extinguishers, and a white propane cylinder.
- **Background (Campus & Sky):**
  - In the background on the left stands the brick and concrete facade of the UNH Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory building with a tall recessed loading bay, where a silver pickup truck is parked next to orange traffic cones.
  - Young deciduous trees just beginning to leaf out in spring green and small evergreen pines frame the lawn under a clear blue sky.
