# Cropped Grayscale Telemetry Banner from USCGC Healy Aloftcon Camera (`gray-crop.jpg`)

## Overview
A narrow, horizontal grayscale image crop extracted from the top status banner of a USCGC *Healy* Aloftcon webcam photograph (`20090201-1601.jpeg`), used to test Optical Character Recognition (OCR) software (`gocr`) for automatically parsing ship position coordinates.

## Visual Details & Visible Text
- **Appearance:**
  - The strip is a low-resolution grayscale raster banner displaying white sans-serif text with a dark drop-shadow/outline over a noisy, mottled gray background with noticeable JPEG compression artifacts.
  - The left half of the banner has a slightly darker gray background box behind the camera name and timestamp, while the right half has a lighter gray background behind the navigation and meteorological sensor readings.
- **Visible Text (Left to Right):**
  - `"Healy Aloft Conn Sun Feb 01 16:00:32 GMT 2009"`
  - `"e:25000"` (rendered in a slightly smaller/narrower font)
  - `"Lat: 48 13.0 N"`
  - `"Long: 123 01.2 W"`
  - `"Air Temp: 43.1 F"`
  - `"Wind(Stdb): 11.7 kt"`
  - `"Rel WindDir: 349"`
