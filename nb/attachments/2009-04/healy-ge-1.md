# USCGC Healy Track and Aloftcon Camera in Google Earth (`healy-ge-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth displaying a KML visualization of the icebreaker USCGC *Healy* (WAGB-20) and its hourly Aloftcon camera images in the Bering Sea / Chukotka region on April 18, 2009. The screenshot shows the Places sidebar on the left, an open placemark info balloon in the center with an embedded sea-ice webcam photograph, and custom ship icons clustered in the water on the right.
