# Photoshop Channels Panel Showing Layer 2 Mask Visibility Toggle (`layer-mask-2.png`)

## Overview
An annotated screenshot of the Adobe Photoshop `Channels` palette on Mac OS X, showing the color channels (`RGB`, `Red`, `Green`, `Blue`) and the `Layer 2 Mask` alpha channel for the right whale tail icon.

## Interface & Channel Details
- **Tabs (Top):** `Layers`, `Channels` (active tab with `x` button), and `Paths`.
- **Channel Rows:**
  1. `RGB` (`⌘~` shortcut): Highlighted in light blue, eye visibility icon active, thumbnail showing a black whale tail on a transparent checkerboard background.
  2. `Red` (`⌘1` shortcut): Highlighted in light blue, eye visibility icon active, thumbnail showing the black whale tail on checkerboard.
  3. `Green` (`⌘2` shortcut): Highlighted in light blue, eye visibility icon active, thumbnail showing the black whale tail on checkerboard.
  4. `Blue` (`⌘3` shortcut): Highlighted in light blue, eye visibility icon active, thumbnail showing the black whale tail on checkerboard.
  5. `Layer 2 Mask` (`⌘\` shortcut, italicized label): Unselected (grey background). On the far left, the empty visibility toggle box is **circled in bright red** to show where the user must click to make the layer mask visible and editable so an image can be pasted directly into the mask. Its thumbnail displays a white whale tail silhouette and water highlights on a black background.
- **Bottom Action Bar:** Four channel buttons at the bottom: Load channel as selection (dotted circle), Save selection as channel, Create new channel, and Delete current channel (trash can).
