# Overhead Photograph of Eight AIS Transceivers, Receivers, and Displays (`many-ais-devices.jpg`)

## Overview
A top-down studio/bench photograph displaying a collection of eight Automatic Identification System (AIS) hardware units—including Class A and Class B transponders/transceivers, dual-channel AIS receivers, and dedicated display/keypad units—arranged neatly on a light gray tabletop.

## Hardware Units by Position
- **Top Left — Raytheon Marine Class A AIS Display & Keyboard (`RAY 101E AIS`):**
  - Large black rectangular control and display unit with `Raytheon Marine` printed at the top left and `RAY 101E AIS` at the top right.
  - Features a greenish-gray rectangular LCD screen in the upper left flanked by four soft keys (`F1`–`F4`), five circular keys below the screen, a 4-way directional arrow keypad with two red/orange buttons above it on the upper right, and a full white-key QWERTY alphanumeric keypad across the bottom half.
- **Top Center & Middle Center — Two West Marine AIS-1000 / ProAIS Class B Transponders:**
  - Two identical off-white/light-gray ribbed die-cast enclosures with four-hole mounting flanges on two sides (top unit oriented vertically, middle unit oriented horizontally).
- **Middle Left — ACR Cobham Nauticast B Class B AIS Transponder:**
  - Translucent blue-gray ruggedized plastic casing with molded mounting tabs at the top and bottom and two coaxial/circular connectors protruding from the right side.
  - Through the semi-transparent cover, the internal green PCB and top label are visible, featuring the `ACR` logo, `Class B AIS Transponder`, and `NAUTICAST B` in red and blue lettering.
- **Bottom Left — Two SR162G and SR162 AIS Receivers:**
  - Two compact black anodized aluminum boxes placed side by side with mounting flanges and top-mounted BNC/SMA antenna and power/data connectors.
  - The left unit is labeled `SR162G` / `AIS Receiver` in white text; the right unit is labeled `SR162` / `AIS Receiver`.
- **Bottom Center — Comar CSB200 Class B AIS Transponder:**
  - Black ribbed extruded aluminum chassis held in a black metal mounting bracket with thumb screws at the top and bottom, two BNC/SO-239 RF connectors on the left side, status LEDs, and a white specification/compliance label featuring `CE 0168` in the center.

  - **Upper Right (Transponder Unit):** Tall dark-gray/black finned metal housing with heavy mounting brackets on the left, multiple top connectors plus a dark rectangular GPS/handle protrusion on the top right, and two white identification labels on the lower front reading `C.N.S. Systems` and `VDL6000` with `CE` marking.
  - **Bottom Right (Display/Keypad Unit):** Compact black rectangular display head mounted on side trunnion knobs, featuring a horizontal green LCD screen at the top (`C.N.S. Systems` printed below the bezel) and a 4x4 white membrane keypad (alphanumeric keys `1`–`9`, `0`, `MENU`, `ESC`, `ENT`, `DIM`, `ALT`, `CLR/ALARM`).
