# Right Whale Tail Fluke Google Earth Icon (`rw-icon-64x64-v4.png`)

## Overview
A 64×64 pixel custom transparent PNG icon created in Adobe Photoshop using a grayscale layer mask from a photograph of a North Atlantic right whale's tail fluke, designed for plotting whale sighting placemarks in Google Earth.

## Visual Details
- **Whale Fluke**: A high-contrast, solid black silhouette of a right whale's broad, V-notched tail fluke rising out of the water at an angle—with the left lobe extending horizontally to the left and the right lobe angled sharply upward toward the top-right corner. Individual identifying markings have been edited out to create a clean, uniform silhouette.
- **Water Surface & Splash**: At the base of the tail stock, soft grayscale ripples and dark horizontal water surface streaks fade smoothly into transparency, giving the icon a natural blended appearance when overlaid on blue ocean imagery in Google Earth.
