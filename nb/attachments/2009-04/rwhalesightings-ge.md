# Northeast Right Whale Sightings in Google Earth (`rwhalesightings-ge.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of an early draft Google Earth visualization of the NOAA/WHOI Northeast U.S. Right Whale Sighting Advisory System (SAS) database, looking northwest across Cape Cod Bay and Provincetown Hook, Massachusetts.

## Map & Scene Description
- **Geographic View**: An oblique 3D perspective looking northwest over the curved sandy spit of Provincetown / Race Point (lower left) and Cape Cod Bay (center), with the mainland Massachusetts coastline and Cape Cod Canal visible in the background under a light blue sky horizon.
- **Placemarks**: Seven square white-background whale-fluke icons are distributed across Cape Cod Bay, each labeled in orange-red text with its sighting date:
  - `2009-Apr-01` (three icons: two clustered near Race Point on the left, one further north)
  - `2009-Mar-26` (two icons: one in west-central Cape Cod Bay, and a larger selected icon on the right)
  - `2009-Mar-30` (north-central bay)
  - `2009-Mar-29` (east-central bay)
- **Google Earth UI**:
  - Top-left time slider indicator shows `Apr 1, 2009 12 am`.
  - Top-right navigation controls (look/compass ring with `N` indicator and pan ring).
  - Bottom status bar reads: `42°00'49.58" N  70°22'17.47" W  elev -49 m` and `Eye alt 17.65 km`.
  - Center-bottom credits: `Image © 2009 DigitalGlobe`, `Data U.S. Navy`, `© 2009 Tele Atlas`, `© 2009 Europa Technologies`, and the `Google` logo in the lower right.

- **Header**: `2009-Mar-26` and centered table title `Right Whale Sighting`
- **Data Table** (with an orange header row `Field` | `Value`):
  - **OOD**: `P.Duley`
  - **RID**: `99`
  - **Date**: `2009-03-26 00:00:00`
  - **Group Size**: `2`
  - **Region**: `Northeast`

  - **Category**: `Aerial`
  - **Action**: `None - within existing protection zone`
- **Comments Section**:
  - **Comments:** `PCCS` (Provincetown Center for Coastal Studies)
- **Footer Banner**: A horizontal banner graphic reading `Northeast US Right Whale Sighting Advisory System (SAS)` with the NOAA emblem and an aerial photo of a right whale.
