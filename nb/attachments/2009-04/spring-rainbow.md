# Spring Rainbow Over Rural New Hampshire Field (`spring-rainbow.jpg`)

## Overview
A landscape photograph taken with a camera phone during an afternoon commute on April 23, 2009, capturing a vivid rainbow segment beneath a dramatic cumulus storm cloud over a rural green pasture and wooded treeline.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Rainbow**:
  - A large, billowy white-and-gray cumulus rain cloud dominates the upper center and right of the frame, with a bright sunlit white core and a dark gray base. Deep blue sky with wispy clouds is visible to the left.
  - Extending diagonally down and to the right from the left edge of the cloud base to the distant treeline is a bright, multi-colored primary rainbow segment displaying distinct bands of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet.
- **Landscape & Foreground**:
  - The lower third of the image shows an open, grassy spring field in shades of bright yellowish-green and tan, crossed by low stone walls or brush lines.
  - Near the bottom center sits a small weathered wooden shed or barn with a pitched roof; another house roof and a distant white tower are visible further right along the edge of the woods.
  - A continuous horizon of brown, mostly leafless early-spring deciduous trees and scattered dark evergreens spans the middle distance, with bare tree branches framing the right edge of the photo.
