# U.S. Coast Guard Environmental Stewardship Layer in Google Earth (`uscg-google-earth.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of Google Earth 5.0 displaying the **U.S. Coast Guard** Google Earth Outreach layer (released for Earth Day, April 22, 2009), showing the Bering Sea and Alaska region with the USCGC *Healy* Aloftcon webcam track and an open information balloon for a grounded fishing vessel response at St. George Island, Alaska.
