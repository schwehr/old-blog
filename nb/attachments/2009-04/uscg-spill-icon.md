# U.S. Coast Guard Oil Spill Google Earth Icon (`uscg-spill-icon.png`)

## Overview
A stylized graphic icon used in the U.S. Coast Guard's Google Earth Outreach layer to represent marine pollution and oil spill response incidents.

## Visual Details
- **Sinking Vessel**: In the upper half of the icon, the bow section of a red-and-black ship is angled sharply upward to the right as its stern sinks beneath the waterline on the left. The ship features a black lower hull, a bright red upper hull and foredeck patterned with black mechanical/chain details, and a small mast at the bow peak.
- **Horizon**: A dark black silhouette of a small three-peaked island or landmass sits on the horizon line to the left of the sinking ship.
- **Oil Slick**: Below the horizontal water surface ripples, a wavy black oil slick spreads downward and toward the lower right corner in meandering pools, accented with several bright red oval droplets and a red squiggly streak near the bottom.
