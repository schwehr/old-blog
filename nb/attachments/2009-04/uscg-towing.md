# U.S. Coast Guard Boats Practicing Towing Near Newcastle, NH (`uscg-towing.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect maritime photograph showing two U.S. Coast Guard boats from Station Portsmouth Harbor (New Castle, New Hampshire) conducting an alongside (on-the-hip) towing drill on sparkling blue water, with historic Wood Island Life Saving Station and Whaleback Lighthouse visible on the horizon.

- **Coast Guard Vessels (Center Foreground)**:
  - A larger aluminum-hulled U.S. Coast Guard utility/motor lifeboat (with an enclosed pilothouse, radar mast, orange-and-blue Coast Guard racing stripe on the bow, and crew members in orange gear on deck) is underway heading right (seaward).
  - Secured tightly alongside its starboard hip is a smaller Coast Guard rigid-hull inflatable boat (RIB) with a bright orange inflatable collar and a white enclosed cabin.
- **Background Landmarks**:
  - **Left**: On a low, rocky island sits the historic **Wood Island Life Saving Station**, a classic weathered gabled building with a lookout tower on its roof.

- **Water & Lighting**: Bright midday sunlight creates dense specular glints across the rippled blue surface of the Piscataqua River / Portsmouth Harbor approach, with a white wake trailing behind the two vessels to the left.
