# West Marine AIS-1000 Class B Rear Label and Connectors (`west-marine-ais-1000-class-b-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of the rear panel of a **West Marine AIS-1000** Class B AIS transceiver, showing its recessed cable/antenna connector ports and regulatory identification label.
