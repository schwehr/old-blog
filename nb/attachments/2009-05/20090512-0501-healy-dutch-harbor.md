# USCGC Healy Aloftcon View of Dutch Harbor, Alaska (`20090512-0501-healy-dutch-harbor.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle photograph captured by the Aloftcon mast camera aboard the icebreaker **USCGC Healy (WAGB-20)** while moored at a container pier in **Dutch Harbor, Alaska**, on May 12, 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Ship's Bow)**: Looking straight down over the elevated white safety railing of the Aloftcon platform onto the red-and-black foredeck and bow of the *Healy*, moored starboard-side to the concrete pier on the right. The jackstaff rises at the tip of the bow.
- **Pier & Port Facilities (Right)**: A long cargo and container terminal pier extends away from the ship along the base of a steep, dark hillside. Two tall gantry container cranes stand in silhouette along the wharf near white container stacks and a long, single-story gray warehouse building with white doors along its side.
- **Harbor & Background (Left & Center)**: Calm, dark, rippled harbor waters stretch across the left and center of the frame toward a distant shoreline with buildings and snow-dusted mountains under a bright early-morning or late-evening sky glowing pale yellow-white above the hill on the right.
