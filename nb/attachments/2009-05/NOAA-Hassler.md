# NOAA SWATH Vessel Ferdinand R. Hassler Under Construction (`NOAA-Hassler.jpg`)

## Overview
A side-by-side composite of two color photographs showing the **NOAA Ship Ferdinand R. Hassler**, a Small Waterplane Area Twin Hull (SWATH) coastal mapping vessel, under construction in a shipyard prior to launch.

## Scene Description
- **Left Photograph (Bow / Starboard Quarter View)**:
  - Shows the forward and starboard side of the vessel resting on heavy keel blocks and cribbing in a dirt and gravel shipyard.
  - The submerged cylindrical lower pontoon hull (torpedo-like SWATH float) is unpainted rusted steel with white primer patches near the bow and a reddish-brown anti-fouling/primer band just above the strut transition.
  - The upper hull and bridge superstructure are painted in teal-green primer, with rectangular bridge windows and an enclosed wing cab visible at the upper right.
  - Two tall lattice-boom shipyard cranes rise behind the superstructure against a blue sky with light clouds.
- **Right Photograph (Stern / Starboard Three-Quarter View)**:
  - Shows the vessel from aft on the starboard side, clearly revealing the twin-hull SWATH cross-structure (box bridge) and the wide tunnel opening between the two submerged cylindrical lower hulls.
  - Scaffolding frames stand beneath the stern arch and along the port and starboard struts.
  - The upper deck and superstructure are painted teal-green, with red-primed structural bulkheads, white ventilation ducts/piping, and several shipyard workers standing on the upper bridge wing at the right.
  - Green trees line the background behind the shipyard.
