# Spring Meadow at Adams Point, New Hampshire (`adams-point.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle landscape photograph of a lush green meadow and mowed grass trail at **Adams Point** on Great Bay / Little Bay in Durham, New Hampshire, taken in early May 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Center**: A wide, mowed path with two faint wheel tracks curves gently away from the bottom center into a bright green grassy field, passing a low rounded thicket of shrubs on the left. Dried brown wildflower stalks stand in the taller grass on the right.
- **Midground (Treeline)**: A mixed New England forest borders the far edge of the meadow, featuring freshly leafed-out deciduous trees, dark evergreen pines, and several flowering trees with white and pale pink spring blossoms.
- **Sky**: A dramatic, partly cloudy spring sky fills the upper half of the frame with billowing white and gray cumulus clouds against patches of blue sky.
