# Tide Station Site Discussions off Appledore Island (`appledore-discussions.jpg`)

## Overview
A color photograph of three researchers in orange personal flotation devices (PFDs) aboard a small outboard-powered skiff off the rocky shoreline of **Appledore Island** (Isles of Shoals), discussing potential locations for a tide station on May 22, 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Left Foreground (Andy M.)**: A man with a goatee wearing a dark navy baseball cap, a maroon long-sleeved sweatshirt (`US...` yellow lettering partially visible near the hip), blue jeans, and a bright orange life vest stands in the boat with his right hand in his pocket and his left arm extended horizontally, pointing toward the shore.
- **Center Midground**: A second crew member wearing a gray cap, brown long-sleeved shirt, and an orange life vest leans forward with his right hand resting on the silver **Honda** outboard motor cowling and his left hand gripping the black tiller handle.
- **Right Foreground**: An older man with gray hair, sunglasses, a gray baseball cap, a black fleece jacket, and an orange-and-black life vest with reflective tape looks up toward the speaker on the left.
- **Background**: Dark blue rippled water and a low, bare granite ledge extending from Appledore Island under a clear blue sky.
