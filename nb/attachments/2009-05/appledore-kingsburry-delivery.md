# R/V John M. Kingsbury Delivering Supplies at Appledore Island (`appledore-kingsburry-delivery.jpg`)

## Overview
A color photograph of the Shoals Marine Laboratory vessel **R/V John M. Kingsbury** moored alongside the rocky granite shore/pier at **Appledore Island** (Isles of Shoals) with an inflatable rigid-hulled tender tied alongside, delivering propane and supplies ahead of the summer student session on May 22, 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Primary Vessel (`R/V John M. Kingsbury`)**:
  - Viewed from the port quarter/beam in calm dark blue water under a cloudless blue sky.
  - Features a dark green lower hull with red boot-topping, a cream/buff upper hull and bulwarks (`JOHN M. KINGSBURY` lettered on the port bow), and a white wheelhouse with twin tall white exhaust stacks aft of the cabin.
  - The side of the wheelhouse displays the **`SHOALS`** logo, and an orange life ring hangs aft of the cabin door.
  - The radar mast atop the wheelhouse carries two white radomes, GPS/VHF antennas, and navigation lights.
  - On the aft working deck, a gray-and-orange hydraulic knuckle-boom crane is mounted near the center, an A-frame leans over the stern, and a crew member in a dark shirt, khaki pants, and hat stands on the starboard aft deck waving.
- **Tender & Background Vessels**:
  - A gray inflatable boat (RIB) with an outboard motor and registration `MS 2816 A` on its bow is tied alongside the *Kingsbury*'s port quarter.
  - In the far distance on the left horizon, the small research vessel **R/V Cocheco** is visible on the open water.
  - Pale gray granite ledges of Appledore Island rise behind the vessel on the right.
