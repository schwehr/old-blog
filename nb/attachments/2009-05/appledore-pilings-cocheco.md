# Old Pier Pilings at Appledore Island with R/V Cocheco in Background (`appledore-pilings-cocheco.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format color photograph looking seaward from the rocky shore of **Appledore Island** (Isles of Shoals) past a row of weathered wooden pier pilings toward the University of New Hampshire research vessel **R/V Cocheco** offshore, taken during a tide gauge site survey on May 22, 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Right (Pilings & Rock Breakwater)**:
  - Seven upright and slightly leaning weathered gray wooden pilings (plus one shorter broken stump) stand in a row in the dark blue water, extending left from a pile of large, angular granite boulders on the right.
  - Two small bright yellow lobster/mooring buoys float in the water—one at the far lower left and another between the first two pilings on the left.
- **Background (Open Ocean & Vessel)**:
  - Beyond the pilings on the upper left, the blue-and-white research vessel **R/V Cocheco** is seen bow-on/port-quarter in the open water.
  - A flat, distant mainland horizon line stretches across the frame beneath a clear blue sky.
