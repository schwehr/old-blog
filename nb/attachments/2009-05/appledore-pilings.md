# Old Pier Pilings and Wind Turbine on Appledore Island (`appledore-pilings.jpg`)

## Overview
A color photograph taken from the water looking toward a cluster of weathered wooden pier pilings and the rocky shoreline of **Appledore Island** (Isles of Shoals), with the island's AIRMAP wind turbine rising on the hill in the upper right, photographed during a tide station site survey on May 22, 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Water & Pilings)**:
  - Dark blue, gently rippled harbor water fills the lower half of the image.
  - Eight weathered, bleached-gray wooden pilings—remnants of a former pier—protrude vertically and at slight angles from the water. Their bases are darkened with marine growth and barnacles at the waterline, and the closest central piling has a rusted wire or chain wrapped around its upper half.
- **Midground & Background (Appledore Island)**:
  - Behind the pilings is a rugged shoreline of large, angular granite boulders and pale bedrock slabs dotted with white nesting seagulls.
  - Above the rocks, low green coastal scrub vegetation covers the slope.
  - On the crest of the island at the upper right stands a tall, slender tower supporting a three-bladed wind turbine against a clear blue sky.
