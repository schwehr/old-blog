# Nesting Great Black-Backed Gull on Appledore Island (`appledore-seagull-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A color photograph showing a nesting gull (a Great Black-backed Gull, *Larus marinus*) standing beside its nest and egg among coastal grass and lichen-covered rocks on Appledore Island in the Isles of Shoals, taken on May 22, 2009 during a site reconnaissance trip for a tide station.

## Scene Description
- **Subject**: An adult gull positioned slightly left of center, facing toward the right in profile. It has a bright white head, neck, and breast, dark slate-gray to black wings and mantle with white-spotted black primary wingtips at the lower left, a yellow hooked bill with a prominent red spot (gonys spot) near the lower tip, and a dark eye with a reddish orbital ring.
- **Nest and Egg**: Directly beneath the bird's breast and legs sits a shallow nest constructed of dried brown grass and plant stems. A single large, speckled olive-brown/grayish egg is visible inside the left side of the nest cup.
- **Surroundings**:
  - Lush, bright green coastal grass and sedges with small brownish seed heads surround the nest in the foreground and middle ground.
  - To the left behind the gull is an angular gray granite rock covered in patches of bright orange-gold crustose lichen.
  - Additional fractured gray granite boulders and cobbles fill the upper background, interspersed with bare woody shrub branches extending across the upper center and lower right foreground.
