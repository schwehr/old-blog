# Herring Gull Sitting on a Nest in Coastal Rocks on Appledore Island (`appledore-seagull-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A color photograph of an adult Herring Gull (*Larus argentatus*) sitting on a large grass nest wedged into a rocky granite crevice on Appledore Island in the Isles of Shoals, taken on May 22, 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Subject**: An adult gull settled low in the center of the frame, facing left. It features a clean white head, neck, breast, and tail; a pale pearl-gray mantle and folded wings; black primary wingtips with small white "mirrors" extending to the right; a pale yellow iris; and a yellow bill with a reddish-orange spot near the lower tip.
- **Nest**: The gull is resting in the center of a thick, circular mat of dried brown grasses, twigs, and seaweed that fills the depression between the surrounding boulders.
- **Rocky Setting**:
  - Large, angular, weathered granite blocks enclose the nest on all sides.
  - The rocks exhibit tan, rust-orange, and light gray surfaces with sharp fractures and dark shadows along the crevices.
  - Bright, direct sunlight illuminates the gull's back and the surrounding rock faces from the upper left.
