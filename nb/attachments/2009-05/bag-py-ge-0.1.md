# Google Earth Visualization of BAG Bounding Boxes and Placemarks (`bag-py-ge-0.1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from Google Earth showing the output of `bag2kmlbbox` (version 0.1), a Python script that reads metadata from NOAA Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) hydrographic survey files and generates KML white bounding boxes and yellow pushpin placemarks over satellite imagery of the Norfolk / Hampton Roads, Virginia coastline.

## Map & Data Details
- **Geographic Area**: Satellite view of the southern Chesapeake Bay / Hampton Roads waterfront in Norfolk, Virginia, showing naval piers along the western edge, Willoughby Spit extending across the upper center, Norfolk International Airport runways in the lower right-center, and coastal inlets.
- **Dense Grid of 1-Meter BAG Files (Upper Right)**:
