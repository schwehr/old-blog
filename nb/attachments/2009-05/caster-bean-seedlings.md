# Potted Castor Bean Seedlings Against a Masonry Wall (`caster-bean-seedlings.png`)

## Overview
A high-angle color photograph taken on May 24, 2009 showing three young green castor bean (*Ricinus communis*) seedlings planted in individual plastic flowerpots lined up along a concrete ledge beside a painted cinder-block wall.

## Scene Description
- **Pots and Seedlings**:
  - Three round plastic flowerpots filled with dark, moist potting soil are arranged in a diagonal row from upper left to lower right.
  - The upper-left and middle pots are dark forest green; the lower-right pot is terracotta-colored.
  - Each pot holds a single castor bean seedling with a slender reddish-brown stem and bright green, palmately lobed leaves with serrated edges. The lower-right seedling also displays a rounded, smooth oval cotyledon leaf near its base.
- **Background & Setting**:
  - To the right, the pots rest against a wall constructed of beveled rectangular concrete blocks painted a pale sage-green / off-white color.
  - Beneath the pots is a light gray concrete step or patio surface, with a patch of green grass and weeds visible beyond the edge in the upper left corner.
