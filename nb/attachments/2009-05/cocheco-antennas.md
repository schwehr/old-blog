# Annotated View of AIS VHF and GPS Antennas on the R/V Cocheco (`cocheco-antennas.jpg`)

## Overview
An annotated color photograph looking aft over the cabin roof of the University of New Hampshire research vessel *R/V Cocheco* moored alongside the UNH pier in New Castle, New Hampshire, highlighting the VHF and GPS antennas used for its Class B AIS transceiver installation.

## Scene Description & Annotations
- **Highlighted Antennas**:
  - **Yellow Rectangle (Upper Left)**: Outlines the tall vertical white fiberglass marine VHF whip antenna mounted on the starboard side of the cabin roof, which was already installed on the vessel with cabling routed below deck.
  - **Red Circle (Middle Left)**: Highlights the newly installed small white mushroom-style dedicated GPS antenna mounted on a short bracket on the starboard forward roof rail, just behind a white hailer horn speaker and another GPS dome.
- **Cabin Roof Equipment**:
  - Center: A white tubular arch supporting a white and blue radar radome labeled `FURUNO` in bold blue letters, topped by a black all-round navigation light mast. Below the arch sits a white cylindrical canister liferaft, and in front of the arch is a rectangular white spotlight mounted on the forward brow.
  - Port side (right): Additional vertical antenna mounts and a white cylindrical sensor/antenna mast.
  - Foreground: The three forward windshield windows of the *R/V Cocheco* pilothouse framed in black rubber gaskets, each equipped with a windshield wiper.
- **Background**:
  - Left: The concrete deck, black timber pilings, and yellow crane of the UNH marine research pier.
  - Right: Gray harbor water and a historic brick and stone coastal fort building (Fort Constitution) on the shore in the distance under an overcast sky.
