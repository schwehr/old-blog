# Overhead Electrical Cabling Compartment Inside the R/V Cocheco (`cocheco-cabling.jpg`)

## Overview
A low-angle interior color photograph looking up into the open electrical and antenna wiring access cavity above the head (restroom) on the UNH research vessel *R/V Cocheco* prior to installing a Class B AIS transceiver.

## Scene Description
- **Framing & Fixture**:
  - Light-colored natural wood trim frames the doorway and ceiling opening in the left and right foreground.
  - On the white ceiling panel at upper left is a round, chrome-rimmed marine dome light fixture with a textured glass lens.
- **Wiring & Electronics Cavity**:
  - Behind a horizontal wood-trimmed crossbeam across the center, an open overhead compartment reveals a dense bundle of marine electrical wires (red, black, green, blue, yellow, and white) and black coaxial antenna cables.
  - Visible inside the cavity are white multi-screw terminal blocks/bus bars, zip-tied cable harnesses, and black electronic modules.
  - Hanging below the crossbeam in the lower center is a small cylindrical gray metal component (such as an inline filter, buzzer, or relay) alongside white coaxial cables tied together with cord.
  - On the interior bulkhead wall at bottom center, green-and-white vertical lettering (`MA...`) is partially visible next to vertical wooden framing studs and orange-booted cable connectors on the right.
