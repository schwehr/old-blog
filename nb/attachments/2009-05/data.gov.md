# Screenshot of NOAA Search Results on the Newly Launched Data.gov Catalog (`data.gov.png`)

## Overview
A webpage screenshot taken on May 21, 2009 of the newly launched U.S. Federal Government open data portal (`Data.gov`), showing the "Data.gov Catalog" page and the two initial results returned when searching for datasets and tools from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).

## Interface & Visible Text
- **Header & Navigation**:
  - Top left logo: `DATA.GOV` in large serif capital letters (`DATA` in gray, `.GOV` in gold/orange).
  - Horizontal navigation bar: `HOME` | `CATALOG` (bold/active) | `ABOUT` | `FAQ` | `CONTACT US` | `SUGGEST OTHER DATASETS`.
  - Heading: `Data.gov Catalog` followed by an introductory paragraph explaining how to access U.S. Federal Executive Branch datasets via the "raw" data catalog and the Tools Catalog, with a gold link to the `Data Policy`.
- **Search Filter Panel (Dark Gray Box)**:
  - **Search "raw" data and tools by keywords**: Text input field with a magnifying glass icon.
  - **Search "raw" data by file type**: Checkboxes for `XML`, `CSV/Text`, `KML/KMZ`, `ESRI`, and `Other`.
  - **Search "raw" data and tools by single/multiple category**: Scrollable checklist with `All Categories` checked, followed by `Births, Deaths, Marriages, and Divorces` and `Business Enterprise`.
  - **Search "raw" data and tools by single/multiple agency**: Scrollable checklist showing `All Agencies` and `Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ)`.
  - Gold `SEARCH` button at the bottom right of the panel.
- **"RAW" DATA CATALOG Section (Gold Header Bar)**:
  - Table columns: `Name (click for metadata and to rate dataset)`, `Rating`, `Agency`, `XML`, `CSV/TXT`, `KML/KMZ`, `ESRI`, `Maps`, `Other`.
  - Single result (`[ Showing 1 out of 1 results ]`):
    - **Name**: `Next Generation Radar (NEXRAD) Locations`
    - **Description**: `This geospatial rendering of weather radar sites gives access to an historical archive of Terminal Doppler Weather Radar data and is used primarily for research purposes....`
    - **Rating**: 5 unlit gray stars.
    - **Agency**: `NOAA`
    - **KML/KMZ**: Red `KML` icon labeled `8KB`.
- **TOOLS Section (Blue Header Bar)**:
  - Table columns: `Name (click for metadata and to rate tool)`, `Rating`, `Agency`, `Category`, `Data Extraction`, `Widget`.
  - Single result (`[ Showing 1 out of 1 results ]`):
    - **Name**: `NOAA Watch Web Widgets`
    - **Description**: `This site contains widgets that track river levels and forecasts with content provided by NOAA. The widgets will update as NOAA updates information.`

    - **Agency**: `NOAA`
