# Aerial Photograph of Little Bay, Durham, and Newington, New Hampshire (`durham-newington.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial color photograph taken from an airplane window on May 10, 2009 looking down over Little Bay, the Oyster River, and the Piscataqua River estuary between Durham, Dover, and Newington, New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Waterways & Bridges**:
  - Deep blue estuary waters wind across the frame.
  - Near the center of the image, the narrow channel at Dover Point / Cedar Point is spanned by the twin bridges of the Spaulding Turnpike (Route 16 / Route 4) and the adjacent General Sullivan Bridge, connecting Dover/Durham on the north/west with Newington on the south/east.
  - To the upper left, the Oyster River branch extends inland toward Durham.
  - Below and to the right of the bridges, Little Bay widens as it curves toward Great Bay (lower center/left) and the Piscataqua River (upper right).
- **Land & Clouds**:
  - The surrounding landscape consists of dark green forested areas interspersed with lighter green agricultural fields, pastures, coastal roads, and shoreline residential development.
  - Several fluffy white cumulus clouds float below the aircraft in the upper left, center, and lower left corner, casting dark patches of shadow onto the land and water below.
