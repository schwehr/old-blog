# ERMA Caribbean Web GIS Interface Showing the Eastern Caribbean (`erma-caribbean.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the University of New Hampshire / NOAA **ERMA (Environmental Response Management Application) Caribbean** web mapping interface zoomed out to show Puerto Rico, Hispaniola, the Lesser Antilles, and the eastern Caribbean Sea.

## Interface & Map Details
- **Top Banner**:
  - Title text: `ERMA | Environmental Response Management Application` with subtitle `Caribbean` over a tropical beach header graphic featuring palm trees and turquoise ocean water.
  - Top navigation bar: `Map` (selected), `Information`, `Help` on the left, and `Login` on the right.
- **Map Viewport (Left)**:
  - Displays the eastern Caribbean Sea at `Scale: 1: 7M`, `Zoom Level: 6`, `Location: -64.97646°, 15.72846°` (marked by a black `X` in the center of the Caribbean Sea).
  - **Visible Geographic Labels**:
    - Upper left (Hispaniola): `Dominican Republic`, `Puerto Plata`, `Moca`, `Nagua`, `Yamasá`, `Hato Mayor`, `San Luis`, `Higüey`, `Azua`, `Baní`, `La Romana`.
    - Upper center: Unlabeled island of Puerto Rico and `British Virgin Islands`.
    - Eastern island arc (Lesser Antilles, top to bottom): `Saint Kitts and Nevis`, `Antigua and Barbuda`, `Montserrat`, `Guadeloupe`, `Dominica`, `Martinique`, `Saint Lucia`, `Saint Vincent and the Grenadines`, `Hillsborough`, `Grenada`, `Scarborough`.
    - Lower left: `Netherlands Antilles` and `Santa Ana de Coro` on the Venezuelan coast.
  - **Map Controls**: Pan directional arrows and a vertical zoom slider bar on the left, below which are a hand (pan) tool icon and a magnifying glass zoom tool icon; three small tool icons (map, ruler/measure, and `i` info) sit at the top right of the map canvas.
- **Right Sidebar (`Layers` Tab)**:
  - Tabs along the top: `Layers` (active), `Legend`, `Zoom`, `Download`, `Print`, with a `reload` button at top right.
  - Expandable layer folders (each with a `+` box on the left and `clear | all` links on the right):
    - `Base Layer`
    - `Public Safety`
    - `ESI`
    - `Bioresources`
    - `Weather Observation`
    - `Data Buoys & Observations`
    - `Charts, Surveys, Ships`
    - `Environmental Quality`
    - `ERMA Tools`
    - `GeoRSS`
    - `BASE`
- **Footer**: Creative Commons `BY-NC-SA` license badge and `©2007 - 2009 University of New Hampshire`.
