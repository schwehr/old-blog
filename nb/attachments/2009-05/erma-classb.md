# ERMA Portsmouth Visualization of Class B AIS Vessel CRRC TEST 1 (`erma-classb.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot taken on May 9, 2009 of the **ERMA (Environmental Response Management Application) Portsmouth, NH** web GIS interface displaying the author's first Class B AIS geospatial visualization: the vessel `CRRC TEST 1` (and nearby `RUTH REINAUER`) overlaid on high-resolution multibeam bathymetry of the Piscataqua River and OpenStreetMap base layers near Newington, New Hampshire.

## Map & Data Details
- **Header & Navigation**:

  - Toolbar: `Map`, `Print`, `Info`, `Help`, `>`, `Legend`, `Layer`, `Data` (highlighted/active), `Tools`.
- **Map Viewport (Left)**:

  - **AIS Vessel Symbols**: Near the center of the channel (by `Frankfort Island`) are two orange triangle ship icons labeled `RUTH REINAUER` and `CRRC TEST 1`. A black `X` marks a clicked location near `Newington Station` to the southwest.
