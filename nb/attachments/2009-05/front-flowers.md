# Red Lily-Flowered Tulips in the Front Yard (`front-flowers.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up overhead photograph of two vibrant red and orange lily-flowered tulips blooming in the grass by a lamp post in Kurt Schwehr's front yard on May 18, 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Flowers**: Two fully opened, star-shaped blooms (lily-flowered tulips) dominate the frame, viewed straight down into their throats:
  - **Petals**: Six long, narrow, sharply pointed tepals per flower curve outward in a dramatic star pattern. They are bright scarlet-red with thin white edges along several of the outer margins.
  - **Centers**: The interior bases of the petals transition from fiery red to a glowing golden-orange and yellow throat. At the center of each flower is a pale yellow-green three-lobed stigma surrounded by yellow-orange stamens and anthers.
  - **Arrangement**: The larger flower on the right overlaps the slightly smaller bloom on the left. Wavy, pale green tulip leaves extend downward from the stems below the blooms.
- **Background**: Surrounding the flowers is lush, bright green lawn grass mixed with patches of dry brown thatch, fallen leaves, and small green clover leaves on the right.
