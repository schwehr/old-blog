# Early Spring Vegetable and Herb Garden Bed (`garden-start.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on May 18, 2009, showing the initial spring planting of Kurt Schwehr's backyard vegetable and herb garden enclosed by a white picket fence.

- **Plants & Seedlings**:
  - **Foreground & Right Bed**: In the lower center, green onion/chive stalks and a feathery green herb (such as dill or fennel) grow near the edge of the bed.   - **Left Bed**: Along the left side of the black plastic path, tiny newly sprouted seedlings and white plant markers are spaced out across the bare soil.
- **Fencing & Trellises**:
