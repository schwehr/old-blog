# USCGC Healy In Port at Seattle in Google Earth (`healy-in-port.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot from May 23, 2009, showing the *USCGC Healy* arriving at and docked in Seattle, Washington, demonstrating a new feature in Kurt Schwehr's GeoRSS/KML feed that suppresses the Aloftcon camera image while the ship is stationed in port and adds a direct Google Maps link.

## Map & Track Details
- **Background Map**: Oblique satellite imagery of Puget Sound, Bainbridge Island (`Bai...` partially visible), and the Seattle waterfront, with light green county/shoreline boundary lines.
- **Ship Icons & Timestamps**: Multiple red-and-white *Healy* vessel icons are shown along the orange track line entering Puget Sound and clustered around the Seattle dock (with radial white leader lines spreading out the overlapping docked positions):

  - Center/right icons near Seattle: **`23 May 16:01`**, **`23 May 17:01`**, **`23 May 18:01`**, **`23 May 19:01`**, **`23 May 20:01`**, and **`23 May 22:01`**.
