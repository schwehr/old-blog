# USCGC Healy Science Instrument Reports in Google Earth (`healy-instrument-report.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot taken on May 19, 2009, displaying hourly automated science and meteorological instrument report placemarks along the *USCGC Healy*'s transit across the Gulf of Alaska, highlighting the transition from shallow continental shelf water (~71 m) to deep ocean basin water (~3697 m).

## Map & Interface Details
- **Geographic View**: Shows the Alaska Peninsula, Kodiak Island, and the Aleutian Trench / Gulf of Alaska bathymetry.
- **Placemarks**: A sequence of purple paddle pins with black dots marks the ship's hourly positions along a blue track line, labeled with orange timestamps running from `14 May 15:30` in the lower left through `15 May`, `16 May`, and `17 May 08:30` in the upper right.
- **Google Earth Controls**:
  - Top-left time slider showing `May 17, 2009 5 pm`.
  - Top-right navigation compass, pan controls, and zoom slider.
  - Bottom-left scale bar reading `358 km`.
  - Bottom status bar reading `lat 58.172803° lon -152.424543°` and `Eye alt 1154.51 km`.
