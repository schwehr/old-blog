# USCGC Healy Ship Track Across the Bering Sea and Gulf of Alaska (`healy-ship-track.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot from May 19, 2009, showing the continuous blue ship track of the *USCGC Healy* (WAGB-20) across the Bering Sea, around the Alaska Peninsula/Aleutian Islands, and across the Gulf of Alaska, created while experimenting with HSV color space and variable track widths.

## Map & Track Details
- **Geographic Region**: Displays a wide regional view of southern and western Alaska, including the Bering Sea shelf (left), St. Matthew and Nunivak Islands, the Alaska Peninsula and eastern Aleutians (bottom-left), Kodiak Island (center), and the Gulf of Alaska with the Aleutian Trench bathymetric drop-off (right).
- **Vessel Track**:
  - Rendered as a blue polyline whose shade and thickness vary along segments of the voyage.
  - In the upper-left (northern Bering Sea near St. Lawrence Island), the track forms several survey loops and grid patterns.
  - It then runs southeastward across the Bering Sea shelf, makes a sharp V-shaped excursion north of the Alaska Peninsula, passes south through Unimak Pass / Dutch Harbor in the Aleutians, and heads northeastward along the continental slope and across the deep Gulf of Alaska toward Southeast Alaska on the far right.
