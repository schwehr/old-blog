# iNavX Marine Navigation Application on the iPhone (`iphone-inavx.png`)

## Overview
A composite screenshot posted on May 18, 2009, showing two side-by-side views of the **iNavX** electronic charting and navigation application running on an Apple iPhone, displaying a NOAA Raster Nautical Chart (RNC) of the Golden Gate in San Francisco Bay along with GPS vessel position and AIS targets.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Navigation Bar**: Both screens feature a blue-gray iOS header bar with a left-pointing back button labeled **`Charts`**, a centered title reading **`San Francisco...`**, and a right button labeled **`Setup`**.
- **Bottom Tab Bar**: Both screens share a black iOS tab bar at the bottom with five icons:
  - **`Chart`** (highlighted with a blue triangle icon)
  - **`WP+ / Waypoints`** (marked with a red-and-white circular notification badge on the left screenshot)
  - **`Tracks`** (dashed line icon)
  - **`00.00 / Instruments`**
  - **`Tides`** (sine wave icon)

## Chart & Navigation Details
- **Left Screen (Golden Gate South / Fort Point)**:
  - Shows the southern tower approach of the Golden Gate Bridge, **Fort Pt** (`FP`), **Presidio Shoal**, **Anita** Rock, and the `G A T E` lettering across the strait (`(chart 18650)`).
  - Features a blue boat icon (own ship) with a green dot at its center pointing west-northwest along a thick blue course/bearing line toward a yellow-and-red rectangular waypoint box labeled **`GGBRG`** on the Golden Gate Bridge span, with a red line extending east-northeast.
- **Right Screen (Golden Gate North / Horseshoe Bay & AIS Targets)**:
  - Shows the northern shore of the Golden Gate including **Lime Pt**, **Horseshoe Bay**, **Pt Cavallo**, and **Yellow Bluff** (`Fl 6s 75ft 7M`).
  - Displays the blue own-ship icon with a green center dot off Pt Cavallo, along with two orange AIS vessel target triangles in the Golden Gate channel—one with a green center dot heading east (`G`) and one with a yellow center dot heading east-northeast (`A T E`)—each projecting an orange heading/speed vector line.
