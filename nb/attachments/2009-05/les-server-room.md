# Les Working in the CCOM Server Room (`les-server-room.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on May 30, 2009, inside one of the server rooms at the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM / JHC) at the University of New Hampshire, showing systems administrator Les performing hardware upgrades on a rack-mounted Linux server (`Datalogger1`).

## Scene Description
- **Server Racks (Left & Center)**:
  - A row of tall, black open-frame 19-inch equipment racks extends from the left foreground into the background.
  - **Leftmost Rack**: Packed with multi-bay black storage arrays / disk shelves with white label strips on the drive caddies.
  - **Second Rack**: Houses several silver-bezel Dell PowerEdge rackmount servers (with blue badges) and yellow vertical caution/warning strips along the rack posts.
  - **Third & Fourth Racks**: Contain additional black multi-bay RAID/storage chassis, Dell servers, and coiled orange fiber-optic and blue Ethernet patch cables at the bottom.
- **Les & Pulled-Out Server (Right)**:
  - Near the top of the far-right rack, a 1U silver rackmount server is slid all the way out on its telescoping rack rails, extending horizontally into the aisle at head height.
  - **Les** stands beneath and slightly behind the extended server chassis against the white wall, looking down thoughtfully at a small hardware component or tool held in both hands. He is wearing a dark navy/black zip-up hoodie over a slate-blue t-shirt, light blue jeans, and brown hiking shoes.
  - On the white wall behind him are printed rack layout diagrams/spreadsheets, and a square metal server top cover leans against the wall by his right leg.
