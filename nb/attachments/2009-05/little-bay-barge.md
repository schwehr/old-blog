# Construction Crane Barge and Tugboat on Little Bay (`little-bay-barge.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on May 8, 2009, near Adams Point in Durham, New Hampshire, showing a local marine construction barge carrying a lattice-boom crawler crane being pushed or tended by a small tugboat across the waters of Little Bay.

## Scene Description
- **Crane Barge (Center-Left)**:
  - A low-profile, rust-brown flat-deck steel spud barge floats on the calm gray-blue water. Two tall vertical steel spud poles rise from the barge deck.
  - Mounted in the center of the barge is a red-and-white tracked crawler crane with a tall black open-lattice boom angled high into the sky on the left, rigged with steel hoist cables.
  - Three crew members in hard hats and work gear are visible on the barge deck around the crane cab and a small gray deck shed on the right end of the barge (which has an orange life ring mounted on its wall).
- **Tugboat & Skiff (Right)**:
  - Positioned tightly against the right (stern) end of the barge is a small red-hulled, white-cabined pusher tugboat with black rubber tire fenders along its side and a crew member visible on its upper helm station.
  - Trailing directly behind the tugboat is a small gray open work skiff with a white outboard motor and a white wake trailing to the right.
- **Background**: Across the water is a heavily wooded shoreline of mixed green pines and early-spring deciduous trees, with a few large waterfront houses visible among the trees on the right and an overcast gray sky above.
