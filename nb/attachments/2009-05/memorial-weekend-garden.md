# Backyard Garden on Memorial Day Weekend (`memorial-weekend-garden.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle photograph taken on May 24, 2009 (Memorial Day weekend), showing Kurt Schwehr's fenced backyard vegetable and herb garden in New Hampshire as more plants and seedlings are added.

## Scene Description
- **Garden Enclosure**: The rectangular garden plot is surrounded on all four sides by a white wooden picket fence (with the tops of the foreground pickets visible along the bottom edge).
- **Beds, Paths & Supports**:
  - Two parallel strips of black plastic sheeting weighted down with bricks run horizontally across the plot, dividing the dark brown soil into distinct planting beds.
  - Tall green vertical garden stakes and an arched wire-mesh trellis span the left and rear sections of the garden.
  - Small wooden stakes, white plant labels, and two bright yellow plastic cups/pots mark rows and individual seedlings in the middle bed.
  - On the right side sit wire tomato cages, three rectangular terracotta-colored plastic planter boxes, and round dark-green plastic nursery pots near the lower-right corner.
- **Surrounding Yard & Houses**:
  - Directly behind the garden is the light sage-green clapboard wall of the house with two white-trimmed double-hung windows.
  - To the right of the house stands a leafy green lilac/shrub and a neighboring dark red clapboard building with a shingled roof and a paned white door.
