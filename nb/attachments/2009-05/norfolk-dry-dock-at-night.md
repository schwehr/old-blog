# Norfolk Dry Dock Illuminated at Night (`norfolk-dry-dock-at-night.jpg`)

## Overview
A nighttime photograph looking across the Elizabeth River in Norfolk, Virginia, toward an illuminated shipyard floating dry dock and tall gantry cranes during the US Hydro 2009 conference in May 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Dry Dock & Cranes**: Along the middle horizon, a large floating dry dock and shipyard facility glow brightly with rows of yellow, white, and greenish-white floodlights. Four tall lattice tower cranes rise above the dock into the pitch-black night sky, their vertical masts and horizontal jibs outlined by red aviation obstruction lights at their peaks and bright white work lights along their booms. Inside the right side of the dry dock, a large rectangular white ship superstructure or enclosure is brightly lit.
- **Water & Reflections**: The foreground is filled by the dark surface of the Elizabeth River, which reflects long, vertical ribbons of warm amber, gold, yellow-green, and bright white light from the shipyard fixtures along the waterfront.
