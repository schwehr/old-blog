# Norfolk Dry Dock and Waterfront in Morning Light (`norfolk-dry-dock-morning.jpg`)

## Overview
A clear morning photograph looking across the calm blue waters of the Elizabeth River in Norfolk, Virginia, showing the same shipyard floating dry dock and cranes seen at night, alongside a passing tugboat and residential/commercial high-rises on the adjacent shore.

## Scene Description
- **Floating Dry Dock & Vessel (Left)**:
  - A dark grey floating dry dock sits along the left side of the frame, straddled by three tall lattice tower cranes with long horizontal jibs extending to the left and right.
  - Inside the dry dock sits a large naval vessel shrouded in white protective scaffolding and tarpaulins over its bow, stern, and boxy central superstructure, with its upper mast and radar platform rising above the enclosure. A white bridge/arch logo (`BAE Systems` / shipyard emblem) is painted near the right end of the dark grey dry dock wall.
- **Waterway & Tugboat (Center-Right)**:
  - The calm, rippled blue water of the Elizabeth River fills the foreground.
  - To the right of the dry dock, a white harbor tugboat is underway toward the viewer, leaving a gentle V-shaped wake behind it.
- **Background Skyline (Right)**:
  - Beyond the dry dock and river, low-rise brick buildings, a mid-rise white hotel or office building, and a tall reddish-tan brick high-rise apartment tower stand along the green tree-lined shoreline under a clear morning sky with a faint diagonal contrail.
