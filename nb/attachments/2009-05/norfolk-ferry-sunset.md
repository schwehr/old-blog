# Elizabeth River Ferry at Sunset in Norfolk, Virginia (`norfolk-ferry-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview
A dusk photograph taken along the Norfolk, Virginia waterfront on May 10, 2009, showing the paddlewheel-style passenger ferry to Portsmouth, Virginia docked on the Elizabeth River against a vibrant pink and orange sunset sky.

## Scene Description
- **Ferry Vessel (Left)**:
  - On the left side of the frame, the Elizabeth River Ferry—styled after a traditional sternwheeler with a decorative paddlewheel at its stern (far left), an enclosed white main passenger cabin with illuminated windows, an open-air canopied upper deck, a tall black smokestack, and an elevated forward pilothouse—is docked at the ferry slip.
- **Slip Pilings & Waterfront (Center to Right)**:
  - Tall vertical guide pilings flank the ferry landing in the center.
  - In the foreground right, dark wooden mooring dolphins and the shadowed edge of the Norfolk waterfront walkway jut into the rippled, blue-grey water.
  - Farther to the right along the marina docks, several white motor yachts and sailboats with tall masts are moored, with warm streetlamp lights glowing along the pier. Across the river in the background sits the silhouetted skyline of Portsmouth, Virginia.
- **Sunset Sky**:
  - The sky transitions from cool slate-blue at the upper left with scattered dark puffs of cloud to bright streaks of coral pink, salmon, and fiery orange-gold across the right horizon.
