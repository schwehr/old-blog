# High-Speed Stealth Craft in Norfolk Harbor (`norfolk-guardian-high-speed-craft-369970121.jpg`)

## Overview
A telephoto photograph taken in Norfolk Harbor, Virginia, during May 2009, showing a low-profile, faceted military catamaran/stealth vessel underway in front of a naval cargo ship and container terminal gantry cranes. In the accompanying blog post, Kurt Schwehr notes receiving an unencrypted AIS broadcast for a High Speed Craft (HSC) named `GUARDIAN` (MMSI `369970121`, callsign `25MPB01`, dimensions 25 m × 6 m, destination `CHESAPEAKE LIGHT`) and questions whether this vessel (actually the experimental M80 Stiletto or a similar faceted hull craft) corresponds to that broadcast.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Vessel**:
  - Centered in the water is a dark grey, angular, faceted-hull stealth craft (twin-hull/M-hull profile) seen from its stern/starboard-quarter aspect as it moves across the harbor, leaving a light trail of disturbed water behind it to the left.
  - A small open mast/frame rises above the center of the low superstructure, and three crew members in dark uniforms stand on the upper right deck overlooking the starboard side.
- **Harbor Markers**:
  - A red-and-dark navigation buoy floats on the left behind the vessel's wake, and a smaller dark buoy/marker is visible in the distance on the right.
- **Background Port Infrastructure**:
  - Directly behind the stealth craft sits a large grey military sealift / roll-on/roll-off cargo ship with twin deck cranes and tall twin superstructures/funnels.
  - To the right of the ship, five tall blue-grey container gantry cranes with raised booms line the shipping terminal along the hazy horizon.
