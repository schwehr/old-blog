# Sunset over the Elizabeth River Marina in Norfolk, Virginia (`norfolk-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic sunset photograph looking across a marina basin on the Elizabeth River in Norfolk, Virginia, taken on the evening of May 10, 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Setting Sun**:
  - The sun sits low in the right-center sky, creating a brilliant white-gold core surrounded by warm amber and orange light filtering through horizontal bands of cirrus and stratus clouds.
  - Toward the upper left, darker blue-grey cloud patches contrast against pale blue sky.
- **Marina & Moored Vessels**:
  - Along the left and center middle ground, several white motor yachts and a tall-masted sailboat are moored at floating docks.
  - Behind the boats on the left stand silhouetted rectangular buildings and a cupola-topped waterfront pavilion.
  - Along the right edge, a wooden pier lined with vintage-style illuminated streetlamps extends toward the background, casting vertical dark shadows into the water.
- **Water Reflections**:
  - The gently rippled surface of the marina basin fills the foreground, reflecting a shimmering path of bright gold and orange light beneath the setting sun alongside dark silhouettes of the docks and boats.
