# Aerial View of Norfolk, Little Creek, and Virginia Beach Looking East (`norfolk-va-beach.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from a commercial airplane window on May 10, 2009, looking east along the southern shore of the Chesapeake Bay across Little Creek, Lynnhaven Inlet, Cape Henry, and the Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel in Norfolk and Virginia Beach, Virginia.

## Geographic & Scene Details
- **Chesapeake Bay & Bridge-Tunnel (Left)**:
  - The calm blue expanse of the Chesapeake Bay occupies the left half of the frame.
  - Extending horizontally from the sandy shoreline just north of Lynnhaven Inlet straight out to the left edge is the southern trestle section of the Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel, terminating at the rock-armored South Thimble Shoal Island tunnel portal near the left margin.
- **Little Creek Harbor (Lower Right / Foreground)**:
  - In the foreground, twin rock jetties guard the entrance channel into Little Creek Harbor (Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek), where numerous naval vessels, piers, and marinas line the branched harbor basin.
  - West (bottom) of the inlet, uniform coastal residential blocks border a wide sandy beach with rhythmic shoreline cusps.
- **Cape Henry & Virginia Beach (Upper Center/Right)**:
  - Beyond Little Creek, the sandy shoreline curves northeastward past the Lynnhaven Inlet bridge and around the wooded headland of First Landing State Park / Cape Henry toward the Atlantic Ocean beachfront of Virginia Beach on the distant horizon.
