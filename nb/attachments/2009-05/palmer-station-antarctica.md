# Leopard Seal on an Ice Floe in Front of Palmer Station, Antarctica (`palmer-station-antarctica.png`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect photograph taken by Roland Arsenault during an Antarctic whale-tagging research cruise in May 2009, showing a leopard seal resting on a small ice floe in the calm, brash-ice-filled waters in front of the United States Antarctic Program's Palmer Station on Anvers Island.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Seal & Ice**:
  - In the lower-right foreground, a large, spotted grey-and-black leopard seal lies stretched out in profile (facing left) atop a low, flat chunk of white and pale-blue sea ice.
  - Surrounding the floe, the calm, slate-grey water reflects the seal's dark silhouette and is dotted with scattered chunks of white brash ice and pancake ice.
- **Background — Palmer Station**:
  - Across the middle horizon rises the snow-covered rocky shoreline of Gamage Point on Anvers Island, where the blue-and-teal metal buildings, fuel tanks, white radome, and tall communications antenna mast of Palmer Station stand against a snowy glacier slope and an overcast, steel-grey polar sky.
