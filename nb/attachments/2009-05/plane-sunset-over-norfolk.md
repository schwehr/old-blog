# Sunset Above the Clouds Over Norfolk from an Airplane (`plane-sunset-over-norfolk.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken from the window of a commercial passenger flight departing Norfolk, Virginia, at the conclusion of the US Hydro 2009 conference on May 15, 2009, capturing the setting sun glowing above a continuous deck of clouds.

## Scene Description
- **Cloud Deck (Lower Two-Thirds)**:
  - A dense, undulating blanket of stratocumulus and soft mist stretches across the lower portion of the frame. The cloud tops are bathed in warm peach and amber light near the center and fade into cool slate-blue and grey shadows toward the left and bottom edges.
- **Setting Sun & Upper Sky (Upper Third)**:
  - Above the main cloud deck, the setting sun shines as a brilliant white-gold orb in the upper right-center, framed by darker grey-brown horizontal cloud bands above and beside it that glow with golden-orange edges.
