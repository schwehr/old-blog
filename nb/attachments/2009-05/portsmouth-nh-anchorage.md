# Eitzen Chemical Tanker in the Portsmouth, NH Anchorage (`portsmouth-nh-anchorage.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime maritime photograph taken on May 22, 2009, during a boat trip between Portsmouth, New Hampshire, and the Isles of Shoals, showing the chemical tanker *Sichem New York* (operated by Eitzen Chemical) anchored offshore in the Portsmouth anchorage.

## Vessel & Scene Description
- **Vessel Profile**:
  - The chemical tanker is seen in port-side profile facing left, sitting on calm, dark-blue rippled water under a clear pale-blue sky.
  - **Hull**: Painted reddish-brown/maroon with `EITZEN CHEMICAL` emblazoned in large white block letters along the midship hull (`EITZEN` on the left, a diamond/dot logo in the middle, and `CHEMICAL` on the right). The ship's name (`SICHEM NEW YORK`, visible in the companion Nobeltec AIS screenshot from the same cruise) is painted in smaller white letters at the bow and stern.
  - **Deck & Superstructure**: A white foremast stands on the forecastle; reddish-brown cargo piping, manifolds, and a white midship hose-handling crane run along the main deck; and a tall, boxy white aft accommodation superstructure is topped by a radar mast and a black-and-red funnel bearing a white `E` (Eitzen) logo, with an orange enclosed free-fall lifeboat mounted on a stern launch ramp.
- **Background Shoreline**:
  - A low, tree-covered green coastline dotted with houses stretches across the distant horizon behind the vessel.
