# ERMA Web GIS Showing CRRC Test 1 During Portsmouth Response Drill (`portsmouth-response-drill.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the NOAA/UNH **ERMA** (Environmental Response Management Application) web GIS interface for `Portsmouth, NH`, captured on May 13, 2009, during the Portsmouth emergency response drill, showing the live Class B AIS track of the vessel `CRRC TEST 1` traveling north up the Piscataqua/Cocheco River over high-resolution multibeam bathymetry.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Banner & Navigation Bar**:
  - Textured dark blue ocean-wave header reading `ERMA` in bold white letters followed by `Portsmouth, NH` on the left and `Admin` on the right.
  - Dark grey menu bar below with links on the left (`Map`, `Print`, `Info`, `Help`), a collapse toggle (`>`), and sidebar tabs on the right (`Legend` [selected], `Layer`, `Data`, `Tools`).
- **Map Canvas (Left)**:
  - **Navigation Controls**: Blue directional pan rose, vertical zoom slider (`+` to `-`), pan hand button, and zoom-box magnifying glass button along the left margin; blue layer/overview expand button (`+`) at the upper right of the map canvas.
  - **Base Map & Geography**: Shows the confluence of Little Bay / Piscataqua River and the Cocheco River / Salmon Falls River near Dover and Eliot/South Berwick, including `US 4` (blue highway with interchange loops on the left), `Wentworth Terrace`, `Crams Corner`, `Sunset Hill 44` (brown triangle), and `State Rd` (yellow road on the right).
  - **Multibeam Bathymetry**: Along the bottom of the map canvas, shaded-relief multibeam bathymetry of the river channel is rendered in greens, yellows, oranges, and browns.
  - **Ship Track & Icon**: A bold reddish-brown line traces the vessel's path from the bathymetry coverage at the bottom, turning north up the narrow river channel to an elongated orange pointer triangle labeled `CRRC TEST 1` near the top left.
- **Right Sidebar (`Legend`)**:
  - **Incident Information**: Red star icon labeled `Command Post`.
  - **Charts, Surveys, Ships**:
    - Orange elongated triangle icon labeled `Ships`.
    - Reddish-brown zigzag line icon labeled `Ship Tracks`.
    - Colorbar ramp from `0` to `40` (`0`, `10`, `20`, `30`, `40`) labeled `Depth (meters)` with a thin black zigzag icon below labeled `Depth`.
- **Bottom Status Bar**:
  - Displays `Scale: 1 : 27K`, `Zoom Level: 14`, and cursor coordinates `Location: -70.82407°, 43.13020°`.
