# PostGIS in Action MEAP Book Promotional Graphic (`postgis-book.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the publisher's promotional web page for the book *PostGIS in Action* by Regina O. Obe and Leo S. Hsu, offered through the Manning Early Access Program (MEAP) in May 2009.

## Layout & Visual Elements
- **Left Column**:
  - **Book Cover**: Displays the standard Manning Publications cover design featuring a dark reddish-brown vertical band on the left with the title "PostGIS IN ACTION" (with "PostGIS" in bold black text and "IN ACTION" in white capital letters) and the authors' names ("Regina O. Obe", "Leo S. Hsu") and the Manning logo near the bottom. The right side of the cover has a white background with a full-length historical illustration of a woman in traditional ornate dark and patterned folk attire.
  - **MEAP Logo**: Below the book cover is a rectangular dark red badge framed in black and white displaying "MEAP" in white serif letters, with the caption "Manning Early Access Program" underneath.
- **Right Column**:
  - **Header**: Bold black title "PostGIS in Action", followed by a grey-bordered box containing red uppercase text: "EARLY ACCESS EDITION".
  - **Metadata**: Lists the authors and publication details in clean black sans-serif font.
