# Northern Right Whale Tail Fluke (`right-whale-tail-noaa.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph from NOAA showing the tail fluke of an endangered Northern Right Whale (*Eubalaena glacialis*) raised above the ocean surface, referenced in a May 2009 blog post discussing the shift and narrowing of the Boston Traffic Separation Scheme (TSS) shipping lanes in the Great South Channel to reduce whale ship strikes.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Whale Tail)**: The dark grey-to-black tail fluke of a right whale rises vertically out of the deep blue water just left of center. The broad flukes curve gracefully outward to pointed tips, with a distinct median notch in the center and a smooth trailing edge. Sunlight glints brightly off wet patches and water droplets along the fluke's central stock and right tip, while small splashes and white foam surround the base where the tail enters the sea.
- **Middle Ground (Ocean)**: Deep royal-blue ocean water textured with small wind waves and scattered whitecaps stretching across the frame.
- **Background (Coastline)**: A rugged, hazy blue-grey mountainous coastline rises along the upper right horizon under a pale blue sky.
