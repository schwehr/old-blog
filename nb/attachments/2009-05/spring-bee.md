# Bee Pollinating White Spring Blossoms (`spring-bee.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up macro photograph taken at Adams Point, New Hampshire, on May 8, 2009, showing a bee pollinating a cluster of white fruit-tree blossoms.

## Scene Description
- **Flowers**: Four open five-petaled white blossoms (apple, pear, or serviceberry/cherry) rise on slender, fuzzy light-green pedicels from a leafy green spur at the bottom center. Each flower features a cluster of pale cream filaments topped with pink, rose, and dark reddish-brown anthers at the center.
- **Bee**: Perched atop the upper-right blossom, facing left, is a fuzzy brown-and-black bee with translucent brownish wings folded over its abdomen, long dark antennae angled forward, and legs gripping the stamens as it feeds.
- **Background**: Softly out-of-focus bright green spring leaves in the upper left, lower right, and background against a bright, overcast white sky.
