# Close-Up of a Pink and Green Parrot Tulip (`twisted-flower.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical close-up photograph of an unusual, intricately twisted pink-and-green flower (a parrot tulip grown from a "mystery bulb" by the fence in Kurt Schwehr's yard in New Hampshire) in mid-May 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Flower Head**: Positioned centrally in the frame, the large blossom has heavily ruffled, curled, and serrated petals that twist around each other in a flame-like shape. The petals are predominantly vibrant leaf-green through their centers and bases, transitioning to feathered,streaked rose-pink and magenta along the fringed outer margins and tips.
- **Foliage & Background**: Long, narrow, dark green grass and bulb leaves rise alongside the stem in the foreground. In the softly blurred background, lush green yard grass, shrubs, and the bottom corner of a pale grey-green wall or fence are visible.
