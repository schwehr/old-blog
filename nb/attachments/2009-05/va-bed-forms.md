# Aerial View of Coastal Sandbars and Bedforms in Virginia (`va-bed-forms.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from a passenger airplane window on May 10, 2009, while flying into Norfolk, Virginia, for the U.S. Hydro 2009 conference, showing submerged coastal sandwaves and tidal bedforms along a vegetated estuary inlet.

## Scene Description
- **Land & Shoreline**:
  - Two wooded peninsulas and marshy islands indented by winding tidal creeks and coves occupy the lower half of the frame, separated by a deep blue navigation channel running vertically through the center.
  - The left peninsula features forested tracts, cleared green agricultural/residential parcels with waterfront homes and small docks along the lower left, and a prominent bright pinkish-tan sandy spit and marsh flat at its northern tip.
  - The right peninsula is covered in dense dark green forest with rectangular clearings and sandy beaches along its northern shore.
- **Submerged Bedforms**:
  - Surrounding the tips and shallow shoals of both peninsulas—where the deeper blue channel meets the coastal waters—parallel, rhythmic underwater sand ridges and megariples (bedforms) are clearly visible through the clear blue-green water, curving around the headlands and flanking the main tidal channel.
