# Purple Bugleweed Flowers in the Front Yard (`weeds.jpg`)

## Overview
A low-angle close-up photograph of a patch of purple-flowering bugleweed (*Ajuga reptans*) blooming amidst bright green lawn grass in Kurt Schwehr's front yard in May 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Flower Spikes)**: Roughly two dozen upright flower spikes stand clustered across the frame. Each stem features whorls of small, two-lipped violet-blue and lavender blossoms interspersed with small purplish-green bracts, rising above a basal rosette of glossy green and bronze-tinged oval leaves at soil level.
- **Background**: Surrounding and behind the flowering patch is a dense carpet of vibrant green lawn grass blades. Along the upper edge of the frame, dark green bushes and a shrub with bright pink/reddish azalea or rhododendron blooms appear in the background.
