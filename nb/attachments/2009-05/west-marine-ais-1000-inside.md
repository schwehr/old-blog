# Disassembled West Marine AIS-1000 Class B Transponder (`west-marine-ais-1000-inside.jpg`)

## Overview
An overhead workbench photograph showing a disassembled **West Marine AIS-1000** Class B AIS transponder after removing 14 Phillips screws, revealing the outer housing, rubber gasket, baseplate, and internal green printed circuit board (PCB) featuring a Texas Instruments DSP chip.

## Hardware Components
- **Top Left (Upper Housing Shell)**:
  - The inverted grey plastic top cover with a black perimeter sealing rim, circular cable gland/connector cutouts along the top edge, and a recessed pocket at the bottom containing a cluster of removed silver Phillips machine screws (with two additional screws resting inside the main cavity).
- **Top Center (Rubber Gasket)**:
  - A flat, rectangular black rubber weatherproofing gasket with 10 perimeter screw holes laid out between the housing shell and backplate.
- **Top Right (Bottom Cover Plate)**:
  - A flat white painted metal or plastic baseplate with mounting flanges on the left and right sides and perimeter screw holes.
- **Bottom Right (Main Electronics Chassis & PCB)**:
  - A rectangular grey plastic inner chassis housing a green surface-mount printed circuit board secured by corner screws:
    - **DSP Processor**: Near the upper-left center of the PCB is a large square black integrated circuit clearly marked `DSP` with the Texas Instruments logo (`TMX320VC5502GZZ200` fixed-point digital signal processor).
    - **RF Shield Cans**: To the right of the DSP are three rectangular perforated silver metal RF shielding cans covering the VHF receiver/transmitter stages.
    - **Supporting Circuitry**: Various smaller black ICs, crystal oscillators, inductors, and passive surface-mount components populate the rest of the green board.
