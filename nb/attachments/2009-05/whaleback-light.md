# Passing Whaleback Lighthouse Aboard R/V Cocheco (`whaleback-light.jpg`)

## Overview

An outdoor maritime photograph taken from the stern deck of the University of New Hampshire research vessel **R/V *Cocheco*** while transiting near Portsmouth Harbor, New Hampshire, and the Isles of Shoals on May 22, 2009. The image shows Captain Ben Smith looking out across the water toward **Whaleback Lighthouse** (`Whaleback Light`).

## Scene Description

- **Foreground (Left)**: Seen from behind, Captain Ben Smith stands near the white cabin bulkhead on the left side of the frame. He is wearing a light khaki baseball cap, a dark navy blue long-sleeved shirt featuring the circular logo of the **Center for Coastal & Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC)** at the **University of New Hampshire**, and a dark blue/black Mustang Survival inflatable life vest (PFD) with a black webbing strap running vertically down his back.
- **Foreground (Bottom Right)**: White frothy wake churns at the stern of the vessel, with a metal chain link line extending diagonally from the lower left toward the stern corner at the bottom right.
- **Midground & Background**: Deep blue-gray ocean water with gentle surface ripples stretches to a low, tree-lined coastal horizon (the Maine/New Hampshire coast at the mouth of the Piscataqua River). Rising from a rocky ledge just left of center is **Whaleback Lighthouse**, a conical stone lighthouse tower with a darker upper lantern room. Tiny white specks of distant boats are faintly visible near the shoreline to the right of the lighthouse under a clear, bright blue sky.

## Visible Text & Logos

- **Shirt Logo** (circular emblem on the back of the navy shirt, partially covered by the vertical PFD strap):
  - Outer ring text: `Center for Coastal & Ocean Mapping • Joint Hydrographic Center` (top/sides) and `University of New Hampshire` (bottom).
  - Center graphic: Blue shaded bathymetric map of the New Hampshire coastline with white text `CCOM / JHC`.
