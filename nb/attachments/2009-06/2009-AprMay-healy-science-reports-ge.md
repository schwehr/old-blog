# USCGC Healy Hourly Science Reports in Google Earth (`2009-AprMay-healy-science-reports-ge.png`)

## Overview

A **Google Earth** satellite map visualization showing hourly underway science and position reports from the U.S. Coast Guard icebreaker **USCGC *Healy* (WAGB-20)** between April 28, 2009, and May 28, 2009, as the vessel transited from Seattle, Washington, through the Inside Passage and Gulf of Alaska to Dutch Harbor in the Aleutian Islands and up into the Bering Sea.

## Map & Track Details

- **Geographic Coverage**: The satellite and bathymetric view spans the northeastern Pacific Ocean, including Vancouver Island and British Columbia on the right (east), Southeast Alaska and the Gulf of Alaska across the top center, the Alaska Peninsula and Aleutian Islands chain extending toward the bottom left (southwest), and the eastern Bering Sea shelf and Bristol Bay in the upper left. Yellow coastal/political boundary lines outline the mainland and islands.

- **Google Earth Time Slider** (semi-transparent overlay panel in the top-left corner):
  - Displays the selected time range spanning from `Apr 28, 2009` (left slider thumb) to `May 28, 2009` (right slider thumb), with the current readout `May 28, 2009 12 am` above the right end of the bar.
