# Google Earth Map Overview of NASA K10 Rover Operations (`ARC-K10-MapOverview.jpg`)

## Overview

A **Google Earth** overhead satellite map screenshot showing planned and executed rover traverses, science stations, and waypoints for the **NASA Ames K10 Black** planetary rover during the June 2009 field test at Black Point Lava Flow, Arizona.

## Map & Waypoint Details

- **Basemap**: High-resolution grayscale aerial/satellite imagery of rocky volcanic terrain at Black Point Lava Flow, with dark basaltic outcrops and lighter sandy/gravelly patches. A vertical Google Earth zoom slider is faintly visible along the right edge.
- **Planned & Executed Tracks**:
  - **Red Line**: Planned rover traverse connecting sequential stations (`Stn`) and intermediate waypoints (`Via`).
  - **Magenta Line**: Secondary/alternate planned path extending diagonally toward the upper left.
  - **Black Line**: Actual recorded rover telemetry track (`K10 Black`) winding across the central and lower-right portions of the map.
- **Labeled Stations, Waypoints & Icons**:
  - **Right Side**:
    - `Stn 0 WAI`: Located at the far right with a cyan directional wedge and star icon.
    - `K10 Landed Here` and `Stn 3 MIC,PML`: Yellow square station markers along the red path heading west-northwest.
    - `Initial Panorama`, `Expected Day 1 Start`, and `WTM 1`: Clustered near a green bullseye target icon and a red pushpin.
  - **Top Right**:
    - `Stn 9 MIC`: Yellow square marker with a hexagonal nut icon at the northern apex of a triangle in the traverse.
  - **Center**:
    - `Stn 10 MIC,PML,LSC`: Near a cyan directional cone and hexagonal icon.
    - `Stn 0 PML,LSC` / `Stn 0 PML,LSC Success` / `WTM 2`: Marked by an orange bullseye circle and red-bordered hexagonal icon near the center.
  - **Left / Northwest**:
    - `Via 5` and `Via 6`: Small diamond waypoint markers where the red traverse turns northwest and then northeast.
    - `Stn 7 MIC`: Hexagonal marker at the corner of the northwest leg.
    - `Stn 8 PML`, `Stn 12 PML`, and `Stn 13 PML`: White directional arrow icons pointing northwest along the upper-left traverse segment.
  - **Bottom Center**:
    - `K10 Black`: Rover icon with four wheels and a yellow status bar, next to a red pushpin labeled `LER Route Finding` (referring to the Lunar Electric Rover).
