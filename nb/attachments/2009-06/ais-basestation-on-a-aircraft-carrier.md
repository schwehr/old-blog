# AIS Basestation Located on Museum Aircraft Carrier USS Yorktown (`ais-basestation-on-a-aircraft-carrier.png`)

## Overview

A **Google Earth** satellite view showing the average position of U.S. Coast Guard Automatic Identification System (AIS) Base Station **`3669793`**, which is mounted atop the island superstructure of the museum aircraft carrier **USS *Yorktown* (CV-10)** at Patriots Point Naval & Maritime Museum in Mount Pleasant (Charleston Harbor), South Carolina.

## Map & Scene Details

- **Aircraft Carrier & Museum Ships (Center-Left)**:
  - Moored along a long pier extending south from the marshy shoreline is the World War II Essex-class aircraft carrier **USS *Yorktown***, oriented roughly east-west with its bow pointing west into Charleston Harbor and static display aircraft visible on its flight deck.
  - A yellow Google Earth pushpin labeled **`3669793`** (the MMSI identifier of the USCG AIS base station) is pinned directly on the carrier's island superstructure.
  - Just east of the carrier along the connecting pier are two smaller naval museum vessels moored side by side: the destroyer **USS *Laffey* (DD-724)** and the submarine **USS *Clamagore* (SS-343)**.
- **Marina & Shoreline (Center-Right to Top)**:
  - Immediately east of the museum ships is a large enclosed yacht marina with five parallel floating dock piers filled with dozens of white recreational boats and yachts, protected by a dogleg breakwater at the south and west edges.
  - North of the piers lies brown salt marsh and tidal mudflats, a paved parking lot, and a large red-roofed resort hotel building with a swimming pool in the upper right corner.
  - A vertical Google Earth zoom slider is partially visible at the top right edge.
