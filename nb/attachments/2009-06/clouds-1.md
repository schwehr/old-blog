# Sunset Clouds After Takeoff from BWI Airport (`clouds-1.jpg`)

## Overview

An aerial photograph taken from an airplane window shortly after takeoff from Baltimore/Washington International Thurgood Marshall Airport (**BWI**) en route to Louisville, Kentucky, in late June 2009, capturing dramatic golden-orange sunlight illuminating multi-layered cloud formations.

## Scene Description

- **Sun & Upper Sky**: Near the upper right-center, the bright white disk of the low sun shines through wispy, striated high-altitude cirrus and altostratus clouds, casting warm amber, gold, and bronze hues across the middle of the frame.
- **Cumulus Tower (Right)**: To the lower right of the sun, a dark, billowy cumulus congestus cloud tower rises in silhouette against the bright solar glare, with golden rim lighting and subtle rainbow lens flare artifacts along its upper-right edge.
- **Lower Cloud Deck (Bottom)**: Below the aircraft, a textured sea of dark gray-green and golden-lit stratocumulus clouds stretches across the bottom of the frame, with soft, misty cloud layers in the middle distance catching the warm sunset light.
