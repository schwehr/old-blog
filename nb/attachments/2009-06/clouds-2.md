# Clouds Just After Takeoff from BWI (`clouds-2.jpg`)

## Overview

A wide-format aerial photograph taken from an airplane window shortly after takeoff from Baltimore/Washington International Thurgood Marshall Airport (BWI), looking out across a golden cloud deck at sunrise or sunset with a distant anvil-shaped cumulonimbus cloud on the horizon.

## Scene Description

- **Sun & Lens Flare (Left):** Bright sunlight enters from the upper left edge of the frame, creating a brilliant white-gold glare and diagonal rays of multicolored lens flare (orange, yellow, green, and cyan spots) streaking toward the lower center.
- **Foreground Cloud Deck & Shadow:** Beneath the aircraft lies an expansive, softly textured layer of clouds bathed in warm amber and orange light. A large, dark, triangular cloud shadow stretches diagonally from a billowy cloud peak on the middle-left across the lower right portion of the frame.
- **Horizon & Anvil Cloud (Right):** Along the distant horizon, the sky transitions from deep golden-orange to a rich twilight blue at the top right. Rising above the horizon on the right side is a distinct, isolated cumulonimbus incus (anvil cloud) with a vertical tower and a flat, spreading top. Smaller turret-like cloud tops poke above the haze to the left of the anvil cloud.
