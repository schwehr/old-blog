# Adjusting Buoyancy on the Gavia AUV at Mendum's Pond (`gavia-balancing.jpg`)

## Overview

A daytime field photograph taken at the floating pier on Mendum's Pond in New Hampshire, showing seven members of the CSHEL (University of Delaware) and CCOM (University of New Hampshire) field team gathered on a modular floating dock as they adjust and check the buoyancy trim of the Gavia Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) in the water.

## Scene Description

- **Team Activity Around the AUV (Lower Left / Center):**
  - Several researchers are kneeling, crouching, or lying prone on the gray modular plastic floating dock, leaning over the edge where the yellow/gold nose and communications mast of the Gavia AUV break the water's surface.
  - One person in a dark blue shirt is lying flat on the dock reaching directly into the water to hold the AUV.
  - Above him, Art Trembanis (wearing a green and black fleece jacket and olive cap) bends forward with his hands braced on the dock, watching the trim adjustment.
  - Next to Art, Val Schmidt (in a black fleece jacket) kneels and looks down at the vehicle, while another person in a light blue and white jacket crouches beside them at the water's edge.
- **Standing Team Members:**
  - **Back Left:** A young man wearing sunglasses, khaki cargo shorts, and a light gray hooded sweatshirt with `COLBY` printed across the chest stands looking down at the AUV.
  - **Back Center-Right:** Alex Forrest (wearing a tan cap, black jacket, and dark jeans) stands in profile looking left toward the water.
  - **Right of Center:** An older man with gray hair, wearing a white long-sleeved shirt over a pink collar and blue jeans, stands with his back to the camera and his right hand on his hip.
  - **Far Right:** A woman with red hair pulled back in a bun, wearing a black jacket and tan cargo pants, stands looking down at a small handheld device or notebook.
- **Boats, Dock & Background:**
  - The bow of a white fiberglass skiff floats in the water on the far left; the bow of another small boat with an outboard motor, coiled mooring line, and an orange hull/float is in the right foreground; overturned white boat hulls rest on the dock behind the group, along with a red and black gear bag on the right.
  - In the background, calm dark green pond water reflects the dense green foliage of the summer forest lining the opposite shore.
