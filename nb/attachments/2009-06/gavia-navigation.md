# Gavia AUV Navigation Track in Google Earth (`gavia-navigation.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot from Google Earth showing a 3D perspective view of a Gavia Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) navigation track plotted from a CSV log (`navigator072508_Excelwork.csv`) recorded on July 25, 2008, with the Google Earth time slider control active in the upper left corner.

## Interface & Time Slider Controls

- **Time Slider Widget (Upper Left):**
  - A semi-transparent dark gray control panel spans the upper left corner of the viewport.
  - **Top Row Buttons:** Step backward (`|◀`), Play/Loop animation toggle (blue circular arrow icon, active), Step forward (`▶|`), Zoom out (`-` magnifying glass), Zoom in (`+` magnifying glass), and Options (wrench icon on the far right).
  - **Timestamp Display:** Reads `Jul 25, 2008  6:03:08 pm`.
  - **Timeline Bar:** A horizontal range slider with a blue bar spanning from `1 pm` on the left to `6 pm` on the right, with white range markers positioned at both ends.

## Track Visualization

- **3D Perspective:** The camera looks out at a low oblique angle across a solid blue water surface toward a thin brown coastline along the distant horizon under a pale blue sky.
- **Survey Lines:** White 3D polylines trace the path of the Gavia AUV:
  - Across the middle ground, a dense set of parallel east–west tracklines forms a "mowing the lawn" survey grid, with rounded teardrop turns at the right end.
  - In the foreground and right-center, a looping, meandering white trackline shows the vehicle maneuvering prior to or after the main survey grid, with a single line extending north toward the distant shoreline near the center horizon.
