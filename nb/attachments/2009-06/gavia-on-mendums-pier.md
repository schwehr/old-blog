# Val Schmidt, Art Trembanis, and Alex Forrest with the Gavia AUV on Mendum's Pier (`gavia-on-mendums-pier.jpg`)

## Overview

A sunny outdoor group portrait taken on the modular floating pier at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, showing researchers Val Schmidt, Art Trembanis, and Alex Forrest kneeling beside the Gavia Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) prior to launch.

## People & Equipment

- **Left — Val Schmidt (CCOM/UNH):** Kneeling on one knee, smiling at the camera, wearing a black The North Face fleece jacket, tan work pants, and hiking boots.
- **Center — Art Trembanis (CSHEL/University of Delaware):** Kneeling behind the middle section of the AUV with his hands resting on the vehicle's hull and tail section, smiling, wearing a light-colored baseball cap with sunglasses perched on the brim, eyeglasses, a dark green and black fleece pullover over a red collared shirt, and blue jeans.
- **Right — Alex Forrest (UBC):** Kneeling on one knee to the right of the AUV, smiling, wearing a tan cap backward with sunglasses on top, a black zip-up fleece jacket, dark gray jeans, and gray trail shoes.
- **Gavia AUV (Center Foreground):**
  - The cylindrical, torpedo-shaped Gavia AUV rests on a black metal deck cradle/frame in the center of the dock, angled diagonally toward the lower right.
  - It features a metallic gold-anodized hull with a black hemispherical nose cone housing a forward camera/sonar window, black cable/clamp bands, a top carrying handle near the nose, and an orange-and-white communications/GPS mast and strobe tower rising near the aft section.
- **Dock & Background:**
  - The team kneels on a gray interlocking plastic cube floating dock (`Jet Dock` style). A red and black tool/gear bag sits in the lower right corner.
  - Behind the dock, the calm dark water of Mendum's Pond stretches to a dense shoreline of tall pine and deciduous trees in bright early-summer foliage.
