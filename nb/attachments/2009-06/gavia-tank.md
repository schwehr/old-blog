# Gavia AUV Viewed Through the Chase Tank Window (`gavia-tank.jpg`)

## Overview

A photograph looking through a circular underwater viewing porthole in the side of the acoustic/engineering test tank at the Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory (University of New Hampshire), showing the Gavia Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) floating just below the water's surface along with its inverted total internal reflection against the underside of the surface.

## Scene Description

- **Viewing Porthole Frame:** A thick, dark-rimmed circular window set into a tan/beige concrete tank wall frames the underwater scene, slightly cropped at the top and bottom edges.
- **Gavia AUV & Surface Reflection:**
  - In the center of the blue water, the cylindrical gold/bronze hull of the Gavia AUV is suspended horizontally just beneath the surface, facing left (tail and black propeller shroud on the right, dark transducer/sensor windows along the bottom and side, and green caution/identification stickers on the aft section).
  - Directly above the vehicle, the flat water-air interface acts as a mirror, producing a sharp, slightly angled inverted reflection of the AUV hull and itsvertical communications tower touching the real tower at the center-right.
- **Tank Interior:** Behind the vehicle, the underwater wall of the test tank is lined with rectangular blue tiles arranged in a grid pattern.
