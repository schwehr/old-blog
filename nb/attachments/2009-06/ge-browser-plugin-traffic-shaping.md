# Google Earth Browser Plugin Showing Vessel Traffic Design in Portland, Oregon (`ge-browser-plugin-traffic-shaping.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of an embedded Google Earth Browser Plugin tour (created by SmarterBridge.org / `bikefuel.com` for the Columbia River Crossing project) showing 3D bridge models and color-coded 3D vessel navigation corridors passing through two bridges across the Columbia River between Portland, Oregon, and Vancouver, Washington.

## 3D Scene & Vessel Traffic Paths

- **Bridges:**
  - **Foreground:** A 3D gray truss model of the Interstate Bridge (I-5) spanning the green waters of the Columbia River, featuring a raised vertical lift span near the left-center.
  - **Background:** Further upriver (to the upper right) is a second multi-span truss bridge (the Burlington Northern Santa Fe railway bridge) rendered in greenish-gray.
- **Vessel Traffic Corridors:**
  - Three colored 3D ribbon paths represent ship navigation trajectories passing through the two bridges:
    - **Bright Green Path:** Runs straight along the eastern side of the river channel, passing directly beneath the raised lift span of the foreground bridge and continuing straight through the eastern span of the background bridge.
    - **Red/Brown Translucent Path:** Enters through the western/left span of the foreground bridge and curves S-shaped across the river to align with the eastern span of the background bridge.
    - **Blue Translucent Path:** Follows the western bank of the river through the left side of both bridges.
- **Surrounding Terrain:** Oblique satellite imagery draped over 3D terrain shows industrial and urban areas along the left (west) riverbank and greener floodplains/islands on the right.

## Plugin Interface & Labels

- **Tour Playback Controller (Lower Left):** A dark translucent media control bar with Rewind (`<<`), Pause (`||` / Play `>`), and Fast-Forward (`>>`) circular buttons, a horizontal progress slider positioned near the middle at `01:22`, a loop/repeat button, and a close (`x`) button.
- **Navigation Controls (Right):** Semi-transparent Google Earth 3D look/compass ring (with `N` rotated to the left, indicating an eastward/southeastward view), pan circle, and vertical zoom slider.
