# Global Air Traffic Over 24 Hours (`global-air-traffic.png`)

## Overview

A still frame from an animated 24-hour global air traffic visualization (produced by ZHAW / Zürcher Hochschule für Angewandte Wissenschaften, FlightStat, and Technorama), depicting worldwide commercial flight activity as bright yellow dots overlaid on a satellite world map with a day/night terminator shadow.

## Map & Visualization Details

- **Base Map & Day/Night Shading:**
  - An equirectangular world map projection showing continents in natural satellite colors (tan deserts in North Africa, the Middle East, and Australia; green vegetation; white snow and ice across Greenland, northern Eurasia, northern North America, and Antarctica).
  - A curved day/night illumination pattern darkens the Atlantic Ocean, the Americas, and the Pacific Ocean while daylight illuminates Europe, Africa, Asia, and Australia.
- **Air Traffic Distribution (Yellow Dots):**
  - Thousands of bright yellow points represent individual aircraft in flight:
    - **North America:** Extremely dense, solid yellow coverage across the eastern and western United States and southern Canada, with distinct flight corridors streaming across the North Atlantic toward Europe and across the Pacific toward Hawaii and East Asia.
    - **Europe & Middle East:** High concentration of yellow flight tracks across Western and Central Europe, extending southeastward across the Mediterranean and Persian Gulf.
    - **East & Southeast Asia:** Dense clusters over Japan, eastern China, South Korea, Taiwan, and Southeast Asia, with routes connecting to Australia and New Zealand.
    - **Southern Hemisphere:** Lighter clusters along the eastern coast of South America, southern Africa, and southeastern Australia.
- **Credits Box (Lower Right):**
  - A white-and-gray rectangular overlay in the bottom right corner contains partner logos, including `FLIGHTSTAT`, `zh aw` (in blue), and `TECHNORAMA`.
