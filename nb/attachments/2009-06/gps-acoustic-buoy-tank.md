# GPS-Acoustic Buoy Testing in the Chase Engineering Tank (`gps-acoustic-buoy-tank.jpg`)

## Overview

A vertical close-up photograph of a CCOM/UNH GPS-Acoustic surface buoy floating in the clear blue water of the indoor test tank at the Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory during pre-deployment testing for the Mendum's Pond field exercises.

## Equipment & Scene Details

- **Flotation Collar:** A thick, bright yellow cylindrical foam float sits at the waterline, with a white mooring line tied to a metal bracket on its left side and trailing off toward the edge of the pool. Faint handwritten markings are visible on the top surface of the yellow foam.
- **Upper Instrument Housing & Mast:**
  - Rising from the center of the yellow foam collar is a cylindrical machined aluminum pressure housing bolted to a square stainless-steel mounting plate with side handles.
  - Above the top flange of the cylinder, an aluminum crossbar supports a white, saucer-shaped GPS antenna dome in the center, flanked by two vertical white/metal masts wrapped with black electrical tape and black coaxial cables routing into waterproof bulkhead connectors on the top endcap.
- **Submerged Lower Assembly:**
  - Extending vertically downward into the clear blue tank water below the yellow float is a long silver aluminum cylinder housing batteries and electronics.
  - At the bottom end of the submerged tube is a protective metal cage/mount holding an acoustic transducer/modem.
- **Tank Environment:** The concrete tank ledge is visible in the upper left corner, and overhead ceiling lights and roof trusses reflect off the calm blue water surface.
