# Apple iPhone 3GS Built-in Compass and Maps Orientation (`iphone-compass-magnetometer.png`)

## Overview

A promotional side-by-side product image showing two Apple iPhone 3GS handsets displaying the new built-in magnetometer features: the native **Compass** application on the left and the **Maps** application oriented to the user's heading on the right.

## Left iPhone: Compass Application

- **Status Bar (Top):** Shows full signal bars, `3G`, `9:42 AM`, Bluetooth icon, and full battery icon.
- **Heading Readout:** Below a small brown triangular lubber-line pointer at the top center, large white sans-serif text reads `315° NW`.
- **Compass Dial:** A circular brass-rimmed compass rose rests against a dark wood-grain background. The white dial is graduated from `0` to `350` degrees in 10-degree increments with 1-degree tick marks, cardinal and intercardinal points (`N`, `NE`, `E`, `SE`, `S`, `SW`, `W`, `NW`), and a red triangle with a white `N` marking North at roughly the 1:30 position.
- **Bottom Bar:** Contains a location/crosshair icon button on the left, geographic coordinates `37°19'55"N, 122°1'46"W` (Infinite Loop, Cupertino, CA) in the center, and an information (`i`) button on the right.

## Right iPhone: Maps Application with Heading Cone

- **Status Bar & Search Bar:** Shows `3G`, `9:42 AM`, Bluetooth, and green battery icon at the top, followed by a search input field with placeholder text `Search or Address` and a blue contacts book icon on the right.
- **Map View (Manhattan, New York City):**
  - Displays a street map of the Upper East Side and Central Park (rotated so northwest is toward the top of the screen, matching the compass heading).
  - Visible labels include `Central Park`, `Turtle Pond`, `East Dr`, `5th Ave`, `Madison Ave`, `Park Ave` (highlighted in yellow), `Lexington Ave`, `3rd Ave`, and numbered cross streets from `E 75th St` through `E 89th St`, along with blue one-way street arrows and a blue `M` subway icon at 86th St.
  - At the intersection of `5th Ave` and `E 83rd St` (near the Metropolitan Museum of Art), a glowing blue location dot emits a translucent white directional cone pointing toward the upper left (northwest).
- **Bottom Toolbar:** Contains the active blue location/compass-mode button on the left, a segmented toggle with `Search` (selected) and `Directions` in the center, and a page-curl map options button on the right.
