# Controlling an Iver2 EcoMapper AUV via an iPhone (`iphone-controls-auv.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up photograph (courtesy of Art Trembanis / CSHEL) showing a user's left hand holding an Apple iPhone in landscape orientation displaying the OceanServer Technology Iver2 Underwater Vehicle Console interface via VNC/remote desktop, with a yellow YSI EcoMapper (Iver2) Autonomous Underwater Vehicle resting on the pavement in the background.

## iPhone Screen Interface

- **Window Title Bar:** A dark blue Windows title bar reads `Iver2 - UVC Underwater Vehicle Console: IslandLake_Fast`.
- **Header & Chart Banner:**
  - On the left is the **OceanServer TECHNOLOGY, INC.** logo with a stylized wave/dolphin graphic.
  - On the right is a nautical chart banner showing depth soundings (e.g., `12`, `14`, `10`, `17`, `15`) and a rocky shoal labeled `Seal Rocks`.
  - Below the banner is a row of toolbar icons (including a right arrow, globe, `M`, calipers/Go-To icon, grid, wrench/settings, and window controls).
- **Mission Information Panel:**
  - **Mission Name:** `IslandLake_Fast`
  - **Left Column:**
    - `Number of WPs:` `136`
    - `Avg. Speed:` `2.5 (2.5) Kn`
    - `SideScan:` `No (No)`
    - `Deploy Tow Float at Park location:` `No (No)`
    - `Calculated Magnetic Variation:` `17° 33' 59"E`
  - **Middle Column:**
    - `Distance:` `686.06 (3,328) Nm`
    - `Avg. Depth:` `0 (0) ft`
    - `Video:` `No (No)`
    - `SRP Mission Loaded:` `No`
  - **Right Column:**
    - `*Mission Time:` `Run: 00:10:02 (00:00:00)` / `Park: 00:05:00 (00:05:00)`
    - `*Mission Area:` `Lat: 57.81031/57.81409 (57.81014/57.81420)` / `Lng: -152.37026/-152.36248 (-152.37005/...)` / `Size: 0.004 x 0.114 NM (0.004 x 0.116)`
- **Bottom Action Buttons:** Rectangular GUI buttons along the bottom include `Show Mission`, a highlighted/boxed `SIMULATE` button, `Instruments`, and `RUN AUV` (in a blue box).

## Background AUV (`ECOMAPPER`)

- Behind the iPhone, a bright yellow torpedo-shaped AUV sits diagonally on wet asphalt.
- Black lettering along the forward hull reads **`ECOMAPPER`**.
- Visible hardware includes an orange-ringed tail propeller shroud at the upper left, a vertical black sensor/antenna mast on top of the forward hull, and a black carrying handle behind the `ECOMAPPER` logo.
