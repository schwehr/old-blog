# NASA JPL Planetary Photojournal RSS Feeds Webpage (`jpl-photojournal-rss.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) Planetary Photojournal website (`http://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/rss/index.html`) showing the newly added directory of RSS feeds organized by solar system target body.

## Header & Banners

- **Top NASA / JPL Header Bar:**
  - Set against a dark blue space background, the circular blue-and-red `NASA` meatball logo sits at the upper left beside white text reading **Jet Propulsion Laboratory** / **California Institute of Technology**.
  - **Upper Right Navigation Links:**
    - Row 1: `JPL HOME`, `EARTH`, `SOLAR SYSTEM`, `STARS & GALAXIES`, `SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY`
    - Row 2: **`BRING THE UNIVERSE TO YOU:`** `JPL Email News` | `RSS` | `Podcast` | `Video`
    - Row 3: A text search input box next to a `search JPL` button.
- **Photojournal Banner:**
  - A wide reddish-orange horizontal banner features a glowing solar/planetary limb graphic on the far left, the title **`PHOTOJOURNAL`** in white serif capitals, and a dropdown trigger on the right reading **`Select An Image Gallery ▸`**.
