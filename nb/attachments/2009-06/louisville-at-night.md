# Bridges Over the Ohio River at Night in Louisville, Kentucky (`louisville-at-night.jpg`)

## Overview

A wide-format nighttime photograph looking across the Ohio River in Louisville, Kentucky, showing the illuminated George Rogers Clark Memorial Bridge (Second Street Bridge) stretching across the water and casting long golden reflections onto the river surface.

## Scene Description

- **Bridge Structure:**
  - A multi-span cantilevered steel truss bridge extends diagonally from the lower right foreground toward the center-left distance.
  - Warm yellow-orange roadway sodium-vapor lamps illuminate the truss framework from within, while occasional purple/blue accent lights glint near the central piers and upper chords.
- **River & Reflections:**
  - Beneath the bridge, the dark surface of the Ohio River is streaked with vertical ribbons of golden-orange, white, red, and blue light reflected from the bridge lights and the distant shoreline.
- **Shoreline & Background:**
  - Along the far bank (Jeffersonville/Clarksville, Indiana, on the left and center-left), a dense cluster of bright white, yellow, and neon city lights glows against the pitch-black night sky, including a brightly lit multi-story rectangular building near the left end of the bridge.
  - Further upriver in the center-right distance, another illuminated bridge and scattered shoreline lights are faintly visible.
  - In the lower right corner, the edge of a multi-story building with horizontal balconies on the Louisville waterfront frames the foreground.
