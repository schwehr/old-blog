# U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Aerial Photograph of McAlpine Locks and Dam (`louisville-lock-acoe-200804.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) looking eastward (upstream) over the McAlpine Locks and Dam complex, Shippingport Island, and the Falls of the Ohio on the Ohio River at Louisville, Kentucky.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Bridges**: Across the bottom of the frame, a dark steel multi-span truss bridge (the K&I Bridge / Kentucky & Indiana Terminal Bridge) crosses the downstream river channel. On the right, an elevated four-lane highway (I-64) curves alongside a tree-lined residential neighborhood and park area on the Kentucky shore.
- **McAlpine Locks Complex**: In the lower-center and middle ground, the Louisville and Portland Canal splits to pass through the parallel lock chambers of the McAlpine Locks. Two side-by-side lock chambers (including the new 1,200-foot lock under construction/completion alongside the existing 1,200-foot chamber) are spanned by a highway/service bridge, with long concrete guide walls extending both upstream and downstream.
- **Shippingport Island & Hydroelectric Plant**: To the left (north) of the lock canal lies heavily wooded Shippingport Island, bordered on its upstream end by the Louisville Gas & Electric (LG&E) hydroelectric power station and dam structure, where white water spills into the rocky lower river channel around the Falls of the Ohio.
- **Upstream Background**: Beyond the canal and dam, the wide upstream pool of the Ohio River curves into the hazy distance toward downtown Louisville, crossed by the Fourteenth Street railroad bridge and additional highway bridges farther upstream.
