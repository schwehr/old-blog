# Towboat and Barges Approaching the Louisville Locks past the Belle of Louisville (`louisville-lock-approach.jpg`)

## Overview
An elevated daytime photograph taken from a high-rise building overlooking the downtown Louisville waterfront and the Ohio River, capturing a large barge tow passing under the downtown bridges as it approaches the entrance to the McAlpine Locks canal.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Waterfront & Highway**: Along the lower right, the elevated multi-lane roadway of Interstate 64 runs parallel to the riverfront, with a white pickup truck traveling along the highway. A tall beige concrete building facade frames the right edge of the shot.
- **Moored Riverboats**: Moored along the paved wharf between the highway and the river in the lower left is the historic sternwheel steamboat *Belle of Louisville*, featuring a white multi-tiered superstructure, red paddlewheel at the stern, twin black smokestacks, a blue-roofed pilothouse, and `"BELLE OF LOUISVILLE"` lettered along the lower hull. Immediately ahead (upstream) of the steamboat is a smaller two-story white floating wharf boat/barge topped with a small cupola.
- **Barge Tow in the Channel**: Out in the river channel, a white commercial towboat is pushing a long, multi-barge tow of dark hopper barges downstream (from upper right toward lower left), leaving a v-shaped wake behind the tug.
- **Bridges in Background**: Directly behind the towboat, the multi-span steel cantilever truss of the George Rogers Clark Memorial Bridge (Second Street Bridge) crosses the river on tall concrete piers. Farther upstream in the upper right, the green-painted multi-arch steel trusses of the Big Four Bridge and John F. Kennedy Memorial Bridge are visible against the distant Indiana shoreline.
