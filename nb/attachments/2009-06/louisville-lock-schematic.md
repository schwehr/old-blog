# Schematic Plan View of the McAlpine Locks Approach Channel (`louisville-lock-schematic.png`)

## Overview
A CAD-style engineering schematic and bathymetric/topographic contour plan map showing the upstream approach channel, guide walls, and monitoring stations for the McAlpine Locks and Dam (Louisville and Portland Canal) on the Ohio River.

## Map & Diagram Details
- **Boundaries & Layout**: A thick dashed black polyline outlines the outer perimeter of the study area, labeled `"MODEL LIMITS"` in the lower-left portion of the drawing.
- **Canal & Island Geometry**:
  - The Louisville and Portland Canal runs diagonally from the upstream entrance on the right toward the lock chambers at the lower left, where two parallel lock approaches are drawn with angled guide walls.
  - Separating the canal from the main river channel to the north (top) is the elongated peninsula/island (Shippingport Island), outlined in red, blue, and green contour lines.
  - At the upper left, a rectangular dam/hydroelectric structure extends northward from the western tip of the island.
  - Vertically crossing the eastern portion of the canal and island is a double line labeled `"CONRAIL RAILROAD BRIDGE"` (the Fourteenth Street Bridge), with a long slotted pier/guide structure just upstream (to the right) of the island tip.
- **Contours & Elevations**: Green contour lines trace river bottom and bank elevations with numerical labels including `420`, `425`, `430`, `440`, and `450`. Red and blue lines mark shoreline/waterline boundaries or design wall alignments along the canal and island perimeter.
- **Monitoring Stations & Coordinates**:
  - Five circular target symbols mark measurement/ADCP stations along the canal and river approach: `"STATION 1"` (near the lower-left lock entrance), `"STATION 2"` (mid-canal), `"STATION 3"` (southern canal bank just west of the Conrail Railroad Bridge, surrounded by small plus-sign markers), `"STATION 4"` (north of the island along the Conrail Railroad Bridge), and `"STATION 5"` (at the far right upstream entrance).
  - Angled state-plane coordinate ticks appear around the perimeter: `"E 1,561,000 / N 285,000"` (top left), `"E 1,565,000 / N 283,000"` (top right), `"N 283,000 / E 1,560,000"` (bottom left), and `"N 281,000 / E 1,563,000"` (bottom right). A small red circle is drawn near the top center-left margin.
