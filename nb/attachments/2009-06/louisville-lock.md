# Towboat and Barge Tow Entering the McAlpine Locks Channel (`louisville-lock.jpg`)

## Overview
An elevated daytime photograph taken from a high vantage point overlooking the Ohio River in Louisville, Kentucky, showing a commercial towboat pushing a long string of loaded barges into the approach channel for the McAlpine Locks and Dam.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**: In the lower-left corner, a multi-lane elevated highway (Interstate 64) runs diagonally along the Kentucky riverbank, with several passenger cars and a red pickup truck traveling across the concrete roadway under tall streetlamps.
- **Vessel & Tow**: Stretching diagonally across the calm, gray-brown waters of the Ohio River from the lower right toward the middle left is a long commercial barge tow. A white multi-deck river towboat at the stern (lower right) pushes a 3-wide by 4-long (or 5-long) configuration of dark, low-profile hopper barges moving upstream/into the canal approach.
- **Midground & Locks Channel**: Just ahead of the lead barges on the left, a vegetated point of land (Shippingport Island) with a small navigation marker/light at its tip separates the main river and the Fourteenth Street Bridge from the entrance to the Louisville and Portland Canal leading to the McAlpine Locks.
- **Background**: Spanning the wide river across the upper-middle right is the Fourteenth Street Bridge—a multi-span railroad truss bridge with a central steel through-truss section and vertical lift/girder spans supported on concrete piers. Farther left in the background, the lift towers and spans of the lock/canal bridges and the distant twin arches of the Sherman Minton Bridge are visible against a hazy blue sky and tree-lined Indiana hills.
