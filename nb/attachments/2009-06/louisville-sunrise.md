# Sunrise Over the Ohio River and Bridges in Louisville (`louisville-sunrise.jpg`)

## Overview
A warm, golden-hour photograph taken at sunrise from an elevated hotel or office window in downtown Louisville, Kentucky, looking east-northeast across the Ohio River and its historic truss bridges.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Sun**: The rising sun shines brightly in the upper center of the frame through thin, glowing layers of morning clouds, bathing the entire sky and river surface in deep amber, gold, and orange tones.
- **Bridges & River**: A shimmering golden reflection path stretches across the calm waters of the Ohio River directly beneath the sun. Spanning the river in the midground is the silhouetted steel cantilever truss of the George Rogers Clark Memorial Bridge, while the multi-arch steel trusses of the Big Four Bridge and John F. Kennedy Memorial Bridge appear farther upstream in the background.
- **Foreground Buildings & Waterfront**: On the right side of the frame, a multi-story concrete building (parking garage or apartment/office tower with horizontal balconies and dark windows) rises in shadow. Along the lower-left riverfront below the elevated lanes of Interstate 64, the white two-story floating wharf building with its rooftop cupola is moored at the bank, and a white van drives along the highway.
