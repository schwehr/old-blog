# Art Trembanis Inspecting AUV Sonar Data at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-art-data-inspection.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up outdoor photograph from the AUV Boot Camp at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, showing Dr. Art Trembanis (University of Delaware) and colleagues reviewing sidescan sonar imagery on a ruggedized laptop under a lakeside canopy tent.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Researchers**:
  - In the left foreground, Art Trembanis—wearing a tan baseball cap with sunglasses resting on the brim and a dark green and black fleece pullover—types on a ruggedized laptop resting on a wooden picnic table.
  - Standing next to him in the center, a second team member wearing a dark navy long-sleeved shirt, dark athletic shorts with a white drawstring, a wristwatch, and sunglasses pushed up on his head leans forward with his hand on his hip, looking intently at the laptop screen.
  - Behind them in the middle distance, a third researcher in a dark jacket and khaki pants stands near the tree line holding white papers or notes.
- **Laptop & Display**: A silver-and-black Panasonic Toughbook sits inside a padded black sun-shade case on the table next to a clear plastic water bottle and a stack of plastic-wrapped binders. On the screen, a sonar visualization window displays golden-brown sidescan sonar backscatter imagery from the pond floor.
- **Background**: Beyond the blue-and-silver frame of the pop-up canopy tent, an orange survey tripod with a vertical antenna mast stands near the sunlit,rippled surface of Mendum's Pond and its wooded far shore.
