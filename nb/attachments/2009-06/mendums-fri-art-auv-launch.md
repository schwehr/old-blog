# Art Trembanis Launching the Gavia AUV at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-fri-art-auv-launch.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor field photograph taken on the final day (Friday) of the June 2009 AUV Boot Camp at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, showing Dr. Art Trembanis wading knee-deep in the water to launch the Gavia Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV).

## Scene Description
- **AUV & Launch Action**: In the lower foreground, the slender, torpedo-shaped Gavia AUV floats horizontally at the surface of the pond, pointing to the left. Its modular hull features a metallic gold-anodized nose and forward sections, a white aft section, a bright orange dorsal communications/GPS sail tower topped with a clear strobe/antenna dome, and a black ducted tail thruster. White water churns at the stern as the propeller spins up.
- **Operator**: Art Trembanis stands knee-deep in the clear, shallow water on the right, wearing khaki cargo shorts, a dark green long-sleeved shirt under a black vest, glasses, and a tan baseball cap with sunglasses on the brim. He leans forward with his right hand gripping the black tail shroud of the AUV to guide it off into deeper water.
- **Background**: Across the calm, dark-green reflections of Mendum's Pond rises a dense, lush shoreline forest of tall pine and deciduous trees, with a small wooden dock on pilings visible along the opposite bank at upper left-center.
