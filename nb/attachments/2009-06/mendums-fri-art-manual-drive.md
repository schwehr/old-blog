# Manually Driving the Gavia AUV Back to Shore at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-fri-art-manual-drive.jpg`)

## Overview
An over-the-shoulder photograph from the final day of the June 2009 AUV Boot Camp at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, showing Dr. Art Trembanis operating a ruggedized Panasonic Toughbook laptop at the water's edge to manually pilot the Gavia AUV back to shore.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Laptop Control**:
  - Viewed over his left shoulder on the right side of the frame, Art Trembanis (wearing glasses, a tan cap, and a dark fleece) has both hands on the keyboard of a Panasonic Toughbook enclosed in a black fabric glare shield, resting on a lakeside rock.
  - The laptop screen displays the Gavia Control Center interface: a blue bathymetric/map pane of Mendum's Pond on the upper left with red survey tracklines and a small directional control pad overlay in its lower-left corner, status/parameter panels on the right, and a scrolling log pane along the bottom.
- **Pond & Surfacing AUV**: Beyond the laptop, the calm surface of Mendum's Pond reflects the dark green pine forest on the right bank and the pale overcast sky on the left. In the upper-center distance, the orange dorsal tower and yellow-gold hull of the Gavia AUV are visible skimming across the water toward the shore, with a wooden dock structure along the wooded shoreline in the upper right.
