# Recovering or Tending the Gavia AUV from an Inflatable Boat at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-fri-auv-and-buoys.jpg`)

## Overview
A lakeside photograph from the June 2009 AUV Boot Camp at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, showing two researchers in a small gray inflatable dinghy tending the Gavia AUV alongside the boat, with GPS-acoustic buoys visible in the distant background.

## Scene Description
- **Inflatable Boat & Crew**: Centered in the foreground on the rippled gray-blue water is a light-gray inflatable raft accented with a blue-and-black rub rail stripe, equipped with black oars on oarlocks and a white Minn Kota electric trolling motor mounted at the stern (left).
  - Seated mid-ship facing the camera with a smile is a bearded man wearing glasses, a dark jacket, shorts, and an orange life vest, holding the tiller handle of the trolling motor.
  - Kneeling at the bow on the right is a second man wearing glasses, a maroon t-shirt, khaki pants, and an orange life vest, reaching over the port tube into the water to hold the nose of the AUV.
- **Gavia AUV**: Floating parallel to the near side of the inflatable boat in the immediate foreground is the yellow-gold cylindrical hull of the Gavia AUV, with its bright orange dorsal sail and clear upper housing visible near the stern of the dinghy on the left.
- **Background**: In the far upper-left distance across the open water of Mendum's Pond, two small yellow GPS-acoustic surface buoys are visible near the tree-lined shore.
