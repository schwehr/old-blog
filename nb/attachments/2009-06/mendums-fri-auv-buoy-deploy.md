# Deploying GPS-Acoustic Buoys and Preparing the Gavia AUV at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-fri-auv-buoy-deploy.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical-orientation photograph from the June 2009 AUV Boot Camp at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, capturing two simultaneous field activities: a team on a wooden dock in the foreground preparing the Gavia AUV for launch, and a canoe crew out on the lake in the background deploying yellow GPS-acoustic navigation buoys.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Dock & AUV Preparation**: Four team members stand at the end of a weathered wooden pier in the lower right, gathered around the Gavia AUV, which rests in its black modular transport cradle on the dock planks:
  - A woman on the left in a dark blue hoodie and blue jeans looks down at the vehicle.
  - Next to her, a man in a tan fleece jacket and dark trousers lifts his right foot over the AUV cradle.
  - On the right, a man in a dark blue t-shirt with white lettering on the back and tan pants bends down to grip the black tail frame of the AUV, while Art Trembanis (in a green-and-black fleece and tan cap) stands just ahead of him facing the water.
- **Background Canoe & Buoys**: Out on the rippled water in the upper-left midground, two people wearing orange life vests sit in a canoe beside two yellow surface buoys equipped with tall vertical white GPS/radio antenna masts. Behind them, dense green pine and hardwood forest lines the hilly shore of Mendum's Pond.
