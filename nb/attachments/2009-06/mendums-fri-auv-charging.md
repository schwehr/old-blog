# Charging the Gavia AUV with a Portable Generator at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-fri-auv-charging.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up field photograph from the June 2009 AUV Boot Camp at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, showing the Gavia Autonomous Underwater Vehicle resting in its frame on a lakeside granite outcrop while its batteries are recharged via a portable gasoline generator.

## Scene Description
- **Gavia AUV & Cradle**: Stretching diagonally from the left foreground toward the center-right background, the metallic gold-brown cylindrical Gavia AUV sits inside a black tubular protective carrying frame set directly on a sloping gray rock ledge.
  - In the left foreground, the black ducted propeller shroud and four-bladed thruster with control fins are seen in sharp detail.
  - Mid-hull, the bright orange dorsal communications tower topped with its transparent dome rises above the gold pressure hull, which bears a `"GAVIA"` logo decal on its side.
- **Charging Setup & Generator**: A black power/data cable is plugged into a port near the base of the dorsal tower and leads across the rock to a small tan/gold rectangular power supply/charger box with dual digital panel meters on its front face. From the charger box, power cords run to a compact red Honda portable inverter generator sitting on the rock at the upper right.
- **Background**: Lush green pine and deciduous foliage frames the rock outcrop, with a glimpse of the dark water of Mendum's Pond at the far left and a pine cone lying on the rock in the lower right foreground.
