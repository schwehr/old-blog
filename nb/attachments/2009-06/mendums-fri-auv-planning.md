# Planning a Gavia AUV Mission on a Toughbook at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-fri-auv-planning.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up over-the-shoulder photograph from the June 2009 AUV Boot Camp at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, showing Dr. Art Trembanis programming a survey mission in the Gavia Control Center software on a ruggedized Panasonic Toughbook laptop.

## Interface & Scene Description
- **Operator & Hardware**: Viewed from over his right shoulder, Art Trembanis (wearing glasses and a tan cap with sunglasses on the brim) rests his hands on the keyboard of a silver-and-black Panasonic Toughbook (`"TOUGHBOOK"` badge at the top right of the screen bezel) sitting on a white folding table outdoors.
- **Gavia Control Center Software**:
  - **Map Pane (Upper Left)**: Shows a light-blue polygon map of Mendum's Pond against a gray background. Red waypoints and parallel lawnmower-style survey tracklines are plotted in the northern and central parts of the pond, accompanied by red text labels and a small 3x3 navigation/zoom button palette in the lower-left corner of the map.
  - **Mission Tree & Parameter Editor (Upper Right)**: The top-right pane shows a hierarchical tree of mission tasks and waypoints, while the middle-right table displays editable mission parameters (such as latitude, longitude, depth/altitude, speed, and timeout values).
  - **Message Log & Status Bar (Bottom)**: Across the lower portion of the window, a timestamped event log lists vehicle status messages alongside a telemetry readout panel at the lower right showing live coordinates, heading, pitch, roll, depth, and battery metrics.
