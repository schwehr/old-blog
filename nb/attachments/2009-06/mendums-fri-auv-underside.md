# Inspecting the Underside Sensors of the Gavia AUV at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-fri-auv-underside.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph from the June 2009 AUV Boot Camp at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, showing a researcher standing knee-deep in the pond and rolling the Gavia AUV onto its side/back in the water to inspect its ventral (underside) acoustic sensors and transducers.

## Scene Description
- **Operator**: On the right side of the frame, a man wearing glasses, a dark navy short-sleeved polo shirt with white piping and sleeve emblem, and khaki cargo shorts stands knee-deep in the clear, brown-tinted shallow water, bending over with both hands holding the middle section of the AUV.
- **Gavia AUV & Ventral Sensors**: The yellow-gold cylindrical hull of the Gavia AUV floats diagonally from the lower left to the center, rotated upside-down so its belly faces upward:
  - Near the lower-left section of the hull, two elongated black sidescan sonar transducer arrays (Marine Sonic Technology 900/1800 kHz staves) are mounted longitudinally along the port and starboard lower chine.
  - Just forward (up and right) of the sidescan staves are two circular orange-red acoustic transducer faces mounted flush in the gold hull (likely a Doppler Velocity Log / DVL or acoustic modem/altimeter).
  - Where the researcher's hands grip the vehicle, a black cylindrical sensor housing (such as a conductivity-temperature/CTD or optical sensor) and a multi-element transducer array protrude from the hull.
