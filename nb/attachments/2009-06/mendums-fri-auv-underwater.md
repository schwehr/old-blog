# Gavia AUV Diving Underwater at the Start of a Mission in Mendum's Pond (`mendums-fri-auv-underwater.jpg`)

## Overview
A lakeside photograph from the June 2009 AUV Boot Camp at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, capturing the Gavia Autonomous Underwater Vehicle just after diving beneath the surface at the start of an autonomous survey mission.

## Scene Description
- **Submerged AUV & Swirl**:
  - In the lower-left quadrant, the elongated yellow-gold hull and orange dorsal tower of the Gavia AUV are visible slightly blurred beneath the dark, tea-colored water of the pond as the vehicle angles away from shore toward the upper left.
  - In the lower-right foreground, a circular ring of surface ripples and eddies marks the spot where the AUV's tail thruster pushed the vehicle down from the surface moments earlier.
- **Pond & Shoreline**: The water surface transitions from a pale sky reflection on the left to deep, almost black-green reflections of the dense pine and hardwood forest lining the right shoreline. A small wooden dock supported on pilings is visible along the wooded bank in the upper right, and a floating platform or raft rests on the water in the upper-left distance.
