# Mendum's Pond MST 900 kHz Sidescan Sonar Overview in Google Earth (`mendums-mst-sidescan-900kHz-overview.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth satellite map screenshot of Mendum's Pond in New Hampshire, showing a white polygonal shoreline boundary outline and the first two bronze/amber sidescan sonar survey swaths collected by the Gavia Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) using a Marine Sonic Technology (MST) 900/1800 kHz sidescan sonar operating in 900 kHz mode on June 1, 2009.

## Map & Sonar Swath Details
- **Pond & Shoreline Boundary:** The dark blue-black water of Mendum's Pond occupies the center of the frame, surrounded by dense green forest canopy. A crisp white polygon line traces the simplified outer shoreline of the pond as well as four wooded islands in the central and eastern portions of the lake.
- **Sidescan Sonar Swaths:** In the east-central basin of the pond (between the central islands and the eastern shoreline), two parallel, slightly angled orange-amber sidescan sonar swaths (~60 meters wide) run roughly southwest-to-northeast:
  - The upper (northern) swath curves at its western end and displays bright yellow acoustic reflections along its northern edge.
  - The lower (southern) swath runs parallel just south of the first line, extending slightly further east toward one of the small islands.
- **Orientation:** Unlike standard north-up maps, the view is rotated (with north-northeast toward the bottom/right, matching the rotated shoreline view compared to `mendums-pond-ge-shoreline.png`).
