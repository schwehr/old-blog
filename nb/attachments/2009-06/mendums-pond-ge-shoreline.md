# Mendum's Pond Shoreline Polygon in Google Earth (`mendums-pond-ge-shoreline.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth satellite map screenshot displaying Mendum's Pond in New Hampshire with a digitized white KML polygon tracing the shoreline and islands, created on June 1, 2009, to generate a shoreline CSV file for Gavia AUV and GPS-acoustic buoy field operations. An orange oval highlights the UNH pier and launch area in the lower left.

## Map & Polygon Details
- **Shoreline & Island Polygons:** A thin white vector outline bounds the irregular perimeter of Mendum's Pond and traces four distinct forested islands within the dark water body.
- **Highlighted Operations Area:** In the lower-left (southwestern) cove of the pond, a thick orange-brown oval encircles a small peninsula and wooden pier jutting northward into the water, marking the UNH recreational/launch facility where the team checked GPS georeferencing (~5–10 meter accuracy).
- **Surrounding Terrain:** Dense green forest surrounds the entire lake, crisscrossed by thin gray road lines and dotted with small clearings and lakeside structures.
