# RTK GPS Base Station and Field Truck at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-rtk.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor field photograph taken on June 3, 2009, during the second day of AUV and GPS-acoustic buoy testing at Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire, showing a Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) GPS base station antenna mounted on a yellow surveying tripod beside a dark blue pickup truck equipped with power and radio gear.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **RTK GPS Tripod (Left Foreground):** A bright yellow collapsible surveying tripod stands on pine-needle-covered ground. Mounted atop the tripod head is a white disc-shaped GPS antenna above an orange/black tribrach adapter, with a dark cylindrical module hanging just below the apex.
- **Pickup Truck & Mobile Setup (Right):** A dark navy-blue Chevrolet 4x4 pickup truck with a black tubular utility rack over the bed is parked on the grass and dirt clearing with its tailgate lowered:
  - Inside the open truck bed and on the tailgate sits an open yellow Pelican-style rugged equipment case containing power/radio electronics and yellow cabling.
  - A tall vertical whip antenna (for broadcasting RTK corrections or receiving telemetry) extends upward from the front-left corner of the truck's utility rack into the tree canopy, connected via a yellow coaxial cable.
  - A white **`4x4`** decal is visible on the rear quarter panel beside the taillight.
- **Background:** Behind the tripod are blue-painted wooden and metal boat/canoe storage racks, green lakeside brush, a glimpse of the blue water of Mendum's Pond through the foliage, and tall pine and deciduous trees under a clear blue sky.
