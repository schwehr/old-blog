# Carrying the Gavia AUV Ashore for Charging at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-weds-charging-gavia.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on Wednesday, June 3, 2009, at Mendum's Pond showing five team members carrying the torpedo-shaped Gavia Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) in its black transport frame up a rocky shoreline toward a field table to recharge its batteries.

## Scene & People Details
- **The Gavia AUV:** Positioned in the lower-center foreground, the cylindrical, gold/bronze-anodized hull of the Gavia AUV rests inside a black tubular metal carrying litter/cradle. Its black nose cone faces the bottom of the frame, with a silver securing strap and small orange top fitting visible along the hull.
- **Team Members:**
  - **Front Left:** A man wearing a white long-sleeved shirt over a dark green t-shirt, dark blue shorts, and dark sandals bends down gripping the left side of the carrying frame.
  - **Front Right:** A man seen from behind wearing a light blue t-shirt with a **"Coast Day"** graphic on the back and khaki shorts carries the right side of the frame.
  - **Middle Left:** A man in a dark slate-blue collared shirt, khaki shorts, and hiking shoes steps onto the granite rock slab while holding the rear-left section of the frame.
  - **Middle Right:** A person with shoulder-length brown hair wearing a dark navy t-shirt and gray cargo pants/capris holds the rear-right handle of the frame.
  - **Top Left (By Table):** A man (Art Trembanis) wearing a tan cap and a dark green and black fleece pullover stands behind a folding field table on the granite outcrop, looking down toward the AUV.
- **Shoreline Environment:** Large gray granite boulders, pine needles, green lakeside shrubs, and tall pine tree trunks frame the scene, with shallow water at the bottom left edge and a yellow cable coiled across the rock slab on the left.
