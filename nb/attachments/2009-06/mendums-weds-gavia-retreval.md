# Retrieving the Gavia AUV from an Inflatable Boat at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-weds-gavia-retreval.jpg`)

## Overview
A lakeside photograph taken on June 3, 2009, at Mendum's Pond showing two researchers in a small gray inflatable dinghy reaching out to retrieve the yellow/gold Gavia AUV floating at the water's surface.

## Scene & Vessel Details
- **Inflatable Boat & Crew:** A light gray Zodiac Zoom inflatable dinghy with a blue-and-black rub rail floats in the center-left foreground on dark blue rippled water. A **`ZOOM`** logo decal is visible on the bow.
  - **Left Crew Member:** A young man with short blond/light-brown hair, wearing a red personal flotation device (PFD) over a dark long-sleeved shirt and khaki pants, sits on the port pontoon near the stern looking toward the AUV.
  - **Right Crew Member:** An older man with gray hair, glasses, a white long-sleeved shirt, and a red PFD leans over the bow of the inflatable boat, extending his right hand toward the AUV's surface mast/float.
- **Gavia AUV in Water:** Just in front of the inflatable boat's bow, the elongated yellow-gold body of the Gavia AUV is partially submerged at the water surface, with its bright orange communication/recovery float and clear top strobe/bottle assembly protruding above the water just below the man's outstretched hand.
- **Background:** Across the dark blue pond, a dense green forest of pine and deciduous trees lines the opposite shore under a clear sky, with a shaded A-frame lakeside cottage and dock barely visible among the trees on the far right.
