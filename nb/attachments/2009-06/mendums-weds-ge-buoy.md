# Buoy Movement Track and Sidescan Swaths in Google Earth (`mendums-weds-ge-buoy.png`)

## Overview
A close-up Google Earth screenshot from June 3, 2009, displaying the timestamped GPS movement track of an acoustic-GPS buoy (`Buoy 2`) plotted as yellow pushpins across two golden-orange 900 kHz sidescan sonar swaths in Mendum's Pond.

## Map, Track & Sonar Details
- **Sidescan Sonar Swaths:** Two diagonal, golden-amber sidescan sonar imagery swaths cross the frame from top-left to bottom-right against the dark mottled satellite background of the pond. Bright yellow high-backscatter acoustic targets (such as boulders or bottom topography) cast dark acoustic shadows along the outer edges of both swaths.
- **Buoy Movement Track:** A dense series of yellow Google Earth pushpin icons forms a path from the lower right (`01 Jun 18:48`) toward the upper left (`01 Jun 18:56`), with grayextruded curtain lines connecting the track points to the surface.
- **Buoy Placemark & Shoreline:** Near the lower right end of the track sits a light-blue teardrop placemark icon labeled **`Buoy 2`**. A crisp white vertical-diagonal line on the right marks the digitized polygon boundary of a nearby island or shoreline.
