# Team Initial Preparation on the Dock at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-weds-initial-prep.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph taken on June 3, 2009, at Mendum's Pond showing the field team gathered on a modular floating dock discussing the first survey run of the day while the Gavia AUV floats tethered nearby in the water.

## Scene, People & Equipment Details
- **Floating Dock & Team (Left Foreground):** Six team members sit or crouch in a circle on a gray-and-black interlocking modular plastic floating dock:
  - Seated in the foreground with backs to the camera are two men in long-sleeved shirts and khaki pants/shorts.
  - On the middle left, a woman with dark hair in a dark jacket and sunglasses sits beside a man in a light gray shirt, a man in a dark striped shirt and tan cap perched on a black folding stool, and an older man facing the group wearing a white shirt and red life vest holding a laptop/tablet.
  - Standing further back on the wooden section of the pier are two men—one in a black t-shirt, cap, and tan pants, and a taller man (Val Schmidt) in a blue jacket over a gray shirt and khaki shorts.
- **Gavia AUV in Water (Lower Right):** Floating at the surface of the calm, dark greenish-brown pond water to the right of the dock is the yellow torpedo-shaped Gavia AUV, equipped with an orange-and-white float module near its nose and secured to the dock by a thin yellow-brown tether line.
- **Boats & Shoreline Background:** Moored and beached along the sandy boat ramp behind the dock are a small motorboat with an outboard engine, a gray inflatable dinghy, and two aluminum/fiberglass skiffs on shore. Further back among tall green pine trees are a white open equipment trailer and a beige lakeside boathouse with a canoe stored on its roof rack.
