# Kurt Schwehr Driving the VideoRay ROV at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-weds-kurt-driving-videoray.jpg`)

## Overview
A lakeside photograph taken on June 3, 2009, at Mendum's Pond showing Kurt Schwehr seated at an aluminum folding table operating a VideoRay Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV) via its ruggedized control box and an external Dell flat-panel monitor.

## Scene, Operator & Equipment Details
- **Operator (Right Foreground):** Kurt Schwehr is seen from the side/back wearing a brown baseball cap and a dark navy-blue hooded sweatshirt with bold white block lettering on the back partially reading **`HE... / SC...`** (USCGC *Healy* Science). His left arm reaches into the open control case to manipulate the ROV joystick/controls.
- **Control Station (Center):** Set up on a slatted silver aluminum folding camp table right at the water's edge:
  - **Left:** A black Dell flat-screen LCD monitor (`DELL` logo centered on the bottom bezel) on a silver V-shaped stand, angled toward the operator.
  - **Right:** A black ruggedized Pelican-style VideoRay control case with its lid propped open, housing an integrated display screen in the upper lid and control knobs/joystick in the lower base.
- **Tether & Pond Background (Left):** The bright yellow floating umbilical tether cable runs from the table into the rippled blue-gray water of Mendum's Pond, curving out toward the left side of the frame where the submerged ROV is operating. Dense green forest lines the opposite shore, with a small wooden dock visible among the trees in the center distance.
