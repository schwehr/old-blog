# Starting a Gavia AUV Mission at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-weds-start-of-mission.jpg`)

## Overview
A lakeside photograph taken on June 3, 2009, at Mendum's Pond showing two researchers in a small motorized inflatable boat releasing the Gavia AUV at the start of an underwater survey mission while a third team member lies flat on the end of a wooden dock reaching into the water.

## Scene, People & Vessel Details
- **Dock Observer (Left):** A man wearing khaki cargo pants, a black jacket, and headphones/earmuffs lies prone on his stomach at the edge of a weathered wooden pier, reaching his left hand down into the water (holding an underwater sensor, hydrophone, or camera).
- **Inflatable Boat & Crew (Right):** Just off the end of the dock floats a gray Zodiac Zoom inflatable dinghy equipped with a white electric trolling motor (`Minn Kota` style) mounted on the stern transom and black oars strapped to the pontoons:
  - **Stern Operator:** A young man wearing a red life vest (PFD) over a dark blue-gray shirt and tan pants sits near the stern, gripping the black tiller handle of the electric trolling motor.
  - **Bow Handler:** An older man wearing glasses, a white long-sleeved shirt, and a red PFD kneels in the bow and leans over the port tube, holding the yellow Gavia AUV alongside the boat in the water.
- **Gavia AUV:** The long, slender yellow torpedo hull of the Gavia AUV floats parallel to the port side of the inflatable dinghy at the water's surface.
- **Background:** Dark blue rippled lake water stretches across the middle ground to a densely forested green hillside along the far shore under a bright sky.
