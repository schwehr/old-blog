# Operations Team Under the Canopy Tent at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-weds-team.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on June 3, 2009, at Mendum's Pond showing the field operations team gathered around a picnic table underneath a blue pop-up shade canopy on the rocky shoreline, with an inflatable support boat out on the lake in the background.

## Scene & People Details
- **Canopy & Base Station Setup:** A royal-blue four-legged pop-up canopy tent is pitched over a wooden picnic table on a shaded granite outcrop right by the water's edge. A tall black vertical radio/Wi-Fi antenna mast rises above the left rear corner of the tent. Orange extension cords snake across the rock in the foreground, and folding camp chairs and plastic gear bins sit to the right.
- **Team Members Under the Tent:** Nine team members are gathered around the table and beneath the canopy:
  - **Far Left:** Two men standing in conversation, wearing gray/black jackets and cargo/khaki pants.
  - **Center Foreground (Seated at Table):** A man in a gray hooded sweatshirt and cap sits on the left bench facing the table, while a person with reddish-brown hair in a black jacket and dark pants sits on the right bench working on an open silver Apple laptop.
  - **Center Background:** Two men standing near the rear edge of the canopy (one in a tan fleece, one in a dark green jacket and cap) looking down toward the water/table.
  - **Right Foreground:** A man standing with his back to the camera wearing a light blue **"Coast Day"** t-shirt and khaki shorts, next to a person in a dark gray hooded sweatshirt and jeans and another partially obscured team member.
- **Background on the Lake:** Beyond the tent, the dark blue rippled water of Mendum's Pond extends to wooded islands and shoreline. In the middle distance on the right, two team members in red life vests operate the small white/gray inflatable boat, while a small marker buoy floats further out in the upper center-right.
