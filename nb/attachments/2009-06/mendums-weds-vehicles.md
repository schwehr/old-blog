# AUV and Support Boats Operating on Mendum's Pond (`mendums-weds-vehicles.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide shot across Mendum's Pond taken on June 3, 2009, showing the three marine platforms operating simultaneously on the lake: the Gavia AUV at the water's surface in the foreground, an inflatable dinghy on the right, and a small survey motorboat on the left.

## Vessels & Scene Details
- **Gavia AUV (Bottom Center Foreground):** The yellow-gold cylindrical hull of the Gavia AUV is barely awash at the dark blue rippled water surface, moving right-to-left with its bright orange tracking/recovery float and clear strobe bottle protruding above the water.
- **Inflatable Support Dinghy (Middle Right):** The light gray Zodiac Zoom inflatable boat floats in the middle distance on the right, carrying two team members wearing bright red life vests (PFDs).
- **Survey Motorboat (Upper Left):** Further out on the left side of the lake is a small gray/aluminum skiff (used for running the GeoSwath sonar and deploying GPS-acoustic buoys) with a vertical mast/antenna pole and crew in orange/red PFDs.
- **Buoy & Shoreline Background:** A small vertical mast from one of the deployed GPS-acoustic buoys is faintly visible in the water near the upper center-right. Dense green pine and hardwood forest lines the far shore and islands under a clear blue summer sky, with a large gray granite boulder and two small lakeside cabins visible along the water's edge.
