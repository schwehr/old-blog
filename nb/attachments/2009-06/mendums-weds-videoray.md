# VideoRay ROV and Control Console Before Deployment at Mendum's Pond (`mendums-weds-videoray.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up lakeside photograph taken on June 3, 2009, at Mendum's Pond displaying the compact yellow VideoRay Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV), its open ruggedized control console case, and an external Dell LCD monitor on a folding table, while two researchers tend to the Gavia AUV from an inflatable boat in the background.

## Equipment & Scene Details
- **VideoRay ROV (Lower Left Foreground):** Resting on the slatted aluminum folding table is the small, bright-yellow VideoRay Pro ROV:
  - Its rounded yellow polypropylene float body features the **`VideoRay`** logo and a blue graphic on the side, a circular top viewport/depth sensor window, and a forward dome viewport.
  - Beneath the yellow body is the black frame, two rear-mounted black ducted horizontal thrusters, a black cylindrical forward-extending manipulator/sonar or accessory pod on the left, and the yellow-and-black umbilical tether connected at the rear.
- **Control Box & Monitor (Center & Lower Right):**
  - **Right:** A heavy-duty black Pelican-style control case sits open, revealing a bright yellow control panel base with black rotary knobs, toggle switches, a central cable recess holding a coiled blue cable, and a black thumb/hand joystick on the right. A flat display screen is built into the inside of the open black lid above a gold control strip.
  - **Center Left:** A black Dell flat-panel LCD monitor (`DELL` logo on the bottom bezel) stands next to the control case for auxiliary viewing.
- **Background on the Water (Top Center):** Just offshore in the rippled pond water, two crew members wearing red life vests sit in the gray inflatable dinghy with a white electric trolling motor on the transom; the orange float of the Gavia AUV is visible in the water just beyond the bow of the dinghy.
