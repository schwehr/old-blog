# Stephan Posing at the 2006 VisLab Stereoscope (`old-stereoscope-user.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph from 2006 showing researcher Stephan seated at the University of New Hampshire CCOM Visualization Lab (VisLab) high-resolution stereoscope, looking into the central angled mirrors. The photo was published in the June 23, 2009 blog post (*"The old VisLab stereoscope"*), noting that Stephan took a break from coding his haptics thesis project to demonstrate how a user positions their eyes in front of the mirror assembly.

## Scene Description
- **Subject (Stephan)**:
  - A man with brown hair pulled back into a low ponytail, a full beard and mustache, and wire-rimmed glasses is seated in a blue upholstered office chair in the center-right of the frame.
  - He wears a dark charcoal-gray polo shirt with white embroidered text on the left chest reading `Healy` (referencing the icebreaker USCGC *Healy*).
  - He leans forward slightly so that the bridge of his nose and glasses are positioned directly against the black central optical mount between the two angled rectangular mirrors, with his right hand reaching forward toward the keyboard tray.
- **Stereoscope & Computer Equipment**:
  - **Central Mirror Assembly**: Mounted on a vertical silver post in front of Stephan's face, the black mechanical stage holds two rectangular mirrors angled outward to reflect the opposing high-resolution displays.
  - **Right-Side High-Resolution Monitor**: Above the desk to the left of Stephan (the right-hand side of the stereoscope rig from the front view), one of the black ViewSonic VP2290b 3840×2400 flat-panel monitors is mounted at an inward-tilted angle on an aluminum support arm; its screen is off/black.
  - **CRT Monitors & Peripherals**: In the lower foreground are the curved dark-gray plastic backs of the two CRT monitors (with perforated ventilation slots), with a small cream-colored wedge-shaped speaker or sensor sitting atop the middle CRT. Behind them, two keyboards rest on a shelf under a clear acrylic cover next to the Linux cluster cases.
- **Room Background**:
  - Behind Stephan is a large white dry-erase board mounted on the wall with a metal marker tray holding markers and an eraser. Green handwritten notes and equations (including `F = ...`) are visible in the upper-left corner of the whiteboard.
  - To the right, the room has a dark blue glossy floor, a blue plastic recycling bin lined with a white trash bag, and a light wooden door with a narrow vertical window frame.
