# Tall Ship Passing Under Raised Memorial Bridge in Portsmouth, NH (`portsmouthnh-tallship-200707.jpg`)

## Overview
A waterfront photograph taken with a mobile phone camera in July 2007 showing a two-masted topsail schooner (tall ship) motoring down the Piscataqua River beneath the raised vertical-lift span of the Memorial Bridge in Portsmouth, New Hampshire, accompanied by a Moran tugboat. The photo was shared in a June 21, 2009 blog post (*"Tall ships coming this summer"*) ahead of the August 2009 Portsmouth tall ships visit.

## Scene Description
- **Vessels**:
  - **Tall Ship (Center-Right)**: A traditional two-masted wooden schooner (resembling the *Pride of Baltimore II*) with a black-and-yellow hull, a long bowsprit extending to the right, and two tall wooden masts raked slightly aft. Its sails are furled in white covers along the yards and booms. A large U.S. flag flies from the stern/mainmast Rigging, a blue flag flies mid-mast on the mainmast, and a yellow pennant flies from the top of the mainmast. The vessel is moving left to right (seaward) across the dark gray river water.
  - **Moran Tugboat (Center-Left)**: Positioned in the background behind the schooner's stern and between the bridge piers is a classic red-and-black tugboat with a white capital letter `M` on its black smokestack (a Portsmouth Moran Towing tug), kicking up a white bow wake as it moves toward the right.
- **Bridges & Waterfront**:
  - **Memorial Bridge (Foreground Left to Center)**: The green-painted steel truss vertical-lift bridge dominates the left and upper portions of the frame. Its central lift span is raised all the way to the top of the two tall steel lattice towers to provide vertical clearance for the schooner's masts. Heavy concrete piers support the towers at the water's edge, with counterweight blocks suspended inside the tower frames.
  - **Sarah Mildred Long Bridge (Background Center-Right)**: Visible in the distance upstream behind the tall ship's masts is the arched steel truss span of the Sarah Mildred Long Bridge.
  - **Shoreline**: Along the far riverbank (Badgers Island / Kittery, Maine side), waterfront buildings, trees, and moored white pleasure boats at marinas are visible under an overcast, moody gray sky.
