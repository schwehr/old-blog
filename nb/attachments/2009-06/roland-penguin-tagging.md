# Roland Practicing DTag Attachment on a Stuffed Penguin (`roland-penguin-tagging.jpg`)

## Overview
A lighthearted indoor photograph showing researcher Roland Arsenault in an office at CCOM/UNH attaching an acoustic/archival marine mammal suction-cup tag (DTag) onto the back of a large plush emperor/king penguin toy. Featured in a June 16, 2009 blog post (*"Penguin tagging"*), the caption jokes that after returning from an Antarctic whale-tagging expedition with Colin Ware, Roland was practicing his techniques for tagging penguins.

## Scene Description
- **Subject & Action**:
  - On the left, Roland—a man with short dark brown hair, wearing a mustard-yellow and brown horizontally striped short-sleeve t-shirt—stands in profile facing right with a wide smile.
  - He extends his left arm to press a white-bodied electronic DTag unit onto the upper back of a large, life-sized black-and-white stuffed penguin standing upright on top of a gray metal filing cabinet on the right.
- **Penguin & Tag Details**:
  - **Stuffed Penguin**: A plush king/emperor penguin figure with a black head, back, and flippers, a white belly, a black beak with a yellow lower mandible stripe, and bright orange-and-yellow auricular (ear) patches on the sides of its head.
  - **DTag Device**: A streamlined white syntactic-foam housing with yellow and red markings on the tail end, black electronic/sensor ports on top, a thin trailing wire antenna hanging down at the rear, and clear rubber suction cups pressed against the plush penguin's back.
- **Background & Office Environment**:
  - **World War I Poster**: Framed in black on the wall directly behind Roland is a vintage WWI U.S. Treasury poster reading `FIGHT OR BUY BONDS / THIRD LIBERTY LOAN` in bold red and black serif typography, accompanied by an illustration by Howard Chandler Christy showing a woman in white drapery carrying an American flag above charging soldiers.
  - **Cabinet & Window**: On the filing cabinet between Roland and the penguin sit a tall stack of CD/DVD jewel cases, a small black electronic box with two DB-9 serial connectors, and loose white papers. Behind the penguin is an interior window framed in dark blue trim looking into an adjacent room where a scientific poster titled `...Tools for Meteorology and Oceanography` with a `System Architecture` block diagram is displayed.
