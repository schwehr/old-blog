# Star Island Tide Station Weatherproof Electronics Box (`star-island-electronics.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph by Andy Armstrong ("Andy M.") showing the open weatherproof fiberglass enclosure housing the datalogger, wireless radio modem, and uninterruptible power supply (UPS) for the UNH/CCOM Star Island tide gauge station at the Isles of Shoals, New Hampshire. The photo appeared in the June 16, 2009 blog post (*"tide station at Star Island"*).

## Equipment & Interior Details
- **Weatherproof Enclosure**:
  - A gray NEMA-style hinged fiberglass utility box mounted to a white-painted wooden wall/structure, with stainless steel latches on the right side and a black rubber weather-stripping gasket lining the inside perimeter of the open left door.
- **Left Door Interior**:
  - **Radio Modem (Upper Left)**: Mounted vertically on a brass/gold bracket is a blue ruggedized industrial wireless Ethernet/serial radio modem labeled `MDS iNET 900` (Microwave Data Systems 900 MHz radio transceiver). Connected to its front panel are an RJ-45 port, a green Phoenix-style terminal block adapter attached to a DB-9 serial connector, status LEDs, and a thick black coaxial antenna cable attached to the bottom RF connector. A white asset/serial sticker is affixed to the door to the upper left of the modem, and a stamped serial number `43046017` is visible on the gray door panel.
  - **UPS Battery Backup (Lower Left)**: Mounted horizontally at the bottom of the door is a black **Tripp Lite** uninterruptible power supply (`TRIPP LITE` logo visible in white on the lower front face) with multiple AC power receptacles and status indicators.
- **Main Enclosure Compartment (Right)**:
  - **Aanderaa Datalogger 3634 (Upper Right)**: Mounted at the top of the main cavity is a beige/gray metal instrument panel labeled `DATALOGGER 3634` (`AANDERAA INSTRUMENTS` logo in the top center). It features a rectangular dark LCD display window on the left, two rotary selector knobs (`MODE` and `FUNCTION`) on the right with printed operating instructions, a row of four black protective caps covering circular sensor/channel receptacles along the lower left, and two silver multi-pin circular military-style connectors attached to black cables on the lower right.
  - **Power Adapters & Wiring (Lower Right)**: Beneath the datalogger are three black AC/DC power bricks/transformers, a red-and-white `CAUTION` label on the metal backplate, and a bundle of black power and serial data cables routing between the datalogger, the MDS radio modem, the Tripp Lite UPS, and exit holes in the bottom of the box.
