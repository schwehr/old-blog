# Torm Mary AIS Track in Google Earth Around Portsmouth, NH Grounding (`torm-mary-ge.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot showing the Automatic Identification System (AIS) GPS position track of the oil tanker *Torm Mary* (MMSI `220112000`, IMO `9246798`) in the Piscataqua River at Portsmouth/Newington, New Hampshire, spanning June 11–12, 2009. Featured in a June 12, 2009 blog post (*"Torm Mary grounding in Portsmouth NH"*), the image illustrates how raw single-point AIS position tracks can obscure a grounding incident (the tanker's stern struck the riverbed around 10:30 p.m. on June 11) and are dominated by ±25 m GPS drift while moored at the dock.
