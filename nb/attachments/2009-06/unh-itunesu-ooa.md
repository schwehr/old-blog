# UNH iTunes U Open Ocean Aquaculture Video Playback (`unh-itunesu-ooa.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X desktop screenshot showing an iTunes video playback window (`Chronicle Aquaculture Research`) overlaid in front of a Mozilla Firefox browser window displaying the "UNH @ iTunes University" web portal (`http://www.itunesu.unh.edu/`). Featured in a June 26, 2009 blog post (*"UNH iTunesU"*), the screenshot highlights video content from the University of New Hampshire's commercial Open Ocean Aquaculture (OOA) project showing a dense school of fish swimming inside a submerged aquaculture net pen.
