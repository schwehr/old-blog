# VideoRay ROV Looking Back at Operator (`video-ray-looking-back.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up outdoor photograph taken during field operations at Mendum's Pond in June 2009, showing an operator piloting a yellow VideoRay remotely operated vehicle (ROV) in shallow water near the shoreline while viewing the ROV's live camera feed on a control console monitor.

## Scene & Equipment Description
- **Foreground (Left & Right)**: Seen from over the left shoulder, an operator wearing a dark olive-green fleece jacket and a khaki/gray cap reaches with their right hand to manipulate a small black joystick control on a yellow control panel housed inside a rugged black Pelican-style carrying case.
- **Control Console & Monitor (Center Right)**:
  - The yellow control deck in the lower right corner displays black text reading **`VideoRay`** along with status indicators and black tether/control cables.
  - Above the control deck, a flat-screen LCD monitor mounted in the open lid of the case displays the live video feed from the VideoRay's camera, which is oriented back toward the shoreline. On the dark screen, three team members are visible standing near a small wooden shoreline structure/shelter against a backdrop of coniferous trees.
- **Water & ROV (Background Center-Left)**: Just beyond the rocky shore, the compact yellow-and-black VideoRay ROV floats at the surface of the rippled pond water, facing back toward the operator. Its bright yellow float block sits atop a black frame with two round white forward lights/thruster housings, and its yellow umbilical tether trails behind it to the left.
