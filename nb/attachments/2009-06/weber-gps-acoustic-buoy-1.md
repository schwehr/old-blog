# Carrying the GPS-Acoustic Buoy on the Mendum's Pond Dock (`weber-gps-acoustic-buoy-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken on June 2, 2009, at Mendum's Pond in New Hampshire, showing researchers Tom Weber and John carrying a custom-built GPS-acoustic spar buoy along a wooden floating dock prior to deployment.

## Scene & Equipment Description
- **Researchers**:
  - **Left (Tom Weber)**: Standing near the center of the wooden dock facing the water, wearing a light gray short-sleeved polo shirt, khaki pants, dark shoes, and a white-and-black cap. He holds the upper mast assembly of the buoy horizontally with his left hand.
  - **Right (John)**: Walking ahead toward the right side of the dock, wearing a dark long-sleeved shirt, an orange-red life vest (PFD) with black straps, light gray cargo shorts, and brown shoes.
- **GPS-Acoustic Buoy**: Held horizontally between the two men, the instrument consists of:
  - A long cylindrical silver aluminum pressure housing on the right (lower submerged section).
  - A thick, bright yellow cylindrical foam flotation collar around the middle, with a white line hanging down to the dock planks.
  - A tall, slender white mast extending to the left, wrapped spirally with a black coaxial antenna cable and topped with a white GPS antenna and a shorter secondary vertical whip antenna.
- **Setting & Background**: In the foreground is a weathered gray wooden dock with the bow of a light gray small boat visible at the lower right corner. Across the dark blue rippled water of Mendum's Pond, a dense, sunlit green forest of deciduous and pine trees lines the opposite shore.
