# Deploying GPS-Acoustic Buoys from the Mendum's Pond Dock (`weber-gps-acoustic-buoy-2.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken on June 2, 2009, at Mendum's Pond in New Hampshire, showing Tom Weber and John lowering a second yellow-and-silver GPS-acoustic spar buoy into the water beside the wooden sailing dock while the first buoy floats upright in the foreground.

## Scene & Equipment Description
- **Foreground Buoy (Deployed)**: Floating upright in the dark blue water against the corner of the wooden dock is the first deployed GPS-acoustic buoy. Its bright yellow cylindrical foam collar sits at the waterline, surmounted by an aluminum housing flange with metal lifting handles, cable glands, and a tall vertical mast wrapped spirally with black cable leading up to a white disc-shaped GPS antenna and a shorter vertical radio antenna.
- **Second Buoy & Researchers**:
  - **Tom Weber (Right)**: Standing on the wooden dock wearing a gray polo shirt, khaki pants, and a white cap, holding the upper section of the second buoy just above its yellow foam collar and tilted white antenna mast.
  - **John (Left)**: Bending down at the edge of the dock in an orange life vest, dark shirt, and white shorts, guiding the lower silver cylindrical aluminum housing of the second buoy into the water.
- **Dock & Sailboats (Background)**: Behind the two men, a row of small white sailing dinghies (including a hull labeled **`CLUB FJ`**) sit on the wooden dock under tan canvas boat covers, with their tall aluminum masts extending skyward against a backdrop of green pine trees, a distant wooded shoreline across the pond, and a clear blue sky.
