# GPS-Acoustic Buoy Deployed in Mendum's Pond (`weber-gps-acoustic-buoy-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide outdoor photograph taken on June 2, 2009, showing one of the CCOM GPS-acoustic navigation buoys deployed and floating upright in the middle of Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire.

## Scene & Equipment Description
- **Deployed Buoy (Lower Center)**:
  - The yellow foam flotation collar of the spar buoy rests horizontally at the water's surface, with a short silver cylindrical top section protruding above the collar.
  - Extending vertically from the top of the buoy are two masts: a taller white pole wrapped spirally with a black cable supporting a GPS antenna at the top, and a shorter white vertical antenna mounted alongside it.
  - A small white/yellow secondary marker float rests on the water just to the left of the main buoy.
- **Water & Shoreline (Background)**:
  - The foreground and midground are filled with the deep blue, gently rippled surface of Mendum's Pond.
  - The background features a lush, sunlit New England forest shoreline dominated by tall eastern white pines and mixed hardwoods, with a large gray granite boulder visible at the water's edge on the far right and a narrow channel opening between wooded points to the right of center.
