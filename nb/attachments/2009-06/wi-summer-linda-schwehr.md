# Late Summer Lake Reflections in Wisconsin (`wi-summer-linda-schwehr.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic landscape photograph taken by Linda Schwehr (Kurt Schwehr's mother) in Wisconsin, showing a dense lakeside forest bathed in warm golden light and reflected across calm water beneath a dark, brooding sky.

## Scene Description
- **Shoreline Forest (Center Band)**: A continuous, dense treeline of mixed northern hardwoods (birch, aspen, maple) and conifers spans the entire width of the frame. The foliage is illuminated by low-angle golden sunlight, giving the predominantly lime- and olive-green canopy warm yellow, orange, and early autumn russet-red highlights—especially on a small bush near the water's edge just left of center. White birch trunks are visible among the trees, and a tiny wooden dock or shoreline structure is faintly visible at the far right edge.
- **Water Reflection (Lower Half)**: The glass-like surface of the lake creates a nearly symmetrical, vertically stretched mirror reflection of the golden-green and russet trees, broken only by a few faint horizontal ripples near the left edge and a patch of dark slate-blue sky reflection along the bottom edge.
- **Sky (Upper Third)**: Above the brightly lit treetops, a heavy, uniform layer of dark slate-gray storm clouds spans the sky, creating a striking contrast with the glowing golden foliage below.
