# Dover 4th of July Aerial Firework Burst (`4th-fireworks-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A night photograph captured with an iPhone 3GS on July 4, 2009, showing a spherical aerial firework shell bursting high in the dark sky above a silhouetted treeline in Dover, New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Firework Burst (Upper Center)**: A spherical chrysanthemum/peony-style aerial shell explodes in the upper portion of the frame. Due to the handheld low-light exposure, the glowing white and golden-orange stars form short, slightly wavy horizontal light trails radiating outward from a bright golden-white center, with a ring of bright white tips outlining the lower and outer perimeter of the sphere.
- **Sky & Horizon (Middle & Bottom)**: The background sky transitions from near-black at the top right to a deep, grainy twilight indigo-blue above the lower left horizon, where the dark, uneven silhouette of a treeline spans the bottom edge of the frame.
