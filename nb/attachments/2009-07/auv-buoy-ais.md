# AUV and Acoustic Buoys Marked with AIS Area Notice Messages (`auv-buoy-ais.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot from July 7, 2009, demonstrating how real-time AIS Area Notice binary messages can be used to broadcast and visualize 50-meter caution zones around an operating Gavia autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) and its two supporting GPS-acoustic navigation buoys (`b1` and `b2`) overlaid on sidescan sonar imagery in Mendum's Pond, New Hampshire.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Buoy 1 (`b1`, Upper Left)**:
  - Marked by an orange buoy icon labeled **`b1`** at the center of a semi-transparent purple circle with a thin pink/red border (50-meter radius).
  - An open white KML pop-up balloon pointing to `b1` displays:
    - **Title**: **`cau underwater ops`**
    - **Subtitle**: *`AreaNotice - Caution Area: Underwater operation`*
    - **Metadata**: `AreaNotice: type=14 start=2009-07-07 14:45:39.121677 duration=10 m link_id=1 sub-areas: 1`
    - **Sub-area**: `1. AreaNoticeCirclePt: Circle centered at (-71.0632,43.1731) - radius 50m`
- **Buoy 2 (`b2`, Upper Right)**:
  - Marked by an orange buoy icon labeled **`b2`** at the center of a semi-transparent olive-green circle with a thin blue border (50-meter radius), positioned just off the wooded eastern shoreline.
- **Gavia AUV (`auv`, Lower Center-Left)**:
  - A 3D cylindrical torpedo-shaped icon representing the Gavia AUV, labeled **`auv`**, is centered inside a semi-transparent dark red circle with a bright red border (50-meter radius).
  - An open white KML pop-up balloon pointing to the red circle displays:
    - **Title**: **`cau auv ops`**
    - **Subtitle**: *`AreaNotice - Caution Area: Underwater vehicle operation`*
    - **Metadata**: `AreaNotice: type=21 start=2009-07-07 14:45:39.145818 duration=10 m link_id=1 sub-areas: 1`
    - **Sub-area**: `1. AreaNoticeCirclePt: Circle centered at (-71.0642,43.1717) - radius 50m`
- **Sidescan Sonar Swath Overlay (Lower Right)**: Two golden-ochre/brown sidescan sonar mosaic swaths extend from the AUV's position toward the right, revealing bright yellow acoustic returns from boulders and bottom topography on the floor of Mendum's Pond.
