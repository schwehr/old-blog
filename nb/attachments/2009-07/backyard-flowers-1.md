# Yellow Backyard Lily with Raindrops (`backyard-flowers-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up outdoor photograph taken in July 2009 in Kurt Schwehr's backyard, showing a bright yellow Asiatic lily in full bloom, covered in fresh raindrops against a backdrop of green garden foliage and a weathered wooden fence.

## Scene & Floral Details
- **Yellow Lily (Center)**:
  - The flower features six recurved, bright lemon-yellow tepals (petals) radiating from a light green star-shaped throat. Each petal has a warm orange-gold flush along its central midrib and a sparse scattering of tiny dark brown/maroon spots near the base.
  - Glistening water droplets cling to the waxy surface of the petals.
  - Five long stamens topped with dark reddish-brown anthers and a central yellow-green pistil with a three-lobed stigma project outward toward the upper right.
  - Directly behind the top petal, a large, unopened green flower bud points vertically upward.
- **Background**: Surrounding the lily are deep green lance-shaped lily leaves, tall blades of grass, leafy garden plants on the right, and vertical gray weathered boards of a wooden fence in the upper left background.
