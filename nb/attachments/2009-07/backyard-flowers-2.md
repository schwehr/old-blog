# Red-Orange Daylily Blossom in the Backyard (`backyard-flowers-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up side-profile photograph taken in July 2009 in Kurt Schwehr's backyard, showcasing a vibrant red-orange daylily (*Hemerocallis*) in full bloom amidst green garden foliage and wildflowers.

## Scene & Floral Details
- **Daylily Blossom (Center)**:
  - The large trumpet-shaped flower has rich crimson-orange petals with slightly ruffled, wavy margins and prominent pale creamy-white midribs running down the center of the inner petals.
  - The throat of the blossom transitions to a bright golden-yellow base.
  - Six long, upward-curving orange stamens topped with dark brown/black anthers and an even longer, slender pale pink-orange style (pistil) extend prominently toward the upper left.
- **Buds & Background**:
  - To the right of the open flower, two unopened green-and-bronze daylily buds grow upright on a sturdy green scape (stem).
  - In the soft-focus green background, a white flat-topped umbel of Queen Anne's lace (wild carrot) is visible at the upper left, along with another reddish-orange daylily blossom lower in the foliage to the left.
