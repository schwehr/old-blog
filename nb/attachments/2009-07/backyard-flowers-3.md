# Golden Squash Blossom with Striped Cucumber Beetle (`backyard-flowers-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up macro photograph taken in July 2009 in Kurt Schwehr's backyard garden, showing a large, bright golden-orange squash or pumpkin blossom with a small striped beetle resting on one of its petals.

## Scene & Floral Details
- **Squash Blossom (Center)**:
  - The wide, five-lobed funnel-shaped corolla of a *Cucurbita* (squash/zucchini/pumpkin) flower fills most of the frame. Its textured, crinkled petals are a vivid golden-yellow to deep orange, with branching veins radiating from a pale greenish-yellow throat where the central yellow stamen/pistil column is visible.
  - On the upper-left petal lobe, a small yellow-and-black striped cucumber beetle rests along the central vein.
- **Surrounding Foliage & Second Flower**:
  - In the lower right corner, a second, partially closed or wilting orange squash blossom is visible beneath the main flower, and a small fuzzy unopened bud sits near the right edge.
  - Lush dark green squash leaves, stems, and small clover-like oxalis leaves frame the upper left and background.
