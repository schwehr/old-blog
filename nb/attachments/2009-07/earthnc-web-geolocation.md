# EarthNC Nautical Chart Viewer with Browser Geolocation (`earthnc-web-geolocation.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the EarthNC Online Nautical Chart Viewer (`http://earthnc.com/chartviewer/`) running in Mozilla Firefox on Mac OS X, demonstrating HTML5 browser geolocation automatically centering the map on the New Hampshire and southern Maine seacoast. Posted on July 4, 2009.

## Interface & Controls
- **Browser Frame**: Mac OS X Firefox window titled `EarthNC - Nautical Chart Viewer for Google Maps` with navigation buttons, address bar (`http://earthnc.com/chartviewer/`), Google search bar, and bottom status bar (`Transferring data from khm2.google.com...` and a `zotero` extension icon on the right).
- **Top Application Banner**:
  - Left: **EarthNC Charts for Google Earth** logo with a globe and red-and-white nautical flag icon, above the white text `Help- Not for Navigation` on a dark background.
  - Center Tabs: Four light-blue tabs labeled `Charts` (active), `Layers`, `Nearby`, and `Routing`. Inside the `Charts` panel is a checked checkbox for `Make Chart Transparent:` and a dropdown menu reading `Loaded: OneChart View - Click for Individ...`.
  - Right: Dark banner with ad/info links (`Enterprise Map - Google Maps API Premier. Full licensing & support. Learn more!`, `Smarter GPS...`) above `EarthNC Chart-Viewer Beta - Feedback - Ma...`.
- **Map Controls**:
  - Top-left pan/zoom control pad and vertical zoom slider.
  - Top-right map type selector buttons: `Map`, `Hybrid`, `Earth`, and `Charts` (selected).
  - Right-side vertical overlay box (`Ads by Google`): links for `Boutique Hotel Boston MA` (`www.jewelboston.com`) and `Used Honda Pilot SUV` (`www.honda.com/google`).
  - Bottom-right Google search bar (`Search the Map` input and blue `Search` button) above `Map data ©2009 Tele Atlas - Terms of Use`.

## Map & Chart Details
- **Geographic Coverage**: Shows southeastern New Hampshire, northeastern Massachusetts, and southern coastal Maine, centered near Durham, Portsmouth, and Kittery (`NEW HAMPSHIRE` printed across the land).
- **Inland Features**: Satellite/hybrid terrain imagery overlaid with major highways (`I-95`, `I-93`, `I-293`, `I-495`, `I-195`, US Route `4`, Route `101`, `16`, `9`, `202`, `11`, `3`, `1`, `213`, `128`) and town labels including `Concord`, `Manchester`, `Nashua`, `Derry`, `Londonderry`, `Exeter`, `Raymond`, `Epping`, `Durham`, `Newmarket`, `Dover`, `Somersworth`, `Rochester`, `Portsmouth`, `Hampton`, `Newburyport`, `Haverhill`, `Lawrence`, `Lowell`, `Gloucester`, `Rockport`, `Kittery`, `York`/`Cape Neddick`, `Wells`, `Kennebunk`, `Kennebunkport`, `Sanford`, and `Biddeford`.
- **Marine Overlay**: Off the coast in the Gulf of Maine (`BIGELOW BIGHT`), semi-transparent NOAA raster nautical charts are overlaid onto the water, showing depth soundings, bathymetric contours, compass roses, and magenta navigational lines.
