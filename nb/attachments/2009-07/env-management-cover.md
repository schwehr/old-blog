# Environmental Management Journal Cover (`env-management-cover.png`)

## Overview
A scan/image of the front cover of the academic journal *Environmental Management* (Volume 42, Number 5, November 2008, published by Springer), featuring a cover story co-authored by Kurt Schwehr on vessel noise and marine mammals in the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary. Posted on July 15, 2009.
