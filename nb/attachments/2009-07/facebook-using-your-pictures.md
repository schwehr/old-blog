# Facebook Privacy Settings for Social Ads (`facebook-using-your-pictures.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the 2009 Facebook web interface showing how to opt out of appearing in "Facebook Ads" under the "Privacy > News Feed and Wall" settings page, posted on July 24, 2009, in an entry titled "Facebook using your pictures in adds."

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Navigation Bar**: Classic blue Facebook header bar with the `facebook` logo on the left, navigation links (`Home`, `Profile`, `Friends`, `Inbox`), and right-side user controls (`Kurt Schwehr`, `Settings` with an open dropdown menu, `Logout`, and a `Search` input box).
- **Settings Dropdown Menu**: Open beneath `Settings`, showing three options with small icons:
  1. `Account Settings` (pencil/page icon)
  2. `Privacy Settings` (padlock icon, highlighted in blue-gray)
  3. `Application Settings` (gear/window icon)
- **Page Header & Tabs**:
  - Breadcrumb heading with a blue padlock icon: `Privacy ▸ News Feed and Wall`.
  - Two horizontal tabs below the heading: `Actions within Facebook` (inactive light gray tab) and `Facebook Ads` (active blue-gray tab).

## Visible Text & Settings Options
- **Explanatory Paragraph**:
  > "Facebook occasionally pairs advertisements with relevant social actions from a user's friends to create Facebook Ads. Facebook Ads make advertisements more interesting and more tailored to you and your friends. These respect all privacy rules. You may opt out of appearing in your friends' Facebook Ads below."
- **Form Control**:
  - Label: `Appearance in Facebook Ads`
  - Dropdown menu: Currently showing `Only my friends` in the closed box, with the dropdown list expanded to show two options:
    - `Only my friends`
    - `No one` (highlighted in blue)
- **Action Buttons**: A blue `Save Changes` button and a gray `Cancel` button at the bottom center.
