# Firefox 3.5 Location-Aware Browsing Demo (`geolocation-working.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Mozilla's "Location-Aware Browsing" demonstration modal (`http://www.mozilla.com/en-US/firefox/geolocation/`) in Firefox 3.5, displaying an embedded Google Map centered on New Hampshire and southern Maine with a "Where am I?" button. Posted on July 3, 2009.

## Interface & Modal Details
- **Background Page**: Dimmed webpage background showing partial text (`...ion-Aware Browsing`, `can te...`, `ind i...`, `. It's a...`, `e it a...`, `Asked...`, `ocatio...`) and an illustration of a character wearing a brown fedora hat on the right.
- **Lightbox Modal**: A centered white modal dialog with a gray border, a close button (`[x]`) at the top-right corner, an embedded Google Map in the center, and a rounded `Where am I?` button centered beneath the map.
- **Map Controls**:
  - Top-left directional pan arrows (`↑`, `←`, `→`, `↓`) and zoom buttons (`+`, `-`), with a yellow tooltip `Zoom In` hovering below the `-` button.
  - Top-right map type buttons: `Map` (selected with bold border), `Satellite`, and `Hybrid`.
  - Bottom-left: `POWERED BY Google` logo.
  - Bottom-right: `Map data ©2009 Tele Atlas - Terms of Use`.
