# Working USCGC Healy Aloftcon GeoRSS Feed in Google Maps (`googlemaps-healy-georss-works.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Maps successfully rendering multiple hourly waypoints from the USCGC *Healy* Aloftcon camera GeoRSS 1.0 feed (`http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/healy/healy-aloftcon-latest.georss`) northeast of Point Barrow, Alaska, on July 28, 2009. Posted in the blog entry "Atom GeoRSS."
