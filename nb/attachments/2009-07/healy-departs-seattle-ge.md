# USCGC Healy Aloftcon and Science Instrument Reports in Google Earth (`healy-departs-seattle-ge.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from Google Earth showing the northbound transit of the U.S. Coast Guard icebreaker *Healy* (`WAGB-20`) off British Columbia (near Vancouver Island and Haida Gwaii) after departing Seattle in July 2009, displaying both Aloftcon camera ship icons and purple instrument report pins with two open pop-up balloons. Posted on July 8, 2009, in "Healy in Google Earth - Instrument reports."

## Map & Track Details
- **Geographic View**: Satellite terrain and bathymetry of the northeast Pacific Ocean along the coast of British Columbia, Canada, with the northwestern tip of `Vancouver Island` visible on the lower right and the southern tip of Haida Gwaii (Queen Charlotte Islands) in the upper center-left, outlined in yellow coastline vectors.
- **Ship Track & Icons**:
  - Along the bottom right and center, a blue track line is studded with purple paddle pins labeled in orange text with timestamps from `07 Jul 15:30`, `07 Jul 18:30`, `07 Jul 19:30`, `07 Jul 21:30`, `07 Jul 23:30` (plus an older southbound track from May 22–23 visible further inshore).
  - Along the lower left, a series of 3D-style side-profile red-and-white icebreaker icons represent hourly Aloftcon camera positions on July 8 (`08 Jul 06:01`, `08 Jul 07:01`, `08 Jul 09:01`, `08 Jul 10:01`, `08 Jul 13:01`, `08 Jul 14:01`).
