# NOAA Office of Ocean Exploration and Research Homepage (`homepage-noaa-oe.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X Firefox web browser screenshot displaying the homepage of NOAA's Office of Ocean Exploration and Research (`http://explore.noaa.gov/`), captured on July 8, 2009 as part of a blog comparison ("Goverment center homepage bake-off") contrasting NOAA's ocean exploration web presence with NASA JPL's homepage.

## Browser Interface & Header Banner
- **Window Title & Controls**: Top window title bar reads `Office of Ocean Exploration and Research` with standard Mac OS X red, yellow, and green window buttons on the left.
- **Navigation Toolbar**: Back/Forward arrows, Reload, Stop, Home, Delicious bookmark/tag buttons, an address bar showing the NOAA emblem favicon and `http://explore.noaa.gov/`, and a Google search box on the right containing the query `ocean exploration noaa`.
- **Tab Bar**: A single open tab titled `Office of Ocean Exploration and R...` with a `+` new-tab button.
- **Page Banner**:
  - **Left Logo**: `OCEAN EXPLORATION & RESEARCH` logo featuring three color-coded square graphic panels under the letters `O C E A N`:
    1. Olive green panel showing the silhouette of a research vessel and a submersible/bathysphere suspended from an A-frame.
    2. Slate-blue panel featuring the white gull/wave emblem from the NOAA logo.
    3. Bright blue panel depicting a scuba diver swimming downward.
    - Below the panels, `EXPLORATION & RESEARCH` is written in blue uppercase text, with the circular NOAA logo replacing the letter `O` in `EXPLORATION`.
  - **Right Photographic Banner**: A composite horizontal photograph showing a white NOAA research ship (`NOAA R 104`, *Ronald H. Brown*) on the left at sea and a woman in a life vest deploying or tending a yellow oceanographic buoy marked `H` on the right, with a `Photo Credits` link in the bottom-right corner and `Text size A A A` controls below it.
