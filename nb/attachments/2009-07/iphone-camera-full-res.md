# iPhone 3GS Full-Resolution Cropped Berry Photo (`iphone-camera-full-res.jpg`)

## Overview
A 1:1 full-resolution cropped section (`600 x 521` pixels out of the original `2048 x 1536` frame) of a photograph taken with an Apple iPhone 3GS camera on the morning of July 3, 2009 in New Hampshire. The image demonstrates the close-up detail and low-light/overcast performance of the newly released iPhone 3GS camera.

## Scene Description
- **Subject**: A tight cluster of ripening berries (blackberries or raspberries) growing on a green bramble stem.
- **Ripening Stages**:
  - A bright red-orange berry on the mid-left shows individual glossy drupelets and fine pale pistils protruding between them.
  - Two reddish-amber berries—one at the top center and one at the bottom center—are partially ripened with a mix of coral-red and yellowish-green tones.
  - A smaller green-and-blush berry sits in the upper right, and a mostly green, unripe berry sits directly in the center of the cluster.
  - Several smaller green, unripe berries and buds are partially visible on the left and far right.
- **Foliage & Stems**: Surrounding the berry cluster are vibrant green serrated leaves with prominent veins and tiny water droplets from recent rain, along with prickly green stems and calyces.
- **Image Characteristics**: Exhibits the typical optical characteristics of the early iPhone 3GS sensor (`3.8 mm` focal length, `f/2.8` aperture, `1/60 s` exposure at ISO 78), with soft pixel-level luminance noise and slight motion/focus softness in the fine leaf textures.
