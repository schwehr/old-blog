# Whale Tagging Team Aboard NOAA Ship Nancy Foster (`national-geographic-whale-tagging-2.png`)

## Overview
A video still frame from a National Geographic News feature showing CCOM/UNH and NOAA scientists—including Colin Ware and David Wiley—gathered around a workbench inside the science lab aboard the NOAA Ship *Nancy Foster* during a humpback whale tagging expedition off Massachusetts.
