# Offshore Construction Vessel Normand Clipper Bow View (`neptune-lng-construction-0.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph (credited to the U.S. Coast Guard) showing a port-bow three-quarter view of the offshore construction vessel *Normand Clipper* during the installation of the Neptune LNG Deepwater Port in Massachusetts Bay in July 2009.

## Vessel & Equipment Details
- **Hull & Bow**: The vessel features a high, flared bright red-orange hull with white draft marks and thruster/anchor symbols painted near the bulbous bow waterline. The ship's name `NORMAND CLIPPER` is painted in bold white uppercase letters along the port bow, with an orange enclosed free-fall lifeboat mounted just aft of the forecastle.
- **Superstructure & Bridge**: A white forward superstructure rises above the forecastle deck, topped by a wide, wrap-around navigation bridge with red-orange trim. Above the bridge sit two large white spherical satellite communication radomes, yellow twin exhaust funnels/masts, and a central black lattice radar/communications mast.
- **Heavy-Lift Crane & STL Buoy**: Aft of the superstructure on the port side towers a massive yellow offshore pedestal crane with its boom raised high against the blue-gray sky. Directly behind the crane base on the aft working deck sits a large yellow-and-black conical Subsea Turret Loading (STL) buoy labeled `APL` near its base. A curved blue chute extends over the port gunwale into the water.
