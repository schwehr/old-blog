# Offshore Construction Vessel Normand Clipper Port Broadside (`neptune-lng-construction-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A port-side broadside photograph (credited to the U.S. Coast Guard) of the offshore construction vessel *Normand Clipper* at sea in Massachusetts Bay during the July 2009 construction of the Neptune LNG Deepwater Port.

## Vessel & Deck Cargo Details
- **Vessel Profile**: Shown from port bow (left) to stern (right) on calm dark blue water under a clear sky. The bright red-orange hull carries `NORMAND CLIPPER` on both the port bow and the port quarter, with heavy vertical stern rollers built into the rounded fantail.
- **Forward Superstructure**: The white bridge and accommodation block sit forward, topped by two white satcom radomes, yellow funnels bearing an `S` logo (Solstad Offshore), and a tall radar mast.
- **Midships Deck & STL Buoys**:
  - A towering yellow heavy-lift crane rises midships with its main hook and auxiliary whip line suspended over the working deck.
  - Two large conical yellow-and-black Subsea Turret Loading (STL) buoys sit side-by-side on the midships deck, each marked `APL` with a stylized tree/triangle emblem at the base.
  - Multiple large blue-and-yellow reels and winches for mooring lines/umbilicals are arranged fore and aft of the two conical buoys, along with a curved blue overboard chute on the port side.
  - Several crew members in orange high-visibility coveralls stand on deck near the base of the second yellow buoy, illustrating the immense scale of the hardware.
- **Stern A-Frame / Pipe-Handling Rig**: At the stern rises a second large yellow gantry/A-frame structure overhanging the aft deck.
