# NOAA Ocean Explorer Homepage (`noaa-oceanexplorer-web.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X Firefox web browser screenshot of the NOAA Ocean Explorer website (`http://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/`), captured on July 13, 2009 as a follow-up to the earlier post about `explore.noaa.gov`.

## Browser Chrome & Top Banner
- **Browser Window**: Titled `NOAA, Ocean Explorer` with Mac OS X traffic-light window controls, navigation buttons, Delicious extension icons, URL bar displaying `http://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/` with an RSS feed icon and bookmark star, a Google search box on the right, and a bottom status bar showing `http://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/explorations/explorations.html` on the left and the `zotero` extension logo on the right.
- **Header Graphic**: A dark blue-green underwater collage banner displaying the title `ocean explorer` in italicized serif lettering, flanked by deep-sea coral/jellyfish imagery, a submersible, a suited diver/submersible pilot, and the circular NOAA emblem on the right.
- **Navigation Bar**: Black horizontal menu bar with links: `EXPLORATIONS | GALLERY | TECHNOLOGY | HISTORY | LIBRARY | EDUCATION | FOR FUN`, followed by a text input box and `Search` button.
- **Hero Photograph**: A large horizontal photograph of the starboard profile of the white research vessel *Okeanos Explorer* (`NOAA R 337`), flying a large U.S. flag from its mast and displaying its yellow stern A-frame and white satellite dome.
