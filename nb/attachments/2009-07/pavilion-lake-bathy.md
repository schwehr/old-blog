# Pavilion Lake 3D Bathymetry and Cross-Section Profile (`pavilion-lake-bathy.jpg`)

## Overview
A composite visualization from the Pavilion Lake Research Project (in British Columbia, Canada) combining a 3D perspective rendering of multibeam bathymetry draped within satellite terrain imagery on the left with a 2D bathymetric cross-section profile plot on the right.

## Left Panel: 3D Bathymetric Trough Visualization
- Shows an oblique 3D view looking along the narrow, winding basin of Pavilion Lake set between steep mountain slopes textured with satellite imagery (`Pavilion Lake` label partially visible along the right shoreline).
- The underwater lake floor is rendered as a high-resolution, color-shaded 3D bathymetric surface:
  - Shallow shelf and steep upper walls are colored bright red, orange, and yellow.
  - Intermediate slopes transition through yellow-green and bright green.
  - Deeper central basin areas are shaded cyan and blue, with black unmapped/shadow zones along the edges.
- In the foreground, a semi-transparent vertical cross-section plane cuts across the green U-shaped lake trough. The intersection curve is traced in bright cyan with circular node handles at the left rim, bottom-right inflection point, and right rim, plus a vertical double-line cursor marker.

## Right Panel: Cross-Section Profile Graph
- A 2D Cartesian plot displaying the bathymetric cross-section corresponding to the slice shown in the 3D view.
- **Vertical Axis (Depth)**: Ranges from `-12` at the top to `-37` (meters) at the bottom in 1-unit increments.
- **Horizontal Axis (Distance)**: Ranges from `0` on the left to `170` (meters) on the right in 10-unit grid increments.
- **Profile Curve & Color Fill**: Shows a steep U-shaped valley starting near `-16 m` on the left wall, dropping to a flat basin floor around `-37 m` between horizontal distances `75` and `105`, and rising steeply up the right wall to `-12 m` at distance `175`. The area under the curve is shaded with a vertical color gradient matching the 3D bathymetry (red/brown on the shallow flanks transitioning to yellow-green and bright green in the deep center).
- **Cursor Readout**: A vertical black line at approximately `x = 126` is labeled at the top of the plot with `Avg. Slope = 15.629` and `2D Dist = 0.2`.
