# Google Earth Right Whale Detection System Demo (`rightwhale-ge-chartdemo-1.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot of a KML demonstration (`ChartDemo-20070920.kmz`) created in September 2007 and posted on July 24, 2009, illustrating the Northeast Gateway (NEG) Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) Right Whale Detection System and vessel speed restriction zones in Massachusetts Bay along the Boston shipping lane.

## Map & Overlay Elements
- **Geographic View**: Overhead satellite and bathymetric relief view of Massachusetts Bay east of Boston and the South Shore (visible on the left), looking toward Stellwagen Bank.
- **Legend Box (Top Left)**: Semi-transparent gray overlay titled `NEG Whale Detection System` with two color-coded square legend items:
  - Bright green square: `No Whales Detected`
  - Bright yellow square: `Whale Detected - 10 knot max`
- **Time Slider**: Overlaid at the top left, displaying `Sep 21, 2007 3:55 am`.
- **Shipping Lane & Acoustic Buoy Zones**:
  - A red polygon outlines the Traffic Separation Scheme (shipping lane) and precautionary area approaching Boston Harbor.
  - A chain of 8 overlapping circular acoustic monitoring buffer zones runs west-to-east across the shipping lane. Seven of the circles are shaded translucent green with green outlines (`No Whales Detected`), while the seventh circle near the eastern edge is highlighted with a thick bright yellow border and yellow tint (`Whale Detected - 10 knot max`), labeled `01:40 (hh:mm) REMAINING`.
  - Two cyan bullseye circle icons mark the offshore LNG port sites just west of the red shipping lane boundary.

## Open Alert Balloon
Pointing to a star icon on the northern rim of the active yellow detection circle is a white Google Earth callout balloon containing:
- **Header**: `01:40 (hh:mm) REMAINING`
- **Main Alert Title**: `Right Whale Detected`
- **Speed Restriction Notice**: `LNG vessels required to stay `**`below 10 knots`** (with `below 10 knots` in bold red text).
- **Timestamps (Monospace)**:
  - `Restriction ends:   2007-09-21 05:18:00z`
  - `Whale detected: 2007-09-20 05:18:00`
- **Links**: `Additional Information` and `Directions: To here - From here`.
