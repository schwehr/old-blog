# Google Earth Right Whale Detection Chart Demo — Full Interface (`rightwhale-ge-chartdemo-2.png`)

## Overview
A full-screen macOS screenshot of Google Earth displaying a September 2007 demonstration (`ChartDemo-20070920.kmz`) of the Northeast Gateway (NEG) Whale Detection System overlaid on a NOAA nautical chart of Massachusetts Bay and the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Base Chart Overlay**: A georeferenced NOAA nautical chart covering Massachusetts Bay, Cape Cod Bay (including Provincetown/Race Point at the bottom right and Plymouth/Duxbury along the western shore), and the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (outlined in a thin red boundary polygon).
- **Shipping Lane & Acoustic Buoys**:
  - A purple-shaded Traffic Separation Scheme (TSS) shipping lane runs northwest-to-southeast across Stellwagen Bank and turns south-southeast offshore of Cape Cod.
  - Ten large, semi-transparent green circles are spaced along the centerline of the shipping lane, representing acoustic monitoring buoy detection zones when no right whales are detected ("No Whales Detected").
  - Near the northwestern end of the lane (upper left of the chart area), two circular cyan/black target icons mark the offshore LNG terminal positions (Northeast Gateway / Neptune LNG area).
- **Time Slider & Legend Overlay (Top Left of 3D View)**:
  - A translucent dark gray time-slider widget displays the current animation timestamp: `Sep 19, 2007  12:00 pm`, with a range from `Sep 19, 2007` to `Sep 22, 2007`.
  - Partially obscured behind the time slider is a legend titled **NEG Whale Detection System**, showing a green square (`No Whales Detected`) and a yellow square (`Whale Detected - 10 knot max`).
