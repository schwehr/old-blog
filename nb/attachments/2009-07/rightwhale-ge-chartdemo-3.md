# Google Earth Right Whale Detection Alert Popup (`rightwhale-ge-chartdemo-3.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot from the Google Earth Right Whale notice chart demonstration (`ChartDemo-20070920.kmz`), showing an active right whale detection alert and speed restriction popup balloon over the Boston harbor approach shipping lane in Massachusetts Bay.

## Alert & Map Details
- **Active Whale Detection Zone**:
  - The westernmost circular buoy zone along the Traffic Separation Scheme (near the two cyan/black offshore LNG port icons) is highlighted in bright yellow with a thick yellow border, indicating an active right whale detection.
  - A yellow star placemark icon sits on the northern edge of the yellow circle, accompanied by bold gray-shadowed map text reading `21:00 (hh:mm) REMAINING`.
  - The adjacent buoy circles further east across Stellwagen Bank remain shaded in translucent green.
- **Information Balloon**:
  - Clicking the alert placemark opens a white popup card containing:
    - **21:00 (hh:mm) REMAINING**
    - **Right Whale Detected** (centered heading)
    - `LNG vessels required to stay` **below 10 knots** (with "below 10 knots" in bold red text)
    - Monospace timestamps:
      - `Restriction ends: 2007-09-21 00:00:00z`
      - `Whale detected:   2007-09-20 00:00:00z`
    - Blue underlined links: `Additional Information` and `Directions: To here - From here`.
