# Google Earth Right Whale Detection Chart Demo — Eastern Buoy Alert (`rightwhale-ge-chartdemo-4.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Google Earth Right Whale notice chart demonstration (`ChartDemo-20070920.kmz`) at a later timestamp in the animation, showing a whale detection alert active at the turning point of the shipping lane on the eastern edge of Stellwagen Bank.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Nautical Chart & Shipping Lane**:
  - The NOAA nautical chart of Massachusetts Bay and Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary forms the background, showing soundings, LORAN lines, compass roses, and coastal features from Plymouth/Duxbury to Race Point on Cape Cod.
  - Ten circular acoustic detection zones follow the purple Traffic Separation Scheme lane.
- **Active Alert Zone**:
  - The sixth buoy circle from the west—located at the bend in the shipping lane near `Wildcat Knoll` and lighted whistle buoy `Y "BD" Fl (4) Y 20s WHIS`—is highlighted in bright yellow.
  - At the top-right edge of the yellow circle, a yellow star placemark is labeled with bold dark text: `01:40 (hh:mm) REMAINING`.
  - All other nine buoy circles (including the westernmost circle near the two cyan/black LNG terminal markers) are green.

## Time Slider & Legend Overlay
- In the top-left corner, the Google Earth time-slider control panel is set to `Sep 21, 2007  3:55 am` (on a timeline spanning `Sep 19, 2007` to `Sep 22, 2007`), overlaying the **NEG Whale Detection System** legend (`No Whales Detected` in green, `Whale Detected - 10 knot max` in yellow).
