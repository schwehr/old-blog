# Klein Sidescan Sonar Towfish on Deck (`sidescan-klein-test-towfish.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle photograph of a stainless-steel/silver Klein sidescan sonar towfish resting on the gray non-skid deck of a survey vessel prior to deployment during a SonarWiz training session in July 2009.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **Klein Towfish**:
  - The cylindrical, torpedo-shaped metal body lies vertically in the center of the frame, with its rounded nose cone pointing toward the bottom of the photo and its tail assembly at the top near the blue-trimmed gunwale.
  - Long black acoustic transducer arrays run along both the port and starboard sides of the main body.
  - A pivoted metal towing arm is mounted along the top centerline of the cylinder, with a black data/tow cable pig-tail strapped to it and a small strip of green tape on the side.
  - Two angled black tail fins form a V-tail at the rear; each fin carries a circular yellow-and-black Klein logo sticker.
- **Deck Surroundings**:
  - To the left, a coil of blue line or cable is partially visible at the edge of the frame.
  - To the lower right, a heavy metal towing block/sheave and a brass/bronze cable grip (come-along / Haven grip) rest on the gray deck.
