# Google Earth Sidescan Sonar Mosaic Near New Castle, NH (`sidescan-newcastle.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot displaying golden-brown sidescan sonar swath mosaics collected with a Klein towfish and processed via SonarWiz and Fledermaus (`20090710-newcastle-sidescan.kmz`), overlaid on satellite imagery at the mouth of the Piscataqua River near New Castle Island, New Hampshire.

## Map & Sonar Mosaic Details
- **Geographic Features & Labels**:
  - **New Castle** (marked with a red dot) and **New Castle Island** occupy the upper-left quadrant, showing residential streets, Fort Constitution, and a marina/mooring field in Little Harbor.
  - **Pest Island** and other small island labels appear along the left edge.
  - Across the river channel to the east (upper right) is the southern tip of Gerrish Island (Kittery, Maine), flanked by **Wood Island**, **White Island**, and **Horn Island**.
  - A thin white line traces the Maine–New Hampshire state boundary down the center of the channel and out to sea.
- **Sidescan Sonar Swaths**:
  - **Northern Swath**: A long, slightly curved golden-orange/brown sidescan sonar mosaic strip runs roughly north-south along the state boundary line between New Castle Island and Wood Island.
  - **Southern Swaths**: Further south, offshore of Jaffrey Point / Odiorne Point, several shorter, overlapping curved golden-brown sidescan swaths capture seafloor rock outcrops (bright acoustic backscatter with dark acoustic shadows) and sandy bottom features.
