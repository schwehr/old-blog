# Google Earth Sidescan Sonar Mosaic Showing Sediment Waves (`sidescan-sediment-waves.png`)

## Overview
A zoomed-in Google Earth screenshot oriented horizontally along the mouth of the Piscataqua River between New Castle Island, NH, and Wood Island, ME, highlighting a golden-brown sidescan sonar mosaic swath that reveals underwater sediment waves and bedforms.

## Map & Sonar Details
- **Orientation & Base Imagery**:
  - The view is rotated so north is to the left/top-left; **Wood Island** (with the Wood Island Life Saving Station) appears in the upper center, while the eastern shoreline of New Castle Island (including Fort Point / Fort Constitution) runs along the bottom.
  - The thin white Maine–New Hampshire boundary line runs horizontally across the middle of the frame.
- **Sidescan Sonar Swath**:
  - A continuous golden-amber sidescan sonar mosaic strip follows the channel, with a dark nadir gap/line running down the center of the swath.
  - In the left-center portion of the swath (where the track bends), distinct rhythmic ripples and elongated ridges of alternating bright acoustic reflections and dark shadows indicate large seafloor sediment waves / sand waves on the channel floor.
