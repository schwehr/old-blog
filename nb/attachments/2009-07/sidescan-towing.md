# Towing a Sidescan Sonar Towfish from the Stern A-Frame (`sidescan-towing.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph looking aft from the stern deck of a survey vessel (such as the *R/V Gulf Challenger* or *R/V Cocheco*) while towing a Klein sidescan sonar towfish through the water during a SonarWiz training session in July 2009.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **A-Frame & Tow Rigging**:
  - A heavy black tubular metal A-frame arches across the stern of the vessel, straddling the blue-trimmed transom and gray deck.
  - Mounted at the top center of the A-frame arch is a rectangular white halogen deck floodlight.
  - Suspended beneath the apex of the arch by a shackle and swivel is a metal towing block/sheave.
  - A blue electromechanical tow cable enters from the upper right (marked with red tape near the top-right corner), passes through the sheave, and angles down into the white-foaming prop wash/wake astern of the boat.
- **Deck Hardware & Background**:
  - On the port (left) side, black safety railings, stainless hydraulic/support rods, and an orange life ring are partially visible.
  - On the starboard (right) side, a large white spherical marine radar/satellite dome and coiled black cables sit near the gunwale.
  - Beyond the vessel's wake, dark blue coastal waters stretch toward a low, tree-lined shoreline under a clear summer sky.
