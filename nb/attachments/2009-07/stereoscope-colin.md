# Colin Ware Viewing Ship-Based Lidar Data in the Stereoscope (`stereoscope-colin.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of UNH CCOM Visualization Lab director **Colin Ware** seated at a custom dual-monitor Wheatstone mirror stereoscope, inspecting a 3D point cloud of Norfolk, Virginia collected by ship-based lidar and rendered in GeoZui.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **Subject**:
  - Colin Ware, seen from the back-left in a light blue short-sleeved button-down shirt and brown trousers, sits in a dark office chair and leans forward to look directly into the central angled mirror assembly.
- **Stereoscope Hardware**:
  - A long horizontal extruded aluminum T-slot rail supports two inward-facing flat-panel LCD monitors—one on the left (facing right) and one on the right (facing left)—mounted on vertical aluminum posts.
  - The left monitor screen is visible to the camera, showing a white-on-black 3D point-cloud rendering of Norfolk harbor structures and piers.
  - Between the two monitors sits a central post holding two angled mirrors at 45-degree angles, reflecting each monitor into the corresponding eye of the viewer.
  - Behind the central mirror post sits a black Dell desktop tower computer and a white coffee mug resting on the aluminum beam.
