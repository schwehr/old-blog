# Lowrance HDS-10 StructureScan and DownScan Sonar Display (`structure-scan.png`)

## Overview
A video frame screenshot (with a translucent `YouTube` watermark in the lower-right corner) showing the front panel and split-screen display of a **Lowrance HDS-10 Insight USA** marine multifunction display demonstrating Lowrance's **StructureScan** (sidescan) and **DownScan** sonar imaging.

## Split-Screen Sonar Views
- **Left Pane (StructureScan Sidescan View)**:
  - Displays a vertical waterfall sidescan sonar image in bronze/sepia tones (`800kHz`).
  - A bright vertical centerline (`0`) with a dark water-column band on either side separates the port (`Left`) and starboard (`Right`) acoustic swaths, which extend out to `60` feet on both sides (with `20` foot range markers).
  - Underwater pilings or bridge/pier structures cast distinct acoustic shadows across the bottom.
- **Upper-Right Pane (DownScan Imaging View)**:
  - Displays a high-frequency (`800kHz`) vertical profile from `0` to `60` feet (with tick labels at `0`, `10`, `20`, `30`, `40`, `50`, `60`), clearly resolving submerged vertical timber pilings/trees and bottom structure in grayscale/sepia tones.
- **Lower-Right Pane (Traditional 2D Broadband Sounder View)**:
  - Displays a conventional `200kHz` color echogram from `0` to `60` feet, showing speckled blue/white fish arches and water-column returns above a reddish-yellow/brown bottom contour.

## Interface & Hardware Controls
- **Bottom Softkey Menu Bar**:
  - `Contrast` (`74%`), `Range -`, `Range +`, `Frequency` (`800kHz`), `View` (`Left + right`), and `Stop sonar` (with a radio circle).
- **Right-Hand Physical Bezel Controls**:
  - Silver 4-way directional keypad/rotary dial at the top right.
  - Four pairs of rectangular push buttons: `ZOUT` / `ZIN`, `EXIT` / `ENTER`, `MENU` / `PAGES`, and `WPT` / `LIGHT/POWER`.
  - Model badge at the bottom right: `HDS 10 INSIGHT USA`.
