# NOAA Tidal Datum Comparison Plot for San Francisco and Alameda (`tide-techniques.png`)

## Overview
A line chart from the NOAA Special Publication NOS CO-OPS 2 (*Computational Techniques for Tidal Datums Handbook*) titled **"Simultaneous Comparison of Mean Range of Tide (Mn) for San Francisco and Alameda, CA"**, comparing monthly mean tidal range values over a 12-month period from March 1997 to February 1998.

## Plot Details & Axes
- **Title**: `Simultaneous Comparison of Mean Range of Tide (Mn) for San Francisco and Alameda, CA`
- **Vertical Axis (`mena range of tide (meters)` [sic])**:
  - Ranges from `0.800` to `1.800` meters in increments of `0.100` (`0.800`, `0.900`, `1.000`, `1.100`, `1.200`, `1.300`, `1.400`, `1.500`, `1.600`, `1.700`, `1.800`).
  - Note the typographical error in the axis title: `mena range of tide (meters)`.
- **Horizontal Axis (`time (months)`)**:
  - Labeled with 12 monthly ticks from March 1997 to February 1998: `3-97`, `4-97`, `5-97`, `6-97`, `7-97`, `8-97`, `9-97`, `10-97`, `11-97`, `12-97`, `1-98`, `2-98`.

## Data Series & Annotations
1. **`Mn - Alameda` (Blue Dashed Line)**:
  - Plots the monthly mean range of tide at Alameda, hovering between `1.49` and `1.55` meters from `3-97` through `1-98`, before dropping sharply to approximately `1.35` meters in `2-98`. An arrow points from the curve to the label `Mn - Alameda`.
2. **`Mn- San francisco` (Solid Black Line)**:
  - Plots the simultaneous monthly mean range of tide at San Francisco, staying relatively flat between `1.26` and `1.31` meters from `3-97` through `1-98`, and dropping sharply in parallel with Alameda to `1.100` meters in `2-98`. An arrow points from the curve to the label `Mn- San francisco`.
3. **`1960-78 Mn - San Francisco` (Red Dotted Horizontal Line)**:
  - A constant horizontal reference line at approximately `1.25` meters representing the 19-year National Tidal Datum Epoch (`1960-78`) mean range of tide at San Francisco. An arrow points from the dotted line to the label `1960-78 Mn - San Francisco`.
