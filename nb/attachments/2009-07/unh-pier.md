## Overview
A sunny, dock-level photograph looking down the concrete floating dock at the University of New Hampshire (UNH) Judd Gregg Marine Research Complex pier in New Castle, New Hampshire, showing the research vessels *R/V Cocheco* and *R/V Gulf Challenger* moored alongside.
