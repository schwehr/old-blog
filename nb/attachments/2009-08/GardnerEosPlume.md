# Kongsberg EM302 Multibeam Water-Column Ping of Northern California Gas Plume (`GardnerEosPlume.png`)

## Overview
A single-ping multibeam sonar water-column backscatter cross-section from a **Kongsberg EM302** multibeam echosounder aboard the **NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer**, published by Jim Gardner (CCOM/JHC) et al. in *Eos, Transactions American Geophysical Union* (Vol. 90, No. 32, August 11, 2009). The figure shows a massive, 1,400-meter-high acoustic gas plume rising through the water column from the continental slope off northern California.

## Plot & Acoustic Details
- **Fan Geometry & Color Scale:** The multibeam swath forms a triangular fan against a black background, extending from the sonar transducer at the apex (`6.5 m` depth) down to the bottom of the plot (`2522 m` depth). Acoustic backscatter intensity is color-coded from dark blue/indigo (low background water-column noise) through cyan and green to yellow, orange, and bright red (high acoustic reflectivity).
- **Seafloor Reflection:** Running horizontally across the lower third of the fan is a bright red, orange, and yellow band traced through its center by a continuous **white bottom-detection line**. The seafloor slopes gently from a rounded ridge on the left (north) across a flat central basin and down to the right (south).
- **Gas Plume Feature:**
  - Rising nearly vertically from a small mound/cone on the white seafloor trace just left of nadir (center) is a tall, narrow, slightly sinuous column of bright yellow, orange, and green acoustic backscatter.
  - A prominent **red arrow** in the upper-center right points diagonally down and left directly at the upper portion of the rising gas plume within the dark-blue mid-water zone.
  - Semicircular side-lobe arc artifacts are visible symmetrically in the water column on either side of the central region.

## Axes & Labels
- **Vertical Axis (Left):** Labeled `water depth (m)` vertically along the left border, with tick marks at `6.5` (top) and `2522` (bottom).
- **Horizontal Axis (Bottom):** Labeled `across-track distance from nadir (m)` at the bottom center, spanning from `-5762` on the far left to `4747` on the far right.
- **Orientation Labels:** White letters `N` (North) in the upper-left corner and `S` (South) in the upper-right corner of the plot frame.
