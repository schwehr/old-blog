# Bathymetric Perspective and Location Map of Northern California Gas Plume (`GardnerEosPlumeLocation.jpg`)

## Overview
A composite figure from Jim Gardner et al. (*Eos, Transactions American Geophysical Union*, Vol. 90, No. 32, August 11, 2009) showing a shaded-relief 3D multibeam bathymetric perspective of the continental slope off northern California where a large gas plume was discovered by the **NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer**, paired with a regional tectonic/bathymetric inset map in the lower-right corner.

## 3D Bathymetric Surface
- **Terrain & Color Shading:** The main background displays an oblique 3D shaded-relief bathymetric model colored by depth from bright green (deeper lower slope in the foreground/left) through yellow-green and yellow to warm orange-yellow (shallower upper slope at the top center). Deep blue background appears in the upper-left corner and in a jagged diagonal data-holiday gap cutting across the lower-left quadrant.
- **Seafloor Morphology & Plume Vent Site:**
  - In the upper-left quadrant, a prominent semicircular amphitheater-like slump scar or depression is carved into the continental slope, bounded by gullied walls.
  - Inside the floor of this depression sits a small, distinct conical mound/mud volcano. A bright **red arrow** points diagonally down and left directly at the summit of this cone, marking the source vent of the 1,400-meter gas plume.
  - To the right of the depression, the slope is dissected by ridges and submarine canyons descending toward the foreground.

## Regional Inset Map (Lower Right)
- **Layout:** A rectangular inset map with a black border in the bottom-right corner illustrates the regional plate-tectonic setting off the coast of northern **California** (shown in light gray on the right).
- **Tectonic & Bathymetric Features:**
  - The offshore region is shaded in two tones of light green, separated by a dashed black contour line labeled `2000 m` (the 2,000-meter isobath) running roughly north–south parallel to the California coast.
  - **Blanco FZ:** Labeled in the upper left along a northwest–southeast trending black fault line.
  - **Gorda Ridge:** Labeled vertically along a double black line with an offset spreading-center segment running north-northeast to south-southwest in the center-left.
  - **Mendocino FZ:** Labeled at the bottom along an east–west trending black fracture zone line extending seaward from Cape Mendocino.
  - **Plume Location Marker:** A bright **red five-pointed star** sits directly on the `2000 m` dashed contour line in the southern part of the basin, just north of the Mendocino Fracture Zone off northern California.
