# AprizeSat Two-Minute S-AIS Coverage Off South Africa (`aprizesat-south-africa-2min.png`)

## Overview
A **Google Earth** screenshot displaying a wide-area 3D globe view of Southern Africa and its surrounding oceans, illustrating two minutes of Space-Based AIS (S-AIS) vessel position data collected by **SpaceQuest's AprizeSat** satellites on July 29, 2009. Over this two-minute window, the receivers captured roughly 7,000 Class A position reports from 121 unique vessels (MMSIs).

## Map & Vessel Placemarks
- **Geographic Coverage:** The view is centered on the southern tip of the African continent, showing South Africa, Namibia, Botswana, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Lesotho, Swaziland, and Madagascar outlined in yellow country/provincial borders, surrounded by the South Atlantic Ocean to the west, the Indian Ocean to the east, and the Southern Ocean to the south.
- **AIS Ship Markers:** Hundreds of inverted teardrop-shaped Google Earth paddle/pin icons mark vessel positions along major maritime shipping lanes rounding the Cape of Good Hope and Cape Agulhas:
  - **Yellow Pins (with black center dots):** Represent individual AIS Class A position reports without full UTC hour/minute communication-state timestamps. Because up to 22 position reports were received per ship during the two-minute pass, closely spaced yellow pins stack tightly on top of each other at each vessel's location.
  - **Green Pins (solid light green):** Represent SOTDMA position reports that include UTC hour and minute timestamps (typically every 8th packet).
  - **Dark Olive / Brown Pins:** A small cluster of darker shaded pins appears near the Namibian coast (around Walvis Bay / Lüderitz).

## Interface & Controls
- **Time Slider Widget (Top Left):**
  - Displays the active two-minute time window: **`Jul 29, 2009 8:53:00 pm`** above a horizontal range bar spanning from **`8:51 pm`** on the left to **`8:53 pm`** on the right.
  - Includes playback step-back, play/loop, and step-forward buttons, zoom-out/zoom-in magnifying glass icons, and a wrench (options) button in the upper right of the widget.
- **Navigation Controls (Right Side):**
  - Standard Google Earth 3D navigation controls on the upper right: a circular look/orientation ring with a north indicator (`N`) and eye icon, a circular pan directional pad with a hand icon in the center, and a vertical zoom slider with `+` and `-` buttons.
