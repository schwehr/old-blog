# Cal Fire S-2T Air Attack 85 Cockpit (`cal-fire-cockpit.png`)

## Overview
An interior photograph looking forward into the cockpit of a **Cal Fire Grumman S-2T Turbo Tracker** air tactical aircraft (**Air Attack 85**) at the Sonoma Air Attack Base in California on August 7, 2009, taken during a NASA Ames GeoCam / DisasterCam field visit.

## Scene Description
- **Occupants:** Two men are seated in the pilot (left) and co-pilot/air tactical group supervisor (right) seats, both turning back over their shoulders toward the camera:
  - **Left Seat:** A man with short brown hair wearing a dark gray short-sleeved t-shirt and light gray cargo pants, resting his right hand on his knee.
  - **Right Seat:** A younger man with dark brown hair, rectangular metal-framed glasses, a blue-and-white plaid collared shirt, and blue jeans, resting his left hand on his knee.
- **Instrument Panel & Flight Controls:**
  - Between and in front of the two seats is the gray main instrument panel packed with analog and digital flight and engine gauges, dual control yokes (black rams-horn style with push-to-talk switches), attitude indicators (artificial horizons), airspeed and altitude dials, a central vertical stack of twin-turboprop engine monitoring gauges, and a central dark avionics/radio stack.
  - On the center pedestal below the main panel are engine condition/power levers with yellow-and-black striped hazard tape and yellow-handled levers, along with radio communication and tactical audio control panels.
  - White checklist and reference placards are affixed to the instrument panel and lower sub-panels.
- **Overhead Panel & Framing:**
  - Above the brightly backlit front windshields (blown out to pure white by the outdoor sunlight) is an complex overhead switch and circuit-breaker panel featuring rows of toggle switches, rotary knobs, and illuminated orange/amber indicator lights along the upper windshield frame.
  - Heavy black-painted, riveted sheet-metal bulkheads and high seat-back structures frame the foreground on both the left and right sides.
