# Cal Fire Dispatch Center Comparing GPhone and Google Earth (`cal-fire-google-earth.png`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken inside a **Cal Fire** command/dispatch center (Napa/Sonoma visit on August 7, 2009) showing a fire official comparing real-time field data on an Android "Google Phone" (GPhone) with a live **Google Earth** visualization on a desktop workstation monitor.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Subject:** Seen from behind and over the left shoulder is a Cal Fire officer wearing a dark navy/black uniform shirt, a dark gray baseball cap with `"Petersen"` embroidered in white across the back, and a navy neck lanyard printed with white `"ZETRON"` logos.
- **Handheld Device:** In his raised left hand, Petersen holds a dark gray/black touchscreen smartphone (an early Android dev phone / T-Mobile G1) in landscape orientation.
- **Workstation & Google Earth Display:**
  - His right hand operates a computer mouse on the desk in front of a wide black **Samsung** LCD monitor (`SAMSUNG` logo centered on the bottom bezel) with a black full-size keyboard and printed reference sheets in front of it.
  - The monitor displays a **Google Earth** window showing satellite terrain imagery of Northern California valleys and coastal mountains overlaid with colored placemark icons (pink/magenta and green circular targets/camera icons) and white instructional text at the top reading `"Double-click a camera icon to zoom into..."`.
- **Command Center Background:**
  - To the left is an angled desk extension holding a ruggedized silver/white laptop/display unit, radio/dispatch hardware, a digital clock/readout with red LED digits, and a plastic iced coffee cup.
  - Above the workstation partition is a second square LCD monitor tilted at a 45-degree angle (powered off or black screen) and a red-and-silver emergency beacon light mounted on the cubicle divider.
  - Covering the back wall are large wall-mounted regional dispatch maps dotted with colored pushpins, along with color-coded emergency resource status boards and charts.
