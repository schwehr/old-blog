# Cal Fire Tanker 85 at Sonoma Air Attack Base (`cal-fire-sonoma-air-station.png`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken at the **Cal Fire Sonoma Air Attack Base** (Sonoma County Airport, California) on August 7, 2009, showing a warning sign in the foreground and a Cal Fire **Grumman S-2T airtanker (Tanker 85)** parked on the tarmac in the background.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Warning Sign:**
  - Mounted on two dark green vertical posts on a manicured green grass lawn is a large dark-green rectangular metal sign with engraved/painted capital lettering:
    - **`DANGER`** (in large reddish-orange letters at the top)
    - **`AIRCRAFT TAXI AREA`** (in large white/cream letters in the middle)
    - **`NO UNAUTHORIZED PERSONNEL`**
    - **`BEYOND THIS POINT`** (in smaller white/cream letters across the bottom two lines)
  - To the right of the sign, green leafy branches of a shrub or small tree frame the right edge of the photo.
- **Aircraft (Cal Fire Tanker 85):**
  - Beyond the grass and asphalt taxiway, parked on a concrete ramp facing away and slightly to the left, is a twin-turboprop high-wing **Grumman S-2T Turbo Tracker** airtanker painted in Cal Fire's distinctive white, red, and black livery.
  - The vertical tail fin features a bold red band with the white aircraft number **`85`** below **`CDF`** in white letters on a black tail cap.
  - Red angled stripes wrap around the white rear fuselage ahead of the tail registration (`N438DF`), and bright red visibility bands are painted on the upper surfaces of both white wings.
- **Background Environment:**
  - Dry golden-brown summer grass borders the paved ramp and taxiway.
  - A line of green oak and eucalyptus trees stands behind the aircraft on the right, while low blue-gray coastal mountains rise in the distance on the left under a clear light-blue sky.
