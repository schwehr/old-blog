# USCGC Healy Multibeam Bathymetry Survey Display (`healy-20090814-multibeam.jpg`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a hydrographic survey navigation display from aboard the **USCGC *Healy* (WAGB-20)** on August 14, 2009 (`HEALY-0905` Arctic cruise), showing real-time multibeam echosounder bathymetry swaths overlaid on a gridded regional bathymetric chart of the Arctic Ocean north of Alaska.

## Map & Bathymetric Features
- **Coordinate Grid & Region:**
  - A blue background map with black latitude/longitude graticule lines:
    - **Longitude labels across the top:** `-153 30 00`, `-152 00 00`, `-150 30 00`, `-149 00 00`, `-147 30 00`, `-146 00 00`, `-144 30 00`, `-143 00 00` (153°30'W to 143°00'W).
    - **Latitude labels along the left side:** Ranging from `77 45 00` (77°45'N) at the bottom up to `79 00 00` (79°00'N) at the top (`77 45 00`, `77 52 30`, `78 00 00`, `78 07 30`, `78 15 00`, `78 22 30`, `78 30 00`, `78 37 30`, `78 45 00`, `78 52 30`, `79 00 00`).
- **Existing Shaded-Relief Bathymetry (Left):**
  - Along the western side of the chart (around 152°W–154°W, over the Chukchi Borderland / Northwind Ridge slope) is a detailed shaded-relief bathymetric surface with depth contours (`-1000`, `-2000`, `-3000`) colored from orange/yellow and light green on shallower highs (~1,000–2,000 m) through light blue to dark purple along the steep deep-water escarpment (>3,000–3,800 m).
- **Multibeam Survey Swaths & Vessel Icon:**
  - **Active Northbound Track (Center):** Running nearly due north along ~147°30'W is a dark purple multibeam swath (where purple represents deep abyssal plain depths of ~3,800 m) with a bright green/cyan centerline trace.
  - **Ship Symbol:** At the northern tip of this central swath (near 79°00'N, 147°30'W) is a white-outlined, green-filled ship icon representing *USCGC Healy* heading north, labeled with a red/white timestamp (`2009/08/14 02:23`).
  - **Waypoints / Expendable Probe Drops:** Along the active central track are orange/yellow labels marking expendable bathythermograph (XBT) / sound velocity casts and waypoints, including `xbt128`, `xbt129`, `w7`, `xbt130`, `w8`, `xbt131`, and `w9`.
  - **Prior Multibeam Tracks:** Another continuous SSW–NNE dark purple multibeam swath runs slightly west of the active line (between ~151°W and 149°W), a dogleg swath connects to the western slope near 78°N, and a short E–W purple swath segment appears near the upper right (~78°45'N, 143°W).
  - **Reference Line:** A thin white curve runs nearly vertically along the right side (near 145°W) with a vertical label at the bottom right.
