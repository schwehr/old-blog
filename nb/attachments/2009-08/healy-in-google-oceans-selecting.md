# Enabling the Ocean Expeditions Layer in Google Earth (`healy-in-google-oceans-selecting.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Layers** tree menu in **Google Earth**, demonstrating how to enable the **Ocean Expeditions** layer under the top-level **Ocean** category to track research vessels such as the USCG icebreaker *Healy*.

## Interface & Layer Hierarchy
- **Parent Category**: At the top left, an expanded disclosure triangle (`▼`), a blue minus-box checkbox (`[-]`), and a small blue globe icon precede the parent label **Ocean**.
- **Child Layers**: Below **Ocean**, twelve indented sub-layers are listed vertically, each with a square checkbox and a custom layer icon:
  1. **Explore the Ocean** — unchecked; circular blue-and-white wave icon.
  2. **National Geographic** — collapsed (`▶`), unchecked; yellow vertical rectangle border icon.
  3. **BBC Earth** — unchecked; purple rectangle icon with white `BBC` text.
  4. **Cousteau Ocean World** — unchecked; grayscale portrait icon of Jacques Cousteau wearing a red beanie.
  5. **Ocean Sports** — collapsed (`▶`), unchecked; orange square icon with a white `W`.
  6. **Shipwrecks** — unchecked; gray sunken ship / propeller icon.
  7. **Ocean Expeditions** — **checked (`☑`)** and highlighted with a light blue selection bar across the row; features a blue tall-ship / sailing vessel icon.
  8. **Marine Protected Areas** — unchecked; blue-and-green globe icon.
  9. **ARKive: Endangered Ocean ...** — unchecked; dark blue square icon with `ARK` in orange/white.
  10. **State of the Ocean** — collapsed (`▶`), unchecked; light blue square icon with a blue line-graph waveform.
  11. **Animal Tracking** — unchecked; gray shark silhouette icon.
  12. **Census of Marine Life** — unchecked; small cluster of colored dots (green, teal, blue, purple).
  13. **Marie Tharp Historical Map** — unchecked; square thumbnail of a purple/blue bathymetric map.
