# USCGC Healy Aloftcon Layer in Google Oceans (`healy-in-google-oceans.png`)

## Overview
A **Google Earth** screenshot taken on August 27, 2009, showing the integration of the **USCGC *Healy* Aloftcon** GeoRSS feed into Google Earth's official **Ocean Expeditions** layer (`Google Oceans`), displaying the icebreaker's high-Arctic position (`81.178° N, 151.196° W`) and live mast-camera imagery.

## 3D Globe & Map View
- **Geographic Perspective:** High-altitude oblique view looking south/southwest across the deep blue bathymetry of the Arctic Ocean (showing the prominent **Northwind Ridge** and **Chukchi Plateau** in the center-left) toward the northern coast of Alaska and Siberia along the curved horizon under a black space sky with a blue atmospheric glow.
- **Expedition Icons:**
  - Near the center (`81.1778° N, 151.1965° W`), a square blue-and-yellow sailing ship icon labeled **`Aloftcon`** marks the current location of the *USCGC Healy*.
  - Farther south near the Alaskan coast (upper left), a second small blue sailing ship icon marks another Ocean Expeditions entry.
