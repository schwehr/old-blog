# Larry Mayer Taking Roll Call Near Landing Craft in Barrow, Alaska (`larry-taking-roll-call-near-landing-craft.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph showing **Larry Mayer** (Director of the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center at the University of New Hampshire) standing on a dark gravel/sand beach in Barrow, Alaska, checking a roll-call list before boarding a landing craft to transfer out to the USCG icebreaker *Healy*.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Right)**: Larry Mayer stands in the right foreground facing slightly right and looking down at small white papers/notes held in both hands with a pen. He is wearing a bright red-orange survival/float suit (exposure suit) with black trim, a black hood collar, a black waist belt, reflective silver tape strips across the shoulders and chest, and a yellow name/agency patch on the right chest.
- **Beach & Terrain**: The foreground and middle ground consist of dark brown, coarse Arctic beach sand and gravel piled into uneven mounds and tire/track ruts leading down to the water's edge.
- **Landing Craft / Barge (Left Background)**: Beached at the shoreline on the left is a black-hulled landing craft / cargo barge with its bow ramp lowered onto the gravel beach:
  - The black bow structure on the port side bears the white painted name **`GRETA`** and has black rubber tire fenders hanging along the hull side.
  - Stacked on the deck behind the bow ramp are large reddish-brown shipping containers and a white container.
  - Several crew members or scientists in dark jackets and jeans are visible near the bow ramp and standing on the deck near blue cargo pallets.
  - Above the red containers rises a white wheelhouse/superstructure with a mast, radar antennas, and red block lettering partially visible across the front reading **`BOWHEAD`** (Bowhead Transport).
- **Water & Sky**: Calm, pale gray-green Arctic Ocean water stretches across the background under an overcast, uniform light-gray sky.
