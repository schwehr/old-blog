# Extended Continental Shelf Constraint Lines Diagram (`law-of-the-sea-extent.jpg`)

## Overview
A cross-sectional schematic diagram illustrating the two maximum constraint lines (**Max 1** and **Max 2**) used to define the outer limits of a nation's **Extended Continental Shelf (ECS)** under Article 76 of the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS).

## Diagram Layout & Geological Cross-Section
- **Title Banner**: A dark blue header bar across the top features bold white text: **`Extended Continental Shelf Constraint Lines`**.
- **Geological Layers**:
  - **Mantle**: At the bottom, an orange-red layer labeled **`Mantle`** on the right.
  - **Crust**: Above the mantle, a medium brown layer thickest near the coast on the left and thinning toward the deep ocean on the right, labeled **`Crust`**.
  - **Sediments**: Above the crust, a layered tan/cream wedge of continental margin sediments sloping down from the coast across the continental shelf, slope, and rise, labeled **`Sediments`**.
  - **Ocean Water**: A light-to-medium blue water column sits above the sediments, topped with a wavy surface line.
- **Horizontal Distance Axis**: Along the bottom edge, tick marks measure distance seaward from the baseline in nautical miles: **`0 nm`**, **`100 nm`**, **`200 nm`**, **`300 nm`**, and **`400 nm`**.

## Boundary Markers & Constraint Lines
- **Coast/Baseline & EEZ**:
  - At `0 nm` on the left, a vertical black line with a downward arrow marks **`Coast/Baseline`** at the shoreline.
  - A horizontal black arrow extends from `0 nm` to a vertical line at **`200 nm`**, labeled **`EEZ`** (Exclusive Economic Zone).
- **Max 1 (350 nm Constraint)**:
  - A long horizontal red arrow extends from the `0 nm` baseline line out to a vertical dashed red line at `350 nm`, labeled **`350 nm`** in red below the arrow and topped with a red badge reading **`Max 1`**.
- **Max 2 (2,500 m Isobath + 100 nm Constraint)**:
  - Over the continental slope (around `260 nm`), a vertical black double-headed arrow extends from the sea surface down to the seafloor, labeled **`2,500 m isobath`** above the surface.
  - From the base of the `2,500 m isobath` arrow on the seafloor, a horizontal green arrow extends `100 nm` seaward to a vertical dashed green line (near `385 nm`), labeled **`2,500 m isobath + 100 nm`** in green on the right and topped with a green badge reading **`Max 2`**.
- **Legend Box**: In the upper right sky area, a light blue-gray bordered box summarizes the rules:
  - **`Max 1: 350 nautical miles (nm) from baselines`** (in red) **`OR`** (in black)
  - **`Max 2: 2,500 meter (m) isobath plus 100 nm`** (in green)
