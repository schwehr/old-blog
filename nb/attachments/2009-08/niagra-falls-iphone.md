# Aerial View of Niagara Falls from an iPhone (`niagra-falls-iphone.png`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken with an Apple iPhone camera from a commercial airplane cruising at 36,000 feet, showing the **Niagara River** and **Niagara Falls** highlighted by an orange oval annotation.

## Scene & Geographic Features
- **Niagara River**: The wide blue ribbon of the upper Niagara River enters from the lower right and splits around **Grand Island** (the large landmass in the lower-left/center foreground) before recombining and flowing toward the left-center.
- **Niagara Falls (Highlighted)**: An orange oval on the middle-left encloses the white mist plume and foamy whitewater of **Niagara Falls** (Horseshoe Falls and American Falls), where the river plunges into the narrower Niagara Gorge winding northward toward the upper center.
- **Surrounding Landscape**: The surrounding terrain of western New York (right/bottom) and Ontario, Canada (left/top) shows a hazy blue-green patchwork of agricultural fields, urban street grids, roads, and bright white rooftops or industrial buildings reflecting sunlight.
- **Image Quality**: Characteristic of an early-generation iPhone camera shooting through an airliner window at high altitude, the image exhibits heavy digital luminance/chrominance noise, atmospheric haze, and soft focus.
