# USCGC Healy Aloftcon Camera View of Arctic Sun (`20090910-0501-healy-aloftcon-small.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph captured by the Aloftcon (aloft conn tower) camera aboard the icebreaker USCGC *Healy* on September 10, 2009 at 05:01 UTC during the 2009 U.S. Law of the Sea Arctic mapping cruise, showing the vessel sailing toward a low sun over ice-strewn polar waters.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Sun:** The bright golden-white orb of the low sun sits just above the horizon slightly right of center, surrounded by a warm orange glow that pierces through low Arctic fog and grey-blue clouds.
- **Ocean & Sea Ice:** A bright vertical reflection path of golden and orange light shimmers across the dark, calm water from the horizon toward the ship's bow. Scattered white and pale blue sea ice floes of varying sizes drift across the water surface, concentrated more heavily on the right side and near the bow.
- **Ship Bow (Foreground):** At the bottom center of the frame, looking down from the aloft conn, the curved forward deck and bow of the USCGC *Healy* are silhouetted, including the central jackstaff mast at the bow tip and white safety railings and deck masts along the port and starboard sides.
