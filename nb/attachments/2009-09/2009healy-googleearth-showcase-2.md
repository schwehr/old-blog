# USCGC Healy Arctic Cruise in Google Earth Browser Plugin (`2009healy-googleearth-showcase-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Google Earth Outreach Showcase project page for "2009 U.S. Law of the Sea - Arctic" by Kurt Schwehr, displaying the USCGC *Healy* cruise track and ship icons rendered live inside the embedded Google Earth Browser Plugin.

## Page Layout & Navigation
- **Top Header:** Features the `Google earth` logo on the top left and a `Change language:` dropdown menu set to `English (US)` on the top right.
- **Left Sidebar Navigation:** Links for `Outreach Home`, `Showcase` (bold/active), `Tutorials`, `Community`, `Grants`, `Earth Home`, and `Download Google Earth`.
- **Project Metadata (Left Column):**
  - Title in green: `2009 U.S. Law of the Sea - Arctic`
  - `Author: Kurt Schwehr`
  - Summary: `Hourly pictures and science reports from the USCGC Healy.`
  - Link: `Download KML file to view in Google Earth`

## Embedded Google Earth Plugin View (Right Column)
- **Map & Bathymetry:** Shows a 3D view over the deep Arctic Ocean seafloor (blue shaded bathymetry) with an orange ship track line looping northward.
- **Ship Icons & Timestamps:** Multiple USCGC *Healy* ship icons sit along the northern portion of the orange track, labeled with red/orange UTC time strings (`05:01`, `06:01`, `07:01`, `09:01`, `10:01`, `15:01`, `19:01`, `20:01`, `21:01`, `22:01`, `23:01`) and a white timestamp `26 Aug 16:30`. A yellow television icon labeled `imeLapse` (TimeLapse) sits along the western branch of the track.
- **Plugin Overlays & Controls:**
  - Top time slider bar showing `Aug 29, 2009 12 am`.
  - Right-side Google Earth navigation controls (compass ring with `N`, look/pan directional pads, and vertical zoom slider).
  - Bottom attribution and status bar: `Data SIO, NOAA, U.S. Navy, NGA, GEBCO`, `Image IBCAO`, `Image © 2009 TerraMetrics`, and coordinates `78°14'21.57" N  149°02'31.74" W  elev -3766 m Eye alt...`.
  - Below the plugin frame is a link reading `Enlarge Google Earth window`.
