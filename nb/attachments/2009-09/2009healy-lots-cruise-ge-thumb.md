# USCGC Healy Icebreaker Thumbnail (`2009healy-lots-cruise-ge-thumb.png`)

## Overview
A small thumbnail photograph (100x50 pixels) of the United States Coast Guard Cutter *Healy* (WAGB-20) breaking through Arctic sea ice, used as the gallery thumbnail for the 2009 U.S. Law of the Sea cruise in the Google Earth Outreach Showcase.

## Visual Details
- **Vessel:** Shows a port-bow aerial view of the USCGC *Healy* with its bright red hull, diagonal white Coast Guard racing stripe near the bow, white superstructure, buff-colored funnel, and dark aloft conn tower rising amidships.
- **Surrounding Environment:** The icebreaker is surrounded by fractured white and grey sea ice floes floating in dark polar water.
