# USCGC Healy 2009 Law of the Sea Cruise KML Visualization (`2009healy-lots-cruise.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth desktop screenshot showcasing the KMZ visualization of the 2009 USCGC *Healy* Law of the Sea (LOTS) Arctic mapping cruise. The view looks northward across the Chukchi Sea and Arctic Ocean toward the horizon, featuring the orange ship track, hourly ship placemarks, and an open balloon with an embedded YouTube Aloftcon time-lapse video.
