# McMurdo Station Ross Island Webcam Screenshot (`antarctica-mcmurdo-webcam.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the United States Antarctic Program (USAP) live webcam interface for McMurdo Station on Ross Island, Antarctica, captured on August 31, 2009 as the station approached the end of the austral winter season.

## Webcam Image & Scene Description
- The main rectangular viewport shows a grey, low-contrast twilight view across snow-covered terrain and distant hills at McMurdo Station under a pale overcast sky.
- In the right foreground, a dark, rocky, snow-dusted slope (Observation Hill / station foreground ridge) angles downward from right to center-left, overlooking the flat white ice of McMurdo Sound and distant snow-covered ridges in the background.
