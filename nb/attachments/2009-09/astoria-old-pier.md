# Astoria-Megler Bridge and Old River Pilings at Dusk (`astoria-old-pier.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph looking northwest across the Columbia River in Astoria, Oregon, showing the green steel cantilever truss span of the Astoria-Megler Bridge rising above a field of old wooden pier pilings in the foreground water under a moody twilight sky.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Pilings):** Hundreds of dark, weathered wooden pilings from a former cannery or shipping pier jut out of the rippled, slate-grey water of the Columbia River in grid-like rows across the lower third of the image.
- **Midground (Astoria-Megler Bridge & Barge):**
  - The large green steel through-truss main span of the Astoria-Megler Bridge angles from the upper right toward the center-left, supported by two tall A-frame steel truss piers resting on wide concrete fenders in the river.
  - Beyond the second pier on the left, the bridge descends into a long, low causeway stretching miles across the river toward the Washington shore.
  - Between the two main bridge piers, a tugboat pushing or towing a long flat barge moves across the channel.
- **Background & Sky:** Low dark hills line the distant Washington coast beneath a dramatic overcast sky tinged with pale lavender, blue, and soft pink dusk light.
