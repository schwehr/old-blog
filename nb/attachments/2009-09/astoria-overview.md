# Hillside View of the Astoria-Megler Bridge (`astoria-overview.jpg`)

## Overview
A sunny panoramic photograph taken from partway up the hillside in Astoria, Oregon, looking north across the Columbia River toward the green steel truss span of the Astoria-Megler Bridge and the hills of Washington State on the opposite shore.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Astoria Waterfront & Trees):** In the lower left, deciduous trees and dark evergreen conifers frame the hillside view. Along the bottom right waterfront sits a light-colored multi-story building (hotel/commercial complex), while the curved elevated approach ramp of the bridge sweeps from the lower left toward the main span.
- **Center (Astoria-Megler Bridge):** Viewed nearly along its length, the tall green steel cantilever truss rises prominently above the deep blue river water, supported by steel-framed piers on concrete bases, before sloping down into the long, low concrete viaduct that stretches across the wide estuary to the distant shore.
- **Background:** Rolling dark ridge lines of the Washington coast span the horizon beneath a clear, cloudless blue sky.
