# M/V Lake Express Ferry Track Across Lake Michigan in Google Earth (`ferries.png`)

## Overview

A Google Earth 3D perspective screenshot from a September 1, 2009 blog post titled *"Ferries"*, illustrating Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel tracking data for a single high-speed ferry crossing Lake Michigan.

## Map & Track Details

- **Geographic Region**: An oblique, tilted satellite view looking north-northwest across the Great Lakes region, prominently featuring **Lake Michigan** in the foreground/left and **Lake Huron** (with Georgian Bay) to the upper right, bordered by yellow coastline outlines over green and brown satellite terrain imagery.
- **Ferry Track**: A straight white line crosses the southern-central basin of Lake Michigan between Wisconsin (Milwaukee) on the western shore and Michigan (Muskegon) on the eastern shore.
- **Highlight & Labels**:
  - An orange oval is drawn around the white crossing track on Lake Michigan.
  - Above the oval, two lines of orange sans-serif text identify the vessel's MMSI number and name:
