# Port-Side Profile of NOAA Ship Ferdinand R. Hassler Suspended from a Crane (`hassler-crane-port-side.png`)

## Overview

A video still frame from a September 29, 2009 blog post titled *"Hassler on a crane"*, showing a full port-side broadside view of the newly built Small Waterplane Area Twin Hull (SWATH) coastal mapping vessel **NOAA Ship *Ferdinand R. Hassler* (S 250)** suspended in mid-air from a heavy shipyard crane.

## Scene Description

- **Hull & Markings**:
  - The upper hull is painted white with a dark gray-black lower SWATH pontoon hull completely out of the water.
  - Near the port bow, bold black lettering reads **`NOAA`** and **`S 250`** alongside the circular blue-and-white NOAA emblem (featuring a stylized white seabird/gull over dark and light blue waves).
  - Below the bow markings are white draft marks and a circular bow thruster tunnel icon (`⊗`).
  - Along the port quarter near the stern, black uppercase text reads **`FERDINAND R. HASSLER`**.
  - At the aft end of the submerged dark gray pontoon, a multi-bladed propeller and a tall vertical rudder are clearly visible.
- **Superstructure & Rigging**:
  - The midships deckhouse and bridge level are coated in olive-green and primer brownish-red paint with rectangular white-framed windows and a black A-frame crane mounted on the aft deck.
  - Four heavy steel lifting slings angle upward from two brown lifting pads along the port hull to the towering lattice crane boom centered behind the ship.
