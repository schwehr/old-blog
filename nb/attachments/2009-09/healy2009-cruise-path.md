# USCGC Healy 2009 Cruise Track from Seattle to the High Arctic in Google Earth (`healy2009-cruise-path.png`)

## Overview

A Google Earth 3D globe screenshot from a September 4, 2009 blog post titled *"Healy 2009 Law of the Sea cruise path"*, displaying the full 2009 cruise track of the U.S. Coast Guard Cutter *Healy* (WAGB-20) from Seattle, Washington, through the Bering Strait to the high Arctic Ocean (covering April 28 to August 4, 2009), along with a Google Earth Ruler dialog measuring the total straight-line distance.

## Map & Track Details

- **Globe Orientation**: An oblique view of the North Pacific and Arctic Ocean with **North oriented to the right**. The west coast of North America (Vancouver Island, British Columbia, and Southeast Alaska) begins at the bottom left, curving past the Aleutian Islands and through the Bering Strait (upper center) into the Chukchi Sea and high Arctic Ocean (bottom right).
- **Cruise Track**: A dense ribbon of purple/lavender Google Earth map pins with black center dots traces *Healy*'s path northward along the Inside Passage/Gulf of Alaska, around the Alaska Peninsula and Aleutians, north through the Bering Strait, and out into complex survey grid patterns in the Arctic Ocean north of Barrow, Alaska, reaching up to 84.222° N.
- **Measurement Line**: A white great-circle line connects a red endpoint dot at the southern start of the track near Seattle (bottom left) to a red endpoint dot at the northernmost survey point in the Arctic (bottom right).
