# MotionX-GPS Lite Waypoint Summary for Pier Gravity Tie in Astoria, Oregon (`iphone-gps-motion-x-waypoint.png`)

## Overview

An iPhone 3GS screenshot from a September 29, 2009 blog post titled *"iPhone GPS"*, showing the **Waypoint Summary** screen in MotionX-GPS Lite for a waypoint (`Wypt 001`) recorded on September 24, 2009 at the 4th tie point (cleat/bollard) from the west on the pier in Astoria, Oregon, during a gravity tie-in for the R/V *Marcus G. Langseth*.

## Interface & Visible Data

- **iPhone Status Bar (Top)**: Displays 5 signal bars for `AT&T`, Wi-Fi icon, current time `9:49 AM`, and a charging battery icon.
- **Header Bar**: Features a silver info button (`i`) on the left, a dark display panel reading `Waypoint Summary` in cyan text, and a red-tinted globe icon on the right.
- **Waypoint Fields** (amber/orange digital text on dark backgrounds):
  - **Name:** `Wypt 001`
  - **Notes:** `Sep 24, 2009  9:42 am`
  - **Bearing:** `274°T?`
  - **Altitude (m):** `5`
  - **Distance (km):** `4,200?` (reflecting the ~4,200 km distance from the user's current location back in New Hampshire to Astoria, Oregon)
  - **Lat:** `46.190456° N`
  - **Lon:** `123.859195° W`
- **Action Buttons (Bottom)**:
  - **Upper Row**: Four rounded rectangular buttons: `Delete` (red), `Edit` (blue), `Photo` (blue), and `View` (blue).
  - **Lower Row**: Three buttons: `Back` (blue left-pointing arrow button), `Share Wypt Now` (wide blue button), and `Start Nav` (bright green button).
