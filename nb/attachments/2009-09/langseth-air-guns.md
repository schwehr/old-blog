# Seismic Air Gun Array Hangar on R/V Marcus G. Langseth (`langseth-air-guns.jpg`)

## Overview

An interior deck photograph from a September 24, 2009 blog post titled *"Tour of the Langseth"*, looking down an aisle of suspended seismic air guns and heavy chains inside the gun deck/hangar aboard the Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory (LDEO) seismic research vessel **R/V *Marcus G. Langseth***.

## Scene Description

- **Air Gun Assemblies**: Arranged in two long parallel rows hanging from overhead tracks and black umbilical bundles along the ceiling. Each assembly consists of heavy, rusted steel link chains suspending cylindrical stainless-steel air gun chambers, coiled metal spring shock absorbers/spacers, high-pressure air hoses, and electrical sensor cables.
- **Deck & Bulkheads**:
  - The steel deck floor is painted dark blue-gray with worn patches, rust stains, and yellow metal tie-down bars and hooks anchored to the floor to keep the suspended guns from swinging at sea.
  - To the right is a white-painted interior steel bulkhead with rust staining along the bottom baseboard and black cables hanging from the overhead rack.
  - At the far left, open deck doorways let in bright daylight overlooking the ship's aft working deck.
