# dKart Navigator ECDIS Display on the Bridge of R/V Marcus G. Langseth (`langseth-dkart.jpg`)

## Overview

A photograph from a September 24, 2009 blog post titled *"Tour of the Langseth"*, showing a bridge console monitor running **dKart Navigator** Electronic Chart Display and Information System (ECDIS) software on the starboard side of the bridge aboard the **R/V *Marcus G. Langseth*** while berthed in Astoria, Oregon.

## Interface & Chart Details

- **Main Chart Pane (Upper Left/Center)**:
  - Displays a small-scale vector nautical chart of the Pacific Northwest coastline (showing the mouth of the **Columbia River** at the bottom right, the Washington coast, Puget Sound, and Vancouver Island extending northward).
  - Centered over the Columbia River estuary (Astoria, OR) is a black concentric target circle marking the vessel's current position, surrounded by a dense cluster of green Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel target symbols and text labels.
  - A straight brown/orange planned route line extends northwestward from the Columbia River mouth out into the northeast Pacific Ocean toward the survey area.
- **Route Waypoint Table (Bottom Left)**:
  - A tabular list under the datum header `WGS 1984` shows columns for waypoint number, `Name`, `Lat`, `Lon`, `Speed kt`, `Rad. gyr. Nm`, `Course °`, `RBD Nm`, and `Total RBD Nm`.
  - Visible waypoint names along the Columbia River departure route include:
    - `2 Desdemona` (`46°11.500'N`, `123°54.400'W`)
    - `3 Youngs Point`
    - `4 Chinook Spit`
    - `5 Sand Island`
    - `6 Red '10'`
    - `7 North Jetty`
    - `8 Red '2'`
    - `9 Columbia River Buoy`
    - `10 Survey Area` (highlighted in blue at the bottom: `53°26.365'N`, `141°04.442'W`, course `303.7°`, distance `785.08` Nm, total distance `802.00` Nm).
- **Right Control & Status Sidebar**:
  - **Title & Time**: `dKart Navigator-ECDIS`, `18:04:28 Wed 23 Sep`, `Under Way Using E...`
  - **Own-Ship Position**: `46°11.351'N`, `123°51.554'W`
  - **Kinematics**: `SOG 0.0 kt`, `COG 248.0°`, `HDG 318.2°`, `Main GPS`
  - **Active Route Info**: `MGL 0911 to site`, `WPT 1 (A) Bern`, `TTW 22:04:36 1 day(s)`, `BTW 325.7°`, `DTW 0.241 Nm`
  - **Chart Scale & Mode**: `Not for coastal nav.`, `1 : 5 000 000` (in red text), `Full`, `W84`
  - **On-Screen Buttons**: Grid of rectangular control buttons including `CHART`, `SCALE`, `LEVEL`, `MOB`, `ROUTE`, `POINT`, `ETA`, `MSG`, `ARPA`, `AIS`, `WARN`, `PLOT`, `NAV`, `RPL`, `LBK`, `LAY`, `2D/M`, `ESM`, `3D/M`, `EXIT`, and zoom/cursor controls at the bottom right.
