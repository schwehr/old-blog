# Top View of LaCoste & Romberg Model G Gravity Meter Used for Pier Tie-In (`langseth-gravity-tie-in.jpg`)

## Overview

A top-down close-up photograph from a September 24, 2009 blog post titled *"Tour of the Langseth"*, showing a portable **LaCoste & Romberg Inc. Model G Geodetic Gravity Meter (`G-207`)** resting on a concrete pier in Astoria, Oregon, during a land-to-ship gravity tie-in calibration for the R/V *Marcus G. Langseth*.

## Instrument Details & Visible Markings

- **Enclosure**: A square, dark gray anodized/painted metal top plate with rounded corners, secured by flathead screws around the perimeter, with a black power/heater cable extending off the lower right edge.
- **Nulling Dial & Counter (Lower Left)**:
  - A large circular brushed-aluminum graduated measuring dial marked from `0` to `9` (with fine tick subdivisions) and a pointer tab fixed at the top (`12 o'clock` position, currently reading just past `6`).
  - Adjacent to the right side of the dial is a small rectangular glass counter window displaying the mechanical digital reading `258` (with a fractional digit to the right).
- **Optical Eyepiece & Spirit Levels (Upper & Right)**:
  - **Eyepiece (Upper Right)**: A black cylindrical microscope/reading telescope tube projects upward toward the viewer.
  - **Spirit Levels**: Two perpendicular glass bubble levels are recessed into the top plate—one horizontal vial near the top center/left and one vertical vial to the right of the eyepiece—plus a third long greenish-yellow tubular window near the lower right.
  - **Clamping / Adjusting Knob (Center Right)**: A smaller black-and-silver knurled knob sits below the eyepiece.
  - **Toggle Switch (Far Right)**: A small metal toggle switch is mounted near the right edge.
- **Identification Plates (Bottom)**:
  - **Manufacturer Nameplate (Bottom Center)**: A silver metal plate stamped with:
    - `[LACOSTE] & ROMBERG, INC.`
    - `GRAVITY METER`
    - **`G-207`** (stamped in the serial number box)
    - `U.S. PATENTS ...` (left) and `MADE IN AUSTIN, TEXAS U.S.A.` (right).
  - **Calibration Plate (Bottom Right)**: A small black plate with white engraved lettering reading:
    - `SENS. 10 DIAL = 10 E. P.`
    - `RDG. LINE 2.70`
