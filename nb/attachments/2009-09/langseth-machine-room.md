# Computer Server and Instrument Rack Room on R/V Marcus G. Langseth (`langseth-machine-room.jpg`)

## Overview

An interior photograph from a September 24, 2009 blog post titled *"Tour of the Langseth"*, looking down the narrow corridor of the computer server and electronics rack room that supports the multi-screen science laboratory aboard the seismic research vessel **R/V *Marcus G. Langseth***.

## Scene Description

- **Server Racks (Left)**:
  - A long bank of floor-to-ceiling blue steel 19-inch equipment racks stretches from the left foreground deep into the background.
  - **First Rack (Foreground Left)**: Contains an industrial rack-mount chassis at the top, an open shelf holding a beige CRT monitor, a pull-out keyboard/trackball tray, a silver laptop computer resting on a lower shelf, and black uninterruptible power supply (UPS) units at the bottom.
  - **Second & Subsequent Racks**: Packed with 1U/2U rack-mount servers (including Apple Xserve, Sun Fire servers, and Linux/Windows rack systems), patch panels, power distribution units, and dense bundles of red, green, blue, gray, and white Ethernet and serial cables routed vertically along the rack rails.
- **Corridor & Workbenches (Right)**:
  - The floor is covered in square beige/tan linoleum tiles leading toward a cream-colored bulkhead at the back of the room, illuminated by rectangular fluorescent ceiling fixtures.
  - Along the right wall are metal workbenches and shelving loaded with spare parts, yellow and red storage bins, and test equipment.
