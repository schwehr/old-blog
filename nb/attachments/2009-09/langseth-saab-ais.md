# R/V Marcus G. Langseth SAAB AIS Display (`langseth-saab-ais.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of the SAAB Automatic Identification System (AIS) and GPS control unit mounted on the bridge console aboard the seismic research vessel R/V *Marcus G. Langseth* while berthed in Astoria, Oregon (taken September 24, 2009).

## Device & Interface Details
- **Unit Housing:** A dark navy-blue/gray rectangular panel-mounted SAAB marine AIS/GPS display unit (`SAAB` logo printed in white near the bottom edge below the screen, with a compass rose icon at the upper left corner).
- **Top Label & Header:**
  - An embossed tan label strip affixed across the top reads: `PRESS GREEN BUTTON TO SILENCE ALARM`.
  - Below the label, printed text reads `AIS` (in red) `GPS` (in bold white) `POSITIONING SYSTEM` (in smaller white capital letters).
- **LCD Screen:**
  - A monochrome backlit LCD screen in the left-center of the unit displays a list of nearby AIS vessel targets.
  - The top status bar shows the ship's own coordinates (`Lat` and `Lon` around `46°11'... N`, `123°51'... W`) and an anchor symbol indicating status.
  - Below that is a tabular list with columns for `MMSI`, `NAME`, `RNG` (range), and `BRG` (bearing), listing at least 8 nearby vessels sorted by distance.
  - Three softkey labels appear along the bottom of the LCD: `Extended Info`, `Send Text`, and `Sort By Distance`.
- **Keypad & Controls:**
  - Five oval gray softkey buttons directly below the screen (`F1`–`F5`) and three vertical function keys along the right edge of the screen.
  - On the right side of the unit is a standard 12-button telephone-style alphanumeric keypad (`1` through `9`, `*`, `0`, `#`) with two oval buttons above it.
  - Below the keypad are navigation controls: a large circular 4-way directional pad, flanked on the left by three smaller round buttons including a prominent green `PILOT` / alarm silence button in the middle.
- **Surrounding Console:** To the upper left on the black bridge panel is a white rotary selector switch (`0`–`1`–`2`) below a yellow engraved placard referencing bow thruster and aft control settings (`0. NONE`, `1. BOW THRUSTER ONLY`, `2. FULL CONTROL AFT`).
