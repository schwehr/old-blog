# R/V Marcus G. Langseth Main Science Lab (`langseth-science-lab.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior wide-angle photograph of the main science laboratory aboard the Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory (LDEO) seismic research vessel R/V *Marcus G. Langseth* while in port at Astoria, Oregon (September 24, 2009).

## Scene Description
- **Monitor Wall (Background):**
  - Across the forward bulkhead of the room is a massive floor-to-ceiling black metal rack structure holding three horizontal rows of flat-panel LCD monitors (roughly 8 to 9 screens wide, totaling over two dozen displays).
  - Several monitors are powered on, showing bright white, blue, and graphical displays (including Kongsberg SIS multibeam sonar control for the EM122 and Knudsen chirp subbottom profiler displays), while others are dark/off.
  - Above the top row near the right-center is a round analog wall clock.
- **Workstations & Tables:**
  - **Foreground Left:** A long wooden-topped workbench with black metal legs holds three open laptop computers (a dark ThinkPad-style laptop on the left, a silver Apple MacBook in the center, and a black laptop on a blue mouse pad on the right next to a small silver equipment chassis), along with a reusable water bottle, papers, and cables. Two black mesh-backed rolling office chairs sit in front of the table.
  - **Center:** A large elevated light table / map layout table with an illuminated white top surface sits in the middle of the room in front of the monitor wall.
  - **Right & Background:** Additional long wooden desks with black legs and black mesh office chairs run along the monitor wall and perpendicular on the right side of the room, where a green hard hat rests on the table beside another rack of monitors.
- **Room Environment:** Light gray/green speckled linoleum tile flooring and a drop ceiling with recessed fluorescent light fixtures.
