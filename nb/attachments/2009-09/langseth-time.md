# R/V Marcus G. Langseth Symmetricom Network Time Server (`langseth-time.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of the front panel of the shipboard master network time server—a rack-mounted Symmetricom SyncServer S250—inside the computer server room of the R/V *Marcus G. Langseth* (September 24, 2009).

## Hardware & Display Details
- **Chassis:** A 1U rack-mounted dark blue metal faceplate with black rack ears and a brushed metal panel below it.
- **Branding & Model:**
  - Left side (partially cropped): `Symmetricom` in white text.
  - Below the central display: `SyncServer S250` on the left and `Network Time Server` on the right.
- **Ports & Status LEDs (Left):**
  - Two stacked USB ports on the far left (`USB 1`, `2`).
  - A 9-pin male DB-9 serial port labeled `Console RS-232`.
  - A vertical column of four glowing green indicator LEDs labeled:
    - `Sync`
    - `Network`
    - `NTP`
    - `Alarm`
- **Asset Label:** A white adhesive label with black printed text reads `TIMESERVE1` between the status LEDs and the LCD screen.
- **Vacuum Fluorescent / LCD Display (Center):**
  - A bright blue-green 4-line alphanumeric matrix display showing real-time GPS timing and position data:
    - Line 1: `GPS RECEIVER STATUS      23:43:29 UTC`
    - Line 2: `Status: Locked     Satellites: 7`
    - Line 3: `Antenna: Good  Lat:  46° 11' 19.524" N`
    - Line 4: `Mode: Dynamic  Lon: 123° 51' 32.018" W`
- **Controls (Right):** A 4-way black directional arrow keypad (up, down, left, right) in a diamond layout, followed by a numeric keypad on the far right.
