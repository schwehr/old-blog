# Right Whale Acoustic Detection Map (`listen-for-whales.png`)

## Overview
A map graphic from Cornell's "Listen for Whales" automatic acoustic detection system showing North Atlantic right whale call detections along the Boston shipping lane in Massachusetts Bay and Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (September 9, 2009).

## Map & Visual Details
- **Header Banner:** Dark brown top bar with bold white text: `2 Right Whale Calls Detected Within 24 Hours.`
- **Geography & Labels:**
  - Depicts the eastern Massachusetts coastline in tan/beige against shaded blue bathymetry of Massachusetts Bay and Cape Cod Bay.
  - Labeled geographic features: `Cape Ann` and `Gloucester` (with a white dot) in the north, `Boston` (with a white dot) on the west coast, `Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary` (outlined with a dark blue polygon boundary), `Cape Cod Bay`, `Provincetown` (white dot at the tip of Cape Cod), and `Cape Cod`.
- **Shipping Lane & Acoustic Buoys:**
  - The Boston Traffic Separation Scheme (shipping lane) is drawn with parallel dark blue lines running east-southeast from Boston harbor, turning southeast past the tip of Cape Cod.
  - Ten circular buoy listening zones are arranged sequentially along the center of the shipping lane:
    - The first eight buoys (west to east) are shown as green spheres (`Online Buoy`) surrounded by green circles (`Buoy Listening Radius`).
    - The last two buoys at the southeastern end (east of Provincetown) are shown with red whale icons (`Right Whale Detected within Listening Radius`) inside red circles.
  - Three gray spheres (`Offline Buoy`) with faint light-blue circles are located inside `Cape Cod Bay`.
- **Map Legends Box (Lower Left):**
  - A translucent rounded rectangle titled `Map Legends` (in blue text) explains five symbols:
    - Four parallel blue lines: `Shipping Lane`
    - Light circle with center dot: `Buoy Listening Radius`
    - Gray sphere: `Offline Buoy`
    - Green sphere: `Online Buoy`
    - Red whale icon: `Right Whale Detected within Listening Radius`
- **Scale & Compass:**
  - Top right: Alternating black-and-white scale bar from `0`, `2.5`, `5`, `10`, `15`, to `20 Nautical Miles`.
  - Bottom right: White compass rose pointing north (`N`).
