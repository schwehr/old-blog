# New Hampshire Foliage Report 2009 Interactive Map (`nh-foliage.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the interactive "New Hampshire Foliage Report 2009" web graphic from `foliage.visitnh.gov`, showing projected autumn leaf color conditions across New Hampshire's seven tourism regions for October 5th.

## Layout & Visual Details
- **Header Banner:**
  - Dark brown top banner with yellow-orange hand-lettered text: `New Hampshire Foliage Report 2009`.
- **Left Panel (State Map & Regions):**
  - Set on a parchment-textured tan background with `October 5th` written in brown script at the bottom.
  - Upper right note reads: `Click on region names for local updates.`
  - An outline map of New Hampshire is divided into seven regions filled with a smooth north-to-south gradient from deep crimson red in the north to golden yellow-green on the seacoast:
    - `Great North Woods (Grand North)` (top/north, deep red)
    - `White Mountains` (upper-middle, bright red)
    - `Dartmouth • Lake Sunapee` (west-central, red-orange)
    - `Lakes Region` (east-central, orange)
    - `Monadnock Region` (southwest, orange-gold)
    - `Merrimack Valley` (south-central, golden yellow)
    - `Seacoast` (southeast coast, yellow-green)
- **Right Panel (Interactive Calendars):**
  - Set on a warm burnt-orange background with white instructional text at the top: `Roll over the dates on the calendar to view approximate foliage conditions for that day.`
  - **September Calendar:** Headed `September` in yellow script, displaying a 5-row by 7-column grid of dark brown rounded-square date buttons (`30`, `31` dimmed from August, `1`–`30` in white text, and `1`–`3` dimmed for October).
  - **October Calendar:** Headed `October` in yellow script, displaying a 5-row by 7-column grid of dark brown rounded-square date buttons (`27`–`30` dimmed from September, `1`–`31` in white text).
- **Footer (Foliage Color Key):**
  - Dark brown bottom bar labeled `Foliage Color Key:` above a horizontal color gradient bar divided into six stages with white handwritten labels:
    - `green` (olive green)
    - `just turning` (yellow-gold)
    - `near peak` (orange)
    - `peak` (bright red)
    - `past peak` (crimson/burgundy)
    - `faded` (dark brown)
  - Bottom disclaimer text in small yellow font: `Roughly based on past year reports. Exact timing differs from year to year.`
