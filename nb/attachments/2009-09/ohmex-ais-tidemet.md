# Ohmex AIS TideMet Aid-to-Navigation Station (`ohmex-ais-tidemet.jpg`)

## Overview
A top-down tabletop photograph of an Ohmex AIS TideMet Aid-to-Navigation (AtoN) meteorological and tide monitoring station kit unpacked on a gray laboratory workbench (September 1, 2009).

## Hardware & Kit Components
- **Main Electronics Enclosure (Upper Left):**
  - A rectangular light-gray powder-coated aluminum box with four corner screws.
  - Black text in the upper left corner reads `OHMEX`, and large cyan/teal stencil letters in the lower right corner read `AIS` above `TIDEMET`.
  - A white manufacturer sticker in the center reads `Ohmex Ltd.` with address details and a handwritten MMSI/serial number (`MMSI 999999900`).
  - Left side features a yellow protective cap over a connector and a silver circular metallic port; bottom edge has two coaxial/sensor connectors; right side has black cable glands.
- **Ultrasonic Weather Sensor & Coiled Cable (Upper Right):**
  - A white cylindrical compact weather station sensor (Gill WindSonic / Ohmex meteorological sensor) with `OHMEX` printed in bold black letters around its upper housing, an open acoustic wind-sensing gap in the middle with a blue ring accent, and a threaded white mounting base at the bottom.
  - Surrounded by a large coil of black marine-grade data cable terminating in a black cylindrical submersible pressure/tide transducer (` vented pressure sensor`) resting horizontally above the CD.
- **Power / Data Wiring Harness (Bottom Left):**
  - A multi-lead black and red power/serial cable assembly with an inline white plastic fuse holder, blue/red spade terminals, a white warning tag, and a circular multi-pin plug with a green seal ring.
- **Software CD-ROM (Bottom Center):**
  - A CD in a white paper sleeve printed with yellow and black text: `OHMEX INSTRUMENTATION`, a large Greek omega/chi logo (`Ωχ`), `SOFTWARE`, and a yellow box listing `SONARMITE`, `TIDEMET`, `METPAK`.
