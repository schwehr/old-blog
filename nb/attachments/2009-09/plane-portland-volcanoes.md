# Cascade Volcanoes on Approach to Portland from an Airplane (`plane-portland-volcanoes.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken out the port-side window of a commercial passenger jet at dusk/sunset during the approach to Portland, Oregon, showing two prominent Cascade stratovolcanoes rising above a hazy landscape (September 2009).

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Terrain:**
  - Dark, forested mountain ridges and valleys of the Cascade Range sit in twilight shadow across the lower half of the frame, with a winding braided river valley visible in the lower right corner.
- **Distant Volcanoes:**
  - Two conical volcanic peaks (Mount St. Helens on the left-center and Mount Adams on the right, or Mount Hood / Mount Jefferson) rise above the low-lying atmospheric haze along the horizon.
- **Aircraft Wing (Upper Left):**
  - The outer section and angled winglet of the airplane's wing extend into the frame from the left edge against the glowing sky.
- **Sky & Lighting:**
  - A warm golden-orange sunset glow illuminates the sky on the left behind the wingtip, transitioning smoothly into soft pinkish-orange and cool twilight blue-gray toward the right edge.
