# Seafloor Gas Plume YouTube Videos Embedded in Google Earth (`plume-videos-in-google-earth.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth displaying a 3D perspective view of the northern California continental margin bathymetry with an open KML placemark balloon embedding a YouTube video of a 1,400-meter mid-water gas plume discovered by the NOAA ship *Okeanos Explorer* using a Kongsberg EM302 multibeam sonar and visualized in Fledermaus (September 10, 2009).
