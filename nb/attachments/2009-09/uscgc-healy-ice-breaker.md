# USCGC Healy Icebreaker in Arctic Sea Ice from Helicopter (`uscgc-healy-ice-breaker.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken from a helicopter showing the port-side profile of the U.S. Coast Guard Cutter *Healy* (`WAGB-20`) stopped in Arctic pack ice during the 2009 U.S. Law of the Sea continental shelf mapping cruise.

## Scene Description
- **The Vessel (USCGC *Healy*):**
  - Viewed broadside from the port beam, facing left (forward), with its bright red icebreaking hull contrasting sharply against the surrounding white sea ice.
  - **Hull Markings:** White hull number `20` near the bow, followed by the iconic U.S. Coast Guard racing stripe (a wide white diagonal band with the Coast Guard emblem, bordered by a thin dark strip and a medium blue diagonal stripe), and large white block lettering reading `U. S. COAST GUARD` along the port side.
  - **Superstructure & Equipment:**
    - White multi-deck forward superstructure topped by the wrap-around bridge windows, with several white spherical satellite communication radomes on the roof and a black mainmast/aloftcon tower rising from the center.
    - Yellow-ochre/buff exhaust stack midships bearing the Coast Guard shield emblem, with a faint plume of gray exhaust drifting aft to the right.
    - Orange lifeboat mounted in davits on the port side, two dark blue shipping containers on the aft working deck, yellow cranes fore and aft, and a large A-frame crane on the stern.
- **Sea Ice & Environment:**
  - Vast, flat white expanses of snow-covered Arctic sea ice floes surround the ship, broken by dark cracks (leads) directly below the bow in the foreground and fragmented ice blocks in the vessel's wake astern.
  - A uniform, overcast pale gray Arctic sky meets the flat white ice horizon in the background.
