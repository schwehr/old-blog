# Spool of Yellow Fiber Optic Cable at CCOM (`ccom-new-fiber-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of a large wooden reel wound with bright yellow fiber optic cable mounted on a floor stand inside the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) building, ready to be pulled into the ceiling for a new fiber run from the server room.

- **Cable & Spool:**
  - A large plywood cable spool is filled with neatly wound coils of bright yellow jacketed fiber optic cable.
  - A loose lead of the yellow cable extends upward and to the upper right out of the frame, while a small loop of slack hangs down near the bottom center of the spool.
- **Mounting Stand & Surroundings:**
  - A horizontal metal axle rod passes through the center of the wooden reel, supported on either side by black molded plastic/metal A-frame jack stands resting on dark gray office carpet.
  - In the upper-left background, a white wall with brown baseboard trim and the base of a dark cylindrical trash can or planter are visible.
