# Fiber Optic Cable Run into Ceiling at CCOM Office (`ccom-new-fiber-2.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph of an office at the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) showing a blue A-frame stepladder positioned below a raised ceiling tile as bright yellow fiber optic cable is routed into the server room above the offices.

## Scene Description
- **Cable & Ladder:**
  - A blue fiberglass A-frame stepladder with a yellow top cap stands in the center of the room in front of a window.
  - Several loops of bright yellow fiber optic cable lie coiled on the dark gray carpet at the base of the ladder; the cable rises up along the left leg of the ladder and disappears into an open drop-ceiling tile at the top center of the frame.
- **Office Interior:**
  - **Walls & Window:** Slate-blue/gray painted wall with a rectangular window framing green trees outside, fitted with white mini-blinds at the top and a wooden windowsill above a white baseboard heater grille.
  - **Right Side:** Five empty white wall-mounted bookshelves run along the right wall.
  - **Left Side:** A light-gray laminate office desk/credenza in the foreground left and a dark gray upholstered rolling office chair in front of another desk in the middle left.
