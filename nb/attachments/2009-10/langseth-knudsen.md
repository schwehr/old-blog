# Knudsen 320B/R Echosounder Rack Unit on R/V *Marcus G. Langseth* (`langseth-knudsen.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of a 19-inch electronics equipment rack in the science lab aboard the research vessel **R/V *Marcus G. Langseth***, featuring a red **Knudsen 320B/R Echosounder** chirp sub-bottom profiler transceiver mounted below a rack-mount industrial PC and a Black Box serial/channel switch.

- **Middle Unit (2U Black Rack-Mount Computer):**
  - Labeled **Industrial PC** in white text at the bottom center of the front bezel, with a keylock above three illuminated red indicator LEDs and optical/drive bays to the right.
- **Bottom Unit (4U Red Rack-Mount Chassis):**
  - Bright red front panel with silver grab handles on the left and right edges.
  - Gold/yellow centered branding:
    - **K KNUDSEN ENGINEERING LIMITED**
    - **320B/R ECHOSOUNDER**

  - A white label reading `KNUDSEN-001` is affixed to the lower-left corner of the red panel.
