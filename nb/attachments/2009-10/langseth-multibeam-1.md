# Kongsberg EM122 Multibeam Transceiver Unit on R/V *Marcus G. Langseth* (`langseth-multibeam-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior photograph of the open electronics cabinet for the **Kongsberg EM122** deep-water multibeam echosounder transceiver unit (TRU) aboard the research vessel **R/V *Marcus G. Langseth***, showing the modular circuit card cages, indicator LEDs, and bundled coaxial/transducer cabling.

  - A dense bundle of gray cables with yellow numbered sleeve markers exits the upper-middle backplane, fanning downward into the middle card cage.

  - A wide horizontal row of closely spaced vertical circuit cards spans the width of the rack.
