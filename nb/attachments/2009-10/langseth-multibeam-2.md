# Kongsberg EM122 Multibeam Transducer Cable Routing (`langseth-multibeam-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph showing heavy black coaxial/transducer cables and strain-relief mounting brackets below a Kongsberg EM122 multibeam echosounder transceiver unit aboard the research vessel *R/V Marcus G. Langseth*.

## Scene Description
- **Mounting Brackets & Strain Relief (Top Center)**:
  - At the very top edge, the bottom lip of a metallic gray Kongsberg enclosure is secured with a row of machine screws.
  - Directly beneath the enclosure, thick black cables exit downward through metallic connector boots and pass through two tiered horizontal aluminum clamp/strain-relief plates fastened with hex bolts.
  - A tan/off-white fabric strap loops around the right side of the upper mounting bracket.
- **Cable Bundles & Labels (Center to Bottom)**:
  - Approximately a dozen heavy-gauge black multibeam cables curve downward from the aluminum clamp plates, each marked with a yellow heat-shrink or wrap-around identification label containing printed black alphanumeric codes.
  - The cables merge into two thick horizontal bundles routed along the bottom of a white bulkhead just above the dark blue-gray carpeted or textured deck floor.
  - On the left, a thick vertical bundle of black cables loops downward and joins the horizontal cable tray.
  - Loose coils of thinner black, white, and gray coaxial and signal cables (some with BNC or coaxial connectors and red/blue/green/white wire breakouts) are tucked into perforated metal cable racks behind the main bundles.
  - On the right, the bundled cables pass through a rectangular cutout in the white bulkhead panel.
