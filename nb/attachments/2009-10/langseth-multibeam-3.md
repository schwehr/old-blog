# Kongsberg EM122 Multibeam Transceiver Cable Termination Boxes (`langseth-multibeam-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of two side-by-side wall-mounted Kongsberg transducer cable termination/interface boxes for the EM122 multibeam echosounder system aboard the *R/V Marcus G. Langseth*.

## Scene Description
- **Enclosures (Upper Half)**:
  - Two rectangular light-gray sheet-metal junction boxes are mounted side by side near the top of a beige/tan interior bulkhead.
  - Each box features a small dark square Kongsberg Maritime logo on the top-left corner of its front panel and faint stenciled model/unit lettering on the top-right corner.
  - Extending downward from each box is an open-sided metal bracket frame with a horizontal white/light-gray front retaining bar secured by bolts.
- **Cable Arrays & Labels (Lower Half)**:
  - Rows of thick black transducer array cables plug into multi-pin connectors recessed under the bottom edge of each gray box, where tan/gold connector housings are visible.
  - The cables hang vertically down through the metal support brackets in neat rows.
  - Just below the horizontal retaining bars, each black cable bears a yellow identification sleeve or label printed with black text, with a few blue labels also visible in the left bundle.
