# Kongsberg EM122 Transceiver Unit Cabinet (`langseth-multibeam-4.jpg`)

## Overview
A front-facing photograph of the rack-mounted Kongsberg EM122 Transceiver Unit (TRU) cabinet with a tinted glass door aboard the *R/V Marcus G. Langseth*.

## Scene Description
- **Cabinet Frame & Exterior**:
  - A square, black-framed equipment rack cabinet with vertically ribbed side trim panels and a large central smoked/tinted glass door.
  - On the right frame post, above the recessed door handle and lock cylinder, is a gold-and-orange Kongsberg Maritime crown crest badge below a small silver nameplate.
- **Internal Components**:
  - Through the tinted glass, an array of vertical circuit board modules (transceiver boards) is mounted in a subrack across the center.
  - Flat gray ribbon cables arc across the top of the vertical modules, and thick black transducer cables connect to the lower portion of the boards above a horizontal copper/metallic grounding bar fastened with bolts.
  - Several red status LEDs glow inside the cabinet: one bright red indicator LED near the upper right above white stenciled text reading **`EM 122`** and **`TRANSCEIVER UNIT`**, and three pairs of small red LEDs illuminated along the middle of the vertical transceiver cards.
- **Reflections**:
  - The glass door reflects the photographer (Kurt Schwehr) wearing a white cap and holding a smartphone/camera, along with overhead fluorescent room lighting.
