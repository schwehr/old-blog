# Custom Laser-Cut Windshield Mount for SPOT Satellite Tracker - Top View (`mmicire-spot-mount-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A top-down overhead photograph showing Mark Micire's custom laser-cut translucent pink acrylic four-suction-cup windshield mount holding an orange-and-black SPOT Satellite Personal Tracker and its belt clip.

  - To the right of the main tracker unit, an orange plastic belt-clip holster attachment is secured to the acrylic plate with a captive D-ring thumb screw at the top.
  - Two additional metal D-ring quarter-turn fasteners are mounted near the bottom left and bottom right corners of the pink acrylic plate.
