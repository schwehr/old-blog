# Concept Mockup of Project Earth with Embedded Google Earth Globe (`radial-earth-concept.png`)

## Overview
A composite concept mockup created by Kurt Schwehr showing the **Earth** (`Earth 0.1`) Ruby on Rails disk-indexing web interface with its central radial directory chart replaced by a 3D Google Earth globe displaying geospatial data points across North America.

## Interface & Visualization Details
- **Web Application Chrome (Outer Frame)**:
  - Retains the header and layout from `Earth 0.1`: green **`Earth`** logo on the top left, breadcrumb path **`root` » `duck.local` » `/Users/matthewl/earth-0.1`**, `(Show empty directories and servers)`, the top-right `Filter` panel (`by filename:`, `by user:`, `Update` button), and the `navigation`, `all files`, `treemap`, and **`radial`** tabs.
  - Footer links `Administration | Project Earth` on the left and `Earth 0.1` on the right.
- **Embedded Google Earth Globe (Center)**:
  - Centered inside the white main panel is a circular 3D satellite globe of the Earth surrounded by a pale green atmospheric halo ring.
  - The globe is centered on North America, Central America, and northern South America, with yellow international and U.S. state boundary lines.
  - Numerous small red and green square point markers are plotted across the eastern United States, the Gulf Coast, the Mississippi River basin, the U.S. East Coast, the Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico.
