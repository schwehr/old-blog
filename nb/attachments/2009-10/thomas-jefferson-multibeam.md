# Multibeam and GPS Equipment Racks Aboard NOAA Ship Thomas Jefferson (`thomas-jefferson-multibeam.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken inside an equipment room aboard the NOAA hydrographic survey ship *Thomas Jefferson* during a port visit in Portsmouth, New Hampshire, in October 2009. It shows three black 19-inch electronics racks housing multibeam sonar, motion sensor, and GPS systems, with Captain Andy Armstrong (Co-Director of the UNH Joint Hydrographic Center) standing on the left inspecting the gear.

## Scene & Person Description
- **Left Foreground (Andy Armstrong)**: A man with white hair stands in profile on the left side of the frame facing right toward the equipment racks, with his arms crossed over his chest. He wears a light blue long-sleeved collared button-down shirt featuring a circular NOAA logo embroidered on the left chest, paired with blue jeans and a dark brown leather belt.
- **Room Lighting**: Bright overhead fluorescent fixtures illuminate the top of the racks and the top of Andy Armstrong's head.
