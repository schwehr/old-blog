# Hydrographic Surveying Station Aboard NOAA Ship Thomas Jefferson (`thomas-jefferson-surveying-station.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of the hydrographic surveying workstation located directly behind the bridge aboard the NOAA ship *Thomas Jefferson*, taken during an October 2009 tour in Portsmouth, New Hampshire. The station features a multi-monitor array displaying navigation charts, sidescan/backscatter sonar waterfall plots, and survey acquisition software.

## Monitors & Displays
- **Top Row (Overhead Monitors)**:
  - Center-left: A small dark-gray marine display unit mounted on a bracket with a white printed notice taped beneath it.
  - Right: Two black CRT closed-circuit television (CCTV) monitors mounted side-by-side, showing live monochrome/blue-tinted camera feeds of the ship's deck and working areas.
- **Middle Row (Four LCD Monitors)**:
  - Two black-bezeled flat-panel monitors on the left (powered off or asleep, with a small purple sticky note on the bottom bezel of the far-left monitor).
  - Center-right: A black-bezeled monitor actively displaying data tables, file lists, and status bars with green and red indicators, along with a smaller dark waterfall window in the lower right.
  - Far-right: A silver-bezeled flat-panel monitor (powered off).
- **Bottom Row (Four LCD Monitors)**:
  - Far-left: A black-bezeled monitor (powered off).
  - Center-left: A black-bezeled monitor displaying a dark sonar swath profile or cross-track graphic.
  - Center-right: A silver-bezeled monitor running hydrographic navigation/line-planning software (such as HYPACK), showing a nautical chart with depth contours, a sounding of `50`, a vessel trackline, a color bar on the left, and a red status readout in the bottom right corner.
  - Far-right: A silver-bezeled monitor displaying a dual-channel brown/copper-toned sidescan or multibeam backscatter waterfall image showing seafloor texture on the port and starboard channels.

- **Desk Surface**: A light wooden countertop holds a black keyboard in the center, a silver trackball mouse to the right, a green hardcover logbook ("Record" book) and white notepad on the left, a gray tissue box, and a small metal control box with a black rotary knob/joystick and a red emergency-style push button on the far left.
- **Wall & Fixtures**: On the wall behind the left monitors are a nautical flag/signal chart, a gray metal electrical panel, and a wall calendar topped with the circular blue-and-white NOAA emblem. A black corded telephone is mounted on the right bulkhead.
