# ERMA Web GIS Showing NOAA Ship Thomas Jefferson and Survey Launches (`tj-and-launches-erma.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **ERMA** (Environmental Response Management Application) web mapping interface for **Portsmouth, NH**, displaying AIS vessel positions and tracks for the NOAA ship *Thomas Jefferson* (`THOMASJEFFERSON`) and two of its hydrographic survey launches (`TJ3101` and `TJ3102`) off the coast of Maine and New Hampshire on October 2, 2009.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Banner**: A dark ocean-wave header banner displays **ERMA Portsmouth, NH** on the left and **Admin** on the right. Below it, a gray toolbar contains **Map**, **Print**, **Info**, and **Help** on the left, and **Legend**, **Layer**, **Data** (highlighted), and **Tools** on the right.
- **Map Navigation**: Along the left edge of the map canvas are a blue circular directional pan pad, a vertical zoom slider bar with `+` and `-` buttons, a hand pan tool icon, and a magnifying glass zoom box icon.
- **Bottom Status Bar**: Reads `Scale: 1 : 217K`, `Zoom Level: 11`, and `Location: -70.59859°, 42.95883°`.

## Map Canvas
- **Geographic Features**: Shows the Piscataqua River between Portsmouth, NH, and Kittery, ME, along with coastal islands including *Wood Island*, *White Island*, *Cutts Island*, *Raynes Island*, *Rocks Nose*, *Cape Neddick Nubble*, *Boon Island*, *Boon Island Pollock Rock*, and the Isles of Shoals (*Duck Island*, *Appledore Island*, *Malaga Island*, *Lunging Island*, *White Island*).
- **Vessels & Tracks**:
  - Offshore east of Kittery (`Rocks Nose`), two orange triangular vessel icons with short red track tails are labeled **TJ3101** and **TJ3102** (the *Thomas Jefferson*'s survey launches).
  - South of Wood Island / Portsmouth Harbor entrance, orange vessel icons mark **PENN#6**, **GYPSUM INTEGRITY**, and **THOMASJEFFERSON** (marked with a black `X` over its orange triangle), along with a red trackline heading north into the Piscataqua River past **R/V COCHECO**, **AQUA PEARL**, and several vessels clustered up the river toward Portsmouth and Newington.
