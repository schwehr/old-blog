# Hydrographic Survey Launch Aboard NOAA Ship Thomas Jefferson (`tj-launch.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical photograph (credited to Colleen Mitchell from *Seacoast Online*) looking down onto a hydrographic survey launch stowed in its davits aboard the NOAA ship *Thomas Jefferson* on the Piscataqua River, with the green steel truss span and lift towers of the Sarah Mildred Long Bridge visible in the background.

## Scene & Vessel Details
- **Survey Launch**:
  - A gray-hulled aluminum hydrographic survey boat sits in cradles along the port side deck of the *Thomas Jefferson*.
  - The launch features an enclosed cabin with three forward-facing windshields (each fitted with a wiper) and three lower cabin windows ahead of the main cabin roof, plus a tubular metal bow rail.
  - Mounted atop the cabin roof are a white radome, GPS/communication antennas, and orange personal flotation gear behind the cabin.
- **Davits & Ship Deck**:
  - Two large white articulated steel davit arms cradle the bow and stern of the launch, rigged with wire cables and yellow blocks.
  - To the right of the launch on the ship's deck, white metal railings, a black hydraulic crane boom, and a yellow equipment storage locker are visible.
- **Background (Piscataqua River & Bridge)**:
  - Dark calm river water extends behind the ship.
  - Spanning the upper portion of the frame is the green steel truss structure and two tall vertical lift towers of the Sarah Mildred Long Bridge connecting Portsmouth, New Hampshire, and Kittery, Maine, under an overcast gray-and-blue sky.
