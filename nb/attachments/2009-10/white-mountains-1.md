# Autumn Foliage View Near North Conway in the White Mountains (`white-mountains-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide landscape photograph taken in October 2009 near North Conway and Route 302 in the White Mountains of New Hampshire, showing peak autumn foliage across a forested valley with layered mountain ridges in the distance under an overcast sky.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**:
  - A dense mixed forest canopy fills the lower two-thirds of the frame.
  - Prominent white paper birch trunks with bare branches rise in the lower left alongside a tall, vibrant red-orange deciduous tree at left-center.
  - Around them are yellow, amber, rust, and orange hardwoods interspersed with dark green conifers (pines and spruces).
  - On the middle right, a tan grassy clearing or open field slopes gently below a dark green tree line and a band of deep red bushes, with a small light-colored house/barn roof visible nestled among the trees above the field.
- **Background & Sky**:
  - Rolling foothills and distant blue-gray peaks of the White Mountains span the horizon from left to right.
  - A thick blanket of gray and lavender-gray stratus clouds covers the sky, with a bright silver-white patch of sunlight breaking through the clouds above the right-center mountain peaks.
