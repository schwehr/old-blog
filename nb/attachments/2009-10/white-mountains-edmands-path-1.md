# White Mountain National Forest Weather Warning Sign on Edmands Path (`white-mountains-edmands-path-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of a weathered yellow U.S. Forest Service metal warning sign posted at treeline (roughly two-thirds of the way up Edmands Path toward Mount Eisenhower) in the White Mountain National Forest, New Hampshire, warning hikers of severe alpine weather.

## Sign & Text Details
- **Sign Appearance**:
  - A rectangular bright-yellow painted metal sign mounted to a wooden post/tree with dark rusted bolts at the top center, bottom center, and bottom corners.
  - Green fir/spruce branches frame the sign on the left, top, and right edges, partially obscuring the upper-left corner of the text. The surface of the sign shows numerous scratches, scuffs, and weathering marks.
  - Flanking the top heading are two blue-and-white U.S. Forest Service shield emblems reading **FOREST SERVICE / U S / DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE**.
- **Visible Text (in bold black capital letters)**:
  - Top header (underlined): **`STOP`**
  - Body text:
    > **[THE AREA] AHEAD HAS THE WORST WEATHER IN AMERICA.**
    >
    > **MANY HAVE DIED THERE FROM EXPOSURE, EVEN IN THE**
    >
    > **SUMMER. TURN BACK NOW IF THE WEATHER IS BAD.**
  - Bottom footer: **`WHITE MOUNTAIN NATIONAL FOREST`**
