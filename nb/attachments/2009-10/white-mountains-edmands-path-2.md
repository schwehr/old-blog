# Overcast Mountain Valley View from Edmands Path (`white-mountains-edmands-path-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic landscape photograph looking west/northwest from high on Edmands Path on the slopes of Mount Eisenhower in the White Mountains of New Hampshire in October 2009, framed by evergreens in the foreground with autumn-colored valleys and distant ridges under a brooding gray cloud deck.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**:
  - On the left side, a tall dark-green balsam fir or spruce tree extends from the bottom to the top edge of the frame, with smaller evergreen saplings in the lower left-center.
  - On the far right edge, another weathered evergreen rises into the right side of the frame.
- **Middle Ground & Valley**:
  - Between the trees, the view opens over a vast forested valley carpeted in muted rust, orange, and reddish-brown autumn foliage mixed with dark patches of conifer forest.
  - Near the center of the valley floor, a small green clearing (likely the Bretton Woods golf course / Mount Washington Hotel grounds along Route 302) and a bright white building roof are visible in the distance.
- **Background & Sky**:
  - Rolling blue-gray and reddish-brown mountain ridges stretch across the horizon, fading into a pale hazy band below a heavy, low ceiling of dark gray stratus clouds.
