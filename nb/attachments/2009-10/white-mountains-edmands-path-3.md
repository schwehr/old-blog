# Trail Cairn at the Top of Edmands Path on Mount Eisenhower (`white-mountains-edmands-path-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A landscape photograph taken at the rocky alpine saddle (approximately 1,322 meters elevation) at the top of Edmands Path below Mount Eisenhower in the White Mountains, New Hampshire, featuring a stacked stone trail cairn in the foreground and sweeping autumn mountain ridges in the background.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**:
  - Rough, lichen-covered gray and greenish-gray granite boulders and talus rocks cover the left and bottom portions of the frame, marking the rocky alpine trail above treeline.
  - At center, a conical stone cairn built from stacked angular granite rocks stands on the edge of the slope to guide hikers in poor visibility.
- **Middle Ground & Background**:
  - Beyond the cairn, a dark conifer-covered ridge drops steeply into a wide valley blanketed in rust-orange and reddish-brown autumn foliage mixed with dark green evergreen bands.
  - Across the valley rises a long, rounded dark mountain ridge, with paler blue-gray mountain ranges stretching toward the distant horizon on the left and center.
- **Sky**:
  - A continuous layer of heavy, textured gray clouds hangs low over the mountains with a brighter white band along the distant horizon.
