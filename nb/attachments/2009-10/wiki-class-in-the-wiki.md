# CCOM Internal MediaWiki "Wiki tour" Presentation Page (`wiki-class-in-the-wiki.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a web browser displaying the **Wiki tour** page (`http://wiki.ccom.unh.edu/index.php/Wiki_tour`) on the UNH Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC) internal MediaWiki site, created by Kurt Schwehr in October 2009 to teach a training class on using the wiki directly from within the wiki itself.
