# Garrison Hill Viewed from Wishmaker Farms in Dover, New Hampshire (`wishmaker-farms-garrison-hill.jpg`)

## Overview
A sunny autumn landscape photograph taken in October 2009 from Wishmaker Farms looking toward Garrison Hill—the highest point in Dover, New Hampshire—under a clear, deep blue sky.
