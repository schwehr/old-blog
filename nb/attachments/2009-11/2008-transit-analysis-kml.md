# 2008 AIS Transit Analysis in Google Earth (`2008-transit-analysis-kml.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot displaying detailed Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel transit tracks across the Stellwagen Bank region off the coast of Massachusetts (near Cape Cod / Provincetown). The visualization highlights three individual ship transits from December 2008, color-coded by receiving station, along with two open KML placemark information balloons showing both overall transit summary metadata and individual position-report diagnostics.

## Map & Track Details
- **Basemap & Geography**: Shaded bathymetric and satellite imagery of Massachusetts Bay and Stellwagen Bank, with the curved hook of Cape Cod (Provincetown) visible at the upper right and eastern edge.
- **Geographic Labels**: Blue text labels on the seafloor mark `Stellwagen Bank`, `Western Spur`, and `The Breakwater`.
- **Study Area Boundary**: A thin yellow polygon outline encloses the Stellwagen Bank study/monitoring zone.
- **Vessel Transit Tracks**:
  - Three thick, overlapping tracks composed of dense sequences of colored circular point markers (predominantly bright yellow-green, interspersed with orange-red and dark purple/maroon markers indicating different AIS receiver stations).
  - **Horizontal Transit (`Transit 50 : 209740000`)**: Starts at a yellow pushpin on the western boundary, travels eastward across the southern portion of Stellwagen Bank (`Western Spur`), and makes a sharp 90-degree turn northward near the eastern boundary before exiting the study zone.
  - **Vertical Transits (`210348000` / `Transit 59 : 210348000`)**: Two roughly parallel north-south tracks running vertically through the center of the bank past `Stellwagen Bank` and `The Breakwater`, marked with yellow pushpins at their northern and southern ends.
- **Expanded Point Cluster (Upper Right)**: Near the northern end of the eastbound-to-northbound track, a radial spider/starburst of white leader lines fans out from a cluster of points to display individual red circular point icons and their numeric point IDs (`3679963`, `3679965`, `3679967`, `3679969`, `3679971`, `3679973`, `3679977`, `3679979`, `3679981`, `3679983`, `3679985`, `3679987`).
