# Sunburst Through Golden Autumn Maple Leaves (`fall-colors.jpg`)

## Overview
An upward-looking outdoor photograph capturing the last golden-yellow autumn leaves on the branches of a maple tree, backlit by bright sunlight against a crisp blue sky in early November.

## Scene Description
- **Foliage**: Broad, lobed maple leaves in vibrant shades of golden yellow, amber, and pale chartreuse green fill the frame, with a few leaves showing small dark spots or tattered edges from late-autumn weathering.
- **Branches & Trunks**: Dark, slender tree branches criss-cross diagonally from the lower right toward the upper center, flanked by heavier vertical tree trunks near the left and right margins.
- **Lighting & Sky**: The bright white disk of the sun shines directly through a small gap in the canopy near the upper center, creating a warm backlit glow through the translucent yellow leaves against a deep, cloudless azure-blue sky.
