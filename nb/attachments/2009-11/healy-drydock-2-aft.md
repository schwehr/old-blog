# Aft End of USCGC Healy Resting on Dry Dock Support Columns (`healy-drydock-2-aft.jpg`)

## Overview
A ground-level photograph taken directly behind the stern of the icebreaker *USCGC Healy* on the floor of the dry dock at Todd Shipyard in Seattle, looking forward beneath the ship's overhanging aft hull at the central steel support columns and the exposed port and starboard propeller shaft bossings.

## Scene Description
- **Central Support Structure (Center)**:
  - Rising vertically from a heavy rectangular steel base on the dry dock floor is a tall, tapered cluster of white/light-grey tubular steel support columns with triangular gusset plates at the bottom and a flat steel cap at the top, wedged directly under the centerline skeg of the ship.
- **Underbody & Rudder Post (Top Center/Left)**:
  - The dark, shadowed underside of the *Healy*'s hull curves overhead.
  - To the left of the central support columns, a vertical cylindrical post (rudder stock/horn area) hangs down from the hull with yellow chain hoists suspended nearby.
- **Port and Starboard Propeller Shaft Bossings & Scaffolding (Left & Right)**:
  - Flanking the central support tower on both the left (port) and right (starboard) are multi-tiered metal scaffolding towers with wooden/metal access stairs on the outer edges.
  - Above each scaffolding platform, the large circular, bolted flange faces of the port (left) and starboard (right) propeller shaft struts/bossings face directly toward the camera, with the propellers removed.
- **Lighting & Equipment**:
  - A blindingly bright work floodlight creates a large white glare on the right side of the frame above a blue boom lift base (`Genie S-...` visible on the lower right arm).
