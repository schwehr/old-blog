# Bow Ice Knife Under USCGC Healy in Dry Dock (`healy-drydock-4-bow-iceknife.jpg`)

## Overview
A ground-level photograph taken on the floor of the Todd Shipyard dry dock beneath the bow of the *USCGC Healy*, showing the ship's massive, wedge-shaped steel "ice knife" (forefoot skeg) designed to break and deflect heavy sea ice, with two visitors standing directly underneath it for scale.

## Scene Description
- **Ice Knife & Bow Underbody (Upper Left & Center)**:
  - Protruding downward from the dark, flat-bottomed forward hull is the large, trapezoidal steel fin known as the ice knife.
  - Its side plating shows mottled patches of bare, brownish-tan weathered steel where the black anti-fouling paint has been scraped away by ice breaking.
- **People for Scale (Bottom Center)**:
  - Two men wearing hard hats stand side-by-side on the dry dock floor directly under the lower edge of the ice knife, looking and pointing upward at it:
    - The person on the left wears a dark jacket, blue jeans, and a red/white hard hat, raising his right hand toward the bottom of the ice knife.
    - The person on the right wears an olive-green jacket, tan pants, and a dark green/black hard hat.
- **Dry Dock Supports (Left & Right)**:
  - To the left behind the two men, a row of rectangular concrete/timber keel blocks runs along the dry dock floor beneath the hull.
  - On the right foreground, a tall, multi-legged light-blue/white tubular steel support cradle angles upward to brace the starboard bow.
