# Removed Old Multibeam Transmit Window Frame on Dry Dock Floor (`healy-drydock-7-old-transmit-window.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on the floor of the Todd Shipyard dry dock alongside the *USCGC Healy*, showing the long, rectangular steel mounting frame and acoustic window from the ship's old SeaBeam 2100 multibeam sonar transmit array (which had been mounted in the aft end of the ice knife) resting on the deck while two visitors in hard hats walk beside it.

## Scene Description
- **Old Transmit Window Assembly (Left & Bottom Center)**:
  - Extending away from the camera in the left foreground is a long, heavy, rectangular steel frame lined with two parallel rows of large circular bolt holes along its raised inner flanges and additional bolt holes along its outer edges.
  - A flat, yellowish-tan acoustic window panel rests along the left side of the assembly, with a small red plastic bag lying near its far end.
- **People (Right)**:
  - Two men seen from behind walk away from the camera along the right side of the window frame on the dry dock floor:
    - The person in the center-right wears a dark jacket, dark pants, and a hard hat.
    - The person to his right wears an olive-green hooded jacket, dark pants, glasses, and a hard hat.
    - In the extreme lower-right corner, the hard hat and shoulder of a third person are partially visible.
- **Dry Dock & Harbor Background (Top)**:
  - To the left behind the window frame sit the massive angled tubular steel support columns and keel blocks bracing the *Healy*'s hull.
  - Through the open end of the dry dock in the center distance, the water of Elliott Bay / Puget Sound and a hillside neighborhood of Seattle are visible under a bright sky, with the high vertical wall of the dry dock rising on the upper right.
