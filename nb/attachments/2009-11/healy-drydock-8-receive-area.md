# Cut-Open Keel Area Under USCGC Healy for the New Multibeam Array (`healy-drydock-8-receive-area.jpg`)

## Overview
A low-light photograph taken directly beneath the centerline bottom of the *USCGC Healy* in dry dock, where shipyard crews have cut open the bottom hull plating and internal fuel tank framing to create a larger housing cavity for the upgraded multibeam sonar array.

## Scene Description
- **Excavated Hull Cavity & Internal Framing (Upper Half)**:
  - A wide horizontal opening has been torch-cut into the flat bottom of the ship's dark steel hull, exposing the interior structural bulkheads and compartments above.
  - Inside the illuminated cavity are vertical steel web frames with oval lightening/access holes, circular pipe penetrations, scorched/ground bare-metal edges from cutting torches, and heavy metal link chains hanging down on both the left and right sides.
- **Crew & Tour Group Below the Keel (Lower Half)**:
  - Directly beneath the horizontal bottom edge of the hull, several people in hard hats and work jackets stand among ladders and scaffolding:
    - In the foreground on the right, two visitors in dark jackets and hard hats look up into the cut-open hull cavity, with one person raising a gloved hand to point at the internal structure.
    - In the background on the left, near an A-frame stepladder and a bright work light, two workers/visitors in blue and dark jackets with hard hats stand conversing.
