# USCGC Healy Dry Dock New Multibeam Receive Array Area (`healy-drydock-9-new-tx-area.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken inside the dry dock beneath the hull of the Coast Guard icebreaker USCGC *Healy* at Todd Shipyard in Seattle, Washington (November 2009). Despite the filename (`new-tx-area`), the blog caption clarifies that this shows the reworked, expanded cavity for the new multibeam sonar **receive array**, oriented athwartships in a Mills Cross array configuration during an upgrade replacing the older SeaBeam 2100 system.
