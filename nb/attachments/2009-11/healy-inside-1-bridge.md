# USCGC Healy Bridge (`healy-inside-1-bridge.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior photograph of the main navigation bridge aboard the Coast Guard icebreaker USCGC *Healy* while in dry dock at Todd Shipyard in Seattle, Washington (November 2009), during an evening tour led by Dale Chayes.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **Bridge Console (Foreground & Center):** A long, dark-gray/black wrap-around ship control console with a wooden handrail trim along the front and top edges.
  - **Foreground Station:** Contains engine/propulsion telegraph throttle levers in the center, square push-button indicator pads with red, green, and white buttons, rotary switches, small analog gauges, and a gray handset/keypad intercom unit.
  - **Angled Upper Panels:** House square CRT/LCD display screens, red digital LED readouts, analog meters, toggle switches, and a white binder or chart pad resting on the middle slanted section.
- **People:**
  - **Left:** A visitor wearing a shiny black hard hat and a bright yellow-green jacket standing behind the console, raising one hand near his face.
  - **Center:** A bearded visitor in a black hard hat, dark jacket, and blue shirt looking toward the camera.
  - **Right:** Tour guide Dale Chayes, with a long gray beard, glasses, black hard hat, and dark jacket with a white visitor/ID badge clipped to the chest, standing in front of the console with his left arm raised as he explains the bridge layout.
- **Background & Flooring:** Large forward bridge windows look out onto the dark shipyard at night with scattered lights visible outside. The deck is covered in black anti-fatigue rubber drainage mats.
