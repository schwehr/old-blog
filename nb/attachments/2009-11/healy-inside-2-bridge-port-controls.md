# USCGC Healy Port Bridge Wing Controls (`healy-inside-2-bridge-port-controls.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior photograph of the port-side bridge wing control console aboard the Coast Guard icebreaker USCGC *Healy* during a dry dock tour in Seattle, Washington (November 2009).

## Console & Interior Details
- **Steering & Propulsion Controls (Left):**
  - A black helm housing on the left edge features a small wooden-rimmed ship's wheel mounted vertically on the side.
  - The flat horizontal surface holds a gray sound-powered or intercom telephone handset, a red phone handset, a blue control panel with a lever/knob, a red control panel, and a central chrome/black propulsion or thruster lever.
- **Slanted Instrument Panels (Upper):**
  - From left to right: a square display monitor, two identical light-gray rectangular intercom/speaker boxes with rows of toggle switches, a larger central display screen, a vertical white handset alongside a smaller screen and keypad, and an angled right-hand panel with rows of illuminated white indicator lights.
- **Cabinet Base & Surroundings:**
  - The black console cabinet has a wooden trim rail along the front edges and a rectangular cutout at the bottom right covered with translucent plastic sheeting taped with bright green painter's tape during shipyard maintenance.
  - To the right of the console is a wooden handrail supported by two vertical stanchions wrapped in yellow padding.
  - Five large bridge windows above the console look out into the dark night, framed by beige/tan insulated bulkheads with electrical outlet boxes and wiring conduits.
