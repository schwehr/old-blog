# USCGC Healy Aft Ship and Winch Control Console (`healy-inside-3-aften-controls.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior photograph of the aft control station aboard the USCGC *Healy* (November 2009), located just aft of the winch room on the starboard side and used for conning the ship and operating science winches during over-the-side deployments.

## Console & Equipment Details
- **Helm & Horizontal Control Deck (Foreground):**
  - On the left is a black pedestal with a small wooden-rimmed steering wheel extending from its left side.
  - In the center of the flat console deck is a chrome-and-black twin throttle/propulsion control lever with a green indicator light illuminated below it.
  - In the lower foreground is a blue-faced panel with a rotary control lever and push buttons, next to a red-faced emergency/communication panel holding a bright red coiled-cord telephone handset.
  - On the far left corner beyond the helm pedestal is a black joystick controller.
- **Slanted Upper Display & Communication Panel:**
  - From left to right: an angled square LCD monitor, two gray multi-switch communication/intercom boxes above a black radio handset unit, a central square display monitor reflecting the overhead fluorescent light, and right-side control panels with gauges, switches, and black corded telephone handsets mounted on the side.
- **Windows & Exterior View:**
  - Three large aft-facing windows with tan padded/insulated frames look out onto the dry dock and Seattle shipyard structures at dusk/night, with bright yard floodlights visible against a deep blue evening sky.
