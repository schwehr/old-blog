# USCGC Healy Knudsen Sub-Bottom Profiler Rack (`healy-inside-4-knudsen.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior photograph of an electronics equipment rack aboard the Coast Guard icebreaker USCGC *Healy* (November 2009), housing the ship's primary and backup Knudsen 320B/R deep-water sub-bottom profiler echosounder systems.

## Equipment & Rack Details
From top to bottom inside the gray 19-inch equipment rack:
1. **Top Black Rack-Mount Chassis (2U):** A black industrial server or interface unit with a central key lock, ventilation perforations, status LEDs on the right, and a white logo on the bottom center.
2. **Rack-Mount Computer (4U):** A black industrial PC chassis with silver rack handles, front ventilation slots on the left labeled with a white sticker reading `KNUDSEN 192.168.18.30`, an Intel Inside sticker at the bottom center, and a recessed drive bay on the right containing two beige optical/tape drives and a floppy drive.
3. **Primary Knudsen Echosounder (Red Chassis):** A deep red/burgundy rack-mount unit with silver front handles, gold text in the center reading `KNUDSEN ENGINEERING LIMITED` and `320B/R ECHOSOUNDER`, two yellow/beige rectangular power/breaker switches on the right, and a rectangular warning label (`WARNING ...`) above them.
4. **Backup Knudsen Echosounder (Lower Red Chassis):** A second identical red `KNUDSEN 320B/R` unit mounted directly below the first, with a large white tape label across the bottom reading `KNUDSEN ECHO #2`.
5. **Bottom Black UPS / Power Unit:** A black rack-mount uninterruptible power supply (UPS) or power conditioner at the base of the rack with glowing green status LEDs on its front panel.

## Background
