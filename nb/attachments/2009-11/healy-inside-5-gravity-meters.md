# USCGC Healy Marine Gravity Meters (`healy-inside-5-gravity-meters.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior high-angle photograph of two marine gravity meters mounted side by side on the deck of an electronics/instrument room aboard the Coast Guard icebreaker USCGC *Healy* (November 2009). As noted in the blog post, the internal component heaters must be kept continuously powered (backed up by batteries for several days) to avoid a six-month recalibration, and the flat tops of the enclosures carry a warning against using them as tables or seats.

## Instrument & Mounting Details
- **Left Gravity Meter Unit:** A square, matte-black enclosure with a ribbed/finned top cover, mounted on a specialized shock-absorbing gimbal/damping frame with metal brackets, heavy-duty corner springs/mounts, and a front serial/data plate with a green indicator tab.
- **Right Gravity Meter Unit:** A second black enclosure with a smooth, slightly domed rectangular lid mounted on a matching shock-isolated base frame.
- **Handwritten Warning Sign:** Taped across the top of the right-hand unit is a large white sheet of paper with bold black handwritten capital letters reading:
  > `THESE ARE`
  > **`NOT`** (double-underlined)
  > `TABLES OR SEATS!`
- **Base Frame & Surroundings:** Both units sit on a shared metallic baseplate bolted to the green tiled deck floor, with a small yellow label (`Healy ...`) stuck to the front right rail. A red cylindrical fire extinguisher or tank is partially visible in the upper right corner.
