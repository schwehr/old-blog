# USCGC Healy Old SeaBeam 2100 Power Amplifier Rack (`healy-inside-6-old-seabeam.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior close-up photograph of the blue electronics rack for the legacy **SeaBeam 2100** multibeam bathymetric sonar system aboard the USCGC *Healy* (November 2009), which was in the process of being removed and replaced with a newer, higher-accuracy multibeam sonar during the ship's dry dock maintenance period in Seattle.

## Panel & Controls Details
- **Enclosure & Branding:** A medium-blue rack-mount cabinet with a white serial number sticker at the top left corner reading `S/N 14102`. Below a wide rectangular foam-filled air filter/ventilation grille framed in silver metal, the front panel features the white stenciled logo `SEA BEAM 2100` on the left and `POWER AMPLIFIER` on the right.
- **Test Points & Indicator Panel (Middle):**
  - **Left Section (`SCALED OUTPUT VOLTAGE 10MV/V` & `SCALED OUTPUT CURRENT 100MV/A`):** Two horizontal rows of six pairs of red and black banana jack test ports labeled `CH1` through `CH6`.
  - **Center Section (`SCALED HVPS VOLTAGE 10MV/V`):** Three pairs of red and black banana jacks labeled `CHANNEL 1 & 2`, `CHANNEL 3 & 4`, and `CHANNEL 5 & 6`, positioned above two indicator lamps labeled `ON` (green) and `FAULT` (red).
  - **Right-Center Section (`POWER SUPPLIES`):** Five green indicator lights arranged in two rows, labeled `+5V`, `+15V` (top row) and `-15V`, `+12V`, `-12V` (bottom row).
  - **Far Right Section (`MAIN POWER CIRCUIT BREAKER`):** A vertical rectangular recessed cutout housing the main breaker switch.
- **Amplifier Modules (Bottom):** Three slide-out vertical power amplifier drawers corresponding to channels `CH1`/`CH2`, `CH3`/`CH4`, and `CH5`/`CH6`. Each module has a chrome pull handle, a `SEA BEAM 2100` logo, status indicators, and a perforated metal mesh vent screen at the bottom.
