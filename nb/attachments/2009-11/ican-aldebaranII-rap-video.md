# Aldebaran II Whale Notices Display in the Boston TSS (`ican-aldebaranII-rap-video.jpg`)

## Overview

## Nautical Chart Display (Right)
- **Geography & Chart Bounds:** Shows the Massachusetts coastline (Boston Harbor on the left, Cape Cod hook at the bottom right) in tan, shallow coastal water in blue, and deeper offshore water in light gray-blue. Green rectangular outlines mark S-57 Electronic Navigational Chart (ENC) cells (`13278`, `13270`, `13267`).
- **Boston TSS & Whale Detection Zones:**
  - Dashed magenta lines outline the inbound and outbound lanes of the Boston Traffic Separation Scheme curving around Cape Cod into Boston.
  - Ten circular whale detection buffer zones (5 nautical mile radius each) are arranged along the center of the TSS. Eight circles are shaded with horizontal **green** hatch lines (indicating no right whales detected in the last 24 hours), while two circles in the middle of the east-west lane are shaded with horizontal **orange** hatch lines (indicating active Right Whale acoustic detections).
- **Vessel Target:** An orange ship vector and track line labeled **`TORM LENE`** is shown transiting eastward through the orange whale notice zones inside the TSS.
- **Scale Bar:** A vertical orange-and-blue range scale bar along the left edge of the chart marks distances at `16 Nm`, `8 Nm`, `0`, `4 Nm`, and `8 Nm`.
