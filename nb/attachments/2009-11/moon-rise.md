# Autumn Dusk Moonrise Over Neighborhood (`moon-rise.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor evening photograph taken in early November 2009 showing a bright full or nearly full moon rising in a pink-and-purple twilight sky above bare autumn trees and neighborhood houses.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Moon:** Near the center of the frame, a small, bright white full moon glows just above the treetops. Thin clouds across the central sky are illuminated in soft salmon-pink and rose hues by the setting sun, transitioning into a dusty blue-gray dusk sky toward the top.
- **Trees:** On the right side of the image, tall deciduous trees stand silhouetted against the twilight sky, their branches almost completely bare of leaves at the end of fall. A few bare branches from a nearby tree hang down into the upper-left foreground.
- **Houses & Fence (Left & Center):**
  - On the left is a single-story house with light-colored horizontal siding, two vertical double-hung windows with white trim, and a tall shiny metal chimney pipe rising from its shingled roof.
  - Behind and to the right of the foreground house are the pitched roofs of neighboring houses/garages, one with a brick chimney.
  - In the foreground, a white picket fence encloses a darkened yard.
