# Seattle Skyline at Dusk from Harbor Island Dry Dock (`seattle-from-harbor-island.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide panoramic photograph of the downtown Seattle skyline and Elliott Bay waterfront at dusk, taken in November 2009 from the dry dock at Todd Shipyard on Harbor Island while visiting the USCGC *Healy*.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Water:** Heavy blue-gray evening clouds blanket the sky above the city. Across the bottom foreground, the dark blue waters of Elliott Bay reflect warm golden and orange lights from the shoreline. In the lower right foreground, a small barge or dock structure with a bright glowing work light sits in the harbor.
- **Downtown Seattle Skyline (Center):** High-rise office towers rise along the waterfront. Several glass and stone facades catch the warm golden-orange glow of the setting sun on their western faces, while the tallest dark skyscraper on the right of the cluster—the **Columbia Center**—stands in deep silhouette against the clouds.
- **Port Facilities & Container Ship (Right):** Along the right side of the shoreline, bright orange-red port lights illuminate industrial waterfront structures and four red container cranes. Moored beneath the cranes is a dark-hulled container ship with large white block letters reading **`H A N J I N`** painted along its starboard side.
