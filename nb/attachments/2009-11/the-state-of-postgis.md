# PostGIS Release Timeline Slide (`the-state-of-postgis.png`)

## Overview
A presentation slide screenshot from Paul Ramsey's talk **"The State of PostGIS"** (OpenGeo, November 2009), showing a two-row chronological timeline of major PostGIS versions and feature milestones from **2001 to 2011**, with the upcoming **`GEOGRAPHY`** feature in version **1.5** highlighted by a red oval.

## Timeline Details

### Top Timeline Row (`2001` – `2006`)
A horizontal black timeline axis labeled `2001` on the left and `2006` on the right, with colored version blocks along the bar and callout boxes above and below:
- **`0.1`** (gray):
  - Above: `Type and index`
- **`0.5`** (tan/yellow):
  - Above: `WKT WKB`
  - Below: `Distance`, `Point-in-polygon`
- **`0.6`** (light brown):
  - Above: `OGC standards`
- **`0.7`** (salmon/terracotta):
  - Above: `PgSQL 7.2`, `Reprojection`
  - Below: `Index selectivity`, `JTS`
- **`0.8`** (lavender/purple):
  - Above: `OGC SFSQL`
  - Below: `GEOS`
- **`0.9`** (light blue):
  - Above: `ANALYZE`
  - Below: `LWGEOM experiment`
- **`1.0`** (light teal):
  - Above: `4D`, `Polygonize`
  - Below: `LWGEOM`

### Bottom Timeline Row (`2006` – `2011`)
A second horizontal black timeline axis labeled `2006` on the left and `2011` on the right:
- **`1.1`** (mint green):
  - Above: `Regression tests`, `Linemerge`
  - Below: `LRS`, `SRS performance`
- **`1.2`** (yellow-green):
  - Above: `ISO SQL/MM`
  - Below: `Curves`
- **`1.3`** (steel blue):
  - Above: `Performance`
  - Below: `Stability`
- **`1.4`** (terracotta/salmon):
  - Above: `Cascaded union`, `New docs`
  - Below: `Prepared geometry`, `Developer improvements`
- **`1.5`** (tan/gold):
  - Above: **`GEOGRAPHY`** — circled with a bright red oval to emphasize spherical earth coordinate support.

## Branding
