# First BAG Image Overlay in Google Earth (`bag-in-google-earth.png`)

## Overview
A full-window screenshot of **Google Earth** on Mac OS X displaying the first test export of a **Bathymetry Attributed Grid (BAG)** (`H11657_1of5_1m_Final_Depth`) converted via GDAL, GMT, Fledermaus, and DMagic into a KML ground overlay in Chesapeake Bay.

## Interface & Sidebar Controls
- **Mac Menu Bar (Top):** Shows the Apple icon, **Google Earth** application menu (`File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Tools`, `Add`, `Window`, `Help`), system tray icons, network throughput monitor (`Tx: 0B/s`, `Rx: 0B/s`), and `8%` CPU indicator.
- **Toolbar:** Standard Google Earth toolbar buttons (hide/show sidebar, add placemark, polygon, path, image overlay, record tour, historical imagery, sunlight, planet switcher, ruler, email, print, view in Google Maps).
- **Left Sidebar:**
  - **Search Panel:** Active **`Fly To`** tab (`Find Businesses`, `Directions`) with prompt `Fly to e.g., 37 25.818' N, 122 05.36' W`.
  - **Places Panel:**
    - `My Places` (checked) -> `sbnms.kml` (checked).
    - `Temporary Places` (checked) -> **`H11657_1of5_1m_Final_Depth_sd`** (checked and highlighted in blue), with subtitle **`KML Exporter: DMagic from IVS 3D`**.
  - **Layers Panel:** `Primary Database` showing unchecked layers (`Geographic Web`, `Roads`, `3D Buildings`, `Street View`, `Traffic`, `Weather`, `Gallery`) and checked **`Borders and Labels`**.

## 3D Map Viewport
- **Geographic View:** Looking north-northwest at an oblique angle across the eastern shore of Chesapeake Bay (Delmarva Peninsula on the right, mainland Virginia and yellow state/county boundary lines in the upper distance) against a black starry sky horizon.
- **BAG Overlay:** In the lower center of the blue bay water sits a small white-bordered rectangular bathymetric patch (`H11657_1of5_1m_Final_Depth_sd`), colored in deep blue on the left grading to cyan-green on the right.
- **Status Bar & Attribution (Bottom):**
  - Center attribution: `© 2009 Tele Atlas`, `Data SIO, NOAA, U.S. Navy, NGA, GEBCO`, `© 2009 Google`, `© 2009 Europa Technologies`.
  - Bottom coordinates bar: **`lat 37.645650° lon -76.010237° elev -7 m`** and **`Eye alt 11.36 km`**.
