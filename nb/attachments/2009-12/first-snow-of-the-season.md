# First Snow of the 2009–2010 Winter in Dover, NH (`first-snow-of-the-season.jpg`)

## Overview
An early morning photograph looking out over a snow-covered detached garage, driveway, and surrounding trees in Dover, New Hampshire, capturing the first accumulating snowfall of the 2009–2010 winter season on December 6, 2009.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Left):** A single-story detached two-car garage with pale gray-green siding, white trim, two paneled garage doors (each with a top row of three 4-pane windows), and a hipped roof blanketed in a smooth, untouched layer of fresh white snow. A vertical metal pipe/mast rises from the left side of the roof.
- **Midground (Right):** A snow-covered yard and driveway stretch behind and to the right of the garage, bordered on the right by heavily snow-laden evergreen boughs and overarching deciduous tree branches coated in thick white snow.
- **Background & Sky:** Tall bare deciduous trees and conifers stand against a soft pinkish-orange and pale purple-gray dawn sky filtering through the branches.
