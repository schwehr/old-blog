# Close-Up of GDAL Color Relief on a Large BAG Grid (`gdal-color-relief-bag-closeup.png`)

## Overview
A zoomed-in view of a `gdaldem color-relief` rendering of a large Bathymetry Attributed Grid (`H11335_0_80m`), illustrating high-resolution multibeam sonar data density, small no-data gaps, and steep nearshore depth transitions.

## Visual & Bathymetric Details
- **Central Plateau (Red):** Most of the frame is filled with a bright red color zone representing the main channel floor. Running across this red surface are intricate, curving, fingerprint-like patterns of tiny black pixels (unfilled no-data grid cells between individual multibeam sonar pings and beams at 80 cm resolution) that trace the vessel's survey track turns and outer-beam spacing.
- **Steep Channel Margins (Rainbow Gradient):** Along the lower-left edge and the right border, the seafloor rises steeply toward the fjord walls, cycling rapidly through a narrow rainbow band of orange, yellow, bright green, cyan, blue, and purple before meeting the solid black (`0 0 0`) no-data background outside the survey boundary.
