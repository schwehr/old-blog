# GDAL Hillshade Bathymetry Image (`gdal-hillshade.png`)

## Overview
A grayscale shaded-relief (hillshade) image of hydrographic survey **`H11657_1of5_1m_Final_Depth.grd`** generated by `gdaldem hillshade` and rendered properly in Adobe Photoshop (contrasting with an earlier corrupted rendering in Apple Preview).

## Visual & Bathymetric Details
- **Swath Structure & Background:** Approximately 23 horizontal E–W multibeam survey swaths span the rectangular frame against a uniform light-gray no-data border and thin light-gray inter-swath gaps.
- **Topographic Shading:** Illuminated from the northwest (upper left), the grayscale shading reveals detailed seafloor morphology without color:
  - **Upper-Right Feature:** A prominent, rugged oval mound or rock outcrop with high-contrast bright highlights on its northwest-facing slopes and dark shadows on its southeast side, set within a broad scoured depression.
  - **Central & Left Ridges:** Gentle, broad arcuate sand ridges and subtle trough slopes curve across the left and central sections of the grid.
  - **Fine Texture:** High-frequency seafloor ripples and subtle along-track sonar nadir/outer-beam texture are clearly resolved along each horizontal strip.
