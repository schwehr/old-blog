# Castle-Themed Christmas Light Display in San Diego (`sd-xmas.jpg`)

## Overview
A nighttime photograph of a two-story suburban house in San Diego, California,elaborately decorated for Christmas with temporary castle battlements, a conical turret, and thousands of holiday lights on Christmas Eve (December 24, 2009).

## Scene Description
- **Castle Facade & Turret (Left)**:
  - The left side of the house is transformed into a stone-textured round castle tower topped with crenellated battlements outlined in bright white rope and icicle lights.
  - Above the tower rises a conical spire of white light strands crowned with a large glowing white five-pointed star.
  - An illuminated Christmas wreath with a red bow hangs on the center of the turret wall, flanked on the lower battlement corners by two glowing blue-and-white angel figures blowing trumpets.
  - Below the battlements stands a tall, conical Christmas tree covered in warm white lights, surrounded by low courtyard walls draped in dense mini-lights and small glowing pathway figures along the front sidewalk.
- **Roofline & Garage Side (Right)**:
  - Along the upper roof peak to the right of the turret, a lighted wireframe display depicts Santa Claus in a red sleigh pulled by four white reindeer taking flight toward the left.
  - The right eave is lined with white icicle lights, flanked by large striped candy-cane arches and a brightly lit rectangular sign panel on the lower roof.
  - Below the eave on the brick-patterned second-story wall, icicle lights trace a smaller three-crenellation battlement motif above another lit wreath with a red bow and three glowing white stars to the right.
  - In the lower-right yard beside the dark garage door, additional holiday figures—including a red light sculpture, small lit trees, and three bright vertical figures along a fence—glow beside the driveway.
