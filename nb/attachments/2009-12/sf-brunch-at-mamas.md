# Brunch Line Outside Mama's on Washington Square in San Francisco (`sf-brunch-at-mamas.jpg`)

## Overview
A street-level photograph showing the exterior of **Mama's on Washington Square**, a famous brunch restaurant at the corner of Stockton and Filbert Streets in San Francisco's North Beach neighborhood, with a line of patrons waiting along the sidewalk under an overcast December sky.

## Scene Description
- **Building & Storefront**:
  - The restaurant occupies the ground floor of a classic white-painted San Francisco corner building featuring curved and angled bay windows on the second floor and an arched recessed doorway on the left.
  - A dark hunter-green canvas awning wraps around the corner entrance, decorated with small pink-and-green rosebud motifs.
- **Visible Signage & Text**:
  - Front awning panel (facing left/Stockton St.): White cursive script reading `Mama's` with a small pink flower above the apostrophe.
  - Corner street sign (mounted on the dark pole in the foreground): White capital letters on a dark plate reading `FILBERT` with a red-white-green Italian tricolor band painted around the pole below.
  - Right awning panel (facing Filbert St.): Partially occluded by the street pole, reading `Mama's on Washington Square` above `BREAKFAST • LUNCH`.
- **People & Street Scene**:
  - A crowd of roughly a dozen people dressed in dark winter jackets and jeans queues along the sidewalk beneath the right side of the green awning, waiting to enter the white double doors.
  - A gold/champagne-colored Mercedes-Benz M-Class SUV is parked along the curb in the lower-left foreground.
  - To the right, Filbert Street slopes upward into the distance, lined with parked cars (including a white sedan beside a white fire hydrant and a gray octagon stop sign back), street trees, and multi-story apartment buildings with fire escapes.
