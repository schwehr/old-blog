# SPOT Personal Tracker Web Interface Showing Track Off Massachusetts Coast (`another-try-with-spot.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the SPOT Satellite GPS Messenger web tracking interface (`share.findmespot.com`), displaying position reports recorded by Kurt Schwehr (`schwehr`) on January 21, 2010, during a trip from the Salem/Peabody area of Massachusetts out to the LNG carrier *Explorer* at the Northeast Gateway terminal in Massachusetts Bay.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Header**: A brushed-metal dark gray banner featuring the white and gray **spot** logo with a globe and radiating signal waves in the upper left.
- **Left Panel (`SPOT` / `Messages`)**:
  - Header bars for `SPOT` (with a `<<` collapse button), `Messages` (with `-` and pop-out icons), and `Map Legend` (with a yellow `i` information icon).
  - Table column headers: `Type`, `Time` (with a downward sort arrow), and `Messenger`.
  - Group header: `[-] Messenger: schwehr` followed by an orange circle icon and `(9 Messages)`.
  - **Message List**: Nine rows, each with a `[+]` expand icon on the left and `schwehr` with an orange circle icon on the right:
    - Rows 1–8: `Track Prc` (Track Progress) | `1 Day ago ( January 21` | `schwehr` (Row 1 is highlighted in light blue).
    - Row 9: `OK` | `1 Day ago ( January 21` | `schwehr`.
- **Right Panel (`Map`)**:
  - Active `Map` tab at the top left of the map frame.
  - A **spot** logo watermark with an orange globe in the upper-left corner of the map.
  - Standard Google Maps pan controls (four directional arrows around a center button) and a vertical zoom slider bar with `+` and `-` buttons along the left side.

## Map & Track Details
- **Geographic Coverage**: The map shows the North Shore and Greater Boston region of Massachusetts, extending from Ipswich Bay, Pigeon Cove, Annisquam, Rockport, and Gloucester in the northeast down through Essex, Hamilton, Wenham, Topsfield, Boxford, Middleton, Beverly, Salem, Marblehead, Peabody, Lynnfield, Reading, Wakefield, Stoneham, Saugus, Lynn, Swampscott, Nahant, Melrose, Medford, Malden, Revere, Chelsea, Somerville, Winthrop, Boston, Brookline, Quincy Bay, and Hull. Major highways (I-95, Route 128, Route 3) are drawn in yellow/orange.
- **Tracked Waypoints**:
  - Waypoint `1`: Marked near the Peabody/Danvers/Salem area with a black-and-orange teardrop pin containing a white wireless signal icon and an orange callout badge labeled `1`.
  - Waypoint `7` (cluster): Located offshore to the southeast in Massachusetts Bay, marked with a black-and-orange teardrop pin containing a white footprint icon and an orange callout badge labeled `7` with a dark drop shadow.
  - A straight tan/orange track line connects the onshore waypoint (`1`) to the offshore cluster (`7`).
