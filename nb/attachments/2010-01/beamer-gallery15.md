# Sample LaTeX Beamer Presentation Slide Showing the Poincaré Inequality (`beamer-gallery15.png`)

## Overview
A sample presentation slide from Rouben Rostamian's UMBC LaTeX Beamer gallery, illustrating how Beamer formats mathematical theorems, equations, itemized lists, and enumerated lists using a classic blue-and-black color theme.

## Slide Layout & Visual Elements
- **Top Header Banner**: A horizontal bar with a left-to-right gradient from medium royal blue to black, displaying the slide title `A sample slide` in white sans-serif text on the left.
- **Theorem Block**:
  - Centered in the upper half of the slide with rounded corners and a subtle gray drop shadow along the bottom and right edges.
  - **Block Header**: A dark blue title bar reading `Theorem (The Poincaré inequality)` in light blue/white sans-serif text.
  - **Block Body**: A pale lavender-gray box containing italicized sans-serif text and a centered mathematical display equation:
    - Text: *Suppose $\Omega \subset \mathbf{R}^n$ is a bounded domain with smooth boundary. Then there exists a $\lambda > 0$, depending only on $\Omega$, such that for any function $f$ in the Sobolev space $H_0^1(\Omega)$ we have:*
    - Displayed equation:
      $$\int_{\Omega} |\nabla u|^2 \, dx \ge \lambda \int_{\Omega} |u|^2 \, dx.$$
- **Lists Section**:
  - Introductory line below the theorem block: `Here is what itemized and enumerated lists look like:` (with *itemized* and *enumerated* in italics).
  - **Left Column (Itemized List)**: Three items (`itemized item 1`, `itemized item 2`, `itemized item 3`), each preceded by a glossy dark-blue 3D sphere/square bullet icon.
  - **Right Column (Enumerated List)**: Three items (`enumerated item 1`, `enumerated item 2`, `enumerated item 3`), each preceded by a dark-blue rounded square badge containing the white numbers `1`, `2`, and `3`.
- **Bottom Footer & Navigation**:
  - Above the footer on the lower right is a row of faint light-blue Beamer navigation symbols (slide, frame, section, subsection, and search/zoom icons).
  - The bottom status bar is split into two halves: a black left half reading `R. Rostamian` in light blue/white text, and a medium-blue right half reading `A short proof`.
