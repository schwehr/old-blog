# Sunset Over Massachusetts Bay Toward Boston (`boston-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect color photograph taken from the water in Massachusetts Bay on the evening of January 21, 2010, capturing the sun setting on the western horizon toward Boston after Kurt Schwehr's visit to the LNG carrier *Explorer*.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Sun**: The setting sun appears slightly right of center just touching the horizon, glowing bright white-yellow at its core and surrounded by vivid orange, red, and golden light. Low, streaky cloud bands radiate diagonally across the orange-pink sky to the left and right, transitioning upward into a clear dusk sky of muted slate blue.
- **Horizon & Silhouette**: Along the flat ocean horizon, dark silhouettes are faintly visible against the orange glow: two small vessel or island profiles to the left of the sun, and the distant, low skyline silhouette of downtown Boston skyscrapers just to the right of the solar disk.
- **Water**: The lower half of the frame is filled with the dark, choppy surface of Massachusetts Bay, rendered in deep navy and violet-blue tones with warm pinkish-orange reflections dancing on the wave crests directly below the setting sun.
