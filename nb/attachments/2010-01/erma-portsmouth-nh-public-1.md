# ERMA Portsmouth Public Web Interface With Introductory Modal Dialog (`erma-portsmouth-nh-public-1.png`)

## Overview
A desktop web browser screenshot (Firefox on Mac OS X) showing the newly launched public view of **ERMA (Environmental Response Management Application)** for Portsmouth, New Hampshire (`https://portsmouth.erma.unh.edu/erma.html#x=-70.75&y=43.06&z=10&layers=`), featuring the `erma_intro.html` welcome and credits popup window over the map.

## Browser & Application Frame
- **Browser Chrome**: Mac OS X Firefox window titled `ERMA` with back/forward buttons, reload, stop, home, Delicious bookmark buttons, the URL bar showing the `portsmouth.erma.unh.edu` HTTPS address, a Google search box on the right, and two tabs both titled `ERMA`.
- **ERMA Header Banner**: Displays `ERMA | Environmental Response Management Application / Portsmouth, New Hampshire` on the left over a panoramic autumn photograph of Portsmouth harbor buildings and trees, with `Information` and `Help` tabs at the bottom left and a `Login` tab at the bottom right.
- **Background Map & Right Sidebar**:
  - Behind the modal dialog, a Google Maps road/terrain view of southeastern New Hampshire and southern Maine (Rochester, Barrington, Dover, Portsmouth, Exeter, Hampton, Amesbury, Haverhill, Newburyport, Wells, Kennebunkport) is visible at `Scale: 1: 433K`, `Zoom Level: 10`, `Location: 43.32718°, -70.64072°`.
  - The right panel shows tabs `Layers` (active), `Legend`, `Zoom`, `Download`, `Print`, a `reload` button, and expandable layer groups (`[+] Background`, `[+] Response Planning`, etc.) with `clear | all` links.
- **Bottom Footer**: Light gray bar with the NOAA emblem on the left, links to `US DOC | NOAA | NOS | NOAA Office of Response & Restoration` and `Privacy policy | Email comments`, and right-aligned text `Coastal Response Research Center / ©2007 - 2010 University of New Hampshire`.
