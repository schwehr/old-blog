# ERMA Portsmouth Public Map View Showing Bathymetry and Restoration Sites (`erma-portsmouth-nh-public-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the main map workspace of the public **ERMA (Environmental Response Management Application)** portal for Portsmouth, New Hampshire, displaying Google Terrain background tiles overlaid with multibeam bathymetry of the Piscataqua River and offshore Portsmouth Harbor approaches, Coast Guard station markers, and NMFS Restoration Project locations.

## Map View & Overlays
- **Geographic Area**: Centered on the Piscataqua River estuary, Great Bay / Little Bay (Newington, Fox Point, Herods Cove, Greenland), Portsmouth, NH, Rye / Rye Harbor State Park, Odiorne Point State Park, New Castle, and Kittery / Eliot / South Eliot / York, Maine (`Scale: 1: 108K`, `Zoom Level: 12`, `Location: 43.13932°, -70.75642°`).
- **Map Overlays**:
  - **Bathymetry**: Color-shaded multibeam bathymetry ribbons following Little Bay, the Piscataqua River channel past Portsmouth and Kittery Point, and fanning out into the Gulf of Maine offshore of Odiorne Point and Rye. Shallow areas are colored warm brown/orange/yellow and deeper channel and offshore areas are green and blue.
  - **Fairway / Channel Lines**: Black boundary lines near Portsmouth Harbor / Wentworth Rd.
  - **Point Markers**:
    - Bright green triangles marking **NMFS Restoration Projects** scattered around Great Bay/Greenland, Fox Point, Portsmouth (South Mill Pond), Wentworth Rd, Odiorne Point State Park, and Rye Harbor State Park.
    - A bright red circle marker near Fort Foster / Shaws Hill at the mouth of the harbor (`ESI Coast Guard Stations`).
    - Small dark-outlined pink/red circles clustered along the coast near Rye Harbor State Park.
- **Map Controls**:
  - Top-left toolbar inside the map: Pan hand icon (selected), zoom-box magnifying glass, `i` (Identify tool), ruler (measure distance), and area-measure icon.
  - Left side: Pan directional circle and vertical zoom slider bar.
  - Bottom left: `POWERED BY Google` logo; bottom right of map: `Map data ©2009 Google - Terms of Use`.
