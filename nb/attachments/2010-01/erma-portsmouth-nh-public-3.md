# ERMA Portsmouth Identify Popup Showing NMFS Restoration Project Details (`erma-portsmouth-nh-public-3.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot of the **ERMA (Environmental Response Management Application)** Portsmouth public web interface demonstrating the interactive `Identify` query tool. Black callout lines and a black rectangle drawn by Kurt Schwehr highlight the green triangle marker in Portsmouth that was clicked to open the `Identify` table popup above it.

## Visual Annotations & Map Context
- In the lower map section around `Portsmouth`, a thick black rectangle frames a bright green triangle marker overlaid with an `X` (located at South Mill Pond in Portsmouth, NH).
- Two thick black lines angle upward and outward from the top-left and bottom-right corners of that black rectangle to the bottom corners of the floating `Identify` window, visually indicating that the popup displays the attributes of the boxed map feature.
- To the right of the boxed marker, the color-shaded bathymetry of the Piscataqua River and a red circle marker near Shaws Hill are visible, along with the right-hand layer tree showing `[-] Restoration` -> `[x] NMFS Restoration Projects`.
