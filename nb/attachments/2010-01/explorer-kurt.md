# Kurt Schwehr on the Deck of the LNG Carrier *Explorer* (`explorer-kurt.jpg`)

## Overview
A color photograph of Kurt Schwehr standing on the main deck of the LNG carrier *Explorer* on a bright, sunny winter day (January 21, 2010) at the Northeast Gateway offshore terminal in Massachusetts Bay, with crew members operating a deck crane and personnel transfer basket in the background.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Subject (Kurt Schwehr)**:
  - Positioned in the lower-right foreground facing the camera.
  - Wearing a white hard hat with a blue logo on the front, a dark green and black Columbia winter jacket over a gray layer, a green lanyard with an ID badge, and a black shoulder strap.
- **Deck & Piping**:
  - A wide gray-painted steel walkway runs along the starboard side of the ship, bordered on the left by white-painted safety railings with angled stanchion braces casting sharp diagonal shadows across the deck.
  - On the right side of the walkway run massive, insulated silver-gray gas transfer pipes and elevated steel manifold platforms and stairways.
- **Background Crew & Crane Operations**:
  - In the middle distance along the deck walkway, five crew members wearing hard hats, high-visibility vests/jackets, and bright orange/white work trousers stand gathered around a large, bright yellow cylindrical weight/equipment unit suspended from a tall white pedestal crane.
  - A crane operator in a green hard hat and blue jacket is visible inside the elevated white cab high up on the crane column.
  - Lying flat on the gray deck just in front of the crew is the deflated/collapsed bright orange ring and netting of the Billy Pugh personnel transfer basket.
  - Another crew member in orange coveralls, a black vest, and a green hard hat stands on the elevated piping catwalk to the right, below tall white vent masts rising against the clear blue sky.
- **Ocean & Horizon**: To the left of the ship's railing, deep blue ocean water stretches to a distant, low coastline strip under a crisp blue sky with a few small puffy clouds near the horizon.
