# Starboard Broadside View of the LNG Carrier *Explorer* at Sea (`explorer-lng-ship.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide horizontal color photograph taken from an approaching workboat (*Gateway Endeavor*) on January 21, 2010, showing the massive red-hulled liquefied natural gas (LNG) tanker *Explorer* moored offshore at the Northeast Gateway terminal in Massachusetts Bay.

## Vessel & Scene Description
- **Hull**: The ship's long, high-freeboard hull spans nearly the entire width of the frame, painted a bright reddish-orange above the waterline with white draft marks and lettering near the bow (left) and white mooring ports along the side.
- **Deck Trunk & Cargo Equipment**: Above the red hull rises a continuous white deckhouse/trunk structure enclosing the membrane LNG tanks, topped by a series of vertical white vent masts, midships manifold piping structures, and a prominent white deck crane angled slightly upward near midships.
- **Aft Superstructure**: Toward the stern (right) stands a multi-story white accommodation block topped by the navigation bridge wings and radar/communications mast, with an orange enclosed lifeboat mounted in davits along the side and a tall white-and-black exhaust funnel just aft of the deckhouse.
- **Water & Sky**: In the foreground, dark royal-blue ocean ripples fill the bottom fifth of the image; behind the ship, a low bank of pale pinkish-tan morning clouds sits near the horizon beneath a clear, pale blue-gray winter sky.
