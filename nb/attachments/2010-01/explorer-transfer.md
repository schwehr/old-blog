# Personnel Basket Crane Transfer Onto the LNG Carrier *Explorer* (`explorer-transfer.jpg`)

## Overview
A low-angle color photograph looking up from the deck of a workboat alongside the LNG carrier *Explorer* on January 21, 2010, showing a passenger being hoisted high in the air in a rope-and-ring personnel transfer basket (Billy Pugh net) suspended from the ship's long white deck crane against a clear blue sky.

## Scene Description
- **Crane & Personnel Basket**:
  - A tall white pedestal crane rises from the *Explorer*'s upper deck on the left, its long box-boom extending diagonally upward and to the right across the bright blue sky, with a walkway and handrail running along the top of the boom.
  - Hanging from the crane cable and a bright red/orange pennant line on the right is a conical rope personnel transfer basket with a wide, padded bright-orange circular base ring and a smaller orange top ring.
  - One person wearing dark clothing stands on the outer rim of the orange base ring, holding onto the angled rope netting while being lifted between vessels, with gear bags stowed inside the center of the basket and a thin tagline trailing down toward the ship's deck.
- **Ship Structure & Deck Crew**:
  - Along the bottom of the frame is the dark reddish-brown upper hull plating and a circular mooring chock (Panama chock) of the *Explorer*, topped by the stark white side wall of the upper trunk deck.
  - Access stairs and walkways climb the white structure on the left next to large gray manifold pipes.
  - Along the upper white deck railing on the right, several crew members in hard hats and high-visibility vests stand watching and tending the transfer operation, flanked by a second white crane boom in the background and a tall white mast structure on the far right.
