# View from the Bridge of LNG Ship Explorer (`explorer-view-from-bridge.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime photograph taken from inside the bridge of the liquefied natural gas (LNG) tanker *Explorer* while moored at the Northeast Gateway (NEG) offshore terminal near Boston, Massachusetts, in January 2010. The photo looks out through the wide bridge windows across the vessel's deck, crane, and open blue ocean.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Bridge Interior)**:
  - A gray metal bridge console runs across the bottom of the frame beneath two large windows separated by a thick vertical window mullion on the left.
  - Sitting on the console counter is an open silver Apple MacBook Pro laptop displaying a nautical chart and navigation software on its screen, with a black non-slip mat to its left and a white information placard to its right.
  - Below the counter edge are labeled cabinets, a dark-framed wheelhouse poster, a long black grab rail, and a black handset telephone mounted near the lower right.
  - Two heavy-duty windshield wipers hang vertically down the center of the main right-hand window, and a beaded window-shade pull chain hangs in front of the left window.
- **Middle Ground (Vessel Deck & Crane)**:
  - Through the left window, the expansive forward deck of the LNG carrier stretches into the distance, crowded with silver and white gas-transfer piping, manifolds, and two tall white vertical masts/vents.
  - Through the main right window, a flat gray deck section surrounded by white safety railings is visible, with a large white deck crane mounted on the starboard side. The crane features a long angled boom with a walkway ladder along its side, hanging wire cables with a yellow hook block, and a bright green tarp cover over its rear housing.
- **Background**:
  - Calm, deep blue ocean water meets a sharp, clear horizon under a bright blue sky.
