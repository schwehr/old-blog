# Workboat Manisee (`manisee-workboat.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle daytime photograph taken from the deck of the LNG tanker *Explorer*, looking down at the workboat (and former car ferry) *Manisee* waiting on the water below to transport visitors back to Salem, Massachusetts, in January 2010.

## Scene Description
- **Vessel (*Manisee*)**:
  - Viewed from above and off its port bow, the vessel has a wide, flat-decked ferry/workboat hull painted black along the bottom and white along the upper hull sides, with the name **`MANISEE`** painted in dark blue block letters on the port bow.
  - A row of round portholes runs along the port side of the main cabin enclosure, which is lined with numerous black rubber tire fenders hanging along both the upper deck railing and lower hull side.
  - Forward, an elevated white pilothouse sits above the cabin roof, topped with radar domes and vertical communications masts.
  - Behind the pilothouse is an expansive, flat gray upper deck enclosed by white railings, where two crew members/passengers stand near the aft starboard corner.
  - The aft section of the main deck is an open wooden/yellow-painted work deck bordered by low yellow bulwarks, holding a bright yellow cylindrical piece of equipment secured with lines, with heavy black rubber fenders mounted on the stern corners.
- **Environment & Foreground**:
  - The boat floats on dark blue-green, lightly rippled ocean water.
  - In the lower right corner, a section of the white metal safety railing and red-orange deck edge of the LNG ship *Explorer* frames the shot.
