# USCG MISLE Incident Icons (`misle-incident-icons.png`)

## Overview
A grid of 22 glossy, rounded-square vector icons designed by Colleen against a dark navy-blue background to represent different types of maritime accidents, casualties, and incidents recorded in the U.S. Coast Guard's Marine Information for Safety and Law Enforcement (MISLE) database.

## Icon Grid Details (Row by Row, Left to Right)
- **Row 1**:
  1. **Power / Electrical Failure (Red background)**: A white-and-yellow ship silhouette below an unplugged green two-prong electrical plug and yellow socket with sparks.
  2. **Sinking / Oil Spill (Green background)**: A dark blue ship bow-down sinking into a large black oil slick spreading in a zigzag pattern below.
  3. **Adrift / Normal Underway Vessel (Orange background)**: A side profile of a white-and-orange ship floating on blue waves.
  4. **Grounding (Light blue background)**: A purple-and-white ship run aground onto a yellow sandy island with a brown-trunked green palm tree under a yellow sun.
  5. **Submerged / Sunk / Man Overboard Splash (Purple background)**: Concentric blue water ripples with three water droplets splashing upward.
- **Row 2**:
  1. **Explosion / Fire on Bow (Dark red background)**: Front bow view of a blue-and-white ship with a bright yellow-and-orange starburst explosion on its starboard side.
  2. **Allision / Collision with Fixed Structure (Teal green background)**: A white ship bow crashing head-on into a vertical white pier/wall with yellow and blue sparks flying.
  3. **Capsizing / Heavy Roll / Listing (Coral red background)**: Front bow view of a blue-and-white ship tilting sharply to one side with white curved motion lines around it.
  4. **Abandoned / Derelict Vessel (Dark blue background)**: Front bow view of a red-and-white ship draped with a white spiderweb on the right and a small white spider dangling on a thread on the left.
  5. **Sinking by the Bow / Engine Smoke (Indigo purple background)**: Side view of a yellow-and-white ship pitching bow-down into the water with white puffs of smoke rising from its stack.
- **Row 3**:
  1. **Alcohol / Intoxication Incident (Pink/magenta background)**: A white martini glass containing a green olive on a toothpick.
  2. **Hull Breach / Structural Failure (Olive-yellow background)**: Side profile of a magenta-and-white ship with a jagged vertical crack splitting the hull near the stern.
  3. **Environmental / Ecological Damage (Gold/orange background)**: A green leaf split down the middle by a jagged crack.
  4. **Vessel Fire (Teal-blue background)**: Front bow view of a green-and-white ship with large orange-and-red flames engulfing its port side.
  5. **Drug / Substance / Broken Glass Incident (Bright violet-pink background)**: A light blue wine/goblet glass cracked vertically down the bowl.
- **Row 4**:
  1. **Loss of Steering / Locked Helm (Salmon pink background)**: A white eight-spoked ship's wheel with a blue padlock attached to its lower right rim.
  2. **Capsized Vessel (Turquoise background)**: An upside-down red-and-white ship hull with its propeller in the air and white superstructure submerged underwater between two orange fish.
  3. **Emergency / Distress Alarm (Yellow-gold background)**: Side profile of a white ship with a flashing red siren beacon mounted atop its cabin roof.
  4. **Vessel-to-Vessel Collision (Light blue-gray background)**: Two white ship bows crashing head-on into each other with yellow and blue impact sparks flying above.
  5. **Severe Weather / Storm Damage (Slate blue-gray background)**: Side profile of a yellow-and-white ship beneath a gray storm cloud pelting the stern with heavy rain/hail streaks.
- **Row 5**:
  1. **Cargo / Crane Rigging Failure (Seafoam green background)**: A black crane hook with a snapped cable above a red corrugated cargo container cracked down the center by a yellow lightning/fracture bolt.
  2. **Standard Vessel Bow View (Bright blue background)**: Front bow view of an orange-and-white ship upright on the water.
