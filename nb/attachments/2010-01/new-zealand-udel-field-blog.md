# University of Delaware Field Party in New Zealand (`new-zealand-udel-field-blog.jpg`)

## Overview
A group photograph of University of Delaware geology students and leaders (from Art Trembanis's Winter 2010 NZ GeolMast field course) posing in yellow hard hats and gas respirators in front of a steaming volcanic fumarole (White Island / Whakaari, New Zealand).

## Scene Description
- **People (Field Party)**:
  - Approximately 24 students and instructors are gathered in two rows across the center-bottom of the frame, all wearing bright yellow hard hats and dark half-face gas respirators over their noses and mouths.
  - Most participants are dressed for warm hiking weather in t-shirts, tank tops, shorts, or light jackets, with a few kneeling or crouching in the front center while the rest stand shoulder-to-shoulder behind them.
- **Volcanic Environment**:
  - Directly behind the group, a massive, dense plume of white volcanic steam and gas billows up from a vent on the right and drifts across the center of the image.
  - To the left and above the steam cloud rises a steep, barren, rocky crater wall streaked with pale gray, tan, and yellow sulfur-stained volcanic rock under a clear, deep blue sky in the upper left corner.
  - The ground in the foreground is a crusty, light-gray volcanic surface with bright yellow and ochre sulfur deposits covering the right side.
