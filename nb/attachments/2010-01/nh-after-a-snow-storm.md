# Snow-Covered Trees in New Hampshire (`nh-after-a-snow-storm.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime winter photograph taken with an iPhone looking out Kurt Schwehr's office window in Durham, New Hampshire, on January 20, 2010, after back-to-back snowstorms coated the surrounding forest in fresh white snow.

## Scene Description
- **Trees & Snow**:
  - Tall, bare deciduous trees stand in the left and right foreground, their dark trunks and intricate web of arching branches lined along their upper surfaces with fresh white snow.
  - In the lower center and background, dense evergreen conifers (pines/hemlocks) are heavily laden with thick mounds of white snow weighing down their boughs.
- **Sky & Lighting**:
  - Bright morning sunlight illuminates the snow-covered branches from the left, contrasting against a vivid blue winter sky dappled with soft, wispy white clouds in the upper left.
