# Spatial Data Entry Using a Sympodium Interactive Pen Display (`ocean-use-data-entry-sympodium.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph showing a participant using a tethered digital stylus on a tilted SMART Sympodium interactive pen display tablet to sketch and enter ocean-use spatial polygons directly into ArcGIS (via ArcSketch), while the same GIS map is projected on a large screen in the background.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (User & Sympodium Tablet)**:
  - On the right side of the frame, an older man with glasses, a gray beard, and a dark plaid collared shirt leans forward while holding a tethered silver stylus pen in his right hand.
  - He is drawing or selecting features on a black-framed SMART Sympodium interactive LCD tablet propped at a steep angle on a desk, with several black power and video cables coiled behind its stand.
  - The tablet screen shows an ArcGIS window with a layer list on the left and a coastal map on the right.
- **Background (Projected GIS Map)**:
  - Behind the tablet on the wall, a bright projector screen displays the same coastal GIS map in blue and white, showing a complex shoreline, islands, and offshore bathymetric/use zones, with a circular receiver puck (from an eBeam whiteboard system) mounted near the upper right corner of the board.
  - To the left of the whiteboard hangs a large sheet of flip-chart paper on the dark wall, and a small round table covered with a white tablecloth is partially visible at the far left edge.
