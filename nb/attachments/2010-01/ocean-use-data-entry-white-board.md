# Spatial Data Entry on a Whiteboard Using eBeam and ArcSketch (`ocean-use-data-entry-white-board.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken in a darkened meeting/command room showing a presenter drawing spatial ocean-use polygons directly onto a projected ArcGIS map on a whiteboard using an eBeam interactive stylus system, while two synchronized computer displays sit on the table in the foreground.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Monitors & Desk)**:
  - On the left side of the desk stands an angled black SMART Sympodium interactive pen display monitor (with its stylus resting flat on the desk in front of it). Its screen shows the full ArcGIS / ArcSketch interface, including a Table of Contents sidebar on the left with colored rectangular legend swatches (yellow, pink, light green, orange-red) and a coastal map view on the right containing several pink-shaded circular/oval polygons inside a rectangular study boundary.
  - In the center sits an open black laptop displaying the exact same ArcGIS map window, with a white computer mouse to its right and a mobile phone and adapters resting on the desk in front of it.
- **Background (Presenter & Whiteboard)**:
  - A bearded man in a light-colored long-sleeve shirt stands in front of a large whiteboard illuminated by a projector, casting his shadow onto the left side of the screen.
  - He reaches up with his right hand holding an eBeam electronic pen to trace polygons on the projected coastal map, which shows brown/tan land on the left, blue ocean water on the right, a white rectangular offshore bounding box, text labels, and light-colored circular/oval ocean-use zones.
  - The circular eBeam sensor receiver is mounted on the upper right corner of the whiteboard, and a flip-chart sheet hangs on the wall to the left.
