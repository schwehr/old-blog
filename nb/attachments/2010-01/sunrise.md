# Winter Sunrise in the New Hampshire Seacoast Area (`sunrise.jpg`)

## Overview
A dawn landscape photograph taken in the New Hampshire Seacoast area on January 16, 2010, capturing vivid pink, orange, and deep blue bands of color in the sky behind silhouetted bare winter trees.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Silhouettes)**:
  - A dense row of bare deciduous trees rises across the lower half of the frame, with taller tree crowns on the right and a dark pitched roof corner of a building visible at the lower left edge.
- **Sky & Clouds**:
  - Along the horizon behind the tree branches, the sky glows bright coral pink, salmon, and warm golden-orange where the sun is about to rise.
  - Above the pink horizon glow, a thick, diagonal band of dark indigo and violet-blue cloud stretches from the middle left up toward the upper right.
  - Across the top left and upper edge of the frame, the sky transitions into a bright cyan and turquoise blue with darker blue cloud wisps.
