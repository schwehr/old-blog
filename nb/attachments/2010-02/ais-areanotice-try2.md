# AIS Area Notice KML Visualization with Merged FreeText (`ais-areanotice-try2.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot showing a test visualization of the NAV55 proposed AIS Binary Message for Area Notices generated by the `ais-areanotice-py` 0.2 Python package. The view looks northeastward over coastal New England (Rhode Island and Massachusetts, including Cape Cod, Boston, and Stellwagen Bank) and displays several geometric test sub-areas and yellow pushpin markers offshore, with an open information balloon demonstrating concatenated free-text sub-areas.

## Map & Geometric Overlays
- **Geographic Context**:
  - Landmasses on the left and top show **Rhode Island** (with a red dot for **Providence**) and **Massachusetts** (with a red dot for **Boston** and the hook of Cape Cod extending into the lower left).
  - Offshore water features are labeled in blue text: **The Breakwater** and **Stellwagen Bank**.
  - Several small blue-and-white square icons and a New York Times (`T`) icon are scattered across the land and coast.
- **AIS Area Notice Test Shapes & Pushpins**:
  - **`point-1`**: Marked with a yellow pushpin near the bottom center-left next to a circular tan area outlined in red and an adjacent solid red rectangle.
  - Small polygon/sector shapes in pale green, pink, and white trail northeastward toward the next marker.
  - **`text-1`**: Marked with a yellow pushpin in the middle of the chain.
  - **`one-of-each`**: Marked with a yellow pushpin on a green circle with a purple sector, next to a larger multi-bladed fan/sector pattern in salmon-pink and purple.
  - **`full-text`**: Marked with a yellow pushpin to the northeast of the `one-of-each` fan shape, with its popup balloon open.
  - Additional rectangular and polygonal shapes outlined in purple and filled with green appear in the lower right corner.
