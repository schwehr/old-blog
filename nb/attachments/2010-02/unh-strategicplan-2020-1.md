# UNH in 2020 Strategic Plan Video: Pointing at GeoZui4D Sonar Visualization (`unh-strategicplan-2020-1.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from the "UNH in 2020" strategic plan video produced by the University of New Hampshire (UNH Video on Vimeo), featuring visualizations from the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM). A male student stands in a blue virtual studio environment pointing at floating 3D panels displaying GeoZui4D sonar and hydrographic data.

- **Top Header Bar (White Background)**:
  - On the left is a dark blue architectural icon of Thompson Hall (UNH's signature building tower).
  - Bold dark gray title text reads **"UNH in 2020"**.
  - Subtitle text reads **"by UNH Video"** in blue with a light-blue **"PLUS"** badge next to it, followed by **"22 hours ago"** in olive green below.
- **Main Video Frame (Blue Virtual Environment)**:
  - A young man with short dark hair wearing a brown button-down long-sleeve shirt, dark jeans, and brown shoes stands near the center of a bright blue chroma-keyed virtual space with a subtle grid floor.
