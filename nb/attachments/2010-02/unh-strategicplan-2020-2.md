# UNH in 2020 Strategic Plan Video: Interacting with Floating CCOM Visualizations (`unh-strategicplan-2020-2.png`)

## Overview
A second screenshot from the "UNH in 2020" strategic plan video by the University of New Hampshire, showing a student in a blue virtual environment with both arms raised, interacting with five floating panels of scientific visualizations from the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM), including a ship grounding risk analysis for Portsmouth Harbor by Brian Calder.

## Scene Description
- **Central Figure & Gesture**:
  - The young man in the brown button-down shirt and dark jeans stands facing forward in the bright blue virtual room with his arms spread wide and hands raised, gesturing toward the floating panels on either side of him (with slight motion blur on his hands and a faint translucent overlay across his torso).
- **Floating Visualization Panels**:
  - **Upper Left Panel**: A large white chart containing two stacked horizontal line/timeseries plots with text labels and data curves.
  - **Lower Left Panel**: A smaller dark GeoZui visualization showing a white seafloor/surface feature against a black background.
  - **Center Background Panel**: A square panel directly behind the student's head and torso showing a vertical reddish/gray column graphic on a light blue-gray background.
  - **Upper Right Panel (Touched by Student's Left Hand)**: A vertical map plot created by Brian Calder showing a grounding risk analysis for a vessel entering Portsmouth, NH harbor, with yellow, orange, and blue-gray color-coded risk zones along the channel.
  - **Lower Right Panel**: A tilted coastal bathymetry/chart visualization with green, yellow, and red depth contours.
  - **Far Right Panel**: The angled GeoZui4D mid-water sonar curtain visualization (EK60 and DeltaT multibeam data).
- **Sign Language Interpreter Inset (Bottom Right)**:
  - A picture-in-picture box at the bottom right shows the woman with glasses and dark hair continuing to interpret the presentation in American Sign Language against a dark background.
