# High-Bay Test Setup for Debugging AIS Aids to Navigation (`aton-debugging.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a portable hardware and laptop test bench set up on a white folding table in the high-bay area at the UNH Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) to debug L3 and Ohmex TideMet AIS Aids to Navigation (AtoN) transmitters and receivers.

## Scene & Equipment Description
- **Laptops (Left Side)**:
  - **Foreground Left**: A black Windows laptop (used by Ted Reed of Ohmex) angled toward the viewer, running Ohmex monitoring and display software on its screen.
  - **Background Left**: A silver Apple MacBook Pro (Kurt Schwehr's laptop) with a glowing white Apple logo on the lid, connected to serial/USB adapters for logging and decoding AIS messages.
  - Between and in front of the laptops are a black laptop sleeve/bag with a bright yellow rectangular label/screen and a small black box (such as an SR162g AIS receiver or USB hub).
- **Batteries, Wiring & Receivers (Center)**:
  - On the white table surface in the center sits a black rectangular lead-acid battery pack wrapped with yellow tape, connected via red and black alligator-clip test leads and wiring harnesses to the AIS units.
  - Behind the wiring sits a small black box with a vertical rubber-duck antenna mounted on top (the USB-powered Ohmex AIS receiver / antenna setup), resting in front of a beige Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) / power supply unit and a pink cloth or bag.
- **AIS AtoN Hardware (Right Side)**:
  - **Back Right**: A gray cube-shaped enclosure with two black circular front connectors/ports (L3 stock AIS AtoN / power box).
  - **Front Right**: A light-gray rectangular **Ohmex TideMet** AIS AtoN station enclosure with "AIS" printed in blue on its top label and two coaxial/serial cables attached to connectors on its front face.
- **Background**:
  - A painted cinder-block wall on the left with a wall-mounted air pressure regulator/filter and a dark blue ribbed roll-up high-bay garage door on the right.
