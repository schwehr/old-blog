# SIMILE Timeplot of CCOM Weather Data (`ccom-wx-timeline.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of an interactive web-based time-series chart generated using the MIT SIMILE Timeplot widget, displaying meteorological sensor measurements (such as temperature and wind speed) recorded at the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) over an afternoon period from before 14:00 to after 18:00.

## Plot Details & Visual Elements
- **Axes & Grid**:
  - **Horizontal Axis (Time)**: Marked with vertical gray grid lines labeled at hourly intervals near the top of the plot: **`14:00`**, **`15:00`**, **`16:00`**, **`17:00`** (partially covered by a callout box), and **`18:00`**.
  - **Vertical Axis (Right Side)**: Numerical scale ticks along the right edge show **`8`**, **`12`**, **`2`**, and **`16`**.
  - **Branding**: Vertical blue text along the far left margin reads **`Timeplot © SIMILE`**.
- **Data Series**:
  - **Red Shaded Area Curve**: A smooth, steadily rising red line with a vertical pink-to-white gradient fill beneath it, climbing from the lower left (around 8 on the right-hand scale before 14:00) up to a plateau near the top right (around 15–16 between 17:30 and 18:45).
  - **Yellow/Gold Jagged Curve**: A highly variable, spiky yellow-gold line plotted over the red curve, fluctuating rapidly across the vertical span of the chart.
- **Interactive Hover Callout**:
  - A vertical orange cursor line is positioned between 16:00 and 17:00, displaying three yellow-bordered callout boxes with bold black numbers:
    - **`16:45:30`** at the bottom on the time axis.
    - **`14.51`** at the top pointing to the red curve.
    - **`1.24`** in the middle pointing to a local trough in the yellow/gold curve.
