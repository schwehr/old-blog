# Seismogram and Earthquake Info for the March 11, 2010 M7.2 Chile Earthquake (`chile-7.2-quake-20100311.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from the IRIS Wilber / seismic data portal showing event metadata and a three-component seismogram recorded at station PLCA (Paso Flores, Argentina) for the magnitude 7.2 earthquake that struck near the coast of central Chile on March 11, 2010.

## Left Panel: Earthquake & Station Info
- **`Earthquake Info` Section**:
  - **World Map**: An oval Robinson-projection physical world map centered on the Pacific Ocean. A red circle and a blue triangle icon mark the earthquake epicenter and seismic station near the central coast of South America (Chile/Argentina).
  - **Event Metadata**:
    - **Region:** `Near Coast Of Central Chile`
    - **Time (GMT):** `2010-03-11 14:39:48`
    - **Magnitude:** `7.2`
    - **Location:** `34.3 S, 71.9 W`
    - **Depth:** `35 km`
    - **Distance:** `6.5 deg/726 km`
  - **Link**: Underlined teal link reading `More about this earthquake`.
- **`Station Info` Section**:
  - **Code:** `GT.PLCA`
  - **Name:** `Paso Flores, Argentina`

## Right Panel: Three-Component Seismogram
- **Header**: **`Three component seismogram for PLCA`**
- **Time Axes (Top and Bottom)**:
  - Title: **`Time since earthquake (h:min:s)`**
  - Tick marks every minute with major labels at **`0:00`**, **`00:20:00`**, **`00:40:00`**, **`01:00:00`**, and **`01:20:00`**.
- **Waveform Traces (Green)**:
  - Three stacked horizontal panels show green seismic waveforms running from `0:00` to approximately `00:48:00`, each featuring two distinct high-amplitude wave arrival packets (one starting shortly after `0:00` and a second arrival around `00:18:00`–`00:25:00`, indicating a second large aftershock within 20 minutes):
    1. **Top Trace (`Vertical`)**: Vertical axis labeled `Amplitude microns/sec` from `-900` to `900` (with ticks at `-900, -600, -300, 0, 300, 600, 900`).
    2. **Middle Trace (`North-South`)**: Vertical axis labeled `Amplitude microns/sec` with visible labels from `-600` to `600`.
    3. **Bottom Trace (`East-West`)**: Vertical axis labeled `Amplitude microns/sec` from `-900` to `600+`.
