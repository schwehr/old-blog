# Roland Arsenault Demonstrating the Four-Projector Panoram Display (`panoram-roland.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of Roland Arsenault standing beside the curved, multi-projector "Panoram on the cheap" display in the visualization lab at the UNH Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM), where four projectors on a table project a wide mountain landscape panorama across a hanging white fabric screen.

## Scene & Display Details
- **Person (Left)**:
  - **Roland Arsenault** stands at the left edge of the curved screen wearing a blue and dark-gray short-sleeve shirt and dark pants, with his left arm folded across his waist and his right hand raised thoughtfully to his chin. Part of the left projector's beam illuminates his forearms.
- **Curved Panoramic Screen (Center & Right)**:
  - A wide, curved white fabric curtain hangs from a ceiling track, divided into four seamlessly adjacent projected segments showing a wide-angle panoramic photograph of rugged, snow-dusted mountain peaks under a blue sky with white clouds (displayed via GigaPan / panoramic viewer software).
- **Projector Table (Foreground)**:
  - In the lower foreground sits the rectangular gray table holding the four black projectors arranged in an arc facing the screen, along with power/video cables, a sheet of white paper, and a silver beverage can near the left projectors.
