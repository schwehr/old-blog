# ERMA New England Display During the SONS 2010 Oil Spill Exercise (`sons-2010-erma-display.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **ERMA (Environmental Response Management Application) New England** web interface running in Mac OS X Safari (`https://newengland.erma.unh.edu/erma.html#x=-70.31250&y=43.14509&z=9&layers=6...`) during the March 2010 Spill of National Significance (SONS 2010) exercise. The map displays the coastline from Casco Bay, Maine, south through New Hampshire to Cape Ann/Gloucester, Massachusetts, overlaid with simulated oil spill trajectories, floating oil concentrations, geographic response strategies (GRS), and vessel closure/exclusion zones.

## Interface & Header Elements
- **Browser Bar**: Mac OS X Safari window titled **`ERMA`** with URL `https://newengland.erma.unh.edu/erma.html#x=-70.31250&y=43.14509&z=9&layers=6` and a Google search box.
- **Application Banner**: Reads **`ERMA | Environmental Response Management Application New England`** over a coastal lighthouse banner image, with navigation buttons **`Information`**, **`Help`**, **`Admin`**, **`Upload`** on the left and **`Logout`** on the right.
- **Bottom Status Bar**:
  - Map status bar: **`Scale: 1: 867K`**, **`Zoom Level: 9`**, **`Location: 43.28120°, -69.48303°`**.
  - Footer links: NOAA logo, `US DOC | NOAA | NOS | NOAA Office of Response & Restoration`, `Privacy policy | Email comments` on the left; `Coastal Response Research Center`, `©2007 - 2010 University of New Hampshire` on the right.

## Map View (Left Panel)
- **Base Map**: Google Maps road/terrain map showing coastal towns and highways from **Falmouth**, **Portland**, **Scarborough**, **Old Orchard Beach**, **Biddeford**, **Kennebunk**, and **Wells** (Maine), past **Portsmouth** and **Hampton** (New Hampshire), down to **Newburyport**, **Rockport**, and **Gloucester** (Massachusetts).
- **Simulated Spill & Response Overlays**:
  - **Vessel Closure Area**: A large polygon filled with yellow horizontal hatching and outlined in red extending from Portland/Casco Bay down past Portsmouth and the Isles of Shoals, with circular exclusion zones around Portsmouth/Seabrook.
  - **Oil Trajectory & Uncertainty**: Large dark blue (Heavy), medium blue (Medium), and cyan (Light) plumes extending southward along the coast, surrounded by a wider light-gray **Uncertainty** envelope offshore and red **`x`** symbols marking **Beached Oil** along the shoreline.
  - **Floating Oil Concentration**: Dark gray, medium gray, and light gray patches offshore southeast of Portland/Biddeford.
  - **Ship Icons**: Black vessel silhouettes marking the simulated casualty vessels (`M/V Tetra` and `M/V Axis Moonlight`) near Portland/Casco Bay.
