# SONS 2010 Spill of National Significance Exercise Web Banner (`sons-drill.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the header and logo graphic from the official website (`www.sons2010.com`) for the **SONS 2010 (Spill of National Significance)** national oil spill response exercise held in Portland, Maine, in March 2010.

## Graphic & Text Elements
- **Top Agency Banner**:
  - Left: The angled red, white, and blue racing stripe and crest emblem of the **United States Coast Guard**.
  - Center-Left Text:
    - Bold black heading: **`United States Coast Guard`**
    - Subtitle: **`U.S. Department of Homeland Security`**
  - Right: A blue-tinted graphic of the stars and stripes of the U.S. flag fading into white toward the center.
- **Navigation Bar**:
  - Horizontal slate-blue menu bar with white text links separated by vertical dividers:
    - **`Home`** | **`Links`** | **`Questions/Comments`** | **`What Is SONS`** | **`Reference Docs`** | **`Participating Agenc...`**
- **Main `SONS 2010` Logo Graphic**:
  - Large stylized typography reading **`SONS2010`** over stylized teal and aqua ocean waves along the bottom:
    - **`SONS`** is rendered in bold black capital letters; inside the circular **`O`** is a black oil droplet suspended above a turquoise wave.
    - **`2010`** is rendered in dark blue numerals:
      - The first **`0`** is a dark blue globe showing a white map of North and Central America, surrounded by concentric gray and teal circular rings.
      - The second **`0`** contains a turquoise water droplet enclosed within crescent-shaped teal wave arcs.
