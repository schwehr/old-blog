# "THIS IS AN EXERCISE" Projection Screen at SONS 2010 (`sons-this-is-an-exercise.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a large projection screen in the command room at the SONS 2010 (Spill of National Significance) exercise in Portland, Maine, displaying SMPTE-style color bars overlaid with the words **"THIS IS AN EXERCISE"** while two U.S. Coast Guard personnel stand in the right foreground.

  - Across the middle of the color bars in large white sans-serif capital letters is the text: **`THIS IS AN EXERCISE`**.
  - Near the bottom right of the projected image is white text reading **`USCG - 3/2...`** (partially obscured by a person's head and a printer in the foreground).
