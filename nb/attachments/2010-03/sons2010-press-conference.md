# SONS 2010 Simulated Press Conference and ERMA Laptop (`sons2010-press-conference.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on March 24, 2010, during the Spill of National Significance (SONS) 2010 exercise in Portland, Maine. It captures an Apple MacBook Pro laptop running the Environmental Response Management Application (ERMA) in the foreground while a simulated television broadcast / press conference plays on a large projection screen in the background.

## Foreground & Workspace Details
- **Laptop Display**: An aluminum Apple MacBook Pro sits angled on a worktable in the lower center-right. Its screen displays the **`ERMA`** (*Environmental Response Management Application — New England*) web interface in Firefox:
  - **Map Pane**: Shows the coastline of Maine and New Hampshire with red shoreline impact overlays and a prominent blue-shaded simulated oil spill trajectory plume extending offshore and into the coast.
  - **Right Panel**: Shows the `Legend` tab for `SONS M/V Tula`, command post icons, and a floating legend window showing color ramps for oil thickness/concentration.
- **Tabletop Items**:
  - To the left of the laptop is a white paper table sign held in a metal clip stand reading **`SITUATION STATUS`** in bold black capital letters.
  - Two beige ceramic coffee mugs, a bottle of dark red Vitaminwater, a spiral notebook in the lower left corner, and a dense tangle of yellow, blue, and black Ethernet and power cables along with Apple MagSafe power bricks across the table.

## Background Projection Screen
- **Projected Video**: A large projection screen on the back wall displays a live or recorded video feed of a man in a light-colored checkered dress shirt and tie speaking directly to the camera in front of a television broadcast control room backdrop (with multiple video monitors, color bars, and digital clocks visible behind him).
- **Exercise Watermark**: Across the top of the projected video feed, the white uppercase text **`FOR EXERCISE ONLY • FOR EXERCISE ONLY • FOR EXERCISE ONLY`** is superimposed to clearly mark the broadcast as part of the drill.
- **Room Environment**: To the left of the screen are wood-paneled double doors beneath a glowing red `EXIT` sign and a black PA speaker mounted on a tripod stand. A participant sits in front of the projection screen, and another laptop and black desktop printer sit on adjacent tables.
