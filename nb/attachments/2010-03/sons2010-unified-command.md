# SONS 2010 Unified Command Table (`sons2010-unified-command.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on the morning of March 24, 2010 (Day 1 of the Spill of National Significance 2010 exercise in Portland, Maine), showing members of the Unified Command and Operations teams gathered around a round table in a large hotel ballroom serving as the incident command center.

## Foreground: Unified Command Table
Six men are seated around a circular table covered with a white tablecloth, all wearing bright orange incident response vests over their uniforms or shirts:
- **Table Sign**: In the center of the table stands a white sign in a metal holder reading **`Unified Command`** in bold black letters, next to a beige multi-line office telephone, a black marker, a water bottle, and yellow/green file folders.
- **Participants (clockwise from lower left)**:
  1. A responder in a dark blue shirt and orange vest with a white **`OPS`** patch on his back and a silver reflective stripe along the waist.
  2. An older man with gray hair and a beard wearing a gray shirt and an orange vest with yellow reflective stripes.
  3. A man with short brown hair and a mustache wearing a dark blue shirt and orange vest, writing notes on a pad of paper.
  4. A man in a dark blue shirt and orange vest resting his forehead against his left hand while looking down at papers on the table.
  5. A man on the far right in an olive-green collared shirt, orange vest with yellow reflective trim, and a white name badge on a lanyard.
  6. A man in the lower right (seen from behind) wearing a dark blue shirt and an orange vest with a clear plastic pocket on the back.
  7. In the lower left foreground (between the first and sixth men), another responder with short hair sits facing the table wearing an orange vest labeled **`OPS`** across the back.

## Background: Command Center Ballroom
- Beyond the foreground table, numerous long rectangular tables covered in white cloths are arranged across a patterned carpet in a large ballroom lit by warm wall sconces.
- Dozens of responders in blue, orange, and white uniforms/vests are working at laptops, talking on phones, or standing in small groups.
- In the left background, a large-format plotter/printer and a whiteboard easel are visible near the wall.
