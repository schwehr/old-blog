# Unfiltered AIS Track Lines with Bad GPS Positions in Boston (`ais-bad-gps.png`)

## Overview
A composite satellite map visualization from April 2010 showing unfiltered Class A Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel track lines for MMSI `366998520` in and around Boston, Massachusetts. The image illustrates severe GPS position errors ("spiderweb" spikes radiating across New England) that occurred while the vessel was docked underneath the Tobin Bridge, contrasted with normal track behavior when underway.

## Main Map View
- **Geographic Extent**: A satellite imagery basemap covering eastern Massachusetts, southern New Hampshire, Rhode Island, eastern Connecticut, Cape Cod, and the offshore islands.
- **Place Labels**:
  - States: **`New Hampshire`**, **`Massachusetts`**, **`Rhode Island`**, and **`ticut`** (Connecticut, partially clipped on the left).
  - Cities & Features: **`Boston`** (marked with a dark red/brown circular dot), **`The Breakwater`** (with a yellow polygon outline in Massachusetts Bay / Stellwagen Bank area), **`Fishers Island`**, **`Gardiners Island`**, **`Block Island`**, **`Martha's Vineyard`**, and **`Nantucket Island`**.
- **AIS Track Spikes**: Radiating outward from the vessel's dock location in Boston are numerous long, straight white lines shooting across inland Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Connecticut, Rhode Island, and out into the Atlantic Ocean south of Martha's Vineyard, caused by erroneous GPS fixes broadcast when the vessel lacked a clean satellite lock under the bridge structure.

## Inset Zoom View (Upper Right)
- Framed in gray with two purple callout lines connecting it to the Boston inner harbor area, the square inset shows a high-resolution aerial view of the Mystic River and the **Tobin Bridge**.
- **Track Detail**: A dense bundle of white vessel track lines follows the main river channel smoothly around the bend under the bridge, while a bright white star-burst cluster of erratic position jumps radiates in all directions from a single berth alongside a large rectangular pier building immediately adjacent to the bridge.
