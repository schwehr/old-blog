# USCGC Healy in the Google Earth Ocean Expeditions Layer (`healy-googleearth-oceans-layer.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth taken on April 13, 2010, showing the USCGC *Healy* (`WAGB-20`) Aloftcon feed integrated into Google Earth's built-in **Ocean -> Ocean Expeditions** layer off the Pacific Northwest coast.

## Left Sidebar Interface
- **Search Panel (Top Left)**:
  - `Fly to e.g., San Francisco` input box containing coordinates **`48.450596616 -126.644314867`** with a checked search result item below it.
- **Places Panel (Middle Left)**:
  - `My Places` (unchecked)
  - `Temporary Places` (checked), containing the checked KML/KMZ item **`healy_aloftcon_2010.kmz`**.
- **Layers Panel (Bottom Left)**:
  - `Primary Database`: `Borders and Labels` (checked), `Places of Interest` (checked), `Panoramio` (unchecked), `Roads` (unchecked), `3D Buildings` (unchecked).
  - **`Ocean`** (expanded):
    - `Explore the Ocean` (unchecked)
    - `National Geographic` (unchecked)
    - `Cousteau Ocean World` (unchecked)
    - `Ocean Sports` (unchecked)
    - `Shipwrecks` (unchecked)
    - **`Ocean Expeditions`** (checked, with a blue ship-in-square icon).
