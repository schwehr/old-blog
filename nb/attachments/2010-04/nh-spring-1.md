# Purple Crocus Buds in Early Morning Light (`nh-spring-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up nature photograph taken in New Hampshire on the morning of April 5, 2010, showing several vibrant purple crocus flower buds emerging among slender green blades of grass and foliage.

## Scene Description
- **Flowers**:
  - Five closed or partially unfurling rich violet-purple crocus buds dominate the frame: two in the lower-left foreground tilting left, two stacked vertically near the upper-center/center, and a cluster on the right side.
  - The petals display delicate, slightly crinkled textures and glossy highlights where early morning sunlight catches their surfaces.
- **Foliage & Background**:
  - Surrounding and crisscrossing in front of the purple buds are narrow, upright dark-green crocus leaves with characteristic central white/silver longitudinal stripes, along with broader blades of green grass in the soft-focus background.
  - Warm, directional morning sunlight illuminates the scene from the left, creating crisp contrast between the bright purple petals and the lush green blades.
