# Side-Profile Close-Up of a White and Yellow Daffodil (`nh-spring-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up morning photograph of a single blooming white-and-yellow daffodil (*Narcissus*) in New Hampshire on April 5, 2010, viewed in three-quarter side profile against dark green stems and leaves.

## Scene Description
- **Flower**:
  - The daffodil is oriented horizontally, facing toward the right-center of the frame.
  - Six delicate, creamy-white outer tepals (petals) surround a bright golden-yellow, ruffled trumpet-shaped corona (cup).
  - One long, pointed white tepal extends prominently to the left, showing fine parallel venation across its surface, while the right-side tepals curve forward in soft shadow.
  - Inside the ruffled yellow cup, the clustered yellow stamens/anthers are visible near the right edge of the opening.
- **Background & Lighting**:
  - A dark green leaf tip presses up against the lower-left side of the flower's white tepals.
  - The background consists of out-of-focus dark green vertical foliage and deep shadow, making the sunlit white petals and vivid yellow cup stand out sharply.
