# Macro Close-Up of a Daffodil Trumpet (`nh-spring-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A tight macro photograph looking directly into the bright yellow ruffled corona (cup) and surrounding white tepals of a blooming daffodil (*Narcissus*) in New Hampshire on the morning of April 5, 2010.

## Scene Description
- **Corona & Reproductive Structures**:
  - The large, flared, trumpet-like corona fills the center and right side of the frame with a vivid golden-yellow hue and intricately frilled, wavy edges.
  - Deep inside the shadowed throat of the cup, the pale-yellow style/stigma and surrounding orange-gold pollen-bearing anthers (stamens) protrude from the dark green base.
- **Outer Tepals**:
  - Surrounding the yellow cup are broad, papery-white tepals extending toward the top, left, bottom, and right edges of the image, exhibiting fine longitudinal ribs and subtle creamy-yellow shading near the base of the corona.
- **Background**:
  - Set against a stark black background with a soft-focus green leaf edge at the lower left and a blurred white-and-yellow flower in the lower-right distance.
