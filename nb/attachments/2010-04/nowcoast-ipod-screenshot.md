# NOAA nowCOAST FlowVis WMS Feed on an iPod Touch (`nowcoast-ipod-screenshot.png`)

## Overview
A composite device mockup and screenshot showing NOAA's `nowCOAST` Web Map Service (WMS) oceanographic forecast guidance layer—specifically surface current flow vectors rendered using Colin Ware's FlowVis visualization technique—displayed inside the WhateverMap application on an Apple iPod Touch (or iPhone) over New York Harbor and the Hudson/East Rivers.

## Device & Interface Controls
- **Device Frame**: A front-facing black-and-silver Apple iPod Touch hardware bezel with the circular Home button centered at the bottom.
- **iOS Status Bar (Top)**: Silver bar reading `iPod` with a blue Wi-Fi signal icon on the left, `9:42 AM` centered in bold black text, and a green battery indicator on the right.
- **Top Application Toolbar**: Translucent dark-gray bar containing:
  - A `Sources` button on the left.
  - The active layer title `nowCOAST - guidance` centered in white text.
  - Up (`▲`) and down (`▼`) layer navigation arrow buttons on the right.
- **Bottom Application Toolbar**: Translucent dark-gray bar with four icons from left to right:
  - A red circular target/crosshair location button.
  - A rectangular map-bounds/zoom-to-fit icon.
  - A green curved left-pointing arrow (previous view/step).
  - A gray/white curved right-pointing arrow (next view/step).

## Map & FlowVis Visualization Details
- **Geographic Coverage**: Covers the New York / New Jersey Harbor Estuary, including:
  - **Waterbodies**: The Hudson River descending from the top center, the East River branching to the upper right, Newark Bay and Kill Van Kull on the left, Upper New York Bay in the center, the Narrows, and Lower New York Bay opening toward the Atlantic Ocean and Rockaway Inlet at the bottom.
  - **Land & Boundaries**: Tan/beige landmasses with orange-red county boundaries and highway lines, labeled `Hudson County`, `NJ`, `Jersey City`, `Hoboken`, `New York County`, `New York`, `Queens County`, `NY`, `Kings County` (Brooklyn), `Richmond County` (Staten Island), `Bayonne`, `Newark Bay`, `Upper Bay`, `Lower Bay`, and `Rockaway Inlet`.
- **Current Flow Visualization**:
  - The waterways are filled with dense, curved white streamlines with small directional arrowheads indicating ebb/flood tidal current flow moving southward down the Hudson and East Rivers, converging through the Narrows, and fanning out southeastward across Lower New York Bay.
  - Background color-shading beneath the streamlines indicates current speed: dark gray/black for slack or very slow water along the western shore of Lower Bay, dark blue for moderate speeds, vibrant purple across the main channel of Upper and Lower Bay, and bright magenta/red in high-velocity constrictions such as the Narrows, the East River (Hell Gate/Brooklyn Bridge area), and off the tip of Sandy Hook/Coney Island.
