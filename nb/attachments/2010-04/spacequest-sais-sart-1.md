# SpaceQuest Satellite AIS and USCG NAIS Reception of Honolulu SART Test (`spacequest-sais-sart-1.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth desktop application screenshot showing an oblique 3D perspective view looking northeast toward the Leeward coast of Oahu, Hawaii (near Honolulu / Kalaeloa / Barber's Point), comparing terrestrial USCG N-AIS station reception (white lines) with orbital SpaceQuest satellite AIS reception (red markers inside a yellow highlight oval) during a January 22, 2010 USCG AIS Search and Rescue Transmitter (AIS-SART) test.

- **Terrain & Coastline**:
  - Oblique 3D satellite/terrain rendering of southwestern Oahu (Waianae Range mountains and coastal plain) with deep blue Pacific Ocean bathymetry in the foreground.
  - Labeled red-dot town placemarks along the coast from left (northwest) to right (southeast): `Maili`, `Nanakuli`, and `Makakilo City`.
