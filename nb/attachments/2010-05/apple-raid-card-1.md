# Apple RAID Card Battery Conditioning Alert Dialog (`apple-raid-card-1.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X system alert dialog notifying the user that the Apple RAID Card battery is undergoing a conditioning cycle.

## Dialog Layout & Visible Text
- **Icon**: On the left is the RAID Utility icon featuring a crossed flathead screwdriver (with a black handle) and a silver open-end wrench behind a green rectangular badge labeled `RAID` in white capital letters.
- **Heading**:
  - **Conditioning the Apple RAID Card Battery** (in bold black sans-serif text)
- **Body Text**:
  - `This process may take up to 12 hours to complete and should not be interrupted.`
  - `Don't restart or shutdown the system until conditioning is finished. Doing so interrupts the process and can cause unintended battery operation. To check battery status, open RAID Utility.`
- **Buttons**:
  - Two Aqua-style rounded push buttons at the bottom right:
    - `Ignore` (white/silver button on the left)
    - `Open RAID Utility...` (pulsing blue default button on the right)
