# Deepwater Horizon Live Underwater Spillcam Video Frame (`bp-oil-spill-live-video.png`)

## Overview
A still frame captured from the live underwater ROV video feed ("Spillcam") released during the 2010 Deepwater Horizon oil spill in the Gulf of Mexico, showing crude oil and gas billowing vigorously from the damaged pipe on the seafloor.

## Scene Description
- **Plume**: A dense, turbulent cloud of dark black/brown crude oil and lighter yellowish-white gas/fluid jets upward from the bottom center, filling the left and central portions of the frame. The right edge of the plume is brightly lit by underwater ROV floodlights, highlighting the churning, billowing texture of the escaping hydrocarbons.
- **Subsea Equipment & Seafloor**:
  - At the bottom center and right, yellow-painted subsea pipes or structural tubing rest along the gray sediment of the seafloor.
  - Behind and to the right of the billowing plume, a circular or loop-like cable/pipe structure and a diagonal dark cable/line with attached fittings are visible against the blue-lit deep-water background.
- **Lighting & Color Palette**: Dominated by deep blue underwater illumination on the left and upper right, contrasting sharply with the opaque black oil cloud, bright whitish-tan illuminated plume edge, and yellow subsea piping at the base.
