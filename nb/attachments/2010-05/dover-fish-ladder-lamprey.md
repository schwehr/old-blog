# Tour Host Holding a Sea Lamprey at the Dover Fish Ladder (`dover-fish-ladder-lamprey.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken during a public tour of the Cocheco River fish ladder in Dover, New Hampshire, showing a New Hampshire Fish and Game host holding up a live sea lamprey (*Petromyzon marinus*) from the holding tank for a group of onlookers.
