# Individual NOAA BAG Bounding Boxes and Icons in Google Earth (`ge-bag-bbox.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot showing a zoomed-in coastal view where a KML `Region` level-of-detail transition has switched from survey-level icons to displaying the individual Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) file bounding boxes and icons for several NOAA hydrographic surveys.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Geographic Context**: A coastal inlet and shoreline (visible across the bottom left and bottom center) opening into blue offshore waters, with a lighter tan/green dredged channel or shoal feature extending toward the top center-left.
- **BAG Bounding Boxes**: Numerous overlapping and tiled white rectangular outlines mark the UTM-derived geographic bounding boxes of individual BAG files within each hydrographic survey.
- **BAG Icons**: Each BAG tile is marked at its center by a square rainbow-colored gridded icon (red at the top-left corner transitioning diagonally through yellow and green to blue at the bottom-right corner, inverted relative to the survey-level icon).
