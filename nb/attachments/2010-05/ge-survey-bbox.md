# NOAA Hydrographic Survey Bounding Boxes in Google Earth (`ge-survey-bbox.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot showing the zoomed-out survey-level KML view of NOAA Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) hydrographic surveys over the same coastal inlet shown in `ge-bag-bbox.png`, where individual BAG tiles are hidden by KML `Region` rules in favor of single overall bounding boxes and icons per survey.

## Map & Overlay Details
- **Geographic Context**: Overhead satellite view of a coastal harbor entrance and shoreline along the bottom, with a ship channel extending northward toward the top left into open blue water.
- **Survey Bounding Boxes**: Large white rectangular outlines represent the aggregate bounding boxes of entire hydrographic surveys.
- **Survey vs. Individual BAG Icons**:
  - **Survey-Level Icons**: Five surveys are represented by square gridded icons with a cyan/blue top-left corner transitioning diagonally through purple/magenta to orange/tan at the bottom-right corner:
    - `H11205` (top center-left)
    - `H11323` (lower left, over the harbor entrance)
    - `H11603` (center)
    - `H11651` (center-right)
    - `H11402` (bottom right)
  - **Individual BAG Icon**: Near the top right, where a larger adjacent survey (`H11789`) has already crossed its zoom threshold, a red-to-blue gridded BAG icon is visible with overlapping labels `H11789_2m_2` and `H11789_2m_4`.
