# Google Crisis Response Gulf of Mexico Oil Spill Map (`google-crisis-response-oilspill.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the interactive map widget on **Google's Crisis Response** page for the 2010 Deepwater Horizon / Gulf of Mexico oil spill (`http://www.google.com/crisisresponse/oilspill/`), displaying the current spill extent polygon overlaid on an ENVISAT synthetic aperture radar (ASAR) satellite image off the coast of Louisiana.
