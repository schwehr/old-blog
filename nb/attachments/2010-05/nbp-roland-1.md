# Panoramic Mosaic from RVIB Nathaniel B. Palmer in Antarctica (`nbp-roland-1.png`)

## Overview
A 360-degree cylindrical panoramic mosaic captured by an automated SLR camera mounted on a pan-tilt unit ("The Droid", set up by Roland Arsenault) atop the mast/superstructure of the NSF icebreaker *RVIB Nathaniel B. Palmer* (NBP) in Antarctica, showing calm open water, scattered ice floes, snow-covered mountains, and a low sun on the horizon.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Ship Superstructure)**:
  - On the left and right edges (wrapping around the 360-degree view) are the ship's upper deck walkways painted dark green with patches of snow, surrounded by yellow-painted safety railings, masts, antennas, and a white spherical satellite communications radome on the right.
  - In the lower center, looking forward down from the mast, the red and white bow of the *Nathaniel B. Palmer* points directly ahead into the dark water, flanked by yellow catwalks and deck cranes.
- **Middle Ground (Ocean & Ice)**:
  - Dark, calm polar water reflects the bright sunlight on the left and scattered small white icebergs and brash ice on the right.
- **Background (Antarctic Coast & Sky)**:
  - Across the center and right horizon rises a dramatic range of snow- and glacier-covered Antarctic mountains and peaks illuminated by sunlight.
  - To the left of the central mountain peaks, a brilliant low sun shines in a clear blue sky with light clouds near the horizon, casting a bright vertical glint across the water.
