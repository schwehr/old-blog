# Panoramic Mosaic of Sea Ice from RVIB Nathaniel B. Palmer in Antarctica (`nbp-roland-2.png`)

## Overview
A 360-degree cylindrical panoramic mosaic captured by the automated pan-tilt SLR camera system ("The Droid") atop the NSF icebreaker *RVIB Nathaniel B. Palmer* (NBP) in Antarctica, showing the vessel navigating through dense pack ice and pancake/floe ice surrounded by snow-covered mountains.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Ship Superstructure & Bow)**:
  - The left and right sides show the elevated green deck, snow accumulations, yellow railings, mast structures, and the white spherical satellite radome on the right.
  - At the bottom center, the red bow of the *Nathaniel B. Palmer* cuts through dark leads of water between broken white ice floes.
- **Middle Ground (Pack Ice)**:
  - The sea surface surrounding the vessel is densely covered with broken white and pale blue sea ice floes separated by narrow dark-water channels.
  - Several dark, blurry smudges appear near the center and upper-center of the mosaic where water droplets or ice crystals on the camera lens housing partially obscure individual frames in the stitched panorama.
- **Background (Mountains & Sky)**:
  - Snow-covered Antarctic mountain ranges and glaciers stretch along the horizon under a bright pale blue sky.
