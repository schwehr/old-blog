# Antarctic Nighttime Panoramic Mosaic from RVIB *Nathaniel B. Palmer* (`nbp-roland-3.png`)

## Overview

A wide-angle nighttime panoramic photograph mosaic captured from an automated SLR camera mounted on a pan-tilt unit ("The Droid") atop the NSF icebreaker RVIB *Nathaniel B. Palmer* (NBP) in Antarctica. The mosaic was created by Roland Arsenault using a Linux capture and stitching program to observe sea ice coverage during polar darkness while tracking whales with Colin.

## Scene Description

- **Ship Superstructure & Deck Lighting**: The foreground and sides of the panorama show the upper deck and superstructure of the *Nathaniel B. Palmer* brightly illuminated by warm yellow-orange shipboard work lights and floodlights:
  - **Left Side**: Two white spherical radomes mounted on yellow tripod/pedestal masts along the yellow deck railing, with a tall floodlight tower glowing brightly at the far left edge.
  - **Right Side**: Another white spherical radome with a dark logo mounted on a pedestal inside the yellow safety railing, flanked by an angled mast structure on the left and a bright floodlight tower on the far right. Patches of snow and ice are visible on the dark deck surface.
  - **Lower Center**: Looking forward/down onto the ship's bow and weather deck, which is intensely lit by warm golden floodlights, showing railings, masts, and deck cranes/machinery.
- **Searchlights & Sea Ice**: Multiple narrow, blueish-white searchlight beams project outward and upward from the ship into the dark polar night, sweeping across the sea ice and open water ahead of the vessel. Due to the sequential pan-tilt exposures stitched into the mosaic, the searchlight beams appear as multiple intersecting diagonal rays in the center of the frame.
- **Sky**: A deep, nearly black polar night sky arches above the horizon, with faint dark cloud textures visible in the upper center.
