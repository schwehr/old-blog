# Google Earth View of Hydrographic Surveys with BAG Count Badges (`survey-number-of-bags.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot from Google Earth showing a regional view of Rhode Island Sound, Buzzards Bay, the Elizabeth Islands, and Martha's Vineyard (`Dukes` County, Massachusetts), demonstrating custom KML survey icons annotated with badges indicating the number of Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) files contained within each NOAA hydrographic survey.

## Map & Overlay Details

- **Geographic Base Map**: Satellite imagery of the southern New England coastline:
  - Upper left shows Narragansett Bay with a black dot and cyan label for `Newport`, Rhode Island.
  - Center-right shows the Elizabeth Islands chain and Martha's Vineyard labeled `Dukes` in cyan text, with Nomans Land island visible to the south.
- **Survey Bounding Boxes & Numbered Icons**:
  - Seven NOAA hydrographic surveys are represented by orange rectangular bounding boxes and centered square rainbow-grid icons. Each icon features a brown/red circular badge in the lower-right corner displaying the count of BAG files in white text (generated via an ImageMagick script):
    1. **`H11310`** (top left, in Narragansett Bay north of Newport): Badge shows `1`.
    2. **`H11996`** (bottom left, offshore in Rhode Island Sound): Badge shows `2`.
    3. **`H11995`** (lower left, overlapping `H11996` and `H11922`): Badge shows `2`.
    4. **`H11922`** (lower center-left, west of Martha's Vineyard): Large orange bounding box with badge showing `2`.
    5. **`H11921`** (center, over the western Elizabeth Islands): Badge shows `4`.
    6. **`H11076`** (center, over the middle Elizabeth Islands): Badge shows `13`.
    7. **`H11920`** (center-right, covering northwestern Martha's Vineyard / Menemsha Bight): Badge shows `18`.
    8. **`H11346`** (far right, covering eastern Martha's Vineyard / Edgartown and Chappaquiddick): Badge shows `1`.
