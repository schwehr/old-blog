## Overview

A time-series graph generated by **RRDtool / NagiosGrapher** monitoring the UNH AIS server (`*.unh.edu(AIS_FEED)`), plotting the number of ship position records received in the preceding 10 minutes at 10-minute intervals on June 17, 2010.
