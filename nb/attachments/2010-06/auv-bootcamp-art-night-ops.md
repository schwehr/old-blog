# Nighttime AUV Operations Departure at the Swains Lake Dock (`auv-bootcamp-art-night-ops.jpg`)

## Overview

A twilight photograph taken at the wooden dock on Swains Lake, New Hampshire, showing Art Trembanis and three other AUV Bootcamp participants preparing a center-console motorboat to head out to the far end of the lake for nighttime AUV operations.

- **Dock & Boats (Foreground / Center)**:

  - Moored on the near (front-left) side of the dock is a small unpainted aluminum utility boat with a red-and-black side stripe and a small transom-mounted electric trolling motor.
  - Moored behind the dock is a larger white fiberglass center-console skiff (`AQUASPORT 175`) equipped with a black Evinrude outboard engine on the left (stern) and an illuminated white navigation/console light in the center.
- **People**:
