# Submerged Aquatic Vegetation / Bio-Fouling Hazard in Swains Lake (`auv-bootcamp-bio-fouling.jpg`)

## Overview

A close-up downward photograph looking into the shallow, murky water of Swains Lake, New Hampshire, showing dense underwater aquatic weeds and stems that pose a bio-fouling and entanglement hazard for Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) propellers and control surfaces during AUV Bootcamp operations.

## Scene Description

- **Submerged Vegetation**:
  - Through the brownish-green lake water, a dense mat of feathery, branching submerged aquatic plants (resembling milfoil or fanwort) fills the entire frame.
  - Long, thin, reddish-brown and tan jointed stems crisis-cross through the upper water column, while bushy olive-green whorls of finely divided leaves cluster thickly below.
  - In the lower-right quadrant, a small dark green clump of algae/weed and a pale floating stem catch the direct sunlight at or just below the water surface.
