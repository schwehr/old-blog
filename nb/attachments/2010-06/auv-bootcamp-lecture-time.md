# Indoor Lecture Session During AUV Bootcamp (`auv-bootcamp-lecture-time.jpg`)

## Overview

An indoor wide-angle photograph showing AUV Bootcamp participants gathered inside a lakeside house at Swains Lake, New Hampshire, attending a presentation projected onto the living room wall regarding AUV mission risks and operational procedures.

## Scene Description

- **Presenter & Projected Slide (Right)**:
  - Standing on the right side of the room next to a white paneled door is a bearded instructor wearing a light blue/teal polo shirt over a dark undershirt and khaki cargo pants, gesturing toward the projected slide while speaking to the group.
  - High on the sage-green and white wall is a projected slide with an ocean/sky background titled **`Mission risks`** (partially washed out by light from the windows), containing bulleted lists of operational hazards including fishing gear/lines, boating traffic, and bottom/obstacle collision risks.
  - Leaning against the wall below the projection is a handheld dry-erase whiteboard covered in handwritten notes.
- **Audience & Room Setup (Left & Center)**:
  - **Left Foreground**: A man wearing a tan baseball cap, glasses, and a dark short-sleeved shirt sits at a wooden table with an open Apple MacBook Pro laptop, a pink zippered case, and a blue patterned water bottle, resting his chin on his hand while watching the presenter.
  - **Center**: Two participants sit on the floor/beanbags in front of a cream-colored dresser—one with long brown hair wearing a black t-shirt and khaki pants, and another to his left wearing a gray shirt and cap.
  - **Projector & Equipment**: A digital projector sits atop a red-lidded plastic storage bin placed on a black equipment case in the center of the room, next to a small black netbook/laptop. Behind them, a silver CRT television and a tray of stemmed glasses sit on the cream dresser, flanked by a wall clock and a silver metal stove flue pipe rising between two windows with floral valances.
