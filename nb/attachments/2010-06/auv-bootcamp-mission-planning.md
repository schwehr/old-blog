# Lakeside AUV Mission Planning on the Dock at Swains Lake (`auv-bootcamp-mission-planning.jpg`)

## Overview

A daytime outdoor photograph at Swains Lake, New Hampshire, showing Art Trembanis, Val Schmidt, and three AUV Bootcamp students gathered on and around a white center-console motorboat at the wooden dock, reviewing an upcoming AUV mission concept on a ruggedized laptop.

## Scene Description

- **People on the Dock (Left)**:
  - **Art Trembanis**: Crouching/kneeling on the wooden dock in profile facing right, wearing a dark sun visor, khaki long-sleeved jacket/shirt, and dark pants.
  - **Val Schmidt**: Standing on the wooden dock beside Art, wearing sunglasses on his head, a light blue polo shirt over a white long-sleeved undershirt, and khaki cargo pants, pointing with his right index finger while explaining the mission concept to the students in the boat.
- **People in the Boat (Center & Right)**:
  - Moored against the dock is a white fiberglass center-console skiff (`NH 7503 BN` printed on the starboard bow) with a black Evinrude outboard motor at the stern and an aluminum utility boat tied on the opposite side of the dock.
  - **Bow (Left)**: A young man in a white tank top and plaid shorts reclines inside the bow of the white boat, listening attentively to Val and Art.
  - **Center**: A woman in a black t-shirt and turquoise shorts sits on the boat bench facing the dock.
  - **Console**: A bearded man in sunglasses and a bright green t-shirt sits at the center console behind a small folding table holding a ruggedized laptop (Toughbook) and electronic gear.
  - **Starboard Gunwale (Right)**: A woman with long dark hair in a ponytail, wearing a black jacket with red-and-white sleeve stripes and blue jeans, sits on the starboard edge of the boat facing the speakers on the dock.
- **Background**: Sunlit green ripples cover Swains Lake, bordered along the far shore by a dense canopy of summer trees and several lakeside cottages.
