# AUV Bootcamp Operations Center at Swains Lake (`auv-bootcamp-operations-center.jpg`)

## Overview

An evening photograph showing the lakeside house, backyard patio, and illuminated screened gazebo ("bug tent") that served as the field operations center at Swains Lake, New Hampshire, during the June 2010 AUV Bootcamp.

## Scene Description

- **Screened Gazebo / Bug Tent (Left)**:
  - On the grassy lawn to the left stands a white-roofed, mesh-walled gazebo glowing warmly from an overhead interior light. Silhouettes of several team members are visible inside and standing by the right-side entrance door (including a person in a light blue shirt outside the door), with a small charcoal grill and fire pit in front of the tent.
- **Lakeside House (Center Background)**:
  - In the center rises a two-story gray-shingled house with white trim and black shutters. The second floor features two flanking windows and a central sliding glass door opening onto a small white-railed balcony with two white patio chairs.
  - On the ground floor, warm orange light shines through a central sliding glass door and adjacent windows. A white camper/RV trailer is parked behind the gazebo to the left of the house.
- **Patio & Yard (Center & Right Foreground)**:
  - Two large white patio umbrellas stand on a low wooden deck/patio in front of the house, lit by several small glowing orange candles or lanterns arranged along the railing.
  - Several bootcamp participants relax and converse on the patio and lawn: one person reclines in a lawn chair in the center foreground, while three others gather near the right umbrella and patio chairs. Dense dark woods frame the house on both the left and right sides.
