# Gavia AUV Operating on Swains Lake During AUV Bootcamp (`auv-bootcamp.jpg`)

## Overview

A photograph taken by Monica showing a Teledyne Gavia Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) cruising at the surface of Swains Lake, New Hampshire, during the June 2010 AUV Bootcamp hosted with the University of Delaware (UDel) team.

## Scene Description

- **Gavia AUV (Lower Right Foreground)**:
  - The yellow-and-orange torpedo-shaped hull of the Gavia AUV is partially submerged at the water surface, moving from left to right across the lower portion of the frame.
  - Its orange communications/GPS tower with a white top cap protrudes above the waterline, and a frothy white propeller wake trails immediately behind its stern.
- **Lake Surface (Middle Ground)**:
  - Grayish-silver lake water covered in gentle ripples fills the majority of the frame.
- **Shoreline & Boathouse (Background)**:
  - Along the top of the frame, the wooded shoreline of Swains Lake is lined with dense green deciduous and pine trees.
  - Toward the upper right stands a single-story wooden lakeside cabin or boathouse with light tan shingled siding, a gabled roof, multiple rectangular windows, and a small sandy beach area beside the water.
