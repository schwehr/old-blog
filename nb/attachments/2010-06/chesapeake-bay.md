# Aerial View of Chesapeake Bay at Sunset (`chesapeake-bay.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken from the passenger window of a commercial airliner at sunset while approaching Norfolk, Virginia, for the MTS TechSurge meeting ("Ocean Observing: Thinking Outside the Basin") in June 2010.

## Scene Description
- **Aircraft Wing & Engine**:
  - The swept left wing of the jet extends from the middle-right edge toward the upper-center, topped by a tall angled winglet silhouetted against the bright glow of the setting sun.
  - Below the wing in the lower foreground is the dark curved cowling of the port-side turbofan engine, with a bright orange-white pinpoint reflection of the sun glinting off the top seam of the nacelle.
- **Geography & Water**:
  - Below the aircraft, the calm, hazy waters of the Chesapeake Bay and its intricate network of inlets, peninsulas, marshes, and barrier islands stretch across the lower two-thirds of the frame.
  - A couple of tiny, bright golden reflections of sunlight glint off small ponds or waterways on the peninsula near the wing root.
- **Sky & Lighting**:
  - A dramatic sunset horizon transitions from a brilliant pale yellow-white solar glow behind the winglet, through vibrant bands of golden orange and deep amber along the horizon line, up into a dusky slate-blue sky at the top of the frame.
  - A thin, hazy layer of atmospheric mist hangs over the bay, softly diffusing the warm evening light across the water and landmasses.
