# Deepwater Horizon Response Overview Graphic (`deepwater-horizon-overview.jpg`)

## Overview
An isometric 3D cutaway infographic produced by the Deepwater Horizon Unified Command (`deepwaterhorizonresponse.com`) illustrating the multi-layered offshore, nearshore, onshore, aerial, and subsea operations responding to the Deepwater Horizon oil spill in the Gulf of Mexico, with summary statistics current as of June 6, 2010.

## Diagram & Scene Details
- **3D Ocean Block & Coastline**:
  - The graphic is rendered as a 3D rectangular block of the Gulf of Mexico with a brown textured seabed at the bottom, a blue water column in the middle, a dark blue sea surface on top, and a yellow sandy shoreline along the upper-left edge.
  - Along the shoreline, three state boundaries are marked with dimension lines and black uppercase text: `LOUISIANA`, `MISSISSIPPI`, and `ALABAMA`.
  - Above the shoreline are three angled dark-gray pill-shaped badges with circular icons on the left and white text:
    - `Air Sampling` (blue circle with white cloud icon)
    - `Beach Clean Up` (green/olive circle with white trash can icon)
    - `Beach Debris` (orange/yellow circle with white debris/cloud icon)
- **Distance & Depth Markers**:
  - Along the left edge of the sea surface, distance markers from shore are labeled in light blue-gray text at regular intervals: `-10MI-`, `-20MI-`, `-30MI-`, `-40MI-`, and `-50MI-`.
  - Along the far-right vertical edge of the water column, a white vertical dimension line is labeled `5,000FT` to indicate the water depth from the surface to the seafloor.
- **Surface & Aerial Operations**:
  - **Aerial/Space**: In the upper right above the water block, a satellite icon is labeled `SATELLITE SURVEILLANCE`, and a red-and-white Coast Guard aircraft is labeled `AIRCRAFT SURVEILLANCE`. A second red aircraft flies near the coast over the `BOOM` area, and a Coast Guard helicopter hovers near the `-10MI-` / `-20MI-` mark.
  - **Nearshore & Mid-Range Vessels**: Yellow containment `BOOM` lines the coast; a white Coast Guard cutter (`CG CUTTER`) patrols near pairs of vessels towing booms with black smoke rising from controlled `BURNING`; multiple vessels tow U-shaped booms labeled `SKIMMERS`; a `FISHING BOAT` operates near the `-50MI-` mark; a `NOAA RESEARCH` vessel and a `VOSS` (Vessel of Opportunity Skimming System) operate further east; and a `DISPERSANT VESSEL` sprays near the offshore site.
  - **Wellhead Site (Far Right)**: Two semi-submersible drilling rigs are labeled `RELIEF WELL` (one upper-right, one lower-right of the cluster), flanking two drillships/vessels labeled `CONTAINMENT VESSELS` positioned directly above the subsea wellhead.
- **Subsea Operations (Right Face of Block)**:
  - Vertical risers/tethers descend 5,000 feet from the surface vessels to the brown seafloor wellhead.
  - Two yellow remotely operated vehicles are labeled `ROV` at mid-depth and near the seabed, along with a subsea `DISPERSANT SPRAY` wand injecting dispersant near the wellhead.
  - Two oval badges on the vertical water-column faces read `Water Sampling` (with a blue water-drop icon on the left face) and `Bio Mass Sampling` (with a blue fish icon on the right face).
