# NOAA BAGs in the EarthNC Online ChartViewer (`earthnc-chartviewer-with-bags.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **EarthNC Online (BETA)** web-based nautical chart viewer centered on Upper New York Bay (showing Liberty Island, Ellis Island, Governors Island, and lower Manhattan/Brooklyn), demonstrating the integration of high-resolution color-shaded NOAA hydrographic survey BAG (Bathymetric Attributed Grid) overlays on top of seamless NOAA raster nautical charts.

## Map & Bathymetry Details
- **Geography & Nautical Chart Base**:
  - The background combines Google hybrid satellite/road imagery on land with a semi-transparent NOAA Raster Nautical Chart (RNC) covering the waterways of New York Harbor.
  - Prominent labeled landmarks include `Liberty State Park` (on the New Jersey shore at left), `Ellis Island` (center-left), `Liberty Island` / `Statue of Liberty` (lower-left), `Governors Island` (lower-right), `South Cove Park` and `Museum of Jewish Heritage` (upper-center at the tip of Manhattan), and the yellow highway line of the `Brooklyn-Battery Tunnel (Toll road)` / Interstate `478` connecting Manhattan to Brooklyn (`Columbia Waterfront`, `Columbia St`).
- **BAG Bathymetry Overlays**:
  - Multibeam bathymetry grids are rendered over the harbor channels in rainbow hillshaded colors: green and yellow along shallower shoals and channel edges (such as around Ellis Island, Governors Island, and the Buttermilk Channel), transitioning to blue, indigo, and magenta/purple in the deeper main shipping channels (Anchorage Channel) east of Liberty Island.
- **Navigation Markers**:
  - Numerous nautical chart symbols and interactive icons are plotted across the harbor, including green can buoys, red nun buoys, red/green lighted buoys, white square waypoint/information markers circling Liberty Island, and red/pink triangular markers.
