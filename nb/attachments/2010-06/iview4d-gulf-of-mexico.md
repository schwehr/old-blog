# Gulf of Mexico Bathymetry in Fledermaus iView4D (`iview4d-gulf-of-mexico.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X application screenshot of **Fledermaus iView4D** (by Interactive Visualization Systems / IVS 3D) displaying a 3D perspective rendering of the NOAA Coastal Relief Model bathymetry and topography (`GulfOfMexico-CoastalRelief.sd`) covering the northern Gulf of Mexico and the continental slope around the Deepwater Horizon site.

## 3D Scene Description (Right Pane)
- **Bathymetry & Topography Surface**:
  - Set against a solid black 3D background, a tilted rectangular block of the northern Gulf of Mexico is rendered with a vertical exaggeration of `6.00`.
  - **Land & Coastline (Upper Left)**: Flat coastal plain topography is colored light gray/off-white with dark green wetlands, river deltas (including the Mississippi River Birdfoot Delta protruding onto the shelf), and bays along the Louisiana/Texas coast.
  - **Continental Shelf & Slope (Center to Lower Right)**: The shallow continental shelf is shaded in pale icy blue, transitioning down the rugged, pockmarked continental slope (the Texas-Louisiana Slope, featuring numerous salt domes, intraslope basins, and submarine canyons such as Mississippi Canyon) into deeper medium-blue abyssal water in the lower-right foreground.
  - **3D Bounding Wall & Grid Ticks**: Along the right edge of the block is a purple vertical cutaway wall with black grid lines; white coordinate labels run along the bottom-left and bottom-right edges of the surface.
- **3D Navigation Widgets & Watermark**:
  - **Center**: A 3D white coordinate axis cross (`-100000.0`, `-50000.0`, `50000.0`, `100000.0` horizontally, and `10000.0`, `20000.0` vertically) topped by a horizontal gray 3D compass ring (`N` visible) and flanked on the left by a vertical gray 3D pitch/tilt ring.
  - **Upper-Right Watermark**: A metallic 3D eye/sphere logo beside silver text reading `iView4D` above spaced lettering `F l e d e r m a u s`.

## Application Interface (Left & Top)
- **Mac OS X Menu Bar**: Shows the Apple icon, bold `iview4d`, `File`, `Camera`, `View`, and `Help`.
- **Window Title Bar**: Standard Mac traffic-light window buttons (red, yellow, green) and the title `iView4D` with a small round application icon.
- **Left Control Panel (`Data Sets`)**:
  - Vertical tab label `Data Sets` on the far left edge.
  - Top input field: A small `x` button next to `Exag:` with a text box containing `6.00`.
  - Tree view below: A checked root node `Scene` with a checked child item `GulfOfMexico-...` highlighted in gray.
