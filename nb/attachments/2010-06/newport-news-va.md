# Aerial Sunset View of the Virginia Peninsula and Hampton Roads (`newport-news-va.jpg`)

## Overview

`newport-news-va.jpg` is an aerial photograph taken from an airplane window at sunset on June 7, 2010, during the approach to Norfolk, Virginia, for the Marine Technology Society (MTS) TechSurge meeting ("Ocean Observing: Thinking Outside the Basin"). It shows the coastal geography, estuaries, and man-made marine infrastructure around Hampton Roads and the Virginia Peninsula.

## Scene Description

- **Sky & Clouds**: The upper third of the frame displays a dramatic sunset sky glowing in warm yellow, gold, and bright orange-red near the horizon. Two prominent, dark blue-gray cumulus cloud formations rise on the left and center-left, silhouetted against the bright glow of the setting sun.
- **Landmass & Waterways**: Below the horizon lies a broad, flat coastal peninsula and estuarine shoreline (around Hampton, Poquoson, and Newport News, Virginia) bathed in dim evening twilight and atmospheric haze. Winding tidal creeks, inlets, and embayments indent the dark landmass on the right.
- **Coastal Infrastructure**:
  - In the lower-left foreground, a long, narrow man-made causeway or bridge-tunnel island (part of the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel / Fort Wool area) extends diagonally across the calm, reflective water before terminating at a small island portal.
  - Along the central shoreline, another straight jetty or causeway projects outward toward an enclosed coastal basin or dredged spoil island on the lower right.
  - The water surface reflects the pale yellow-orange light of the sunsetsky, contrasting with the dark silhouettes of the land and breakwaters.
