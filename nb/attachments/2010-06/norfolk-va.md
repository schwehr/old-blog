# Norfolk Shipyard, Drydock, and Tug-Barge Operations (`norfolk-va.jpg`)

## Overview

`norfolk-va.jpg` is a photograph taken in June 2010 along the Elizabeth River waterfront in Norfolk, Virginia, during the MTS TechSurge meeting ("Ocean Observing: Thinking Outside the Basin"). The image captures active maritime and shipyard operations at dusk, featuring a floating crane barge in the foreground, a tugboat pushing a deck barge across the channel, and a naval amphibious warfare vessel inside a large floating drydock in the background.

## Scene Description

- **Foreground Crane Barge**: Moored alongside a low floating dock at the bottom left is a flat-deck crane barge equipped with black rubber cylindrical fenders along its hull. Mounted on the right side of the barge is a white-and-black operator cab with a tall lattice boom angling sharply upward to the upper left, rigging cables hanging down toward yellow hook blocks, and a vertical spud pole near the center of the deck.
- **Midground Tug and Barge**: Moving from right to left across the dark blue-gray river is a long, low-profile gray tank or deck barge being pushed from astern by a white-and-black tugboat. A white wake trails behind the tugboat on the right.
- **Background Shipyard & Drydock**:
  - Across the water sits a massive dark-gray floating drydock flanked by tall lattice portal cranes.
  - Inside the drydock rests a large U.S. Navy amphibious transport dock (LPD-17 *San Antonio*-class vessel, with its distinctive enclosed pyramidal mast structure) undergoing maintenance, illuminated by warm golden evening sunlight and work lights.
  - Brick industrial buildings and illuminated pier structures line the left side of the shipyard, while modern mid-rise waterfront buildings appear along the distant shoreline on the far right beneath a dark blue-gray overcast sky.
