# OilSpillStats.com Simile Timeline of the Deepwater Horizon Incident (`simile-timeline-deepwaterhorizon.png`)

## Overview

`simile-timeline-deepwaterhorizon.png` is a screenshot of the web application `http://oilspillstats.com/` created by Jim Meyer using the open-source SIMILE Timeline widget and Google Maps to track events, press briefings, and spatial closures during the 2010 Deepwater Horizon oil spill response in the Gulf of Mexico.

## Interface & Content Details

- **Header**: Across the top, spaced lowercase lettering reads: `g u l f   o f   m e x i c o   o i l   s p i l l   r e s p o n s e` (`gulf of mexico` in light gray and `oil spill response` in dark gray).
- **Upper-Left Map Panel**:
  - An embedded Google Map shows the northern Gulf of Mexico coastline from Louisiana (`New Orleans`, `Lake Pontchartrain`, `Chandeleur Sound`) across Mississippi and Alabama (`Mobile`) to the Florida Panhandle (`Pensacola`).
  - A large, dark olive-green polygon covers a vast offshore region of the Gulf of Mexico (representing the federal fisheries closure or cumulative oil slick extent), with a small yellow circle near the southern edge.
  - Multi-colored shoreline segments in blue, yellow, orange, red, and purple trace the coastal bays, barrier islands, and beaches indicating shoreline cleanup and oiling status.
  - Standard Google Maps pan/zoom controls appear on the left, four layer toggle icons sit at the top left, a scale bar (`50 km` / `50 mi`) is at the bottom left next to the `POWERED BY Google` logo, and `Map data ©2010 Google - Terms of Use` is at the bottom right.
  - Below the map, a caption reads: `Snapshot of shoreline cleanup and marine pollution as of 26 Jun 2010 from GeoPlatform.gov/gulfresponse` (with a blue hyperlink).
- **Upper-Right Day Counter**:
  - Large bold black text reads: `69 days`
  - Below it: `since the explosion and fire on the MODU Deepwater Horizon` (hyperlinked in purple/blue).
- **Lower Simile Timeline Widget**:
  - A horizontally scrolling multi-band timeline (`Timeline © SIMILE` watermark oriented vertically at the bottom left) displays events across late June 2010:
    - **Top Hourly Band**: Marked in 1-hour increments (`19hr`, `20hr`, `21hr`, `22hr`, `23hr`, `0hr`, `1hr`, `2hr`) across the boundary between `Jun 28` and `Jun 29`. Visible event labels with purple circular bullet icons include:
      - `Closed Area in Gulf of Mexico` (partially clipped on the left)
      - `The Ongoing Administration-Wide Response to the Deepwater BP Oil Spill`
      - `[Pr]esident for Energy and Climate Change Policy Carol Browner visit New Orleans to receive update on the BP oil spill response`
      - `Press Briefing by National Incident Commander June 28, 2010`
    - **Middle Daily Band**: Divided into daily columns labeled `Jun 27`, `Jun 28`, `Jun 29`, `Jun 30`, `Ju[l 1]`, with vertical blue tick marks indicating individual event timestamps and a highlighted white window around `Jun 28`–`Jun 29`.
    - **Bottom Overview Band**: A wider time scale showing `Jun 27` and `Jul 4` with clusters of blue event ticks.
  - **Footer Caption**: `Source: Deepwater Horizon Unified Command website. To move the Timeline: use the mouse scroll wheel, the arrow keys or grab and drag the Timeline. Created using Simile Timeline.`
