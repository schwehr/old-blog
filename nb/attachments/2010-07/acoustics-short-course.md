# CCOM Acoustics Short Course Demonstration (`acoustics-short-course.jpg`)

## Overview

`acoustics-short-course.jpg` is an indoor photograph taken in July 2010 at the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) at the University of New Hampshire, showing Val Schmidt and a colleague demonstrating an underwater acoustics exercise in a small glass fish tank in front of an audience and a large projection screen.

## Scene Description

- **Projection Screen (Left/Center)**:
  - Dominating the left half of the room is a large wall-mounted projection screen displaying a software window with a white plot background and a grid overlay.
  - Plotted in bright solid blue is an acoustic time-series waveform (an initial high-amplitude transmit pulse on the far left followed by a series of decaying multipath/target echo envelopes along the horizontal time axis).
- **Instructors & Demonstration Tank**:
  - Just to the right of the projection screen stands a rectangular glass aquarium/fish tank supported on a wooden stand, used as a miniature acoustic test tank. Two ceremonial flags topped with eagle finials stand behind the tank against the back wall.
  - Two instructors stand near the tank: one in a dark shirt immediately behind the tank looking toward the screen, and Val Schmidt on the right in a light collared shirt facing the tank and screen.
- **Audience & Classroom**:
  - In the foreground and along the right side of the room, six attendees are seated in dark reddish-brown upholstered office chairs, watching the live waveform demonstration.
  - On the far left table sits an open Apple MacBook Pro laptop with its glowing white Apple logo visible.
