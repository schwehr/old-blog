# E/V Nautilus Telepresence Video Feed of ROVs Argus and Hercules Recovery (`argus-and-hercules.png`)

## Overview

`argus-and-hercules.png` is a screenshot of a multi-camera live video stream from `http://nautiluslive.org/` taken on July 2, 2010, during a nighttime recovery of the remotely operated vehicles (ROVs) *Argus* and *Hercules* aboard the exploration vessel E/V *Nautilus*, monitored via the telepresence console at UNH CCOM.

## Multi-Panel Video Layout

The composite video frame is divided into six camera views arranged in a 2-row layout:

- **Top-Left Panel (Deck View of ROV *Hercules*)**:
  - Shows the aft deck at night illuminated by bright work lights, with a gray compass rose watermark (`N`) in the upper-left corner and a circular plus (`+`) icon in the upper-right corner.
  - The large yellow-and-syntactic-foam ROV *Hercules* sits inside its metal frame on deck while a crew member wearing a blue hard hat, life vest, and khakis tends a line next to the vertical crane cable and green deck storage boxes.
- **Top-Right Panel (Aft A-Frame & Crane Recovery)**:
  - Wide view looking aft over the brightly lit stern deck against the pitch-black night sea (`N` compass rose watermark at top left).
  - The ship's large articulated knuckle-boom crane extends out over the starboard quarter, hoisting one of the vehicles from the water while the A-frame gantry and another ROV sit on the deck to the left. A crew member in a dark cap is visible in the lower-left foreground.
- **Bottom-Left Panel (Control Van Interior)**:
  - Darkened interior of the shipboard ROV control van (`N` compass rose at top left).
  - A red digital LED clock above the main monitor bank reads `21:25:48`.
  - A row of four glowing flat-screen monitors at desk level and a larger wide-screen display above them show live deck camera feeds and telemetry plots.
- **Bottom-Center-Top Panel**:
  - Dark camera feed (`N` compass rose on the left) showing a dimly lit hull or underwater/over-the-side view at night.
- **Bottom-Center-Bottom Panel (Winch Drum)**:
  - Close-up overhead camera view of the heavy traction winch drum wound with yellow/brown armored umbilical cable and its blue level-wind mechanism.
- **Bottom-Right-Top Panel**:
  - Smaller duplicate thumbnail of the top-left camera view showing the crew member in the blue hard hat standing beside ROV *Hercules*.
- **Bottom-Right-Bottom Panel (Hydraulic / Pressure Gauges)**:
  - Close-up view of an equipment panel featuring a horizontal row of five circular analog dial gauges above a dark rectangular enclosure with a warning label.
