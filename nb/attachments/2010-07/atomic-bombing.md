# Vintage Book Cover: "Atomic Bombing: How to Protect Yourself" (`atomic-bombing.png`)

## Overview

`atomic-bombing.png` is a color scan of the vintage illustrated cover/title page of the Cold War civil defense book *Atomic Bombing: How to Protect Yourself*, prepared by Science Service, Inc. and published by Wise Publication (`atomicbombing00scierich.pdf` on the Internet Archive).

## Visual & Textual Details

- **Illustration**:
  - Set against a deep navy-blue background sky, a towering atomic mushroom cloud rises from a devastated city skyline along the bottom edge.
  - The city buildings at the bottom are rendered in dark crimson red with skeletal scaffolding and ruined walls, casting black geometric shadows across anochre-yellow ground plane. Concentric shockwaves and billowing clouds of yellow, orange, olive-green, and white surge outward from the base of the blast column.
  - The mushroom cap at the top is a large, Fluffy white cloud rimmed in slate blue on top and golden orange/brown underneath.
- **Typography & Labels**:
  - **Main Title (Inside the Mushroom Cloud)**: Bold, hand-lettered brush-style red capital letters slanted slightly upward read:
    ```
    ATOMIC
    BOMBING
    ```
  - **Subtitle (Inside a Yellow Shield)**: Superimposed over the right side of the mushroom stem is a bright golden-yellow heraldic shield containing bold navy-blue sans-serif text:
    ```
    HOW TO
    PROTECT
    YOURSELF
    ```
  - **Bottom Attribution**: Along the bottom left in small black sans-serif text: `Prepared by Science Service, Inc.`
  - **Corner Banner**: Across the bottom-right corner, a diagonal white ribbon banner displays navy-blue serif lettering: `A Wise PUBLICATION`.
