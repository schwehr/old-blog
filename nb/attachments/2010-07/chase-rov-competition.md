# Student ROVs Lined Up at the UNH Chase Ocean Engineering Test Tank (`chase-rov-competition.jpg`)

## Overview

`chase-rov-competition.jpg` is a photograph taken in late July 2010 inside the high-bay facility at the Jere A. Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory at the University of New Hampshire (UNH), showing a row of ten student-built remotely operated vehicles (ROVs) lined up along the concrete coping of the deep engineering test tank prior to a Tech Camp 2010 student ROV competition.

## Scene Description

- **Foreground Row of Student ROVs**:
  - Ten small, open-frame educational ROVs (SeaPerch-style vehicles) are arranged in a neat line along the concrete ledge at the edge of the tank.
  - Each ROV is constructed from white PVC pipe and elbow fittings forming a rectangular cage, topped with a pair of rounded blue-and-white syntactic/foam buoyancy floats and fitted with small thrusters and mesh bottom screens.
  - Long blue umbilical tether cables trail from the vehicles down the right side of the concrete tank wall (where red stenciled safety lettering partially reads `ALL PERSONNEL MUST W[EAR...]`) into neat coils on the floor at the bottom right.
- **Engineering Test Tank & Bridge Crane (Midground)**:
  - Behind the row of ROVs lies the large rectangular indoor water tank filled with clear blue water, bordered by wooden deck planks on the left.
  - Spanning the width of the tank is a massive bright-yellow steel overhead travelling bridge crane/gantry with a central carriage and an angled blue access ladder/stairway extending down toward the water surface.
- **Background High-Bay Facility**:
  - On the far beige wall above the tank hangs a large projection screen displaying a photograph of a polar bear standing on snow/ice next to scientific gear.
  - Warm high-bay lighting illuminates the upper walls and ceiling trusses.
