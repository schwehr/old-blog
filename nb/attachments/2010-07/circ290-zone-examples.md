# IMO Circular 290 Area Notice Zone Examples (`circ290-zone-examples.png`)

## Overview
A page excerpt from International Maritime Organization (IMO) Circular `SN.1/Circ.290` ("Guidance for the Presentation and Display of AIS Application-Specific Messages Information"), showing Annex page 5 on **Area Notice – Broadcast (FI = 22)**. The document presents two visual examples illustrating how North Atlantic right whale acoustic detection zones in the Boston Traffic Separation Scheme (TSS) and Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary are displayed both on a shore-based web map and on a shipborne Electronic Chart System (ECS).

## Example 1: Shore-Based Geographic Display
The upper figure shows a shore-based map titled **"227 Right Whale Calls Detected Within 24 Hours."** set in a dark brown header banner:
- **Geographic Area**: Covers Massachusetts Bay and Cape Cod Bay, with white text labels for **Cape Ann**, **Gloucester**, **Boston**, **Provincetown**, and **Cape Cod**, and dark blue labels for **Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary** and **Cape Cod Bay**.
- **Buoys & Detection Zones**: Ten circular listening zones follow the shipping lane curve from Boston Harbor east and southeast past Race Point, plus two additional listening zones inside Cape Cod Bay.
  - Most zones along the shipping lane and both zones in Cape Cod Bay contain red whale silhouettes (**Right Whale Detected within Listening Radius**).
  - The westernmost and easternmost buoys in the shipping lane, as well as the eastern buoy in Cape Cod Bay, are shown as bright green circles (**Online Buoy**).
- **Map Legends & Scale**:
  - A semi-transparent rounded legend box in the lower-left corner lists: **Shipping Lane** (parallel grey lines with blue tint), **Buoy Listening Radius** (light blue dashed circle), **Offline Buoy** (grey circle), **Online Buoy** (green circle), and **Right Whale Detected within Listening Radius** (red whale icon).
  - A scale bar at the top right spans `0`, `2.5`, `5`, `10`, `15`, `20 Nautical Miles`, and a white compass rose with an **N** sits in the bottom-right corner.
- **Caption**: *"Example 1 – This is an example of shore-based geographic display of a marine sanctuary area, traffic separation scheme, locations of passive-acoustic buoys, and acoustic detections of North Atlantic right whales (an endangered species). The red-green colour scheme indicates the status of buoy operation."*

## Example 2: Shipborne Electronic Chart System (ECS) Display
The lower figure is a screenshot of an Electronic Chart System (ECS) application window displaying the same region on an Electronic Navigational Chart (ENC):
- **Chart Overlay**: Semi-transparent circular detection zones are overlaid along the magenta dashed Traffic Separation Scheme (TSS) lanes approaching Boston. The five westernmost and five easternmost circles are shaded with green hatching (no recent whale detections), while the four middle circles over Stellwagen Bank are shaded in yellow hatching (active right whale detections).
- **Interface Controls**: The left sidebar contains navigation and AIS control tabs (`AIS Tx`, `AIS Rx`, `All Targets`, `VTS`, `GPS`, `Survey`, `NavAid`, `Buoy Tending`, `Right Whale`, etc.) above a tabular list of received `Timed Circular Notice` entries with date/time groups (`DTG`) and descriptions (`Right whale detection`).
- **Caption**: *"Example 2 – This is an example of data pertaining to the date/time detection and location of North Atlantic right whales (an endangered species) in a traffic separation scheme within a marine sanctuary area. Transmitted via AIS Application-Specific Message from an Operations Centre, this information is displayed on shipborne Electronic Chart System (ECS) as semi-transparent red-yellow-green colours that do not obscure the underlying Electronic Navigational Chart (ENC) data."*
