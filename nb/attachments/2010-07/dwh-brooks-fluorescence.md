# R/V Brooks McCall Subsurface Fluorescence Map and Depth Profile (`dwh-brooks-fluorescence.png`)

## Overview
A figure from the Joint Analysis Group (JAG) Report 1 examining subsurface fluorescence measurements taken by the research vessel *R/V Brooks McCall* between May 8 and May 25, 2010, near the Deepwater Horizon wellhead in the Gulf of Mexico. The graphic combines a spatial station map with an inset vertical depth profile comparing Colored Dissolved Organic Matter (CDOM) fluorescence and potential density anomaly.

## Station Map Details
- **Title**: `(ppb QSDE) at Bottom Depth of Cast (~1315 m to ~1560 m)` (partially cropped at the left edge).
- **Wellhead & Range Rings**:
  - A red circle with a black `X` marks the **Wellhead Location**, pointed to by a black arrow with bold red label text.
  - Concentric dashed range rings radiate outward from the wellhead at `1 Km`, `2 Km`, `3 Km`, `4 Km`, `5 Km`, `6 Km`, `7 Km`, and `8 Km`.
- **Sampling Stations**: Color-coded circular markers indicate *R/V Brooks McCall* CTD/fluorescence cast stations around the wellhead, Primarily clustered to the southwest:
  - **Orange circles (`Cruise 2 (05/15...)`)**: `B22`, `B23`, `B24`, `B34`, `B35`, `B47`, and clustered labels `B20`, `B21`, `B26`, `B36`, `B38`, `B42`, `B52`.
  - **Cyan circles (`Cruise 3 (05/19...)`)**: `B32` and `B33` (farther west, between 7–8+ km), `B37` (1 km south), `B39` (1.5 km east), and `B40` (1 km north).
  - **Purple circles (`Cruise 4 (05/23...)`)**: `B41`, `B43`, `B44`, `B46`, and a station near `B35`/`B20` linked by a red line to the inset plot.

## Inset Depth Profile Plot
An inset graph on the left displays vertical water column profiles from `0` to `1600` meters depth:
- **Axes**:
  - Vertical axis: `Depth (m)` from `0` (top) to `1600` (bottom) in increments of `200`.
  - Top horizontal axis: `Fluorescence (ppb QSDE)` from `0` to `50`.
  - Bottom horizontal axis: `Potential Density Anomaly (kg/m³)` from `25` to `28`.
- **Plotted Curves**:
  - **Red line (`CDOM Fluorescence Sta 20`)**: Remains near baseline (`~5 ppb QSDE`) down to `1000 m`, with a minor spike around `600 m`, then exhibits a large subsurface plume anomaly between `1100 m` and `1350 m`, peaking near `35 ppb QSDE` around `1300 m` depth. A red pointer line connects this deep peak directly to Station B20 on the map.
  - **Black line (`CDOM Fluorescence Sta 25`)**: Stays flat near the baseline (`~5 ppb QSDE`) throughout the entire depth column.
  - **Blue and Green curves (`Potential Density Sta 20` and `Potential Density Sta 25`)**: Nearly identical smooth pycnocline curves increasing from `~25.3 kg/m³` near the surface to `~27.7 kg/m³` at `1500 m`.
