# USCGC Healy Aloftcon Photo Feed in Google Earth on the iPad (`ge-ipad-healy-photos.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth running on an Apple iPad (3G model on AT&T), displaying the USCGC *Healy* (`WAGB-20`) Aloftcon bow camera KML feed off the northern coast of Barrow (Utqiaġvik), Alaska. The image demonstrates that while the custom ship icons render along the vessel track, the embedded photograph inside the selected placemark balloon fails to load.
