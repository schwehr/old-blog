# Right Whale Acoustic Detection Zones in Google Earth on the iPad – Layers Menu (`ge-ipad-rap-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth on the iPad showing the Cornell Right Whale Acoustic Partnership (RAP) buoy detection zones (`test-zones`) in the Boston Traffic Separation Scheme north and east of Cape Cod, after re-exporting the KML from desktop Google Earth so that custom polygon colors and icons render properly via Google My Maps.

## Map & Detection Zones
- **Geographic Area**: Shows Massachusetts Bay, **Stellwagen Bank**, and **Cape Cod** (with **Barnstable** labeled at the bottom).
- **Buoy Listening Zones**:
  - Overlapping circular zones (`Zone 3` through `Zone 10` visible) follow the shipping lane around Cape Cod.
  - **Zones 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 9**: Displayed as semi-transparent bright green circles with dark green borders and white teardrop markers at their centers, indicating no right whale detections in the last 24 hours.
  - **Zones 8 and 10**: Displayed as semi-transparent orange/amber circles with bright red borders and black whale-fluke icons at their centers, indicating active right whale acoustic detections.
