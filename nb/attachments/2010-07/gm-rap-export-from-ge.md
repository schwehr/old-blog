# Right Whale Acoustic Detection Zones in Google Maps After Exporting from Google Earth (`gm-rap-export-from-ge.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Maps (`maps.google.com`) displaying the test KML file (`http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/rap/testing/boston-whale-zon...`) after exporting it from desktop Google Earth. Moving the `<Style>` definitions outside of the `<Folder>` elements restored the proper green and red/amber zone colors and custom whale-fluke icons in Google Maps.

## Map Viewport
- **Geographic Area**: Satellite hybrid view centered on **Massachusetts Bay**, **Cape Cod Bay**, and **Cape Cod** (showing **Plymouth**, **Provincetown**, and **Cape Cod National Seashore**).
- **Restored Zone Styling**:
  - **Zones 1–7 and Zone 9**: Displayed as semi-transparent bright green circles with dark green outlines and white teardrop pin markers at their centers.
  - **Zones 8 and 10**: Displayed as semi-transparent yellow-amber circles with bright red outlines and black whale-fluke icons at their centers, clearly highlighting active right whale detections off Cape Cod.
