# USCGC Healy Science GeoRSS Feed Displayed in Google Maps (`healy-rss-feed.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Maps displaying a custom GeoRSS 2.0 feed (`healy-science-google.rss` hosted on `vislab-ccom.unh.edu`) of hourly scientific and navigational updates from the icebreaker USCGC *Healy* in the Chukchi Sea off northern Alaska on July 5, 2010. The image illustrates how embedding an HTML table inside the RSS `<description>` tag provides a human-readable tabular display inside the Google Maps info window.

## Map & Placemark Details
- **Geographic View**: Satellite view of northern Alaska, the **Chukchi Sea**, the **Beaufort Sea**, and the **Arctic Ocean**.
- **Placemarks**: A tight row of blue Google Maps teardrop pins lies in the Chukchi Sea northwest of Point Barrow, representing hourly ship positions.
