# iBooks Dictionary Popup on the iPad (`ipad-ibook-dict.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **iBooks** application on an Apple iPad in landscape orientation, displaying a two-page spread of Bram Stoker's novel *Dracula* with an interactive dictionary definition popover open for the word **"caleche"**. This image was featured in Kurt Schwehr's July 29, 2010 blog post *"the dictionary in iBooks rocks"*.

## Interface & Book Layout
- **Top Status Bar:** Black iOS status bar showing `iPad` and a Wi-Fi icon on the left, `4:40 PM` in the center, and `95%` with a battery icon on the right.
- **Skeuomorphic Book Frame:** The e-reader interface mimics an open physical hardcover book resting on a warm brown wooden surface, complete with a central spine shadow and stacked page edges along the left and right borders.
- **Left Page Header & Navigation:**
  - Two rounded rectangular buttons in the top-left corner: `Library` and a bulleted list icon (Table of Contents).
  - Centered brown header text reading `Bram Stoker`.
  - Bottom page indicator showing a slider dot near the left end of a dotted progress line across both pages, with `44 of 1140` printed in brown below the left page.
- **Right Page Header & Controls:**
  - Centered brown header text reading `Dracula`.
  - Four brown utility icons in the top-right corner: a brightness sun icon, a font size `AA` icon, a magnifying glass search icon, and a ribbon bookmark icon.
