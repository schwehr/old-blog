# L3 Communications Desktop AIS ATON Transceiver (`l3-desktop-aton.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of an **L3 Communications Desktop ATON (Aid to Navigation)** AIS transceiver unit resting on a gray desk surface, with an inset close-up of its manufacturer identification label in the bottom-left corner. Featured in Kurt Schwehr's July 23, 2010 blog post *"L3 Desktop AIS ATON"* regarding indoor testing and deployment for NOAA and the U.S. Coast Guard at the Cape Cod National Seashore visitor center.

## Hardware & Enclosure Description
- **Chassis:** A rectangular, bright yellow metal enclosure with a large light-gray faceplate on the top surface and a brushed aluminum connector panel along the front edge.
- **Top Faceplate Branding & Port Labels:**
  - Centered near the top is the red circular **L3** logo with white lettering, directly above the title **DESKTOP ATON** in black sans-serif capital letters.
  - Along the bottom edge of the top faceplate, aligned with the front panel ports from left to right, are four printed black labels:
    1. `RS-232 I/O`
    2. `GPS ANT`
    3. `VHF ANT`
    4. `DC POWER` with `10.8-15.6 VDC` and a center-positive barrel jack polarity diagram (`- (• +`) below it.
- **Front Connector Panel:**
  - Four physical connectors mounted on the brushed metal front plate corresponding to the top labels:
    1. A black circular multi-pin serial connector (`RS-232 I/O`).
    2. A threaded coaxial RF connector (`GPS ANT`, TNC/BNC style).
    3. A larger threaded coaxial RF connector (`VHF ANT`, SO-239 / N-type style).
    4. A black DC power jack (`DC POWER`).

## Inset Identification Label
In the lower-left corner, a rectangular yellow-bordered inset shows a close-up photo of the unit's white serial/identification sticker:
- **Header:** `DESKTOP ATON`
- **Left Column Specifications:**
  - `PNR ATN01-340-00`
  - `SNR 00063[blurred]` (the last digits of the serial number are intentionally pixelated/blurred for privacy)
  - `MFR 06141`
  - `DMF 11/2009`
  - `OPERATING RANGE: 12VDC`
  - `NOMINAL/15.6V MAX/10.8V MIN`
- **Right Side:** A square 2D DataMatrix barcode above the **L3 communications** logo in the bottom-right corner.
