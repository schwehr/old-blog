# Trimble's "Map the Spill" Website Screenshot (`mapthespill-trimble.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Map the Spill** website (`mapthespill.org`), a public web mapping portal and mobile reporting platform created by Trimble during the 2010 Deepwater Horizon oil spill in the Gulf of Mexico. Featured in Kurt Schwehr's July 8, 2010 blog post *"Trimble and their web mapping + mobile app Deepwater Horizon"*.

## Header & Navigation
- **Banner:** A dark textured black banner at the top featuring a white silhouette of a brown pelican perched on a rock on the left, next to the large white serif title **Map the Spill**.
- **Call-to-Action Badge:** Tilted beige rectangular tag on the far left reading `CLICK HERE TO Join the Cause!`.
- **Navigation Bar:** A white horizontal menu bar with dark serif links: `Home`, `Mission`, `Spill Map`, `My Reports`, `Blog`, `Forums`, `Mobile Apps`, and `Login | Register` on the far right.

## Interactive Map Panel
On the left side of the main content area is a dark-headed panel titled **Interactive Map** displaying a Google Maps **Hybrid** view of the southeastern United States (Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, Florida, Georgia, South Carolina) and the northern Gulf of Mexico:
- **Map Controls:** Pan/zoom controls in the top-left corner; map type buttons (`Map`, `Satellite`, `Hybrid` selected, `MyTopo`) in the top-right corner.
- **Oil Spill Footprint & Markers:**
  - A bright green polygon in the northern Gulf of Mexico off the Louisiana/Mississippi/Alabama coast representing the oil slick footprint.
  - A circular **NOAA** logo near the lower-left coast above a white legend box reading: `Potential Oiling Footprint - NOAA/NESDIS (Green) observed on 2010-07-06`.
  - White square map pins containing black exclamation-point droplet icons clustered around Mobile Bay / Pensacola and one in central Florida near Orlando/Tampa.
- **Open Info Window:** A white speech-bubble popup pointing to the cluster of markers near Mobile Bay, Alabama, reading:
  - **Report DI24 8 Jun**
  - `2010` by `Alabama Coastal Foundation / Mobile Baykeeper`
  - `30 Jun 2010 | 9 incidents | 0.00 mi`
