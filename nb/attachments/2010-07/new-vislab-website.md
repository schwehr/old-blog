# CCOM/JHC Data Visualization Research Lab Website (`new-vislab-website.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the redesigned homepage for the **Data Visualization Research Lab (VisLab)** at the University of New Hampshire's Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM/JHC), `www.ccom.unh.edu/vislab`, designed by Colin Ware and Colleen. Featured in Kurt Schwehr's July 29, 2010 blog post *"New CCOM VisLab web site"*.

## Header & Visual Theme
- **Background:** Dark charcoal-gray background (`#333333`).
- **Title Banner:** Large white serif text on the upper left reading:
  `The Data Visualization`
  `Research Lab`
  with a dark drop shadow, superimposed over an abstract, flowing, multi-colored 3D ribbon/plume graphic in muted teal, peach, and gray tones.

## Project Thumbnail Grid
Below the header is a 3-row by 5-column grid (13 items total, with 2 empty slots at the bottom right) of rectangular project thumbnails with white sans-serif captions underneath:

### Row 1
1. **About the VisLab** (text overlaid directly on a blurred pastel brushstroke background; no caption below).
2. **Chart of the Future** — A 3D electronic chart and bathymetric coastal perspective view with inset photographs and a text log window at the bottom.
3. **Flow Vis** — Colourful 2D ocean/atmospheric current streamlines with white directional arrows over purple, red, and blue vorticity contours.
4. **GeoZui4D** — A 3D underwater visualization showing golden-brown bathymetry, a bright cyan seafloor patch, a yellow-and-blue block, and an inset top-down map.
5. **AIS** — An illustrated maritime traffic concept diagram showing vessels, coastal radar/AIS stations, satellites, and inset data plots over a coastline.

### Row 2
6. **TrackPlot** — A twisting, ribbon-like 3D marine mammal dive track with yellow, blue, and gray chevrons and a green cube marker.
7. **GeoCam** — A 3D rendering of an orange ROV/submersible tethered by an red line above a blue-and-cyan sloped seafloor surface.
8. **Networks** — A node-link graph diagram on a white background with red, green, blue, and gray hexagonal nodes connected by colored edges, with one red hexagon outlined in black.
9. **AR Simulator** — A photograph of a man sitting in front of a ship bridge simulator projection screen, looking at a small handheld/mounted display and controller.
10. **Semiotics** — A dense 2D array of red circular symbols overlaid with gray directional arrows and fine red radiating lines.

### Row 3
11. **FishTank VR** — A 3D perspective rendering of white cylinders and a green cylinder standing on a gray-and-white checkerboard plane.
12. **Stereo** — A warm yellow-lit profile photograph of a man (Colin Ware) wearing bulky active-shutter 3D stereo glasses.
13. **3D Navigation** — A 3D oblique view of green coastal terrain and blue/cyan bathymetry with purple/brown navigation channel tubes.
