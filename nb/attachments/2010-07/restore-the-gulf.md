# RestoreTheGulf.gov Deepwater Horizon Response Website (`restore-the-gulf.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the U.S. federal government's official Deepwater Horizon oil spill response portal, **RestoreTheGulf.gov**, shortly after its launch in July 2010. Featured in Kurt Schwehr's July 7, 2010 blog post *"New Deepwater Horizon web page"*.

## Header & Navigation Bar
- **Logo:** At the top left, `Restore` in bold olive-green sans-serif text followed by `TheGulf.gov` in thin gray text, with light green coastal marsh grass blades rising behind the word "Restore".
- **Navigation Bar:** A solid olive-green horizontal menu bar containing five white uppercase links separated by vertical pipes:
  `HOME | NEWS | ASSISTANCE | THE RESPONSE | ABOUT`

## Featured Carousel Banner
A large dark teal-blue banner section highlighting slide `3` (indicated by numbered square buttons `1`, `2`, and a yellow-outlined `3` in the lower left):
- **Left Text Column:**
  - Heading: **Health and Safety Information**
  - Caption text: *"Pensacola, Fla. - Workers clean oil from a Pensacola beach on June 23rd. After about two months of containing the oil through skimming and boom, large cleanup efforts were finally necessary on Santa Rosa Island."*
- **Right Photograph:**
  - A beach cleanup scene showing seven workers on white sand near the surf line wearing white protective Tyvek hazmat pants/suits, yellow rubber boots, green nitrile gloves, T-shirts, and sun hats/caps, using long-handled shovels and white trash bags to scoop up oiled sand.
  - Photo credit in the bottom-right corner: `U.S. Air Force photo by Tech. Sgt. Emily F. Alley`.
