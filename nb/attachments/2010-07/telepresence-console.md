# CCOM Telepresence Console During Nautilus and Okeanos Explorer Cruises (`telepresence-console.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of two members of the local education/outreach team seated at the multi-monitor **Telepresence Console** at the University of New Hampshire's Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM), monitoring live deep-sea ROV video feeds from the exploration vessel *E/V Nautilus* (and audio from *NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer*). Featured in Kurt Schwehr's July 2, 2010 blog post *"CCOM Telepresense Console in action"*.

## People & Console Desk
- **Foreground Left Operator:** A woman with shoulder-length white/blonde hair wearing a cream-colored long-sleeved sweater/cardigan, seated at the left side of the console desk with a notebook in front of her, reaching forward with her right hand to press a button on a rack-mounted intercom/audio panel.
- **Foreground Right Operator:** A woman with dark brown hair wearing a dark navy-blue polo shirt with yellow side panels, seated to the right and looking toward the left desktop monitor.
- **Console Desk & Equipment:** A long light-colored desk equipped with black keyboards, mice, open notebooks, angled rack-mounted touchscreens/control panels displaying orange and blue status blocks, and a push-to-talk intercom panel with illuminated buttons.

## Wall & Desktop Displays
- **Upper Wall-Mounted Screens (3 Large Silver-Bezel Displays):**
  1. **Left Large Screen:** Displays a deep blue underwater ROV camera feed with a circular compass rose icon in the upper-left corner and a faint bright spot near the center.
  2. **Center Large Screen:** Displays another deep blue underwater ROV camera view with a compass rose icon in the upper-left and the top of an ROV frame/instrument visible along the bottom edge.
  3. **Right Large Screen:** Shows a 2×2 quad-split video feed of shipboard and control-van cameras, including deck/winch views and equipment bays.
- **Lower Desktop Monitors (4 Black-Bezel LCD Monitors):**
  1. **First Monitor (Left):** Shows the **Nautilus Live** (`NAUTILUS LIVE`) web interface on a blue background with embedded video feeds.
  2. **Second Monitor (Center-Left):** Powered off or displaying a blank black screen.
  3. **Third Monitor (Center-Right):** Also displays the **Nautilus Live** web page, with a small black webcam perched on top of its bezel.
  4. **Fourth Monitor (Far Right):** Displays a dark multi-window control/monitoring interface.
