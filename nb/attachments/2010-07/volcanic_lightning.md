# Eyjafjallajökull Volcanic Ash Plume and Lightning (`volcanic_lightning.gif`)

## Overview
An animated GIF photograph showing a massive, billowy dark gray-blue volcanic ash plume rising above the snow-covered **Eyjafjallajökull** glacier in Iceland against a twilight blue sky, with small flashes of volcanic lightning appearing near the base of the eruption column. Featured in Kurt Schwehr's July 12, 2010 blog post *"ScienceBlogs exodus"* celebrating the geology blog *Highly Allochthonous* moving to its own domain.

## Scene & Landscape Description
- **Volcanic Plume:** A dense, towering, cauliflower-textured ash column erupts from the left side of the ice cap and drifts to the right across the frame, filling most of the middle and right sky in shades of slate gray and dusty blue.
- **Volcanic Lightning:** Within the lower-left base of the dark ash column, just above the white glacier surface, small bright pink/orange/white electrical discharges (volcanic lightning) flicker intermittently across frames of the GIF.
- **Glacier & Mountains:** Below the ash plume stretches a broad, snow- and ice-covered mountain range and glacier cap (Eyjafjallajökull) illuminated in cool blue-white twilight, with dark rocky ridges peeking through the lower slopes.
- **Foreground:** A flat, golden-brown grassy plain and low hill in the foreground with a small cluster of rural Icelandic farm buildings (dark pitched roofs and white walls) situated near the bottom-left/center-left.
