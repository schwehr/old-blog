# ANSS Earthquake Catalog Visualization in Google Earth: South America Map View (`anss-google-earth-southamerica-1.png`)

## Overview
A top-down map view screenshot from Google Earth centered on **South America** and the surrounding Pacific, Atlantic, and Southern Ocean plate boundaries, showing earthquake epicenters/hypocenters (`mag > 4.5`) from the ANSS catalog plotted against a black globe and white coastlines. Featured in Kurt Schwehr's August 19, 2010 blog post *"ANSS quake catalog in Google Earth"*.

## Visualization Design & Encoding
- **Black Background & White Coastlines:** The Earth's surface is masked in solid black, with thin white vector lines showing the coastlines of South America, Central America (upper left), the Caribbean islands (top), and the western coast of Africa (far right) elevated at 700 km altitude.
- **Depth Color Coding:** Circular earthquake markers are colored by depth from white (shallow, ~0–10 km) through pink (intermediate depth) to bright red (deep, ≥200 km).

## Tectonic & Seismic Patterns Visible
- **Andean Subduction Zone (Nazca Plate beneath South America):**
  - A heavy, continuous band of shallow and intermediate earthquakes (white and pink circles) hugs the entire western coastline of South America along the Peru-Chile Trench from Colombia and Ecuador down through Peru and Chile.
  - Further inland (eastward) beneath Peru, Bolivia, northern Argentina, and western Brazil, distinct clusters and linear chains of bright red circles mark deep-focus earthquakes (≥200 km depth) down the subducting Nazca slab.
- **Mid-Ocean Ridges & Transform Boundaries:**
  - **Mid-Atlantic Ridge (Right):** A curving, single-file chain of shallow white earthquake dots traces the Mid-Atlantic Ridge down the right side of the image, equidistant between the eastern bulge of Brazil and western Africa.
  - **East Pacific Rise, Chile Rise, & Galapagos Spreading Center (Left):** Faint lines of shallow white dots trace the boundaries of the Nazca, Cocos, Pacific, and Antarctic plates across the southeastern Pacific Ocean on the left and bottom left.
  - **Scotia Arc / South Sandwich Trench (Bottom Right):** A tight arc of white, pink, and red earthquake markers at the bottom edge southeast of Tierra del Fuego.
  - **Middle America Trench & Caribbean (Top Left):** Dense seismic activity along the Pacific coast of Central America and the Lesser Antilles arc.
