# Yellow Garden Spider (*Argiope aurantia*) on Gravel/Asphalt (`argiope-aurantia-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A top-down close-up photograph of a female yellow garden spider (**Argiope aurantia**) resting on a dark, coarse gravel/asphalt surface. This is the second photograph featured in Kurt Schwehr's August 23, 2010 blog post *"Critters in the yard"*.

## Subject & Scene Description
- **Spider Details:**
  - Positioned near the center-bottom of the frame, facing toward the upper right.
  - **Abdomen:** Large, plump oval abdomen displaying bold bright-yellow lateral bands with black stripes surrounding a central black longitudinal band marked with two pairs of yellow dots.
  - **Cephalothorax:** Covered in short, silvery-white hairs at the anterior end of the abdomen.
  - **Legs:** Eight long legs radiating outward onto the stone aggregate, showing reddish-brown upper segments (femora) transitioning to solid black lower segments.
- **Background Surface:** A dark gray/purplish-black weathered asphalt or crushed-stone driveway/walkway embedded with angular pebbles of white, tan, reddish-brown, and gray rock.
