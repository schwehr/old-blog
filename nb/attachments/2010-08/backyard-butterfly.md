# Backyard Crescent or Fritillary Butterfly (`backyard-butterfly.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of a small orange-and-black patterned butterfly (such as a Pearl Crescent, *Phyciodes tharos*, or similar checkerspot/fritillary species) resting with its wings spread open on a gray painted wooden surface. Featured at the end of Kurt Schwehr's August 23, 2010 blog post *"Critters in the yard"* with the caption *"And for those who don't like spiders..."*.

## Subject & Scene Description
- **Butterfly Appearance:**
  - Viewed from above with both forewings and hindwings fully open, angled slightly toward the lower left.
  - **Wing Pattern:** Bright orange ground color intricately patterned with dark brown/black veins, wavy bands, and a submarginal row of small black spots inside orange crescents/squares, bordered by a dark brown outer margin with tiny white fringe spots at the wingtips.
  - **Body & Antennae:** Dark brownish-gray thorax and head, with two slender, white-and-black ringed antennae extending forward toward the lower left, tipped with small dark clubs.
- **Background:** A flat, muted gray surface with a subtle horizontal wood-grain or brushed texture, and a rough gray concrete/stone ledge along the top edge of the frame.
