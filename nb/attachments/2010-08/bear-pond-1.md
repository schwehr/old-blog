# Morning Mist and Cloud Reflections on Bear Pond (`bear-pond-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A serene early-morning landscape photograph of Bear Pond in Maine, taken from water level at around 6:00 AM during a canoe paddle on August 1, 2010. The image captures calm lake water reflecting a purple-and-pink overcast sky, with a thin layer of morning mist hovering along the forested shoreline and a wooded hill rising on the left.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Water Surface**: The lower two-thirds of the frame are filled by the calm, glassy surface of Bear Pond. Gentle, small-scale ripples texture the left side of the water (caused by either a light breeze or water striders/canoeing wake), while the central and right portions act as a smooth mirror reflecting the soft pastel purples, pinks, and slate blues of the cloud cover overhead.
- **Mist & Shoreline**: A thin, wispy band of white morning mist drifts directly across the water's surface along the far shoreline, partially veiling the base of the trees and small shoreline cabins/docks spaced along the bank.
- **Forested Hills**: Across the middle distance, a dense mixed-conifer and deciduous forest lines the shore in deep hunter green. On the left side, the tree-covered terrain rises into a rounded green hill or low mountain peak, sloping gently downward toward the right side of the frame where more distant rolling ridges appear on the horizon.
- **Sky & Lighting**: The upper third shows a layered stratocumulus cloud deck illuminated by soft pre-sunrise or early-sunrise light, blending lavender, mauve, slate gray, and pale cream openings on the far left and near the right horizon.
