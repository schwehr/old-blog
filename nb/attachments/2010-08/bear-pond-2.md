# Sunrise Over Bear Pond Viewed from a Canoe Bow (`bear-pond-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A first-person photograph taken from inside a canoe looking over its bow toward the rising sun across Bear Pond in Maine on the morning of August 1, 2010. Golden sunlight breaks through a textured layer of clouds above a dark, tree-lined horizon and reflects off the misty,rippled lake surface.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Canoe Bow)**: Positioned in the lower center of the frame is the pointed cream/off-white bow of a canoe, angled slightly to the left of dead center. The molded plastic or fiberglass deck plate, aluminum or vinyl gunwales, and a dark painter line (bow rope) looped through a hole in the bow deck and resting inside the hull are clearly visible.
- **Water & Mist**: The calm lake water stretches from the foreground to the far shore. A diffuse layer of white morning mist hangs just above the water along the distant shoreline. Directly ahead of the canoe bow, bright golden and amber reflections of the rising sun shimmer across gentle horizontal ripples, while the surrounding water reflects cool slate-gray and blue-gray tones.
- **Shoreline Silhouette**: A continuous dark silhouette of tall pines and mixed forest spans the horizon from left to right, dipping slightly in the center where low hills meet the sky.
- **Sky & Crepuscular Rays**: The upper half of the frame features a dramatic sky filled with dappled gray and purple-gray clouds. Just above the central dip in the treeline, brilliant golden-white sunlight breaks through gaps in the clouds, casting faint downward crepuscular rays toward the mist-covered lake.
