# Canoe Bow Facing Twin Wooded Hills on Bear Pond (`bear-pond-4.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken from a canoe on Bear Pond in Maine on the morning of August 1, 2010, showing the front section of the canoe pointing across mirror-like water toward two forested hills under a blue sky scattered with pink-tinted morning clouds.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Canoe Interior & Bow)**: Extending from the bottom edge into the lower-left center of the frame is the tan/cream interior hull of a canoe. Prominent details include:
  - A molded cream-colored bow seat mounted between the gunwales.
  - Coiled dark-green or black rope resting on the floor of the hull in front of and beneath the bow seat, tied through the bow eye at the pointed tip.
  - Beveled gunwales with small black screw/rivet heads along the top edge.
- **Lake Surface & Reflections**: The water of Bear Pond is exceptionally still and glassy, creating a crisp inverted reflection of the dark green forested hills, the blue sky, and the scattered clouds directly on the water surface. A faint, thin veil of mist lingers near the far shoreline, where a small yellow/black floating swim raft and a couple of moored boats are barely visible at the base of the trees on the right.
- **Background Hills & Forest**: Two prominent, rounded, densely forested green hills rise on the left and right sides of the horizon, separated by a lower saddle in the center. Tall pine trees frame the right edge of the image.
- **Sky**: A bright azure blue morning sky occupies the upper half of the frame, dotted with small, puffy cumulus and altocumulus clouds shaded in slate gray with warm rose and peach highlights on their undersides.
