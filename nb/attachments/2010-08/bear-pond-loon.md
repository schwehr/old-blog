# Common Loon Swimming in Morning Mist on Bear Pond (`bear-pond-loon.jpg`)

## Overview
A wildlife and landscape photograph capturing a Common Loon (*Gavia immer*) swimming across the misty surface of Bear Pond in Maine during an early-morning canoe paddle on August 1, 2010, backed by a dense stand of tall lakeside pine trees and cottages.

## Scene Description
- **Subject (Common Loon)**: Positioned near the center of the frame, slightly below the mist line, a Common Loon swims in profile facing right. Its distinctive dark black head, pointed bill, and white neck collar marking are visible above the water, leaving a long, faint V-shaped wake trailing behind it to the left.
- **Water Surface & Mist**: The foreground water is a muted olive-gray and slate-green color, speckled with tiny dimples and ripples (noted by the author as tens of thousands of water striders/skaters on the surface). Across the middle distance, a thick horizontal band of white morning fog hovers over the water, softening the reflections of the shoreline trees.
- **Shoreline & Forest**: Behind the mist rises a lush, dense wall of tall eastern white pines and mixed conifers in vibrant emerald and forest green. Nestled among the tree trunks along the water's edge on the right and left are several lakeside cottages, camps, and docks, along with a few small white motorboats moored along the bank.
- **Background Hills & Sky**: A tree-covered hill rises in the left background and higher ridges ascend toward the upper right, beneath a soft, overcast lavender-gray morning sky.
