# ESCI 401 Plate Boundaries Lab in Google Earth (`lab01.png`)

## Overview
A composite screenshot illustrating the Google Earth visualization created by Kurt Schwehr and Margaret Boettcher for the University of New Hampshire (UNH) ESCI 401 course ("Lab 1 - A Plate Tectonics Jigsaw Puzzle") in August 2010. The image combines a left-hand view showing the Google Earth sidebar and 3D earthquake/volcano distribution over South America against a black globe, and a right-hand view displaying global seafloor age and plate boundaries over the Atlantic Ocean.

## Interface & Left Panel (`Places` Sidebar)
- **Search Panel (Top Left)**: Standard Google Earth search pane with tabs `Fly To` (active), `Find Businesses`, and `Directions`, and an input box labeled `Fly to e.g., 1600 Pennsylvania Ave, 20006`.
- **Places Tree (Middle Left)**: Displays the hierarchical KML structure of the lab inside `Temporary Places`:
  - `ESCI 401 Lab 01` (checked, expanded)
    - `Earthquakes` (checked)
    - `Volcanoes` (checked, highlighted in light blue)
    - `Earth Backgrounds` (checked, expanded)
      - `Blank globe` (radio button selected)
      - `Sea Floor Ages` (radio button unselected)
      - `Topography` (radio button unselected)
    - `Extra Files` (unchecked, collapsed)
    - `Credits` (checked)
- **Layers Panel (Bottom Left)**: Collapsed at the bottom with an `Earth Gallery >>` button.

## Center 3D Globe View (South America Subduction Zone)
- **Visual Style**: The globe is wrapped in a solid black background (`Blank globe`) with orange-brown continental outlines (World Coast Line) and gray/white plate boundary lines suspended at 700 km altitude.
- **Earthquakes & Volcanoes**: Viewed looking northward along the western margin of South America (Andes) up into Central America and the Caribbean:
  - **Volcanoes**: Plotted as bright red-and-yellow erupting volcano cone icons aligned along the Andean volcanic arc, Central America, and the Lesser Antilles, plus oceanic hotspots (such as Galápagos and Easter Island/Juan Fernández).
  - **Earthquakes**: Plotted as circular markers color-coded by depth (from white/light blue at shallow depths near the trench to deeper royal blue descending eastward beneath the continent, clearly delineating the subducting Nazca plate slab).
- **Status Bar**: Bottom coordinates read `22°30'59.54" S  76°40'48.78" W  elev -4267 m  Eye alt 5929.48 km`, with credits for `Image © 2010 TerraMetrics`, `Image © 2010 DigitalGlobe`, `Data SIO, NOAA, U.S. Navy, NGA, GEBCO`.

## Right 3D Globe View (Seafloor Age Overlay)
- **Color Scale Legend (Top Left of Right Pane)**: A horizontal rainbow color barranging from `0` (red) through orange (`40`), yellow/green (`80`–`120`), cyan (`160`), blue (`200`–`240`), to magenta (`280`), titled **`Age of the Sea Floor (million years)`**.
- **Globe Visualization**: Centered on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge between South America and Africa (`1°07'41.49" N  28°39'09.11" W  elev -4096 m  Eye alt 19101.64 km`). White lines trace the plate boundaries along the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, Caribbean, and Scotia plates, surrounded by symmetrical bands of young (red/orange) to old (green/blue) oceanic crust and shaded continental topography.
