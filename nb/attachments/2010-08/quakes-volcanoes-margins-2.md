# Earthquakes, Volcanoes, and Plate Margins: Central America and the Caribbean (`quakes-volcanoes-margins-2.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth visualization screenshot displaying earthquake hypocenters, Holocene volcanoes, coastlines, and tectonic plate boundaries across Mexico, Central America, the Caribbean Sea, the eastern Pacific Ocean (Cocos, Nazca, and Pacific plates), and northwestern South America.

## Map / Visualization Details
- **Base & Coastlines**: A solid black globe backdrop with white vector coastlines outlining Baja California, mainland Mexico, the Gulf of Mexico, Florida, the Yucatán Peninsula, Central America, Cuba, Hispaniola, Puerto Rico, the Lesser Antilles, and northern South America (Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador).
- **Plate Boundaries**:
  - **Orange lines (Divergent / Spreading Ridges)**: Trace the East Pacific Rise running southward from the Gulf of California down the left side of the frame, and the Galápagos Spreading Center branching eastward toward South America.
  - **Yellow lines (Convergent / Subduction Zones)**: Trace the Middle America Trench along the southwestern coast of Mexico and Central America, the Colombia-Ecuador Trench along northwestern South America, the Lesser Antilles subduction zone on the eastern edge of the Caribbean Plate, and the North Panama Deformed Belt.
  - **Green & Purple lines (Transform / Complex Boundaries)**: Trace the Cayman Trough / Swan Islands and Septentrional-Oriente fault zones along the northern Caribbean boundary, transform fracture zones offsetting the East Pacific Rise and Panama Fracture Zone, and the Gulf of California transform-rift system.
- **Earthquakes & Volcanoes**:
  - Dense bands of white (shallow) to blue (intermediate/deep) circular earthquake markers follow the Middle America Trench, dipping northeastward beneath Mexico and Central America, as well as descending beneath the Lesser Antilles and the northern Andes.
  - Chains of red-orange volcano icons line the Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt, the Central American Volcanic Arc, the Lesser Antilles island arc, the northern Andes of Colombia and Ecuador, and the Galápagos Islands cluster in the bottom center-left.
