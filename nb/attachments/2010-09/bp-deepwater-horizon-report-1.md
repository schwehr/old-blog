# BP Deepwater Horizon Accident Investigation Report: Eight Barriers Were Breached (`bp-deepwater-horizon-report-1.png`)

## Overview
A presentation slide/diagram from BP's September 2010 *Deepwater Horizon Accident Investigation Report* titled **"Eight Barriers Were Breached"**, illustrating a cross-section of the Macondo well and *Deepwater Horizon* rig on the left alongside a numbered list of the eight physical and operational barriers that failed on April 20, 2010.

## Diagram & Visual Layout
- **Header**: Green text at the top left reads `Deepwater Horizon Accident Investigation Report`, with the green-and-yellow BP Helios logo in the top right corner. Below it is the main bold black heading: **`Eight Barriers Were Breached`**.
- **Left Wellbore & Rig Cross-Section**:
  - Shows the semi-submersible *Deepwater Horizon* rig floating on the sea surface, connected via a **`Riser`** through the light-blue water column down to the yellow **`BOP`** (Blowout Preventer) sitting on the **`Sea Floor`**.
  - Below the seafloor (tan/brown subsurface), the **`Casing`** extends downward into the yellow-and-cyan layered **`Reservoir`** at the bottom.
  - Eight numbered white ovals (`1` through `8`) are placed at their corresponding physical locations:
    - `(1)` and `(2)` at the bottom of the well inside the **`Reservoir`** and shoe track.
    - `(8)` at the **`BOP`** just above the **`Sea Floor`**.
    - `(4)` along the **`Riser`** in the water column.
    - `(3)`, `(5)`, `(6)`, and `(7)` clustered on the *Deepwater Horizon* rig platform at the surface.

## Visible Text & Numbered Findings (Right Column)
- **Well integrity was not established or failed**
  - `(1)` `Annulus cement barrier did not isolate hydrocarbons`
  - `(2)` `Shoe track barriers did not isolate hydrocarbons`
- **Hydrocarbons entered the well undetected and well control was lost**
  - `(3)` `Negative pressure test was accepted although well integrity had not been established`
  - `(4)` `Influx was not recognized until hydrocarbons were in riser`
  - `(5)` `Well control response actions failed to regain control of well`
- **Hydrocarbons ignited on the *Deepwater Horizon***
  - `(6)` `Diversion to mud gas separator resulted in gas venting onto rig`
  - `(7)` `Fire and gas system did not prevent hydrocarbon ignition`
- **Blowout preventer did not seal the well**
  - `(8)` `Blowout preventer (BOP) emergency modes did not seal well`
