# BP Deepwater Horizon Report Slide 11: Key Finding #2 – Shoe Track Mechanical Barriers (`bp-deepwater-horizon-report-2.png`)

## Overview
Slide 11 from BP's September 2010 *Deepwater Horizon Accident Investigation Report* presentation, titled **"Key Finding #2: The shoe track mechanical barriers did not isolate the hydrocarbons"**, showing a full wellbore overview on the left, a zoomed-in cutaway diagram of the bottomhole shoe track assembly in the center, and explanatory text on the right.

## Diagram Details (Left & Center)
- **Full Well Schematic (Left)**: Shows the *Deepwater Horizon* rig at the top, `Riser`, `BOP`, `Sea Floor`, `Casing`, and `Reservoir` at the bottom, with a gray callout cone expanding from the bottom of the casing in the reservoir to the detailed central cutaway.
- **Shoe Track Cutaway (Center)**:
  - Depicts the bottom section of the well casing passing through yellow-dotted hydrocarbon-bearing reservoir sands.
  - **Labels & Components**:
    - `Hydrocarbon Flow Path` (white box pointing to a thick red arrow rising up the center of the casing)
    - `Float Collar` (housing two internal flapper/plunger valves)
    - `Check Valves` (two arrows pointing to the upper and lower check valves inside the float collar)
    - `Shoe track cement` (porous gray-and-white speckled cement column below the float collar)
    - `Centralizers` (red bow-spring centralizer brackets along the exterior of the casing in the annulus)
    - `Flow Ports` (three black arrows at the very bottom of the reamer shoe)
  - **Flow Path**: A bright red line with arrows traces hydrocarbons entering from the lower yellow reservoir zone into the annulus, passing around the bottom of the casing through the `Flow Ports`, and traveling straight up through the contaminated `Shoe track cement` and unsealed `Check Valves` in the `Float Collar` into the main casing above.

## Visible Text & Bullet Points (Right Column)
- **Header**:
  - **`Key Finding #2`** (underlined, dark gray)
  - **`The shoe track mechanical barriers did not isolate the hydrocarbons`**
- **Gray Summary Callout Box (White Bold Italic Text)**:
  > *Tail cement is displaced down the casing into the shoe track. The tail cement is designed to prevent flow from the annulus into the casing. The float collar valves, which provide a second barrier, must close and seal to prevent flow up the casing.*
- **Bullet Points**:
  - `Shoe track had two types of mechanical barriers: cement in the shoe track and the double check valves in the float collar`
  - `Shoe track cement failed to act as a barrier due to contamination of the base slurry by break out of nitrogen from the foam slurry`
  - `Hydrocarbon influx was able to bypass the float collar check valves due to either:`
    - `Valves failed to convert or`
    - `Valves failed to seal`
  - `Flow through shoe confirmed by fluid modeling and Macondo static kill data`
- **Footer**: `Deepwater Horizon Accident Investigation   11`
