# CCOM/JHC Seminar Poster: Environmental Response Management Application (ERMA) by Kurt Schwehr (`ccom-seminar-schwehr-erma.png`)

## Overview
A promotional seminar poster designed by Colleen Mitchell for Kurt Schwehr's presentation in the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / NOAA-UNH Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC) Seminar Series on Friday, September 24, 2010, titled *"Environmental Response Management Application: from Portsmouth Response to NOAA's GeoPlatform Gulf Response"*.

## Visual Design & Photography
- **Full-Bleed Background Image**: A dramatic photograph of the burning *Deepwater Horizon* offshore drilling rig engulfed in towering plumes of dark black smoke and bright orange flames on the left, while an offshore supply/firefighting vessel in the lower right sprays a stream of water toward the blaze.
- **Left Vertical Filmstrip (Five Inset Photographs)**: A semi-transparent vertical strip along the left side displays five framed aerial and surface photographs from the Deepwater Horizon oil spill response:
  1. **Top**: Thick black smoke billowing from an in-situ controlled burn of corralled surface oil, with a secondary smoke plume rising in the distance.
  2. **Second from top**: Vertical aerial view of a white-and-green response vessel skimming or spraying along dark reddish-brown emulsified oil slicks snaking across turquoise-green water.
  3. **Middle**: Aerial view of a twin-engine response aircraft (white fuselage with red-and-blue wing markings) banking low over shimmering oil sheens on the ocean surface, trailing white spray/dispersant plumes.
  4. **Fourth from top**: Aerial view of three small white skimmer/towing boats pulling containment booms through dark, swirling brown and orange oil slicks.
  5. **Bottom**: Overhead shot of a tugboat/response vessel cutting a white frothy wake through rainbow-iridescent and metallic-blue oil sheen on the water.
