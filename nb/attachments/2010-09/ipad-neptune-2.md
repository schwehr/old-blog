# NEPTUNE Canada iPad App Sensor List (`ipad-neptune-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **NEPTUNE Canada** iPad application showing the main "Observatory" view, which lists oceanographic sensors and instruments grouped by seafloor observatory node locations (**Barkley Canyon** and **Folger Passage**).

## Interface & Controls
- **Status Bar (Top)**: Black bar showing `iPad` with Wi-Fi icon on the left, `7:49 AM` in the center, and `100%` battery on the right.
- **Navigation Bar**: Blue-gray gradient iOS toolbar containing a centered segmented toggle control with two buttons: **`All Devices`** (selected, dark blue) and **`Favourites`** (unselected, lighter blue-gray). A circular reload arrow button sits at the far right.
- **List Layout**: White table rows separated by light gray horizontal lines. Each row has a light gray star icon on the left (for favoriting), a bold black instrument name, a smaller gray `ID:` number underneath, and a right-pointing disclosure chevron (`>`) on the far right.
- **Bottom Tab Bar**: Black bar with three icons:
  - **`Observatory`** (selected, highlighted with a blue glowing seafloor node/starburst icon)
  - **`Media`** (gray television/video monitor icon)
  - **`Info`** (gray circular `i` information icon)
