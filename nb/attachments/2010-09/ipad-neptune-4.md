# NEPTUNE Canada iPad App Seafloor Video Feed (`ipad-neptune-4.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **NEPTUNE Canada** iPad application playing a monochrome underwater video clip from a seafloor observatory camera, showing the muddy ocean bottom, a benthic cable or instrument frame rod, a sea urchin, and small reflective organisms.

## Interface & Controls
- **Status Bar & Top Bar**:
  - Top status bar showing `iPad`, Wi-Fi icon, `7:50 AM`, and `100%` battery.
  - Translucent dark overlay bar directly below with a blue pill-shaped **`Done`** button in the upper-left corner.
- **Bottom Video Playback HUD**:
  - Centered translucent black rounded control panel near the bottom of the screen.
  - Top row of icons (left to right): 30-second rewind circular arrow (`30`), pause button (`||`), fast-forward/skip-to-end button (`>>|`), and exit-fullscreen diagonal inward arrows.
  - Bottom row: Horizontal scrubber bar filled in blue up to about 85% of the clip duration, with a white circular thumb handle.
