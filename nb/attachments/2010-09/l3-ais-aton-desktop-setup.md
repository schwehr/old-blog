# L3 AIS Aid-to-Navigation (AtoN) Desktop Bench Setup (`l3-ais-aton-desktop-setup.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a laboratory desk setup used to test an **L3 Desktop AtoN** (Automatic Identification System Aid to Navigation) transponder unit alongside an older black CNS6000 transponder, a Mac Mini, a USB-to-serial hub, and an IBM ThinkPad laptop.

## Scene & Hardware Components
- **Center Foreground — L3 Desktop AtoN**:
  - A bright yellow rectangular metal enclosure with a white top label and a brushed aluminum front panel.
  - Top label features a red circle containing white **`L3`** lettering above **`DESKTOP ATON`**.
  - Along the bottom edge of the top label, four port labels correspond to the connectors on the front panel (left to right):
    1. **`RS-232 I/O`**: Connected via a circular multi-pin plug wrapped in red-striped clear spiral cable protector, splitting into three black cables routed to three DB-9 serial adapters.
    2. **`GPS ANT`**: Connected to a black coaxial cable with a BNC/TNC connector.
    3. **`VHF ANT`**: Connected to a black coaxial cable with a large silver UHF/N-type connector.
    4. **`DC POWER`**: Connected to a black barrel power plug with an illuminated green LED indicator directly above it.
- **Right Foreground — Black Transponder / Controller Unit**:
  - A matte black rectangular rack/desktop box (CNS6000 Class A transponder with Blue Force firmware).
  - Its front panel has a green status LED on the left, a glowing blue backlit LCD screen in the middle, and a five-button blue backlit directional keypad (up, down, left, right, center, plus a small lower-left key) on the right.
- **Left Foreground — Small Black Radio/Modem Box**:
  - A small black metal box at the far left edge of the desk with a vertical black whip antenna and two front coaxial connectors with thin black and red wires trailing off the front edge of the desk.
- **Left Background — Mac Mini & Serial Hub**:
  - A silver-and-white Apple **Mac Mini** sits behind the yellow L3 box.
  - Resting on top of the Mac Mini is a black **Keyspan** (or similar) multi-port USB-to-serial hub with four beige DB-9 serial cables plugged into its front ports, coiled neatly on the desk between the Mac Mini and the L3 unit.
- **Right Background — IBM ThinkPad Laptop**:
  - A black **IBM ThinkPad** laptop sits open on the back right of the desk with its black AC power brick to its left.
  - The screen displays a classic blue Windows XP desktop with icons on the left, white `ThinkPad` wallpaper text on the right, and an open configuration or terminal application window in the lower right.
