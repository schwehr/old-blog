# MIT AUV Lab Reef Explorer (Rex) V2 in UNH Engineering Tank (`mit-auv-lab.jpg`)

## Overview
An underwater photograph taken through a viewing window into the deep test tank at the University of New Hampshire's Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory, showing the MIT AUV Lab's **Reef Explorer (Rex) Version 2.0** surface-tethered autonomous underwater vehicle hovering mid-water above artificial plants and a second vehicle/basket on the tank floor.

## Scene & Vehicle Description
- **Reef Explorer (Rex) V2 AUV (Upper Left / Center-Left)**:
  - A compact, boxy white-framed underwater vehicle suspended mid-water in the bright blue tank.
  - **Front Camera Dome**: A large circular optical viewport with a metallic rim sits prominently on the front face of the white housing, housing an internal camera lens.
  - **Thrusters**: Two shrouded 3-blade propellers in yellow/green cylindrical ducts extend horizontally from the left and right sides of the body on metal brackets, with yellow power cables routed into the main frame.
  - **Top Tether Spool**: Mounted inside the upper cutout of the white frame is a black reel wound with bright yellow tether line, with a vertical guide arm extending above the vehicle.
  - **Lower Cylinder**: A dark gray cylindrical pressure housing is mounted horizontally beneath the main white body frame.
- **Tank Environment & Floor Objects**:
  - **Walls & Water**: Smooth turquoise-blue painted tank walls and floor, illuminated by overhead lighting that casts soft diagonal light rays and shadows across the background wall. The dark diagonal frame of the observation window is visible in the bottom-left corner.
  - **Artificial Kelp / Seaweed**: A long vertical strand of plastic aquatic plants rises through the center of the frame from a clear square plexiglass base on the tank floor, where several bushier artificial plants are anchored. Another short piece of artificial kelp hangs near the top-right edge.
  - **Second Vehicle / Cage on Floor (Bottom Right)**: Resting on the tank bottom to the right is a boat-hull-shaped white base topped with a black arched mesh cage enclosing green and red spherical floats.
  - **Target Panel (Far Right)**: A rectangular blue-and-green flat panel leans vertically against the back right wall of the tank.
