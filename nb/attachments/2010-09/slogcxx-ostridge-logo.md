# `slogcxx` Ostrich Logo (`slogcxx-ostridge-logo.png`)

## Overview
A humorous, color-inverted close-up image of an ostrich face staring directly into the camera lens, used by Kurt Schwehr as the project logo for **`slogcxx`** (his C++ logging library hosted on GitHub).

## Visual Details
- **Subject & Composition**: An extreme wide-angle close-up of an ostrich's head filling almost the entire rectangular frame, bordered by a thin black inner frame and white outer edge.
- **Color Palette (Negative/Inverted Effect)**: The image has a solarized or color-inverted appearance:
  - The background sky at the top corners is pitch black, while the lower corners show a pale lavender-blue tint.
  - The ostrich's fluffy head feathers appear bright silver-gray and white.
  - The large circular eyes on either side of the beak glow a vivid icy cyan-blue with soft purple-gray centers (pupils).
  - The wide, flat beak extending across the lower center is dark charcoal gray along its upper bridge and nostrils, transitioning to pale gray and white along the mouth line.
