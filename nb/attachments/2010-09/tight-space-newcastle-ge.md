# Google Earth Ruler Measurement of Boat Berths in New Castle, NH (`tight-space-newcastle-ge.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot from **Google Earth** on Mac OS X showing an aerial satellite view of a marina and boat slips in New Castle, New Hampshire, with the **Ruler** dialog box measuring a `7.55 Meters` (`200.63 degrees`) distance between two adjacent boat berths to illustrate why a 17-meter coordinate grid resolution in IMO Circular 289 AIS Area Notices is too coarse to distinguish neighboring slips.

## Map & Satellite Scene
- **Marina & Shoreline**:
  - The lower left and bottom center show rows of white recreational boats moored in finger slips extending into dark harbor water.
  - A narrow yellow measurement line with two bright red square endpoints (`7.55 Meters` long) is drawn vertically across a small boat slip near the bottom center (`Heading: 200.63 degrees`).
  - Tree-lined residential shoreline with light gray house roofs and a paved road appears in the upper left, and a rocky/sandy beach with trees appears in the lower right.

## Interface & Ruler Dialog
- **Window Position**: Positioned in the upper right quadrant of the screenshot with standard Mac OS X window controls (three gray traffic-light buttons on the left) and title **`Ruler`**.
- **Tabs**: Segmented tab control at the top center with **`Line`** selected (highlighted in blue) and **`Path`** unselected.
- **Measurement Readouts**:
  - **`Length:`** `7.55` with a dropdown menu set to **`Meters`** (with blue up/down selector arrows).
  - **`Heading:`** `200.63 degrees`
- **Controls (Bottom Row)**:
  - Checked blue checkbox labeled **`Mouse Navigation`** on the left.
  - Rounded **`Save`** and **`Clear`** buttons on the right.
