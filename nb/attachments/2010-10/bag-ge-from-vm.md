# BAG Visualization in Google Earth Generated from a Virtual Machine (`bag-ge-from-vm.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth displaying a 3D oblique view of a Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) survey (`H12059_2m_MLLW_4of6.bag`) with an open KML information balloon. The visualization was generated inside a Fedora 13 Linux virtual machine using `bag-py` and GDAL during Kurt Schwehr's technology transfer visit to NOAA's National Geophysical Data Center (NGDC).

## Map & Visualization Details
- **Background & Perspective**: An oblique, low-angle view in Google Earth looking across a blue ocean surface toward a dark horizon band at the top.
- **Survey Bounding Box**: A thin white rectangular outline on the water surface marks the geographic bounding box of the survey area.
- **Bathymetric Grid Overlay**: Inside the left half of the frame, a purple-and-blue shaded relief overlay represents the multibeam bathymetry of survey `H12059_2m_MLLW_4of6.bag`, showing parallel northeast-southwest survey tracklines and crosslines.
- **Placemark Icon**: Near the center of the bathymetric overlay is a small square rainbow-colored grid icon labeled `H12059...` in white text, from which the information balloon extends to the right.
