# Boston College Geology Field Trip Van with Geologic Maps (`bc-field-trip-0.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken at the start of the Boston College 2010 Eastern Section Seismological Society of America (ES-SSA) field trip, *"Fault-related rocks in eastern Massachusetts,"* led by Yvette Kuiper. The image shows geologic maps and handwritten notes magnet-mounted to the side of a maroon Boston College passenger van surrounded by autumn foliage.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Left**: Field trip leader Yvette Kuiper stands in profile facing right, wearing eyeglasses, a dark forest-green hooded sweatshirt, and her blonde hair tied back in a ponytail with a patterned brown scrunchie. She is looking toward the charts attached to the van.
- **Vehicles**:
  - **Primary Van**: A dark maroon/burgundy full-size passenger van occupying the left and center of the frame. White lettering along the side panel below the windows reads: `Boston College Geology & Geophysics`.
  - **Second Van**: Behind and to the right of the primary van is the front end of a second red Ford Econoline van parked facing forward, displaying a Massachusetts license plate (`13975`) on its front bumper.
- **Display Charts & Maps**: Secured to the side of the maroon van with colorful round magnets (red, blue, green, yellow, and white):
  - **Left Sheet**: A large sheet of brown kraft paper with hand-drawn black marker diagrams, arrows, and geological notes.
  - **Center-Left Poster**: A white printed geological map showing fault zones and terranes in eastern Massachusetts (shaded in gray and dark blue-gray), titled with a legend in the upper left and labeled `Nashoba Terrane` and `Southeastern New England Composite Avalon Terrane`.
  - **Center-Right Poster**: A brightly colored geological/aeromagnetic or tectonic map in yellow, red, orange, and blue tones over the side window.
  - **Right Posters**: Two stacked white sheets on the rear quarter panel containing cross-sections, evolutionary tectonic diagrams, and explanatory text blocks.
- **Background**: Tall deciduous trees in peak autumn foliage with vibrant orange, rust-red, and golden-yellow leaves.
