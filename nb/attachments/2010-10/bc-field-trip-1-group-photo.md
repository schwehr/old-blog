# Boston College 2010 ES-SSA Field Trip Group Photo (`bc-field-trip-1-group-photo.jpg`)

## Overview
A full-group outdoor portrait of approximately 19 geologists and seismologists attending the Boston College 2010 ES-SSA field trip (*"Fault-related rocks in eastern Massachusetts"*), posing in front of a weathered rock outcrop on a sunny autumn day.

## Scene & Group Composition
- **Setting**: The group stands on leaf-strewn ground at the base of a rugged, brownish-tan rock cliff face framed by small trees and autumn brush on the left and right. Most participants wear white conference name badges on lanyards around their necks and carry field trip guidebooks or canvas tote bags.
- **Left Group (Standing, Left to Right)**:
  - A man in a bright red jacket over a dark shirt and khaki pants, wearing a backpack.
  - A man in a tan cap, dark fleece jacket, and blue jeans.
  - A bearded man in an olive-green zip-up jacket and blue jeans.
- **Front Kneeling Row**:
  - Field trip leader **Yvette Kuiper** kneeling on the left in a dark green sweatshirt, tan multi-pocket field vest, khaki pants, and hiking boots.
  - A man kneeling to her right wearing an orange cap backward, eyeglasses, a gray hooded sweatshirt, and khaki cargo shorts.
- **Center & Right Group (Front/Middle Standing Row, Left to Right)**:
  - A man in sunglasses, a maroon hooded sweatshirt, and khaki pants.
  - **Margaret Boettcher** in an orange jacket over a black top and dark pants, smiling at the camera.
  - A man in a white cap, teal/green open jacket over a light shirt, and blue jeans.
  - In front of him, a woman with curly brown hair and sunglasses wearing a red scarf, a striped burgundy sweater, blue jeans, and holding a white canvas tote bag.
  - A woman in a black hooded sweatshirt with bright green trim and dark jeans, holding white printed handout pages.

  - On the far right, a man in a dark navy jacket over a red t-shirt and blue jeans, holding a white canvas tote bag with maroon straps.
