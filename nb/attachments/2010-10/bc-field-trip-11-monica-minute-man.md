# Monica Wolfson with a Minuteman Reenactor at the Old North Bridge (`bc-field-trip-11-monica-minute-man.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph of Monica Wolfson posing with a costumed Revolutionary War Minuteman reenactor next to the Old North Bridge in Concord, Massachusetts, during the Boston College 2010 ES-SSA geology field trip.

## Scene Description
- **Subjects (Foreground Left & Center)**:
  - **Minuteman Reenactor (Left)**: A bearded man smiling warmly at the camera, dressed in 18th-century colonial militia attire: a black three-cornered tricorn hat, a tan/taupe buttoned wool coat over a white collared shirt, crossed white canvas and brown leather shoulder straps, a brass crescent gorget hanging from a cord around his neck, and holding the wooden stock and brass-fitted barrel of a flintlock musket upright in his right hand.
  - **Monica Wolfson (Center)**: Standing beside the reenactor and smiling at the camera, wearing a dark navy-blue hooded sweatshirt with a white circular graphic logo on the left chest reading `PUGET SOUND ... SEATTLE, WA USA`.
- **Background**:
  - **Right**: The wooden pedestrian span of the Old North Bridge stretches into the background, bordered by heavy timber railings. Around a dozen visitors and tourists (including a parent carrying an infant in a front carrier) are walking across the sunlit wooden planks in both directions.
  - **Left**: The calm, rippling blue water of the Concord River bordered by grassy banks, a low stone wall near the bridge abutment, and tall deciduous trees with green and early golden-orange autumn foliage under a bright sky.
