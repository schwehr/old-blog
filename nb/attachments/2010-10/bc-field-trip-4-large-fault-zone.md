# Yvette Kuiper Explaining a Large Fault Zone Outcrop (`bc-field-trip-4-large-fault-zone.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide field photograph from the Boston College 2010 ES-SSA geology field trip showing leader Yvette Kuiper gesturing toward a massive, iron-stained orange-brown rock cliff representing a large fault zone in eastern Massachusetts.

## Scene Description
- **Rock Outcrop**: Dominating the center of the frame is a tall, craggy rock face weathered to a rich rust-orange and golden-brown color. Steeply dipping fractures and fault planes cut vertically and diagonally across the massive outcrop.
- **People**:
  - **Speaker**: In the lower right at the base of the cliff, Yvette Kuiper (wearing a dark green shirt, tan field vest, khaki pants, and lanyard badge) faces the group with her right arm extended upward, pointing toward the upper structure of the fault zone.
  - **Observers (Foreground, Left to Right)**: Four field trip participants stand with their backs to the camera looking up at the outcrop:
    1. Far left: A man in a dark navy-blue jacket next to a light gray angular boulder.
    2. Center-left: A person with curly reddish-brown hair in a bright pink/red jacket.
    3. Center-right: A person with brown hair in a brown jacket.
    4. Right: A man in a blue jacket and dark cap, and on the far right, a man in a dark wide-brimmed hat and olive-gray jacket.
- **Vegetation**: Framing the outcrop are slender bare saplings in the center-right foreground, bright yellow-green autumn leaves on tree branches at the left, pine needles behind the left side of the rock, and deep crimson-red shrubs crowning the top of the cliff.
