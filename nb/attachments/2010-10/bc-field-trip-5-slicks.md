# Examining Slickensides on an Outcrop (`bc-field-trip-5-slicks.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph from the Boston College 2010 ES-SSA field trip showing Yvette Kuiper and a group of geologists gathered at the base of a sunlit, fractured rock face containing fault slickensides.

## Scene Description
- **Outcrop & Geological Features**:
  - A steep, blocky gray rock face rises on the left and center, with planar fracture surfaces stained rusty orange-brown by iron oxide weathering.
  - Near the center of the image, just to the left of Yvette Kuiper, a blue-handled Estwing rock hammer rests vertically against the light gray rock ledge for scale/reference.
- **People**:
  - **Yvette Kuiper** stands near the center facing the group, wearing her tan field vest over a dark green hooded top and lanyard badge, gesturing with both hands as she explains the fault kinematics.
  - **Left Foreground**: A man in a bright red jacket and khaki pants with a black backpack stands looking toward the speaker.
  - **Center Foreground (Backs to Camera)**:
    - A woman in a blue denim jacket.
    - A woman in a black hoodie with a bright lime-green hood lining and green undershirt over dark jeans.
    - A person in a bright coral-orange/salmon jacket with tree branch shadows cast across their back.
  - **Right Group**:
    - A man in a black cap and light gray/blue jacket leans forward with his right hand extended toward a slickensided surface on the lower rock face.
    - Behind him stands a young man in glasses and a red vest/shirt, a man in a brown jacket, and in the lower-right foreground, two women with long hair (one blonde, one curly brown hair in a navy sweatshirt) and a man in a black hat and gray jacket.
- **Foliage**: Autumn sumac leaves in green and reddish-pink tones appear in the lower-left foreground, with bare branches and golden fall trees visible in the upper right against a blue sky.
