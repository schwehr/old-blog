# Field Trip Group Examining a Steep Roadside Rock Cut (`bc-field-trip-9.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken along a highway shoulder during the Boston College 2010 ES-SSA field trip in eastern Massachusetts, showing participants gathered along the base of a towering, dark gray metamorphic roadcut draped with bright red autumn vines.

## Scene Description
- **Roadcut Outcrop (Right Two-Thirds)**:
  - A tall, jagged, steeply dipping dark gray rock wall rises from the grassy roadside ditch on the right side of the frame.
  - Cascading down the upper-middle section of the dark gray cliff are vibrant crimson-red leaves of Virginia creeper or poison ivy vines, contrasting sharply against the charcoal-gray rock.
  - Near the base of the cliff in the middle distance, a rectangular patch of reddish-brown paint covers an area of the rock (likely covering graffiti).
- **Field Trip Participants (Left & Center-Left)**:
  - **Foreground Left**: A cluster of men standing on the gravel shoulder between the white painted edge line of the paved road and the grassy ditch:
    - Closest to the camera in profile facing right is a man wearing a tan baseball cap, black fleece pullover, blue jeans, and a beige camera/gear pouch at his hip, holding a small notebook or camera.
    - Just behind him to the left stand three men looking up at the high cliff face: one in a tan wide-brimmed hat, gray long-sleeved shirt, and khaki pants; one in a white cap and red jacket; and one partially visible in a dark red top.
    - Slightly further back stand two men in olive-green and gray jackets.
  - **Background Center-Left**: A second cluster of about six participants stands closer to the rock face (including people in a bright green-hooded black sweatshirt, a white jacket, and a red jacket with white pants).
- **Roadway & Background**: On the far left, the asphalt edge of a road curves toward an intersection in the background where a green highway direction sign, a white rectangular regulatory sign, a red-and-white triangular `YIELD` sign, and an orange diamond/rectangular construction sign are visible in front of autumn woods.
