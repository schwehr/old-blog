# Sunrise on the Flatirons from Boulder Hotel (`boulder-hotel-view.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format early morning photograph showing the red-orange glow of sunrise illuminating the Flatirons mountain range in Boulder, Colorado, taken from the Best Western hotel during Kurt Schwehr's October 2010 visit to NOAA's National Geophysical Data Center (NGDC).

## Scene Description
- **Mountains (Middle & Background)**:
  - Stretching across the horizon are the iconic, steeply tilted sandstone slabs of the **Flatirons** rising along the eastern flank of Green Mountain and Bear Peak.
  - The low morning sunlight bathes the craggy rock faces and forested ridges in a vivid, warm reddish-orange alpenglow.
- **Sky**: Above the mountains, a layer of gray-violet morning clouds covers the left and center sky, giving way to a patch of clear pale blue sky on the upper right.
- **Foreground**:
  - Across the lower third of the image is the long, horizontal roofline of a commercial or industrial building with a light tan upper fascia band and a darker reddish-brown lower wall, brightly lit by the rising sun.
  - Several tall, slender parking-lot light poles and small deciduous trees stand in the foreground in front of the building, with a boxy beige rooftop wall structure at the lower-right corner.
