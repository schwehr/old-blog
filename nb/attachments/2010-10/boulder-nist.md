# NIST Boulder Laboratories Campus with the Flatirons (`boulder-nist.jpg`)

## Overview
A sunny outdoor photograph of the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) campus in Boulder, Colorado, framed against the dramatic backdrop of the Flatirons and a clear blue sky, taken during Kurt Schwehr's October 2010 visit to the adjacent NOAA facility.

## Scene Description
- **NIST Building Complex (Foreground & Midground)**:
  - Spanning the foreground is a modernistconcrete and native stone architectural portico consisting of a long row of square tan concrete columns supporting a horizontal beam/walkway canopy.
  - On the right side of the entrance facade, large dark blue block letters spell `NIST` next to a circular agency seal mounted on a stone-veneered wall.
  - Rising behind the left side of the walkway is a taller, multi-story beige concrete main building wing with dark lettering (`U.S. DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE ...`) near the roofline and a tall metal exhaust stack/antenna on the roof.
  - A large, conical blue-green spruce tree stands in front of the taller building wing on the left, complemented by ornamental grasses illuminated in golden morning light along the base of the colonnade and a manicured green lawn in the immediate foreground.
- **Background (Mountains & Sky)**:
  - Directly behind the low-rise campus buildings, grassy golden foothills slope up to the jagged, steeply dipping reddish-brown sandstone slabs of the **Flatirons** along the Front Range.
  - The sky above is a deep, cloudless cobalt blue.
