# NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center Forecast Office (`boulder-noaa-spaceweather-command.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph of the Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC) operations and command room inside the NOAA David Skaggs Research Center in Boulder, Colorado, taken during a building tour led by Marcus in October 2010.

## Room Layout & Equipment
- **Foreground Console**:
  - A curved, light-gray wrap-around command desk occupies the foreground.
  - Seated at the console on the left (seen from behind) is a woman with brown hair wearing a black three-quarter-sleeve top and blue jeans, with her right hand on a computer mouse in front of two angled flat-panel monitors embedded in a gray console housing.
  - To the left of her is an empty high-backed burgundy-red office chair.
  - On the desk surface to the right of the monitor housing sit a beige multi-line push-button telephone/intercom unit, several binders, notebooks, and printed reference sheets.
- **Tour Guide (Right)**:
  - Standing on the right side of the room is **Marcus**, wearing a light blue long-sleeved collared shirt, dark jeans, and an ID badge on a black lanyard around his neck. His arms are spread wide (holding a red folder/notebook in his left hand) in slight motion blur as he explains the operations center.
- **Back Wall & Display Screens**:
  - A long bank of recessed workstation monitors and two large overhead flat-screen displays line the back wall:
    - **Upper-Left Large Display**: Shows real-time solar imagery grids, including green and red false-color ultraviolet/coronagraph disks of the Sun and a blue solar wind/coronagraph plot.
    - **Upper-Right Large Display**: Features a large bright red-orange solar disk image (such as SOHO/SDO extreme ultraviolet imagery) alongside a dark blue magnetosphere or auroral oval plot.
    - **Lower Wall Consoles**: Pairs of smaller monitors below the main screens display tabular telemetry, time-series geomagnetic/solar flux plots, and status dashboards.
  - **Agency Emblems**: Mounted on the dark wall cabinetry between the monitor bays are the circular blue-and-white **NOAA** logo (center-left) and a winged **U.S. Air Force / Weather Agency** crest (far right).
