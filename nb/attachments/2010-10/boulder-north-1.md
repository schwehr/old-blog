# Morning View Near Wonderland Lake in North Boulder (`boulder-north-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A landscape photograph taken with an iPhone just before sunrise during a morning walk near Wonderland Lake on the north side of Boulder, Colorado, during Kurt Schwehr's October 2010 visit to NOAA's National Geophysical Data Center (NGDC).

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Midground**: A broad, gently sloping golden-brown grassland hill occupies the lower right and center of the frame, with a faint dirt trail curving across the lower left. Near the trail on the left stands a small cluster of bright golden-yellow autumn deciduous trees and scattered low bushes. Further down the valley in the midground, a dense line of green and yellow autumn trees borders an open green field on the far left and partially obscures residential houses nestled along the base of the foothills on the right.
- **Background & Foothills**: To the right, dry grass-covered foothills dotted with dark evergreen trees and rocky outcrops rise steeply. In the center background, a prominent multi-peaked mountain massif (part of the Front Range / Flatirons area) stands silhouetted in dark purplish-blue against the dawn sky.
- **Sky & Lighting**: The early morning sky is bright pale white and warm peach-gold near the left horizon where the sun is about to rise, transitioning to soft light blue toward the upper right, with a few thin, dark horizontal cloud wisps floating above the central mountain peak.
