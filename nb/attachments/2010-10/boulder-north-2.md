# Foothills Trail Near Wonderland Lake in North Boulder (`boulder-north-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A landscape photograph taken with an iPhone just before sunrise along a trail near Wonderland Lake on the north side of Boulder, Colorado, in October 2010. As noted in the accompanying blog post, deer were present up on the hillside during the walk, though too small and distant to be clearly distinguished in the mobile phone capture.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**: A narrow dirt and gravel walking trail extends straight ahead from the lower center into the grassy foothills. Tall, dry golden-brown grasses border both sides of the path. On the left, a weathered wooden fence post stands in the grass; on the right, a low wooden post-and-rail fence runs alongside the trail next to a dense cluster of dark green upright juniper or pine shrubs.
- **Hillside & Background**: Directly ahead, a wide, rounded foothill covered in warm reddish-gold and tan dry grass rises across the entire width of the frame. Scattered dark green ponderosa pines and shrubs dot the slopes and form a jagged silhouette along the crest of the ridge. A tiny bright speck is visible mid-slope slightly left of center.
- **Sky & Lighting**: Above the ridge line, the pre-dawn sky is a uniform pale greyish-blue, while warm early-morning light casts a soft golden-orange glow across the dry grassy slopes.
