# Sunrise Over East Boulder from Wonderland Lake Area (`boulder-north-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle sunrise photograph looking east over the plains from the foothills near Wonderland Lake on the north side of Boulder, Colorado, captured on an iPhone in early October 2010.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Horizon**: The lower third of the image is in deep shadow, showing the dark silhouettes of leafy tree canopies on the left and right, with darker wooded residential land and a flat, distant horizon stretching across the center. A couple of faint, reflective bodies of water (small lakes or reservoirs on the plains) glimmer as thin horizontal silvers just below the horizon to the right of center.
- **Sky & Clouds**: The sky displays a vivid dawn color gradient, shifting from a glowing red-orange band along the horizon to bright golden-yellow, and then to pale cyan-blue at the top of the frame. Just left of center above the horizon, a brilliant white and yellow patch of sunlit cloud marks the position of the rising sun. Above it to the left, scattered dark grey and rose-tinted clouds drift across the upper sky.
