# Foothills and Autumn Trees Near Wonderland Lake (`boulder-north-4.jpg`)

## Overview
A pre-sunrise landscape photograph taken near Wonderland Lake on the north side of Boulder, Colorado, looking across a grassy meadow toward the Front Range foothills in October 2010.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**: A flat expanse of tall, dry tan and golden-brown marsh reeds and meadow grasses fills the lower third of the image, bordered at the very bottom by darker green grass in shadow.
- **Midground**: Beyond the meadow stands a line of deciduous trees in autumn foliage, displaying warm shades of orange, yellow, and olive green. On the far left, a tall deciduous tree rises to the top edge of the frame, with two diagonal overhead utility wires cutting across the sky from the upper left toward the central mountain.
- **Background & Sky**: Rising behind the trees in the center is the dark, purple-grey silhouette of a prominent multi-peaked mountain ridge, with a wooded foothill slope ascending on the far right. The clear dawn sky transitions from a warm peach-pink glow near the left horizon to a cool lavender-blue overhead, with a few faint horizontal cloud wisps near the mountain peak.
