# Small Milkweed Bugs on a Milkweed Pod at Adams Point (`bugs-at-adams-point.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up nature photograph showing a cluster of small milkweed bugs (*Lygaeus kalmii*) gathered on a pale milkweed seed pod at Adams Point in Durham, New Hampshire, taken during an October 2010 walk near the Jackson Estuarine Laboratory.

## Scene Description
- **Milkweed Pod**: A large, pale greenish-white, warty milkweed seed pod (*Asclepias syriaca*) runs diagonally from the upper left to the lower right of the frame, with a curved stem-like projection near its lower tip. The textured surface of the pod is covered with small bumps and tiny dark speckles.
- **Insects**: Seven adult small milkweed bugs (*Lygaeus kalmii*) are visible on the pod (with one partially hidden on the underside edge at lower left-center). Each bug has a distinctive black and bright orange-red aposematic pattern on its dorsal side: a black head with two segmented antennae, a black pronotum bordered with an orange-red band, and dark wings marked with a prominent orange-red X-shaped cross pattern and small white spots on the dark wing membrane. One bug sits on the left side of the pod, two are near the lower middle, and four are clustered tightly together along the right edge.
- **Background Foliage**: Behind the pod is a blurred background of wild vegetation, including bright crimson-red autumn sumac leaflets dangling below the pod, green narrow-leaved plants at the top, and green and dried grasses below.
