# Clean Gulf 2010 Webcast: Deepwater Horizon Lessons Learned (`cleangulf-dwh-lessons-learned.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a live split-screen webcast broadcast from the 2010 Clean Gulf Conference & Exhibition on October 19, 2010, featuring a panel session and presentation slide on lessons learned from the Deepwater Horizon oil spill.

## Interface & Layout
The webcast interface consists of a white top banner above a two-pane display on a grey background:
- **Top Banner**:
  - **Left**: The blue-and-white `CLEAN GULF` logo showing a stylized map of the US Gulf Coast states (Texas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, Florida) with a water droplet icon.
  - **Right**: Text reading `Sponsored by:` next to the logo for `The Response Group` (`Emergency Response | Pre-Planning & Support`) with a grey and dark blue swoosh arc.

## Left Pane: Live Video Feed
- Shows a stage set with a blue-draped panel table on the left and right, separated by a central podium in front of a blue curtain backdrop.

- **Panelists & Speaker**: Five people are visible on stage:

  - The tops of several audience members' heads are visible along the bottom edge.
