# School Desk Concept Design: Perspective Views (`desk-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An iPhone photograph of a page from Kurt Schwehr's 1996 Mechanical Engineering 101 sketchbook showing hand-drawn perspective and assembly views of a modular, semi-circular school desk design.

## Sketch Details & Layout
Drawn in dark green/black ink on white paper (with a curved cutout paste-up visible on the upper left and spiral notebook binding teeth glimpsed at the far top-left edge), the sheet illustrates three three-dimensional views of the desk concept:
- **Bottom Left (Closed Perspective View)**: Shows the U-shaped / trapezoidal wraparound desk from an elevated oblique angle. The central section features a recessed display/work area under a panel, flanked by two angled side wing compartments with flat closed lids. A square floor cushion or pillow with an X-creased top sits on the floor in the central cutout space where the student sits. A small arrow points toward the base of the left wing box.
- **Bottom Right (Open Perspective View)**: Shows the same wraparound desk from the front-top perspective with its side storage compartments open. The left wing lid is open, and the right wing lid is shown hinged upright with dashed curved lines indicating the opening arc of the lid. The central section has a raised back lip and an inset panel.
- **Upper Center/Right (Detail of Hinged Side Box)**: Two stacked detail sketches show how the side storage box and hinged top lid assemble. The upper sketch shows a rectangular lid lifting off an open-topped rectangular bin with a vertical pin rod to the right and an arrow labeled `HINGE` pointing to the rear corner joint. Beneath it, a cutaway view shows the lid hinging open attached to the angled central desk surface.
