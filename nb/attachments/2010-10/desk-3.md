# School Desk Concept Design: Exploded Assembly View (`desk-3.jpg`)

## Overview
A slightly blurry iPhone photograph of a hand-drawn exploded isometric assembly diagram from Kurt Schwehr's 1996 Mechanical Engineering 101 project, showing the individual structural components of the wraparound school desk and how they fit together along dashed alignment lines.

## Diagram Details & Visible Labels
The ink drawing on white paper breaks the desk into three main vertical assembly columns connected by vertical dashed explosion lines:
- **Left Wing Assembly**:
  - **Top**: A flat rectangular hinged lid panel.
  - **Bottom**: A deep, open-topped rectangular storage bin (for a backpack). Extending to the lower left from the top rear edge of the bin is a long hinge rod with an arrow and handwritten label reading `PIN FOR HINGE`.
- **Center Desk Assembly**:
  - **Top**: A rectangular transparent cover plate with vertical screws/fasteners depicted at its left and right corners, and an arrow at the top right pointing to the plate with a slightly blurred label (`PLEXIGLASS`).
  - **Upper-Middle**: A rectangular frame or spacer bar directly beneath the cover plate.
  - **Middle**: The main angled wraparound desktop bridge piece, featuring a recessed rectangular central cutout and upturned side flanges that mate with the left and right storage bins.
  - **Bottom**: Two vertical rectangular support legs/pedestals shaded with diagonal hatching, positioned beneath the central desk section.
- **Right Wing Assembly**:
  - **Top**: A flat rectangular hinged lid panel mirroring the left side.
  - **Bottom**: An open-topped rectangular storage box (for books and supplies) with dashed lines showing where the lid and central desk flange attach.
