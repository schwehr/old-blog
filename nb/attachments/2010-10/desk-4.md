# School Desk Concept Design: Idea Topology Mind Map (`desk-4.jpg`)

## Overview
An iPhone photograph of a full spiral-bound sketchbook page from Kurt Schwehr's 1996 Mechanical Engineering 101 course titled `IDEAS I REALLY LIKE - TOPOLOGY`, presenting a radial mind map of evolutionary school desk sketches leading from a standard four-legged table at the center to various ergonomic, modular, and humorous desk concepts.

## Page Layout & Sketch Branches
- **Page Header & Binding**: Along the right edge is the twin-loop wire spiral binding of the sketchbook; along the left edge are three green index tabs sticking out, with visible labels including `INITIAL IDEAS` and `... EVALUATION`. At the top center, handwritten in pencil/ink, is the heading `IDEAS I REALLY LIKE` above a boxed subtitle `TOPOLOGY`.
- **Center (`START`)**: In the middle of the page is a simple isometric sketch of a traditional rectangular four-legged school desk labeled `START`, with arrows radiating outward in all directions to alternative design branches:
  - **Upper Left Branch (Wraparound & Modular Desks)**: An arrow points up-left to an L-shaped desk, which branches further to a curved wraparound desk bar and top-down diagrams showing triangular/trapezoidal desks with circular seats arranged side-by-side, plus a V-shaped desk with a person seated inside the V.
  - **Upper Center Branch (Low Floor Desk)**: An arrow points straight up to a low, coffee-table-height rectangular desk positioned in front of a long cushioned bench/couch against a wall.
  - **Upper Right Branch (Rolling / Reclining Desk)**: Arrows lead to a tilted desk surface on T-leg supports with a separate small rolling cart, and above it a side-profile sketch of a student sitting on a low padded floor chair/recliner writing at a low box desk.
  - **Middle-Left & Lower-Left Branch (Cutout & Flip-Top Desks)**: Arrows point left to a desk with a curved front cutout (`LEG SPACE` arrow underneath), which leads downward to a desk with side storage compartments and then to a desk with `FLIP UP TOPS` on the left and right wings.
  - **Lower Center-Left Branch (Pedestal Desk)**: An arrow points down-left from `START` to an angled-top desk mounted on two sleigh-style floor runners, leading down to a cutaway view of an under-desk storage tray.
  - **Lower Center Branch**: An arrow points down to a desk with an angled central reading/laptop slope flanked by flat side wings.
  - **Lower Right Branch ("The Desk That Eats Kids")**: Via a simple flat frame sketch at middle-right, an arrow points to a humorous cartoon sketch in the bottom right corner: a deep box-like desk on two front legs that have shoes on their feet and two rear horizontal wall-braces. A student is being swallowed headfirst inside the open desk bin with only their legs and a hand sticking out, a speech bubble on the right reading `GULP!`, and droplets dripping onto the floor below.
