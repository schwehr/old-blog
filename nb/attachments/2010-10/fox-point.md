# Walking Group at the "Newington Residents Only" Sign at Fox Point (`fox-point.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor snapshot taken on Sunday, October 3, 2010 (posted October 9, 2010), showing three friends smiling next to a restrictive town park sign at Fox Point in Newington, New Hampshire, overlooking Little Bay.

## Scene Description
- **People & Sign (Foreground)**:
  - On the right stands a weathered white rectangular metal sign with a thin black border mounted on two dark metal posts, reading in large, bold, dark green/black capital letters:
    `NEWINGTON`
    `RESIDENTS`
    `ONLY`
  - **Left**: A man with short dark hair, a beard, and a mustache stands in three-quarter profile, smiling broadly at the camera while wearing a black fleece jacket.
  - **Center**: A woman with wavy, chin-length brown hair and red-framed glasses smiles at the camera, wearing a black quilted down vest over a dark long-sleeved shirt and an orange-and-grey patterned scarf, with her right hand tucked into her vest pocket.
  - **Right**: A woman with pulled-back dark brown hair stands directly behind the left side of the `NEWINGTON RESIDENTS ONLY` sign, smiling warmly in a dark navy/black hooded sweatshirt.
- **Background**: Behind the group, a dirt and gravel road curves away to the left toward the bright water of Little Bay, which is also visible through the trees on the right. Parked in a small clearing behind the center and left figures are a white SUV (Land Rover Discovery) and a white sedan. Leafy green and early-autumn trees line the shoreline and roadway.
