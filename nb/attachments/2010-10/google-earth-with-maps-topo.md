# Google Maps Topographic Overlay Draped in Google Earth (`google-earth-with-maps-topo.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth on Mac OS X showing a 3D perspective view of rugged canyon terrain (in the Grand Canyon region) where a screenshot of the Google Maps terrain/topographic layer has been manually georeferenced and draped as an image overlay texture onto the 3D elevation model, alongside an open Google Earth `Ruler` measurement dialog.

## Map & 3D Terrain Details
- **Foreground & Midground (Draped Topographic Map)**: Roughly the lower two-thirds of the 3D terrain is covered by a pale sage-green shaded-relief topographic map texture captured from Google Maps. Fine brown contour lines and elevation labels in feet (including `4400 ft`, `4800 ft`, `5200 ft`, `6000 ft`, and `6400 ft`) follow the steep ridges, spurs, and valleys of the 3D terrain mesh.
- **Measurement Path**: A bright yellow multi-segment polyline (`Path`) with red control vertices traces a ridgeline and valley route from the middle-left edge across the central peaks to the middle-right.
- **Background (Standard Satellite Imagery)**: Beyond the top edge of the draped topographic overlay, the native Google Earth satellite imagery of the Grand Canyon is visible, showing dark green forested plateaus on the upper left and steep reddish-brown, tan, and dark grey canyon walls on the upper right.

## Interface & Controls (`Ruler` Window)
Floating in the upper-right center of the viewport is a Mac OS X utility window titled `Ruler`:
- **Tabs**: Two tabs at the top center: `Line` (selected, highlighted in blue) and `Path`.
- **Readouts**:
  - `Length:` `5.37` with a dropdown menu set to `Kilometers`
  - `Heading:` `142.89 degrees`
- **Controls**: A checked checkbox labeled `Mouse Navigation` on the bottom left, and two pill-shaped buttons on the bottom right labeled `Save` and `Clear`.
