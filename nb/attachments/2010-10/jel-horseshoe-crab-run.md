# Horseshoe Crab Tracking Enclosure on the Mudflats at Jackson Estuarine Lab (`jel-horseshoe-crab-run.jpg`)

## Overview
A coastal landscape photograph taken at low tide at Adams Point in Durham, New Hampshire, looking across a mudflat cove toward the University of New Hampshire's Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL) and showing an experimental fencing enclosure built by Professor Win Watson's team to track horseshoe crab behavior with video.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Mudflat & Experimental Fence)**: Expansive, glistening dark grey-brown intertidal mudflats fill the foreground and middle ground at low tide. Running diagonally from the upper left shore to the lower right edge is a long, straight barrier fence constructed of wooden stakes and upright A-frame supports holding taut orange plastic construction netting over lower dark mesh fencing pinned into the mud.
- **Midground (JEL Pier & Boats)**: At the far left end of the mudflat, golden-brown marsh grass and green shoreline trees meet a stone bulkhead pier at the Jackson Estuarine Laboratory. A vertical metal tower/frame stands on the end of the pier, and several small white research motorboats are moored along the floating dock extending into the water to its right. Further out in the calm grey water of Little Bay/Great Bay on the right is a moored white sailboat next to a rectangular blue-floated aquaculture/research raft.
- **Background**: Across the bay, a low, dark green wooded shoreline stretches horizontally beneath an overcast sky filled with layered grey and white clouds.
