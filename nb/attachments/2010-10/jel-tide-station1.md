# Ohmex TideMet Tide and Weather Station at Jackson Estuarine Lab (`jel-tide-station1.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on the pier at the University of New Hampshire's Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL) at Adams Point in Durham, New Hampshire, showing the equipment enclosures, antennas, staff gauge, and meteorological sensors of an Ohmex TideMet tide station mounted along the dock railing overlooking Little Bay.

## Equipment & Scene Details
- **Pier & Mounting Board (Foreground)**: At the bottom edge is the weathered wooden planking of the JEL pier, with a metal cleat on the far left and rusty vertical metal railing posts linked by slack metal chains. Mounted horizontally across the railing posts is a wide wooden backboard supporting several instruments and weatherproof junction boxes:
  - **Far Left Mast**: A tall vertical white PVC pole topped with a small beige dome GPS/satellite antenna, with black coaxial cables coiled at its base.
  - **Left Desiccant Cylinder**: Next to the mast is a clear vertical cylindrical canister filled with pale/blue desiccant beads and topped with a black cap and vent tube.
  - **Left-Center Gray Box**: A large light-grey fiberglass enclosure with a circular black-and-white oval logo (`Ohmex`) on its front door.
  - **Center Small Gray Box**: A smaller square light-grey fiberglass junction box mounted between the two larger cabinets, with a small label in its lower right corner.
  - **Right Metal Cabinet**: A beige/grey industrial metal cabinet with stenciled black text on its upper panel reading `CABINET 1471` and larger stenciled text on its lower panel reading `UNH OCEAN ENG`. Large black and grey data/power cables hang in neat coils beneath the cabinet on the wooden deck.
- **Sensor Mast & Staff Gauge (Upper Right)**:
  - Rising behind the `UNH OCEAN ENG` cabinet is a white vertical mast topped with a black horizontal cross-arm unit labeled `TIDEMET` in green/white lettering. A white cylindrical dome sits at the center of the cross-arm, flanked by a small dark wind/temperature sensor on the left arm and a white multi-plate radiation shield / ultrasonic wind sensor on the right arm, plus a tall whip antenna extending straight up.
  - Directly behind the TideMet mast is a tall vertical tide staff gauge with black-and-white graduations, and a white PVC stilling-well pipe sticks up just to the right behind the wooden backboard.
  - On the far right post stands a rectangular blue sign with white uppercase text reading:
    `JACKSON`
    `ESTUARINE`
    `LABORATORY`
    `EMPLOYEES`
    `ONLY`
    `Beyond this Point`
- **Background**: Calm, rippled blue-grey water of Little Bay stretches to a wooded shoreline in the distance under a blue sky with light clouds. Several white sailboats and a small white lobster/work boat (just left of the blue sign) are moored on the bay, while a rocky, tree-lined point juts out on the upper left.
