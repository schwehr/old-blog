# Jackson Estuarine Laboratory Tide Station Stilling Well (`jel-tide-station2.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle outdoor photograph looking down at a vertical PVC stilling well and mounting brackets for tide station instrumentation along the rocky, seaweed-covered shoreline at the University of New Hampshire's Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL) on Adams Point in Durham, New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
- **Stilling Well & Mounts**:
  - A vertical white PVC pipe runs down the center-left of the frame into the water, secured against a vertical wooden post.
  - Two rectangular metal support frames extend from the shoreline wall on the left to hold the vertical pipe in place: an upper rusted steel bracket with a curved pipe clamp, and a lower grey painted metal bracket with a flat collar plate around the PVC tube.
  - Near the bottom of the visible PVC pipe, a short wooden board is strapped to the outside with white zip ties.
- **Cables & Conduit**:
  - Several black sensor cables run along the upper metal bracket and hang down the side of the white PVC pipe toward the water.
  - A white bent conduit pipe and a thin blue wire are partially visible at the far left edge on the upper bracket.
- **Shoreline & Water**:
  - Beneath the brackets on the left, coastal rocks are thickly draped in dark green-brown rockweed/seaweed exposed above the water line.
  - The right half of the frame shows calm, slightly Rippled, greenish-grey estuarine water of Little Bay / Great Bay.
