# Andy McLeod Explaining the Phoenix ROV at Know the Coast (`know-the-coast-andym.png`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken during the "Know the Coast" open house event at the University of New Hampshire's Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory, showing Andy McLeod explaining the internal components of the large yellow Phoenix Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV) to a young visitor.

## Scene Description
- **People**:
  - On the left, Andy McLeod wears a dark red collared shirt, blue jeans, and a grey baseball cap with a blue logo on the front. He gestures with both hands toward an internal cylindrical component of the ROV while speaking.
  - In the foreground on the left, a child with shoulder-length brown hair stands with their back to the camera, wearing a grey-and-white horizontally striped long-sleeve shirt and looking up at the vehicle.
- **The Phoenix ROV**:
  - The large underwater vehicle rests on a flat flatbed platform/cart with a bright blue ribbed bumper edge along the bottom.
  - The top section features a large, bright yellow syntactic foam flotation module with rounded front edges and black cable coils hanging at the upper right.
  - Below the yellow syntactic foam, the open aluminum frame exposes a dense array of marine engineering hardware:
    - Multiple vertical clear acrylic pressure housings/cylinders containing electronics and coils.
    - A silver manifold block in the center with rows of hydraulic fittings and black hoses.
    - Yellow and green tubing and cabling routed throughout the chassis.
    - A black thruster propeller assembly visible near the upper left beneath the yellow hood.
    - On the right (front of the ROV), two heavy-duty articulated stainless-steel and anodized-aluminum robotic manipulator arms are stowed across the lower front frame alongside a metal sample basket/tray with diagonal slots.
