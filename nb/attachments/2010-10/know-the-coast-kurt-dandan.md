# Kurt Schwehr and Dandan Discussing Nautical Chart Source Diagrams (`know-the-coast-kurt-dandan.png`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph from the "Know the Coast" outreach event at the University of New Hampshire's Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory, showing Kurt Schwehr and Dandan standing in front of a large projection wall displaying a nautical chart's "Source Data" diagram and an inset historical photo.

## Scene Description
- **People**:
  - On the left, Kurt Schwehr stands in profile facing right, wearing glasses, a dark grey baseball cap, and a medium-blue short-sleeved CCOM/JHC polo shirt. He holds his hands up while explaining the projected chart diagram.
  - On the right, Dandan stands facing left toward the projection screen, wearing glasses, her dark hair tied back, and a matching blue polo shirt, holding a dark grey cap in her hands.
- **Projected Slide**:
  - Projected onto the light grey wall between them is a tan and light-blue coverage diagram from a British Admiralty nautical chart titled **`SOURCE DATA`** at the top center.
  - An inset photograph in the lower-left portion of the projection shows a person seated at a brightly lit drafting table working manually on a paper chart.
