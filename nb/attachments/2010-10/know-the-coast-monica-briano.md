# Monica Wolfson and Brian O. at the Acoustic Aquarium Station (`know-the-coast-monica-briano.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle indoor photograph taken from an elevated walkway overlooking the acoustic test tank room at UNH's Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory during the "Know the Coast" open house event, showing Monica Wolfson and Brian O. demonstrating an acoustic lab setup in a small glass aquarium.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Tank Edge**:
  - In the lower-left foreground, the bright blue water of the large deep engineering/acoustic test tank is visible, bordered by a concrete and wood ledge and silver tubular metal railings with dark cables draped across them.
- **Station & Equipment**:
  - Just beyond the tank wall on the concrete floor stands a mobile equipment rack/cart topped with a flat-screen computer monitor and two small black desktop speakers. A blue informational sign is taped to the front of the cart, and a white presentation board stands on a black tripod easel to the right.
  - Next to the computer rack is a rectangular glass aquarium partially filled with water and small submerged objects used to demonstrate underwater acoustics / sonar principles.
- **People**:
  - To the right of the monitor, Monica Wolfson stands wearing a dark navy shirt, jeans, glasses, and a dark cap, gesturing with her right hand toward the computer screen while explaining the display.
  - Behind the aquarium and monitor stand two visitors/participants listening intently: a smiling woman in a dark vest and grey scarf on the left, and a man in a dark jacket and baseball cap in the center. Another person is blurred in motion behind them near a tall blue curtain partition.
