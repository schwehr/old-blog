# LibreMap USGS Topographic GeoTIFF Detail of Pawtuckaway (`libremap-pawtuckaway-geotif.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot showing a crisp, uncompressed Digital Raster Graphic (DRG) GeoTIFF excerpt of a USGS topographic quadrangle map covering the Pawtuckaway area in New Hampshire, downloaded from LibreMap.org to illustrate clean raster line work without JPEG compression artifacts.

## Map Details & Features
- **Topography & Contours**:
  - Dense brown contour lines depict steep, northeast-southwest trending ridges on the left and upper-right, opening into a broader valley and wetland basin toward the center-right and bottom.
  - Index contours are drawn with thicker brown lines and labeled with elevations **`600`** (lower left-center) and **`650`** (top center).
- **Vegetation, Hydrography & Boundaries**:
  - Light green shading covers the majority of the wooded terrain, with a white unshaded clearing containing blue marsh/swamp symbols at the bottom center.
  - Blue marsh/wetland tuft symbols and thin blue intermittent streams drain southeastward through the right half of the map.
  - Heavy dashed black boundary lines and thinner dashed trails/unimproved roads cross the map, along with thin black grid lines.
