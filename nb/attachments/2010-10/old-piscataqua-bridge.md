# Historic Site Marker at the Old Piscataqua Bridge Abutment (`old-piscataqua-bridge.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken at Fox Point in Newington, New Hampshire, showing an engraved granite historical marker stone resting on a grassy stone-walled abutment overlooking Little Bay / the Piscataqua River where the historic 1794 Piscataqua Bridge once stood.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Monument**:
  - A grassy terrace is bordered along the water's edge by cut granite blocks forming the old bridge abutment.
  - In the center foreground stands a rough-hewn grey granite boulder with a flat front face bearing a carved inscription. A smaller rounded rock sits in the grass to the far left.
- **Visible Inscription**:
  - Carved in dark uppercase letters across three lines on the center stone:
    - **`SITE OF`**
    - **`THE PISCATAQUA BRIDGE`**
    - **`1794 — 1855`**
- **Water & Background**:
  - Beyond the stone abutment lies the calm grey-blue water of Little Bay. A small dark green navigation buoy floats in the channel to the left of center, just in front of a low rocky reef/islet.
  - Across the channel rises a heavily wooded island/peninsula (Goat Island / Adams Point area) covered in dense green trees showing early hints of autumn color.
  - In the far right distance, a modern multi-span highway bridge crosses the water beneath an overcast, pale grey-blue sky.
