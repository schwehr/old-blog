# Sunrise Illuminating Early Fall Foliage in Dover, New Hampshire (`start-of-fall-in-dover.jpg`)

## Overview
An early-morning outdoor photograph taken in Dover, New Hampshire, capturing warm golden-red sunrise light illuminating the top of a tall deciduous tree beginning to turn autumn colors above a shadowed neighborhood house.

## Scene Description
- **Trees & Foliage**:
  - Dominating the center of the frame is a large, full-crowned deciduous tree (maple). While its lower branches remain in deep morning shadow, its upper right canopy catches the direct low-angle rays of the rising sun, glowing vividly in bright orange, scarlet, and golden-yellow autumn hues.
  - Immediately behind and to the left of the maple stand tall, dark evergreen conifers silhouetted against the sky.
  - Lower trees along the right horizon also catch faint golden highlights along their uppermost tips.
- **House & Foreground**:
  - In the lower-right foreground, the pitched gable roof and upper two stories of a light-colored traditional New England residential house are visible in the shade, featuring two upper-story and two lower-story multi-pane windows flanked by dark shutters.
  - Two dark utility wires cross horizontally through the lower half of the frame.
- **Sky**:
  - The morning sky transitions from cool periwinkle blue in the upper left to soft violet-pink and warm peach-gold striated clouds stretching across the right side.
