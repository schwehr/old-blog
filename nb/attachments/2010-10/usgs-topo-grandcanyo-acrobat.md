# Grand Canyon USGS GeoPDF in Adobe Acrobat Pro with Plug-in Warning (`usgs-topo-grandcanyo-acrobat.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of Adobe Acrobat Pro displaying a full 30x60-minute (1:100,000 scale) USGS topographic quadrangle map of the Grand Canyon (`Grand_Canyon_F36112A1_geo.pdf`), overlaid by a modal JavaScript warning dialog stating that the TerraGo `MAP2PDF` GeoPDF plug-in is required.

## Interface & Controls
- **Window Header & Toolbar**:
  - Window title bar at top: **`Grand_Canyon_F36112A1_geo.pdf`**.
  - Top toolbar buttons: **`Create PDF`**, **`Combine Files`**, **`Export`**, **`Start Meeting`**, **`Secure`**, **`Sign`**, **`Forms`**, and **`Review & Comment`**.
  - Secondary navigation bar: open/print/save icons, page indicator **`1 / 1`**, selection and hand tools, zoom controls set to **`26.1%`**, page layout icons, and a **`Find`** search box.
- **Modal Warning Dialog**:
  - Centered near the top of the map is a gray Mac dialog titled **`Warning: JavaScript Window - GeoPDF(TM) Plug-in Required`**.
  - On the left is a yellow warning triangle with an exclamation mark and a small Adobe PDF badge.
  - Dialog message text:
    > This PDF is a GeoPDF(TM) created by MAP2PDF. This viewer does not have the required plug-in installed to access the full geospatial functionality.
    >
    > Do you want to download the free MAP2PDF plug-in now?
  - Two buttons at the bottom right of the dialog: **`No`** and **`Yes`** (highlighted in blue).

## Map Details
- **Grand Canyon 1:100,000 Quadrangle**:
  - Beneath the dialog, the full map sheet shows the deeply incised canyon network of the Colorado River snaking from right to left across the center, rendered in dense brown contour lines, surrounded by green-shaded forested plateaus (Kaibab National Forest, Grand Canyon National Park, Hualapai Indian Reservation).
  - The right margin collar includes the header **`Grand Canyon ARIZONA`**, a dark silhouette location diagram of Arizona, the circular USGS logo, scale/projection blocks, and a **`Topographic Map Symbols`** legend at the lower right.
