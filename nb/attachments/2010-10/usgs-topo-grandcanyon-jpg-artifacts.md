# Heavy JPEG Compression Artifacts in USGS Grand Canyon GeoPDF (`usgs-topo-grandcanyon-jpg-artifacts.png`)

## Overview
A zoomed-in screenshot of a section of the USGS 1:100,000-scale Grand Canyon GeoPDF topographic map (`Grand_Canyon_F36112A1_geo.pdf`), illustrating severe JPEG blockiness, mosquito noise, and color bleeding around contour lines, grid ticks, and text labels.

## Visual Details & Artifacts
- **Compression Artifacts**:
  - Throughout the image, 8x8 pixel JPEG macroblocks and fuzzy ringing ("mosquito noise") are clearly visible in the white background, along the edges of the pale green woodland tint polygons, and surrounding all fine line work.
  - Thin red township/section grid lines and black dashed trails appear blotchy and pixelated, with pinkish-grey haloing in adjacent white pixels.

  - **Map Interior**:
    - Red section numbers **`36`** (left section square) and **`31`** (center section square) inside the red grid squares.
    - Brown topographic contour lines winding across the green-tinted and white terrain, with a brown contour elevation label **`1750`** oriented diagonally in the upper right quadrant.
    - Thin blue stream/drainage lines and black dashed trails winding roughly north-south through the center.
