# USGS Map Locator Download Pop-up (`usgs-topo-map-locator-download.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the USGS Store "Map Locator" web application showing an interactive Google Maps interface centered over the Grand Canyon region in Arizona, with a pop-up info balloon listing downloadable topographic map quadrangles and an annotation highlighting the download link for the 2009 Havasupai Point digital map.

## Map & Pop-up Balloon Details
- **Header Banner**: A dark blue-slate header bar reads **"Map Locator"**, with a help line directly underneath: `Having trouble? Call: 1-888-ASK-USGS (1-888-275-8747) or Write: usgsstore@usgs.gov for help.`
- **Map View**: A Google Maps view set to **Hybrid** mode (selected among `Map`, `Satellite`, `Hybrid`, and `Topo` buttons at top right of the map frame). Standard Google pan/zoom controls appear on the left, along a scale bar (`20 mi` / `50 km`) and copyright notice (`Imagery ©2010 TerraMetrics, Map data ©2010 Google - Terms of Use`) at the bottom.
- **Coordinate Box**: A white box in the lower right corner of the map displays `36-32.962N, 110-59.692W` and `USNG 12S WF 0045 4488`.
- **Map Footprints & Markers**: Yellow diagonal-hatched rectangular grids represent 7.5-minute quadrangle footprints overlaid across the Grand Canyon / Kaibab National Forest / Tusayan area. Two red Google Maps teardrop markers are placed near the Grand Canyon (Havasupai Point area).
- **Info Balloon**: A white speech bubble points to the lower red marker and contains a scrollable table (with a Mac OS X Aqua blue scrollbar on the right) listing three available maps:
  1. `Havasupai Point "Digital Map - Beta"` | `7.5X7.5` | `2009` | `order` | `download` | `14213 KB` — A bright red oval is drawn around `order download 14213 KB` to highlight the download link.
  2. `Havasupai Point` | `7.5X7.5` | `1988` | `order` | `download` | `6944 KB`
  3. `Grand Canyon` | `30X60` | `1984` | `order` | `download` | `11757 KB`
