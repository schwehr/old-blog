# USGS Map Locator Mark Points Selection (`usgs-topo-map-locator-placemark.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the USGS Store "Map Locator" web application illustrating how to switch from map navigation mode to point-marking mode (`MARK POINTS on the map`) in order to place a marker on the Grand Canyon in Arizona and query available USGS topographic quadrangles.

## Map View Details
- **Header Banner**: A dark blue header bar titled **"Map Locator"** with support contact text below: `Having trouble? Call: 1-888-ASK-USGS (1-888-275-8747) or Write: usgsstore@usgs.gov for help.`
- **Google Maps Canvas**: Displayed in **Hybrid** mode (selected among `Map`, `Satellite`, `Hybrid`, and `Topo` buttons at the top). Pan arrows and a vertical zoom slider appear along the upper-left edge; the Google logo, scale bar (`20 mi` / `50 km`), and attribution (`Imagery ©2010 TerraMetrics, Map data ©2010 Google - Terms of Use`) appear along the bottom.
- **Geographic Features & Labels**: Shows northern Arizona and southern Utah, including labels for `Zion National Park`, `La Verkin`, `Hurricane`, `Washington`, `St George`, `Colorado City`, `Kanab`, `Fredonia`, `Kaibab`, `Big Water`, `Page` (near Lake Powell), `Bitter Springs`, `Kaibito`, `Kaibab National Forest`, `Supai`, `Grand Canyon Village`, `Tusayan`, and `Tuba City`, along with U.S. Route `89` shields.
- **Marker & Coordinates**: A single red teardrop placemark pin is dropped in the Grand Canyon south of Kaibab National Forest. In the bottom-right corner of the map, a white coordinate box displays `36-30.578N, 113-31.963W` and `USNG 12S TF 7318 4346`.

## Right-Hand Instructions & Controls ("STEP 1.")
- **Search Section**:
  - `SEARCH: [Search Help]`
  - A text input box with a rounded **Go** button to the right.
  - `Search Type:` dropdown menu set to **`Address or Place`**.
- **Interactive Map Mode Selection**:
  - Heading: `OR FIND A PLACE ON THE MAP [Navigation Help]`
  - `FIRST,` (unselected radio button) `NAVIGATE around the map: double click to re-center, click and drag to pull the map around, zoom in and out.`
  - `THEN,` (selected blue radio button) `MARK POINTS on the map:` — A bright red oval is drawn around this entire line to emphasize the required radio button selection.
  - Below the oval: `click on a place to add a marker` followed by a small red teardrop pin icon.
  - `NOTES: You can switch between Navigate and Mark Points at any time.`
  - `The following map footprints appear when` (cut off at bottom).
