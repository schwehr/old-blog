# USGS Store Map Locator Web Interface (`usgs-topo-map-locator.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the USGS Store "Map Locator" web application (`store.usgs.gov`), showing an embedded Google Map of North America alongside multi-step instructions and controls for searching, marking points, and downloading GeoPDF topographic quadrangles.

## Interface Layout & Controls
- **Header**:
  - Dark blue title bar reading **`Map Locator`**, followed by a support line: `Having trouble? Call: 1-888-ASK-USGS (1-888-275-8747) or Write: usgsstore@usgs.gov for help.`
- **Left Column (Map & Map Controls)**:
  - **Embedded Google Map**: Shows the United States, Canada, and Mexico in standard `Map` view (with `Map`, `Satellite`, `Hybrid`, and `Topo` buttons along the top right of the map canvas, pan/zoom slider on the left, Google logo and `1000 mi / 1000 km` scale bar at lower left, and a coordinate readout box `28-08.970N, 77-20.625W / USNG 18R TS 6984 1598` at lower right).
  - **Buttons Below Map**: Gray rounded buttons for **`Clear Markers`** and **`Reset Map`**, and an orange button labeled **`Show US Topo and "Digital Maps - Beta"`**.
  - **Links**: Four blue hyperlinks to the right of the buttons: **`About USGS Maps`**, **`About GeoPDF Maps`**, **`About US Topo`**, and **`US Topo Quickstart`**.
  - **TerraGo Promotional Box**: A bordered box at the bottom left featuring the **`terraGo TECHNOLOGIES`** logo, a bright red **`Download Now`** button, and bullet points:
    - `Do more with GeoPDF:`
    - `• Annotate with geospatial information`
    - `• Measure distances and area, in your own coordinate system`
    - `• Integrate with GPS tracking`
    - `All with the Free TerraGo Toolbar!`
- **Right Column (Instructions & Search)**:
  - **`STEP 1.`**:
    - **`SEARCH:`** (`[Search Help]`) with a text input field, a **`Go`** button, and a `Search Type:` dropdown set to **`Address or Place`**.
    - **`OR FIND A PLACE ON THE MAP`** (`[Navigation Help]`):
      - `FIRST,` radio button (selected) **`NAVIGATE`** `around the map: double click to re-center, click and drag to pull the map around, zoom in and out.`
      - `THEN,` radio button (unselected) **`MARK POINTS`** `on the map: click on a place to add a marker` (with red map pin icon).
      - `NOTES: You can switch between Navigate and Mark Points at any time.`
      - `The following map footprints appear when you are in the Mark Points mode and zoomed in:` followed by a dropdown menu set to **`7.5 and 15 Minute`**.
  - **`STEP 2.`**:
    - **`SELECT AND GET YOUR MAPS`**:
      - `FIRST, click on the marker [pin icon] to see an information bubble showing maps available. THEN, click on "order" and/or "download" to get the maps you want.`
