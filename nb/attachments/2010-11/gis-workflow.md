# Hand-Drawn Sketch: "What is GIS" Workflow (`gis-workflow.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a hand-drawn pen-on-paper flowchart dated `14 NOV 2010` and titled `"WHAT IS GIS"`, illustrating Kurt Schwehr's conceptual model of how geographic space-time data flows from initial capture and format transformation into a data store, analysis/visualization stages, and ultimately human or machine decisions.

## Diagram Elements & Visible Text
- **Date & Title**:
  - Top-left corner: `14 NOV 2010` inside a small rectangular box with diagonal drop-shadow hatching along the bottom and right edges.
  - Upper center: `WHAT IS GIS` inside a hand-drawn rectangular box.
- **Left Side (Capture & Exchange)**:
  - A cloud-shaped bubble at upper left reads: `X, Y, Z, T, VALUES, META`.
  - An arrow points straight down from the cloud to `INFO CAPTURE`.
  - From `INFO CAPTURE`, one arrow points down across a wavy dividing boundary line to `INFO EXCHANGE`, and another arrow points down-right to `TRANSFORM FORMAT`.
  - A bidirectional arrow (`<->`) also connects `INFO EXCHANGE` across the wavy boundary line to `TRANSFORM FORMAT`.
  - Along the bottom of the wavy boundary line, the two sides are labeled `OTHERS` (to the left/inside the curve) and `YOU` (to the right of the curve).
- **Center (`DATA STORE`)**:
  - A bidirectional arrow (`<->`) connects `TRANSFORM FORMAT` to `DATA STORE` in the center of the diagram.
- **Right Side (Transformations, Outputs, and Decisions)**:
  - From `DATA STORE`, four connections radiate outward to the right and bottom:
    1. A bidirectional arrow (`<->`) pointing up-right to `TRANSFORM CONTENT`.
    2. A bidirectional arrow (`<->`, with a small crossed-out mark on the left arrowhead) pointing down-right to `VISUALIZE`.
    3. A single-headed arrow (with an open triangular arrowhead) pointing down-right to `QUERY`.
    4. A single-headed arrow pointing down-left to `MAPS / PLOTS`.
  - Dotted arrows extend from `VISUALIZE` upward to `TRANSFORM CONTENT` and downward-left to `QUERY`.
  - In the lower-right corner is a small cloud-shaped bubble labeled `DECISIONS`.
  - Solid arrows from `MAPS / PLOTS`, `QUERY`, and `VISUALIZE` all converge onto the `DECISIONS` cloud.
