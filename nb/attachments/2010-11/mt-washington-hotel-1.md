# View Looking East from the Mount Washington Hotel (`mt-washington-hotel-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic landscape photograph looking east from the Mount Washington Hotel in Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, on a snowy late-November day (November 26, 2010), showing snow-dusted forests, the Ammonoosuc River below, and low purple-blue clouds obscuring the higher peaks of the Presidential Range.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Midground**:
  - A mixed forest of dark green conifers (spruce and fir) and bare winter deciduous trees with pale gray/white trunks (birch and hardwoods) covers the valley floor.
  - Winding through the trees in the lower center is a bend of the unfrozen river, bordered by white snow-covered banks, open clearings, and a small dark wooden structure near the water's edge.
  - Further back in the middle distance is a broad, flat snow-covered clearing (part of the hotel golf course/grounds) ringed by conifers.
- **Mountains & Sky**:
  - Rolling, tree-covered ridges of the White Mountains rise from left to right across the horizon in cool blue and violet tones, with the lower slopes of the Presidential Range ascending on the left and Mount Washington hidden in the cloud deck just off the right side of the frame.
  - Dramatic, heavy stratocumulus clouds fill the upper half of the frame, illuminated from behind by a bright patch of sunlight near the top center that casts vividviolet, magenta, and deep indigo hues across the cloud undersides.
