# View Looking South Toward Crawford Notch from the Jewel Terrace (`mt-washington-hotel-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken from the snow-covered roof garden of the new wing (the Jewel Terrace) at the Mount Washington Hotel in Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, looking south toward Crawford Notch under moody blue winter clouds.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Jewel Terrace Roof)**:
  - A broad rectangular rooftop terrace enclosed by a low, white-painted classical balustrade with paneled square pilasters and vertical balusters along the left, back, and right edges.
  - The terrace surface is blanketed in fresh white snow, through which several landscaped garden beds of reddish-brown and golden-tan low shrubs and ornamental grasses protrude in curved islands. Near the center-back of the garden beds is a small stacked-stone cairn or rock feature.
- **Background (Forest & Crawford Notch)**:
  - Just beyond the left side of the balustrade, tall evergreen trees rise above the roofline.
  - Across the valley, dense dark forests lead toward the steep mountain walls of **Crawford Notch** in the center distance, framed by the rounded peak of Mount Willard / Mount Field on the left, a lower gap at the notch in the center, and ascending mountain slopes on the right.
  - Above the mountains, a heavy overcast sky is rendered in deep cool blues and slate grays with a brighter streak of light breaking through the clouds above the central mountain ridge.
