# USCG Interagency Coordinating Committee on Oil Pollution Research Website (`uscg-iccopr.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the homepage for the U.S. Coast Guard's **Interagency Coordinating Committee on Oil Pollution Research (ICCOPR)** website (`http://www.iccopr.uscg.gov/`), displaying its mission statement under the Oil Pollution Act of 1990 alongside a sidebar gallery of the world's largest oil spills.

## Header & Navigation Bar
- **Banner Image**: A wide coastal photograph of ocean waves breaking onto a sandy shoreline under a hazy sky.
- **Banner Title**: Centered in green serif text:
  - **`Interagency Coordinating Committee on Oil Pollution Research (ICCOPR)`**
  - Subtitle in green italics below: *`Advancing Spill Prevention and Response Capabilities`*
- **Search Box**: In the lower right of the banner, a text input field reading `Search ICCOPR` next to a blue rounded **`Go`** button.
- **Horizontal Navigation Tabs**: Seven tabs across a pale green bar:
  - **`Home`** | **`About ICCOPR`** | **`Documents`** | **`Meetings and Notices`** | **`Member R&D Links`** | **`Other Links`** | **`In the News`**

## Main Content & Sidebar
- **Left Column (`Origin and Purpose`)**:
  - A light green text box with a large drop-cap `T`:
    > "Title VII of the Oil Pollution Act of 1990 (Section 7001) established the thirteen-member Interagency Coordinating Committee on Oil Pollution Research, the Interagency Committee, to 'coordinate a comprehensive program of oil pollution research, technology development, and demonstration among the federal agencies, in cooperation and coordination with industry, universities, research institutions, state governments, and other nations, as appropriate, and shall foster cost-effective research mechanisms, including the joint funding of the research.' The Chairman of the Interagency Committee, represented by the Coast Guard, is required to submit a biennial report to Congress on activities carried out under Section 7001 in the preceding two fiscal years, and on activities proposed to be carried out under this section in the current two fiscal year period."
- **Left Column (`Navigating ICCOPR`)**:
  - Bullet 1: **`About ICCOPR`** (inside a green-bordered button box) — `Meet the Chairman, the Interagency Committee members, and responsibilities of ICCOPR.`
  - Bullet 2: **`Documents`** (partially visible at the bottom edge).
- **Right Sidebar (`World's Ten Largest Oil Spills`)**:
  - A scrollable panel (with a Mac OS X blue scrollbar on the right) containing two visible photographs:
    1. **`Deepwater Horizon`** (with a `> More >>` button): Shows the blazing Deepwater Horizon offshore drilling rig sending up billowing black smoke while supply vessels spray fire-fighting water arcs.
    2. **`Amoco Cadiz`** (with a `> More >>` button): Shows the bow/hull of the wrecked tanker *Amoco Cadiz* listing and awash in rough, foaming surf.
