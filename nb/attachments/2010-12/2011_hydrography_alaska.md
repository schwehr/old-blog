# NOAA 2011 Alaska Hydrographic Survey Plan (`2011_hydrography_alaska.png`)

## Overview
A map diagram illustrating the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) planned hydrographic survey projects in Alaska for the 2011 field season. The graphic accompanies a December 30, 2010 blog post titled *"NOAA's 2011 Hydrographic Survey Season"*.

## Map & Survey Area Details
- **Base Map**: The landmass of the state of **ALASKA** is rendered in solid medium gray with a thin black coastline border against a light pale-blue ocean background. Bold black uppercase text reading `ALASKA` is centered across the interior of the state.
- **Color-Coded Survey Polygons & Arrows**: Planned survey areas along the southern, southwestern, and southeastern coasts of Alaska are highlighted in bright red or blue polygons, with matching colored arrows pointing from text labels to the survey footprints:
  - **Red Polygons / Red Arrows** (typically NOAA Ship *Rainier* `RA` and NOAA Ship *Fairweather* `FA` projects):
    - `OPR-O190-RA-11, West of Prince of Wales Is., AK` (pointing to a red area at the southern tip of Prince of Wales Island in Southeast Alaska).
    - `OPR-O322-FA-11, Chatham Strait, AK` (pointing to a red area along lower Chatham Strait in Southeast Alaska).
    - `OPR-P136-FA/RA-11 No. Coast of Kodiak Island, AK` (pointing to a cluster of red polygons on the northern side of Kodiak Island).
    - `OPR-P183-RA-11, Shumagin Islands, AK` (pointing to a red polygon in the Shumagin Islands south of the Alaska Peninsula).
    - `M-R908-FA-11, (FISHPAC)` (pointing to a large, elongated diagonal red rectangle extending southwest-to-northeast across the eastern Bering Sea / Bristol Bay approaches).
    - `OPR-R381-FA-11, Cape Newenham, AK` (pointing to a small red area off Cape Newenham at the northern entrance to Bristol Bay).
  - **Blue Polygons / Blue Arrows** (contract hydrographic surveys `KR`):
    - `OPR-R341-KR-11, Approaches to Kuskokwim River, AK` (pointing to a blue polygon at the mouth of Kuskokwim Bay in southwestern Alaska).
    - `OPR-Q191-KR-11, Krenitzin Is., AK` (pointing to a blue polygon in the Krenitzin Islands of the eastern Aleutian chain).
