# NOAA 2011 Continental US Hydrographic Survey Plan (`2011_hydrography_conus.png`)

## Overview
A map diagram illustrating the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) planned hydrographic survey projects across the Continental United States (CONUS), the Great Lakes, Puerto Rico, and the U.S. Virgin Islands for the 2011 field season. The graphic accompanies a December 30, 2010 blog post titled *"NOAA's 2011 Hydrographic Survey Season"*.

## Map & Survey Area Details
- **Base Map**: The contiguous 48 United States are shown in light gray with thin black state borders against a pale-blue background. A rectangular inset box in the bottom-left corner shows **Puerto Rico** and **USVI** (U.S. Virgin Islands).
- **Color-Coded Survey Polygons & Arrows**: Survey sites are marked with red, blue, or green polygons and matching arrows indicating the type of survey asset (red for NOAA hydrographic ships such as *Rainier* `RA`, *Fairweather* `FA`, and *Thomas Jefferson* `TJ`; blue for contract surveys `KR`/`KRL`; green for Navigation Response Teams `NRT` and R/V *Bay Hydro II* `BH`):
  - **Pacific Coast (West Coast)**:
    - `OPR-N161-RA-11, Georgia Strait, WA` (red arrow to northern Puget Sound / Strait of Georgia).
    - `OPR-N395-FA-11, Central Puget Sound, WA` (red arrow to central Puget Sound).
    - `OPR-L430-NRT6-11, SanFrancisco, CA` (green arrow to San Francisco Bay).
    - `OPR-L418-NRT6-11, LA/LB, CA` (green arrow to Los Angeles / Long Beach Harbor).
  - **Great Lakes**:
    - `OPR-X905-NRT4,11, Thunder Bay, MI` (green arrow to Thunder Bay on Lake Huron).
    - `OPR-W408-NRT4-11, Detroit River, MI` (green arrow to the Detroit River / Lake St. Clair).
  - **Northeast & Mid-Atlantic Coast**:
    - `OPR-A375-NRT5-11, Eastport, ME` (green arrow to Eastport in eastern Maine).
    - `OPR-A366-KR-11, Penobscot Bay, ME` (blue arrow to Penobscot Bay, Maine).
    - `OPR-B301-NRT5-11, Narragansett Bay, RI` (green arrow to Narragansett Bay, Rhode Island).
    - `OPR-B370-TJ-11, E. Long Is. Sound, NY` (red arrow to eastern Long Island Sound).
    - `OPR-B363-TJ-11, Block Is. Sound, RI` (red arrow to Block Island Sound).
    - `OPR-E349-BH-11, Central Chesapeake Bay, MD` (green arrow to central Chesapeake Bay).
    - `OPR-D304-TJ-11, Approaches to Chesapeake Bay, VA` (red arrow to the mouth of Chesapeake Bay).
    - `OPR-D302-KR-11, VA Coast` (blue arrow to the Delmarva / Virginia Atlantic coast).
  - **Southeast & Gulf of Mexico**:
    - `S-G904-NRT2-11, Fernandina, FL` (green arrow to northeastern Florida / Georgia border).
    - `S-G901-NRT2-11, St Johns River, FL` (green arrow to Jacksonville / St. Johns River, Florida).
    - `OPR-H355-KRL-11, Florida Keys NMS (Lidar)` (blue arrow to the western Florida Keys / Dry Tortugas).
    - `OPR-J417-NRT1-11, Tampa, FL` (green arrow to Tampa Bay on Florida's Gulf coast).
    - `OPR-J910-NRT1-11, Panama City, FL` (green arrow to the Florida Panhandle).
    - `OPR-J399-KR-11, Brenton Sound, MS` (blue arrow to the Mississippi/Louisiana sound east of the Mississippi River delta).
    - `OPR-J348-KR-11, Approaches to Mississippi River` (blue arrow to the Mississippi River birdfoot delta).
    - `OPR-K354-KR-11, Lousiana Coast` (blue arrow to coastal central Louisiana).
  - **Caribbean Inset (Bottom Left)**:
    - `OPR-I169-KRL-11, Pillsbury Sound, USVI` (blue arrow pointing to a bright blue polygon covering St. Thomas, St. John, and Pillsbury Sound in the U.S. Virgin Islands).
