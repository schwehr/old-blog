# Three Generations of Apple iMac G4 Computers (`IMac_G4_Generations.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph showing all three display-size generations of the Apple iMac G4 ("Gumdrop" or "Sunflower" iMac) side by side on a wooden desk. The image is sourced from Wikipedia (`File:IMac_G4_Generations.jpg`) and featured in a December 31, 2010 blog post titled *"10 years of fink"*, where the author recalls receiving a 15-inch Gumdrop iMac as his first personal Mac.

## Scene & Hardware Details
- **Computers (Left to Right)**:
  1. **15-inch iMac G4 (Left)**: Features a 4:3 aspect ratio flat-panel LCD monitor encased in a white bezel with a clear polycarbonate outer halo, mounted on an adjustable chrome stainless-steel neck attached to a hemispherical white "gumdrop" base (10.6-inch diameter) bearing a silver Apple logo and slot/tray optical drive door. Flanking the base are two spherical, clear-plastic Apple Pro Speakers showing their silver-and-black internal cones.
  2. **17-inch Widescreen iMac G4 (Center)**: Features a wider 16:10 widescreen flat-panel LCD monitor on an articulated chrome arm above the same style hemispherical white base with a metallic Apple logo on the front.
  3. **20-inch Widescreen iMac G4 (Right)**: Features the largest 20-inch widescreen LCD panel suspended on a thicker chrome neck above a hemisphere base with a perforated ring of ventilation holes at the top and a silver Apple logo on the front.
- **Background & Desk Environment**:
  - All three monitors are powered off with dark black screens.
  - The computers sit on a warm honey-colored wooden tabletop.
  - Behind the center iMac is a larger black flat-panel display.
  - A blue toy car and small electronic accessories are partially visible at the bottom left, and a cup of pens and plastic packaging sit at the bottom right.
