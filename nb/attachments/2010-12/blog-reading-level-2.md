# Google Advanced Search Reading Level Dropdown (`blog-reading-level-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Google **Advanced Search** web page showing how to enable the Reading Level annotation filter for a specific domain (`site:schwehr.org/blog`). The screenshot accompanies a December 20, 2010 blog post titled *"My blog's reading level - intermediate"*.

## Interface & Form Details
- **Header**: Classic `Google` logo on the top left alongside a light-blue banner reading **Advanced Search**, with links on the right for `Advanced Search Tips | About Google`.
- **Query Summary Box**: A white text bar at the top of the light-blue form container displays `site:schwehr.org/blog`.
- **Search Form Sections**:
  - **Find web pages that have...**:
    - `all these words:` (empty text input)
    - `this exact wording or phrase:` (empty text input, `tip` link on right)
    - `one or more of these words:` (three empty text inputs separated by `OR`, `tip` link on right)
  - **But don't show pages that have...**:
    - `any of these unwanted words:` (empty text input, `tip` link on right)
  - **Need more tools?**:
    - `Reading level:` — An open macOS-style dropdown menu currently selecting **`annotate results with reading levels`** (highlighted in blue). The full list of dropdown options is:
      - `no reading level displayed`
      - `annotate results with reading levels` (selected)
      - `show only basic results`
      - `show only intermediate results`
      - `show only advanced results`
    - Below `Reading level:` are additional field labels on the left (`Results per page:`, `Language:`, `File type:`) partially covered by the open dropdown menu, and at the bottom `Search within a site or domain:` filled with `schwehr.org/blog`.
