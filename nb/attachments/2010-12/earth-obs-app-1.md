# Earth Observer iPhone App Intro Screen (`earth-obs-app-1.png`)

## Overview
An iPhone screenshot of the **Earth Observer** mobile application (companion app to Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory's GeoMapApp) showing its opening global bathymetric and topographic map view. The screenshot appears in a December 23, 2010 blog post titled *"GeoMapApp for the iPhone/iPad - Earth Observer"*.

## Interface & Map Details
- **iOS Status Bar (Top)**: Displays `Searching...` next to a Wi-Fi signal icon on the left, `3:47 PM` in the center, and Bluetooth and green battery icons on the right.
- **Map View**:
  - Framed by a bright green border around the map viewport.
  - Displays a global shaded-relief topographic and bathymetric map covering North and South America, the Atlantic Ocean, Europe, Africa, the Indian Ocean, and western/southern Asia.
  - Ocean basins are labeled in italicized white text with dark drop shadows: `Pacific Ocean` (partially cut off on the left), `North Atlantic Ocean`, `South Pacific Ocean`, `South Atlantic Ocean`, and `Indian Ocean`. Mid-ocean ridges (such as the Mid-Atlantic Ridge and Southwest/Southeast Indian Ridges) and continental shelves are clearly visible in the bathymetric shading.
- **Overlays & Controls**:
  - **Scale Bar (Top Right)**: A black bracketed scale bar labeled `2000 Km` with a center tick mark.
  - **Instructions Button (Center Bottom)**: A rounded white rectangular button with bold dark-blue text reading `Instructions`.
  - **Institutional Logo Banner (Bottom Left)**: A white rectangular box with a thin black border containing green serif text `Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory` above gray uppercase text `COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY | EARTH INSTITUTE`.
  - **Menu / Layer Button (Bottom Right)**: A circular blue button with a white border and a right-pointing white chevron arrow (`>`).
