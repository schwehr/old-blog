# Earth Observer App with High-Resolution Bathymetry (`earth-obs-app-2-high-res-bathy.png`)

## Overview
An iPhone screenshot of the **Earth Observer** application displaying high-resolution multibeam bathymetry off the U.S. Mid-Atlantic coast (Chesapeake Bay, Delaware Bay, and the Atlantic continental slope/rise) with a depth query pin. The image accompanies a December 23, 2010 blog post titled *"GeoMapApp for the iPhone/iPad - Earth Observer"*.

## Interface & Map Details
- **iOS Status Bar (Top)**: Shows `AT&T` with 1 signal bar, Wi-Fi icon, and network activity spinner on the left; `12:58 PM` in the center; and Bluetooth and green battery icons on the right.
- **Map & Bathymetric Features**:
  - Framed by a bright green border, with thin yellow bounding-box lines marking the boundaries of loaded high-resolution tiles.
  - On the left, the coastal plain of Maryland, Delaware, Virginia, and southern New Jersey is visible in muted green and tan, including Chesapeake Bay and Delaware Bay.
  - Moving offshore to the east, the shallow continental shelf transitions sharply into a darkly shaded, high-resolution multibeam swath over the continental slope, submarine canyons, and deep-sea fan/rise tracks extending into the western North Atlantic basin.
  - **Depth Marker**: Near the center-right, at the edge of a deep-water multibeam swath, a light-blue inverted triangle marker with a black outline points to a spot labeled **`-4098 meters`** in light-blue bold text with a black outline.
- **Overlays & Controls**:
  - **Scale Bar (Top Right)**: A black scale bar with end and center ticks labeled `200 Km`.
  - **Opacity / Layer Slider (Right Edge)**: A vertical blue slider track with a circular white thumb positioned at the top (100% opacity).
  - **Data Attribution (Bottom Left)**: White outlined text reading `NOAA-NGDC, USGS, LDEO`.
  - **Navigation / Layer Button (Bottom Right)**: A circular blue button with a white border and a right-pointing white chevron (`>`).
