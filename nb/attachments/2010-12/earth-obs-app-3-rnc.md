# Earth Observer App with Raster Nautical Charts (`earth-obs-app-3-rnc.png`)

## Overview
An iPhone screenshot of the **Earth Observer** application showing a semi-transparent NOAA Raster Nautical Chart (RNC) overlaid on shaded-relief bathymetry along the continental shelf edge and submarine canyon heads. The image accompanies a December 23, 2010 blog post titled *"GeoMapApp for the iPhone/iPad - Earth Observer"*.

## Interface & Chart Details
- **iOS Status Bar (Top)**: Shows `AT&T` with 1 signal bar, Wi-Fi icon, and network activity spinner on the left; `12:59 PM` in the center; and Bluetooth and green battery icons on the right.
- **Warning Banner (Top Left)**: A prominent bright-red horizontal banner spanning two-thirds of the top edge with bold black text reading **`Not For Navigation`**.
- **Chart & Bathymetric Overlay**:
  - Framed by a bright green border around the map viewport.
  - Shows a NOAA Raster Nautical Chart blended at partial transparency (~25% opacity via the right-side slider) over shaded multibeam bathymetry of a submarine canyon incising the continental shelf/slope.
  - **Visible Chart Markings & Soundings**:
    - Soundings (in fathoms) printed across the shelf and canyon slope: `35`, `30`, `34`, `42`, `53`, `78`, `165`, `820`, `1161` along the upper section; `38`, `45`, `49`, `53`, `198`, `377`, `662`, `1190`, `1243`, `42` across the upper-middle; `249`, `59`, `360`, `60`, `868`, `1188`, `1344` across the middle; and `73`, `318`, `652`, `169`, `710`, `865`, `651`, `1088`, `1237`, `1396`, `1446`, `1381`, `1476` across the lower canyon and slope.
    - **Aids to Navigation & Features**:
      - On the left shelf: `Subm pipe (approx 40 fms rep)` and seabed character labels `S Sh` (sand and shells) and `M` (mud).
      - Near the center canyon head: a magenta buoy symbol with diamond and flare labeled `Y "E" Fl Y 6s Priv` (yellow buoy "E", flashing yellow every 6 seconds, privately maintained) and seabed label `Cy` (clay).
      - Wavy magenta lines (submarine cables) and dashed magenta lines run across the chart, along with a tilted LORAN-C grid of fine blue/magenta lines.
- **Overlays & Controls**:
  - **Scale Bar (Top Right)**: A black bracketed scale bar labeled `20 Km`.
  - **Opacity Slider (Right Edge)**: A vertical slider track with a circular white thumb set in the lower third of the bar, allowing the underlying shaded relief to show through the chart.
  - **Data Attribution (Bottom Left)**: White outlined text reading `NOAA`.
  - **Navigation / Layer Button (Bottom Right)**: A circular blue button with a white border and a right-pointing white chevron (`>`).
