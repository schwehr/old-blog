# Earth Observer App with Electronic Navigational Charts (`earth-obs-app-4-enc.png`)

## Overview
An iPhone screenshot of the **Earth Observer** application displaying a semi-transparent NOAA Electronic Navigational Chart (ENC) overlaid on shaded-relief continental slope bathymetry in the North Atlantic Ocean. The screenshot accompanies a December 23, 2010 blog post titled *"GeoMapApp for the iPhone/iPad - Earth Observer"*.

## Interface & Chart Details
- **iOS Status Bar (Top)**: Shows `AT&T` with 1 signal bar, Wi-Fi icon, and a partial network spinner on the left; `12:59 PM` in the center; and Bluetooth and green battery icons on the right.
- **Warning & Unit Banners (Top Left)**:
  - A bright-red horizontal banner spanning the upper-left two-thirds of the map area with bold black text reading **`Not For Navigation`**.
  - Directly below the red banner on the left is a semi-transparent white rectangle with black text reading **`Depths in meters`**.
- **Chart & Bathymetric Overlay**:
  - Framed by a bright green border around the map viewport.
  - Shows vector ENC bathymetric contours, depth areas (light blue-gray on the continental shelf/upper slope transitioning to darker blue over the deep slope), and metric spot soundings blended with shaded-relief bathymetry.
  - **Visible Metric Soundings & Labels**:
    - Shelf and upper slope (left): `62.1`, `76.8`, `98.9`, `69.4`, `89.6`, `96.9`, `82.2`, `455.3`, `107.8`, `658.3`, `109.7`, `658.3`, `309`, `33.5`, `1192.3`, `581.5`, `1190.5`, and seabed type `mud`.
    - Mid-slope (center): `142.6`, `362.1`, `689.4`, `1210.6`, `1587.3`, `1587.3`, `1298.4`, `1581.9`, `1989.7`, `2262.2`, and seabed type `clay` next to a small cyan dot.
    - Deep slope/rise (right): `1468.5`, `2176.2`, `2273.1`, `2172.6`, `2457.9`, `2717.5`, `2553`, `2644.4`, `2856`, `2525.5`, `2699.3`.
    - Yellow and dark brown text labels in the lower left and lower right read `North Atlantic Ocean`, `Cape Sable to Cape Hatteras`, and `Newport, Rhode Island to Bermuda (Plotting Sheet)`.
- **Overlays & Controls**:
  - **Scale Bar (Top Right)**: A black bracketed scale bar labeled `20 Km`.
  - **Opacity Slider (Right Edge)**: A vertical slider track with its circular white thumb positioned in the lower third.
  - **Data Attribution (Bottom Left)**: White outlined text reading `NOAA-NRL`.
  - **Navigation / Layer Button (Bottom Right)**: A circular blue button with a white border and a right-pointing white chevron (`>`).
