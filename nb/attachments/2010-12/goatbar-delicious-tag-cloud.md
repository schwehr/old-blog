## Overview
A screenshot of Kurt Schwehr's (`goatbar`) **Delicious** bookmark tag cloud as displayed on his personal website (`schwehr.org`). The image accompanies a December 16, 2010 blog post titled *"Delicious going away?"*, written in response to news that Yahoo! planned to shut down or sell Delicious.

- **Header**: Top-left heading featuring the classic four-square Delicious logo icon (blue, light-blue, black, and light-gray squares arranged in a 2x2 grid) followed by bold dark-purple sans-serif text reading **`My Delicious Tags`**.
- **Tag Cloud Structure**: Ten lines of alphabetically ordered, lower-case (with a few uppercase exceptions) blue hyperlink tags on a white background, where font size and weight scale proportionally with the number of bookmarked links per tag.
