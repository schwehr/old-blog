# Andy Installing the StarDot Camera at Jackson Estuarine Lab (`jel-cam-andy-install.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on November 23, 2010 showing Andy installing a weatherproof StarDot network camera on a rooftop pole at the University of New Hampshire's **Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL)** on Adams Point in Durham, New Hampshire. The image appears in a December 11, 2010 blog post titled *"Stardot Camera at Jackson Lab"*.

## Scene Description
- **Subject & Action**:
  - In the right foreground, a man (Andy) with short brown hair and a goatee, wearing a navy-blue long-sleeve rugby shirt with bold yellow and white horizontal stripes over a white collared shirt, stands in profile facing left.
  - With his right hand, he reaches inside the hinged, flipped-open top cover of a beige weatherproof outdoor camera enclosure while steadying the mounting bracket beneath the housing with his left hand.
  - The camera enclosure is mounted at chest/eye level on a vertical galvanized metal mast/pole that extends upward out of the top of the frame.
- **Background & Setting**:
  - Directly below the rooftop in the lower left, the concrete pier and floating dock of Jackson Estuarine Laboratory extend into the calm gray-blue waters of Great Bay / Little Bay (Piscataqua River estuary). Two small white workboats are moored alongside the floating dock, and a small davit crane stands near the end of the fixed pier.
  - Across the water, a dense shoreline forest of leafless deciduous trees and dark evergreens stretches horizontally under a dramatic sky filled with gray and sunlit white stratocumulus clouds.
