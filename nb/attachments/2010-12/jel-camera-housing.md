# StarDot Network Camera Inside Weatherproof Housing at JEL (`jel-camera-housing.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of a **StarDot Technologies** network camera mounted inside an open weatherproof outdoor enclosure on the roof of the University of New Hampshire's **Jackson Estuarine Laboratory (JEL)**. The photograph accompanies a December 11, 2010 blog post titled *"Stardot Camera at Jackson Lab"*.

## Camera & Hardware Details
- **Weatherproof Enclosure**:
  - A beige/off-white hinged aluminum/polycarbonate outdoor camera housing shown with its top lid swung wide open vertically.
  - Black rubber weather-stripping gaskets line the front window frame and the rear semicircular edge of the housing, with a stainless-steel latch clasp visible in the bottom-left foreground.
- **StarDot NetCam Unit**:
  - Nestled inside the enclosure is a rectangular beige metal box camera with the **`StarDot Technologies`** logo (black text with a stylized multi-colored six-pointed star icon featuring a red dot) printed on its right side panel.
  - **Rear Panel Ports & Labels**:
    - Top-left green two-pin terminal block labeled `24VAC`.
    - Top-center DB-9 male serial connector labeled `S1 (AUX)`.
    - Bottom-left green four-pin terminal block labeled `I/O` with pin numbers `1 2 3 4`.
    - Bottom-center BNC coaxial connector with a connected black cable labeled `VIDEO`.
    - Bottom-right barrel power jack with a connected black power cord labeled `12VDC` above a polarity diagram.
- **Background (Overlooking Great Bay)**:
  - Beyond the open front glass of the housing, the JEL pier and floating docks are visible in the upper right, where a white lobster/research boat with a cabin and two smaller skiffs are moored on the calm waters of the estuary.
  - Below the roof edge in the lower right are stacked wooden lobster traps/aquaculture tanks and shoreline rocks; bare branches of a deciduous tree rise on the left.
