# La Crosse Technology 9611IT Weather Station Reading 8.7°F (`lacrosse-9611it.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of a **La Crosse Technology 9611IT** digital wireless weather station sitting on a windowsill, displaying an outdoor morning temperature of **8.7°F**. The image accompanies a brief December 10, 2010 blog post titled *"Winter"*.

## Device & Display Details
- **Enclosure & Buttons**:
  - A rectangular dark charcoal-gray plastic frame with a brushed silver inner bezel surrounding a monochrome LCD screen.
  - Above the LCD screen is a square blue-and-white logo reading **`The Weather Channel`**, and above that on the top dark-gray bar is raised lettering reading **`SNOOZE / SUN`**.
  - Along the left dark-gray bezel are vertical labels for buttons **`SET`** (top left) and **`MIN/MAX`** (bottom left).
  - Along the right dark-gray bezel are vertical labels for buttons **`ALM`** (top right) and **`CH/+`** (bottom right).
  - Below the LCD on the silver bezel is printed **`BY LA CROSSE TECHNOLOGY®`**.
- **LCD Screen Readouts**:
  - **Left Column (`TIME` / `ALARM` / `MOON` / `SUN`)**:
    - Under the **`TIME`** header bar: a radio-controlled atomic clock tower reception icon (`am`) and seven-segment digits reading **`7:09`**, with **`am 6:00`** directly below above the **`ALARM`** bar.
    - Under the **`ALARM`** bar: a sun-rising-over-horizon icon next to **`pm 4:12`** (sunset time) and a waxing crescent moon icon next to **`CON`** above the bottom **`MOON - SUN`** bar.
  - **Center Column (Weather Forecast Boy Icon)**:
    - Shows a sunshine icon at the top left and an upward-pointing pressure/temperature trend arrow at the top right.
    - In the center stands the classic La Crosse animated character ("Forecast Guy") bundled up for freezing weather in a winter cap, a scarf blowing to the right, a long heavy winter coat, mittens, and boots, with a tiny snowman icon next to his left foot.
  - **Right Column (`INDOOR` / `OUTDOOR`)**:
    - Under the **`INDOOR`** header bar: **`56.1°F`** and relative humidity **`34% rh`**.
    - Under the **`OUTDOOR`** header bar: wireless reception waves icon and **`8.7°F`** above the bottom **`CH`** bar.
