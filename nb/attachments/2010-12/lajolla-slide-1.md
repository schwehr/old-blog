# Roadside Slope Landslide in La Jolla, California — View Down the Street (`lajolla-slide-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A street-level photograph taken on the rainy morning of December 22, 2010 in **La Jolla, San Diego, California**, showing a fresh shallow hillside slump/landslide below a row of luxury hillside homes. The photograph accompanies a December 22, 2010 blog post titled *"Active geology - landslide in San Diego"*.

## Scene Description
- **Hillside & Landslide**:
  - Rising steeply from the left side of a wet asphalt residential street is a landscaped coastal bluff topped by a terraced white concrete-block retaining wall and glass-panel balcony railings belonging to a row of multi-story Mediterranean-style stucco houses with red tile roofs.
  - Below the retaining wall in the upper-left and center-left, a section of the saturated brown hillside soil and iceplant/shrub vegetation has given way and slumped downward toward the curb and gutter, exposing bare brown earth and several white PVC drainage/irrigation pipes sticking out of the scarp.
- **Street & Traffic Control**:
  - A line of eleven bright orange traffic cones (several with reflective white collars) is arranged in an arc along the wet pavement around the toe of the slide to cordon off the slumped mud and bushes.
  - Parked along the right side of the glistening wet street is a white commercial service van with green side panels and phone lettering, ahead of which a silver SUV/minivan and a dark pickup truck are parked along the left curb beyond the slide zone.
  - The sky overhead is heavy, overcast, and gray following the winter rainstorm.
