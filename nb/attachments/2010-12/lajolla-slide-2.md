# Roadside Slope Landslide Scarp and Worker in La Jolla, California (`lajolla-slide-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A closer street-level photograph of the December 22, 2010 hillside landslide in **La Jolla, San Diego, California**, looking directly at the exposed brown mud scarp as a worker in a red hoodie walks along the coned-off street edge. The image is the second photograph in the December 22, 2010 blog post *"Active geology - landslide in San Diego"*.

## Scene Description
- **Landslide Scarp & Retaining Wall**:
  - In the center of the frame, a steep, freshly exposed scar of wet brown dirt and sand drops from the base of a stepped, cream-colored concrete-block retaining wall down to the street curb.
  - Three long, thin white PVC irrigation pipes protrude at angles from the upper portion of the exposed soil scarp where the surrounding hillside slumped away.
  - Dense green ornamental shrubs and groundcover flank both sides of the slide and sit in a displaced mound at the toe of the slope.
  - Above the retaining wall stand large tan/pinkish stucco homes with red Spanish-tile roofs, columned patios, glass deck railings, and palm trees under a breaking sky of blue patches and white clouds.
- **Foreground & Street Scene**:
  - A worker wearing a bright red hooded sweatshirt, dark jeans, and tan work boots walks from right to left across the wet black asphalt in front of the slumped bushes.
  - Five orange traffic cones (two with white reflective bands) are spaced across the foreground pavement around the mud and water runoff pooling in the gutter.
  - The rear corner and taillight of a white-and-green work van are visible at the far right edge of the frame.
