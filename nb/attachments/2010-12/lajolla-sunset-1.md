# Post-Storm Sunset in La Jolla, California (`lajolla-sunset-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format evening photograph taken in La Jolla, San Diego, California on December 23, 2010, capturing the warm post-storm sunset glow over coastal residential rooftops and succulent plants.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**: Dominated in the center-right by the dense, upward-reaching cylindrical stems of a firestick succulent (*Euphorbia tirucalli* 'Sticks on Fire'), silhouetted and tinted warm orange-red by the fading evening light.
- **Midground**: Spanish-style residential houses with dark terracotta tile roofs, white stucco walls, arched multi-pane windows, and dark green ornamental trees and shrubs sit just beyond the foreground plant. Two tall, slender palm trees stand side by side on the far right horizon.
- **Sky & Lighting**: Heavy, dark gray post-storm clouds hang across the upper left and center of the frame, breaking open toward the upper right to reveal a bright, pale yellow-white sky. Along the horizon beneath the cloud deck, the sky glows with a warm golden-orange gradient.
