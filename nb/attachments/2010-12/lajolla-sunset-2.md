# Bird of Paradise Silhouetted Against a La Jolla Sunset (`lajolla-sunset-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format photograph taken in La Jolla, San Diego, California on December 23, 2010, showing a Bird of Paradise (*Strelitzia*) flower silhouetted in profile against a bright post-storm sunset and framed by a patio enclosure.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground**: A striking Bird of Paradise blossom rises from the lower center and angles toward the left, with its boat-shaped bract and pointed orange and blue petals backlit and glowing translucent amber where the setting sun shines directly behind them.
- **Framing & Architecture**: White metal beams and vertical posts of a glass-paneled balcony or patio enclosure angle across the upper right and right side of the frame.
- **Background & Sky**: Beyond the enclosure, residential rooftops with chimneys and scattered trees—including the same pair of tall palm trees silhouetted near the center-right horizon—sit beneath a dramatic sky. Heavy slate-blue and gray storm clouds fill the upper left, while the clouds on the upper right catch warm salmon-pink and golden highlights from the setting sun positioned directly behind the flower.
