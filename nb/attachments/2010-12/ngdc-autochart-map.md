# NOAA NGDC AutoChart Bathymetric Contour Map (`ngdc-autochart-map.gif`)

## Overview
A bathymetric contour map generated by the beta version of the NOAA National Geophysical Data Center (NGDC) AutoChart web service using Generic Mapping Tools (GMT), covering a section of the western North Atlantic continental margin off the U.S. East Coast (from 30°N to 40°N, 75°W to 69°W).

## Map / Plot Details
- **Geographic & Bathymetric Features**:
  - Covers the outer continental shelf, continental slope, submarine canyons (including Hudson Canyon and neighboring canyons along the Mid-Atlantic Bight in the north), the Blake Ridge / Carolina Trough region in the south, and the abyssal plain extending toward the Hatteras Abyssal Plain.
  - Multibeam swath bathymetry coverage is shaded with a continuous color palette ranging from pale pink and tan on the upper slope (`-500 m` to `-2500 m`), through yellow-green on the continental rise (`-2900 m` to `-4100 m`), to bright green and cyan-green in the deep basin (`-4500 m` to `-5500 m`).
  - Unsurveyed areas appear white, clearly showing individual ship multibeam survey tracklines radiating across the lower right (southeastern) portion of the map and thin pink single-beam/trackline contours on the shallow continental shelf in the upper left.
- **Contour Lines**: Black isobath contour lines are plotted at regular intervals with bold labeled index contours at `-2000`, `-3000`, `-3500`, `-4000`, `-4500`, and `-5000` meters.
