# NOAA NEFSC Right Whale Sighting Advisory System on iPhone (`noaa-nefsc-iphone.png`)

## Overview
An iPhone mobile Safari screenshot displaying the newly launched NOAA Northeast Fisheries Science Center (NEFSC) North Atlantic Right Whale Sighting Survey (NARWSS) and Sighting Advisory System (SAS) webpage, captured at 9:37 AM on December 9, 2010.

- **iOS Status & Browser Bars (Top)**:
  - Status bar displays 1 signal bar (`AT&T`), Wi-Fi icon, `9:37 AM`, location services arrow, Bluetooth icon, and a full green battery icon.
  - Safari title bar reads `PSB:NARWSS and SAS`, above the URL field (`www.nefsc.noaa.gov/p...`) with a reload icon and the `Google` search box.
- **Webpage Layout**:
  - **Header**: Displays the `NOAA FISHERIES SERVICE` logo on the left, `Northeast Fisheries Science Center` on the right, a green search bar (`Search`, `Go` button, radio buttons for `PSB only`, `NEFSC`, `NMFS`), and a vertical `NOAA` watermark banner running down the dark blue right margin.

  - **Main Content**: Centered blue title (`North Atlantic Right Whale Sighting Survey and Sighting Advisory System`) beside a profile illustration of a North Atlantic right whale. Below is an embedded interactive Google Earth / Google Maps satellite view of the Gulf of Maine and New England coast covered in black whale-tail sighting icons, flanked on the right by seasonal layer checkboxes (`Spring`, `Summer`, `Winter`, `Fall`).
  - **Advisory Text (Bottom)**: Paragraph advising mariners of 10-knot speed restrictions in Seasonal Management Areas (SMAs) and Dynamic Management Areas (DMAs) for vessels 65 feet or greater, along with right whale sighting reporting phone numbers (`978-585-8473` from VA to ME, and `904-237-4220` from NC to FL).
