# NOAA NEFSC North Atlantic Right Whale Sighting Advisory System Webpage (`noaa-nefsc-sas.png`)

## Overview
A desktop web browser screenshot of the NOAA Northeast Fisheries Science Center (NEFSC) Protected Species Branch webpage for the North Atlantic Right Whale Sighting Survey (NARWSS) and Sighting Advisory System (SAS), posted on December 9, 2010.

## Interface & Map Details
- **Left Navigation Sidebar**: Vertical stack of blue navigation links:
  - `Sighting Advisory System`, `Right Whales`, `Conservation`, `Aerial Surveys`, `Shipboard Surveys`, `Other Sighting Sources`, `NARWSS Reports`, `Sighting Maps by Year`, `Sighting Details by Year`
  - Darker navy links below: `Protected Species Home`, `NEFSC Home`
  - Social sharing bar at the bottom: Facebook, email, favorites star, print icon, and `+ More`.
- **Header & Whale Illustration**:
  - Centered blue title text on the left: `North Atlantic Right Whale` / `Sighting Survey` / `and` / `Sighting Advisory System`.
  - On the right: A detailed lateral illustration of a dark North Atlantic right whale (*Eubalaena glacialis*) showing white callosities on its rostrum and a white belly patch, captioned `(right whale illustration courtesy of Pieter Folkens, ©2011)`.
- **Interactive Sightings Map**:
  - Introductory text above the map: `Interactive map of right whale sightings collected by the Sighting Advisory System between 2002 and 2009. Click on a symbol for more information on each sighting. Use the + and - to zoom in and out. Also, see more sightings made by the aerial survey team, mapped on OBIS.`
  - The embedded Google Maps (`Satellite` view) shows the northeastern U.S. and Canadian Maritimes from Long Island and Cape Cod across the Gulf of Maine to Nova Scotia and the Bay of Fundy.
  - Hundreds of custom black whale-fluke icons (created by Kurt Schwehr) with grey drop shadows are densely clustered across Cape Cod Bay, the Great South Channel, Stellwagen Bank, Jeffreys Ledge, the central Gulf of Maine, Jordan Basin, and south of Long Island.
  - Map controls include a circular pan wheel, orange Pegman Street View icon, vertical zoom slider on the left, `Satellite` dropdown in the upper right, `200 km` / `100 mi` scale bar at bottom left, and `Imagery ©2010 TerraMetrics - Terms of Use` at bottom right.
- **Seasonal Filter Panel (Right of Map)**:
  - Heading: `Right Whale Sightings by Season` / `Turn map layers on and off`
  - Four checkboxes: `Spring` (unchecked), `Summer` (unchecked), `Winter` (checked), and `Fall` (unchecked).
