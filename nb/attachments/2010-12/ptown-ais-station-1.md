# Provincetown AIS Station Rack After L3 ATON Upgrade (`ptown-ais-station-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph taken by Mike Thompson on December 14, 2010 inside the basement of the Cape Cod National Seashore Province Lands Visitor Center near Provincetown, Massachusetts, showing the equipment rack after upgrading the station to a yellow L3 Aid to Navigation (ATON) AIS transceiver unit.

## Hardware & Equipment Details
- **Upper Left Monitor**: A small black-bezeled rack-mounted LCD screen displays a Windows desktop running a web browser showing real-time AIS vessel targets (colored green, red, and yellow markers) around the hook of Cape Cod and Provincetown Harbor on EarthNC Online.
- **Upper Right 1U Rack Unit**: A black rack-mounted appliance featuring a blue backlit LCD screen (`Crystalfontz CFA-635` / `www.crystalfontz.com`), four vertical green/yellow status LEDs on the left, and a 5-button blue directional keypad with red/blue indicator icons on the right.
- **Middle Left (L3 ATON & Power Supply)**:
  - A bright yellow rectangular metal enclosure—the **L3 ATON AIS unit**—sits atop a grey power supply box (`CNS 6000` power/accessory unit).
  - Four grey DB9/DB25 serial cables with white adhesive labels are connected to a multi-port adapter on top of the yellow ATON unit, with a red spiral-wrapped cable harness looping across the front to the right side connectors.
- **Middle Right (Minimum Keyboard Display)**: A black gimbal-mounted `CNS Systems` Minimum Keyboard Display (MKD) unit with a green monochrome LCD screen above a 4×4 white membrane keypad (`1`-`9`, `0`, `MENU`, `ESC`, `ENT`, `DIM`, `ALT`, `CLR/ALARM`).
- **Background**: Painted grey concrete cinderblock wall with coiled coaxial, serial, and network cables.
