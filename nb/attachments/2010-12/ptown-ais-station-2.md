# Provincetown AIS Station Dual Equipment Racks Before Upgrade (`ptown-ais-station-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide photograph taken by Mike Thompson in December 2010 showing the full two-rack AIS and radio communications installation in the basement of the Cape Cod National Seashore Province Lands Visitor Center near Provincetown, Massachusetts, prior to installing the yellow L3 ATON unit.

## Hardware & Rack Layout
- **Left Rack (`BLACK BOX` 2-post open relay rack on a grey metal desk)**:
  - **Top**: Small black LCD monitor mounted on the upper left post (powered off), next to the white `BLACK BOX` logo on the top crossbar, and a 1U black unit with a blue Crystalfontz LCD and keypad. Behind the rack on the cinderblock wall is a white wall-mounted enclosure.
  - **Upper Shelf**: Grey power supply box on the left and the black gimbal-mounted CNS 6000 Minimum Keyboard Display (MKD) with its 16-button white keypad on the right.
  - **Middle Shelf**: Grey metallic CNS 6000 RF transceiver and controller unit with coaxial and serial cables attached to its front panel.
  - **Lower Shelves**: A 1U Tripp Lite horizontal rack PDU / power strip, a silver Mean Well (`MW`) switching power supply box sitting on a black rack keyboard tray with a white optical mouse, and a black APC Smart-UPS rackmount uninterruptible power supply at the bottom with green status LEDs illuminated.
- **Right Rack (Open 2-post frame on a wooden workbench)**:
  - **Background / Top**: Three tall, vertical white cylindrical VHF cavity filters / duplexer cans stand behind the rack, with black coaxial jumper loops and brass tuning rods protruding from their tops.
  - **Middle Shelf**: A small grey junction box on the left, a black 1U appliance with a Crystalfontz blue LCD and directional buttons in the center, and a small silver/black device on the right.
  - **Lower Shelf**: Blue network routers/modems (including a Linksys unit) and a black multi-port switch with red/green status LEDs sitting above a 1U power strip.
  - **Bottom**: A second black rackmount APC Smart-UPS unit with illuminated green battery/line status LEDs.
- **Foreground**: An orange canvas tool bag filled with hand tools sits on a small wooden stool in front of cardboard shipping boxes beneath the workbenches.
