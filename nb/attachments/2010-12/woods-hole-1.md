# Sun Pillar Over the Highway Near Woods Hole, Massachusetts (`woods-hole-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format photograph taken while traveling toward Woods Hole, Massachusetts around December 8, 2010, capturing a bright atmospheric sun pillar rising vertically above the low sun along a tree-lined highway.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Optical Phenomenon**: A vivid vertical column of bright white and golden-yellow light—an atmospheric sun pillar caused by sunlight reflecting off ice crystals in high clouds—extends straight upward from the setting/rising sun just above the horizon, piercing through a band of dark amber-lit clouds. The surrounding sky grades from deep fiery orange near the horizon to muted amber-grey and slate blue at the top of the frame.
- **Roadway & Horizon**: The dark asphalt highway stretches straight ahead toward the vanishing point directly beneath the sun, with faint dashed center lane markings and a solid white right edge line visible in the dim foreground. Distant vehicle headlights/taillights glint on the left side of the road, while dense, dark silhouettes of pine and bare deciduous trees line both sides of the highway.
