# Nobska Point Light Decorated with a Holiday Wreath in Woods Hole, Massachusetts (`woods-hole-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-format daytime photograph of Nobska Point Light (Nobska Lighthouse) and the adjacent keeper's house in Woods Hole, Massachusetts, decorated with a large holiday wreath under a crisp winter sky on December 8, 2010.

## Scene Description
- **Lighthouse & Keeper's House**:
  - At the center stands the cylindrical white cast-iron tower of Nobska Point Light with a black lantern room and gallery deck at the top. A large evergreen holiday wreath with a red bow at its base is mounted prominently on the front of the white tower.
  - Behind and flanking the tower is the historic dark-shingled keeper's dwelling with steep gables, dark red roofs, two brick chimneys, and white-trimmed multi-pane windows.
  - To the left of the lighthouse stands a white flagpole flying the U.S. flag above two smaller nautical/service flags, alongside a white picket fence and a low wooden barrier fence.
  - To the right of the keeper's house rises a tall, slender steel lattice radio/communications tower.
- **Foreground & Sky**: A sandy and grassy coastal path leads from the foreground toward the base of the bluff, bordered by dormant brown winter brush, coastal shrubs, and large glacial boulders. The sky above is a deep, vivid blue with a few small, wispy white clouds on the upper right.
