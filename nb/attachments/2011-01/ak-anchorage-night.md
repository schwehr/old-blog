# Anchorage Waterfront at Night (`ak-anchorage-night.jpg`)

## Overview
A nighttime photograph looking out from an elevated vantage point at the Hotel Captain Cook in downtown Anchorage, Alaska, toward the ice-covered waters of Knik Arm / Cook Inlet during January 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Architecture**:
  - Two multi-story modern office buildings frame the left and right sides of the image, warmly illuminated by orange sodium-vapor streetlamps and interior office lights shining through horizontal bands of windows.
  - Between the larger office towers sits a smaller commercial building with a parking lot containing several parked cars on the left, a two-story pitched-roof house in the center-right, and a flat-roofed two-story building in the lower center.
  - A dark diagonal roofline or window ledge cuts across the bottom-right corner of the frame.
- **Midground (Cook Inlet / Knik Arm)**:
  - Beyond a dark coastal bluff line with bare winter trees, an expanse of sea ice and mudflats stretches across the water, reflecting a muted orange-brown glow from the city lights.
  - Dark leads and fractures in the ice are visible on the left side.
- **Background**:
  - Across the inlet, a dark horizon line with a few faint, distant pinpoint lights separates the frozen water from the pitch-black night sky.
