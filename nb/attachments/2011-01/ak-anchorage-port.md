# Port of Anchorage in Winter (`ak-anchorage-port.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime panoramic photograph of the Port of Anchorage and the ice-filled waters of Knik Arm (Cook Inlet) in Anchorage, Alaska, taken from an upper floor of the Hotel Captain Cook in January 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Right Edge**:
  - In the lower-right corner, the angled reddish-brown metal frame and reflective glass panels of a modern building's peaked roof structure are visible, with bare winter tree branches just below along the bottom edge.
  - A snow-covered shoreline curves from the lower right toward the center left, bordered by large flat sheets of gray shorefast and floating sea ice.
- **Midground (Port Facilities & Waterway)**:
  - Extending along the snowy spit and industrial waterfront on the right are shipping containers (in blue, orange, white, and red), storage tanks, industrial buildings, and tall port cranes rising against the sky.
  - On the far left, a long pier or trestle extends out into the icy gray water toward a small tug or work boat.
- **Background**:
  - Across the gray, ice-strewn waters of Knik Arm, the snow-speckled bluffs and low hills of the opposite shore (Point MacKenzie / Matanuska-Susitna Borough) stretch along the left and far right horizon.
  - Overhead, alayered overcast sky shows soft pale yellow and peach-tinted cloud bands above the distant horizon.
