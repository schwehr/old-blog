# Lynx and Hare Ice Sculpture in Anchorage (`ak-cat.jpg`)

## Overview
A nighttime photograph of an illuminated ice sculpture titled "The Predator," depicting a lynx pursuing a snowshoe hare in Town Square Park, downtown Anchorage, Alaska, in January 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Ice Sculpture**:
  - Dominating the center of the frame is a large, intricately carved translucent ice sculpture of an Alaskan lynx (wild cat) stepping down a slanted ice rock formation.
  - The lynx has pointed ear tufts, textured fur markings carved into the ice, and large paws brightly backlit by warm white/orange ground lights.
  - At the lower left base of the slanted ice ledge sits a smaller carved ice rabbit/hare facing left.
- **Visible Text & Labels**:
  - Carved into the base of the ice rock beneath the lynx's front paws are the words **"The Predator"**.
  - Faintly visible on the dark building facade in the background behind the sculpture's front legs is signage reading **"...VENUS OUTFITTER..."**.
- **Background**:
  - Behind the sculpture is a multi-story parking garage or commercial building with illuminated interior decks and a lit ground-floor storefront on the left where a few pedestrians are visible.
  - Trees flanking both the left and right sides of the park are wrapped in strings of bright blue holiday lights.
