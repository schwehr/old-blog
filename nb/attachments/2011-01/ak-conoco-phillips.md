# Evening Sun Reflecting off the ConocoPhillips Building (`ak-conoco-phillips.jpg`)

## Overview
A late-afternoon photograph of the downtown Anchorage, Alaska skyline in January 2011, featuring a brilliant golden reflection of the low winter sun off the glass facade of the ConocoPhillips tower.

## Scene Description
- **Central Subject**:
  - The tall, boxy ConocoPhillips building stands at center, with horizontal bands of dark windows on its shaded left face and a blazing, star-like golden-white reflection of the setting sun illuminating its right-hand glass facade.
  - On the upper right facade of the tower, the **ConocoPhillips** logo is faintly visible above the glare.
- **Surrounding Skyline**:
  - **Left Foreground**: A brown-and-tan grid-patterned high-rise facade (part of the Hotel Captain Cook complex) frames the left edge of the image.
  - **Left Midground**: A sleek, blue-green glass office tower reflects the sky and clouds, with a multi-level white concrete parking garage in front of it.
  - **Right Midground**: Another tall beige/tan commercial building stands on the right, with a dark octagonal rooftop structure in the lower center foreground.
- **Background**:
  - Beyond the city buildings, the snow-covered peaks of the Chugach Mountains rise against a clear blue-to-amber gradient winter sky, with wisps of steam rising from building rooftops in the cold air.
