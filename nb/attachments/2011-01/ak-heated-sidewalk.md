# Heated Sidewalk at the Anchorage Museum (`ak-heated-sidewalk.jpg`)

## Overview
A daytime street-level photograph showing a completely dry, snow-free heated plaza sidewalk outside the Anchorage Museum in downtown Anchorage, Alaska, contrasting with the surrounding winter snow in January 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Heated Walkway)**:
  - A wide expanse of dark gray square concrete pavers stretches from the bottom foreground toward the street, completely bare and dry due to underground sidewalk heating.
  - To the right of the dark gray pavers, a pristine blanket of white snow covers the adjacent ground, forming a sharp diagonal boundary along the edge of the heated zone.
- **Left Side (Museum Facade)**:
  - A modern curtain wall of tall vertical glass panels lines the left side of the walkway, reflecting thebeige-and-windowed office building across the street.
- **Background**:
  - Across the street sits a multi-story light-beige commercial building with three long horizontal bands of dark windows.
  - Along the right side of the plaza, young bare deciduous trees and tall black light poles stand in the snow, with snow-dusted evergreen spruce trees and a parked silver sedan visible near the street on the far right.
