# Kurt Schwehr with Polar Bear Ice Sculpture (`ak-kurt.jpg`)

## Overview
A nighttime photograph of Kurt Schwehr posing behind a carved polar bear ice sculpture labeled "ALASKA" in a downtown Anchorage, Alaska park during January 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Ice Sculpture)**:
  - A clear, detailed ice sculpture of a polar bear walking toward the left stands atop a thick rectangular ice pedestal.
  - Carved into the front face of the ice block base in large capital letters is the word **"ALASKA"**, illuminated by bright white lighting from below.
- **Subject**:
  - Standing just behind the polar bear sculpture is Kurt Schwehr, wearing glasses, a black knit beanie hat, and a black-and-green winter jacket (with "Columbia" visible on the right chest), looking directly at the camera.
- **Background**:
  - To the right behind Kurt is a low wall built from large rectangular blocks of snow/ice.
  - To the left and in the background, a snow- and ice-covered park plaza shows other illuminated ice sculptures (including one lit in bright green light) and trees decorated with blue and multicolored holiday lights.
