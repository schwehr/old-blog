# Moonrise Over the Chugach Mountains and Anchorage (`ak-moon-rise.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide panoramic photograph of downtown Anchorage, Alaska, and the snow-covered Chugach Mountains illuminated by warm golden light, with a faint full moon rising just to the left of the mountain range in January 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Midground (Anchorage Skyline)**:
  - Several mid-rise and high-rise buildings occupy the foreground, catching warm orange-gold sunlight on their western faces while casting long shadows.
  - Prominent on the right is the white multi-story Hilton Anchorage hotel topped by a golden-lit upper tower and antenna spire, with a warm-toned brick/concrete building in the lower-right corner.
  - Plumes of white steam rise vertically from several building heating vents into the crisp winter air.
- **Background (Chugach Range & Moon)**:
  - Stretching from the center to the right horizon is the jagged, snow-draped crest of the Chugach Mountains, glowing in pink and golden alpenglow.
  - Just to the left of where the mountain ridge slopes down toward the flat horizon (around the left-center of the sky), a pale, circular moon is faintly visible in the peach-and-blue twilight haze.
