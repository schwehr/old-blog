# Ship Leaving the Port of Anchorage Across Ice-Covered Cook Inlet (`ak-shipping-out.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide panoramic photograph taken from the 15th floor of the Hotel Captain Cook in Anchorage, Alaska, showing a large cargo ship outbound across the ice-covered waters of Cook Inlet at dusk/sunset in January 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Left Side**:
  - On the far left stands a modern multi-story glass-and-concrete office building catching warm orange sunset reflections in its upper windows.
  - Along the bottom of the image, fractured shorefast ice and snow-covered mudflats border the Anchorage coastline.
- **Midground (Cook Inlet & Vessel)**:
  - A vast expanse of blue-gray sea ice and leads stretches across Cook Inlet from left to right.
  - Slightly right of center in the distance, a large cargo/container ship is visible in profile heading left (outbound from the Port of Anchorage) through the icy channel.
- **Background**:
  - Along the distant horizon on the right half of the frame rises the silhouetted profile of the Alaska Range (including Mount Susitna / "Sleeping Lady" and surrounding peaks), while a flat bluff line extends on the left.
  - The clear winter sky transitions from a warm golden-orange glow along the horizon to pale yellow and cool blue overhead, streaked with wisping clouds on the left.
