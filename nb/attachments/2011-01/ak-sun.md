# Sun Dog in the Evening Sky Over Anchorage (`ak-sun.jpg`)

## Overview
A late-afternoon photograph looking out over Anchorage, Alaska, capturing a bright winter sun alongside a distinct rainbow-tinted sun dog (parhelion) in the sky in January 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Atmospheric Phenomenon**:
  - On the left side of the sky, the low winter sun blazes as a large, brilliant white-and-gold orb surrounded by an amber glow.
  - To the right of center, at roughly the same altitude above the horizon as the sun, a vertical, prismatic **sun dog** (parhelion) glows brightly in red/orange on its inner (sun-facing) edge transitioning to yellow, white, and faint blue on its outer right side.
  - Scattered altocumulus and cirrus clouds drift across a soft blue-and-gold sky.
- **Foreground & Cityscape**:
  - Across the bottom of the frame, the dark, shadowed expanse of Anchorage stretches toward the flat distant horizon, with small white plumes of steam rising from buildings in the cold air.
  - In the lower-right corner, the dark silhouette of a multi-story building with rooftop antennas rises against the twilight sky.
