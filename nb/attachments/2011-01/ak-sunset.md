# Sunset Over Anchorage and Cook Inlet (`ak-sunset.jpg`)

## Overview
A twilight photograph of a vibrant orange-and-gold sunset over Anchorage, Alaska, and Cook Inlet in January 2011, with an aircraft contrail streaking across the upper left sky.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Sunset**:
  - At the center of the horizon, the setting sun glows brightly in yellow-white surrounded by bands of deep orange and gold illuminating a low layer of clouds.
  - Above the cloud band, the clear sky transitions into cool slate-blue, with a sharp, thin white jet contrail angled downward from the top-left corner toward the upper center-left illuminated by the setting sun.
- **Foreground & Cityscape**:
  - On the right stands the dark silhouette of a modern multi-story office building with a faintly orange-lit upper glass window band and a small strip of icy Cook Inlet water visible at the far right edge.
  - Across the lower left and center, low- and mid-rise buildings and trees of Anchorage sit in deep twilight shadow, with a tan multi-story building visible at the bottom center.
