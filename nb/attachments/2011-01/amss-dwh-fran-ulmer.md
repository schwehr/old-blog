# Fran Ulmer Speaking on the Deepwater Horizon Commission at AMSS (`amss-dwh-fran-ulmer.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken from the audience during the plenary session of the Alaska Marine Science Symposium (AMSS) at the Hotel Captain Cook in Anchorage, Alaska, in January 2011, showing University of Alaska Chancellor and National Oil Spill Commission member Fran Ulmer delivering a talk on the Deepwater Horizon disaster.

## Scene Description
- **Speaker & Stage**:
  - Just below the right corner of the large projection screen, Fran Ulmer stands behind a dark wooden podium with the Hotel Captain Cook ship-logo crest on its front, illuminated by a podium light.
  - On the far right wall hangs a vertical blue-and-green banner for the **Alaska Marine Science Symposium**.
  - A bright crystal chandelier hangs from the ceiling at the top right.
- **Projected Slide**:
  - A large overhead projection screen at top center displays a slide with a light-blue title banner reading **"Preventing Accidents: Changing Business As Usual"**.
  - The main slide image shows an aerial photograph of two semi-submersible offshore drilling rigs on blue water (the *Development Driller III* and *Discoverer Enterprise* / relief well operations at the Macondo site).
  - In the lower-left corner of the slide, white text reads **"...Compelling Need [for a] Culture of Safety"** above a small white rectangular logo box.
- **Foreground (Audience)**:
  - The backs of several seated conference attendees fill the foreground, with a person in a brown shirt on the left and a person with shoulder-length brown hair in an olive top on the right.
