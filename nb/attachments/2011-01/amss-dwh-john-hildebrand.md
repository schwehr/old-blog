# John Hildebrand Presenting at the AMSS Deepwater Horizon Workshop (`amss-dwh-john-hildebrand.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of Scripps Institution of Oceanography researcher John Hildebrand speaking at a podium in front of a projected schematic diagram during the Deepwater Horizon Lessons Learned workshop at the Alaska Marine Science Symposium (AMSS) in Anchorage, Alaska, in January 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Speaker & Podium**:
  - John Hildebrand stands in profile facing left behind a dark wooden podium with a gooseneck microphone, wearing eyeglasses and a light yellow/cream long-sleeved dress shirt.
- **Projected Slide**:
  - Behind him, a large projection screen viewed at a steep angle displays a scientific schematic cross-section of the ocean water column titled **"Schematic..."** in white text at the top left.
  - The diagram illustrates a deepwater oil and gas blowout plume rising from the seafloor along a sloped continental margin on the right, showing subsurface plumes ("Dissolved gas and oil"), rising droplets ("Oil droplets rise to surface"), surface slicks, and a marine mammal (whale/cetacean silhouette) near the surface wave line on the upper left.
  - In the bottom-right corner of the slide, a citation in white text reads **"... et al. 2010"**.
- **Room Environment**:
  - The hotel ballroom's coffered ceiling with recessed circular downlights and warm brown paneled walls are visible on the left.
