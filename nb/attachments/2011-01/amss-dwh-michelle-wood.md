# Michelle Wood Presenting at the AMSS Deepwater Horizon Workshop (`amss-dwh-michelle-wood.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of NOAA researcher Michelle Wood speaking at the podium during the Deepwater Horizon Lessons Learned workshop at the Alaska Marine Science Symposium (AMSS) in the Hotel Captain Cook ballroom in Anchorage, Alaska, in January 2011.

- **Speaker & Podium**:
  - On the far right, Michelle Wood stands in profile facing left behind a dark wooden Hotel Captain Cook podium with a gooseneck microphone, wearing a dark patterned top and looking toward the audience/stage monitor.
  - The edge of a bright white projection screen is visible directly behind her on the upper right.
- **Conference Room & Equipment**:
  - In the lower center, an angled flat-screen confidence monitor sits on a draped stand facing the podium so the speaker can view their slides.

  - In the foreground on the left, a blonde audience member in a dark jacket is seated facing the stage, with additional workshop attendees seated at round tables in the background against the warm brown walls of the ballroom.
