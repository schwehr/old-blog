# Phil McGillivary Introducing the AMSS Deepwater Horizon Workshop (`amss-dwh-phil-mcgillivary.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of U.S. Coast Guard science liaison Phil McGillivary speaking at the podium to introduce the evening workshop on Lessons Learned from the Deepwater Horizon oil spill at the Alaska Marine Science Symposium (AMSS) in Anchorage, Alaska, in January 2011.
