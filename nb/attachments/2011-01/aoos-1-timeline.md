# AOOS Transition Data Portal: Models and Remote Sensing Timeline (`aoos-1-timeline.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X Safari web browser screenshot showing the Alaska Ocean Observing System (AOOS) Flex-based web mapping application (`http://data.aoos.org/maps/coverage.php`), displaying surface wind model fields and an interactive timeline preview control over Alaska and surrounding seas in January 2011.

## Interface & Controls
- **Browser Chrome**:
  - **Window Title**: `AOOS Transition Data Portal: Models and remote sensing`
  - **Address Bar**: `http://data.aoos.org/maps/coverage.php` alongside a Google search bar in the top right.
  - **Status Bar (bottom)**: `One error in opening the page. For more information, choose Window > Activity.`
- **Top Application Header**:
  - Left title: **Models and remote sensing** with a blue question-mark help icon.
  - Right navigation tabs: **Arctic Assets**, **North Pacific Seabirds**, **Real Time Sensors**, and the **AOOS** logo.
- **Right Sidebar Panels**:
  - **Legend Panel**:
    - NOAA emblem and source title: **Wave Watch III (WW3) - 1/16° x 1/8° - North Pacific Marine**
    - Active layer: **Wind at the Surface**
    - **Local time displayed**: `Thu Jan 20 2011 12:00:00 AM`
    - **UTC time displayed**: `2011-01-20T09:00:00UTC`
    - Checkbox (unchecked): **Virtual sensor mode**
    - Slider: **Layer opacity** (set to 100% on the right)
    - Horizontal color scale bar ranging from **30** (red, left) through **22.5** (orange/yellow), **15** (green/cyan), **7.5** (light blue) to **0** (dark blue, right) in units of **m/s**.
  - **Layers Panel**:
    - Toggle links: `Sensors | All layers` and a **Search** text input box.
    - Category list: **Air Temperature**, **Currents**, **Ground Temperature**, **Precipitation**, **Salinity**, **Snow Depth**, **Snow Water Equivalent**, **Water Level (Tides)**, **Water Temperature**, **Waves**, **Winds**.

## Map & Timeline Details
- **Main Map View**:
  - Uses a grayscale **B&W Terrain** base map (selected in the top-right map dropdown) showing **Alaska**, eastern Russia/Chukotka, and **Yukon**.
  - Overlaid across the Bering Sea, Chukchi Sea, Beaufort Sea, and Gulf of Alaska is a gridded color-shaded wind speed field with black directional wind vector arrows. Strong winds (yellow-orange, ~15–25 m/s) are visible in the western Bering Sea and south of the Aleutians.
  - Standard Google pan/zoom controls appear in the upper left, a red vertical **feedback** tab on the middle left edge, a `200 mi / 500 km` scale bar and `POWERED BY Google` logo in the bottom left, and an inset overview map in the bottom right.
- **Interactive Timeline Control (Bottom)**:
  - A horizontal playback scrub bar sits below the map with step-backward/step-forward buttons on the left and play/step buttons on the right.
  - A red triangle marker on the timeline indicates the currently displayed time, while hovering/scrubbing at a yellow triangle marker pops up a thumbnail preview window showing the forecast map state for **`Sat Jan 22 2011 09:00:00 AM`**.
