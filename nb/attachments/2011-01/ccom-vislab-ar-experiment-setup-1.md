# CCOM VisLab Augmented Reality Experiment Setup (`ccom-vislab-ar-experiment-setup-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken by Colleen Mitchell in the Visualization Lab (VisLab) at the University of New Hampshire's Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM). It shows researcher Roland Arsenault standing inside a curved, multi-projector panoramic simulator display running FlightGear, testing a head-tracked and handheld augmented reality (AR) / mobile device setup.

## Scene Description
- **Panoramic Projection Screen**: A wide, semi-cylindrical white fabric projection screen stretches across the background, tensioned along top and bottom curved metal truss/pipe frames with evenly spaced lacing cords.
- **Projected Simulation**: Three seamlessly adjacent projector views display a virtual maritime/coastal scene from FlightGear, showing a bright blue sky with scattered white clouds above a gray water surface and a distant city skyline with high-rise buildings along the horizon.
- **Ceiling Projectors & Rigging**: Mounted to an overhead aluminum framework below the acoustic tile drop ceiling are multiple black digital projectors (at least four visible in the foreground cluster) with power and video cables routed along the overhead rails, plus small white track spotlights.
- **Subject & Equipment**:
  - Roland Arsenault stands in the right-center foreground facing left toward the screen, wearing a dark long-sleeved shirt (`DROID DOES` partially visible), jeans, and a wide belt.
  - He wears a black head-mounted tracking sensor / eyepiece rig with a small boom-mounted optical display/camera positioned in front of his eyes and trailing cables behind his back.
  - In both hands, he holds a small handheld device / controller with a strap.
- **Workstations & Hardware**:
  - In front of Roland on a low table is the back of a flat-panel LCD monitor, a gray electronic control box with a small tracking sensor/emitter on top, a computer tower, a mouse, and a wooden mount holding a cylindrical tracking bar.
  - To the far right is a wooden computer desk with another LCD monitor, keyboard, and black office chair next to a doorway with a wood-grain door.
