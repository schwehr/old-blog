# Portrait of Captain James Cook with "A Chart" (`cook-a-chart.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a framed oil portrait of Captain James Cook displayed inside the Hotel Captain Cook in Anchorage, Alaska, inspired by Nathaniel Dance-Holland's famous 1776 portrait of the British explorer.

## Scene Description
- **Portrait Subject**: Captain James Cook is depicted seated in three-quarter view against a dark, muted green-brown background, wearing a powdered white wig tied back, a white cravat, a buttoned cream waistcoat with gold trim,cream breeches, and a dark navy Royal Navy captain's coat with gold-braided cuffs and lapels.
- **Table & Objects**:
  - On the table to the left rests Cook's dark blue tricorn hat trimmed with gold braid, sitting atop a thick green-bound volume.
  - Spread across the table in the foreground is a large parchment nautical chart. Cook's right hand rests on the chart with his index finger pointing to a feature, while his left hand holds the right edge of the paper.
- **Visible Text on the Chart**: In the lower-left corner of the painted parchment map, upside-down relative to the viewer, the chart is generically inscribed in capital serif letters: `A CHART` along with the partial date `177...`.
- **Framing**: The right and left edges of a warm wooden picture frame are visible along the borders of the photograph.
