# 2011 Alaska Marine Science Symposium BP Deepwater Horizon Intro Slide (`lessons-workshop.png`)

## Overview
A presentation title slide created by Kurt Schwehr for the evening workshop on "Lessons Learned from the Deepwater Horizon Spill in the Gulf of Mexico" at the 2011 Alaska Marine Science Symposium (AMSS) in Anchorage, Alaska.

## Visual & Text Details
- **Background Photograph**:
  - A dramatic aerial photograph of the *Deepwater Horizon* offshore drilling rig engulfed in bright orange flames and billowing thick black smoke into the sky.
  - Five offshore supply and firefighting vessels surround the burning rig on the dark blue water of the Gulf of Mexico, spraying high-pressure arcs of water toward the fire while orange light from the blaze reflects across the sea surface in the foreground.
- **Text Overlays**:
  - Across the top in white sans-serif lettering:
    - `2011 Alaska Marine Science Symposium`
  - Centered in the upper middle inside a semi-transparent dark gray rectangular box with a thin white border:
    - `BP Deepwater Horizon`
    - `Lessons Learned`
    - `Presentations`
  - Centered in the lower middle inside a wider semi-transparent dark gray rectangular box with a thin white border:
    - `http://tinyurl.com/BpLessons`
