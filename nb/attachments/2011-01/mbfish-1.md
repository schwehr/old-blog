# Midwater Multibeam Sonar 3D Prototype — Full Swath Sequence (`mbfish-1.png`)

## Overview
A 3D visualization rendering created in 2006 by Kurt Schwehr using Coin3D, showing an early prototype for displaying midwater column multibeam sonar pings collected over the University of New Hampshire (UNH) offshore aquaculture fish cages.

## Visual Details
- **Layout & Perspective**: Set against a solid black background, a series of 19 parallel, semi-transparent rectangular cross-sectional slices (representing sequential multibeam sonar swath pings) are arranged diagonally from the lower left (foreground) to the upper right (background).
- **Sonar Swath Content**:
  - Each rectangular slice contains a triangular fan-shaped sonar swath radiating downward from the transducer apex at the top center of the frame.
  - Water-column acoustic backscatter is colored in shades of dark and bright blue, with radial beam artifacts and curved sidelobe arcs visible within the fan.
  - Near the bottom of each swath, a prominent horizontal yellow-green and cyan high-intensity band marks the acoustic return of the seafloor.
  - Above the seafloor near the center of the swath, a compact bright cyan/blue acoustic target (the submerged fish cage and fish school) is suspended in the midwater column.
- **Highlighted Slice**: The 14th slice from the front (near the upper right) is highlighted at full brightness with an opaque white background outside the sonar fan, making the bright cyan seafloor reflection and the suspended midwater fish-cage target stand out sharply against the dimmer, semi-transparent gray-bordered slices before and after it.
