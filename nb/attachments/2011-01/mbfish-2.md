# Midwater Multibeam Sonar 3D Prototype — Close-Up Oblique View (`mbfish-2.png`)

## Overview
A close-up 3D rendering created with Coin3D showing an oblique view of sequential midwater column multibeam sonar swath slices over the UNH fish cages, with one central ping slice highlighted at full opacity.

## Visual Details
- **Layout & Perspective**: Set against a black background, 9 parallel rectangular sonar ping slices are arranged from upper left to lower right in a closer perspective than `mbfish-1.png`.
- **Highlighted Central Slice**:
  - The 5th slice from the upper left is rendered at high brightness with a solid white upper background framing the triangular blue multibeam fan.
  - Inside the blue swath, radial beam lines extend from the top-center apex down to a bright yellow, orange, and cyan horizontal seafloor echo along the bottom edge.
  - Just above the seafloor, slightly right of the central nadir line, a distinct bright cyan blob represents the acoustic return from the midwater fish cage / fish school, accompanied by a faint semicircular sidelobe ring curving upward to the left and right.
- **Surrounding Semi-Transparent Slices**: Four dimmer, semi-transparent gray-backed slices precede the highlighted ping toward the upper left, and four semi-transparent slices follow it toward the lower right, allowing the 3D progression of the seafloor and midwater target to be traced through the volume.
