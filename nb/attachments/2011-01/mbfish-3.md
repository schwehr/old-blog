# Midwater Multibeam Sonar 3D Prototype — Axial Tunnel View (`mbfish-3.png`)

## Overview
A 3D visualization rendering created with Coin3D looking directly along the vessel's trackline through a stack of semi-transparent midwater multibeam sonar swath slices over the UNH fish cages.

## Visual Details
- **Layout & Perspective**: Viewed head-on in one-point perspective against a black border, approximately 10 parallel rectangular sonar ping frames recede from the large foreground slice toward a brightly illuminated slice at the far end of the stack, creating a concentric tunnel-like effect.
- **Sonar Features**:
  - Each overlapping frame shows the triangular multibeam fan radiating downward from the top center, with translucent gray/white upper corners outside the swath.
  - The cumulative overlap of the semi-transparent blue swaths creates a glowing cyan central corridor along the nadir beam and across the horizontal seafloor reflection at the bottom.
  - On the brightly lit rear slice at the center of the tunnel, the triangular blue swath, semicircular sidelobe arc, bright cyan seafloor line, and the suspended cyan midwater target (the UNH fish cage just right of the central beam) are clearly visible.
  - In the foreground slices along the bottom edge, the seafloor return appears as a mottled yellow-green horizontal band.
