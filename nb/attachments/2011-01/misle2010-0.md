# USCG MISLE Vessel Incidents Global View (`misle2010-0.png`)

## Overview

A cropped screenshot of the 3D globe in Google Earth displaying a global overview of maritime casualty and pollution incidents from the July 2010 public snapshot of the United States Coast Guard (USCG) Marine Information for Safety and Law Enforcement (MISLE) database, visualized using custom ship incident icons.

## Globe & Spatial Distribution Details

- **Globe Orientation**: The 3D Earth globe is centered over North America against a black starfield background, showing the contiguous United States, Alaska, Canada, Greenland, Mexico, Central America, the Caribbean, northern South America, and the eastern Pacific and western Atlantic oceans.
- **Dense Coastal & Waterway Clusters**:
  - **East Coast, Gulf Coast, & Inland Waterways**: Extremely dense overlapping clusters of colorful square incident icons cover the US Atlantic coast, the Gulf of Mexico, the Great Lakes, and the Mississippi/Ohio/Missouri river systems across the eastern half of the United States.
  - **West Coast & Alaska**: A continuous band of dense icons runs up the Pacific coast of Mexico, California, Oregon, Washington, British Columbia, and throughout southern, southeastern, and western Alaska (including the Aleutian Islands and Bering Sea).
  - **Hawaii & Offshore Pacific**: A dense cluster marks the Hawaiian Islands in the central Pacific (left), surrounded by scattered offshore incident markers across the Pacific Ocean.
  - **Caribbean & Atlantic**: Clusters appear around Puerto Rico, the US Virgin Islands, and shipping lanes across the western Atlantic and Caribbean Sea.
  - **High-Latitude & Anomalous Points**: Scattered icons appear far offshore, along the northern coast of Alaska, over the Greenland ice sheet (due to erroneous coordinates in the raw dataset), and near the southern edge of the visible globe.
- **Incident Icons**: Each placemark uses a small, color-coded square icon (designed by Colleen Mitchell) in shades of orange, pink, light blue, yellow, red, green, and purple representing different MISLE event types (such as groundings, allisions, collisions, material failures, sinkings, and pollution/environmental damage).
