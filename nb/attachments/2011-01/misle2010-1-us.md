# USCG MISLE Public Database in Google Earth - North America View (`misle2010-1-us.png`)

## Overview

A full application screenshot of Google Earth on Mac OS X displaying 69,049 parsed vessel incident placemarks from the USCG Marine Information for Safety and Law Enforcement (MISLE) public database (`misle.kml`), spanning from 1994 to 2010 and categorized into folders by event type.

- **Mac OS X Menu Bar**: At the very top, the menu bar displays the Apple icon, `Google Earth`, `File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Tools`, `Add`, `Window`, `Help`, and system status icons on the right including network throughput (`Tx: 0B/s`, `Rx: 0B/s`) and CPU/memory graphs (`12%`, `12%`).
- **Search Panel (Top Left)**: Expanded `Search` pane with `Fly To` (active tab), `Find Businesses`, and `Directions` tabs, a text input box with prompt `Fly to e.g., San Francisco`, and a magnifying glass search button.

## 3D Globe & Timeline Overlay

- **Time Slider Widget**: In the upper-left corner of the 3D viewport, the Google Earth timeline control shows the selected time range from `Jun 23, 1994` to `Jun 12, 2010` on a bar labeled `1994` to `2010`, accompanied by playback, loop, zoom in/out, and wrench (options) buttons.
- **Globe Visualization**: Centered over North America (`lat 41.128736°`, `lon -96.876311°`, `elev 422 m`, `Eye alt 6588.99 km`), showing thousands of color-coded square icons densely packed along the US West Coast, Alaska, the US East Coast, the Gulf of Mexico, the Great Lakes, and the Mississippi River basin, with scattered points across the Pacific and Atlantic oceans and over Greenland.
