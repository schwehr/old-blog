# USCG MISLE Public Database in Google Earth - New England View (`misle2010-2-new-england.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of Google Earth on Mac OS X zoomed in to the New England coastline (including Maine, New Hampshire, Massachusetts, Cape Cod, and the Gulf of Maine), displaying filtered maritime incident placemarks from the USCG MISLE database between February 2006 and June 2010.

## Sidebar & Filter State

- **Places Panel (Left Sidebar)**: Under `Temporary Places` -> `misle.kml`, the full folder names and incident counts are visible because the pane is slightly wider:
  - Checked folders: `Personnel Casualties (1)`, `Set Adrift (3315)`, `Implosion (2)`, `Material Failure (Diving) (8)`, `Grounding (8848)`, `Evasive Maneuvers (425)`, `Falls into Water (28)`, `Material Failure (Non-vessels) (311)`, `Sinking (2065)`, `Fouling (406)`, `Explosion (84)`, `Blowout (15)`, `Fire (1333)`, `Damage to Cargo (95)`, `Loss of Electrical Power (1147)`, `Loss of Stability (222)`, `Allision (7883)`, `Flooding (2819)`, `Capsize (375)`, `Abandonment (360)`, `Collision (3543)`.
  - Unchecked folders (hidden to reduce clutter): `Damage to the Environment (11793)`, `Vessel Maneuverability (8430)` (highlighted in light blue), `Emergency Response (1196)`, and `Material Failure (Vessels) (14345)`.
- **Time Slider Control**: Positioned at the top left of the map view, spanning `1996` to `2010`, with the active time window narrowed to `Feb 4, 2006` through `Jun 13, 2010`.

## Map View & Incident Icons

- **Geographic Area**: Shows the New England coast from southern Maine down through New Hampshire, Boston Harbor, Cape Cod, Buzzards Bay, Martha's Vineyard, and Nantucket, extending offshore into the Gulf of Maine and Georges Bank (`lat 43.788921°`, `lon -71.492637°`, `elev 260 m`, `Eye alt 325.03 km`, scale bar `96 km`).
- **Custom Incident Symbology**: Distinct square custom icons designed by Colleen Mitchell are clearly recognizable at this zoom level:
  - **Light blue squares with a boat grounded on a palm-tree island**: `Grounding` incidents clustered along the coast, Cape Cod, and islands.
  - **Orange squares with a boat on blue waves**: `Set Adrift` incidents scattered both nearshore and far offshore in the Gulf of Maine.
  - **Green squares with a white vessel striking a vertical pier/wall**: `Allision` incidents near ports such as Portland, Portsmouth, Boston, and New Bedford.
  - **Teal squares with a green vessel and orange flames**: `Fire` incidents.
  - **Red squares with a sinking yellow/white vessel and bubbles**: `Sinking` incidents.
  - **Dark blue squares with a red-hulled ship viewed bow-on with water flooding**: `Flooding` incidents.
  - **Purple squares with a yellow boat tilting/capsizing**: `Capsize` incidents.
  - **Pink/purple squares with a wine/martini glass**: `Personnel Casualties` / alcohol-related or specific incident markers offshore.
  - **Orange squares with a black question mark (`?`)**: Unclassified or miscellaneous event types (`Collision` / other).
- **Attribution**: Bottom text reads `Image USDA Farm Service Agency` and `Data SIO, NOAA, U.S. Navy, NGA, GEBCO`.
