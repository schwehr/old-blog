# USCG MISLE Public Database in Google Earth - New Hampshire Seacoast View (`misle2010-3-nh.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of Google Earth on Mac OS X zoomed in to the New Hampshire seacoast, Great Bay, the Piscataqua River, Portsmouth Harbor, and the Isles of Shoals, showing USCG MISLE vessel incidents from 1994 to 2010 with all event categories enabled.

## Interface & Sidebar State

- **Places Panel (Left Sidebar)**: All 25 incident category folders under `Temporary Places` -> `misle.kml` are checked, including `Damage to the Environment (11793)`, `Vessel Maneuverability (8430)`, `Emergency Response (1196)`, and `Material Failure (Vessels) (14345)`.
- **Time Slider Control**: Located at the upper left of the map view, showing the full date span from `Aug 16, 1994` to `Jun 13, 2010`.

## Map View & Local Incident Details

- **Geographic Features**:
  - In the center-left is **Great Bay** and **Little Bay** in New Hampshire, with the bright white runways of **Pease International Tradeport** (Portsmouth) clearly visible just east of the bay.
  - Flowing southeast from Little Bay to the Atlantic Ocean is the **Piscataqua River**, separating New Hampshire (Portsmouth/New Castle on the south bank) from southern Maine (Kittery/Eliot on the north bank).
  - In the lower-right offshore area are the **Isles of Shoals** surrounded by a patch of higher-resolution aerial imagery against the blue bathymetric background.
  - Map coordinates at the bottom read `lat 43.196334°`, `lon -70.879284°`, `elev 39 m`, `Eye alt 29.92 km`, with a scale bar of `8.66 km`.
- **Incident Markers**:
  - **Orange squares with a green leaf**: `Damage to the Environment` (pollution/oil sheen) incidents form a dense line all along the Piscataqua River channel from Little Bay past Pease and downtown Portsmouth out to the river mouth, as well as further inland at Dover/Somersworth and Exeter.
  - **Light blue squares with a boat on a palm-tree island**: `Grounding` incidents near the mouth of Portsmouth Harbor and south along the Rye/Hampton coastline.
  - **Yellow-green squares with a pink boat**: `Material Failure (Vessels)` incidents offshore in the Gulf of Maine and at the Isles of Shoals.
  - **Pink square with a white ship's wheel**: `Vessel Maneuverability` incident near the upper right coast in Maine.
  - **Green square with a white boat hitting a pier**: `Allision` incident near the upper center and along the Piscataqua River bridges/docks.
