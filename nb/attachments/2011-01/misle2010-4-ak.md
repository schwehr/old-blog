# USCG MISLE Public Database in Google Earth - Alaska View (`misle2010-4-ak.png`)

## Overview

A cropped view of Google Earth centered over Alaska and surrounding waters (the Bering Sea, Chukchi Sea, Beaufort Sea, Gulf of Alaska, Cook Inlet, Prince William Sound, and the Inside Passage), illustrating the distribution of USCG MISLE maritime casualty and pollution incidents across the state.

## Map & Incident Distribution Details

- **Geographic Coverage**: Shows the state of Alaska outlined in yellow border lines, including the Alaska-Yukon border on the right, the Bering Strait and Russian Far East (Chukotsk Peninsula) at the upper left, the Seward Peninsula and Norton Sound in the west-center, the North Slope at the top, and the Gulf of Alaska and Southeast Alaska panhandle along the bottom.
- **Northern & Western Alaska (Sparse Clusters)**:
  - **North Slope / Arctic Coast**: A pink martini-glass icon (`Personnel Casualties` / incident) near Barrow (Utqiaġvik), and `Grounding` (light blue island) and `Fire` (teal with flames) icons near Prudhoe Bay.
  - **Northwest & Interior**: `Set Adrift` (orange boat), `Grounding` (light blue island), `Capsize` (purple tilting boat), and `Damage to the Environment` (orange with green leaf) icons around Kotzebue Sound, Nome/Norton Sound, the Yukon River, and Fairbanks (`Damage to the Environment` and `Grounding` in the interior).
- **Southern Alaska, Cook Inlet, & Southeast Panhandle (Dense Clusters)**:
  - **Bristol Bay, Kodiak, Cook Inlet, & Prince William Sound**: Heavy, overlapping stacks of incident icons fill the southern coast, including `Vessel Maneuverability` (pink ship's wheel), `Material Failure (Vessels)` (olive-green with pink ship), `Damage to the Environment` (orange with green leaf), `Grounding` (light blue island), `Sinking` (red with sinking boat), `Allision` (green with pier), `Fire` (teal with flames), `Explosion` / `Loss of Electrical Power` (green with lightning bolt), and `Falls into Water` (purple water splash).
  - **Southeast Alaska / Inside Passage**: A very dense ribbon of stacked icons stretches along the entire Alexander Archipelago and Inside Passage at the lower right.
