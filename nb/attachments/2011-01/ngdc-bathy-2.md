# NOAA NGDC Bathymetry Data Viewer - Identified Hydrographic Surveys (`ngdc-bathy-2.png`)

## Overview

A screenshot of the NOAA NGDC Bathymetry Data Viewer zoomed in to the southern coast of Puerto Rico (`PUERTO RICO (USA)` / `Caribbean Sea`), showing overlapping NOS Hydrographic Survey polygons on a satellite imagery basemap and an open `Identified Features (6)` popup window.

## Map View & Survey Polygons

- **Basemap & Location**: The `Imagery` basemap button is selected in the top-right control bar, showing coastal satellite imagery of southern Puerto Rico (near Ponce / Guayanilla / Jobos Bay) with green mountains, urban areas, coastal reefs, and labels for `PUERTO RICO (USA)` and `Caribbean Sea`.
- **Scale & Coordinates**: The bottom-left status bar shows `Position: -66.577, 17.951` next to a scale bar ranging from `0` to `2km`.
- **Survey Bounding Polygons**: Semi-transparent orange, brown, and olive-yellow polygons represent the footprints of historical and modern National Ocean Service (NOS) hydrographic surveys. One intricately outlined coastal survey polygon in the center is highlighted in bright golden yellow with a black border and a small gray `x` near its center, showing cut-outs around shallow reefs/cays.

## Interface Panels & Identified Features Popup

- **Identified Features Dialog**: A floating window titled `Identified Features (6)` with a red close (`x`) button overlays the lower-right portion of the map. It lists six overlapping hydrographic surveys at the clicked point:
  - `H09186` — `NOS Hydrographic Surveys: Digital`
  - `H09135` — `NOS Hydrographic Surveys: Digital`
  - `H11036` (highlighted in dark blue, with a `Zoom To` link) — `NOS Hydrographic Surveys: Digital`
  - `H02420` (highlighted in light blue) — `NOS Hydrographic Surveys: Non-Digital`
  - `H02419` — `NOS Hydrographic Surveys: Non-Digital`
  - `H01514` — `NOS Hydrographic Surveys: Non-Digital`
  - A `Show Summary` button sits at the bottom left of the dialog.
- **Legend Panel (Right Sidebar)**: The `Legend` accordion pane is expanded on the right, showing color-coded categories by `Survey Year` (`1950 - 1959`, `1960 - 1969`, `1970 - 1979`, `1980 - 1989`, `1990 - 1999`, `2000 - present`) for `Trackline Bathymetry`, `Multibeam Bathymetry Surveys`, `NOS Hydrographic Surveys: Digital Data`, and `NOS Hydrographic Surveys: Non-Digital`.
