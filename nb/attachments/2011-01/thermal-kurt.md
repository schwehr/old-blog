# Infrared Thermal Camera Portrait of Kurt Schwehr (`thermal-kurt.jpg`)

## Overview

A false-color infrared thermal camera image of Kurt Schwehr taken at the Imaginarium Discovery Center / Alaska Science Museum in Anchorage, Alaska, showing him waving with his left hand and giving a thumbs-up with his right hand.

- **Subject Pose**: Kurt is standing from the waist up, facing slightly to the right while looking toward the thermal camera. His left arm is raised with an open palm in a friendly wave, and his right hand is held up in the foreground making a thumbs-up gesture.
