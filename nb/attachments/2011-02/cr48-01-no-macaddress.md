# Google Cr-48 Chrome OS Laptop Bottom Regulatory Sticker (`cr48-01-no-macaddress.jpg`)

## Overview
This photograph shows a close-up of the silver-and-black regulatory and identification label on the underside of a Google Cr-48 pilot program Chrome OS notebook. In the accompanying February 2, 2011 blog post (*"Google Chrome Cr-48 first impressions"*), the author notes that the sticker lacks the Wi-Fi MAC address needed for router MAC-address filtering.

## Label Layout & Visible Text
The rounded rectangular metallic-silver sticker is affixed to the dark matte casing of the laptop and is divided into a silver upper header and a black lower panel:

### Upper Silver Section
- **Barcode & Redaction**: At the top center is a vertical barcode partially covered by a solid black privacy redaction rectangle on the right side.
- **Identification Fields** (printed in black sans-serif text):
  - `Serial No.51012` (remainder redacted by the black box)
  - `HWID: IEC MARIO FISH 2330`
  - `MEID: A100001762AE91`
  - `MODEL: Mario` (referring to the internal codename "Mario" for the Inventec-manufactured Cr-48 reference hardware)

### Lower Black Section
- **Regulatory Logos**:
  - Top left: `I.T.E.` / `C (UL) US` / `LISTED` / `E131920` certification mark.
  - Top center: `FC` (FCC logo).
  - Top right: Crossed-out wheeled bin WEEE directive symbol above a solid white bar.
- **FCC Identifiers**:
  - `Contains FCC ID: PPD-AR5BHB92` (Atheros Wi-Fi card)
  - `Contains FCC ID: PPD-AR5BBU12` (Bluetooth module)
  - `Contains FCC ID: PKRNVWE296` (Novatel Wireless Gobi 2000 3G WWAN modem)
- **Power Specification**:
  - `INPUT: 19.5Vdc` followed by the direct current symbol (`⎓`) and `2.05A`
- **FCC Compliance Statement**:
  > This device complies with part 15 of the FCC Rules.Operation is subject to the following two conditions:
  > (1) This device may not cause harmful interference, and
  > (2) This device must accept any interference received,including interference that may cause undesired operation.
- **Manufacturing Codes** (bottom left):
  - `ivt` (Inventec Corporation)
  - `PRODUCT OF CHINA I5`
  - `6060B1218101`
