# Google Cr-48 Chrome OS "Get Started" Tutorial Screen (`cr48-02-getting-started.jpg`)

## Overview
This photograph captures the LCD screen of a Google Cr-48 Chrome OS notebook displaying the initial interactive **"Get Started with your Chrome notebook"** onboarding tutorial inside the Chrome browser.

- **Tab Strip**: A single open browser tab at the top left reads `Get Started with your C...` with a close (`x`) button and a new-tab (`+`) button next to it.
- **System Status Area (Top Right)**: Faintly visible in the dark upper-right bezel area is the time and status icons.
- **Navigation Toolbar**: Below the tab strip is the standard Chrome toolbar featuring Back (`←`), Forward (`→`), Reload (`↻`), an empty omnibox/address bar with a magnifying glass icon and a bookmark star (`☆`), a network/status icon, and the wrench (`🔧`) settings menu icon on the far right.
