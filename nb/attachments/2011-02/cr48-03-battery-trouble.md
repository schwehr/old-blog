# Google Cr-48 Chrome OS Battery 0% Status Popup (`cr48-03-battery-trouble.jpg`)

## Overview
This close-up photograph shows the top-right system status tray of the Google Cr-48 Chrome OS notebook screen, illustrating a known hardware/firmware issue where the battery failed to charge and remained stuck at `0%` despite being plugged into AC power.

## Interface & Status Details
- **Top Status Bar**: Against a black background at the top right of the screen, four status elements are displayed in light gray/white:
  - **Clock**: `6:52 AM`
  - **Keyboard Layout Indicator**: `US`
  - **Network Signal Icon**: Four ascending vertical signal bars.
  - **Battery Icon**: An empty horizontal battery outline with a small lightning bolt / plug indicator on its right tip, hovered over by a black mouse cursor arrow with a white border.
- **Tooltip & Dropdown Menu**:
  - Directly beneath the mouse cursor is a small rounded tooltip box reading `Battery: 0%`.
  - Below the status bar is an open white rectangular status dropdown menu displaying two lines of dark gray sans-serif text:
    - `Battery: 0%`
    - `Calculating time until full`
