This close-up photograph of the Google Cr-48 screen shows the **"Status Icons"** / **"Select and Activate 3G"** section of the Chrome OS onboarding tutorial, explaining how to connect to Wi-Fi or activate the built-in Verizon Wireless 3G cellular modem.
