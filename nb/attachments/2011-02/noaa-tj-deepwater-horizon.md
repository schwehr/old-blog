# NOAA Ship Thomas Jefferson at the Deepwater Horizon Site (`noaa-tj-deepwater-horizon.jpg`)

## Overview
A maritime photograph taken in the Gulf of Mexico showing the white bow and forward superstructure of the hydrographic survey vessel **NOAA Ship *Thomas Jefferson* (S 222)** in the foreground, with drillships, offshore support vessels, and flames and dark smoke rising from controlled flaring/burning operations at the *Deepwater Horizon* oil spill site along the distant horizon.

## Foreground: NOAA Ship *Thomas Jefferson* (S 222)
- **Hull & Bow Markings**: The starboard bow of the white-painted ship enters from the right side of the frame, cutting through deep blue water and kicking up a white foam bow wave along the black boot-topping at the waterline.
- **Insignia & Text**:
  - Large dark blue block letters **`NOAA`** painted high on the starboard bow above **`S 222`**.
  - Adjacent to the lettering is the circular NOAA emblem featuring a white bird silhouette separating a dark blue upper half and light blue lower half.
  - Lower down near the waterline is a black circle containing an `X` (marking the bow thruster location).
- **Deck Equipment & Superstructure**:
  - A black anchor is stowed in the starboard hawsepipe with reddish-brown rust streaks trailing down the white hull plating.
  - A tall, slender yellow-tan forward mast rises vertically from the bow tip.
  - On the foredeck sits a prominent black hydraulic knuckle-boom/telescoping crane with a red hook suspended from its cable.
  - White safety railings, circular portholes along the hull side, an orange-and-white life ring mounted on the rail, and the lower portion of the white bridge wing and pilothouse windows are visible at the upper right.

## Background: Deepwater Horizon Response Fleet
Along the flat horizon line to the left of the *Thomas Jefferson*'s bow, under a blue sky scattered with cumulus clouds:
- **Far Left**: A large offshore semi-submersible rig or drillship with a tall derrick tower.
- **Center-Left**: Several offshore supply and response vessels clustered near a bright orange-yellow flame at the water's surface, sending a diagonal plume of gray-black smoke rising into the sky.
- **Center (Just Left of the Bow)**: Another large drillship with a tall lattice derrick and a bright flare flame burning off a boom on its right side, accompanied by smaller support vessels.
