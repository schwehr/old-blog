# Substructure Orion Survey Vessel: Real-Time Multibeam Acquisition Screens (`substructure-acquisition-screens.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior cabin photograph aboard Substructure's hydrographic survey vessel *Orion* during an open house demonstration in Portsmouth, New Hampshire (February 2011), showing a three-monitor workstation mounted against the bulkhead running real-time multibeam echosounder and HYPACK hydrographic survey acquisition software as the boat passes the Portsmouth commercial dock.

## Monitor Layout & Displays

### Top-Left Monitor: 3D Waterfall / Pier Piling Views
A ruggedized black flat-panel display split into two side-by-side vertical swath windows against black backgrounds:
- **Left Pane**: A color-coded bathymetric waterfall display (shaded in bright green with yellow/red highlights along the left edge and a vertical rainbow colorbar on the right) showing the vertical face and spaced structural pilings of the Portsmouth commercial dock on the left side of the swath.
- **Right Pane**: A grayscale shaded-relief / backscatter waterfall view of the same seafloor swath and dock pilings protruding along the left edge.

### Top-Right Monitor: Real-Time Multibeam Swath Profile
A second ruggedized flat-panel monitor displaying the real-time cross-track multibeam sonar wedge and seafloor profile:
- A black gridded polar/fan sector plot showing the sonar swath geometry, with a green-and-red seafloor bottom detection line sloping downward from left to right across the center of the fan.
- Numerical status panels and parameters are arranged along the bottom of the window.

### Bottom Center Monitor: HYPACK Navigation & Survey Control
A wider Samsung widescreen LCD monitor mounted below the two upper displays, showing the main Windows XP desktop and survey software windows:
- **Main Left Window (Chart / Map View)**: A nautical chart of the Piscataqua River / Portsmouth waterfront showing tan/brown land and pier structures on the left, green-tinted shoal areas, light blue and cyan depth areas, planned parallel survey tracklines extending across the channel, and a black vessel icon located just off the commercial dock face. A vertical color scale bar runs along the far left margin.
- **Right Side Sub-Windows**:
  - **Top Right**: A status/data window with green and red indicator bars and numerical telemetry readouts.
  - **Middle Right**: A red/orange waterfall intensity display showing acoustic returns.
  - **Bottom Right**: A prominent digital readout box displaying **`35.2`** in large bold green/black digits alongside an adjacent coordinate/status panel.
- **Mounting & Cabling**: The monitors are secured to a gray metal mounting plate in front of a cabin window, with black power and video cables routed neatly to the right.
