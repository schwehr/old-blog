# Substructure Orion Hydrographic Survey Vessel (`substructure-orion-survey-vessel.jpg`)

## Overview
An exterior dockside photograph of **Substructure's *Orion* hydrographic survey vessel** moored alongside a wooden floating dock in Portsmouth, New Hampshire, during a February 2011 open house.

## Vessel Description (*Orion*)
- **Hull & Cabin**: A compact, rigid off-white/cream-colored fiberglass/composite workboat with a fully enclosed forward pilothouse cabin and an open, flat gray non-skid aft working deck.
- **Pilothouse & Branding**:
  - Two rectangular tinted side windows along the port side of the cabin and an aft cabin window next to the open rear doorway.
  - Inside the cabin doorway stands a person wearing a dark green jacket and dark cap, facing forward toward the helm/survey station.
  - On the port side of the cabin below the forward side window is a rectangular blue-and-white logo decal reading **`SUBSTRUCTURE`** with **`www.substructure.com`** printed underneath.
- **Aft Deck & Equipment**:
  - Neatly coiled yellow, white, and tan lines/ropes hang from a rack mounted on the aft cabin bulkhead below a grey snow shovel and deck brush.
  - A flush rectangular hatch cover (over the moon pool / engine / equipment access) is set into the deck sole just aft of the cabin door.
  - Mounted on the starboard gunwale is a metallic hand winch/reel (`CastAway CTD` deployment winch) with cable/wiring routed below.
  - Two white cylindrical fenders with black tops hang along the port hull against the wooden dock, secured by mooring lines tied to deck cleats.
- **Roof & Sensor Mast**:
  - A sturdy cream-colored aluminum radar/sensor arch sits atop the cabin roof, supporting a white Furuno radome (`...UNO` visible in blue letters), fixed GPS antennas mounted on short rigid stanchions at the outer corners, deck floodlights, and a central masthead navigation light/sensor tower.

## Background & Surroundings
- **Left**: The wooden plank dock in the foreground leads toward another workboat moored ahead (showing part of a white cabin with `"SHORE"` lettering and a tall white mast/exhaust stack) and red-brick commercial buildings along the Portsmouth waterfront.
- **Right**: Dark harbor water reflects the vessel and a granite seawall/pier topped with patches of winter snow, bare trees, utility poles, a steel lattice electrical transmission tower, and a silver sedan parked onshore under an overcast gray winter sky.
