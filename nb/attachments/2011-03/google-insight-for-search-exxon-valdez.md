# Google Insights for Search: "exxon valdez" (`google-insight-for-search-exxon-valdez.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Insights for Search** illustrating worldwide web search interest for `"exxon valdez"` from 2004 to early 2011. The graph highlights a massive spike in searches for the 1989 *Exxon Valdez* disaster during mid-2010, coinciding with media comparisons to the *Deepwater Horizon* oil spill in the Gulf of Mexico.
