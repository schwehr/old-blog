# Jeppesen Mobile TC App on Apple iPad Displaying FAA Approach Chart (`jeppesen-mobile-tc-ipad-faa.jpg`)

## Overview
A promotional product graphic showing the upper portion of an Apple iPad running the **Jeppesen Mobile TC** (Terminal Charts) aviation application against a dark blue gradient background. White leader lines and text callouts above the tablet annotate the app's toolbar controls while the screen displays a georeferenced instrument approach chart for San Francisco International Airport (`KSFO/SFO`).

White dotted leader lines point from labels at the top of the image to icons on the black application navigation bar (located directly below the iOS status bar showing `iPad`, Wi-Fi icon, `4:20 PM`, and battery indicator):
* **Left Icons:**
  * Airplane icon: **Airports, Charts, Groups** `(Search, manage airpots, charts, groups and favorites)` *(note the "airpots" typo in the graphic)*.
  * Italic `i` icon: **Chart Change Notices**.
* **Center Title:** `11-1 ILS Rwy 19L`.
* **Right Icons:**
  * Solid star with list lines: **View Favorite Charts**.
  * Sun/brightness icon: **Brightness Control**.
  * Outlined star icon: **Add Chart to Favorites**.
  * Gear icon: **Settings**.
