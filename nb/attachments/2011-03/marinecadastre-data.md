# Multipurpose Marine Cadastre Data Download Page (`marinecadastre-data.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the data download catalog page from the BOEMRE/NOAA **Multipurpose Marine Cadastre** website (`marinecadastre.gov`), showing the **Navigation and Marine Infrastructure** category and its downloadable spatial datasets.

## Layout & Navigation Menu
* **Background:** Dark slate-blue theme (`#1e3346`).
* **Left Sidebar Navigation:** A vertical stack of ten dark blue category buttons with white text:
  1. `Data Home`
  2. `Jurisdictional Boundaries and Limits`
  3. `Federal Agency Regions`
  4. `Federal Georegulations`
  5. `Navigation and Marine Infrastructure`
  6. `Marine Habitat and Biodiversity`
  7. `Geology and Seafloor`
  8. `Human Use`
  9. `Web Map Services`
  10. `Marine Cadastre Spatial Database`
