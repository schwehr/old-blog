# Multipurpose Marine Cadastre Web Map Querying LNG Sites (`marinecadastre-query.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Multipurpose Marine Cadastre** interactive web GIS viewer (developed jointly by the Bureau of Ocean Energy Management, Regulation, and Enforcement and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) focused on the New England coastline and Gulf of Maine, displaying an active query on a Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) terminal site in Massachusetts Bay.

## Header, Toolbar & Footer
* **Top Banner:** White header displaying blue title text **Multipurpose Marine Cadastre** and subtitle **BUREAU OF OCEAN ENERGY MANAGEMENT, REGULATION, AND ENFORCEMENT AND NATIONAL OCEANIC AND ATMOSPHERIC ADMINISTRATION**, flanked on the right by the Department of the Interior / BOEMRE and NOAA logos.
* **Map Control Bar:** Dark translucent bar across the top of the map containing 9 square tool icons (map extent, identify `i`, measure/draw tools, `xy` coordinates, print, link, download, help `?`) followed by live coordinate and scale readouts: `Latitude: 42.2598`, `Longitude: -71.8696`, `Scale: 1:2,311,162`.
* **Footer:** Links to `United States Department of Commerce`, `National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration`, `National Ocean Service`, `Contact Us`, `Privacy Policy`, `Link Disclaimer`, and `USA.gov`.

## Floating Windows & Map Content
* **Table of Contents Window (Left):**
  * Tabs for `Layers` (active) and `Legend`, and basemap selectors `Street`, `Physical` (active, bold), and `Satellite`.
  * **Available Layers** tree:
    * `Jurisdictional Boundaries and Limits` (checked)
    * `Geology and Seafloor` (unchecked)
    * `Federal Georegulations` (unchecked)
    * `Federal Agency Regions` (unchecked)
    * `Navigation and Marine Infrastructure` (expanded, partially checked):
      * `Shipping`
      * `Collision Regulations Boundary`
      * `Oil and Natural Gas Wells`
      * `Drilling Platforms`
      * `Liquefied Natural Gas Sites` (checked and highlighted in blue)
      * `Selected Pipelines`
      * `Wrecks and Obstructions`
      * `Anchorage Areas`
      * `Military Danger Zones`
      * `Aids to Navigation`
    * `Human Use` (unchecked)
    * `Marine Habitat and Biodiversity` (unchecked)
* **Map View (Center):**
  * Shows eastern New England (New Hampshire, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Connecticut) with major highways (`93`, `95`, `495`, `90`, `195`, `395`), cities (`CONCORD`, `Manchester`, `Lowell`, `BOSTON`, `PROVIDENCE`, `Bridgeport`), coastal state waters shaded in lime green, offshore OCS lease grid blocks, bathymetric/jurisdictional boundary dashed lines, and two blue/red `X` markers in Massachusetts Bay east of Boston.
  * Scale bar at bottom left shows `100 km` and `50 mi`.
* **Identify Window (Right):**
  * Displays results for `Active Layer: Liquefied Natural Gas Sites`.
  * Left pane selects feature `5` under `Liquefied Natural...`.
  * Right pane displays the attribute table:
    * `Company`: `SUEZ LNG`
    * `Project Name`: `Neptune (South)`
    * `Status`: `Approved`
    * `Region`: `ATL`
