# NASA Ames GeoPapers KML Overlay in Google Mars (`nasa-arc-geopapers.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth (Mars mode)** displaying a KML layer from NASA Ames Research Center's **GeoPapers** project (`alderaan.arc.nasa.gov/geopapers`), which georeferences planetary science publications to specific surface features on Mars.

## 3D Globe & Surface Features
* **Terrain View:** An oblique 3D perspective looking across the rust-orange cratered plains and deep canyon systems of **Valles Marineris** on Mars, set against the black background of space at the top horizon.
* **Labeled Martian Features:**
  * Foreground and center canyons/plateaus: `Valles Marineris`, `Coprates Chasma`, `Coprates Catena`, `Coprates`, `Ophir Catenae`, `Ophir Planum`.
  * Left side: `Felis Dorsa`, `Martin`, `Sinai`, `Melas Dorsa`, `Thaumasia Planum` (partially cut off at bottom left).
  * Upper center/distant horizon: `Melas Chasma`, `Melas Labes`, `Ophir Labes`, `Coprates Labes`, `Candor Chaos`, `Candor Chasma`.
  * Bottom right: `Wallula`.
* **Map Markers:** Numerous yellow pushpins mark locations linked to scientific papers, alongside white-ringed black target circles and small square photo/layer icons (blue, orange, and red squares, plus a green hiker icon near `Melas Chasma`).
* **UI Controls & Credits:**
  * Vertical zoom slider (`+` / `-`) in the upper-right corner.
  * Bottom center attribution: `Image NASA / USGS`, `ESA / DLR / FU Berlin (G.Neukum)`.
  * Bottom right watermark: `©2010 Google`.
