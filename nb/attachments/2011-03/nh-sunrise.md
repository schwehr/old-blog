# New Hampshire Sunrise Over Rooftops (`nh-sunrise.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect photograph capturing a vibrant early-morning sunrise sky over residential rooftops and bare winter/early-spring trees in New Hampshire.

## Scene Description
* **Sky & Clouds:** The sky is filled with textured stratocumulus and altocumulus clouds illuminated from below by the rising sun. Colors transition from glowing golden-orange and fiery red-orange near the lower horizon to deep magenta, rose, and dusky purple-blue across the upper cloud deck.
* **Foreground Silhouettes:**
  * On the right, the dark silhouette of a pitched house roof and a tall vertical chimney (topped with a flat rain cap) rises nearly to the top edge of the frame.
  * Along the bottom and left, bare deciduous tree branches and a smaller neighboring house gable with a chimney are silhouetted in black against the bright orange glow along the horizon.
