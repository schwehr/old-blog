# NOAA California Ocean Uses Atlas Web Map (`noaa-ca-ocean-use-atlas.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **California Ocean Uses Atlas** interactive Flash web mapping application produced by the NOAA National Marine Protected Areas (MPA) Center (`mpa.gov`), visualizing cumulative human uses of the ocean across the California Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ).

## Application Header & Toolbar
* **Top Banner:** Dark blue-green header featuring the circular NOAA logo on the left and bold white italic title text **California Ocean Uses Atlas**.
* **Sub-header Bar:** Label `Ocean Use Categories` with `Show All` and `Hide All` pill buttons on the left, and a `HELP` button on the far right.
* **Main Category Toolbar:** Five large silver gradient tab buttons with icons:
  1. `Non-Consumptive` (sailboat on waves icon)
  2. `Fishing` (blue fish and hook icon)
  3. `Industrial/Military` (red factory with smokestack icon)
  4. `Layers` (stacked map layers icon, currently open with a dropdown menu)
  5. `Tools` (crossed wrench and screwdriver icon)
* **Layers Dropdown Menu:**
  * `Marine Protected Areas (MPA)`
  * `✓ Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ)` (checked)
  * `Territorial Sea`
  * `State Boundary`
  * Separator followed by `Show All` and `Hide All`.

## Map View & Legends
* **Geographic Coverage:** Shows California, western Nevada, northern Baja California, and the adjacent Pacific Ocean, with major interstate highways (`5`, `80`, `505`, `880`, `210`, `215`, `15`, `8`) and cities (`Redding`, `Chico`, `Reno`, `Carson City`, `Santa Rosa`, `Sacramento`, `Oakland`, `San Jose`, `Modesto`, `Salinas`, `Fresno`, `Bakersfield`, `Las Vegas`, `Palm Springs`, `Mexicali`).
* **Ocean Use Overlay:** Within the black-outlined 200-nautical-mile **Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ)** boundary off the California coast, overlapping polygons and shipping/activity corridors are shaded primarily in dark and medium blues offshore, transitioning to cyan, green, and yellow hotspots along the immediate coastline (such as off San Francisco Bay, Monterey Bay, and the Southern California Bight near Santa Barbara, Los Angeles, and San Diego).
* **Floating Widgets (Bottom Right):**
  * **Ocean Uses Count for a Location:** A stepped pyramid color legend ranging from `1` at the wide dark-blue base up through cyan (`4`), green (`7`), yellow (`10`), orange (`13`), red (`16`), to dark brown/black (`19`) at the narrow apex.
  * **Transparency:** Two horizontal sliders for adjusting the opacity of `Ocean Uses` and `Marine Protected Areas`.
* **Scale Bar & Zoom:** Vertical zoom slider on the upper left; dual scale bar at the lower left showing `400 km` and `300 mi`.
