# NOAA OCRM in Your State: Maine/New Hampshire MPA Popup (`noaa-ocrm-nh-item.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the NOAA **OCRM in Your State** web map focusing on an open information modal titled **MAINE MARINE PROTECTED AREA — MAPPING HUMAN USES OF THE OCEAN**, which highlights a January 2010 ocean-use mapping workshop conducted with the Coastal Response Research Center (CRRC) at the University of New Hampshire.

## Pop-up Information Dialog (Left)
* **Header Bar:** Bright cyan banner with a circular wave/dolphin MPA icon on the left, black title text **MAINE / MARINE PROTECTED AREA**, and a close button (`X`) on the right.
* **Body Content:**
  * Subheading in blue: `MAPPING HUMAN USES OF THE OCEAN`
  * Full paragraph text:
    > Oftentimes, it is the users themselves who know the most about how people use the ocean and coastal waters. The commercial fishing boat captain knows the best spots to go for big strip­ers; the kayaking guide knows the most popular paddling routes. OCRM's National Marine Protected Areas Center wanted to take advantage of this user experience, and in January 2010, tapped the knowledge of local ocean experts in a mapping workshop in New Hampshire. Partnering with the Coastal Response Research Center at the University of New Hampshire, the MPA Center successfully mapped patterns of nearly 20 human uses of the ocean, from the high tide line to out to 200 nautical miles throughout the region, from Cape Small in Phippsburg, Maine to the New Hampshire/Massachusetts border. The resulting data, maps, and analytical products were used in emergency response planning and made available to state and federal ocean management agencies and all other interested parties.
  * **Embedded Thumbnail Map:** A side-by-side nautical chart of the Gulf of Maine / New Hampshire / southern Maine coast showing brown-shaded polygons of commercial fishing grounds (`New Hampshire & Southern Maine Ocean Uses Atlas Project`).
  * **Bottom Hyperlink:** `Learn more about the Marine Protected Areas Center here`
