# NOAA OCRM in Your State Web Map: New England Coast (`noaa-ocrm-nh-ma.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of NOAA's **OCRM in Your State** (`Investing in America's Coastal Communities`) interactive web map showing coastal management and conservation program markers across New Hampshire, southern Maine, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, and Connecticut.

## Banner & Interface Controls
* **Top Banner:** Panoramic coastal marsh and ocean header image featuring a circular blue wave emblem on the left, white title text **OCRM IN YOUR STATE** with subtitle `Investing in America's Coastal Communities`, and the **NOAA** watermark logo on the right.
* **Zoom & Pan Controls:** Vertical zoom slider (`+` / `-`) in the upper-left corner of the map; pan-arrows button in the lower-right corner.
* **Control Panel (Right):**
  * Decorated with a white 3D gear icon at its upper-left corner and a `Hide >>` toggle link.
  * Top buttons: `About` and `Instructions`.
  * Dropdown selector displaying `New Hampshire`.
  * `Background Map:` selector with three buttons: `Streets`, `Topo`, and `Imagery` (active).
  * Six checked layer toggles with custom icons:
    * `State` — yellow-outlined state polygon
    * `CZMP` — orange circular icon (Coastal Zone Management Program)
    * `NERR` — green circular marsh grass icon (National Estuarine Research Reserve)
    * `CELCP` — maroon circular coastal landscape icon (Coastal and Estuarine Land Conservation Program)
    * `MPA` — cyan circular wave/dolphin icon (Marine Protected Area)
    * `CRCP` — purple circular coral/fish icon (Coral Reef Conservation Program)
  * Date/version code `20110304` printed at the bottom right of the panel.

## Map View & Markers
* **Geographic Area:** Satellite imagery of New England with yellow state boundaries and coastal water boundaries outlining New Hampshire (highlighted with a slightly lighter olive tint over the land), Vermont (darker green), Maine, Massachusetts (including Cape Ann, Boston Harbor, Cape Cod, Martha's Vineyard, and Nantucket), Rhode Island (Narragansett Bay), Connecticut, and eastern Long Island.
* **Program Markers:**
  * **Southern Maine (Wells area):** 1 green `NERR` marker.
  * **New Hampshire Seacoast / Great Bay:** 1 green `NERR` marker, 1 orange `CZMP` marker, and 1 cyan `MPA` marker clustered near Portsmouth and Great Bay.
  * **Southern Massachusetts (Waquoit Bay / Cape Cod):** 1 green `NERR` marker and 1 orange `CZMP` marker.
  * **Rhode Island (Narragansett Bay & coast):** 1 green `NERR` marker and 2 orange `CZMP` markers.
  * **Connecticut Coast:** 1 orange `CZMP` marker along Long Island Sound.
