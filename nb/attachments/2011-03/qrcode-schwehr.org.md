# QR Code for schwehr.org (`qrcode-schwehr.org.png`)

## Overview
A standard square black-and-white Quick Response (QR) 2D matrix barcode generated via `qrstuff.com` that encodes the URL for Kurt Schwehr's personal website (`http://schwehr.org`), intended for printing on business cards.

## Visual Details
* **Matrix Structure:** A crisp 21×21 (Version 1) or 25×25 (Version 2) black module grid centered inside a wide white quiet-zone border.
* **Finder & Alignment Patterns:**
  * Three large 7×7 square position-detection (finder) patterns sit in the top-left, top-right, and bottom-left corners, each consisting of a solid 3×3 black center square surrounded by a 1-module white ring and a 1-module black outer border.
  * A single 5×5 alignment pattern is positioned near the bottom-right quadrant (confirming a Version 2 QR code, 25×25 modules).
* **Color & Style:** Pure monochrome black (`#000000`) modules on a solid white (`#FFFFFF`) background with no embedded logo or text caption.
