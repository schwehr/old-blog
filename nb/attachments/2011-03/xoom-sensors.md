# Built-in Sensors List on Motorola Xoom Tablet (`xoom-sensors.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph taken with an iPhone 3GS in the CCOM/JHC Visualization Lab showing a **Motorola Xoom** tablet held in portrait orientation, displaying an Android sensor-monitoring app that lists the device's built-in hardware sensors.

## Tablet & Screen Details
* **Hardware & Orientation:** The black-bezeled Motorola Xoom is held upright (portrait mode) by two hands in front of an office desk and dark grey filing drawers. The `MOTOROLA` logo is along the upper-right bezel, the front camera is centered on the right bezel, and the `verizon` logo is at the lower-right bezel.
* **Displayed Sensor List:**
  * The screen shows horizontal rows of light-blue/white sensor entries separated by dark grey headers, listing the onboard sensors of the Motorola Xoom (such as 3-axis accelerometer, 3-axis gyroscope, magnetometer/compass, ambient light sensor, barometer, orientation, gravity, linear acceleration, and rotation vector sensors), along with a blue control bar and Android 3.0 Honeycomb system navigation icons at the bottom.
* **Whiteboard Reflection:** Because of the glossy glass screen, a prominent reflection of Roland's whiteboard behind the photographer is superimposed over the display, showing hand-drawn blue, red, and black marker diagrams (3D perspective grids, boxes with dots, arrows, and handwritten notes).
* **Desk Background:** On the grey desk behind the tablet sit a black tablet dock, a white mouse, plastic packaging, and a clear plastic tub with a red lid.
