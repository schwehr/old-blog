# Whale Sightings and Marine Debris Web Map (`blue-ocean-whale-sightings.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a Mozilla Firefox browser window on Mac OS X displaying the **Whale Sightings and Marine Debris** interactive web mapping application hosted by the University of New Hampshire (`http://cegis.unh.edu/whalesanddebris/`), developed in partnership with the Blue Ocean Society and UNH Cooperative Extension.

## Browser & Header
- **Browser Chrome**: Mozilla Firefox window on Mac OS X with back/forward navigation buttons, address bar showing `http://cegis.unh.edu/whalesanddebris/`, and a Google search bar on the right.
- **Page Banner**: A blue banner with a faint nautical chart background titled **"Whale Sightings and Marine Debris"** in bold white text.
- **Navigation Links**: Along the bottom-right of the blue banner are links separated by vertical bars:
  - `PDF: Website Basics`

## Main Map View
- **Geographic Area**: The Gulf of Maine coastline stretching from southern Maine (**Kittery**) through coastal New Hampshire (**Portsmouth**, **Rye**, **Hampton**, and offshore **Isles of Shoals**) down into northern Massachusetts (**Newburyport** and **Cape Ann**).
- **Base Layers**: Green land masses with dark red state/regional boundary lines, white/gray nearshore shoal areas, and shaded purple/blue bathymetric depth contours in the offshore Gulf of Maine waters.
- **Map Controls**: Pan directional arrows and a vertical zoom slider (`+` / `-`) in the upper-left corner of the map canvas. A partially visible yellow instructional text box appears along the lower-left edge over the land.
- **Data Markers**:
  - White circular markers with black outlines indicating **Coastal Locations** (Kittery, Portsmouth, Rye, Isles of Shoals, Hampton, Newburyport, Cape Ann).
  - A dense cluster of circular icon markers concentrated offshore between Rye/Isles of Shoals and Cape Ann (covering Jeffreys Ledge and surrounding waters), predominantly red circles with white borders containing small white whale tail/fluke or debris icons, mixed with smaller numbers of green, yellow/gold, and black circular markers representing specific tracked whales and marine debris items.
