# IHO Data Quality Questionnaire Screenshot (`iho-data-quality-survey.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of an online questionnaire hosted on SurveyMonkey by the International Hydrographic Organization (IHO) Data Quality Working Group (DQWG), asking mariners about how data quality is depicted on nautical charts.

## Introductory Text
At the top of the page is a paragraph in black sans-serif text:
> Paper charts and ENCs indicate the quality of the data in nautical charts in many different, and sometimes subtle, ways. These are set out below. Please answer the questions relating to any of the methods that apply to you. The questions are not intended to be a "test" so please do not look up the answer so as to get a good "score". The questions are intended to identify which of the existing chart data quality identifiers are not used or are not well understood and to use this information to design a better method of depicting quality in the future.

## Survey Question
Below the introduction is Question 7 in bold text, followed by two unselected radio buttons:
- **`7. Do the charts you use have a source or reliability diagram? (For example, see image below)`**
