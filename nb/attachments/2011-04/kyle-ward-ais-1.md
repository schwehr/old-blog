# Kyle Ward Presentation on AIS Vessel Traffic (`kyle-ward-ais-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A low-light photograph taken during a presentation by Kyle Ward at the US Hydro 2011 conference in Tampa, Florida, showing a speaker at a podium next to a large projection screen displaying Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel position data.

## Scene Description
- **Speaker & Podium (Right)**: Kyle Ward stands behind a dark wooden lectern bearing a rectangular **`Marriott`** plaque on the front. He is wearing a light-colored long-sleeved dress shirt, a tie, and a conference lanyard, looking toward the audience/screen while holding a presenter remote.
- **Projection Screen (Left)**: A large rear- or front-projected screen displays a geographic visualization of Nationwide Automatic Identification System (NAIS) vessel reports along the U.S. East Coast near the Chesapeake Bay / Norfolk, Virginia region:
  - **Coastline & Bathymetry**: The land on the left is rendered in grayscale satellite/aerial imagery with white place-name labels, transitioning into gray continental shelf waters and a dark brown/purple deep-ocean background to the east.
  - **AIS Tracks**: Bright yellow dots and dashed/solid track lines radiate intensely from the entrance of the Chesapeake Bay (Norfolk/Hampton Roads) and follow coastal shipping lanes north and south, as well as offshore trajectories heading into the Atlantic where AIS reception becomes sparser and breaks into intermittent dotted lines as vessels leave coastal receiver coverage.
