# Larry Mayer Keynote on Deepwater Horizon Water Column Acoustics (`larry-mayer-ushydro-dwh-acoustics.jpg`)

## Overview
A low-light photograph taken during Larry Mayer's keynote presentation at the US Hydro 2011 conference in Tampa, Florida, illustrating 3D acoustic water-column mapping of subsurface oil and gas plumes during the Deepwater Horizon incident.

## Scene Description
- **Speaker & Audience**: On the right, Larry Mayer stands behind the podium wearing a dark suit jacket, light shirt, and conference badge, looking down toward his notes/lectern. The heads of several audience members are silhouetted in the foreground along the bottom of the frame.
- **Projected Visualization (Left)**:
  - **Label**: In the upper-left corner of the slide, blue/white text reads **`Pass 8: 5.8 km`**.
  - **3D Seafloor & Acoustic Curtain**: A grey/white 3D shaded-relief bathymetric surface represents the seafloor topography (including salt domes/knolls) against a warm brown/orange background. Rising vertically from the seafloor is a tall rectangular acoustic curtain (water-column echogram) rendered in false color (blue, cyan, green, yellow, orange, and red), showing horizontal acoustic scattering layers and deepwater plume structures suspended in the water column alongside a white vertical reference cylinder.
