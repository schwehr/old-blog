# USCG MISLE Incidents in the Gulf of Mexico (`misle2005-09-gomex.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a Google Earth visualization displaying 12,430 U.S. Coast Guard Marine Information for Safety and Law Enforcement (MISLE) incident markers across the northern Gulf of Mexico from January 2005 to June 2010.

## Geographic Extent
- **Coastline & Labels**: Shows the northern Gulf Coast including eastern Texas (**`Houston`** marked with a red dot and white label on the left), **`Louisiana`** (labeled at top center with white state border lines), **`New Orleans`** (marked on the right), and coastal Mississippi/Alabama/Florida panhandle to the east.
- **Base Map**: Satellite land imagery in olive/brown tones and shaded deep-blue ocean bathymetry (showing the continental shelf edge, slope, and Sigsbee Escarpment).

## Incident Icons
Thousands of overlapping square icon markers densely blanket the coastal ports, Intracoastal Waterway, Mississippi River corridor, and offshore Gulf of Mexico continental shelf:
- **Orange square icons with a green leaf**: Environmental/pollution or oil/hazmat spill incidents (the most visually dominant icon type across offshore Louisiana and Texas).
- **Green, blue, orange, and yellow vessel icons**: Various marine casualty and vessel incident categories (groundings, collisions, allisions, sinkings, equipment failures, and vessels on fire).
- **Pink ship's wheel (helm) icons**: Navigation or steering/operational incidents.
- **Light-blue tropical island/palm-tree boat icons**: Recreational boating or coastal incidents.
