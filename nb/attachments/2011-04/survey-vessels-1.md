# Overhead View of Survey Vessels at US Hydro 2011 (`survey-vessels-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle aerial photograph taken from an upper floor of the Tampa Marriott Waterside Hotel & Marina, looking almost straight down at seven vessels docked in slips along a concrete floating pier during the US Hydro 2011 conference.

## Scene Description
- **Water & Pier**: Dark water fills the upper portion of the frame with bright white sun glitter reflecting off ripples in the upper-left corner. Along the bottom runs a light-colored concrete floating walkway with six perpendicular finger piers extending upward into the water, flanked by tall mooring pilings.
- **Vessels (Left to Right)**:
  1. **Slip 1 (Far Left)**: A grey/white cabin workboat/survey vessel moored bow-in (pointing down toward the dock) with a large flat white canopy/roof over the aft deck.
  2. **Slip 2**: A smaller white center-console or open cabin launch with a dark T-top/canopy and twin outboard engines at the stern.
  3. **Slip 3 (Center-Left)**: A sleek white-hulled express cruiser/yacht with a dark windshield and hardtop, backed into the slip.
  4. **Slip 4 (Center)**: A dark grey aluminum hydrographic survey boat moored bow-in, showing equipment mounts on the cabin roof and twin outboard motors at the stern.
  5. **Slip 5**: A medium grey/white survey launch with a dark cabin top, overhead sensor/antenna arch, and twin outboards at the stern.
  6. **Slip 6**: A larger white flybridge motor yacht/cruiser moored stern-in.
  7. **Slip 7 (Far Right)**: Two matching grey aluminum cabin workboats/survey launches moored side-by-side on either side of the rightmost finger pier, each outfitted with cabin-top air conditioning/electronics enclosures and outboard engines.
