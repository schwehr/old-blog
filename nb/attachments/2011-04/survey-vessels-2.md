# Close-up of Hydrographic Survey Vessels in Tampa Marina (`survey-vessels-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A dock-level photograph showing two matching aluminum hydrographic survey launches moored in adjacent slips at the marina outside the US Hydro 2011 conference in Tampa, Florida.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Survey Launch (Right)**: A bare-metal welded aluminum cabin workboat moored along a floating finger pier. It has a forward-slanted windshield with three front windows, three side cabin windows, twin rectangular bow push knees on the stem, a white rooftop air-conditioning/equipment unit, an aft davit/A-frame structure, and an outboard motor on the stern. Blue lettering along the side of the cabin reads **`SEAS`** followed by smaller text.
- **Middle Survey Launch (Center-Left)**: A nearly identical welded aluminum cabin survey boat in the next slip over, with red lettering on the cabin side reading **`A Nickel More`**, a white rooftop dome/radome and air conditioner unit, and twin bow push knees.
- **Background**: Beyond the two aluminum survey boats is the white hull and cabin of a larger motor yacht on the left, calm marina water reflecting the boats in the foreground, and a row of waterfront buildings, palm trees, and moored boats lining the opposite side of the channel.
