# ERMA Web Application Tracking Cruise Ship in Tampa (`tampa-erma.png`)

## Overview
A composite screenshot of the NOAA/UNH **ERMA (Environmental Response Management Application) – Gulf of Mexico** web interface tracking the cruise ship *Radiance of the Seas* in downtown Tampa, Florida, overlaid with an inset photograph of the ship taken from the 22nd floor of the Tampa Marriott Waterside hotel.

## Top Banner & Toolbar
- **Banner**: Styled with a coastal wave background and titled **`ERMA | Environmental Response Management Application / Gulf of Mexico`**.
- **Top Controls**: `Information`, `Help`, `Upload` tabs on the left, a `Find` search input box in the center, and a `Logout` button on the far right.
- **Map Toolbar**: Above the upper-left corner of the map are six tool icons (pan hand, zoom box, measure, identify `i`, etc.), along with a pan-wheel and vertical zoom slider.

## Map View & Annotations
- **Geographic Area**: A Google Maps street map of downtown **Tampa, Florida**, showing the Garrison Channel, Seddon Channel, Ybor Turning Basin, Harbour Island, Davis Islands, the Tampa Convention Center, St. Pete Times Forum, and Tampa General Hospital.
- **Red Circle Annotation**: A red oval is drawn around the waterfront footprint of the Tampa Marriott Waterside Hotel & Marina along the north side of the Garrison Channel (just east of the Convention Center and North Harbour Island Boulevard bridge), indicating where the author was located.
- **AIS Vessel Symbols**: Three white triangular vessel vectors are plotted in the shipping channel east of Harbour Island:
  - **`RADIANCE OF THE SEAS`** (pointing south-southwest at the Y-junction of the channels)
  - **`JULIE`** (tugboat nearby to the northeast)
  - **`G FREEDOM`** (nearby to the east)

## Inset Photograph (Upper Right)
- Framed in a black border over the right-hand legend panel, with red callout lines connecting to the **`RADIANCE OF THE SEAS`** icon on the map.
- Shows a photograph taken through a hotel window looking across city buildings and trees toward the white cruise ship *Radiance of the Seas* underway in the channel under an overcast sky.

## Right Sidebar: Legend Panel
Under the tabs `Layers`, **`Legend`** (selected), `Query Tools`, `AOI`, `Labels`, `Zoom`, `Download`, `Print` is the heading **`Charts, Surveys, Ships`**:
- **NAIS - All Vessels** legend symbols:
  - White arrow: `General`
  - White arrow: `General2`
  - Orange arrow: `Response`
  - Green arrow: `Research`
  - Yellow arrow: `Skimmer`
  - Blue arrow: `Government`
  - Red arrow: `Source Operations`
