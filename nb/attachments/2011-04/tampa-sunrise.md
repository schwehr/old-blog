# Sunrise over the Marina in Tampa, Florida (`tampa-sunrise.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic morning photograph of sunrise over the waterfront and marina in downtown Tampa, Florida, taken during the US Hydro 2011 conference.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Sun (Center-Left Background)**: The rising sun glows bright yellow-white and orange just above a low bridge spanning the water channel in the distance. Wispy dark grey and golden-lit clouds stretch across the warm dawn sky.
- **Waterfront Promenade (Left)**: A paved walkway runs toward the bridge in the distance, bordered along the water's edge by a white railing fitted with a row of illuminated bollard lights glowing warm orange. Above the promenade are curved gooseneck streetlamps and the silhouette of a tall palm tree against the morning sky.
- **Marina & Boats (Right Foreground)**: Several motor yachts and cabin cruisers with canvas bimini tops and cockpit covers are moored in slips along the floating dock in the foreground and midground, with tall wooden/concrete mooring pilings rising between them.
- **City Skyline (Right Background)**: Across the calm water channel, mid-rise and high-rise buildings on Harbour Island are silhouetted against the golden clouds.
