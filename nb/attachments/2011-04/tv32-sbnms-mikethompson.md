# TV32 Software Displaying Right Whale AIS Area Notice off Cape Cod (`tv32-sbnms-mikethompson.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of a computer monitor running the U.S. Coast Guard / Volpe Center **TV32** AIS display software (`TV32 ver 7.7.48 -- Jun 19 2009 - 14:11:22 -- STARTUP\DEFAULT.CONF -- Running Since: 04/16/2011`), showing AIS vessel tracks and a circular chain of right whale Dynamic Management Area / Area Notice zones stretching from Massachusetts Bay across Stellwagen Bank and around outer Cape Cod.

## Nautical Chart & Overlays
- **Base Chart**: A NOAA raster nautical chart covering the coast of **Massachusetts** from **Cape Ann** (top left) through **Boston Harbor**, **Massachusetts Bay**, **Cape Cod Bay**, and the hook of **Cape Cod** (bottom center), extending eastward into the Gulf of Maine / Wilkinson Basin.
- **Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary Boundary**: Outlined in a grey/blue polygon stretching between Cape Ann and Race Point at the tip of Cape Cod.
- **AIS Vessel Targets**: Colored triangles (orange, green, yellow, blue) clustered mainly in Boston Harbor and Massachusetts Bay, with leader lines connecting them to their callout boxes in the left sidebar.
- **Whale Notice Circular Zones**:
  - Three overlapping green-outlined circles in Massachusetts Bay extending east from Boston Harbor.
  - A curved chain of nine overlapping translucent red-shaded circles (with small square center markers) extending eastward across Massachusetts Bay and Stellwagen Bank, then curving south-southeast along the outer (eastern) backside of Cape Cod, representing right whale warning/management areas broadcast via AIS Aid to Navigation (ATON) / Area Notice messages.
- **Bottom Status Bar**: Shows cursor coordinates (`42°01.187' -69°32.225'`), `Done` status indicator, scale `1:500000`, and Windows XP taskbar at the very bottom.
