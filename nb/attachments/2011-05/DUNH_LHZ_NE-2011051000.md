# UNH Seismometer Low-Pass Filtered Helicorder Plot (`DUNH_LHZ_NE-2011051000.png`)

## Overview
A 24-hour seismic helicorder plot displaying low-pass filtered, long-period vertical-component (`LHZ`) waveform data recorded on May 10, 2011, by station `DUNH` (`NE` network) at the University of New Hampshire in Durham, New Hampshire. The low-frequency filtering removes local high-frequency building and cultural noise, clearly isolating distant teleseismic earthquake waves.

## Plot Layout & Axes
- **Header (Top Left)**:
  - `May10,2011`
  - `DUNH LHZ NE --`
  - `(UNH - Durham, NH [Vertical | Low-Pass])`
- **Vertical Axes (Hours)**:
  - **Left Axis (`EDT`)**: Hourly rows labeled from `21:00` (second row below `EDT`) through `00:00` and down to `19:00`.
  - **Right Axis (`UTC`)**: Corresponding UTC hours labeled from `02:00` (second row) down to `00:00`.
- **Horizontal Axis (`TIME (MINUTES)`)**:
  - Spans `00` to `60` minutes with labeled vertical grid lines every 5 minutes (`00` to `60`) and 1-minute tick marks along the bottom axis.
- **Footer Scale Notes**:
  - Bottom left: `Each Vertical Division = 20.00 microvolts`
  - Bottom right: `Traces clipped at plus/minus 10 vertical divisions`

## Waveform Details
- Hourly traces alternate in color between black, red, blue, and green.
- During quiet hours (most of the day), the low-pass filtered traces are nearly flat horizontal lines with minimal background noise.
- A major teleseismic event starts near minute `55` of the red `04:00` EDT (`09:00` UTC) trace (`04:55` EDT / `09:55` UTC), continues across the entire blue `05:00` EDT (`10:00` UTC) trace with P- and S-wave arrivals, and reaches maximum clipped amplitude (`±10` vertical divisions) between minutes `05` and `17` on the blue `06:00` EDT (`11:00` UTC) trace as long-period surface waves arrive.
- Lower-amplitude coda waves persist across the green `07:00` EDT (`12:00` UTC) and black `08:00` EDT (`13:00` UTC) traces, with smaller long-period wave packets visible later in the day on the green `11:00` EDT (`16:00` UTC) trace (minutes 33–45) and red `13:00` EDT (`18:00` UTC) trace (minutes 15–25).
