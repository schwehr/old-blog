# R/V Coastal Surveyor Bridge Console with Coastal Explorer (`coastal-surveyor-console.jpg`)

## Overview
An interior photograph of the helm and navigation console aboard the University of New Hampshire's research vessel *R/V Coastal Surveyor* during a sea trial on May 27, 2011, showing multiple LCD monitors, marine navigation instruments, helm gauges, and Rose Point *Coastal Explorer* electronic charting software.

## Scene & Equipment Details
- **Monitors (Upper Console)**:
  - **Left Monitor**: A black Dell flat-panel LCD monitor angled toward the port side, resting above a stack of white paper manuals and cables.
  - **Center Monitor**: A square-aspect LCD screen in a black bezel displaying a light gray application window.
  - **Right Monitor**: A black Dell LCD monitor mounted in front of the forward cabin windows, displaying *Coastal Explorer* Electronic Navigational Chart (ENC) software with a dark-toned chart of the Portsmouth/New Castle coastal area (brown land mass on the left, blue-gray depth contours in the channel, and a vessel list sidebar on the right). A white label sticker is affixed to the top bezel.
- **Control Panels & Input Devices (Lower Console)**:
  - **Center Angled Pod**:
    - Mounted along the top edge of the pod is a gray Furuno radar/navigation control keyboard with a trackball on the right and an array of round push buttons and rotary knobs.
    - Below left is a silver-and-black Logitech wired trackball mouse with a dark red ball.
    - Below center are two black marine instrument displays (such as depth/wind/autopilot repeaters) with small LCD screens and push-button pads.
  - **Right Helm Panel**:
    - A black full-size computer keyboard rests just ahead of the instrument panel.
    - Next to the center pod sit an orange-and-yellow aluminum beverage can and a clear plastic water bottle with a blue label and white cap.
    - Flush-mounted into the gray helm dash are black rocker switches, a small rectangular thruster/autopilot controller pad, and a cluster of six circular analog engine gauges (including a large central tachometer/dial flanked by smaller temperature, pressure, and voltage gauges).
- **Foreground**:
  - In the lower right corner, the bare right forearm and hand of the vessel operator (Ben) rest on the edge of the console.
