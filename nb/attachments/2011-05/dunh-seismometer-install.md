# Configuring the UNH Seismometer with a Palm Pilot (`dunh-seismometer-install.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up indoor photograph taken in early May 2011 in the basement of James Hall at the University of New Hampshire, showing researchers configuring the new UNH seismometer data logger using a handheld Palm Pilot PDA inside a wooden equipment cabinet.
