# EdgeTech Sidescan Sonar and HYPACK Survey Acquisition Monitors (`edgetech-and-hypack.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of the dual-monitor hydrographic data acquisition workstation aboard the *R/V Coastal Surveyor* on May 27, 2011, showing EdgeTech sidescan/sub-bottom sonar logging software on the left monitor and HYPACK hydrographic survey software on the right monitor.

## Workstation & Screen Details
- **Left Monitor (EdgeTech DISCOVER Acquisition Software)**:
  - A black HP widescreen LCD monitor sits behind a black computer keyboard.
  - **Left Pane**: Displays a yellow status/configuration region at the top and a multi-column numerical telemetry/status readout table below.
  - **Main Waterfall Display**: Shows a two-channel (port and starboard) sidescan sonar waterfall plot rendered in warm copper/orange/black tones, with bright vertical nadir returns down the center gap and acoustic backscatter textures across the swath on either side.
  - **Bottom Bar**: Displays digital time/ping readouts and the embossed `EdgeTech` logo in the lower right of the application window.
- **Right Monitor (HYPACK Survey & Sonar Interface)**:
  - A matching black HP widescreen LCD monitor angled slightly toward the viewer.
  - **Left Window (Chart View)**: Displays a color-shaded bathymetric grid and nautical chart of the Piscataqua River / Portsmouth Harbor mouth (tan land area on the left, cyan/green bathymetry in the channel, and depth soundings).
  - **Right Stacked Windows (Profile / Real-Time Displays)**: Two black-background graph windows showing green and cyan cross-track profile lines against horizontal grid lines, with a vertical rainbow color scale bar (`red` to `green` to `blue`) along the far right edge.
  - **Bottom Center Window**: A smaller black sub-window with a vertical color bar.
- **Desk & Background**:
  - Black power and data cables run behind the monitor stands, and yellow cabling is bundled along the dark bulkhead at the top of the frame.
