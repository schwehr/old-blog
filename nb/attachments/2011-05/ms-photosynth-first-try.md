# Microsoft Photosynth iPhone Panorama of CCOM Office Cubicles (`ms-photosynth-first-try.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle stitched panoramic photograph created using the Microsoft Photosynth app on an iPhone 3GS in May 2011, showing the open-plan cubicle workspace inside the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM / JHC) at the University of New Hampshire, with colleague Brian O. seated at a desk on the right.

## Scene & Composition
- **Panorama Framing**:
  - The stitched mosaic spans roughly 180–270 degrees across the office interior, with irregular black unphotographed borders along the bottom foreground and upper right where individual frames did not cover the scene.
- **Office Environment**:
  - **Left & Center**: Rows of dark blue-gray fabric-walled office cubicles with gray laminate desk surfaces. On the back wall at left-center hangs a large color bathymetric/topographic wall map illuminated by overhead fluorescent ceiling troffers.
  - **Upper Background**: White drop-ceiling tiles with recessed rectangular fluorescent light fixtures, red glowing `EXIT` signs, and exterior windows along the far walls.
- **Subject (Lower Right)**:
  - Brian O., wearing a light blue/gray long-sleeved button-down shirt and khaki trousers, sits in a dark office chair inside a cubicle on the right. He turns his head over his right shoulder toward the camera with an animated, open-mouthed expression while resting his hands near a laptop computer on the corner desk.
