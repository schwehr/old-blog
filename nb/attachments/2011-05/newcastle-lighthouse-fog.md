# Portsmouth Harbor (New Castle) Lighthouse in Thick Fog (`newcastle-lighthouse-fog.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide horizontal maritime photograph taken from the *R/V Coastal Surveyor* on May 27, 2011, showing Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse (Fort Constitution Light) at New Castle, New Hampshire, emerging from a dense layer of coastal fog over the dark waters of the Piscataqua River mouth.

## Scene Description
- **Lighthouse & Shore Structures (Center)**:
  - The white cylindrical tower of Portsmouth Harbor Lighthouse with its black lantern room and gallery rises prominently just left of center, softly illuminated against the gray-tan fog bank behind it.
  - To the right of the lighthouse stand the historic stone walls of Fort Constitution and a white building with a pitched red roof and green-shuttered gable windows, backed by green treetops and distant shoreline buildings fading into the mist on the right.
- **Fog & Water**:
  - A low, thick band of white-gray sea fog hovers directly over the water surface, partiallyveiling the base of the lighthouse, the fort walls, and the entire left side of the horizon.
  - The foreground consists of dark slate-blue rippled harbor water, with a faint vertical reflection of the white lighthouse tower shimmering on the water surface below it.
