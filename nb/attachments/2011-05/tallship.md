# Tall Ship Escorted by Fire-Boat Tug in Portsmouth Harbor (`tallship.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide horizontal maritime photograph taken from the *R/V Coastal Surveyor* on May 27, 2011, showing a three-masted tall ship under sail entering the Piscataqua River / Portsmouth Harbor, escorted by a tugboat spraying celebratory water arcs from its fire monitors and accompanied by several smaller vessels in the hazy morning light.

## Scene Description
- **Tall Ship (Right of Center)**:
  - A large three-masted barque/sailing vessel with a white hull, moving from right to left in profile, with dark triangular staysails and jibs set between its masts and a small flag flying from the mizzen peak.
  - A dark-hulled escort tugboat runs closely alongside its port quarter, and shimmering vertical reflections of the masts extend across the calm harbor water below.
- **Escort Vessels & Fire-Hose Display (Left & Center)**:
  - **Far Left**: A tugboat sends two tall, curving white plumes of water into the air from its fire hoses against the wooded shoreline background.
  - **Left of Center**: A dark-hulled patrol or work boat with a raised pilothouse moves ahead of the tall ship.
  - **Surrounding Small Craft**: Several smaller pleasure boats and escort craft trail behind and alongside the tall ship across the wide river channel.
- **Water & Atmosphere**:
  - Calm, Rippled gray-tan water fills the foreground with two small dark mooring/channel buoys floating in the mid-ground. A low wooded shoreline stretches across the horizon beneath a pale, hazy sky.
