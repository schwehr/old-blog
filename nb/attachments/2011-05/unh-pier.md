# UNH Judd Gregg Marine Research Complex Pier in New Castle (`unh-pier.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide horizontal photograph taken on the morning of May 27, 2011, showing the University of New Hampshire (UNH) pier and harbor basin at the Judd Gregg Marine Research Complex (and adjacent US Coast Guard Station Portsmouth Harbor) in New Castle, New Hampshire, prior to departing on the *R/V Coastal Surveyor*.

## Scene Description
- **UNH Pier & Vessels (Left to Center-Left)**:
  - A concrete/timber commercial pier extends from the left shoreline out toward the center of the harbor, equipped with streetlamps, pilings, a yellow shore structure on the left, and an orange-yellow davit/crane at the outer end of the pier.
  - Several small research and work boats (including white-, blue-, and red-hulled vessels with cabin superstructures) are moored side-by-side along the inner face of the pier.
- **Harbor Basin & Floating Docks (Center & Right)**:
  - Calm, glassy gray water reflects the sky and surrounding structures.
  - In the mid-ground right of the pier end floats a low gray research barge/platform with a small A-frame or davit.
  - On the right side of the basin stands a large two-story light-blue/gray boathouse with a pitched roof on a stone/concrete bulkhead, connected via a gangway to a floating dock.
  - In the right foreground, just beyond the marsh grass, a square wooden floating dock frame (open in the center) rests on the water.
- **Foreground & Background**:
  - The foreground shore is lined with green grass, tall dry reeds/marsh stalks, and a rocky riprap bank on the far left.
  - Across the Piscataqua River in the distant background stretches the low, tree-lined Kittery, Maine shoreline under a hazy pale morning sky.
