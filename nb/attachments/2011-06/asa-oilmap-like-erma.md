# ASA OILMAP Software Web Page Screenshot (`asa-oilmap-like-erma.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Applied Science Associates (ASA) product web page for **OILMAP™**, an oil spill model and response system. Taken in July 2011, the page outlines the software's capabilities, applications, features, and highlights, alongside a photograph of a grounded bulk carrier.

## Page Layout & Visual Elements
- **Header Banner**: At the very top is a light blue-gray wave graphic banner with the word **"Software"** in white text on the left. Below it is a sub-header bar with **"OILMAP™"** on the left and a PDF icon labeled **"DOWNLOAD PDF"** in reddish-brown text on the right.
- **Product Logo & Title**: On the upper left is the **OILMAP™** logo, featuring a blue circular globe/wave emblem with three small green hexagons above the stylized text **"OILmap™"** ("OIL" in thin dark gray uppercase letters and "map" in bold dark gray lowercase letters). To the right of the logo is the main heading: **"Oil Spill Model and Response System"**.
- **Navigation Sidebar ("More on this Software")**: On the upper right, under a gray-underlined heading **"More on this Software"**, is a vertical list of links, each preceded by a small blue square icon containing a white right-pointing triangle:
  - Software Demo Video
  - OILMAP Overview
  - Version 6 Highlights
  - v6.1.25 New Features
  - OILMAP User's Blog
  - Updates & Support
- **Ship Photograph**: Below the navigation links on the right is a color aerial photograph of the stern and starboard side of the grounded bulk carrier *New Carissa* in heavy surf near a sandy shoreline. The ship has a dark hull, white aft superstructure, a tan funnel with a logo, and deck cargo cranes.

## Visible Text & Feature Lists
- **Introductory Paragraph** (left column, accented by a light green vertical rectangle on the far left margin):
  > **OILMAP** provides rapid predictions of the movement of spilled oil. It includes simple graphical procedures for entering both wind and hydrodynamic data and specifying a spill scenario.
- **Applications for OILMAP** (bulleted list with green square bullets):
  - Oil spill response decision support
  - Oil spill response training
  - Spill drill exercises
  - Contingency planning studies
  - Litigation support
  - Management of spill related data
  - Communicate spill scenarios
- **Features** (bulleted list with green square bullets in the lower-left column):
  - Uses ASA's own GIS or can be used in other GIS software such as ArcView®
  - Updates predictions with over flight data
  - Incorporates Boom-Oil interaction
  - Dispersant Application
  - Use of NOAA's Adios oil database of nearly 1,000 oils for weathering calculations
  - Develop Coastal Planning and Management Data Bases using the interactive GIS
  - Capability to integrate response plans
  - Online web map and metocean data services
  - Seamless integration of ASA's EDS: Environmental Data Server™ real-time and historical global environmental data from top data providers
- **OILMAP Highlights** (enclosed in a rounded gray-bordered box in the lower-right column, with a blue heading and green square bullets):
  - World-wide capability
  - 2D and 3D capability
  - Weathering and surface/subsurface transport of spilled oil
  - Predicts the probability of key areas being impacted from a given spill
  - Backtracking to determine the likely spill site position
  - Risk assessments for important resources
  - Oil model interactions with shoreline, reed beds, and ice coverage
  - EDS: Environmental Data Server™ integration
