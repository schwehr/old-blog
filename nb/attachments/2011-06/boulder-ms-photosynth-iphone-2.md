# Microsoft Photosynth Panorama of a Trail and Hillside in Boulder, Colorado (`boulder-ms-photosynth-iphone-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide panoramic photograph of a grassy hillside and hiking trail in Boulder, Colorado, created using the Microsoft Photosynth iPhone application in June 2011. The image illustrates both the wide field of view and the characteristic jagged black borders and stitching artifacts produced by handheld panorama capture.

## Scene Description
- **Hillside & Vegetation**: The right half of the scene shows a steep, grassy foothill rising toward a rocky ridge line dotted with scattered ponderosa pines and scrub. In the grassy foreground just right of center lies a large, weathered, leafless fallen tree branch or dead shrub with gray, twisting limbs.
- **Trails & Person**: Two narrow dirt singletrack trails cut through the green grass—one running vertically from the bottom left-center into the distance, and another curving up toward the hillside on the right. Near the center of the panorama, standing on the grassy rise between the trails next to pine trees, a person in dark athletic clothing is partially visible (slightly fragmented by a stitching seam).
- **Left Vista & Sky**: On the left side, a line of dark green pine trees frames a distant view of the flat plains and horizon under a bright, overcast white and gray sky, with patches of blue sky visible above the ridge to the right.
- **Panorama Borders**: Irregular, stepped black regions frame the top and bottom edges of the composite image, with a prominent black step jutting upward into the lower-right foreground where lower-angle frames were not captured.
