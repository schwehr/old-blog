# Microsoft Photosynth Panorama of Boulder, Colorado Foothills (`boulder-ms-photosynth-iphone.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide, stitched panoramic photograph of the foothills and open grassland in Boulder, Colorado, captured at low sun (sunrise or sunset) using the Microsoft Photosynth app on an iPhone in June 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Topography & Hills**: The center and right portions of the panorama are dominated by a long, rounded foothill ridge bathed in warm golden-orange sunlight. Sparse scrub and rocky outcrops dot the upper crest of the ridge, while a large dark shadow stretches across the lower slope and valley floor. Further in the distance on the left-center, a lower mountain peak is silhouetted against the horizon.
- **Foreground & Trails**: In the lower foreground, two dirt paths or roads meet in a wide curve within the shadowed grassland, leading from the lower right toward the middle distance at the base of the sunlit slope.
- **Trees & Left Horizon**: On the far left, a cluster of dark, leafy trees stands in silhouette against the bright pale-yellow and light-blue sky near the horizon.
- **Sky & Stitching**: The sky transitions from a pale, bright horizon on the far left and far right to a deep, clear blue overhead at the center. Because the image is an uncropped mosaic from the Photosynth iPhone app, the outer edges have a slightly curved, stepped black border where individual frames were projected and stitched together.
