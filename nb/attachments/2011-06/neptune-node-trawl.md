# Google Earth View of a Vessel Track Over NEPTUNE Canada Barkley Canyon Nodes (`neptune-node-trawl.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of **Google Earth** displaying an AIS-derived vessel track (`316011441.kml`) passing directly over the underwater **NEPTUNE Canada** cabled observatory infrastructure at **Barkley Canyon** off Vancouver Island, British Columbia, between February 17 and February 19, 2011. The visualization was generated using Kurt Schwehr's `noaadata` package to investigate a February 18, 2011 incident in which a fishing trawler dragged its net across the seafloor observatory.

## Interface & Controls
- **Window Title & Toolbar**: At the top is the standard Mac OS X window title bar labeled **"Google Earth"** with red, yellow, and green window buttons on the left. Below it is the Google Earth icon toolbar featuring buttons for toggling the sidebar, adding a placemark, polygon, path, image overlay, recording a tour, showing historical imagery, showing sunlight, switching between Earth/Sky/Mars/Moon, ruler, email, print, and viewing in Google Maps.
- **Left Sidebar (`Search` & `Places`)**:
  - **Search Panel**: Shows three tabs (`Fly To` selected in blue, `Find Businesses`, and `Directions`), the prompt `"Fly to e.g., 37 25.818' N, 122 05.36' W"`, and an empty text input box with a dropdown arrow and magnifying glass button.
  - **Places Panel**: Shows a tree view with `My Places` and `Temporary Places` expanded:
    - Checked KML file: `316011441.kml` (highlighted in light blue)
    - Checked subfolder: `316011441 (4024)`
    - Checked KML layer: `NEPTUNE Canada cable and no...`
- **Time Slider Overlay**: In the upper-left corner of the 3D map viewport is the Google Earth timeline control displaying:
  - Start timestamp at the top: `2/17/2011 8:00 pm`
  - End timestamp above the right slider thumb: `2/19/2011 12:49 am`
  - Range labels below the bar: `2/17/2011` on the left and `2/19/2011` on the right, with a wrench (options) icon and close (`X`) button in the upper right of the widget.

## Map & Bathymetry Details
- **Bathymetric Base Map**: Shows shaded-relief ocean floor bathymetry in shades of blue, depicting submarine canyons and ridges along the continental slope. Faint dark text in the background reads `"Image by K. Schwehr"`, and light blue map labels mark **`Barkley Canyon`** at the bottom center (as well as a partially obscured canyon label under the time slider).
- **Observatory Nodes**: Two overlapping yellow pushpin icons in the center mark **`Barkley Canyon Node`** (labeled to the left) and **`Barkley Canyon Branching Unit`** (labeled to the right).
- **Vessel Track**: A thin white polyline enters from the upper right, heads southwest down the slope, makes a sharp loop/turn at the bottom right near the mouth of Barkley Canyon, passes northwest directly across the yellow pushpins of the `Barkley Canyon Node` and `Barkley Canyon Branching Unit`, and then turns southeast again before heading back northeast.
