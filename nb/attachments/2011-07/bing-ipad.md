# Bing App Home Screen on an Apple iPad (`bing-ipad.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Microsoft **Bing** application running in landscape orientation on an Apple iPad (taken at 4:26 PM on July 26, 2011, while Kurt Schwehr was visiting Google in Mountain View, California). The interface features Bing's daily background photograph of the Seattle/Elliott Bay waterfront at sunset, a top search bar, and a bottom ribbon of live information tiles.

## Interface & Controls
- **iOS Status Bar**: Across the very top is the black iOS status bar showing **`iPad`** and the Wi-Fi signal icon on the left, **`4:26 PM`** in the center, and the location services arrow, orientation lock icon, **`66%`**, and battery icon on the right.
- **Top Search & Navigation Bar**: Below the status bar is a dark navy/charcoal toolbar containing:
  - A wide white search input box with gray placeholder text **`Search the web`** and an orange square magnifying glass button on its right end.
  - A white microphone icon for voice search to the right of the search bar.
  - On the right side, an orange overlapping-squares icon, a white 2x2 grid icon, a white gear (settings) icon, and the white **`bing`** logo.
- **Main Background Image**: Occupies the central expanse of the screen, showing an aerial view looking west across **Elliott Bay** in Seattle, Washington, under dramatic dark blue storm clouds lit by bright golden-yellow sunlight on the left horizon. A cargo ship is underway on the golden water on the left, while the illuminated waterfront, grain terminal pier, marina, and urban buildings of Seattle sit in the lower right foreground. A small white copyright symbol (`©`) is visible in the lower right corner of the photo.
- **Bottom Content Tiles**: A horizontal row of six translucent dark-headed cards spans the bottom of the screen:
  1. **WEATHER**: Shows a bright yellow sun icon, **`74°`**, **`Clear`**, **`Mountain View, CA`**, and attribution **`Map, AIRNow, 1 min(s) ago`**.
  2. **NEWS**: Headline reads **`Tim DeChristopher: Do crimes of civil disobedience deser...`** with source **`Los Angeles Times, 3 hr(s) ago`**.
  3. **MAPS**: Displays a road map centered on **`Mountain View`** (marked with a blue-and-white circular location dot), showing **`Palo Alto`**, **`East Palo Alto`**, **`Stanford`**, **`Los Altos`**, and **`Sunnyvale`**, along with highways 101, 85, and 280.
  4. **MOVIES**: Shows movie poster thumbnails for *Transformers: Dark of the Moon* and *Horrible Bosses* (`HORRIBLE BOSSES`).
  5. **TRENDS**: Shows a photograph of television commentator Glenn Beck with his right hand raised.
  6. **FINANCE**: Lists three major stock indices with red downward-pointing triangles:
     - **`DOW`** `▼ 12,501.30`
     - **`NAS`** `▼ 2,839.96`
     - **`S&P`** `▼ 1,331.94`
     - Attribution at bottom: **`Comstock, 3 hr(s) ago`**.
