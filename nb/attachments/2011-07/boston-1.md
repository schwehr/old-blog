# Aerial View of Boston Harbor, Nahant, and Logan Airport from an Airplane (`boston-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique aerial photograph taken through an airplane window looking southwest over **Broad Sound**, **Nahant**, **Winthrop**, and **Boston Harbor**, Massachusetts, under scattered white clouds.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Coastline**: In the lower-right quadrant, the wooded island of **Nahant** juts out into the water, connected to the mainland at **Lynn** on the far right by the narrow, sandy strip of the Nahant Causeway / Nahant Beach. Across the bay to the upper center-left is the curved shoreline of **Revere Beach** and the peninsula of **Winthrop** extending toward **Deer Island**.
- **Boston Harbor & Logan Airport**: Beyond Winthrop in the middle distance lies **Boston Logan International Airport**, with its crisscrossing runways visible on the flat peninsula protruding into Boston Harbor, surrounded by harbor islands on the left.
- **Water & Vessels**: The dark blue-green waters of Broad Sound and outer Boston Harbor fill the lower half of the frame, where several small boats and ships leave thin white wakes on the surface.
- **Clouds & Wingtip**: Clusters of puffy, sunlit white cumulus clouds float in the middle distance above the metropolitan area, while a small section of the airplane's wingtip is visible in the bottom-left corner.
