# Aerial View Over Massachusetts Bay and Nahant with Airplane Wing and Engine (`boston-2.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken from a passenger seat just aft of the port wing and jet engine of a commercial airliner flying over the dark blue waters of **Massachusetts Bay** / **Broad Sound** near **Nahant**, Massachusetts.

## Scene Description
- **Aircraft Wing & Engine**: Across the bottom of the frame is the white upper surface of the airplane's wing and the forward cowling/pylon of an underwing turbofan engine on the right, with a black leading-edge stripe and rows of rivets clearly visible.
- **Ocean & Vessels**: The central expanse of the image is filled with deep blue coastal water. Several vessels and small boats—including a larger cargo ship or tanker near the left-center and smaller craft with white wakes—are scattered across the water, along with a small rocky outcrop or island near the lower left above the wing.
- **Shoreline in the Background**: In the upper portion of the frame, the distinctive tombolo of **Nahant** and its long curved sandy beach/causeway connect to the mainland coast of **Lynn** and the North Shore of Massachusetts.
