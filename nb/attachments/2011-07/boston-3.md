# Banking Airplane Wing Over Boston Harbor (`boston-3.jpg`)

## Overview
An oblique photograph looking out a passenger cabin window along the upturned port wing and winglet of a commercial airliner banking over **Boston Harbor**, Massachusetts.

## Scene Description
- **Airplane Wing & Winglet**: The airplane's silver-white wing angles steeply upward from the bottom center toward the upper center-right, tipped with a tall, dark blue/red angled winglet catching a bright glint of sunlight at its base. Two red-painted flap track fairings project beneath the trailing edge of the wing on the left. The dark frame of the cabin window is visible along the far left edge.
- **Boston Harbor & Islands**: To the left of the wing, far below through a light atmospheric haze, lies **Boston Harbor**, showing the **Winthrop** peninsula, **Deer Island**, **Logan International Airport**, and several elongated harbor islands (such as Long Island and Spectacle Island) surrounded by small boat wakes.
- **Sky & Clouds**: Above the distant horizon on the left and right are scattered layers of white cumulus and stratocumulus clouds against a blue sky.
