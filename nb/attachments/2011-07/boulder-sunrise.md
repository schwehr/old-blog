# Sunrise Over a Pond and Open Space in Boulder, Colorado (`boulder-sunrise.jpg`)

## Overview
A scenic landscape photograph taken at dawn in Boulder, Colorado, looking across a grassy hillside and a calm, tree-lined pond toward a band of pink and orange sunrise light along the eastern horizon under a cloudy sky.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Sunrise Glow**: The upper half of the image is filled with a textured layer of dark blue-gray stratocumulus clouds broken by bright silvery-white gaps. Beneath the cloud deck along the flat distant horizon is a warm horizontal band of salmon-pink and soft orange morning light.
- **Pond & Midground**: In the middle distance, a still pond reflects the pale peach and silvery tones of the dawn sky, framed by dark silhouettes of deciduous trees, grassy banks, and a few residential houses nestled among the trees on the left and right.
- **Foreground Hillside & Trails**: The lower foreground consists of an open meadow covered in tall, pale green and golden-tan grasses sloping down toward the pond. A narrow paved or gravel walking path winds from the lower right corner (where a small white sign post stands next to a tree) across the green grass toward the left midground.
