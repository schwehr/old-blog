# Paddling a Canoe on Bellamy Reservoir at Early Morning (`canoe-june-21.jpg`)

## Overview
A first-person photograph taken from the stern of a canoe looking forward over the bow across the calm, mirror-like waters of **Bellamy Reservoir** in New Hampshire during an early morning paddle on June 21, 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Canoe Bow**: In the bottom center of the frame, the pointed bow of a dark-hulled canoe with warm wooden gunwales and a wooden bow thwart points straight ahead toward the northern shore of the reservoir.
- **Sky & Clouds**: Above the treeline, an expansive sky is filled with dappled, mackerel-like altocumulus and stratocumulus clouds lit from the upper right by the rising morning sun, creating a mix of dark blue-gray cloud undersides, patches of clear blue sky, and bright golden-white highlights.
- **Water Reflection & Shoreline**: The still surface of the reservoir acts as a mirror, reflecting the textured clouds, blue sky, and the dark silhouette of the coniferous and deciduous forest lining the distant shore and the taller pine trees on the right bank.
