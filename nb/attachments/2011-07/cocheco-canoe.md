# Canoeing on the Still Waters of the Cocheco River (`cocheco-canoe.jpg`)

## Overview
A point-of-view photograph looking forward over the bow and cane front seat of a canoe gliding along a calm, tree-lined stretch of the **Cocheco River** upstream of the Dover dam in New Hampshire during an early morning paddle in July 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Canoe Interior & Bow**: Occupying the lower-right foreground is the forward section of the canoe, showing its smooth gray-brown interior hull, varnished wood gunwales, a wooden bow carry handle/thwart, and the woven cane bow seat supported by light wood framing.
- **River & Reflections**: The glassy surface of the Cocheco River fills the middle of the frame, sharply reflecting the pale blue sky on the left and the sunlit green canopy and pale leaning tree trunks along the banks. A faint wisp of morning mist hovers just above the water line along the far bank.
- **Forested Banks**: Dense summer foliage—including tall white pines, birches, and overhanging hardwoods illuminated by warm morning sunlight— encloses the river bend ahead and along both the left and right shorelines.
