# Early Morning Rapids and Bridge on the Cocheco River (`cocheco-early-am.jpg`)

## Overview
A water-level photograph looking upstream at a rocky set of low falls and rapids on the **Cocheco River** in New Hampshire, with a highway overpass bridge and wooded riverbank in the background during early morning.

## Scene Description
- **Rapids & Rocks**: The lower half of the image shows white, frothy river water tumbling over dark, jagged, exposed rocks and ledges across the width of the channel, swirling into foam-streaked eddies in the immediate foreground.
- **Bridge**: Spanning the right half of the midground just above the top of the rocky rapids is a horizontal steel-girder and concrete road bridge with a metal guardrail along its deck, supported by a cylindrical concrete pier rising from the river.
- **Trees & Sky**: Behind the rapids on the left and beyond the bridge on the right, a lush green forest of deciduous trees and tall pines lines the riverbanks under a bright, clear pale morning sky.
