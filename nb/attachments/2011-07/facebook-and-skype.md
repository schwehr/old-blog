# Facebook Profile Action Buttons with Skype Video Call Feature (`facebook-and-skype.png`)

## Overview
A cropped screenshot of the upper-right navigation bar and profile action buttons on **Facebook** (taken July 6, 2011), highlighting the newly introduced **"Call"** video chat button powered by Skype.

## Interface & Visible Text
- **Top Blue Navigation Bar**: Shows the classic dark blue Facebook header bar with white bold text links on the right: **`Home`**, **`Profile`**, and **`Account ▾`** (with a small white downward-pointing dropdown triangle).
- **Action Button Row**: Below the header bar against a white background is a horizontal group of three rectangular light-gray gradient buttons with gray borders and dark charcoal icons and labels:
  1. **`Message`** — preceded by a dark gray speech-bubble icon.
  2. **`Call`** — preceded by a dark gray video-camera icon (used to initiate a Facebook-to-Facebook Skype video call).
  3. **`Poke`** — preceded by a dark gray pointing-hand icon.
