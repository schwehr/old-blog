# GigaPan Time Machine Plant Growth Viewer (`gigapan-timemachine.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **GigaPan Time Machine** web player displaying a high-resolution, zoomable time-lapse video of flowering plants (`Plant Growth`). The interface combines panoramic deep-zoom navigation controls with video playback and speed controls.

## Scene Description
- **Plant Time-Lapse Viewport**:
  - Set against a dark black/charcoal background, two white plastic seedling flats/trays with square cells sit at the bottom of the frame.
  - Growing out of the soil cells are numerous green leafy stalks (such as *Brassica* / fast plants) topped with bright yellow flower clusters.
  - The plants are shown mid-growth at timestamp `00:49.22` out of `01:57.96`, with tall stems reaching varying heights across the frame.

## Interface & Controls
- **Header Banner**:
  - A dark gray/black top banner features the title `GigaPan Time Machine`, with `GigaPan` in light gray sans-serif font and `Time Machine` in bold white font.
- **Zoom & Fullscreen Overlay (Left)**:
  - Positioned vertically along the left side of the video canvas:
    - A circular `+` button at the top for zooming in.
    - A vertical gray slider track with a rectangular white thumb handle near the bottom of the range.
    - A circular `-` button at the bottom of the slider for zooming out.
    - Below the zoom slider is a circular fullscreen/expand button showing four outward-pointing diagonal arrows.
- **Playback Control Bar (Bottom)**:
  - Directly beneath the video frame is a horizontal timeline scrubber bar (filled in slate blue up to roughly 42% of the way across, with a white rectangular playhead handle).
  - Below the timeline is a light gray control bar containing:
    - A large square play button (`►`) on the far left.
    - A horizontal playback speed/rate slider labeled with tick marks `-1`, `-.5`, `.5`, and `1`, with the slider thumb set at `1`.
    - The current time and total duration readout in bold black text: `00:49.22/01:57.96`.
    - A size/resolution dropdown menu currently set to `Small ▼`.
    - A rectangular `Help` button on the far right.
