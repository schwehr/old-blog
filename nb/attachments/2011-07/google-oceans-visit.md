# Entrance to the Google Earth Building at the Googleplex (`google-oceans-visit.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph taken outside building **GWC 1 (2025 Garcia Ave)** at the Googleplex in Mountain View, California, during Kurt Schwehr's visit to the Google Oceans team in July 2011. The photo features a Google Maps Street View car, a giant three-dimensional Google Maps red pin sculpture, and the building entrance sign.

## Scene Description
- **Google Maps Street View Car (Left)**:
  - Parked on the paved walkway in front of the building is a compact hatchback automobile wrapped in a colorful Google Maps livery (green, white, yellow road lines, and blue water accents).
  - The front driver-side door displays the text `Google maps` and `Street View` in white lettering, with `google.com/streetview` along the lower rocker panel and a small "Pegman" yellow figure painted on the rear door.
  - An orange wheel chock rests under the front tire.
  - Mounted atop the roof rack is a tall black-and-white mast supporting the red spherical multi-lens Street View camera rosette.
- **Giant Google Maps Pin (Center)**:
  - Standing vertically on the edge of the lawn right next to the car is a large, human-sized 3D sculpture of the classic Google Maps drop pin marker, with a bright red face, a central dark circular dot, and thick black sides.
- **Building Sign & Landscaping (Right)**:
  - In the right foreground, set in a small garden bed with ornamental grass and small purple flowers surrounded by green lawn, is a rectangular off-white monument sign.
  - The sign features the classic multicolored serif `Google` logo above dark raised lettering reading:
    - `GWC 1`
    - `2025 Garcia Ave.`
- **Background**:
  - Behind the car and sign is the facade of the office building, consisting of tall, dark-tinted reflective glass windows framed by light gray concrete columns, reflecting dense evergreen trees.
