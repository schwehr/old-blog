# Pink Water Lily Flower Among Lily Pads (`lilipad-flower-pink.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up nature photograph taken from a canoe on Bellamy Reservoir in New Hampshire, showing a vibrant pink water lily flower (*Nymphaea*) blooming on the surface of dark pond water surrounded by floating lily pads of various sizes.

## Scene Description
- **Pink Water Lily (Lower Right)**:
  - Positioned in the lower-right quadrant is a cup-shaped pink water lily blossom in full bloom.
  - Multiple layers of soft pink petals curve upward around a bright golden-orange center of stamens.
  - Reddish-pink outer sepals rest on the water's surface at the base of the bloom.
- **Lily Pads**:
  - Numerous oval and circular lily pads float across the entire frame, ranging from small bright-green young leaves to larger, mature dark-green and reddish-bronze pads with prominent radial veins and a characteristic V-shaped notch (sinus).
  - Near the far left edge, a small unopened flower bud pokes just above the water.
- **Water & Reflections**:
  - The calm water between the pads acts like a dark mirror, reflecting bright white and gray clouds in the sky overhead, with subtle surface tension ripples curving around the edges of each floating leaf.
