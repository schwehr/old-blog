# Nagios / RRDtool Graph Showing NAIS Feed Downtime (`nais-down.png`)

## Overview
A time-series monitoring graph generated by RRDtool / NagiosGrapher titled `(AIS_FEED) - 2 Daily Graph`, illustrating the rate of incoming Automatic Identification System (AIS) records over roughly a 38-hour period ending on Thursday, July 21, 2011 at 12:25. The plot highlights a multi-hour outage on Thursday morning where the record rate dropped to zero before being restarted.

## Plot Details & Axes
- **Title & Metadata**:
  - Top center title: `(AIS_FEED) - 2 Daily Graph`
  - Top left generation time watermark: `(0.216517s)`
  - Right edge vertical watermark: `RRDTOOL / TOBI OETIKER / NagiosGrapher by NETWAYS GMBH`
  - Bottom right timestamp: `[12:25/2011-07-21]`
- **Y-Axis (`new records / Records`)**:
  - Ranges from `0.0` at the bottom to `6.0 k` at the top, with major horizontal dotted red gridlines every `1.0 k` (`0.0`, `1.0 k`, `2.0 k`, `3.0 k`, `4.0 k`, `5.0 k`, `6.0 k`) and minor gray gridlines every `0.2 k`.
- **X-Axis (Time)**:
  - Spans from Tuesday evening (~19:00) through Thursday at `12:00`.
  - Labeled every 12 hours at `Wed 00:00`, `Wed 12:00`, `Thu 00:00`, and `Thu 12:00`.
  - Solid vertical red lines mark midnight boundaries (`Wed 00:00` and `Thu 00:00`).

## Data Series & Outage Event
- **Legend (Bottom Left)**:
  - Green square: `Records`
  - Blue square: `AIS Feed Average Records`
- **Trend & Downtime**:
  - The **blue line** (`AIS Feed Average Records`) runs horizontally across the entire chart just below `5.0 k` (~`4.95 k`).
  - The **green line** (`Records`) remains steady between roughly `5.5 k` and `5.8 k` throughout Tuesday night and all of Wednesday.
  - Around `02:30` on Thursday morning, there is a brief dip down to `4.9 k` before recovering to `5.5 k`.
  - Around `06:00` on Thursday morning, the green line plummets sharply in steps to `0.0`, remaining flat at `0.0` for roughly five and a half hours until around `11:45`, when the `AISUser.jar` process was restarted and the rate jumped back up to `5.5 k`.
