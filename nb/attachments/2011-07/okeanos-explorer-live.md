# NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer Live Multibeam Mapping Stream (`okeanos-explorer-live.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the NOAA Ocean Explorer web page streaming live video of the Kongsberg SIS (Seafloor Information System) multibeam sonar acquisition screen aboard the **NOAA Ship *Okeanos Explorer*** on July 27, 2011. The display shows real-time bathymetric swath mapping alongside full water-column acoustic backscatter data.

## Webpage Frame & Header
- **Top Banner**: A dark blue graphic banner displaying the NOAA emblem and the stylized title `OKEANOS explorer`.
- **Navigation**: A `◄ Back` link appears in the upper right above the video player.
- **Video Watermark**: Inside the upper-left corner of the video feed is a semi-transparent overlay featuring the NOAA logo and the text `OKEANOS EXPLORER 2011`.
- **Footer Troubleshooting Instructions**:
  Below the video stream, italicized text reads:
  > *You may need to download the [Flash browser plugin](#) to view the feed. If this stream does not play, please try the following temporary fix:*
  > *1. Right-click on the player window and select "Settings"*
  > *2. Select the TV icon at the lower left of the settings menu.*
  > *3. Uncheck the "Enable Hardware acceleration" checkbox*
  > *4. Refresh the page.*

## Multibeam Acquisition Interface (Kongsberg SIS)
- **Left Column Panels**:
  - **Beam Intensity (Top)**: Bar/line plot showing red and blue acoustic return intensity across the sonar beams.
  - **Crosstrack Depth Profile (Upper Middle)**: Displays the red/blue seafloor profile across the swath with a large digital depth readout in white text: `2490` meters.
  - **Seabed Image (Lower Middle)**: Grayscale scrolling waterfall plot of seafloor acoustic backscatter intensity.
  - **Time Series (Bottom)**: Multi-colored line graph tracking vessel motion/attitude parameters (pitch, roll, heave) over time.
- **Center Panels**:
  - **Geographical Plan View (Top Center)**: A gridded map view on a dark blue background showing the green-shaded multibeam bathymetry swath being painted along the vessel's trackline heading northeast (`56.39°`), with a rainbow depth color scale bar at the bottom of the pane.
  - **Water Column Display (Bottom Center)**: A fan-shaped acoustic water-column echogram showing full-swath midwater backscatter from surface to bottom. The fan is colored blue and green in the midwater with a bright red/orange/yellow curved band marking the strong seafloor reflection across the swath.
- **Right Column (`Numerical display`)**:
  - A vertical table of real-time navigation, sensor, and sonar parameters, including:
    - Position (`N ...`, `W ...`)
    - `Heading`: `56.39`
    - `Speed kn`: `11.01`
    - `Date`: `2011 7 27`
    - `ZDA Time` / `PU Time`: `22:12:55` / `22:12:54`
    - `Mode`: `DEEP`
    - `Depth`: `2492.05`
- **Windows Taskbar (Bottom of Video)**: Shows the Windows `Start` button and running applications (`SIS`, `Runtime parameters`, etc.) at the bottom of the remote desktop screen.
