# Thunderhead Storm Cloud at Sunset from an Airplane – View 1 (`storm-airplane-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken through a commercial airliner window looking west at sunset on July 10, 2011, showing a massive, dark cumulonimbus thunderhead storm system illuminated from behind by the setting sun.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Aircraft Engine)**:
  - Along the bottom edge of the frame is the dark silhouette of the aircraft's jet engine nacelle, with a thin silver highlight from the sky glinting off its curved upper cowling.
- **Middle Ground (Storm Cloud & Sunset Glow)**:
  - A dense, towering wall of dark storm clouds fills the lower two-thirds of the image in deep shadow.
  - Near the center and left-center, a dramatic gap beneath the overhanging anvil cloud reveals a fiery orange-red glow from the setting sun, with faint crepuscular rays slanting downward through the rain shaft and haze.
  - Along the top of the dark cloud deck runs a horizontal shelf of slate-gray and blue-gray cloud layers.
- **Background (Upper Sky & Cloud Tops)**:
  - Rising above the left side of the horizontal cloud shelf is a billowing cumulonimbus turret rimmed in bright white and pale gold sunlight.
  - Along the central horizon line, the blinding white-gold light of the setting sun peeks over the flat cloud top beneath a clear, gradient blue upper atmosphere.
