# Thunderhead Storm Cloud at Sunset from an Airplane – View 2 (`storm-airplane-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A second aerial photograph taken from a commercial airliner window looking north as the plane passed around a large thunderstorm cell at sunset on July 10, 2011. The image captures the stark contrast between the brilliant orange sunset on the left and the turbulent, dark blue-gray storm column on the right.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Aircraft Engine)**:
  - At the bottom of the frame is the curved top of the airplane's jet engine nacelle, rendered in metallic blue-gray tones with panel lines visible and a soft reflection of the bright sky along its leading edge.
- **Storm Structure & Sunset Lighting**:
  - **Left Side**: Beneath the overhanging cloud canopy, the sky and lower cloud tops glow a vivid, saturated fiery orange and amber from the setting sun off to the left (west).
  - **Center & Right Side**: A massive vertical column of dark gray and indigo storm clouds rises from the lower cumulus billows up into a wide, spreading anvil canopy that stretches across the upper half of the frame.
  - A dark, flat lenticular-like shelf or cloud finger juts out horizontally into the bright orange gap near the center of the image, while lower billowy cumulus clouds gather around the base of the updraft.
- **Upper Sky**:
  - Above the dark blue-gray anvil cloud along the top edge, the high-altitude sky is a bright cyan-blue, with white sunlit cloud tops glowing in the upper-left corner.
