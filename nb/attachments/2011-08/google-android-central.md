# Android Central Sculpture Lawn at Google (`google-android-central.png`)

## Overview
A wide-angle screenshot captured from a Microsoft Photosynth interactive panorama showing the courtyard outside the Android building ("Android Central", Building 44) at the Google campus in Mountain View, California, featuring oversized Android release sculptures.

## Scene Description
- **Building Facade**: In the center background stands a two-story modern office building with light gray concrete panels, curved glass windows, and a prominent central arched glass entryway. Spanning the tall arched window above the double glass doors is a large green vinyl graphic of the Android robot mascot. A couple of bicycles are parked near the entrance.
- **Sculptures & Lawn**:
  - **Android Robot & Nexus One (Right)**: On the right lawn stands a giant, bright-green 3D Android robot figure with white eyes and antennae. Propped in front of its left side is an oversized replica of a smartphone (Nexus One) displaying the colorful Nexus "X" live wallpaper on its dark screen.
  - **Honeycomb Sculpture (Left)**: On the left lawn is a large yellow-and-orange hexagonal honeycomb structure representing Android 3.0 (Honeycomb), featuring a large black-and-yellow bee and a small green Android robot peeking out from one of the lower cells.
  - **Walkway & Landscaping**: A wide, square-tiled gray concrete walkway leads straight down the center from the foreground to the building entrance, flanked by manicured green hedges, trees, a dark green trash receptacle on the left, and a vertical light post with four round fixtures.
