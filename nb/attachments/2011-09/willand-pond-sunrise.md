# Sunrise over Willand Pond (`willand-pond-sunrise.jpg`)

## Overview
A serene early-morning landscape photograph capturing the sun rising just above the tree line across Willand Pond in Dover/Somersworth, New Hampshire, on September 3, 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Sun**:
  - Near the center-left horizon, the rising sun appears as a bright, glowing white-orange orb framed by the dark branches of lakeside pine trees, surrounded by a vivid red-orange halo.
  - The upper half of the frame features soft, layered morning clouds illuminated in warm golden, peach, and pale yellow tones near the sun, transitioning to cool slate blue-gray toward the upper right and a band of dusty rose-pink along the distant horizon.
- **Trees & Shoreline**:
  - A dark silhouette of tall evergreens and mixed forest forms a prominent wooded point on the left side of the frame, with a lower, continuous band of tree silhouettes lining the far shore across the center and another dark cluster of pines framing the right edge.
- **Water & Reflections**:
  - The calm, glassy surface of Willand Pond fills the lower half of the composition, acting as a mirror for the sky and trees.
  - A bright vertical streak of orange-white sunlight reflects directly below the sun on the water's surface, while the dark silhouettes of the shoreline trees and the pastel peach and blue-gray clouds are softly mirrored across the foreground water.
