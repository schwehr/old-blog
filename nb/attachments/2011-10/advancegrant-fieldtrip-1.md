# Advance Grant Geology Field Trip: Wally Setting the Stage (`advancegrant-fieldtrip-1.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph from an NSF ADVANCE Grant geological field trip to the rocky coast of New Castle, New Hampshire, showing geologist Wallace ("Wally") Bothner gesturing as he explains coastal outcrop geology to a group of UNH faculty and graduate students against a backdrop of the Atlantic Ocean.

## Scene Description
- **People**:
  - **Right**: Wally stands in right profile wearing a white canvas bucket hat, glasses, and a dark green-and-black plaid long-sleeved shirt, holding both hands up mid-explanation.
  - **Center & Left**: Four participants sit or crouch on the dark coastal rocks listening attentively:
    - A man in the center wears a bright green John Deere cap, dark jacket, cream t-shirt, and blue jeans, looking toward Wally.
    - In the lower-left foreground, a woman in a black fleece jacket looks toward Wally in profile.
    - At the far left edge, a person in a dark blue jacket and patterned knit beanie looks down at the rocks.
    - In the center foreground (back to camera), a woman with dark curly hair wearing a tan sweater and an orange wristwatch holds a small hand lens or camera near her face.
- **Coastal Setting & Background**:
  - The group is gathered on dark, rugged metamorphic/igneous shoreline rocks (featuring mylonite and pseudotachylyte structures) bordering calm, deep blue ocean water.
  - On the distant horizon under a clear, cloudless blue sky, a red-hulled ship is visible in the center alongside a tall coastal lighthouse tower (Whaleback Light) to its right and a rocky island with a fort/structure on the far left.
