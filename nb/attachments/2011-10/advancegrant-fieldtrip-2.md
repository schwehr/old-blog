# Advance Grant Geology Field Trip: Examining the Outcrop (`advancegrant-fieldtrip-2.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph from the UNH ADVANCE Grant geological field trip in New Castle, New Hampshire, showing five participants huddled closely around a rocky coastal outcrop to inspect fine-scale deformation structures.
