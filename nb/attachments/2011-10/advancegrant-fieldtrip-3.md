# Advance Grant Geology Field Trip: Taking Notes on the Outcrop (`advancegrant-fieldtrip-3.jpg`)

## Overview
An outdoor photograph from the UNH ADVANCE Grant field trip in New Castle, New Hampshire, showing five geologists and students standing on a sunlit, fractured coastal rock platform while reviewing handouts and taking field notes.
