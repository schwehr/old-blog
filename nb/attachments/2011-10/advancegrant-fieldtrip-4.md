# Advance Grant Geology Field Trip: Up Close with Deformation (`advancegrant-fieldtrip-4.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical close-up outdoor photograph from the UNH ADVANCE Grant field trip in New Castle, New Hampshire, showing Professor Margaret Boettcher smiling and pointing directly at fine-scale mylonite/pseudotachylyte deformation features on a rock outcrop alongside a hand lens for scale.

## Scene Description
- **Subject & Gesture**:
  - Margaret bends forward over the outcrop, smiling warmly as she looks down at the rock surface. She wears a black quarter-zip fleece pullover, dark blue jeans, and a silver wristwatch on her left wrist, and holds a blue folder/clipboard tucked under her right arm.
  - Her left index finger points directly at a finely laminated, deformed zone on the gray rock surface.
- **Rock Surface & Hand Lens**:
  - At the bottom center, resting directly on the textured rock surface just below her finger, is an unfolded metal-and-black geological hand lens (loupe) attached to a black patterned neck lanyard.
  - A second person's right hand enters from the lower-right edge, holding the lanyard strap steady against the rock.
- **Background**:
  - Behind Margaret is a shallow, amber-tinted tidal pool reflecting the surrounding bright, sunlit coastal boulders and dark seaweed-covered rocks.
