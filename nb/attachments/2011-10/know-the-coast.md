# ERMA Presentation Title Slide for Know the Coast Day (`know-the-coast.png`)

## Overview
A presentation title slide created by Kurt Schwehr for his talks on ERMA (Environmental Response Management Application) at Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory during the University of New Hampshire's "Know the Coast Day" on October 15, 2011.

## Slide Layout & Visual Elements
- **Background**: A vertical gradient transitioning from solid black across the top third to dark navy blue in the upper-middle and muted slate-gray/blue-gray toward the bottom.
- **Top Row Graphics**:
  - **Top Left**: A stylized cartoon illustration of a bright lime-green lobster with red eyes, long curved antennae, and open claws, angled diagonally as if swimming.
  - **Top Center**: A rectangular photograph framed with a thin tan border showing a breaking ocean wave heavily contaminated with dark reddish-brown and amber crude oil droplets and slicks (from the Deepwater Horizon oil spill).
  - **Top Right**: A black-and-white QR code inside a white square with a thin tan border.

## Visible Text & Labels
All text is rendered in a clean white sans-serif font (Gill Sans) and centered horizontally:
- **Main Title**:
  - `ERMA` (large uppercase text in the center)
  - `Environmental Response Management Application` (subtitle directly below)
- **Footer / Author Information**:
  - `Kurt Schwehr`
  - `http://schwehr.org` (underlined hyperlink)
  - `http://www.unh.edu/knowthecoast/` (underlined hyperlink)
