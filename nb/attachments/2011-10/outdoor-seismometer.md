# Outdoor Seismometer Experiment Outside CCOM (`outdoor-seismometer.jpg`)

## Overview
A color outdoor photograph showing a temporary field seismometer station set up by Margaret Boettcher and her research group outside the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM) at the University of New Hampshire in October 2011.

## Scene Description
- **Left Side (Seismometer Enclosure)**:
  - A large, flat-topped gray granite boulder sits embedded in grassy ground at the edge of tall brush and weeds.
  - Inverted on top of the flat rock surface is a white 5-gallon plastic bucket protecting the seismic sensor underneath.
  - A dark gray stone rests on top of the upturned white bucket to weigh it down against wind and movement.
  - A white paper notice/sign is taped to the front of the bucket (warning passersby not to touch the bucket), with blue commercial lettering partially visible along the right side of the bucket.
  - Damp patches are visible on the rock surface around the base of the bucket.
- **Right Side (Electronics Box & GPS/Telemetry Mast)**:
  - Sitting on the dirt and grass just to the right of the boulder is a dark navy-blue plastic storage bin (`Roughneck` / `Tote` style with a white retail label on the front).
  - A dark gray cable runs from underneath the white bucket across the rock and enters the left side of the blue bin through a curved white PVC pipe elbow (weatherproof conduit entry).
  - Additional gray cables loop from the bin up a vertical galvanized metal pole driven into the soil in front of the bin, with freshly dug dirt visible around the base of the pole.
  - Mounted at the top of the metal pole via a rectangular metal bracket and U-bolt clamps is a black hockey-puck-style GPS antenna.
- **Background**: Dense green and yellowing autumn weeds, tall grasses, and shrubs illuminated by warm sunlight in the upper background, while the foreground sits in shadow.
