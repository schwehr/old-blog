# University of Delaware CSHEL Website Homepage (`udel-cshel-art.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the homepage for the Coastal Sediments, Hydrodynamics, and Engineering Lab (CSHEL) at the University of Delaware (`http://cshel.geology.udel.edu/`), headed by Dr. Art Trembanis, posted in conjunction with his October 7, 2011 seminar at CCOM/JHC on AUV habitat mapping of Conch Reef, Florida.

## Layout & Visual Elements
- **Background & Central Imagery**:
  - Set against a deep underwater teal-blue vignette background that darkens to black around the edges and reveals a coral reef with small fish along the bottom margin.
  - In the center, a sea turtle swims toward the left with its front left flipper raised and small air bubbles rising near its head and below its body.
- **Top Navigation Bar**:
  - Centered uppercase links across the top: `WELCOME` (highlighted in white), `ABOUT US`, `RESEARCH`, `ACADEMICS`, `PUBLICATIONS`, `SOCIAL MEDIA`, `CV` (in muted teal-blue).
- **Left Header Title**:
  - `CSHEL` in a large, distressed/stylized white display font.
  - Subtitle below in bold white uppercase sans-serif text: `COASTAL SEDIMENTS, HYDRODYNAMICS, AND ENGINEERING LAB`.
- **Right Logo & Motto**:
  - A slightly tilted rectangular blue-and-white emblem featuring `C S H E L` at the top between wavy white lines, a yellow side-profile silhouette of an autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) / submarine in the center, and `UNIVERSITY OF DELAWARE` along the bottom.
  - Below the logo, inside a tilted dashed light-blue rectangular box, is the handwritten-style motto in light blue: `" Ad aqua per aspera"!`
- **Bottom Left Callout**:
  - Large light-blue handwritten-style text angled slightly upward:
    `Dive in...Explore`
    `The Planet!`
