# Columbia Ocean Terrain Synthesis Cruise Tracks in Google Earth Plug-in (`ldeo-cruise-tracks.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Google Earth browser plug-in displaying global research vessel multibeam sonar cruise tracks from the Columbia Ocean Terrain Synthesis (Global Multi-Resolution Topography / GMRT synthesis developed by Columbia University's Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory), captured on November 26, 2011.

## Globe & Map Details
- **Viewpoint**: An orthographic 3D globe view centered over the eastern and central Pacific Ocean, with North America (United States, Canada, Alaska, Mexico, Baja California, and Central America) visible on the right and the Hawaiian Islands on the left.
- **Cruise Track Overlays**:
  - Hundreds of multicolored polylines (green, yellow, magenta, purple, orange, cyan, blue, red, and white) crisscross the Pacific Ocean and along the continental margins.
  - Dense hubs of radiating ship tracks converge on major oceanographic ports and study regions, notably San Diego / Southern California, the Pacific Northwest (Oregon/Washington/Vancouver Island), Hawaii, the East Pacific Rise off Mexico and Central America, the Gulf of California, and the Aleutian trench / Gulf of Alaska.
