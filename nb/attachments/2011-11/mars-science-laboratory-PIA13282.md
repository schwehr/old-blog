# Mars Science Laboratory (Curiosity) Entry, Descent, and Landing Sequence (`mars-science-laboratory-PIA13282.jpg`)

## Overview
A NASA/JPL-Caltech diagram (Planetary Photojournal ID `PIA13282`) illustrating the Entry, Descent, and Landing (EDL) sequence for the Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) Curiosity rover, including a detailed inset of the final "Sky Crane" maneuver. Posted on November 25, 2011, the day before MSL's launch.

## Main Trajectory Stages (Top Left to Bottom Right)
A continuous black trajectory curve traces the spacecraft's descent from the upper left down to the reddish-orange Martian surface at the lower right, annotated with color-coded parameters (`Altitude` in blue, `Velocity` in green, and `Time` in purple):

1. **Entry Interface** (upper left):
   - `Altitude: ~125 km`
   - `Velocity: ~5,900 m/s`
   - `Time: Entry + 0 s`
2. **Peak Heating**: Aeroshell capsule surrounded by an orange-red heat glow along its leading heatshield.
3. **Peak Deceleration**: Capsule surrounded by a yellow shock/deceleration arc.
4. **Hypersonic Aeromaneuvering**: Capsule pitching/banking along a gentler glide slope.
5. **Parachute Deploy**: Supersonic parachute deployed behind the capsule:
   - `Altitude: ~10 km`
   - `Velocity: ~470 m/s`
   - `Time: Entry + ~240 s`
6. **Heatshield Separation**: Front heatshield detaches and falls away ahead of the backshell:
   - `Altitude: ~7 km`
   - `Velocity: ~160 m/s`
   - `Time: Entry + ~260 s`
7. **Radar Data Collection**: Capsule suspended under the parachute emitting radar waves downward while the separated heatshield tumbles below.
8. **Backshell Separation**: The powered descent stage and rover drop free from the backshell and parachute:
   - `Altitude: ~1.8 km`
   - `Velocity: ~100 m/s`
   - `Time: Entry + ~345 s`
9. **Powered Descent**: Descent stage fires its retro-rockets while radar beams scan the surface.
10. **Sky Crane**: Descent stage hovers above the Martian terrain, lowering the rover on bridles to the surface.
11. **Flyaway**: The descent stage flies upward and away to the right after rover touchdown.

## Sky Crane Inset Box (Bottom Left)
A rectangular callout box expands the `Sky Crane` phase into six sequential steps above the orange-brown Martian surface:
1. **Throttle Down to 4 MLEs**: Descent stage hovering on four Mars Lander Engines (`MLEs`) with the rover tucked underneath.
2. **Rover Separation**: Descent stage begins lowering the rover on blue tether cables:
   - `Altitude: 20 m`
   - `Velocity: ~0.75 m/s`
   - `Time: Entry + ~380 s`
3. **Mobility Deploy**: The rover's wheels and rocker-bogie suspension deploy downward while suspended from the bridles.
4. **Touchdown**: The rover's wheels contact the Martian surface while still tethered to the hovering descent stage:
   - `Altitude: 0 m`
   - `Velocity: ~0.75 m/s`
   - `Time: Entry + ~392 s`
5. **Activate Flyaway Controller**: Tether/umbilical release triggered at the rover deck.
6. **Flyaway**: The rover rests on the surface while the descent stage rockets away diagonally upward to the right on blue-and-magenta exhaust plumes.
