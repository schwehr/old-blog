# YouTube Analytics World Map of Views by Country (`youtube-analytics-researchtools-2.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the interactive geographic map view in YouTube Analytics captured on November 30, 2011, showing the global distribution of views by country for Kurt Schwehr's Research Tools video series, with a hover tooltip active over Germany.

## Map & Interface Details
- **Top View Switcher Bar**:
  - Light gray header bar with two toggle buttons on the left: `Line chart` and `Map` (currently selected/depressed in gray).
- **Choropleth World Map**:
  - Displays country boundaries across North and South America, Europe, Africa, Asia, and Australia (excluding Antarctica).
  - **Color Shading**:
    - The **United States** (contiguous 48 states and Alaska) is shaded in the darkest medium-blue color (`269` views max).
    - **Germany** is shaded in a noticeably lighter pale blue-gray and highlighted with a slightly darker border.
    - Most other countries (including Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, etc.) are shaded in a very faint ice-blue that is nearly indistinguishable from unvisited countries, which Kurt notes in his blog post ("The color ramp for the countries makes it really hard to tell them apart").
- **Interactive Hover Tooltip**:
  - A white rounded rectangle with a drop shadow points to Germany in central Europe, displaying:
    - `Germany` (bold black text)
    - `Views: 74`
- **Color Scale Legend (Bottom Left)**:
  - Horizontal gradient bar ranging from `0` (pale ice-blue on the left) to `269` (medium-dark blue on the right), with a small black downward-pointing triangle marker positioned at approximately 27% along the scale (corresponding to Germany's `74` views).
