## Overview
A screenshot from the MarineCadastre.gov AIS Data Handler webpage showing the **"Other Resources"** bibliography and **"Acknowledgments"** sections, which cite two publications co-authored by Kurt Schwehr alongside Brian Lane's AIS parser and NOAA/USCG resources.

## Layout & Styling
The text is rendered in a clean sans-serif font on a dark slate-blue background (`#253746`), with section headings in bold white, body text in white, and URLs highlighted in orange-gold (`#f49b25`).
