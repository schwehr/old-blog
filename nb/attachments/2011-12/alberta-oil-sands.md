# Athabasca Oil Sands in Google Earth (`alberta-oil-sands.png`)

## Overview
A Mac OS X screenshot of **Google Earth** displaying satellite imagery of extensive surface mining operations and tailings ponds in the **Athabasca oil sands** along the Athabasca River north of Fort McMurray, Alberta, Canada.

## Satellite View & Geographic Features
- **Surface Mines & Tailings Ponds**: Large light-gray, white, and tan open-pit mines and diked tailings ponds contrast sharply against the surrounding dark green boreal forest. The Athabasca River runs roughly north-northwest through the center of the industrial complex.
- **Scale & Overlays**:
  - Bottom-left scale bar marked at `5.93 mi`.
  - Center-bottom copyright overlays in white text:
    - `Image © 2011 GeoEye`
    - `© 2011 Google`
    - `Image © 2011 DigitalGlobe`
    - `© 2011 Cnes/Spot Image`
  - Bottom-right `Google earth` logo next to an inset overview map of North America with a red crosshair over western Canada.
  - Bottom status bar reads: `56°59'53.42" N 111°30'33.42" W elev 952 ft` and `Eye alt 25.88 mi`.

## Interface & Controls
- **Mac OS X Menu Bar**: Shows the Apple icon, `Google Earth`, `File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Tools`, `Add`, `Window`, and `Help`.
- **Window Toolbar**:
  - Search input box on the far left showing coordinates `43.081263, -70.56375` (New Hampshire) with a magnifying glass search button.
