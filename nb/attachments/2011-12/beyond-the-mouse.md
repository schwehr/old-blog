# "More Philosophy ..." Slide from Beyond the Mouse (`beyond-the-mouse.png`)

## Overview
A presentation slide titled **"More Philosophy . . ."** from Ronni Grapenthin's 2011 short course on programming at the University of Alaska (*Beyond the Mouse 2011 - The (geo)scientist's computational chest*, `01_thinking_programs.pdf`), highlighting Jon Claerbout's famous principle of reproducible computational research.

## Slide Layout & Visual Design
Formatted in a clean LaTeX Beamer theme (Warsaw/Boadilla style) on a white background with royal blue title banners and light lavender-blue content blocks with subtle drop shadows.

## Visible Text
- **Top Header Bar** (white text on dark blue banner):
  `More Philosophy . . .`
- **Upper Block**:
  - **Block Header**: `Jon Claerbout (a geophysicist), as quoted in “WaveLab and Reproducible Research”:`
  - **Block Body** (italicized black text, with "not" and "advertising" in bold upright font):
    > *An article about computational science in a scientific publication is* **not** *the scholarship itself, it is merely* **advertising** *of the scholarship. The actual scholarship is the complete software development environment and the complete set of instructions which generated the figures.*
- **Lower Block**:
  - **Block Header**: `Implications . . .`
  - **Bullet Points** (marked with glossy blue 3D sphere bullets):
    - `publications should include data and code (example: Okada)`
    - `figures should be reproducible by readers`
    - `write code that others can use!`
