# NCAR AGU Poster: IRC via Satellite for Mission Control (`ncar-irc-via-sat.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph taken at the 2011 AGU Fall Meeting showing a section of poster **`IN11B-1282`** from the **National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR)**, illustrating how Internet Relay Chat (IRC) over a satellite communications relay connects airborne research aircraft with ground mission control.

## Poster Header
- Top white banner displays the AGU poster number **`IN11B-1282`** on the left and the blue **`NCAR`** (`NATIONAL CENTER FOR ATMOSPHERIC RESEARCH`) logo on the right.

## Diagram & Visual Elements
Set on a pale yellow poster background with dashed red/orange arrows indicating communication links:
- **`SatCom Relay`** (upper left): A cartoon illustration of an Iridium-style communications satellite with solar panels and antennas surrounded by small blue four-pointed stars. Dashed red arrows point from lower poster elements (including a partially visible rack labeled `Displays` at bottom left) up to the satellite, and a bidirectional dashed red arrow connects the satellite to the IRC window on the right.
- **`World-wide Internet Relay Chat for Mission Control`** (upper right):
  - Below this heading is a screenshot of an **mIRC / IRC client window** with a black terminal background showing timestamped chat messages in white, green, cyan, and purple text, with usernames in red, blue, and yellow in the left column and a participant list sidebar on the right (`ground_...`, `raf_...`, etc.).
- **Mission Control Photograph** (bottom right):
  - A photograph of a brightly lit operations/mission control room with a drop ceiling and recessed fluorescent lights, where numerous scientists and engineers sit at computer workstations. A dashed red arrow runs from the people in the room up into the IRC chat window.
