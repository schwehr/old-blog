# Harvard CGA Quantum GIS Workshop Webpage (`qgis-harvard-tutorial.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Quantum GIS Workshop** tutorial website (`http://cga-5.hmdc.harvard.edu/qgis/`) created by Lex Berman at Harvard University's Center for Geographic Analysis (CGA).

## Layout & Navigation
- **Top Header Bar**: Slate-gray bar with a dark blue badge on the left reading **`QUANTUM GIS WORKSHOP`** in bold white text and an orange button on the right reading **`HOME`**.
- **Navigation Bar**: Medium blue horizontal menu bar with links in light blue/gray bold sans-serif text:
  `Install` | `Navigate` | `Import` | `Projection` | `Join` | `Edit` | `Symbolize` | `Export` | `Links`
- **Footer Bar**: Slate-gray bar at the bottom reading:
  - Left: **`CGA`** `Harvard University`
  - Center: `Instructor:` **`Lex Berman`**
  - Right: Creative Commons **`(cc) BY-NC`** license badge.

## Main Content Area
White background flanked by two logos at the upper left and upper right:
- **Left Logo**: The classic Quantum GIS (QGIS) logo — a yellow capital letter **`Q`** pierced diagonally by a green arrow pointing toward the upper right.
- **Right Logo**: The Harvard CGA globe logo — a 3D sphere divided diagonally into blue-gray and green hemispheres with a white latitude/longitude graticule.
- **Heading**: **`Open Source Desktop GIS with Quantum`**
