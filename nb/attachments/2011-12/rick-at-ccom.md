# CDR Rick Brennan Presenting at UNH CCOM/JHC Seminar (`rick-at-ccom.png`)

## Overview
A video frame from the UNH CCOM/JHC Seminar Series lecture given on December 9, 2011 by NOAA Corps Officer **CDR Richard T. ("Rick") Brennan** titled *"Coastal and Ocean Mapping: A Nautical Cartographer's Perspective"* at UNH's Chase Ocean Engineering Laboratory.

## Scene & Speaker
- On the left side of the frame, **CDR Rick Brennan** stands in front of a projection screen/wall in the seminar room beneath a white acoustic tile ceiling. He is wearing a dark vest over a brown/olive button-down plaid shirt and holding a small presenter remote in his left hand while gesturing toward the slide.

## Presentation Slide
Occupying the right two-thirds of the frame is a digital capture of his NOAA **Office of Coast Survey** slide:
- **Background & Branding**: Medium-dark blue background with a lighter blue bottom banner reading **`O f f i c e   o f   C o a s t   S u r v e y`** in spaced white sans-serif letters on the left and the circular **NOAA** emblem on the right.
- **Left Panel (2D Cross-Section Point Cloud)**:
  - Black background with a dark gray rectangular grid.
  - Vertical depth axis on the left labeled from `9.57` down to `22.99` (meters) and horizontal distance axis along the bottom labeled from `2.00` to `34.00`.
  - Shows a dense green/cyan seafloor profile sloping gently downward from left to right, with a tall vertical plume/mast structure rising from the seafloor around `12.00–16.00` on the horizontal axis, colored green at the base, yellow/orange in the middle, red above, and white at the shallowest tips, plus blue multipath/noise points below the seafloor.
- **Right Panel (3D Perspective Point Cloud)**:
  - Black background showing a 3D perspective rendering of the same hydrographic multibeam sonar point cloud: a bright green swath of seafloor with the vertical wreck mast/obstruction (colored yellow, orange, red, and white by depth) rising sharply above the bottom.
