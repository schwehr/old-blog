# Echo Envelope Trace Schematic Slide (`tami-trace-schematics.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a presentation slide from Tami Beduhn's December 16, 2011 CCOM/JHC seminar on single-beam sonar bottom detection and eelgrass mapping in Great Bay, New Hampshire. The slide illustrates how full-waveform echo envelope traces correspond to acoustic backscatter returns from submerged eelgrass versus a bare seafloor.

## Slide Layout & Plot Details
- **Background**: The same underwater theme as the presentation title slide, featuring blue water with diagonal sunbeams at the top and a black silhouette of eelgrass blades along the bottom edge.
- **Title**: Centered at the top in large golden-yellow sans-serif text: `Echo EnvelopeTrace`.
- **Echogram Display** (center-right): A rectangular single-beam sonar echogram plotted against a deep royal-blue background:
  - On the left half of the echogram, a shallow flat seafloor return appears as a bright cyan/yellow horizontal line with a tall, fuzzy cloud of vertical blue/cyan speckles rising above it (representing acoustic backscatter from an eelgrass bed). An **orange rounded rectangle** highlights a section of this eelgrass zone.
  - Near the middle of the echogram, the eelgrass bed ends at a drop-off where the seafloor slopes downward to the right as a sharp, thin, high-intensity yellow-and-cyan line without vegetation above it. A **green rounded rectangle** highlights a section of this bare, sloping bottom.
- **Stylized Echo Envelope Traces** (left of the echogram): Two vertical schematic waveform traces drawn to the left of the echogram illustrate the amplitude vs. depth (time) profile for each highlighted region:
  - **Orange Trace (left)**: Corresponds to the orange box over the eelgrass bed. It shows a broad, jagged upper return zone marked by an orange vertical bracket `[` on the left (representing the acoustic return from the eelgrass canopy) peaking at the bottom interface, followed by a smaller secondary reflection peak deeper down.
  - **Green Trace (inner left)**: Corresponds to the green box over the bare seafloor at a deeper depth. It shows a flat baseline down to a single sharp, narrow high-amplitude spike marked by a small green vertical bracket `[` (representing the clean hard-bottom return), plus a faint secondary multiple peak further down.
