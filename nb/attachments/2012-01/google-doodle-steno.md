# Nicolas Steno Geology Google Doodle (`google-doodle-steno.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Google Search homepage taken on January 11, 2012, featuring the Google Doodle commemorating the 374th birthday of 17th-century Danish pioneer of geology and stratigraphy Nicolas Steno (1638–1686).

## Doodle Illustration
- The word **Google** is sculpted out of three-dimensional cutaway blocks of sedimentary rock strata topped by green grassy landscapes, illustrating Steno's principles of stratigraphy (such as the law of superposition):
  - **Bottom Layer (Tan/Beige Sandstone/Limestone)**: Contains embedded white marine trilobite and brachiopod shell fossils.
  - **Second Layer Up (Teal/Blue-Green Rock)**: Contains coiled ammonite fossils and fish skeletons.
  - **Third Layer Up (Yellow/Orange Rock)**: Contains vertebrate skeletons, cave/rock art figures (on the `g`), and dinosaur/reptile fossils.
  - **Upper Layer (Pinkish-Red & Brown Soil/Rock)**: Contains mammal bones.
  - **Top Surface (Green Grass)**:
    - Above the capital `G`, a large mammoth or mastodon skull with curved tusks and exposed ribs rests on the grass while tiny birds fly overhead.
    - Across the `o` and `o`, a winding blue stream flows across the grass.
    - Pine trees grow on the right side of the second `o` and the `e`, with small birds flying above the `g`.
