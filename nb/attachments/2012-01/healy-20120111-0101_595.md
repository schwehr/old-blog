# USCGC Healy Escorting Russian Tanker Renda Through Sea Ice (`healy-20120111-0101_595.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle photograph captured by the Aloftcon mast camera aboard the U.S. Coast Guard icebreaker *USCGC Healy* (WAGB-20) on January 11, 2012, at 01:01:01 UTC during its winter mission escorting the Russian-flagged fuel tanker *Renda* through heavy Bering Sea pack ice toward Nome, Alaska.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (USCGC Healy Bow)**: Viewed from high up in the Aloftcon station (with the curved upper window frame and a hanging cable visible at the top of the frame), the rounded bow and foredeck of the *Healy* appear at the bottom center, complete with its forward mast, white radar dome, and snow-covered deck equipment.
- **Escorted Vessel (Tanker *Renda*)**: Positioned across the middle-left of the frame is the red-hulled ice-class tanker *Renda* with a white aft superstructure, red-and-black funnel emitting a faint plume of exhaust steam, and red forward mast and deck piping.
- **Sea Ice & Broken Channel**:
  - Surrounding both vessels is a vast, continuous expanse of fractured white and gray sea ice stretching to the flat horizon.
  - A long, straight channel ofchurned brash ice cut by the *Healy* extends diagonally from the tanker's bow in the center out toward the middle-right edge of the image, while a small patch of dark open water sits immediately astern of the tanker on the left.
- **Lighting**: Low-angle golden Arctic sunset/twilight illuminates the ice floes and the white superstructure of the tanker, casting warm orange and amber highlights across the uneven ice ridges and a soft golden glow along the horizon beneath a pale blue sky.
