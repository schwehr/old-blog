# USCGC Healy at Night in Pack Ice Near Nome (`healy-20120111-1601.jpeg`)

## Overview
A nighttime monochrome (black-and-white) photograph captured by the Aloftcon camera aboard the U.S. Coast Guard icebreaker *USCGC Healy* (WAGB-20) on January 11, 2012, at 16:01:01 UTC as the ship navigated through sea ice in the northern Bering Sea near Nome, Alaska.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Illumination**: Viewed looking forward from the high Aloftcon station, the bow of the *USCGC Healy* sits at the bottom center, with its forward mast, radar dome, and side railings silhouetted against a bright pool of white light cast by the icebreaker's powerful forward floodlights onto the snow-covered ice directly ahead of the bow.
- **Sea Ice Field**: Beyond the brightly lit foreground pool, textured pack ice with low pressure ridges and leads stretches across the middle distance in shades of dark gray, with a small dark ice hummock or open lead spot visible to the left of center.
- **Distant Lights**: Near the center of the dark horizon line, two distinct bright white points of light shine across the ice.
- **Sky**: A pitch-black Arctic night sky fills the upper half of the frame, with the curved edge of the Aloftcon housing faintly visible in the top-left corner.
