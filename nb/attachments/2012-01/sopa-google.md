# Google SOPA / PIPA Protest Censored Logo (`sopa-google.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the Google Search homepage taken on January 18, 2012, during the internet-wide protest against the proposed U.S. Stop Online Piracy Act (SOPA) and PROTECT IP Act (PIPA) legislation.

## Visual Elements & Layout
- **Redacted Google Logo (Top Center)**:
  - The standard Google logo on a white background is covered by a large, slightly tilted solid black censorship bar (resembling a piece of black redaction tape with a subtle drop shadow).
  - Only the very bottom blue curve of the lowercase `g` peeks out from beneath the lower edge of the black rectangle.
- **Search Interface (Middle)**:
  - A wide, empty text input box outlined in bright blue sits below the redacted logo.
  - Centered beneath the search box are the two standard light-gray rectangular buttons with dark gray bold text: `Google Search` and `I'm Feeling Lucky`.
- **Call to Action (Bottom)**:
  - Centered at the bottom of the screenshot is a single line of text: `Tell Congress:` in black sans-serif font, followed by the blue hyperlink `Please don't censor the web!`.
