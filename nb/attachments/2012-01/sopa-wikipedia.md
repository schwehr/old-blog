# English Wikipedia SOPA / PIPA Blackout Splash Screen (`sopa-wikipedia.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the English Wikipedia 24-hour blackout protest page taken on January 18, 2012, opposing the proposed U.S. Stop Online Piracy Act (SOPA) and PROTECT IP Act (PIPA) bills.

## Visual Design & Graphics
- **Background & Lighting**: A dark radial gradient background transitions from a medium-gray spotlight behind the Wikipedia logo on the upper left to deep charcoal and black on the right and bottom edges.
- **Wikipedia Wordmark & Shadow (Left Column)**:
  - A large black serif capital `W` (with the characteristic crossed-V Wikipedia typography) sits above the black serif wordmark `WIKIPEDIA`.
  - Directly beneath `WIKIPEDIA`, an inverted, foreshortened reflection of the word `WIKIPEDIA` and a dramatic, elongated black shadow of the giant `W` stretch downward toward the bottom-right corner, as if lit from behind and above.

## Visible Text & Controls (Right Column)
- **Headline** (large white serif text):
  > Imagine a World
  > Without Free Knowledge
- **Explanatory Paragraph** (white serif body text):
  > For over a decade, we have spent millions of hours building
  > the largest encyclopedia in human history. Right now, the U.S.
  > Congress is considering legislation that could fatally damage
  > the free and open Internet. For 24 hours, to raise awareness,
  > we are blacking out Wikipedia. **Learn more.** (underlined link)
- **Representative Lookup Form**:
  - Bold white serif sub-heading: `Contact your representatives.`
  - Below it, the white sans-serif label `Your ZIP code:` followed by a small white text input box and a rounded light-gray button labeled `Look up`.
