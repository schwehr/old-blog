# Google Earth Ocean Bathymetry After Update (`google-ocean-updated.jpg`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Earth displaying the updated, seamless view of the Massachusetts coastline, Massachusetts Bay, Stellwagen Bank, and Cape Cod following the February 2012 Google Ocean bathymetry and Google Earth 6.2 terrain/imagery update.

## Map & Bathymetric Details
- **Geographic Region**: The same view of eastern Massachusetts and Cape Cod Bay at an eye altitude of `72.94 mi`, covering Cape Ann, Boston Harbor, the South Shore, Cape Cod Bay, and outer Cape Cod.
- **Seamless Coastline & Bathymetry**: The blocky vertical strips of dark water satellite imagery and harsh onshore imagery seams seen in the pre-update view are gone. Instead, a clean, dark blue coastal vignette transitions smoothly from the green land satellite mosaic into the blue shaded-relief bathymetry.
- **Seafloor Features**: Submarine topography in Massachusetts Bay, Stellwagen Bank (just north of Cape Cod's tip), and the channels/shoals off outer Cape Cod are clearly visible in shaded blue relief.
- **Target Marker**: A small red circular marker sits in the middle of Cape Cod Bay.
