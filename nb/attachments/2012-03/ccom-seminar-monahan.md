# CCOM/JHC Seminar Poster: Dave Monahan on Random-Track Bathymetry (`ccom-seminar-monahan.jpg`)

## Overview
A promotional poster for a seminar at the UNH Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / NOAA-UNH Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC) titled *"The Value of Collecting New, Random-Track, Bathymetry Data"* by Dave Monahan.

## Poster Design & Central Graphic
- **Background**: Deep teal-blue background featuring a large, semi-transparent watermark of a question mark (`?`) spanning the height of the poster.
- **Central Map Figure**: A framed bathymetric swath map (`15° 00'` to `17° 00'` N latitude, `-109° 30'` to `-106° 30'` W longitude) illustrating overlapping multibeam and single-beam ship tracklines across the seafloor over a white unmapped background.
  - **Question Mark Track**: A prominent red question mark (`?`) is drawn directly over the map, following a curved survey trackline around a seamount/ridge feature and ending with a red circle at the bottom.
  - **Color Bar**: Beneath the map is a horizontal `Depth (m)` color scale ranging from `-4200` (dark blue) through `-3400` (cyan/green) and `-3000` (yellow) to `-2200` (orange/white).
