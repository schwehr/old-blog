# Overview of NOAA VDatum Zones Across the Continental United States in Google Earth (`vdatum-zones.jpg`)

## Overview
A Google Earth satellite view of the contiguous United States displaying white rectangular bounding boxes and yellow pushpin placemarks for NOAA VDatum regional vertical datum transformation zones, generated by parsing `.met` metadata files into KML.

## Geographic Distribution of VDatum Zones
- **U.S. West Coast (Pacific)**: A continuous chain of 8 overlapping white rectangular bounding boxes with yellow pushpins extending from Puget Sound / Washington State down through Oregon and Northern/Central/Southern California to the Mexican border. Visible labels in California include `CAmorrob01_8301` (Morro Bay / Central Coast) and `CA_socal02_8301_03` (Southern California).
- **Gulf of Mexico Coast (Texas to Florida)**: A dense series of bounding boxes along the Texas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, and Florida panhandle/west coasts. Visible labels include:
  - Texas/Louisiana: `TXlagmat01_8301`, `TXshlmat01_8301`, `TXlaggal01_8301`, `TXshlgal01_8301`, `LATXwest01_8301`
  - Central/Eastern Gulf: `LAmobile02_8301`, `GOM-rig`, `FLpensac02_8301`, `FLandrew02_8301`, `FLjoseph02_8301`, `ALFL_gom02_8301`, `FLapalach01_8301`, `FLwest01_8301`, `Untitled Placemark`, and `FLsouth01_8301`.
- **U.S. East Coast (Atlantic)**: Overlapping bounding boxes running from South Florida up to the Gulf of Maine. Visible labels from south to north include:
  - Southeast: `FLGAeast01_8301`, `GASCNCSab01_8301`
  - Carolinas & Mid-Atlantic: `NCccentr01_8301_03`, `NCpamlis01_8301_03`, `NCcnorth01_8301_03`, `MDVA_chb01_8301_03`, `DEVA_emb01_8301_03`, `NJVA_mab01_8301_03`, `DEdelbay02_8301_03`, `NJcstem01_8301_03`
  - New York & New England: `NYNJ_hbr02_8301_03`, `NYgrsbas02_8301_03`, `RICT_bis02_8301_03`, and `MENHMAgome01_8301` (Gulf of Maine).
