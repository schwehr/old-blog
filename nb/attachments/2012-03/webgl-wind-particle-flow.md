# Hint.fm WebGL/HTML5 U.S. Wind Particle Flow Visualization (`webgl-wind-particle-flow.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the interactive `wind map` web application (`http://hint.fm/wind/`) created by Fernanda Viégas and Martin Wattenberg, visualizing live surface wind currents across the contiguous United States on March 29, 2012 using animated white/gray particle trails.

## Wind Flow Map & Tooltip
- **Map Silhouette**: The continental United States rendered in dark charcoal gray, overlaid with thousands of dense, flowing white and light-gray streamlines representing wind speed and direction.
- **Flow Patterns**:
  - Strong, bright white streamlines sweep southward and southeastward down the Great Plains (from the Dakotas/Montana through Nebraska, Kansas, and Oklahoma into Texas) and across the Pacific Northwest/Rockies.
  - Swirling circulation patterns and calmer (darker gray) regions appear over the Southwest and Southeast.
- **City Markers**: White circular dots with labels mark major U.S. cities: `Seattle`, `San Jose`, `Los Angeles`, `San Diego`, `Phoenix`, `Dallas`, `Houston`, `Chicago`, `Columbus`, `Jacksonville`, and `New York`.
- **Interactive Hover Tooltip**: Near the center of the country (around the South Dakota/Wyoming border) is a white rectangular popup box displaying:
  - `9.3 mph wind speed`
  - `43° 36' N, 104° 00' W`
  - `click to zoom`
