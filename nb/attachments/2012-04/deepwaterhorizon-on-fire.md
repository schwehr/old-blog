# Deepwater Horizon Drilling Rig on Fire (`deepwaterhorizon-on-fire.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken by the U.S. Coast Guard showing the *Deepwater Horizon* offshore semi-submersible drilling rig engulfed in flames and billowing thick black smoke in the Gulf of Mexico following the April 20, 2010 blowout and explosion, while several offshore supply and firefighting vessels spray water cannons onto the burning structure.

## Scene Description
- **Burning Drilling Rig**: At the center of the frame, the *Deepwater Horizon* platform sits low in the water, partially obscured by dense, dark gray and black smoke plumes rising diagonally toward the upper left corner of the image. Bright orange and yellow flames erupt intensely from the central deck area of the rig. The forward/starboard section of the deck, including the elevated helideck on the right side of the rig structure and a crane boom silhouetted against the fire, remains visible above the water.
- **Firefighting & Response Vessels**: Four offshore supply/firefighting vessels surround the rig and direct high-pressure arcs of water toward the base of the fire and the rig's hull:
  - **Lower Left Vessel**: An offshore supply vessel with a dark blue hull, orange cargo deck bulwarks, and a white superstructure forward, positioned off the port/left side of the rig and spraying a stream of water up toward the left deck edge.
  - **Lower Center Vessel**: An offshore supply vessel with a bright red/orange hull and a cream/yellow forward wheelhouse, angled toward the rig in the foreground and spraying a wide vertical fan of water toward the burning central structure. Orange fire reflections shimmer on the dark water surface directly beneath this vessel's bow.
  - **Far Right / Background Vessels**: One vessel is partially visible at the far right edge with a dark hull and white superstructure spraying a long arc of water toward the right side of the rig, while another vessel further back on the upper right projects a high, misting water plume toward the helideck side of the platform.
- **Environment**: The surrounding Gulf of Mexico waters are calm with light surface ripples under a hazy light-blue sky with a visible horizon line in the upper third of the frame.
