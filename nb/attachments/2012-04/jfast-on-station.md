# JFAST Drilling Vessel on Station off Japan in Google Earth (`jfast-on-station.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth satellite and bathymetric map view of Japan and the western Pacific Ocean showing the position of the *Chikyu* drilling vessel on station for the Japan Trench Fast Drilling Project (`JFAST`) over the Japan Trench east of Honshu.

## Map & Geographic Details
- **Landmasses & Borders**:
  - The main islands of Japan—Honshu, southern Hokkaido at the top, and northern Shikoku/Kyushu at the bottom left—are rendered with green and brown satellite imagery outlined by thin yellow coastline borders.
  - A small portion of the Asian mainland coastline appears at the upper left corner.
- **Bathymetry**:
  - Shaded ocean floor topography clearly illustrates the deep, dark-blue trough of the **Japan Trench** running roughly north-south parallel to the east coast of Honshu, transitioning into the Kuril-Kamchatka Trench to the northeast and the Izu-Bonin Trench to the south, with seamounts scattered across the Pacific plate to the east.
- **Placemarks & Labels**:
  - `Sea of Japan` in cyan text with black outlining over the body of water west of Honshu.
  - `Japan` in pale yellow text over central Honshu, near a green mountain icon representing Mount Fuji.
  - `Tokyo` in white text next to a red-and-white star capital icon on the Kanto plain.
  - `JFAST 37.95103 143.90532` in bold white text next to a blue front-facing ship icon positioned directly on the western slope of the Japan Trench off the Tohoku coast (at latitude `37.95103° N`, longitude `143.90532° E`).
