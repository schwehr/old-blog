# NOAA Stellwagen Bank WhaleALERT Web Page (`noaa-sbnms-whale-alert-app.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the NOAA Gerry E. Studds Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary webpage introducing the **WhaleALERT** mobile application (`Aiding Mariners and Protecting Right Whales`) for iPad and iPhone.

## Page Layout & Visual Elements
- **Top Banner**:
  - Set against a dark blue underwater banner featuring a whale silhouette on the right.
  - On the left is a white stylized whale-fluke sanctuary logo next to white capital lettering:
    - `GERRY E. STUDDS`
    - `STELLWAGEN BANK`
    - `NATIONAL MARINE SANCTUARY`
- **Left Navigation Sidebar**:
  - Glossy light-blue pill-shaped buttons stacked vertically over a dark blue/black background:
    - `Home`
    - `About the Sanctuary`
    - `Visitor Information`
    - `Education`
    - `Science`
    - `Management`
    - `Maritime Heritage`
    - `Resource Protection` (expanded with white sub-menu links: `Guidelines & Regulations`, `Protecting Whales from Tuna Gear`, `Enforcement`, `Incident Response`, `Contingency Plans`, `Reporting`)
    - `News & Events`
    - `Galleries`
    - `Library`
    - `Get Involved`
- **Main Content Area (Top Graphic)**:
  - A humpback/right whale tail fluke splashes out of the top-left border of a light-blue graphic panel.
  - Red header text between horizontal red lines reads `RESOURCE PROTECTION`.
  - Below is the main title `WhaleALERT` (`Whale` in white, `ALERT` in dark red) with the dark blue italic subtitle `Aiding Mariners and Protecting Right Whales`.
  - **Device Mockups**:
    - On the left, a black iPad in landscape orientation displays the WhaleALERT splash screen: a split over/underwater composite showing a yellow acoustic monitoring buoy on the surface and a right whale emitting concentric acoustic waves underwater, along with partner logos along the bottom (NOAA, Stellwagen Bank NMS, IFAW, EarthNC, Cornell Lab of Ornithology, EOM Offshore, Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, UNH CCOM/JHC, NYK Line, etc.).
    - On the top right is the rounded square **WhaleALERT app icon** showing a red-and-white whale tail over a blue compass rose/radar graphic.
    - Below the app icon is a black iPhone in portrait orientation displaying the same WhaleALERT launch screen.
    - Beneath the devices is a black `Available on the App Store` badge.
- **Main Content Area (Bottom Text & Map Preview)**:
  - Under the heading ***About the App***, blue italic body text reads:
    > *WhaleALERT is an app designed to augment existing ship navigation tools informing mariners of the safest and most current information to reduce the risk of ship and right whale collisions.*
    >
    > *- Seasonal Management Areas*
  - To the right of the text is a smaller landscape iPad mockup displaying the app's live NOAA nautical chart view of Massachusetts Bay and Cape Cod with acoustic buoy markers and management zones.
- **Right Decorative Border**:
  - A vertical underwater photograph strip along the right edge shows a fish swimming above yellow-orange sponge and rocky reef habitat in green-tinted water.
