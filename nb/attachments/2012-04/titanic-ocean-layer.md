# Finding the RMS Titanic via Google Earth's Shipwrecks Layer (`titanic-ocean-layer.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of **Google Earth** demonstrating how to locate the *RMS Titanic* wreck site using the search bar and the built-in `Ocean > Shipwrecks` layer, displaying the `Sinking` tab of the *RMS Titanic* popup balloon.

## Sidebar & Layer Controls
- **Search Results (Top Left)**:
  - Shows the search result item `titanic > Titanic` selected and highlighted in gray, encircled by a **bright yellow oval** added by the author.
- **Places Panel**: Collapsed (`► Places`).
- **Layers Panel**:
  - `Primary Database` is expanded with `Borders and Labels` and `Places` checked (`Photos`, `Roads`, `3D Buildings` unchecked).
  - `Ocean` is expanded and checked, with all sub-layers enabled:
    - `Explore the Ocean`
    - `ARKive: Endangered Ocean Sp...`
    - `Shipwrecks` (highlighted in gray with a sunken ship bow icon)
    - `Census of Marine Life`
    - `Cousteau Ocean World`
    - `Marine Protected Areas`
    - `Dead Zones`
    - `Animal Tracking`
    - `National Geographic`
    - `Ocean Sports`
  - Below `Ocean` are unchecked layers: `Weather`, `Gallery`, `Global Awareness`, and `More`.
