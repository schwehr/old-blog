# Starting and Controlling the Titanic Tour in Google Earth (`titanic-starting-tour.png`)

## Overview
A close-up screenshot of **Google Earth** illustrating how to launch and control the interactive **Titanic Tour** KML/KMZ animation, annotated with four yellow ovals highlighting the tour item, the record/play tour button, the playback transport controls, and the close button.

## Sidebar Controls & Highlights
- **Places Panel (Top Left)**:
  - Shows the bottom of the expanded `Titanic Resources` list (`Titanic First Class Cabin`, `Titanic Deck`, `Sunken Titanic`, `NOAA Titanic Expedition 2004`), followed by `Titanic Bow Model`, `Titanic Aft Model`, and the selected `Titanic Tour` item (encircled by a **yellow oval**).
  - At the bottom-right corner of the `Places` panel, next to the opacity slider, a second **yellow oval** highlights the small **video camera play button** used to start the selected tour.
- **Layers Panel (Bottom Left)**:
  - Partially visible below the `Places` panel, showing `Earth Gallery >>` and `Primary Database` (`Borders and Labels`, `Places`, and `Ocean` checked).

## 3D Viewport & Tour Playback Controller
- **3D Wreck Model**:
  - In the upper right of the viewport, the rusted starboard hull and white bow railing of the 3D *Titanic* bow model are visible at a steep angle against the dark blue underwater backdrop.
- **Tour Playback Bar (Bottom Right)**:
  - A dark translucent horizontal media controller floats near the bottom of the 3D view:
    - **Transport Controls (circled in yellow)**: Rewind (`<<`), Pause/Play (`|>`), Fast-Forward (`>>`), and a horizontal progress scrubber bar with the thumb positioned near the middle.
    - **Elapsed Time & Loop**: Displays `00:56` next to a square loop/repeat button with two curved blue arrows.
    - **Close Button (circled in yellow)**: A small `x` in the upper-right corner of the tour controller bar used to exit the tour mode.
  - Below the controller on the status bar is a green clock icon and a `2003` historical imagery button.
