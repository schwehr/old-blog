# US Tide Stations KML in Google Earth (`vdatum-tide-stations-google-earth.png`)

## Overview
A **Google Earth** globe view of North America and the eastern Pacific Ocean displaying a custom KML layer of NOAA tide stations across the United States coastlines, with an open popup balloon for Station `9410230` (La Jolla, CA) showing tidal datum values converted to meters.

## Globe & Placemark Distribution
- **Tide Station Markers**: Small circular white-and-red target icons line the coastlines of the United States, including:
  - The Aleutian Islands and southern Alaska coast in the upper left.
  - The Pacific Northwest, California coast, and Hawaiian Islands (lower left).
  - The U.S. East Coast, Gulf of Mexico, Bermuda, and Caribbean islands (Puerto Rico / U.S. Virgin Islands) on the right.
- **Credits & Logo**: Bottom center reads `Data SIO, NOAA, U.S. ... Image © 2012 TerraMetrics © 2012 Cnes/Spot Image`, with the `Google earth` logo at the lower right.
