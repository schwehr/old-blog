# WhaleALERT iPad App in Use on a Ship's Bridge (`whalealert-ifaw-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph (shared by Jake Levenson of the International Fund for Animal Welfare, IFAW) showing two ship's officers on a vessel's bridge consulting a paper NOAA nautical chart alongside an iPad running the **WhaleALERT** application.

## Scene Description
- **Mariners & Bridge Chart Table**:
  - Two uniformed deck officers stand on either side of the frame, leaning over a wooden bridge chart table in front of the forward bridge windows:
    - On the **left**, an officer wearing a white short-sleeved uniform shirt with black-and-gold striped shoulder epaulets (four gold stripes visible) rests his left forearm near the chart and points with his right hand toward a traffic lane / plotting area on the paper chart.
    - On the **right**, a second officer in a white short-sleeved shirt rests his right hand on the right side of the paper chart while looking toward the left officer's hand.
- **iPad Running WhaleALERT (Foreground Center)**:
  - Mounted or propped in a black holder in the lower-center foreground is a black iPad displaying the **WhaleALERT** app.
  - The screen shows the digital NOAA nautical chart of Cape Cod, Nantucket Sound, and the Great South Channel / Georges Bank area, overlaid with a yellow-shaded Seasonal Management Area (SMA) and a prominent red-orange polygon indicating the Great South Channel mandatory/management area east of Cape Cod and Nantucket, along with the vertical column of round navigation/tool buttons on the upper right edge of the screen.
- **Paper Nautical Chart**:
  - Spread across the chart table behind the iPad is a large paper NOAA nautical chart:
    - The bottom-left margin corner bears the chart number `13200` (`LORAN-C OVERPRINTED`).
    - The upper-right title block of the chart displays the NOAA emblem and the title `GEORGES BANK AND NANTUCKET SHOALS`.
    - Hand-drawn pencil track lines and shipping lane boundaries are visible across the center of the chart.
- **Background View Through Bridge Windows**:
  - Through the sloped bridge windows (with two vertical windshield wiper blades visible), the ship is berthed in a sunlit harbor/port with a pier and water on the right, and coastal city buildings, palm/deciduous trees, and parking lots visible ashore in the upper background.
