# WhaleALERT iPad App on a Ship's Bridge (`whalealert-ifaw-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken by Jake Levenson of the International Fund for Animal Welfare (IFAW) showing an Apple iPad mounted on the bridge console of a large ship, running the WhaleALERT mobile application while docked in a harbor.

## Foreground & Bridge Console
- **Mounted iPad**: Positioned at the lower center-right on a polished wooden bridge counter, held in a black cradle mount attached to the window or counter surface.
- **WhaleALERT App Display**: The iPad screen (in landscape orientation) displays a NOAA nautical chart centered on Massachusetts Bay, Cape Cod, and the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary, overlaid with colored management area polygons (including a blue boundary line, a yellow/tan region, and a red wedge-shaped zone) and a vertical column of five circular control icons along the right edge of the screen.
- **Cables & Equipment**:
  - A black 30-pin dock connector cable plugs into the left side of the iPad and coils loosely across the wooden counter to the left, alongside a black power adapter unit with a small red indicator LED on the far left edge.
  - A small black rectangular electronic box sits directly behind the top-right corner of the iPad.
  - A silver compact digital camera rests on the counter near the far right edge.
  - Two thin vertical cables or antenna leads run down along the dark window frame behind the iPad.

## View Through the Bridge Windows
- **Ship's Bow**: Looking forward through the wide bridge windows, the vessel's broad wooden foredeck stretches toward a pointed white bow. White bulwarks with orange lifebuoys line the port and starboard sides, and a white foremast structure rises at the bow tip.
- **Harbor & Tall Ship**: Beyond the bow across gray-green harbor water, a historic three-masted tall ship with a black hull and gold stripe is moored alongside a concrete pier on the left. White tour buses, vans, and palm trees are visible along the waterfront street and multi-story buildings behind the pier.
- **Adjacent Pier**: To the right of the bow, a long covered pier structure with a sloped roof shelters a row of parked cars alongside the berth.
