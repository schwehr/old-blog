# WhaleALERT App Back Online Showing All Green Buoys (`whalealert-back-online.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **WhaleALERT** iOS application (framed by a thin bright green border) displaying a NOAA nautical chart of Massachusetts Bay, Boston Harbor, Cape Cod Bay, and Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary after network maintenance at UNH CCOM completed, with all ten acoustic listening buoys showing green whale-fluke icons (indicating no right whale detections in the last 20 minutes).

## Map & Management Overlays
- **Base Chart**: A NOAA raster nautical chart covering coastal Massachusetts from Cape Ann in the north, past Boston and Massachusetts Bay, down to Cape Cod Bay, Buzzards Bay, and Narragansett Bay/Providence in the southwest.
- **Acoustic Listening Buoys**: A curved line of 10 overlapping bright green circular icons, each featuring a black whale tail fluke silhouette and a glowing green outer ring, follows the Boston Harbor traffic separation scheme from just east of Boston Harbor eastward and southeastward across Stellwagen Bank.
- **Management & Sanctuary Boundaries**:
  - **Black Polygon**: Outlines the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary boundary stretching between Cape Ann and Cape Cod.
  - **Purple Lines**: Mark the shipping lane / traffic separation scheme (TSS) and its southern leg extending into Cape Cod Bay.
  - **Blue Outline**: Traces the coastal and offshore boundary of the Northern Right Whale Critical Habitat / seasonal management area.
  - **Yellow/Olive Shaded Box (Right)**: Covers the offshore eastern portion of the chart.
  - **Red Shaded Polygon (Bottom Right)**: Marks the Great South Channel area to the southeast of Cape Cod.

## Interface Controls (Right Edge)
A vertical stack of five dark blue-gray rounded buttons along the top-right edge of the screen:
1. **Crosshair / Target Icon**: Center map on current GPS position.
2. **Information (`i`) Icon**: Open information/legend panel.
3. **Home (House) Icon**: Reset map to default home view.
4. **Settings (Wrench) Icon**: Configure app settings.
5. **Up-Chevron (`^`) Bar**: Collapse the button toolbar.
