# Aerial View of Greenland from an Icelandair Flight (`greenland.jpg`)

## Overview
An aerial photograph taken out the port-side window of an Icelandair passenger jet showing the rugged, snow- and glacier-covered mountains and fjords of coastal Greenland under a clear blue sky.

## Scene Description
- **Aircraft Wing & Winglet (Top Left)**: The swept port wing of the aircraft extends from the upper-left edge toward the top center, tipped with a dark blue vertical winglet bearing the golden-yellow stylized Icelandair logo.
- **Mountains, Glaciers, and Fjords (Lower Two-Thirds)**:
  - Sharp, jagged dark rock peaks and ridges rise through a thick blanket of white snowfields and valley glaciers.
  - A deep, dark blue-gray glacial fjord cuts diagonally from the middle-left toward the lower-right foreground, bordered by steep shadowed mountain walls.
  - Further toward the horizon in the upper middle, broad white ice sheets and coastal pack ice meet a strip of open blue water and a distant cloud bank.
- **Sky & Window**: A deep cobalt-blue sky at the top transitions to a pale, hazy white horizon line. Faint speckled patterns across the middle of the frame are caused by condensation or frost crystals on the outer airplane window pane.
