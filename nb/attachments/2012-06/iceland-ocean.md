# Coastal View of Faxaflói Bay and Mountains from Reykjavik, Iceland (`iceland-ocean.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect coastal photograph looking across the blue waters of Faxaflói Bay from the rocky shoreline near Seltjarnarnes / Reykjavik, Iceland, toward the flat-topped mountain massif of Esja (Esjan) and distant snow-capped peaks under a bright blue sky.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Shoreline (Left & Bottom)**:
  - Along the left edge, a sloped seawall/breakwater is constructed of large, angular gray basalt boulders.
  - Below the boulders, a broad intertidal flat covered in golden-brown and olive-brown seaweed/kelp extends into the shallow water at low tide.
- **Bay Waters (Middle)**: The deep blue and turquoise water of the bay stretches across the center of the image, dotted with small whitecaps ruffled by the wind.
- **Distant Mountains & Sky (Background)**:
  - Across the water, the steep, flat-topped volcanic range of Mount Esja rises on the left and right, with snow-streaked peaks visible in the central gap beneath a band of white cumulus clouds.
  - A clear, vivid blue sky fills the upper half of the frame, with a single fluffy white cloud near the upper right edge.
