# Moon Over the North Atlantic Near Iceland (`moon-over-the-atlantic.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-aspect photograph taken through an Icelandair airplane window at twilight/dawn as the flight approached Iceland, showing the aircraft's wing and winglet silhouetted against a pastel orange-and-blue horizon with a faint reddish moon low over the North Atlantic Ocean.

## Scene Description
- **Aircraft Wing & Winglet (Left)**: The port wing stretches from the lower-left edge up toward the upper center, ending in a dark blue vertical winglet emblazoned with the glowing yellow-orange Icelandair logo and a small white navigation light at the trailing tip.
- **Moon & Horizon (Center)**: Just below the outer section of the wing, a small, soft pinkish-orange disk of the rising moon sits in the dusky purple-gray haze layer above the ocean surface.
- **Twilight Sky**: Above the dark lavender-gray marine layer, a warm band of peach and golden-orange twilight glow transitions smoothly into a pale blue upper sky. Small out-of-focus speckles from moisture/ice crystals on the cabin window are faintly visible against the sky.
