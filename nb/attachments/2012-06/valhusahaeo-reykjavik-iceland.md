# View from Valhúsahæð Park in Seltjarnarnes / Reykjavik, Iceland (`valhusahaeo-reykjavik-iceland.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide panoramic photograph taken from the grassy hill of Valhúsahæð Park in Seltjarnarnes (greater Reykjavik), Iceland, looking across waterfront houses and Faxaflói Bay toward Mount Esja under a sunny blue sky with scattered clouds.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Valhúsahæð Park)**: A grassy, rock-strewn hill and meadow with a narrow gravel walking path curving across the lower right and a grassy mound with small yellow wildflowers on the lower left.
- **Middle Ground (Coastal Neighborhood & Bay)**:
  - A row of low-rise residential houses lines the shore below the hill, including a gray modern flat-roofed house on the left, a white two-story house, a white house with a bright red gabled roof, a pale pink house with a white gabled roof, and a dark-roofed house on the right, surrounded by green hedges and small trees.
  - Beyond the houses, the calm deep-blue water of the bay stretches across the width of the frame, with low rocky islets/spits visible in the water and white cylindrical storage tanks on a peninsula at the far right edge.
- **Background (Mount Esja & Sky)**: Across the bay rises the long, flat-topped volcanic massif of Mount Esja with patches of snow along its upper crest, topped by a bright blue sky and a band of sunlit white and gray cumulus clouds.
