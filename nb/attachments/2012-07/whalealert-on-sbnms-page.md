# WhaleALERT on the Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary Webpage (`whalealert-on-sbnms-page.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the NOAA Gerry E. Studds Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary website (`stellwagen.noaa.gov/protect/whalealert.html`) promoting the **WhaleALERT** mobile application for iPad and iPhone, designed to aid mariners and reduce the risk of ship collisions with endangered North Atlantic right whales.

## Header & Navigation Sidebar
- **Top Banner**: Dark blue underwater banner featuring a stylized white whale-tail logo on the left, the title `GERRY E. STUDDS STELLWAGEN BANK NATIONAL MARINE SANCTUARY` in white serif capitals, and an underwater photograph of a humpback whale on the right.
- **Left Navigation Menu**: Glossy blue pill-shaped buttons stacked vertically:
  - `Home`
  - `About the Sanctuary`
  - `Visitor Information`
  - `Education`
  - `Science`
  - `Management`
  - `Maritime Heritage`
  - `Resource Protection` (expanded with white sub-links on a dark blue background):
    - `Guidelines & Regulations`
    - `Protecting Whales from Tuna Gear`
    - `Enforcement`
    - `Incident Response`
    - `Contingency Plans`
    - `Reporting`
  - `News & Events`
  - `Galleries`
  - `Library`
  - `Get Involved`

## Main Content Area & Graphics
- **Section Heading**: `RESOURCE PROTECTION` in red uppercase letters between two thin red horizontal rules, flanked on the left by a cutout photo of a whale fluke breaking the water surface and on the right by a vertical curved underwater reef border showing yellow sponge coral and a swimming cod/haddock in green water.
- **Title Banner**: Large bold text reading `Whale` (in white) `ALERT` (in dark red), with the dark blue italic subtitle: `Aiding Mariners and Protecting Right Whales`.
- **Device Mockups & App Icon**:
  - **iPad (left)**: Shown in landscape orientation displaying the WhaleALERT splash graphic—a split above/below-water illustration showing a yellow acoustic monitoring buoy on the surface, a whale swimming underwater emitting blue concentric acoustic waves toward a hydrophone beneath the buoy, a faint nautical chart overlay in the sky, the `WhaleALERT` logo, and partner logos along the bottom (`NOAA`, `Stellwagen Bank`, `IFAW`, `EarthNC`, `Cornell Lab of Ornithology`, `EOM Offshore`, `WHOI`, `Massport`, `Excelerate Energy`, etc.).
  - **App Icon (top right)**: Rounded square icon featuring a red whale tail centered over a compass rose with white concentric sound waves against a blue map background.
  - **iPhone (bottom right)**: Shown in portrait orientation displaying the same WhaleALERT splash screen and partner logos.
  - **App Store Badge**: Black button at the bottom right reading `Available on the App Store` with an iPhone silhouette icon.
