# MSL EDL Press Conference Using Google Mars – Parachute Descent (`googlemars-msl-1.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken from the audience inside the von Karman Auditorium at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) on August 10, 2012, during a Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) *Curiosity* press briefing, showing the Entry, Descent, and Landing (EDL) team seated on stage beneath a large projection screen displaying a 3D Google Earth (Mars mode) trajectory visualization.

## Stage & Panelists
Six panelists sit behind a long, dark gray fabric-draped table against a blue backdrop curtain, with white nameplates along the front edge of the table:
1. **Allen Chen** (far left, in a dark polo shirt, looking down at notes/monitor)
2. **Gavin Mendeck** (second from left, in a white shirt)
3. **Devin Kipp** (third from left, in a light blue button-down shirt)
4. **Fourth panelist** (center-right, in a light blue shirt, nameplate partially obscured by an audience member's head—Steve Sell)
5. **Jody Davis** (second from right, blonde woman from NASA Langley Research Center who produced the Google Mars visualization)
6. **Ben Cichy** (far right, in a white shirt)

## Projection Screen & Visualization
- **Main Screen (above stage)**: Shows a 3D perspective view in Google Earth (Mars mode, with the `Google earth` logo in the bottom-right corner and the `NASA` and `Mars Science Laboratory` logos in the top-left corner).
  - Across the rust-orange Martian terrain of Gale Crater, a red trajectory line traces the spacecraft's descent path, accompanied by a wireframe cone/parachute deployment graphic and a text label (`Heatshield Separation` / parachute descent phase).
- **Secondary Monitor (far right)**: A smaller flat-screen display near the stage right exit mirrors the same Google Mars visualization.
- **Foreground Audience**: Several reporters and team members sit in dark blue auditorium chairs, seen from behind.
