# MSL EDL Press Conference Using Google Mars – Curiosity Rover on the Surface (`googlemars-msl-2.jpg`)

## Overview
A second photograph taken from the audience inside JPL's von Karman Auditorium during the August 10, 2012 Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) Entry, Descent, and Landing (EDL) press conference, showing the projection screen zoomed all the way down to the 3D model of the *Curiosity* rover on the surface of Gale Crater in Google Earth (Mars mode).

## Stage & Panelists
Seated behind the dark gray paneled table in front of a blue curtain backdrop are the six EDL panelists:
1. **Allen Chen** (far left, dark polo shirt)
2. **Gavin Mendeck** (second from left, white shirt)
3. **Devin Kipp** (third from left, light blue shirt)
4. **Steve Sell** (fourth from left, light blue shirt)
5. **Jody Davis** (second from right, NASA Langley visualization lead)
6. **Ben Cichy** (far right, white shirt)

## Projection Screen & Visualization
- **Main Screen (above stage)**: Displays a ground-level 3D perspective in Google Earth (`Google earth` logo at bottom right; `NASA` and `Mars Science Laboratory` logos at top left) looking across the orange Martian plains toward the rim/mound of Gale Crater on the horizon.
  - A vertical red descent line drops straight down from the sky to a 3D model of the **MSL Curiosity Rover** resting on the surface, labeled `MSL Curiosity Rover` in white text.
  - A bright horizontal white/yellow line marks the ground track across the plain, with a scale bar at bottom left and NASA/USGS image credits at bottom center.
- **Secondary Monitor (far right)**: Displays the same ground-level rover view for audience members on the right side of the room.
