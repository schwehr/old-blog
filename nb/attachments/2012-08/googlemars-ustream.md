# Google Mars EDL Trajectory Visualization on UStream (`googlemars-ustream.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a recorded UStream video broadcast (`36:52` into the August 10, 2012 Mars Science Laboratory press conference) showing NASA Langley's 3D Entry, Descent, and Landing (EDL) animation inside Google Earth (Mars mode) as *Curiosity*'s red trajectory curves across Martian highland terrain into Gale Crater.

## Scene & Trajectory Details
- **3D Mars Viewport**:
  - Looks northward/northwestward at an oblique angle across cratered tan Martian terrain toward **Gale Crater** and its central mound (**Mount Sharp**), which is covered by a large rust-red high-resolution elevation/imagery overlay block. The curved Martian horizon and a starry black sky appear at the top.
  - **Red Trajectory Curve**: Enters from the bottom center and curves slightly leftward toward the northwestern floor of Gale Crater, annotated with white text milestones along its flight path (from foreground to background inside the crater):
    - `Bank Reversal 2`
    - `Bank Reversal 3`
    - `Heading Alignment`
    - `Backshell Separation`, `Parachute Deploy`, `Heatshield Separation`, `Touchdown`, and `MSL Curiosity Rover` clustered near the landing site.
    - `Gale Crater` is labeled with a small circle at the central peak to the right of the landing track.

## Overlays & Player Controls
- **Top-Left Logos**: Official `NASA` meatball insignia above the `MARS SCIENCE LABORATORY` mission crest.
- **Top-Right Watermark**: `USTREAM RECORDED LIVE` in white sans-serif text.
- **Bottom Viewport Overlays**: Scale bar on the left, `Image NASA / USGS` and `ESA / DLR / FU Berlin (G. Neukum)` credits in the center, and the `Google earth` logo on the right.
- **UStream Player Bar (bottom)**: White video player bar with a pause button, speaker/volume icon, timeline scrubber with playhead tooltip at `36:52` (and `00:35:59` on the right), `Share` button, loop icon, and fullscreen toggle.
