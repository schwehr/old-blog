# ICODE-MDA Contributors Custom Map on Google Maps (`icode-mda-on-google-maps.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of Google Maps (`My Places` custom map) showing the global distribution of contributors to the **ICODE-MDA** (*International Collaborative Development of Open Source Tools for Enhanced Maritime Domain Awareness*) project, with an open humorous info-window for `"Joe the Penguin"` in Antarctica.

- **Top Google Bar**: Shows the classic `Google` logo, search input bar with microphone icon and blue search button, signed-in account `schwehr@google.com`, notification counter `0`, `+ Share` button, and Kurt's profile avatar on the far right.
