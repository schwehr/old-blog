# NASA Social Tour Group Inside the JPL Digital Image Animation Laboratory (DIAL) (`msl-aug03-1-nasasocial-in-dial.jpg`)

## Overview
A candid indoor photograph taken on Friday, August 3, 2012 (Landing Minus 2 days for the Mars Science Laboratory *Curiosity* rover) showing participants of the **MSL NASA Social** tour seated inside the **Digital Image Animation Laboratory (DIAL)** at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL).

## Scene & People
- **Setting**: A dimly lit computer visualization and media lab at JPL with gray acoustic wall panels on the left, a wooden door in the center background, and a glass-walled equipment/storage room with wooden framing and black binders on shelves to the right. An Apple Cinema Display monitor is visible on a desk at the far left, and a curved wooden conference table edge appears at the far right with green digital status numbers glowing underneath.
- **Tour Group**: Around ten visitors wearing blue NASA/JPL lanyards sit closely together in office chairs facing toward a speaker or display off-camera to the right:
  - In the foreground, a person in a bright blue T-shirt (left) and two men in dark shirts (right) are seen from behind/three-quarter profile.
  - In the middle row, a man in a white long-sleeve dress shirt and dark trousers leans forward attentively, seated next to a man with white hair in a yellow shirt.
  - Along the back wall and doorway sit several more attendees in casual shirts and jeans, including a woman in glasses holding a mobile device and taking notes.
