# JPL Space Flight Operations Facility – MSL Flight Control Consoles (`msl-aug04-1-flight-controllers.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide-angle indoor photograph taken on Saturday, August 4, 2012 (one day before the Mars Science Laboratory *Curiosity* landing) inside the **Space Flight Operations Facility (Mission Control)** at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) in Pasadena, California, showing rows of curved flight controller consoles, wall displays, and the JPL/NASA insignia.

## Room Layout & Visual Details
- **Foreground & Consoles**:
  - Curved rows of dark gray flight control consoles stretch diagonally across the room, with empty black ergonomic office chairs in the immediate foreground and several engineers/controllers seated or standing at the middle rows.
  - Along the top rim of the nearest console bank are illuminated station signs flanked by small NASA and JPL logos, reading `Thermal` (twice) and `Propulsion`. A tall travel mug sits on the left end of the console divider.
- **Flight Controllers**:
  - A bearded flight controller in a dark long-sleeve shirt stands near the center console row looking toward the left of the room, surrounded by five seated colleagues partially visible behind the console partitions.
- **Back Wall & Displays**:
  - **Left Wall Monitors**: Large wall-mounted projection screens display mission telemetry plots, multi-panel camera feeds of the control room, and a circular mission logo graphic on a black background.
  - **Right Wall**: Features a large **United States flag** hung horizontally next to the blue **NASA** meatball logo and bold red-and-black wall lettering reading:
    - `Jet Propulsion Laboratory`
    - `California Institute of Technology`
