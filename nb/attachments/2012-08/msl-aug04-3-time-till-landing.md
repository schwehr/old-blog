# MSL Time Until Landing Display and UTC Clock (`msl-aug04-3-time-till-landing.jpg`)

## Overview
A close-up photograph of a wall-mounted digital LED clock and an LCD monitor at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) on Saturday, August 4, 2012, displaying the current local time, Universal Coordinated Time (UTC), and the countdown timer until the landing of the Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) *Curiosity* rover.

## Display Details & Visible Text
- **Top Digital LED Clock**: Mounted on the wall above the monitor is a black rectangular digital clock with bright red seven-segment LED numerals reading:
  - `217` (Day of Year: August 4 in the 2012 leap year)
  - `15:40 32` (15 hours, 40 minutes, 32 seconds UTC)
- **Bottom LCD Monitor**: Directly below the LED clock is a widescreen flat-panel monitor running a Microsoft Excel spreadsheet window (the window title bar at top left reads `Microsoft Excel - Mars Viewer Tool ...` and sheet tabs at the bottom include `Clock`, `Tactical Schedule`, `Notification Distribution`, `Options`, and `Display`). The screen has a clean white background divided into three horizontal rows:
  1. **Top Row (Local Time)**: Red bold **"JPL"** logo on the left, followed by black bold sans-serif text: **`Saturday 08/04/2012 08:40:32 AM`**.
  2. **Middle Row (UTC / ISO Day-of-Year Time)**: An oval Robinson-projection world satellite map icon on the left, followed by bright blue bold text: **`2012-217T15:40:32   Week 31`**.
  3. **Bottom Row (Landing Countdown)**: The official **Mars Science Laboratory** mission emblem (a trapezoidal crest showing the *Curiosity* rover on Mars with the NASA logo and "JPL" at the top, and **"MARS SCIENCE LABORATORY"** in a tan banner below) on the left, followed by red bold text: **`Landing - 1 day 13:36:20`**.
