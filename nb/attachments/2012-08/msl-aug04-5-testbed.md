# Mars Science Laboratory Testbed Rover at JPL (`msl-aug04-5-testbed.jpg`)

## Overview
A high-angle indoor photograph of the Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) engineering testbed rover (Vehicle System Test Bed / " Scarecrow" or flight-like test rover) situated in an indoor simulated Martian sand pit at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) on August 4, 2012.

## Scene Description
- **Testbed Rover**: The full-scale six-wheeled rover sits near the center of a dark gray/brown coarse sand floor, angled toward the upper right:
  - **Wheels & Suspension**: Five of the six black cleated aluminum wheels are clearly visible in the foreground and right side, attached to the white and gold/black titanium tubes of the rocker-bogie suspension system.
  - **Chassis & Power Source**: The rectangular white rover body has exposed cabling, gold-colored Kapton tape, and instrumentation boxes on its top deck. At the rear (left side of the rover), the angled tan/olive housing of the Multi-Mission Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator (MMRTG) simulator projects outward between two protective side fins. A tall black cylindrical tube stands vertically near the rear left corner of the chassis, while the Remote Sensing Mast (camera mast) is absent from the front deck.
  - **Robotic Arm**: At the front (upper right) of the rover, the stowed robotic arm and turret assembly—carrying drill and contact science instruments—is visible.
  - **Umbilical Cable**: A thick bundle of tan and black power/data umbilical cables trails from the underside/rear of the rover across the sand toward the bottom left corner.
- **Test Arena Environment**:
  - **Simulated Terrain**: Behind the rover near the top center is an artificial outcrop of stacked, angular tan and reddish-brown rocks. Smaller scattered flat rocks lie in the lower right corner.
  - **Background**: A painted canvas backdrop depicting brown Martian hills and slopes hangs along the upper left wall. In the upper right corner, the sand pit meets a white tiled laboratory floor with metal equipment racks and stools.
