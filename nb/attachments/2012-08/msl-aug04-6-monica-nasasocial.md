# Monica at the MSL NASA Social Event (`msl-aug04-6-monica-nasasocial.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor photograph taken on August 4, 2012, at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) showing participant Monica smiling and holding a small brown plush toy at a table full of laptops and tablets during the Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) *Curiosity* NASA Social media event.

Resting on her upheld palms is a round, brown plush character (a " Chuzzle"-like or furry round mascot with a tuft of white hair on top, small eyes, and stubby ears/feet).
