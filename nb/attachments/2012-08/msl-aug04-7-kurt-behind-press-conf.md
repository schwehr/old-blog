# Kurt Schwehr at the Back of the MSL Press Conference (`msl-aug04-7-kurt-behind-press-conf.jpg`)

## Overview
A self-portrait (selfie) photograph taken by Kurt Schwehr standing at the back of the von Karman Auditorium at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) during a Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) *Curiosity* pre-landing press briefing on August 4, 2012.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Kurt Schwehr)**: Positioned in the left foreground, Kurt faces the camera with a slight smile. He has short brown hair, rectangular metal-rimmed eyeglasses, a small hoop earring in his left ear, and wears a light gray T-shirt with a black lanyard printed with white letters (`...tory`, from "Jet Propulsion Laboratory") around his neck.
- **Left Background (Spacecraft Model)**: Directly behind Kurt's right shoulder, illuminated in dramatic blue stage lighting, is a large scale model of a spacecraft (featuring a large white high-gain parabolic dish antenna at the top and a truss structure below).
- **Right Background (Press Conference Auditorium)**: Looking down the center aisle of the auditorium toward the stage:
  - **Audience & Media**: Rows of dark blue cushioned chairs are occupied by journalists and attendees. A woman with long blonde hair wearing a black short-sleeved shirt and dark pants stands in the aisle in the middle ground with her hands on her hips, facing the stage. A black camera tripod leg is visible in the bottom right corner.
  - **Stage & Panelists**: At the front of the room, five panelists sit behind a long draped table, with a sixth speaker standing at a podium on the left next to a U.S. flag.
  - **Projection Screen**: Above the panelists hangs a large bright projection screen displaying the circular NASA/JPL Mars Science Laboratory logo on the left and a graphic of the *Curiosity* rover on the Martian surface on the right.
