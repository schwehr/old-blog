# Android Mascot Visits the MSL Press Conference (`msl-aug04-8-droid-visits-press-conf.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken from the back of the von Karman Auditorium at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) during a Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) *Curiosity* pre-landing press briefing on August 4, 2012, featuring a small green Android ("Droid") figure peeking up in the immediate foreground.

- **Foreground (Android Mascot & Camera Rig)**: In the lower left foreground, slightly out of focus, a small lime-green Android robot figurine ("Droid") with white eyes and two antennae peeks over a dark horizontal strap or equipment bar. To the right, two professional video cameras on tall silver and black tripods stand on a raised platform at the rear of the auditorium, with a camera operator in a black T-shirt and dark pants standing beside the right tripod.
- **Middle Ground (Aisle & Audience)**: A wide central aisle illuminated by purple/blue accent lighting leads toward the stage. Along the left side of the aisle, two staff members in dark clothing stand near a small illuminated lectern.   - **Podium & Panelists**: On the left side of the stage next to a standing U.S. flag, a woman in a white blazer stands at the NASA JPL wooden podium. To the right, five panelists sit behind a long dark-draped table with white nameplates in front of a blue backdrop.
