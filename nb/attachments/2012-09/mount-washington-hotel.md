# Omni Mount Washington Resort in Bretton Woods, New Hampshire (`mount-washington-hotel.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide panoramic photograph of the historic Mount Washington Hotel (Omni Mount Washington Resort) in Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, viewed from its long curving approach driveway against the backdrop of the Presidential Range of the White Mountains under an overcast late-September sky.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Approach Road**: A two-lane paved driveway curves gently from the bottom center into the middle distance, where a single dark sedan is driving toward the hotel. Expanses of green lawn and golf course turf flank both sides of the road. Two black vintage-style post lanterns stand along the right edge of the roadway, and low boulders mark the left shoulder.
- **Middle Ground (Mount Washington Hotel & Grounds)**:
  - **Left Forest**: A dense stand of dark green conifers mixed with early autumn foliage borders the left side of the lawn.
  - **The Hotel**: Nestled just right of the trees in the left-center distance sits the sprawling, white-stuccoed Spanish Renaissance Revival hotel topped with its signature bright red roofs and two prominent octagonal towers flying flags.
  - **Right Foothills**: To the right of the hotel, rolling hills and forest patches show warm orange and rust-colored autumn foliage.
- **Background (Mountains & Sky)**:
  - **Presidential Range**: Rising directly behind the hotel and stretching across the right half of the horizon are the dark blue-gray slopes and peaks of the White Mountains, with the upper ridges partially veiled by low cloud bases.
  - **Sky**: A dramatic layer of thick gray and white stratocumulus clouds blankets the upper half of the frame, with a small patch of pale blue sky peeking through at the upper left.
