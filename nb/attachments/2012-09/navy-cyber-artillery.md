# U.S. Navy Futuristic "Cyber Artillery" and Holographic Interface (`navy-cyber-artillery.jpg`)

## Overview
A still frame from a dramatic U.S. Navy promotional/recruitment concept video depicting a military operator wearing a futuristic brain-computer interface (BCI) headset and dark visored glasses while interacting with a floating, translucent holographic heads-up display (HUD) of an aircraft carrier.

## Scene Description
- **Operator**: Positioned in the center of the frame against a dark green gridded background, a short-haired man in desert digital camouflage utilities faces slightly to the left:
  - **Headgear**: He wears sleek black wrap-around tactical glasses/goggles and an elaborate black neuro-headset with multiple curved sensor arms terminating in circular electrodes pressed against his scalp and forehead.
  - **Gesture**: His right hand is raised to an earpiece or temple control on the right side of his headset, while his left hand is held open in the foreground with fingers spread as if manipulating the virtual interface.
- **Holographic Display & Visible Text**: Floating in mid-air to the left of the operator is a glowing blue-white rectangular projection frame viewed from the reverse side (so all text and numerals appear horizontally mirrored):
  - **3D Aircraft Carrier**: At the bottom center of the hologram, a glowing cyan and green wireframe/holographic model of a *Nimitz*- or *Ford*-class nuclear aircraft carrier sits atop a dark blue wireframe ocean grid, with yellow highlights underneath its hull.
  - **Mirrored Data Readouts**: In the upper right corner of the translucent panel (overlapping the operator's face), white mirrored digital numbers read **`80156`** (mirrored) above a small status box showing three angled missile/aircraft icons, and **`53267.50`** (mirrored) below.
  - **Mirrored Text Panels**: To the left within the hologram are two smaller rectangular sub-windows titled **`OVERVIEW`** (mirrored at top right of the box) containing lines of blurred cyan and white status text.
