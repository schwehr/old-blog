# U.S. Unemployment Rate Time Series in Google Public Data Explorer (`unemployment-google-public-data.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **Google Public Data Explorer** web application taken in September 2012, displaying a line chart of the seasonally adjusted U.S. national unemployment rate from 1986 through July 2012, with a callout box highlighting the 7.8% unemployment rate in January 2009.

## Interface & Controls
- **Top Header Bar**:
  - Classic multi-colored **Google** logo at top left, above red section title **`Public Data`**.
  - Search input box containing `Search public data or change visualization` with a blue magnifying glass button.
  - User account indicator on the right reading **`Kurt Schwehr`**, notification badge `0`, **`+ Share`** button, and user avatar thumbnail.
- **Left Sidebar Navigation**:
  - **Data Metric Tree**: Expanded under **`Unemployment in the U.S.`** with options `Employment`, `Labor force`, `Unemployment`, and bolded **`Unemployment rate`** (selected), followed by a `Clear` button.
  - **Geographic Filter**: **`Compare by`** dropdown set to `Country`, with a checked box for **`United States`** and unchecked state sub-options (`Alabama`, `Alaska`, `Arizona`, `Arkansas`, `California`, `Colorado`, `Connecticut`, `Delaware`, `District of Columbia`), followed by a `Clear selections` button.
  - **Collapsed Filters**: Bottom rows for `▸ Age`, `▸ Seasonality  Seasonally Adjus...`, and `▸ Sex`.
- **Chart Toolbar (Top Right)**: Four chart-type toggle buttons (line chart selected, bar chart, map, bubble chart), a gear settings dropdown, and a link icon button.

## Plot Details & Visible Data
- **Title**: **`Unemployment rate - Seasonally Adjusted`** with a `[?]` help icon.
- **Axes**:
  - **Vertical (Y) Axis**: Labeled from **`0%`** to **`10%`** in increments of 2% (`0%`, `2%`, `4%`, `6%`, `8%`, `10%`).
  - **Horizontal (X) Axis**: Labeled in two-year intervals from **`1986`** to **`2010`**, ending with **`Jul 2012`** on the far right.
- **Time-Series Curve**: A single blue line labeled **`United States`** (with a blue `[x]` box on the right) traces monthly unemployment:
  - Starts near 7% in 1986, drops to ~5% in 1989–1990, rises to a peak near 7.8% in 1992, declines steadily to a low of ~3.8% in 2000, rises to ~6.3% in 2003, falls to ~4.4% in 2006–2007, surges sharply during the 2008–2009 recession to a peak of 10.0% in late 2009, and gradually declines to ~8.2%–8.3% by July 2012.
- **Highlighted Data Point**: A blue circular marker is placed on the steep upward slope at January 2009, with a floating white tooltip reading:
  - **`7.8%`**
  - **`Jan 2009`**
- **Timeline Range Slider & Footer**: Below the X-axis is a full-range timeline slider spanning **`1950`** to **`2010`** in 5-year ticks, with the active viewing window highlighted between late 1985 and mid-2012. The footer reads:
  - `Data from U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics   Last updated: Sep 3, 2012`
