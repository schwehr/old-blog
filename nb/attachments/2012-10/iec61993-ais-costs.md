# IEC Webstore Publication Detail for IEC 61993-2 ed2.0 (`iec61993-ais-costs.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) Webstore product page for the standard **IEC 61993-2 ed2.0** (*Maritime navigation and radiocommunication equipment and systems - Automatic identification systems (AIS) - Part 2: Class A shipborne equipment...*), published on October 19, 2012. The image was featured in Kurt Schwehr's October 20, 2012 blog post ("The cost of costs") to illustrate the high price barrier (295 Swiss francs) blocking public and engineering access to safety-critical maritime specifications.
