# NOAA Ship Thomas Jefferson Long Island Sound Bathymetry in Google Earth (`noaa-tj-bathy-in-google-earth-oceans.png`)

## Overview
A Google Earth screenshot showcasing high-resolution multibeam bathymetry collected by the NOAA Ship *Thomas Jefferson* in Long Island Sound, overlaid on satellite imagery and the Google Ocean seafloor layer. The image was shared in Kurt Schwehr's October 12, 2012 blog post ("AAG April 2013 meeting - DEM advances and challenges") from the NOAA Coast Survey blog.

## Map & Bathymetric Details
- **Geographic Region**: Long Island Sound, bounded to the north by the Connecticut coastline (visible at the upper left) and to the south by the North Shore of Long Island, New York (visible along the bottom).
- **Color-Coded Bathymetric Overlay**:
  - The newly surveyed seafloor corridor stretches east–west across the southern and central portions of Long Island Sound, with an additional rectangular block extending northward on the eastern side and coastal coverage reaching into the western bays and harbors along the Connecticut shore.
  - Depths are rendered using a rainbow color ramp with shaded relief illuminating underwater sand waves, channels, shoals, and glacial ridges:
    - **Red and Orange**: Shallow nearshore shoals, coastal shelves, and prominent sand ridges along the Long Island shoreline and western Connecticut coast.
    - **Yellow and Green**: Intermediate water depths across the broad central basin and eastern shelf.
    - **Cyan, Blue, and Dark Purple**: Deeper axial channels, scour depressions, and troughs running through the center and eastern sections of the sound.
- **Background Seafloor & Land**: Unsurveyed portions of the sound in the upper-center display Google Earth's standard muted blue-gray regional bathymetry. Surrounding land areas show green and brown satellite imagery with roads, urban development, and coastal inlets.

## Interface Elements & Labels
- **Top Left**: Official circular **NOAA** (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration / U.S. Department of Commerce) logo on a white square background.
- **Top Right**: Semi-transparent Google Earth navigation controls (compass ring, look/pan circle, street-view pegman, and vertical zoom slider).
- **Bottom Right Inset Map**: A red-bordered overview map of the entire Long Island Sound region (showing Connecticut, Rhode Island, New York City, and Long Island) with the color-coded bathymetric survey footprint highlighted in the center.
- **Bottom Status Bar & Credits**:
  - **Bottom Left**: `Imagery Date: 3/30/2012`
  - **Bottom Center**: Attribution text `Data SIO, NOAA, U.S. Navy, NGA, GEBCO` above the coordinate readout `41°01'05.29" N  73°01'17.77" W  elev -101 ft`.
  - **Bottom Right**: `Google earth` logo and camera altitude `Eye alt 31.12 mi`.
