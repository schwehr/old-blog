# MarineRegions.org Homepage Screenshot (`marineregions.org.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the homepage of **Marineregions.org**, a marine georeferenced place-names and maritime boundaries portal, captured shortly after its launch in November 2012. Featured in Kurt Schwehr's November 30, 2012 blog post ("Marineregions.org").

## Header & Navigation Bar
- **Logo & Title**: In the upper left is a 3D blue-and-teal globe icon resting on a soft gray drop shadow. To its right is the serif title **Marineregions.org** in dark blue with a subtle drop shadow, underlined by the gray tagline `towards a standard for georeferenced marine names`.
- **Navigation Menu**: A horizontal teal-blue bar spans the width of the page, containing six white navigation links:
  `About` | `Marine Gazetteer` | `EEZ boundaries` | `Sources` | `Statistics` | `Downloads`

## Main Content & Background Graphic
- **Background Image**: The central body features a faded photograph of a sculpted white iceberg reflected in calm polar water.
- **About Marine Regions (Right-Aligned)**:
  - Heading: **About Marine Regions** (dark blue)
  - Text: *"Marine Regions is a standard list of marine georeferenced place names and areas. It integrates and serves geographic information from the VLIMAR Gazetteer and the MARBOUND database and proposes a standard of marine georeferenced locations, boundaries and regions."*
  - Link: `Read more...` (blue underlined link)
- **Statistics (Right-Aligned)**:
  - Heading: **Statistics** (dark blue)
  - Summary list:
    - `25,369 marine georeferenced places`
    - `32,481 marine place names`
    - `5,578 polygons of geographic places`
    - `12 marine geographic regional/global classifications`
    - `Maritime Boundaries (EEZ) of the world`
