# Sandy Island Bathymetric Profile in Google Earth — Small Version (`sandy-island-google-earth-small.png`)

## Overview
A medium-resolution (`600 × 357` pixels) inline version of [`sandy-island-google-earth.png`](file:///usr/local/google/home/schwehr/backup/cowfish/schwehr/blog/nb/attachments/2012-11/sandy-island-google-earth.png), embedded in Kurt Schwehr's November 25, 2012 blog post ("Sandy Island") to illustrate a bathymetric cross-section across the phantom "Sandy Island" in the Coral Sea using Google Earth.

## Interface & Map Summary
- **Central Satellite & Seafloor View**: Shows the Coral Sea east of the Chesterfield Islands (visible as light-blue reef structures to the left of center). A horizontal red transect line crosses the reefs and passes directly over the dark, elongated north–south shape of Sandy Island (`lat -19.341344° lon 159.392068°`), where a red vertical arrow points to the island with labels `1 m`, `217 km`, and `0.0%`.
- **Bottom Elevation Profile**: Plots elevation (`-3726 m` to `1 m`) against distance (`0` to `504 km`). The pink-shaded profile curve shows the Chesterfield Reef plateau on the left, a broad rise in the middle peaking at **`1 m`** at **`217 km`** (marking the erroneous Sandy Island elevation spike in the global bathymetry dataset), and a sharp submarine peak near `400 km`.
- **Left Sidebar**: Displays Google Earth's `Search`, `Places` (with `Sandy Island Profile` selected under `Temporary Places`), and `Layers` panels.
- **Upper-Right Dialog (`Google Earth – Edit Path`)**: Displays the path name `Sandy Island Profile` and a description box explaining how to create the profile: *"Use the measure icon. Create a line across the area. Then right click on the profile and set the Altitude to "Clamp to Seafloor". Then right click the places item and create a profile."*
