# WildAid 2012 MPA Conference Webpage Screenshot (`wildaid-2012-mpa-conference.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the event webpage for the **WildAid 2012 Marine Protected Area (MPA) Conference** (`wildaid.org/event/2012-mpa-conference`), held November 25–29, 2012, in San Francisco, California. Featured in Kurt Schwehr's November 27, 2012 blog post ("Wild Aid 2012 MPA Conference"), where he and Jenifer Austin Foulkes presented on the Google Ocean Project.

- **Top Bar**:

  - Upper right: A bright yellow **DONATE** button in bold black uppercase text.
- **Left Column Navigation & News**:
  - **Logo**: White **WILDAID** logo at top left.
  - **Main Menu (Right-Aligned Gray Text)**: `ABOUT`, `PROGRAMS`, `WHERE WE WORK`, `TAKE ACTION`, `MEDIA`.
