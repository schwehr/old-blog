# Seafloor Explorer Citizen Science Website (`seafloor-explorer.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the homepage for **Seafloor Explorer** (`seafloorexplorer.org`), a Zooniverse citizen science project created in collaboration with the HabCam team and the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution (WHOI) to classify marine species and ground cover in seafloor imagery along the northeast US continental shelf. Featured in the January 4, 2013 blog post *"Citizen science - get involved with the ocean"*.

## Layout & Interface Details
- **Top Zooniverse Bar**: A white top bar reading `Seafloor Explorer is a Zooniverse project.` on the left, and login controls on the right (`username` input box, `password` input box, `Login` button, `Sign Up` button, and a `Forgot Password?` link).
- **Main Header & Navigation**: Set against a teal-blue background (`#3597b8`), featuring a white compass/circle icon on a white tab at top-left, the title `SEAFLOOR EXPLORER` in tall white sans-serif lettering, and horizontal navigation links: `Home` (selected in a darker teal pill), `About`, `Science`, `Classify`, `Profile`, `FAQ`, `Talk`, and `Blog`.
- **Interactive Map Banner**:
  - A wide map powered by `CARTODB` (logo in bottom-right corner) with `+` and `-` zoom buttons in the top-left corner.
  - Shows the eastern/central United States and Atlantic coastline in white with gray city labels (`Chicago`, `Detroit`, `Cleveland`, `Pittsburgh`, `New York`, `Philadelphia`, `Baltimore`, `Washington`, `Boston`, `Providence`, `Portland`, etc.) and teal-blue water.
  - Orange dots mark seafloor survey camera stations offshore of New England (around Georges Bank, Gulf of Maine, and south of Cape Cod/Nantucket) and off the Delmarva Peninsula / Chesapeake Bay mouth.
- **Five Colored Statistics Cards**: Positioned directly below the map in a horizontal row, each showing a category label on a colored upper band and a live numerical counter on a light gray lower band:
  1. **Orange**: `Total number of scallops marked` — `877538`
  2. **Light Blue**: `Total number of fish marked` — `76301`
  3. **Purple**: `Total number of seastars marked` — `1737135`
  4. **Olive/Chartreuse Green**: `Total number of crustaceans marked` — `70164`
  5. **Dark Navy/Black**: `Total number of images classified` — `1146320`
- **Call-to-Action Footer Box**:
  - A light gray box at the bottom containing bold black heading text `Help explore the ocean floor.`, subheading `The HabCam team and the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution need your help!`, and description `Identify species and ground cover in images of the seafloor, and help create a library of seafloor life in the habitats along the northeast continental shelf.`
  - On the right side of the box is a large orange button labeled `Dive in!`.
