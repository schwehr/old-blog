# SubseaObservers Citizen Science Portal (`subsea-observers.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of the **SubseaObservers** (`subseaobservers.com`) citizen science web portal led by Art Trembanis at the University of Delaware, where volunteers analyze Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) seafloor photographs to assess the health and abundance of the Mid-Atlantic scallop fishery. Featured in the January 4, 2013 blog post *"Citizen science - get involved with the ocean"*.
