# ERMA Gulf Response Map: BP Deepwater Horizon Oil Spill and Sea Turtle Sightings (`erma-gulf-response-bp-spill-sea-turtle-sightings_noaa.jpg`)

## Overview
A screenshot of NOAA's **ERMA Gulf Response** (Environmental Response Management Application) web GIS interface displaying the May 31, 2010 surface oil trajectory estimate for the BP Deepwater Horizon oil spill alongside cumulative confirmed sea turtle observations (alive and dead) and incident command locations across the northern Gulf of Mexico. Featured in the March 5, 2013 blog post *"ERMA makes the NOAA blog post on ERMA"*.

## Map View & Geospatial Layers
- **Geographic Coverage**: Satellite base map (`Map data ©2012 Google Imagery ©2012 TerraMetrics`) showing the northern Gulf of Mexico coastline from southeastern Louisiana (`Baton Rouge`, `New Orleans`, `Houma`, `Morgan City`, and the Mississippi River Delta) across Mississippi (`Gulfport`, `Biloxi`, `Pascagoula`), Alabama (`Mobile`, `Orange Beach`), and the western Florida Panhandle (`Pensacola`).
- **Oil Spill Trajectory Polygon (`31-MAY-10 2100 CDT`)**:
  - A large bright cyan polygon offshore of the Mississippi River Delta and south of Mississippi/Alabama delineates the light/very light oil extent, enclosed within an outer black trajectory boundary line.
  - Darker royal-blue and navy interior polygons indicate moderate and heavy oil concentrations near the Deepwater Horizon wellhead surface location.
- **Sea Turtle Observations (`12-July-2010`)**:
  - **Yellow circular icons (`Alive`)**: Clustered offshore inside the cyan/blue oil slick polygons near the wellhead and southwest toward the Mississippi Delta, with scattered sightings near shore.
  - **Orange circular icons (`Dead`)**: Heavily concentrated along the barrier islands, beaches, and nearshore waters of Mississippi Sound, Gulfport/Biloxi, Dauphin Island/Mobile Bay, Orange Beach, Pensacola, Chandeleur Sound, and Barataria Bay.
- **Command Locations**: White square/circular badges labeled `G` (GCIMT/UAC in New Orleans and Mobile), `Br` (Branch markers along the Louisiana and Alabama/Florida coast), and `Div` (Division markers across coastal sectors).
- **Map Controls**: Top-left pan/zoom slider bar and five tool buttons (hand pan, zoom box, measure, etc.).

## Header, Legend Sidebar & Status Bar
- **Top Banner**: Dark blue header titled `ERMA GULF RESPONSE` on the left with `Information`, `Help`, `Recent Data` buttons and a `Find` search bar; on the right is `Environmental Response Management Application (ERMA)` and the NOAA logo.
- **Right Sidebar (`Legend` tab active, alongside `Layers`, `Query Tools`, `Zoom`, `Download`, `Print`)**:
  - **BP Deepwater Horizon Oil Spill**: `Wellhead Surface Location` (`Wellhead` yellow circle icon).
  - **Turtle & Marine Mammal Observations (NMFS)**: `NMFS Cumulative Confirmed Sea Turtle Observations 12-July-2010` with legend icons for `Alive` (yellow) and `Dead` (orange).
  - **May 2010**: `Trajectory Estimate for 31-MAY-10 2100 CDT` showing a color scale bar and thumbnail diagrams for oil distribution density (`Trajectory Boundary`, `Light`, `Medium`, `Heavy`).
  - **Areas of Operation**: `Command Locations` symbols for `G` (`GCIMT/UAC`), `Br` (`Branch`), and `Div` (`Division`).
- **Bottom Status & Footer Bar**:
  - Status bar displays north arrow `N`, `Scale: 1: 2M`, `Zoom Level: 8`, and `Location: 28.26316°, -86.80241°`.
  - Dark blue footer lists partner agencies (`National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration | Environmental Protection Agency | U.S. Coast Guard | U.S. Department of the Interior | U.S. Department of Homeland Security | University of New Hampshire`) and `Powered by ERMA®` on the right.
