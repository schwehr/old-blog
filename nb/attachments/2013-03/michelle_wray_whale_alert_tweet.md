# Cartoon of a Right Whale Tweeting a Ship by Michelle Wray (`michelle_wray_whale_alert_tweet.jpg`)

## Overview
A humorous hand-drawn cartoon illustration by Michelle Wray (`michw.com` / *Deep Sea News*) depicting an underwater North Atlantic right whale using a smartphone to tweet at an approaching cargo ship (`BIGBADSHIP`) to slow down. Featured in the March 3, 2013 blog post *"MichelleW post on Whale Alert"*.

## Scene & Illustration Details
- **Surface Ship (Top-Left)**:
  - The bow and brown wheelhouse of a large reddish-brown vessel sit at the wavy blue waterline against a pale blue sky.
  - The hull is labeled `BIGBADSHIP` in black capital letters.
- **Underwater Right Whale (Bottom-Right)**:
  - A dark gray North Atlantic right whale with distinctive white callosity patches on its head is submerged in bright cyan water.
  - The whale holds a black smartphone displaying the light-blue Twitter bird logo in its flippers, with a yellow lightning-bolt signal curving upward from the phone toward a large central tablet/phone screen.
- **Central Twitter Feed Display**:
  - A large dark-blue tablet/smartphone frame sits in the center of the water, topped with a light-blue Twitter bird icon and displaying two stacked tweet cards:
  - **First Tweet**:
    - Avatar: Green square showing a dark gray whale icon.
    - Handle: `@ImaRightWhale`
    - Message text:
      ```text
      Hey @bigbadship:
      Slow it down already
      #SpeedKills
      ```
  - **Second Tweet (Reply)**:
    - Avatar: Light blue square showing a small reddish-brown ship icon.
    - Handle: `@BigBadShip`
    - Message text:
      ```text
      Okay, fine. Be
      safe, little buddy
      #savethewhales
      ```
