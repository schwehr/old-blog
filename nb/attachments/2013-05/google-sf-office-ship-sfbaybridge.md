# Small Work Boat Heading Under the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge (`google-sf-office-ship-sfbaybridge.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide, panoramic morning photograph taken from the 6th floor of the Google San Francisco office (Hills Plaza along the Embarcadero), looking east across San Francisco Bay toward the western suspension span of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge and Yerba Buena Island as a small work boat motors toward the bridge.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Vessel**:
  - In the lower-left quadrant, a small dark-hulled work boat or pilot/patrol craft is underway heading left-to-right (eastward) toward the Bay Bridge.
  - A long, straight, thin white wake trails behind the vessel to the left edge of the frame across the calm, dark blue-gray surface of the bay.
- **Bridge & Landmarks**:
  - The western suspension span of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge enters from the upper right and recedes diagonally toward the center-left, supported by three visible X-braced steel suspension towers and the central concrete anchorage pier.
  - Beyond the bridge spans, the wooded silhouette of Yerba Buena Island and the lower flat profile of Treasure Island to its left sit against the hazy morning horizon.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**:
  - Bright morning sunlight from the right casts a soft haze over the water and creates a pale glare beneath the right-hand bridge span, while the upper sky transitions from clear blue at the top left to bright white-blue toward the sun on the right.
