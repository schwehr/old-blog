# Alcatraz Cruises *Hornblower Hybrid* Ferry (`hornblower-hybrid.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph of the *Hornblower Hybrid* passenger catamaran ferry docked in San Francisco Bay (taken during an April 2013 trip to Angel Island and Alcatraz), showcasing its distinctive rooftop vertical-axis wind turbines and solar array alongside a second Alcatraz Cruises ferry in the background.

## Vessel & Scene Details
- **Foreground Vessel (*Hornblower Hybrid*)**:
  - A multi-deck catamaran ferry with a black lower hull and a white superstructure angled toward the lower right.
  - **Markings & Logos**:
    - `"HORNBLOWER HYBRID"` is painted in bold black capital letters along the white bow quarter above the black hull.
    - The oval orange, blue, and white `"ALCATRAZ CRUISES"` logo is displayed prominently centered on the forward face of the upper deckhouse between the two tiers of forward-slanted dark windows.
    - A stylized vertical `"H"` logo (composed of small solar/grid squares) is painted on the starboard side of the upper cabin.
  - **Wind & Solar Power Equipment**:
    - Mounted atop the roof of the pilot house are two large white helical Savonius/Darrieus-style vertical-axis wind turbines (one on the port side and one on the starboard side), flanking a central mast with radar, GPS/navigation antennas, and anemometer sensors.
- **Background & Surroundings**:
  - To the left across the gray-green water of the bay, a second white-and-black passenger ferry (`"ALCATRAZ CRUISES"` visible across its upper brow) is underway or maneuvering off the pier.
  - To the right behind the *Hornblower Hybrid*, a dark brown/gray pier building and boarding gangway structure with white life rings are visible under a partly cloudy sky with bright sunlit cloud tops.
