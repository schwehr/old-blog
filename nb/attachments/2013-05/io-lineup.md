# Attendee Lineup Outside Moscone West for Google I/O 2013 (`io-lineup.jpg`)

## Overview
A street-level wide photograph showing the exterior corner of the Moscone West convention center in San Francisco (4th and Howard Streets) the day before Google I/O 2013 began, with a long line of conference attendees queued along the sidewalk beneath large Google I/O window graphics and a giant red Google Maps pin.

## Scene Description
- **Building & Conference Branding**:
  - The modern glass-and-steel facade of Moscone West fills the background.
  - Across the upper glass curtain wall on the left (and repeated on the right face around the corner), giant decals spell `"Google"` in white serif lettering next to the 2013 `"I/O"` logo composed of a translucent blue vertical rectangle (`I`), a white forward slash (`/`), and a translucent red circle (`O`).
  - Above the main ground-floor entrance doors in the center stands an oversized, three-dimensional bright red Google Maps pin marker.
  - A large rectangular black exterior display screen is mounted on steel supports at the second-floor corner of the building.
- **Crowd & Street Activity**:
  - A dense line of attendees stretches from the entrance below the red map pin all the way down the left sidewalk along the side of the building.
  - Several pedestrians are crossing the white-striped crosswalk in the foreground, while three vehicles—a brown sedan, a yellow-and-white taxi SUV, and an orange crossover SUV—wait or pass along the street on the right.
