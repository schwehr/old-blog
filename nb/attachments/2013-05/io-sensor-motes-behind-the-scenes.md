# Data Sensing Lab Sensor Motes Behind the Scenes at Google I/O 2013 (`io-sensor-motes-behind-the-scenes.jpg`)

## Overview
An indoor behind-the-scenes photograph taken during setup at Google I/O 2013 showing rows of custom wireless environmental sensor nodes ("sensor motes") mounted on folded white informational tent cards, staged on a counter prior to being deployed across the three floors of Moscone West by the Google Cloud / Data Sensing Lab team.

## Scene Description
- **Sensor Motes & Display Cards**:
  - Approximately seven folded, A-frame white cardboard/plastic tent signs are lined up in two staggered rows along a narrow white counter.
  - Attached to the left side of each tent card (over a blue rectangular backing strip) is a custom yellow/cream printed circuit board (PCB) stacked on an Arduino-compatible microcontroller base, equipped with a wireless radio module (XBee/ZigBee style) at the bottom, surface-mount components, and a protruding cylindrical metal gas/air-quality sensor on the rightmost board.
  - Coiled black power cables are tucked inside the base of each folded tent sign, threaded through circular grommet holes near the bottom corner.
- **Visible Text on Cards**:
  - The headings on the white cards read `"Hi, I'm a sensor mote."` or `"Don't mind me, I'm only a sensor mote."` in gray sans-serif type, followed by smaller orange-red explanatory paragraphs describing how five hundred sensor motes around the venue continuously monitor temperature, humidity, pressure, light, air quality, and audio noise levels and stream the data to the Google Cloud Platform.
  - Small white rectangular asset/ID labels are affixed at the top of each blue mounting strip above the circuit boards.
- **Background**:
  - A long yellow plastic cable protector ramp rests across the tops of several cards.
  - In the background inside the convention hall, a woman with dark hair sits behind a blue-and-white counter next to water bottles, coffee cups, cardboard boxes, and wall-mounted flat-screen monitors.
