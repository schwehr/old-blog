# NOAA Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (SBNMS) Acknowledgment Graphic (`io-thanks-noaa-sbnms.png`)

## Overview
A composite slide/acknowledgment graphic from Kurt Schwehr's Google I/O 2013 presentation ("All the Ships in the World: Visualizing Data with Google Cloud and Maps") thanking the NOAA Stellwagen Bank National Marine Sanctuary (SBNMS) for providing AIS vessel-tracking data. It pairs a satellite map of eastern Massachusetts and Cape Cod Bay with the official NOAA emblem.

## Visual Elements
- **Left Panel (Satellite Map with Receiver Pins)**:
  - A Google Earth / Maps satellite view showing the eastern coastline of Massachusetts, including Cape Ann at the top, Boston Harbor near the upper-left curve, the South Shore, Cape Cod's hooked peninsula extending into the Atlantic Ocean, and Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket at the bottom.
  - Bathymetric relief of Massachusetts Bay, Cape Cod Bay, and Stellwagen Bank is visible in shaded blue tones offshore.
  - Three pink teardrop map pins mark coastal AIS receiver station locations:
    1. **Northern pin**: Cape Ann / Gloucester area.
    2. **Middle pin**: Scituate / Marshfield along the South Shore.
    3. **Eastern pin**: Provincetown at the northern tip of Cape Cod.
- **Right Panel (NOAA Logo)**:
  - Set on a white background is the circular emblem of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration: a white seabird silhouette soaring between dark navy blue sky (with `"NOAA"` in bold white stencil letters) and light cyan-blue ocean waves, encircled by blue text reading `"NATIONAL OCEANIC AND ATMOSPHERIC ADMINISTRATION"` along the top rim and `"U.S. DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE"` along the bottom rim.
