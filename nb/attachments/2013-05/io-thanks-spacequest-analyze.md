# SpaceQuest and Analyze Corporation Acknowledgment Graphic (`io-thanks-spacequest-analyze.png`)

## Overview
A composite slide/acknowledgment graphic from Kurt Schwehr's Google I/O 2013 presentation ("All the Ships in the World: Visualizing Data with Google Cloud and Maps") thanking SpaceQuest and Analyze Corporation for providing global satellite AIS vessel-tracking data. It displays a photograph of a SpaceQuest microsatellite on the left and the corporate logos for SpaceQuest and Analyze on the right against a white background.

## Visual Elements
- **Left Side (SpaceQuest Microsatellite)**:
  - A cutout studio photograph of a cube-shaped SpaceQuest microsatellite (such as AprizeSat) viewed from an isometric corner angle.
  - The aluminum frame is covered on its side panels with grids of dark black rectangular solar cells and small rectangular yellow identification/warning plaques centered on the two visible side faces.
  - The top deck features smaller solar cell patches, gold/yellow Kapton-style trim around a central vertical whip antenna, a white cylindrical patch antenna disk on the upper-left corner, and small corner-mounted antennas/fasteners.
- **Right Side (Company Logos)**:
  - **Top Right (`SpaceQuest`)**: The word `"SpaceQuest"` in bold black italic serif/sans-serif lettering below a thin black orbital arc that curves up and to the right, with a small red 3D cube icon positioned along the upper-right trajectory.
  - **Bottom Right (`Analyze`)**: The word `"Analyze"` in bold medium-blue sans-serif lettering, preceded on the left by a connected 3D blue ball-and-stick line-graph icon of six spheres rising toward the wordmark.
