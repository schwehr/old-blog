# Navigation Range Markers and Tour Boat *Heritage* in Portsmouth, NH (`nav-aids-portsmouth-nh.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken on the Piscataqua River in Portsmouth Harbor, New Hampshire, showing the tour boat *Heritage* ("Portsmouth Harbor Cruises") motoring past a wooded shoreline where a steel navigation range tower with two bright orange-and-white dayboards rises among the trees near the Peirce Island wastewater treatment plant.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground Vessel (*Heritage*)**:
  - In the lower-left and center-left, a classic white-hulled, single-deck wooden/fiberglass passenger tour boat is underway moving right-to-left, leaving a frothy white wake astern to the right.
  - `"HERITAGE"` is painted in dark capital letters on the port bow, and a long signboard atop the cabin roof reads `"PORTSMOUTH HARBOR CRUISES"`.
  - Dozens of passengers are seated and standing along the open bow, side decks, and stern beneath the cabin canopy, with an American flag flying from the stern flagpole.
- **Shoreline & Navigation Aid**:
  - Rising above the dense green deciduous trees on the rocky shoreline in the upper-right quadrant is a galvanized steel lattice skeleton tower supporting two vertically stacked rectangular range dayboards—each bright international orange with a central vertical white stripe—and range lights mounted above them.
  - To the left of the trees, a utility pole, a white domed tank/structure, and a fenced concrete facility (part of the Peirce Island sewage treatment plant) are visible on a grassy slope under a partly cloudy sky.
