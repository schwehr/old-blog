# Webcam Capture of Rascal the Cat Napping (`rascal-nap.gif`)

## Overview
A low-resolution wireless webcam image (animated/time-lapse GIF) captured inside a room in December 2010 and shared in a May 18, 2013 blog post ("Cat nap"), showing the author's black cat, Rascal, walking through an open doorway toward a cushioned cat bed in the foreground.

## Scene Description
- **Timestamp**:
  - Across the top-left corner, a pixelated black-on-gray digital camera timestamp reads `2010/12/10 09:58:00` (with subsequent frames in the GIF advancing through the morning/afternoon as the cat naps on the cushion).
- **Foreground & Cat**:
  - In the lower-left corner, a black cat (Rascal) is seen in profile facing right with one front paw extended forward, emerging from behind a gray piece of furniture and an ajar white paneled interior door.
  - Dominating the lower-center and lower-right foreground is a dark brown tufted rectangular pet cushion resting atop a light-blue-and-white patterned fabric cover.
- **Background Room Details**:
  - Through the open white door on the upper left, a dark trash bin sits against a white wall.
  - On the right side of the room, a metal wireframe shelving unit holds stacked papers, books, and boxes, with a glowing green LED indicator light near the baseboard and a rectangular cardboard cat scratcher pad lying flat on the beige carpet in front of the shelves.
