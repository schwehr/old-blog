# Tug and Barge Heading Under the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge (`sf-bay-bridge-tug-and-barge.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide, panoramic photograph taken from the Google San Francisco office looking east across San Francisco Bay toward the western suspension span of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge and Yerba Buena Island, capturing a tugboat towing a flat barge on a long hawser alongside two high-speed passenger ferries in the distance.

## Scene Description
- **Vessels on the Bay**:
  - **Tug and Barge (Center to Right)**: In the lower right, just in front of the nearest bridge tower pier, a compact tugboat is underway heading left-to-right (eastward) toward the Bay Bridge span. Trailing a long distance behind it on a towline (near the lower center of the frame) is a low-profile dark flat deck barge.
  - **Ferries (Left)**: Near the left horizon, two white passenger ferries are traveling left-to-right in tandem, each kicking up a distinct white foamy wake across the gray-blue bay water.
- **Bridge & Landmarks**:
  - On the right, the steel suspension towers (with characteristic X-bracing) and central concrete anchorage of the western Bay Bridge span stretch northward to the dark, tree-covered hill of Yerba Buena Island and the flat shoreline of Treasure Island extending to the left.
  - The sky is covered in soft, hazy high clouds with bright sunlight illuminating the water and bridge from the right.
