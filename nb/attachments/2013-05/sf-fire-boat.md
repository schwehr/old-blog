# San Francisco Fire Department Fireboat *Guardian* Under the Bay Bridge (`sf-fire-boat.jpg`)

## Overview
A bright afternoon photograph of the San Francisco Fire Department (SFFD) fireboat *Guardian* (Fireboat No. 2) moored alongside a wooden pier on the San Francisco Embarcadero directly beneath the western suspension span of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge.

## Scene Description
- **Fireboat (*Guardian*)**:
  - Positioned in the lower center, angled slightly toward the right, is a classic red-and-white fireboat with a dark red hull, red main deckhouse, white pilothouse, and a tall white-and-red central water-monitor tower topped by a brass/red deluge gun.
  - Additional red water cannons (monitors) are mounted on the bow deck and aft superstructure, accompanied by radar domes, antennas, life rings, and blue equipment covers.
  - White lettering along the port hull side reads `"SAN FRANCISCO FIRE DEPT."` amidships and `"GUARDIAN FIREBOAT NO. 2"` near the bow, while `"SFFD"` is painted in red on the side of the white pilothouse below the bridge windows.
- **Pier & Bay Bridge**:
  - To the right of the fireboat is a single-story white pier shed with a tan pitched roof supported on dark wooden pilings (Pier 22½, SFFD Station 35).
  - Towering overhead from left to right across a clear, deep blue sky is the steel double-deck truss roadway, vertical suspender cables, main suspension cables, and massive X-braced steel tower (Pier W2) of the San Francisco–Oakland Bay Bridge, resting on its concrete fender pier in the left background.
