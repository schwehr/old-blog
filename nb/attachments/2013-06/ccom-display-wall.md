# CCOM VisLab Panoramic Display Wall (`ccom-display-wall.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a Facebook photo post from the Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center (CCOM/JHC) at the University of New Hampshire, showing a five-panel curved panoramic LCD display wall in their Visualization Lab (VisLab) rendering 3D global bathymetry.

## Scene & Hardware Description
- **Display Array**:
  - Five large flat-panel monitors mounted vertically (in portrait orientation) side by side, angled slightly inward to form a continuous, curved panoramic display wall.
  - Each monitor is supported by an extruded aluminum T-slot floor stand with dual horizontal feet resting on a blue speckled laboratory floor.
  - **Displayed Visualization**: Across all five screens is a continuous 3D perspective rendering of North Atlantic and Arctic ocean bathymetry and surrounding landmasses (including Greenland on the left in white and brown, Iceland, and the Mid-Atlantic Ridge running down the center in shades of light blue, cyan, and deep purple). Near the center screen (third panel from left), a 3D vessel model is shown at the surface with a vertical line extending down to the seafloor and numeric coordinate/depth labels (`25000.0`, `30000.0`).
- **Room Environment**:
  - White drop-ceiling tiles with recessed lighting and ceiling-mounted track lights above.
  - On the far left foreground, a small desk edge holds electronics and control knobs; on the far right foreground, a red plastic bin sits on the floor with small boxes on top.
