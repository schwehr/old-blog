# Google Earth World Oceans Day Google+ Post (`google-ocean-plus-post-world-ocean-day.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a Google+ post by the official **Google Earth** page celebrating World Oceans Day (June 2013) by previewing high-resolution ocean bathymetry updates created in partnership with NOAA's National Geophysical Data Center (NGDC) and the University of Colorado CIRES program.
