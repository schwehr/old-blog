# Newspaper Clipping: "TROV searches for treasures" (`trov-1995-tahoe-news-paper.jpg`)

## Overview
A scanned black-and-white newspaper clipping dated Sunday, September 10, 1995, featuring an Associated Press article and photograph about NASA Ames Research Center's Telepresence Controlled Remotely Operated Vehicle (TROV) mission in Mono Lake, California, led by Dr. Carol Stoker and paleontologist Jack Farmer.

## Visual Elements & Photograph
- **Photograph**:
  - On the left/center sits the **TROV** underwater robot resting on a flat block or table inside its tubular metal protective frame. A dark cylindrical thruster/housing on the upper left side of the vehicle is stenciled with white lettering reading **`NASA / MARS`**. Forward-mounted lights, cameras, and thrusters are visible inside the cage.
  - On the right stands **Dr. Carol Stoker**, dressed in dark clothing, wearing a black helmet fitted with a bulky white stereoscopic head-mounted video display (VR visor) over her eyes and holding a small rectangular control box in her hands.
