# Research Houseboat on Mono Lake during 1995 TROV Expedition (`trov-house-boat-mono-lake-1995.jpg`)

## Overview
A 1995 color photograph showing the houseboat used as a floating operations base on Mono Lake, California, during the NASA Ames Telepresence-ROV (TROV) expedition, with the snow-dusted Sierra Nevada mountains in the background.

## Scene Description
- **Vessel (Houseboat)**:
  - Shown in starboard profile floating on dark, choppy water with small whitecaps.
  - The vessel has a dark black hull with a blue lower cabin stripe and a white upper cabin structure.
  - Along the side of the white cabin are two small horizontal rectangular windows stacked vertically toward the stern (left), a square sign reading **`Digger Bay`** in the middle, and two larger curtained windows toward the bow (right), which features a covered front porch deck.
  - A person stands on the aft deck at the far left, holding onto a vertical ladder leading to the roof.
  - On the flat roof of the houseboat sit a wooden A-frame structure near the stern, white storage barrels/tanks, life jackets/gear, and a white canoe or skiff stowed upside-down/horizontally.
- **Background**:
  - To the right beyond the bow rise dark volcanic hills and the steep, snow-capped peaks of the Eastern Sierra Nevada under a dramatic, moody sky filled with dark gray clouds.
