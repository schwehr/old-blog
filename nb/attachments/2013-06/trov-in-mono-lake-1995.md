# TROV Submerged in Mono Lake (`trov-in-mono-lake-1995.jpg`)

## Overview
A vertical color photograph from 1995 showing the NASA Ames Telepresence Remotely Operated Vehicle (TROV) floating just below the calm, deep-blue surface of Mono Lake, California, with the snow-capped Sierra Nevada mountain range reflected in the distance.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (TROV Underwater)**:
  - In the lower third of the frame, submerged just beneath the dark, clear water surface, is the rectangular tubular frame of the TROV.
  - Bright red/orange syntactic foam buoyancy blocks occupy the top of the ROV, while its dark green/black chassis and forward components are visible below.
  - A yellow-and-white tether cable extends from the top of the ROV toward the right edge of the frame.
- **Midground (Mono Lake & Shoreline)**:
  - Rippled, glassy blue water reflects the sky and mountains across the middle of the image.
  - Across the lake lies a sunlit tan and white shoreline of Paoha Island or the western shore.
- **Background (Sierra Nevada)**:
  - Rising steeply behind the sunlit shore are the rugged, purple-gray peaks of the Eastern Sierra Nevada, topped with patches of white snow under a blue sky with scattered white clouds.
