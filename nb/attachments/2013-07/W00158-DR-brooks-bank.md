# 3D Bathymetric Perspective of Brooks Bank from NOAA Survey W00158 (`W00158-DR-brooks-bank.png`)

## Overview
A 3D shaded-relief bathymetric block diagram from the Descriptive Report (DR) for NOAA hydrographic survey `W00158` (conducted by the *R/V AHI* in the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands / Brooks Bank), illustrating a flat-topped submarine bank (guyot) with terraces, a submarine canyon, and possible slump blocks.

## Map / Plot Details
- **3D Surface & Color Scale**:
  - Displays a steep-sided, flat-topped seamount/bank rising from a deep magenta seafloor (`-600` m) to a flat orange summit plateau (`-50` to `-100` m).
  - **Color Bar (`BATHYMETRY (m)`)**: Positioned in the upper right, ranging from `-600` (magenta/purple) through `-550`, `-500`, `-450` (dark blue), `-400`, `-350` (cyan), `-300`, `-250` (green), `-200`, `-150` (yellow), to `-100` and `-50` (orange).
- **Axes & Coordinates**:
  - **Vertical Axis (Left)**: Labeled `Depth (m)` with ticks from `-600` to `0` (labeled at `-600`, `-400`, `-200`, `0`).
  - **Bottom Axis (Latitude)**: Ticks labeled from left to right: `23°55' 48"N`, `23°57' 00"N`, `23°58' 12"N`, `23°59' 24"N`, `24°00' 36"N`, `24°01' 48"N`, and `24°03' 00"N`.
  - **Right Axis (Longitude)**: Ticks labeled from front-right to back-right: `166°37' 48"W`, `166°40' 12"W`, `166°42' 36"W`, and `166°45' 00"W`.
  - **North Arrow**: A black arrow labeled `N` near the bottom-right corner pointing to the right (along the latitude axis toward higher northern latitudes).
- **Geological Annotations**:
  - **`terraces`**: Upper left label with two black arrows pointing to stepped concentric ledges around the orange/yellow upper rim of the bank.
  - **`canyon`**: Upper right label with a black arrow pointing to a steep vertical gully incised into the green/yellow flank of the bank.
  - **`slump blocks ?`**: Lower left label with a black arrow pointing to hummocky, blocky debris along the cyan/blue mid-slope at roughly `-300` to `-350` m depth.
