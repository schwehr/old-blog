# David Gallo Presenting at Ocean Exploration 2020 (`david-gallo-whoi-orig.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken inside the auditorium at the Aquarium of the Pacific in Long Beach, California, during the July 2013 **Ocean Exploration 2020** national forum, showing Dr. David Gallo of the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution (WHOI) speaking at the podium in front of a projected slide about ocean science.
