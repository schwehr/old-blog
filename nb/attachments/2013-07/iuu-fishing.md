# Cover Graphic: IUU Fishing Roundtable Notes (`iuu-fishing.png`)

## Overview
A title-page graphic from the summary of proceedings for the **IUU (Illegal, Unreported, and Unregulated) Fishing Roundtable** hosted by Analyze Corporation on June 19, 2013, featuring the report title inside a blue semicircular arc surrounded by the logos of nine participating organizations.
