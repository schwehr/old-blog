# Jenifer Austin Foulkes Presenting Google Ocean at Ocean Exploration 2020 (`jaustin-google-ocean.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph from the July 2013 **Ocean Exploration 2020** workshop at the Aquarium of the Pacific in Long Beach, California, showing Google Ocean manager **Jenifer Austin Foulkes** speaking at the podium while presenting a slide on embedding the Google Maps Street View API with underwater coral reef imagery from the Catlin Seaview Survey / Terramar Project.

## Scene Description
- **Speaker & Podium (Bottom Left)**:
  - **Jenifer Austin Foulkes** stands illuminated at the dark Aquarium of the Pacific lectern, wearing a pink/red top over white and speaking into the podium microphone.
  - The podium front bears the white wave emblem and gold text **`AQUARIUM OF THE PACIFIC`** above the **`OCEAN EXPLORATION 2020`** sign.
- **Projected Slide (Center)**:
  - **Banner Heading**: Dark gray text on a white header bar reading:
    **`Embed Street View API on your own website.`**
  - **Main Screenshot**: A webpage from **`THE TERRAMAR PROJECT`** (`THE DAILY CATCH` / `SPECIES FINDER`) displaying an interactive 360-degree underwater Street View panorama of a sunlit coral reef with a sea turtle swimming over coral heads in clear blue water.
  - Street View pan/zoom controls are visible in the upper-left corner of the underwater viewport, and a vertical column of thumbnail images runs down the right edge of the embedded viewer.
- **Graphic Recording Board & Audience (Bottom Right)**:
