# Panoramic View of Rainbow Harbor in Long Beach, California (`long-beach-ca.jpg`)

## Overview
A wide horizontal panoramic photograph of Rainbow Harbor in Long Beach, California, taken on an overcast day during the July 2013 Ocean Exploration 2020 meeting near the Aquarium of the Pacific.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground (Wooden Docks & Water)**:
  - Calm, glassy gray-blue harbor water fills the center of the panorama, reflecting the buildings and sky above.
  - Wooden floating docks angle inward from the bottom left, bottom center (marked with an orange vertical pylon post), and bottom right, where a person in dark clothing is leaning over the dock edge near a yellow dock box.
- **Left Background (Downtown & Tour Boats)**:
  - Palm trees line the waterfront on the far left behind a white multi-deck passenger excursion vessel moored at a pier, with a teal-green mid-rise commercial building rising behind it.
- **Center Background (Shoreline Village)**:
  - Across the harbor sits the colorful red-roofed waterfront complex of Shoreline Village, featuring a red-and-white cupola tower in the center, a taller peaked tower to the right, and a marina filled with sailboat masts to the left.
  - In the distant gap to the right of Shoreline Village, the hulls and superstructure/funnels of the *Queen Mary* and cruise/port infrastructure are faintly visible across the outer harbor.
- **Right Background (Lions Lighthouse for Sight)**:
  - On the right side, a rock-armored breakwater peninsula curves into the harbor, topped by a grassy green hill with the white tower of the **Lions Lighthouse for Sight** and a small cluster of palm trees under a uniform white/light-gray cloud cover.
