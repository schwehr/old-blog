# Michael T. Jones Presenting "Elephants in the Room" at Ocean Exploration 2020 (`oe2020-mtj-elephants-in-the-room.jpg`)

## Overview
A photograph taken during the July 2013 **Ocean Exploration 2020** meeting at the Aquarium of the Pacific in Long Beach, California, showing Google Chief Technology Advocate **Michael T. Jones (MTJ)** speaking at the podium in front of a slide comparing spatial imaging resolutions using aerial photography of an elephant herd alongside satellite and sonar/map insets.
