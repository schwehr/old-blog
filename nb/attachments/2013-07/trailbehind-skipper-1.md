# TrailBehind Skipper App — Instruments and San Francisco Bay Chart (`trailbehind-skipper-1.png`)

## Overview
An iPad screenshot of TrailBehind's Skipper iOS charting app in landscape orientation, displaying the `Instruments` sidebar, GPS accuracy popover, and a NOAA nautical chart of the central California coast around San Francisco Bay and Monterey Bay.

## Left Sidebar (`Instruments`)
The left third of the screen is occupied by the `Instruments` panel:
- **Top Bar:** Dark brown header titled `Instruments` with a blue satellite icon button in the upper-left corner.
- **GPS Popover:** Triggered from the satellite icon, a modal box shows a horizontal color-gradient bar (red to yellow to green, filled to the green mark) and text reading:
  - `GPS Accuracy: ±229 ft`
  - `37° 24' 23.27" N, 121° 56' 15.34" W`
- **Compass Rose:** A circular marine compass rose oriented to `126°SE` (displayed in white text inside a blue center rectangle), with the red `N` marker rotated to the lower-left.
- **Action Buttons:** Two grouped white list buttons with right chevrons:
  - Group 1: `Record Track`, `Mark Waypoint`
  - Group 2: `Saved Tracks`, `Saved Waypoints`
- **Bottom Tab Bar:** Four icons at the bottom of the left panel: `Instruments` (selected, white compass icon), `Weather` (sun/cloud icon), `Route` (waypoints path icon), and `Settings` (gear icon).

## Map View & Controls
The right two-thirds of the screen shows a seamless mosaic of NOAA raster nautical charts overlaid on a satellite/terrain basemap of Northern/Central California:
- **Geographic Features & Labels:** Shows the coast from `Pt Arena` and `Bodega Head` down past `Pt Reyes`, `Pt Bonita`, `SAN FRANCISCO`, `Farallon Is`, `Pt Montara`, `Pigeon Pt`, `Santa Cruz`, `Monterey Bay`, `Pt Pinos`, and `Pt Sur`. Offshore features include `Arena Canyon`, `Bodega Canyon`, `Cordell Bank`, `Pioneer Seamount`, `Guide Seamount`, `Monterey Canyon`, Loran-C lines, and weather buoys (`ODAS "46014"`, `ODAS "46013"`, `ODAS "46012"`, `ODAS "46042"`). Inland cities on the satellite basemap include `Sacramento`, `Elk Grove`, `Stockton`, and `Modesto`.
- **Current Position Marker:** An orange navigation arrowhead icon is positioned on the San Francisco Peninsula near Palo Alto/Mountain View (`37° 24' N, 121° 56' W`).
- **Map Overlays & Controls:**
  - Upper left: Layers stack icon.
  - Upper center: Digital compass tape showing `120` to `150`.
  - Upper right: Search bar (`Search`), target/locate icon, `End Pro` button with a red `X`, and a blue loading spinner icon.
  - Left edge: Teal collapse tab with a left-pointing triangle.
  - Bottom left of map: `50 Nm` scale bar.
  - Bottom telemetry bar: Dark translucent bar with a collapse arrow at top center, displaying `Press and hold to set stats`, `0.0 knots Current Speed`, `0.0 knots Average Speed`, `-1°N Course`, and `0.00 ft Distance`.
