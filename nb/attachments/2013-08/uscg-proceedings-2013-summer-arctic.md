## Overview
A cropped cover image of the Summer 2013 issue of *Proceedings of the Marine Safety & Security Council*, the official journal of the United States Coast Guard, themed `"The Arctic: Emerging frontier, new opportunities"`.

The cover photograph depicts a towering, vertical white glacial ice cliff (tidewater glacier or tabular iceberg) rising sharply out of calm, glassy polar waters under a clear deep-blue sky. Small floating ice chunks (bergy bits and brash ice) drift in the foreground water, which mirrors the vertical striations and shadows of the ice wall.
