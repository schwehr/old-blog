# NOAA Press Release for Great Lakes ERMA (`erma-great-lakes-press.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of a NOAA press release webpage dated September 9, 2013, titled `"NOAA launches new online mapping tool for Great Lakes region"`, announcing the launch of the Great Lakes Environmental Response Management Application (`ERMA®`).

## Article Text & Media
- **Headline:** `NOAA launches new online mapping tool for Great Lakes region`
- **Dateline:** `September 9, 2013`
- **Body Text:** Explains that `NOAA's Office of Response and Restoration` launched the `Great Lakes Environmental Response Management Application (ERMA®)` tool integrating static and real-time data from `NOAA's Great Lakes Query Manager database` to assist with cleanup of contaminated sediment and restoration in `Areas of Concern` across the Great Lakes Basin (from Minnesota to New York and Ontario to Quebec). Credits collaboration with the `EPA`, `U.S. Coast Guard`, and `University of New Hampshire` under the `Great Lakes Restoration Initiative`.
- **Inset Photograph (Upper Right):** Shows two environmental scientists standing knee-deep in a river wearing waders, life vests, and hard hats (one white, one green). The scientist on the left holds a yellow `SUPER PAIL` bucket while the scientist on the right uses a long-handled net next to an orange traffic cone in the water. Caption reads: `Scientists take samples that can inform cleanup and restoration actions for the Kalamazoo River in Michigan. High resolution (Credit: NOAA)`.
- **Inset Screenshot (Lower Right):** Partially visible screenshot of the Great Lakes ERMA web mapping interface showing the Great Lakes basin dotted with orange and red markers.
