# Great Lakes ERMA Web Mapping Application (`great-lakes-erma.png`)

## Overview
A screenshot of NOAA's Great Lakes Environmental Response Management Application (`ERMA`) web GIS interface, displaying Areas of Concern (AOCs), sediment chemistry stations, and watershed boundaries across the Great Lakes Basin.

## Header & Footer Chrome
- **Top Banner:** On the left, `ERMA` in large grey serif letters next to a vertical divider and `Environmental Response Management Application / Great Lakes`. The right side of the banner shows a scenic coastal photograph of blue water and distant tree-covered islands.
- **Bottom Status & Attribution Bar:**
  - Upper strip: `↑N`, `Scale: 1: 7M`, `Zoom Level: 6`, `Location: 46.18562°, -81.33702°`.
  - Lower blue-gray strip: Left side shows the NOAA emblem and links `US DOC | NOAA | NOS | NOAA Office of Response & Restoration / Disclaimer | Privacy policy | Email comments`. Right side reads `Coastal Response Research Center / ©2007 - 2013 University of New Hampshire`.

## Map View (Left Pane)
A satellite basemap (`Imagery ©2013 TerraMetrics`) of the Great Lakes region (Lakes Superior, Michigan, Huron, Erie, and Ontario) with state/provincial outlines and a thick grey-white boundary delineating the Great Lakes Watershed:
- **Sediment Chemistry Stations:** Hundreds of small brown square markers (`Sediment`) densely line the shorelines, rivers, and tributaries throughout the basin, as well as along the Mississippi/Illinois river system to the southwest.
- **Labeled Areas of Concern (Yellow Text Labels & Colored Point Symbols):**
  - **Lake Superior:** `Thunder Bay` (red circle), `Nipigon Bay` (red circle), `Jackfish Bay` (orange triangle), `Peninsula Harbour` (red circle), `St. Louis River and Bay` (blue square), `Torch Lake` (blue square), `Deer Lake` (blue square).
  - **Lake Michigan:** `Manistique River` (blue square), `Menominee River` (blue square), `Fox River/Southern Green Bay` (blue square), `Sheboygan River` (blue square), `Milwaukee Estuary` (blue square), `Waukegan Harbor` (blue square), `Grand Calumet River` (blue square), `Kalamazoo River` (blue square), `Muskegon Lake` (blue square), `White Lake` (blue square).
  - **Lake Huron & Connecting Channels:** `St. Marys River` (green diamond), `Spanish River Mouth` (orange triangle), `Severn Sound` (red star), `Collingwood Harbour` (red star), `Saginaw River and Bay` (blue square).
  - **Lake Erie & St. Clair:** `Clinton River`, `Rouge River`, `Detroit River`, `River Raisin`, `Maumee River`, `Black River`, `Cuyahoga River`, `Ashtabula River`, `Presque Isle Bay` (blue star).
  - **Lake Ontario & St. Lawrence:** `Buffalo River`, `Eighteen Mile Creek`, `Rochester Embayment`, `Oswego River` (blue star), `Hamilton Harbour` (red circle), `Metro Toronto` (red circle), `Port Hope` (red circle), `Bay of Quinte` (red circle), `St. Lawrence River` (green diamond).
