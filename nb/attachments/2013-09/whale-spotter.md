# Whale Spotter (Spotter Pro) iOS App Screenshots (`whale-spotter.png`)

## Overview
A promotional cover-flow graphic on a dark charcoal gradient background with bottom mirror reflections, showcasing five iPhone screenshots of the `Whale Spotter` (`Spotter Pro`) marine mammal field data capture app developed by Conserve.IO in partnership with Point Blue Conservation Science and NOAA National Marine Sanctuaries.

Displayed flat and front-facing in the center is the `Sighting Details` data entry screen:
- **Status & Navigation Bar:** iOS status bar showing `Carrier`, Wi-Fi icon, `9:17 AM`, location arrow, and battery icon. Below is a blue navigation bar with a `Cancel` button on the left, title `Sighting Details` in the center, and `Save` button on the right.
- **Form Fields (on a light grey pinstripe background):**
  - `Bearing (degrees)` heading above a white rounded row with a slider from `0` to `360` set near the left and blue value `66` on the right.
  - `Distance (meters)` (red label): `400`
  - `Latitude` (red label): `37.340916`
  - `Longitude` (red label): `-122.023453`
  - `Photos Taken?` (red label): iOS toggle switch set to `YES` (blue).
  - `Calf Present?` (red label): iOS toggle switch set to `NO` (grey).
  - `Birds Present?` (red label): iOS toggle switch set to `YES` (blue).
