# Research Vessel Antenna Farm Diagram (`antenna-farm.png`)

## Overview
This image is an annotated slide from a presentation by Dale Chayes (Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory, Columbia University) titled *Geophysical Data: "Real data has real warts"*. It shows a dense array of navigation, communication, and meteorological sensors and antennas ("antenna farm") mounted on the mast and upper deck structure of a research vessel, complete with red callout circles, arrows, and labels identifying each instrument.

## Scene Description
- **Main Photograph**: A view looking up at a white steel H-frame mast and platform structure atop a vessel's superstructure against an overcast gray sky. Multiple vertical poles with blue-painted upper sections support various GPS, satellite, and meteorological sensors along the crossbar and vertical masts. A student wearing a safety harness is standing on the platform inside the safety railing next to the right-hand mast support.
- **Bottom-Left Inset Photograph**: A secondary angle taken under a clearer blue sky showing two people on the deck and platform structure—one standing by the railing in a dark sweater and jeans, and a student in a climbing harness higher up on the white mast frame.

## Visible Text & Labels
Boxed black text labels around the perimeter point via red arrows to red ovals encircling specific antennas and sensors:
- **Top Left**:
  - `ADU5(4)` — Points to a top-mounted GPS attitude antenna on the left vertical pole.
  - `L-Band` — Points to a whip antenna along the lower-center section of the crossbar.
  - `MX512` — Points to a differential GPS receiver antenna mounted on the far-left vertical pole.
  - `AIS` — Points to two whip antennas (one on the left mast in the main photo and one in the lower-left inset photo).
  - `Met` — Points to meteorological sensors mounted along the lower-left pole in both the main photo and the bottom-left inset.
- **Top Center**:
  - `Time` — Points to a small white timing antenna mounted on a bracket near the left-center of the crossbar.
  - `P-Code` — Points to a dark puck-shaped military/precise GPS antenna on the center of the crossbar.
  - `AG132` — Points to a yellow Trimble AG132 DGPS antenna mounted on the center of the crossbar.
- **Top Right**:
  - `Long Wave Radiation` / `Short Wave Radiation` — Points to radiometer sensors mounted near the right-center of the crossbar.
  - `PAR` — Points to a Photosynthetically Active Radiation sensor beside the radiometers.
  - `CNav (2)` — Points to a C-Nav satellite navigation antenna atop a pole on the right side.
  - `POS/MV (2)` — Points to an Applanix POS/MV inertial/GPS dome antenna mounted below the CNav antenna on the right side.
- **Right Side Callout**:
  - `Summer Student for scale` (in large red Comic Sans text inside a rectangular box) — Two red arrows point to the harnessed student in both the main photograph and the bottom-left inset photograph.
