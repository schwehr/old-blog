# USCG Proceedings Fall 2013 Table of Contents Excerpt (`uscg-proceedings-cmsp-1.png`)

## Overview
This image is a screenshot of the masthead and the "National Ocean Policy" table of contents section from the Fall 2013 issue (Vol. 70, Number 3) of *Proceedings of the Marine Safety & Security Council*, the official journal of the United States Coast Guard. It highlights the article *"Marine Sanctuaries and Marine Planning"* co-authored by Dr. Kurt Schwehr.

## Layout & Design
- **Header**: At the top left, the title `PROCEEDINGS` is rendered in large, bold, rust-brown serif capital letters with a subtle drop shadow. At the top right is the U.S. Coast Guard racing stripe emblem (a blue stripe, thin white gap, and wide red/orange stripe bearing the white Coast Guard shield and crossed anchors).
- **Issue Metadata**: Directly below the header is `Fall 2013` aligned to the left and `Vol. 70, Number 3` aligned to the right in clean black sans-serif text.
- **Section Banner**: A horizontal orange bar spans the width of the table of contents with the centered white heading `National Ocean Policy`, bordered on the left by a thin vertical orange rule extending down beside the article listings.
