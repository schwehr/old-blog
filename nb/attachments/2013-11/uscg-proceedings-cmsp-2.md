# USCG Proceedings Fall 2013 Cover — Marine Planning (`uscg-proceedings-cmsp-2.png`)

## Overview
This image is the front cover graphic from the Fall 2013 issue of *The Coast Guard Proceedings of the Marine Safety & Security Council*, featuring a vessel traffic density map across the U.S. East Coast, the Great Lakes, and major inland waterways alongside the issue theme: *"Marine Planning: Visions for the Future"*.

## Map & Visual Details
- **Base Map**: A light gray political map showing the outlines of the northeastern and midwestern United States and southeastern Canadian provinces.
- **AIS Vessel Traffic Density Overlay**: Ship tracks are plotted as a heatmap where low-density individual tracks appear in dark blue, moderate traffic in green and yellow, and high-density shipping lanes and port approaches in bright orange and red:
  - **Great Lakes & St. Lawrence Seaway**: Distinct orange/yellow shipping corridors traverse Lake Superior, Lake Michigan, Lake Huron, Lake Erie, Lake Ontario, and the St. Lawrence River.
  - **U.S. East Coast & Atlantic Ocean**: Dense webs of blue, green, yellow, and red vessel tracks trace coastal shipping routes, major ports (such as New York/New Jersey, Delaware Bay, Chesapeake Bay, and the Gulf of Maine), and offshore transit lanes.
  - **Inland River System**: Thin ribbons of vessel traffic follow the Mississippi, Ohio, and Illinois rivers through the central United States.
