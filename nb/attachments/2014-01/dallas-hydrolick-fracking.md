# Aerial View of Hydraulic Fracking Pads and Water Treatment Near Dallas (`dallas-hydrolick-fracking.jpg`)

## Overview
This oblique aerial photograph, taken from a commercial flight with an iPhone 4S in January 2014, looks down upon a mix of suburban, agricultural, riparian, and industrial land near the Dallas/Fort Worth area in Texas, showing rectangular cleared hydraulic fracturing (fracking) well pads alongside a major water treatment facility.

## Scene Description
- **Water Treatment Plant (Bottom Right)**: A large, sprawling industrial complex enclosed by a perimeter road, featuring dozens of circular clarifier tanks, rectangular aeration basins, settling ponds, and processing buildings.
- **Settling / Evaporation Ponds (Top Center-Right)**: A large geometric grid of dark blue and brown rectangular retention ponds bordered by dirt berms.
- **Riparian Corridor (Center)**: A winding river or creek lined with dense, dark brown winter deciduous woodland snakes from the top left through the center to the bottom center of the frame.
- **Hydraulic Fracking Pads & Cleared Rectangles (Left & Center)**: Several bright white/tan rectangular cleared earth pads dot the grassy and agricultural fields on the left and center—including a prominent white rectangular pad along the straight road just northwest of the water treatment plant, another near a dark curving pond in the green pasture at center-left, and additional pads in the upper-left quadrant.
- **Suburban Development (Right & Upper Left)**: Residential neighborhoods with curving streets and densely packed houses appear on the far right and in the upper-left corner, separated from the floodplain and industrial zones by a curving highway.
