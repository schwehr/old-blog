# Aerial View of Meandering Desert Canyon Topography (`desert-topopgraphy.jpg`)

## Overview
This oblique aerial photograph, captured from a commercial aircraft in January 2014, showcases an arid desert plateau deeply incised by a winding river canyon and dendritic side-canyon drainage networks in the American Southwest (Colorado Plateau region).

## Scene Description
- **Meandering River Canyon (Center)**: A deeply carved, sinuous canyon winds from the top center toward the bottom right. Because of the low winter sun angle, the steep canyon walls cast deep blue-black shadows across the canyon floor, creating the appearance of a dark blue ribbon winding through the desert.
- **Tributary Side Canyons**: To the left of the main channel, a prominent side canyon joins from the west, also filled with dark blue shadow. Finer dendritic washes and gullies branch across the upper-center and right plateaus.
- **Geological Strata & Badlands (Lower Left & Center)**: Warm reddish-tan, salmon, and ochre sandstone mesas, cliffs, and eroded badland ridges catch the low-angle sunlight along the lower-left and central portions of the frame, contrasting with the cooler purplish-blue atmospheric haze toward the upper right.
