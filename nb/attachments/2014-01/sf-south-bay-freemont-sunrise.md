# Sunrise Over San Francisco South Bay and Fremont (`sf-south-bay-freemont-sunrise.jpg`)

## Overview
This aerial photograph, taken from a commercial flight shortly after takeoff in January 2014 using an iPhone 4S, captures dawn breaking over the East Bay hills and the city lights of Fremont and the southern San Francisco Bay Area.

## Scene Description
- **Sky & Horizon (Top Third)**: A dramatic pre-sunrise twilight sky transitions from deep slate-blue at the upper left through bands of dark gray clouds to a vivid glowing band of fiery orange, red, and golden light along the eastern horizon.
- **Silhouette of Hills (Middle Third)**: The Diablo Range and East Bay hills form a nearly pitch-black silhouette stretching across the middle of the frame below the glowing horizon line.
- **City Lights (Bottom Third)**: Across the lower foreground, the urban grid of Fremont and the South Bay sparkles with thousands of warm amber, orange, and white streetlights, residential lights, and brighter commercial corridors arranged in rectilinear and curving neighborhood patterns.
