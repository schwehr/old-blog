# Aerial View of the Sierra Nevada, Lake Tahoe, and Mono Lake in Drought (`sierras-tahoe-and-mono-lake.jpg`)

## Overview
This wide aerial photograph, taken from a commercial aircraft in late January 2014 with an iPhone 4S, looks across the Sierra Nevada mountain range toward Lake Tahoe and Mono Lake at sunrise. As noted in the accompanying blog post, the mountains—which would normally be heavily blanketed in snow by late January—are strikingly bare due to California's severe winter drought.

## Scene Description
- **Foreground & Midground (Sierra Nevada)**: Rugged granite peaks, ridges, and valleys of the Sierra Nevada stretch across the lower half of the frame. Instead of a continuous winter snowpack, the mountains appear mostly dark brown and gray-blue with only sparse, isolated patches of white snow clinging to high-elevation gullies and ridgelines.
- **Distant Lakes & Basins**:
  - **Left Midground**: A portion of Lake Tahoe / adjacent alpine lakes and valleys is faintly visible amid the dark mountain ridges on the left.
  - **Center-Left Distance**: The flat, hazy expanse of Mono Lake and the Mono Basin lies beyond the eastern escarpment of the Sierras under a thin layer of morning haze.
- **Sunrise Sky (Top Half)**: Soft pastel bands of rose-pink, peach, warm yellow, and pale blue-gray illuminate the eastern horizon above a hazy atmospheric layer over the Great Basin.
