# Aerial View of the Southern Snake Range and Great Basin National Park (`southern-snake-range.jpg`)

## Overview
This oblique aerial photograph, taken from a commercial flight in January 2014 with an iPhone 4S, shows the Southern Snake Range and Great Basin National Park in eastern Nevada near the Utah border under a clear blue sky.

## Scene Description
- **Southern Snake Range (Center)**: A prominent, elongated fault-block mountain range rises sharply from the surrounding arid valleys, running diagonally across the center of the image. The highest peaks—including Wheeler Peak—are capped with bright white snow that catches the morning sunlight, while the lower slopes are cloaked in dark blue-gray shadow and forest.
- **Basin and Range Topography (Foreground & Background)**:
  - **Foreground (Lower Left to Bottom Right)**: Darker desert ridges and alluvial fans in the foreground transition into a wide, flat, tan-gray intermontane basin (Spring Valley) to the west of the Snake Range.
  - **Background (Upper Left & Right)**: Beyond the Southern Snake Range lie the Northern Snake Range (with a snow-capped crest visible at the upper left), the broad desert floor of Snake Valley to the right, and distant parallel mountain ranges fading into the pale blue horizon across western Utah.
- **Sky (Top Third)**: A rich gradient of deep cerulean blue at the top fading to pale cyan haze along the distant horizon.
