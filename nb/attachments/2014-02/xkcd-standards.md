# xkcd Comic #927: "Standards" (`xkcd-standards.png`)

## Overview
A black-and-white three-panel webcomic strip by Randall Munroe (*xkcd* #927, titled *"Standards"*), used to illustrate a February 18, 2014 blog post titled *"A unified marine sensor logging format"* discussing the proliferation of incompatible raw data logging formats in the marine sensor and sonar communities.

## Layout & Visual Elements
- **Style**: Hand-drawn black line art and all-caps lettering on a white background, divided into three vertical rectangular panels of equal height (the middle panel is wider than the left and right panels).
- **Left Panel**: Contains only centered text describing the initial state of competing technical standards.
- **Middle Panel**: Depicts two stick-figure characters standing facing each other below their dialogue—Cueball on the left (a basic stick figure with a round head) and Ponytail on the right (a stick figure with a ponytail). Speech lines connect their heads to the text above them.
- **Right Panel**: Features a small rectangular caption box in the top-left corner and centered text matching the layout of the first panel, showing the ironic outcome.

## Visible Text & Labels
- **Header (above panels)**:
