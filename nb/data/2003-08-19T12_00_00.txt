Just met Dave Caress from MBARI.  Dave was kind enough to quickly find
and fix a bug in mbm_arc2grd for me... however, the moment was spoiled
by the UCSD Police who gave his wife a ticket while she was waiting in
the car.  Anyway, the fix is grd2xyz in the mbm_arc2grd script should
have "-H0" and not "-H6".  That's it!

Also, got a glimps of his new 3D visualization tool.  It's pretty
speedy.  One interesting component is that he doesn't let OpenGL do
the shading.  He does this himself before rendering.
