AUTHOR: Kurt

BODY:
I am now all setup for being on the MER team.  Getting a tour of the
facilities and talking to everybody has me totally fired up for this
mission.  It's going to be rockin!  The ops center is enough to knock
your socks off.  Looks like many of the lessons learned from
Pathfinder and Mars Polar Lander have been applied to setup.  One
major change from last time is a major shift in the OSes uses for the
heavy lifting during operations.  The dominant OSes in use are Linux
and Mac OS X.  I'll have my home base on a shiny new G5!  There are a
few Solaris file servers around plus the occasional MS Windows and SGI
boxes around, but they don't get that much use.  The ops center is
much more prepared for laptops this time around, so using my G4 15
inch titanium laptop will be a breeze.  Now I just have to learn me a
couple hundred acronyms and I'll be ready to go.  Woohoo!
