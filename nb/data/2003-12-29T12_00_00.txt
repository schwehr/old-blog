Arg.  Got nailed by the cold that is going around everywhere.  Almost
over it.  Bought a USB-TO-PS2 adaptor at Radio Shack after the great
products one from Fry's.  I can't believe that Fry's didn't have any
USB split keyboards.  Too many damn wireless keyboards.  They are nice
and all, but I got over the excitement when I had an IBM PCjr back in
the days.  The most important keyboard feature is not causing me pain
followed by my typing rate.  Apple has pretty pathetic keyboards.  Too
small for my hands and hard on the wrists.

The family all went to the Quail Gardens light show the night before
Christmas.  Now we can say that we did it.  They need a better
designer.  Could have been awesome, but too many white lights just
thrown on things.  The waterfall was off.  For $5 a head, it's not
really worth it.

Now to get ready for the 3rd when we land on Mars.  My sympathies for
the Beagle 2 team.  I hope it does wake up.  I sat through the waiting
with the Mars Polar Lander.  I know what that is like.
