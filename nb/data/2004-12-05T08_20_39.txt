Just installed webalizer for my site.  Very nice tool.  Thanks Myche!
The options I used are:

<pre>
   /sw/bin/webalizer -p -N 2 -D /path/to/dnscache -n schwehr.org \
      -o /path/to/webalizer/output /var/log/httpd/access_log
</pre>

Then I did a <b>contab -e</b> and put an entry like this

<pre>
  45 0 * * * /sw/bin/webalizer all my args
</pre>

Takes a while the first time to run do to all the dns lookups, but
then once it has a cache, it is much quicker.

Also, try Apple-&lsquo; to switch between windows within one application.
Works great for X11!  Combined with Apple-Tab and Shift-Apple-Right
Arrow, I'm getting pretty fast at moving around.
