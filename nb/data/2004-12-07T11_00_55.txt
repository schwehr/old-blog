I've been meaning to look this up for ages and I finally took the 30
seconds to do it.  How do you transpose stuff in emacs (ti to it)?

<pre>
  C-t     transpose 2 chars
  M-t     transpose 2 words
  C-M-t   transpose 2 expressions
  C-x C-t transpose 2 lines
</pre>
