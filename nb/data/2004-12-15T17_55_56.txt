Got a demo from <a href="http://ads.harvard.edu">ADS</a>.  This is
like ISI's Web of Science but has some cool features beyond: You can
add your abstracts if they are missing.  It's free.  You can add
comments to your paper.  Overall, very nice demo (they used my AGU
abstract for the demo.)  I talked to them about custom RSS feeds and
it sounds like they may be able to add that to the webserver.  Would
be awesome to have the latest abstracts in a news feed each morning.
I would then do a much better job of keeping up with the literature.
