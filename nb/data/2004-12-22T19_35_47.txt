Just added xtermcontrol to fink's x11 unstable tree.  Nice to not have
to lookup the special xterm escape codes.  Now it is easy to do
something like iconify a script running in a window and then
de-iconify and raise it when an error occurs.  Plus the title and
color are generally very handy.

<pre>
  xtermcontrol --title='Doh\!'
  xtermcontrol --lower
  xtermcontrol --raise
  xtermcontrol --iconify
  xtermcontrol --de-iconify
  xtermcontrol --file=FILE    # Use a config file
</pre>
