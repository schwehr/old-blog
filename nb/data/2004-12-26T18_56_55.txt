I was thinking that you could create an rss feed for each registered
user or by url with an embedded query.  For example I would like to
get a feed update once a day with any of the keywords:

  mars
  anisotropy of magnetic susceptibility
  eel river
  gaviota
  sediment waves

or with the authors

  driscoll
  johnson, c. l.
  mayer, l.
  schwehr
  tauxe

Of course, maybe you would want to limit this to say once a week for

latest paper abstracts along with slashdot, and others.  Some journals
seem to be doing this kind of thing, but ADS could be the equivalent of an
