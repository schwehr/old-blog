Here is the first little bash scriptlet that I teach people.  This
uses ImageMagick to translate images from tif to jpg.  If you want to
play with how it works, change the <b>convert</b> to <b>echo</b>.

<pre>
  for file in *.tif; do
    convert $file ${file%%.tif}.jpg
  done
</pre>
