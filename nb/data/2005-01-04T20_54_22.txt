Hey, we have concretions coming out of  bedrock at SIO up by the
soccer field at the head of the canyon.  I wonder what has happened to
them with all the rain?

They are saying that this has to be an evaporite (salt flats) due to
the high content of sulfate rocks.  Steve says that is impossible to
explain without water.  Then there is the ripple evidence.  Ask me
what I really think.

Oh boy, here is the DC salty sea presentation where they used hardly
any graphics.  Steve talked for over 5 minutes without any images.
CNN dropped random mer images over him talking until they cut away.
Sigh.  That was hard to get quality materials transfered back to DC
last minute.  Looks much better now on NOVA.  Thanks!
