Here is a bit of code to keep in mind if I get back to working on my
drivers for the Space Mouse.  This snipped by Peder sends a mouse
button up event to the viewer: 
<pre>
  SoMouseButtonEvent event;
  event.setPosition(get_current_mouse_pos());
  event.setButton(SoMouseButtonEvent::BUTTON1);
  event.setState(SoButtonEvent::UP);

  myviewer->getSceneManager()->processEvent(&event);
</pre>
