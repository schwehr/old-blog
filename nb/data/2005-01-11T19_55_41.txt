Right after that last entry, I sat down at Steve Cande's computer and
tried to install gmtplus via fink with gmt 3.4.4 already installed in
fink.  I ran into trouble right away with GMT_LEVEL not existing and
not having netcdf in the BuildDepend line.  That netcdf thing caught
me off guard.  I would have expected that in gmt-dev.  I had to update
gmtplus to not try to use GMT_LEVEL for GMT 3, so gmtplus-3.4.1 was
quickly born.  That seems to have installed fine and now Steve should
be testing it.
