Jerry is retiring after this week.  Congrats!  But that is a big loss
for us :(   Jerry described another pmag cube removal trick this
morning.  Make a wooden handle with two spatula blades a cubes width
apart.  Then put a thin wire between them at the bottom.  Slide this
down past the cube and use the wire to cut right along the bottom of
the cube then use the wire to pull out the cube.  Sounds really good.

getting a bit tired of everything and its cousin requiring a database
