While waiting to board the plain to SJO, I opened the SPIE Electronic
Imaging list of talks and the first thing I see is Justin Maki's
picture.  Excellent.  That will go well with my two talks.  The first
one on Monday is an overview type talk where I will cover general
concepts that are important to the scientific end users visualization
tools.  There will be movies made from both Fledermaus and
OpenInventor/density.  The imagery will focus on the Spirit rover and
Gusev crater.

My second talk is more a systems overview.  This will be a look at the
flexibility require for working on visualization system for a wide
variety of crafter from Mars surface rovers (what if Hugyens had been
a rover with and RTG?) back to peopled ships on Earth and underwater
vehicles.  The key is being able to throw out or tweak any part that
is necessary.  I will start with the Viz architecture and show how it
lets you layer on top of plain old ivview.  People watching Viz 1.0
did not always realize that they were seeing more than ivview until a
3D model appears out of nowhere :) I will show examples from Mars
Polar Lander and Dex.  I will then show how you can step sideways and
ditch the network talking to the viewer and go for the viewer
commanding externals such as web browsers and other OpenInventor based
viewers.  We will travel from lunar orbit down to northern California
into the STRATAFORM study area and the Humboldt slide.  We will then
dive underwater to the Driscoll Chirp Seismics and a Piston core from
my PhD Thesis.  We will not stop there!  We will continue until we see
the microscopic smear slide and an paleomagnetic AF demagnetization
Zidervelt(sp?) diagram from a 8cc sample cube.  If only I could zoom into
other cores in the worlds core lockers like this.  I wrap up with
adding a volumetric representation of anisotropy of susceptibility
data from the Ardath Shale, La Jolla, CA.  We need places to show
this, so I will briefly show how we use the SIO Visualization Center.
If I have time I will wrap up with a Lightwave rendered movie 
choreographed by Zareh Gorjian doing the same kind of site to
microscopic imager as Opportunity drives up and RAT's King A.  Not
the big difference that in using Lightwave over density 0.18.  Density
does not have the same control of the camera flight path.  It will be
a long while before I catch up to Lightwave!

I probably have to cut the second talk down substantially.  Well see
