There was an assertion that pro graphics cards are basically the same
as the high end gaming cards.  I have intentionally ignored the
details of graphics cards for the last 4 or 5 years since that
knowledge goes out of date year.  But, with statements like that, what
really is the difference between the $500 cards and the $2-5K cards?
Is the texture ram different?  Is the quality of graphics different?
Quality of rendering used to be massively different.  There was less
effort spent making sure the graphics are 100% correct in the consumer
cards when I looked a few years back.  Can the cheaper cards do LCD
shutter glasses in a window?  Can they blend two neighboring screens
for  Panorams?

What is this SIFT algorithm for making control point panoramas?  Is
this a valid patent with JPL's Vicar software being around for a
while?
