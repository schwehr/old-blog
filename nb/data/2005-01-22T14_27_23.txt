As for everyone who is upset about the Hubble, I understand, but my
opinion is that we should spend that money for another space
telescope and launch it with something other than the shuttle.  I know
there there is another space telescope set for 2011, but why not make
it two?  These projects are so valuable.  Why waste time, energy, and
possibly lives when we could build something for probably around the
price of repair?  Ok, so politics makes it easier to repair than get
something new which has a high likelihood of being canceled by
congress.
