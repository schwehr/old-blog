I have always had a strong dislike for Fortran.  It only got worse
after seeing what the compiler vendors did with F90 and F95.  Why
would I want to pay $900 for a junky Fortran compiler?  I have tried 3
different compilers at about that price each.  I only use Fortran for
legacy code at this point.
