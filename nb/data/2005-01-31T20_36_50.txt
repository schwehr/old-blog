On your Mac, try this:
<pre>
  Highlight some text in your web browser
  Apple-C copy it
  Go to a terminal or xterm window and run

  pbpaste  
</pre>
pbpaste dumps the contexts of the clipboard to standard out.  Use
pbcopy to put text into the clipboard (aka pasteboard).  I did not
know that there are 4 different pasteboards.  Crazy.  What other
little handy programs are laying around the Mac OSX filesystem?
