Sugarbush is back to life.  I did an nmap and found way to much open
so it was time to shut off services:
<pre>
  chkconfig --del mysql
  chkconfig --del nfs  
  chkconfig --del smb
  chkconfig --del portmap
  chkconfig --del nfslock
  /etc/init.d/nfs stop
  /etc/init.d/portmap stop
  /etc/init.d/mysql stop
  /etc/init.d/smb stop
  /etc/init.d/nfslock stop
</pre>
