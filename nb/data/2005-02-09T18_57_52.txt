with posibilities for future collaboration.

Today I resurrected some paleomag data from last weekend.  It had been
quite a while since I had used flow with cubes, so I had put the cubes
in with the arrow point down into flo rather ran out of the side of
flow/up at the ceiling.  Lisa's pmag library has a nice little program
call di_geo to help that I wrapped into a little script:
<pre>
<font color="0000ff"><strong>#!/bin/bash</strong></font>
rm -f better.mag
cat bad.mag | while read line; do
    <font color="#2040a0">decinc_bad</font>=`echo <font color="#2040a0">$line</font> | awk '{print <font color="#2040a0">$5</font>,<fo
nt color="#2040a0">$6</font>,0,-90}'`
    <font color="#2040a0">decinc_good</font>=`echo <font color="#2040a0">$decinc_bad</font> | di_geo`
    <font color="#2040a0">first</font>=`echo <font color="#2040a0">$line</font> | awk '{print <font color="#2040a0">$1</font>,<font co
lor="#2040a0">$2</font>,<font color="#2040a0">$3</font>,<font color="#2040a0">$4</font>}'`
    <font color="#2040a0">last</font>=`echo <font color="#2040a0">$line</font> | awk '{print <font color="#2040a0">$7</font>,<font col
or="#2040a0">$8</font>}'`
    echo <font color="#008000">&quot;<font color="#2040a0">$first</font>    <font color="#2040a0">$decinc_good</font> <font color="#20
40a0">$last</font>&quot;</font> &gt;&gt; better.mag
done
</pre>
By the way, I used code2html to make html out of the script.
