TITLE: crontab for updatedb
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 02.10.2005 09:22
DESC: Howto crontab locate
-----
BODY:
Here is the crontab for root that updates man pages and the locate
commands database.  Do "sudo crontab -e"
<pre>
MAILTO=''
5 0 * * * /sw/bin/updatedb
30 0 * * * /usr/libexec/locate.updatedb
15 0 * * * /usr/libexec/makewhatis /sw/share/man
</pre>
To view your crontabs you can do:
<pre>
  crontab -l
  sudo crontab -l
</pre>
