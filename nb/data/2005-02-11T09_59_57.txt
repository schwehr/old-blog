I am now trying out using <a href="http://www.myriad-online.com/en/index.htm">Galerie</a> to generate the web pages for core
sampling photos.  The generally look pretty good accept 2 of the
photos have their aspect ratios munched on the first batch.  Here are
the parameters that I used:
<pre>
Main page title: Sampling 1GW Sec 1
Link:  BPSIO04 
Link addr: http://schwehr.org/Gaviota/bpsio-Aug04/core-sampling/
Thumbs:
  Max size: 160
  Cols 4
  Rows 2
  Add title
  Add date
Template: Black
Pictures:
  Max: 600
  Add titel, Add date, Add EXIF, Add IPTC
  Add full-size orig
Quality 80
Create separate folders
</pre>
