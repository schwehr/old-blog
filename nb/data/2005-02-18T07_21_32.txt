I have been looking at how to pass text via stdin to programs called
from python.  os.system can not do this.  Looks like <a
href="http://docs.python.org/lib/module-popen2.html">os.popen,
os.popen2, or os.popen3.</a>  I have yet to find a nice working
example on the web.  Maybe one of these modules would do the trick:
pipes or commands.  Hey wait, I found something that works!
<pre>
