AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 02.19.2005 10:03
DESC: NetNewsWire Lite good

BODY:
My beta of NewsFire expired this week.  That was enough motivation
to really go start using NetNewsWire Lite.  I am starting to really
like this new world.  Not quite as clean and simple as NewsFire, but
it does have things like letting you know when it is checking each
news feed.  I still have not played with the Firefox RSS capabilities
which might be pretty cool.
