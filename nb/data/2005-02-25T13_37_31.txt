Cooked up a quick script to put in the crontab for sorcerer.  This
generates a 400 MB compress tar.bz2 archive.
<pre>
<font color="0000ff"><strong>#!/bin/bash</strong></font>
cd ~pmag
<font color="#2040a0">TAR</font>=~/Desktop/rawdat-`date <font color="#008000">&quot;+%C%y%m%d&quot;</font>`.tar
echo <font color="#008000">&quot;Create a tar of RAWDAT&quot;</font>
tar cf <font color="#2040a0">$TAR</font> RAWDAT
echo <font color="#008000">&quot;Compressing the tar&quot;</font>
cd ~/Desktop
bzip2 -9 <font color="#2040a0">$TAR</font>
echo <font color="#008000">&quot;Done.  Pleae write the tar on your desktop to a CD and take it home!&quot;</font>
</pre>
To work with the bziped archive:
<pre>
  View:  tar tfjvv rawdat-20050225.tar.bz2
  Extract: tar xfjvv rawdat-20050225.tar.bz2
</pre>
