I tried to use the older CTF magnetometer (known as Bubba) last night
since it is right next to the SI-4 AF demagnetizer.  Two hours later,
that was it.  No more.  BAD Bubba.  It is not setup well for pmag
cubes, which have to SIT on a pedestal as they are lowered down to the
squids.  I got CSDs that were like 3-5 which is not good.  Plus the
boxes were having drift of up to 4-5 x 10-8.  I must have been doing
something wrong.  The AF demag plots looked like garbage.  When I
switched back to using Flo (the 2-G magi), things looked much better.
The machined cube holder that Jeff and I had done a couple years ago
makes things much better.  CSD's of like 0.7-1.0 until the sample is
really demagnetized.

"Squids measure Emus"
