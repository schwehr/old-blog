Just ran into these notes on how to use the Sutar Box Corer on the
Sproul from the Sept 2003 cruise off of San Onofre.  Chief Scientist
was Babcock.

<b>Proceedure for using a Sutar Box Core</b>
<ol>
<li>Put out two i bolts on the deck far enough apart to fit the whole box
core frame.
<li>Transfer to under the A-Frame
<li>Strap it down with ratchet strap.
<li>Set the top door with pins
<li>Tie the ropes from bottom doors to the loops with light string
<li>Pull surgical tubing over trigger
<li>Attach a frame winch to red O link using a shackle
<li>Attach tag lines through the two rope loops attached to handles and
clip one end to the aft cleets of the A-Frame
<li>Remove rachet strap.
<li>(Leave the life lines in place -- back railing)
<li>Lift box core up so that the weights are up and the core is off the deck
<li>Pull the two flagged safety pines
<li>A-frame out
<li>lower winch
<li>When box corer is in the water, remove tag lines.
<li>Record GPS time and location
<li>lower box core to 50 meters above the sea floor at 25 m/sec
<li>then 20 m/sec to the bottom
<li>let out 3 extra meters of line to let the core get into the mud.
<li>record GPS time and location
<li>life to 50 m/sec below the surface
<li>up to the top at 15 m/s
<li>Use two recovery poles to grab the two garden hose covered loops.
<li>Pull the lines into the ship and ditch the recovery poles.
<li>Raise the box core up and over the life lines using the crane and the
a-frame at the same time.
<li>Place the box core just above the deck.
<li>Put the two safety pins in the sides.
<li>Then lower the frame onto the deck.
<li>Secure it with a rachet strap and remove the winch wire.
<li>Secure the winch wire.
<li>Lift up the top door and secure it with the pin.
<li>Siphon the water off with some flexible tubing.  Need 6 feet
of tubing to do this easily.
<li>(FIX: insert tube prep info above.)
<li>Push core liner tubes in the mud, down to the bottom.
</pre>
