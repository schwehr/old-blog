I am now into the swing of things with gnuplot-py.  First, aquaterm is
started by gnuplotx.  That is how gnuplot is showing the results with
the default terminal.  Then my big bug from yesterday was why my lists
were all corrupted.  Here is what <b>NOT</b> to do when you initialize
lists.  This is what I assumed would work from my C/C++ background:
<pre>
    depth = tau1 = tau2 = tau3 = []
</pre>
Turns out that this makes four references to ONE list.  Whoops.  Here
is the working version.  It makes a png image of the graph.  You will
not be able to reproduce this until I publish and release the data,
but the concept will hopefully be a good template for those needing a
jumpstart.
