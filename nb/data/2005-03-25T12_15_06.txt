There are lots of reviews of graphics cards lately and they are
starting back into the multiple GPU world again, but what seems
missing from reviews are any tests on the accuracy of rendering.  I
have noticed that many cards may be fast, but they are sacrificing
quality of rendering for speed.  That is one thing I liked about SGI
IRIX based hardware - rendering was done right.  Little artifacts are
a big problem for scientific work.  There needs to be an OpenGL
conformance and quality section folks!  <a
