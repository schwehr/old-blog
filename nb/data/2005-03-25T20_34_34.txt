<a href="http://wiki.mirbsd.de/MirPorts">MirPorts</a> is another Mac
OSX packager that is just starting up.  Which brought up licensing of
fink packages and patches.  I was just wondering the other day how I
can make sure that my patches and packages are the as usable to
everyone as possible.  Now I know from DRM's message.
<pre>
  Fink joined the metapkg project a few years ago, in which several open
  source packaging projects agreed to share porting tips and patches.  In
  this spirit, you are welcome to use any of fink's patches in another
  packaging project.
</pre>
