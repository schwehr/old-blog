TITLE: Photoshopping a digital xray
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 04.05.2005 14:25
DESC: a quick tip
-----
BODY:
Here are some quick notes about processing the digital xrays to bring
out the features.  The auto levels and auto contrast work a bit, but
it is better to use the levels feature as you have a bit more
control.  What you really want is to use
<pre>
    Image-&gt;Adjustments-&gt;Levels
</pre>
This will present a histogram of your 16 bit grayscale image.  There
are usually large dead zones on the left and right, so pull the out
sliders into those edges to get a better look.  Most of my xrays are
two humped camels.  The hump on the left is the frame background, so
drag the outer sliders to just the large right hump and your xray
should start showing a lot more in the way of features.  Then try
dragging all three around a bit to see what give you the best results.
