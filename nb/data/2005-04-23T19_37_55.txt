From the Mom news wire: <a
href="http://www.usatoday.com/tech/science/space/2005-04-23-NASA-culure-clash_x.htm">From
bad to worse? NASA in culture conflict since Columbia</a>:
<ul>
<li>"Everything is about return to flight and nothing is about return
to right ... return to the right culture," says Dr. Jon Clark, a NASA
neurologist who lost his astronaut-wife aboard Columbia.
<li>"NASA is making solid progress," notes the behavioral science
company hired to improve the space agency's culture.
<li> "We haven't really changed the way we're doing business and
making decisions, so we're headed down the same road we've been on,"
says former space shuttle commander James Wetherbee, who quit NASA out
of frustration in January.
<li> "We've made great strides. Now, it is one of those things that
you never reach perfection and you've got to continue to work on every
day," says deputy shuttle program manager Wayne Hale, who perhaps more
than anybody at NASA has confronted its long-lived, deep-rooted
culture.
