I can't say that I helped out at all with this nor have I tested any
of my packages.  The core guys on the fink team ROCK!  The <a
href="http://fink.sourceforge.net/news/#2005-04-29%20Fink%20and%20Tiger."">Fink
and Tiger announcement</a>.
<pre>
  Fink is ready for OS X 10.4! There are several ways you can update:
  A binary installer will be available within a few weeks, for
  binary-only users. For users of the stable tree, we recommend that
  you delete your current fink with sudo rm -Rf /sw and then
  "bootstrap" an installation of fink-0.23.9 using
  fink-0.23.9.tar.gz. After running bootstrap.sh in that directory,
  you'll need to run "fink selfupdate". For users of the unstable
  tree, you may be able to upgrade just by installing fink-0.24.5. Be
  sure to reinstall this fink package ("fink reinstall fink") after
  upgrading to 10.4.
</pre>
It will be a while before I get anywhere near tiger.  But I can't
