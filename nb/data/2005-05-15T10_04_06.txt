I tried to install Viha 0.0.1a and gave stumbler a try.  I really just
wanted to see how many wifi access points are in view and what
channels they are using.  No go:
<pre>
  ./Stumbler
  IOConnectMapMemory (ipc/send) invalid destination port (10000003)
  Abort trap
</pre>
And then I run it the top right menu bar goes blank and then comes
back.  Scary.  Time to throw out the framework.  Maybe I need to get
and build 0.0.2 from source, but I am not going to spend the time to
try that.

Instead, I got <a href="http://www.macstumbler.com/">MacStumbler</a>
to work right off.  Turns out that just about everyone in range is
using channel 6.  Not good!  The only thing about macstumbler that I
don't like is that it is actively interrogating all channels and
devices.  kismet/Viha does a passive grab of packets.
