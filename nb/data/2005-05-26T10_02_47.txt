This is an example of how to quickly create an sqlite database using
segy python and the sqlite module.  This is nothing fancy and it is
missing 99.9% of all the information available, but it is small and
thus easier to understand (I hope).  First the code.  This is the
previous example, but with a little extra sqlite code.
<pre>
<font color="#444444">#!/usr/bin/env python</font>
<strong>from</strong> <font color="#2040a0">segy</font> <strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">Segy</font>
<strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">sqlite</font>

<font color="#2040a0">dbFilename</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">'bpsegy.db'</font>
<font color="#2040a0">cx</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#2040a0">sqlite</font>.<font color="#2040a0">connect</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">dbFilename</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<font color="#2040a0">cu</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#2040a0">cx</font>.<font color="#2040a0">cursor</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>

<font color="#444444"># traceCount is the trace offset in a file</font>
<font color="#2040a0">createStr</font>  <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">&quot;CREATE TABLE traces&quot;</font>
<font color="#2040a0">createStr</font> <font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">&quot; ( traceCount INTEGER, lat REAL, long REAL );&quot;</font>
<font color="#2040a0">cu</font>.<font color="#2040a0">execute</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">createStr</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<font color="#2040a0">cx</font>.<font color="#2040a0">commit</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>

<font color="#2040a0">xstar</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#2040a0">Segy</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#008000">'bpsio01L00.xstar'</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>

<strong>for</strong> <font color="#2040a0">i</font> <strong>in</strong> <font color="#2040a0">range</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#FF0000">1</font>,<font color="#2040a0">xstar</font>.<font color="#2040a0">getNumberOfTraces</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">traceHdr</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#2040a0">xstar</font>.<font color="#2040a0">getTraceHdr</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">i</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>

    <font color="#2040a0">x</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#2040a0">traceHdr</font>.<font color="#2040a0">getHeader</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#008000">'X'</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>/<font color="#FF0000">3600</font>.
    <font color="#2040a0">y</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#2040a0">traceHdr</font>.<font color="#2040a0">getHeader</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#008000">'Y'</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>/<font color="#FF0000">3600</font>.

    <font color="#2040a0">insertStr</font>  <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">&quot;INSERT INTO traces&quot;</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">insertStr</font> <font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">&quot; (traceCount, lat,long)&quot;</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">insertStr</font> <font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">&quot; VALUES&quot;</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">insertStr</font> <font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">&quot; (&quot;</font> <font color="4444FF">+</font> <font color="#2040a0">str</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">i</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">insertStr</font> <font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">&quot; ,&quot;</font><font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="#2040a0">str</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">x</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">insertStr</font> <font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">&quot; ,&quot;</font><font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="#2040a0">str</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">y</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">insertStr</font> <font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">&quot; );&quot;</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">cu</font>.<font color="#2040a0">execute</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">insertStr</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>

<font color="#444444"># Flush this to the db... </font>
<font color="#2040a0">cx</font>.<font color="#2040a0">commit</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
</pre>
Once this is run, you will now have a little sqlite database in the
same directory.  Here is a simple query to look at a range of traces.
<pre>
  sqlite bpsegy.db "select * from traces where traceCount&gt;40 and \
  traceCount&lt;50;" | tr '|' ' '

  41 -120.101111111 34.4061111111
  42 -120.101111111 34.4061111111
  43 -120.101111111 34.4061111111
  44 -120.101111111 34.4061111111
  45 -120.101111111 34.4061111111
  46 -120.101111111 34.4061111111
  47 -120.101388889 34.4061111111
  48 -120.101388889 34.4061111111
  49 -120.101388889 34.4061111111
</pre>
That is it for now.
