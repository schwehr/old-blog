BODY:
Yesterday, I talked to Becca about GMT issues again.  I need to be
able to make a GMT basemap of some seismic lines.  For this, it would
be nice to have the full and high coastline maps which are not
included with the standard fink gmt install.  I created a quick fink
info package called <a
href="http://schwehr.org/software/fink/gmt-full.info">gmt-full.info</a>

I then went about testing these files.  I have not directly used GMT
in quite a while, so I started working on some examples.
Unfortunately, I ran into a seg fault with one of the pscoast
examples:
<pre>
  pscoast -R-30/30/-40/40 -Jm0.1i  -B5  -I1/1p/0/0/255  -I2/0.25p/0/0/255
          -N1/0.25tap -W0.25p/255/255/255 -G0/255/0 -S0/0/255 -P > africa.ps
</pre>
It seems that "-W0.25p/255/255/255" is the trouble.  Remove the first
'/' and all seems well.  To test the full database of coasts and
rivers, just add a '-Df'.  It is a little hard to tell from the whole
of Africa, but it goes through okay.

The Iceland example actually can show you the difference in the
coastlines:
<pre>
   low res:
     pscoast -I1 -R-30/-10/60/65 -Jm1c -B5 -Gp100/28 -W > iceland-lr.ps
     open iceland-lr.ps
   full res:
     pscoast -Df -I1 -R-30/-10/60/65 -Jm1c -B5 -Gp100/28 -W > iceland-fr.ps
     open iceland-fr.ps
</pre>
