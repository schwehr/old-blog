I just had Saturn of San Diego do the big 90 thousand mile
inspection/maintenance.  Today, I found out that the muffler is
totally toast.  It has three holes in it and would not pass New
Hampshire's inspection.  Yet Saturn of Sunnyvale just passed this car.
You have got to be kidding me that they actually checked out my car
when stuff like this went missing.

<b>Update 12-Aug</b>: Saturn of San Diego seems very obstinate.  The
said "Um... sorry.  Nothing we can do."  Then Saturn Corporate tried
calling and said any decision is up to Saturn of San Diego.  What?  No
influence what so ever?  The Saturn here said they are not sure how to
help, but would refund the inspection money to the customer if such a
thing happened from one of their inspections.

I haven't even mentioned all the other problems that we had with these
San Diego guys.  And they say that anything that is not a perfect
review is failure, so they failed in my book.  Avoid Saturn on Convoy.
That's my advice.  I hate to put really negative things on my blog,
but these guys give me no choice.
