TITLE: D_FORMAT in gmt
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 09.06.2005 14:20
DESC: don't loose precision
-----
BODY:
Somehow this did not make it into my blog before, so here is a
critical warning.  When working with xyz data two and from gmt grds
(grd2xyz and xyz2grd) use <b>gmtset D_FORMAT = "%.9lf"</b> so that you
don't loose precision.  That caused me lots of grief.
