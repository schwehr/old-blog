The tar is not named quite right on the web site, so I had to download
pyproj.tar.gz change it to /sw/src/pyproj-1.1.tar.gz.  Then I wrote
this little python script that converts one utm point (core 1 TTN136B)
from Lat/Lon to Zone 10 UTM.
<pre>
<font color="#444444">#!/usr/bin/env python</font>

<strong>from</strong> <font color="#2040a0">pyproj</font> <strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">Proj</font>

<font color="#2040a0">params</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="4444FF"><strong>{</strong></font><font color="4444FF"><strong>}</strong></font>
<font color="#2040a0">params</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#008000">'proj'</font><font color="4444FF">]</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#008000">'utm'</font>
<font color="#2040a0">params</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#008000">'zone'</font><font color="4444FF">]</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#FF0000">10</font>
<font color="#2040a0">p</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#2040a0">Proj</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">params</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<strong>print</strong> <font color="#2040a0">p</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">-</font><font color="#FF0000">120.108</font>, <font color="#FF0000">34.36116666</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<font color="#444444">#765975.64       3805993.13 0</font>
</pre>
