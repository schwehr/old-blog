<a href="http://www.zigbee.org/">Zigbee</a> is a wireless protocol for
low power sensor to be used in home and industrial situations.  There
is talk about 1 AA battery lasting a couple of years on a sensor.  If
this succeeds and is cheap/easy to implement, this could be a huge win
for science applications.  Rightnow, instrumenting an area can be an
expensive prospect.
