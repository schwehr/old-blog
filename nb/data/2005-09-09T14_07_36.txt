I just wrote a new program for segy-py called segytextheader.py.  This
program dumps the text file header at the beginning of a SEG-Y file.
That is not that exciting since that is just the first 3200 bytes.
The exciting part is that it can convert EBCDIC text headers to
ASCII.  Woo hoo!
