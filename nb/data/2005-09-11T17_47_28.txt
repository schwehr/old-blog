I took a short break from dealing with the seismic data for my thesis
and gave the Seismic Unix tutorial a go.  I figured this is something
that is still along the lines of processing my data, so I'm not
wasting too much energy :)

I played around with the tsa script in Chapter 3 and had some fun.
First I wanted to see how to get the generated spikes into gnuplot
which needs ascii data.  SU writes a binary file format and I don't
want to parse that.  The trick is to use <b>suascii bare=1</b>.  The
bare=1 prevents the time index column and the header from being
written.  I don't mind the time index, but the header confuses
gnuplot.
<pre>
suspike <font color="#2040a0">nt</font>=<font color="#2040a0">$nt</font> <font color="#2040a0">dt</font>=<font color="#2040a0">$dt</font> <font color="#2040a0">ntr</font>=1 <font color="#2040a0">nspk</font>=1 <font color="#2040a0">ix1</font>=1 <font color="#2040a0">it1</font>=20  | sugain <font color="#2040a0">scale</font>=0.9 &gt; spike1.data
echo -n '#' &gt; spike1.xy
suascii &lt; spike1.data &gt;&gt; spike1.xy

gnuplot
plot 'spike1.xy' with lp
</pre>
