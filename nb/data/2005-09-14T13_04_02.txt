I just went through and created a side-by-side comparision on the two
Huntec channels.  Channel 2 is much more ragged.  Here is how I
created the ch2 image:
<pre>
suwind &lt; line43.ch2.su key=tracl min=4000 count=3000 &gt; line43.ch2.4000-7000.su
sushift &lt; line43.ch2.4000-7000.su tmin=0.510 tmax=0.866 &gt; line43.ch2.4000-7000.nodelay.su

supsimage x2beg=5500 x2end=6350 d1num=0.01 x1beg=.61 x1end=.68 \
    hbox=10 wbox=18 verbose=1 d1=.000125  \
    &lt;  line43.ch2.4000-7000.nodelay.su &gt; ps2 && gv ps2
</pre>
Channel 2 is the lower frequency receiver on a streamer behind the
fish.  It is flapping in the breeze a bit so you can see artifacts
that look the same a ship heave (looks wavy).
