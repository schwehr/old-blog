<pre>

I am using wbt3 which is described on Paul's <a
href="http://sioseis.ucsd.edu/wbt.html">wbt</a> page.  I then added a
prout to display the water bottom picks.  I wanted to see what the
picks look like.  The <b>l50</b> entry in prout is the water bottom
value.  I got that by finding wbt in <a
href="http://sioseis.ucsd.edu/segy.header.html">segy.header</a>.

The default.gains file looks like this:
<pre>    0.0000    1.0000
    0.0005    1.0000
    0.0020    5.0000
    0.005     10.0000
    0.0200    15.0000 
    0.0500    15.0000
    0.1       40.000</pre>
I then have a pltsegy file that plots the new file.  I will not
include the pltsegy file since it is very long.

I then wanted to take a look at the water bottom picks and the
resulting pltsegy output.  How could I draw the wbt picks right on top
of the graph?  A little grep and awk will make a table of the picks by
wbt3:
<pre>./wbt.bash | grep -v WARNING | awk '{print $1,$4,$6}' | \
grep '1\.0' &gt; foo</pre>
I then took a look at them with gnuplot.
<pre>
gnuplot
plot 'foo' using 1:3 with l
</pre>

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2005-09/wbt-pics-l50.gif">

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2005-09/wbt3-pltsegy.png">
Note that this is a time pick, so as the picks get shallower, the plot

So at this point, I don't have a nice figure, but I am getting much
