About an hour and a half ago a small coast guard and an equal sized NH
patrol craft zoomed up to the I95 overpass with sirens going.  Now the
was just a sweep by a Coast Guard helicopter.  There is an ambulance
at the north size of the 1 draw bridge.  The NH patrol boat is close
to the 1 draw bridge on the north side.

  the area below the Piscataqua River Bridge Sunday for a person

  Authorities received a report of a white male between 15 and 18
  years old having climbed over the rail of the bridge at about 2

  Portsmouth Harbor station in New Castle.
