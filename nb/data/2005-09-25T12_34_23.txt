Just created an interesting little water bottom pick visualization.
This is in the total hack catagory!

First, in my sioseis script, I have been trying to add gains hung off
the water bottom with no luck.  But here is what I have:
<pre>
wbt3
  lprint 2
  thres 10000 track 0.5 end
end
</pre>
I picked 10000 as a guess based on what I saw in this graph of the
100th trace in line43.sgy.  I used 0.5 seconds for track, so that it
basically would not track at all.
<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2005-09/line43-00100.gif">
The then ran the sioseis plot script with gave lots of these warnings:
<pre>
