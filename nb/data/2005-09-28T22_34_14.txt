Now that I am getting close to graduating, at Christie's prompting I
gave the UCSD VPN a try.  The docs are <b>really</b> confusing.  For
the mac, I started here:

<a
href="http://www-ono.ucsd.edu/documentation/vpn/">http://www-ono.ucsd.edu/documentation/vpn/</a>

Then registered on this page:  <a href="https://www-no.ucsd.edu/cgi-bin/vpnsignup/signup.pl">https://www-no.ucsd.edu/cgi-bin/vpnsignup/signup.pl</a>

Then click on the Mac 4.7 client link to get to this page: <a href="http://www-ono.ucsd.edu/documentation/vpn/mac/47/index.html">http://www-ono.ucsd.edu/documentation/vpn/mac/47/index.html</a>


Under step 2, there is a little <b>here</b> link to download the Cisco VPN client software...
<a
href="http://www-ono.ucsd.edu/documentation/vpn/mac/47/UCSD-vpnclient-4.7.dmg">http://www-ono.ucsd.edu/documentation/vpn/mac/47/UCSD-vpnclient-4.7.dmg</a>

After downloading the VPN software, double click the dmg to open the
Disk Image.  Double click the "Cicso VPN Client.mpkg".  Go through the
install process, but do not eject this dmg!

Go into your applications folder and run the VPN client program.  You
now need to go through the import button twice.  There are two setting
that you need to pull in from the Profiles folder of the dmg:
<ul>
<li>allthruucsd.pcf
<li>split.pcf
</ul>
Now connect and use the allthruucsd mode... that means, make all your
network traffic look like it is coming from UCSD.

You will need to enter your UCSD campus username and password.  This
is the same as your UCSD username and password for pop, ieng9, squirl
mail, etc.  NOT your IGPP username and password.

Keep the VPN running as long as you need services that require being
at UCSD.
