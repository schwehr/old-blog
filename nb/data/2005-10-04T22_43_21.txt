want to able to specify a flag 0 or more times and build a list of
what the user gave you, then you must use the append option.

                  <font color="#2040a0">action</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#008000">&quot;append&quot;</font>, <font color="#2040a0">type</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#008000">&quot;string&quot;</font>, <font color="#2040a0">dest</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#008000">&quot;filename&quot;</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
