AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 10.10.2005 10:00
DESC: Use DISTINCT
-----
BODY:
I wanted to get a list of samples for a paricular core so I can do a
PCA to give the best direction.  Here is what I ended up doing:
<pre>SELECT DISTINCT(samplename) FROM ams WHERE corenum=1;</pre>
These did NOT work:
<pre>
sqlite&gt; SELECT UNIQUE(samplename) FROM ams WHERE corenumber=1;
sqlite&gt; SELECT samplename FROM ams WHERE UNIQUE (SELECT samplename FROM ams);
</pre>
I am not totally sure that I am using UNIQUE correctly.  It is also
possible that UNIQUE is not something that sqlite2 understands.  I saw
this from oracle that makes me think that UNIQUE just is not there.
<pre>
If I say:
  SELECT UNIQUE(COLUMN) FROM TABLE
I get the same results as saying
  SELECT DISTINCT(COLUMN) FROM TABLE
</pre>
