now just the labeling.  The projections of points will come later.
<pre>thetagrids( range(0,360,45), ('E', 'NE', 'N','NW', 'W', 'SW', 'S', 'SE') )</pre>

<pre>ring_angles = [ x * (pi/180.) for x in range(0,100,10)]
ring_labels = [ str(x) for x in range(0,100,10)]
ring_labels.reverse()
rgrids (ring_angles,ring_labels)</pre>
