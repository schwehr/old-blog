This does not handle upper and lower hemispheres, but it does the
basics.  decdip2pylabpolar transforms the points into the radians and
directions needed by pylab's polar plot.  Here is the snipped that I
added to handle that.
<pre>
<strong>from</strong> <font color="#2040a0">projections</font> <strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">deg2rad</font>
<font color="#2040a0">def</font> <font color="#2040a0">decdip2pylabpolar</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">dec</font>, <font color="#2040a0">dip</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
    <font color="#008000">&quot;&quot;</font><font color="#008000">&quot;
    Convert two lists of dec and dip to the coordinates needed to pass
    to pylab's polar plots.  pass in degrees, get back radians
    &quot;</font><font color="#008000">&quot;&quot;</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">decnew</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="4444FF">]</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">dipnew</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="4444FF">]</font>
    <strong>assert</strong> <font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">len</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">dec</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#2040a0">len</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">dip</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
    <strong>for</strong> <font color="#2040a0">i</font> <strong>in</strong> <font color="#2040a0">range</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">len</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">dec</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
        <font color="#2040a0">decdeg</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#FF0000">360</font> <font color="4444FF">-</font> <font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">dec</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#2040a0">i</font><font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">-</font><font color="#FF0000">90</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
        <font color="#2040a0">dipdeg</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#FF0000">90</font> <font color="4444FF">-</font> <font color="#2040a0">dip</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#2040a0">i</font><font color="4444FF">]</font>
        <font color="#2040a0">decnew</font>.<font color="#2040a0">append</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">deg2rad</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">decdeg</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
        <font color="#2040a0">dipnew</font>.<font color="#2040a0">append</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">deg2rad</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">dipdeg</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
    <strong>return</strong> <font color="#2040a0">decnew</font>, <font color="#2040a0">dipnew</font>

<font color="#2040a0">dec</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="4444FF">[</font> <font color="#FF0000">0</font>, <font color="#FF0000">10</font>, <font color="#FF0000">20</font> <font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">;</font> <font color="#2040a0">dip</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="4444FF">[</font> <font color="#FF0000">0</font>, <font color="#FF0000">5</font>, <font color="#FF0000">10</font> <font color="4444FF">]</font>
