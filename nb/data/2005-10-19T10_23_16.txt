From the apple store...

The Power Mac G5 Quad, with a suggested retail price of $3,299 (US), includes:
<ul>
 <li> <b>two dual-core 2.5GHz PowerPC G5 processors;</b>
 <li> 512MB of 533 MHz DDR2 SDRAM expandable up to 16GB;
 <li> 250GB Serial ATA hard drive running at 7200 rpm;
 <li> NVIDIA GeForce 6600 with 256MB of GDDR SDRAM
 <li> three open PCI Express expansion slots: two 4-lane slots and one 8-lane slot;
 <li> dual Gigabit Ethernet ports;
 <li> 16x SuperDrive with double-layer support (DVD+R DL/DVD±RW/CD-RW); and ships with Mighty Mouse and Apple Keyboard.
</ul>
