Jenna is working up some more layback info for the chirp.  The hope is
to have a lookup table of ship speed to a range of layback angles.
Here is a little bit on the commands I am using to generate the
velocity input table for the work.  Hopefully, the results of this
will end up in <a
href="http://schwehr.org/software/segy-py/docs/layback.html">layback.py</a> 

The first task is to remove any bad xstar segy files.  Neal's chirp
always writes an ASCII header at the front that starts with a company
entry like this:
<pre>
C1  COMPANY:
</pre>
A quick grep will list those files without the company entry and move
them out of the way.  They will confuse segysql.py and cause troubles.
<pre>
mkdir Bad
for file in `grep --files-without-match 'C1  COMPANY' *.xstar`: do
  mv $file Bad
done
</pre>
This gives me all the munch files:
<pre>
ls Bad/

line183.sgy     line350.sgy     line396.sgy     line400.sgy     line406.sgy
line336.sgy     line351.sgy     line397.sgy     line403.sgy     line407.sgy
line347.sgy     line352.sgy     line398.sgy     line404.sgy     line408.sgy
line349.sgy     line353.sgy     line399.sgy     line405.sgy     line409.sgy
</pre>
The most likely cause of the errors are bad tape reads or attempting
to read past the end of data on a tape.  But don't quote me on that
since I am not an expert with tape drives!

Next, we need a short script to generate databases from the xstar
files.  Since sqlite 2 gets really slow with monster databases, I am
going to break them into 5 groups of 100 files.  Here is make-db.bash:
<pre>
#!/bin/bash

 segysql.py --noisy -d xstar -f lines001-099.db line[0-9].sgy line[0-9][0-9].sgy
 segysql.py --noisy -d xstar -f lines100-199.db line1[0-9][0-9].sgy
 segysql.py --noisy -d xstar -f lines200-299.db line2[0-9][0-9].sgy 
 segysql.py --noisy -d xstar -f lines300-399.db line3[0-9][0-9].sgy 
 segysql.py --noisy -d xstar -f lines400-499.db line4[0-9][0-9].sgy 
</pre>

Once the database is created, here is a sample line used to make the
lookup table.
<pre>
sqlite lines001-099.db 'select shotpoint,speed/10.0,x/3600.0,y/3600.0 \
                        from segytrace' \
                        | tr '|' ' ' > 001-099.shot-speed
</pre>
