Just saw the <a href="http://bhami.com/rosetta.html">Rosetta Stone for
Unix</a> and saw an interesting Mac OSX command.  Not a clean as hinv
on IRIX (like that was clear), but it does say a lot about your
mac...  There is also sysctl, bless, pdisk, ifconfig, otool, ktrace,
kdump, and the netinfo stuff.   Never forget lsof (list of open files)
for those bad days.  I do miss the IRIX 'par -SS' command.
