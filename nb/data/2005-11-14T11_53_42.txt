AUTHOR: Kurt

BODY:
Just discoved another interesting BASH trick.  If you are running bash
as your shell, open up an xterm (or I suppose a Terminal.app window if
you must) and try this:
<pre>
    # echo $RANDOM
    31162
</pre>
Returns a 16 bit unsigned integer (ushort).  Never knew this before!
