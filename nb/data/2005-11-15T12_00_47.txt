In the strange ideas catagory, it would be interesting to see what is
possible by using AIS signals for positioning of a receiver.  Could
use a combination of signal strength, direction, and timing info.
This would be like the new receivers that do positioning from TV
towers, but here the base stations are not FIXED TV broadcast towers,
but now (mostly) mobile systems that are sending out their GPS
location and velocity vector.
