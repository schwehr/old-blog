I just finished writing a little python program to calculate the
median destructive field (MDF) for my cores.  This should give me a
sense of the coercivity the magnetic carrier minerals down core.
Clearly they are pretty different at the top of the core.  The program
is called mdf.py.  Right now, it only works off of sqlite databases
layed out in the same way as my thesis.

<img src='http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2006-01/mdf.gif'>

The purple line is the maximum applied afdemag field in mT.  Here is a
bit of the output from mdf.py:
