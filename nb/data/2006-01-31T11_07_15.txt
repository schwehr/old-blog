I just worked out how to control the citations in my thesis.  I first
read <a
href="http://www.andy-roberts.net/misc/latex/latextutorial3.html">Getting
to Grips with Latex - Bibliography Management</a> by Andy Roberts.
Then here is what I did.  The beginning of my thesis document looks
like this:
<pre>
  \documentclass[12pt]{report}
  \usepackage{natbib}
  \bibpunct{(}{)}{;}{a}{,}{,}
  \usepackage{ucsd-thesis}
  \usepackage{graphicx}
  \begin{document}
</pre>
I just added the natbib and bibpunct commands.  Then I modified the
end of the document to look like this:
<pre>
  a \nocite{schwehr2003}
  b \cite{schwehr2003}
  c \citet{schwehr2003}
  d \citep{schwehr2003}
  e \citet*{schwehr2003}
  f \citep*{schwehr2003}
  %\bibliographystyle{plain}  % this was numbered, which I do not want.
  \bibliographystyle{plainnat}
  \bibliography{schwehr}
  \end{document}
</pre>
The results come out something like this:
<pre>
  a
  b Schwehr and Tauxe (2003)
  c Schwehr and Tauxe (2003)
  d (Schwehr and Tauxe, 2003)
  e Schwehr and Tauxe (2003)
  f (Schwehr and Tauxe, 2003)

  Bibliography
    K.D. Schwehr and L. Tauxe. Characterization of soft sediment deformation:
    dectection of crypto-slumps using magnetic methods. Geology, 31(3):203-206,
    2003.
</pre>
BTW, the bibtex entry for schwehr2003 is:
<pre>
@article{
schwehr2003,
   Author = {Schwehr, K.D. and Tauxe, L.},
   Title = {Characterization of soft sediment deformation: dectection of crypto-slumps using magnetic methods},
   Journal = {Geology},
   Volume = {31},
   Number = {3},
   Pages = {203-206},
   Year = {2003} }
</pre>
