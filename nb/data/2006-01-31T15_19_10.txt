Here are two little python scripts to help out those who have to deal
with decimating navigation data from the SIO XStar system.  This will
not work close to the prime meridian because of a bug in the
processing software or chirp... that would be the lines that looks for
+/- .1 degrees.

To use these commands, just pass a list of files in like "./filter.py
foo.txt bar.txt sos.tex justshootmenow.txt"

The first script sends the results to stdout (a.ka. the screen or console).
<pre>
<font color="#444444">#!/usr/bin/env python</font>
<strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">sys</font>
<strong>for</strong> <font color="#2040a0">file</font> <strong>in</strong> <font color="#2040a0">sys</font>.<font color="#2040a0">argv</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#FF0000">1</font><font color="4444FF">:</font><font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
    <strong>print</strong> <font color="#2040a0">file</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">linenum</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#FF0000">0</font>
    <strong>for</strong> <font color="#2040a0">line</font> <strong>in</strong> <font color="#2040a0">open</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">file</font>,<font color="#008000">'r'</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>.<font color="#2040a0">readlines</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
        <font color="#2040a0">fields</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#2040a0">line</font>.<font color="#2040a0">split</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
        <strong>if</strong> <font color="#2040a0">fields</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#FF0000">0</font><font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#008000">'lat'</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
            <strong>print</strong> <font color="#2040a0">line</font>, <font color="#444444"># keep the header</font>
            <strong>continue</strong>    <font color="#444444"># but do NOT count it</font>
        <strong>if</strong> <font color="#2040a0">float</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">fields</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#FF0000">0</font><font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">)</font> <font color="4444FF">&lt;</font> .<font color="#FF0000">1</font> <strong>and</strong> <font color="#2040a0">float</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">fields</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#FF0000">0</font><font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">)</font> <font color="4444FF">&gt;</font> <font color="4444FF">-</font>.<font color="#FF0000">1</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
            <strong>continue</strong> <font color="#444444"># skip nasty bad nav points from the xstar</font>
        <strong>if</strong> <font color="#FF0000">0</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#2040a0">linenum</font><font color="4444FF">%</font><font color="#FF0000">500</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
            <strong>print</strong> <font color="#2040a0">line</font>,
        <font color="#2040a0">linenum</font> <font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#FF0000">1</font>
</pre>
The next one gets a little be more tricky.  It dumps the decimated
data to a new file with ".500" appended to the original file name.
<pre>
<font color="#444444">#!/usr/bin/env python</font>
<strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">sys</font>
<strong>for</strong> <font color="#2040a0">file</font> <strong>in</strong> <font color="#2040a0">sys</font>.<font color="#2040a0">argv</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#FF0000">1</font><font color="4444FF">:</font><font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
    <strong>print</strong> <font color="#2040a0">file</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">linenum</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#FF0000">0</font>
    <font color="#2040a0">out</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#2040a0">open</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">file</font><font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="#008000">'.500'</font>,<font color="#008000">'w'</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
    
    <strong>for</strong> <font color="#2040a0">line</font> <strong>in</strong> <font color="#2040a0">open</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">file</font>,<font color="#008000">'r'</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>.<font color="#2040a0">readlines</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
        <font color="#2040a0">fields</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#2040a0">line</font>.<font color="#2040a0">split</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>

        <strong>if</strong> <font color="#2040a0">fields</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#FF0000">0</font><font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#008000">'lat'</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
            <strong>if</strong> <font color="#2040a0">linenum</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#FF0000">0</font><font color="4444FF">:</font> <font color="#2040a0">out</font>.<font color="#2040a0">write</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">line</font><font color="4444FF">)</font> <font color="#444444"># keep the header</font>
            <strong>continue</strong>    <font color="#444444"># but do NOT count it</font>

        <strong>if</strong> <font color="#2040a0">float</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">fields</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#FF0000">0</font><font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">)</font> <font color="4444FF">&lt;</font> .<font color="#FF0000">1</font> <strong>and</strong> <font color="#2040a0">float</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">fields</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="#FF0000">0</font><font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">)</font> <font color="4444FF">&gt;</font> <font color="4444FF">-</font>.<font color="#FF0000">1</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
            <strong>continue</strong> <font color="#444444"># skip nasty bad nav points from the xstar</font>

        <strong>if</strong> <font color="#FF0000">0</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="4444FF">=</font><font color="#2040a0">linenum</font><font color="4444FF">%</font><font color="#FF0000">500</font><font color="4444FF">:</font>
            <font color="#2040a0">out</font>.<font color="#2040a0">write</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">line</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>

        <font color="#2040a0">linenum</font> <font color="4444FF">+</font><font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#FF0000">1</font>
</pre>
The second script also drops any following header info about which
field is which when generated by the lsd command.

Enjoy!
