I am having trouble with \bibliographystyle{plainnat}.  It is
converting things like proper names to lower case.  I went looking and
stumbled into a very nice document.  Here a professor lays out his
rules for writing and solves solves my problem.  Nice!  

<a href="http://www.csc.ncsu.edu/faculty/mpsingh/local/advice/editing.html">http://www.csc.ncsu.edu/faculty/mpsingh/local/advice/editing.html</a>  by Munindar P. Singh.
<pre>
  BibTeX has a bad habit of putting all article and chapter titles in
  sentence case; this can mess up the capitalization of
  words. However, if you put the words in braces, their capitalization
  is safe, e.g.,

  title = {Life on the {Internet}} 
</pre>
I like many of his suggestions (but not all).  What a great resource!
