I am not sure why this is a "disruptive change" as general CPU's are
becoming more and more able to do these kinds of DSP tasks.  We don't
need the TI DSP chips as much anymore.  Many of us have been thinking 
along these lines (e.g. Alex) since at least 1999, but not for
specifically military applications.  The Cell architecture is going to 
take a couple years to come into its stride as the compiler and other 
tools get better.
