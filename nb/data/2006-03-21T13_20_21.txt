I am finally converting my CVS repository that I used throughout my
time working on my thesis into a subversion (svn) repository.  Here is
what I had to do.  The first step was to tar up the CVS repository on
my server.  Names are ever evolving, so I will just put them in for
historical kicks.
<pre>
ssh my.server.com
#
cd projects/xcore/
ls -l Master
total 0

drwxr-xr-x   8 schwehr  schwehr   272 21 Mar 09:34 dotfiles
drwxr-xr-x   5 schwehr  schwehr   170 12 Feb 10:58 xcore

tar cf Master.tar Master
bzip2 -9 Master.tar
mv Master.tar.bz ~/

cd ~/Desktop/

And finally, to import my stuff into the new svn tree...  first to see what dump-only does.  This file is pretty large.  My 30MB CVS repository jumped to 180MB
<pre>
cvs2svn --dump-only --force-branch=start --dumpfile schwehr.svndump svnrepo/schwehr
</pre>
So maybe build the dump the way roland did:
<pre>
svnadmin dump schwehr --deltas > schwehr.svndump.deltas
</pre>
