I am working on understanding how to use svn's ignore tag so that svg
status skips things like python byte compiled files (*.pyc).  This
does not work the same way that .cvsignore works.  Here is a quick
example.
<pre>
$ svn status
?      version.pyc
?      verbosity.pyc
?      BitVector-1.2
?      dist
?      html
?      MANIFEST
?      ais.pyc
M      ais.py

$ cd && svn propset svn:ignore "*.pyc" .
property 'svn:ignore' set on '.'

$ svn status
?      BitVector-1.2
?      dist
?      html
?      MANIFEST
 M     .
M      ais.py
</pre>
That is great, but I also want to ignore the html subdirectory.
<pre>
$ cd && svn propset svn:ignore "html" .
property 'svn:ignore' set on '.'

$ svn status
?      version.pyc
?      verbosity.pyc
?      BitVector-1.2
?      dist
?      MANIFEST
?      ais.pyc
 M     .
M      ais.py
</pre>
Whoops.  *.pyc came back to life!  Instead, I discovered that propedit
will bring up my text editor to do the job and that new lines between
each entry works. 
<pre>
  svn propedit svn:ignore .
</pre>
I then put in this
<pre>
html
*.pyc
MANIFEST
dist
BitVector*
</pre>
And now my svn status is much nicer.
<pre>
$ svn status
 M     .
M      ais.py
</pre>
