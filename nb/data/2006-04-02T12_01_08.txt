BODY:
I took two messages and combined them into one long one like this:
<pre>
!AIVDM,2,1,1,A,58Lh=j02;?TTQ&lt;=@000&lt;P4hu@Pu8r0p58DD00016A0L7D50m0Hl3lU4k,0*3B
!AIVDM,2,2,1,A,@00000000000000,2*55'
</pre>
Becomes:
<pre>
!AIVDM,1,1,,A,58Lh=j02;?TTQ&lt;=@000&lt;P4hu@Pu8r0p58DD00016A0L7D50m0Hl3lU4k@00000000000000,0*49
</pre>
Then I converted this NMEA string from the message 5 format and found that the strings contain:
<pre>
HSCT
CHALOTHORN NAREE
X4C4%D3P
</pre>
When I look at the stern of the ship, I can see that the name is
correct!
