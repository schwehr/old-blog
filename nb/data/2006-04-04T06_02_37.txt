Just yesterday, I got the latest UNH computing group news letter.  In
that letter they said that it is critical to run Repair Permissions
BEFORE and AFTER any update to a Mac.  So I have found a "them" from
this article...
<a href="http://daringfireball.net/2006/04/repair_permissions">Daring
Fireball - Repair Permissions</a>
<pre>
  We are told to repair permissions before and after each update by
  whom? Certainly not by Apple. Perhaps Cruse means "them", the same
  "them" who, in the classic Mac OS era, recommended zapping your PRAM
  every time you need to reboot your Mac after the system was wedged
  by a crashed app.
</pre>
For the record, since my very first Mac in 2001, I have never once run
repair permissions.  fsck: yes.  repair permissions: no.
