Brian and I got a schema to work that adds two attributes to to root
level ais tag.  It has been a tough couple of hours trying to
understand what is going on (which I am pretty sure I still do not
understand this).  To start with, I have a new Makefile validation rule:
<pre>
<font color="8b2252"><strong>validate</strong>:</font>
	xmllint ais.xsd
	xmllint ais.xml
	xmllint --schema ais.xsd ais.xml
	xml val -e ais.xml
	xml val -e ais.xsd
	xml val -e --xsd ais.xsd ais.xml
</pre>
Then the new xml that needs to be validated includes version and date
attributes.
