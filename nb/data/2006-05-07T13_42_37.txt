House bill calls for $2.4 billion in grants for high-risk locations

Port security legislation approved by the House Homeland Security
Committee last month authorizes the award of $2.4 billion in grants to
high-risk ports over six years.

The grant money can pay for purchasing and upgrading security
equipment, including IT products, to enhance terrorism preparedness.

Eligible equip- ment is expected to include video surveillance
systems, waterside surveillance vessels, vehicle inspection stations,
closed-circuit telecommunications and access-control equipment for the
main gate.
