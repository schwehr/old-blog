The strip tries to get rid of white space only tails.  The results:
<pre>
  tag -- opening
   text -- "Dear John"
  tag -- body
   text -- "call Jane at "
    tag -- number
     text -- "603-555-1234"
     tail -- ".
Hurry, John. Time is running out!"
  end -- body numchildren = 1
</pre>
The funny business with the tail comes from there being a newline
embedded within the text before the end of the tag.  Now I can get to
all of the content in an xml file.
