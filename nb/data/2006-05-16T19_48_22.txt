I have been exploring the space of C++ unit testing systems.  I
thought that CppUnit would be the way to go hands down.  However, my
first look into into CppUnit got me kind of frustrated with CppUnit.
There was just so much typing to get anything done.  After python, I
am just not into that any more.  I did some digging and finally found
an interesting article that I liked: <a
href="http://www.gamesfromwithin.com/articles/0412/000061.html">Exploring
the C++ Unit Testing Framework Jungle</a>.  His conclusion is that <a
href="http://cxxtest.sourceforge.net/">CxxTest</a> was the winner.
However, his article is from Dec 2004.  That is a long time in the
software world.  Some of the other test frameworks that look vaguely
interesting:
<ul>
<li>unit-- <a href="http://unit--.sourceforge.net/">http://unit--.sourceforge.net/</a></li>
<li>CUT <a href="http://sourceforge.net/projects/cut">http://sourceforge.net/projects/cut</a></li>
<li>cpptest <a href="http://cpptest.sourceforge.net/">http://cpptest.sourceforge.net/</a></li>
<li><a href="http://freshmeat.net/projects/cxxtester/">http://freshmeat.net/projects/cxxtester/</a></li>
<li><a href="http://yaktest.sourceforge.net/">http://yaktest.sourceforge.net/</a></li>
<li>cutee <a href="http://codesink.org/cutee_unit_testing.html">http://codesink.org/cutee_unit_testing.html</a></li>
<li>TUT <a href="http://tut-framework.sourceforge.net/">http://tut-framework.sourceforge.net/</a></li>
</ul>
The author of that report just released UnitTest++ <a
href="http://unittest-cpp.sourceforge.net/">http://unittest-cpp.sourceforge.net/</a>
two years later.  It looks that UnitTest++ is too new to be used in a big project yet.  Bummer, as all the other projects looks like they have stagnated for two years.
