<a href="http://www.seacoastonline.com/news/06102006/news/107185.htm">'Red tide' brings shellfishing ban</a>
<pre>
Chris Nash, the shellfish program manager for the N.H. Department of
Environmental Services, said hundreds of thousands of gallons of
sewage flowed into offshore areas during the recent rainfalls.

Following that event, paralytic shellfish poisoning - commonly
referred to as "red tide" - was detected, resulting in the May 3
closing of shellfishing of all species in the New Hampshire tidal
waters of Little Harbor, Hampton/Seabrook, Great Bay, Little Bay, the
Bellamy River and near shore areas of the Atlantic Coast.
