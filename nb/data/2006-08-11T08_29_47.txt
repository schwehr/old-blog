Here is about the simplest example of using SDL_net.  I am just
reading from a port.  The key is to make sure that the process does a
non blocking read and then tells the OS to reschedule.  The usleep(1)
allows other stuff to get a chance.  This is basically poll mode.

The idea is that a process will fetch all of the available data in a
timer callback from an OpenInventor program.

The first couple lines are a cleaver hack to make the source an
executable script that compiles the program.
