Starting Friday afternoon, my passwd stopped working for imap and
webmail.  I had no idea what was wrong.  This morning, Will explained
to me what happened.  My Active Directory passwd had expired.  Since I
never use windows, nothing told me that I needed to change to a new
passwd.  Loging into a windows box at work popped up a change passwd
dialog and life is back to normal.
