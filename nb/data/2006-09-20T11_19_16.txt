This is one of the coolest things that has come my way for music.  I
still do not buy music from iTunes, but this morning I discovered <a
href="http://pandora.com/">pandora.com</a>.  I picked an artist (in
this case Depeche Mode) and pandora started playing similiar music.
Surprisingly, it did a GREAT job.  Then I tried some obscure
industrial.  That worked too.  Now I am trying some punk using AFI and
I am definitely enjoying the playlist.  As you go, you rate the songs
with a thumbs up/thumbs down.  What a great way to discover new music!

Love it!
