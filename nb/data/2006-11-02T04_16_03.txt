I got all excited about this new feature, but then I realized that
image will not be able to have an associated elevation model.  Drat.
Still cool for clouds that can be flown under, but not as cool as I
had hoped.

<a href="http://www.gearthblog.com/blog/archives/2006/11/new_google_earth_4_b.html">New Google Earth 4b</a> [gearthblog]
<pre>
A very important new feature is that image overlays can now have
altitude. This means weather satellite photos and images can be placed
above the ground. When you zoom in, the overlay will disappear
allowing you to view the ground photos without interference. I really
love the "Global Clouds" when you add altitude to it (edit the
properties for the Global Cloud image overlay, select "Altitude" and
move it up towards space)
</pre>
