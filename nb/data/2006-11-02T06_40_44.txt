I have been talking a lot with people about how Apple has not updated
their X11 in a long time.  This morning, I found out that they just
updated it.
<pre>
This update addresses several issues in the X11 for Mac OS X package,
enabling it to better handle:

GLX stereo visuals
offscreen rendering to GLX Pbuffers and Pixmaps
To tell X11 to take advantage of stereo visuals, enter:

 $ defaults write com.apple.x11 enable_stereo -bool true
</pre>
Not much detail, put when I update my next machine, I will make a list
of files before and after to see what really has been changed.
