Time to write a blog entry while I wait for 6 gigs of svn stuff to
come across the internet from the East Coast to West coast.  1 MB/s
seems slow when you have 6 GB to go.

I finally got svn to upgrade from 1.2.3 to 1.4.2.  I kept getting
strange conflicts.  The trick was to complete remove all svn 1.2.3 and
any svn doc packages.  Then I installed things one by one with the
svn-client-ssl last.  Now I have svn in my path with ssl and it works.
