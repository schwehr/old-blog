Yesterday, I added Placemarks from Google Earth into Google Maps.  I
noticed that Google Maps can't handle polygons, but here is an example
of using a georeferenced image.  This still begs the question, "why
use Google Maps if you have Google Earth?

This example uses the CCOM/JHC Logo that I have placed on the ocean
floor just off the NH coastline. <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/GoogleEarth/CCOM_JHC_Logo.kmz">CCOM_JHC_Logo.kmz</a>

I'll start by adding the kmz to my instance of Google Earth just to
show what we are working with.
