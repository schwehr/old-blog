<a href="http://www.thinkgeek.com/gadgets/security/8212/">TrackStick GPS Data Logger</a> [thinkgeek.com] (link from Roland)
<pre>Containing 1MB of memory it can store up to 4000 records allowing for
months of travel. When the TrackStick is not moving, memory is not
used. The record interval is adjustable to anything between 1 and 15
minutes (this is used to save memory and will not extend the battery
life). It's so small you can hide it for covert applications. There
are no special software applications to buy and the raw data can be
exported in RTF, XLS, HTML, or Google Earth KML formats.</pre>
Why only 1MB of RAM and why so expensive?  Other than that, this thing looks
pretty cool/simple.<p>
