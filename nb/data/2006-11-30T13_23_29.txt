Just found an example of how to use kmz files from Google Maps in a URL...  the trick it to use "http://maps.google.com/?q=" and then the url.

Try this link, but make sure to zoom out!

<a href="http://maps.google.com/maps?q=http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/GoogleEarth/CCOM_JHC_Logo.kmz">http://maps.google.com/maps?q=http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/GoogleEarth/CCOM_JHC_Logo.kmz</a>
