TITLE: Lidar to Fledermaus to GoogleEarth polygon of a road
AUTHOR: Kurt

BODY:
Just managed to get my first 3D object into Google Earth.  I have some
NOAA Lidar (laser altimetry from an aircraft) that I need to extract
the local surroundings and import them into GE so that I can give a
view of the local environment.  Google does not have buildings for the
area.  Roughly here is what I did.

First I loaded the xyz data into PFMDirect and made a pfm object.  I
then imported the PFM into Fledermaus.  I selected the whole area and
flagged points as invalid that are not the road (this was the tricky
part for me).  I will try to document this process more as I get more
comfortable with it.  Then I tried to export the soundings, but this
seems to hang Fledermaus.  Instead I loaded the pfm in AvgGrid and
built a surface there.  Then in Fledermaus, I loaded the surface and
selected the outline of one half of the road.  I created a polygon and
set if from the selection.  Saved that out as xyz and then ran a
little python script to export a filled polygon kml.
