Two interesting mac programs:

I don't have a personal gps, so using Google Earth to geocode images
is a nice idea.  <a
href="http://craig.stanton.net.nz/software/Geotagger.html">Geotagger</a>
takes the current position from Google Earth when you drop a jpeg on
the icon and adds the gps coords to the image meta info.  Then use <a
href="http://craig.stanton.net.nz/software/iPhotoToGoogleEarth.html">iPhotoToGoogleEarth</a>
(by the same guy) to make a kmz of photos.  WARNING: For me, it seems
to hang after producing the kmz.  I have to Force Quit iPhoto after
each use.  I have emailed a bug report to the author.

<a href="http://www.giss.nasa.gov/tools/panoply/">Panoply</a> is a
NASA Goddard netcdf world dataset viewing program written in Java.
