AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 02.14.2007 15:42
DESC: why do people still do this?

BODY:
I made my coworkers switch from rsh/rcp/telnet to ssh/scp in 1997, but
SIO just had a <b>Solaris 10</b> machine broken into via telnetd!!  Oh
why, oh why is telnetd available on a Solaris 10 machine?  telnet
really should only be used for testing ports and data logging these
days.  There is no reason to have telnetd, extra especially on
something like Solaris 10.

<a href="http://isc.sans.org/diary.html?storyid=2220">Another good
reason to stop using telnet</a> [sans]

Just like the don't do drugs campaign, we need a don't do telnet
campaign.  Or just nuke all telnetd's that you can find.
