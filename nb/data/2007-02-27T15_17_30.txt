AUTHOR: Kurt

BODY:
One of my macs does not seem to think that I have installed security
patch 2007-02.  I have now installed the patch 2x and it still wants
me to install the patch.  Yet, I do have a receipt for it!
<pre>
ls -l /Library/Receipts/SecUpd2007-002Ti.pkg/Contents/
total 104

What gives?

href="https://bugreport.apple.com/">https://bugreport.apple.com/</a>)
and got this response.  At least I know I am not alone.
<pre>
This is a follow up to Bug ID# 5026512.  After further investigation
it has been determined that this is a known issue, which is currently
being investigated by engineering.  This issue has been filed in our

.
If you have any additional questions related to this bug or wish to
