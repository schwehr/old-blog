to a serial port.  Turns out that this is not so hard to do.  The only
trick is to not try to do this on win64 (why did I try the xp64 box
first?).  I tried Microsoft's suggestion of portmon (<a
href="http://www.microsoft.com/technet/sysinternals/utilities/portmon.mspx">http://www.microsoft.com/technet/sysinternals/utilities/portmon.mspx</a>)

<a
href="http://www.serial-port-monitor.com/">http://www.serial-port-monitor.com/</a>.
