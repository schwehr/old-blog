I was finally able to talk to the base station I have sitting on my
desk.  Here is the query that worked as a python string:
<pre>query="$xxBSQ,BCF,*07\x0D\x0A"</pre>
When I wrote that to the serial port, I got this response:
<pre>$AIBCF,12345,7,4731.0,N,05249.0,W,1,2087,2088,2087,2088,1,1,3,0,AI*51</pre>
The main trick was outputing DOS style new lines.
