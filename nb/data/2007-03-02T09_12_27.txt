BODY:
I have now been able to decode an AIACA (AIS Regional Channel
Assignment Message).  This is not that exciting, but it is progress
and showed that I had a weak implementation of the NMEA checksum
algorithm (now fixed).  The message string:
<pre>$AIACA,0,,,,,,,,,5,2087,0,2088,0,0,0,I,1,000000*15</pre>
The results of nmea.acaDecode(msg):
