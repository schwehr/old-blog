The first half of the Google Earth session has been enjoyable.  The
talks have primarily been applications of Google Earth and tie-ins to
other data products.  It warms my heart to see cores in Google Earth.
I had fun following along with the talks on my laptop, throwing
lat/lon pairs in the GE search window.

Declan did a quick sketchup demo, but I have to point out that there
is also a plugin that lets you pull georeferenced data from Google
Earth into Sketchup.
