I finally got around to trying out <a href="http://unittest-cpp.sourceforge.net/">UnitTest++</a> with some of my code.  I
am really in need of testing right now in some of my development.
Somehow, I had the impression that using ut++ might be a bit
complicated.  For simple tests, it is really easy!  Check this out...
<pre>
<font color="0000ff"><strong>#include <font color="#008000">&lt;UnitTest++.h&gt;</font></strong></font>
<font color="#444444">// Some tests</font>
<font color="#2040a0">TEST</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">Boom</font><font color="4444FF">)</font> <font color="4444FF"><strong>{</strong></font>
    <font color="#2040a0">CHECK</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><strong>false</strong><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">;</font>
<font color="4444FF"><strong>}</strong></font> 
<font color="#2040a0">TEST</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">Yeah</font><font color="4444FF">)</font> <font color="4444FF"><strong>{</strong></font>
    <font color="#2040a0">CHECK</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><strong>true</strong><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">;</font>
<font color="4444FF"><strong>}</strong></font>
<font color="#444444">// Make it all go from main</font>
<strong>int</strong> <font color="#2040a0">main</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><strong>int</strong>, <strong>char</strong> <font color="4444FF">*</font><font color="4444FF">[</font><font color="4444FF">]</font><font color="4444FF">)</font> <font color="4444FF"><strong>{</strong></font>
    <strong>return</strong> <font color="#2040a0">UnitTest</font><font color="4444FF">:</font><font color="4444FF">:</font><font color="#2040a0">RunAllTests</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font><font color="4444FF">;</font>
<font color="4444FF"><strong>}</strong></font>
</pre>
Now compiling and running the tests.  One test should success and other fail.
<pre>
g++ try-ut.cxx -o try-ut -I/sw/include/UnitTest++ -L/sw/lib -lUnitTest++
/try-ut
try-ut.cxx:4: error: Failure in Boom: false
FAILURE: 1 out of 2 tests failed (1 failures).
Test time: 0.00 seconds.
</pre>
I have put together an info file for Mac OSX fink users:
