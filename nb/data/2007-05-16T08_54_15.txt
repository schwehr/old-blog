<a href="http://www.mlswa.org/secchi.htm">THE SECCHI DISK - WHAT IS IT?</a>
<pre>
The Secchi disk is used to measure how deep a person can see into the
water. It is lowered into the lake by unwinding the waterproof tape to
which it is attached and until the observer loses sight of it. The
disk is then raised until it reappears. The depth of the water where
the disk vanishes and reappears is the Secchi disk reading. The depth
level reading on the tape at the surface level of the lake is recorded
to the nearest foot.
</pre>
