AUTHOR: Kurt

BODY:
I don't know what to make about tidal energy in the places like the
seacoast waterways.  I worry about the environmental impact and the
hazards to people and vessels in the area.  These companies proposing
tidal power need to be much more open and they need to approach the 
marine research communities.

<a href="http://seacoastnrg.org/2007/05/19/nh-hb-694-passes-senate-committee/">NH HB 694 passes senate committee</a> [seacoastnrg.org]
<pre>
NH HB 694, which proposes to study the potential for tidal energy
development under the Little Bay and General Sullivan bridges on the
Piscataqua River, was endorsed by the NH Senate's Energy, Environment
and Economic Development Committee yesterday, by a vote of 3-0.
Sponsored by NH state representative Tom Fargo (D) of Dover, the bill
would establish a commission to identify and collect information, and
investigate the necessary regulatory requirements to determine the
feasibility of building a system for tidal power generation at the
bridge sites.  To be included in the study are:
</pre>
<ul>
    <li> Permitting standards, including regulatory barriers to permitting of such projects;</li>
    <li> The public's and business community's attitudes toward the project;</li>
    <li> The environmental and wildlife impacts; and</li>
    <li> The cost and benefits of the project and potential for compensation to the state and municipalities.</li>
</ul>
