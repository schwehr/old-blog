<a href="http://www-robotics.jpl.nasa.gov/news/newsStory.cfm?NewsID=69">First Public Release of CLARAty Software</a>

I left NASA Ames and switched to CMU just as CLARAty was kicking off.
I worked on some of the predecesors to CLARAty and know many of the
folks that have worked on CLARAty over the last 10 years.  It is
really great to have this code at least partially visible to the
outside world.  I would have prefered a more open license such as the
GPL and a clear path to a tarball would be nice.  I always push for
more, but this is fantastic!
