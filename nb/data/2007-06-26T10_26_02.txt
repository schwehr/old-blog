These screens are hooked to a series of Mac Pros, two displays per Mac
Pro. The Macs are simply networked together via gigabit
ethernet. Chromium will take any OpenGL app without modification,
divide the display into segments and send those segments to the
appropriate Mac node for rendering to display.
