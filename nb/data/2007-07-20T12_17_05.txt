BODY:
We are back from ORT 4.  The test was a huge success from our point of
view.  We learned a ton, trained up on lots of mission software, and
started the integration process with the science teams.  Here are some
photos that show a bit of ORT 4.

First, here is the space craft test bed in the PIT.  Right now, there
are just foam blocks and a small portion of floor replaced with a
sandbox, but in future ORT testing, there will be progressively more
interesting terrains for the teams to explore.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-07/ort4-1-pit.JPG">

This is a blurry me standing in front of the test bed but outside the
static electricity safety zone.  Dry desert air combined with sand
means that I am going nowhere near the electronics.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-07/ort4-2-kurt-in-pit.JPG">

Out in the vast open area around the pit, there is a mock-up that looks
more like the actual spacecraft than the test bed.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-07/ort4-3-kurt-with-mockup.JPG">

Nick and Neil were busy working to get all the equipment setup for ORT
4 and future events at the Science Operation Center (SOC).  Here they
are figuring out how best to configure a Mac mini to display mosaics
of images from the Surface Science Imager (SSI) cameras on the old
MERboard plasma displays.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-07/ort4-4-nick-and-neil.JPG">

This is a shot from one of the Sol 3 science team meetings.  This is
probably the midpoint meeting about mid day.  The SOC can be a busy
and crowded place.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-07/ort4-5-science-planning-meeting.JPG">

There are areas inside the SOC that will become the press area (I
think).  The project is still working to ramp up and get equipment and
furniture into the building, so some spaces are just starting to get
setup.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-07/ort4-6-wide-open-spaces.JPG">

There are some fun things to discover in Tucson.  Here is something I
saw about 2 blocks from where I was staying.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-07/tucson-nights.JPG">
