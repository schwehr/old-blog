This is one of those things that I really wish I had done.  About 10
years ago, Alex Fossil and I talked about merging his ground
penetrating radar (GPS) runs with my 3D stereo terrains from our work
in Haughton Crater on Devon Island (the Candian arctic).  I ran out of
time to do more than just show that I the stereo system worked
fantastically in artic mars-like terrain.  I have long wanted to do
this visualization and now Ames has gone an done it.  This graphic is
really awesome!
