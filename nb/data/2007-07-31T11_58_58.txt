But before it can start its work, the Phoenix Mars Lander must survive
landing on the surface of the rocky, dusty Red Planet, which has a

land spacecraft on Mars, only five have made it.

McCuistion, who heads the Mars program at NASA headquarters.

embarrassing Mars failures in 1999, will blast off from Cape
Canaveral, Fla., aboard a Delta II rocket on a 423-million-mile
trip. The three-week launch window opens Aug. 3.
