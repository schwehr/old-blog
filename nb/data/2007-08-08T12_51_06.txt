that is pretty awesome!

Now on to AIS data and gnuplot...

I am not there yet on the visualization end of things, but here is a
quick look from gnuplot.  There are three surfaces in the plot.  The
red is the base grid showing the level of no ships present.  The cyan
and blue are two difference categories of ships from within this list:
<pre>
    categories={
       0: 'cargo_container'
      ,1: 'fishing'
      ,2: 'passenger'
      ,3: 'service_research'
      ,4: 'tanker'
      ,5: 'tug'
        }
</pre>
The command to use in the upcoming noaadata release is something like
the following command.  Do not forget to add the region and step size
or you will default to one of my study areas :)
<pre>
./ais_pg_grid2.py -d my_ais_2006 --basename='passenger' --category=2
</pre>
Then in gnuplot, turn on the gridding and hidden surface removal.  I set the
grid size to be what my region happens to be.
<pre>
set dgrid3d 37,54
set hidden3d
splot 'redbaselevel-grd.dat' with l, 'sometype-cells.dat', 'another-cells.dat'
</pre>
