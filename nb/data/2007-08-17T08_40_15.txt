<a
href="http://gcaptain.com/maritime/blog/radar-is-out-stop-cursing-and-grab-a-plotting-sheet/">Radar
is out? Stop cursing and grab a plotting sheet.</a>.  He suggests
plotting AIS contacts with a grease pencil.  Why not have an AIS
charting system that can plot contacts on the chart.  Then spend most
of your time "looking out the windows".  Most of these ships have very
few people on the bridge.

However, learning to use a grease pencil and plotting is a very
valuable skill even in the computer age.
