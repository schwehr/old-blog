<b>Enviromagnetic Fingerprints Recovered From Modern Sediments (GP06)</b>
.
Modern sediments are valuable enviromagnetic archives. The session
will focus on the ability of enviromagnetic parameters (e.g. low field
susceptibility, frequency-dependent susceptibility, anhysteretic
remanent susceptibility, bivariate ratios, bivariate plots) to
differentiate sedimentary environments. Environmental magnetism is
able to characterize processes in modern fluvial, deltaic, lacustrine
and marine environments, as well as in modern soils. Case studies
concerning aquatic systems are particularly welcomed. Presentations on
the modified magnetic signatures recorded in ecosystems under natural
and/or anthropogenic pressure are strongly encouraged. Calibration of
sediments, e.g. by magnetic susceptibility, and correlation with
geochemical and/or geoecological data are also topics of interest.
.
Convener: Sorin-Corneliu Radan
Co-Convener: Ann M. Hirt
