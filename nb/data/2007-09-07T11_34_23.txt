TITLE: Bought a Evermore SA 320-5HZ GPS
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 09.07.2007 11:34
DESC: Data logging is fun
-----
BODY:
Roland came into my office with a GPS this morning... for $75 with
shipping, this thing is a serial pole mounted GPS that comes with a
USB-to-Serial adapter that will power the unit and we installed the
driver for the USB and I was able to log data right away (but we were
indoors so no real data).  The <a
href="http://www.semsons.com/evsaprgpsrec.html">Evermore SA
320-5HZ</a> outputs position at 5 Hz.  Can't wait to get my own unit
and try it out.  Just need a more portable logging device than my
laptop!

<a
href="http://www.silabs.com/tgwWebApp/public/web_content/products/Microcontrollers/USB/en/mcu_vcp.htm">CP210x USB to UART Bridge VCP Drivers</a> from silabs.com.  Installed and
rebooted.  Then I saw that /dev/tty.SLAB_USBtoUART showed up.
<pre>
./serial_logger.py -p /dev/tty.SLAB_USBtoUART -b 9600 -v
