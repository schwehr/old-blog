AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 10.05.2007 16:07
DESC: an interesting way to make shapes

BODY:
Steve showed me a trick on how to make ellipses and circles in
Photoshop to highlight features in images.  I had been using
Illustrator, but it is better to do that in Photoshop and preserve the
image size.

First make a new layer and select it in the layer window.

First switch to the Elliptical Marque Tool in the Tools window.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-10/ps-ellipse-2.png">

Select a region.  Here I am highlighting the AC unit on top of some
random house in Tucson.  The highlight edges are where the ellipse
will go.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-10/ps-ellipse-3.png">

Now select Edit -&gt; Stroke.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-10/ps-ellipse-4.png">

Pick your color and how many pixels thick the line will be.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-10/ps-ellipse-5.png">

Hitting okay will reveal the ellipse.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-10/ps-ellipse-6.png">

Now to may a semi-transparent filling by doing Edit -&gt; Fill.  Make
the Opacity lower.  Here I used 20%.  Select Use: Color (or reselect
it) to get the color picker to pop up.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-10/ps-ellipse-7.png">

I now have a shaded and highlight region.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-10/ps-ellipse-8.png">

Here is using the Magic Wand tool to outline/highlight an irregular
region.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-10/ps-ellipse-9.png">

Here is after changing the Stroke and Fill.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-10/ps-ellipse-10.png">

And the final result.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2007-10/ps-ellipse-11.png">
