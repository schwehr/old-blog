TITLE: Kernel panics with Mac OSX - Verizon
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 11.02.2007 10:42
DESC: a bit of frustration
-----
BODY:
I have been experiencing many kernel panics and complete lockups
lately on my two Mac OSX laptops (one PPC/one Intel).  These lockups
always seem to be associated with doing something with my Verizon USB
wireless modem.  I've had three such events in two days.  The nice thing
is that my Intel mac comes back up in about 30 seconds, but that should
not matter.  Hopefully, upgrading to 10.5 will make some of this go away.
This feels much like when I tried to use Microsoft's Virtual PC.  Suddenly
I had 10 kernel panics a day all with a nice MS Network driver notice.
I removed Virtual PC and have not had a kernel panic on the desktop in
a year despite heavy (even abusive) use.

My most recent lockup came from trying to add unh.edu to the search domains.
I hit save and that was all she wrote.

I really do like having the Verizon mobile internet service, but it
can be frustrating.  Between that and spending all day next to a
Verizon repeater that was cycling itself every 5-10 minutes yesterday,
the service can be less than wonderful sometimes.  It has worked for
me pretty much everywhere I've been (accept in a moving vehicle) and
has saved me lots of stress already.  Now if we can just get Verizon
to test their drivers a bit better and live up to that "unlimited" service
that they advertise I would telling everyone to sign up for it.
