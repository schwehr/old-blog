VTS works much like the Air Traffic Control system, and has it's own
set of rules. The Navigation Rules state, "Such ships may discontinue
their watch on VHF channel 16 while in the VTS area." Think of VTS
(shipping lanes) as a highway with a median.  The Captains of
commercial vessels know the Rules, but aren't always sure of what to
expect from smaller boats. Vessels smaller than20 meters are required
to keep out of the way of a vessel in a VTS lane.
