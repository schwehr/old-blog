In preparation for eNavigation, I've released version 0.30 of
noaadata-py.  I have been looking at the proposed right whale notice
with a number of people and have updated it to take 2 slots.  There is
currently space for 21 bits at the end that I have booked with
"Spare2."  Notice that in timetoexpireN that a value of 0 now means
that a ring is not currently active (aka has no speed restriction).
That allows electronic chart systems (ECS) to display the monitored
zones that are currently clear without having to keep the regions in
their software distributions.  Also, we have converted the buoy
(stationid) from an AIS string 6 to an 8-bit unsigned integer.  This
gives the option for 256 buoys in each region.
