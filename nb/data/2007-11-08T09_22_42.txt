Slashdot had a very interesting article last night.  <a
href="http://science.slashdot.org/article.pl?sid=07/11/07/2318208">Bill
to Require Open Access to Scientific Papers</a>.  This would
definitely shake things up in the academic world.  Would other
research areas follow this too if the bill actually passes?

<pre>
Congress is expected to vote this week on a bill requiring
investigators funded by the National Institutes of Health (NIH) to
publish research papers only in journals that are made freely
available within one year of publication.
</pre>
