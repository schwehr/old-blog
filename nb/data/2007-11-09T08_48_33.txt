<a href="http://www.noaanews.noaa.gov/stories2007/20071108_oilspill.html">NOAA Responds to San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge Oil Spill</a>
<pre>
NOAA organizations are on scene in San Francisco aiding in the
response to the estimated 58,000 gallons of intermediate fuel oil
spilled yesterday morning when the container ship M/V Cosco Busan
struck the Oakland Bay Bridge in San Francisco Bay.
.
While the incident occurred at the Bay Bridge, the spill is now
showing impact signs as far away as Muir Beach, which is outside and
north of the Golden Gate Bridge, as well as at Alcatraz and Angel
Islands. The Gulf of Farallones and Monterey Bay National Marine
Sanctuaries lie just outside the entrance to San Francisco Bay and are
areas of particular concern to marine resource managers.
...
</pre>
