All crewmembers and the Pilot that were onboard the COSCO BUSAN at the
time of the accident were alcohol tested within the required two hour
time limit. The results of the tests were negative. All crewmembers
involved in the navigation of the COSCO BUSAN including the Pilot
provided samples to be drug tested within the first few hours. The
results for the drug samples will not be available until early next
week.
