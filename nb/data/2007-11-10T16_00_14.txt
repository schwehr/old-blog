Next week is the eNavigation 2007 conference in Seattle.  This is an
opportunity for us as a community to work on the related Wikipedia
pages.  In particular, the <a
href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automatic_Identification_System">AIS
Wikipedia page</a> needs work.  I started a little bit today by adding
a link to the conference and editing the class A section just a bit.
You will see my changes under the username "goatbar." (That would be
from the Goats Bar in CO) I removed some overly vender specific stuff
and corrected some typos in the section on China's shore based system,
but that is only a start.

We desperately need a table with web links (or at least states the
name) for each of the relevant specification documents.  There are
quite a few of these documents around.  Please log into Wikipedia and
make your changes.  Let me know if you do make wikipedia changes and
to what wikipedia pages and I will link to them.
