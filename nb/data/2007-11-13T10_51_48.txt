TITLE: LRIT - Long Range Identification and Tracking
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 11.13.2007 10:51
DESC: This will be important for marine sanctuaries far from the cost
-----
BODY:
Chris Shivery is talking about LRIT.  Couple key points:
<ul>
<li>Will transmit 4x per day for ships &gt; 300 GT</li>
<li>Data will be ID, Pos, and time of pos</li>
<li>Cost is likely $0.25 per position for US</li>
<li>The US government will pay for all transmission costs for US flagged ships</li>
<li>The US will get all US flag vessel data plus everthing within 1000nm of US territory</li>
<li>There is an option to poll ships at up to every 15 minutes for SAR (Search and Rescue)</li>
<li>The US will pay to develop the international data center</li>
<li>There will be a USCG Request For Information coming soon.</li>
<li>Lights on is Dec. 2008</li>
</ul>
