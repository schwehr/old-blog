Yesterday I presented to the RTCM working group that is focused on AIS
binary messages for enhancing how Vessel Traffic Serverices work.  I
talked about the application of binary messages for getting water level
and right whale notices to ships.  As a part of that talk, I updated my
martime domain awareness (MDA) slide to be a bit more complete.  The 
background image that sets the location is from the USCG.
