The UK AIS network, which was developed by Kongsberg Norcontrol IT and
the MCA, went live in April 2006. All 18 MCA run Maritime Rescue
Co-ordination Centres (MRCC) are networked around the UK coast to
provide one integrated maritime domain awareness system...
...
hase three includes new functionality within the C-Scope User
Interface, including: BMT SARIS support - search and rescue graphical
display: VHF Direction Finding - triangulating position of a vessel
through radio transmission: Transmission and receipt of AIS Messages -
including safety text and control messages: Track reclassification -
mark specific vessels for easy identification and tracking: high
visibility warning object - no erroneous settings used for extended
periods.
