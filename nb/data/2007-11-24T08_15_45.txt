BODY:
I keep seeing posts about strategies for successful blogging and I
realize that I am not a normal blogger.  Beth Canter has interesting
articles on social communities and <a
href="http://beth.typepad.com/beths_blog/2007/11/loyality-rss-su.html">RSS
Subscriber loyalty</a>, but that is just not me.  Some of my friends
are blogging on systems like LiveJournal (LJ) and when they post, many
others (including me) comment on their posts.  It is interesting to see
just how many responses they get.  Quite amazing.

I don't allow comments (another than people emailing me) and I don't
track how many people read my blog.  I don't have any adds and I am
not going to add any.  Something like 95% of my posts are really only
meant for me as research notes.  I think I've scare off my family from
the text long ago and they only occasionally look at my blog for the
pictures.  Here are a couple things that have happened to me in the
last year that tell me that my blog is working for me:
<ul>
<li>I walked onto a job site last year and the first thing the team said was "We like how you are using python"</li>
<li>Just last week I went to a conference and someone told me that my blog was the reason that they knew about the conference and were attending</li>
<li>And the most important of all... every time I go back to my blog as a reference</li>
</ul>
My blog is primarily a research tool for me, but it is also fun and
anyway that people use it is a-okay by me.  The main downside is the
phenomenon of I'm not sure what to call it... But when you make I
google search and the first couple hits are my blog.  I'm googling for
<b>additional</b> information and don't have the answer.  This has got
to come from the fact that I generally refer to the topics the same
way and my vocabulary is not quite in sync with others.  It also
probably comes from having my articles grouped by month.  Google can't
see where one article ends and the next begins, so I am sure it is
grouping all words/phrases on that page together.  The best/more
ridiculous is when I googled something and the first hit was a post of
mine saying "I wish I knew the answer to XXX..."  Not helpful :)
