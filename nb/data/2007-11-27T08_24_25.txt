Hmmm... I just don't get it. Why does python not know who I am?
<pre>
% ipython
In [1]: import os
In [2]: os.getlogin()
Out[2]: 'root'
In [3]: os.getuid()
Out[3]: 501
</pre>
The bash shell seems to know:
<pre>
& echo $USER
schwehr
& whoami
schwehr
</pre>
And if I make a file, I am definitely not root:
<pre>
% touch foo
% ls -l foo
-rw-r--r--   1 schwehr  schwehr  0 Nov 27 08:15 foo
</pre>
Perhaps I should be doing <b>os.getenv('USER')</b> instead.  That at
least knows who I am.

BTW, this is with Mac OSX 10.4.11 intel and seems to be indepent of which version of python.  <b>But... if I open a new terminal, it all works</b>
<pre>
In [1]: import os
In [2]: os.getlogin()
Out[2]: 'schwehr'
</pre>
Gotta love problems that are not reproducable.
