<pre>
Whether you are using XSD, DTD, or RELAX NG, there are some
validations that grammar-based schema languages just can’t express or
that, for practical or business reasons, you do not want to build into
your basic XML models. Solution? Schematron!
.
Schematron is a rules-based validation/reporting language that works
by making assertions about patterns found in XML documents and
reporting back messages about the truth (or otherwise) of those
assertions. There is no need to validate that the complete structure
of a document is correct, just check one small constraint, or
three. Schematron is small, powerful, and easy to learn. It can
provide the best error/reporting messages in the world (you craft them
for your specific situation). Even if you already use a schema
language, Schematron can help for fast checking; pinpoint reporting of
element and attributes presence, absence, values and value ranges, and
co-constraints and other hard-to-crack edge cases.
