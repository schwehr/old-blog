I finally know an easy way to sum a list in python.
<pre>
from operator import add
sum = reduce(add, data)
</pre>
This is from <a
href="http://jaynes.colorado.edu/PythonIdioms.html">Python Idioms</a>
by Rob Knight.  A great document to have on hand.
