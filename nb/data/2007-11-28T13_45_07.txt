I don't think that Google Earth will go away anytime soon.  Maps and
Earth are different beasts for people who do visualization.  Still a
very interesting idea.  Thanks to stochasticgirl for the link.

<a href="http://www.techcrunch.com/2007/11/28/google-earth-heading-towards-extinction">Google Earth Heading Towards Extinction?</a> [techcrunch]
<pre>
Google has announced two new features for Google Maps that mimic
features in Google Earth, begging the question: is Google Earth on
borrowed time?
.
The first new feature is the additional of terrain in Google Maps. The
terrain fly over feature has long been available in Google Earth, but
now you can fly over a map and see the contours of the land, all
without the need to download Google Earth.
.
The second new feature mimics the community contribution feature of
Google Earth. "Our Maps" brings wiki-style collaboration to Google
Maps, with users able to annotate places and share those notes with
friends or the greater public.
</pre>
