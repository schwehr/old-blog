<a href="http://www.noaanews.noaa.gov/stories2007/20071129_ports.html">NOAA PORTS Ocean Observing System Installed in Mobile Bay</a>
<pre>
...
The Mobile system brings to 14 the number of PORTS in operation
around the nation. Studies have shown more than a 50 percent decrease
in vessel groundings following the installation of PORTS in other
areas. Estimates of economic benefits directly attributed to PORTS
range from $7 million per year for Tampa Bay to $16 million per year
for Houston-Galveston. The Port of Mobile is the 11th largest in the
United States, handling 24 million tons of cargo in 2006, accounting
for $89.5 million in revenue.
