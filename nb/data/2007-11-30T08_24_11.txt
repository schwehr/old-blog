<a href="http://blog.nj.com/njv_guest_blog/2007/11/letter_to_the_editor.html">In defense of the Coast Guard</a> [nj.com]
<pre>
...
The response to the oil spill here was anything but weak and slow as
your editorial asserted. Within 15 minutes Coast Guard pollution
investigators were directed to the scene and they arrived through
heavy fog 28 minutes later.
.
A commercial oil spill response vessel and oil skimmer were underway
within 40 minutes and in less than six hours (the regulatory standard)
nine times the equipment required by law was in use. This response
resulted in the recovery of 8,000 gallons of fuel in the first 10
hours, a remarkable feat given the weather conditions. As of today the
unified response has recovered nearly 30 percent of the spilled fuel,
exceeding the 5-20 percent that is generally recovered.
.
Despite good work by spill response forces on the water, the federal
and state governments could have done much better in communicating
with City of San Francisco officials early on in this incident and
throughout the day.
...
