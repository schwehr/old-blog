I can't seem to find this online, but sources outside of NOAA/CCOM/JHC
sent me a PDF from the NOAA AUV Roadmap Workshop.  I have to share a
small portion.
<pre>
<i>NOAA Ocean Service</i>
<b>Office of Coast Survey:</b>
<ul><li> Hydrographic AUV Project: Develop technical
expertise, define functional and technical
requirements, and develop standard operating
procedures to transition AUV technology into
NOAA's operational hydrographic survey fleet.</li>
<li>Baseline Port Survey Project: Incorporate
AUVs into port security surveys to: facilitate
rapid and flexible response; protect survey
personnel by providing greater standoff during
high risk surveys.</li>
</ul>
<b>NOAA-UNH Joint Hydrographic Center:</b>
<ul><li> Incorporate multi-beam sonar into AUV.</li>
<li>Develop harbor tracking system for AUVs.</li>
<li>4D AUV mission planning, monitoring and
play back software.</li>
<li>Adaptive AUV Control software.</li>
</pre>
