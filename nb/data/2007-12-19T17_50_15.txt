I was just thinking about how open source might fit into the
government procurment world and I got home to see this article.
<a
href="http://www.popularmechanics.com/science/air_space/4236758.html">NASA
Will Tinker With Open-Source Rocket for Return to Moon</a> ... go
behind the scenes of NASA's new Moon spacecraft, and here for an
astronaut's take on how to survive the new lunar base.
<pre>
The "brains" of the Ares I rocket that will send four astronauts back
to the moon sometime in the next 12 years will be built by Boeing,
NASA announced today, but the specifications will be open-source and
non-proprietary, so that other companies can bid on future
contracts. The avionics unit will provide guidance, navigation and
control for the launch rocket, which will carry the Orion crew vehicle
into Earth orbit.
</pre>
