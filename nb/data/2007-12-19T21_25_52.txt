Here is a rouch estimate of my recipe for Caribbean/Cuban shrimp.  If
you use 151, <b>I am not responsible for any damage that may occur</b>.
<ul>
<li> Start saffron rice from a packet as this takes about 20 minutes</li>
<li> Begin with a little olive oil in skillet</li>
<li> Add some cashews, red onions, and a clove of garlic</li>
<li> Brown the cashews just slightly</li>
<li> Add spices... tumeric, crushed red pepper, curry, etc.  Might want to try a bit of ginger, white pepper and what ever else might work</li>
<li> Add a frozen banana still in the peel.  Cut the short way in the middle</li>
<li> Add 1 mango that is ripe to the point the skin is starting to wrinkle.  Remove the skin and pit before putting it in</li>
<li> You might want to <b>skip this step</b>: Add a small amount of rum,
about 1 shot, but do it right after putting the banana and mango in so
as not to get any flames.  Do NOT use 151.  I tried it and thankfully
the flames didn't get anything</li>
<li> Give it 2 or 3 minutes on high</li>
<li> Add 1/2 lbs of uncooked, peeled shrimp (40 cnt is a reasonable size)</li>
<li> Cook on high until the shrimp are good and pink</li>
<li> Turn off heat and remove banana peel</li>
<li> Put rice on a plate and pore shrimp and mango over rice</li>
<li> Top with 1/2 teaspoon of rum</li>
</ul>
Makes enough for two.  Suggest that you serve it with a side of fried
yucca served with a each piece having slice of roma tomato and romaine
lettuce.  After dinner, I suggest a little 151 over ice cream.  Only
light the 151 on the ice cream once the 151 bottle is away.  No
explosions please.
