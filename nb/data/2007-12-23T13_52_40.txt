Sometimes you need a way to tell a mac to go to sleep.  Mac OSX hints
just had a little example for 10.5 machines that will not autosleep:
<a
href="http://www.macosxhints.com/article.php?story=20071220142440219">10.5:
A python script to work around a sleep issue</a>

At the heart of that python script is an os.system call to do this:
<pre>
/usr/bin/osascript -e 'tell app "Finder" to sleep'
</pre>
And another interesting 10.5 hint for iChat that sounds interesting,
but I tend to use Adium.  <a href="http://www.macosxhints.com/article.php?story=20071220165703606">10.5: Enable Data Detectors in iChat</a>
<pre>
defaults write com.apple.iChat EnableDataDetectors 1
</pre>
