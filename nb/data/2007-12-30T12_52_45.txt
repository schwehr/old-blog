I am definitely a proponent of open course type projects.  The CEP
Blog had a post that reminded me about the MIT project which is one of
the largest and earliest projects: <a
href="http://thecepblog.com/2007/12/30/opencourseware-get-smart-for-complex-event-processing/">OpenCourseWare:
Get Smart for Complex Event Processing!</a>

I try to follow a similar model by putting all of my lectures up on
the web with my <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/Classes/">Classes</a> page.
Especially to me is my first university course for credit that I did
last semester with Colin Ware as a part of his regular Interactive
Data Visualization: <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/Classes/2007VisCourse/">2007
Vis Course</a>.  In that course I tought how to create visualizations
using Google Earth and KML.
