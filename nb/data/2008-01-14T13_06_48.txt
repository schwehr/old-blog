Liquid helium is already becoming a serious problem for people doing
paleomagnetics.  Julie mentioned at AGU that the rising prices for
Helium are making it become a larger fraction of research budgets that
are already pretty tight.  Places are looking to start recycling
helium, but many research labs are far from any place that could handle
re-liquifying the stuff.

From slashdot (<a
href="http://science.slashdot.org/article.pl?sid=08/01/14/0219246">Helium
Crisis Approaching</a>)...  "<a
href="http://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2008/01/080102093943.htm">the
world's largest reserve, outside of Amarillo, Texas, that supplies are
expected to be depleted there within the next eight years</a>."
[sciencedaily.com]
