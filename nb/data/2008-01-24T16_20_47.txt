Thanks to Etsushi Kato, I fixed a long standing bug in the fink
package for gdal.  gdal-config --cflags was returning /sw/include when
is should have been returning /sw/include/gdal1.  The magic trick is
to add this line to ConfigureParams:
<pre>
--includedir=%p/include/gdal1
</pre>
