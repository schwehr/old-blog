BODY:
I need to be able to convert WKB (well-known-binary) strings returned
by postgis without AsText to usable geometry.  Thankfully, there is
GeoTypes, which I just added to the fink 10.4 and 10.5 trees.  If you
leave off the AsText, you get the raw hex string like this:
<pre>
0020000001000010E6C051D30925D1DA0B4044A79AE924F228
</pre>
Here is my demo code:
<pre>
<font color="orange">#!/usr/bin/env python</font>
<strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">psycopg2</font>
<strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">psycopg2</font>.<font color="#2040a0">extensions</font>
<strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">GeoTypes</font>
<font color="orange">#</font>

<font color="#2040a0">parser</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#2040a0">GeoTypes</font>.<font color="#2040a0">HEXEWKBParser</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">factory</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<font color="orange">#</font>
<font color="#2040a0">c</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#2040a0">psycopg2</font>.<font color="#2040a0">connect</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#008000">&quot;dbname='ais' user='schwehr'&quot;</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>

<font color="#2040a0">cu</font>.<font color="#2040a0">execute</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#008000">'SELECT position FROM position LIMIT 1;'</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
