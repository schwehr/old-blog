Andy M. has mounted a projector with a wireless link in the high bay
area between the two big tanks.  Here you can see the projector on a
vertical i-beam.  A wave staff is measuring the waves propagating down
the tank from right to left.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-02/wave-tank-projector.jpg"/>

Here you can see the waves heading into screens that dissipate the
energy.  Note that I took this without a flash.  As a results, the
waves have a smeared texture from the long exposure.
