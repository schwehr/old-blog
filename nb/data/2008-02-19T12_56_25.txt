BODY:
Rob B. helped me figure this one out:
<pre>
% ipython
from osgeo import gdal
f = gdal.Open('a.gif')
b = f.GetRasterBand(1)
c = b.GetRasterColorTable()
for i in range(c.GetCount()):
    print c.GetColorEntry(i)
</pre>
