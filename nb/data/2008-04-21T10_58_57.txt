Just talked to Dave Monihan about Google Earth.  He attended the AAG
Google Earth section.  Quite a few things came up in our discussions
and I'd like to highlight 3 things.

First is that Google Earth will now tell you when the imagery was
acquired.  In the image below, you can see that they think they took
the image of CCOM during 2003.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-04/ge-4.3-age.png"/>

My second item is another feature of the above image, contained within
the Wikipedia layer.  Notice that there is a Wikipedia Icon over CCOM.
I created a <a
href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Coastal_%26_Ocean_Mapping">CCOM
wikipedia page</a> on the 2nd of March this year.  Google has scrapped
the geographic location out of the wikipedia page and added it to
Google Earth.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-04/ge-ccom-wikipedia.png"/>

I encourange people to create Wikipedia pages for other research
groups and update existing ones.

My last topic is terrains in Google SketchUp.  There is a module
called <a
href="http://sketchup.google.com/support/bin/topic.py?topic=8804">SketchUp
Sandbox Tools</a> that Dave told me about.  I have not had a chance to
try it.
