This AGU EOS publication has an article this week on <em>Making
Undergraduate Geoscience Quantitative</em>:

<a href="http://serc.carleton.edu/quantskills/">Teaching Quantitative Skills in the Geosciences</a> [Carlton]

From my quick look, I didn't see any mention of python and they do
mention Excel.  I try to encourage students to learn tools that
can grow with them.  Excel is not a tool that can really grow to
the level that advanced research often needs.  Excel can do some
amazing things, but I would de-emphasize it.
