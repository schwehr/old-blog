BODY:
I just setup up 6 headed mac to run a 5 projector 60 ft display.
Apple Remote Desktop is the key to making this thing easy to run.  I
can sit in the audience and control it.  Screen 1 is not on the
projectors such that I prep material and then throw it onto the other
5.  Each projector is 1280x1024.  This works pretty well.
