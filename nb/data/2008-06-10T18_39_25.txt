any errors, but I really want to code to keep going.  I wrapped the
whole processing of one in a try, but that just hid what was going
wrong for some lines.  There are occasional bad lines in the data.
I added two lines to end of the <a href="http://www.python.org/doc/current/lib/traceback-example.html">basic exception example</a> in the python
documentation:
