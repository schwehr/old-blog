When properly using timestamps in python, you can indeed get UTC time.
No fancy conversions needed.  All that is need is for NTP to actually
be working (doh!)
<pre>
<font color="#444444">#!/usr/bin/env python</font>
<font color="#444444">#</font>
<strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">time</font>
<strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">datetime</font>
<strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">os</font>
<strong>import</strong> <font color="#2040a0">sys</font>
<font color="#444444">#</font>
<strong>print</strong> <font color="#008000">'     date now: '</font>,
<font color="#2040a0">sys</font>.<font color="#2040a0">stdout</font>.<font color="#2040a0">flush</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<font color="#2040a0">os</font>.<font color="#2040a0">system</font> <font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#008000">'date'</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<font color="#444444">#</font>
<strong>print</strong> <font color="#008000">'utc date now: '</font>,
<font color="#2040a0">sys</font>.<font color="#2040a0">stdout</font>.<font color="#2040a0">flush</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<font color="#2040a0">os</font>.<font color="#2040a0">system</font> <font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#008000">'date -u'</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<font color="#2040a0">sys</font>.<font color="#2040a0">stdout</font>.<font color="#2040a0">flush</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<font color="#444444">#</font>
<font color="#2040a0">now</font> <font color="4444FF">=</font> <font color="#2040a0">time</font>.<font color="#2040a0">time</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<font color="#444444">#</font>
<strong>print</strong>
<strong>print</strong> <font color="#008000">'timestamp:'</font>, <font color="#2040a0">now</font>
<strong>print</strong>
<font color="#444444">#</font>
<strong>print</strong> <font color="#008000">'    date from timestamp:'</font>,<font color="#2040a0">datetime</font>.<font color="#2040a0">datetime</font>.<font color="#2040a0">fromtimestamp</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">now</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
<strong>print</strong> <font color="#008000">'UTC date from timestamp:'</font>,<font color="#2040a0">datetime</font>.<font color="#2040a0">datetime</font>.<font color="#2040a0">utcfromtimestamp</font><font color="4444FF">(</font><font color="#2040a0">now</font><font color="4444FF">)</font>
</pre>
