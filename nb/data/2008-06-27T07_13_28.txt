TITLE: Why powerpoint is not an image transport format
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 06.27.2008 07:13
DESC: don't do it!
-----
BODY:
Powerpoint is a fine tool for making presentations, but people always
wonder why I tell them not to use ppt to transfer images.  Here is an
example that I have from yesterday.  I crafted this image that is now
released as: <a
href="http://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA10915">PIA10915:
Phoenix's Wet Chemistry Lab</a>

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-06/post-ppt-PIA10915.jpg"/>

Here is the original that I had with a serious white dot problem.  I
traded this for a color problem with export or copy/paste that would have
been uncorrectable.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-06/ppt-white-dots.png"/>

As you can see in the final, I got rid of most of the white dots, but I 
ran out of time, so I didn't get to the ones at the bottom.

<img src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-06/post-ppt-missed-dots.png"/>

Moral of the story: Don't give me a power point of the figure that you
worked really hard on.  Give me the original source images.  Also,
Illustrator PDF layer files are good, but if you make a PDF from
PowerPoint that is even worse.  PowerPoint is afflicted with a
clipping plane problem where it puts in 50 billion clipping planes
that I have to hunt down and remove/alter to transform a figure into
a public web ready figure.
