Tonight was the big night for the Queen of the Night flower to bloom.
This is a once a year event and very impressive with dry desert
lightning in the distance and candles all along the paths of Tohono
Chul.  The local news covered it:

<a href="http://www.azstarnet.com/sn/hourlyupdate/246635.php">Free
event tonight at Tohono Chul for night-blooming cereus flowers</a> [azstar]

Here is my best photo of the flowers.  Some of them did not smell
much, but others were very strong.

Me next to the flowers.
