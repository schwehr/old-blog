BODY:
Matt and I spent all of yesterday doing a photo survey of much of
Boston Harbor.  We put on about 100 miles and took over 3000 photos.
I think we both have sore arms from the cameras.  We had along two
Cannons.  Matt had the big zoom lens, I had the wide angle.  The goal
was to get all of the key features that a mariner might use for
navigation.  Things like this:
