After an hour and a half bouncing things off of pogma and akh, I've got it
figured out.  Turns out that extension handling for sqlite3 in fink is turned
off.  Might be a tcl build issue.  I haven't tried the latest sqlite (3.6.2)
to see if things are better.  The configure script for sqlite3 does not
seem very well behaved.
<pre>
  perl -pi -e 's,#define HAVE_LOAD_EXTENSION,#undef HAVE_LOAD_EXTENSION,' src/connection.c
</pre>
Update to the latest pysqlite2-py25 in fink and all should be well.
