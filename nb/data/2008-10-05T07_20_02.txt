The really new, and surprising, info about Class B is illustrated in
the slide below. The USCG has decided that B transponders should not
transmit boat names, as in many cases they are not official or
data-based. Instead of a name, that field will be filled with "US#"
and the boat's state registration or federal documentation number.
...</pre>
This will likely make it easier for the USCG, ACOE, and NOAA to
monitor these and match them to actual ships.  Trying to find out what
type of vessel I see in AIS can be a very challenging task.  Having a
name is sometimes not enough for us to figure out who it is.  But that
comes from the MMSI not being know or registered in a database that we
can't access.  I haven't tried accessing a state registration db yet.

I bet there will be a lot of mariners memorizing there registration 
number and/or callsign in the near future.  Navgear has a take on this:
<a href="http://www.navagear.com/2008/10/class-b-ais-no-boat-names/">Class B AIS: No Boat Names!?</a>
