Just looking at Delicious book marking a bit more... they have a
tagroll feature that shows a tag cloud:

<script type="text/javascript" src="http://feeds.delicious.com/v2/js/tags/goatbar?title=My%20Delicious%20Tags&icon&count=100&sort=alpha&flow=cloud&name&showadd&color=73adff-3274d0&size=12-35"></script>

<script type="text/javascript" src="http://feeds.delicious.com/v2/js/goatbar?title=My%20Delicious%20Bookmarks&icon=m&count=5&sort=date&tags&extended&name&showadd"></script>

<script type="text/javascript" src="http://feeds.delicious.com/v2/js/networkbadge/goatbar?showadd&icon=m&name&itemcount&nwcount&fancount"></script>
