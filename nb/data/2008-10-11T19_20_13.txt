I had been wondering what this thing taking over my garden is.  Last
night, several people were convinced that it is a squash.

<img title="10 inch vegi" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-10/what-is-this.jpg"/>

We went ahead and harvested it today to discover that it is definitely
a green pumpkin with a little bit of orange that was hiding
underneath.  It is currently in the process of becoming a part of a
modified version of: <a
href="http://www.toaster-oven.net/recipes/beef-casseroles/stuffed-pumpkin.html">Recipe
For Cooking Stuffed Pumpkin</a> [toaster-oven.net]

<img title="Pumpkin with only a little orange" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-10/green-pumpkin.jpg"/>

The results were very tasty!
