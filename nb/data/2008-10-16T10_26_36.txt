It's great to see the idea catching on!  I first demoed the idea on a
Nov 2006 cruise on the R/V Revelle.  Then in Feb 2007, IVS added Google
Earth export to Fledermaus.  Finally, at Hydro 2007, we presented:
<pre>Schwehr, K., Sullivan, B., Gardner, J. V. (2007), Google Earth
Visualizations: Preview and Delivery of Hydrographic and Other Marine
Datasets, US Hydro 2007, 14-17 May.</pre>

The UK Hydro office now has: <a href="http://www.mcga.gov.uk/c4mca/mcga07-home/shipsandcargoes/mcga-shipsregsandguidance/mcga-dqs-hmp-hydrography/civil_hydrography_programme_results.htm">Civil Hydrography Programme Results</a>

Now they just need a master kml that loads the data on demand over the network.
