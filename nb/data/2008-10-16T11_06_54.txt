<pre>
A boat sank to the bottom of the Piscataqua River Thursday morning in
the area between Geno's Chowder & Sandwich Shop and the Peirce Island
boat launch.
...
A buoy was attached to the sunken boat to alert other boaters. New
Hampshire Marine Patrol officers estimated the boat was 34 feet long.
.
It is not known at this time what caused the boat to sink.
