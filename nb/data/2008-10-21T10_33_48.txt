TITLE: Finding a vessel's MMSI
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 10.21.2008 10:33
DESC: it is a bit of work, but can be done
-----
BODY:
When you don't have the right MMSI for a vessel, you have to go through
the shipdata AIS messages to try to find the vessel's name.  Here is how
I did it for one vessel.  First get the all the 2 part AIS messages from the
stations that you know are in the area.  The station is after the normal AIS
message in a USCG formatted AIS message log.
<pre>for file in ais-logs-*.bz2; do
    echo $file
    base=${file%%.bz2}
    bzcat $file | egrep 'station1|station2|station3' | egrep 'AIVDM,2,[12],[0-9]?,[AB],' &gt;&gt; 2parts
done</pre>
Now you need to normalize the AIS messages such that they are one line each.
<pre>cat 2parts | ais_normalize.py -t -w 10 > 2parts.norm</pre>
You only want the msg 5/shipdata messages.
<pre>egrep '!AIVDM,1,1,[0-9]?,[AB],5'  2parts.norm > 2parts.norm.5</pre>
Then create a sqlite3 database of these shipdata messages.
<pre>ais_build_sqlite.py  -C 2parts.norm.5 -d shipdata.db3</pre>
Now look for your vessel by a partial name.
<pre>% <b>sqlite3 shipdata.db3 'select userid,name,callsign from shipdata where name like "%ODYSSEA%" LIMIT 5;'</b>
338208000|ODYSSEA GIANT       |WCZ7319
338208000|ODYSSEA GIANT       |WCZ7319
338208000|ODYSSEA GIANT       |WCZ7319
338208000|ODYSSEA GIANT       |WCZ7319
338208000|ODYSSEA GIANT       |WCZ7319
</pre>
Now I have the MMSI (aka UserID) for the Odyssea Giant.  Better yet, I can
find out the basic stats of the vessel and if they have changed over the time period:
<pre>% <b>sqlite3 shipdata.db3 'select userid, imonumber, callsign, name, \
   shipandcargo, dimA+dimB, dimC+dimD, destination, draught \
   from shipdata where name like "%ODYSSEA%" ;' | sort -u</b>
338208000|7408055|WCZ7319|ODYSSEA GIANT       |31|15|35|BOSTON MASS.        |13.5
338208000|7408055|WCZ7319|ODYSSEA GIANT       |31|15|35|BOSTON MASS.        |3.5
338208000|7408055|WCZ7319|ODYSSEA GIANT       |32|0|0|BOSTON MASS.        |3.9
338208000|7408055|WCZ7319|ODYSSEA GIANT       |32|15|35|BOSTON MASS.        |3.5
338208000|7408055|WCZ7319|ODYSSEA GIANT       |32|15|35|BOSTON MASS.        |3.9
338208000|7408055|WCZ7319|ODYSSEA GIANT       |32|15|35|TUXPAN.MEX.         |3.9
</pre>
shipandcargo of 31 and 32 are as expected for this ship:
<pre>31: towing
32: towing length exceeds 200m or breadth exceeds 25m</pre>
