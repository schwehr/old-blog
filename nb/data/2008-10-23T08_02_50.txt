BODY:
Yesterday, I briefly met with several people from the <a
href="http://nga.mil/">National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency</a>
(NGA).  They showed me their work with the international Sailing
Directions that can be found under the <a
href="http://www.nga.mil/portal/site/maritime/?epi_menuItemID=15b2c858fe35f7e25b2a7fbd3227a759&epi_menuID=35ad5b8aabcefa1a0fc133443927a759&epi_baseMenuID=e106a3b5e50edce1fec24fd73927a759">volumes
page</a>.  I had only been focusing on the NOAA Coast Pilots and the
Sailing Directions for New Brunswick thanks to Andy's Masters Thesis
that used the local harbor.  I ran into some trouble at first.  NGA
packages their PDF documents as a zip containing a MS Windows exe
installer.  As a Mac user without a Windows VMWare image at the
moment, I had to break out the Windows tablet to get at the pdfs.  It
would be helpful to the community and the web search engines to also
put the straight pdfs on the web.

<img title="Using Windows to get the PDF" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-10/nga-install-140-crop.png"/>

(Planning Guide)".  This gives an overview of the countries and
gives a map to show what pub to use for each region of the world.
