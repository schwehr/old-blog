Shallow Survey 2008 is finishing up today.  Time to start thinking
about Hydro in the spring...
<a href="http://www.ushydro2009.com/">http://www.ushydro2009.com/</a>
<pre>The U.S. Hydro 2009 Conference, sponsored by The Hydrographic Society
of America, will be held at the Sheraton Waterside Hotel in Norfolk,
Virgina on May 11-14, 2009. U.S. Hydro 2009 is a continuation of the
series of hydrographic conferences that alternate between the United
States and Canada. This is the twelfth U.S. Hydrographic conference
and follows on the very successful U.S. Hydro 2007, also held in
Norfolk, Virginia.
<!-- -->
In addition to the technical papers, the conference will feature an
extensive series of workshops, social program, and an exhibition hall.
<!-- -->
The conference will include technical sessions on the latest
developments and applications in hydrographic surveying, multibeam and
side scan sonar, data management, electronic charting, marine
archaeology, and related topics.
<!-- -->
Authors are invited to <b>submit abstracts of 300 words</b> or less for
papers on these topics to the Program Chairman, Captain Guy Noll, NOAA
(papers@ushydro2009.com) by <b>November 14, 2008</b>. Notification of
acceptance will be made by February 1, 2009. The deadline for
submission of papers and inclusion on the CD will be March 7, 2009.</pre>
Shallow Survey 2011 will be in Wellington, New Zealand.
