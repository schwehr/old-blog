Several of us are all working on generating log files of data from
sensors.  It's not a terribly hard thing to do and we each have
slightly different requirements.  I spent some time today working on
the noaadata port_server script that connects to a TCP/IP port and
logs the data while allowing other clients to receive the passed
through port_server.  I added a couple things:
<ul>
<li>Logging both the UTC human readable time of open and close of a log file</li>
<li>UNIX UTC timestamp seconds to open and close</li>
<li>On open, the logger calls "<b>ntpq -p -n</b> to find out how time is doing</li>
<li>Records the logging host OS, name and version</li>
<li>Optionally adding a USCG style station and UNIX timestamp to each line</li>
<li>Optional file extension... e.g. .ais or .txt</li>
</ul>
Running the command on a computer to test it:
<pre>% <b>port_server.py -v -i 35000 -o 40000 --in-host=eel.ccom.nh -r -lrnhsomewhere -srnhsomewhere  -v -v -u -e .ais</b></pre>
