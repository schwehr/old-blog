I've been trying to find a good data exploration tool for 2D plots.
There are lots of options and I just tried another one that Jeff Gee
introduced me to back in 2002 or so: <a href="http://plasma-gate.weizmann.ac.il/Grace/">Grace</a>.  

I've got data that is time based.  It's a waterlevel pressure sensor
that logs the time and water level in UNIX UTC seconds.  In python I
convert the timestamps to be ISO 8601 dates for Grace:
<pre>a_datetime = datetime.datetime.utcfromtimestamp(timestamp)
a_datetime.strftime('%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%S') # ISO 8601</pre>
Running grace in the GUI mode like this brings up the data:
<pre>% <b>fink install grace</b>
% <b>xmgrace event.dat</b></pre>
I then see this.  I am not sure how to change the x-axis into
something friendlier.  I added the circle in photoshop to highlight
the data that I am trying to explore.
