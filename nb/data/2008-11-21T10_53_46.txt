AUTHOR: Kurt

BODY:

eNavigation 2008 finished on Wednesday and yesterday we had the RTCM
SC121 Working Group on Expanded Use of AIS in VTS (focusing on AIS
Binary Broadcast Messages [BBM] at the moment) at the Port of Seattle, Pier
69 in Seattle.  The RTCM meeting had some intense and good discussion
on the Zone message that is now renamed to Timed Area Notice [TAN] to
follow my convention and remove confusion with the word zone.  We also
started looking into the waterways management messages.  We will be
using the St Lawrence Seaway and European RIS messages as background
material to support the our work.  If you know of other waterway
management AIS binary messages, please send them along.
