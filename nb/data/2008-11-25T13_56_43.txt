The Google Earth Blog today pointed to <a href="http://www.gearthblog.com/blog/archives/2008/11/ocean_biogeographic_maps_in_google.html">Ocean Biogeographic Maps in Google Earth</a> 
<pre>OBIS-SEAMAP, Ocean Biogeographic Information System Spatial
Ecological Analysis of Megavertebrate Populations, is a spatially
referenced online database, aggregating marine mammal, seabird and sea
turtle data from across the globe. The collection can be searched and
visualized through a set of advanced online mapping applications.</pre>
Check out the restrictions on the site.  The first usage restriction
is a bit weird.  I am surprised that you have to ask permission for
publications.  I get the commercial restriction and the citation.
<pre>* Not to use data contained in OBIS-SEAMAP in any publication,
  product, or commercial application without prior written consent of
  the original data provider.
* To cite both the data provider and OBIS-SEAMAP appropriately after
  approval of use is obtained.
...</pre>
