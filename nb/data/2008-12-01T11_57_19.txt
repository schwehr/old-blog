I got a bit turned around trying to add my first machine to OpenNMS.
Turns out, I just had to read the whole paragraph before trying it.  I
was just hitting the search button with no luck on any combination.
Here is what I did:

Go to <b>Admin</b> and select <b>Configure Discovery</b>.

<img title="OpenNMS web interface" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-12/opennms-start-1-configure-discovery.png"/>

Go to <b>Include Ranges</b> and add your range to search.  I'm going
to only one one machine here and there.  I have single machines placed 
about the country that I want to monitor.

<img title="OpenNMS web interface" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-12/opennms-start-2-configure-discovery.png"/>

After pressing <b>Save and Restart Discovery</b> at the bottom of the
previous page, wait a mint or two and check out the <b>Node List</b>.
I see here that it picked up the main services on the machine.

<img title="OpenNMS web interface" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-12/opennms-start-3.png"/>
