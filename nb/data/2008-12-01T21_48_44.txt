Sean Gillies has an interesting article today: <a
href="http://sgillies.net/blog/845/how-to-lay-out-python-project-code/">How
to lay out Python project code</a that uses <a
href="http://pythonpaste.org/script/developer.html">Paste Script</a>

same.
