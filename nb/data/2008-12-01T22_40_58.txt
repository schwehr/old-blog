My personal rough guidelines of blogging:
<ul>
  <li>"If you don't write about it, it didn't happen" -NWD</li>
  <li>Science and engineering are about reproducibility</li>
  <li>Email doesn't capture what is going on very well.  High bandwidth, low signal</li>
  <li>Don't revise prior posts other than typos.  Call out updates with a date</li>
  <li>I don't allow comments, but I accept email feedback</li>
  <li>When I get material elsewhere, I try to attribute what I can to the original sources</li>
  <li>I love paper notebooks, but they are not electronically searchable (google or grep)</li>
  <li>Pictures are critical (wish I had started adding pictures sooner)</li>
</ul>
