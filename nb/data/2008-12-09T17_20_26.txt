Christiana Arsenault has been working on getting her Highland Light
model from 3D Studio into Google Earth.  I loaded up her Sketchup
model and tried out the placement tools in Sketchup.  I did okay, but
not as good as Chris.

<img title="Placing in Sketchup 7" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-12/highland-light-sketchup.png"/>

Here is what it looks like in Google Earth.  I definitely noticed that
some part of the process has degraded or dropped the surface normals
from the model.  The original model in 3D studio was so good that many
people were surprised to find out that it is a computer rendering.

<img title="The model displayed in Google Earth" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-12/highland-light-googleearth.png"/>

There were some trouble spots.  I have Sketchup Pro 6, but only
regular Sketchup 7.  Could this have been done better with the Pro 7?
Sketchup does not seem to want to import other formats and 6 can't
import the default 7 models.  Still, the process is not too hard.

I took a look at the 3D Studio source model using <a
href="http://meshlab.sourceforge.net/">MeshLab</a>.  It didn't seem to
load the texture, but it shows that source has got proper surface normals.
I photoshopped a couple views together from MeshLab:

<img title="MeshLab viewing Highland Light" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2008-12/highland-light-meshlab.png"/>
