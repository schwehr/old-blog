TITLE: Pydro using I-AIS for realtime safe water contour
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 12.15.2008 07:21
DESC: binary message demo
-----
BODY:
This project was done to illustrate how AIS Binary Messages might
provide real-time maritime decision support.

<a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qo2fLnW7vr8">Pydro demonstrating realtime waterlevel/tide via AIS</a>
<pre>Produced by Rick Brennan, Jack Riley, Barry Gallagher, and Kurt
Schwehr. Pydro is receiving Internet Automatic Identification System
(I-AIS) packets from NOAA's PORTS/CO-OPS waterlevel (tide)
database. The background is a high resolution chart created by the Bay
Hydrographer. Safe water contours are created in real-time from the
combination of waterlevel derived from TCARI and the
bathymetry. Waterlevel has been exaggerated by a multiplier to better
demonstrate the system.</pre>
