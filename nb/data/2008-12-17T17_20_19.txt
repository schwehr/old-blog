It's been a busy time at AGU.  Monday I was at NASA Ames with the
Intelligent Robotics Group (IRG), Tuesday I gave a Google Tech talk,
and today we had some discussions about GMT and MB-System.  I met Paul
Wessel (who is a coauthor of GMT) and many other people who do really
impressive work.  We had some discussion about how we might do testing
of open source geospatial tools.  Paul told us about <a
href="http://www.imagemagick.org/script/compare.php">compare</a> in
ImageMagick and there were discussion of grd diff (by which I think
they meant grdmath).  This is an exciting and vibrant group of people.

Geoff Davis introduced himself to me and he mentioned his use of <a href="http://www.cfengine.org/">cfengine</a>.
I spent quite a while grilling him about the success that he has.  It sounds
like the learning curve is well worth it.

This is just 1/2 of the room for posters.
