I've got two geologic maps from the Great Basin National Park (GBNP)
that I worked hard to put into Arc/Info back in 1994-5.  With help
from <a href="http://pubpages.unh.edu/~mlg52/">Monica</a>, I've at least got some of the data back.  My archived
data is tar'ed in gbnp-1995.tar.bz2 which can be found in my <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/data/1995-gbnp/">GBNP
archive</a> Back in 1995, I did a whole series of 3D visualizations
using GRASS on an SGI.  The results were really good!  I wish I had
managed to save the grass project.

<img title="Visualized in GRASS" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-01/wheeler-peak-gbnp-grass.png"/>

I could not figure out how ot get gdal to read the Arc/Info V7 project
with the <a href="http://www.gdal.org/ogr/drv_avcbin.html">avcbin</a>
driver.  Monica used <a
href="http://avce00.maptools.org/avce00/index.html">AVCE00</a> to
convert the files to e00.  I took a converted e00 file and ran it
though gdal:
<pre>% <b>ogr2ogr -f KML minerva-canyon.{kml,e00}</b></pre>
After a little bit of tweaking, here is the view in Google Earth with only a few of the placemarks.

<img title="In Google Earth" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-01/minerva-canyon-gbnp-ge.png"/>

How do you parse the INFO database files?  Perhaps this: <a href="http://avce00.maptools.org/docs/v7_bin_cover.html#ARC.DAT">v7_bin_cover.html#ARC.DAT</a>
