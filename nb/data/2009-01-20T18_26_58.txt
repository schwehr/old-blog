Matt and I gave a first go at looking into working with the Google
Earth Browser Plugin.  I like what I see so far.  I took Christiana's
Highland Light and exported a kmz from Google Sketchup Pro.  We
started working from the <a
href="http://code.google.com/apis/earth/">Google Earth API</a> page.
I threw the model kmz on a server and added it to the <a href="http://earth-api-samples.googlecode.com/svn/trunk/demos/helloearth/index.html">Hello Earth</a>
example on the <a href="http://code.google.com/apis/earth/documentation/examples.html">examples page</a>.
