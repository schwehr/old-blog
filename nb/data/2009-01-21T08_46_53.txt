BODY:
This is a super easy way of displaying a kmz!  I saw this on the
Google Earth Design blog: <a
href="http://googleearthdesign.blogspot.com/2009/01/gadget-experiment.html">Gadget
Experiment<a>.  I went to the <a
href="http://www.gmodules.com/ig/creator?synd=open&url=http://hosting.gmodules.com/ig/gadgets/file/114026893455619160549/embedkmlgadget.xml">Google
Earth Gadget page</a>.  Then I plugged in the URL to <a href="http://sketchup.google.com/3dwarehouse/details?mid=5b4acc5f6f4083dc7efb52376d7391bd">Christiana's
Highland Light model</a> converted to a KMZ (not as pretty as her original
model).  You don't even need a Google Maps API key for this!
