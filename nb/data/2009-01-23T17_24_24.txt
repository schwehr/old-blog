The report mentions AIS on Page 26:
<pre>Automatic Identification System (AIS) for large vessels was generally
seen as an important database.  An overarching question is how to
integrate AIS with other geospatial databases to be the most useful in
describing and predicting anthropogenic contributions to marine
ambient noise and potential impacts</pre>
Leila Hatch was the chair of <i>Session III - Non-Regulatory
Incentives to Reduce Sound Emission from Large Commercial Vessels</i>.
Since then, we've published a paper that used AIS in looking at vessel
noise:

Study Using the Gerry E. Studds Stellwagen Bank National Marine
