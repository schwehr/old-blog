NOAA has a public preview beta of their new chart viewer: <a
href="http://www.charts.noaa.gov/InteractiveCatalog/nrnc.shtml">Paper
and Raster Nautical Chart Catalog (beta)</a>.  Warning... it does
<b>not</b> support Internet Explorer.  Use firefox or similar.

Zoom in and bounding boxes start to appear.  Click in a box to start seeing chart listings.

<img title="Beta interface" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-02/noaa-chart-viewer-beta.png"/>

Click on the chart number to pop up the raster chart.

<img title="Beta interface - Rasterx" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-02/noaa-chartviewer-rnc.png"/>
