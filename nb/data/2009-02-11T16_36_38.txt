<a href="http://www.space.com/news/090211-satellite-collision.html">U.S. Satellite Destroyed in Space Collision</a>
<pre>Two satellites, the Iridium 33 (US commercial) and the Russian Cosmos
2251 collided over the South Pole, resulting in total destruction of
both spacecraft and 260 new pieces of space junk. The good news is
that it appear to be of no risk to ISS or STS. However, the collision
took place just 100 KM above the EOS polar track and may become a
debris risk to all EOS polar orbiter satellites.</pre>
