Jim G. just showed me two trick in the Mac finder that really make it
easier to use.  In the orange circles is the <i>Show Path Bar</i> that
puts the path at the bottom.  When working through a lot of files, I
often get turned around.  This is a great help.  In the red circle is
the icon that lets you change the width of any column.  Good for long
file names.  Maybe these were obvious to many people, but I'd never tried them.

<img title="Customizing the Mac Finder" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-02/mac-finder-tricks-1.png"/>

This second image shows editing the Toolbar.  There are a couple other
good tools such as the <i>Get Info</i> and <i>Path</i> buttons at are
nice to have.

<img title="Customizing the Mac Finder" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-02/mac-finder-tricks-2.png"/>

I also started using Ctrl-Apple-5 to sort the icons on my desktop when
it gets messy.
