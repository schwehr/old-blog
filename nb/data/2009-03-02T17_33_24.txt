As a researcher, I often get tours of vessels and facilities that are
not open to many other people.  I try to share what I can, but it is
great to see what others can share.  Via <a
href="http://gcaptain.com/maritime/blog/maritime-monday-151/">Maritime
Monday</a> on gCaptain, I found this video that gives a bit of a tour
of the Littoral Combat Ship, including a small introduction to their
autonomous aircraft.
