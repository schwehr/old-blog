<a
href="http://google-latlong.blogspot.com/2009/03/and-award-goes-to.html">And
the award goes to...</a> Google Lat Long Blog announces the winners of
the KML in Research contest.

I took a quick peek at the <a href="http://www.google.com/gadgets/directory?synd=earth&cat=featured">Google Earth Gallery</a> and saw that they have this entry: <a href="http://www.google.com/gadgets/directory?synd=earth&cat=featured&url=http%3A%2F%2Fmapplets.googlepages.com%2Fguiweather_wdtinc.xml">Buoy and Ship Observations</a> by Weather Decision Technologies Inc.
<pre>In this KML, you can explore real time sea data for hundreds of
locations across the globe. The data, updated every 30 minutes, will
provide you with information that has been collected by buoys and
ships, offering facts such as temperature, pressure, wave height and
general weather conditions. This kind of KML is not only extremely
useful to those who enjoy a life of the ocean wave, but also to those
interested in and studying environmental and weather patterns.</pre>
I think they are using VOS, but it's hard to tell.  Don't forget <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/vos/">Ben Smith's VOS KML</a>
