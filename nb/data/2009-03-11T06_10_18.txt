From the report, I can get the id that identifies this incident to the USCG: 
<pre>MISLE Activity Number: 3095030</pre>
First, lets see what the USCG says about the Vessel.  Go to the <a
href="http://cgmix.uscg.mil/PSIX/PSIXSearch.aspx">Port State
Information eXchange Search</a> and put "Cosco Busan" in the <i>Vessel
Name</i> field.  This results in the following URL:

<a href="http://cgmix.uscg.mil/PSIX/PSIXDetails.aspx?VesselID=512403">http://cgmix.uscg.mil/PSIX/PSIXDetails.aspx?VesselID=512403</a>

<img title="PSIX info on the Cosco Busan" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-03/uscg-psix-cosco-busan-512403.png"/>

This shows us that the Cosco Busan was recertified on Oct 25, 2007.
Since I have the MISLE Activity number, I should be able to go to <a
href="http://cgmix.uscg.mil/IIR/IIRSearch.aspx">Incident Investigation
Reports (IIR) Search</a> and get the report.  Entering 3095030 in the
search field currently gives no results.  Hopefully, the Cusco Busan
report will end up in the IIR Search page soon.
