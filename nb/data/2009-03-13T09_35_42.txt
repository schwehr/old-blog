Yesterday, Brian C. showed me how easy it is to access SQLite data
from inside of Matlab.  Looking at my code to access the same database
in Python with sqlite3, made me look for an easier way.  I looked at
SqlAlchemy and was starting to wonder why it was such a pain to
specify all these object relationships.  Then I saw <a
href="http://www.sqlalchemy.org/trac/wiki/SqlSoup">SqlSoup</a>.  As
usual, I'm working with AIS data from msgs 1-3 and 5.
