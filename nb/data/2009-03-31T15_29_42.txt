Back in January, I talked about <a
href="http://schwehr.org/blog/archives/2009-01.html#e2009-01-04T17_48_16.txt">AIS
Encoding with JSON</a> and <a
href="http://schwehr.org/blog/archives/2009-01.html#e2009-01-12T18_47_02.txt">AIS
Binary Message queuing format</a> for the USCG Fetcher-Formatter.
Here is a supper simple format for AIS messages that is more like the
JSON format that is a lot simpler than the Maritime Domain Awareness
(MDA) Data Sharing Community of Interest (DS COI) by Tollefson et al.:
<a href="www.uscg.mil/hq/cg9/NAIS/RFP/SectionJ/MDA-COI-vocab.pdf">Data
Management Working Group Spiral 2 Vocabulary Handbook Version 2.0.2
Final Release 15 November 2007</a> [PDF]

Being that this is XML, you can drop in other things with easy.  For
example, you could include the binary 011011 representation, AIS
channel (A or B), a BBM or ABM command to transmit the message, the
VDM message, any of the other information that was in the
Fetcher-Formatter queuing metadata (bounding box, priority), or what
ever else you need.
