Turns out, it is just easier to go read a bag on my own with python.  I just added <a href="http://code.google.com/p/h5py/">h5py</a> to fink.
<pre><tt><i><font color="#9A1900">#!/usr/bin/env python</font></i>
<!-- -->
<b><font color="#000080">import</font></b> h5py
<!-- -->

<!-- -->
