In python, is there a better way to build a dictionary for a lookup table pulled
from sqlite3?  Yup! It's a little weird, but this makes it a one
liner (aside from the imports and db setup):
<pre>import sqlite3
<!-- -->
cx = sqlite3.connect('classb.db3')
cx.row_factory = sqlite3.Row 
<!-- -->
mmsi_prefixes = dict([tuple(row) for row in  cx.execute('SELECT code,country FROM mmsi_prefix;')])</pre>
The resulting dictionary:
<pre>{
  512: u'New Zealand', 
  750: u'Guyana', 
  514: u'Cambodia (Kingdom of)', 
  515: u'Cambodia (Kingdom of)', 
  516: u'Christmas Island (Indian Ocean)', 
  ...
}</pre>
