<a href="http://www.fosters.com/apps/pbcs.dll/article?AID=/20090412/GJNEWS_01/704129855/">Time to ditch your DVDs? Alternative delivery catches on for movies, TV</a> [Fosters]

We've already ditched Cable and Satellite TV.  The main thing missing
is decent news.  Like CNN did to Cable many years ago, someone needs
to build a decent on demand news system for Netflix and other
streaming systems.  I'd definitely pay a couple dollars a month for
good streaming news video... especially if there were a way to pick
a few categories of news.  Why not build a news cast for me based on
my preferences including amount of available time?
