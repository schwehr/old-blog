<img title="AIS transceivers" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-04/many-ais-devices.jpg"/>

As my co-author  Brian pointed out, these units don't really operatate as
<i>transponders</i> very often.  They probably should be refered to as
<i>transcievers</i>.  If you ask Dictionary.com about <a
href="http://dictionary.reference.com/browse/transponder">transponder</a>:
<pre><b>transponder</b>
a radio, radar, or sonar transceiver that automatically transmits a
signal upon reception of a designated incoming signal.
<!-- -->
Origin:
1940-45; trans(mitter) + (res)ponder</pre>
So only in the case when the unit is being interogated, does these
devices work as a transponder.
