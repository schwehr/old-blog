What timing.  I just found out that National Geographic has an article
on the CCOM/JHC Law of the Sea mapping this month: <a
href="http://ngm.nationalgeographic.com/2009/05/healy/funk-text">Healy
Mapping Mission - Arctic Landgrab</a>.

I'm just now finishing up a short project this week to put the Healy
<a
href="http://mgds.ldeo.columbia.edu/healy/reports/aloftcon/2009/">Aloftcon
images</a> into Google Earth such that you can see the vessel track
and the hourly images that are sent over the satellite link:

<a href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/healy/">Healy Aloftcon in Google Earth</a>

I've got a small kml file with a NetworkLink that checks every 10 minutes: <a href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/healy/healy-aloftcon-updating.kml">healy-aloftcon-updating.kml</a>.  I could figure out how to tell Google Earth to only check just after I run the update cron job every hour.

<img title="Healy in Google Earth" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-04/healy-ge-1.png"/>

<img title="Healy in Google Earth" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-04/healy-ge-2.png"/>

I'm also creating a <a href="http://georss.org/simple">GeoRSS Simple</a> feed that you can use to see the
images.  <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/healy/healy-aloftcon-latest.georss">healy-aloftcon-latest.georss</a>.
I've likely made mistakes in creating this feed as this is my first
try.  If you know GeoRSS and can offer advice, I am all ears.  Things like
specifying coordinates in y,x threw me off.
