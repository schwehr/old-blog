Last week, <a
href="http://brillig.org/people/acd/images/Arctic98/">Alex (ACD)</a>
gave me a hand evaluating the potential to pull positions out of the
text written on the Healy Aloftcon images.  He suggested looking at <a
href="http://jocr.sourceforge.net/">GOCR/JOCR</a> and if that didn't
work, to try <a
href="http://code.google.com/p/tesseract-ocr/">tesseract-ocr</a>

I was able to give GOCR a try with moderate success, but don't have
the time to look into it more.  I started with an aloftcon image: <a
href="http://mgds.ldeo.columbia.edu/healy/reports/aloftcon/2009/20090201-1601.jpeg">20090201-1601.jpeg</a>

The first line looks like it might be seeing the text and after that,
it degenerates into interpreting the noise.  We then tried cropping the
image and making it gray scale.

<img title="Text for OCR" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-04/gray-crop.jpg"/>
<pre>% <b>gocr gray-crop.jpg</b>
=C 00uuu=06i0csJ;000 =000 ci ,v c rW,2,M'-'!   er!n,.tr 3;x4X</pre>
It probably does not help that there are two different fonts here and that
there are substantial JPEG compression artifacts.  Croping to just the
position that I'd like to parse doesn't really help.

<img title="Text for OCR" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-04/gray-crop2.jpg"/>
<pre>% <b>gocr gray-crop2.jpg</b>
, lt,3 e'l,,,,</pre>
However, thresholding the image has better results, but is not quite there.

<img width="150" title="Text for OCR" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-04/gray-crop3.jpg"/>
<pre>% <b>gocr gray-crop3.jpg</b>
48 1 3.C N</pre>
Closer.  Trying to constrain the characters does not appeart to change how it is matching.
<pre>% <b>gocr -C "0123456789.NSEW " gray-crop3.jpg</b>
48 1 3. N</pre>
And changing the certainty still does not let it pick up the 0.
<pre>% <b>gocr -a 0 -C "0123456789.NSEW " gray-crop3.jpg</b>
48 1 3. N</pre>
Setting the space width helps with the spacing, but still doesn't get the 0.
<pre>% <b>gocr -a 0 -C "0123456789.NSEW " gray-crop3.jpg -s 9</b>
48 13. N</pre>
