BODY:
I couldn't figure out how to get Photoshop to allow me to paste an
image into the layer mask in Photoshop, so I decided it was time to
pull out the big guns.  I talked to <a
href="http://www.ainet.com/hagen/">Joel Hagen</a> and two minute later
I had what I wanted.  I've worked with Joel on a number of spacecraft
missions over the last 12 years... Joel is a master of design with
Photoshop.  Here is generally what I did:

First, I started with a photo of a Right Whale tail.  I turned the
image from color to gray scale.  Then I altered the levels by doing
Image-&gt;Adjust-&gt;Levels.  I pulled in the two outer bounds of the
image and then tweaked the outer bounds to give a mostly black tail,
some water highlights, and otherwise white image.  I used the eraser
to knock down the left over sky artifacts.  I used the the blur tool
on the water to soften up the deep shadows that I had created.
Finally, I touched up the whale to remove the markings that would
identify the individual whale.

Then I copied the image to a separate buffer and did
Image-&gt;Adjustments-&gt;Invert.  I copied this to the clipboard.

Then I went back to the original layer, made the image RGB and hit the
"Add layer mask" icon on the bottom of the Layers view (small red
circle).  Then I switched over to the Channels view.

<img title="Photoshop Layer Mask" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-04/layer-mask-1.png"/>

Then I had to go to the "Layer 2 Mask", activate the layer to get the
eyeball on the left.  I then selected the layer so that it was light
blue and did a paste.

<img title="Photoshop Layer Mask" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-04/layer-mask-2.png"/>

This shows the process with early on the left and a final on the right.

<img title="Photoshop Layer Mask" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-04/layer-mask-right-whale-tail.png"/>

I might have to do a bit more work for this to come out right in
Google Earth, but I am very pleased with the results.
