Thanks to a Googalite for telling me how I can set the time slider for
a dataset.  Before, I cut out the TimeStamp nodes because the view was
coming up with no whales visible and it was confusing.  By adding the
gx namespace and setting an overall view with a gx:TimeSpan, the placemarks
open up and set the time range to show all the whale sightings.
