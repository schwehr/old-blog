A while back, I posted a comment on Mac OSX Hints to this post: <a href="http://www.macosxhints.com/article.php?story=20090426003359543">10.5: Back to My Mac fails to start due to incorrect time</a>

Rob Griffiths has followed up with a Macworld blog post: <a
href="http://www.macworld.com/article/140509/2009/05/timeservers.html">How
to use a pool of time servers</a>.  Rob is kind enough to credit me down at the bottom of the post:
<pre>Thanks to Mac OS X Hints reader Kurt Schwehr for today's tip.</pre>
Also, to restart ntp on the mac from the command line (at least in 10.5):
<pre>sudo launchctl stop org.ntp.ntpd
sudo launchctl start org.ntp.ntpd</pre>
