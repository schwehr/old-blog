Last week there was much discussion about iPhone applications for mariners and this morning, I saw Capt Ben Smith looking at tides on his Nokia N800.  He was kind enough to let me share a photo of the device.  This is a port of XTides called <a href="http://mike.yi.org/projects/gtktide/">gtktide</a>

<img title="gtktide on a Nokia N800" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-05/nokia-n800-tides.png"/>

Last week, the folks at NOAA said that in recent months, two thirds of
their downloads of NOAA raster nautical charts (RNC) are coming from
the iPhone via the <a href="http://www.inavx.net/">iNavX</a>
application.  This little application will even display AIS targets.

<img title="iNavX electronic navigation system for the iPhone" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-05/iphone-inavx.png"/>

I encourage developers of mobile applications to look for more ideas
in the maritime domain!  Now we just need the iShipScience application
that monitors all the science systems on UNOLS vessels.

And... the iPhone has a tide application too... <a href="http://www.tidegraph.com/">TideGraph</a>

<img title="iPhone TideGraph" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-05/iphone-tide-graph.png"/>

And yet I don't have an iPhone, gPhone, or a Nokia N800... I'm
definitely falling behind the leading edge.
