BODY:
I while back, I requested a new gnuplot feature: <a
href="http://sourceforge.net/tracker/?func=detail&atid=352055&aid=2753994&group_id=2055">sql
queries (e.g. sqlite)</a> or some sort of more direct way to access
sqlite without dropping temporary files on the disk.  Juergen was kind
enough to point me to "help plot special-filename" in gnuplot.  It isn't
totally what I was thinking, but it is definitely much better than what
I had before.  One drawback is that this is awefully hard to debug what
is going wrong... turned out that I've got NULLs in my depth values when
there is not CTR NMEA report from the Healy.
<pre>% <b>gnuplot</b>
gnuplot&gt; <b>set term x11</b> # Aquaterm doesn't allow rotation with splots
gnuplot&gt; <b>plot '&lt; sqlite3 healy.db3 "SELECT lat,-depth FROM healy_summary WHERE depth IS NOT NULL;" | tr "|" "  "' t 'depth'</b> </pre>
<img title="2D gnuplot from sqlite" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-05/healy-gnuplot-depth-lat.png"/>
<pre>gnuplot&gt; <b>splot '&lt; sqlite3 healy.db3 "SELECT lon,lat,-depth FROM healy_summary WHERE depth IS NOT NULL;" | tr "|" "  "' t 'depth'</b></pre>
<img title="3D splot with gnuplot" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-05/healy-gnuplot-splot-depth.png"/>

I was really hoping to just transition to matplotlib, but I keep coming
back to gnuplot.
