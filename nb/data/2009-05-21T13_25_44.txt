The USCG has it's own survey planning tool for Windows: <a href="http://walrus.wr.usgs.gov/marfac/YonavSoftware.html">YoNav</a>

David Finlayson just put a newer version up here and mentioned it on
the mbsystem mailing list:

<a href="http://walrus.wr.usgs.gov/ftp/pub/users/davidf/YoNavII/">http://walrus.wr.usgs.gov/ftp/pub/users/davidf/YoNavII/</a>

<pre>We at the USGS Coastal and Marine Geology group have a fully
featured survey planning and operations tool called YoNav. It is
basically a drop in replacement for WinFrog or HyPack (well, the
survey-centric stuff).</pre>
