Ever wonder what drives those menus that RPM and DEB packages use then
you have to configure the system for a new package?  I noticed a "<a
href="http://nixdoc.net/man-pages/Linux/whiptail.1.html">whiptail</a>"
process running on a linux box... turns out this is the deal.
<pre>% <b>whiptail --yesno "Is the sky blue?" 10 30</b></pre>
I'm sure this is a curses based system, but it sure is easier to use.
