We needed a shoreline CSV file of Mendums Pond, so I made a quick one
from Google Earth by adding polygons and pulled the coordinates out of
the kml...  I check the georeferencing with a GPS coordinate at the
end of the pier and it matched within 10 meters or so (not being
overly careful)... the georeferencing here is probably good to 5
meters.
