And just as we were finishing up, Hydro International announced two
orders of Gavia AUV's: <a href="http://www.hydro-international.com/news/id3215-First_Gavias_AUVs_for_Oil_and_Gas.html">First Gavias AUVs for Oil and Gas</a>
<pre>An order has recently been placed by NCS Survey for two Gavia AUVs
with a capital expenditure of GBP1.5m - one 500m-rated system and one
1,000m rated system. These systems are specifically aimed at the
shallow water market and are understood to be the first Gavia AUVs to
enter the Oil & Gas market as a service provided by a survey
contractor.</pre>
