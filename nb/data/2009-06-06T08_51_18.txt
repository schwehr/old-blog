This week was my first time really using wireshark.  I've used tcpdump
forever, but wireshark is a really great tool when you can run a
graphical application.  Here is the screen showing a UDP broadcast of
the RTK GPS position of one of the two buoys on Mendum's Pond.  This
message will drive the AIS zone message that will announce the buoy's
position to vessels in the area.
