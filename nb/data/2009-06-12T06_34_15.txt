For waters around countries running a rescue operations, this kind of
thing is possible.  But for high seas, the current legal restrictions
around AMVER are likely to make this kind of thing difficult.  A
country is allowed to receive the positions from vessels with its flag
and any vessel within 1000 (km, nautical miles, m ?) of its shores.
This will make the system less useful than it could be.

Also, if any country is willing to share its LRIT data with me, please
contact me.  I don't mind if the data is delayed.  It's a shame that
there isn't a clause in the rules that allow full release of the
position data with a delay (say 6 months or a year).
