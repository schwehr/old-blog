AIS is pretty handy but it can't tell you everything.  A case in
point: Last night, the Torm Mary ground in Portsmouth, NH.  Thankfully,
no one was hurt and there wasn't anything spilled.

<a href="http://www.fosters.com/apps/pbcs.dll/article?AID=/20090612/GJNEWS_01/706129901/">Oil tanker moored after striking bottom in the Piscataqua River</a>
<pre>...
The tanker struck bottom around 10:30 p.m. Coast Guard station
Portsmouth Harbor dispatched a 25-foot response boat crew at 11:19
p.m. after receiving a report that Torm Mary's stern had struck the
bottom of the riverbed.
...</pre>
It helps to have the full vessel on the display, but looking at the
raw position information, it really is hard to see what is going on.
