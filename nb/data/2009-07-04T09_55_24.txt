BODY:
Virgil just sent me a note that he has updated his <a
href="http://earthnc.com/chartviewer/">EarthNC ChartViewer</a> web map
interface to use the latest in browser geolocation.  If you are using
Firefox 3.5 (HTML 5) it will ask you at the top to share your
position.  For other browsers, it will try a couple of other methods.
Once it gets your position, the chart view will snap over to your
location.
