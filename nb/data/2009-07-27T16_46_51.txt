Sean was kind enough to take a look at what I did.  He wondered about
the &gt;div&lt;, but concluded that the xhtml div is the right way.
So if validates, but is this the right answer?  I don't think so.

First, I really don't understand what an <a href="http://www.ietf.org/rfc/rfc3987.txt">IRI</a> is supposed to look like
in the <i>id</i> field.  I ended up copying what Sean did in his feed
by making it
"tab:<i>domain_name</i>,<i>year</i>:<i>username</i>:<i>key</i>".  For
the feed, I made the key be a unique string ("healysci") concatonated
with the unix time.  For an entry, I added an MD5 sum of the content.
These IDs are unique, but are they right.  The specification is not easy
to follow.

Next question... if I have a Simple GeoRSS and a GML GeoRSS, should
the IDs match or be different?

Now for the real question: Will it work in practice?  My image feed for
the Healy that has been running since April working in Google Maps:

<a href="http://maps.google.com/maps?q=http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/healy/healy-aloftcon-latest.georss">http://maps.google.com/maps?q=http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/healy/healy-aloftcon-latest.georss</a>

<img title="RSS 1.0 w/ GeoRSS in Google Maps" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-07/googlemaps-healy-georss-works.png"/>

Now if I try to put in the Atom feed, it tanks:

<a href="http://maps.google.com/maps?q=http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/healy/feeds/healy-science-simple.atom">http://maps.google.com/maps?q=http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/healy/feeds/healy-science-simple.atom</a>

<img title="Atom w/ Simple GeoRSS in Google Maps" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-07/googlemaps-healy-georss-broken.png"/>

For starters, I was doing <i>lon lat</i> but Simple GeoRSS wants
<i>lat lon</i>.  I fixed that, but still no luck.  So what is the typo
in point?
