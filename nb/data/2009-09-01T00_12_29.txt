BODY:
SQLiteCC is definitely dead, and it would be nice to have another tool that just works.

I just checked out <a
href="http://pysqlitegui.ospdev.net/">pySQLiteGUI</a> and I'm pretty
impressed with what Milad Rastian did in just under 900 lines of
python.  It doesn't do everything, but it's still pretty nifty.  It
doesn't do any sort of paging, so with a database of any reasonable
size, it will be really slow.
