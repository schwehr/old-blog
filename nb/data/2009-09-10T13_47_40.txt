These videos are definitely getting noticed.  <a href="http://www.hydro-international.com/news/id3413-Metre_Plume_Discovered.html">1400 Metre Plume Discovered</a> [Hydro International]
<pre>Fledermaus' mid-water visualisation technology has helped scientists
to discover a 1400m plume off the northern California (USA)
margin. While on a cruise to test the new Kongsberg EM302 multibeam
sonar in May 2009, the NOAA ship Okeanos Explorer, discovered a 1400m
high plume rising from the seafloor. The feature was noticed in the
online display of the water-column data of the sonar, and further
analysed in the new Fledermaus mid-water visualisation tool.
...</pre>
