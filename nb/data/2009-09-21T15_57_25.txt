Thanks to the folks at <a href="http://gcaptain.com">gCaptian</a>, I have just activated a <a href="http://www.findmespot.com">SPOT
Messenger</a> GPS to Satellite hand held tracking system.  Right now,
the device is just sitting in my car outside of the building at work.
We shall see how it does out at sea!  The device seems simple enough, but blinking LEDs are a bit confusing.  After seeing 4 position reports from the same location, I would saw that this thing does not have <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wide_Area_Augmentation_System">WAAS</a>.
