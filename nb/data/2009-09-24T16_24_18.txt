Some photos from around the ship...

The science area.  The ship has 3 rows of screens in the front of the
room.  The SIS display controlling the EM122 multibeam and the Knudsen
chirp subbottom profiler.

<img title="Langseth" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-09/langseth-science-lab.jpg"/>

The computer server room that supports all those screens in the

<img title="Langseth" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2009-09/langseth-machine-room.jpg"/>
