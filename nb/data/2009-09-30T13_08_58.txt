I've created a Google Earth visualization of the 2009 Healy cruise for
the U.S. Law of the Sea mapping.  Thanks to the LDEO team for
producing the data feeds that I used for this visualization.  The
visualization includes the ship track in orange, a time lapse video
via YouTube, the aloftcon images at each point, and the hourly science
summary that include the weather and water depth.
