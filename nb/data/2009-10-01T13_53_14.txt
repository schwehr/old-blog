This took a little while to sort out.  I want to be able to use proj
to convert to and from the Healy Law of the Sea multibeam data.  That
far north, geographic (<a
href="http://spatialreference.org/ref/epsg/4326/">EPSG:4326</a>) is
terrible.  They use a North Polar Stereographic project that is
centered on -160 degrees longitude with a latitude of true scale of
75N.  Thankfully, I have some information to help me out.  First,
Brian C. has created files for each line that have the data in
geographic, the usual 0 centered north stereographic, and the 160W
centered version used for the Healy data.
<pre>% <b>head -2 *.txt</b>
==&gt; 2009_241_depth.txt &lt;==

-117.1904645 83.8008067 -605972.446 311300.061 2709.800

Plus Larry had this in his Fledermaus projection list that he created.  And
it is fantastic that Fledermaus is using proj!
<pre>PROJCS["FPC_WGS_84_PS__75_160_HEALY",
GEOGCS["FG_WGS_84",
DATUM["FD_WGS_1984",
SPHEROID["WGS 84",6378137,298.257223563,AUTHORITY["EPSG","7030"]],AUTHORITY["EPSG","6326"]],
PRIMEM["Greenwich",0,AUTHORITY["EPSG","8901"]],
UNIT["degree",0.01745329251994328,AUTHORITY["EPSG","9122"]],AUTHORITY["EPSG","4326"]],
UNIT["meter",1,AUTHORITY["EPSG","9001"]],
PROJECTION["Polar_Stereographic"],
PARAMETER["latitude_of_origin",75],
PARAMETER["central_meridian",-160],
PARAMETER["scale_factor",1.0],
PARAMETER["false_easting",000000],
PARAMETER["false_northing",000000],
AUTHORITY["EPSG","32661"],
AXIS["Easting",
UNKNOWN],
AXIS["Northing",
UNKNOWN]]</pre>
Using the above information and looking at <A
href="http://spatialreference.org/ref/epsg/32661/">EPSG:32661</a>, I
was able to figure out first the line for north polar stereographic:
<pre><b>echo "-117.1741715 83.8010313" | proj +proj=stere +lat_0=90 +lat_ts=90 +lon_0=0 +k=0.994 +x_0=0 +y_0=0 +ellps=WGS84 +datum=WGS84 +units=m +no_defs</b>
-612841.39      314608.54</pre>
And for the Healy data (lat_ts == Latitude of True Scale):
<pre><b>echo "-117.1741715 83.8010313" | proj +proj=stere +lat_0=90 +lat_ts=75 +lon_0=-160 +k=0.994 +x_0=0 +y_0=0 +ellps=WGS84 +datum=WGS84 +units=m +no_defs</b>
463082.24       -499631.35</pre>
Now I can use invproj to pull data from the project back to geographic.
