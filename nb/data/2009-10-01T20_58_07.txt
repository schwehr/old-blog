BODY:
I've updated <a href="http://trac.osgeo.org/proj/">proj</a> to 4.7.0 in fink.  With this update, the change
notice says that projection EPSG:3857 is now in place for the Google
Merc.  As a result, I'm dropping the patch for Google as 900913.  If
you still need it, just uncomment these two lines in 
/sw/fink/10.4/unstable/main/finkinfo/sci/proj.info:
<pre>  #echo "# Google Mercator projection" &gt;&gt; nad/epsg
  #echo "&lt;900913&gt; +proj=merc +a=6378137 +b=6378137 +lat_ts=0.0 +lon_0=0.0 +x_0=0.0 +y_0=0 +k=1.0 +units=m +nadgrids=@null +no_defs" &gt;&gt; nad/epsg</pre>
<a href="http://gfoss.blogspot.com/2009/09/new-proj-470-library-available.html">New PROJ 4.7.0 library available</a>
