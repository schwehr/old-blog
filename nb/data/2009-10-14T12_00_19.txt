TITLE: Apple not testing hardware before returning apple care repaired computers
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 10.14.2009 12:00
DESC: Dear Apple, WTF?
-----
BODY:
In general, I have been happy with Apple Care support for my Macs.  I
call the 800 number, get an overnight mailer the next day, and get the
hardware back in a couple days.  At this point, my MacBook Pro circa
2007 is pretty much an entirely new machine except for the outer
shell.  However, this time, I decided to try the Hardware Tests before
doing anything more with this machine.  It was definitely a lemon
since day 1.  I put in the <b>original</b> OS disk that originally
came with the computer, and shutdown the machine.  I then powered on
the laptop while holding down the 'd' key.  This boot into the "Apple
Hardware Test".  I selected the default test (not the extended test).
Three minutes later I had an error result: "4SNS/1/40000000: TG0D" (or
TGOD?).  Dear Apple, please use a font that puts a slash through
zeros!  This apparently means that one of the temperature sensors is
not properly hooked up on the motherboard (aka logicboard).  It would
have been nice for Apple to run this test before sending me the laptop
back.

For the extended test, it gave the same error.  The run time turned
out to be 1 hour 36 minutes on a 2.4GHz Core2Duo with 4GB of 667MHz
RAM.
