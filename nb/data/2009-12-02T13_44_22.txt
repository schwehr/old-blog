I just gave gdal (svn as of today) a spin with its new Bathymetry
Attributed Grid (BAG) support.  I will be following up with more, but this is
a good beginning.  Note that gdal 1.6.3 does not include BAG support.  Thanks
to Howard Butler (hobu) for some quick fixups that got me up and running.
