<li>H00001-H02000: Hydrographic Surveys</li>
<li>H02001-H04000: Hydrographic Surveys</li>
<li>H04001-H06000: Hydrographic Surveys</li>
<li>H06001-H08000: Hydrographic Surveys</li>

<li>H10001-H12000: Hydrographic Surveys</li>

<li>L02001-L04000: Lake surveys</li>

</ul>
