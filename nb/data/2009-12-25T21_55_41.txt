The UNH fish cages off of the Isle of Shoals in NH. Visualized in
Fledermaus by <a href="http://www.ivs3d.com/">IVS3D</a>. Midwater
visualization based off of research done in <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/GeoZui4D/">GeoZui4D</a>. Water column
data can provide "as built" documentation and performance metrics for
underwater structures.
