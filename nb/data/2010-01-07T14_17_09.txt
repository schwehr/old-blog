I broke down and got myself a copy of iShowU HD.  It works pretty
well.  It used about 1.5 GB of space while recording a 4.5 min video,
but that gets reclaimed when the movie is finished.  The results are
quite nice.  Here is a the same AGU BAG visualization in Google Earth.
I really should make a Google Earth tour for the visualization.
