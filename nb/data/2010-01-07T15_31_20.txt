TITLE: Location aware emacs org-mode and geotagging?
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 01.07.2010 15:31
DESC: Possibilities
-----
BODY:
While working on a entry in my emacs org-mode work log, I wrote down
the position of an instrument that one of our engineers just deployed
in the field.  I suddenly realized that I had not seen anything in org
that implied that is location aware or able to tag where things are.
The closest that I have seen is in the calendar mode for <a
href="http://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/manual/html_node/emacs/Sunrise_002fSunset.html#Sunrise_002fSunset">sunrise
and sunset times</a>:
<pre>(setq calendar-latitude 43.135397)
(setq calendar-longitude -70.939533)
(setq calendar-location-name "Durham, NH")</pre>
But what I really want is the ability to tag locations in the
document.  Then I could ask for log entryies that are within 1 km of
say the light house in New Castle, NH.  There may already be a
location tagging strategy, but I can't find anything in there.
Perhaps something based on <a
href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Well-known_text">WKT</a> would
work.  This would allow other shapes, not just points.  However, I don't really want to have to go look up where I am
currently at with a GPS, an address geocoding tool, or with location
detection service.  It has to be possible to do something like the 
Firefox <a
href="http://www.mozilla.com/en-GB/firefox/geolocation/">geolocation
with Location-Aware Browsing</a>.   If only I were a decent lisp
programmer (working on that).

Then, once geotagging is possible, we could create a KML export mode
that would take the time and position tags to produce something like
my <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/resume/schwehr-resume.kml">Google
Earth Resume (GER)</a>.  Imagine a field scientist out with an iPhone
or Android phone taking notes in a org-mode style and able to tag
those notes (and photos) such that back in the office, they can export
a visualization of all of those notes.

I emailed the idea to the org-mode mailing list: <a
href="http://www.mail-archive.com/emacs-orgmode@gnu.org/msg21109.html">[Orgmode]
geolocation / geotagging in org-mode</a>

Open tools are great.  I just need more time to learn the stuff and
write code for what I'd like to be added.

Too bad Mano Marks' upcoming workshop on <a
href="http://randommarkers.blogspot.com/2010/01/workshop-working-with-geospatial-data.html">Working
with Geospatial Data Using Open Source Tools</a> is in Berkeley, CA (on the
wrong coast for me.)
