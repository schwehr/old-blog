BODY:
I have been watching the Stanford iPhone Winter 2010 class from
iTunesU on my XBox360.  I subscribed to the class in iTunes on my Mac.
I then start up Rivet.  Then in the XBox, under My Xbox -&gt; Video
Library.  I then navigate to my laptop's iTunes directory and select
the Lecture.

The picture is much better that the picture from my iPhone shows.
This is great as I have the video playing on the HDTV and I have the
laptop's screen free, plus it works fine with the laptop on wireless.
