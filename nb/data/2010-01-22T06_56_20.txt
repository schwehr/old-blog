Yesterday, I got the chance to go out to the LNG ship Explorer, which
is currently at the North East Gateway (NEG) terminal.  This was my
first time on a super tanker.  In the morning, I met the Gateway
Endeavor in Salem.

<img title="Gateway Endeavor workboat" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-01/gateway-endeavor.jpg"/>

We had a short ride out that included training on how to go through a
basket transfer.  You put your arms through the ropes and stand on the
outside of the orange ring at the bottom.  You throw your luggage
luggage in the middle.  Then they crane you from ship to ship.  It wasn't a big deal on such a nice day, but it must be pretty crazy if you have to do that in foul weather.

<img title="How we transfered to the LNG ship" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-01/explorer-transfer.jpg"/>

This is the ship that we transfered onto.  She is HUGE.  She is off
loading gas to the pipeline right now and providing natural gas to
heat homes in New England.

<img title="LNG ship Explorer" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-01/explorer-lng-ship.jpg"/>

We headed up to the bridge and got a look at the whole ship from up high.  You can see on the left that it extends out crazy far.

<img title="Trying to see the length of the whole ship" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-01/explorer-view-from-bridge.jpg"/>

Here is what the bridge looks like.  The Explorer is a very new ship
and bridge has a very clean design.  I noticed that pilot port for AIS
is right up front in the middle and has a power outlet right next to
it.

<img title="" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-01/explorer-bridge.jpg"/>

Here is me on the deck of the ship next to the piping that transfers
gas around the ship.

<img title="Kurt on the Explorer" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-01/explorer-kurt.jpg"/>

We were waiting for our crane ride off the ship to the awaiting
Manisee workboat that (eventually) took us back to Salem.  Apparently,
in 1986, the Manisee was used in the movie <a
href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0091680/">One Crazy Summer<a> when it used to be a car car ferry boat.

<img title="Manisee" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-01/manisee-workboat.jpg"/>

To finish off the day, I got a great view of the sun setting over Boston.
