This was always the big research goal: be able to motor long distances
to get to exciting new geologic targets.  Robotic exploration is really the

prefer Scott's blogging to the press release process.  Perhaps that comes from
being a part of the all night discussions on just how to craft particular 
press releases.  Or maybe it comes from me from being a former robot driver (Marsokhod and Nomad in Chile).  Yeah, I'm biased.
