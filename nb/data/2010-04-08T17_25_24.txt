One thing that is a bit weird with python's datetime module is that it
is hard to interate over a range of days.  I would think that this
should be trivial, but I can't find the simple pythonic solution.
However, this does give me a chance to create a "generator" in python
that is much simpler than the ones I've created in the past.
Hopefully everyone can follow this one.
<pre><tt><i><font color="#9A1900">#!/usr/bin/env python</font></i>
<!-- -->
<i><font color="#9A1900"># Python 2.4 or newer</font></i>
<b><font color="#000080">import</font></b> datetime
<b><font color="#0000FF">def</font></b> <b><font color="#000000">date_generator</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font>start_date<font color="#990000">,</font> end_date<font color="#990000">):</font>
    cur_date <font color="#990000">=</font> start_date
    dt <font color="#990000">=</font> datetime<font color="#990000">.</font><b><font color="#000000">timedelta</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font>days<font color="#990000">=</font><font color="#993399">1</font><font color="#990000">)</font>
    <b><font color="#0000FF">while</font></b> cur_date <font color="#990000">&lt;</font> end_date<font color="#990000">:</font>
        yield cur_date
        cur_date <font color="#990000">+=</font> dt
<!-- -->
<b><font color="#0000FF">for</font></b> d <b><font color="#0000FF">in</font></b> <b><font color="#000000">date_generator</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font>datetime<font color="#990000">.</font><b><font color="#000000">datetime</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font><font color="#993399">2006</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">11</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">27</font><font color="#990000">),</font> datetime<font color="#990000">.</font><b><font color="#000000">datetime</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font><font color="#993399">2006</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">12</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">2</font><font color="#990000">)):</font>
    <b><font color="#0000FF">print</font></b> d
<!-- -->
<b><font color="#0000FF">print</font></b> <font color="#FF0000">'case 2'</font>
<b><font color="#0000FF">for</font></b> d <b><font color="#0000FF">in</font></b> <b><font color="#000000">date_generator</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font>datetime<font color="#990000">.</font><b><font color="#000000">datetime</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font><font color="#993399">2006</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">12</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">27</font><font color="#990000">),</font> datetime<font color="#990000">.</font><b><font color="#000000">datetime</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font><font color="#993399">2007</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">1</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">2</font><font color="#990000">)):</font>
    <b><font color="#0000FF">print</font></b> d
<!-- -->
<i><font color="#9A1900"># Alternative without a generator:</font></i>
start_date <font color="#990000">=</font> datetime<font color="#990000">.</font><b><font color="#000000">date</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font><font color="#993399">2006</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">12</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">29</font><font color="#990000">)</font>
end_date   <font color="#990000">=</font> datetime<font color="#990000">.</font><b><font color="#000000">date</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font><font color="#993399">2007</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">1</font><font color="#990000">,</font><font color="#993399">2</font><font color="#990000">)</font>
<b><font color="#0000FF">for</font></b> a_date <b><font color="#0000FF">in</font></b> <font color="#990000">[</font>datetime<font color="#990000">.</font>date<font color="#990000">.</font><b><font color="#000000">fromordinal</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font>d_ord<font color="#990000">)</font> <b><font color="#0000FF">for</font></b> d_ord <b><font color="#0000FF">in</font></b> <b><font color="#000000">range</font></b><font color="#990000">(</font>start_date<font color="#990000">.</font><b><font color="#000000">toordinal</font></b><font color="#990000">(),</font> end_date<font color="#990000">.</font><b><font color="#000000">toordinal</font></b><font color="#990000">())</font> <font color="#990000">]:</font>
    <b><font color="#0000FF">print</font></b> a_date</tt></pre>
