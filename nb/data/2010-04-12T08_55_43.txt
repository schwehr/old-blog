AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 04.12.2010 08:55
DESC: aloftcon

BODY:
The USCGC ice breaker Healy is back underway.  The feed is not yet
back in the Oceans layers of Google Earth, but you can download the <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/healy/healy-aloftcon-latest.kml">updating
kml</a> and watch the Healy getting back into shape after being in the
ship yard.  The <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/healy/">feeds are online</a>
and you are welcome to check out the <a
href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/software/healy-py/">healy-py
source code</a>.  The science gear isn't online and the images may not
always be updating as the team works on the systems.  I just do the
visualizations, it is the <a
href="http://ilab.ldeo.columbia.edu/">LDEO folks</a> who do all the
work.
