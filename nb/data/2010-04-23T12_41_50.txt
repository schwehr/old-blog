I haven't looked at the details of this vessel, but when parked at the
doc under the Tobin bridge, the GPS feeding its class A AIS position
reports had some really bad position estimates.  Outside of that, it
looks to have done a great job.  The AIS unit should have broadcast that it did not have a GPS lock in these cases.

<img title="MMSI 366998520 - Odd GPS positions" withgrayborder="True"
src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-04/ais-bad-gps.png"/>

I now have ais_decimate_traffic working.  It defaults to 200 meters
between reports or 10 minutes, which ever comes sooner.  
<pre><b>ais_decimate_traffic.py -f 2009-filtered.db3 -x -71.155 -X -69.36 -y 41.69 -Y 42.795</b>
keep_cnt:  275997
toss_cnt:  897231
outside_cnt:  51287</pre>
The above image is not filtered, but you can see below that filtering
keeps the general sense of when and when the ship was but with about
and order of magnitude fewer points.
