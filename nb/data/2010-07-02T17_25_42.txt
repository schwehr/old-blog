Right now, we have two cruises that are going through the CCOM
telepresense cruise.  Turns out that the audio was left on for the
Okeanus Explorer, so they were listening in to discussions for the
Nautilus.  What a really cool accident!  To have one ship in the
Pacific and the other in the Aegean (?) hearing each other while
talking to multiple shore side stations.  Now all they need are some
IRC chat sessions.
