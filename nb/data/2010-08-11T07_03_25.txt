The Ocean Bytes blog just posted <a href="http://www.oceanbytes.org/2010/08/11/reson-seabat-8101-multibeam-echosounder/">Reson Seabat 8101 Multibeam Echosounder</a>these two videos of Brian Kidd that
show a Reson 8101 in use on the RV Hugh R. Sharp.  One comment on the
first video: Luciano coached me to refer to the intensity displays
from <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multibeam_Sonar">multibeam sonar</a> systems as "backscatter" rather than "sidescan".  A
traditional <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Side-scan_sonar">sidescan</a> does not know where the return comes from.
