BODY:
Through the magic of screenshots and photodraping, I have a working
solution.  I take a screenshot of Google Maps in an area with the topo
overlay turned on.  Then I do an image overlay in Google Earth and use
that as the texture to drape on the 3D globe.  Works pretty well.  I
would rather Google do this as a part of Google Earth, but this will
work until they do that.  My registration job is not perfect, but it is
good enough for now.

I saw online a company selling the USGS topo
DRG's for the US for $5500.  You have to be kidding me.
