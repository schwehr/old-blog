Thanks to Peter for pointing me to <a
href="http://libremap.org/">Libre Map</a> that has most USGS Topo
Quads online as GeoTiffs.  No cost.  This is exactly what I was
looking for and the quality appears to be better than what the
USGS is giving out in their GeoPDFs.
