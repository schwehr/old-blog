This last week, I spent two days with NOAA's Natioanl Geophysical Data Center (<a href="http://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/">NGDC</a>) in Boulder, CO.
What a spectacular location.  I was busy enough that I never got
a chance to blog for a while.  First the NOAA site.  The new building
has sandstone from a quarry nearby in the Flatiron mountains.  

<img title="Boulder" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-10/boulder-noaa.jpg"/>

Marcus gave me a quick tour of the building.  It's amazing how much
space there is in this very large building and the diversity of
research areas is overwhelming.  There is the weather forecasting
command center and near by the space weather command center.

<img title="Boulder NOAA" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-10/boulder-noaa-spaceweather-command.jpg"/>

<a href="http://www.nist.gov/">NIST</a> is located next door.  This is quite the government research area.

<img title="Boulder NIST" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-10/boulder-nist.jpg"/>

Sunrise on the flatirons...  The hotel was okay.  However, they had
bikes available for free and the mountains are right in front of you.
It was a short walk in to NOAA from the Hotel.

<img title="Boulder Best Western" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-10/boulder-hotel-view.jpg"/>

I went for a morning walk just before sunrise near Wonderland Lake on the north side of
Boulder.  You can't seem them in the iphone photos, but there were
lots of deer up on the hill.
