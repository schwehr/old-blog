BODY:
The new version of Azarus/Vuze comes with some crapware called "Tune
Up" or "TuneUp".  It does not warn you enough that the Tune Up package will be
installed at the same time until it's already done.  The uninstaller is burried.

<a
href="http://discussions.info.apple.com/message.jspa?messageID=12360783">Apple
Discussions: Topic : How do I uninstall TuneUp?</a>:
<pre>open ~/Library/Application\ Support/TuneUp_Support/TuneUp_Uninstaller</pre>
I made the mistake of trashing /Applications/TuneUp.  The uninstaller
is too stupid to handle the app being missing.  I also found this cruft:

<pre><b>mdfind TuneUp | grep $USER</b>
~/Library/Application\ Support/{TuneUp_Support,TuneUpMedia_Gecko}
~/Library/Preferences/TuneUpMediaPreferences/TuneUpPreferences.xml
~/Library/Preferences/com.TuneUpMedia.TuneUp.plist
~/Library/TuneUpMedia
~/Library/Caches/TuneUpMedia_Gecko
~/Library/Caches/ TuneUp   # Yes, these jokers put a space in there
~/Library/Preferences/TuneUpMediaPreferences
~/Library/Caches/com.TuneUpMedia.TuneUp
~/Library/iTunes/iTunes Plug-ins/TuneUp/TuneUp Visualizer.bundle
~/Library/iTunes/iTunes Plug-ins/TuneUp</pre>
Good riddance!
