I am working on being able to add graphs in my org-mode documents.  I
want to try ditaa, but first, I wanted to take a look at graphviz.
Graphviz has been on my "to try" list for a very long time.  The
basics are pretty easy, but it gets challenging if you want much in
the way of control.  I used my GIS workflow figure that I made with
PowerPoint last week as my example.  In the future, I will be using
graphviz to show sensor networks with their status and sensor values.

Here is how I ran all the different graphviz layout programs:
<pre>for mode in dot neato twopi circo fdp sfdp; do 
  ${mode} -Tpng gis.dot -o gis-${mode}.png
done</pre>
