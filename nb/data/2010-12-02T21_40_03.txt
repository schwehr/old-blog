Monica managed to get the camera to work on the home network.  This
trick may be to set the password to something other than admin.  You
have to do all the configuration and set the wireless system to use
through the ethernet connection.  The IPset program is only good to
tell it to use DHCP.  I never was able to get the secure view program
to work.

Once I had the info I needed from tech support, I was able to figure
out how to grab a frame.
<pre><b>wget http://192.168.0.13/cgi/jpg/image.cgi</b>
--2010-12-02 21:08:24--  http://192.168.0.13/cgi/jpg/image.cgi
Connecting to 192.168.0.13:80... connected.
HTTP request sent, awaiting response... 401 Unauthorized
Authorization failed.
<!-- -->
<b>curl --user admin:mypassword http://192.168.0.13/cgi/jpg/image.cgi &gt; test.jpg</b></pre>
The key is to know the IP address of the device and to also specify
your username (admin) and your new password.
