I have a general request to all groups (government, industry, and
academic) that have installations in the waters around Alaska: Please
consider transmitting your data to the USCG NAIS system.  I have
access to satellite coverage of the area, but it is infrequent.  I
have worked a bit with the folks in Barrow, AK, but there are a lot of
other sites in the arctic where people could forward AIS from.  This
is especially important for oil and gas companies in the Prudhoe Bay
and Nuiqsut area.  Other areas in AK that could really use AIS
receivers to contribute realtime data are: St. Lawrence Island,
Whales, Nome, and Point Hope.  However, any where that can contribute,
would be really helpful.  If you have a vessel up there (of any size)w,
please try to get at least a Class B transceiver on your vessel.

By passing this data to the USCG now, you will be helping to prepair
the government to respond to any sort of emergency up in your area.
Getting this type of infrastructure working during a crisis is much
more difficult.

If you would like to talk to me about this, I will will be a the <a
href="http://www.alaskamarinescience.org/workshops.html#gulf">Lessons
Learned from the Gulf of Mexico Oil Spill</a> session of the Alaska
Marine Science Symposium in Anchorage, AK on Jan 19.
