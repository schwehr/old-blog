AUTHOR: Kurt

BODY:
It was great timing that, while I was working with the NOAA team, they
released their new web page: <a
href="http://www.nefsc.noaa.gov/psb/surveys/index.html">North Atlantic
Right Whale Sighting Survey and Sighting Advisory System</a>.  The
release was announced by Christin: <a href="http://cbkhan.blogspot.com/2010/12/update-new-nefsc-website-is-live.html">{Update} New NEFSC website is live!!! PSB:NARWSS and SAS</a>.
<pre>New and improved with all sorts of juicy stuff including a Google
Earth map display of right whale sightings by season, yearly aerial
survey team reports, how to report a right whale sighting, and links
to other important right whale resources!</pre>
It's pretty awesome that they used my right whale icon.  I created
that right whale icon for anyone to use, so if you like it, feel free
to grab it.
