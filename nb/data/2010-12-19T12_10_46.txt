I found their online instructions for <a href="http://nuance.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/6117/~/how-to-uninstall-dictate-and%2For-macspeech-scribe">removing Dragon Dictate</a>, but here is
what I did:
<pre><b>cd ~/Library/Preferences/
ls | egrep -i 'nuance|speech|dragon|dictate'
rm com.dragon.dictate.plist
cd ~/Library/Application\ Support/
ls | egrep -i 'nuance|speech|dragon|dictate'
rm -rf Dragon
open /Applications</b># Throw Dragon Dictate in the trash</pre>
Don't forget to find your profile if one got created.
