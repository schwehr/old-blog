The Science of Blogging blog has a post titled: <a
href="http://scienceofblogging.com/how-readable-is-your-blog-a-quantitative-assessment-of-science-blog-accessibility/">How
readable is your blog? A quantitative assessment of science blog
accessibility</a>.  They talk about using the Google <b>Reading
Level</b> feature to evaluate your blog.  Is it good or bad that my
blog scores an "Intermediate"?  

<a href="http://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Aschwehr.org%2Fblog&tbs=rl%3A1">schwehr.org/blog reading level</a> by Google.

<img title="Google reading level for schwehr.org" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2010-12/blog-reading-level-1.png"/>

How did I do that?  I used "site:schwehr.org/blog" in a google search, select advanced search, 
and then selected to show reading level.
