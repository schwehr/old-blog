CNS 6000 Blue Force (BF) Class A for an L3 Desktop ATON unit at Cape
Cod.  That all seemed great except for the ATON reports AIS messages
received as ANVDM instead of AIVDM.  That <b>N</b> as the second letter
confuses some software.
