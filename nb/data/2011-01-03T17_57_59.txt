Basically, I stripped the whole thing down to be a bunch of do nothing stubs.

My test org-file looks (hello.org) like this:
<pre>Execute these two lines with C-x C-e
(require 'org-hello)
(org-hello-export)</pre>
But first, I needed to tweak my .emacs.  I checked out org-mode from
git and pointed to the experimental directory to get the org-export.el
file, which I also byte compiled with M-x emacs-lisp-byte-compile.
<pre>(setq load-path (cons "~/org-7.4/lisp" load-path))
(setq load-path (cons "~/org-7.4/contrib/lisp" load-path))
(require 'org-install)
<!-- -->
; Experimental section
(add-to-list 'load-path "~/projects/external/org-mode/EXPERIMENTAL/")
(require 'org-export)</pre>
Then when I started emacs and opened hello.org, I was able to execute ( C-x C-e ) the require and the hello export.  I then get a buffer that contains:
<pre>Hello</pre>
I know it's not exciting, but this at least gets me moving.
