I've never heard of the Handbook of Offshore Surveying until the Hydro
International note <a
href="http://www.hydro-international.com/news/id4512-Handbooks_of_Offshore_Surveying.html">Handbooks
of Offshore Surveying</a> announcing the 2nd edition.  At roughly <a href="http://www.skilltradebookstore.nl/index.php?page=shop.product_details&flypage=flypage_images1.tpl&product_id=24&category_id=7&option=com_virtuemart&Itemid=20">$500
for the set</a>, I'm not surprised that I haven't heard of it before.  If
anyone has reviewed it, please let me know so I can link to your
review.  I took a peak at the contents, but don't know any of the authors.

Skilltrade doesn't make it very easy to see the contents of the books,
so I've pulled them out here:
