This is way cool.  Thanks to ACD for pointing out <a
href="http://www.google.com/insights/search/">Insights for Search</a>
- a great tool by Google.  I gave it a quick try, and I quickly realized
that the results might be very hard to understand.  But with a little
leg work, it can be a very valuable tool.  I really like it.

The first query I tried was: <a
href="http://www.google.com/insights/search/#q=%22automatic%20identification%20system%22">Automatic Identification System</a>
This begs the question of how people generally refer to something.  If
there is a general change in the way people refer to a topic, that
might strongly influence the search results.  For example, if people
have been to switching to mostly searching for "AIS" over time, that
might explain the decrease.

I wanted to see something where I felt like I knew what the response
would likely be before I tried it - <a
href="http://www.google.com/insights/search/#q=%22deepwater%20horizon%22&date=1%2F2010%2013m&cmpt=q">Deepwater
Horizon</a> for the last year is a great example of the onset of a
search term and the fall off of interest.
