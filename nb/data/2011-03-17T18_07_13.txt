UNH has a policy about exactly how business can look and what
information they can contain.  Nuts!  My next card won't be done
through them.  I definitely want my web site on it (<a
href="http://schwehr.org">schwehr.org</a>) and I'm considering adding
the QR code for that on the back.  Turns out it is really easy to
generate QR codes.  Just go to <a
href="http://www.qrstuff.com/">qrstuff.com</a>.  Then people can just
pull the info into their phone with one of the many free apps.  Lots
of people misspell my last name, so this way, there won't be any
confusion...  What do you think?  Good or bad idea?  And no, I'm not
getting a QR code tatoo!
