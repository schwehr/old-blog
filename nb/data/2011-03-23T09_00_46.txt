TITLE: Michele Jacobi on Marine Debris
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 03.23.2011 09:00
DESC: Google LatLong blog

BODY:
Michele Jacobi of NOAA's <a
href="http://response.restoration.noaa.gov/">Office of Response and
Restoration (ORR)</a> has been working
at Google in Mt View for a while.  She just posted on the Google Lat
Long Blog: <a
href="http://google-latlong.blogspot.com/2011/03/marine-debris-is-everyones-problem.html">Marine
debris is everyone's problem</a>.  I met Michele while working on the
beginnings of ERMA.  She and Rob were the ones who really kicked off
ERMA.  Michele introduced me to problem of <a href="http://marinedebris.noaa.gov/">marine debris</a> in 2007 at the CRRC workshop on <a href="http://www.crrc.unh.edu/workshops/data_standards/">Environmental Response Data Collection Standards</a>.  Since that workshop the world has changed with
Android and iOS phones/tablets becoming common.  I now wonder if the
best solution for the data entry problem in remote areas could be
easily solved with these mobile devices (e.g. using <a
href="http://code.google.com/p/opendatakit/">Open Data Kit (ODK)</a> combined with portable
cellular data systems based on <a
href="http://openbts.sourceforge.net/">OpenBTS</a> and/or portable and
mobile wifi system (e.g. SWAP, <a
href="http://www.panbo.com/archives/2011/01/digital_yacht_boatranet_something_truly_different.html">BOATraNET</a>,
etc.).
