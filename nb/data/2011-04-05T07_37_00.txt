I just found the <a href="http://www.uscg.mil/proceedings/Spring2011/Spring%202011.pdf">Spring 2011 issue</a> of The Coast Guard Journal of Safety &amp; Security at Sea - Proceedings of the Marine Safety &amp Security Council.  This issue has a number of must read articles for me:

Maffia, Detweiler, Lahn, <i>Collaborating to Mitigate Risk</i>: Ports and Waterways Safety Assessment (PAWSA).

Burns, <i>Vessel Traffic Services as Information Managers</i>.

Cairns, <i>e-Navigation: Revolution and evolution</a>.

Jorge Arroyo: <i>The Automatic Identification System: Then, now, and in the future.</i>

Krouse and Berkson: <i>Coast Guard Cooperation in the Arctic: USCGC Healy's continental shelf mission.</i>

And there are more interesting reads...
