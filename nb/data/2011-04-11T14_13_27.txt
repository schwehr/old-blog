TITLE: ITU 1371-4 defining AIS is now a free download
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 04.11.2011 14:13
DESC: good news
-----
BODY:
I don't know when this happened, but I just found that <a href="http://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-M.1371-4-201004-I/en">ITU-R M.1371-4</a>,
released in April 2010 is a free download.  This is the document that
currently defines the core of AIS: "Technical characteristics for an
automatic identification system using time-division multiple access in
the VHF maritime mobile band.  The next thing ITU needs to do is adopt
an open license for their documents (e.g. Creative Commons Attribution
CC BY).

The IEC AIS documents are still closed and very pricy, but this is a
start!
Anyone know the story of how and when this happened?
