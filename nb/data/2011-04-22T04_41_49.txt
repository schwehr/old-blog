Today, we have Tim Cole from NOAA NEFSC coming to give a talk at CCOM.
I've been working with Tim and the rest of the crew of the <a
href="http://www.nefsc.noaa.gov/psb/surveys/">North Atlantic Right
Whale Sighting Survey and Sighting Advisory System (SAS)</a> for a
couple years now and you might recognize the Google Earth
visualization of the group's whale sightings on their web page from my
past web blogs.  We have been collaborating on some database work that
I hope to write about soon.

Come join us for Tim's talk - either in person or via the webinar!

Tim Cole - Research Fisheries Biologist

<b>Right Whale Distribution and Protection in the Gulf of Maine</b>
<!-- -->
webinar registration link: <a href="https://www2.gotomeeting.com/register/734726586">https://www2.gotomeeting.com/register/734726586</a>
<!-- -->
NOAA's Northeast Fisheries Science Center right whale aerial survey
program is dedicated to locating and recording the seasonal
distribution of right whales within U.S. federal waters north of 41º
20' N and east of 72&deg; 50' W.  The objectives of the surveys include:
(1) to provide right whale sighting locations to mariners to mitigate
whale/ship collisions, (2) to census offshore areas where dedicated
survey effort had been absent since at least 1992, and (3) to
photographically identify individual right whales found in these
offshore areas.  This talk will describe our survey methods and
findings, and discuss the ecology of right whales in the Gulf of
Maine.</pre>
