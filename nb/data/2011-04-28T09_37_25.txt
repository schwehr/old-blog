TITLE: Conferences should release bibtex files
AUTHOR: Kurt
DATE: 04.28.2011 09:37
DESC: making research easier
-----
BODY:
I think conferences owe it to their communities to release bibtex
files for the presentations and posters.  To lead (at least partially)
by example, I've been sitting in presentations this morning and
producing at bibtex file in JabRef.  Providing this information,
preferably with full abstracts and links to full PDFs, will hopefully
make research much easier for everyone.

If you know how to use bibtex files, you can download the bib file:

<a href="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2011-04/ushydro-conference.bib">ushydro-conference.bib</a>

If you'd like to see a pretty version exported from JabRef, take a
look at the HTML version.  

<a href="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2011-04/ushydro-conference.html">ushydro-conference.html</a>

I have put in abstracts when available and at least my paper has a
link to the full paper pdf.  The rest of papers, the URL just takes you to the US Hydro program.

<img title="Schwehr US Hydro paper"
withgrayborder="True"
src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2011-04/ushydro-schwehr-bibtex.png"/>
