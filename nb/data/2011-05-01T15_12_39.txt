I've released the latex and bibtex source to my US Hydro paper so that
others can use the bibtex and layout style should they choose and to
provide an example for how I organized the paper for those who are
curious.  While the text of the paper and the figures are <a
href="http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/3.0/">Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivs
3.0 Unported (CC BY-NC-ND 3.0)</a>, the formatting, makefile, bibtex
are up for use by anyone.  If you want to use the figures or some of
the text and need permission, please just contact me.

<a href="http://vislab-ccom.unh.edu/~schwehr/papers/2011-schwehr-ushydro-dwh.tar.bz2">2011-schwehr-ushydro-dwh.tar.bz2</a>
