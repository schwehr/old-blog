While the results of MS Photosynth from an iPhone 3GS (sorry, no 3G
support) is not nearly as good as what we do at JPL or NASA Ames with
mosaics, it is pretty awesomely handy and super easy.  An excellent
free phone app to have for any field scientist.  Who is going to be
first to see what happens from the deck of a ship (preferably underway
in a harbor)?  Should produce some interesting artifacts :)

Looks like exporting through facebook rips out the geotagging or there
never was any geotagging for this image.  I did answer that it was
okay to use location info in the app.
