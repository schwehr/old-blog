Briana showed me a really interesting demo last week using the Google
Maps v3 API.  You can now query the bathymetry (or topography) over a path.
Pretty cool!  And you could manage to pull out a grid.  Just a reminder that
Google's data is not in MLLW (Mean Low Low Water) datum, so you have yet another reason not use this data for any sort of navigation!  But it is handy to be able to demonstrate navigation type concepts.
