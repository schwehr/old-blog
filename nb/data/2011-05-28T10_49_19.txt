Yesterday was my first day (really 1/2 day) at sea in a very long
time.  We left from the UNH pier on the R/V Coastal Surveyor.

<img title="UNH Pier" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2011-05/unh-pier.jpg"/>

We had to pause for rush hour as a tall ship came through escorted by
two tugs (one from the Navy ship yard) with fire hoses going.  There
were tons of other ships following along.

<img title="Tall ship with many of the escorts not in view" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2011-05/tallship.jpg"/>

The fog got really thick after a while, so we had to head up river to be safe.

<img title="Newcastle Lighthouse with Fog" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2011-05/newcastle-lighthouse-fog.jpg"/>

Here is the current setup of the Coastal Surveyor's console with
Coastal Explorer.  It beeps and highlights military vessels in the
ship list when it sees them via AIS.  Ben showed me how the software
locks up forwarding NMEA to other serial ports when certain dialog
boxes are up.  Definitely file that as a bug for the otherwise
excellent ENC software.
