AUTHOR: Kurt

DESC: More reasons to want to go to google

BODY:
Frank Warmerdam, who has really pushed GDAL and Proj into becoming
fantasic tools, is <a
href="http://fwarmerdam.blogspot.com/2011/06/joining-google.html">Joining
Google</a>.  What that means for <a
href="http://www.gdal.org/">GDal</a>, <a
href="http://trac.osgeo.org/proj/">Proj.4</a> (aka just "proj"), <a
href="http://www.remotesensing.org/libtiff/">libtiff</a>,  <a
href="http://trac.osgeo.org/geotiff/">geotiff</a> and the other tools that
he contributes to is that we all as a community need to step up to
help.  I'm guessing that Frank and the Googlers he is around will be
stepping up some serious development, but I am sure there will be gaps
left behind of various sizes and types.

It's going to be a really exciting summer at Google and I'm hoping to also see
a lot of the NASA Ames crew (especially the Pirate Lab... Arrr!  a.k.a IRG).
