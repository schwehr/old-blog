For those like me who didn't know... there is a new <b>different</b> firefox plugin for <a href="http://delicious.com">delicious.com</a>: <a href="https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/delicious-extension/">Delicious Extension</a>.
<pre>Delicious Firefox 5.0 Extension (Beta) integrates your bookmarks
and tags with Firefox and syncs them for convenient access. For more
information, go to: www.avos.com/terms</pre>
And don't forget to regularly backup your bookmarks: <a href="https://secure.delicious.com/settings/bookmarks/export">https://secure.delicious.com/settings/bookmarks/export</a>.
