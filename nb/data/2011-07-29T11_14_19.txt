This program was signed back in March of the year.  OCR'ed text... expect typos.
<pre>The Federal Initiative for Navigation Data Enhancement (FINDE) is a joint Federal
effort to provide more complete, accurate and reliable navigation information
critical for providing an accurate picture of commercial cargo and vessel activity
on our Nation's waterways, enforcing regulations, and making decisions regarding
capital investment.
...
FINDE will leverage expertise, data, and services, to the extent permitted by law,
across the Internal Revenue Service (IRS), U.S. Coast Guard (USCG), and U.S.
Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) to support the strategic goals of multiple
Federal agencies.</pre>
