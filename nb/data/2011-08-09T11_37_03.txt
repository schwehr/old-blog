It was great to finally meet John Konrad, founder of <a
href="http://gcaptain.com">gCaptain</a> when he visited Google MTV
(Mountain View, CA).  John and I have "talked" online quite a bit over
the last few years via email.  I was never quite sure what part of the
world John was in when he was emailing me.  John and the gCaptain community
have been a great sounding board for me. It was even better to be able to
talk to him for several hours in the Google Cafe in the building where I worked.

I've been waiting for John to do a post on the Deepwater Horizon model
that is in Google Earth.

I am now back from my month of time at Google.  My brain is full up
with all sorts of new ideas and things I'd like to share about using
the Google infastructure from the outside.  I will be slowly working
through the ideas as I get them formulated, coded, and written.  A few
of the ideas will have to wait until Google is able to release some
new features.  They seem to have an endless supply of new things they
are innovating.  And I still have to catch up on a number of Google
I/O talks that are in my "to watch" list.
