The audio for Research Tools Lecture 5 is now online.  In this class
we talked about the Delicious bookmarking service, unpacked a tape
archive (tar), used the "file" command to indentify the type of data
inside a file (yes, that's confusing), used imagemagick's identify and
gdal's gdalinfo to look at geotagged images.  At the end, there is a
taste of emacs and starting to write shell scripts.
