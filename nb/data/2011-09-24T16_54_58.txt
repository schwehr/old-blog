I finally got to sit down at they keyboard and record a video that
goes through some of the basic keystrokes of using emacs (without the
mouse).  I couldn't find any introduction to emacs videos that I
really liked, so I tried my hand.  I am not totally satisfied with
this video, but I hope it is useful.  Feel free to add comments.
It's a bit long at 24 minutes, but it takes a while to go through even
the basics.
