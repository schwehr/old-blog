This video gives a quick introduction to modes in emacs.  Try out some
of the minor modes while working on a file of your choosing.
<pre>M-x auto-fill-mode &lt;--- wrap text
  C-u 4 0 C-x f  &lt;--- set fill column to 40
  M-q     &lt;--- refill / reflow paragraph
  M-o M-x &lt;--- center line
  M-x fill-region &lt;--- reflow all paragraphs in a region
M-x flyspell-mode &lt;--- turn on real time spell checking
M-x yas/minor-mode &lt;--- be able to paste commonly used text
M-x overwrite-mode &lt;--- step on text rather than new text</pre>
