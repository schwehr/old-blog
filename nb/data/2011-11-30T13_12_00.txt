I got early access to Google Scholar Citations, but in the craziness
of the semester, I forgot to set it up to take a look.  Here is
my Google Scholar Citation page:
<a href="http://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fXbCGw0AAAAJ">Kurt
Schwehr - Google Scholar Citations</a>.  The best part about waiting
till now to try it is that the service is open to all, so I'm not
talking about something that other people can't use.

<a
href=http://googlescholar.blogspot.com/2011/11/google-scholar-citations-open-to-all.html">Google
Scholar Citations Open To All</a> on the Google Scholar Blog, Nov 16, 2011.
