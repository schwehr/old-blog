Yesterday, Tami presented her masters research project as a part of
the CCOM seminar.  Over the last couple months, Tami has gotten really
good at using python to process full wave form single beam sonar.  I
watched her presentation over the <A href="http://www.ivs3d.com/">IVS
3D</a> GoToMeeting channel and grabbed a couple of screenshots that am
posting to show off what Tami did.  (Tami, I hope you don't mind!)
She did a great job of creating an algorithm in <b>python</b> to pick
the top of the eel grass and the "hard" bottom in her 2011 survey
lines.
