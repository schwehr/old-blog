I decided to try to at least one class every day of <a href="https://plus.google.com/112724324866129338402/about">Dawn Wright</a>'s
class: <a
href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/itunes-u/geographic-information-systems/id430913514?ls=1">Geographic
Information Systems and Science - GEO 580, OSU Geosciences (2010)</a>
(on iTunesU).  Dawn is now at ESRI as <a
href="http://www.esri.com/news/releases/11-2qtr/esri-appoints-dawn-wright-as-chief-scientist.html">ESRI
Chief Scientist</a>.  I haven't ever had the chance to meet her.  As a
total non-related point, I finally got to meet <a
href="http://www.mbari.org/staff/jgb/">Jim Bellingham</a> at AGU this
year.  I was supposed to meet him back 12 years ago or so, but AUV
operations and other things caused us to not cross paths till now.  As
is typical of AGU, I can't remember where it was or why I met him.

So far I'm 1.5 classes in and not sure what to think of it.  I cowrote
a GRASS GIS tutorial at NASA Ames in 1993 and was an Arc/Info user in
the 1994-5 time range at the USGS.  It might be good for me to think through
the material that she presents in her class, especially since she has a very different style than I do.

She also has these two courses on iTunesU: <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/itunes-u/geographic-information-systems/id430913080?ls=1">Geographic Information Systems and Science - GEO 465_565 2009</a> and
<a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/itunes-u/geovisualization-lecture-series/id430916428?ls=1">Geovisualization Lecture Series - Winter Geography/Geology Seminar 2009</a>.
