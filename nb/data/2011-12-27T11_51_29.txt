<a href="http://news.slashdot.org/story/11/12/27/143255/china-begins-using-new-global-positioning-satellites">China Begins Using New Global Positioning Satellites</a> [slashdot]

Seriously?  From the wikipedia entry on the Chinese version of GPS:
<ol>
<li>A signal is transmitted skyward by a remote terminal.</li>
<li>Each of the geostationary satellites receive the signal.</li>
<li>Each satellite sends the accurate time of when each received the signal to a ground station.</li>
<li>The ground station calculates the longitude and latitude of the remote terminal, and determines the altitude from a relief map.</li>
<li>The ground station sends the remote terminal's 3D position to the satellites.</li>
<li>The satellites broadcast the calculated position to the remote terminal.</li>
</ol>
There should be 1 more step: everyone who has hacked any encryption
keys can track all navigation people or devices.  Additionally, it
should also be possible to snoop the transmissions of the devices as they 
broadcast their requests for position also giving away positions.
Do they have the bandwidth and processing power to scale this is there 
end up being many many nodes all asking for positions?
This also means that units have to expend power to transmit when they want a
position.  Seems like a seriously flawed design.  But then again, if you
are the Chinese government and want to track everyone in the world, this
is a smart design.
