I don't know any more about this thank the CNN article.  Sometimes these
things are a big deal, other times not.  I'm reminded of the time that I
was IM'ing with Art T. while he and Roland were in the Black Sea and I got
the IM saying they were abanding ship because the captain finally admitted 
that the shaft seal had gone and the pumps were not keeping up.  I believe 
the last IM I got before they disconnected was "Roland says to tell
his wife that he loves her."  Needless to say, they were transfered to
Bob Ballards ship and treated to fine dinning, so they never were in
any real danger, but the ship they had been on was definitely a mess.
