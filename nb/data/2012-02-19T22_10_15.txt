I know, I know.  I work for Google and I should be making "decks" in
Google Docs with the Presentation tool.  I've been doing that, but
life would not be complete if I never tried making a LaTeX beamer
presentation.  Beamer, LaTeX, org and related are for those people who truly value the concept of
creating content without worrying about the form.  Content over form
for sure.   Sometimes the planets align (or I just had enough red wine) to step
from the known to the undefined of the new.  That, plus a blip in my RSS feeds, <a href="http://emacsworld.blogspot.com/2012/02/using-beamer-animations-to-simulate.html?">Using Beamer Animations to simulate Terminal Input and Output</a>So, I've finally taken the step.
I was really impressed with Dan Pineo's thesis presentations done with Beamer
while he was doing his PhD.  Now I need to take the plung and get emacspeak 
sending org to my Google docs account.

I used <a
href="http://www.math.umbc.edu/~rouben/beamer/quickstart-Z-H-3.html#node_sec_3"</a>A
Beamer Quickstart</a> by Rouben Rostamian.  Latex is not my favorite
markup as I definitely prefer org-mode, but I feel more complete now.
<pre><tt><i><font color="#9A1900">% -*- compile-command: "pdflatex helloworld-beamer.tex &amp;&amp; open helloworld-beamer.pdf" -*-</font></i>
<!-- -->
<b><font color="#0000FF">\documentclass</font></b><font color="#009900">{beamer}</font>
<b><font color="#0000FF">\usetheme</font></b><font color="#009900">{default}</font>
<b><font color="#0000FF">\begin</font></b><font color="#009900">{document}</font>
<!-- -->
<b><font color="#0000FF">\begin</font></b><font color="#009900">{frame}{A universal law}</font>
<!-- -->
Hello world!
<!-- -->
<b><font color="#0000FF">\end</font></b><font color="#009900">{frame}</font>
<b><font color="#0000FF">\end</font></b><font color="#009900">{document}</font></tt></pre>
Hello world is the universal constant.  Put the above into a file called helloworld-beamer.tex in emacs and use M-x compile (if you are on a Mac), so view the results.  On linux, change open to xdg-open.

<img width="600" height="513" title="Beamer Hello Worl" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2012-02/latex-beamer-helloworld.png"/>

And yes, you can do beamer right from org mode.  Just ask it to!
<pre>#+LaTeX_CLASS: beamer
<!-- -->
* A universal constant
<!-- -->
-Hello world</pre>
Then just do <b>C-c C-e d</b> and you'll be looking at a PDF of your
presentation.  I expected there to be an export to beamer under the
latex options, but alas, I was surprised to see that you have to 
set beamer as an option.
