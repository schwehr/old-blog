Tomorrow is the 100th anniversary of the sinking of the <a
href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMS_Titanic">R.M.S. Titanic</a>.
There has been tons of press coverage leading up to this date.  I've
been posting some of this on a Google Plus (G+) page: <a
href="https://plus.google.com/u/0/b/104429686404863416366/">G+ RMS
Titanic</a>.  One nice Google Earth example is posted on the Google
Earth Blog (not a part of Google): <a
href="http://www.gearthblog.com/blog/archives/2012/04/the_voyage_of_the_titanic_in_google.html">The
voyage of the Titanic in Google Earth</a>

Today, in honor of the memory of the Titanic, Google released a LatLon
blog post, <a
href="http://google-latlong.blogspot.de/2012/04/remembering-titanic.html">Remembering
the Titanic</a>, that includes a tour of the site that is now in the Google Earth Gallery:
<a href="http://tinyurl.com/titanic-earth-gallery">http://tinyurl.com/titanic-earth-gallery</a>

<img width="600" height="342" title="Titanic in the Gallery" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2012-04/titanic-google-earth-gallery.png"/>

<img width="600" height="392" title="Touring the Titanic" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2012-04/titanic-tour.png"/>

If you are not familiar with tours, hopefully, this will help.  Open
up the folders until you see the picture of the camera and "Titanic
Tour" that I've circled in yellow below.

I suggest you first sit back and let Jenifer take you on the tour in this Google Oceans YouTube video!

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="http://www.youtube.com/embed/2qT5fddzELU" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Additionally, the Titanic can now be found via the search window in
the top left corner of Google Earth.  Make sure to have at least the 
"Shipwrecks" layer enabled (if not all of them).

<img width="600" height="336" title="Finiding the Titanic in Earth" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2012-04/titanic-ocean-layer.png"/>

There is also an embedded video in the shipwreck popup.  Check out all
three tabs.  Take a look at your favorite parts of the ocean for ship
wrecks.  

<img width="600" height="329" title="Finiding the Titanic in Earth" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2012-04/titanic-ocean-layer-video.png"/>

I hope you enjoy the experience of learning some key history.  The
suffering of the people who were on Titanic led to many improvements
that we count on today to be safe while out at sea.
