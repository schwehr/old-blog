I just realized that I never blogged about my "appearance" on talk of
Alaska last month.  I was visiting the Google Cambridge, MA offices
and they called me in for a 1 hour discussion show that aired across
Alaska.  Thanks to Bernie for dropping me an email durning the show to
let me know that he was hearing me on the radio :)  I am on through
out the show with Ray being the 1st half hour and Samantha the 2nd half.

<a
href="http://www.alaskapublic.org/2012/03/16/oil-spill-lessons/">Oil
Spill Lessons</a> hosted by Steve Heimel with guests: Kurt Schwehr,
Ray Highsmith, and Samantha Joye.
<pre>In the 23 years since the tanker Exxon Valdez hit the reef, oil
spill prevention has improved greatly in Alaska. Nobody wants another
spill.  But if it happens will we be ready to respond?  Two years ago,
a spill in the Gulf of Mexico showed there is still room for
improvement.</pre>
