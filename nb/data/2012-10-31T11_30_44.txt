And in case you know of any AIS Application Specific Messages
(e.g. Msg 6, 8, 25, or 26) that are out there in use, but not
documented, please register them.  I appreciate emails about such
messages, but I only proposed the registry and do not run a registry.
Please see:

<a href="http://www.iala-aism.org/iala/files/newitems3.php">IALA-AISM AIS Application Specific Messages:</a> "Collection of regional applications for AIS Application Specific Messages of regional applications for AIS Binary Messages.

<a href="http://www.e-navigation.nl/asm">http://www.e-navigation.nl/asm</a>

Remember that when creating AIS messages to: 
<ul>
<li>Include sample AIVDM messages and the associated decodes</li>
<li>Have your messages be 8 bit aligned</li>
<li>Reviewed by an outsider (by writing a decoder) to make sure they are understandable</li>
<li>If you change the bits of a message, use a new dac/fi pair</li>
<li>use the minimum amount of spare</li>
<li>keep it simple</li>
<li>develop the message in the open and publish the spec for free on the internet (not behind any pay or click through nonsense)</li>
<li>Do NOT create new dac/fi pairs for existing messages.  Just don't.  It is okay to use another country's DAC/FI pair in your country.  Really.</li>
</ul>
