So here was the comment that I posted:

This kind of research has been done for quite a while at UNH Center
for Coastal and Ocean Mapping (CCOM). Roland Arsenault, Dan Pineo,
Matt Plumlee, Colin Ware and others there have done eye tracking,
modeling the human vision system task performance, and much more with
a strong bent towards what tasks mariners perform. (note: I worked at
CCOM from 2005-11 and am still Affiliate Faculty w/ CCOM). Check out
the AR Simulator and Flow Vis projects for starters and PhD theses
from Matt Plumlee and Dan Pineo.

It's great to see more people getting into the topic!

See more at: <a href="http://ccom.unh.edu/vislab/index.html">CCOM VisLab</a>.
