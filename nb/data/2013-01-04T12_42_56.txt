Need a new years resolution?  Why not make a resolution that is easy
to follow through on?  Get involved with science!  It's easy.  You can
do it from home.  On your desktop or laptop.  You don't need a diver
certification or to get wet.  No science degree required.  Humans come
with some pretty awesome built-in science analysis equipment.

My good friend <a href="http://cshel.geology.udel.edu/">Art Trembanis</a> has <a
href="http://subseaobservers.com">SubseaObservers</a> where you can help them
identify scallops on the sea floor.  This stuff is really important.  The results
will help human's protect the sea and understand how to manage critical resources in the 
ocean.  These images from an Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) have a lot more than just
scallops.  What else can you discover?

<img width="544" height="600" title="Subsea Observers" withgrayborder="True" src="http://schwehr.org/blog/attachments/2013-01/subsea-observers.png"/>

Last week, I got to sit down with Richard Taylor of the GeoHab
project.  GeoHab uses a sled with cameras towed behind a ship.  More
cool imagery and another project where anyone can lend a hand:
<a href="http://www.seafloorexplorer.org/">Seafloor Explorer</a>.
