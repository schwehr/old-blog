If you've wondered why my blogging has decrease for the last while,
here is one of several of the reasons.  I've been working hard on
material for Google I/O.  I am super excited that we will be sharing
examples of how to work with geospatial ocean data at Google IO.  We
built some demonstrations using AIS data show of quite a few of the
cloud services that Google offers.  I look forward to hearing how
the community uses these features and what kinds of data they are
working with!
