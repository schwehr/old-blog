I have a long term goal of automating more of my machine setup notes.
I do a lot to new machines and I rarely get to where I think I should be.
For example, what makes the best ntp setup?  And do I have emacs24 in my 
new Ubuntu 12.04 LTS virtual machine?

I missed out on going to the <a
href="http://puppetlabs.com/resources/puppetconf-2013">2013 PuppetConf
in San Francisco</a>, but the conference just posted videos of the
talks.  I just watched <a
href="http://puppetlabs.com/presentations/getting-started-puppet">Getting
Started with Puppet</a>.  The talk doesn't really match what I would
like to see in a getting started, but it's still great.

<iframe width="640" height="480" src="//www.youtube.com/embed/TdAmAj3eaFI" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>
