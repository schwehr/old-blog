I just recently got to fly from West Coast to East Coast (US that is).  I always enjoy
trying to capture good pictures when I have access to a window.  A few
years back, I always seemed to get people sitting next to me that
claimed there was no way to get good photos from a commercial flight.
Thankfully that has dropped off.

Images taken with an iPhone 4S.  The color is never quite up to what I
see with the eye and iPhoto's color tools are not good enough to do
much of a fix.
