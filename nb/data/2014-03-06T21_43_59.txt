Last time I went looking for Software Defined Radios (SDR) to play
with, the price was in the $300-$500 and up.  That is quite different
than some of the options we have today.  I just ordered at <a
href="http://www.nooelec.com/store/software-defined-radio/sdr-receivers/tv28tv2-sdr-dvb-t-usb-stick-set.html">NooElec
NESDR Mini SDR & DVB-T USB Stick (R820T) w/ Antenna and Remote
Control</a>.  It's a R820T tuner IC made by Rafael Micro with a
frequency range of ~ 25MHz to 1750MHz.  It apparently can output <a href="http://sdr.osmocom.org/trac/wiki/rtl-sdr">2.5M
S/s (million samples per second)</a>.
